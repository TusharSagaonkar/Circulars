Circular CR No. 340 dated 27th December 2024

Operational Guidelines for Gold Loans/SODGLD

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Loans and advances sanctioned against pledge of Gold Ornaments are considered as the safest finance.

During past few years our Bank had detected some frauds in Gold Loans/SODGLD, where pledged gold ornaments were found to be spurious i.e. silver ornaments with gold plating. The same was detected at the time of auction of the Gold after the accounts turned NPA.

In order to overcome such lapses in gold valuation and appraisal processes, we have framed Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Gold Loans/ SODGLD.

The SOP will be made effective with immediate effect. All branches should follow the procedure scrupulously while sanctioning Gold Loans/SODGLD at the branch and also for post disbursement follow up.

Guidelines regarding recovery procedure in case the account turns into NPA are also included in the SOP. Branches and recovery dept. should follow the same.

Detailed SOP for Gold Loans/ SODGLD is as under:–

Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Gold Loans/ Limits

Gold loans have emerged as a popular financial product in our country, allowing individuals to unlock the value of their gold jewellery or ornaments to meet their immediate financial needs. The gold loan process in India provides a convenient and accessible way for individuals to access funds quickly, often without the need for extensive documentation. This collateral-based approach allows lenders to offer loans with minimal risk, as the value of gold serves as security against the loan amount.

In order to lay down the procedure from processing till closure of loan against gold which each of staff member should follow a standard operating procedure is framed as under.

At present our bank has under mentioned three types of scheme against gold ornaments:-

1)  Bullet type loan, product type defined as BGLLN.

2)  EMI type loan, product type defined as EGLLN.

3)  Limit type facility, product type defined as SODGLD.

Pre-sanction & Disbursement Stage:-

1)  Eligibility :-

Borrower should be customer of the bank and whose KYC documentation is updated & operation in the account is satisfactory. One has to check that KYC documents is complied with latest guidelines before proceeding further. No need of taking KYC documents & photo again.

The said scheme is for individual person’s and proprietorship firms.

Borrower (individual person) should be above 18 years.

2)  Income Proof :-

Declaration of income from the concerned customer for the facility upto Rs.2.00 lakh to be obtained, where income proof is not on record. Otherwise, latest ITR to be obtained to arrive repayment capacity.

3)  Loan/Limit Amount :-

     i.        Bullet type facility Minimum loan amount is Rs.10,000/- and Maximum loan amount is Rs.2,00,000/-.

   ii.        EMI type facility Minimum loan amount is Rs.10,000/- and Maximum loan amount is Rs.10,00,000/-.

  iii.        Overdraft type facility Minimum loan amount is Rs.25,000/- and Maximum loan amount is Rs.10,00,000/-

4)  Rate of Interest :-

          As per latest ILA.

5)  Repayment Period :-

Repayment period is one year.

     i.        For bullet type facility Principal and interest amount can be repaid at end of one year.

   ii.        For EMI type facility EMI will be paid on monthly basis for a year.

  iii.        For Overdraft facility Interest will be paid on monthly basis.

Ever greening of loan should not be done.

6)  LTV ratio

Loan to gold ornaments value (LTV) will not be more than 75% i.e. minimum margin of 25% on gold value to be maintained.

Credit Monitoring department has to monitor that minimum margin of 25% is maintained on running basis. In case the margin is reduced then the same has to be informed to the branch for recovering the difference amount. Department has to follow the same till the required margin is maintained. The cases where LTV is not maintained, the same to be reported to CEO on monthly basis. In case LTV is not maintained for more than 90 days, then such accounts should be marked as NPA.

 

7)  Security

Loan/ Limit is sanctioned against pledge of gold ornaments only, it cannot be sanctioned against gold coin / biscuit/ gold bar etc.

Self-declaration to be obtained from the borrower that the gold ornaments belong to him/ her and her family member. In case of one of the gold ornament is in the form of Mangalsutra and borrower is male then NOC from his spouse to be taken and kept on record.

 

8)  Gold Value Assessment

Gold value will be determined on the basis of average of the closing price of 22 carats gold for the preceding 30 days as quoted by the Indian Bullions and Jewellers Association.

Gold ornaments of only 18 carat and its above carat are acceptable. On the basis of 22 carat, gold value of below carat gold, will be worked-out on proportionate basis and for above 22 carat gold, the value of 22 carat gold to be taken.

Credit department will assess the gold price and provide the price of gold on monthly basis.

9)  Purpose

For Bullet & EMI type loans

For any legitimate purpose, not for speculative purpose.

For Limit type facility

For business/consumption purpose only.

 

10)     Membership

For loan/ limit upto Rs.1.00 lakh, customer to be admitted as Nominal member of the bank. Branch has to check existing consumer loan outstanding of the borrower and in case consumer loan outstanding amount and proposed gold loan is more than Rs.1.00 lakh, then in such cases borrower has to become regular member of the bank with minimum required number of shares.

If the Borrower is already a member of the Bank and sanctioned credit facility below Rs. 1.00 Lakh, share linkage is applicable for such loans.

For loan/ limit above Rs.1.00 lakh, he/ she has to be admitted as regular member of the bank and has to maintain share linkage @2.5% of the loan amount/ limit.

Incase, he/she is already regular member then branch has to check that he/ she should be holding minimum required number of bank shares, otherwise he has to purchase the difference of minimum required shares. Overall in case loan/ limit of above Rs.1.00 lakh, then borrower has to maintain share linkage on total loan outstanding, linkage @2.5% on secured loan and @5% on unsecured loans.

11)     Documentation:-

Before execution of below mentioned documents, staff has to inform the customer about the procedure of the loan such as required margin, documentation charges, applicable membership fee, valuer charges etc.

Concerned documents to be filled except valuation part and loan amount before execution of loan documents:-

     i.        Application-cum-Agreement for loan on Gold ornaments. The said document is filled & executed with another two copies (i.e. total three copy of said application), one with white colour copy to be franked as per stamp act of the concerned state, pink copy to be provided to the concerned customer and third yellow copy to be kept with gold packet bag. The said document to be executed by the concerned borrower and valuer has to mention about description of the gold ornaments along with gross weight & net weight, gold rate, certified value under his/her signature.

          Nominee related column in Application form to be filled and executed by the borrower, without fail.

   ii.        Demand Promissory Note to be executed on 1-rupee revenue stamp by the customer and he/ she has to sign across the revenue stamp and another signature to be taken near to the revenue stamp, not across the stamp, i.e. two signature to be taken on DP note.

  iii.        Letter of undertaking from the concerned borrower w.r.t declaration and consent for sharing of credit facilities to the Credit Information Agencies etc.

 iv.        Standing instruction to be executed by the borrower for repayment of proposed loan EMI/ interest.

For overdraft against gold ornaments, Application for Secured Overdraft facility against Gold documents to be executed and franked as per stamp act of the concerned state along with documents as mentioned above from Sr no. ii to iv by the borrower.

Branch will issue sanction letter to the customer with above terms & conditions.

12)     Service Charges :-

          As per service charges manual.

13)     Gold Appraisal Charges :-

          As per service charges manual.

Customer should directly pay to the gold valuer for their charges from their account.

Note :- No cash payment as appraisal charges to be paid to the valuer’s, it should be routed through customer account with us only.

Procedure for appraising gold ornaments & keeping gold ornaments in the custody of the bank:-

1.   Appointed gold valuer of the branch, will assess the gold ornaments for its purity and will arrive at the net weight of the gold from gross weight as per gold weight machine in the branch. Gold value will be arrived on the basis of the gold rate provided by the HO-Credit Department of the bank. Gold valuer will mention the type of each ornaments and certify its gross weight & net weight and put his signature as valuer certification on concerned loan documents.

         

          BM and officer level staff also cross verify & confirm the gross weight of the           ornaments.

 

2.   Gold ornaments to be kept in the cloth bags (made for keeping gold ornaments) with one copy (yellow) of Application-cum-Agreement for Gold Ornaments/ Application for Secured Overdraft Facility Against Gold Ornaments, in presence of concerned customer/ borrower, gold valuer & branch officials and put branch seals thereon. A small tag slip with borrower name, loan account number & date, which is to be signed by borrower, branch official and valuer to be tagged with the gold bags.   

          Kindly note that gold ornaments are to be sealed in front of borrower, gold valuer & branch officials. At the same time, one copy (Pink) of Application of           Gold loan/ limit to be given to the borrower without any delay.

3.   Record of gold packet kept & taken in branch custody to be maintained on daily basis and outstanding gold packets to be tallied with physically gold packets.

 

4.   Proceeds’ of the gold loan to be credited to the borrowers account with us and necessary charges to be recovered.

Procedure to be followed during Post-disbursement of loan/ limit:-

1.   Branch should monitor the repayment of dues of the loan and also monitor the limit of accounts.

2.   2) In case of default in repayment of EMI/ Interest, branch should immediately follow up with the concerned borrower on regular basis. In case of default for more than one EMI/ Interest, then branch has to issue overdue/ overdrawn letter to the concerned customer.

3.   In case the account turns into NPA, then branch should immediately follow-up with the borrower for clearance of over dues and issue a letter informing about NPA account status & also inform for clearance of overdues. Whenever branch feels that recovery of amount is not possible, then they have to transfer the account to recovery department for taking further course of action. However, branch should not take more than three months for clearance of overdues at their end.

 

 

4.   Testing the purity of Gold ornaments and valuation:-

          Branch should get cross verification of purity and weight done at least 10 packets of pledge ornaments picked up at random basis and not take cross           verified earlier packets, once in a six month where loan/ limit was disbursed during last six month, through another approved valuer of the bank.

          Verification of gold packets should be done in presence of joint custodians at the base branch, gold valuer of base branch, invited gold valuer, concerned           borrower’s whose packet will be cross checked and one official of other          department other than the gold loan department.

          After re-checking of gold loan packets, the same should be packed and sealed in   presence of concerned borrower(s), both gold valuer and bank officials. The   record of the same to be maintained In case of any discrepancy/ anomaly observed, the same to be reported to the head of the credit department and also           to audit department.

Gold Valuer’s Charges for verification of ornaments as under:-

i) For checking 10 gold packets or less in a day

a. Invited gold valuer  - Rs. 1000/- on lumpsum basis

b. Gold valuer attached to the branch - Rs. 500/- on lumpsum basis

ii) For checking beyond 10 gold packets in a day

a.   Invited gold valuer - Rs.100/- for each additional gold packet

b.   Gold valuer attached to the branch - Rs.50/- for each additional gold packet

          Whenever invited gold valuer is required to travel from a distance beyond           25 kms from base branch, then apart from the charges, he/ she should also be     reimbursed reasonable travelling expenses, which is decided by the concerned          branch. Concerned branch has to bear such gold valuers charges and travelling     charges.

          Audit department staff during visit to the branches for audit purpose, should           also re-check purity of gold ornaments and valuation, such that a minimum of      10 packets or 10% of existing gold loan and gold limit account’s should be covered, whichever is on higher side. Audit staff will only select the packets and do the re-check for purity & weight of gold ornaments in their joint custody with branch officials, visiting gold valuer who will check the purity & value and           another gold valuer which have done earlier valuation of concerned loan/ limit account. A list of rechecked accounts to be part of audit report.

5.   On closure of loan/limit, gold packets to be handed over to the concerned borrower and his/her signature to be taken in the register and it should be mentioned in the register that gold ornaments are taken in good condition and

 

 

as per articles mentioned in concerned Application-cum-Agreement / Application for Secured Overdraft facility. Also, it is to be mentioned in the register that in future concerned borrower will not complain against the bank/ branch against gold ornaments type, condition, weight.

          In-case of non-presence of borrower, the gold ornaments can be handed over to      close family member after taking authority letter of the borrower, authorised in      favour of such person along with KYC documents & photo of the person who is taking gold ornaments on behalf of the borrower.

          Before releasing the gold ornaments, direct & indirect liability(ies) of the borrower to be checked, in case of any other liability then the approval of the           branch manager to be taken and in-case of overdue then approval from next           higher authority (controller of the branch) to be taken.

          No need to maintain other register, other than register which maintain record of   daily packets and another register for release of gold packets under signature of        the borrower.

6.   In-case pledge ornaments are not taken by the borrower after closure of accounts, then safe custody charges will be applicable at the rate of Rs.500/- + GST per month. Charges will be applicable after sending of notice to the borrower that charges are applicable in case of failure of taking back of gold ornaments within fourteen days from receipt of such notice.

Recovery Action in case of account turns NPA & recovery of dues:-

1.   Whenever recovery dept. receive the intimation from the branch for further course of action of the gold loan/ limit accounts where account turns NPA, then in such cases department should take steps to recover the dues at the earliest.

2.   2) After taking observation of steps taken by the branch for recovery of dues from gold loan/ limit accounts, they immediately follow the recovery procedure so that banks dues are recovered within a six months from NPA date.

3.   Firstly, department will issue the notice to borrower for auction of gold ornaments within 15 days.

4.   Then, through publishing the final notice, the recovery department will give 21 days time to borrower to repay the dues otherwise, department will go for auction.

5.   In-case borrower fails to clear the dues, then department will carry gold auction and inform the concerned parties for the same. Auction will be conducted by bank officials in the rank of Senior management and under appointed gold valuer. Auction procedure is followed as per procedure laid down by the bank. Gold packets can be collected within two working days before auction date, if loan/ limit is still not closed.

         

          Value of gold ornaments should be taken of latest gold value as quoted by the    Indian Bullions and Jewellers Association on the date of auction. In case           market value of gold value is in decreasing trend from last few days, approval of         the CEO to be taken for gold value to be considered.

          Gold valuer who has done earlier valuation / revaluation of concerned gold           packets (of auction accounts) shall be invited for auction so that packet will be     opened in their presence and in case they are not coming then we have to           inform them about their responsibility in case of any discrepancy(ies) found in      gold packets.

6.   In-case of auction success, the auction money will be credited to loan/limit account for clearance of total dues (including auction charges etc) and if any balance left over then the same to be transferred / paid to the concerned borrower deposit account, accordingly borrower has to informed about our actions. In case of short fall towards the dues, then department has to follow-up with the borrower for the remaining dues. In case of failure, the department should take immediate steps for filing of case under Arbitration.

7.   In-case of failure of auction, then auction can be re-arranged after a period of 15 days after re-issuing of required notice to the borrower & news paper publication.

8.   Intimation of auction money and outstanding balance amount with interest to be informed to the concerned borrower and record of the same to be kept by the department.

All branches/recovery dept. are instructed to take note of the SOP for Gold Loan/SODGLD and follow the same.
