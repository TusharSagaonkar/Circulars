Circular CR No. 338 dated 28th November 2024

 

Monitoring of End-Use of Funds

 

     Reserve Bank of India is time and again instructing the Bank’s to ensure proper end use of funds. Our earlier guidelines / credit policy also cover the said guidelines.

     It is to be noted that credit facility is to be sanctioned for productive purpose and not for speculative purpose. While sanctioning credit facility, branch official(s)/ recommending authority/ sanctioning authority, they have to ensure that the end use of funds which is to be lent is justifiable. Further, disbursement authority has to ensure that disbursement amount is utilised for the purpose for which credit facility is sanctioned.

     So, it was instructed to all staff members to ensure end use of the funds which is to be lent by the bank.

1)  For term loans other than consumer loan upto Rs.2.00 lakh & house repair loan upto Rs.5.00 lakh.

     For sanctioning of term loan type of advances, it is ensured at the time of processing / preparation of scrutiny note and sanction note, concerned staff(s) has to clearly specify the purpose for which term loan is to sanctioned and where the funds are to be credited / transferred favouring vendor / seller etc.

     Disbursement officer’s shall take due care while disbursement and confirm that the purpose and details mentioned in processing and scrutiny note are the same. On disbursement of loan & release of payment and inter-alia has to certify in the end of disbursement note by mentioning the details as “funds are released as per terms and conditions of the sanction and they have ensure the end use of the funds” in below mentioned format :-

Note :- Where payment is made for purchase of property / materials / machinery / equipments/ vehicle etc; payment receipt/ tax invoice of the same to be kept on record.

For term loan, where payment terms is in the form of part payment, then branch has to check before release of another payment that end use of earlier payment is ensured and further payment are to be made as per terms & condition. For e.g. in case of housing loan / shop loan where stage wise payment is to be made as per completion of work, then branch has to ensure that architect certificate for status of work completion for which payment is called and payment to be made as per terms & condition of sanction, latest inspection report of property is on record, at least latest of last three months.

2)  In case of consumer loan upto Rs.2.00 lakh and house repair loan upto Rs.5.00 lakh, end use can be certify on the basis of declaration.

     In case of loan towards purchase of consumer durable, the payment need’s to be made directly to the vendor and supported by tax invoice / bill need’s to be taken on record confirming end use of fund. In case of house repair / renovation, during processing of loan proposal, a self declaration is to be obtained towards estimated work and on disbursement, a self declaration regarding utilisation of funds needs is to be obtained from borrower.

3)  In case of credit facility where type of facility is of limit type and facility is for Working Capital requirement.

     In case of credit facility where type of facility sanctioned is of limit type and facility is for Working Capital requirement, then in such cases branch has to ensure that each debit transaction is for business purpose and payment is released towards creditor’s payment or related to the business and not for other purpose. List of creditors to be entered in CBS system for new working capital limit. Initially at least for three months, branch has to certify that every payment is made towards business purpose such as creditor’s payment etc.

4)  In case of credit facility where type of facility is of limit type other than Working Capital requirement

     For other type of limit facility, branch has to certify at least for three months that funds are utilised for the purpose for which credit facility is sanctioned for.

5)  In case of credit facility against Gold ornaments / NSC’s / LIP’s / KVP / Bank own Term Deposit / Liquid assets etc.

     No separate certificate toward end use of fund is needed. Branch has to obtain self declaration and monitor the end use of the funds ensuring that it should not be used for speculative purpose and unproductive purpose.

 

In case such as mortgage loan / others, where customer is not willing to disclose the proper purpose / end utilisation, such proposals shall not be entertained.

The concurrent / internal auditor has to cross verify and check that the end use of funds in each and every sanctioned file and give their confirmation in this regard.

In case of failure to adherence of mentioned guidelines in the circular, strict action against concerned staff(s) will be taken.

 

Earlier circular in the context of end use of funds is superseded with issue of this circular.

Kindly bring the contents of this circular to the notice of each and every member of staffs of your branch/ department/ cluster/ zone.
