Circular CR No. 277 dated 28th June 2021

 

Gold Loan & SOD against Pledge of Gold Ornaments

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

 

I.   Please refer our Circular CR No. 268 dated 26th February, 2021 wherein we had given eligibility of Loan/ SOD amount for advance against pledge of gold ornaments.

 

II. It may be noted that price of Gold in the bullion market varies from time to time. Eligibility of granting Gold Loan under EGLLN/BGLLN/SODGLD is therefore revised from time to time ensuring that it is in conformity with instructions of RBI which inter alia stipulate that Loan to Value ratio (LTV) should not exceed 75.

 

III. Gold ornaments/jewellery to be pledged with us as security are required to be valued at the average of the closing price of 22 carat gold for the preceding 30 days as quoted by the Indian Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd. If the gold is of purity less than 22 carat, it needs to be valued proportionately. However, if the gold is of purity higher than 22 carat, eligibility of loan / SODGLD amount for such gold ornaments shall remain same as that for 22 carat gold.  

 

IV. Keeping in view the latest trend in the market, the proposal to revise the eligibility was placed before the Board Meeting held on 11th June 2021 wherein it was decided that branches/ Credit Cell Dept. shall sanction, w. e. f. 1st July, 2021, loans and SOD against gold ornaments as per the table below :-

V. Whenever there are drastic changes in the price of gold, branches will be advised purity-wise new eligible loan amounts accordingly.

VI. Monitoring of existing Gold Loans/SOD GOLD Accounts:

1.   While the new eligibility given in table as per Para III above is higher than that advised through the previous circular under reference, it should always be kept in view that eligibility may go down sometimes. Therefore, outstanding amounts in Gold Loan/SODGOLD accounts should be constantly monitored to ensure that they do not exceed the level permissible worked out on the basis of the ruling eligibility at the material time. If outstanding amount in any account is more than that worked out according to the ruling eligibility, the excess amount should be recovered immediately from the borrower.

2.   Exercise as described in sub-Para 1. above will have to be done each time there is reduction in value of the gold ornaments as worked out on the basis of the latest circularised eligibility.

3.   Needless to add that interest on SODGOLD is to be recovered every month.

 

VII. In terms of circular no. RBI/2014-15/283 UBD.BPD.(PCB).Cir.No.25/ 13.05.001/ 2014-15 dated 30th October, 2014, RBI has increased the quantum of loan that could be granted under the Bullet Repayment Scheme up to Rs. 2 Lakh. Branches are therefore advised as under: -

1.   The maximum loan under Bullet Repayment Scheme will be Rs. 2.00 Lakh for a period not exceeding 12 months.

2.   Loan and SOD against gold ornaments other than Bullet Repayment Scheme will also be for a period not exceeding 12 months.

3.   As the period of limit for SOD against gold ornaments will also be for 12 months, limits should be renewed for another year with new documentation prescribing new date of their expiry. The SOD limit will be renewed subject to account being regular.

4.   Renewal should generally be done one month before expiry of the limit and operations in the account should be allowed only for a maximum of 30 days from expiry of the limit. In case of non-renewal, notice for renewal should be sent. Account will be treated as NPA beyond 90 days’ overdue status as per extant IRAC norms.

5.   Maximum amount of gold loan and SOD against gold ornaments should not exceed Rs. 25.00 Lakh. The maximum amount of gold loan under Bullet Repayment Scheme is already mandated not to exceed Rs. 2.00 Lakh. Branch-in-charges are advised to ensure that these limits are strictly adhered to and stipulations regarding (a) maximum period of 12 months, (b) renewal of SOD limit to be done before expiry of the limit.

6.   All loanees availing Loan/SOD limit exceeding Rs. 1.00 Lakh should be made Regular Members of the Bank.

7.   Branches should remain prompt & vigilant in respect of servicing by customers of interest & principal. All other instructions in regard to Gold Loan with Bullet Repayment & other Loan/SOD against Gold Ornaments issued from time to time remain unchanged.

1. Without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, we mention following instructions, at the cost of repetition, in view of their breach observed on several occasions.

i.     Only one SODGOLD facility is to be granted to an individual.

ii.   Till further instructions, no Gold loan, SODGOLD, or BGLLN loan should be sanctioned for the purpose of House repair.

 

2. All other instructions, in regard to Gold loan/SODGOLD issued from time to time remain unchanged and must be followed. In this connection, attention of branches is invited to the instructions contained in the “Credit Policy W. E. F. 15.7.2019”.

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch and ensure compliance.
