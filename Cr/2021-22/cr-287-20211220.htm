Circular CR No. 287 dated 20th December 2021

Reversal of Entries in respect of

Expired Bank Guarantees for

Management of Risk Capital

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

       I.   Attention is invited to extant instructions issued repeatedly through circulars and IDCs on the captioned subject. Important aspects thereof are summarised hereunder.

1.     Outstanding Bank Guarantees constitute contingent liability of Bank.

2.     Regulations require capital of Bank to be earmarked for contingent liabilities including Bank guarantees.

3.    i. Apart from accounting of Bank Guarantees issued, there is a system in Bank

for accounting of contingent liability towards any Bank Guarantee by passing contra entries as under at the time it is issued.

Debit :- Constituent liability for acceptance, endorsement and other obligations as per contra (Accounting Code:-AEODR)

Credit :- Acceptance Endorsement and other obligations as per contra (Accounting Code:- AEOCR)

ii. Bank’s outlay of capital on account of contingent liability for outstanding Bank Guarantees is computed on the basis of debit balance in the “Acceptance Endorsement and other obligations as per contra (Accounting Code:- AEOCR)”. Therefore, as and when a Bank Guarantee including claim period expires, apart from regular entries through the relative Guarantee Account, following contra entries towards reversal are also passed so that computation of contingent liability regarding the same gets appropriately reduced.

Debit: Acceptance Endorsement and other obligations as per contra (Accounting Code: -AEOCR)

Credit: Constituent liability for acceptance, endorsement and other obligations as per contra (Accounting Code: - AEODR)

     II.   It is regretted that branches are not paying attention to the expired BGs in spite of clear instructions in this behalf. This results in inflating Bank’s contingent liabilities leading to avoidable outlay of capital funds on their account and depriving Bank of other opportunities of credit expansion due to blockage of capital.

    III.   In view of the above, we are reiterating the procedure in respect of expired BGs under the instant circular.

 

(Note-A :- Any Bank guarantee will be deemed to have expired only after 12 months’ claim period which is specifically provided vide Circular CR No. 186 dated 24th March 2017.

 

Note-B :- Only computation of overall liability is dealt with hereunder. Passing of entries at the time of issuance of individual Bank Guarantees and their subsequent extinguishment upon expiry, will have to be done as otherwise prescribed).

 

1.  Expired Inland Bank Guarantee:

When a Bank Guarantee including claim period expires, there are 3 possible scenarios which require dealing with as under.

            i.   Original Bank Guarantee, duly discharged by the Beneficiary, is returned upon expiry

a.  In such a case, Branch must take immediate steps to pass the reversal entries as mentioned in Para I.3.ii. above.

 

b. At present, we are not issuing Bank Guarantees through SFMS. When we become active on the said platform, suitable instructions will be issued in that behalf.

          ii.   Original Bank Guarantee is not returned by the beneficiary. Instead, a discharge letter is provided by the beneficiary stating that the Bank Guarantee stands cancelled in their books.

a.  In such a case, branch must take immediate steps to pass the reversal entries as per Para I.3.ii. on the day of receipt of the discharge letter from the Beneficiary.

b. However, immediately upon passing the necessary entries, branch should send an email as well as a letter as per Annexure-A by Registered Post with AD, to the beneficiary informing about closure of the Bank Guarantee from Bank’s end.

         iii.   Neither Original Bank Guarantee nor Discharge letter is provided by the Beneficiary

a.   In such a case, Branch must send a notice as per Annexure-B hereto, through Registered Post AD, to the beneficiary immediately upon expiry of the Guarantee period including claim period, asking the beneficiary to return the Bank Guarantee duly discharged within 10 days of the date of the notice, clearly stating that the Bank guarantee period has expired. Simultaneously, notice is also required to be sent to the Applicant, through Registered Post, AD for the Bank Guarantee as per Annexure-C hereto.

b.   If there is no response from the beneficiary, Branch should send a reminder as per Annexure-D hereto (again through Registered Post with AD) after a period of 15 days from the date of first notice giving the beneficiary 7 days’ time to return the Bank Guarantee duly discharged.

c.    In case of no response even after the second letter, the Branch should pass contra entries as per Para I.3.ii. This must take place within 30 days from the date of the first notice.

 

Note-1: - However, in case of Expired Bank Guarantees, which also includes claim period where there is restraining order of a competent court of Law, the accounting entries should not be reversed and Bank would have to abide by the order of the Court in this regard.

 

Note-2 :- Henceforth, the revised versions of letters, which are to be sent upon expiry of claim period of any Bank Guarantee, as per Annexures A, B, C and D attached hereto alone shall be used by branches. The previous versions thereof (see Circular CR No. 49 dated 26th August 2011) should not be used.

 

2. Closure of expired Foreign Bank guarantees:

i.   Immediately upon expiry of a Bank Guarantee, Branch should send a SWIFT message to the Advising/Beneficiary Bank as under and request for acknowledgement:

"Bank Guarantee (with reference), has expired and no claim has been received by our Bank within the validity of BG. The beneficiary is therefore no more entitled to invoke the guarantee and Bank is closing the BG at our end”

ii.  In case, no acknowledgement is received from the Advising/Beneficiary Bank, Branch should send a reminder SWIFT after 7 days from the date of first SWIFT message. In case no response is received even after second message, Branch should pass the reversal entries within 15 days from the date of first SWIFT message.

 

    IV.   Bank guarantees issued through other banks, whether Inland or Foreign

We are issuing Bank Guarantees, inland as well as foreign, through other banks. In case of expiry of such Bank guarantees, the entries shall not be reversed unless confirmation in that behalf is received through our Forex Department, Dadar.

 

     V.   Regional Offices & Zonal Offices should keep a track of expired BGs in branches under them and follow up for passing of appropriate reversal entries in related account.

 

    VI.   Please bring the contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned and ensure meticulous compliance.
