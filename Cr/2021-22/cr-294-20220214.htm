Circular CR No. 294 dated 14th February 2022

External Credit Rating of Borrowers having exposure of Rs.1000 Lakh and above

_____________________________________________________________________________________

 

I.    Introduction

1.   Bank has exposure to borrowers doing business across various sectors and having wide range of market share. Borrowers show diversity of performance in terms of financials, economics, repayment, documentation and banking history.

2.   Bank has developed an internal Credit Rating system in which diverse aspects like those mentioned above are captured as part of appraisal (whether being done for the first time or at the time of renewal/review). The system results in awarding marks and assigning grades to the credit facilities on the basis of which rates of interest are determined.

3.   It was however perceived that borrowers with higher exposure require deeper scrutiny and analysis in terms of sector-wise impact, effect of market and economy, and such other factors. There were audit observations also in this behalf. In view of this, it was decided that External Credit Rating be obtained in respect of the borrowers having total (i.e. Funded plus non-funded) exposure of Rs.1000.00 Lakh and above from an agency accredited by Reserve Bank of India for the purpose. After invitation of bids and selection of the most competitive one among them, a submission was placed before the Board Meeting dated 20th January’ 2022 to approve “Acuité Ratings & Research Limited’ (SMERA Group) for carrying out External Credit Rating exercise of our borrowers with the indicated level of borrowings.

4.   Instructions as under are now being issued to operationalise this arrangement.

 

II.  Operationalisation of the arrangement

1.  Action on the part of Branches

i.    Branches will issue a letter to each borrower having total exposure (i. e. Fund-based and non-Fund-based) of Rs.1000 Lakh and above, soliciting their cooperation for the external Credit Rating exercise. The Format of the ‘Letter to Borrower’ is given in Annexure-1.

ii.  An acknowledgement towards acceptance of the Credit Rating Exercise will be obtained from the borrower within 1 week of issue of the said letter.

iii.The borrower will be required to give ‘Rating Mandate’ to the agency before commencement of the Credit Rating exercise. Format for the Rating Mandate will be provided to the borrower after receipt of acknowledgement as per sub-Para 2. above.

iv.Thereafter, documents and information, indicative (but not exhaustive) list of which is given below, will be collected from the Borrower and the same will be furnished agency.

Indicative (but not exhaustive) list of documents/ information required to be obtained from the Borrower:

a.  Contact and other details of borrower- Attached in Annexure-2/ Part A.

b. Annual Reports/ Audited Financial Statements for last 3 years (or since inception, if entity is incorporated within the last 3 years)

c.  Provisional Financial Statements (Quarterly/ Half- yearly / Annual), as may be applicable.

d. Financial Projections for the next two years with the relevant assumptions and Year-to-Date financials of the current financial year.

e.  Latest sanction letter from bank.

f.   Future business plan or corporate actions (acquisition, mergers, business restructuring etc.)

g. Change in management, if any

h. Operational Data- Attached in in Annexure-2/ Part B.

i.   Year-to-Date Data- Attached in Annexure-2/ Part C.

j.   Debtors/creditors statement

k.  Latest Stock statement

The agency may seek information other than the above depending upon need.

v.   Branches will update the external Credit Rating in the additional field to be provided by IT department (see sub-Para 2. Below).

 

2.  Action on the part of IT department

IT department is advised to create an additional field for registering External Credit Rating.

 

3.  Collection and payment of fees and other commercial terms

i.   The fees for the entire Credit Rating process will be debited to the borrower’s account, immediately after the receipt of Rating Mandate from the borrower.

ii.  The Credit Rating fees consist of two parts:

a.  Initial Rating Fee (IRF) which is payable at the beginning of the Rating Exercise.

b. Annual Surveillance Fee (ASF) which is payable after one year from date of the initial rating, and further on annual basis till redemption of the debt/withdrawal of Rating.

iii.          In addition to the above, GST, as applicable (which is 18% at present], will be chargeable on the initial rating and the surveillance fees.

III.    Timeline for completion

Please note that external Credit Rating in respect of all the eligible borrowers must be completed by 31st March 2022. Therefore, branches should accord top priority for the same.

 

IV.   Support and facilitation by Credit Rating Agency

1.   Acuité Ratings & Research Limited will contact all the eligible borrowers to whom Bank addresses letters, and shall obtain Rating Mandates from all of them.

2.   The agency will designate a dedicated person from their side to coordinate with all the borrowers signing up for the exercise.

3.   Dedicated personnel from the agency will co-ordinate with officials at branches. They will address all the queries in relation to the Credit Rating process, documentation, mandate etc.

 

V.     The borrower who has recently done (within one year) Credit Rating independently need not go through the Rating process once again. Similarly, the borrower who is willing to do Credit Rating independently may be allowed to do so, subject to obtaining Credit Rating from any of the top Rating Agencies and Rating should be submitted to the bank within the stipulated time, preferably before 15th March’ 2022.

It is reiterated that external Credit Rating exercise to which all the borrowers with aggregate exposure of Rs. 10 crore and above are subject, is Board-mandated. Therefore, it must receive top priority at branch level. Please bring contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the concerned at branch and ensure meticulous compliance.
