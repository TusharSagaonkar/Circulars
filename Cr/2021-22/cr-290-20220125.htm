Circular CR No. 290 dated 25th January 2022

Special Housing Loan Scheme on the Occasion of 73rd Republic Day A scheme at concessional rate of interest for limited period

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.    Currently, there is a great demand for Housing Loans in the market from persons desirous of acquiring residential flats and houses. Most of our competitors (which include finance companies apart from banks) are vying with one another to launch more and more competitive products to increase their share of the market. In light of this, it was examined whether there was a need to come out with a special housing loan scheme with concessional rate of interest to attract such prospective buyers of residential flats and houses. The time of the year around the Republic Day was considered most opportune to conceive and launch such a product.

 

II.  1. A “Special Housing Loan Scheme on the occasion of 73rd Republic Day” has thus been conceived. It has following competitive features.

i.    Concessional rates of interest depending upon slabs.

ii.  Relaxation in respect of surety in some slabs.

iii.                Concession in service charges.

 

 2. The scheme has received due approval of the authorities in the Board Meeting dated 20th January 2022. The details of the scheme are given in the Annexure hereto.

3. The scheme has been launched on the date of the instant circular itself and will be open for receiving applications and according sanctions there under until 31st March 2022. The said scheme is also applicable for Housing Loans which are under process & pending for sanction.

 

III.    The advantageous features of the “Special Housing Loan Scheme” must be effectively publicized by branches and maximum business should be booked during the limited period in which it is open. All the staff members at branches should therefore be involved in the marketing effort.

 

IV.All other extant terms & conditions for Housing Loan as per the “Credit Policy w.e.f. 15.07.2019” remains unchanged.

 

V.     Planning and Marketing department should extend the necessary support for the marketing effort of branches by developing appropriate publicity materials followed by their distribution and display.

 

We look forward with extreme interest to the grand success of the scheme. Therefore, please bring the contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the members of the staff at the branch to actualize the expectations.

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
