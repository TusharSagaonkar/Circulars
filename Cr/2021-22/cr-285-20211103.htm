Circular CR No. 285 dated 3rd November 2021

1)      Constitution of Credit Sub-Committee

2)      Delegation of Financial Powers to Credit Sub-committee

3)      Process of routing and sanction of proposals falling under financial powers of Credit Sub-committee

 

 

I.     Currently, credit facilities above the financial powers delegated to Managing Director are placed for consideration and sanction by entire Board of Directors (BoD). However, BoD, being the apex management body of Bank, is required to look into other aspects of Bank’s functioning as well. Allocation of tasks, which among other things also results in sharing of work of supervision pertaining especially to credit related management, to a specialised group is expected to provide benefits through leveraging of expertise of its members. A specialised group also brings focus to this important banking function, eliminates problems such as conflicts of interest and reduces number of occasions calling for examination of accountability.

 

II.   Keeping in view the considerations as above, recommendations were placed before the Board meeting dated 13th July 2021 for constituting a Credit Sub-committee of the Board with five members, for strengthening the process of credit decisions in Bank. The sub-committee to be so constituted was proposed to be comprised of five existing members of Board of Directors. Recommendations were also made for vesting the Credit Sub-Committee with specific financial powers. The recommendations met with approval in the said Board meeting dated 13th July 2021.

 

III.            In accordance with the approval by Board as mentioned in Para II above, the Credit sub-committee has since been constituted and started functioning with immediate effect. We give hereunder names of members of the Credit Sub-committee and its financial powers.

1.  Members of the Credit Sub-committee

i.     Shri. Achyut L. Kandpile

ii.   Shri. Ashok R. Chalke

iii.  Shri. Rajendra K. Shelke

iv.  Shri. Bharat M. Ghandat

v.   Shri. Madhav R. Thavare

2.  Financial powers for sanction of credit facilities, and other decisions in respect thereof such as concession in ROI, exchange/release of securities or permission to maintain current account with other banks, determined on the basis of aggregate exposure (which include fund-based as well as non-fund-based facilities) to be exercised by the Credit Sub-committee, are as under.

i.     Credit facilities above Rs.1.00 Cr and upto Rs.5.00 Cr. (which include fresh credit facilities, as also enhancement in credit facilities such that aggregate credit facilities including enhancement are above Rs. 1 Cr. and up to Rs. 5 Cr.).

ii.   Renewal of Credit facilities above Rs.5.00 Cr & upto Rs.10.00 Cr.

iii.  Concession in Rate of Interest for credit limits upto Rs. 5 Cr.

iv.  Exchange of collateral securities in respect of credit facilities above Rs.1.00 Cr and upto Rs.5.00 Cr., irrespective of the value of security.

v.   Release of security in existing running and regular loan accounts above Rs.1.00 Cr and upto Rs.5.00 Cr.

vi.  Permission to Borrowers enjoying credit facilities above Rs.1.00 Cr and upto Rs. 5 Cr. to maintain Current A/c. with other Banks.

3.   All the proposals for credit facilities above the powers of the credit sub-committee will be placed for decision by the full Board.

 

IV.   Routing of credit proposals and processing

1.  All the proposals in respect of credit facilities, whether falling within the powers of the credit sub-committee, or beyond, shall be placed before the Board of Management (BoM) for obtaining their expert advice. In this connection, please refer Para V.1. of the Circular OC No. 857 dated 17th September 2021. Therefore, credit departments in Zonal Offices and Head Office shall necessarily put up ALL THE PROPOSALS falling under the powers of the Credit Sub-committee of Board, or above (i.e. to the full Board), first to BoM.

2.   The proposals will be examined by BoM for suitability and its inputs or remarks will be recorded thereon under signature of chairman of BoM and Managing Director.

3.  The proposals, upon examination by BoM, shall be segregated by Credit department at H. O. into those (i) falling within the powers of the credit sub-committee and (ii) those beyond its powers. The segregated proposals will be appropriately forwarded to the authority concerned.

4.   The proposals falling within the powers of the credit sub-committee will be examined by it for sanction or otherwise, in light of the inputs recorded by BoM.

5.   The decision of sanction or otherwise by Credit Sub-committee shall be conveyed under signature of MD to the office/department which had put up the proposal.

(Note:- Needless to add that the proposals above the powers of the credit sub-committee will be placed before the full Board for sanction or otherwise, in light of the inputs recorded by BoM).

V.   Please mark note of the instant circular against Para 19 (on page 22), Annexure 12 ‘A’ (on page 99), and also on one of the blank pages provided at the end (for noting references) of the Credit Policy W.E.F. 15.7.2019.

VI. Please bring contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned and ensure meticulous compliance.
