Circular CR No. 282 dated 20th September 2021

Observations in RBI inspection report for the year ended 31st March 2020

Under section 35 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949

Incorrect classification of credit facilities under PSL and WS

Granting ROI lower than permissible under CRS

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.     Irregularities as under, together with some others, have been pointed out in the captioned Inspection Report.

1.   There were instances of incorrect classification of credit facilities under Priority Sector Lending (PSL).

2.   Wide divergence was also observed in respect of lending to Weaker Sections (WS) within overall PSL.

3.   Interest at a lower rate than that permitted by Bank’s Internal Rating System was found to have been charged in some cases.

 

II.   In this connection, we observe as under.

1.  Incorrect classification of credit facilities under PSL and WS

i.     The problem of incorrect classification under PSL including that under the category of WS persists in the Bank in spite of several instructions to prevent the same. The main reason for this state of affairs is that branches assign wrong classification to the credit facilities. Another reason is non-adherence to the norms laid down for inclusion under MSMEs, regarding investment in Plant & Machinery, and Turnover, as prescribed by Government of India and published by RBI, guidelines in respect of which have been issued by us vide circular CR No.274 dated 16th June 2021.

ii.   Branches are therefore once again advised to ensure that the credit facilities must be classified under PSL, and under Weaker Section within PSL, solely on the basis of respective definition and eligibility criteria. It may be noted that the activities themselves need to be eligible for classification under Priority Sector so that their further sub-classification under Weaker Section can be done.

iii.  Branches are hereby being instructed to do one-time checking of classification of all the credit facilities on their books to determine whether they are correctly classified under PSL and its sub-sectors. This exercise must be completed as on 30th September 2021.

iv.  It shall always be the responsibility of each Branch-In-Charge to ensure correct classification and sub-classification of credit facilities on branch books under PSL and its sub-sectors in the CBS system.

2.  Charging a lower rate of interest than that permitted by Bank’s internal rating system

i.     As per the Credit Policy of Bank, interest rate lower than that permitted by its Internal Credit Rating System is charged on a case-to-case basis, having regard to,

a. long business relationship,

b. value of account,

 

c. satisfactory track record,

 

d. securities available,

 

and,

e. competitive ROI offered by other Banks

 

ii.   In view of the observations in the Inspection Report under reference, branches and Zonal Offices are advised to invariably indicate, in the credit proposal placed for sanction before the Competent Authority, the justification for an interest rate lower than that permitted by Internal Credit Rating System. Credit department in Head Office will re-examine the justification and, if found appropriate, shall incorporate the same in the note placed before the Board of Directors of the Bank.

 

III.  All the Branches, Regional and Zonal Offices are advised to follow the above instructions meticulously and ensure compliance diligently.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
