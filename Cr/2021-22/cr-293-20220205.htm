Circular CR No. 293 dated 5th February 2022

 

Housing Loans in MHADA Colony Charkop, Kandivali and MHADA Colony, Gorai

1.    Reinstatement of Loans for Demolition and Reconstruction

2.    Updating of all the Instructions on the Subject

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.     Background

1.   The MHADA colony in Charkop is a World Bank Project.  There are plots of different sizes in the colony. Tenements on plots of sizes 40 sq. meters and below are necessarily under a registered co-operative society. The plots of sizes more than 40 sq. meters have independent bungalows thereon and they are not part of any society.

2.   After opening of our Charkop, Kandivali (W) branch, we started extending Housing Loans for construction of additional area on the existing tenements on plots of sizes 40 sq. metres & below. Though construction of additional rooms/structure results in better living conditions of family members, it was observed that customers/applicants found it difficult to obtain and submit NOC/Permission from MHADA or Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai for constructing such additional rooms/structure, and therefore, the submission of the same was waived when we started extending Housing Loans for renovation/ furnishing/purchase/demolition & reconstruction/extension of tenements/flats/ bungalows in MHADA colonies.

3.   We also started granting loans in respect of independent bungalows (not part of any society) for renovation/furnishing/demolition and reconstruction/purchase with or without renovation/furnishing.

4.  Reference to extant circulars on the subject

i.     Instructions on the subject were updated and consolidated the last time vide Circular CR No. 227 dated 5th April 2019.

ii.   Thereafter, in terms of the Circular CR No.  237 dated 3rd February 2020, following changes were made in the scheme.

a.   The part of the scheme of Loans for Repairs/ Renovation/ Extension/ Demolition with Reconstruction was suspended,

b.   The Plot Area-wise ceiling on loans was lowered, and,

c.    NOC from MHADA for Sale/ Purchase was made mandatory.

iii.   Subsequently, Plot-area-wise ceiling on Loans was revised upward in terms of Circular CR No. 279 dated 12th July 2021.

 

II.     Recent review and approval

Recently, the suspension on granting new loans for repairs, renovation and demolition with reconstruction came up for review. As a result, it was considered,

and decided as appropriate, to reinstate that part of the scheme which pertained to Loans for Repairs/ Renovation/ Demolition with Reconstruction. The decision also extended to construction of a new bungalow in place of an old one.

 

III.    Clarification on Plot area-wise ceiling on loans

A need has also arisen to clarify if other loans can be granted to borrowers having loans under the MHADA scheme. It is now being clarified that the Plot area-wise ceilings on loans for the aggregate of loans, considered together with personal loans etc. if any availed by a borrower shall normally apply, but not when the other loans are eligible for sanction on independent merits (including availability of unencumbered primary/collateral security).

 

IV.   This opportunity has been taken to  up-to-date and consolidate instructions on the subject, in supersession of all the previous instructions thereon, through the instant circular as under, which also incorporates (1) reinstating of the part of the scheme which pertained to Loans for Demolition with Reconstruction, and (2)  clarification on applying the Plot area-wise ceilings on aggregate loans by a borrower, with exceptions in case of loans qualifying on the basis of independent merits and unencumbered primary/collateral security.

 

V.     Loans under the scheme

1.   Types

Housing loans as under are considered under the two MHADA Colonies, namely Charkop & Gorai. 

i.     Loan to existing owner of the tenement for demolishing the existing structure and reconstructing.

ii.   Loan required for purchase of tenement from the original allottee as well as for demolition and reconstruction of the same.

iii.  Loan required for purchase of tenement which has undergone multiple sales transactions as well as for demolition and reconstruction of the same.

iv.  Loan for purchase /reconstruction of independent bungalow.

Note(s)-A :- Loan for reconstruction of MHADA tenements is to be considered as Housing Loan only if the borrower has not availed Housing Loan earlier, or the earlier Housing Loan is closed. However, if fresh proposal for Housing Loan includes components of reconstruction, then it will be considered as single housing loan and will qualify as such.  It shall be subject to further conditions as under: -  

·        Effective Rate of Interest applicable shall be the one as per the extant circular instructions (ILA) in respect of Housing Loans.

·        Tenure – upto 120 months

·        Disbursement- Payment to be made to the Contractor.

·        If the borrower has an existing Housing Loan, then he/she will not be eligible for the scheme. However, House Repairs Loan up to Rs. 5.00 lakh can be sanctioned to him/her subject to usual conditions. The rate of interest shall be as per the extant circular instructions (ILA) in respect of House Repair Loans.

·        A borrower may, however, avail reconstruction loan of MHADA tenements if the existing Home Loan is closed.

 

Note-B :- If a borrower applies for any further retail loans such as Surety Loan, Education loan, Business loan etc. after availing loan for MHADA reconstruction, the Plot area-wise ceiling for loan (maximum permissible loan) indicated under Table at sub-Para 2 above shall be applicable, unless such further retail loans qualify for sanction on the basis of independent merits and unencumbered primary/collateral security.

 

3.   Title Deeds to be submitted in respect of the above referred five categories of Housing loans shall be as under: -

i)   Existing owner of the tenement wants to avail loan for demolishing the existing structure and reconstructing.

a)   Original letter of allotment by MHADA to the original tenement owner, if available.

b)   Letter from the society that the applicant is existing owner of the tenement.

c)    Original share certificate issued by Regd. Society of the cluster of the tenement should be in the name of the applicant.

d)   NOC from the society as well as MHADA to the Bank for mortgage of tenement.

e)    Original Payment Receipt in respect of payment made to MHADA towards plot/tenement and/or any other documentary proof, if available.

 

ii) Loan required for purchase of tenement from the Original Allottee as well as for demolition and reconstruction of the same.

a)   Original letter of allotment by MHADA to the original tenement’s owner, if available.

b)  Letter from the society that the seller is original allottee of the tenement.

c)   NOC from MHADA for the purchase transaction between the original allotee of the tenement and the applicant-borrower is must.

d)  NOC from the society as well as MHADA to the Bank for mortgage of tenement.

e)   Duly stamped and registered Agreement between original allottee and purchaser.

f)    Original share certificate issued by Regd. Society in the name of Seller.

g)  NOC from the society to the Bank for transfer in the name of the purchaser after completion of sale transaction.

h)  Original Payment Receipt in respect of payment made to MHADA towards plot/tenement and/or any other documentary proof, if available.

 

iii)       Loan required for purchase of tenements under multiple sales transactions as well as for demolition and reconstruction of the same.

a)   Original letter of allotment by MHADA to the original tenement owner, if available.

b)  Letter from the society that the seller is a member of the society.

c)   NOC from MHADA for the purchase transaction between the present seller of the tenement and the applicant-borrower is must.

d)  The original agreement duly stamped and registered, executed by and between the present seller and a person from whom the present seller has purchased i. e. seller’s seller.

e)   The original agreement duly stamped and registered executed by and between the present seller and present purchaser (applicant of loan).

f)    Original share certificate issued by Regd. Society and endorsed in the name of present Seller.

g)  NOC from the society as well as MHADA to the Bank for mortgage of the said tenement.

h)  The chain of documents if available to be collected, perused and kept on record after verifying that there is no discrepancy as regards the title of all the previous sellers.

i)    Payment Receipts issued by seller to present purchaser towards proof of margin money and loan amount.

 

iv)      Loan for renovation / furnishing /purchase / demolition and/or reconstruction of a new independent bungalow.

a)   Allotment letter in respect of the plot to the original allottee is the basic Title Deed. Subsequent Sale Deeds between 1st & 2nd party, 2nd & 3rd party, & so on, will constitute the necessary chain of documents.

b)   If purchase transaction is involved, NOC from MHADA for the purchase transaction is a must.

c)    MHADA is the direct authority maintaining records of allotment & transfers.

d)   Applicant has to produce requisite title deeds as per Mortgageability Scrutiny Note. It should also be ensured to obtain NOC from MHADA for mortgaging the property which is a must.

 

4.     Other Terms and Conditions

                i)            The visit by the Branch officials to the place of residence and business/ employment is compulsory. Verification is to be done by the said officials in respect of ownership of the tenement. The visit and discussion with the Chairman/Secretary of the Society (other than in respect of loan for a Bungalow) is must; the same should be recorded in Visit Report. The details of visit to place of employment/business should also be noted as per extant guidelines.

ii)       Loan amount will be subject to the repayment capacity of the loanee, co-applicant and worth of the sureties, as per the Credit Policy norms, and not solely on the size and value of tenement/plot.

iii)     Applicant should be a member of the society and holder of share certificate in his/her name (other than in respect of loan for a Bungalow).

iv)     Applicant should submit the estimate for the work to be carried out with stage-wise payment-schedule from the contractor along with the construction plan duly signed by the contractor and also by the applicant.

v)       Where the construction has already been completed not more than 3 months before the date of application, such a loan application be considered for sanction and disbursement of loan can be made in lump-sum to the contractor for the balance amount to be paid or by way of reimbursement to the borrower on the basis of Cheque Payment to the contractor in addition to the sufficient proof of bills/receipts to be obtained from the contractor.

a)   Period of 3 months shall be verified from local enquiry with Chairman/ Secretary of the society, wherever applicable, and by reference to payments by cheque.

b)  In case construction is in progress or is yet to start, stage-wise disbursement shall be made directly to the contractor by PO in favour of the contractor with the mention of his Bank and Branch and A/c. Number.

vi)     The loan will be secured by equitable mortgage of the tenement with the notice of intimation to Registrar in addition to the loan documents and registration of mortgage charge with CERSAI.

vii)   After execution of the equitable mortgage, a copy thereof will be sent to the society for registering our charge towards mortgage in society’s books/records.

viii)  A letter of confirmation should be obtained from the society (other than in respect of loan for a Bungalow) for having registered Bank’s charge towards mortgage in its books/records. The branch should depute staff to the society’s office for verification in this behalf.

ix)     In respect of a loan involving bungalow, registration of charge with MHADA and a letter of confirmation from them in this behalf is a must.

 

5.   The guidelines contained in the “Annexure-5 on Housing Loan Scheme-General Instructions” of the rules under the “Credit Policy W. E. F. 15.7.2019” so far as they are not inconsistent with the relaxations under the special scheme under reference shall be followed by branches

 

VI.   Hitherto, Loans for housing in MHADA Colony, Charkop, were sanctioned only at our Kandivali (W)-Charkop Branch. It has now been decided to sanction  loans for housing in MHADA, Gorai as well as Charkop  from any of our branches in Mumbai area where the customer is maintaining his/her Saving / Current account.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned at the Branch/ ZO/Credit Dept. and ensure compliance.
