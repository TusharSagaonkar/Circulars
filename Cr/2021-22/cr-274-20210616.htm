Circular CR No. 274 dated 16th June 2021

Guidelines on Priority Sector Lending (PSL)

Updated as on 1st June 2021

Classification of Various Credit Facilities under Priority Sector

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

I.     Please refer Circular CR No. 261 dated 3rd November 2020 on the subject of guidelines on & classification of various credit facilities under Priority Sector.

 

II.   1. The comprehensive and updated instructions together with guidelines on Priority Sector Lending (PSL )for all the banks, including Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks (UCBs), issued by RBI vide its communication no.RBI/FIDD/2020-21/72-Master Directions FIDD.CO.Plan.BC.5/04.09.01/2020-21 dated 4th September, 2020 have been fine-tuned by its update posted on the website on 31st May 2021. The instructions contained therein as/applicable to our Bank, being part of the UCB sector, are given hereunder with updates over the previous ones wherever necessary, and updated operational guidelines in respect thereof are also given.

 

2. Instructions contained herein supersede all the previous instructions on the subject.

 

3. Lending previously classified under PSL but not in conformity with the revised & updated instructions and guidelines will have to be removed from Priority Sector Classification except those for Housing and Education, and lending to women under Weaker Section category. Lending to the said three categories specifically named (Housing, Education and Women) will continue to remain classified under Priority Sector within specified sub-categories until closed or renewed.

 

III.     Broad categories under priority sector for the purpose of lending will continue to be as under.

1) Agriculture

2) Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises

3) Export Credit

4) Education

5) Housing

6) Social Infrastructure

7) Renewable Energy

8) Others

V.   Broad sub-sectors eligible for priority sector lending are given below: -

1.    Agriculture

                         i.        Various sub-sectors within Agriculture and details of loan facilities with eligibility criteria & ceiling on loan amount etc. if any are given at Sr. No. 1 and its sub-points in the Annexure-I hereto.

                       ii.        List of permissible activities under Food & Agro-processing up to an aggregate sanctioned limit of Rs. 100 Crore per borrower from the banking system is given in Annexure-I-A.

     Advances granted to other than corporate farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs)/(FPC) Companies of Individual Farmers, Partnership firms and Co-operatives of farmers under ‘Farm Credit’ should be classified under respective category of MSME as per Udyam certificate) However advances for activities related to Agri. Infrastructure, Ancillary Services and Food Processing should be classified under Agriculture.

 

2.   MSMEs

i.       The definition of MSMEs will be as per Government of India (GOI), Gazette Notification No. S.O.2119(E) dated June 26, 2020 read with circular RBI/2020-2021/10 FIDD.MSME & NFS.BC.No.3/06.02.31/2020-21 read with FIDD. MSME & BC.No.4/ 06.02.31/2020-21 dated July 2, 2020, August 21, 2020 respectively on ‘Credit Flow to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Sector’ and updated from time to time.

ii.      All the credit facilities to MSMEs conforming to the “Revised Definitions of MSMEs w.e.f. 1st July, 2020” shall now qualify as Priority Sector lending. Definitions of MSMEs & important points of their governance structure are given at Sr. No. 2 and its sub-points in the Annexure-I hereto.

iii.     All the points, including operational guidelines, pertaining to lending/to MSMEs/in light of the “Revised Definitions of MSMEs w.e.f. 1st July, 2020” have been dealt with in detail in our Circular CR No. 254 Dated 31st July, 2020 as modified by the Circular CR No. 257 dated 28th August, 2020. Therefore, reference should be made to the said two CR circulars mentioned above for further guidance.

iv.    Credit facilities extended to an Individual/Professional for industrial or business purpose, like Purchase of Shop, Purchase of Vehicle, & existing Mortgage and Rent securitization loans shall be classified under Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises provided the units satisfy prescribed composite criteria for investment in Machinery/Equipment and turnover.

v.      It should be noted that with effect from 1st April 2021, Udyam registration is mandatory for all the units desirous of status as MSME; lending to them shall be classifiable under MSME sub-sector of priority sector only upon their production of Udyam registration certificate.

vi.    It is mandatory to obtain Udyam Registration certificate from all MSME customers for considering the advances under priority sector. It is advised to follow up with the concerned borrower who have not yet submitted Udyam Certificate and such borrowers should be persuaded to register for Udyam certificate and submit the same immediately. Branches should write a letter to all such units so that they act accordingly. This exercise must be completed before 31st July 2021. In the meanwhile, credit facilities to such units should be classified MSME if criteria therefore are otherwise fulfilled.

vii.   Traders, wholesale as well as retail does not form part of MSME and hence not eligible for Classification under Priority Sector. Distributors, Commission Agents, and units engaged in Network Selling, being essentially traders, shall also not be classifiable under Priority Sector.

viii.  Transport Vehicle Aggregators and Aggregators of other businesses will come under priority sector provided they fulfill composite criteria for investment in Machinery/ Equipment and turnover for MSMEs, provided further, of course, that such entities have obtained Udyam Registration Certificate.

 

3.    Export Credit: -

·     Export credit under agriculture and MSME sectors are allowed to be classified as PSL in the respective categories viz. agriculture and MSME.

·     Export Credit (other than in agriculture and MSME) will be/classified/on the basis of incremental export credit over corresponding date of the preceding year, up to 2 per cent of ANBC or CEOBE whichever is higher, subject to a sanctioned limit of up to Rs. 40 crore per borrower.

·     Export credit includes pre-shipment and post-shipment export credit (excluding off-balance sheet items) .

 

4.    Education: - All loans sanctioned to individuals for educational purposes, including those for vocational courses not exceeding Rs. 20 Lakh will be considered as eligible for priority sector classification. Loans currently classified as priority will continue till maturity.

5.    Housing :- All Loans sanctioned to individual as per limits prescribed below are eligible for priority sector classification:

i.   Loans to individuals up to Rs. 35 Lakh in metropolitan centres (with population of ten Lakh and above)/and up to Rs. 25 Lakh in/other/centres/for/purchase/construction of a dwelling unit per family* provided the overall cost of dwelling unit at a metropolitan centre and at any other centre does not exceed Rs.45 Lakh and Rs.30 Lakh respectively. Existing individual housing loans currently classified under PSL but not in conformity with these norms will continue as PSL till maturity or repayment.

     * Definition of Family – Husband, wife and Unmarried Children (Earning adult regardless of marital status may be treated as separate family).

 

(Note-A:-It may be noted that, internally, the overall cost of the dwelling unit will continue to be as per the Agreement for Sale/Sale Deed., as per our extant policy).

 

(Note-B:- Following branches of our bank are at non-metropolitan centres.

·           Kankavali

·           Savedi Road, Ahmednagar

·           Ashok Nagar, Nanded

·           Samrat Nagar, Nanded

·           City Branch, Udupi

·           Shirva, Udupi

     The rest are all at metropolitan centres).

 

ii.   Loans up to Rs. 10 Lakh at metropolitan centres and up to Rs. 6 Lakh at other centres for repairs to damaged dwelling units conforming to the overall cost of the dwelling unit as prescribed in sub-Para no. i. above, also come under Priority Sector.

(Note-a:- The loan for repairs to damaged dwellings will be such that unit cost of the/relative dwellings shall not be over & above the ceiling for unit costs at Rs. 45 Lakh & Rs. 30 Lakh at metropolitan and other centres respectively).

 

(Note-b:- In respect of a dwelling unit for which Housing Loan is not granted, loan for repair thereto up to the ceiling for respective loan amounts in metropolitan and other centres shall come under priority sector subject to cost of the dwelling unit conforming to the stipulation indicated in sub-Para i.)

 

(Note-c :- Housing loans and House Repair Loans, above the ceilings for cost of dwelling unit & amount of loans prescribed for priority sector, can be considered if they are otherwise in accordance with the Credit Policy; they will however come under Non-priority sector classification).

 

     (Note-d :- To justify unit cost in respect of loans for house repair, documentary proof should be obtained in the form of either of the copy of sale agreement, Index – II, Registration Receipt, or/Self declaration).

 

(Note–e :- In terms of IDC Ref. No. HO/MD-920/1636/2020-21 dated/26th December, 2019 it has been instructed to put on hold New “Loans for Repair & Renovations of Flats/House including the purpose).

 

iii. Housing loans to bank’s own employees do not qualify for classification under the priority sector. The details are given at Sr. No.5 in the Annexure – I hereto.

 

6.    Social infrastructure

Bank loans up to/a limit of Rs. 5 Crore per borrower for setting up schools, drinking water facilities and sanitation facilities including construction/ refurbishment of household toilets and water improvement at household level, etc. and loans up to a limit of Rs. 10 Crore per borrower/for building health care facilities including under ‘Ayushman Bharat’.

In case of UCBs, the above limits are applicable only in centres having a population of less than one lakh.

We will not grant loans under this category as none of the centres where we are established conform to the stipulation of a population of less than one lakh. This item corresponds to the point at Sr. No.6 in the Annexure-I hereto

 

7.    Renewable Energy

Loans up to a limit of Rs.30 crore per borrower for purposes like solar based power generators, biomass-based power generators, wind mills, micro-hydel plants and for non-conventional energy based public utilities, viz. street lighting systems and remote village electrification etc., are eligible for Priority Sector classification. For individual households, the loan limit will be Rs.10 lakh per borrower. This item corresponds to the point at Sr. No.7 in the Annexure-I hereto.

 

8.    Others Categories of Loans Coming Under Priority Sector

Following loans as per the prescribed limits are eligible for priority sector classification:

i.      Loans not exceeding Rs.1.00 lakh per borrower provided directly to individuals and individual members of SHG/JLG, provided the individual borrower’s household annual income in rural areas does not exceed Rs.1.00 lakh and for non-rural areas it does not exceed Rs.1.60 lakh; and loans not exceeding/Rs. 2.00 lakh provided directly by banks to Self Help Group (SHG)/Joint Liability Group (JLG) for activities other than agriculture or MSME, viz., loans for meeting social needs, construction or repair of house, construction of toilets or any viable common activity started by the SHGs.

ii.    Loans to distressed persons (other than distressed farmers indebted to non-institutional lenders) not exceeding Rs.1.00 lakh per borrower to prepay their debt to non-institutional lenders.

iii.  Loans sanctioned to State Sponsored Organisations for Scheduled Castes/ Scheduled Tribes for the specific purpose of purchase and supply of inputs and/or the marketing of the outputs of the beneficiaries of these organisations.

iv.  Loans up to Rs.50 crore to Start-ups, as per definition of Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Govt. of India that are engaged in activities other than Agriculture or MSME.The details are given at Sr. No. 8 in the Annexure-I hereto

 

VI.  WEAKER SECTIONS

(IT MAY BE NOTED THAT THE ACTIVITIES THEMSELVES NEED TO BE ELIGIBLE UNDER PRIORITY SECTOR)

The list of categories of borrowers, loans to whom are eligible for classification under the sub-sector of Weaker Sections, is as under.

1.       Small and Marginal Farmers.

2.       Artisans, village and cottage industries where individual credit limits do not exceed Rs.1.00 lakh.

3.       Beneficiaries under Government Sponsored Schemes such as National Rural Livelihood Mission (NRLM), National Urban Livelihood Mission (NULM) and Self Employment Scheme for Rehabilitation of Manual Scavengers (SRMS).

4.       Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

5.       Beneficiaries of Differential Rate of Interest (DRI) scheme.

6.       Self Help Groups.

7.       Distressed farmers indebted to non-institutional lenders.

8.       Distressed persons other than farmers, with loan amount not exceeding/Rs.1.00 lakh per borrower to prepay their debt to non-institutional lenders.

9.       Individual women beneficiaries up to Rs.1.00 lakh per borrower.

       [Note :- It may be noted that the category is now only for individual women with individual loans up to Rs. 1.00 Lakh. (loans to women currently classified under Weaker Section category of Priority Sector will continue to be classified as such till maturity or repayment)].

10.   Persons with disabilities

11.   Minority communities as may be notified by Government of India from time to time. The details are given at Sr. No. II in the Annexure – I hereto.

12.   Overdraft availed by PMJDY account holders as per limits and conditions prescribed by Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance from time to time may be classified under Weaker Sections.

 

VII.  1. All the activities not covered under Annexure-I will be treated as non-priority Loans & Advances.

 

2. Advances extended to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and Minority communities are classified under WS-II. However, advances granted to OBC and NT should not be classified under Weaker Section.

 

3. While prioritizing advances falling under multiple sub-categories of weaker sections; they should be first categorised under WS-IV i.e. Person with Physical Disabilities (PWD), thereafter under WS-II which is for Minority Community, SC/ST & thereafter under WS-III for (only individual) women. If the advance also falls under WS-I category, then the same should be classified under WS-I category of weaker section.

 

VIII.  Other guidelines for priority sector loans as prescribed by RBI

Branches must comply with the following common guidelines for all categories of advances under the priority sector.

 

1.  Service charges

No loan-related and ad hoc service charges/inspection charges should be levied on priority sector loans up to Rs. 25,000. In the case of eligible priority sector loans to SHGs/ JLGs, this limit will be applicable per member and not to the group as a whole.

 

2.  Receipt, Sanction/Rejection/Disbursement Register

A register/electronic record should be maintained by branches wherein the date of receipt, sanction/rejection/disbursement with reasons thereof, etc. should be recorded. The register/electronic record should be made available to all inspecting agencies.

 

3.  Issue of acknowledgement of loan applications

     Branches should provide acknowledgement for loan applications received under priority sector loans. Time limit prescribed under the extant Credit Policy for communicating decision in writing to the applicants must be adhered to.

 

IX.    INTEREST RATES

There is a likelihood that some loans & advances under Non-priority Sector will come under Priority Sector with the changes in classification of advances under priority sector. In such circumstances, the current rates of interest applicable to them will continue until the fresh gradation if credit rating is applicable or immediately after submitting copy of Udyam Registration Certificate.

 

X.  IMPORTANCE OF ACHIEVING TARGETS UNDER PRIORITY SECTOR

Importance of achieving goals and sub-goals under priority sector lending has been emphasised repeatedly through various circulars and other communications on the subject. With stipulation of graded/increase in the said goal from 45% as on 31st March 2021/to 50% by 31st March 2022, 60% by 31st March 2023 and 75% by 31st March, 2024 of the corresponding ANBC or CEOBE whichever is higher, importance & urgency of achieving the goals & sub-goals have increased many fold. Such achievement is monitored by RBI on the basis of our quarterly returns and is taken into account while granting regulatory clearances/approvals for various purposes.

With our vast base of customers & reach of the network of branches, it should not be difficult to achieve them within the prescribed timelines without compromising quality of lending. Branches, Regional Offices and Zonal Offices should take initiative in this direction.

 

 

XI.    Instructions to IT department

IT department to provide suitable menu in OMNI for recording PAN wise, customer wise details of investment in Plant &machinery /equipment (WDV) and turnover, which are required to be updated periodically.

 

XII.  Instructions to branches

Branches are advised to periodically update details of investment in Plant & Machinery/Equipment (WDV) and turnover in all credit facilities of the customer except personal/loan/accounts,/linked/to/the/same/PAN./These updations should be carried out yearly on the basis of latest audited financial statements or “Udyam Registration certificate” of the borrower. They are further advised to ensure correctness of the “Micro/ Small/ Medium” Enterprise and Priority/Non-priority classification entered in OMNI in all business loan accounts of the customer linked to the same PAN./

 

XIII.    Instructions to MIS department

1. MIS dept will provide necessary data to branches for making required changes in Omni.

2. It will monitor & report on achievement of targets Priority Sector Lending target on ‘quarterly’ basis.

3. It will furnish the data (on priority sector lending in the reporting formats ‘Statement I’ and ‘Statement II (Part A to D)’ at quarterly and annual intervals, to the Regional Office of DoS, RBI.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.
