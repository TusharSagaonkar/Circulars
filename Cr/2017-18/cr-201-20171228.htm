REVISED

Circular CR No. 201 Dated 28th December, 2017

 

Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act 2016

Implications for Improving Quality of Loans & Advances

Obtaining Undertaking from Borrowers

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

                I.    As you are aware, individual housing loans is our thrust area. Bank has extended very competitive rates of interest on housing loans and also enhanced discretionary powers across the authority structure to grant such loans. Bank has formally entered into an agreement with HUDCO to grant loans to eligible applicants belonging to EWS/LIG/MIG-I&II categories under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana. It is expected that all the initiatives as above will help build a portfolio of quality loans for the Bank. However, considering that most of the applications for housing loans are in respect of new flats in housing projects undertaken by Promoter(s), lack of assurance to loanees and lenders (including Banks such as ours) regarding quality, pricing, timeliness, transparency & overall legality had been a matter of major concern.

 

              II.   While we are not taking new exposure to Commercial Real Estate Sector, we grant loans for establishing MSME units which may involve acquisition of galas, tenements or shops for conducting their activities although we may not necessarily finance the component of outlay for such Shop/Tenements/Gala. Therefore, all the concerns regarding individual housing loans are also applicable to such cases.

 

             III.   An important development in the field of Real Estate is enactment of Real Estate (Development & Regulation) Act, 2016 (RERA). The Rules under RERA have been notified in all the three states in which our Bank operates i. e. Maharashtra, Karnataka & Gujarat. Regulatory & appellate authorities under RERA have also been established in these states. The act has filled up an important regulatory gap and is certain to directly benefit, among others, the entities & persons booking residential & commercial units in Real Estate Projects. It is therefore in the interest of our Bank as also that of our borrowers to ensure that a unit (be it a residential flat/unit or a gala/tenement/ shop from which some commercial/industrial activity is proposed to be run) is part of a project which is compliant with RERA. Branches are therefore being instructed to ensure, before considering any individual housing loan or loan to any MSME unit being set up in some Real Estate Project (whether or not we finance component of outlay for Gala/tenement/shop etc.) that provisions of RERA are complied with.

 

            IV.   Salient features of RERA are listed in Annexure-I hereto. Branches should carefully study the features as detailed therein so that a qualitative scrutiny is facilitated in respect of an individual housing loan or an MSME loan (latter to a borrower whose project involves financed or non-financed Gala/Tenement/Shop etc.)

 

              V.   We also extend housing loans, and loans & advances to MSME units, where the accommodation or place of business is acquired/proposed to be acquired on ready-possession basis or through second sale. Branches need to particularly note that only those projects (from among the ones involving area of land proposed to be developed exceeding 500 sq mtrs., or number of apartments proposed to be developed exceeding 8 inclusive of all phases-others being exempted in entirety) are exempted from registration under RERA which have received completion certificate or occupancy certificate before the date of commencement of the act /notification of the rules under the act. Therefore, it will have to be ensured that completion certificate or occupancy certificate has been received in respect of the Real Estate Project in which an applicant intends to acquire residential unit/gala/tenement/shop etc. on a ready-made basis or through second sale.

 

            VI.   Apart from verification of documents in respect of registration under RERA for ongoing projects, or completion certificate or occupancy certificate in respect of complete projects, a Declaration-Cum-Undertaking as per Annexure-II of the instant circular must be obtained from the borrowers.

 

          VII.   One important provision under RERA is that promoter cannot accept a sum more than 10 percent of the price of the apartment/ plot as advance payment/ application fees. For any further collection towards the apartment/ plot cost, the promoter is required to enter into an ‘Agreement for Sale’ with the allottee. Another provision under RERA is that the promoter has to deposit minimum 70% of amounts realised for real estate project from allottees, in a separate account to be maintained in a scheduled bank to cover cost of construction and land cost and amount deposited therein is required to be used only for that purpose. These two provisions together afford good opportunity to us to ensure end-use of funds. Therefore, a provision is made in the Declaration-Cum-Undertaking (Annexure-II mentioned above), to obtain particulars such as name of the bank with which the promoter has opened the account to route mandatory project-wise 70 % of the outlay & the specific number of such account. Same document also provides consent of the applicant for disbursement by us of the loan directly for credit of such project-specific mandatory account of the Bank. The applicant can always pay up to 10 % of the total outlay to the builder before an Agreement to Sale is executed & such payment can be reckoned as margin money. Therefore, there should be no occasion for branches to disburse loan in any manner other than into the designated mandatory project-specific bank account.

 

         VIII.   Branches are advised to make best possible use of the Act & rules framed thereunder by verifying the information already available in public domain or eliciting such additional information as is available with the regulatory authority so as to ensure quality of loans & advances.

 

            IX.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department.
