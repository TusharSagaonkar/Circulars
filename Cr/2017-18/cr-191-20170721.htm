III.   Accounting: Branches will debit the customer’s account with the aggregate incidence and; while the two components of the GST, namely CGST & SGST will be credited to the respective Tax Payable Account at the Branch, the Charge/Fee proper will be credited at the first stage to the CERSAI Payable Account. The Charge/Fee so parked will be transferred to HO New Account at the second stage when Security ID and Asset ID are received from Zonal Office. We give below an example of entries to be passed.
