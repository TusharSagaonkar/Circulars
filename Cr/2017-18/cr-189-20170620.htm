Circular CR No. 189 Dated 20th June, 2017

 

UDYOG VIKAS LOAN SCHEME

(For Priority Sector Lending)

_____________________________________________________________

 

I.   One of the current concerns of the Bank is that Priority Sector Advances as on  31st March 2017 are only 37.99 % of our Adjusted Net Bank Credit and it has become necessary to focus on Priority Sector Advances during the Financial Year 2017-18. While measures such as reduction in service charges in respect of certain retail advances and reduction in interest rates across the board with effect from 1st April 2017 have already been taken, it was considered necessary to introduce a new loan scheme with some concessions to intending borrowers incorporated therein to give a boost to priority sector lending. The scheme so drafted was put up before the Board and met with approval in the meeting dated 12th June 2017. The same is reproduced below:

Purpose

For purchase of Fixed Assets like Shop, Gala, Factory Shed, Machinery, Furniture, Computers, Business Development/ Expansion and Working Capital Facility

Max. Amount

Term Loan/Cash Credit -Manufacturing Sector upto Rs.500.00 Lakh

 - Service Sector upto Rs.200.00 Lakh

 - Retail Trade upto Rs. 20.00 Lakh

Constituent

Individual/Proprietorship Firm/Partnership Firm/LLP/Pvt. Ltd. Cos. carrying on Small Business/Rendering Specialised Services/ Manufacturing Activity

Eligible Category

Loans & Advances to be considered only for following categories of Priority Sector Advances

1)    Micro & Small Enterprises (MSEs) are primarily recognised on the basis of original cost of investment in Plant & Machinery in case of manufacturing sector and original cost of investment in equipments in case of Service Sector.

i)             Manufacturing Enterprises :– Original cost of investment in Plant & Machinery should not exceed Rs. 500.00 Lakh.

ii)           Service Enterprises :– Original cost of investment in Equipments should not exceed Rs. 200.00 Lakh.

 

2)    Small Road and Water Transport Operator

3)    Small Businessmen

 

4)    Enterprises engaged in providing or rendering services for selling of goods on commission basis, booking agents, clearing & forwarding agents, estate agents, photo studio/shop, service providers viz. Pest Control, Plumbing, Electrician, Contractor etc. , Hair Dressing Saloons, Photocopiers, Workshops carrying repair/maintenance jobs for Automobiles, Machinery etc., Enterprises engaged in Advertising/ Marketing/Industrial/Management Consultancy, Industrial R & D Labs, Testing Labs, Laundry & Dry Cleaners, Restaurant & Cafes, Tailoring Firms, Weigh Bridge Operators, Business of providing Equipments on Rental/Lease basis, Desktop Publishing, Beauty Parlors, Health Spa.

Eligibility

1] Term Loan eligibility:-

(a)  In case of loans considered on the basis of their present income:- Minimum take home Income after EMI should be 40% of net income subject to minimum of Rs. 10,000/- and maximum Rs. 20,000/-

(b)  In case of loans sanctioned on the basis of their projected Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss a/c.:- DSCR should be worked out to ascertain the repayment capacity as per the Balance Sheet & Profit & Loss a/c. for the entire period the loan would be serviced/repaid with interest. The DSCR should be more than 1.5.

(c)  Cost of project minus margin amount.

2] Working Capital – 20% of estimated/projected turnover

Repayment

Upto 84 EMIs including moratorium period (in exceptional cases, it can be extended upto 120 EMIs)

Rate Of Interest

Priority Sector Advance : PLR – 0.50 i.e. 11.50% p.a. (floating)

Weaker Section Advance : PLR – 1.00 i.e. 11.00% p.a. (floating)

Margin

As per Credit Policy

Membership

Regular Membership for the Applicant and Sureties

Prime Security
  1. Mortgage of Shop/Gala/factory to be purchased
  2. Hypothecation of Stock and Book Debts, Plant & Machinery and Equipments

Collateral Securities

1.    Term Loan for acquiring Office/Shop Premises – NIL

2.     Term Loan for acquiring other assets and/or working

 capital limit

a) upto Rs. 10.00 Lakh – NIL

b)    Above Rs. 10.00 Lakh upto Rs. 50.00 Lakh – 25% of the advance

c)    Above Rs. 50.00 Lakh – 50% of advance

Surety

One Surety with good means

Service Charges

0.25% of the sanctioned amount

Share Linkage

Applicant - 2.5% of sanctioned amount

Surety – Rs. 1000/-

Insurance

Comprehensive insurance of all Assets hypothecated/mortgaged with Bank Clause.

Note:

1)  The advances falling under Priority Sector may further belong to Weaker Section classified into following sub-categories:

(a)  ST, SC & Minorities excluding OBC & NT. Minorities include Sikhs, Muslims, Christians, Zoroastrians, Buddhists and Jains.

(b)  Women.

(c)  Persons with disabilities.

 

2)  This Loan Scheme is applicable for new clients and existing clients who may apply for new facility , if total exposure comes under Priority Sector.

 

3)   Credit Rating/Gradation should be done, though interest rate is not linked to Credit Rating.

 

4)   Distress sale value of the assets offered as collateral to be considered and not market value.

 

II.  Opening of a New General Ledger Head

1.  The IT dept. will make arrangements to open a new GL head named ESCLNUV.

2.  Branches will open accounts under the scheme under such separate General Ledger head namely ESCLNUV.

III.   The Loan Scheme as above is a liberalized one in various respects and contains several concessions. Branches will find it easier to market the same and increase Loans & Advances under Priority Sector. The Incharge of branches are advised to study the Scheme in detail and inform all the staff members about its features so that each staff member will be in a position to market the same.

IV.   DGMs of Zones are advised to review performance of all the branches on a weekly basis and ensure that the latter put in concerted efforts by involving all staff members which should result in substantial increase in Loans & Advances in Priority Sector. They are further advised to submit branch-wise monthly Performance Report to the General Manager (Credit) for perusal, review and submission before the Board.

V.    Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members at your Branch/ Department to ensure compliance.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
