Circular CR No. 199 dated 22nd November, 2017

 

Vehicle Loan and Housing Loan

Set of Security Documents in the Form of Booklets

(Code Nos. 1141 & 1143)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.                 A set of all the necessary security documents required to be obtained from Borrower and Guarantors in respect of Surety Loans has already been introduced in the form of a Booklet (Code No.1116). Branches have found the booklet immensely useful as their task of obtaining documents in respect Surety Loans has become easy.

 

II.  There is currently a multiplicity of documents required to be obtained from Borrower/s and Guarantor/s for securing various aspects of Housing Loans and Vehicle loans. This state of affairs causes annoyance to customers. Some documents can be integrated and a few are redundant. We have, therefore, rationalised the documents required to be executed by Borrower(s) and Guarantor(s) for these loans also and prepared their sets in the form of two booklets. Information regarding a few changes which have been carried out in them and the list of documents in each booklet is given below.

 

1. Booklet of Security Documents Pertaining to Vehicle Loan:

                   i.    The set of rationalised documents securing various aspects of vehicle loans is printed in the form of Booklet Code No. 1141.

 

                 ii.    One important point regarding rationalisation of vehicle loan documents is that certain clauses from previous documents namely (a) Undertaking (Code No.79), (b) Vehicle Booklet (Code No.31) and (c) Stamp duty Confirmation Letter (Code No.1094) have been incorporated in the document bearing Code No.1140 – Agreement for Hypothecation of Vehicle. Henceforth, the new document as per Code No.1140 for Vehicle Loans shall be obtained instead of document as per Code No. 400 (existing Agreement for Hypothecation of Vehicle), 79, 1094 and RTO Booklet (Code No.31) which have been discontinued.

 

                iii.    The Booklet for Vehicle Loans (Code No.1141) consists of following documents & they alone shall be used for all vehicle Loans.

2. Booklet of Security Documents Pertaining to Housing Loan:

i.   The set of rationalised documents securing various aspects of housing loans is printed in the form of Booklet Code No. 1143.

 

ii.  Certain clauses from previous documents namely the (a) Undertaking (Code No.79), (b) Stamp duty Confirmation Letter (Code No.1094) and (c) Declaration regarding construction of flat according to plans sanctioned by Authorities (Code No.1009 & 1010) have been incorporated in the document Code No.1142 –Agreement for Housing Loan. Henceforth the new document as per Code No.1142 for Housing Loans shall be obtained instead of documents Code No.596 (existing agreement for Housing Loan), 79, 1094 and Declaration (Code No.1009/1010), which are now discontinued.

 

iii.    The Booklet for Housing Loans (Code No.1143) consists of following documents & only they shall be used for Housing Loans sanctioned to Individuals for purchase of flat/house and construction of House etc. [except that additional documents as mentioned in point (iv) below will be required to be taken outside the booklet].

iv.    We have not included Memorandum of Equitable Mortgage (Code No.597) and Declaration regarding the mortgage of the property (CodeNo.598) in the Housing Loan Documents Booklet. Since these documents are required to be taken from the Branches which are situated in non-notified towns to the executing Branches, and as copies are also required to be taken out for filing with Notice of Intimation, Branches are advised to obtain the same separately by affixing appropriate stamp duty payable on these documents, as per extant guidelines.

 

III.   Further, Branches are advised not to obtain following Documents along with these Booklets [Code No.1116 ( For Surety Loan), 1141 (For Vehicle Loan) and 1143 (For Housing Loan)]:

1.   Declaration from the Borrower/ Guarantor/s stating that they are/ are not the relatives of the Directors of our Bank, as this clause is incorporated in the newly printed Applications and Consent Letters.

2.   Details of legal heirs on a separate paper as the details of the family members are incorporated in the Loan Application and Consent Letters.

3.   Letter regarding supplying the information of Borrowers to RBI/CIBIL etc.

4.   Debit Balance confirmation letter at the time of execution of documents.

5.   LAD at the time of execution of documents.

6.   Separate letters (Declaration) for utilization (end use) of the loan amount sanctioned.

 

IV.  Copies of the new Booklets will be supplied by the Stationery Dept. to all Branches. Credit Dept., H.O., shall post these Booklets in the Public Folder-> LOAN HO ->Formats->Agreements. In case of non-availability of Booklets in time, Branches may download these Booklets and, together with other documents as mentioned in Para II.2.iv, put them to immediate use.

 

V.    The Stationery Dept. shall discontinue printing of Stationery Code Nos.31, 400, 596, 1009 and 1010 and discard/scrap the existing stock lying with it. The Branches are also advised to discontinue using of Stationery Code Nos.31, 400, 596, 1009 and 1010 and discard/scrap them on receipt of new Booklets.

 

VI.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.
