Circular CR No. 247 dated 30th April, 2020

 

ABHYUDAYA RELIEF SCHEME

Business Disruption due to COVID-19 Pandemic Finance to Manage Liquidity Mismatch

____________________________________________________________

 

I.   Entire world is currently reeling under COVID-19 crisis which has been declared as a Global Pandemic by World Health Organisation. World economy as also the Indian economy is suffering due to its outbreak.

 

II. There is drastic decline in cash flows of several businesses due to the above. Some of our constituents to whom we have granted credit facilities to run their businesses may have become vulnerable during the current pandemic & may be facing liquidity crunch.

 

III.  Our customers currently facing liquidity mismatch due to the COVID-19 pandemic may require infusion of liquidity for some limited period. A scheme, named “Abhyudaya Relief Scheme”, has therefore been drawn and the same has been approved in the Board meeting dated 29th April, 2020. This is a new product, to run for a limited period, available to our existing borrowers enjoying Fund Based Working Capital limits. Details thereof are as under.

 

Sr. No.

Parameter

Details

1.

Name of the Facility

Abhyudaya Relief Scheme

2.

Facility Type

Term Loan

3.

Total Period

24 Months (Maximum)

4.

Purpose

To meet the liquidity mismatch due to COVID-19 crisis.

5.

Share Money

0.50 % of the sanctioned amount

6.

Validity of the Scheme

Scheme will be in force upto 30.06.2020

7.

Eligibility

All borrowal accounts availing Fund Based Working Capital Limit

with classification as Standard as on the date of application

as well as on the date of disbursement.

 

Sr. No.

Parameter

Details

8.

Loan Amount

a)  Limit: - Up to 10% of the existing Fund Based Working Capital Exposure.

Maximum amount : Rs. 1.00 crore

b) The Facility will be considered as an exposure on the borrower and guidelines

stipulated in CR Circular No. 242 dated 31.03.2020 shall be adhered to.

c)  The facility shall be need based and on specific request from the borrower.

9.

Disbursement

Disbursement of sanctioned limit shall be made directly to the suppliers

/ vendors of inputs, LC Payment and/or may be disbursed

for payment of pending salary, wages and statutory dues.

10.

Repayment

Repayable in maximum 24 Equated Monthly Instalments (EMI) with (inclusive)

 moratorium of 3 months from the date of disbursement of the loan.

Interest during the moratorium shall be serviced as and when due.

11.

Margin

NIL

12.

Interest Rate

Rate of interest @ 10.50% (Fixed)

13.

Security

HP of Stock & Book Debts and Continuation of existing collateral securities.

14.

Sureties

Existing sureties

15.

Documentation

/ ROC Charges

Documents as applicable to Term Loans as per the extant guidelines

are to be executed. (Code No.1006)

Registrations of security with CERSAI shall be completed within

one week’s time from the date of execution of security documents.

Registrations with ROC, if applicable, shall be completed within

the stipulated period from the date of execution of security documents.

16.

Processing Fee

0.25 % of Sanctioned limit

17.

Others

I.    The facility shall be made available at the specific request of the borrower, stating

II.  that the business has been adversely affected by COVID-19.

III. The facility shall not be allowed to be used for payment of existing overdue

IV.         of the borrower with our Bank or any other bank or for any speculative purposes.

V.  This facility will not be reckoned for drawing power

VI.         End use to be ensured.

18.

Delegation

As per respective delegation of powers for sanction without clubbing existing facilities

of the borrower but within over all Individual/Group exposure limit

 

IV.  Opening of a New General Ledger Head

1) The IT dept. will make arrangements to open a new GL head named ESCLNRLF & Related PL Head in consultation with Accounts dept.

2) Branches will open accounts of the scheme under such separate General Head.

V. Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to notice of all staff members at your Branch/Department for creating awareness.

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
