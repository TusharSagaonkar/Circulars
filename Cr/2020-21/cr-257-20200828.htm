Circular CR No. 257 dated 28th August, 2020

 

Revision in Definitions of MSME

Investment in Plant and Machinery or Equipment to be at Written Down Value

Revised Formats of Letters to Borrowers and Certificate from CA or Self-Declaration

_______________________________________________________________

I.                 Please refer Circular CR No. 254 Dated 31st July, 2020 on the captioned subject. In view of the changes in definition, drafts of letters to be addressed to borrower-enterprises and forms for Certificates to be obtained from CA, or Self-Declaration to be obtained from the MSME units themselves, were provided in the annexures to the circular under reference.

 

II.  1. Now, in terms of Circular No. RBI/2020-2021/26 FIDD.MSME & NFS.BC.No.4/ 06.02.31/2020-21 dated 21st August, 2020, RBI has communicated the clarification [under OFFICE MEMORANDUM No. 21(5)2019-P&G/Policy (pt. IV) dated 6th August, 2020] issued by the Ministry of MSME that the value of Plant and Machinery or Equipment for all purposes of the Notification No. S.O. 2119(E) dated June 26, 2020 and for all the enterprises shall mean the Written Down Value (WDV) as at the end of the Financial Year as defined in the Income Tax Act and not cost of acquisition or original price, which was applicable in the context of the earlier classification criteria.

 

2. The said clarification also covers the point relating to time-limit (which is up to 31st March, 2021) for validity of registrations already made up to 30th June, 2020 by existing enterprises, & recommendation for filing new registrations on “Udyam Registration Portal” well before 31st March, 2021.

 

III.   1. In view of the above, the references to “investments in Plant & Machinery or Equipment” being “original” in the circular under reference shall be deemed in terms of the instant circular as omitted and all such investments shall be deemed and interpreted to be at “Written Down Value”.

 

2. The draft letters to our borrowing enterprises with credit facilities more than Rs.10 Crore & borrowing enterprises with credit facilities up to Rs.10 Crore at Annexure II-A & Annexure II-B respectively have been modified to include the clarification, as mentioned in Para II.2 above regarding time-limit (which is up to 31st March, 2021) for validity of registration as EM Part II/or UAM made by existing enterprises up to 30th June, 2020, and recommendation to them for re-registration on “Udyam Registration Portal” well before 31st March, 2021. The said Annexures, are posted as ms-word files in Public Folder of Loans HO->Other formats.

3.The formats of Certificates to be obtained from CA, or Self-Declarations to be obtained from the MSME units themselves, previously provided in four annexures to the circular under reference i.e. Annexure-marked-II-A-Certificate, Annexure-marked-II-B-Self-Declaration, Annexure III-Certificate & Annexure IV-Self-Declaration have been modified in view of the clarification by the Ministry of MSME as mentioned in Para II.1 above. The four revised Annexures are given herewith.

 

4. Annexure-marked-II-A-Certificate & Annexure-marked-II-B-Self-Declaration will be attached to the letters as per Annexure II-A & Annexure-II-B respectively when the latter are sent to the respective category of borrowing enterprises.

 

5. Annexure III-Certificate or Annexure IV-Self-Declaration shall be obtained from enterprises applying for credit facilities (above Rs. 10 Crore or up to Rs. 10 Crore, respectively) with new set-up of Plant & Machinery or Equipment.

 

IV.   If letters namely Annexure II-A & II-B, as may be applicable, attached to the previous Circular CR No.254 dated 31st July, 2020 have already been sent to some of the borrowing enterprises the new set of corresponding letters as per the instant circular need not be sent again. However, the said borrower enterprises need to be apprised of the WDV method of calculation of investment & limited validity of old registrations upto 30th June, 2020 & need for re-registration on “Udyam Registration Portal” well before 31st March, 2021.

 

V. All other instructions contained in the CR No. 254 Dated 31st July, 2020 remain unchanged.

 

VI.  Please bring the contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the staff-members concerned for meticulous compliance.

 

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
