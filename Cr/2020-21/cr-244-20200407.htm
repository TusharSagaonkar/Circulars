Circular CR No. 244 dated 7th April, 2020

 

Reporting of Large Exposures to Central Repository of Information on Large Credits (CRILC) - UCBs

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.  RBI has historically been having a system of compiling information on large credits by commercial banks. The previous system of compiling information on such large credits was subsumed under “Central Exposure Across Banks” in September, 2013 and subsequently refined under the “Credit Repository of Information on Large Credits (CRILC)” notified in terms of Circular No. RBI/2013-14/452 DBS No.OSMOS.9862/ 33.01.018/2013-14 dated 13th February, 2014.

 

II.  The same has now been extended to Urban Co-op Banks in terms of RBI Circular RBI/2019-20/125 DOR (PCB).BPD.Cir.No.7/13.05.000/2019-20 dated December 27, 2019. The credit department at HO with the assistance of MIS & IT departments had compiled the data & information on the subject for the quarter ended 31st December, 2019 and submitted to RBI. However, from now onwards, some data and information to be included in the CRILC report will have to be collected from branches, as currently entire data required to be submitted to RBI is not available through system and manual intervention is required.

 

III.   The information regarding CRILC & the system of reporting thereunder is hereby being shared with branches & all the departments/offices so that there is better understanding of the importance of credit discipline, maintenance of correct data in respect thereof & the need for submitting prompt & accurate information herein as required thereunder.

 

IV.   Important aspects of CRILC as applicable to our Bank :

 

1)     Credit information, including classification of an account as Special Mention Account (SMA), on borrowers having aggregate exposure (all fund-based and non-fund based exposure, including investment exposure of the borrower) of Rs. 5 Crore & above, at the end of every quarter, is to be reported to RBI (CRILC – UCB return) on XBRL Reporting Platform of RBI.

 

2)     PAN is the unique identifier in CRILC reporting. All details of a borrower are linked to PAN. Any mistake in recording & reporting of PAN of a borrowing party will result in rejection of the return. Hence branches are instructed to take utmost care in feeding correct PAN and ensure that the name of the account is as per PAN (Permanent Account Number).

 

3)     It is mandatory to submit the return within 30 days from the end of the relative quarter on XBRL reporting platform of RBI. It is likely that the return will be required to be submitted more frequently in future.

 

4)     The CRILC – UCBs return is divided in three sections (Annexure – I):

i)      Exposure to Large Borrowers (This will be compiled by MIS department through data generated from system with the help of IT dept., supplemented by other relevant information manually submitted by branches, duly checked & corrected by and collected through respective zonal offices (as per the Section – I of Annexure-I):- Under this, bank needs to report the credit information, of all borrowers including NPA accounts having aggregate exposures (fund-based, non-fund based and investment exposure) of Rs. 5 Crore and above. (Please see Para VI.3) for more information on security). It is also necessary to provide investment exposure & trade finance related information by the departments concerned i.e. Treasury dept & Forex dept.

ii)    Reporting of Technically/Prudentially Written-off Accounts (to be provided by Recovery department):- The data on the amount written off, if any, for borrowers whose technically/prudentially written off amount is Rs. 5 Crore or more and which are not reported in Section I is to be reported in Section – II (Annexure - I).

iii)  Reporting of Balance in Current Account: (To be provided by MIS department):- Under this, the bank needs to report the data in Section - III of Annexure – I on current account holders whose

·      balance (either credit or debit) in current account as on reporting date is Rs. 1 Crore and above or

·      Total of credit summation (sum of all credit transactions) during the reporting quarter is Rs. 5 Crore and above or

·      Total of debit summation (sum of all debit transactions) during the reporting quarter is Rs. 5 Crore and above.

·      The information of current accounts which is reported in Section - I should not be reported again in Section - III of Annexure - I.

 

V.    The formats of Annexure - I (having three sections), Annexure - II & Annexure - III are enclosed herewith.

 

VI.   In respect of Annexure – I, since data of all the fields are not available through system, certain information is required to be called from branches as per Annexure – II. Information in respect of some fields will be provided by MIS dept. In this regard, following points need to be noted.

 

1)  Information required as per the Annexure - I should ideally be available directly from OMNI system. It is observed that correct and updated data is not fed by branches in relative fields of OMNI system. Branches are instructed to take care for making complete and accurate data entry.

 

2)  Data regarding aggregate of fund-based & non-fund based exposures of any party cannot, at present, be generated directly from Omni System. Therefore, Branches are instructed to update the information as per Annexure – III which will be provided by the Credit Dept through Zonal Office.

 

 

3)Even though there are guidelines about considering secured/unsecured portion, and prime/collateral securities, there are no guidelines on apportionment of security for deciding secured/un-secured portion of credit facilities extended to borrower. The matter is important also in view of requirement for reporting to various authorities including RBI (apart from the instant CRILC – UCB Return).

 

Following guidelines should be adhered to for working out secured portion of Fund based and Non-fund based credit facilities.

 

Ø Fund based :

·      CC Facility – Prime security is stock & book debts + accrued value of FDRs offered as security for sub limit. If the said value is sufficient to cover the limit or outstanding whichever is higher, then value of securities should be shown only equal to the outstanding amount.

 

·      In case of WCTL, only that part of the available amount of valuation of stock and book debts which is equivalent to outstanding amount of WCTL should be considered for calculating secured portion for WCTL. If there is surplus of valuation of stock & book debts over & above the outstanding WCTL, it should be shown as security for CC (only upto the outstanding amount or limit whichever is higher). Thus available amount of valuation of stock & book debts should be first apportioned towards WCTL/STCL and balance amount of valuation be reckoned for CC limit.

 

·      In case of Term Loan: Value of Plant & Machinery, Land & Building, vehicle purchased etc. as per the latest valuation, not more than outstanding in the loan account, should be considered for working out secured portion.

 

Note: - If there is deficit in value of prime security as mentioned above, then towards such deficit, an equivalent amount from the value of Immovable Property and present value of any other cash collateral, if available, should be apportioned.

Excess value of security above outstanding amount is not accepted on the XBRL Reporting Platform. Therefore, the maximum value of securities which can be reported is only equal to the outstanding amount.

 

Ø Non- fund based :

·      In case of Non-fund based facilities where 100% cash margin is available, they are to be excluded for arriving at exposure. Value of such FDRs which are taken as 100% cash margin should not appear anywhere else either i.e. as secured portion of other Non-fund based credit facilities, or even any Fund based facilities.

 

·     If any Non-fund based facility is sanctioned with less than 100% margin in the nature of cash collateral, then for resultant deficit, an equivalent from the value of immovable property (excluding value considered for funded facility) should be considered.

 

4)  IT department is hereby instructed to modify programme with the help of vendor for perfection of accounting/reporting of non-fund based facilities in OMNI system, with a view to integrating data arising therefrom with that across all the credit facilities of a client.

 

 

VII. As mentioned in Para II above, the credit department at HO with the help of IT & MIS departments will finalise the return on the basis of data generated from OMNI with due modification & validation on the basis of Annexure II compiled by branches on the basis of Back-up register & submit the same to Reserve Bank of India. It is reiterated that correctness of data must be ensured while submitting CRILC Report. Please note that accuracy and integrity is of paramount importance while submitting the information to RBI failing which Bank will be liable for penal action. Therefore, submission of incorrect/incomplete information will be viewed seriously and necessary action will be initiated against the staff members concerned.

 

VIII.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.
