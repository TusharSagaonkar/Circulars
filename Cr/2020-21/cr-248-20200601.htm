Circular CR No. 248 dated 1st June, 2020

COVID-19 PANDEMIC

1.  Continuation of RBI’s Regulatory Package extending the previously declared moratorium on Term loans and deferment of recovery of interest on Working Capital facilities by 3 more months so as to make it for total 6 months from 1st March, 2020 to 31st August, 2020.

2.  Funded Interest Term Loan to account for accrued interest on working capital finance over the said six months recoverable in seven EMIs i.e. from September, 2020 to March, 2021- Instructions to follow later

3.  Shifting of residual tenure for Term Loans further by such extended period.

4.  After 31st May, 2020, reduced margin at 5 % for working capital finance to continue only in justifiable cases

5.  Extension of Abhyudaya Relief Scheme up to 30th September, 2020.

 

I.    Please refer Circular CR No. 243 dated 31st March, 2020 in terms of which relief was granted, against the backdrop of COVID-19 pandemic, to borrowers by way of 3 months’ moratorium on EMIs of Term Loans, moratorium in respect of interest on working capital advances & reduction in margin on stocks & receivables in respect of working capital advances. The circular was issued as per the Board approved policy which itself was framed in light of the RBI circular Ref: DOR.No.BP.BC.47/ 21.04.048/2019-20 dated 27th March, 2020.

 

II.      RBI has now issued the Circular No. RBI/2019-20/244 DOR.No.BP.BC.71/ 21.04.048/2019-20 May 23, 2020 in terms which it has recommended continuation of relief measures beyond the initially declared period of 3 months for another 3 months up to 31st August, 2020, i.e. over a total period of 6 months counted from 1st March, 2020. This has been done to continue easing of financial stress caused by the disruptions due to the COVID-19 pandemic. Specific advices have been issued to banks, including ours, towards this for improving borrowers’ access to working capital by mitigating the burden of debt-servicing. The larger motive is to prevent transmission of financial stress to the real economy and ensure continuity of viable businesses & households. Extension of relief measures continues to be subject to supervisory review as to justifiability.

 

III.    In light of the RBI’s aforesaid circular, recommendations were submitted to the Board for formulating a policy. The submission in this behalf has been approved in the Board meeting dated 28th May, 2020. In terms of the instant circular, operative guidelines are being issued to give effect to the policy.

 

IV.Specific Aspects of Policy & Actions in Respect thereof:-

1) Term Loans :-

i)   Extension of moratorium by another 3 months

The moratorium on term loan installments has been extended by another 3 months, i.e. from 1st June, 2020 to 31st August, 2020, in addition to the 3 months allowed in terms of our previous circular CR No. 243 dated 31st March, 2020 (i.e. 6 months in total from 1st March, 2020 to 31st August, 2020) across the board without seeking applications from borrowers.

ii)     Accrual of interest on Term Loan during moratorium

The interest accrued & accruing over the entire period of moratorium i.e. from 1st March, 2020 to 31st August, 2020. In case the borrower is willing to pay the interest accrued during moratorium period then such interest can be recovered. If borrower is unwilling to pay accrued interest then instructions in said matter will be issued in due course.

iii)   Extension of Residual Tenure

Residual tenure of all the term loans will be extended by 3 additional months i.e. 6 months in total [comprised of (a) 3 initial months according to advice under the RBI Circular dated 27th March, 2020, & (b) 3 more months now advised under RBI’s circular dated 23rd May, 2020].

iv)    As this is only extension of the reliefs currently granted,

a. The provisions as above will continue to be uniformly applicable to all the term loans including Housing Loans, Mortgage Loans, Vehicle Loans, Surety Loans, Gold Loans, Education Loans & other Term Loans etc. as on March 1, 2020.

b. Whenever installment(s) has/have already been paid by the borrower, the relief will be applicable for the EMIs payable for subsequent months during the said moratorium.

c.  If any borrower intends to make payment of EMI during this moratorium, the same can be treated as advance payment and will be adjusted against the principal plus interest i.e. outstanding amount of the loan.

d. ECS/SI option will not be exercised during this period. However, branch officials should make concrete efforts to contact the borrowers & persuade them to pay EMI. If payment of EMI is not possible, the borrower should be persuaded to pay the amount of accrued interest. Since the relief is extended for installments of loan which are due only between 1st March 2020 and 31st August, 2020, the installments/other amounts overdue on or before 1st March 2020 in any of the loan account were required to be paid before 31st March 2020 in order to avoid penal charges, down-gradation of account and slippage in credit rating. It needs to be ensured that appropriate action was taken in regard to deviations such as above.

e. No penal interest or charges will be collected for non-payment of EMI during the moratorium period.

 

2) Accrual of interest on working capital finance during moratorium

In respect of cash credit facilities sanctioned the interest during the period from 1st March, 2020 up to 31st August, 2020 shall be debited to accounts and the overdrawn amount equivalent to the interest debited for such period will be treated as Ad-hoc limit. Such Ad-hoc limit will be cancelled automatically after expiry of the period, i.e. w.e.f. 1st September, 2020. However, such Ad-hoc limit will not be applicable to FLXLN, SOD, SODIMP, SALCC and SODGOLD Accounts. Banks, at their discretion, are permitted to consider such accrued interest i.e. interest from 1st March, 2020 to 31st August, 2020 for conversion into Funded Interest Term Loan (FITL) A/c. recoverable in 7 EMIs from September, 2020 to March, 2021. Instructions, regarding scrutiny/appraisal/recommendation/sanction and accounting of accrued interest during the said 6 months of moratorium on working capital facilities under FITL, & its recovery, are being issued separately.

 

3) After 31st May, 2020, reduced margin at 5 % for working capital finance to continue only in justifiable cases

i)  Uniform reduction of margin by 5 % on Stocks & Receivables in respect of all the standard working capital facilities as on 29th February, 2020 was only up to 31st May, 2020. Powers to Branch-in-Charge for reducing margin by 5 % over & above the uniform reduction has also, therefore, lapsed after 31st May, 2020.    

ii) However, Branch In-charge is now vested with power to continue the uniformly reduced 5% margin, beyond 31st May, 2020 till 31st August, 2020, to those borrowers in respect of whom he/she is satisfied that the same is necessitated on account of the economic fallout from COVID-19. This will be upon written request of the borrower justification of which will be recorded by the Branch In-charge by way of a note. The request & the note, both, shall be kept on record.

 

V.   Extension of Abhyudaya Relief Scheme up to 30th September, 2020.

1) Please refer Circular CR No. 247 dated 30th March, 2020 in terms of which formulation of the captioned scheme, in the wake of the COVID-19 pandemic, was advised together with its details. In view of the COVID-19 pandemic continuing to affect liquidity of businesses and households, the Abhyudaya Relief Scheme has been extended up to 30th September, 2020.

2) Branches will therefore continue to consider applications for loans under the scheme up to 30th September, 2020, on merits. All other Terms & conditions pertaining to the scheme mentioned in the said circular continue to remain operative.

 

VI.    IT dept. is hereby advised to arrange to send SMS to all the borrowers and to put Notice on Bank’s Website regarding extension of the relief measures.

 

VII.  It is reiterated that relaxations as per the instant circular given in respect of administration & accounting of loans & advances are only in the context of the COVID-19 pandemic & its adverse fallout. These relaxations should not result in complacency and lowering of guard on the part of the staff engaged in sanction, delivery & supervision of credit.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
