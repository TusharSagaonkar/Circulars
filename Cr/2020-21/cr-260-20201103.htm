Circular CR No. 260 dated 3rd November, 2020

 

RESOLUTION PLAN FOR PERSONAL LOANS

AND

MICRO, SMALL & MEDIUM ENTERPRISES (MSMEs) ABOVE Rs. 25 CRORE

________________________________________________________________________________­­­­­_____

I.   INTRODUCTION

 

Objective:

The objective of the Board Approved Policy dated 16th October 2020 for Resolution Plan for personal Loans and (MSMEs) Above Rs. 25 Crore is to provide a framework for restructuring of borrower accounts under financial stress caused by the COVID-19 pandemic. The resolution/ restructuring will enable the eligible borrowers to relieve the financial stress which is affecting their cash flows and in-turn their debt servicing capabilities.

1) i) In terms of the circular No.RBI/2020-21/16 DOR.No.BP.BC/3/21.04.048/2020-21

dated 6th August, 2020 (“6th August, 2020 Circular”). RBI came out with the ‘Resolution Framework for COVID-19-related Stress’. It is applicable, subject to specific conditions, to eligible borrowers,

a) who are corporate persons or otherwise (excluding MSME borrowers with exposure to Banking Industry at Rs. 25 Crore or less*),and,

b) who have taken personal loans.

 

ii) All the norms applicable to implementation of a resolution plan are to be applicable under this framework.

(* For loans up to Rs. 25 Crore to MSMEs, there is already a separate dispensation which has been put in place by Bank & circularized vide our circular CR No. 256 dated 20 August 2020, under guidance from RBI under the Circular RBI/2020-21/ 17DOR.No. BP.BC/4/21.04.048/2020-21 which is also dated 6th August 2020).

2)    RBI also constituted an Expert Committee to recommend a list of financial parameters which, in their opinion, would be required to be factored into the assumptions that go into each resolution plan, and the sector-specific benchmark-ranges for such parameters. The committee submitted its report to RBI on 4th September, 2020. Recommendations in the report were broadly accepted by RBI and RBI issued the circular no. RBI/2020-21/34 DOR.No.BP.BC/13/21.04.048/2020-21 dated 7th September, 2020 (“7th August, 2020 circular”), as follow up to the “6th August, 2020 Circular”. The financial parameters recommended by the expert committee and approved by RBI are mandatory for the Financial Institutions.

 II.   POLICY APPROVED BY THE BOARD

1)    RBI issued guidelines for each financial institution, including our Bank, to put in place a Board approved policy within the broad contours of the two circulars namely “the 6th August, 2020 Circular” and “7th September, 2020 Circular”. A policy framed in accordance with the guidelines & within the contours was placed in the Board meeting dated 16th October, 2020 which met with approval.

 

2)    Instant circular contains advices and operational instructions in respect of the said policy.

 

III.  MANDATORY CONDITIONS

Conditions as under need to be strictly followed:

1)    Facility is to be extended only to the borrowers having stress on account of COVID-19.

 

2)    Borrower-account must have been classified as standard as on 1st March, 2020; additionally, it must not be in default for more than 30 days as on March 1, 2020. (Such accounts continue to remain standard under RBI’s special dispensation of freezing asset classification of standard assets as on 1st March, 2020, which has been further prolonged beyond 31st August, 2020 due to the stay by the Hon. Supreme Court on 3rd September, 2020.)

 

3)    The eligible borrowers’ accounts need to continue to be classified as Standard till the date of invocation of resolution under the framework.

 

4)    i. For Personal Loans: Resolution under this framework must be invoked not later

 than 31st December, 2020 and must be implemented within 90 days from the date of invocation;

ii. For Other Exposures: The resolution should be implemented within 180 days from

 the date of invocation.

 

5)    The reference date for the outstanding amount of debt that may be considered for resolution is March 1, 2020.

 

IV.   ELIGIBLE LOAN TYPES:

1)    *The bank has decided to provide relief under Resolution plan for following seven categories in case of personal loans:

i)      EDNLN- Education Loan

ii)    ESCLN- Secured Loan

iii)   ESCLNUV- Secured Loan Udyog Vikas

iv)   ESDLN- Salary Deduction Loan

v)    ESRLN- Surety Loan

vi)   HSGLN- Housing Loan

vii) HSG-PMAY- Housing Loan (Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana)

 

2)    These specific 7 categories of loans are selected considering the factors like type of customers, securities offered, probable impact of the COVID-19 pandemic etc.

 

3)    In case of Other Loans, Bank has identified specific customers for Resolution Plan who will be considered at Head office through respective Zonal Office as their aggregate exposure exceeds Rs. 25 Crore.

 

*Branch-wise list of identified customers to be eligible for the relief under Resolution Framework would be provided separately.

 

V.   FEATURES UNDER PRUDENTIAL FRAMEWORK ADOPTED BY BANK:

Bank has decided to adopt following features permitted under Prudential Framework for providing relief to eligible customers so as to enable them to address the ongoing stress caused by the current COVID-19 Pandemic:

1)    *Moratorium (including unexpired moratorium if any granted already) to be granted up to a maximum of 1 year from the date of implementation.

 

2)    Rescheduling of repayment of existing outstanding amount (including amount of interest accrued and to be accrued) by a period not more than 2 years from the date of implementation. Such maximum rescheduling is to be inclusive of moratorium, extended or otherwise.

(*Moratorium granted would be principal moratorium for the specified period in which customer would be servicing the interest on the outstanding amount.)

VI.   POLICY IMPLEMENTATION

1)            Manpower Allocation -

All the Branches and Zonal Offices will assign manpower for implementation of Resolution Plan in respective branches/offices. The dedicated personnel will be responsible for entertaining a customer, assessing his/her/their requirement, collecting from him/her/them details/information, documents (mentioned in following Paras), and maintaining a separate register in respect of the Resolution plan.

2)            Collection of Documents and details

            Requirements for execution of Resolution Plan in respect of different categories of borrowers are as given below:

i)  Personal Loans

a) Borrowers can apply for restructuring of credit facilities by visiting base branch and submitting necessary documents and information. Bank will review the application for restructuring based on the documents submitted and the information furnished by the borrower.

 

b)          List of Documents

Documents as per the following list should be submitted by borrower while applying for loan-restructuring under resolution plan:

·      Salaried Individual:

ü Application with Declaration/undertaking for Resolution Plan (Application Form attached as Annexure1-A)

ü Salary slips from January 2020 up to the month of application for restructuring.

ü Detailed bank statements from January 2020 up tothe month of application in respect of the account in which salary is credited.

ü Proof of job loss from previous employer (in case of job loss).

ü Declaration of estimated salary after end of 24 months’ period in case moratorium is to be availed.

 

·      Individuals with Non-salary income:

ü Application with Declaration/ undertaking for Resolution Plan

ü Detailed bank statements and/or operating account statements for the previous 12 months.

ü Income documents for the previous12 months.

ü GST Returns for H1 FY 2020, H2 FY 2020 and H1 FY 2021 (if applicable).

ü Declaration of estimated income after the end of 24 months’ period in case of availing the moratorium.

 

ii)     Other Loans – Sole Banking (MSMEs and other with aggregate exposure exceeding Rs. 25.00 Crore)

a) Application with Declaration/ undertaking for Resolution Plan (Application Form attached as Annexure1-B)

b) Applicant’s letter detailing the adverse impact on business of COVID-19 pandemic

c) Sales and purchase statement from January 2020

d) Audited stock statement as on a recent date (except for service industry)

e) GST Returns for H1 FY 2020, H2 FY 2020 and H1 FY 2021

f)  Audited financials for FY 2020 and Provisional financials till Q3 2021.

g) Financial model/ CMA with projections for atleast 5 years on realistic basis containing sensitivity scenarios for optimum-case and worst- case scenarios.

 

iii) Other Loans – Multiple Banking/ Consortium banking (MSMEs and other with aggregate exposure exceeding Rs.25.00 Crore)

a) All the documents applicable for sole banking would apply here.

b) The customers enjoying Multiple Banking/ Consortium banking would have to furnish following additional documents:

·      Details of all the facilities- Funded and Non-Funded availed in all other banks and NBFCs.

·      Asset classification of each facility in each bank and NBFC.

 

3)            Customer Identification and Scrutiny :

i)   As mentioned in Para III. ( “Mandatory Conditions”) and Para IV. (“Eligible Loan Types”) above, only those borrowers whose accounts fit in the criteria for consideration under the Resolution Plan and who agree to comply with the conditions prescribed under the Bank’s policy therefore shall be considered for relief under the Resolution Plan.

ii) The documents collected from a borrower will be retained at the branch, or forwarded to the Zonal Office concerned or Head Office, as per the pre-determined ‘Implementation Levels’ mentioned in Para "Sanctioning delegation” below.

Key aspects to be considered for scrutiny

The standard of scrutiny should be rigorous. The scrutinizing official should keep in view following aspects while appraising the proposal for a Resolution Plan:

(While scrutinizing the proposal, separate Scrutiny Notes are to be used.

1)  In case of Individual Borrowers &

2)  In case of non-individual borrowers

Revised Scrutiny Notes will be posted in Public Folder > Loan HO > Formats).

 

a)General

·     Whether the borrower is doing adequate disclosures about facts.

·     Whether any contradiction is evidenced by the documents provided by borrower.

·     Whether borrower has ability to repay the EMIs where moratorium is not opted.

 

b)          Self-employed and Other Loans

·     Whether borrower has performed a preliminary assessment of business’s ability to overcome the problems faced due to COVID-19.

·     Were there any interruptions in income generation/cash flow due to COVID-19 outbreak

·     Type of industry/ sector in which the borrower is operating. Effect of COVID-19 on that particular sector.

·     Borrower’s ability to prepare timely financials or other required information.

·     Impact of lost revenue & cash flow on business.

·     Impact due to pricing and volume volatility.

·     Impact of FOREX fluctuations including impact of hedging arrangements.

·     Cost associated with temporary suspension of business

·     Inability to honor certain terms of contracts/ agreements leading to penalties.

·     Impact of the ongoing slowdown of overall economy on the sector concerned and further resultant impact on cash flows of business.

 

4)Sanctioning delegation:

i)      Branch: The proposal with scrutiny note (keeping in view key aspects as stated above) in respect of each eligible case will be recommended by designated officer/ Assistant Manager. The branch in-charge will sanction such proposals with outstanding loan amount up to Rs. 10.00 Lakh.

ii)    Zonal Office: The proposal with scrutiny note (keeping in view key aspects as stated above) in respect of each eligible case will be recommended by designated officer. The Deputy General Manager will sanction the proposals in range of outstanding loan amount from Rs. 10.00 Lakh up to Rs. 1.00 Crore.

iii)   Head Office: The proposal with scrutiny note (keeping in view key aspects as stated above) in respect of each eligible case will be recommended by Credit Department. The General Manager (Credit & Forex) will sanction the proposals with outstanding loan amount of Rs. 1.00 crore and above.

 

5)Selection of Adopted Feature(s) for Resolution Plan

Branch-In-Charge / Sanctioning Authority may discuss the features of the Prudential Framework adopted by Bank (see Para IV above) with the customer so that the best possible alternative may be selected which will enable the customer to address the ongoing stress due to current COVID-19 Pandemic

6)Additional Interest of 0.50 % p. a.

Bank has decided to charge additional interest of 0.5% p. a. over and above the current pricing for the remaining tenure of the loan, in order to offset partial cost of additional provisions required to be made by the Bank as per statutory guidelines.

7)Documentation

Following documents will have to be executed.

i)    Restructuring Agreement for credit facility to be signed by borrower and all guarantors- Annexure 2.

ii)   Code no. 586- Borrower signing in language other than in English or are illiterate (wherever applicable).

 

VII.   PROVISIONING AND REVERSAL

1)            Provisioning

Provisions to be kept from the date of implementation shall be higher of the provisions held as per the extant IRAC norms immediately before implementation, or 10 percent of the residual debt.

 

2)            Reversal

Half of the provisions may be written back upon repayment of at least 20 per cent of the residual debt without slipping into NPA post implementation of the plan, and the remaining half may be written back upon the borrower paying another 10 per cent of the residual debt without slipping into NPA subsequently.

 

3)            Responsibility to make provisions & reversals

The recovery department will make provisioning and reversal in accordance with rules stated above for all the loan accounts covered under Resolution Plan.

 

VIII.   CREATION OF NEW FIELD FOR RESOLUTION PLAN

1) IT dept. will create a new field for Resolution Plan with two sub-fields, viz. (i) Moratorium and (ii) Rescheduling of repayment, under various loan-heads as mentioned under Para V above which will facilitate the identification of loans under Resolution Plan for further generation of MIS report to be submitted to higher authorities/ regulators.

2) MIS dept. will arrange to generate report of the credit facilities for which Resolution Plan is implemented.

3) Branches shall maintain Register for the loans restructured under Resolution Plan in the format given in Annexure-3.

(Note: All the relevant Annexures- 1-A, 1-B, 2 and 3 will be posted separately in public folder of Loan dept.)

IX.   Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members at your Branch/Dept. for compliance.
