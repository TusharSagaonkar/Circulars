Circular CR No. 263 dated 10th November, 2020

Interest Subvention Scheme for MSMEs

Extension to Co-operative Banks

_______________________________________________________________________________________

 

I.  Introduction

1. Government of India, Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) had announced the Interest Subvention Scheme for MSMEs 2018 on November 2, 2018 for Scheduled Commercial Banks. Salient features and operational guidelines for implementation of the Scheme were released by the Ministry.

2. RBI, through the Circular RBI/2020-21/48 DOR (PCB).BPD.Cir No.3/13.05.001/ 2020-21 October 7, 2020, has advised that Government of India has decided to include Co-operative Banks also as Eligible Lending Institutions effective from 3rd March, 2020.

3. The Scheme provides for an interest relief of two per cent per annum to eligible MSMEs on their outstanding fresh / incremental term loan / working capital during the period of its validity. The coverage of the Scheme is limited to all term loans / working capital to the extent of Rs.100 lakh. The loan accounts on the date of filing claim should not have been declared as NPA as per the extant guidelines in force. No interest subvention shall be admissible for any period during which the account remains NPA.

 

II.  Approval of the Board

Considering that the scheme is beneficial to our MSME borrowers, a submission recommending its implementation in our Bank was placed in the Board meeting dated 26th October, 2020 which met with approval. In furtherance thereof, advices as under are being issued.

 

III. Salient Features of the Scheme:

1. Purpose, Scope and Duration :

i.      The Scheme aims at encouraging both manufacturing and service enterprises to increase productivity, and provides incentives to MSMEs.

ii.     The Scheme was initially in operation for a period of two financial years FY 2019 and FY 2020. The same has been extended & modified by the Government so that it is now valid till 31st March, 2021. Further, co-operative banks have been brought in its fold.

iii.   Accordingly, fresh or incremental term loan / working capital limit extended by Co-operative Banks with effect from March 3, 2020 will be eligible for coverage under the Scheme.

 

2. Eligibility for Coverage :

i.      Initially, all the MSMEs were to be eligible as beneficiaries under the Scheme if they met the following criteria :

a.  Valid Udyog Aadhaar Number (UAN)

b. Valid GSTN Number

 

ii.     The scheme has now been modified by the Government so that :

a.  Requirement of Udyog Aadhaar Number (UAN) may be dispensed with for units eligible for GST. Units not required to obtain GST may either submit Income Tax Permanent Account Number (PAN) or their loan account must be categorized as MSME by the concerned Bank.

b. Trading activities without UAN have also been allowed to be covered under the Scheme.

c.  Acceptance of claims in multiple lots for a given half-year by eligible institutions is permitted.

d. MSME exporters availing interest subvention for pre-shipment or post-shipment credit under Department of Commerce will not be eligible for assistance under Interest Subvention Scheme for Incremental credit to MSMEs 2018.

e.  MSMEs already availing interest subvention under any of the Schemes of the State / Central Govt. will not be eligible under the proposed Scheme.

 

3. Other aspects :

i.      Branches shall be responsible for submission of the accurate data and monitoring of the scheme.

ii.     Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI) acts as Nodal Agency for the purpose of channelizing of interest subvention to various participant lending institutions through their Nodal Office. It is the single national level nodal implementation agency for the Scheme.

iii.   Each participating Financial institution is required to appoint a Nodal Officer. He/she will submit its half yearly claims to SIDBI. In our Bank, Mr. Umesh Marathe, Industrial Finance Manager has been designated as Nodal Officer for receiving claims from Branches/Zones, forwarding them to SIDBI & following them up.

 

4. Claim Submission

i.   The Nodal officer will submit Bank’s half yearly claims to SIDBI as per the format given in Annexure-I. Information with respect to loans disbursed and interest relief claimed (branch-wise) will be submitted in soft copy in excel in the format given in Annexure-II.

ii.  Branches will compile data in respect of claims as per Annexure-III. The same shall be submitted by branches directly to the Nodal Officer at Head Office with a copy thereof to Zonal Office/Regional Office.

 

iii.   All claims must be got duly certified by the Statutory Auditors of the Bank. The certificate shall include statement on verification of individual accounts with regard to amount, incremental / fresh lending, interest charged and amount claimed. It will have to be ensured that amounts of relief claimed as indicated in Annex I, II and III are matched.

iv.   Interest subvention will be released only on the basis of claim duly certified by the Statutory Auditors of Bank. SIDBI will not be liable for any inaccurate submission of data by Bank.

v.     Inspection department to arrange for such certification through statutory auditors.

vi.   The amount of Interest subvention shall be released by SIDBI subject to availability of funds from Government of India.

vii. Interest subvention amount shall be released by SIDBI subject to availability of funds from GOI. Also, MoMSME, GOI will be the final authority for all interest subvention related matters and their decision would be final and binding. Receipt of funds by the eligible institutions would be treated as Utilization Certificate of the Fund.

 

IV. Please bring contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned and ensure that claims regarding interest subvention are submitted in time in respect of all the eligible borrowers with due accuracy.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
