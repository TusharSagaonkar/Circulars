Circular CR No. 261 dated 3rd November, 2020

Revised guidelines on Priority Sector Lending (PSL)

Classification of Various Credit Facilities under Priority Sector

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

I.    Please refer Circular CR No. 214 dated 6th September, 2018 on the subject of guidelines on & classification of various credit facilities under Priority Sector.

 

II.  1. RBI has come out with comprehensive and updated instructions together with guidelines on Priority Sector Lending (PSL)for all the banks, including Primary(Urban) Co-operative Banks (UCBs), vide its communication no.RBI/FIDD/2020-21/72-Master Directions FIDD.CO.Plan.BC.5/04.09.01/2020-21 dated 4th September, 2020. The Master Direction provides differential instructions for different sectors of banking industry. Such of the instructions contained therein as are applicable to our Bank, being part of the UCB sector, are given hereunder with updates over the previous ones wherever necessary, with operational guidelines.

 

2. Instructions contained herein supersede all the previous instructions on the subject.

 

3. Lending previously classified under PSL but not in conformity with the revised & updated instructions and guidelines shall continue to be classified under PSL until closed or renewed.

 

III.   Broad categories under priority sector for the purpose of lending will continue to be as under.

1) Agriculture

2) Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises

3) Export Credit

4) Education

5) Housing

6) Social Infrastructure

7) Renewable Energy

8) Others

V. Broad sub-sectors eligible for priority sector lending are given below: -

1.    Agriculture

                           i.    Various sub-sectors within Agriculture and details of loan facilities with eligibility criteria& ceiling on loan amount etc. if any are given at Sr. No. 1 and its sub-points in the Annexure-I hereto.

                         ii.    List of permissible activities under Food & Agro-processing up to an aggregate sanctioned limit of Rs. 100 Crore per borrower from the banking system is given in Annexure-I-A.

 

2.    MSMEs

i.     The definition of MSMEs will be as per Government of India (GOI), Gazette Notification No. S.O.2119(E) dated June 26, 2020 read with circular RBI/2020-2021/10 FIDD.MSME& NFS.BC.No.3/06.02.31/2020-21 read with FIDD.MSME & BC.No.4/06.02.31/2020-21 dated July 2, 2020, August 21, 2020 respectively on ‘Credit Flow to Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Sector’ and updated from time to time.

ii.    All the credit facilities to MSMEs conforming to the “Revised Definitions of MSMEs w.e.f. 1st July, 2020” shall now qualify as Priority Sector lendings. Definitions of MSMEs, & important points of their governance structure are given at Sr. No. 2 and its sub-points in the Annexure-I hereto.

iii.   All the points, including operational guidelines, pertaining to lending to MSMEs in light of the “Revised Definitions of MSMEs w.e.f. 1st July, 2020” have been dealt with in detail in our Circular CR No. 254 Dated 31st July, 2020 as modified by the Circular CR No. 257 dated 28th August, 2020. Therefore, reference should be made to the said two CR circulars mentioned above for further guidance.

iv.  Credit facilities extended to an Individual/Professional for industrial or business purpose, like Purchase of Shop, Purchase of Vehicle, & existing Mortgage and Rent securitization loans shall be classified under Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises provided the units satisfy prescribed composite criteria for investment in Machinery/Equipment and turnover.

v.   Traders (wholesale as well as retail), Distributors, Commission Agents, Units having Selling Network, Transport Vehicle Aggregators etc. will come under priority sector provided they fulfill composite criteria for investment in Machinery/ Equipment and turnover for MSMEs and such entities have obtained Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum (UAM)/ Udyam Registration Certificate.

 

3.    Export Credit: - Export Credit under agriculture and MSME sectors are allowed to be classified as Priority Sector Lending in the respective categories viz. agriculture and MSME. Export Credit (other than in agriculture and MSME) will be allowed to classified as priority sector. Incremental export credit to borrowers over corresponding date of previous year, subject to a maximum of Rs. 40 Crore per borrower. There is no limit on turnover for eligibility as PSL. There is a ceiling of 2% of ANBC or CEOBE whichever is higher, which will be monitored at the level of MIS department. This item corresponds to point at Sr. No.3 in the Annexure-I hereto.

 

4.    Education :- All loans sanctioned to individuals for educational purposes, including those for vocational courses not exceeding Rs. 20 Lakh will be considered as eligible for priority sector classification. Loans currently classified as priority will continue till maturity. This item corresponds to the point at Sr. No.4 in the Annexure-I hereto.

 

5.    Housing :- All Loans sanctioned to individual as per limits prescribed below are eligible for priority sector classification:

i.    Loans to individuals up to Rs. 35 Lakh in metropolitan centres (with population of ten Lakh and above) and up to Rs. 25 Lakh in other centres for purchase/construction of a dwelling unit per family* provided the overall cost of dwelling unit at a metropolitan centre and at any other centre does not exceed Rs.45 Lakh and Rs.30 Lakh respectively. Existing individual housing loans presently classified under PSL will continue as PSL till maturity or repayment.

* Definition of Family – Husband, wife and Unmarried Children (Earning adult regardless of marital status may be treated as separate family).

 

(Note-A:-It may be noted that, internally, the overall cost of the dwelling unit will continue to be as per the Agreement for Sale/Sale Deed., as per our extant policy).

 

(Note-B:- Following branches of our bank are at non-metropolitan centres.

·  Kankavali

·  Savedi Road, Ahmednagar

·  Ashok Nagar, Nanded

·  Samrat Nagar, Nanded

·  City Branch, Udupi

·  Shirva, Udupi

The rest are all at metropolitan centres).

 

ii.     Loans up to Rs. 10 Lakh at metropolitan centres and up to Rs. 6 Lakh at other centres for repairs to damaged dwelling units conforming to the overall cost of the dwelling unit as prescribed in sub-Para no. i. above.

 (Note-a:- The loan for repairs to damaged dwellings will be such that unit cost of the relative dwellings shall not be over & above the ceiling for unit costs at Rs. 45 Lakh & Rs. 30 Lakh at metropolitan and other centres respectively. Such a loan may be sanctioned if, together with the housing loan proper, it remains within overall ceilings of respective unit costs, after lapse of a reasonable period of granting the said housing loan proper, so that financing for repairs to a damaged dwelling may be justified).

 

(Note-b:- In respect of a dwelling unit for which Housing Loan is not granted, loan for repair thereto up to the ceiling for respective loan amounts in metropolitan and other centres shall come under priority sector subject to cost of the dwelling unit conforming to the stipulation indicated in sub-Para i.)

 

(Note–c :- In terms of IDC Ref. No. HO/MD-920/1636/2020-21 dated 26th December, 2019 it has been instructed to put on hold New “Loans for Repair & Renovations of Flats/House including the purpose).

 

(Note-d:- Housing loans and House Repair Loans, above the ceilings for cost of dwelling unit & amount of loans prescribed for priority sector, can be considered if they are otherwise in accordance with the Credit Policy; they will however come under Non-priority sector classification).

 

iii.   Loans to any governmental agency for construction of dwelling units or for slum clearance and rehabilitation of slum dwellers subject to dwelling units with carpet area of not more than 60 sq.m.

 

iv.   Loans for affordable housing projects using at least 50% of FAR/FSI for dwelling units with carpet area of not more than 60 sq.m.

 

v.     Loans to HFCs (approved by NHB for refinance) for on-lending, up to Rs.20 lakh for individual borrowers, for purchase/construction/reconstruction of individual dwelling units or for slum clearance and rehabilitation of slum dwellers, subject to an aggregate loan limit of Rs.20 lakh per borrower (to ensure & monitor which, bank should maintain necessary borrower-wise details of the underlying portfolio) and up to an overall limit of five percent of the bank’s total priority sector lending.

 

vi.   Housing loans to bank’s own employees do not qualify for classification under the priority sector. The details are given at Sr. No.5 in the Annexure – I hereto.

 

6.    Social infrastructure

Bank loans up to a limit of Rs. 5 Crore per borrower for setting up schools, drinking water facilities and sanitation facilities including construction/ refurbishment of household toilets and water improvement at household level, etc. and loans up to a limit of Rs. 10 Crore per borrower for building health care facilities including under ‘Ayushman Bharat’.

In case of UCBs, the above limits are applicable only in centres having a population of less than one lakh.

We will not grant loans under this category as none of the centres where we are established conform to the stipulation of a population of less than one lakh. This item corresponds to the point at Sr. No.6 in the Annexure – I hereto

 

7.    Renewable Energy

Loans up to a limit of Rs.30 crore per borrower for purposes like solar based power generators, biomass-based power generators, wind mills, micro-hydel plants and for non-conventional energy based public utilities, viz., street lighting systems and remote village electrification etc., are eligible for Priority Sector classification. For individual households, the loan limit will be Rs.10 lakh per borrower. This item corresponds to the point at Sr. No.7 in the Annexure-I hereto.

 

8.    Others Categories of Loans Coming Under Priority Sector

Following loans as per the prescribed limits are eligible for priority sector classification:

i.      Loans not exceeding Rs.1.00 lakh per borrower provided directly to individuals and individual members of SHG/JLG, provided the individual borrower’s household annual income in rural areas does not exceed Rs.1.00 lakh and for non-rural areas it does not exceed Rs.1.60 lakh; and loans not exceeding Rs. 2.00 lakh provided directly by banks to Self Help Group (SHG)/ Joint Liability Group (JLG) for activities other than agriculture or MSME, viz., loans for meeting social needs, construction or repair of house, construction of toilets or any viable common activity started by the SHGs.

ii.    Loans to distressed persons (other than distressed farmers indebted to non-institutional lenders) not exceeding Rs.1.00 lakh per borrower to prepay their debt to non-institutional lenders.

iii.  Loans sanctioned to State Sponsored Organisations for Scheduled Castes/ Scheduled Tribes for the specific purpose of purchase and supply of inputs and/or the marketing of the outputs of the beneficiaries of these organisations.

iv.  Loans up to Rs.50 crore to Start-ups, as per definition of Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Govt. of India that are engaged in activities other than Agriculture or MSME.The details are given at Sr. No. 8 in the Annexure-I hereto

 

VI.  WEAKER SECTIONS

(IT MAY BE NOTED THAT THE ACTIVITIES THEMSELVES NEED TO BE ELIGIBLE UNDER PRIORITY SECTOR)

 

The list of categories of borrowers, loans to whom are eligible for classification under the sub-sector of Weaker Sections, is as under.

1.     Small and Marginal Farmers.

2.     Artisans, village and cottage industries where individual credit limits do not exceed Rs.1 lakh.

3.     Beneficiaries under Government Sponsored Schemes such as National Rural Livelihood Mission (NRLM), National Urban Livelihood Mission (NULM) and Self Employment Scheme for Rehabilitation of Manual Scavengers (SRMS).

4.     Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

5.     Beneficiaries of Differential Rate of Interest (DRI) scheme.

6.     Self Help Groups.

7.     Distressed farmers indebted to non-institutional lenders.

8.     Distressed persons other than farmers, with loan amount not exceeding Rs.1 lakh per borrower to prepay their debt to non-institutional lenders.

9.     Individual women beneficiaries up to Rs.1 lakh per borrower.

[Note: -It may be noted that the category is now only for individual women with individual loans up to Rs. 1 Lakh.  (Existing loan to women will continue to be classified as weaker section till maturity or repayment)].

10.  Persons with disabilities

11.  Minority communities as may be notified by Government of India from time to time. The details are given at Sr. No. II in the Annexure – I hereto.

 

VII.    Other guidelines for priority sector loans as prescribed by RBI

Branches must comply with the following common guidelines for all categories of advances under the priority sector.

 

1. Service charges

No loan-related and ad hoc service charges/inspection charges should be levied on priority sector loans up to Rs. 25,000. In the case of eligible priority sector loans to SHGs/ JLGs, this limit will be applicable per member and not to the group as a whole.

 

2. Receipt, Sanction/Rejection/Disbursement Register

A register/electronic record should be maintained by branches wherein the date of receipt, sanction/rejection/disbursement with reasons thereof, etc. should be recorded. The register/electronic record should be made available to all inspecting agencies.

 

3. Issue of acknowledgement of loan applications

    Branches should provide acknowledgement for loan applications received under priority sector loans. Time limit prescribed under the extant Credit Policy for communicating decision in writing to the applicants must be adhered to.

 

VIII.            INTEREST RATES

There is a likelihood that some loans & advances under Non-priority Sector will come under Priority Sector with the changes in classification of advances under priority sector. In such circumstances, the current rates of interest applicable to them will continue until the fresh gradation if credit rating is applicable or immediately after submitting copy of Udyam Registration Certificate.

IX.                IMPORTANCE OF ACHIEVING TARGETS UNDER PRIORITY SECTOR

Importance of achieving goals and sub-goals under priority sector lending has been emphasised repeatedly through various circulars and other communications on the subject. With the increase in the said goal from the current 40 % of the ANBC or CEOBE whichever is higher from the previous 40% to 75 % by 31st March, 2024, importance& urgency of achieving the goals & sub-goals have increased many fold. Such achievement is monitored by RBI on the basis of our quarterly returns and is taken into account while granting regulatory clearances/approvals for various purposes.

With our vast base of customers & reach of the network of branches, it should not be difficult to achieve them within the prescribed timelines without compromising quality of lendings. Branches, Regional Offices and Zonal Offices should take initiative in this direction.

 

X.    Instructions to IT department

IT department to provide suitable menu in OMNI for recording PAN wise, customer wise details of investment in Plant &machinery /equipment (WDV) and turnover, which are required to be updated periodically.

 

 

XI.   Instructions to branches

Branches are advised to periodically update details of investment in Plant & machinery/ equipment (WDV) and turnover in all loan accounts of the customer except personal loan accounts, linked to the same PAN. These updations should be carried out yearly on the basis of latest audited financial statements or “Udyam Registration certificate” of the borrower. They are further advised to ensure correctness of the “Micro/ Small/ Medium” Enterprise and Priority/Non-priority classification entered in OMNI in all business loan accounts of the customer linked to the same PAN.

 

XII.    Instructions to MIS department

1. MIS dept will provide necessary data to branches for making required changes in Omni.

2. It will monitor & report on achievement of targets Priority Sector Lending target on ‘quarterly’ basis.

 

3. It will furnish the data (on priority sector lending in the reporting formats ‘Statement I’ and ‘Statement II (Part A to D)’ at quarterly and annual intervals, to the Regional Office of DoS, RBI.

 

XIII.    Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.
