Circular CR No. 250 dated 20th June, 2020

Partial Modification to CR No. 249 dated 6.6.2020

Special Scheme for Vehicle Loans for a

Limited Period for Two-wheeler Vehicles

___________________________________________________________

 

Please refer to our CR No. 249 dated 06.06.2020 on Special Scheme for Vehicle Loans for a limited period for purchase of Four-Wheeler/Two Wheeler vehicles for Private Use at reduced Rate of Interest and Processing charges.

 

In the Executive Meeting held on 19.6.2020, it has been decided to extend relaxation in respect of obtaining surety. No Surety to be obtained for loan applied under the above Scheme for purchase of two-wheeler upto Rs. 1.00 Lakh subject to:

 

(i)          The borrower has a permanent resident in his/her own name/family members and the same is declared and recorded.

(ii)        The salary of the borrower is credited to our Bank through ECS/NEFT for many months/year or confirmed SDL.

(iii)       The borrower employed with reputed company/firm having net salary of Rs. 25000/- or above p.m. Branch should confirm credit of salary to his/her Bank a/c. by obtaining statement of account of last 6 months.

 

All other terms and conditions as per circular CR No. 249 dated 06.06.2020 remain unchanged.

 

Marketing Dept is advised to give wide publicity of the said relaxation and branches are expected to market the scheme by highlighting the above relaxation.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
