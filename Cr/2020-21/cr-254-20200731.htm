Circular CR No. 254 Dated 31st July, 2020

 

REVISION IN DEFINITIONS OF

MICRO, SMALL & MEDIUM ENTERPRISES (MSMEs) W. E. F. 1ST JULY, 2020

____________________________________________________________

I.   Please refer Annexure-13 of the “Credit Policy W. E. F. 15.07.2019” on the subject of “Classification of various credit facilities under priority sector”. The said Annexure provided, among other things, definitions of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSMEs). The definitions given therein have undergone a change in terms of the Government of India’s Gazette Notification No. S.O.2119 (E) dated 26th June, 2020. The revised definitions, which are effective from 1st July, 2020 have been reiterated by RBI through the Circular RBI/2020-2021/10 -FIDD.MSME & NFS.BC.No.3/06.02.31/2020-21 dated 2nd July, 2020. Apart from the revised definitions, the Gazette notification as well as the RBI circular referred to above provide guidelines on:

1. Method of computation & cross-checking by Government of Investment in Plant & Machinery or Equipment, and Turnover,

2. Registration of MSME on the designated portal of the Central Government,

3. Treatment to be given to enterprises graduating to the next level, or those moving in the reverse direction, and,

4. Other related matters.

 

II.    Important contents of the Gazette notification as well as the RBI circular referred to above are as under:

 

1. The difference between manufacturing and service enterprises in respect of amount of investment, whether in Plant and Machinery, or Equipment, has been removed.

2. Now, there is a composite criterion, inclusive of two sub-criteria, the first being regarding Investment in Plant and Machinery, or Equipment, and the second regarding Turnover, both of which need to be simultaneously met, to enable classification in the three sub-categories of MSMEs. The differential ceilings for investment & turnover across the three sub-categories of MSME are NOW as under:

                         i.    A micro enterprise is one in which the investment in plant and machinery or equipment does not exceed Rs. One Crore and turnover does not exceed Rs. Five Crore;

                        ii.    A small enterprise is one in which the investment in plant and machinery or equipment does not exceed Rs. Ten Crore and turnover does not exceed Rs. Fifty Crore; and

                      iii.    A medium enterprise is one in which the investment in plant and machinery or equipment does not exceed Rs. Fifty Crore and turnover does not exceed Rs. Two Hundred Fifty Crore.

3. If an enterprise crosses the ceiling limits specified for its present category in respect of either of the two sub-criteria of investment or turnover, it will cease to exist in that category and be placed in the next higher category; however no enterprise shall be placed in the lower category unless it goes below the ceiling limits specified for its present category in terms of both the sub-criteria, i.e. pertaining to investment as well as turnover.

4. All units with Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) listed against the same Permanent Account Number (PAN) shall be collectively treated as one enterprise and the amount of turnover and investment of such entities shall be clubbed together; and only the aggregate value thus arrived at will be considered for deciding the category as micro, small or medium enterprise.

5. There is a process of registration of all the MSMEs on a portal named Udyam Registration Portal.

6. Investment in Plant and Machinery or Equipment:

                         i.    Investment in Plant and Machinery, or Equipment, whether purchased first hand or second hand, shall be on the basis of invoice value without GST (as also without installation charges, freight etc. for items purchased domestically according to the clarification previously issued by Central Government. However, for imported Plant & Machinery, certain costs, also indicated previously by Central Government, need to be capitalised). This is in accordance with the current instructions of our Bank on the subject given in Annexure 4 to Circular CR No. 214 dated 6th September, 2018.

                        ii.    Calculation of investment in Plant and Machinery or Equipment will be linked to the Income Tax Return (ITR) of the previous year filed under the Income Tax Act, 1961.

 [Explanation: - This means that the Original Cost Price of those items of Fixed Assets which are required to be included in Plant & Machinery, or Equipment, reduced by the permitted excludable costs incurred in respect of their acquisition, should be in accordance with the Balance Sheet attached to the Income Tax Return].

                      iii.    Relaxation from linkage with IT returns

a. In case of a new enterprise for which no prior ITR is available, the investment to be uploaded on the Govt. portal for registration will be based on self-declaration of the promoter of the enterprise.

b. In such cases, the invoice value [excluding Goods and Services Tax (GST)] of plant and machinery, or equipment, whether purchased first hand or second hand, will need to be taken by the enterprise for the purpose of self-declaration.

c. The relaxation implied herein (in respect of not referring to IT returns, as they do not exist at that time) shall end after the 31st March of the financial year in which the enterprise files its first ITR.

7. Turnover:

                         i.    The component of turnover related to export of goods or services or both, shall be excluded for determining if within the eligible limit of turnover of any enterprise, whether micro, small or medium, for the purposes of classification under reference.

                        ii.    Information as regards turnover (and exports turnover, to enable exclusion) of an enterprise shall be linked to the Income Tax Act or the Central Goods and Services Tax Act (CGST Act) and the GSTIN.

                      iii.    The turnover of that enterprise which does not have PAN will be reckoned (at the Udyam Registration Portal under reference) for the purpose of classification whether Micro, Small or Medium enterprise, on the basis of self-declaration (uploaded by the enterprise on the said Udyam Registration Portal), for a period up to 31st March, 2021; thereafter, PAN and GSTIN shall be mandatory.

 

8. Need to register as a micro, small or medium enterprise:

                         i.    Any person who intends to establish a micro, small or medium enterprise may file Udyam Registration online on the Udyam Registration Portal, based on self-declaration with no requirement to upload documents, papers, certificates or proof.

                        ii.    On registration, an enterprise (referred to as “Udyam” in the Udyam Registration Portal) will be assigned a permanent identity number to be known as “Udyam Registration Number”.

                      iii.    An e-certificate, namely “Udyam Registration Certificate” will be issued on completion of the registration process.

 

9. The Gazette notification, copy whereof is provided as Annexure-I hereto, gives detailed instructions in respect of the matters listed below.

                         i.    Registration in Udyam Registration Portal of new MSME enterprises w. e. f. 1st July, 2020.

                        ii.    Re-registration on Government Portal of MSME enterprises already registered up to 30th June, 2020.

                      iii.    Periodic updation of information on Udyam Registration Portal and classification during transition period.

                      iv.    Cross-checking by Government of data relating to investment & turnover through IT & GST returns

                        v.    Shifting by Government of enterprises across sub-categories of MSME or beyond, or into MSME & its sub-categories, based on data uploaded by enterprises cross-checked by Govt.

                      vi.    Issue of communication by Government to an enterprise conveying change about its status as Micro, Small, Medium Enterprise, or otherwise.

(NOTE:-In case of an upward change in terms of investment in plant and machinery or equipment or turnover or both, and consequent re-classification, an enterprise will maintain its prevailing status till expiry of one year from the close of the year of registration, & in case of movement of an enterprise in reverse direction, the enterprise will continue in its present category till the closure of the financial year and it will be given the benefit of the changed status with effect from 1st April of the financial year following the year in which such change took place).

 

III.  Guidelines for operating staff :-

1. All the branches shall address a letter in duplicate, or send an email, as per the text in Annexure-II-A or Annexure-II-B hereto to each of their borrowers pursuing industrial & business activities, regardless of whether they come under MSME or not, according to the old or new definitions, (Annexure-II-A being to borrower-enterprises with credit facilities above Rs.10 Crore & Annexure-II-B being to borrower-enterprises with credit facilities up to Rs.10 Crore). The text of the letter/email provides information regarding revision in definition of MSMEs, the need to register or re-register the enterprises on the Udyam Registration Portal, cross-checking of information by Govt. in respect of investment in P&M/E and Turnover on the basis of IT & GST returns; & suggests that it is beneficial for units to be compliant of the provisions so as to derive benefits from institutional mechanism. Most importantly, the text of the letter/email of Annexure-II-A advises its target borrower-enterprises to obtain & produce a certificate from Chartered Accountant as per Annexure-marked-II-A-Certificate, & the text of the letter/email of Annexure-marked-II-B-Self-Declaration advises its target borrower-enterprises to furnish a Self Declaration as per Annexure-marked-II-B-Self-Declaration, every year. Record of dispatch of the said communication/printout of the email sent shall be carefully preserved in the respective loan file.

(NOTE-i :- Certificate from Chartered Accountant as per Annexure-marked-II-A-Certificate is required because Investment in Plant & Machinery/Equipment and Turnover will now get cross-checked at the Udyam Registration Portal with Income Tax & GST returns. (If the certificate given by any CA deviates from the text prescribed, it may be accepted if it covers the essential part regarding Original Investment in Plant & Machinery/Equipment and Turnover as counterchecked with previous year’s Income Tax & GST returns). It must bear UDIN. It will be obtained by branches every year from existing borrower-enterprises and from prospective borrower-enterprises, with credit exposure or credit demand resulting in prospective exposure above Rs.10 Crore).

(NOTE-ii :- Self-Declaration as per Annexure-marked-II-B-Self-Declaration is required for the same purpose as mentioned in Note:-i above; however, it is prescribed, in lieu of a certificate from Chartered Accountant, for borrower-enterprises with credit exposure or credit demand resulting in prospective exposure up to Rs.10 Crore only).

2. i. The Certificate or a Self-Declaration, as the case may be shall be required to be submitted by borrower-enterprises every year, immediately after finalisation of annual accounts and in any case not later than one month after the stipulated date for submission of Income Tax Return for the respective category of the borrowing unit applicable for such returns provided that,

a) In case of renewal of a facility at the existing level, it will be sufficient to obtain only a Self declaration from enterprises belonging to all the categories, and,

b)In case of enhancement / additional facility, Branch should obtain CA certificate/Self-declaration, as applicable to the enterprise depending upon credit exposure including the additional/enhanced exposure, as mentioned above.

Note:- a) or b) as applicable will be required only if same is not submitted during last one year

3.  i. In respect of applicant-enterprises applying for credit facilities above Rs.10 Crore for proposed industrial or business activities, and seeking recognition as MSME unit, the format of the certificate to be obtained from Chartered Accountant is given in Annexure-III hereto. (If the certificate given by any CA deviates from the text prescribed, it may be accepted if it covers the essential part regarding Original Investment in Plant & Machinery/Equipment and Turnover as counterchecked with previous year’s Income Tax & GST returns.)

ii. Similarly, in respect of applicant-enterprises applying for credit facilities up to Rs.10 Crore for proposed industrial or business activities, and seeking recognition as MSME unit, the format of the Self-declaration to be obtained from enterprises themselves is given in Annexure-IV hereto.

4. A copy of the Udyam Registration Certificate shall be obtained, along with the related annexures and kept on file.

5. A self-certified copy of communication (if any issued to the enterprise) from Central Government regarding the latest status of the enterprise, as to whether it is MSME or not, and if MSME, to which sub-category does it belong, must be inquired about & if issued, obtained from each borrowing unit, desirous of continuing under MSME category and deriving benefits of institutional mechanism available to MSME units.

6. Reclassification in OMNI system, of units as MSME or otherwise, and sub-category-wise reclassification within MSME, shall be carried out at branches on an ongoing basis. The effect of such reclassification must appear in the return on “Credit to Priority Sector”. The said reclassification in Omni system, whether graduation or movement in the reverse direction, will be either on the basis of CA Certificate /Self-declaration or Udyam Registration Certificate or letter, if any, issued by Govt. to the enterprise. Needless to add, letter, if any issued by Govt. to the enterprise in this behalf, will prevail over the CA Certificate /Self –declaration.

7. i. Failure to update the relevant information within the period specified in the

online Udyam Registration Portal will render the enterprise liable for suspension of its status. After receipt of such information, necessary modification in OMNI system must be carried out immediately.

ii. New applicants desirous of recognition as MSME but not fulfilling the conditions as above shall not be entertained.

IV.  1. IT department will modify currently available fields in OMNI system for recording particulars of credit facilities to Priority Sector and develop new fields, in consultation with Credit department, for recording information regarding composite criteria of Investment in Plant & Machinery/Equipment & Turnover, as also that regarding Udyam registration.

 

2. MIS department will redesign the tables for capturing & compiling information relating to Advances to Priority Sector, in consultation with IT department on the one hand & Credit department on the other, considering the new definitions of MSMEs.

 

V.    1. It should be noted that there is no change in definitions of sub-sectors, other than MSMEs, of the broad Priority Sector.

 

2. It should be further noted that the level of credit facilities to the Micro enterprises which has been prescribed at 7.5 % of the ANBC or credit equivalent of Off Balance Sheet Exposure, whichever is higher, holds good and is required to be attained by us.

 

VI.  In view of mandatory nature of registration & cross-checking of data by Government, with regard to Investment in Plant & Machinery/Equipment, and Turnover, through Income Tax and GST returns respectively, it will be necessary on the part of our operating staff to educate existing & potential borrower-enterprises with regard to the contents of the Gazette notification.

 

VII.          It should be noted that the extant guidelines provided in the “Credit Policy W. E. F. 15.07.2019” and the Circular CR No. 214 dated 6th September, 2018 on the subject of definitions, validation and classification so far as they pertain to MSME enterprises, stand superseded hereby.

 

Please bring contents of the instant circular to the notice of the staff members concerned and ensure meticulous compliance.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
