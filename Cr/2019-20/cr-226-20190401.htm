Circular CR No. 226 dated 1st April, 2019

 

Revision in Additional Interest for Non-adherence to Credit Discipline

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

             I.  Please refer Circular CR No. 215 dated 20th September, 2018 in terms of which instructions on the subject of “Additional Interest for Non-adherence to Credit Discipline” were updated & advised.

 

           II.  Applicability of GST on such Additional Interest was under examination for some time. After due deliberations and consultations, items of Additional Interest involving incidence of GST & those not involving its incidence, have been identified.

 

          III.  In view of the incidence of GST on some of the items of Additional Interest, it was considered necessary to reduce the burden of Additional Interest on the borrowers. A submission in this be half was placed in the Board Meeting dated 22nd March, 2019 & the same met with approval. The resultant structure of Additional Interest for “Non-adherence to Credit Discipline” with downward revision, with updated guidelines on circumstances which would make it applicable, is being advised as under:

 

1)    Auditor's certified Stock & Book Debts statement: Borrower enjoying CC & WCTL/STCL/RCL limit/s above Rs.25.00 Lakh is required to submit their Auditor's certified Stock & Book Debts statements as on 30th September every year by 31st October. If the borrower fails to submit the same within stipulated period, additional interest @ 1.75% plus GST at applicable rate will be applied on outstanding amount from 1st of November for the period till the same is submitted.

 

 

2)    Additional interest @ 1.75% plus GST at applicable rate will be applied on outstanding amount in case of monthly average turnover being less than 25% of the sanctioned limit. The average monthly turnover shall be calculated for the first half-yearly period from April to September and for second half-yearly period from October to March. Additional interest will accordingly be charged from subsequent 15th of October and 15th of April respectively. There are finer and additional points which need to be taken into consideration for which please refer Annexure – 12 of the extant Credit Policy.

 

3)    In case a borrower, whose accounts are subject to credit rating fails to submit the audited Profit & Loss Statement, Balance Sheet & ITR by 31st October, additional interest @ 1.75% plus GST at applicable rate will be applied on outstanding amount in such accounts from 1st November for the period till the same are submitted.

 

4)    In case of non-renewal of account within one month from the due date for renewal, additional interest @ 1.75% plus GST at applicable rate will be charged on outstanding amount after expiry of one month’s period (which works out to a grace period of one month for applying additional interest) till renewal of account.

 Note:- Grace Period of one month is made applicable to all types of CC facilities like FLXLN, all type of SOD accounts, STCC and Salary CC. Accordingly, while opening account under such facilities, the branch has to consider the concept of grace period.

 

5)    In case of Non-review of account within one month from the due date for review, additional interest @ 1.75% plus GST at applicable rate will be charged on outstanding amount till review of account.

 

6)    Additional interest will be applied @ 1.75% plus GST at applicable rate on outstanding amount in the relative credit facilities, if the charged assets remain uninsured. The additional interest in such cases will be applied from expiry of the insurance policy till its renewal. However, if the borrower submits the policy in due course, which is renewed from the due date of expired policy, Additional Interest should not be levied (Interest for unrenewed period to be collected).

 

7)    Additional interest will be applied @ 1.75% plus GST at applicable rate on outstanding amount in the absence of drawing power due to non-submission of Stock & Book Debt Statements in CC accounts.

 

8)    Additional interest will be applied @ 1.75% plus GST at applicable rate on overdrawn amount due to failure to maintain drawing power in CC accounts.

 

Note: Clauses of additional interest for Non-submission of Stock and Book Debt statements & /or failure to maintain drawing power are also applicable for Term Loans (WCTL) sanctioned against Drawing power (Stock and Book Debts).

However, if Term loan is along with CC limit, then above clauses are applicable for CC Limits Only.

 

9)    Additional interest will be applied @ 1.75% on overdue amount in standard term loan account including loans to an Educational Institution.

 

10)          Additional interest will be applied @ 1.75% on outstanding amount for overdue period (i.e. where payment is not received on or before the due date) in each purpose-wise separate account under RCL, DBD, SBD & Channel financing.

 

11)          Additional interest will be applied @ 1.75% on entire outstanding amount when account turns into an NPA, & will continue to be applied till it’s upgradation to Standard Asset.

 

12)          Interest @ 18% will be charged to the extent of overdrawn amount above the sanctioned limit due to monthly interest debited, TOD/excess drawals allowed, etc. to all CC accounts & CC type facilities like FLXLN, all type of SOD accounts, STCC and Salary CC.

 

13)          Interest @ 18% will be charged on entire outstanding amount in case of Invoked BG & Forced L/C A/c.

 

         IV.  In case of multiple defaults as stated above in any single account, the total additional interest to be charged in such accounts shall not exceed 3.50% plus GST wherever applicable, for all the defaults taken together except point no.12 and 13 mentioned above.

 

           V.Branches will display Notice regarding revision of Additional Interest as per Annexure-I for non-Adherence to Credit Discipline in branch premises. There is no need to write separate letters to borrowers because the rates have been revised downward; and with inclusion of GST wherever applicable, burden on the borrowers has been generally compensated.

 

         VI.Accounts department will review the accounting mechanism for changes if any necessitated hereby; and cause IT department to undertake such changes in Omni system as may be required. Accounts department will also notify branches regarding modified accounting procedure if any.

 

       VII.IT department will instruct branches regarding specific action points for implementation by latter including debiting charges manually by branches if any.

 

      VIII.   Instructions contained herein supersede all the previous instructions on the subject of applying interest at rates beyond the contracted, whether issued through circulars or through “Credit Policy – Credit Rules & Procedures w. e. f. 24.8.2016”.

 

         IX.    Branches should ensure that clauses, regarding applying additional interest as per the new norms of 1.75% & 18% in respect of breach of the terms & conditions of sanction, are incorporated in documentation pertaining to new sanctions. Branches will use existing stock of stationery until they are supplied with new stock.

 

           X.    Branches are hereby instructed not to make any modification in offset master “in OMNI” as instructed vide earlier Circular CR No. 215 dated 28th September, 2018 and also not to collect the charges manually as mentioned in the said circular. However, branches are further instructed to note such charges separately & do necessary/requisite updation in separate module developed “in OMNI system” for which separate instructions are being issued by IT department.

 

 

         XI.    Stationery Department will continue to ensure printing of stationery including Sanction-Cum-Agreement Letters & other loan documents as applicable, with incorporation of clauses for charging additional interest plus GST wherever applicable in case the terms & conditions of sanction are breached as mentioned above.

 

       XII.   MIS Department is advised to carry out required changes in scrutiny notes etc. and to upload the revised formats in Public folder.

 

      XIII.   Inspection Dept is advised to ensure that instructions in respect of applying additional interest on credit facilities in case of default as mentioned above are adhered to. They should point out deviations if any during Inspection/Audit.

 

     XIV.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
