Circular CR No. 231 dated 17th September, 2019

 

 

1)   Registration of Bank’s Charges with Ministry of Corporate Affairs (ROC)

2)   Charges Created/Modified before 02.11.2018 & not Registered so far can not be Registered Now

3)   Component of Ad valorem Fees introduced over & above Additional Charges for Delay

4)   Charges Created Subsequent to 02.11.2018 will not be Registered after 120 Days

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

I.Government of India has promulgated “The Companies (Amendment) Ordinance, 2018”, amending thereby among other provisions, section 77 relating to e-form CHG-1 for creation or modification of charge. In view of the above, all the instructions contained in the Circular CR No. 125 dated 14th November, 2014 have hereby been superseded. The revised instructions on the subject are being given hereunder.

i)      There is a provision to allow registration by paying additional fees as a multiple of normal fees and also ad valorem fees for delays as described in Sub-Para ii) hereunder. However, it needs to be noted that if a charge created or modified before 02.11.2018 has not been registered already; it will not now be registered by ROC in any case. We trust there are no such charges remaining to be registered. If such unregistered charges do exist in our books, the matter should be immediately referred to GM (Credit & Forex Dept.) for guidance and instructions. Please also see the point no. VII.3 below for further course of action.

iii)  In respect of the above also, an application authenticated by the company is to be made, which will undergo due process and will be submitted to the registrar.

    Example: - 1) Considering loan / charge amt of Rs.1 Cr for a non-small company, after 01.08.2019. calculation of ROC fees will be as under :

i)      Within 30 days :- Maximum Fees Rs.600/-

v)   It should be carefully noted that charges will not be taken on record by MCA after 120 days of their creation/modification.

 

vi)  Omission/mis-statement in the charge created or modified can however be registered after 120 days subject to Condonation of Delay.

V. In addition to the fees, Bank shall recover the professional expenses from the borrowing company which are claimed by the Company Secretary appointed by the Bank.

 

VI.   1) Section 77 (3) of the companies Act, 2013 as amended upto date, is reproduced to emphasise the importance of registration of charge.

 

“Notwithstanding anything contained in any other law for the time being in force, no charge created by a company shall be taken into account by the liquidator appointed under this Act or the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, as the case may be, or any other creditor unless it is duly registered under sub-section (1) and a certificate of registration of such charge is given by the Registrar under sub-section (2)”.

VII.  In view of the above, branches are advised as under:

1. Examine which newly created/modified charges remain to be registered.

2. Immediate steps should be taken to get registered those charges in respect of which registration is possible.

3. A detailed report should be submitted to the GM (Credit), through the respective Zonal Office, in respect of those charges which cannot be registered any more, with

a)        name of Branch In Charge and the officer concerned during whose tenure the opportunity to register the charge lapsed, and

b)        recommendations for remedial action.

 

VIII.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department and ensure compliance.
