Circular CR No. 239 dated 14th February, 2020

GOLD LOANS

1)  STRENGTHENING SYSTEM OF DELIVERING ORNAMENTS TO CUSTOMER AFTER CLOSURE OF ACCOUNT

2)  MONITORING QUALITY OF PERFORMANCE OF EMPANELLED GOLD VALUERS

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.   Recently, we came across an instance where delivery of ornaments in respect of closed Gold Loan accounts was obtained through impersonation. Instances also continue to be reported where Bank’s empanelled Gold Valuers have been less than professional. In this regard, we issue instructions as under.

 

1) Strengthening System Of Delivering Ornaments To Customer After Closure Of Account

i)   Existing procedure with a few more steps for safety

a)  A person applying for a Gold Loan is normally an existing customer. In such a case, KYC is already done. In case of a non-account–holder person seeking a Gold Loan, it is customary to get a saving (or a current) account opened by such person and the Gold Loan is granted to him/her thereafter. The KYC process necessarily gets completed in such cases.

b) Gold Ornaments can be delivered only to the customer who had obtained the Gold Loan, he/she being the owner of the ornaments as per his/her own declaration.

c)  When the customer closes his/her Gold Loan account and seeks delivery of the pledged ornaments, his/her identification as an account holder should not be a problem if he/she visits the branch frequently and is known to the operating staff.

d) In case of a customer visiting the branch infrequently, it is necessary to establish his/her identity by referring to the KYC documents submitted at the time of opening his/her Saving/Current account.

e)  Under the extant procedure, the person pledging the gold ornaments is given a pink slip evidencing the pledge, & the person is advised to produce this pink slip at the time of obtaining delivery of the ornaments upon closure of the loan.

f)    It has been noticed that such pink slip is not given at branches to the Gold Loan borrower at the time of pledge. It is sometimes found to have been retained at the branch on the relative file. It is hereby being instructed that issuing pink slip to the Gold Loan borrower is mandatory.

g)  However, placing reliance solely on holding of the pink slip for identifying the owner is fraught with risk as misuse of such a pink slip by an impersonator has been reported as mentioned in Para I above. Therefore, while collecting the pink slip at the time of delivering the ornaments is essential, its possession by itself by a person seeking delivery should not construed as ownership of the ornaments.

h) Branches should educate the customers obtaining gold loans regarding importance of maintaining custody of the pink slip so that it is not misused by some other person for obtaining delivery of the ornaments upon closure of the account.

i)   Considering that significant margin is maintained on the market value of gold while granting loan & gold prices are also generally on the increase, an impostor has sufficient incentive to close a gold loan from his/her own resources and obtain delivery by creating an impression of ownership of ornaments. Therefore, our staff members are being instructed not to presume that a person putting in funds to close a gold loan is owner of the ornaments, and must establish identity of such person with reference to the KYC documents unless he/she is well known, as mentioned above.

 

ii) Additional stipulation of photographs on Gold Loan Application

In order to avoid delay in making a reference to the KYC documents at the time of delivering the gold ornaments upon closure of the relative account, it is now being stipulated as under.

a)  After identity of the applicant for Gold Loan is established with reference to his /her KYC documents, one passport-size photograph of the customer should additionally be obtained in all the cases. The same shall be pasted on the Application for Gold Loan with a signature across the photograph running onto the page beneath.

b) While delivering the gold ornaments upon closure of the account, identity of the person seeking their delivery must be established on the basis of photograph pasted on the relative Application for Gold Loan. This will be in addition to the collection of the pink slip which is given to the borrower at the time of granting the gold loan.

 

2) MONITORING QUALITY OF PERFORMANCE OF EMPANELLED GOLD VALUERS

i)   Sometimes, lapses are noticed in gold valuation done by our empanelled valuers. Branches are hereby instructed to report such instances to DGM of the respective zone with CC to Vigilance Department. Investigation shall be conducted in such instances by the vigilance department to determine whether there was any malicious intent, gross negligence or sheer incompetence on the part of valuers.

ii)  Depending upon results of the investigation, a decision whether or not to depanel the Gold Valuer will be taken by DGM of the Zone. The same will be communicated to the branch concerned for implementation.

 

II.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department & ensure compliance.

 

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
