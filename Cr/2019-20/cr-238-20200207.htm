Circular CR No. 238 dated 7th February, 2020

 

Central Registry of Securitisation Asset Reconstruction and Security Interest of India (CERSAI) – Revised Guidelines

 

I.   1) As per extant guidelines, our Bank has been recording its mortgage and hypothecation charge with CERSAI. As stated in Circular CR No. 170 dated 8th September, 2016, there were amendments in the DRT and SARFAESI Act, titled “The Enforcement of Security Interest and Recovery of Debts Laws and Miscellaneous Provisions (Amendment) Act, 2016;” however, the amendments relating to CERSAI were not notified by the Government as necessary infrastructure was still to be put in place for giving effect to certain amendments. The Central Government through the amendments in SARFAESI Act, for the purpose of providing a central database, has decided to integrate the registration records with the records of CERSAI. The said amendments also provide that taxes due from Assessees against whom attachment order is issued against any property or attachment order on property issued by any competent court/authority will also have to be registered by the person concerned with CERSAI. The claim of a secured creditor or a creditor holding attachment shall have priority over any subsequent security interest (charge) created upon such property and over any transfer by way of sale, lease, assignment, license or attachment subsequent to such registration. The right of enforcement of security by the Secured Creditor can be exercised only if the charge is filed/registered with CERSAI.

 

2) The amendments as above made in Chapter IV-A in the SARFAESI Act, 2002 have come into force w.e.f. 24th of January, 2020 as notified in Govt. of India Gazette.

Revised guidelines in accordance therewith are issued in supersession of all the earlier instructions issued on the subject. The changes with respect to the filing of charge with CERSAI are given in brief as under:

i)       Creditors other than Secured Creditors (as defined under section 2(1)zd of SARFAESI Act), in whose favour security interest (charge) is created have now to file their charge with CERSAI.

ii)     Filing of security interest with CERSAI shall be deemed to constitute a Public notice from the date & time of filing with CERSAI.

iii)   Attachment orders issued by Revenue Authorities for recovery of Govt. dues are required to be filed with CERSAI.

iv)    Attachment orders issued in favour of any person by Courts/Tribunals are required to be filed with CERSAI.

v)     Secured Creditors [as defined under section 2(1)(zd)] shall be entitled to enforce securities under SARFAESI Act only if the security interest is filed with CERSAI.

vi)    Filing of Security interest or attachment orders with CERSAI shall establish priority over any subsequent security interest created upon such property.

vii)  After registration of security interest with CERSAI by secured creditors, their dues will be paid in priority over Govt. dues.

viii)Penalty provisions for default in filing, modifying and satisfaction of security interest (section 27) will no longer be applicable.

ix)   30-day time period for filing the transactions of creation/modification of security interest with CERSAI (section 23) is no longer applicable.

x)     CERSAI has discontinued registration of hypothecation charge on Vehicles w.e.f. 26th July, 2019; hence branches should not file with CERSAI charge created on vehicles.

 

II.  1) In view of the said changes, Branches/Regional Offices/Zonal Offices/Credit Dept., H.O/ Recovery dept shall continue to file/register Bank’s charge with CERSAI. Registration of Bank’s charge shall be done for all types of mortgages, Lien Noting, hypothecation of Movable Assets and Intangible assets with CERSAI. Similarly, any attachment in favour of our Bank on the personal properties of the defaulters of our Bank issued by Special Recovery Officer or Authorised Officer for execution of Award/Recovery Certificate/Arbitral award or injunction or attachment issued by the Arbitrator, DRT or any other authority or Court must be immediately filed with CERSAI.

2) Procedural guidelines to be followed in this regard are as under :

i)    Filing of charge with CERSAI. Considering that filing of charge with CERSAI shall be deemed to constitute a public notice, it has been decided that the CERSAI record should be checked at the time of checking of mortgageability of property itself, to ensure that there is/are no prior charge/s of any other Bank/Financial institution or any other person or authority on the said property. If any charge is found on the said property either for loans or for tax claim/s etc., then such property should not be considered for the purpose of security by our Bank. Branches shall continue to ensure that security interest created in Movable and Immovable properties in favour of Bank by way of hypothecation of movable assets and mortgages (both Equitable and Registered Simple Mortgages) be reported to Zonal Office immediately for creation of charge with CERSAI. In case of take-over proposals, after receipt of original title deeds and execution of mortgage/hypothecation our charge should be filed immediately with CERSAI by creation of mortgage/hypothecation in favour of our Bank. It should be ensured that charge/charges of earlier Bank(s)/Financial Institution(s) stand(s) satisfied in CERSAI record, without any delay.

ii)   a) In order to ensure that there is proper compliance, Branches shall continue to

submit Excel Sheet (format of which is already with them) duly filled in by furnishing particulars of this letter under the head of “Date of creation of Charge” to their respective Regional /Zonal Office. In case of any doubt/difficulty, the matter should be referred to Manager, Credit Information Cell at Head Office. Branch-in-charge of designated branch shall continue to note this Registration Number in Mortgage Register under authentication and preserve this document in the respective Loan File as proof for having registered the mortgage transaction with CERSAI.

b) It may be noted that though the time period for filing charge with CERSAI has been removed, it is necessary to immediately record charge with CERSAI in order to get priority over any subsequent charge that may be recorded against the same property.

iii) It is hereby further clarified that no disbursement of the loan/credit facility (other than in respect of take over proposal) should be done by our Bank unless and until the filing of charge with CERSAI has been done and CERSAI ID has been received for the same, and it should be further got confirmed that there is no prior charge of any other authority or person on such property.

iv)   Once a charge is created with CERSAI on hypothecation of stock and book debts for Cash credit limits, Letter of Credit limits & Bank Guarantee Limits, charge will not be created in such cases at subsequent renewal or review of the accounts. In case of enhancement in working capital limits where security by way of hypothecation of stock and book debts/extension of mortgage is created, charge for the enhanced amount has to be promptly created with CERSAI.

v)   Filing with CERSAI, attachment obtained by our Bank on personal property of defaulters. Whenever any property has been attached in any decreed/case filed account of our Bank, the attachment on such personal property of the defaulters should be recorded immediately with CERSAI. Such order in respect of defaulted account of our Bank is generally issued by the SRO, authorised officer appointed for execution of arbitral award, Civil/High Court or even by Arbitrator who passes injunction/attachment order in respect of personal property of the defaulters, in recovery case filed by our Bank. However, CERSAI details of personal properties pursuant to attachment order obtained by Bank are not to be fed in Omni to avoid complications. It should be noted that filing/recording of such attachment order on personal properties in CERSAI record would be deemed to be a public notice and hence would restrict any further encumbrance on said properties. All those concerned are required to immediately file with CERSAI the existing attachment orders obtained by the Bank on personal properties of the defaulters. The departments concerned have already been informed by separate communication from Head office in this regard and it must be ensured that the entire exercise is completed by the end of this month. In the event of any difficulty in noting of charge with CERSAI, Credit Information Cell at Head Office may be contacted.

vi) Keeping Registration Updated on Ongoing Basis.

Pursuant to instructions issued from time to time, it is assumed that Branches have completed Registration of all subsisting charges with CERSAI. Branches shall continue to maintain information in soft version under an excel sheet/Open Office as per Annexure – I hereto in respect of charges created from 1st February, 2019 and registration of security interest in respect thereof. A printout of the information sheet (Annexure – I) will be taken out at the end of each month & will be authenticated under signatures as indicated. Branches shall prepare a consolidated monthly report as per Annexure-II attached herewith, based on the information contained in the information sheet (Annexure – I) & the same shall be

submitted to respective Zonal Office as at the end of each month. Branches shall ensure that such information reaches respective Zonal Office by 7th day of the succeeding month.

In the same manner, for any attachment order obtained by the Bank on personal property of the defaulters, record shall be maintained in soft version in the same excel sheet/open office and the information should be updated by the Dept/Branch/RO/ZO concerned where the loan account is maintained. Consolidated monthly statement of such charges registered with CERSAI is to be sent to Credit Dept. H.O. by 10th of the succeeding month. The said information is to be provided as per Annexure - I & Annexure - II respectively which are attached herewith.

 

III.   Record Maintenance at Branches in OMNI for secured assets & Certificate on Monthly Basis by Br/ZO

1) We invite attention of all the branches/offices to the provision for noting of security-wise CERSAI number (Asset ID) in OMNI. It is however observed that some of the branches are not recording the necessary details in the said field. Branches are instructed to record these particulars without any exception. Any lapse in this regard will be viewed seriously.

2) IT department is advised to ensure that generation of reports providing details noted in the field as per Para III. 1) above is facilitated. Zonal Offices, in turn, shall submit a certificate to the AGM, Credit Monitoring Cell at HO as per Annexure-III that all the security interests, in respect of charges created, have been registered with CERSAI. The certificate should be accompanied by a consolidated report as per Annexure-II (A) hereto to be compiled by consolidating information reported by branches. Such certificate & the consolidated Annexure-II (A) must reach the Head Office by 10th day of the succeeding month.

3) The MIS dept shall generate quarterly report to identify any CERSAI details which may not have been fed in OMNI record, and submit such report to the Branch and Zonal office concerned for compliance. A copy of the report shall be marked to General Manager, Credit dept. H.O. for further action.

 

IV.  Satisfaction of Charge :- 1) Once a borrower has repaid the entire amount of Loans & Advances with interest and has fulfilled all his obligations, Branches should note to convey the message to Zonal Office/Regional Office by way of IDC signed by the Branch-In-Charge quoting the CERSAI’s Registration Number for satisfaction of mortgage charge/hypothecation charge. On receiving the IDC from the Branch, Zonal Office/Regional Office will mark this charge as ‘Satisfied’ in Central Registry System conveying that the property is now free from Bank’s charge. In addition to the above, branches should submit Account Closing Advice and Direct/Indirect liability information of the borrower to respective Zonal Office/Regional Office along with the IDC for their verification and necessary action. Branches should also ensure that CERSAI charges are recovered from the borrower while closing the account.

2) It is to be noted that where the same mortgaged property is obtained as security by our Bank for two or more loans and only one of the loans is closed, then only the loan outstanding in CERSAI record should be shown as Zero and there will be no satisfaction of charge with CERSAI as the mortgage property would continue as security for the remaining loans.
