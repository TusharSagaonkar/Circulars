Circular CR No. 227 dated 5th April, 2019

 

Housing Loans in MHADA Colony Charkop, Kandivali & MHADA Colony, Gorai

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.  Please refer previous circulars no. CR 113 dated 10th June, 2014, CR 124 dated 13th November, 2014 & IDC No. HO/CD/309/2017-18 dated 19th June, 2017 on the captioned subject.

 

II.   1. We have been extending Housing Loans for renovation/furnishing/purchase/ demolition & reconstruction/extension of tenements/flats/bungalows in MHADA colonies.  The MHADA colony in Charkop is a World Bank Project.  There are plots of different sizes and tenements on plots of sizes 40 sq. meters and below are necessarily under a registered co-operative society. The plots of sizes more than 40 sq. metres have independent bungalows thereon and they are not part of any society. After opening of our Charkop, Kandivali (W) branch, we started extending Housing Loans for construction of additional area on the existing tenements on plots of sizes 40 sq. metres & below. Though construction of additional rooms/structure results in better living conditions of family members, it was observed that customer/ applicant finds it difficult to obtain and submit NOC/Permission from MHADA or Municipal Corporation of Greater Mumbai for constructing such additional rooms/structure and therefore the submission of the same was waived. We also started granting loans in respect of independent bungalows (not part of any society) for renovation/furnishing/demolition & reconstruction/purchase with or without renovation/furnishing.

 

2. Instructions on behalf of the above were updated and communicated from time to time, the last three of which have been cited at the outset in Para I above.

 

3. Currently, housing loans as under are considered under the two MHADA Colonies, namely Charkop & Gorai. 

                i)   Existing owner of the tenement wants to avail loan for its extension.

              ii)   Existing owner of the tenement wants to avail loan for demolishing the existing structure and reconstructing it with addition of mezzanine/ first floor.

             iii)   Loan required for purchase of tenement from the original allottee as well as for demolition and reconstruction of the same.

             iv)   Loan required for purchase of tenement which has undergone multiple sales transactions as well as for demolition and reconstruction of the same.

               v)   Loan for purchase /reconstruction of independent bungalow.

 

 

III.               Considering the current market trend, Kandivali (W) branch recently recommended enhancement in the loan ceilings in the aforesaid categories of loans. The note containing the recommendations was placed in the Board meeting dated 16th March, 2019 & the same met with approval. While communicating the enhanced ceilings, all the extant

instructions on the subject have been consolidated and are being circularised as under through the instant circular, in supersession of all the instructions contained in the previous circulars & the IDC, but subject to normal instructions in the extant Credit Policy which are not specifically overridden herein.

2)The Title Deeds to be submitted in respect of above referred five categories of Housing loans are as under:-

i)                  Existing owner of the tenement wants to avail loan for extension of tenement

a)     Original letter of allotment by MHADA issued to the original tenement owner,              if available.

b)     Letter from the society that the applicant is existing owner of the tenement and Member of the society. 

c)     Original share certificate issued by Regd. Society of the cluster of the tenements should be in the name of the applicant.

d)     NOC from the society to the Bank for mortgage of tenement.

e)     Original Payment Receipt in respect of payment made to MHADA towards plot/tenements and/or any other documentary proof, if available.

 

ii)                Existing owner of the tenement wants to avail loan for demolishing the existing structure and reconstructing with addition of mezzanine/first floor

a)     Original letter of allotment by MHADA to the original tenement owner,                       if available.

b)     Letter from the society that the applicant is existing owner of the tenement.

c)     Original share certificate issued by Regd. Society of the cluster of the tenement should be in the name of the applicant.

d)     NOC from the society to the Bank for mortgage of tenement.

e)     NOC from the society for demolition and reconstruction of the existing structure is not required as society does not issue such NOC.

f)       Original Payment Receipt in respect of payment made to MHADA towards plot/tenement and/or any other documentary proof, if available.

 

iii) Loan required for purchase of tenement from the Original Allottee as well as for demolition and reconstruction of the same.

a)     Original letter of allotment by MHADA to the original tenements owner,                         if available.

b)     Letter from the society that the seller is original allottee of the tenement.

c)     Duly stamped and registered Agreement between original allottee and purchaser is must.

d)     Original share certificate issued by Regd. Society in the name of Seller.

e)     NOC from the society to the Bank for mortgage of tenements  and for transfer in the name of the purchaser after completion of sale transaction.

f)        Original Payment Receipt in respect of payment made to MHADA towards plot/tenement and/or any other documentary proof, if available.

 

iv)  Loan required for purchase of tenements under multiple sales transactions as well as for demolition and reconstruction of the same.

 

a)     Original letter of allotment by MHADA to the original tenement owner,                         if available.

b)     Letter from the society that the seller is a member of the society.

c)     The original agreement duly stamped and registered, executed by and between the present seller and a person from whom the present seller has purchased i.e. seller’s seller.

d)     The original agreement duly stamped and registered executed by and between the present seller and present purchaser (applicant of loan).

e)     Original share certificate issued by Regd. Society in the name of Seller.

f)       NOC from the society to the Bank for mortgage of the said tenement and undertaking to transfer the Share Certificate in the name of the purchaser.

g)     The chain of documents if available to be collected, perused and kept on record after verifying that there is no discrepancy as regards to title of all the previous sellers.

h)     Payment Receipts issued by seller to present purchaser towards proof of margin money and loan amount.

 

v)                Loan for renovation/furnishing/purchase/demolition and/or reconstruction/ extension of a new independent bungalow.

a)     Allotment letter in respect of the plot to the original allottee is the basic Title Deed. Subsequent Sale Deeds between 1st & 2nd party, 2nd & 3rd party, & so on, will constitute the necessary chain of documents.

b)     MHADA is the direct authority maintaining records of allotment & transfers. Therefore, an investigation will have to be made for Title in the MHADA records.

c)     Applicant has to produce requisite title deeds as per Mortgageability Scrutiny Note. It should also be ensured to obtain NOC from MHADA which is a must for mortgaging the property.

 

3)   Other Terms and Conditions:-

 

i)       The visit by the Branch officials to the place of residence and business/ employment is compulsory & verification is to be done by the said officials in respect of ownership of the tenement. The visit and discussion with the Chairman/Secretary of the Society (other than in respect of loan for a Bunglow) is must and the same should be recorded in Visit Report. The details of visit to employment/business should also be noted as per extant guidelines.

ii)    Mortgage:- Permission from MHADA/MCGM/Society for reconstruction of tenement and extension thereon is waived (other than in respect of loan for a Bunglow in which case it is a must).  NOC from society would be only for mortgage of existing tenements and not for reconstruction as the society is not empowered to issue such NOC. 

iii)  Loan amount will entirely depend on repayment capacity of the loanee, co-applicant and worth of the sureties, as per the Credit Policy norms, and not on the value of tenement.

iv)  Applicant should be a member of the society and holder of share certificate in his/her name (other than in respect of loan for a Bunglow).

v)    Applicant should submit the estimate for the work to be carried out with stage-wise payment-schedule from the contractor along with the construction plan duly signed by the contractor and also by the applicant.

vi)  Where the construction has already been completed not more than 3 months before the date of application, such a loan application be considered for sanction and disbursement of loan can be made in lump-sum to the contractor for the balance amount to be paid or by way of reimbursement to the borrower on the basis of Cheque Payment to the contractor in addition to the sufficient proof of bills/receipts to be obtained from the contractor.

 

a)    Period of 3 months could be verified from local enquiry with Chairman/ Secretary of the society, wherever applicable, and by reference to payments by cheque.

b)    In case where the construction is in progress or yet to start, stage-wise disbursement shall be made directly to the contractor by PO in favour of the contractor with the mention of his Bank and Branch and A/c. Number.

 

vii) The loan will be secured by equitable mortgage of the tenement with the notice of intimation to Registrar in addition to the loan documents and registration of mortgage charge with CERSAI.

viii)            After execution of the equitable mortgage, a copy thereof will be sent to the society for registering our mortgage charge in society’s books/records.

ix)  A letter of confirmation should be obtained from the society (other than in respect of loan for a Bungalow) for having registered Bank’s mortgage charge in its books/records. The branch should depute staff to the society’s office for verification.

x)    Loan will be disbursed only after Bank’s mortgage charge is registered in the books/records of the society (other than in respect of loan for a Bungalow) and written confirmation thereof is received from the society.

 

IV.  The guidelines issued from time to time in respect of mortgageability of flats/tenements in old MHADA colonies namely Abhyudaya Nagar, Nehru Nagar, Vikhroli, Pant Nagar (Ghatkopar), Charkop, Kandivali, Mulund, Gorai etc. will also be applicable.  

 

V.    Explanation of phrase “If available”:- We have mentioned “If available” in case of some Title Deeds/documents.  Generally, the documents like allotment letters, payment to MHADA etc. would be available. Hence, they should be asked for. However, if an applicant cannot produce the same & the circumstances under which he/she cannot produce the same are genuine, documents can be waived by the Incharge of Branch.

 

VI.  Loans for housing in MHADA Colony, Charkop, will be sanctioned only at our Kandivali (W)- Charkop Branch for easy monitoring irrespective of the fact that applicant may be having savings/current A/c. with other branches of our Bank. Such other branches maintaining savings/current a/cs. of the applicants shall extend all the necessary help to enable expeditious sanction of loans by Kandivali (W)-Charkop Branch. Loans for housing in MHADA, Gorai can however be sanctioned at any of the branches in Mumbai area.

 

VII.            Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all the staff members concerned at the Branch/ ZO/Credit Dept. and ensure compliance.
