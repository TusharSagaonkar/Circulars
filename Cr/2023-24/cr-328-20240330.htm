Circular CR No.328 dated 30thMarch 2024

 

I.      Implementation of Fair Lending Practice

II.    Discontinuation of additional interest as a measure to inculcate discipline in borrowers

III.   Introduction of additional charges instead

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

I.    The RBI has,in its circular no. RBI/2023-24/53 DoR.MCS.REC.28/01.01.001/ 2023-24 dated the 18th August 2023, observed that the REs (which include banks) followdivergent practices in the matter of applying penal (read additional) interest and this has been found to lead to customer grievances and disputes. Therefore, directions have been issued through the said circular to remedy the situation. The timeline initially prescribed for implementation of the guidelines was subsequently extended by the RBI.

 

II.  To give effect to the directions given by the RBI, a submission was placed before the appropriate authority to adopt the directions and approve a set of instructions to implement them in our Bank. The submission met with approval on the 20th March 2024.

 

III.               In respect of the above, the following instructions are being issued. The timelines for their implementation, wherever applicable, with operational instructions to the Departments or functionaries concerned are also indicated.

1.   The system of applying additional interest on credit facilities for deviations from the terms and conditions of sanction will be discontinued with effect from 1st April 2024 for all the credit facilities.

2.   Inculcation of discipline on the part of borrowers and dissuading them from deviations from the terms and conditions of sanction will henceforth be only through the imposition ofadditionalcharges.

3.   Additional charges imposed as a measure of disciplineshall not be capitalizedi.e. no interest shall be computed on such charges. The normal procedure for compounding of interest in loan accounts shall of course continue.

A.  Operational instructions for IT department :-The IT department will make an appropriate change in the CBS so that interest is not computed on additional charges applied for breach of terms and conditions of loans and advances. This rule must be implementedfrom 1st April 2024.

Notes:-

i.         If there are multiple breaches of credit discipline attracting additional charges, then the maximum additional charge to be applied shall be the aggregate of those for the two incidences carrying the highest amounts.

Ø  In the case of NPA accounts, an additional charge of 1% of the outstanding amount shall be applied.

ii.       In the case of loans up to Rs. 25,000/- to borrowers under the Priority Sector, no additional charges for breaches of credit discipline shall be applied.

5.   It may be noted that additional charges for breaches of credit discipline in case of facilities sanctioned to ‘individual borrowers’, for purposes other than business, shall in no case be higher than the similaradditional charges applicable to non-individual borrowers for similar breaches of credit discipline.

6.   Whenever reminders for non-compliance of material terms and conditions of loan are sent to borrowers, the applicable additional charges shall be communicated. Further, any instance of levy of additional charges and the reason therefor shall also be communicated. The draft of the letter is given in Annexure hereto.

 

IV.    All the branches, Zonal Offices and offices/departments performing functions relating to sanction, monitoring and recovery of credit facilities are instructed to ensure meticulous compliance with the instructions herein.
