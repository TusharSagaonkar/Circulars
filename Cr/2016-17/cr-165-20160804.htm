Circular CR No.165 Dated 4th August 2016

 

Facility for Housing Loan Borrowers to switchover

to current rate of interest 

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          The rate of interest on loans charged to the Borrowal accounts is in accordance with Circular ILA No 77 dated 1.6.2016. Accordingly, rate of interest on housing loans from the new borrowers is 9.75% p.a for housing loan upto Rs. 50.00 Lakh and 10.25% p.a for housing loan above Rs.50.00 Lakh and upto Rs. 70.00 Lakh.

 

For the borrowers who have availed housing loan until August 2014, the rate of interest still ranges between 10% p.a to 13.50% p.a. Therefore, the borrowers whose rate of interest on their housing loan is above 9.75% or 10.25% (present ROI) are requesting Bank to reduce their ROI to the present rate of interest. Due to this variation/difference in rate of interest on Housing loans between existing and new borrowers, many of the borrowers are shifting to other Banks where they are getting low rate of interest on their housing loan. The borrowers availing Housing loans are generally from middle income group and their decision to  shift to other Banks shows that they are sensitive to the interest rates which is higher by almost 3.25 % p.a.  (13.5 % - 10.25 %).

 

The matter was put up and discussed in the Board meeting dated 30.7.2016, and it has been decided to permit our housing loan borrowers to switch over to the current Housing loan interest slab by charging switchover fee of 1% on outstanding amount of the Housing loan provided their past performance of repayment in the loan account is satisfactory and all the sanctioned terms and conditions have been complied including creation of charge with the concerned authorities and insurance coverage etc.

 

Accordingly, the following guidelines are issued:

1)  Our Housing Loan Borrowers who intend to switch over to the current housing loan interest slab must submit their duly filled in application in the format given in Annexure 1 enclosed herewith, which must be duly signed by the Borrowers and Guarantors.

2)  The Switch over fees is 1% of the outstanding housing loan dues

3)  Even on switch over to the current housing loan rate of interest, the same housing loan account number and documents would continue.

4)  The Branch is required to scrutinize the request and submit with their recommendation to the concerned Dy. Gen. Manager of the Zone.

5)  After approval of the request for switchover by the Dy. Gen. Manager, Branch should issue letter to the Borrowers conveying acceptance of their request and send copies to Guarantors. This letter should be sent as per specimen format which is at Annexure 2 enclosed herewith.

6)  On accepting of our letter/terms by the borrowers and guarantors and on payment of switch over fees, which is to be taken under the head “Switchover Fees under Misc. Income Group”, Branch is required to modify the interest rate in the housing loan account as

per extant ILA Circular. The interest rate applied would be as per original sanctioned loan amount slab. The Rate of Interest is floating and linked to Bank’s PLR and will change as and when rate of interest for that particular slab will change in future.

7)   Apart from the above, no further documentation would be required as all the other existing terms of sanction would remain unchanged.

8)  There are chances of some Housing Loan borrowers may approach branch for reducing EMI amount.  These cases would be few.  Such request of the Borrower would have to be incorporated by the borrower in the format at Annexure 1 and the Branches would have to deal with the same when referring the matter for switchover facility to Zonal Office.  It is to be noted that there should not be any change in number of balance EMI and maturity date of the loan must remain unchanged. If request for reduction of EMI is also approved by Zonal DGM then reply of the Branch in Annexure 2 has to also be modified suitably by incorporating the revised EMI amount.

 

Accounts Dept. shall make necessary arrangement to open Switchover Fees A/c. under Misc. Income Group.

 

Zonal Office will send by 5th of the succeeding month, a monthly report of approval given by them during the month as per enclosed format at Annexure-3 to  Credit Dept., Head Office for placing the same before Higher Authorities and Credit Dept, HO would in turn place a consolidated statement before the Board for information. 

 

The Branches are advised that switchover facility is not required to be advertised but it is to be provided only to the Housing loan borrowers who apply for the same and fulfills the conditions as mentioned above.  

 

All concerned are requested to note of the said instructions and bring the same to the notice of the staff.

 

 

P.R. Shetty

Managing Director
