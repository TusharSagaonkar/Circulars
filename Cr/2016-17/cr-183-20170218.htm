Circular CR No. 183 Dated 18th February, 2017

 

Current Accounts Maintained by Cash Credit Account Holders with Other Banks

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

I.It has been the experience in banking industry that customers enjoying credit facility(ies) from one or a few banks open current accounts with other banks and route sale proceeds through such current accounts. Sometimes, there is a genuine need for the borrowing customers opening current accounts outside the lending bank/ group of banks for paying taxes/ receiving refunds or some special purposes which are not served by the lending bank(s). However, such current accounts are likely to be misused for diversion/siphoning of funds from business depriving the lending bank/banks of control over the functioning of the unit.

 

II.   1. In order to ensure that liberty to borrowers to open current accounts with non-lending banks does not lead to circumventing of lending discipline, RBI has, in terms of Para 4.2 of their Master Circular-Management of Advances-UCBs no. RBI/2015-16/77 DCBR.BPD.(PCB) MC No.14/13.05.000/2015-16 dated July 01, 2015 advised all UCBs to avoid opening of current accounts of entities which enjoy credit facilities from another bank without obtaining a No-Objection Certificate (NOC) from the existing financing bank (response to which, positive, conditional or otherwise; is expected from the latter within a period of 15 days). Para 4.3 (with its sub-paragraphs 4.3.1, 4.3.2 & 4.3.3) of the same Master Circular provides guidelines for opening current accounts with the same end in view. There are parallel guidelines from RBI to all commercial banks in respect of opening current accounts so that liberty to customer to choose a bank for opening an account is counterbalanced with need for credit discipline on the part of banking industry as well as borrowers in general.

 

2. Our Bank’s “Credit Policy –Credit Rules & Procedures” in Para relating to “Current Account(s) with Other Banks” of Annexure-13 on “Monitoring of Cash Credit and Other Loan Accounts” (on page 144 thereof) lays down guidelines for monitoring of transactions in accounts of cash credit accountholders who do not bank exclusively with us (i) by obtaining statement of account every quarter, perusing the same & monitoring transactions, (ii) sharing of information on credit facilities enjoyed by the accountholders with us and requesting the other banks not to lend to such of our borrowers, & (iii) advising other bank(s) to close account(s) of our borrowers with them in the event of borrowers being found to misuse them.

 

3. The “Credit Policy –Credit Rules & Procedures” (vide Annexure 4 & 4A with sub-annexures on pages numbering from 104 to 117 thereof) also lays down guidelines on Multiple Banking and Sharing/Dissemination of Information among Banks-Lending under Consortium Arrangement/Multiple Banking Arrangements).

 

III.1. In our Bank, we take a continuous review of operations in cash credit accounts and observations in stock audit reports of cash credit accountholders. It is observed that most of the cash credit accountholders who are defaulters with our Bank maintain current accounts with other banks. Such accounts when opened with our permission are usually with nationalized banks for payment of taxes. They are also sought to be opened for receiving export proceeds/getting import documents released/ realization of upcountry cheques etc. Many times, accounts are opened with nationalized or other banks without permission from us and the pretext offered, when our borrowers are confronted with revelation of their having opened such accounts, is need for instant realisation of their cheques received from debtors.

 

2. While granting permission for opening accounts to facilitate payment of taxes etc., it is expected that the current account would be used only for the stated purpose(s). It is however observed that some cash credit accountholders route large amount of their book debts through such accounts with other banks bypassing our Bank and diverting the funds. Simultaneously, they issue cheques on our Bank and avail “TOD against clearing limit” to honour such cheques. In such situations, monitoring of transactions in cash credit accounts becomes difficult. Such practices also put our Bank’s interests in jeopardy.

 

IV.    In view of the unsatisfactory situation prevailing as above, it has been considered necessary to consolidate the current set of instructions on the subject as under.

 

A.   Where borrower seeks permission to open a current account with other bank

 

i.       Our existing borrowers may approach us for permission for opening a current account with some other bank for an acceptable reason such as payment of taxes, receipt of export proceeds/getting import documents released/realization of upcountry cheques etc. If permission is to be granted in such situations, branches should follow standard operating procedure as under.

                                                                                       

ii.     A letter seeking permission should be obtained from the borrower as per Annexure A-I, explaining in detail the requirement for opening a current account with another bank and undertaking not to avail credit limit from that Bank, and to transfer amount credited in such accounts due to realization of upcountry cheques/export bills to cash credit account with our Bank. The letter should contain an undertaking to submit to our Bank for perusal statement of such account at quarterly intervals, and transactions therein should be monitored.

 

iii.   The fact of having permitted the borrower-party to open a current account with other bank should be communicated to such other bank by letter as per the draft at Annexure A-II. The draft contains advices to the other bank that the cash credit accountholders enjoy limit(s) against hypothecation of Stocks and Book Debts and the bank should not extend any credit facilities to them.

 

iv.   Compliance on the part of borrowers as per the undertaking should be monitored on an ongoing basis. Further, statements of accounts with other banks should be subjected to further analysis at quarterly intervals and at the time of renewal/review and enhancement of CC limit, to ensure that there is no diversion of funds through current accounts with other banks.

 

v.     Branch should maintain a register in respect of firms maintaining current accounts with other banks recording therein the quarterly receipt of statements of accounts, the fact of having scrutinized the statement received every time, adverse findings if any, and remedial action in respect of the adverse findings including letters etc. issued to borrowers as well as the other bank.

 

vi.   In case misuse of the facility is evident either through scrutiny of the statements of accounts or through any other means but the account continues to be standard, a letter to such cash credit accountholders should be sent as per the format enclosed at Annexure A-III to get the account with other bank closed. All efforts should be made to persuade the borrowers either to close the account with other bank or to abide by the conditions under which the account opening was allowed.

 

vii.  If our borrowers’ accounts have slipped into irregular/NPA category, a letter as per Annexure A-IV should be sent to the Bank concerned as well to close the account.

 

viii.       If the borrowers’ accounts with us have become irregular/NPAs, and if the other bank with which the current account was opened with our permission does not close the same and does not remit credit balances as requested, a letter along the lines of Annexure A-V should be sent to them alerting them of the consequences of their not heeding our advices & requests.

 

B.Where borrower has not taken permission from our Bank to open account with other bank

 

i.                                              In some cases, maintenance of current account with other banks by our cash credit accountholders is revealed for the first time at the time of renewal/review and enhancement of CC limit. Such information is also available in the balance sheet pertaining to any Financial Year either submitted by the borrowers themselves, through search reports in respect of records with Registrar of Companies or through some other source. The asset

classification of accounts of the borrowers may be either “Standard” or otherwise and we need to deal with cases keeping in view the credit history and asset classification. Accordingly, branches should follow procedure as under.

 

ii.      A letter as per Annexure B-I should be sent to the borrowers, whose accounts may be either standard or otherwise, taking exception to their act of opening an account with another bank contrary to the agreed terms & conditions and asking them to close such account.

 

iii.    A letter as per the draft at Annexure B-II should be sent to the other bank with whom our borrowers have opened an account without our permission. The letter should contain limited information on credit facilities which the party enjoys from our Bank against hypothecation of stocks and book debts. In case of other secured loans etc., mention should be made of other loans against securities such as immovable properties etc. However, amount of Cash Credit & Loan(s) should not be mentioned in the said letter. Further, confidential report on our borrower should be called from such bank along with a statement of account since its opening with them. The statement of account, if & when received, should be closely scrutinized to ascertain whether sale proceeds/realised book debts are routed through such account resulting in diversion of funds causing harm to our Bank’s interests.

 

iv.    If loans and advances to the borrowers are “Standard”, if reply from the other bank to our letter as per Annexure B-II is to our satisfaction and if conduct of the account as revealed through the confidential report is not found to be detrimental to our Bank’s interests, a letter as per Annexure B-III should be invited from our borrowers containing request for allowing a current account already opened by them with other bank subject to conditions. We may allow continuation of such account by issuing a letter to the other bank as per Annexure B-IV.

 

v.     If reply & confidential report from the other bank is not received in respect of borrowers maintaining asset classification as “Standard”, sufficient follow up should be made with the other bank in this behalf.

                                                                    

vi.    If the loans and advances to the borrowers are irregular/NPAs, a letter as per Annexure B-V should be addressed to it reminding about collective duty of bankers to enforce credit discipline and regulatory risk the other bank is exposed to if the duty is abdicated. The letter should be sent by Regd. A.D. and acknowledgement should be held on record.

 

V. Structured procedures as above are expected to curb the tendency on the part of some borrowers to deviate from credit discipline. Therefore, all the functionaries in the area of credit management are advised to follow them meticulously.

 

VI.  Inspection Department shall ensure that Branches have maintained Register in respect of the borrowers operating account with other banks and follow instructions as given under Point IV.A.v of this circular

 

VII. Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all staff/officials concerned at your Branch/Department.

 

 

 

 

P.R. Shetty

Managing Director
