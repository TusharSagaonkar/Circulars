Circular CR No.162 dated 20th May, 2016

Extension of OTS scheme and Introduction of Compromise Settlement Scheme for NPA Accounts

 

During the last few years main thrust of the Bank has been to reduce NPAs. The bank used various strategies to reduce the percentage of NPAs which is still very high as compared to peers and scheduled commercial Banks. The bank has also intensified Recovery action in NPA accounts at all fronts. Resultantly the borrowers/sureties are coming forward to settle accounts which are chronic NPAs. The Bank has implemented OTS schemes formulated by Reserve Bank of India in the past. After conversion of our Bank into Multi State Co op Bank, we have formulated OTS scheme based on the guidelines issued by RBI and implemented the same up to 31/03/2016 by extending the cutoff date for eligibility from time to time. We have also implemented Compromise Settlement Scheme for the NPA Accounts which are not eligible under OTS Scheme. The main purpose of implementing OTS and Compromise Settlement Scheme is to accelerate recoveries in such accounts as well as in chronic NPA accounts within a reasonable period so that the funds are recycled for better use. Due to acceleration of recovery efforts by the Bank, some of the borrowers and even sureties are approaching the Bank for settlement of accounts which are written-off under Prudential Write-Off.

There are also large no. of surety/SDL Account having principal amount less than Rs.25,000/- which have been transferred to Prudential Write-Off after certification by Statutory Auditors. In such accounts the cost involved in follow up and recovery is high. It has been decided to settle such accounts by accepting principal outstanding amount and accordingly changes have been made in current OTS Policy to incorporate such type of small written off accounts also.

 Accordingly, in supersession of instructions given in Circular No CR No.45 dated 08/07/2011 and subsequent IDCs issued from time to time regarding OTS/CSS Scheme, following revised guidelines are issued for implementation of OTS/CSS Scheme.

A)      GUIDELINES FOR ONE TIME SETTLEMENT (OTS) SCHEME

 I)      The Scheme has been extended to all eligible accounts upto 31.03.2017 as under:-

a)  The Last date of submission of application for OTS is 28/02/2017.

b) The Last date for processing the applications by Bank is 31/03/2017.

 II)                    Following Borrowal Accounts will not be covered under this scheme

a)   Cases of willful default, fraud and malfeasance.

b)  Loans availed of or guaranteed by Directors or by close relatives of Directors or by Firms/Co./Institutions in which the Directors are interested including Directors of Merged banks or their relatives. Loans availed of or Guaranteed by Ex - Directors of the bank including Ex - Directors of merged banks.(However such proposals may be considered subject to permission of RBI as per provision of section 20A of Banking Regulation Act 1949.)

c)   Loans guaranteed by Govt. (including cases where Govt. Guarantee has been invoked and not honoured by Govt.).

d) Loans with tie-up arrangement for recovery with employer (SDL).However it has been decided to give benefit to the borrowers who have lost their job due to permanent lockout/liquidation of their companies/firms but who otherwise fulfill all the other terms and conditions of the OTS scheme. Further in respect of prudential write off accounts it has been decided to extend the benefit of

 OTS scheme to SDL loan accounts in which borrower is deceased, retired, suspended, physically disabled, unemployed if he/she fulfills all other terms and conditions of OTS scheme.

e)    Loans due from Govt. Dept./Undertakings.

f)     Loans under Govt. directed Programmes.

 

III)    The Scheme will be applicable to the following Loans :

a)   This Scheme will be applicable for all loans which have become Doubtful or Loss assets as on 31/03/2013 with outstanding balance of Rs.10.00 Crore and below on the cut off date.

b)  The guidelines will also cover NPA A/cs classified as sub-standard on 31/03/2013, which have subsequently become doubtful or loss.

c)  The NPA Accounts (Sub-Standard,Doubtful or Loss) having outstanding balance of more than Rs.10.00 Crore on cut of date (Date of Doubtful) may be settled with prior approval of Central Registrar Co-op. Societies, New Delhi.

 

d)  The Surety/SDL loan account having principal outstanding less than or equal to Rs.25000/- as on 31/03/2016, which have been classified as Doubtful NPA as on 31/03/2010 and transferred to Prudential Write Off may be settled by accepting minimum principal amount outstanding as on the date of approval of OTS proposal. In such cases the borrower has to pay the settlement amount within 45 days from the date of approval of OTS application.

 

e)   The Scheme will be applicable only to those accounts classified as NPA by the Banks and which have been included in the NPA Statement and reported to RBI as per their guidelines.

 

IV)    Settlement formula – Minimum amount to be recovered:

a)  NPAs classified as Doubtful or Loss on 31.03.2013.

      The minimum amount that shall be recovered in respect of NPAs classified as Doubtful or Loss as on 31.03.2013 will be 100% of outstanding balance (Principal + Interest) in the account on the date when the account was categorized as doubtful NPAs.

 

b) NPAs Classified as Sub – Standard as on 31.03.2013, which have been classified as doubtful or loss subsequently.

The Minimum amount that shall be recovered in respect of NPAs classified as sub-standard as on 31.03.2013, which have been classified as doubtful or loss subsequently would be 100% of outstanding balance (Principal + Interest) in the account as on the date on which the account was categorized as doubtful or loss.

The amount paid to the bank after classification of account as doubtful in both the cases shall be taken into consideration at the time of settlement. However, the minimum amount to be recovered shall not in any case be less than the principal amount outstanding as on the date of approval by the Bank of the OTS application.

[Example : In one loan A/c the outstanding balance as on 31.03.2013 (date of doubtful) is principal Rs.100/- plus interest Rs.40/- and the borrower has paid Rs.60/- after 31.03.2013 which is adjusted towards interest, in such case the minimum principal amount to be recovered is Rs.100/-].

V)  Repayment Period :

a)   The borrower should pay the settlement amount at one stroke within 45 days from the date of approval of application under One Time Settlement Scheme. No interest will be charged on settlement amount.

 

b)  If the borrower opts to pay in installment(s) he should pay minimum 25% of settlement amount within one month from date of approval of application under One Time Settlement Scheme and balance amount to be paid in maximum 11 monthly installments with interest @ PLR (At present PLR is 13.5% p.a) on the settlement amount on daily reducing balance till repayment of entire dues under One Time Settlement Scheme.

 

c)   Alongwith the OTS application the borrower is required to pay 10% of the amount outstanding (Principal + Accounted Interest outstanding on the date of application), which will be adjusted towards initial payment of 25% under One Time Settlement Scheme.

d)  The entire OTS amount shall be paid within one year from the date of approval.

 

VI)    General Instructions for One Time Settlement :

a)   Penal interest, legal charges & other charges may be waived.

b) One Time Settlement Scheme will be non-discriminatory and non     discretionary.

c)   After approval of proposal under OTS scheme, if the account is under case file status Consent Terms may be filed before appropriate Authority/Court. If the borrower fails to pay the dues as per consent terms the Award/order passed by Authority/Court will be executed for recovery of entire outstanding dues.

d)  In case of non-case filed account, as well as for SRO/ARB account, if the borrower fails to repay settlement amount within stipulated period or where 25% of the amount is not received within the stipulated period under the Scheme recovery proceedings will be initiated/continued against borrower & sureties.

e)   The calculation will be checked and certified by Auditor appointed by the Bank. The Auditors fees has to be borne by the borrower, which will not be refunded.

f)    All applications under this Scheme after certification by Auditor will be placed before Recovery Sub-Committee for its approval.

g)  The decision of the bank with respect of eligibility and amount payable under the OTS shall be final.

h)  No borrower can claim any right in respect of the OTS Scheme.

i)    The shares of the borrower company/firm, its proprietor, partners, and directors along with shares of sureties will be adjusted with consent/prior intimation to borrower, sureties & their Prop./Partners/Directors provided there is no other direct/indirect liability. Borrower/ Surety(ies) will not be eligible to become member of the bank and to avail finance from our bank or to give guarantee for five years from date of cessation of membership.

j)    Only those NPA Accounts, which have been classified as NPA as on 31/03/2013 and which have been included in NPA statement and reported to RBI & other authorities would be eligible for concession under this OTS Scheme.

k)  The OTS proposal which were sanctioned upto 31/03/2014 and where the borrower failed to settle the account but have now again approached for settlement then the said proposals may be considered as fresh proposal. Further, OTS proposals sanctioned on or after 01/04/2014 and not settled before due date, but they are now willing to settle the account, in such cases the accounts may be settled by charging simple interest @ PLR prevailing at the time of approval of OTS on settlement amount till closure of the account. Provided he/they should deposit 25% of balance of OTS amount with application for extension of OTS period and pay balance amount with interest within 6 months from date of approval for extension. The decision for permitting the extension of period on the request of the borrower/surety will be taken by the Board.

l)   The scheme is made applicable for settlement of NPA accounts of our bank as well as merged banks.

 

B)     GUIDELINES FOR COMPROMISE SETTLEMENT SCHEME

 This Scheme will be applicable for all NPA accounts irrespective of date of NPA which are not eligible under OTS scheme.

However the scheme will not cover following accounts.

a)   NPA accounts classified as willful defaulter, Fraud and Malfeasance.

b)  Loans availed of or guaranteed by Directors or by close relatives of Directors or by Firms/Co./Institutions in which the Directors are interested, including Directors of Merged banks or their relatives. Loans availed of or Guaranteed by Ex - Directors of the bank including Ex - Directors of merged banks.(However such proposals may be considered subject to permission of RBI as per provision of section 20A of Banking Regulation Act 1949.).

II)     SETTLEMENT FORMULA FOR SECURED LOANS :-

Consideration of proposal for settlement of NPA A/cs will be linked to available securities and status of business as under:

a)   Present value of moveable/immovable properties, hypothecated/ mortgaged to the bank (both prime & collateral) as well as value of properties of borrower and sureties attached by SRO/Receiver/Court having clear and marketable title to be taken into account. If such properties are mortgaged to some other bank/FI the outstanding dues of such bank/FI will be ascertained by the Bank.

 

b)  If the business is running, then the value of stock & debtors (as per last Audited Balance Sheet and updated stock and Book debt statement) should be taken into account.

 

c)   If, the value of such movable & immovable properties [referred above II (a) & (b)] is more than the outstanding (Principal + Interest) as on the date of consideration of proposal, the borrower will have to pay 100% of principal outstanding as on the date of approval plus simple interest@ contracted rate or PLR (prevalent at the time of approval) – 2%, whichever is lower but not less than 8%(Present Cost of Deposit) from date of NPA till the date of approval. The amount paid after the date of NPA will be deducted. However the minimum settlement amount should not be less than principal amount outstanding on the date of approval.

 

d)  If, the value of such movable & immovable properties (referred above II (a) and (b) is less than the total outstanding in account (Principal + Interest) but more than 50% of the amount outstanding in the account (Principal + Interest) as on the date of consideration of proposal then the amount to be recovered would be 100% of principal outstanding as on the date of approval plus simple interest @ Contracted rate or PLR (prevalent at the time of approval) – 3%, whichever is lower but not less than 8% (Present Cost of Deposit) from the date of NPA till the date of approval, the amount paid after date of NPA will be deducted. However the minimum settlement amount should not be less than principal amount outstanding on date of approval.

 

e)   If the value of such movable & immovable properties (referred above II (a) and (b) is less than 50 % of the amount outstanding in the account (Principal + Interest) as on the date of consideration of proposal then the amount to be recovered would be 100% of principal outstanding as on the date of approval plus simple interest @ Contracted rate or PLR (prevalent at the time of approval) – 4 % whichever is lower

but not less than 8%(Present Cost of Deposit) from the date of NPA till date of approval. The amount paid after date of NPA will be deducted. However the minimum settlement amount should not be less than principal amount outstanding on date of approval.

III)    Payment of Settlement Amount in case of Secured Loans :

i)        20 % of outstanding amount (Prin + Accounted Interest.)on date of application by way of cash / DD/RTGS/NEFT at the time of application which will not be refunded in case the compromise proposal is not approved by the Bank or it is not acceptable to the applicant.

 

ii)       The entire (i.e.100%) settlement amount must be paid within 45 days from date of approval after deducting the amount already deposited with application. No interest will be charged on settlement amount.

OR

iii)     (a) 40% of settlement amount to be paid within 45 days from date of approval after deducting the amount already deposited with application.

(b) Balance of settlement amount to be paid within 06 months from date of approval. The interest to be charged at PLR prevailing at the time of date of approval on settlement amount from date of approval till closure of account.

(c) If the borrower fails to settle the account within stipulated period of 06 months, additional time may be given on receiving his application alongwith deposit of 50% of balance payment. In such cases Bank may consider extension of further period of 06 months from the date of expiry. However interest @ PLR( applicable on the date of approval) +1% shall be charged on settlement amount from the date of expiry till closure of the account. The decision on the request of the borrower/surety to grant additional time will be taken by the Board.

 

IV)     Settlement of Surety/SDL accounts

a)     The accounts which have become NPA on or before 31/03/2013 to be settled by accepting outstanding principal amount + accounted interest + charges on the date of approval. The unaccounted interest to be waived. The account should be settled within 45 days from the date of approval.

 

b)    The accounts which have become NPA after 31/03/2013 to be settled by charging simple interest at 10% p.a. from date of NPA till closure of account. The account should be settled within 45 days from the date of approval.

 

V)     Payment of Settlement Amount in case of Surety/SDL Accounts :

i)        20 % of outstanding amount on the date of application (Prin +Accounted Interest.) by way of cash / DD/RTGS/NEFT at the time of application which will not be refunded in case the compromise proposal is not approved by the Bank or it is not acceptable to the applicant.

 

ii)       100 % of settlement amount within 45 days from date of approval after deducting the amount already deposited with application. No interest will be charged on settlement amount in respect of accounts referred in IV (a) above.

 

        VI)    General Instructions applicable for Compromise Settlement Scheme for secured/Surety/SDL Accounts

I)            The expenses incurred by Bank for Publication of notices, valuation, watch and ward, legal expenses including Advocate fees, expenses incurred for taking possession of property etc. will be recovered.

II)           After approval of proposal under Compromise Settlement if the account is under case file status Consent Terms may be filed before appropriate Authority/Court. If the borrower fails to pay the dues as per consent terms the Award/order passed by Authority/Court will be executed for recovery of entire outstanding dues.

III)         In case of non-case filed account as well as SRO/ARB accounts if the borrower fails to pay settlement amount within stipulated period or where 40% of the amount is not received within the stipulated period under the Scheme (as referred above under clause B3 (iii) (a)), necessary and available recovery proceedings will be initiated/continued against borrower & sureties.

IV)        The calculation will be checked and certified by the Auditor appointed by the Bank. The Auditor's fees will have to be borne by the borrower which is non-refundable.

V)   All application under this Scheme after certification by Auditor will be placed before Recovery Sub-Committee for approval.

VI)        The decision of the bank with respect of eligibility and amount payable under the compromise settlement scheme will be final.

VII)       The shares of the borrower company/firm, its proprietor, partners, and directors along with shares of sureties will be adjusted with consent/prior intimation to borrower, sureties & their Prop./Partners/Directors provided there is no other direct/indirect liability. Borrower/ Surety(ies) will not be eligible to become member of the bank and to avail finance from our bank or to give guarantee for five years from date of cessation of membership.

VIII)     While ascertaining value of Moveable/Immoveable properties charged/mortgaged to the Bank or attached by SRO/Receiver/Civil Court/High Court etc as the case may be, the market value of the properties should be taken into account as per valuation of Govt. approved valuer on bank’s panel. If the value of the property is more than Rs.50.00 lakh Valuation reports to be obtained from two independent valuers on Bank’s panel. The outstanding dues of the

          society, claims of sales Tax, workers dues, provident fund / ESIS dues and any other dues, which have priority over secured creditors are to be deducted from the valuation of properties.

 

(C) General Guidelines for OTS and Compromise Settlement Scheme.

 

I)      If the borrower approaches for settlement under OTS/CSS after sale of mortgaged/hypothecated or attached property and if the sale proceeds are more than the settlement amount under OTS/CSS, refund of amount should not be considered and the entire sale proceeds should be appropriated towards outstanding dues as OTS/CSS amount. (Refund of surplus sale proceeds can be considered only if entire dues i.e. without any sacrifice is recovered from the sale proceeds).

 

II)   If Tender/Auction Notice is published & loanee approaches the

Bank for OTS/CSS settlement, then Tender/Auction could be cancelled if the loanee make down payment of 50% of OTS/CSS amount.

 

III) If the borrower is having Multiple NPA Accounts, he should apply for OTS/CSS of all accounts at a time.

E)     PROCEDURE TO BE FOLLOWED BY BRANCHES/ZONAL/REGIONAL OFFICES AND LEGAL & RECOVERY DEPARTMENT FOR BOTH ONE TIME SETTLEMENT (OTS) AND COMPROMISE SETTLEMENT SCHEME (CSS) :

I)      Procedure to be followed for implementation of OTS and Compromise Settlement scheme by branches under Central, Western and Navi Mumbai Zones

a)   To obtain applications in enclosed format (Annexure “A”) from   eligible defaulter borrowers/sureties for settlement of account    under OTS scheme and for Compromise Settlement in enclosed format (Annexure “B”),duly signed and submit the same to     Legal and Recovery Dept along with following documents for processing and obtaining approval from the Recovery Sub-Committee.

                             i) Statement of accounts from beginning (i.e. from the date  of                             NPA) with interest receivable statement duly certified.

                             ii) Authenticated proof of classification of account as  Sub-Standard/doubtful

                             iii) General and security files of credit facility account.

b) To collect Rs.500/- per account being auditors fees and remit the same to Recovery Dept. Please note that Auditor’s fees are not refundable. This should be made known to the applicants.

c) To keep the amount deposited by the applicant along with application for OTS/Compromise in OTS initial deposit A/c and adjust the same to the account after approval/non approval of the application by the Recovery Sub-Committee.

II)     Procedure to be followed for implementation of OTS and Compromise Settlement scheme by branches under Pune Zone, Regional Office Udupi and Regional Office Ahmedabad.

a)   To obtain applications in format Annexure “A” from eligible defaulter borrowers/sureties for settlement of account under OTS scheme and for Compromise Settlement in format Annexure “B”, duly signed and send the same to concerned Zonal/Regional Office along with following documents for processing and obtaining approval from Recovery Sub-Committee.

i. Statement of accounts from beginning (i.e. from the date of NPA) with interest receivable statement duly certified.

ii. Authenticated proof of classification of account as Sub Standard/doubtful.

iii. General and Security files of credit facility account.

b)  To collect Rs.500/- per account being auditors fees and remit the same to concern Zonal/Regional Office. (The fees are non refundable).

c)   To keep the amount deposited by the applicant along with application for OTS/Compromise in OTS initial deposit A/c and adjust the same to the account after approval/non approval of the application by the Recovery Sub-Committee.

III)    Procedure to be followed for implementation of OTS and Compromise Settlement Scheme by Zonal Office Pune, Regional Office Udupi and Regional Office Ahmedabad.

a)      After receipt of application for settlement under OTS/Compromise settlement Scheme, Zonal Office/Regional Office should thoroughly scrutinize the proposal and prepare process note for OTS proposal in enclosed format.

(Annexure “C”) and process note for Compromise settlement in enclosed format (Annexure “D”) and get the same certified from Auditor on Banks panel.

b)       To send the process note duly recommended and certified

by auditors along with application for OTS/Compromise Settlement, statement of account, authenticated proof of classification of account as sub standard/doubtful. If the proposal is under Compromise Settlement, in addition to above documents submit valuation reports, copy of updated stock/book debt statement and balance sheet taken into consideration while determining value of security in respect of Compromise Settlement proposals and send the same to Legal & Recovery Dept. with consolidated statement of all proposals in enclosed format (Annexure “E”) for placing the same before the Recovery Sub Committee for approval.

c)        After receipt of approval from board send letters to the

applicants in enclosed format (Annexure 'F' or Annexure  'G') as the case may be and obtain confirmation letter from them for having accepted the terms and option opted by them for payment of settlement amount.

d)       If the account is under case file status, file consent term before the appropriate authority/court where the case is pending.

e)       To keep constant follow up with the applicant for payment of settlement amount within stipulated period.

f)         If the applicant/borrower fails to pay as per terms and conditions of settlement, initiate/continue legal/recovery action immediately.

g)       Send monthly report of recovery under OTS/Compromise settlement to Legal & Recovery Dept in enclosed format (Annexure 'H') before 5th of succeeding month.

IV)    Procedure to be followed for implementation of OTS and Compromise Settlement Policy by Legal and Recovery Dept.

a) After receipt of application for settlement under OTS/Compromise settlement Scheme from branches under Central, Western, Navi Mumbai Zone, Legal and Recovery Dept. should scrutinize the proposal and prepare process note for OTS proposal in format Annexure “C” and process note for Compromise settlement in enclosed format Annexure “D” and get the same certified from Auditor on Bank’s panel.

b)Legal & Recovery Dept. shall cross verify the proposals received from Zonal Office Pune, Regional Office Udupi and Ahmedabad before placing the same before the Recovery Sub Committee. If any discrepancy is found, get the same rectified from the In-Charge of concerned Zone/RO.

c) After receipt of approval from Recovery Sub-Committee, send letters to the applicants except applications received from Zonal Office Pune , Regional Office Udupi and Ahmedabad in format Annexure 'F' or Annexure  'G' as the case may be and obtain confirmation letter from the applicants for having accepted the terms and option opted by them for payment of settlement amount.

d)If the account is under case file status, file consent term before the appropriate authority/court where the case is pending.

e) To keep constant follow up with the applicant for payment of settlement amount within stipulated period.

f)  If the applicant/borrower fails to pay as per terms and conditions of settlement, initiate/continue legal/recovery action immediately.

g)To call monthly report of recovery under OTS/Compromise settlement from Zonal Office Pune, Regional Office Udupi and Ahmedabad before 5th of succeeding month and place the same before the Recovery Sub-Committee meeting of succeeding month along with report of OTS/Compromise Settlement proposals processed by Legal and recovery Dept.

h)To impart training/provide guidance to the concerned staff of Zonal Office Pune, Regional office Udupi and Ahmedabad regarding processing of OTS/Compromise settlement proposals.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members at your Branch/ Department and ensure strict compliance.
