Circular CR No. 170 dated 8th September, 2016

Registration of Securities, Reconstruction or Creation of Security Interest with Central Registry (CERSAI)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Please refer to our Circular CR No. 54, Circular CR No. 62, Circular CR No. 114 wherein we have instructed to register the Bank’s charge created by all types of mortgages with CERSAI on all properties mortgaged to the Bank.

 

We have instructed to register Bank’s charge created by way of all types of mortgages with CERSAI i.e. Registered Simple Mortgage, Registered Equitable Mortgage, Equitable Mortgage or any other type of mortgage which are still in existence (Subsisting Transactions) and all such transactions in future. Where Bank’s charge has not been created by way of any type of mortgage but the charge has been created by way of Lien Noting with the Society/Builder on the property, such charge shall also be registered with CERSAI on receiving duly signed letter from the Society/Builder for having noted the Lien.

 

Recently, we have received communication from CERSAI wherein they have stated that due to an amendment to the Securitization and Reconstruction of Financial Assets and Enforcements of Security Interest (Central Registry) Rules 2011 vide gazette notification number GSR 102(E) (F.No. 3/2/2014-Recovery) dated 22 January 2016, the following types of security interest are now also required to be filed on the CERSAI Portal.

 

1.  Particulars of creation, modification or satisfaction of security interest in hypothecation of plant and machinery, stocks, debt including book debt or receivables, whether existing or future shall be filed with CERSAI.

It means the Bank has to create charge on the movable properties which are hypothecated to it like hypothecation of Stock and book debts, plant and machinery, equipments, furniture, fixtures, fees receivable, Vehicle, etc.

 

2.  Particulars of creation, modification or satisfaction of security interest in intangible assets, being knowhow, patent, copyright, trade mark, licence, franchise or any other business or commercial right of similar nature, shall be filed with CERSAI-

 

 The security interest in intangible assets need to start from now onwards. At present in our Bank, we do not obtain intangible assets as security for the credit facilities extended to the borrower.

 

3.   Particulars of creation, modification or satisfaction of security interest in any under construction residential or commercial building or a part thereof by an agreement or instrument other than by mortgage shall be filed with CERSAI.

 

The filing for records of security interest in any under construction residential or commercial building or a part thereof by an agreement or instrument other than by mortgage will be started at a later date and will be advised accordingly.

 

Registration of Bank’s charge created by all types of mortgages, Lien Noting, hypothecation of Movable Assets and Intangible assets with CERSAI is mandatory.

 

The Branches were advised vide our IDC HO/CD/600/ 2016-17 dated 11.08.2016 to complete creation of charge on movable assets hypothecated to the Bank for the period 22.1.2016 to 10.8.2016 by 19.8.2016. We are sure that by this time all branches must have completed the above assignment.

 

Further vide email dated 19.08.2016 of General Manager (Credit, Forex and Recovery Dept.), it was instructed not to create the charge on hypothecation of stock and book debts in case of review of cash credit limit. The charge shall be created of those accounts at the time of RENEWAL OF THE ACCOUNT or as and when CERSAI issues instructions for creation of charges of hypothecations created before 22.01.2016.
 

It is further advised by CERSAI that all security interest created on or before 22.01.2016 i.e. the filing of subsisting transactions will start at a later date which will be specified by the Central Govt.

 

Henceforth, the branches shall ensure that security interest created in Movable and Immovable properties in favour of Bank by way of hypothecation of movable assets and mortgages (Both Equitable and Registered Simple Mortgages) be reported to Zonal Office within 3 days for creation of charge with CERSAI.

 

Any delay in filing transactions related to movable assets and immovable assets will attract Penalty. Therefore, all are requested to complete the said work within stipulated time period.

 

The stipulated time period means charge with CERSAI to be registered within 30 days from the date of execution of Security/Loan Documents.

 

Once the charge is created with CERSAI on hypothecation of stock and book debts for Cash credit limits, Letter of Credit limits, in such cases the charge will not be created at subsequent renewal or review of the accounts. In case of enhancement in working capital limits where security by way of hypothecation of stock and book debts/extension of mortgage is created, charge on enhanced amount has to be created with CERSAI.

 

        Once the borrower has repaid entire amount of Loans & Advances with interest and has fulfilled all his obligations, Branches should note to convey the message to Zonal Office/Regional Office by way of IDC signed by the Branch-In-Charge quoting the CERSAI’s Registration Number for satisfaction of mortgage charge/hypothecation charge. On receiving the IDC from the Branch, Zonal Office/Regional Office will mark this charge as ‘Satisfied’ in Central Registry System conveying that the property is now free from Bank’s charge.

 

        In case of Satisfaction of Charge, the same has to be satisfied within 30 days from the date of closure of the account.

 

Part-II- CERSAI would be implementing the following amendments in future and we would intimate the branches when the same is notified for implementation.

 

          By recent amendment in SARFAESI Act, the Central Govt. for the purpose of providing a central database would integrate the registration records (which includes registration under Companies Act, 2013, the Registration Act, 1908, the Motor Vehicle Act, 1988 or other such records) with the records of CERSAI. After integration of records of various registration systems, the Central Govt. will issue necessary notification. The amendments also provide that taxes due from Assessees who are defaulters, attachment order issued against any property passed by any competent authority will have to be registered by the concerned authority with CERSAI. The claim of secured creditor or other creditor holding attachment shall have priority over any subsequent security interest created upon such property and any transfer by way of sale, lease, assignment, licence or attachment subsequent to such registration. The right of enforcement of security by the Secured Creditor can be exercised only if the charge is registered with CERSAI.

 

Part-III – Procedure for Collection of Fees:-

 

Further, it is also observed from our record that there is huge difference in amount credited to CERSAI and collected from the borrowers. This is mainly due to non observance of instructions given vide our various circulars. We again reiterate the instructions as under:

                i.      At the time of transaction itself the fees being paid to CERSAI for mortgage registration or for any other transaction will be recovered from the concerned borrower/constituent/ customer

 

The accounting entries will be passed as under:

Debit: Cash or borrower/ constituent/ customer’s A/c

Credit: CERSAI Payable Account

 

                     ii.        On receiving Registration Number or confirmation of charge as ‘created’ as the case may be, from CERSAI, Branches will pass following entries:

 Debit: CERSAI Payable Account.

    Credit: Head Office (HO) New A/c – CERSAI.

 With mentioning particulars as Name of the borrower/ constituent /customer, A/c No.___________________-, Type of Transaction, etc.

 

             iii.      For all other charges, branches may credit the amount directly to Head Office (HO) New A/c – CERSAI.

 

                   iv.        After crediting the amount to Head Office (HO) New A/c - CERSAI, Braches will send advices to respective Zonal Office/Regional Office and Accounts Departments.

 

At present, the CERSAI is not levying any fees for satisfaction of charge.

 

On monthly basis, branches will make a list of pending entries in their ‘CERSAI Payable Account’ and will ensure that no entries are outstanding for more than 10 days. The list of entries pending for more than 10 days will be submitted to respective Zonal Office/ Regional office for their information and necessary action.

 

               v.     After passing the necessary entries, branches will send advices to respective Zonal Offices and Accounts Department.

 

              vi.     All Zonal Offices shall submit consolidated report on monthly basis on or before 5th of next month for all transactions held during the month to Accounts Department for the purpose of reconciliation at their end.

 

Accounts Department shall reconcile all the entries on monthly basis on or before 10th of succeeding month.

         

Above procedure to be invariably observed by the Branches, Zonal Offices, Account Dept. and all concerned.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department and ensure strict compliance.
