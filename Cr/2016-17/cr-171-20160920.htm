Circular CR No. 171 Dated 20th September, 2016

 

Guidelines on effecting loan recoveries from the Salary/Bank A/cs./

Properties of the Borrowers & Guarantors and Execution of

Arbitral Awards through the Recovery Officers appointed by the

Hon’ble Central Registrar, Co-op. Societies, New Delhi.

         

In supersession of Circular No. Circular No. CR – 100 dated 20/03/2004, the revised guidelines on the captioned subject are issued hereunder.

Consequent upon registration of our Bank by conversion under the Multi-State Co-op. Societies Act, 2002 w.e.f. 11/01/2007, we have been filing recovery cases before the Hon’ble Arbitrator appointed by the Hon’ble Central Registrar, Co-op. Societies, New Delhi. Earlier the Hon’ble Arbitrator was issuing ‘Arbitral Award’ providing for an appointment of the concerned Branch Manager as a ‘Receiver’ for effecting recovery through execution of such Award. However, as per the Court decision, the Hon’ble Arbitrator has stopped appointing the Branch Managers as Receivers for execution of the Awards. In the absence of judicial powers to execute the Arbitral Awards, the branches were facing difficulties in recovering the awarded dues. Hence, the Hon’ble Central Registrar, Co-op. Societies, New Delhi, on the request of the Bank, have by their communication dated 19/08/2016 authorized the following Officers of our Bank for execution of Arbitral Awards U/s. 97 of the Multi State Co-op. Societies Act, 2002 :-

A copy of the order dated 19/08/2016 of the Hon’ble Central Registrar authorizing the above mentioned officers as Recovery Officers has separately been forwarded to the concerned officers solely for their information and record.

It may be noted that vide Circular No. CR – 100 dated 20/03/2004, detailed guidelines were issued for initiating necessary steps, before filing of the recovery case and after receipt of the Arbitral Award. It was also advised in the said Circular that if no recovery is forthcoming in the loan accounts even after exhausting all specified measures, then Branches should forward such Loan files to the Dy. Gen. Manager (Legal & Recovery) for filing execution proceedings before the concerned Civil Court. However, since the above mentioned Officers of the Bank have now been authorized for effecting recovery in pursuance of the Arbitral Awards, we can attach the Salary/ Wages/Service Dues, Bank Deposit accounts and personal properties of the Judgement Debtors, through the said designated Recovery Officers. However, for sale of the attached properties, the matter be referred to the Recovery Dept., Parel for guidance.

Accordingly, the branches are advised to take the following steps for effecting recoveries through salary/wages/service dues, Bank Deposit accounts and properties of the Judgement Debtors :-

 

 

I.      Before filing of recovery case -

1)      When the Loan A/c. is classified as NPA and loan file is being processed for taking case file decision wherein the borrower or guarantors are salaried persons, Branch should first exhaust the remedy of recovering the loan dues from the salary of the borrower and guarantors, as per the guidelines given in the Recovery Policy. The specimen of letters to be issued to the employer of the Borrower and employer of Guarantors are enclosed as Annexure-1 and Annexure-2 respectively.

2)      In case, no reply to such request letter is received from the employer of the borrower and/or guarantors within one month then the Branch should immediately issue reminder letter as per Annexure-3.

 

II.     After receipt of Arbitral Award -

3)      The Branch should forward a copy of the Award to all the Judgement Debtors (borrower and sureties) alongwith a covering letter within two working days of its receipt. The specimen of the covering letter is enclosed as ‘Annexure-4’.

4)      If no response was received from the employer, then the Branch should send a reminder alongwith the copy of Award within 7 working days from the date of receipt of Award, impressing upon the employer to start deduction of loan dues from the salary of the borrower and guarantors. The specimen of the said letter is enclosed as ‘Annexure-5’.

5)      If actions for recovering the loan dues from the salary of the borrower and guarantors were not initiated before filing of recovery case and there is no response from the borrower/sureties to the letter forwarding a copy of Award, then the Branch should send Authority Letter obtained u/s. 60 of Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002 alongwith copy of the Award to the employer of each of the Judgement Debtors with covering letter on bank’s letter head as per ‘Annexure-6’ within 7 working days from the date of receipt of Award. The Branch/Recovery Dept. should demand the Awarded dues to be deducted and remitted every month from the salary of the Judgement Debtors. It is advised that Branch should demand monthly amount as prescribed in the authority letter obtained u/s 60 of Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002 from each of the Judgement Debtor.

6)      If no response is received from the employer, then the Branch should issue reminder to the employer, as per specimen enclosed as ‘Annexure-7.’

7)      If the Judgement Debtors are maintaining deposit account in single name with other Branches of our Bank, then Branch can attach the said deposit a/c of such Judgement debtor by virtue of Section 55 of the Multi State Co-operative Societies Act, read with Bankers’ right of general lien and set off, after giving due notice to the said Judgement Debtor. The specimen of Notice to be sent to the Judgement Debtor is enclosed as ‘Annexure-8’.

 

III.   After issuance of aforesaid Letters & Notices :

Even after exhausting all the above mentioned remedies if recovery is not forthcoming, then Branches shall proceed for attachment of the Salary and Bank Deposit accounts of the Judgement Debtors, through the above mentioned designated Recovery Officers, as stated hereunder :-

1.   Issue attachment orders to the Employers of the Judgement Debtors, as per the specimen enclosed as Annexure-9.

2.   Issue reminders to the Employers, as per the specimen enclosed as Annexure-10.

3.   Issue attachment orders to the Banks where the Judgement Debtors are maintaining the deposit accounts, as per the specimen enclosed as Annexure-11.

4.   Issue reminders to the Bankers of Judgement Debtors, as per the specimen enclosed as Annexure-12.

5.   Issue attachment orders to the Secretary/Chairman of the Housing Societies/Talathi/Tahsildar requesting them to note Bank’s lien/attachment on the properties of the Judgement Debtors, as per specimen enclosed as Annexure-13.

The Incharge of Branches under Western, Central and Navi Mumbai Zones shall prepare the attachment orders as per the specimens enclosed hereto (making suitable modifications wherever found necessary so as to incorporate the facts) and forward the above mentioned attachment orders duly counter-signed by them to the designated Recovery Officers at Legal & Recovery Dept. at Parel. All other branches shall forward all such attachment orders to their concerned Zonal Office/Regional Office. The designated Recovery Officers shall sign the attachment orders and return the same to the concerned branches for issuance of the said attachment orders to the respective Authorities. The Branches/Departments should ensure that proper acknowledgements of all the notices/attachment orders are retained in the respective loan files, as the same would be necessary to produce in the Court, in the event the Award is challenged. The branches shall follow up with the Employers and Bankers’ of the Judgement Debtors to ensure compliance of the attachment orders. The designated Recovery Officers shall maintain proper record of the attachment orders issued by them under the authorization U/s. 97 of Multi-State Co-op. Societies Act, 2002. The Branches shall submit monthly report by the 7th of the succeeding month to their concerned Zonal/Regional Office in respect of all the ARB loan accounts with them with a copy to the Recovery Dept. at Parel for review, in the proforma enclosed as Annexure-14.

 

The Branches are advised to take stock of the ARB loan files wherein Employers and Bankers’ of the Judgement Debtors have not complied with our earlier letters/notice and immediately arrange to serve the attachment notices to them under the signature of the above mentioned Designated Recovery Officers. The powers now conferred upon the above mentioned designated Recovery Officers for execution of Awards will serve as a strong tool for effective recovery in ARB accounts. Therefore, branches should ensure that persistent efforts are made for recovery of dues in the Decreed/Awarded accounts by attachment of salary, deposit accounts and properties of the Judgement Debtors, through the above mentioned designated Recovery Officers. All concerned are advised to take of the above instructions and ensure proper compliance.
