Circular CR No. 169 Dated 31st August 2016

Repair & Renovation Loans

-----------------------------------------

          Vide our Circular CR No. 75 dated 1.10.2012, the Bank had introduced Loan Scheme titled as Loans for Repair/Renovation of Flat/House/Shop & Gala etc.   We had stopped sanction of these loans from March 2015.  Again we had introduced House Repair/Renovation Loans to Housing Loan Loanee vide our Circular CR No. 149 dated 24.6.2015.

 

          Considering the focus this year on sanction of Retail Loans & Advances to remain competitive in the market, we have revised the above Schemes.  This Circular is issued in supersession of Circular CR No. 75  and 149.  The features of these schemes are as under:-

 

A]     Loans for Repairs/Renovation of Flats/House(other than Housing Loan Borrower), Shops & Galas etc.:- 

 

   i.      Constitution of the Borrower:-

Individuals, Proprietorship firm, Partnership Firm, Pvt./Ltd. Co. etc.

  ii.      Purpose:- For renovation of flats/house, shops, Galas, etc.

iii.      Amount of Loan: Maximum Rs. 10.00 Lakh

iv.        Rate of Interest:      PLR + 0.50 i.e.13.50% p.a. (floating) on daily reducing balance.

  v.      Margin:  NIL

 vi.      Service Charges: 0.70% of the sanctioned amount.

vii.      Repayment Period:- Upto 120 months.

viii.      Security:- Equitable mortgage of Flat/House/Shop/Gala etc. The following documents are to be obtained.

(a)Original Sale Deed/Agreement with Registration Certificate

(b)         Original Share Certificate, if issued.

(c)  NOC from the society/Builder 

 

        It has been decided not to insist for  chain of documents and other documents. If the chain of documents are produced, the same be obtained, verified and kept on record.  The mortgageability would be verified by the Mortgage Section of Legal Dept., H.O. on the basis of available documents and  above 3 documents only. 

 

        The visit by the officials to the place of residence/business is compulsory & verification is to be done by the said officials in respect of ownership of Flat/House/Shop/Gala etc. The visit and discussion with the Chairman/Secretary of the Society and main partner/Director/proprietor of Builder firm/company  is must  and the same should be recorded in Visit Report.

 

 ix.      Loan Eligibility:- Take home salary/income of Loanee:-

Salaried Persons/Businessmen:  Net Take Home income after EMI Rs. 10,000/- p.m.

The loan amount should not exceed 80% of the present Market Value of flat/house.

  x.      Surety: One surety with good means including family members.

 

 xi.      Share Amount:  1% of loan amount. Max. Rs.5,000/-.

xii.      End Use of Funds: Quotation-cum-Declaration as per Annexure-I.  

xiii.      Insurance of mortgaged property.

 

B]     House Renovation/Repairs Loans to Housing Loan Borrowers

 

1.  Eligible Loanee under House Repairs and Renovation Loan Scheme: A person (Applicant and Co-applicant of the Housing Loan) who has availed housing loan from our Bank and his/her Housing Loan is regular, would be eligible Loanee under the scheme.

 

2.  Purpose: Renovation /Repairs of flat/House/Extension of Row houses. (with Approval from CIDCO/ MHADA/Municipal Corporation/Gram Panchayat as the case may be for extension)

 

3.  Amount of Loan : Maximum Rs. 10.00 Lakh.

          Permissible Loan amount calculation would be as under:

The total of outstanding in Housing Loan a/c. and the amount of House Renovation & Repair Loan to be sanctioned should not exceed 80% of the market value of the flat/house OR as per Repayment capacity of the Loanee  whichever is lower.

 

4.  Eligible Loanees:  All home loan Loanees with a satisfactory repayment track record of at least for last 6 months (possession of the flat compulsory). In case of Resale, where actual repairs required for the flat, in such cases, the above condition of six months may be waived.

 

In case of takeover of Housing Loan from other Bank, in addition to Housing Loan,  we may consider House Repair loan to them upto Rs. 10.00 Lakh if date of availment of original housing loan is more than six months.

 

5.  Interest Rate:   Housing Loan Rate of Interest + 2.00%

 

6.  Service Charges:  0.70% of loan amount

 

7.  Repayment Period: Maximum 120 EMI  irrespective of the balance period of Housing Loan.  However, the repayment period be linked to the retirement age in case of salaried person.  In case of businessmen, the repayment period would be upto their 70 years of age subject to maximum 120 months (EMI). (IDC No. HO/MD-920/519/2015-16 dated 20.7.2015)

 

8.  Eligibility of the Loan:-

Salaried Persons/Businessmen      : Net Take Home income after EMI  Rs. 10,000/- p.m.

9.  Sureties: NIL

 

10.            Share Amount: 1% of the Loan amount. Max. Rs. 5,000/- in addition to existing shares of Housing Loan.

 

11.            End use of Funds:

i.     Undertaking to be obtained from the Loanee stating that the loan amount shall be utilized only for the purpose of renovation/repair/extension of houses/flat and that it will not be utilized for any speculative purpose or illegal purpose.

ii. Quotation cum Declaration to be obtained as per Annexure -I enclosed. 

 

12.            Security: Extension of existing mortgage of the property {Flat/ House} with appropriate stamp duty

(i) Original Title Deeds                                      

(ii) Original Mortgage Deed                                    of existing Housing Loan

 

(iii) Bank’s charge noted with Builders/Society.

 

13.            If any Loanee repays overdues just to avail this loan but is always irregular in repayment of EMI, then he/she is not eligible for this loan.

 

14.            Valuation of the flat/house should be done by the Manager/Incharge of branch only.  Visit to flat/house is must.  Visit Report should be prepared and kept on record.  It should be ensured that the Loanee is in possession of  the flat/house. 

 

15.            Interview of the Loanee should be taken and form duly filled in and signed by Manager should be kept in Loan file.

 

16.            Top up loan shall be opened under the Head of ESCLN category with a purpose of House Repair/ Renovation under the head of Business sub type in OMINI.

 

The above Loan Schemes are liberalized in all respect with many concessions.  Branches will find it easier to market the same and increase Loans & Advances.  The Incharge of branches are advised to study all salient features of above Schemes, hold staff meeting and inform all staff members in detail so that each staff member will be in a position to market loans and advances under above schemes.

 

 

 

P.R. Shetty

Managing Director
