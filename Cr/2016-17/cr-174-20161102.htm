Circular CR No. 174 dated 2nd November, 2016

Un-renewed/Un-reviewed Cash Credit/SODIMP Accounts

Permission to Allow Operations in Account beyond Grace Period

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

As per the Delegation of Powers, officers & executives are empowered to allow operations in un-renewed/un-reviewed CC/OD accounts beyond expiry date of respective limits (such extended period hereinafter being referred to as Grace Period) as follows:-

Delegated Powers

Asst. Gen. Manager/

Sr. Manager/ Manager

 

Allowing operations in an account which is regular

but renewal application not received.

Up to 1 month beyond expiry date of the relative limit

i. e. Grace Period of one month.

Allowing operations in an account which is regular

and renewal application received.

Up to 1.5 months beyond expiry date of the relative limit

 i. e. Grace Period of one and half months.

 

However, it has been noticed that some of the branches are allowing operations in un-renewed/un-reviewed CC/SODIMP accounts beyond the grace period of one month even when application for renewal is not received; and beyond the grace period of one & half months in case application for renewal is received. This is in violation of Head Office instructions and is viewed with concern.

It is expected that branches should initiate action inviting application for renewal/review, necessary financial papers and other information to facilitate renewal /review well in advance before expiry of the CC/SODIMP Limit through regular follow–up and visits. If an account is not renewed/reviewed before it’s due date, the matter is required to be escalated to higher authorities for seeking their instructions and for follow up at their end so as to ensure that the CC/SODIMP accounts are not downgraded to NPA category due to their un-renewed/un-reviewed status.

It has now been decided that the limits sanctioned at branch level will invariably be put up for approval to the Deputy General Manager at Zonal Office concerned (i.e. one stage above the sanctioning authority) with recommendations for continuation beyond the grace period. It respect of limits sanctioned by Deputy General Manager and other superior authorities, recommendations for their continuation beyond the grace period will be required to be submitted by the branches to the Zonal Office for approval at appropriate level. Approval for continuation beyond grace period in respect of such limits sanctioned by DGM & superior authorities will fall under the discretionary powers of the respective original sanctioning authority. It is being reiterated that a Branch-In-Charge has no authority to allow operations in Un-renewed/Un-reviewed CC/SODIMP accounts beyond the grace period regardless of the fact that the original limit was sanctioned at his/her level.

We are providing in the Annexure hereto a common format in which permission is to be obtained from respective authority in both the cases i. e. where the original sanction was at the branch level, and where the original sanction fell within the powers of the authority at Zonal Office & above, the latter including Deputy General Manager, General Manager (Credit), Managing Director or the Board.

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

 

 

P.R. Shetty

Managing Director
