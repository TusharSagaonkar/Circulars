Circular CR No. 179 Dated 4th January, 2017

“ABHYUDAYA VIDYA VARDHINI SCHEME”

New Education Loan Scheme

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

I.   We have currently two Education Loan Schemes as under:

1. Scheme for Education Loan for Higher Education in India & Abroad

i)   For studies in India – Maximum up to Rs. 10 lacs.

ii) For studies abroad – Maximum up to Rs. 20 lacs.

2. Scheme for Education Loan for Vocational Courses.

i)   For courses of duration up to 1 year - Rs. 50,000/-.

ii) For courses of duration above 1 year - Rs. 1,50,000/-.

 


Higher limits for vocational courses as under can be considered for specific courses offered by

reputed institutions having regard to the nature of such courses and employability etc.

a) Rs. 75,000/- for courses up to 1 year.

b) Rs. 2,00,000/- for courses above 1 year.

 

3. Extant instructions in respect of the two Education Loan Schemes as above are covered under Circular CR No. 164 dated 15th July, 2016.

 

4. The above-mentioned two loan schemes come under priority sector advances and are eligible for getting interest subsidy from Government of India.

 

II. While the two schemes as above continue, a need was felt for some time to introduce an Education Loan Scheme to cater to the needs of students aspiring to pursue higher studies involving larger outlay of funds. With this end in view, a new loan scheme by name “Abhyudaya Vidya Vardhini Scheme” has been approved by the board in the meeting dated 29.10.2016. The details of the scheme are enclosed in Annexure I hereto.

 

III.   The instant Circular has also another annexure i.e. Annexure II containing a Letter of Ratification when a student is initially not a borrower, being minor at the material time, but is required to be made a co-borrower upon attaining majority. This Annexure II hereto corresponds to Annexure-C of our Circular CR No. 164 dated 15th July, 2016 but contains some modifications. Branches shall obtain Letter of Ratification Annexure II (apart from obtaining Promissory Note and admitting him/her as a regular member) from a student, who was minor when loan was availed, to make him/her a co-borrower upon attaining majority, in all the cases i.e. whether in respect of loans granted under existing Education Loan Schemes in terms Circular CR No. 164 dated 15th July, 2016, or new Education Loan Scheme under the instant circular.

 

IV. Branches are advised to go through the details of the “Abhyudaya Vidya Vardhini Scheme” & bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all the employees concerned for successful implementation thereof.
