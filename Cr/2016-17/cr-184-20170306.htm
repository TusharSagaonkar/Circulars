Circular CR No. 184 Dated 6th March, 2017

 

Gold Loan & SOD against pledge of Gold Ornaments

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

I.   Please refer our Circular CR No. 177 dated 22nd December, 2016 wherein we have given eligibility of Loan/SOD amount for advance against pledge of gold ornaments as advised by RBI.

 

II. Gold ornaments/jewellery to be pledged with us as security/collateral are required to be valued at the average of the closing price of 22 carat gold for the preceding 30 days as quoted by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd. If the gold is of purity less than 22 carat, gold needs to be valued proportionately. However, branches should carefully note that if the gold is of purity higher than 22 carat, the eligibility of loan amount for such gold ornaments shall be equal to the eligibility of loan amount prescribed for 22 carat gold.                                                                         

 

III.  It has been witnessed that price of Gold in the bullion market is on the increase. The gold price as on date is Rs. 29,345/- per 10 gms for 24 Carat (999 purity) as per the website of Indian Bullion Jewellers Association Ltd.

 

IV.  In conformity with RBI instructions that Loan to Value Ratio (LTV) should not exceed 75 per cent for lending against gold jewellery (including bullet repayment loan against pledge of gold jewellery), it was proposed to revise the rates of Gold Loan. The proposal in this behalf was placed before Board Meeting held on 27.02.2017 and it was decided that branches shall sanction loans and SOD against gold ornaments as prescribed below w.e.f. 8th March, 2017.

V.    Whenever there are drastic changes in the price of gold, branches will be advised new eligible loan amount accordingly.

 

VI.  In terms of circular no RBI/2014-15/283 UBD.BPD.(PCB).Cir.No.25/ 13.05.001/2014-15 dated October 30, 2014, RBI has increased the quantum of loan that could be granted under the Bullet Repayment Scheme up to Rs. 2 Lakh. Branches are therefore advised as under:-

 

                 i.   The maximum Loan under Bullet Repayment Scheme would be Rs.2.00 Lakh for a period not exceeding 12 months.

 

                ii.   Loan and SOD against gold ornaments other than Bullet repayment scheme would also be for a period not exceeding 12 months.

 

               iii.   As the period of limit for SOD against gold ornaments would also be for 12 months, limits should be renewed with new documentation prescribing new date of expiry of the limit. Limits could be extended for another year on the basis of the application as per enclosed format. The SOD limit will be renewed subject to account being regular and business activity continuing.

 

              iv.   Renewal should generally be done one month before expiry of the limit and operations in the account should be allowed only for a maximum of 30 days from expiry of the limit. In case of non-renewal, notices for renewal should be sent. Account will be treated as NPA beyond 90 days’ overdue as per extant IRAC norms.

 

               v.   Maximum amount of gold loan and SOD against gold ornaments should not exceed Rs. 25.00 Lakh. Similarly, maximum amount of gold loan under Bullet Repayment scheme should not exceed Rs. 2.00 Lakh. (Ref: Annexure-26 “Gold Loan Scheme” on page 193 of Credit Policy – Credit Rules and Procedures). The In-charge of branches are advised to ensure that these limits are strictly adhered to and stipulations regarding (a) maximum period of 12 months, (b) renewal of SOD limit to be done before expiry of the limit and (3) further renewal for 12 months, should also be strictly adhered to.

 

              vi.   In terms of Circular CR No. 134 Dated 9th March 2015, all loanees availing Loan/SOD limit exceeding Rs. 1.00 Lakh should be made Regular Members of the Bank.

 

             vii.   All other instructions in regard to Gold Loan with Bullet Repayment, other Loan/SOD against Gold Ornaments issued from time to time remain unchanged.

 

 

VII.Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at your Branch/ Department and ensure compliance.
