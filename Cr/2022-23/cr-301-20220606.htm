Circular CR No. 301 dated 6th June 2022

1.  Legal Entity Identifier mandatory for credit exposures (Three levels of exposures-Staggered timelines)

2.  Branches to advise borrowers to obtain LEI and follow up with them for subsequent renewals

  3.  New credit exposures or renewals of existing ones, without LEI, not to be entertained after timelines

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

I.  Previous reference

Please refer to the Circular OC No. 845 dated 17th February, 2021 on the subject of Legal Entity Identifier being made mandatory for parties initiating NEFT/RTGS transactions above Rs.50 Crores.

 

II. LEI for credit facilities

1.     For commercial banks etc.

RBI had, in its another circular in the year 2017 issued instructions for some banks and financial institutions (but not UCBs) to ensure that the parties having credit exposures above certain thresholds including and above Rs. 5 Crore must take Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) code for themselves, and the timelines were staggered for different thresholds in the order of the highest threshold becoming applicable at the earliest, and so on.

 

2.  Extension of LEI for credit facilities by UCBs

i.     Now, RBI, in terms of the circular no. RBI/2022-23/34 DOR.CRE.REC.28/ 21.04.048/2022-23 dated 21st April 2022, has instructed most banks, including UCBs, and Financial Institutions that all the parties with credit exposures above three different thresholds must obtain a Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) code. The timelines for the different thresholds are staggered so that LEI will apply first to the highest threshold and so on. The thresholds and the relative timelines are given in Para No. V below.

ii.   It is incumbent upon our Bank that our existing borrowers with credit facilities above the given threshold levels obtain LEI codes within the respective timelines.

iii.  It is also incumbent upon us to ensure that any party seeking credit facilities from us so as to take their total exposure from the banking and financial system above a threshold limit obtains the LEI code before the relative timeline so that the credit proposal can be entertained.

 

III. Reiteration of the concept and implementation of LEI

1.     LEI is a key measure to improve the quality and accuracy of financial data systems for better risk management post the Global Financial Crisis.

2.     It is a 20-character unique identity code assigned to the entities which are parties to a financial transaction.

3.     Globally, the use of LEI has expanded beyond derivative reporting and it is being used in areas relating to banking, securities market, credit rating, market supervision, etc.

4.     In terms of RBI notification No. RBI/2016-17/314 dated 1st June 2017, it was decided to implement the LEI system for all participants in the OTC (Over the Counter) markets for Rupee Interest Derivatives, Foreign Currency Derivatives and Credit derivatives in a phased manner.

5.   Our Bank also applied and obtained for itself an LEI code which is 335800KG5M297IU4T791.

6.     Previous instructions to us in respect of NEFT/RTGS transactions, and those to other banks in respect of credit facilities, have now been extended to us, being a UCB, in respect of credit facilities.

7.     Entities can obtain LEI from any of the Local Operating Units (LOUs) accredited by the Global Legal Entity Identifier Foundation (GLEIF) – the entity tasked to support the implementation and use of LEI. In India, the LEI code may be obtained from Legal Entity Identifier India Ltd (LEIIL), a subsidiary of the Clearing Corporation of India Limited (CCIL), which has been recognised by the Reserve Bank as an issuer of LEI under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 and is accredited by the GLEIF as the Local Operating Unit (LOU) in India for issuance and management of LEI.

8.     After obtaining the LEI code, banks shall also ensure that borrowers renew the codes as per GLEIF guidelines.

9.     The rules, procedures and requirements regarding documentation are available at the hyperlink  LEIIL provided in circular no. RBI/2017-18/82 DBR.No.BP.BC.92/ 21.04.048/2017-18 2nd November 2017 on the subject of “Introduction of Legal Entity Identifier for large corporate borrowers” issued by RBI. In the alternative, the website https://www.ccilindia-lei.co.in may also be visited for the purpose.

 

IV. Definition of Exposure

For the purpose of mandatory applicability of LEI, exposure is defined by RBI to include all fund based and non-fund based (credit as well as investment) exposure of banks/FIs to the borrower. Aggregate sanctioned limit or outstanding balance, whichever is higher, shall be reckoned for the purpose. Branches and departments/offices handling credit may ascertain the position of aggregate exposure based on information available either with them or the CRILC database or declaration obtained from the borrower.

V.    Thresholds and timelines for adherence with LEI

VI. Role of I.T. Dept./Development Department/Branches

1.     I. T. Department will provide a field in the CBS for registering LEI code in the customer master of each borrower and shall provide for its mandatory insertion on or after the relative timeline.

2.     Development department shall provide boxes in the standard application form for loans as also in the form of scrutiny notes to record the LEI.

3.     i. Branches will immediately address a letter as per the Annexure hereto to all

 the borrowing units having existing credit exposure of Rs.5 Crore and above with banking and NBFCs to proactively obtain an LEI code.

 ii. Branches will further monitor the obtaining of LEI codes by units coming or likely to come under the purview of LEI at least three months before the actual date on which the LEI’s applicability for them will start.

4.     Zonal office to ensure that branches falling under their Zone comply with the thresholds and timeline for adherence with LEI of all borrowing units falling under the mentioned thresholds.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned and ensure compliance.
