Circular CR No. 312 dated 20th February 2023

1.     Introduction of e-registered Agreements between Builders and First Purchasers / Allottees in Maharashtra

2.     Implications for Mortgage by Deposit of Title Deeds (Equitable Mortgage)

3.     Registered Simple Mortgage now made compulsory by our Bank to charge such properties

---------------------------------------------------------------------------   

I.   Extant instructions

1.   In terms of the extant Credit Policy, there are instructions to obtain a Mortgage by Deposit of Title Deeds (colloquially referred to as “Equitable Mortgage”) in the states of Maharashtra and Karnataka as primary or collateral security of land/building/ house/flat/gala etc. for credit facilities up to Rs. 500.00 Lakh with the exception of cases of Take Over from other banks/financial institutions/ Grampanchayat properties (only Simple Registered Mortgage being obtained in the cases of exceptions). Further, in those cases where the original duly stamped and registered title deeds are not available or there are shortcomings in the set of title deeds, Simple Registered Mortgage is required to be obtained.

2.   In the state of Gujarat, however, Simple Registered Mortgage is required to be obtained regardless of the amount of the credit facility.

 

II.   E-registered Agreements between Builders and First Purchasers/ Allottees in Maharashtra

1.   With a view to augmenting the e-registration of documents, the Office of the Chief Inspector of Registration and Stamp Collector has issued guidelines vide resolutions dated 27th April 2020 and 14th October 2021 laying down steps to facilitate easy e-registration of documents by allowing builders /developers to register documents from their own office subject to a set of terms and conditions.

2.   It is clarified that such e-registration is possible only in respect of the primary sale by the builder/developer to the prospective purchaser (“the First Sale”).

3.   A consequence of such e-registration is that there is no singular or original “Agreement of Sale”. The system enables the generation of any number of copies of e-registered agreements. This state of affairs is fraught with risks for Mortgage by Deposit of Title Deeds (“Equitable Mortgage”) as creation of multiple mortgages on the basis of different copies becomes possible in respect of the same property.

4.   Therefore, it is being instructed that only a Simple Registered Mortgage shall hereafter be obtained in respect of properties offered as security if they are acquired under First Sale by a builder/developer with the registration of such “First Sale” having been e-registered.

5.   Before getting such Simple Registered Mortgage executed, following Documents are required to be obtained:

                 i)      A certified copy of the Agreement with Index-II from the concerned Sub Registrar of Assurances.

               ii)       The Title & Search Report by an empanelled advocate pertaining to the property to be mortgaged.

             iii)      A charge release letter/No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Builder’s Bank.

              iv)      Other documents shall be obtained as per the Mortgageability Note prepared by the Legal Managers of the concerned Zonal Offices in case of properties situated in Mumbai & Navi Mumbai region and as per Title & Search Report prepared by the empanelled Advocates in case of properties situated outside Mumbai & Navi Mumbai. 

                v)      The official designated by the branch concerned for the site inspection of the property to be mortgaged, shall, during the said inspection,  inquire and satisfy himself/herself about the eligibility of the Builder/Developer for availing e-registration facility with the fulfilment of following two conditions on the date of e-registration: 

a.   The Builder/Developer should have a live and subsisting registration in respect of the project with the Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority.(RERA)

b.   There should be at least 50 Agreements of Sale remaining to be   executed and registered in respect of units in the project.

              vi)     Before executing the mortgage and release of the credit facility, the branch concerned shall ensure with CERSAI that no previous charge exists on the property to be mortgaged (be it of any bank, financial institution or any other entity). 

            vii)      It is to be noted that such security created through e-registration shall only be accepted in case of Housing Loan facility.

 

III.   Please bring the contents of the instant circular to the notice of the members of the staff and ensure strict compliance.
