Circular CR No. 343 dated 26th June 2025

Revised guidelines on Priority Sector Lending (PSL)

Classification of Various Credit Facilities under Priority Sector

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

I.     Please refer Circular CR No. 261 dated 3rd November, 2020 on the subject of guidelines on & classification of various credit facilities under Priority Sector.

 

II. 1. RBI has come out with comprehensive and updated instructions together with guidelines on Priority Sector Lending (PSL) for all the banks, including Primary(Urban) Co-operative Banks (UCBs), vide its communication No.RBI/FIDD/2024-25/128-Master DirectionsFIDD.CO.PSD.BC.13/04.09.001/2024-25dated 24thMarch, 2025 effective from 01st April, 2025. The Master Direction provides differential instructions for different sectors of banking industry. The instructions applicable to our bank (UCB sector) are given hereunder with updates and operational guidelines.

 

2. Instructions contained herein supersede all the previous instructions on the subject.

 

3. Lending previously classified under PSL but not in conformity with the revised & updated instructions and guidelines shall continue to be classified under PSL unless closed or renewed.

Presently our bank is financing for agricultural infrastructure including construction of cold storage for storage of agricultural products. The same can be categorized under priority sector of Agriculture head.

3.    Export Credit: - Export Credit under agriculture and MSME sectors are allowed to be classified as Priority Sector Lending in the respective categories viz. Agriculture and MSME.

Export Credit (other than that classified under agriculture and MSME) will be allowed to classified as priority sector on the basis of incremental export credit to borrowers over corresponding date of preceding year, subject to a maximum of Rs. 50.00 crore per borrower. There is a ceiling of 2% of ANBC or CEOBSE whichever is higher, which will be monitored at the level of Forex Department.

 

4.    Education:-All loans sanctioned to individuals for educational purposes, including those for vocational courses, not exceeding Rs.25.00 lakh will be considered as eligible for priority sector classification.

6.    Social infrastructure

i.   Bank loans up to a limit of Rs. 8.00crore per borrower for setting up schools, drinking water facilities and sanitation facilities including construction/ refurbishment of household toilets and water improvement at household level, etc.

 

ii.    Bank loans up to a limit of Rs. 12.00 Crore per borrower for building health care facilities. In case of UCBs, the above limits are applicable only in centers having a population of less than one lakh. We will not grant loans under this category as none of the centers where we are established, conform to the stipulation of a population of less than one lakh.

 

7.    Renewable Energy

Loans up to a limit of Rs.35.00 crore per borrower for purposes renewable energy-based power generators like solar based power generators, biomass-based power generators, wind mills, microhydel plants and for renewable energy based public utilities (non-conventional energy based public utilities), viz., street lighting systems and remote village electrification etc., are eligible for Priority Sector classification. For individual households, the loan limit will be Rs.10.00 lakh per borrower.

 

8.    Others Categories of Loans Coming Under Priority Sector

Following loans as per the prescribed limits are eligible for priority sector classification:

i.      Loans not exceeding Rs.1.00 Lakh per borrower provided directly to individuals and individual members of SHG/JLG, provided the individual borrower’s household annual income in rural areas does not exceed Rs.1.00 lakh and for non-rural areas, it does not exceed Rs.1.60 Lakh. (It is related to individual members of SHG/ JLG).

ii.    Loans not exceeding Rs. 2.00 Lakh provided directly by banks to Self Help Group (SHG)/ Joint Liability Group (JLG) for activities other than agriculture or MSME, viz., loans for meeting social needs, construction or repair of house, construction of toilets or any viable common activity started by the SHGs. (It is related to SHG/ JLG).

iii.  Loans to distressed persons (other than distressed farmers indebted to non-institutional lenders) not exceeding Rs.1.00 lakh per borrower to prepay their debt to non-institutional lenders.

iv.  Loans sanctioned to State Sponsored Organizations for Scheduled Castes/ Scheduled Tribes for the specific purpose of purchase and supply of inputs and/or the marketing of the outputs of the beneficiaries of these organizations.

v.    Loans up to Rs.50.00 Crore to Start-ups, as per definition of Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Govt. of India that are engaged in activities other than Agriculture or MSME.

 

V.   WEAKER SECTIONS

A-The list of categories of borrowers, loans are eligible for classification under the sub-sector of Weaker Sections, as under.

 

i. Small and Marginal Farmers. (Marginal farmers are those who cultivated upto 1 hectare (2.50 acres) of land, while small farmers cultivate more than 1 hectare and upto 2 .        hectars of land).

ii. Artisans, village and cottage industries where individual credit limits do not exceed Rs.2.00 Lakh.

iii. Beneficiaries under Government Sponsored Schemes such as National Rural Livelihood Mission (NRLM), National Urban Livelihood Mission (NULM) and Self Employment Scheme for Rehabilitation of Manual Scavengers (SRMS).

iv. Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.

v. Beneficiaries of Differential Rate of Interest (DRI) scheme.

vi. Self Help Groups/Joint Liability Groups.

vii. Individuals and individual members of SHGs/JLGs, satisfying criteria as prescribed in Master Direction on Regulatory Framework for Microfinance Loans Directions,

dated March 14,2022.

viii. Individual Women Beneficiaries.

ix. Distressed farmers indebted to non-institutional lenders.

x. Distressed persons other than farmers, with loan amount not exceeding Rs.1.00 lakh per borrower to prepay their debt to non-institutional lenders.

xi. Persons with disabilities.

xii. Transgender

xiii. Minority communities as may be notified by Government of India from time to time.

 

B. Overdraft availed by PMJDY account holders as per limits and conditions prescribed by

 Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance from time to time may be

 classified under loans to Weaker Sections.

 

 

Note:-

a.    Advances extended to Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes and Minority communities are classified under Weaker Section. However, advances granted to OBC and NT should not be classified under Weaker Section.

 

b.   In the case of a partnership firm, if the majority of the partners, to one or the other of the specified minority communities, advances granted to such partnership firms may be treated as advances granted to minority communities. Further, if the majority beneficial ownership in a partnership firm belongs to the minority community then such lending can be classified as advances to the specified communities.

      A company has a separate legal entity and hence advances granted to it cannot be classified as advances to the specified minority communities.

 

VI.         Other guidelines for priority sector loans as prescribed by RBI

Branches must comply with the following common guidelines for all categories of advances under the priority sector.

 

1. Service charges

No loan-related and ad hoc service charges/inspection charges should be levied on priority sector loans up to Rs. 50,000/-. In the case of eligible priority sector loans to SHGs/ JLGs, this limit will be applicable per member and not to the group as a whole.

 

2. Receipt, Sanction/Rejection/Disbursement Register

A register/electronic record should be maintained by branches wherein the date of receipt, sanction/rejection/disbursement with reasons thereof, etc. should be recorded. The register/electronic record should be made available to all inspecting agencies.

 

3. Issue of acknowledgement of loan applications

    Branches should provide acknowledgement for loan applications received under priority sector loans. Time limit prescribed under the extant Credit Policy for communicating decision in writing to the applicants must be adhered to.

 

 

VII. INTEREST RATES

There is a likelihood that some loans & advances under Non-priority Sector will come under Priority Sector with the changes in classification of advances under priority sector. In such circumstances, the current rates of interest applicable to them will continue until the fresh gradation if credit rating is applicable or till the submitting copy of UDYAM Registration Certificate.

 

VIII.  IMPORTANCE OF ACHIEVING TARGETS UNDER PRIORITY SECTOR

Importance of achieving goals and sub-goals under priority sector lending has been emphasized repeatedly through various circulars and other communications on the subject. As per the revised RBI guidelines dated 24th March 2025, the Priority Sector target is fixed at 60 % of the ANBC or CEOBE whichever higher, importance of achieving the goals & sub-goals have increased manifold. Such achievement is monitored by RBI on the basis of our quarterly returns and is taken into account while granting regulatory clearances/approvals for various purposes.

With our vast base of customers reach, and the network of branches, it should not be difficult to achieve the target within the prescribed timelines without compromising on quality of lending. Branches, Cluster and Zonal Offices should take initiative in this direction.

 

IX.     Instructions to IT department

During disbursement of Credit facility (ies) which is categorized as under Priority Sector in omni system, then IT dept. should restrict that concerned staff should feed data related to “Investment in Plant & Machinery/ Equipment (WDV) and business turnover” .

 

 

X.       Instructions to branches

Branches are advised to periodically update details of investment in Plant& machinery/equipment (WDV) and turnover in all loan accounts of the customer except personal loan accounts, linked to the same PAN. These updations should be carried out yearly on the basis of latest audited financial statements or “UDYAM Registration Certificate” of the borrower. They are further advised to ensure correctness of the “Micro/ Small/ Medium” Enterprise and Priority/Non-priority classification entered in OMNI in all business loan accounts of the customer linked to the same PAN.

 

XI.     Instructions to MIS department

1. MIS dept will provide necessary data to branches for making required changes in Omni.

2. It will monitor & report on achievement of targets of Priority Sector Lending on ‘Quarterly’ basis to Board/ Administrator committee.

3. It will furnish the data (on priority sector lending in the reporting formats ‘Statement I’ and ‘Statement II (Part A to D)’ at quarterly and annual intervals, to the Regional Office of DoS, RBI.

 

Please take suitable note of the revised instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure strict compliance.
