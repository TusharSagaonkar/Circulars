Circular CR No. 354 dated 9th October, 2025

 

Introduction of RakshaKavach Loan for New & Existing Housing Loan Borrowers through SBI Life Insurance Company Ltd.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

It has been decided to offer “RakshaKavach” Loan for securing life of new& existing Housing Loan Borrowers through “Family Shield RiNnRaksha”, a Group Credit Life Insurance Plan from SBI Life Insurance Co. Ltd.

The main objective of this product is to provide financial security to the borrowers as well as risk mitigation for our Bank. In the event of the unfortunate death of the borrower/s (Life Insured) during the tenure of loan, the outstanding loan amount will be repaid directly to the Bank by the Insurance Company. This ensures that the borrower’s family is not burdened with repayment of the loan in absence of borrower/s.

 

Key Benefits:

·      Covers loan liability in case of death of borrower/s (Life Insured).

·      One-time premium payment, with an option to finance premium amount along with the Housing Loan amount by the Bank.

·      Provides peace of mind to the borrower/s and their family, as the loan liability does not become a financial burden.

SBI “Family Shield RiNnRaksha” plan thus acts as a strong financial shield, ensuring protection & security for borrowers & their loved ones.

 

Tie-upArrangement:

This cover will be offered to customers / Borrowers of our Bank through SBI Life Insurance Company Ltd. For this purpose, the Bank has completed the formalities of creating a Group Master Policy with the Insurance Company.The sum assured under the policy will be to the tune of outstanding loan amount of Primary Housing Loan Account as per ideal repayment schedule.

Implementation Procedure:

At the time of Loan Application / Sanction / Documentation or even after disbursement (before 180 days of first disbursement), the borrower/s shall be informed about the benefits and cost of the RakshaKavach Loan introduced by our Bank.

Upon obtaining the consent of borrowers & sureties and fulfilling the eligibility criteria of Insurance Company, the Bank will also sanction the separate loan for Insurance Premium amount along with the primary Housing Loan amount, subject to repayment capacity of Borrower/s. In such case, two separate Loan Accounts as below will be opened and disbursed:

1. Housing Loan (HSGLN)

2. Insurance Premium Loan Account (RAKSHALN). IT Department will enable this product (Loan type) in Omni system.

Rate of Interest, tenure and all other terms & conditions for RakshaKavach Loan (RAKSHALN) will be same as that of primary Housing Loan Account and said loan is subject to IRAC norms/guidelines issued by Reserve Bank of India.

The Insurance Policy will cover the Bank as the beneficiary to the extent of the outstanding loan amount as per ideal repayment schedule, i.e. default loan amount over & above Ideal Drawing Power will not be covered under said policy. In case of a claim death of the life insured, the Insurance Company will directly pay the claim amount as per ideal repayment schedule to the Bank towards loan account/s.

·           In case the claim amount is more than outstanding amount of Loan/s, the remaining amount will be settled with the Nominee.

·           However, in case the claim amount is lower than outstanding amount of Loan/s, the remaining dues of the Bank will be recovered as per laid down procedure of the Bank.

·           The claim amount received will be appropriated towards RakshaKavach Loan Account first and then towards primary Housing Loan Account.

Eligibility Criteria:

·        Minimum age of customer for policy is 18 years. Maximum age of policy holder for entry is 55 years with maturity age limit of 70 years.

·        Eligible & wiling Customer/s must apply for insurance cover before disbursement of loan (new customer) or within 180 days of first disbursement of loan (existing borrower/s).

·        It may be possible that one or more customers/borrowers are not eligible for said insurance cover due to eligibility criteria set by Insurance Company. In such case, claim shall be initiated only on death of insured customer / borrower.

·        In case more than one customers / borrowers are eligible for insurance cover and are ready to avail benefit under RakshaKavach, the premium amount for insurance will be calculated on the basis of age of all such eligible customers / borrowers opting for life cover under RakshaKavachscheme.

·        The premium calculator for RakshaKavachis made available on the Exchange Server in Public Folder of Insurance Department. This calculator will provide indicative premium rates applicable to customers based on their age. The final premium, however, may vary in case medical examination is required. Instructions for branches:

1.   Application form for RakshaKavach Loans should be printed from the SBI Life Insurance website:https://www.sbilife.co.in. A sample form is enclosed for reference as Annexure –A.

2.   The duly filled and signed form by the Applicant/s or Borrowers & Guarantor/s must be submitted to the field staff of SBI Life for further processing. Copy of the Insurance Policy should be kept in loan file of RakshaKavach Loan.

3.   We are enclosing proforma of request letter/s, Agreement, Declaration and Sanction Letter for Loan to be executed for RakshaKavach Loan as below :

Annexure – B :   Request letter to be obtained from Applicant/s & Guarantors/s for new Housing Loan.

Annexure – C :       Request letter to be obtained from Borrower/s & Guarantors/s for existing Housing Loan.

Annexure – D :       Agreement for Loan to be executed by Borrower/s and Guarantors for availing loan for insurance premium amount. Said agreement attracts stamp duty as per applicable stamp duty rate for Agreement for Loan in respective states.

Annexure – E :       Declaration to be executed by Borrowers/Mortgagors to secure loan sanctioned under RakshaKavach Loan.

Annexure – F:       Sanction Letter to be issued for RakshaKavach Loan in addition to sanction letter for Primary Housing Loan.

4.   Branch is required to get executed separate security documents such as Sanction Letter (Annexure-F), Agreement for Loan (Annexure-D), Demand Promissory Note (30), Declaration (Annexure-E) for existing Borrowers, Undertaking for Loan (79), Salary Deduction Letter (335/336), Declaration for Signature/s in Vernacular (586), Letter of Acceptance of Terms & Conditions (590), etc.

 

Execution of documents & treatment of RakshaKavach Loan:

·        As stated above, loan sanctioned under RakshaKavach scheme is subject to terms & conditions applicable to primary Housing Loan account and IRAC norms / guidelines issued by Reserve Bank of India.

·        For new customers, loan sanctioned under RakshaKavach scheme must be secured by way of mortgage of immovable property alongwith primary Housing Loan.

·        For existing customers, duly stamped & notarized declaration is required to be obtained from Borrowers & Mortgagors for securing debt.

·        Branch is required to create/modify charge on immovable property with CERSAI invariably, as the case may be.

 

Responsibility of Branches

·        Educate and counsel all housing loan borrowers regarding the benefits of insurance cover.

·        Ensure prompt remittance of premium to SBI Life after sanction.

·        Maintain records of all applications, sanction letters and declarations.

 

Claim Process

 

The following procedure shall be followed for lodging and settlement of claims:

 

1.  Intimation of Claim

The nominee / legal heir / branch must intimate the claim to the nearest branch of the Bank or to the SBI Life Insurance Company Ltd.

2.  Submission of Documents

The claimant / nominee must submit following documents along with claim form (duly filled):

·      Original / certified copy of Death Certificate issued by the local authority.

·      Identity & Address Proof of nominee / claimant.

·      Post Mortem Report / Police Report (in case of accidental death, if applicable).

·      Any additional documents as required by the Insurance Company.

 

3.  Verification by Branch

The Bank branch will verify:

·      Loan account details.

·      Insurance coverage validity at the time of death / event.

·      Claim will be made for the outstanding Housing Loan amount + outstanding RakshaLN A/c.

·      Nominee details as per Bank / Insurance records.

·      After due verification, the branch will forward the complete claim documents to the Insurance Company through the field staff assigned by SBILI to the branch.

4.  Processing by Insurance Company

·      The Insurance Company will scrutinize the claim papers.

·      Additional requirements, if any, will be communicated to the claimant / Bank branch.

·      Upon satisfactory completion, the Insurance Company will process and approve the   claim.

5.  Settlement of Claim

·      The claim proceeds will be directly adjusted towards the outstanding loan liability of the insured borrower.

·      If the claim amount is higher than the outstanding loan amount, the balance will be paid to the nominee / legal heir as per policy terms.
