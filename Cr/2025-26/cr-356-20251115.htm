Circular CR No. 356 dated 15th November 2025

 

Revised Policy on Quick Mortality Accounts and Fixing of Staff Accountability

 

Refer to our Circular No. CR No. 60 dated 12-01-2012 and CR No. 339 dated 04-12-2024 wherein we have issued detailed guidelines and instructions regarding segregation of Quick Mortality Accounts and also fixing of Official / Staff accountability on account of lapses in pre-sanction and post sanction follow up of Loans and Advances and placing the report to Administrator Committee. In the Administrator’s Committee Meeting held on 09-09-2025 Committee suggested some modification in the policy which are covered in point No. 3

The account is categorized as Quick Mortality Account (QM) if-

i) For term loan type account

 

Loan turns into NPA within one year from the date of disbursement of loan/1 part disbursed loan amount (in-case where loan is disbursed in stage-wise payment), it is considered as 'Quick Mortality Account".

 

E.g. :- Loan disbursed/ first part disbursed amount on 1 April, 2024, it qualifies as a Quick Mortality Account if it turns into NPA on or before 31 March, 2025.

 

ii) For limit type account

 

The limit account turns into NPA within 12 months from the date of disbursement of limit, it falls under the category of "Quick Mortality Account".

 

E.g.:- CC limit disbursed on 1 April, 2024 it qualifies as a Quick Mortality Account if it turns into NPA on or before 31st March, 2025.

 

iii) For loans, where moratorium period is given -

 

In case of Loan, where moratorium period is given, if such loan turns into NPA within one year from the date of completion of moratorium period, it is considered as 'Quick Mortality Account".

 

E.g.:- Loan disbursed on 1st April, 2024, moratorium period given is three months i.e. upto 31st July, 2024. It qualifies as a Quick Mortality Account if it turns into NPA on or before 31st July, 2025.

 

iv) For case of existing borrower

 

Newly sanctioned loan/ limit type accounts is classified as NPA due to earlier availed credit facility(les) becoming NPA, such newly sanctioned loan/limit type accounts shall not fall under category as Quick Mortality Account if such account(s) is regular is not out of order.

Also where credit is availed in form of limit, such accounts where renewal /review is done cannot be treated under Quick Mortality Account within period of one year from renewal date.

 

v) Bullet Gold Loans, SOD against NSC/LIC, FLXLN, TD-LOAN are to be excluded from Quick Mortality.

 

2) On the basis of aforesaid category, accounts which fall under Quick Mortality Accounts, MIS dept. will provide such account data to Inspection Dept. Inspection dept.after making thorough study of these account(s) will categorize the accounts as under:

 

A. The Loans & Advances accounts which have become Quick Mortality accounts due to the reason(s) which are beyond the control of borrowers such as death of borrower, loss of employment, loss in the business due to uncontrollable reason, change in Govt. Policy, natural calamities, political and/or social disturbances or any other reason.

 

B. The Loans & advances which have become Quick Mortality accounts where proposals have been sanctioned as per rules and regulations and compliances of post sanction terms.

 

C. The Loans & Advances Accounts which have Quick Mortality accounts where verification of genuineness of the facts/papers/ documents was beyond the reach/control of the sanctioning authorities, although the loans & advances were sanctioned as per rules and regulations and proper follow-up was made after disbursement.

 

D. The loan & Advances accounts which have been turned into NPAs within 12 months (as defined above) of their disbursements due to below mentioned reasons:-

 

i) Laxity/negligence in pre-sanction scrutiny, wrong/false/ untrue reporting and laxity/negligence in verification of movable/immovable properties/assets or providing untrue facts, figures, and concealing the information either intentionally or unintentionally.

 

ii) Preparation of appraisal in a wrong way and not in accordance with the credit policy framed by the Bank, deviations from the norms.

 

iii) Non-compliance/laxity in complying with the stipulations laid down at the time of sanctioning the loan & advances.

 

iv) Laxity/Negligence in post disbursement follow-up. Follow-up is not in accordance with the guidelines given in our Credit Policy and various Circular instructions given from time to time and not reporting or reporting wrongly to higher authorities in regard to irregularity of these NPA accounts.

 

v) Acts/Actions facilitating the undeserving borrower to avail loan from the Bank.

 

vi) Any other act on the part of our staff, with guile either directly or indirectly.

 

While dealing with these cases the Inspection Dept. will make thorough scrutiny of the proposals in respect of pre-sanction and post-sanction follow-up with discrete enquiries/detailed discussion with the concerned recommending as well as sanctioning authorities and will verify the facts as per extant instructions.

 

Based on the factual findings, the Inspection Dept. will prepare the branch-wise reports for all cases as A, B, C, D or jointly with another category(ies).

 

3) As discussed in the Administrator’s Committee Meeting, henceforth after thorough scrutiny, the report of Quick Mortality to be forwarded to Vigilance Department for closure / ascertaining of staff accountability within One month from the date of receipt of Quick Mortality list from MIS Department. On receipt of Q.M. reports from inspection department the Vigilance department will confirm that account(s) have been correctly classified with details reasoning under various categories. The Vigilance Department may differ with the classification made by inspection department by substantiating the reason thereof. Vigilance Department will make through study of each case and will discuss with respective sanctioning authority and where necessary / warranted, they will call for clarification, explanation from concerned recommended and sanctioning authorities who are responsible for pre sanction lapses as well as the officials / staff who are responsible for post disbursement follow up action for these accounts. Closure of the Quick Mortality reports / ascertaining of staff accountability should be completed within One month from the receipt of Q.M. report from inspection department. In case of fixing staff accountability, the matter will be routed through HRM dept., for fixing of staff accountability. For fixing of staff accountability, HRM dept will represent detail report for action to be taken against concerned staff, before the Administrator’s committee Meeting.

 

Vigilance Department will compile the data of closure / ascertaining of staff accountability from concerned authorities and will represent details report of Quick Mortality accounts along with the action taken will be put before Administrator’s Committee Meeting on Quarterly basis.

 

4) Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at your Branch / Department and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

Chief Executive Officer
