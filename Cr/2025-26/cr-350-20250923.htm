Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for Gold Loans/ Limits

Gold loans have emerged as a popular financial product in our country, allowing individuals to unlock the value of their gold jewellery or ornaments to meet their immediate financial needs. This security based approach allows lenders to offer loans with minimal risk, as the value of gold serves as security against the loan amount.

 

Pre-sanction & Disbursement Stage:-

1)  Eligibility :-

Borrower should be customer of the bank and the KYC documentation has to be updated. The operation in the account should be satisfactory. One has to check that KYC documents is complied with latest guidelines before proceeding further. No need of taking KYC documents only latest photo to be obtained.

The said scheme is for individual’s and proprietorship firms.

Borrower (individual person) should be above 18 years. For illiterate prospective borrower, important terms and conditions shall be explained in their understandable language in presence of witness, who shall not be employee of the bank.

 

Loan cannot be extended to other lenders, entities or individuals by accepting gold pledged to such lenders, entities or individuals by their borrowers as security.

 

2)  Income Proof :-

Declaration of income from the applicant for the facility upto Rs.2.50 lakh to be obtained, where income proof is not available. For facility above Rs.2.50 lakh, ITR/ Income proof with details to be obtained to access repayment capacity.

 

Detailed credit assessment including assessment of borrower’s repayment capacity shall be undertaken in case the total of loan amount against gold ornaments is above Rs.2.50 lakh.

 

3)  Purpose

Gold loan/overdraft for income generating /consumption purpose only.

 

Definition

“Income Generating Loan” means loans extended for the purpose of productive economic activities such as farm credit, loans for business or commercial purposes, loans for creation or acquisition of productive assets etc.

 

 “Consumption Loan” means any permissible loan that does not fit the definition of income generating loans .

 

4)  Membership

For loan/ limit up to Rs.1.00 lakh, customer to be admitted as Nominal member of the bank. Branch has to check existing consumer loan outstanding of the borrower and in case consumer loan outstanding amount and proposed gold loan is more than

Rs. 1.00 Lakh, then in such cases borrower has to become regular member of the bank with minimum required number of shares.

For loan/ limit above Rs.1.00 lakh, he/ she have to be admitted as regular member of the bank and have to maintain share linkage @2.5% of the loan amount/ limit.

In case, he/she is already regular member then branch has to check that he/ she should be holding minimum required number of bank shares, otherwise he has apply for the difference of minimum required shares. Overall in case loan/ limit of above Rs.1.00 lakh, then borrower has to maintain share linkage on total loan exposure, linkage @2.5% on secured loan and @5.00% on unsecured loans.

5)  Loan/Limit Amount :-

As required in terms of the extant RBI directions, shall include, inter alia, appropriate single borrower limits and aggregate limits for the portfolio of loans against eligible as gold ornaments.

    i.      Bullet type facility:- Minimum loan amount is Rs.10,000/- and Maximum loan amount is Rs.2,50,000/-.

   ii.        EMI type facility:- Minimum loan amount is Rs.10,000/- and Maximum loan amount is Rs.10,00,000/-.

  iii.        Overdraft limit type facility:- Minimum loan amount is Rs.25,000/- and Maximum loan amount is Rs.10,00,000/-.

Note :- i) No fresh/ additional loan will be given in order to clear overdue of existing loan.

ii) Bullet loan/Overdraft limit can be renew with existing loan/ limit account only after payment of accrued interest if any.

 

In bullet type facility, repayment of principal and interest are due for repayment at the maturity of the loan. In overdraft limit, interest has to serve on monthly basis.

In case of multiple loan(s)/ limit(s) to be avail by the borrower, overall exposure of such borrower should not cross Rs.10,00,000/-.

Running multiple loans simultaneously to a single borrower or a group of related borrowers may be prone to misuse and susceptible to fraud. Consequently, such practices shall be subject to stricter internal audit and supervisory examination.

6) Rate of Interest :-(as revise time to time)

For bullet loan and EMI type       :- 9.50% p.a. (PLR – 2.75%)

For Overdraft limit             :- 9.70 % p.a. (PLR – 2.55 %)

 

7)  Repayment Period :-

Maximum repayment period is 12 months.

     i.        For bullet type facility Principal and interest amount can be repaid at end of one year.

   ii.        For EMI type facility EMI will be paid on monthly basis for a year.

  iii.        For Overdraft facility as operating a/c where at least Interest will be paid on monthly basis.

 

8)   Security

Loan/ Limit is sanctioned against pledge of gold ornaments only, it cannot be sanctioned against gold biscuit/ gold bar etc.

Self-declaration to be obtained from the borrower that the gold ornaments belong to him/ her and her family member. In case of one of the gold ornament is in the form of Mangalsutra and borrower is male then NOC from his spouse to be taken and kept on record.

 

9)   Service Charges :-

As per latest service charges manual and should be collected separately and not from disbursed amount.

 

10)  Gold Appraisal Charges :-

As per latest service charges manual/communication.

Note :-No cash payment as appraisal charges to be paid to the valuer’s, it should be routed through customer account with our bank only.

In case of bullet loan, maximum LTV ratio restricted to 75%, in order to maintain LTV upto 85% on loan maturity date, including interest accrual for one year.

 

Credit monitoring department has to monitor the LTV as per the above table on a monthly basis to avoid breach of LTV. In case of LTV breach, the same has to be informed to the branch for recovering the difference amount. Department has to follow this procedure till the required margin is maintained.

Report on LTV maintenance will be reported to CEO on monthly basis. In case LTV is not maintained for more than 90 days, then such accounts should be marked as NPA.

 

12) Gold Value Assessment

Gold value will be determined on the basis of lower of “average of the closing price of 22 carats gold for the preceding 30 days” OR closing price of 22 carat gold on the preceding day(i.e. last date of 30th) as quoted by the Indian Bullions and Jewelers Association.

Gold ornaments of only 18 carat and its above carat are acceptable.

Credit department will assess the gold price and provide the price of gold on monthly basis. Valuation of other carat gold is valued as under:-

i)Value for gold below 22 carat, will be worked-out on proportionate basis.

ii) Value for above 22 carat gold, will be same as value of 22 carat gold .

For the purpose of valuation, only the intrinsic value of the gold contained in the eligible ornaments shall be reckoned and no other cost elements, such as precious stones or gems, shall be added thereto. The deductions relating to stone weight, fastenings, lac, alloy, strings, fastenings etc., damage, breakage or defects, if any, as part of the assaying procedure shall be explained to the borrower(s) and such details incorporated in the concerned loan processing form . Gross weight and Net weight of gold ornaments to be mentioned in concerned gold loan application form and difference of Gross & Net weight to be explain to the borrower.

 

Loans against ornaments and coins shall be subject to the following.

i)         The aggregate weight of ornaments pledged for all loans to a borrower shall not exceed 1 kilogram for gold ornaments.

ii)        The aggregate weight of coin(s) pledged for all loans to borrower shall not exceed 50 grams in case of gold coins.

 

13) Procedure followed for appointment of Gold Valuer :-

On receipt of an application from gold valuer for appointment as gold appraiser, Branch Manager obtains following documents from the valuer :–

1)  Application of Gold Appraiser on their Letter Head.

2)  Latest passport size photograph.

3)  Self attested copy of Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Shop and Establishment License/ Udyog Aadhaar Registration certificate.

4)  Self attested copy of certification course of gold jewellery valuation.

5)  Self attested Networth Certificate and latest Income Tax return copy.

 All above documents/copies are verified with original by the Branch Manager with due stamp and signature.

After obtaining all the documents, visit is made by Branch Manager to shop/business place and residence of Gold Valuer, if Shop/Residence is on rental basis, then obtain copy of Leave & License Agreement. Gold Valuer application, all KYC documents of Gold Valuer along with the visit Report to be forwarded to respective Cluster. In return, respective Cluster forwards the same to BDO Dept. with their recommendation. BDO office will examine the same and accordingly BDO-GM will approve the same on the merit basis and send to Development Dept. to issue appointment letter.

On receipt of all the documents, along with submission, the Incharge, Development Dept. issue an appointment letter to Gold Valuer with a copy to concerned Branch and respective Cluster. Branches have to obtain following documents before assigning the work of Gold Valuation to the newly appointed Valuer.

1. Regular Membership of Bank to Gold Appraiser and Guarantor

2. Security Deposit in the form of FDR amounting to Rs.25,000/-

3. Undertaking Cum Indemnity on Rs.600/- on stamp paper, duly notarized (as per Bank’s Format)- to be taken from Gold Valuer.

4. Letter of guarantee on Rs.500/- stamp paper duly notarized (as per Bank’s format) – to be taken from Guarantor.

All the original documents so executed with initial application, approved submission, recommendations, visit reports, Fixed Deposit Receipt duly discharged and line marked etc. are kept on record at Branch and copies are kept on record at Development Dept.

14)   Roles & responsibilities of Gold Valuer :

1.   Gold Valuer will remain present in the Branch till completion of all the related work and can leave the premises, only with the prior permission of the Incharge of the Branch.

2.   He is required to become a regular member of our Bank.

3.   He would be required to appraise the gold ornaments being tendered by our prospective Gold Loan Borrowers at respective Branch.

4.   He shall not appraise the gold loan ornaments of prospective borrower, who are his blood relatives or in any way related to him or where there is any conflict of interest and he should immediately bring the same to the attention of the Branch Officials.

5.   He will test the purity of the gold and certify its valuation not exceeding the prescribed/ market rate, in our printed form. Only the gold contents of the ornaments in proportion to the carat should be valued and not the other fittings. Ornaments containing less than 75% gold contents should not be accepted for valuation / appraisal. If any certification done by him is found to be incorrect at any later date then he will be held responsible for the same and he will be required to bear the loss, costs, expenses and consequences that may arise there from.

6.   He will be paid the agreed service charges for appraising of gold. Appraising of service charges are subject to change from time to time.

7.   After valuation, the gold ornaments should be sealed in the presence of Bank Official and borrower and custody of the sealed packet should be handed over to the concerned Officials of the Branch.

8.   In case any loss or damage is caused / occurs to the Bank due to any mistake /defect / deficiency in his service and / or due to wrong / incorrect valuation made by him, he will have to make good such loss/damage suffered by the Bank. For such eventuality, he is required to execute an Indemnity Bond in favour of the Bank (format enclosed) with stamping of Rs.600/- indemnifying the Bank and its officers against any such loss or damage.

9.   He will submit necessary copies of documents such as Goldsmith Membership Certificate in his name & Income Tax Return filed etc. showing his professional skill to work as Gold appraiser.

10.               He is required to submit Net worth Certificate or Income Tax return filed on yearly basis to branch.

11.               He will be required to keep a security deposit of Rs.25,000/- with respective Branch in the form of Fixed Deposit duly discharged by him, till the closure of all gold loan account which has been appraised by him.

12.               He should give surety of a businessman, acceptable to the Bank, having Permanent Account Number (PAN) and paying regular income tax. The said surety will execute the necessary consent letter/guarantee letter in favour of the Bank.

13.               He should give one month’s notice in advance in case he wish to discontinue his services.

14.               Gold Valuer’s opinion/certification would be an input for our decision making. Therefore, we would like to state that in the event his opinion / certification turns out to be untrue and factually incorrect causing loss to the Bank, we may seek such clarifications as may be required to investigate the matter and fix the responsibility. In the event, it is established that there was gross negligence on his part or he had colluded with our borrower in causing pecuniary damage / loss to the Bank then in addition to other remedy available against him, we may recommend his name for including in the caution list being maintained by the Indian Banks’ Association for circulation amongst member Banks.

15.               Any dispute between Gold Valuer and the Bank, would be governed under the provisions of the Multi State Co-op. Societies Act and/or other law as applicable to Multi State Co-op. Banks.

16.               The Bank reserves the right to terminate his appointment as an appraiser at any time without assigning any reason thereof.

 

15) Standardization of Documents and Communication

Concerned documents to be filed except valuation part and loan amount before execution of loan documents:-

1)  Application-cum-Agreement for loan on Gold ornaments. The said document is filled & executed with another two copies (i.e. total copy of said application).One with white color copy to be franked as per stamp act of the concerned state, pink copy to be provided to the concerned customer and third yellow copy to be kept with gold packet bag. The said document to be executed by the concerned borrower and valuer has to mention about description of the gold ornaments along with gross weight & net weight, gold rate, certified value his/her signature.

Nominee, related column in Application form to be filled and executed by the borrower, without fail.

2)  Demand Promissory Note to be executed on 1-rupee revenue stamp by the customer and he/she has to sign across the revenue stamp and another signature to be taken near to the revenue stamp, not across the stamp,i.e. two signature to be taken on DP note.

3)  Letter of understanding from the concerned borrower w.r.t declaration and consent for sharing of credit facilities to the Credit Information Agencies etc.

4)   Standing instruction to be executed by the borrower for repayment of proposed loan EMI/interest.

For overdraft against gold ornaments:- Application for Secured Overdraft facility against Gold documents to be executed and franked as per stamp act of the concerned state along with documents as mentioned above Sr.no. 2 to 4, by the borrower.

Branch has to issue sanction letter to the customer with above terms & conditions.

All communication with the borrower, especially, the terms and conditions of the loan, or other important communication which affects the interest of the borrower or the lender, shall be in the language of the region or in a language as chosen by the borrower. For an illiterate borrower, important terms and conditions shall be explained in the presence of a witness, who shall not be an employee of the bank.

16)Restrictions and Ceilings

No loan or advance will be against primary gold* or financial assets backed by primary gold e.g. Units of Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs)or units of Mutual Funds.

*Primary gold means gold in any form other than in the form of a jewellery, ornaments and coins.

Branch shall not extend a loan wherein ownership of the collateral in doubtful. A suitable document or declaration shall be obtained from the borrower in all cases to the effect that the borrower is the rightful owner of the eligible gold ornaments.

17)Credit of Gold Loan amount

Branch shall ensure that:

(i)          Loan disbursals are made to the borrower’s account and not to a third-party accounts; and

(ii)         Loan servicing, repayment, etc. is executed by the borrower directly in the lenders’ bank account without any pass-through account or pool account of any third party.

 

18) Gold Ornaments Management Handling and Storage of Gold Ornaments

Branch shall ensure that necessary infrastructure and facilities are put in place and appropriate security measures taken for gold ornaments.

Branch shall ensure that the gold security is handled only in its branches and only by its employees.

Gold ornaments to be kept in the cloth bags (made for keeping gold ornaments) with one copy (yellow) of Application-cum-Agreement for Gold Ornaments/ Application for Secured Overdraft Facility Against Gold Ornaments, in presence of concerned customer/ borrower, gold valuer & branch officials and put branch seals thereon. A small tag slip with borrower name, loan account number & date, which is to be signed by borrower, branch official and valuer to be tagged with the gold bags.

Kindly note that gold ornaments are to be sealed in front of borrower, gold valuer & branch officials. At the same time, one copy (Pink) of Application of Gold loan/ limit to be given to the borrower without any delay.

In case of death of borrower, branch has to follow written-down procedure for closure/ settlement of loan/limit.

Record of gold packet kept & taken in branch custody to be maintained on daily basis and outstanding gold packets to be tallied with physically gold packets.

Branch shall store the gold ornaments only in its branches which are manned by its employees and having safe deposit vaults fit for storing gold and silver.

19) Procedure for release of Gold Ornaments

Branch should release or return the pledged eligible gold ornaments held as security to the borrower on the same day but in any case, not exceeding a maximum period of seven working days upon full repayment and after checking his/her other liabilities. In case of other direct/ indirect liability(ies) is due, then approval from concerned authority (controller of the branch) to be obtained, in case of non-release of ornaments, accordingly borrower has to be informed immediately within a day in writing.

At the time of release of pledged gold ornaments to the borrower(s)/ legal heir(s), the gold ornaments shall be verified for correctness as per details in the certificate to the borrowers’ satisfaction.

On closure of loan/ limit, gold packets to be handed over to the concerned borrower/ legal heir(s) and his/her signature to be taken in the register and it should be mentioned in the register that gold ornaments are taken in good condition and as per articles mentioned in concerned Application-cum-Agreement / Application for Secured Overdraft facility. Also, it is to be mentioned in the register that in future concerned borrower/ legal heir(s) will not complain against the bank/ branch against gold ornaments type, condition, weight.

In-case of non-presence of borrower, the gold ornaments can be handed over to close family member after taking authority letter of the borrower, authorised in favour of such person along with KYC documents & photo of the person who is taking gold ornaments on behalf of the borrower.

No need to maintain other register, other than register which maintain record of daily packets and another register for release of gold packets under signature of the borrower.

 In-case pledge ornaments are not taken by the borrower after closure of accounts, then branch shall issue periodic reminders to the borrowers/ legal heir(s) through registered AD, email or SMS which is registered in our banks record. In first reminder branch has to mentioned about safe custody charges will be applicable at the rate of Rs.500/- + GST per month. Charges will be applicable after sending of notice to the borrower that charges are applicable in case of failure of taking back of gold ornaments within fourteen days from receipt of such notice.

20) Procedure incase for loss of Red Copy of Application-cum-Agreement for loan on Gold ornaments/Application for Secured Overdraft facility against Gold (issue to the borrower)

Branch has to obtained letter from the borrower for lost of concerned red copy and then Indemnity (as per banks format) to be obtained from borrower. After completion of said procedure, branch can issue the gold ornaments to the borrower as per written procedure.

21)Procedure to be followed during Post-disbursement of loan/ limit

1) Branch should monitor the repayment of dues of the loan and also monitor the limit of accounts.

2) In case of default in repayment of EMI/ Interest, branch should immediately follow up with the concerned borrower on regular basis. In case of default for more than one EMI/ Interest, then branch has to issue overdue/ overdrawn letter to the concerned customer.

3) In case the account turns into NPA, then branch should immediately follow-up with the borrower for clearance of over dues and issue a letter informing about NPA account status & also inform for clearance of overdues. Whenever branch feels that recovery of amount is not possible, then they have to transfer the account to recovery department for taking further course of action. However, branch should not take more than three months for clearance of overdues at their end.

22) Testing the purity of Gold ornaments and valuation

Branch should get cross verification of purity and weight done at least 10 packets of pledge ornaments picked up at random basis and not take cross verified earlier packets, once in a six month where loan/ limit was disbursed during last six month, through another approved valuer of the bank.

Verification of gold packets should be done in presence of joint custodians at the base branch, gold valuer of base branch, invited gold valuer, concerned borrower’s whose packet will be cross checked and one official of other department other than the gold loan department.

After re-checking of gold loan packets, the same should be packed and sealed in presence of concerned borrower(s), both gold valuer and bank officials. The record of the same to be maintained In case of any discrepancy/ anomaly observed, the same to be reported to the head of the credit department and also to audit department.

Gold Valuer’s Charges for verification of ornaments as under:-

i) For checking 10 gold packets or less in a day

a) Invited gold valuer            - Rs.1000/- on lumpsum basis

b) Gold valuer attached to the branch - Rs.500/- on lumpsum basis

 

ii) For checking beyond 10 gold packets in a day

a) Invited gold valuer            - Rs.100/- for each additional gold packet

b) Gold valuer attached to the branch - Rs. 50/- for each additional gold packet

Whenever invited gold valuer is required to travel from a distance beyond 25kms from base branch, then apart from the charges, he/ she should also be reimbursed reasonable travelling expenses, which is decided by the concerned branch. Concerned branch has to bear such gold valuers charges and travelling charges.

Audit department staff during visit to the branches for audit purpose, should also re-check purity of gold ornaments and valuation, such that a minimum of 10 packets or 10% of existing gold loan and gold limit account’s should be covered, whichever is on higher side. Audit staff will only select the packets and do the re-check for purity & weight of gold ornaments in their joint custody with branch officials, visiting gold valuer who will check the purity & value and another gold valuer which have done earlier valuation of concerned loan/ limit account. A list of rechecked accounts to be part of audit report.

23) Recovery Action in case of account turns NPA & recovery of dues

1) Whenever recovery dept. receive the intimation from the branch for further course of action of the gold loan/ limit accounts where account turns NPA, then in such cases department should take steps to recover the dues at the earliest.

2) After taking observation of steps taken by the branch for recovery of dues from gold loan/ limit accounts, they immediately follow the recovery procedure so that banks dues are recovered within a six months from NPA date.

3) Firstly, department will issue the notice to borrower for auction of gold ornaments within 15 days.

4) Then, through publishing the final notice, the recovery department will give one month time to borrower to repay the dues otherwise, department will go for auction.

5) In-case borrower fails to clear the dues, then department will carry gold auction and inform the concerned parties for the same. Auction will be conducted by bank officials in the rank of Senior management and under appointed gold valuer. Auction procedure is followed as per procedure laid down by the bank. Gold packets can be collected within two working days before auction date, if loan/ limit is still not closed.

24)Transparency in Auction Procedure

The Branch shall give adequate notice to the borrower(s)/ legal heir(s), as applicable, through available means of communication to repay or settle the loan dues prior to initiating the auction procedure. A copy of the notice and acknowledgement thereof shall be kept on record in both scenarios.

In case the branch is unable to locate the borrower(s)/ legal heir(s) despite best efforts and even after issuance of a public notice, it may proceed with the auction, provided that a period of one month has lapsed from the date of the public notice. Overall in any case, auction to be conducted after one month of issue of auction public notice.

Bank has to conduct auction through physical type. Before auction, if borrower or/and his/her representative repay the dues, then in such cases gold auction should not be done for concerned account and gold ornaments will be release as per procedure mentioned above.

Bank shall implement a transparent auction procedure, which shall include, inter alia, announcement of the auction to the public by issue of advertisements in at least two newspapers, one in the regional language and another in a national daily.

The pledged eligible gold ornaments shall be auctioned by a bank only through its employee having necessary experience and/ or training or an auctioneer empanelled by the lender as per its policy.

In cases where auctions are conducted by a bank through their employees, necessary safeguards such as surprise visits by regional/ controlling officials on periodic basis, coverage under internal audit, etc., shall be put in place.

A Bank shall declare a reserve price for the gold ornaments at the time of auction, which shall not be less than 90 per cent of its current value**, Provided that in case auctions fail twice, a reserve price not less than 85 per cent of its current value** shall be adopted.

**Current value will be arrived as mentioned above in sr. no.11- Gold Value Assessment.

The first auction shall be conducted physically in the same district in which the lending branch is located. However, in case of failure of first auction, a bank may conduct the auction in an adjoining district or conduct online auction.

As a matter of policy, staff or/and its relative shall not participate in the auctions to ensure that there is no potential conflict of interest.

After the auction, the bank shall mandatorily provide full details of the value fetched at the auction and the dues adjusted to the borrower(s)/ legal heir(s). The surplus, if any, from the auction of the gold ornaments, shall be refunded to the borrower(s)/ legal heir(s) within a maximum period of seven working days from the date of receipt of the full auction proceeds. The bank may recover shortfall, if any, as per terms of bank’s recovery policy.

25)Unclaimed Gold ornaments

The pledge gold ornaments lying with a lender beyond two years from the date of full repayment or settlement of loan shall be treated as unclaimed. Branch shall periodically undertake special drives to ascertain the whereabouts of the borrower (s)/legal heir(s) in respect of such unclaimed gold ornaments.

A report on unclaimed gold ornaments shall be informed to Audit dept. to be put up to CEO / GM-BDO, as the case may be, at half-yearly intervals for a review and to the Board/ Committee once in a year.

 

26) Compensation

In case of any damage to the pledged eligible gold ornaments by the branch during the tenor of loan, the cost of repair shall be borne by the branch through concerned staff responsible, if applicable; after taking approval from head office.

In case of loss of the pledged eligible collateral and/ or any loss emanating from deterioration or discrepancy in quantity or purity observed during internal audit or otherwise including at the time of return or auction of gold ornaments, branch shall suitably compensate the borrower(s)/ legal heir(s) , if applicable; after taking approval from head office.

In case of delay in release of the pledged gold ornaments after full repayment or settlement of loan by the borrower, where reasons for delay are attributable to the branch, the branch shall compensate the borrower(s)/ legal heir(s) at the rate of ₹5,000 for each day of delay beyond the timeline. If the delay is not attributable to the branch, it shall communicate reasons for such delay to the borrower(s)/ legal heir(s).
