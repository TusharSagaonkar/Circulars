Circular CR No. 357 dated 4th December 2025

Noting of Files

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

In order to ensure that sanctioning authority has sanctioned the credit proposals as per credit policy of the bank, all sanctions need to be noted by higher authorities hence noting has been started since 10.02.2020. As per office Order No. 584 dated 06.09.2024 at the time of establishment of Cluster, review & noting of files sanctioned by D.G.M., B.D. & G. M., B.D. & O are under delegation of CEO. Hence for ease & fast processing and to ensure that file is noted within time frame, revised instructions in respect of noting are as under :-

·        Cluster Level Sanction :-

All Credit proposals which are sanctioned at cluster level should be noted by respective D.G.M., Business & Development Department before disbursement of credit proposals.

 

·        D.G.M. Level Sanction:

Credit proposals sanctioned by D.G.M., Business & Development Department will be noted by the General Manager, B.D. & O before disbursement of credit proposal.

 

·        General Manager Level Sanction:

Credit proposal sanctioned by the General Manager, B.D. & O will be noted by The Chief Executive Officer at Head Office before disbursement of credit proposal.

 

The Noting exercise should be completed within 2 working days & any discrepancy if any should be informed to the concern sanctioning authority on receipt of proposal immediately and resolve the same with in the time period of 2 to 3 days & return the file to respective branch after noting by respective authority.

 

·        Chief Executive Officer Level Sanction:

Credit proposal sanctioned by C.E.O. will be noted by Administration Committee on monthly basis. The disbursement of loan should not be held back only for noting.

 

All Branches/Dept./Cluster are instructed to take note of the same and bring the content of the circular to the notice /information of the concerned staff.

The said circular will be implemented with immediate effect and earlier circular/ communication related to noting has been withdrawn.

 

 

 

Chief Executive Officer
