Circular ILA No. 84 dated 27th September, 2019

 

Revision in Rates of Interest on Housing Loans Sanctioned on or after 01.10.2019

 

 

I.                 Please refer Circular ILA No. 83 dated 22nd January, 2019. Attention of branches is particularly invited to the Row No. XI. iv. (a & b) of the Table of Interest Rates annexed to the said circular.  The said row indicates the interest rates which were applicable for loans sanctioned on or after 01.06.2017.

 

II.  1) In the face of competitive reduction of interest rates by commercial banks over some period, it was considered necessary to reduce the rates of interest on new Housing Loans. The matter was placed in the Board Meeting dated 25th September, 2019 & as approved therein, the rates of interest on Housing Loans which will be sanctioned on or after 1st October, 2019; stand reduced by 10 basis points, i.e. 0.10 %.

 

2) Therefore, while the present structure of ROI on Housing Loans as per Circular ILA No. 83 dated 22nd January, 2019 will remain as it is, the ROIs for Housing Loans to be sanctioned on or after 1st October, 2019, will be as under.
