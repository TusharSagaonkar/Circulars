Circular ID No. 151 dated 30th April 2025

 

Interest Rates on Saving Deposits (Domestic as well as NRO / NRE)

_________________________________________________________________________________

 

         

I.  A review was recently taken, in the Administrator’s Committee Meeting held on 28th April 2025 with regards to cost of deposits in the current competitive environment & a view emerged therein that there was a need to revise interest rates being offered by Bank on Domestic as well as Non Resident Saving Deposits.

 

II.    Considering the financial impact, a reduction of 0.05% in the interest rate is estimated to result in reduction in cost and profit maximization. In accordance therewith, it is hereby being advised that, w.e.f. 1st May 2025, the revised rates of interest on Saving Deposits (Domestic as well as NRO / NRE) shall be 2.70 % p.a.

 

III.    The rate of interest for staff deposits in Saving Bank account will be 3.70% i.e. 1% above the rates applicable to Public.

 

IV.   I.T. Dept. is instructed to display the rates on Bank’s Website and to make necessary changes in the system to give effect to this revision w.e.f. 1st May 2025.

 

V.  Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all the staff members at your Branch/Dept. for compliance.

 

                                     

       Chief General Manager
