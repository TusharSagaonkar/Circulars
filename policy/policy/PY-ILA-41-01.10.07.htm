Notes on Credit Rating: The following changes will be effective in respect of the facilities mentioned under Notes on Credit Rating in ILA 40 (ref. item XIX) from 1/10/2007.

 

i) New Loans and Advances Accounts: The revised rate of interest applicable w.e.f. 1/10/2007 will be under:

          Priority               -        13%

          Non-priority       -        14%

Gradation will be done after one year and rate of interest will be revised as per Credit Rating.

ii) Builders, Developers and other Civil Contractors:  No grading will be applicable.  The revised rate of interest applicable w.e.f. 1/10/2007 for new accounts will be @ 15%

 

2.     In view of the changes in interest rates as above branches are advised that they should send an intimation of the change to the existing borrowers whose accounts will be affected by the change.  Form in which the intimation (Notice) is to be given to the cash credit/DBD/Secured loan/WCTL limit borrowers is enclosed.  Further, consequent upon changes in interest rates on housing loans there will be changes in EMIs of such loans, the position in this regard has been explained separately in our IDC no. HO/L&A/Addl.GM/223/2007-08 dated 26th September, 2007 already issued to branches.  Branches should follow in this regard the procedure as advised in the said IDC wherever necessary.

 

        The notice to housing loan accounts would be generated through system as per aforesaid IDC dated 26th September, 2007 and separate communication would be sent to you by IT Dept. for revision in interest rates effective from 1/10/2007.

 

        The interest on housing loan is floating rate of interest.  Hence branches should incorporate the same in sanction letter by writing therein that rate of interest is floating.  Some branches still use old form of sanction letter wherein rate of interest is printed as “fixed” which should be substituted by the word “floating”. 

 

        It may please be noted that rate of interest on existing housing loans with floating rate of interest would only be changed as per above instructions.  There will not be change in the rate of interest in housing loan accounts where branch has already given housing loan with fixed rate of interest as per sanction letter (not in principal approval letter).

 

3.     All concerned are advised to note changes advised above and ensure that the interest is charged at the revised rates w.e.f. 1/10/2007, wherever applicable.  It may also be noted that all other instructions, contained in the Circular ILA No. 40 dated 18th May, 2007 remain unchanged except where changes as above are made.
