Circular ID No. 105 dated 26th August, 2013

 

Revision in Interest Rates on Term Deposits for Bulk Deposit of Rs 50.00 Lakh & above.

 

Please refer to our ID No. 104 dated 31.07.2013. 

 

It has been observed that there is decreasing trend of placing deposits and renewal of existing deposits on its maturity by Non-Scheduled Urban Co-operative Banks. To retain existing Term Deposits and also to attract bulk deposit particularly from Non-Scheduled Co-operative Banks, the Board in its meeting held on 22nd August 2013, has decided to revise the Rate of Interest from 10.00% to 10.25% for Bulk Deposits of Rs 50.00 Lakh and above in the slab of 15 months.
