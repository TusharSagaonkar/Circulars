Circular ILA No. 90 dated  30th June 2022

 

Revision of Lending Rates w.e.f. 1st July 2022

 

I.  The Prime Lending Rate in the Bank has undergone a change to 11.50 % in terms of the submission in this behalf approved in the Board Meeting held on 30th May 2022.

 

II.   Consequent to the revision in PLR as above, recommendations for revision in rates of interest on loans and advances, with suitable changes in differentials over or below the PLR keeping in view of the need to balance profitability with competitiveness, were placed in the Board Meeting held on 29th June 2022, and the same met with approval for implementation with effect from 1st July 2022. The revised rates of interest on Loans & Advances, which are to take effect from 1st July 2022 as per the approval, are given in the chart enclosed herewith and marked as Annexure-I.

 

III.  Further, the maximum rate of interest which may be applied, inclusive of additional incidence of interest if any, on any credit facility cannot exceed 16.50% p. a.

 

IV.        Procedure for modification of ROI in OMNI System: -

1.   The changes in rates of interest for Housing Loans, Gold Loans, Surety Loans, NSC Loans and Education Loans shall be made by I.T. Dept. Changes in rates of interest for all the other Loans & Advances shall be made manually by Branches.

2.   In the case of Fort and Bhiwandi branches where concessionary rates of interest have been given to Paper & Stationery Traders, and Textile Manufacturers & Traders, changes in rates of interest shall be made by those Branches.

3.   (a)  In case of such of the Cash Credits, other  working  capital  limits  and Secured

Loan accounts, which are eligible for Credit Rating, revision in interest rates as per the enclosed chart shall be made manually by Branches. Therefore, it is necessary to ensure prompt feeding of the correct Credit Rating in Omni. The official who is entrusted with the work of authorization of rate of interest for such an account shall ensure that entry of the proper Credit Rating based on audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss account as on 31st March 2021/2022 (as available) is made and change in interest rate is made as per the Chart.  

(b) Further, in cases where Credit Rating exercise is applicable, the same based on audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account as on 31st March 2022 is required to be completed within one month of the last statutory date of filing of Income Tax Return (ITR) as declared by Govt. The Credit Rating should be done by the Branches as and when the latest Audited Financial Statements are received and the same should be forwarded to Zonal Offices for the Credit Rating to be carried out immediately and the account shall be charged new ROI based on the revised Credit Rating.

4.   In cases of CC, DBD, SBD, Channel Credit & RCL accounts, feeding of revised rates of interest shall be done by branches.

5.   In the case of Kankavli and Udupi Branches, special ROI is given to certain Loans & Advances accounts (a) up to Rs.100.00 Lakh and (b) above Rs.100.00 Lakh up to Rs.500.00 Lakh. These branches will have to ensure that the correct rate of interest is fed in the OMNI system. The same shall be manually done by those Branches.

6.   In respect of rates of interest on Case-filed/SRO/ARB accounts, the existing practice of application of rate of interest will continue.

7.   If necessary, the IT Dept. will issue necessary guidelines for procedures for modification in ROI in the OMNI system.

 

V. We have observed that some branches have not correctly calculated and applied the effective rate of interest, which should be done by making appropriate changes in the Interest Offset Master, resulting in short /excess Interest Income. Branches must ensure that the interest rate applied on each credit facility is correct.

 

VI. We are offering a lower rate of interest to some good borrowers in order to retain their accounts in the face of competition from other banks. These borrowers have been given a fixed/concessionary rate of interest for 1 or 2 years. It has been decided to continue the concessionary rate of interest duly approved by the Board in such cases till the respective validity period expires.

 

VII.    1. In respect of Additional Interest to be charged for “foreclosure/takeover of Loans & Advances Accounts” and other “Guidelines on Loans & Advances Interest Rates”, excluding interest rate to be charged for Ratings & defaults in adherence to Credit discipline, branches shall refer principles laid down in the current “Credit Policy W. E. F. 17.05.2022” (Pricing – Fixation of Interest rate).

 

2. However, for actual incidences (of additional interest or absolute monetary incidences, as may be the case) for defaults in adherence to credit discipline, instructions contained in Circular CR No.271 dated 8th April 2021, as modified by Circular CR No.272 dated 28th April 2021 and Annexure to the same, shall be followed.

 

VIII.  External Credit Rating – Circular CR No.294 dated 14.02.2022

      A concession of 0.25% in the rate of interest in respect of credit facilities is available to enterprises which earn any of top three ratings and also any of our Bank’s internal top three ratings.

 

(For example, if our borrower is internally rated as any of the three i.e. AAA, AA or A, and is also simultaneously rated by Credit Rating Agency as any of their top three ratings, a concession of 0.25% in the rate of interest will be offered in what would otherwise be applicable according to our own internal rating).

 

This concessional ROI will be extended for one year and will be applicable to whom ROI is levied as per Credit Rating grade without further concession.

                                                                                                             

IX.   SMS, Notice on Bank’s website and Notice at branches

1.   IT dept. shall send a general SMS to the borrowers in respect of interest rates / EMIs having been changed.

2.   IT department shall display a suitable notice to this effect on the Bank’s website.

3.   A notice as per Annexure-II hereto shall be displayed on Notice Board at branches.

 

X.   Other Instructions:-

1.   The number of EMIs is typically stipulated taking into consideration the earning-life span of the borrower and the number of maximum EMIs permissible under any scheme. Therefore, there is hardly any scope left in respect of most of the loans to increase the number of EMIs while maintaining the amount of the EMI at the existing level. Therefore, branches should appropriately increase the amount of EMI in each case so as to maintain the number of EMIs same as at present. Branches should report such cases to their Zonal offices for ratification.

2.   Planning & Marketing Dept. should disseminate the information in respect of the revision of rates of interest on all types of Loans & Advances.

3.   Inspection Dept. shall ensure through internal inspection that changes in rates of interest are fed in the system properly by branches.

 

XI.  Although an increase in the rates of interest on loans and advances may initially not be appreciated by the borrowers, branches should skillfully bring it to their notice that increasing the rates of interest, necessitated by economic considerations, is a phenomenon across all the banks, and Abhyudaya Bank is increasing rates only to the barest minimum.  Branch-In-Charges must continue their efforts of marketing the retail loans and advances and ensure that the targets given are achieved well in advance of the year-end.

 

XII.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all officials/staff members at your branch and ensure strict compliance.

 

     P. S. Salian

Managing Director
