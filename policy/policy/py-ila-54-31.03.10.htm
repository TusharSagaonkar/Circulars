Circular ILA No. 54 dated 31st March, 2010

 

I)      Interest Rates on Premises Loans (PRLN) and Rent Securitization Loans

II)    Interest Rate on Housing Loans

III)   Rate of Interest for New Private as well as Commercial Four Wheeler Loans (Repayable in 36 EMIs or less)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

 

I)        In terms of instructions contained in our Circular ILA No. 51 dated 1st July, 2009, we charge interest @ 13% p.a. to Premises Loans (PRLN) i.e. Loans & Advances granted to the lessors of our Branch Premises. However, we charge interest @ 12% p.a. to the Rent Securitization Loans sanctioned to various other customers. Though the nature of security(ies) for both type of these Loans is same, comparatively the PRLN is more secured as the Premises are occupied by the our Branches and are in Bank's possession. The Board of Directors in its meeting held on 18.03.2010, have decided to charge the interest @ 12% p.a. w.e.f. 01.04.2010 for both types of these Loans. In case rate of interest is revised in future interest rate will be charged at par for these loans.

 

II)       A)       In  terms  of  our Order   No.  292    dated  14th   August,  2009

and Office Order No. 302 dated 15th September, 2009, we have introduced Abhyudaya Special Housing Loan Scheme with fixed rate of interest upto 30.09.2011 for all Housing Loans upto Rs.20.00 Lakh sanctioned on or before 31.03.2010. The Board of Directors in its meeting held on 18.03.2010, have decided to extend the period for sanction of loans under Abhyudaya Special Housing Loan Scheme upto 30.06.2010. Therefore, the proposals for Housing Loans upto Rs.20.00 Lakh sanctioned on or before 30.06.2010 will be covered under the scheme for which the fixed rate of interest @ 9% p.a. will be charged upto 30.09.2011.

 

B)      Further, we are charging floating rate of interest for Housing Loans as follows :

 

i) Housing Loans upto Rs.20.00 Lakh   -        10% p.a. (Floating)

ii) Housing Loans above Rs.20.00 Lakh         -        11% p.a. (Floating)

    upto Rs.50.00. Lakh

 

 

Since our rate of interest is higher for Housing Loans above Rs.20.00 Lakh, we are getting very few Housing Loan proposals above Rs.20.00 Lakh.

                                                                    

The Board has therefore decided in the above said meeting that for all New Housing Loans upto Rs.50.00 Lakh, except the housing loans sanctioned under Abhyudaya Special Housing Loan Scheme for Rs.20.00 Lakh upto 30.06.2010, rate of interest will be charged @ 10% p.a. (Floating) w.e.f. 01.04.2010.         

 

III)      In terms of instructions contained in our Circular ILA No. 51 dated 1st July, 2009 we charge rate of interest @ 11% p.a. for New Private Four Wheeler Loans for self use if the loan is repayable in 36 EMIs or less. However the rate of interest is charged @ 12% p.a. for New Loan for Four Wheeler for Commercial use even if the loanee is prepared to repay the loan in 36 EMIs or less. The Board in the meeting held on 23.03.2010 has decided to charge interest @ 11% p.a. instead of 12% p.a. even for the loan sanctioned for commercial vehicles provided that borrower is agreeing to repay the entire loan in 36 EMIs or less. In cases where we have sanctioned the loan @ 11% p.a. on commitment of the borrower to repay the entire loan in 36 EMIs or less and if borrower has not repaid the loan within the stipulated period, he/she should be charged interest @ 12% p.a. from the date of initial disbursement.
