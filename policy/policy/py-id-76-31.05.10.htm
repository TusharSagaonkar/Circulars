Circular ID No. 76 dated 31st May, 2010

 

Special Term Deposit - Shubhamkaroti

 

                                                             

                                                                                                                                                                                             

Please refer to Our Circular ID No. 75 dated 21st May, 2010 informing about the Launching of Special Term Deposit Scheme "Shubhamkaroti". The scheme was restricted to accept the deposits only under QID Scheme. However in order to make the scheme more attractive and with a view to mobilize more deposits, it has been decided by the Board to allow the branches to accept the deposit under this Special Term Deposit Scheme of "Shubhamkaroti" for both QID as well as QICRIP Scheme.

                                                                        

In view of the above, the salient features of the Special Term Deposit Scheme "Shubhamkaroti" has been devised as under :-

 

 

Name of scheme

Period

Rate of interest

Details

Shubhamkaroti Deposit Scheme

39 months

8.50% for all categories i.e. General Public, Trusts,                 Co-operative Societies, Institutions, Senior Citizens, Staff, etc., including for the Deposit amount of Rs.15.00 Lakh and above i.e. same rate will be made applicable  for Bulk Deposits also.

TDR will be issued only under QID & QICRIP Scheme. This scheme will be valid for one month only i.e. from       1st June 2010 to 30th June 2010.

 

 

All other instructions as per Circular ID No. 75 dated 21.05.2010 and conditions applicable to domestic Term Deposit issued earlier from time to time continue to remain in force.

 

Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members at your Branch/Dept. for compliance.

 

                                      

V. S. Morye

       Managing Director
