(viii)   Non-submission of Stock and Book Debt statement at monthly intervals. Such statements showing the position of Stocks/Book Debts on the last day of the month should be received latest by 15th of the following month, Circular OC No. 311 dated 14.7.2005 should be followed for guidance in this regard.

 

 

(ix)    Auditor certified Book debts statement: As per the decision of 35th Audit Committee Meeting, borrower enjoying CC & WCTL limit with limit above Rs. 25.00 lacs and also with component of Drawing Power against Book Debt above Rs. 25.00 lacs, are required to submit auditor certified Book Debt statements as on 30th June and 31st December every year.  These statements are required for Stock Audit purpose. These accounts will be charged penal interest @ 3% p.a. in case of non-receipt of Auditor certified Book Debt statements by 31st August and 28th February. However, Book Debt statements alongwith stock statements without certificate by Auditor will be accepted by 15th of succeeding month and reckoned for Drawing Power.
