Registration of our Bank as Multi-State Urban Co-op. Bank.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

       You  may  be aware that our bank  had applied to the Central   Registrar, New Delhi  for registration  of our bank  under the Multi-State Co-op. Societies  Act, 2002.  We are now pleased to inform you that our Bank has been registered as a Multi-State Urban Co-op. Bank under Section 22 of the Multi-State Co-op. Societies Act, 2002 and Rules thereunder.  The registration number of our bank will henceforth be read as MSCS/CR/249/2007 dated 11 January 2007. The area of operation of our bank will now  comprise  of the States  of Maharashtra and Karnataka.  A copy of the amended bye-laws  which have been approved  by the registering authority will be circulated in due course.  An announcement  (computer generated) as indicated below may be displayed  prominently in all office premises for information of our customers and shareholders visiting the offices.

 

 

 

                                                                             Addl. General Manager
