Circular ILA No. 74 dated 26th August, 2014

 

Revised Rate of Interest on Housing Loans and Bill Discounting under L/C (Inland) w.e.f. 01.09.2014

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Kindly refer to our Circular ILA No. 73 dated 30.06.2014 advising revision in Rate of Interest on Housing Loan w.e.f. 01.07.2014. It has been observed that the Housing Loan portfolio has not really picked up as expected. As the bank’s policy is to focus on Retail Loan Portfolio and with a view to encourage the housing sector, it has been proposed for a further Rate cut. As Bill Discounting under L/C (Inland) is another form of secured loan, it was felt necessary to reduce the rate of interest under this head.  This proposal was placed before the Board meeting held on 25.08.2014. The Board felt the need to improve the Retail Loan Portfolio and decided for a reduced rate of interest in Housing Loans and Bill Discounting under L/C (Inland).
