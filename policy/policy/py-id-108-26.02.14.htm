Circular ID No. 108 dated 26th February, 2014

 

Reduction in Interest Rate on Bulk Deposit above Rs. 25 Lakh

And

Increase in Interest Rate on Term Deposits of above 36 months.

The matter of reduction in the interest rate on bulk deposits above Rs. 25 lakh in order to curtail the cost of deposits and also the matter of increasing the quantum of long term deposits by increasing the interest rate on term deposits above 36 months was discussed at length in the ALCO Meeting held on 15th February, 2014. Accordingly, in terms of ALCO recommendations, Board in its meeting held on 21.02.2014 decided to reduce the rate of interest on bulk deposits of above Rs. 25.00 Lakh and increase in the rate of interest on Term Deposits above 36 months          w.e.f. 01.03.2014.
