Circular ILA No. 86 dated 30th April, 2020

 

Revision of Lending Rates w.e.f. 1st May, 2020

 

I.   The Prime Lending Rate in the Bank has undergone a change to 12 % in terms of a submission in this behalf approved in the Board Meeting held on 27th April, 2020. Consequent revision in rates of interest on loans and advances, with suitable changes in differentials over or below the PLR keeping in view the need to balance profitability with competitiveness, as also been recommended in the Board meeting held on 29th April, 2020 & the same has met with approval for implementation with effect from 1st May, 2020. The revised rates of interest on Loans & Advances, which are to take effect from 1st May 2020 as per the approval, are given in the chart enclosed herewith.

 

II.  Procedure for modification of ROI in OMNI System:-

1.  It has been decided that changes in rates of interest for Housing Loans, Gold Loans, Surety Loans, NSC Loans and Education Loans shall be made by I.T. Dept. Changes in rates of interest for all the other Loans & Advances shall be made manually by Staff/Officials at Branches.

 

2.  In case of Fort and Bhiwandi branches where concessionary rate of interest have been given to Paper & Stationery Traders, and Textile Manufacturers & Traders, changes in rates of interest shall be made by staff/officials at the Branches.

 

3.  (a) In case of such of the Cash Credits, other working capital limits and Secured

Loan accounts, which are eligible for Credit Rating, revision in interest rates as per the enclosed chart shall be made manually by staff/officials at Branches. It is therefore necessary to ensure that proper Credit Rating is fed in the system. The official who is entrusted with the work of authorisation of rate of interest for such an account shall ensure that entry of the proper Credit Rating based on audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss account as on 31st March, 2019 is made and change in interest rate is made as per the Chart.

 

(b) In cases where Credit Rating exercise is applicable, the same based on audited

Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account as on 31st March, 2020 is required to be completed by 30th November 2020. However, if any accountholder submits the audited financial statements pertaining to 31st March, 2020 earlier than 30th November 2020, the Credit Rating shall be carried out immediately and the account shall be charged new ROI based on the revised Credit Rating.

 

4.  In case of CC, DBD, SBD, Channel Credit & RCL accounts, feeding of revised rates of interest shall be done by the staff and officials at branches.

 

5.  In case of Kankavali and Udupi Branches, special ROI is given to certain Loans & Advances accounts (a) up to Rs. 100.00 Lakh and (b) above Rs. 100.00 Lakh up to Rs. 500.00 Lakh. These branches will have to ensure that the correct rate of interest is fed in the Limit Master. The same shall be manually done by staff and officials at those Branches.

 

6.  In respect of rates of interest on Case filed/SRO/ARB accounts, the existing practice of application of rate of interest will continue.

 

7.  We have offered lower rate of interest to some good borrowers in order to retain their accounts in the face of competition from other banks. These borrowers have been given fixed/concessionary rate of interest for 1 or 2 years. It has been decided to continue the concessionary rate of interest duly approved by the Board in such cases till the respective validity period expires.

 

III.  In respect of Additional Interest to be charged for Non-compliance of Terms & Conditions & foreclosure/takeover of Loans & Advances Accounts, instructions contained in the current “Credit Policy W. E. F. 15.07.2019” (Point No. 23. Pricing – Fixation of Interest rate) shall be followed.

 

IV.   External Credit Rating - Tie up with CRISIL for availing their services: - A concession of 0.25% in rate of interest in respect of credit facilities is available to enterprises which earn any of CRISIL’s top 3 ratings and also any of our Bank’s internal top three ratings.

 

(For example, if our borrower is internally rated as any of the three i.e. AAA, AA or A, and is also simultaneously rated by CRISIL as any of their top three i.e. MSE 1/SME 1, MSE 2/SME 2 or MSE 3/SME 3, a concession of 0.25% in rate of interest will be offered in what would otherwise have been applicable according to our own internal rating).

 

V.  As there is overall reduction in effective rates of interest across all the schemes and types of loans & advances, we perceive no need to communicate changes to individual borrowers. Instead, we shall send a general SMS of interest rates having been changed. Further, a notice to this effect shall be displayed on Bank’s website and on Notice Board at branches.

 

VI.   Other Instructions:-

1.  There are chances of some borrowers approaching branches for reducing the amount of EMI. Such cases are expected to be few. Branches are authorised to consider their request provided there is no change in the number of balance EMIs, and maturity date of the loan remains unchanged. Branches should report such cases to their Zonal offices for ratification.

 

2.   Planning & Marketing Dept. shall arrange for printing of advertising material with the new rates. The department should give wide publicity to the revision and reduction in rates of interest on all types of Loans & Advances.

 

3. Inspection Dept. shall ensure through internal inspection that changes in rates of interest are fed in the system properly by branches.

 

VII.  It may be noted that except fixed-interest-rate loans, there is reduction in interest rates on all types of loans and advances. It is therefore, need of the hour to increase Retail loans including Housing Loans in the coming months. It may be noted that the existing definition of retail loans is now reduced from Rs. 5 Crore to 2 Crore. The In-charge of branches are advised to involve all members of the staff in marketing of retail loans and advances and ensure that the targets given are achieved well in advance.

 

VIII.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all officials/staff members at your branch and ensure strict compliance.
