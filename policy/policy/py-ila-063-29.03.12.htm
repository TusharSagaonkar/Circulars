Circular ILA No. 63 dated 29th  March, 2012

Rate of Interest on

Commercial Loans/Advances at Kankavali and

Branches of Udupi Region

 

With a view to give impetus for business growth and to compete fairly with the other Banks in the area, our Bank has offered concessional rate of interest @ 12% upto 31.03.2012 on all commercial Loans & Advances sanctioned upto and including Rs.100.00 Lakh at our Kankavali Branch and Branches at Udupi Region vide our Circular ILA No. 53 dated 17.02.2010.

 

 Now, Board in its meeting held on 28.02.2012 has decided as under:-

On all Commercial Loans and advances sanctioned upto and including Rs. 100.00 Lakh at our Kankavali Branch and Branches at Udupi Region, the rate of interest will be charged @ 13% p.a. for a period of 1 year i.e. from 1.04.2012 to 31.03.2013. This revised rate of interest would be applicable to all existing as well as for new Loans & Advances.

 

Branches should convince their customers when they visit the branch and also by sending a letter as per Annexure I enclosed that due to increase in cost of deposit and repo rate, the rate of interest has been increased only with 1% p.a. and made @ 13% p.a. which is also concessional and at par with our present PLR. The credit rating exercise where applicable will be done although the rate would be made applicable @ 13% p.a. fixed upto 31.03.2013. However, Penal interest would be made applicable if credit rating exercise will not be made as per Credit Policy. The sanction letter shall contain all these terms & conditions as per Annexure enclosed with our Circular ILA No. 53 dated 17.02.2010.

 

We still hope that with this concession in rate of interest, a good number of viable and feasible proposals of creditworthy entrepreneurs will be attracted towards our above mentioned branches and the expected business growth in advances portfolio will be achieved.

 

These branches should carefully note that the interest rates on loans & advances other than commercial loans & advances sanctioned upto Rs. 100.00 Lakh i.e for  Housing Loans, Education Loans, Rent Securitization Loans, Gold Loans, Vehicle Loans etc. will be applied as per our Circular ILA No. 61 dated 22.09.2011 and IDC No. MD-920/1714/2011-12 dated 06.03.2012.

 

Branch-in-charge of Kankavali Branch and Branch In-charges of Udupi Regional Branches will take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all concerned staff members and borrowers at their branches and ensure strict compliance. The letter to all eligible Lonees/Borrowers as per Annexure I shall be sent on or before 5th April, 2012 & copies thereof shall be preserved in respective security files of each Loans/Advances.

 

 

 

 

     V. S. Morye

Managing Director
