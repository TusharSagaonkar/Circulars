3. It has also been decided to revise the interest rate for deposits under ATSS to 6.00 % p. a. w.e.f. 1st July 2021.

 

4. Interest rate on Saving Deposits reduced

Interest rate on saving deposits (domestic as well as NRO/NRE) shall be 2.75 % p. a. with effect from 1st July 2021.

 

III.   Instructions regarding withdrawal of Additional Interest on QID as advised in terms of Circular ID No.130 dated 31st March 2021 continue to hold good.

 

IV.   Other instructions:

1.   The rate of interest for staff deposits for all slabs/durations will be 1% more than the rates applicable for the General Public.

 

2.   The Staff/checking officials of Term Deposit section at the branch should check correctness of interest rate quoted on deposit receipt as per the instructions above before handing over the relative receipt to the depositor.

 

3.   IT Dept. is advised to display this information on Bank’s website and make necessary changes in the system.

 

4.   All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to deposits and interest thereon issued from time to time continue to remain in force.
