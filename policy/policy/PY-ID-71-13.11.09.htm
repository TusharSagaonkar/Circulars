Circular ID No. 71  dated 13th November 2009

 

Removal of Anomaly in interest rates

for bulk deposits and for General Public

 

         

1.       In terms of our Circular ID 68 dated 7th September, 2009 we have revised the interest rates on bulk deposits as follows:-

 

181 days and above but less than 1 year                 6.50 %

1 Year and above upto & including 2 years          7.25 %

 

          While revising the interest rates on Term deposits for General Public vide our Circular ID 70 dated 22nd October, 2009 we have quoted the rates as under:-

 

181 days and above inclusive of 12 months                   7.00 %

Above 12 months and upto and inclusive of 18        7.25 %        

Months                                                                 

 

          You will find that rate of interest for 12 months period for General Public has been offered at 7.00% and for Bulk Deposits it has been offered at 7.25%.  In order to remove this anomaly we are giving fresh bucket rates for bulk deposits to be accepted from other than individuals and Co-operative Housing Societies as under:-
