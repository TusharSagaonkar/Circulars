A reference is invited to our Circulars ILA No. 49 dated 30th January 2009 & ILA No. 50 dated 3rd March 2009. The economic conditions of our country are improving during the recent past few months and other Banks have already started reducing the deposits as well as lending rates. Taking into account all these aspects, a meeting of our Assets and Liabilities Committee (ALCO) was held on 12.06.2009. The committee emerged with certain recommendations. The recommendations alongwith certain New Loan Schemes and concept of PLR linked rate of interest devised by ALCO were put up to the Board in its meeting held on 19.06.2009. As approved by the Board we give below the salient features of New Schemes/concept & schedule of Revised Interest Rates on Loans and Advances. The revised rates, New Schemes and New Gradation concept would be made effective from 01.07.2009 :-

1.     Abhyudaya Special Housing Loan Scheme :-

          Since the improvement in economic conditions of our country, many banks have come with New Housing Loan Schemes with attractive interest rates. State Bank of India have come with special rate of 8% fixed for one year to attract the new beneficiaries of Housing Loans. It is learnt that their new scheme was successful. In view of this, ALCO has recommended New Housing Loans Scheme as under:-

i)             The scheme is open for a limited period of 9 months i.e. from 01.07.2009 to 31.03.2010.

ii)           The rate of interest for the New Housing Loan beneficiaries will be @ 9% p.a. fixed upto 31.03.2011, subject to Re-set clause w.e.f. 01.04.2011.

iii)         Housing Loans sanctioned only on or before 31.03.2010 will be covered under the scheme.

iv)          Housing Loans only upto Rs.20.00 Lakh will be covered under the scheme.

v)            Takeover of Housing Loan from any Financial Institutions/Banks during the period of 01.07.2009 to 31.03.2010 will be eligible under the scheme.

vi)          A format of new Sanction Letter Code No. 1052 is enclosed as Annexure-A.

vii)        The eligibility of loan amount will be calculated as if we are charging rate of interest @ 10% p.a. However, actual EMI will be calculated at the rate of interest @ 9% p.a. The revision of EMI will be made from 01.04.2011.

Example :

A person of 30 years age approaching for Housing Loan with a Net Salary of Rs.15,000/-. His/her other deduction are Rs.2,500/- and take home salary after EMI for loans upto Rs.10.00 Lakh is Rs.4,500/-, hence, Salary available for EMI factor is Rs.8,000/- [Rs.15000 – (2500 + 4500) ].

 

The EMI per Lakh @ 9% for 15 years is Rs.1,014/-. His/her Loan eligibility would be Rs.7.88 Lakh.

And EMI per Lakh @ 10% for 15 years is Rs.1,075/-. His/her Loan eligibility would be Rs.7.44 Lakh.

In terms of above instruction, Branches will consider Loan eligibility for Rs.7.44 Lakh Only and EMI will be Rs.7,544/- (at the rate of 9%).  

viii)       The other terms of the Housing Loan will remain unchanged.

ix)          Obtain modified Loan Agreement (Code No. 1055) for this New Housing Loan Scheme.

x)           Branches should note that New Housing Loan Scheme is applicable only upto and inclusive of Rs.20.00 Lakh Loan amount and cases sanctioned on or before 31.03.2010. While issuing sanction letters to these Borrowers and obtaining New Modified Loan Agreements from them, sufficient care should be taken. Branch-in-charges should not sanction any Loan under the scheme after 31.03.2010. If the loan is sanctioned after 31.03.2010 and/or for loan amount for more than Rs.20.00 Lakh and if it is found that a New Sanction Letter (Code No. 1052) has been issued and a modified Loan Agreement (Code No. 1055) has been obtained from these Housing Loan Borrower(s), the Branch-in-charge alongwith the concerned Clerk/Assistant Accountant/Accountant will be made personally responsible and accountable. Any loss to the Bank due to such default(s) will be recovered from the concerned official(s)/Staff Member(s), which please note.

We are sure that many customers desirous of having a dwelling unit will be attracted towards our New Scheme provided proper care is taken for marketing the scheme.

2.     Change of Terms in Educational Loans :-

          At present we are granting maximum Moratorium period of 36 months for all Educational Loans irrespective of study period. When study period itself is more than 36 months for example, Engineering, Medical etc. courses, it has been observed that in such cases repayment of the loan becomes difficult and cumbersome particularly to the middle class people when neither a student has completed the course nor he/she has been gainfully employed. With a view to attract more students towards our Educational Scheme, we have revised the terms of the Educational Loans as under:

 

i.   Moratorium/Holiday Period :           -        Duration of the Course + 6 months

 

ii.  Repayment :                                    -        EMIs will be upto 60 months and

                                                                      will start on completion of Holiday

                                                                      period as mentioned above.

 

iii. Interest during Holiday Period         -        To be serviced every month during the

                                                                      Holiday Period.

iv.  The other terms of Educational Loans are as under:-

     a) Eligible Courses         : Studies in India – Diploma/ Graduation/ Post-

graduation courses in various disciplines, Computer certificate courses in reputed institutes accredited to the Dept. of Electronics.

Studies Abroad – For job-oriented Professional/ Technical/Medical courses offered by reputed Universities, MCA, MBA, MS etc. Courses conducted by CIMA, London, CPA in USA etc.

                                                  : Pilot training in India and Abroad.

 

            b) Loan Amount             : For studies in India Max. Rs.10.00 Lakh. For

                                                     studies abroad Max. Rs.20.00 Lakh.

 

            c) Student Eligibility      : An Indian national who has secured admission

to eligible course. The loan will be sanctioned to the parents of the student and the student will be Co-Borrower, if major.

 

            d) Security                     : (a) Loan upto Rs.2.00 Lakh – 2 guarantors

                                                          salary/income Rs.6,000/- and above p.m.

                                                          In case of lien on flat only one guarantor.

(b) Loan above Rs.2.00 Lakh to Rs.10.00 Lakh

Equitable Mortgage of residential flat and two guarantors, salary/income Rs.10,000/- and above p.m.

(c)  Loan above Rs.10.00 Lakh to Rs.20.00 Lakh – collateral security 100% of the sanctioned limit in the form of equitable mortgage of the property and/or pledge of tangible security like NSCs/KVP/LICs/FDR, RBI Bonds etc.

2 guarantors, salary/income Rs.15,000/- and above p.m.

                                                 

          With the above mentioned changes in Moratorium Holiday Period and period for repayment, more and more middle class students will be attracted towards our Educational Loan Scheme.

6.  Credit Rating –

The rate of interest applicable for Borrowers  enjoying  only Cash Credit limit above Rs.10.00 Lakh or only loan limit above Rs.25.00 Lakh or  either of CC or Secured Loan limit  being  more than Rs.10.00 Lakh will be subject to gradation. For example, the rate of interest for Borrower with Cash Credit Limit of Rs.5.00 Lakh and Secured Loan of Rs.11.00 Lakh or vice-versa be determined according to the Credit Rating assigned to the account. If a unit having Cash Credit limit of Rs.9.00 Lakh and secured loan limit of Rs.9.00 Lakh, although the sum total of these two limits is more than the total in the earlier example the unit is not eligible for gradation as both the limits are below Rs.10.00 Lakh and will be treated as other than graded account.   

 

Notes on Credit Rating:

While taking over of any priority sector advanced Grading is applicable at the time of initials stage itself.

The exercise of Credit Rating should be done for all Textile Manufacturing Units.

For other instructions please refer Credit Policy 2009-2010.

         Please note that financing for Group Loans under various categories of groups (i.e. Group A, B, C mentioned in our Circular ILA No. 44 dated 18.07.2008) had been discontinued. For the existing loan accounts under these categories there is no change in the rate of interest to be applied.

10.   Notes for all Loans and Advances:

i)         In case of flat/house given on rental basis, no extra interest will be charged w.e.f. 01.07.2009 for all New as well as Existing Housing Loan Accounts. Branches should invariably advise to all the Existing Housing Loan Borrowers regarding this change. However, Branches should obtain Undertaking from the Borrower that he/she will not create any third party interest on flat/house mortgaged to the Bank. Branches should also ensure that no third party interest is created on flat/house mortgaged to the Bank. For this purpose Branch Official should visit the flat/house on half yearly intervals and meet Society/Builders to ascertain the fact from them. A record of visits should be properly maintained.

 

ii)       We invite your attention towards a new scheme devised for new private four wheeler loan for self use which is repayable in 36 EMIs or less.

iii)     It may be noted that interest rate changes are not applicable to Housing Loans which have been sanctioned and disbursed under Fixed Rate of Interest during the period 01.07.2005 to 30.06.2006.

iv)      In case of takeover of Cash Credit and other loan accounts by other Bank(s), prepayment charges or penal interest @ 2% will be charged on the amount of outstanding as on the date of takeover/closure. No prepayment charges or penal interest to Surety Loan Accounts to be levied eventhough taken over by other Bank.

v)        The rate of Penal interest has already been reduced from 3% to 1% p.a. which will be levied for the period of default on the amount of overdues. 

vi)      In case of Cash Credit NPA A/c., penal interest will be 1% p.a. from the date of NPA till it is regularized.

vii)     Loan facility against NSC/KVP (100% of the Accrued Value), RBI Bonds (100% of the Face Value) and LIC Policy (100% of Surrender Value) or Rs.2.00 Lakh whichever is less may be granted to all permanent employees/officers of the bank at interest rate of 1% less than what is applicable to the Borrowers of the Bank. The employees/officers should own the said securities solely or jointly with his/her spouse.  

In cases where the employees/officers wish to avail loan above Rs.2.00 Lakh, the criteria applicable to general customers in respect of margin and rate of interest will be applicable to them.

viii)   There is need to create awareness among Borrowers to secure higher Credit Rating by them to get reduction in the rate of interest which is beneficial to them.  This will also help to inculcate the habit of submitting financial statements in time.  Branches are advised to write to all eligible Borrowers  whose facilities are subject to Credit Rating  as mentioned above to submit audited statements on or before 31st December and avail the benefit of lower rates upon higher gradation.  

ix)      The penal interest will be 1% p.a. over the rate applicable as per the existing grading. Credit rating exercise shall be completed within 5 days at Branch and 6 days at Zonal Office. Thus credit rating exercise shall be completed within 11 days of receipt of audited financial statements.  Borrowal accounts will not be charged penal interest after receipt of these statements.  It will be the responsibility of Branch and Zonal Office to complete this exercise within 11 days of the receipt of audited balance sheet and profit and loss account statements.  If the gradation is revised at any time subsequently and penal interest continued to be charged, it will be charged at 1% p.a. over the revised rate of interest for the facility. Since last date of submission of audited balance sheet and profit and loss account statements is 31st December, the penal interest will be charged from 1st January of next year.

x)        In-charge of Branches are authorized to determine the sub limit of CC against FDR collaterals as per above by obtaining request letter from the Borrower and informing the Borrower and the guarantors of the benefit.

 

xi)      At the time of renewal of limits, principal amount alongwith accrued interest of FDR(s) shall be taken into consideration to decide sub limits against FDR(s) within overall cash credit limits. Similarly, in case of renewal of matured FDR(s) before renewal of limit, the change in rate of interest if any shall be revised immediately against renewed FDR(s). The Borrower should be informed about revision in rate of interest.     

11.   Credit discipline:

          In order to inculcate the desired credit discipline among the Borrowers, Branches are advised to address letters to all the borrowing customers specifically emphasizing the importance of adherence to credit discipline and that the bank will be levying penal interest for non-adherence to the credit discipline. Branches should carefully note the Penal Interest at 1% p.a. will be charged on all borrowing accounts without any exception for the period of default in cases of following defaults. Branches should also note that there is no waiver or concession for applying Penal Interest. Therefore, Branches should not forward/recommend any such request from any Borrower to this office.  This aspect should be specifically pointed out to the borrowing customers.

(i)      Non-repayment   of    loan   installments on its due date.

(ii)     Non-payment of interest when it becomes due.

(iii)    Availing excess over sanctioned limit or excess over Drawing Power.

(iv)     Non-renewal/non-review above 3 months of cash credit limit due to non-submission of documents/papers. In case of unreviewed CC A/cs default period will end on 1st day of next month, after receipt of all documents for review.

(v)      Bills discounted not realized on their due dates.

(vi)     Submission of audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account for the year ended March, after 31st December of the next immediate financial year. This is applicable for Credit Rating of borrowal accounts.

(vii)    Auditor's certified Book debts statement: As per the decision of the Audit Committee Meeting, Borrower enjoying CC & WCTL limit with limit above Rs.25.00 Lakh and also with component of Drawing Power against Book Debt above Rs.25.00 Lakh, are required to submit auditor's certified Book Debts statements as on 30th June and  31st December every year.  Those statements are required for Stock Audit purpose. These accounts will be charged penal interest @ 1% p.a. in case of non-submission of Auditor's certified Book Debts statements by 31st August and 28th February. However, Book Debts statements alongwith stock statements without certificate by Auditor will be accepted by 15th of the succeeding month and reckoned for Drawing Power. 

The Branches are advised to send reminder letters in the month of June and December every year to all eligible borrowal accounts who are required to submit Auditor's certified Book Debts statement.

(viii)     It is necessary to advise all Borrowers the revised rate of interest.
