Circular ILA No. 62 dated 27th December, 2011

 

Interest Rates on Educational Institution

Loans & Advances

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Considering Bank’s satisfactory experience of repayment of Educational Institutional loans, we are charging concessional interest rate @ PLR - 0.5% i.e. 12.5% to these Institutional loan Accounts.

To discourage irregular repayment we are charging penal interest at the following rates.

             If irregular by 1 or 2 installment(s)       :   PLR -0.5% i.e. 12.5%

                                                                                     +    

                                                                        2.5% Penal Interest i.e. 15%  

 

             If account has been turned as N.P.A     :   PLR – 0.5% i.e. 12.5%

                                                                                      +

                                                                        2.5% Penal Interest i.e. 15%  

Thus for charging penal interest, we are not making any difference between Overdue/Irregular Account and NPA Account.

 

2.   On representation, the matter was placed before the Board in its meeting held on 17.12.2011 and accordingly in terms of Board’s decision we advise as under:-

i.

For regular accounts interest will be charged

: @ PLR – 0.5% i.e 12.5%

ii.

If account is irregular by 1 or 2 installment (s),

Penal interest w.e.f . 01.12.2011 will be charged

: @ PLR -0.5% i.e. 12.5%

                  +

  1% Penal Interest i.e. @ 13.5%

iii.

If accounts has been turned as NPA,

Penal interest w.e.f. 01.12.2011 will be charged

: @ PLR -0.5% i.e. 12.5%.

                    +

 2.5% Penal Interest i.e. @ 15%

 

iv

On becoming the account regular/standard

Interest from 1st of following month will be charged

: @ PLR – 0.5% i.e. 12.5%

 

 

v.     The above penal interest rates will be made applicable to Group accounts of the borrowers where such Sub-PLR rates are considered to the group accounts alongwith Educational Institutional Loans.

 

vi.  Branches should communicate this changed pattern of applying penal  interest to all Educational Institutions as per enclosed format (Annexure A). One copy of such letter shall be retained in loan file.

 

vii. Request from any Educational Institution for waiver of penal interest charged will not be entertained in future.

 

3.           Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the concerned staff at the Branch/Dept. and ensure compliance.  

 

     V. S. Morye

Managing Director
