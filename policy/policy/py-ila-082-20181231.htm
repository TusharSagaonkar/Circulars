Circular ILA No. 82 Dated 31st December, 2018

 

Revision of Lending Rates w. e. f. 01.01.2019

                                           

 

I.                 The PLR of the Bank has been worked out to 13% on the basis of actuals for the first 6 months of the current financial year 2018-19, & projections for the remaining part of the current financial year. The revised PLR was occasioned by higher costs. The revision in PLR to 13 % has been approved in the Board Meeting held on 12.10.2018.

 

II. Consequent upon the revision in PLR, interest rates on all the loans & advances have been revised with suitable changes in differentials over or below the PLR, keeping in view the need to balance profitability with competitiveness. The chart of revised interest rates which is attached herewith will be EFFECTIVE FROM 1ST JANUARY, 2019.

 

III.  The instructions regarding changes in OMNI system whether by branches or IT Department, resetting the EMI, notices to be addressed by branches to borrowers against acknowledgement, resetting of SI and ensuring revised mandates through borrowers to salary disbursing authorities in respect of ECS/NACH /SDLs, will be issued separately.

 

 

IV. We have offered lower rate of interest to some good borrowers, upon approval of the Board, in order to retain their accounts in the face of competition from other banks. The fixed/concessional rate of interest is given for some specific period. Such concessional rates of interests which are approved by the Board will continue for the period for which they have been so approved.

 

 

V.    Additional interest has been prescribed in certain situations as under.

 

1)     Auditor's certified Stock & Book Debts statement: Borrowers enjoying CC & WCTL/STCL/RCL limit/s above Rs.25.00 Lakh are required to submit their Auditor's certified Stock & Book Debts statements as on 30th September every year by 31st October. In the absence of submission of the same within the stipulated period, additional interest @ 2% will be applied on outstanding amount from 1st of November for the period till the same is submitted.

 

Action:- Branch has to create offset interest master for applying additional interest and reset the same after the submission of the said statements.

 

2)     Additional interest @ 2% will be applied on outstanding amount in case monthly average turnover is less than 25% of the sanctioned limit. The average monthly turnover shall be calculated for the first half-yearly period from April to September and for second half-yearly period from October to March. Additional interest will accordingly be charged from subsequent 15th of October and 15th of April respectively. There are finer and additional points which need to be taken into consideration for which Annexure – 12 of the extant Credit Policy may please be referred to.

Action:- Branch has to calculate additional interest manually and collect the same.

 

3)     If audited Profit & Loss statement, Balance sheet & ITR are not received by 31st October in respect of a borrower whose accounts are subject to credit rating, additional interest @ 2% will be applied on outstanding amount in such accounts from 1st November for the period till the same is submitted.

Action:- Branch has to create offset interest master for applying additional interest and reset the same after the submission of the statements.

 

4)     In case an account remains to be Renewed within one month from the due date for renewal, additional interest @ 2% will be charged on outstanding amount after expiry of one month’s period (which works out to a grace period of one month for applying additional interest) till renewal of account.

 Note:- Grace Period of one month is made applicable to all types of CC facilities like FLXLN, all types of SOD accounts, STCC and Salary CC. Accordingly, while opening account under such type of facilities, branches will have to consider the concept of grace period.

Action:- The system will apply additional interest of 2% on outstanding amount inclusive of overdrawn amount.

 

5)     In case an account remains to be Reviewed within one month from the due date for review, additional interest @ 2% will be charged on outstanding amount till review of account.

Action:- Branch has to create offset interest master for applying additional interest and reset the same after the review is done facilitatedly.

 

6)     Additional interest will be applied @ 2% on outstanding amount in the relative credit facilities, if the charged assets remain uninsured. The additional interest in such cases will be applied from expiry of the insurance policy till its renewal.

Action:- Branch has to create offset interest master for applying additional interest and reset the same after the status of insurance is rectified.

 

7)     Additional interest will be applied @ 2% on outstanding amount in the absence of drawing power due to non-submission of Stock & Book Debt Statements in CC accounts.

 

Action:- The system will apply additional interest of 2% on outstanding amount

 

8)     Additional interest will be applied @ 2% on overdrawn amount if drawing power is not maintained in CC accounts.

Action:- The system will apply additional interest of 2% on outstanding amount.

 

Note: Clause of additional interest for Stock and Book Debt statements not having been submitted & /or drawing power is not maintained are also applicable for Term Loans (WCTL) sanctioned against Drawing power (Stock and Book Debts).

However where working capital Term loan is along with CC limit, the clauses as above are applicable for CC Limits only, as DP will be first apportioned for WCTL.

Action:- In case of absence of Stock and Book Debts Statement, Branch has to create offset interest master in Term Loan Account (WCTL) for applying additional interest and reset the same after the default is complied. In case of failure to maintain drawing power, Branch has to calculate additional interest manually and collect the same through Term Loan Account (WCTL) by using the activity “INTRECDR”.

 

9)      Additional interest will be applied @ 2% on overdue amount in standard term loan account including loans to an Educational Institution.

Action:- The system will apply additional interest of 2% on overdue amount.

 

10)  Additional interest will be applied @ 2% on outstanding amount for overdue period (i.e. where payment is not received on or before the due date) in each purpose-wise separate account under RCL, DBD, SBD & Channel financing.

Action:- Branch has to calculate additional interest manually and collect the same for the days where the payment is overdue.

 

11)  Additional interest will be applied @ 2% on entire outstanding amount when account turns into an NPA, & will continue to be applied till it’s upgradation to Standard Asset.

Action:- The system will apply additional interest of 2% on outstanding amount inclusive of overdrawn amount where limit is sanctioned & also for Loan accounts.

 

12)  Interest @ 18% will be charged to the extent of overdrawn amount above the sanctioned limit due to monthly interest debited, TOD/excess drawals allowed, etc. to all CC accounts & CC type facilities like FLXLN, all type of SOD accounts, STCC and Salary CC.

Action:- The system will apply additional interest of 18% on overdrawn amount.

 

13)  Interest @ 18% will be charged on entire outstanding amount in case of Invoked BG & Forced L/C A/c.

Action:- Branch has to create offset interest master for applying additional interest.

 

VI.    In case of multiple situations inviting application of additional interest as stated above in any single account, the total additional interest to be charged in such accounts shall not exceed 4% for all the situations considered together except point no.12 and 13 mentioned above.

 

VII.  In case of multiple situations (except those where additional interest is collected through system) warranting additional interest as above, branches should ensure that the applicable additional interest is recovered as per actions advised.

 

VIII.   Branches need not send separate letters in respect of situations warranting application of additional interest as such letters must have been sent already vide IDC Ref. No. HO/CD/465/2018-19 dated 18th July, 2018.

 

IX.   It is presumed that Instructions issued to branches, stationery department , IT department, MIS Department & Inspection Department regarding applicability of additional interest, & follow up action in respect thereof in certain situations as advised under CR No. 215 dated 28th September, 2018 have either been complied with already, or will be complied with as & when necessity thereof arises..

 

X. Additional charges for foreclosure/takeover of Loans & Advances A/cs.:-

(a)   In case of Housing Loan accounts proposed to be taken over by other banks, additional interest for foreclosure /prepayment will not be levied whether rate of interest is floating or fixed.

 

(b)    Also, in case of other loans (both secured & unsecured) in the names of individuals proposed to be taken over by other banks, additional interest will also not be levied for foreclosure/ prepayment.

 

(c)    In case of Loans & Advances sanctioned to non-individual Loanees/ borrowers proposed to be taken by other Banks/FIs, foreclosure/Take Over charges @ 2% + GST will be levied on outstanding amount as on the date of foreclosure/ prepayment.

 

(d)    In case of Loans & Advances closed before maturity by non-individual borrowers through their own sources, no additional interest will be levied for foreclosure.

 

XI.   In respect of Loans/SODs against NSC/KVP/RBI Bonds/FDR (Accrued Value)/ LIPs (Surrender Value) to Officers & Staff of our Bank, rate of interest will be 1% less than what is applicable to the borrowers of the Bank. The employee should own the said securities solely or jointly with spouse (as per MOU).

 

XII. In case LATD/SOD is sanctioned against Term Deposits to a Proprietorship firm, Partnership Firm, Limited Liability Partnership Firm or Private Limited Company and, if the said Term Deposits are in personal name(s) of the Proprietor/Partners/ Directors, the Term Deposits should not be treated as belonging to Third Party and rate of interest applicable will be 1% above TD interest rate. In case the LATD/SOD is sanctioned against third party deposits, rate of interest will be 2% above the interest applicable to Term Deposits.

 

XIII.   It s reiterated that Facility for Housing Loan Borrowers for switchover to current rate of interest has been discontinued vide Circular ILA 81 dated 17th October, 2018.

 

XIV.  External Credit Rating - Tie up with CRISIL for availing their services: - A concession of 0.25% in rate of interest in respect of credit facilities is available to enterprises which earn any of CRISIL’s top 3 ratings and also any of our Bank’s internal top three ratings.

 

(For example, if our borrower is internally rated as any of the three i.e. AAA, AA or A, and is also simultaneously rated by CRISIL as any of their top three i.e. MSE 1/SME 1, MSE 2/SME 2 or MSE 3/SME 3, a concession of 0.25% in rate of interest will be offered in what would otherwise have been applicable according to our own internal rating).

 

XV.    Loan & Advances accounts likely to be closed during the month of January, 2019:- As the revised rates of interest are in the process of being updated by IT Department, branches will ensure manual recovery of interest at the revised rates applicable from 1st January, 2019 till such updating takes place.

 

XVI.   Other Instructions

 

1.  Planning & Marketing Dept. shall arrange to carry out the necessary modifications in advertising material with the new rates.

 

2.  I.T. Dept. shall make necessary changes in our Bank’s website and in our system accordingly. It will also send SMS to all the borrowers/sureties as per the text given below:-

“Bank has revised rates of interest on Loans & Advances w.e.f. 1st January, 2019. Contact Base Branch for further details.”

3.  Inspection Dept. shall ensure through Concurrent Audit that change in rate of interest is done properly by inspecting randomly sampled accounts of all types of loans.

4.  Branches will arrange to display the notice of revision in rates of interest on Loans & Advances as per Annexure hereto.

 

XVII.    While interest rates have increased for most of the categories, there is reduction in interest rates for a few categories. Branches may be approached by customers seeking clarifications for the changes in interest rates. It is, therefore, need of the hour to explain to the customers the necessity for bringing about changes.

 

XVIII.  It has been emphasised time & again to maintain & increase the quality portfolio of loans & advances. Increase in rates of interest should not result in dampening the enthusiasm in our march towards this goal. Incharge of branches are advised to involve all staff members in our earnest efforts in this direction. It should be ensured that the targets given in respect of loans and advances are achieved well in advance.

 

XIX.  Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all officials/staff members at your branch and ensure strict compliance.
