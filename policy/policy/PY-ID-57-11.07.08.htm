Circular ID No. 57 dated 11 July 2008

 

Interest Rates on Term Deposits - Revision

 

          A reference is invited to the Circular ID No. 52 dated 28 May 2007 on revision in interest rates. A reference is also invited to the Circular ID No. 56 dated 12th January 2008 on payment of additional interest on bulk deposits.  You are aware that Reserve Bank of India has initiated various measures to contain inflationary pressure.  As a result there is upward trend in interest rates on bank deposits and many banks have revised interest rate on deposits.  The position in regard to the deposit growth in our Bank as also in regard to the prevailing interest rates on term deposits in the market was reviewed and recommended by ALCO to increase the interest rates on term deposits by 0.50% in the period of 91 days and above but upto 24 months for General Public including Registered Trusts, Co-operative Societies and Senior Citizens and also to offer 0.50% extra rates on bulk deposit of Rs.15.00 lacs and more.  The Board in its Meeting held on 10th July 2008 approved the recommendation made by ALCO and decided to revise the interest rates on term deposits as under.  The revision in interest rates becomes effective from 14th July 2008 and will continue to be in force till further notice.

3.      Bulk Deposits: - It has been decided to introduce the concept of offering additional interest over and above the rates stated in para 2 above on bulk deposits of Rs.15.00 lacs or more for duration of above 12 months kept by depositors in all categories as under :-

Amounts

Rs. 15.00 lacs and above

Bulk deposit means deposit of Rs.15.00 lacs and above accepted in a single day in an individual branch and for which single receipt is issued.

 

4.      The interest rates on the deposits under “Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme” (ATSS) will be as under:

                   General Public:      9.00% p.a.

                   Senior Citizens:     9.50% p.a.

          The rates under the above Scheme remains unchanged.

                  

5.      All the revised term deposit rates as above to be offered to all customers will be made applicable to only fresh deposits and on renewal of maturing deposits.  The premature closure of existing fixed deposit account for the purpose of reinvestment in fixed deposit to avail higher rate of interest will attract penalty at the prevailing rate.

 

6.        The deposits of the Bank’s Staff will continue to earn additional interest of 1% over and above the applicable rate of interest subject to the conditions stipulated in the Circular ID No. 48 dated 29 September 2006.

 

7.      All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposits, issued earlier from time to time, continue to remain in force.
