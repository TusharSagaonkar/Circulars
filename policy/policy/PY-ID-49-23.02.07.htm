Circular ID No. 49   dated  23   February 2007

 

 

 

Interest Rates on Term Deposits - revision

 

 

          Interest rates on deposits were last  revised by our bank w.e.f. 7 February 2005,  vide Circular ID No. 46 dated 5 February 2005.  Partial revision in these rates was  introduced w.e.f. 1 October 2006 as advised in the circular  ID No. 48 dated 29 September 2006. Consequent upon declaration of Monetary and Credit Policy review by the Reserve Bank in the month of January 2007, a rising trend in  interest rates on deposit has been observed. Even some of the leading Urban Co-op. Banks and large Public Sector banks like SBI, have revised their interest rates on deposit upwards.  The Board considered the emergent  market  trends in this regard in their meetings held on 21and 22 February 2007 and have decided to raise the interest rates offered by our bank on term deposits  and also effect some changes in the period wise  slabs  for term deposit.

          The above interest rates will become effective from 26 February 2007 and will continue to be in force till 31 March 2007.  Further changes in interest rates, if any, will be advised in due course.  The revised term deposit rates to be offered to all customers will be made applicable  to only fresh  deposits and on renewal of maturing deposits during this  period. The rate of interest on term  deposits under Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme as advised in Circular OC No. 363 remains unchanged.  The deposits of the  Bank’s Staff will continue to earn additional interest of 1% over and above the applicable rate of interest  subject to the conditions stipulated in the circular  ID No. 48 dated 29 September 2006.

 

          All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposits, issued  earlier from time to time, continue to  remain in force.
