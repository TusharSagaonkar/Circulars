3.   Loans to Paper & Stationery Traders at Fort:-

At present the rate of interest applicable to Paper & Stationery Traders at Fort branch  in terms of Circular ILA No. 64 dated 1.5.2012 is PLR + 0.50% i.e. 14.00% p.a. for all existing as well as new credit facilities.  There was representation from existing borrowal accounts for reduction in rate of interest.  This matter was discussed in the aforesaid Board Meeting and it has been   decided to continue interest @PLR + 0.50% i.e. 14.00% p.a. with rebate of 0.50%.  This Rebate will be paid on half yearly basis in the month of October and April subject to borrower complying with the following terms & conditions:-

(i)   No Excess Drawals

(ii)  Non-issue of cheques without maintaining adequate balance in the account.

(iii)    Regular & timely submission of Stock & Book Debts statements  and DP covering total outstanding of all working capital limits.

(iv)    Monthly Turnover more than 25%

(v)  Payment of monthly interest before 15th of succeeding month

(vi)    Timely submission of Audited Financial Statements alongwith Audit Report

(vii)  Timely submission of renewal/enhancement or review proposals.

 

 

4.     Loans for repairs & renovation of flat/house/shops/galas etc.:-

 

        New Loan Scheme as per Circular CR No. 73 dated 31.08.2012

 

        Rate of Interest:  PLR  + 0.50% i.e. 14% p.a.

 

5.     Loans & Advances to Medical Practitioners:-

 

        Rate of Interest:        Upto 84 months: PLR – 0.50% i.e. 13.00%p.a.

                                Above 84 months  upto 120 months:  Equal to PLR

                                i.e. 13.50%

 

        6.     Loans to professional other than Doctor:-

                Rate of Interest:        Priority        - PLR – 0.50% i.e. 13% p.a. (floating)

                                        Non-Priority         - PLR + 0.50% i.e. 14% p.a. (floating)               

The Rate of Interest on other Loans & Advances as per ILA No. 64 dated 1.5.2012 shall remain unchanged.

All other terms and conditions as incorporated in the Credit Policy 2012-13 remain unchanged.

        Officials/staff and ensure compliance
