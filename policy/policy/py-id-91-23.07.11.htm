Circular ID No. 91 dated 23rd July, 2011

 

Revision in Interest Rates on Term Deposits

to Co-operative Societies including Housing, Credit, etc. and Bulk Deposits

 

         

 

Please refer to our Circular ID No. 90 dated 29.06.2011. ALCO in its meeting held on 19.07.2011 reviewed the position of deposits mobilized by our Bank and its deployment and the rates of interest offered by various Banks. The committee felt that the rate of interest offered to Co-op. Societies including Housing, Credit etc. and Bulk Deposit for the period “Above 12 months and inclusive of 24 months” is on higher side and therefore it was recommended to revise the same and keep it @ 10.35% as offered to General Public and Trusts.

 

Board in its meeting held on 19.07.2011 decided to revise the rates of interest w.e.f. 25.07.2011 on term deposits for the category of Co-op. Societies including Housing, Credit Societies, and Bulk Deposits only for the period “Above 12 months and upto and inclusive of 24 months” which is 10.35%. For this period i.e. for “Above 12 months and upto and inclusive of 24 months” the rate of interest for Senior Citizens remains unchanged i.e. 10.50%.

2.     Staff Term Deposit :

 

You are advised to note that rate for Staff Term Deposit for all slabs will be 1.00% more than the rate applicable for General Public and Trust.

Further we advise that the rate of interest of 0.5% offered for Staff Term Deposit above 12 months and upto and inclusive of 24 months as per Circular ID No. 90 will continue. Thus, 1% higher rate for this slab is with prospective effect which please note.    

 

 

3.     I.T. Dept. is advised to arrange to display this information on Bank's website and make necessary changes in the system.
