Circular ID No. 148 dated 9th April 2025

Payment of additional interest on Domestic Term Deposits of Senior Citizens – Clarification

_________________________________________________________________________________

 

Reserve Bank of India has issued the Letter in the form of Advisory Ref. No. CO.DOS.DSD.No. S7974/12-22-350/2024-25 dated 10.02.2025 to Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks on subject “Interest Rates on term deposits for senior citizen”. As per said letter RBI has observed certain instances wherein higher eligible rates of interests were not provided to some resident Indian senior citizens.

 

Further, multiple instances have also been observed wherein the prevailing (non- senior citizen) rate of interest continue to be applied on renewal of term deposits of customers even after attaining the senior citizen status. This may be due to absence of system –generated alerts, lack of periodic review of such accounts or other operational lapses.

 

Our Bank has also drafted the Policy of “Interest Rate on Deposits” which was placed before Administrator’s Committee Meeting dated 02.12.2024 and same met with the approval. As per said Policy, Bank will pay additional interest over and above the normal interest rates to resident Indian Senior Citizens as approved by Board from time to time.

 

Senior Citizens will be paid additional interest on term deposits as decided from time to time.

 

In this connection the following procedure suggested for adoption by the Indian Banks' Association (IBA) to be followed by the Branches while allowing additional interest to senior citizens.

 

(a) Eligibility Criteria: - A person who has completed the age of 60 years to be treated as a senior citizen for being eligible to get the benefit of the additional interest on his term deposit/s.

(b)     Verification of Age:-

     Opening of a New Account: At the time of opening a new deposit account of a senior citizen, branches should satisfy about the age of the depositor through verification of any of the following documents :-

-      Secondary School Leaving Certificate indicating date of birth

-      Aadhar Card

-      Driving License

-      Voter's Identity Card

-      Pension Payment Order

-      Birth Certificate issued by the competent authority

-      Passport

-      Any other OVD document acceptable to the bank.

 

(c) Renewal of Accounts of Senior Citizens: - once the age of senior citizen is verified for accepting deposits under the special scheme for senior citizens, it is not necessary to seek proof of age for renewal of the deposit or while taking subsequent deposits from him.

 

(d)                Treatment of Joint Deposits: - While accepting deposits of senior citizens jointly, with other persons below the age of sixty, it may be ensured that the name of the Senior Citizens is given as the first name in the application if benefit of additional interest is sought/given.

 

Existing Deposits of Senior Citizens: - The existing as well as new senior citizen depositors will be eligible for the higher interest on term deposits. In case of the existing senior citizens depositors claiming higher interest rate, branches should confirm the age proof and verify their age in the same manner as indicated in (b) above. The said benefit of eligible rate of interest for senior citizen will be applicable only for fresh deposit & /or for deposits renewal/auto renewal on attaining the age of senior citizen. Deposits kept prior to attaining age of senior citizen will not be eligible for higher rate of interest till maturity of such deposits.

 

Example:  If a depositor whose date of birth is 16.01.1964 and he kept the deposit on 10.11.2023 for 2 years then applicable interest for general public will be paid. On renewal/auto renewal of the deposit on 10.11.2025, such depositor will be eligible for higher rate of interest due to attaining the senior citizen status as on date of renewal.

i)    In case of auto renewal/renewal by personal visit of the deposit kept by Senior Citizen on due date, Bank will renew such deposit with similar term at the interest rate applicable to Senior Citizen.

ii)  In case of death of Senior Citizen after the date of auto renewal, the Bank will pay additional interest applicable for Senior Citizen till date of maturity.

iii) In case of death of Senior Citizen before the date of auto renewal i.e. date of maturity, the Bank will pay interest at contracted rate till the date of maturity & after the date of maturity i.e. date of auto renewal, the interest applicable for saving deposit will be paid and extra interest paid if any will be recovered by the Bank while making final payment.

iv) At the time of payment of deposit in the name of Senior Citizen with his/her first name with other person and with either or Survivorship clause, the branch will ensure that Senior Citizen is alive and the deposit receipt is duly discharged by him.

 

The benefit of additional interest rate will not be applicable on the term deposit standing in the name of HUF or the Karta of the Hindu Undivided Family (HUF), even if the Karta is a resident Indian Senior Citizen.

 

In the case of terms deposits kept by the Karta in the name of HUF the deposit represents joint ownership of all coparceners and as such benefit of additional interest should not be allowed in case of such deposits.

 

Upon receiving directive from RBI as mentioned in Para (I), we approached our IT Department to implement the necessary system updates to automate the process. However, IT Department has informed us that auto-updates for this functionality are not available in the system and the process needs to be handled manually by branches till the system is develop.

 

As the update could not be executed through the system, a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) have been prepared to ensure uniform compliance across all branches. This SOP outlines the step-by-step process to identify and extend the additional interest benefit to eligible customers. A copy of the SOP is enclosed as Annexure.

 

IT Dept be instructed to generate the list of such senior citizen well in advance who are eligible for the benefit of applicable interest rate & same should be made available to branches as well as to CDEC Dept for necessary action.

 

The responsibility of such modification will be that of CDEC Dept. on the date of renewal/auto renewal if such depositor is having status of senior citizen.

 

Branches should display prominently, the revised interest rates and the higher interest rates offered to senior citizens on their term deposits in the branch premises.

 

All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposit issued from time to time continue to remain in force.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure strict compliance. Non Compliance of above guidelines will be treated as regulatory violation.

 

 

 

 

 

 

       Executive Officer
