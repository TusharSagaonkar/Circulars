Circular ID No. 81 dated 10th February, 2011

[1]

Discretion to offer higher rate of Interest on

Bulk Deposits

 

         

 

To retain the existing Bulk Deposit as well as to attract more and more new Bulk Deposit in the present era of tough competition from other nationalized and leading Co-operative Banks, the Board in its meeting held on 07.02.2011 conferred the powers to General Manager (HRD, Development, Accounts, Inspection & Vigilance) for recommending the cases to quote slightly higher rate of interest upto a certain level than the rate quoted in our I.D. Circular for Managing Director's approval.

 

2.       The higher rate of interest for bulk deposits to be recommended by General Manager (HRD, Devlpt. Accounts, Inspection & Vigilance) and to be approved by Managing Director will depend on Bank's fund position and its requirement on day to day basis.

 

3.       However the rate of interest for bulk deposits to be recommended by General Manager (HRD, Devlpt. Accounts, Inspection & Vigilance) and to be approved by Managing Director on any particular day for the same maturity and same size of deposits will not have any discrimination.

 

4.       Branches are therefore advised that if opportunities are there to get new bulk deposits or branch-in-charges are in the dilemma of retaining the existing bulk deposit for want of higher rate of interest, such cases should be referred to General Manager (HRD., Devlpt. Accounts, Inspection & Vigilance) by giving full details of the case who will recommend the case for higher rate of interest on bulk deposits for Managing Director's approval.

 

5.       We hope that with this relaxation, substantial amount of new bulk deposit will be attracted as also the existing Bulk Deposit will be retained.

 

6.       All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposits, issued earlier from time to time, continue to remain in force.

 

7.       Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all concerned staff members at your branch for getting maximum bulk deposits.

 

                                      

V. S. Morye

       Managing Director
