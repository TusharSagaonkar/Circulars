Circular ID No. 128 Dated 17th October, 2020

SHUBHAMKAROTI - III

______________________________________________________________

 

         

         I.  A new deposit scheme, named “Shubhamkaroti-III”, is being launched on the auspicious occasion of commencement of “Navaratri” on 17th October, 2020, the approval for which was granted in the Board meeting dated 16th October, 2020.

 

       II.  Salient features of the scheme are as under.

 

1)    Name of the Scheme :- "SHUBHAMKAROTI - III"

 

2)    Date of launch :- 17th October, 2020.

 

3)    Date of closure :- 31st December, 2020. Deposits under the scheme shall be accepted till closure of business on 31st December, 2020 whereafter the scheme will be closed for acceptance of deposits.

 

4)    Period of Term Deposit :- 18 months       

 

5)    Types of deposits permissible :- QID and QICRIP (No additional interest for QID scheme)

 

6)    Rate of Interest :- 7 % p. a., which is uniform for all categories of persons, including staff, senior citizens & entities.

(Note:- Rates of interest currently applicable for deposits for all the maturities, including for the maturity of 18 months outside the “Shubhamkaroti-III” scheme, remain unchanged).

 

7)    Persons & entities eligible to place deposits :- Members of the General Public, Staff Members, Senior Citizens, Co-op. Societies, other bodies & entities.

Non-Residents are not eligible to place deposits under this scheme.

 

8)    Whether bulk deposits can be accepted? :- Yes.

 

9)    Effect for deposits sought to be placed on the last day i.e. 31st December, 2020 of the scheme through cheques/instruments realizable through clearing :- Such instruments will be accepted towards deposits under the scheme. However, the Term Deposit Receipt for 18 months will be issued from the date when effects are cleared and interest thereon shall commence from the date of realisation.

 

10) All the other instructions and terms and conditions, applicable to Domestic Term Deposits, issued earlier from time to time continue to remain in force.

 

      III.  Branches will open accounts under two heads for the two types of deposits, namely “SHUB3QIC” for QICRIP and “SHUB3QID” for QID, in the OMNI system.

     IV.  Focus during deposit mobilization

While targeting potential depositors under the scheme, Branches are advised to focus more on mobilization of such deposits from Individuals, Senior Citizens and Co-op. Housing Societies, as such deposits are more stable in nature as compared to the bulk Deposits from Co-op. Credit Societies / Banks.

 

        V.    Marketing Department is advised to urgently do advertisement of the scheme, and supply to branches banners, pamphlets and other marketing material in respect thereof.

 

      VI.    I.T. Dept. is advised to arrange for display of information of the scheme on the Bank's website.

 

    VII.    Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members at your Branch/Dept. for compliance.

 

 

                              

 

                                     

P. S. Salian

       Managing Director
