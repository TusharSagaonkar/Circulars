Circular ID No. 101 dated 29th June, 2012

 

Revision  in Interest Rates on  Term Deposits

 

RBI in its Annual Policy Statement in April 2012 has already given a rate cut of 50 basis points. Further, the interest rates in the market have started softening. It is also observed that the interest rates offered by our Bank are on the higher side as compared to other Banks. Moreover in order to maintain CD Ratio below 70% we were forced to accept Term Deposits at higher rate of interest. This has resulted in substantial increase in cost of deposits of the Bank affecting the profitability of the Bank. 

In view of the above, Board in its meeting held on 26.06.2012 decided to reduce the interest rates on deposits by 50 basis points in the duration ‘Above 13 months & upto & inclusive of 24 months’ & by 25 basis points in the duration ‘ 181 days & above upto & inclusive of 13 months’ w.e.f. 09.07.2012.
