CREDIT POLICY 2011-12

 

1.           Broad Objectives:-

The broad objectives of Credit Policy are:

i)             To give clarity about various aspects of credit including acceptance, appraisal, scrutiny, sanction, delegation, documentation and post-disbursement follow-up.

ii)           To give proper direction to the Branches and other functional heads about the areas in which credit is to be encouraged and to fix maximum credit to be given under different sectors and purposes.

iii)          To manage the Credit Risk i.e. to identify, measure and mitigate the Credit Risk.

iv)          To encourage/achieve and continue minimum of 40% of total Adjusted Net Bank Credit towards Priority Sector Advances and 10% towards Weaker Section.

v)            To maintain the CD Ratio 65% throughout the year and to ensure that the Total Exposure of the Bank includes the Amount Sanctioned but not Disbursed, Un-utilized portion of CC and other Working Capital facilities.

vi)          To strengthen credit appraisal, improve post sanction follow-up, monitor credit facilities and to improve quality of credit.  

vii)        To diversify loan portfolio to different sectors and new areas like export finance / retail finance etc.

viii)       To reduce the gross NPAs of the Bank to a reasonable level during the year by strengthening appraisal, sanction, post disbursement supervision and efficient recovery steps.

ix)          To maximize profitability and return on capital.

3.           Credit Exposure Norms:-

 

Exposure ceiling on credit to Individual/Group Borrowers

    

(i)           Credit Exposure to an individual Borrower does not exceed 15% of capital funds

(ii)          Credit Exposure to a group of Borrowers does not exceed 40% of capital funds.       

(iii)        The maximum per party limit will be Rs. 60.00 Crore and Group limits Rs. 190.00 Crore.

(iv)        In respect of non-fund based facilities, sanctioned limit or outstanding amount whichever is higher, is to be taken into account for computing of exposure level.  

(v)          In case of fully disbursed Term Loan, the principal and interest outstanding in the account will be reckoned for arriving at the amount of credit exposure.

(vi)        In case of partly disbursed Term Loan and working capital limits like CC, RCL, PCL etc., the sanctioned amount or outstanding amount whichever is higher will be reckoned for arriving at the amount of credit exposure.

(vii)       Sector-wise maximum exposure of total Advances will be as under:-

(a)          Housing                                                       -        15%

(b)          Educational Institution                                 -        25%

(c)          Builders & Developers                                   -        10%

(d)          Iron & Steel                                                 -        15%  

                                                            

(viii)   Real Estate Exposure: Real Estate including Housing, Real Estate and Commercial Real Estate shall not exceed 10% of the total Assets on the audited Balance Sheet as on   31st March of preceding financial year.

Notes:-       

1.    An additional limit of 5 per cent of total assets will be available for housing loans granted to individuals by the UCBs for purchase/ construction of dwelling units up to  ` 10 lakh.

2.    For reckoning total assets, losses, intangible assets, contra items like bills receivables etc. would be excluded.

3.    Wherever the outstanding has already exceeded the above exposure limits, Bank will have to reduce exposure to bring within the exposure limit.

7.           Co-Applicants (Co-Borrowers):-

(i)           The Co-applicant is necessary for Housing Loans. Co-applicant will mainly be spouse or son or daughter or father or mother of the Principal Borrower and he/she may be an earning or non-earning member. In these cases the income of the Co-Applicant can be clubbed for calculation of eligible loan amount. In the absence of the above Co-applicants, Brother or Sister may be taken as Co-applicant, but their Income shall not be clubbed for calculation of eligible loan amount.

(ii)          In case of joint Agreements in Housing Loans, the Purchasers (Owners) will be the Borrowers and hence there is no need for separate Co-Applicants.

(iii)        In case of Education Loans to Students, the student shall be accepted as Co-applicant if he/she is not a minor. Borrower will mainly be father, mother, brother, sister or any earning relatives of the Co-Applicant.

(iv)        In case of Mortgage Loans etc. the Owner of the Properties can be accepted as Co-applicant if he/she is non-earning member. Borrower will mainly be spouse or son or daughter or father or mother of the Co-Applicant. In these cases the income of the Borrower shall be taken into the for calculation of eligible loan amount.

(v)          The status of Co-applicant in respect of recovery is different from the surety. The Co-applicant being joint Borrower, the award can be obtained against him/her as the Borrower (Judgment Debtor) in case of default.

(vi)        The Co-applicant shall be enrolled as Regular Member of the Bank.

13.        A.  Calculation of Working Capital requirements/Term Loan     

                 I.    Working Capital (Cash Credit/DBD/SBD/WCTL/Channel Credit/ AGCLG/CHQD etc.) Limits:-

(i)       Upto Rs. 1.00 Crore for Borrowers other than SMEs and Rs. 5.00 Crore for SMEs:

(a)      The Assessment of Working Capital requirement of Borrowers other than SMEs requiring fund based Working Capital limits upto Rs. 1.00 Crore and SMEs requiring fund based Working Capital limit upto Rs. 5.00 Crore from banking system shall be made on the basis of their realistic projected annual Turnover.

(b)      The Working Capital requirement is assessed at 25% of the realistic projected Turnover to be shared between the Borrower and the Bank i.e. Borrower contributing 5% of the Turnover as Net Working Capital and Bank providing minimum of 20% of the Turnover.

(c)      Drawals against the limits should however be allowed on the basis of the Drawing Power calculated based on timely submission of monthly statements of Stocks, Receivables and Sundry Creditors and also periodical verification of such statements vis-à-vis physical stocks by Branch Officials.

(ii)      Above Rs. 1.00 Crore for Borrowers other than SMEs and Rs. 5.00 Crore for SMEs:

Here the Bank may adopt Working Capital Gap Method or Production/Process Cycle Method for assessing the Working Capital requirements.

a.        Working Capital Gap Method:-

Current Assets – Current Liabilities excluding outstanding Working Capital Facilities = Working Capital Gap (WCG) – 25% of WCG = Eligible Maximum Permissible Bank finance (MPBF)

b.        Production/Processing Cycle Method:-

Sr. Manager/Manager, Industrial Finance Dept. will visit the industrial unit and work out requirement of working capital based on the existing production/processing cycle as well as realistic Projected Cycle. He will take into account credits received on purchase of raw material, credits allowed on sales, inventory levels of raw materials, work in process, finished goods etc. comparing the same with industry level.

In order to arrive at the future requirements of the unit, he will scrutinize the projected sales for next two years and work out the requirement of the working capital.

               II.    Credit facilities to Contractors/Builders/Developers: 

Credit facilities to Contractors/Builders/Developers shall be worked out as follows:

The basis of fixing the eligible Loan amount is on the basis of projected realistic cash flows till the entire Contract/Project is completed. Here while calculating the projected cash flows, Branches should also take into account the Deposits/Tender amount kept with the principals, the period for preparing the Bills and the time gap between submission of Bills and realization of payment as well as the terms of the contract whereby the period for payment for Bill is specified. They should also consider the period of credit available by the suppliers. After going through the Tender/Project, the Branches should get a fair idea of realistic cash flows and arrive at net deficit until the entire project/contract is completed. The Bank’s fund will be the maximum net deficit minus minimum margin.

The Bank may also consider the sanction of SOD against Immovable Properties (SOD IMP) up to a limit of Rs.300.00 Lakh, to the Builders & Developers of repute, against the mortgage of immovable properties.              III.    Funded Interest Term Loan (FITL):

Funded Interest Term Loan is extended for funding the interest on loans. This has to be considered very judiciously and can be extended to the Borrower when the account is Standard Asset or had never been an NPA account and when the Borrower does not have alternative source of    servicing   the interest on Term Loan.  To consider such a finance,  Bank will   have   to  satisfy  that  the Borrower really does not  have  cash  inflow  generation  from  any  other source to service interest and when there is genuine deviation from the initial projections  like  project/ implementation   delay   for   reasons beyond the control of the Borrower.  Hence, the Borrower does not pay interest but Bank capitalizes the interest through a separate loan account i.e. FITL. (Please refer to detailed Rules and Procedures in the matter).

 

 

 

 

             IV.    Working Capital Term Loan:

Working Capital Term Loan limit is extended in the following cases:-

                     i.    To units/trading accounts where large cash credit limit is being enjoyed, but Drawing Power (DP) is regularly and continuously not available and Bank decides to bifurcate working capital limit into cash credit and loan (WCTL) taking into account the available DP and collateral securities. The CC limit will be covered by DP and WCTL limit shall be secured by hypothecation Book Debts above 90 days (other than Bad Debts) and collateral securities. Weight-age shall also be given to new/additional collateral securities offered for availing WCTL limits. 

                   ii.    WCTL is also granted while reviving the sick unit under rehabilitation package. Under rehabilitation & restructuring of sick units, additional finance is given for increasing production capacity, purchase of raw materials and for cost of production including payment of salaries and wages etc. In both above cases the DP is not applicable to WCTL.

                  iii.    To Contractors/ Industrial & Manufacturing Units/ other Trading accounts where Cash Credit limit is being enjoyed/ applied and Drawing Power (DP) is available and Bank may also sanction WCTL taking into account the available DP, MPBF and additional collateral securities. The CC & WCTL limit shall be covered by DP. The Bank may sanction only WCTL instead of CC Limits considering their nature of business activities, to the Applicants who are not enjoying CC limits but requested for CC Limits. Weight-age shall also be given to collateral securities offered for availing WC limits.

Agencies beyond their projected Sales/ Turnover. The amount of STCL shall not exceed Rs.5.00 Crore and maximum period of repayment shall not exceed 12 months. The STCL will be secured by the extension of the existing mortgage or any other securities and/or hypothecation of stock &/or book debts. Repayment will be made in lump sum and interest has to be paid every month by the Borrower. Service Charges will be 1.2% of the STCL amount and Rate of Interest will be the actual rate applicable to the Borrower. (Refer Annexure No.48)

   VII.      Medium Term Corporate Loan (MTCL):

Service Charges and Share money will be as applicable to Secured Loans.

34.        Grant of Moratorium Period & Rescheduling of various Term Loans:- 

A)      Grant of Moratorium Period:- The Branch/Industrial Finance Dept.  Officials and DGM of Zonal Offices should discuss in detail about the schedule of the project implementation, gestation period and contingencies involved in   completion of the project. Thereafter, in consultation with the Borrower or their consultant, the officials should work out realistic schedule of completion of the project. Based upon the same, disbursement of loan shall be made. These officials will work out the initial moratorium period required for commencement of the production, conversion into sales, realization of Book Debts and ultimately determine the month from which the Borrower will be in a position to repay   Bank’s   dues   along with interest.   However,   interest payment shall be made monthly from the date of disbursement. The proper approach in working out moratorium period will help in completion of project smoothly and avoid occurrence NPAs. In no case the repayment period of the Term Loan shall exceed 84 months including the moratorium period except expressly sanctioned by the Board.     

In case of WCTL limit sanctioned within total Working Capital limits, duly secured by hypothecation of stocks and Book Debts, number of EMIs should be determined by Branches/Zonal Offices and recommended accordingly.

B)      Rescheduling of various Term Loans:- It is observed that initial holiday period or moratorium period is not properly determined. This results into Borrowers approaching the Bank again for Reschedulement of repayment of their loans. Hence, grant and determination of moratorium period at the time of sanction assumes great importance.

          Re-Schedulement of Loan or Debt Restructuring can be done in genuine cases. However, as per RBI’s guidelines, if the Bank restructures or reschedules the Standard loans and working capital limits, such accounts have to be downgraded to sub-standard category and that of other categories to one step below of the existing category. Hence, proper care should be taken while recommending moratorium period & repayment period by IFD & Zonal Offices.

          In case the Borrower repays in advance considerable amount of Term Loan and requests for reduction of the amount of EMI, The Branch In-charge can reschedule such loans and it will not be treated as Re-schedulement. However, the total number of EMIs shall not be changed.
