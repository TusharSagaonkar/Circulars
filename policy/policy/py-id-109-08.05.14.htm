Circular ID No. 109 dated 8th May, 2014

 

i.  Revision of Interest Rates on certain slabs of Term Deposits.

ii. Discontinuation of Abhyudaya Golden Jubilee Special Deposit Scheme.

w.e.f. 12.05.2014

 

Considering the rising cost of deposits, interest rates scenario in Banking Industry as a whole and on receiving the recommendations from ALCO, our Board in its meeting held on 25.04.2014 has decided to reduce rates of interest on Term Deposits for certain slabs for General Public as well as for Senior Citizens. The revised rates are as under which will come into effect from Monday, 12th May, 2014.

4. Definition of Bulk Deposits: Bulk Deposit means Term Deposit of ` 15.00 lakh and above accepted on a single day in any individual branch either in lumpsum or in piecemeal received through cash/clearing/transfer and/or by way of renewal of various term deposits maturing on the same day, in the name of any Natural or Legal Person.

 

5. Other instructions:

1.   Offering of the rates of interest for Abhyudaya Golden Jubilee Special Deposit Scheme for the duration of 24 months and upto & inclusive of 36 months for Domestic as well as for NRE Deposit as per Circular ID No. 107 dated 16th January, 2014 have been discontinued and new rates would be made applicable from 12th May, 2014 as per this circular.

 

  1. The rate of interest for Staff deposit for all slabs/durations will be 1% more than the rates applicable for General Public.

 

  1. The interest rate for deposits under ATSS i.e. 9.75% remain unchanged.

 

  1. The Staff/checking Officials of Term Deposit section at the branch should check the correctness of interest rates quoted on deposit receipt as per above instructions before handing over the receipt to the depositor.

 

  1. Marketing Dept. is advised to update and provide pamphlets and other printing material to the Branches.

 

  1. I.T Dept. is advised to display this information on Bank’s website and make necessary changes in the system.

 

  1. All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposit issued earlier from time to time continue to remain in force.
