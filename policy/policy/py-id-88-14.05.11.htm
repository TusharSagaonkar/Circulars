Circular ID No. 88  dated 14th May, 2011

Issue of Term Deposit with the first name of family members of Bank's staff

 

         

Please refer to our Circular ID No. 10 dated 2nd July, 1996 and Circular ID No. 48 dated 29th September, 2006.

 

As per instructions contained in the last paragraph of circular ID No. 48  dated 29.09.2006 referred to above, it was decided that in cases where benefit of additional interest is offered to staff members on deposits, it should be specifically ensured that the staff member must be the first holder of deposit in case of deposits kept in joint names. It is also reiterated that declaration from the staff member/depositor stating that the monies deposited belongs to the staff.

 

In the Board Meeting held on 10.05.2011, it has been decided as under :

 

i)             Additional interest as decided by the Bank payable to a member or a retired member of Bank's staff will be paid on Term Deposit in the joint names of spouse, son(s), daughter(s), brother(s), sister(s), mother and father who are dependent on our staff members. Such Term Deposit accounts may be opened with first name of the above mentioned relatives, subject to the conditions that staff member should submit the declaration as per enclosed format given in Annexure – I and that the amount of Term Deposit is raised by debit to staff member's account maintained with our Bank.

 

ii)           A member of the bank's staff means a person employed on a regular basis, whether full-time or part-time, and includes a person recruited on probation or employed on a contract of a specified duration or on deputation and an employee taken over in pursuance of any scheme of amalgamation, but does not include a person employed on casual basis;

 

iii)          Provided further that in the case of persons taken on deputation for a fixed duration, or on a contract of a fixed duration, the benefit shall cease to accrue on the expiry of the term of deputation or contract, as the case may be;

 

iv)          A retired member of the Bank staff means an employee retiring whether on super-annuation or otherwise, but does not include an employee retired compulsorily or in consequence of disciplinary action.  

 

The above instructions will come into effect from 16th May, 2011.        

 

Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members at your Branch/Dept. for compliance.

 

                                      

V. S. Morye

       Managing Director
