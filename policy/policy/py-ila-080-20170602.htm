II.  Correction in Loans to Staff & Officers against Cash Collaterals as per MOU/MOA.

 

1. Please refer Para III. 3. (on Page 3) of the circular under reference (i. e. ILA No. 79 dated 15th April, 2017). An error had crept in the abovementioned Para in as much as Loans/SODs against FDR got clubbed together with Loans/SODs against NSC/KVP/RBI Bonds/LIPs (Surrender Value) to officers & staff of our Bank. In an exercise to rectify the error, it is being advised that the said Para should be read after ignoring the mention therein of (Loans/SODs against) “FDR”. The revised Para after rectification as above will read as under:

 

“In respect of Loans/SODs against NSC/KVP/RBI Bonds/LIPs (Surrender Value) to Officers & Staff of our Bank, rate of interest will be 1% less than what is applicable to the borrowers of the Bank. The employee should own the said securities solely or jointly with spouse (as per MOU).”

 

2. A suitable correction may also be made in item no. A (xi) under the subject “Pricing & Grading and Interest Policy” on page no. 45 of “Credit Policy-Credit Rules & Procedures w. e. f. 24.08.2016”.

 

III. Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all officials/staff members at your branch and ensure strict compliance.
