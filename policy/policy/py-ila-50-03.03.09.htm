Thus the rate of interest to be charged to all Educational Institutions on their Loans and Advances will be 12% w.e.f. 01-04-2009 irrespective of date of sanction/disbursement as well as for new loans. This change should be advised to all Educational Institutions as per format enclosed as Annexure 'A'.

 

2.       In terms of our Credit policy it is our practice to obtain Audited Balance Sheet & Financial statements from all the Educational Institutions to whom we have granted Loans and Advances mainly with a view to assess financial viability and health of the institutions. This has no relation with the rate of interest to be charged on these accounts as per gradation. If these institutions are not submitting their audited Balance Sheet and Financial Statements within the stipulated period, such institutional accounts were to be charged 3% penalty. The aspect of penalty was not properly communicated to these institutions in the past.  There was also lack of clarity in interpretation of penalty clause. Some of the institutions, therefore, represented the matter to the Bank for waiver of penalty. Considering the views of the institutions the board has, taken a decision not to charge any penalty for accounts of these Educational Institutions upto this financial year. If any branch has levied any penalty the entry should be reversed under advice to respective Educational Institutions. Further, to streamline the procedure we advise our branches to send a letter to all the Educational Institutions as per Format enclosed herewith as Annexure 'B', requesting them to


submit their audited Balance Sheet and Financial Statements for last year (as on 31-03-2008) latest by 31-03-2009. In case they fail to submit the same within the stipulated time, penalty @ 1% will be made applicable w.e.f. 01-04-2009, upto the date of submission of their audited Balance Sheet and Financial Statements.

 

3.       Branches should note that it is necessary to advise all the borrowers the revised rate of interest. A letter in the enclosed format 'C' will be generated and sent to all Cash Credit Account holders and secured loan accounts by I.T. Department. For ready reference format is enclosed herewith as Annexure 'C'.

1.           Consequent upon the reduction of interest rate @ 10% on Cash Credit (secured OD) & Loans against collateral securities like NSCs/RBI Bonds/LIC/KVP for New Sanction as per our Circular ILA No. 49 dated 30th January 2009, a note was submitted by one of our branches that the revised rate of 10% should be extended to the existing loan accounts also, as customers including the staff members who are enjoying SOD facility are opting for closure of existing accounts (sanctioned before 01-02-2009) and applying for new limits. The issue was put up to the board in its meeting held on 24-02-2009. In terms of the Board's decision we advise that instructions given in our Circular ILA No. 49 dated 30th January 2009 will remain unchanged. The SOD borrowers including our staff members will be allowed to close the existing accounts and to have the new limits subject to levy of service charges as laid down in our 'Service Charges Manual' without any exemption to staff SODs.

 

2.           Further, as per our Circular ILA No. 49 dated 30th January 2009, we have reduced the rate of penalty from 3% to 1% for the defaulting account. However, there is no mention that for every default, 1% penalty has to be charged also there is no mention for the maximum penalty to be charged for the defaulting accounts having more than one defaults. In terms of the decision taken by the Board in its meeting held on 27-02-2009 it has been decided that only 1% penalty will be charged for one or any number of defaults simultaneously.

 

3.           Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all concerned staff members at your branch and ensure compliance. The letters as per Annexure 'A' & 'B' should be sent to all the Educational Institutions having borrowal accounts with us immediately on receipt of this circular and a copy should be preserved in the respective loan file.
