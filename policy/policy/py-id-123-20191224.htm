Circular ID No. 123 dated 24th December, 2019

 

“Shubhamkaroti - II”

A Special Deposit Scheme

__________________________________________________________________________

         

I.   Bank has launched a Special Deposit Scheme named “Shubhamkaroti - II”. Deposits under the scheme will be accepted by branches with effect from 25th December, 2019 until 31st January, 2020, whereafter acceptance of deposits under the scheme will be discontinued.

 

II.    1) Period for which the deposits will be accepted under the scheme shall be 18 months.

2) Rates of interest under the scheme shall be as under.

i)      8.10 % for General Public, Trusts, Co-op Societies, Institutions, Staff etc.

ii)     8.25 % for Senior Citizens.

3) Deposits will be accepted & Fixed Deposit Receipts will be issued only under QID & QICRIP.

 

III.  1) IT Department is hereby being instructed to create a different head for the “Shubhamkaroti – II” deposit scheme in the OMNI Software. It will also develop a printing programme for the “Shubhamkaroti – II” deposit receipts so that the schemes will get indicated in Ref. No. Column as “SHUB2QID” & “SHUB2QIC”. Anticipating that creation of the new head will take some time, branches are hereby being instructed to park, in the meanwhile, the deposits meant for “Shubhamkaroti – II” under “Sundry Deposits” & transfer them to the proper accounting head after the same is created. Issue of the receipts to customers against the deposits may be postponed until the relative software in installed & enabled.

 

2) The rate of interest on the funds meant for the scheme, but parked under “Sundry Deposits” in the interim, shall be as per the interest rate applicable under the Scheme from the date of their interim parking.

 

IV.   All other extant instructions in respect of fixed deposits placed with the Bank will continue to be applicable for the deposits under “Shubhamkaroti – II”.

 

V.     Our Bank has decided upon a goal to mobilise deposits to the tune of Rs. 200 Crore under the scheme for which we need to strive over the next 38 days. Branch-wise sub-goals are advised in terms of the excel sheet annexed. Therefore, branches are exhorted to launch an active campaign for mobilisation of deposits under “Shubhamkaroti – II” and garner maximum deposits thereunder, at least to the extent of goals indicated. We shall watch the efforts made in this behalf with keen interest.

 

VI.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all members of the staff at the Branch and ensure grand success.

 

 

                                     

P. S. Salian

       Managing Director
