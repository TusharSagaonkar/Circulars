Sub: Abhyudaya Gold Deposit Scheme.

          A review of the achievement in mobilizing deposits by branches under the above special scheme was carried out on completion of the first fortnight since launching of the scheme on 16.07.2007.  Based on the observations made by the Higher Authorities, the following clarifications are being issued for guidance of the branches & their staff:

 

(i)      The deposit mobilization figures reported by branches do not show a growth of real new deposits.  A major portion was seen to comprise of the deposits transferred from Saving Bank A/c of the existing depositors or re-investment of matured fixed deposits.  Though such switching of deposits by the existing depositors is legitimate, it is not conducive to the objective of the scheme viz. mobilizing of fresh deposits.

 

(ii)     The extra interest of 0.25% offered is payable on bulk deposits of Rs.25 lacs & above received by the branch at a time in a single day.  It is further clarified in this context that such extra interest will not be admissible where the deposit amount of Rs.25 lacs or more is made up of cumulative amounts of deposits including fixed deposits kept prior to 16th July, 2007 (kept on various days during the period) or of the deposits kept under the scheme at different branches. 

 

          Further, it has been decided in the Board meeting held on 25.07.2007 the depositors who deposit Rs.2.00 lacs & above in fixed deposits for a period of 12 months & above & in the new Abhyudaya Gold Deposit Scheme can be made regular members.  The branches may take necessary action as per the existing procedure to enroll the eligible depositors under the scheme as regular members, if they are new customers and are not already holding membership of the banks.   However, their membership is to be made coterminous with the maturity of their deposits & therefore, a membership resignation application and letter should also be obtained alongwith the application for membership.  The branch should retain the membership resignation application and letter with the relative account opening form and ensure the cancellation of the membership on maturity of deposit & take further action for refunding the amount of share capital.  The depositor may be advised at the time of repayment of the FD that the amount of share capital will be refunded to him separately by a pay order.  They should affix a stamp “membership to be cancelled” on account opening form and note under special instruction on the computer.  Care should also be taken to ensure that such member has not incurred any direct/indirect liability subsequently, before cancelling the membership.

 

          Finally, it is needless to emphasize that hardly a fortnight is left for conclusion of deposit mobilization month & we are far away from the target of Rs.150 crores set for the scheme.  All staff members should, therefore, work wholeheartedly for making the deposit mobilization drive successful.
