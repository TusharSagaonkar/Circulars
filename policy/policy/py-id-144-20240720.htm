Circular ID No. 144 dated 20th July 2024

 “ABHYUDAYA-200”and “ABHYUDAYA-100”

Extension of the above two Special Deposit Schemes for Mobilisation till 31st March 2025

on the same Respective ROIs, Terms, and Conditions and under same codes

_______________________________________________________________________________________

 

         I.    Attention of branches is invited to the Circular ID No. 140 dated the 15th December 2023 and the Circular ID No. 143 Dated 7th March 2024 on the two captioned schemes respectively. In terms of the latter Circular ID No. 143 Dated 7th March 2024, modifications were also made in the “ABHYUDAYA-200” scheme.

 

       II.    The deposit mobilised under the captioned scheme have matured or are maturing. The matured deposits are currently automatically parked in the TDMTD account and the customers are withdrawing such deposits.

 

     III.    In view of the above, in terms of a submission approved by the Administrator’s committee on the 11th July 2024, it has been decided to continue mobilisation of deposits under the two captioned deposit schemes with the same codes, at the same respective applicable rates of interest, and the same respective terms and conditions as mentioned in the relative circulars under reference, until 31st March 2025 unless contrary instructions are given in this behalf.

 

     IV.    Instructions to branches for renewal of proceeds lying in TDMTD :- Branches are advised to renew manually the proceeds of the deposits currently lying in TDMTD account from the date of transfer of the proceeds to the TDMTD. The exercise for renewal of the deposit lying in the TDMTD account should be completed before 22.07.2024. Further, the deposits under captioned scheme maturing from 20.07.2024 will be auto renewed in OMNI system by IT department.

 

       V.    Instructions to IT department for auto-renewal of deposits maturing on or after the date of the instant circular :- In respect of the deposits under the two captioned schemes which will fall due from the date of the instant circular onwards, the IT department will arrange to auto-renew them under respective schemes under advice to the respective customer through a text message (SMS).

 

     VI.    Branches and the Zonal Offices under which they function need to note that the goals for deposit mobilisation which were prescribed but not reached must now be at least reached if not exceeded.

 

   VII.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

                                      

P. S. Salian

       Executive Officer
