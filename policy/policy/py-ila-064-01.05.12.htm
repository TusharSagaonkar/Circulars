Circular ILA No. 64 dated 1st May, 2012

 

Revision of lending rates w.e.f. 01.05.2012

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Please refer to our ILA NO. 61 dated 27.09.2011 whereby we have revised interest rates on Loans & Advances w.e.f. 01.10.2011 which are linked to PLR.

 

In view of increase in overall rates of interest on term deposits, payment of interest on S.B. Deposits on the basis of daily products w.e.f. 01.04.2011 coupled with 0.5% increase in rate of interest on Savings Bank Deposits w.e.f. 03.05.2011 as per RBI directives, increase in establishment cost, the cost of funds has increased substantially.

 

Considering all these aspects and in view of the recommendations made by ALCO, the Board in its meeting held on 24.04.2012 has decided to increase Bank’s PLR from 13.00% to 13.50% w.e.f.01.05.2012.  Resultantly, on all our Credit Facilities, the rate of interest to be charged will be increased by 0.5%. However, there is no change in effective interest rates in the recently revised rates vide our IDC Ref.No.MD-920/    /2011-12 on Surety Loans, Housing Loans, Mortgage Loans and SODIMP sanctioned on or after 07.03.2012. We enclose the revised Chart for rates of interest on various Credit Facilities effective from 01.05.2012.

 

 

Our Kankavli Branch and Branches under Udupi Region, are advised to act as per our Circular ILA No. 63 dated 29.03.2012 in case of Commercial Loans and Advances sanctioned upto and including ` 100.00 Lakh.

 

Branches, MIS Dept. and Accounts Dept. should note that the housing loans sanctioned before 01.04.2011 upto Rs.20.00 Lakh and Housing Loans sanctioned on or after 01.04.2011 upto Rs. 25.00 Lakh will be classified  under Priority Sector.

As our Bank’s lending Rates are linked with PLR, Branches should note to charge the revised rate whatever and wherever applicable. Penal interest @ 1% should be charged for non submission of statement of account and balance sheet within prescribed time.

In case of Housing and other loans, it has been decided not to increase the amount of EMI but to extend period of repayment of loan (i.e. No. of EMIs are increased accordingly). The purpose of extending period of EMI is to ensure that the loanee is in a position to repay the loan as done in the past. This will ensure that there will not be default in repayment of loan consequent upon increase in interest rate. In case of Housing loan, maximum no. of EMI is 240 months and hence the increase in no. of EMIs could be made upto 240 months only. In case the loan is already sanctioned for 240 months and EMI is determined accordingly, there is no alternative but to increase the amount of EMI.   

Branches are advised to display prominently the changed Chart of Rates of Interest w.e.f. 01.05.2012 on Public Notice Board and also send letters to the Borrowers as per Annexure - C , Annexure - D and Annexure - E enclosed regarding upward revision in rate of interest through peons/courier. In case of Housing Loans and other loans, I.T Dept. will generate the letters as per Annexure – A, Annexure - B, Annexure – F and Annexure - G  advising the revision in rate of interest as done in the past. The branch wise letters in duplicate will be sent to the respective branches which will arrange to send the same through post/hand delivery. They will retain the second copy/acknowledged copy in the security file of the respective loans.

 Planning & Marketing Dept. shall arrange for printing of advertising material with the new rates.

I.T. Dept. shall make necessary changes in our website and in our system accordingly and also send SMS to such Borrowers where revision is made.          

Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of the concerned officials/staff members at your branch and ensure compliance.
