Circular ID No. 102 dated 27th February, 2013

 

Revision  in Interest Rates on  Term Deposits w.e.f. 01.03.2013

 

The overall growth of the total deposits of the Bank is commensurate with the target for the year ending 31.03.2013. The deposits as on 26.02.2013 is `. 7,900 crore against the target of `. 8,000 crore for the year 2012-13. Thus, the target of `. 8,000 crore would certainly be achieved by 31.03.2013.  However, compared to the increase of total deposits of `.1,381 crore the increase in the term deposit is `.1,133.00 crore.  The increase in Current & Saving Deposits (CASA) is only `. 248.00 crore.  The substantial increase in term deposits is very high compared to increase in CASA deposits. The cost of deposits has increased from 6.89% as on 31.03.2012 to 7.79% as on 31.12.2012. Secondly, the RBI in their 3rd quarter review Monetary Policy 2012-13 have reduced Repo rate from 8.00%  to 7.75% and CRR from 4.25% to 4.00%. Further, the interest rates in the market have started softening. It is also observed that the interest rates offered by our Bank are on the higher side as compared to other Banks.

 

In view of the above, Board in its Meeting held on 25.02.2013 decided to revise the interest rates on Domestic/NRO/NRE Term Deposits w.e.f. 1st March, 2013. The  criteria for Bulk Deposits has also been revised from `. 15.00 Lakh to `. 1.00 Crore & above.

3.  Bulk Deposits :

The Bulk deposits means deposit of `. 1.00 Crore and above accepted in a single day in any individual branch under single deposit receipt. The rates for Bulk Deposits except for Senior Citizen have been shown separately as above.

 

4.   The rate of interest for Staff deposits for all slabs/durations will be 1% more than the rates applicable for General Public.

 

5.   The interest rates for deposits under ATSS i.e. 9.75% remains unchanged.

 

6.   I.T Dept. is advised to display this information on banks website and make necessary changes in the system.

 

7.   All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposit issued earlier from time to time, continue to remain in force.
