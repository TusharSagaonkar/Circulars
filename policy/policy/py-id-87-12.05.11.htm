Circular ID No. 87 dated 12th May, 2011

Shubhamkaroti- II

Launching of New Deposits Scheme on the Eve of Foundation Day Celebration

 

         

Since last year our Bank has decided to celebrate Foundation Day on  25th June every year. As a part of this year's celebration as approved by the Board in its meeting held on 10th May, 2011, it has been decided to launch new Term Deposit Scheme to mobilize maximum amount of deposits. The salient features of the new Deposit Scheme are as under :-

 

 

i)         Name of the Scheme : "SHUBHAMKAROTI -II"

 

ii)       "Shubhamkaroti- II Scheme" will be launched on 16th May, 2011 and will be closed as at the close of business on 30th June, 2011 for General Public, Staff members, Senior Citizens, Co-op. Societies and Bulk Deposits.

 

iii)      Period of Term Deposit                   -        500 Days

 

iv)      The Deposits will be accepted under any of the MID/QID/QICRIP Schemes.

 

v)        Rate of Interest :

 

10.25%

For General Public (for staff members 0.50% additional)

10.50%

For Sr. Citizens, Co-op. Societies and Bulk Deposits

                         

 

vi)      Applicable rate of interest for Shubhamkaroti – II will be extended to all such Depositors who deposit clearing cheques as on the close of business on 30.06.2011, although the effects will be cleared after 30.06.2011. In all such cases the Term Deposit Receipt for 500 days will be issued from the date when effects are cleared.

 

vii)     Branch wise target for mobilization of Deposits under the scheme :

             

Branches headed by Asst. General Managers

Rs.5.00 Crore

Branches headed by Sr. Manager

Rs.3.00 Crore

Branches headed by Manager

Rs.2.00 Crore

                                                                           

viii)   While achieving the targets, branches are advised to concentrate more on mobilization of Deposits from individuals, Senior Citizens and Co-op. Housing Societies as these deposits are of perpetual nature compared with the Bulk Deposits from Co-op. Credit Societies / Banks.

 

ix)      Branches should note that special awards to be declared for branches and individual staff members will be on the basis of deposits mobilized from 16th May, 2011 to 20th June, 2011 although the Shubhamkaroti-II Scheme will be kept open upto and including 30.06.2011.

                                                      

 

2.       All other rates on Term Deposits for different periods as mentioned in our Circular ID No. 84 dated 7th April, 2011, other than the deposits for the duration of 500 days under Shubhamkaroti-II Scheme will remain unchanged till further instructions.

 

3.       Marketing Department will do the needful for advertising and supplying the banners/marketing material to branches will before 16th May, 2011.

 

4.       I.T. Dept. is advised to arrange to display this information on Bank's website.

 

5.       All other instructions issued earlier from time to time in regard to domestic Term Deposits continue to remain in force.

 

 

6.       Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members at your Branch/Dept. for compliance.

 

                                      

V. S. Morye

       Managing Director
