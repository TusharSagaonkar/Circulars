Circular ILA No. 99 dated 1st April 2026

Extension of Revised Rate of Interest on Loans & Advances  upto 30.04.2026

____________________________________________________________________

I.     The Prime Lending Rate of the Bank is unchanged, i.e. 12.25% p.a. as approved by the competent authority on 07.02.2024.

II.   Please refer to the Office Order No. 604 dated 10.12.2025 regarding the extension of Rate of Interest upto 31.03.2026. Further, in the Administrator’s  Committee meeting held on 23.03.2026, the position of Loans & Advances was reviewed and it has been decided to extend the Revised Rate of Interest on Loans & Advances as per ILA-96 dated 07.08.2025, ILA-97 dated 19.09.2025, ILA-98 dated 18.10.2025 and Office Order No. 604 dated 10.12.2025 for a further period upto 30.04.2026. The Loans & Advances sanctioned on or before 30.04.2026 and disbursed till 31.05.2026 are only eligible for the Rate of Interest as per ILA-96, 97, 98 and Office Order No. 604.

 

III.The Rate of Interest on Loans & Advances are given in the chart enclosed herewith and marked as Annexure-I. The Rate of Interest mentioned in Annexure is for New Loans/Advances only. For Existing Loans/Advances present Rate of Interest is to be continued. (No changes to be made in rate of interest for existing all Loans)

 

IV. Please bring the contents of this communication to the notice of all staff members at your Branch/Department.

 

 

 

Chief Executive Officer
