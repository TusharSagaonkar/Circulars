Circular ID No. 60 dated 22 October 2008

Clarification on some points regarding our Circular ID No. 59 dated 10th October, 2008

On receipt of our Circular ID No. 59 dated 10.10.2008 the following queries/doubts were raised: -

i.       In terms of para 5 of our above circular, whether renewal by premature closure of existing Fixed Deposit to avail higher rate without any penalty clause is applicable to Fixed Deposits issued under ATSS?

ii.      In terms of para 6 of our above circular, whether additional 1% interest is applicable to staff under ATSS Scheme?

          In this regard we have to clarify as under: -

i.       Our Circular ID No. 59 dated 10.10.2008 has been related only with our normal Fixed Deposit Schemes and the rates are applicable to normal Fixed Deposit Schemes and not for Fixed Deposits issued under ATSS.

ii.      The rates of interest and other terms related with the ATSS will remains unchanged.  As such instructions in regard to renewal by premature closure of Fixed Deposit to avail higher rate without any penalty will apply only to our normal Fixed Deposit Schemes and not applicable to Fixed Deposits issued under ATSS.  Similarly instructions in regard to 1% additional rate payable to Bank's Staff are applicable only to normal Fixed Deposits Schemes and are not applicable to Fixed Deposits issued under ATSS.

          Please note the instructions carefully and act accordingly.

                                      M. C. Shetty

       Managing Director
