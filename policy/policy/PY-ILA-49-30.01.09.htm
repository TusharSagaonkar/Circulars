The recommendations of ALCO were put up to the Board in its meeting held on 09.01.2009. Notes on Credit Rating:

 

i)     New Loans and Advances means all loans and advances eligible for Credit Rating on the date of sanction.  These accounts will be charged interest rates as per XIV above except takeover Priority Sector accounts with limit of Rs.25.00 Lakh and above.  The rate of interest will be charged as per changes made from time to time till one year from the date of documentation.  These accounts will be eligible for interest rate applicable as per Credit Rating after completion of one year from the date of documentation. 

ii)  Credit Rating exercise of takeover Priority Sector accounts with limit of Rs.25.00 Lakh and above will be done in the initial stage at the time of takeover and rate of interest will be applied to them as per grading. (Please refer Credit Policy modification for the year 2008-09)               

iii) Builders, Developers and other Civil Contractors:  No grading is applicable. But yearly Credit Rating exercise should be done.

iv) For Mortgage Loans, Premises Loans, Rent Securitization Loan, Credit Rating is not applicable.

v)  Credit Rating is also not applicable for non-funded limits such as Bank Guarantee, L/C.  These Loans/limits should not be clubbed to existing facilities to ascertain where Credit Rating is applicable.

vi) In case of Loans to Educational Institutions, yearly Credit Rating should be done. Similarly, Credit Rating exercise of Loans and Advances to Paper and Stationery Traders at Fort Branch should also be done. 

 

XXIV.  Case filed and SRO accounts – The rate of interest after filing of cases against the defaulters will be the rate as per Demand Promissory Note i.e. the rate of interest prevailing at the time of disbursement of loans and advances or 14% whichever is lower.

XXVI.      Please note that financing for Group Loans under various categories of groups (i.e. Group A, B, C) have been discontinued for the time being until further notice. For the existing loan accounts under these categories there is no change in the rate of interest to be applied.

              However, individual loans for the employees of Central Railway at Dadar, Parel, Sewri and Abhyudaya Nagar Branches will be sanctioned with the existing rate of interest (14%).   

 

Notes for all Loans and Advances:

i)       Interest will be charged on daily reducing balance and on monthly basis. 

 

ii)      The rebate of 1% allowed to Housing Loans is withdrawn since 01.04.2008. However, penal interest @1% will be charged for the period of default as per extant instructions.

 

iii)         It may be noted that interest rate changes are not applicable to Housing Loans which have been sanctioned and disbursed under Fixed Rate of Interest during the period 01.07.2005 to 30.06.2006.

 

iv)      In case of takeover of Cash Credit and other loan accounts by other Bank(s), prepayment charges or penal interest @ 2% will be charged on the amount of outstanding as on the date of takeover/closure. No prepayment charges or penal interest to Surety Loan Accounts to be levied eventhough taken over by other Bank.

 

 

v)       The rate of Penal interest has now been reduced from 3% to 1% p.a. which will be levied for the period of default on the amount of overdues. 

 

vi)      In case of Cash Credit NPA A/c., penal interest will be 1% p.a. from the date of NPA till it is regularized.

 

vii)     Loan facility against NSC/KVP (100% of the Accrued Value), RBI Bonds (100% of the Face Value) and LIC Policy (100% of Surrender Value) or Rs.2.00 Lakh whichever is less may be granted to all permanent employees/officers of the bank at interest rate of 1% less than what is applicable to the borrowers of the Bank. The employees/officers should own the said securities solely or jointly with his/her spouse.   

 

In cases where the employees/officers wish to avail loan above Rs.2.00 Lakh, the criteria applicable to general customers in respect of margin and rate of interest will be applicable to them.

 

viii)    Cash Credit accounts which are eligible for rate of interest on the basis of Credit Rating, their Rates will be revised as per gradation done every year.  The rate of interest on Term Loan and SBD/DBD accounts will also be revised as per gradation of Cash Credit Account, wherever borrower enjoys both limits and is eligible for Credit Rating.

 

ix)      In order to link the interest rates to the credit rating of borrowal accounts effectively, credit rating exercise should not be confined only at the time of renewal/review of the credit limits.  The grading should be done by branches as and when the latest audited financial statements are received and the same should be forwarded through the concerned Zonal Office to H.O. for approval.   

 

x)       There is need to create awareness among borrowers to secure higher Credit Rating by them to get reduction in the rate of interest which is beneficial to them.  This will also help to inculcate the habit of submitting financial statements in time.  Branches are advised to write to all eligible borrowers  whose facilities are subject to Credit Rating  as mentioned above to submit audited statements on or before 31st December and avail the benefit of lower rates upon higher gradation.  

 

xi)      The penal interest will be 1% p.a. over the rate applicable as per the existing grading. Credit rating exercise shall be completed within 5 days at branch and 6 days at Zonal Office. Thus credit rating exercise shall be completed within 11 days of receipt of audited financial statements.  Borrowal accounts will not be charged penal interest after receipt of these statements.  It will be the responsibility of Branch and Zonal Office to complete this exercise within 11 days of the receipt of audited balance sheet and profit and loss account statements.  If the gradation is revised at any time subsequently and penal interest continued to be charged, it will be charged at 1% p.a. over the revised rate of interest for the facility. Since last date of submission of audited balance sheet and profit and loss account statements is 31st December, the penal interest will be charged from 1st January of next year.

 

xii)     In case the Credit Rating exercise is done on any day during the month, the revised rate of interest will be applicable from the 1st day of that month. 

 

xiii)    In-charge of branches are authorized to determine the rate of interest on CC against FDR and sub limit of CC against FDR collaterals as per above by obtaining request letter from the borrower and informing the borrower and the sureties of the benefit.

 

xiv)    At the time of renewal of limits, principal amount alongwith accrued interest of FDR(s) shall be taken into consideration to decide sub limits against FDR(s) within overall cash credit limits. Similarly, in case of renewal of matured FDR(s) before renewal of limit, the change in rate of interest if any shall be revised immediately against renewed FDR(s). The borrower should be informed about revision in rate of interest.     

 

3.      Credit discipline:

 

          In order to inculcate the desired credit discipline among the borrowers, branches are advised to address letters to all the borrowing customers specifically emphasizing the importance of adherence to credit discipline and that the bank will be levying penal interest for non-adherence to the credit discipline. Branches should carefully note the Penal Interest at 1% p.a. will be charged on all borrowing accounts without any exception for the period of default in cases of following defaults. Branches should also note that there is no waiver or concession for applying Penal Interest. Therefore, Branches should not forward/recommend any such request from any Borrower to this office.  This aspect should be specifically pointed out to the borrowing customers.

(i)      Non-repayment   of    loan   installments on its due date.

(ii)     Non-payment of interest when it becomes due.

(iii)    Availing excess over sanctioned limit or excess over Drawing Power.

(iv)     Non-renewal/non-review above 3 months of cash credit limit due to non-submission of documents/papers. In case of unreviewed CC A/cs default period will end on 1st day of next month, after receipt of all documents for review.

(v)      Bills discounted not realized on their due dates.

(vi)     Submission of audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account for the year ended March, after 31st December of the next immediate financial year. This is applicable for Credit Rating of borrowal accounts.

(vii)    When turnover in the account is less than 25% of the limits.  Turnover  charges at the penal interest shall be applied for half yearly  period for borrowers enjoying  facilities for round the year business and at yearly intervals for borrowers engaged in business of seasonal nature in terms of guidelines in Circular OC No. 376 dated 14.12.2006. After identifying the borrowers eligible for levy of penal interest for turnover of less than 25%, the penalty should be levied only for the months in which monthly turnover was less than 25% of the limits.

(viii)   Non-submission of Stock and Book Debt statement at monthly intervals. Such statements showing the position of Stocks/Book Debts on the last day of the month should be received latest by 15th of the following month, Circular OC No. 311 dated 14.07.2005 should be followed for guidance in this regard.

(ix)    Auditor's certified Book debts statement: As per the decision of the Audit Committee Meeting, borrower enjoying CC & WCTL limit with limit above Rs.25.00 Lakh and also with component of Drawing Power against Book Debt above Rs.25.00 Lakh, are required to submit auditor's certified Book Debt statements as on 30th June and 31st December every year.  Those statements are required for Stock Audit purpose. These accounts will be charged penal interest @ 1% p.a. in case of non-submission of Auditor's certified Book Debt statements by 31st August and 28th February. However, Book Debt statements alongwith stock statements without certificate by Auditor will be accepted by 15th of the succeeding month and reckoned for Drawing Power. 

The branches are advised to send reminder letters in the month of June and December every year to all eligible borrowal accounts who are required to submit Auditor's certified Book Debts statement.

(x)         It is necessary to advise all borrowers the revised rate of interest and EMI amount.  The EMI for Loan Accounts should be calculated with the present amount outstanding in the Loan A/c., revised rate of interest and balance period of the loan.

(xi)        Branches are advised to use mutasis mutandis the draft of notices and letters to be sent to loanees enclosed with our Circular ILA No. 44 dated 18.07.2008.
