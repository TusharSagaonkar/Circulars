Circular ILA No. 58 dated 1st March, 2011

 

Revision of lending rates w.e.f. 01.03.2011

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

 

 

In view of the recent upward changes made by Reserve Bank of India in Repo Rate, increase in rates of interest on our term deposits more particularly increase in interest rate for retaining and attracting New Bulk Deposits, the cost of funds has been increased substantially. Considering all these various aspects the ALCO Meeting held on 12.02.2011 has recommended to increase the existing Prime Lending Rate (PLR) from 12% to 12.5%.

 

 

It was therefore inevitable for the Board of Directors to increase the rate of interest on our Credit Facilities. As approved by the Board in its meeting held on 18.02.2011, we give in the enclosed Annexure the rates of interest on various Credit Facilities effective from 01.03.2011.

 

 

The rate of interest has been increased only by 0.5%, however there is no increase of Rate of Interest for Educational Loans to students and Individual Housing Loans.

 

 

Please note that as per Circular ILA No. 53 dated 17.02.2010, the rate of interest @ 12% p.a. will be continued upto 31.03.2012 on Commercial Loans and Advances sanctioned upto and including ` 100.00 Lakh at our Kankavli Branch and Branches under Udupi Region.

 

 

Where Bank has granted concessional/special rate for Credit Facilities to Groups for certain facilities, such Credit Facility Accounts will be charged with additional rate of 0.5% w.e.f. 01.03.2011.

 

Credit Rating Interest Rates are linked with PLR. Branches are therefore advised to send a letter as per Annexure 'C' regarding revision in rate of interest on all graded accounts consequent upon the upward revision in PLR i.e. from 12% to 12.5% w.e.f. 01.03.2011.

 

Branches are advised to display the Annexure in Public Notice Board and also send letters to all CC Borrowers as per Annexures and other letters generated by I.T. Dept. for surety and secured loans regarding upward revision in rate of interest. The office copy of this letter should be filed in respective Loan File.

 

Planning & Marketing Dept. shall arrange for printing of advertisement material with the new rates.

 

 

I.T. Dept. shall make necessary changes on our website and in our system accordingly and also send SMS to the Borrowers informing the revision of PLR to 12.50%. IT Dept. shall also generate letters to be sent to Loanees, who have availed Secured / Surety Loans.

 

Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of concerned officials/ staff members at your branch and ensure compliance.

 

     V. S. Morye

Managing Director
