Circular ID No. 132 dated 30th July 2021

 

Interest on Overdue Term Deposits

 

 

I.  In terms of extant instructions, if a domestic Term deposit matures and proceeds are unpaid, the amount left unclaimed with Bank attracts rate of interest as applicable to saving account.

 

II.   Change in rule

1.   RBI has, vide its circular no. RBI/2021-22/66 DoR. SPE. REC. 29/13.03.00/ 2021-2022 dated 2nd July 2021, informed banks that the rule for payment of interest on proceeds of a domestic Term Deposit which has matured and remains unclaimed has been changed. The revised rule reads as under.

"Interest on overdue domestic deposits :- If a Term Deposit (TD) matures and proceeds are unpaid, the amount left unclaimed with the co-operative bank shall attract rate of interest as applicable to savings account or the contracted rate of interest on the matured TD, whichever is low.”

2.   The said changed rule is incorporated in the Master Direction - Reserve Bank of India (Co-operative Banks - Interest Rate on Deposits) Directions, 2016 [RBI/DCBR/2015-16/23 Master Direction DCBR.Dir.No.1/13.01.000/2015-16 dated 12th May 2016 (Updated as on 2nd July 2021)].

3.   In view of the above, branches shall ensure, with immediate effect, that interest on overdue domestic Term Deposits which remain unclaimed with Bank shall be paid at the rate applicable to saving account or the contracted rate of interest on the matured TD, whichever is low.

 

III.  Not to apply to Term Deposits which are auto-renewed under mandate

The rule as per Para II above will not apply to the Term Deposits which get auto-renewed upon maturity as per the mandate obtained from depositors at the time of placement of deposit.

 

Please bring the instructions contained in the instant circular to the notice of all the staff-members and ensure compliance.                                     

P. S. Salian

       Managing Director
