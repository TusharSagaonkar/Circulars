Circular ID No.104 dated 31st July, 2013

 

Revision in Interest Rates on Term Deposits w.e.f. 01.08.2013

 

Reserve Bank of India, in their 1st quarter review of Monetary Policy 2013-2014, declared on 30.07.2013, have maintained status quo in respect of policy rates.  However, on 15.07.2013, Reserve Bank of India have increased Marginal Standing Facility rate from 8.25% to 10.25%.  It also increased Bank rate from 8.25% to 10.25%.  They have not changed Repo, Reverse Repo and CRR rates.  The main reason for this new monetary measures by Reserve Bank of India was that foreign exchange market has come under severe stress, prompting the Reserve Bank of India to initiate liquidity tightening measures to contain the volatility.   

 

In view of this change by Reserve Bank of India and very slow growth in deposits of our Bank during quarter ended 30.06.2013, it has been decided in the Board Meeting held on 30.07.2013 to revise the interest rates on Domestic/NRO/NRE Term Deposits w.e.f. 1st August, 2013 as under :

2.   The rate of interest for Staff deposits for all slabs/durations will be 1% more than the rates applicable for General Public.

 

3.   The interest rate for deposits under ATSS i.e. 9.75% remain unchanged.

 

4.   Marketing Dept. is advised to provide pamphlets and other printing material to the Branches and also advertise in newspapers etc.

 

5.   I.T Dept. is advised to display this information on Bank’s website and make necessary changes in the system.

 

6.   All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposit issued earlier from time to time continue to remain in force.

 

Cost of deposits of our Bank during quarter ended 30.06.2013 have increased from 7.80% to 7.86%.  Considering the cost of deposits and adverse money treasury, it is not advisable to offer higher rate of interest on Term Deposits which will further increase cost of deposits and adversely affect the profitability of the Bank.  However, to tide over temporary liquidity problem, it has been decided to increase interest rates on Term Deposit for 15 months.  This is a special deposit scheme offering 10% rate of interest to General public and all other depositors including bulk depositors.  As senior citizens have always patronized our Bank, it has been decided to offer them interest @ 10.25% p.a.  All officials and staff members at Branches and Depts. should also mobilize the deposits under this scheme as per individual target given to them in Action Plan of the Bank 2013-2014 vide           Office Order No.411 dated 01.04.2013.   The Incharge of Branches are advised to involve all officials and staff members of the Branch and ensure that maximum deposits are mobilized under this special deposit scheme.
