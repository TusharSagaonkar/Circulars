Circular ID No. 98 dated 20th March, 2012

Revision in Interest Rates on Term Deposits for the period from 20.03.2012 to 20.06.2012. –

Mobilization of Deposits under “Shubhamkaroti” Scheme.

 

Considering the recommendations of ALCO & in view of the present market scenario with regard to interest rate undergoing constant changes and cut-throat competition in mobilizing deposit, it has been decided to revise interest rates. The revised interest rates will be applicable during the period from 20.03.2012 to 20.06.2012.

1.           The special deposit scheme for 400 days stands discontinued w.e.f. from 22.03.2012.

 

2.           The special deposit scheme of 100 days will continue to remain the same as per our Circular ID No. 97 dated 17.02.2012.

 

3.           The rate of Interest for Bulk Deposit to be decided in consultation with the Chief General Manager and by the Managing Director in the absence of Chief General Manager.

 

4.           The rate of interest for staff term deposit for all slabs/duration will be 1% more than the rate applicable for General Public, Trust and NRO.

 

5.           I.T dept. is advised to display this information on bank’s website and make necessary changes in the system.

 

6.           We are required to mobilize `. 500 crore deposits during the period 20.03.2012 to 20.06.2012. As such every staff member are given individual  target for mobilization of deposits which is compulsory. The target set for the staff and officers are as under.

 

a.   For Officers (Managers and above)           `. 50.00 Lakh          

 including Officers in H.O Dept.

 

b.   For Clerical Staff upto Accountant          `. 10.00 Lakh

including staff in H.O Dept

 

c.    For Sub-Staff                                         `. 1.00 Lakh

Including sub-staff in H.O Dept.

 

7.           Branches are advised to make maximum efforts to mobilize the retail deposits with these substantially revised rates of interest. 1 highest achiever from each branch and 5 best achievers from amongst the staff and officers will be felicitated.

 

8.           The In-Charge of branches/departments will maintain a register with separate folio for each staff member to record the deposits mobilized and shall review the performance at weekly interval. Non performance by any staff member will be viewed seriously. 

 

9.           All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposits, issued earlier from time to time, continue to remain in force.

 

10.       Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all staff members at your branch/department.   

     

                                      

V. S. Morye

       Managing Director
