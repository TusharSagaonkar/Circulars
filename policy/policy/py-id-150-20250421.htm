Circular ID No. 150 dated 21st April 2025

 

Interest Rates on Deposits w.e.f. 23rd April 2025

 

I.   A submission of revision in interest rates on Term Deposits was placed before the Administrator’s Committee Meeting held on 17th April 2025 wherein it was decided to lower the interest rates on the Term Deposits and the interest rate on deposits under the SHUBHAM-401 & Abhyudaya-200 scheme and the same met with approval.

III.    Other general instructions as under also continue to hold good.

1.   The rate of interest for staff deposits for all slabs/durations will be 1% more than the rates applicable for the General Public.

2.   The staff/checking officials of Term Deposit section at each branch should check correctness of interest rate quoted on deposit receipt as per the instructions above before handing over the relative receipt to the depositor.

3.   The IT Dept. is advised to display the updated information as above on the Bank’s website and make necessary changes in the system.

4.   All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to deposits and interest thereon issued from time to time continue to remain in force.

 

IV.  Each Branch In-Charge shall bring to the attention of the staff members, the changed structure of interest rates.
