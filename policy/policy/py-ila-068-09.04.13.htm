Circular ILA No. 68 dated 9th April, 2013

Revision of Lending Rates w.e.f. 01.04.2013

 

The growth of Loans and Advances of our Bank during the year 2012-2013 has not been as per monthly targets. During the year there was no growth in retail Loans like Surety Loans, Housing Loans and other small business loans. The increase in Loans and Advances was mainly due to increase in sanction and disbursement of large amounts of Loans and Advances proposals. In order to have a balanced growth in loans and advances, it is necessary to increase the retail loans and advances.

Compared to the interest rates on loans and advances of Nationalized Banks and other Co-operative Banks, our rates of interest on following Loan Schemes seem to be on higher side. Secondly, State Bank of India, other Nationalised Banks and other Co-operative Banks like Saraswat Co-operative Bank have resorted to repeated advertisements in print and electronic media, offering lower rate of interest on Housing Loans, Vehicle Loans and other retail loans during last 2 months.

The matter was placed before the Board meeting held on 08.04.2013 and in order to be competitive and to attract retail Loans and Advances, Board has approved the reduction in rates of interest on the following schemes of Retail Loans as under:-

The rate of interest on Housing Loans have been considerably reduced to attract more and more customers wishing to purchase a dwelling unit and also restrict the shifting of Housing Loans to Nationalised Banks/ other Co-operative Banks/ Financial Institutions due to our existing high rate of interest. Branch officials should convince Housing Loan Borrowers who wish to shift the loans to other Banks/Institutions regarding considerable reduction in our Housing Loan interest rates. Moreover they should be informed that in case they shift their advances to other Banks/Institutions they will have to bear heavy charges for equitable mortgage (now registered), legal fees and other expenses. The branches should make utmost efforts to retain the Housing Loan Advances with our Bank.

At present, branches are considering surety loans upto `. 2.00 Lakh except employees of Central Railway, Matunga Workshop. Now it has been decided to extend Surety Loans upto `. 3.00 lakhs to all salaried persons and business men with the existing take home Salary/Income criteria after deduction of EMI of Loan amount sanctioned. Henceforth, net Take Home Salary/Income of `. 6000/- pm can be considered for sanction of Surety Loans upto `. 2.00 Lakh and Rs 8000/- pm for sanction of Surety Loans above `. 2.00 Lakh and upto `. 3.00 Lakh. However, in case of the borrowers who have repaid earlier Loans regularly, net Take Home Salary/Income of  `. 7000/- pm may be considered for sanction of Surety Loans above `. 2.00 Lakh and upto `. 3.00 Lakh.

Planning & Marketing Dept. is advised to provide necessary publicity material to the Branches.

 

Branches are advised to take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of concerned officials/staff and ensure compliance.
