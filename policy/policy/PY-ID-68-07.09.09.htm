Circular ID No. 68  dated 7th September 2009

 

Interest Rates on Bulk Deposits – Revision thereof

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          A reference is invited to our Circular ID No. 67 dated 10.08.2009 in regard to revision of interest rates on Term Deposits.

 

2.       In the said circular we have not differentiated any rates for Bulk and Non-Bulk Deposits. The interest rates were same for Bulk as well as Non-Bulk Deposits. However, with a view to balance the composition of deposit portfolio, particularly in Bulk Deposits, it has been decided in the Board Meeting held on 04.09.2009 to revise the rate of interest on Bulk Deposits to be accepted from the Depositors other than Individuals and Co-operative Housing Societies. The revised rates on Bulk Deposits will be effective from 10.09.2009 which are as under :-

I)        Bulk Deposits :

We would like to re-iterate that bulk deposits means deposits of Rs.15.00 Lakh and above accepted in a single day in an individual branch.

 

II)       Bulk Deposits will not be accepted under QICRIP Scheme.

 

III)          The above mentioned revised rates of interest will be applicable to all Bulk Deposits other than Deposits of Individuals and Co-operative Housing Societies.

IV)     Please note that the interest rates for the Deposits applicable to Individuals and Co-operative Housing Societies even for their Bulk Deposits, will be as per ID No. 67 dated 10.08.2009. Also these deposits can be accepted under any of the Deposit Schemes.

 

3.       Branches are advised to take note of the above revision in interest rates and ensure compliance.

 

4.       All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposits, issued earlier from time to time, continue to remain in force.
