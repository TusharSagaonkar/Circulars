3.       BULK DEPOSITS :-

The Bulk Deposit means deposit of ` 15.00 Lakh and above accepted in a single day in any individual branch under single deposit receipt. The rates for Bulk Deposits have been shown separately as above. However, if the funds position and the quantum of deposit allows the Bank to quote different rate, Bank may quote slightly higher rate of interest on the basis of size of deposits on any day for the deposits to be accepted particularly for the period of 181 days and above and inclusive of 12 months. Branches may therefore refer such cases to General Manager (HRD, Development, Accounts, Inspection & Vigilance) who will recommend the interest rate for the day and forward it to Managing Director for approval.

 

 

Such Bulk Deposits will be accepted in any of the Bank's Schemes like MID, QID, QICRIP, etc.

         

          The above rates are applicable to all new deposits as well as for existing deposits for the depositors who want the benefit of revision in rate of interest by premature closure and for reinvestment. However such depositors will be paid applicable rate of interest for the period from the date of original contract upto date of premature renewal without any penalty and after renewal of the deposit it should remain with the Bank for a longer period than the remaining period of original contract.

In all other cases, we reiterate our earlier instructions that Branches will apply penalty @ 1% while allowing pre-matured withdrawals of Term Deposits.
