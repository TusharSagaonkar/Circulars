Circular ID No. 83 dated 17th March, 2011

 

Charging of penalty for premature closures for purpose of reinvestment

 

         

 

For premature closure of existing Fixed Deposits for the purpose of reinvestment in Fixed Deposits to avail Higher Rate of Interest, our Bank's instructions were as under :-

 

 

(a)  In case of premature closure of Term Deposit for reinvestment purpose, interest will be paid/payable for the period from the date of original contract up to date of premature renewal at the applicable rate without charging penalty of 1% provided deposit after reinvestment remains with the bank for a period longer than remaining period of original contract.

 

(b)  If such deposit is withdrawn before the expiry of the period of original contract, the penalty of 1% to be levied from the date of original contract up to date of the pre-mature withdrawal after reinvestment. If the premature withdrawal is made after due date of the original contract then the penalty of 1% is to be levied from the date of reinvestment to the date of premature withdrawal.

 

 

 

Reserve Bank of India has advised the Bank to formulate its own policy in regard to the premature closure of Term Deposits for purpose of reinvestment. Accordingly in terms of our Circular O.C. No. 551 dated 20.01.2011 we have revised the policy which reads as under :-

 

"On request from the depositor, branches shall allow closure of a Term Deposit, a deposit in a form of daily deposit, or recurring deposit to enable the depositor to immediately reinvest the amount lying in the aforesaid deposit with our branches for any suitable period in another Term Deposit, by paying interest in respect of such term deposit at the rate applicable for which the deposit has remained with the Bank, by charging the penalty of 1%."

 

The above instructions have been made effective from 1st February, 2011.

 

In case if depositor is approaching first time for premature withdrawal for making reinvestment there is no difficulty to calculate and pay interest for the period for which the deposit has remained with the Bank with 1% penalty. However, if depositor is approaching second or third time for premature withdrawal of the same deposit for the purpose of reinvestment for getting higher rate of interest, in such cases branches find it difficult to adopt Clause (b) mentioned above and in such cases we are receiving number of queries from branches. Moreover customers are also objecting for charging of penalty from the date of original contract, as the penalty clause has been made effective from 01.02.2011.

 

The matter was put up to the Board in its meeting held on 11.03.2011. As per the Board's decision we advise as under :-

 

In cases where depositors are approaching second or third time for premature withdrawal for reinvestment for getting higher rate of interest, it is decided that deposit will be reinvested at prevailing rate for any period as per depositors' wish. Interest will be paid for the period from the date of last renewal to the date of presenting the receipt with the request for premature withdrawal for reinvestment at the applicable rate prevailing on the date of last renewal by charging penalty of 1%. In all such cases branches will take into account only the date of last renewal and need not take into account the date of original contract and date(s) of previous renewal(s) with premature closure as well as payment of interest made at each occasion.

 

Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members at your branch for immediate compliance.

                                       

V. S. Morye

       Managing Director
