Circular ID No.  51  dated  30  March 2007

 

        Interest Rates on Term Deposits

 

 

I  Continuation of Higher Interest Rates after 31 March 2007

 

        A reference is invited to Circular  ID No. 49 dated 23 February 2007, advising   upward revision in the interest rates on term deposits.  The revised interest rates became effective from 26 February 2007 and it was advised  that the revised interest rates would continued to be in force till  31 March 2007.  The position in this regard was reviewed by the Board in the meeting held on 28 March 2007 and it has been decided that the interest at the rates advised in the Circular  ID No. 49 dated 23 February 2007 should continue to be paid to the depositors till further notice. Branches are advised to take a note of the decision and act accordingly.

 

          Incidentally, if branches  have displayed any posters/sign boards in branch premises to the effect that higher interest  was offered only till 31 March 2007 such posters/sign boards may be removed so as  to avoid misinforming of the customers. New publicity material  will be provided  to branches  shortly by the  Marketing Cell at Head Office.

 

II Interest Rates on Staff Deposits  - Clarification on Circular ID No. 48

       

          A reference is invited to item II of Circular ID  No. 48 dated 29 September 2006, regarding  payment of additional interest on Fixed Deposits of staff members. It has been  observed during the inspections of some of the branches that the staff members have kept Fixed Deposits singly in the name of their minor children  and the accounts are operated by the concerned staff members or such deposits are kept jointly with first name  being that of the minor with parent staff member appearing as joint holder.  As advised in the aforesaid  Circular ID  No. 48 dated 29 September 2006, additional  interest is payable only if Fixed Deposits is  kept in the name of the staff member and in case of joint account with family  member/s to be eligible for additional interest, the first name should be that of the  staff member.  Accordingly, it is hereby clarified that if the deposit is held singly in the minor child’s name  of a staff member and the staff member is operating the account or in case of  jointly held Fixed Deposits with the first name of a minor child of the staff  who happens to be  the joint holder,  additional  interest admissible to the staff members should not be paid on deposits so held. Branches are advised to note the clarification and take action accordingly.

 

 

                                             M. C. Shetty

      Managing Director
