Circular ID No. 133 dated 25th January 2022

 

“Republic 73 Deposit Scheme”

A Fixed Deposit scheme with 18 months’ maturity with special rate of interest open over a limited period

 

I.   The matter of revising rate of interest for one or another bucket of Fixed Deposits to adjust to the current conditions of the market was under consideration for some time. After due deliberation, it was considered appropriate to launch, to coincide with the 73rd Republic Day of the country, a Fixed Deposit Scheme of 18 months’ maturity, carrying a special rate of interest, which would be open to customers for a limited period to join. A submission in this behalf was placed before the Board meeting dated 20th January 2021 and the same met with approval.

 

II.  1. Accordingly, a special fixed deposit scheme, named “Republic-73 Deposit Scheme”, is being launched with effect from 27th January 2022 and it will be open for acceptance of deposits up to 31st March 2022. Rates of interest on the deposits under the scheme will be as under.

2. The scheme will not extend to NRIs. Similarly, it will not include deposits under ATSS.

3. It will be permissible to close a deposit account under the scheme before maturity and loan can also be extended against it.

4. The rate of Interest for staff deposits will be 1% more than the rates applicable for the General Public.

III. IT department is advised to create in CBS, two accounting heads for deposits, one each for QICRIP and QID, and two heads to account for interest, for the purpose of respective PL interest. Branches need to be advised in this behalf urgently.

 

IV.  Branches shall make use of the current security forms for issue of receipts by embossing thereon the superscription with rubber stamp reading “Republic-73 Deposit Scheme”

 

V.    The scheme has been launched with the specific goal of mobilising deposits aggressively during the available period of a little more than two months. Therefore, all out efforts must be made by all members of the staff across entire hierarchy and network of branches, departments and offices, to bring maximum possible deposits in Bank by making widely known the higher rate of interest available under the scheme.

 

VI.  Planning and Marketing department should develop, and, distribute and display, the necessary publicity material across branches. Other marketing support may also be extended to branches.

 

VII. Please bring contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the members of staff at the branch/office and make the “Republic-73 Deposit Scheme” a grand success.  

 

                                  

P. S. Salian

       Managing Director
