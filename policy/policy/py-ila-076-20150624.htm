Circular ILA No. 76 dated 24th June, 2015

 

Reduction in Rate of Interest on Gold Loans & SOD against

pledge of Gold Ornaments from 13% to 12% w.e.f. 01.07.2015

 

You are aware that recently Bank has taken the decision to increase retail loans in a big way during the current financial year i.e. 2015 – 2016. As Gold Loan is one of the retail loans which is fully secured by pledge of gold ornaments, Board in its meeting held on 17.06.2015 has decided to reduce the rate of interest on all types of Gold Loans from PLR-0.50% i.e 13% to PLR-1.5% i.e. 12% w.e.f. 01.07.2015 with a view to increase the quantum of loan amount sustainably under this portfolio.

 

Thus, the rate of interest for all types of Gold Loans including SOD against Pledge of gold ornaments would be PLR-1.5% i.e. 12% w.e.f. 01.07.2015 subject to 1% penal interest on entire outstanding Loan amount from the date the account becomes NPA till it is fully regularised/closed. The rate of interest @ 12% p.a. on daily reducing balance would be applicable to all gold loans & SOD against gold ornaments sanctioned and disbursed on or after 01.07.2015 only.

 

Planning & Marketing Dept. shall arrange for printing of attractive advertising material with the new rates.

 

I.T. Dept. shall make necessary changes in website and in the system accordingly.

 

Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of the officials/staff members concerned at your branch and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

 V. S. Morye

Managing Director
