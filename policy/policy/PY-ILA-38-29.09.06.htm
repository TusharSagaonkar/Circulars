Circular ILA No. 38 dated  29 September 2006

 

Reduction of Interest Rate on Loans to Educational  Institutions.

 

 

        A reference is invited to Circular ILA No. 37 dated 1 July 2006 regarding interest rates on loans and advances that are in force from 1 July 2006.

 

2.     The interest rate on loans to educational institutions  has been fixed at 11% p.a. in terms  of the said Circular (Vide para 2 item VIII of the table)  and D.Y Patil Group A/cs, Datta Meghe Group A/cs, Atharva Education Trust  which run  educational institutions are also mentioned specifically under Note XX (B) under para 2 of the circular in this regard as the institutions eligible for loans at 11% p.a.  Representations from various educational institutions for reduction in the interest rate were considered and discussed by the Board in the meeting held on 20 September 2006. It was noted that performance of the institutions that have availed the loans has been very regular and their account operations have been very  satisfactory.  The Board was also of the view that for retaining these existing borrowers and for attracting other similar good borrowers, viz. reputed educational institutions,  it will be advisable to  reduce our lending rate  as represented by some of the borrowers.  The Board has accordingly approved reduction of interest rate on loans and advances to all educational institutions from 11% p.a. to 10% p.a. The reduced interest rate will be  effective prospectively from 1 October 2006.

 

3.     There are no other changes in the interest rates and  other instructions contained in the Circular ILA No. 37 dated 1 July 2006 and they continue to remain in force.

 

                                          M. C. Shetty

      Managing Director
