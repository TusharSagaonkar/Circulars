Circular ID No. 53 dated 7 July 2007

 

Deposit Mobilization Month 16.07.2007 To 14.08.2007

 Launching of “Abhyudaya Gold” Deposit Scheme.

 

        It may be recalled that during the discussions at the Performance Budget  2007-08 meeting held on 2nd June 2007, it had been emphasized that there was a pressing need for our bank to take additional efforts to mobilize substantial fresh deposits on a priority basis, over and above the deposit target of Rs.2525.00 Cr. for the year, indicated in the Performance Budget.  It was also pointed out that a focused drive for raising such additional deposits is essential to maintain a comfortable CD ratio by 31st March 2008.  Action plan in this regard was, therefore, drawn up and it has been decided, with the approval of the Board, to launch a special deposit scheme viz. “Abhyudaya Gold” Deposit Scheme to be offered to the public as a special product in Deposit Mobilization Month to be observed by all the branches from 16.07.2007 to 14.08.2007.  The package “Abhyudaya Gold” Deposit Scheme to be offered during the month will have two variants to suit the needs of the depositors. 

 

          Under “Abhyudaya Gold” (Growth) Deposit Scheme the interest will be compounded at quarterly rests and paid together with the principal at the end of the term of 500 days (QIRP).  The other option offered will be “Abhyudaya Gold” (Income) Deposit Scheme which will run for 18 months under which interest accrued will be paid out every quarter-end to the depositor (QIDs).  The details of the two variants are given below:

            The other conditions applicable specifically to the “Abhyudaya Gold” Deposit Scheme will be: -

          i)       No deposit account in the name of staff members under the Scheme will be opened.

          ii)       Premature closure of the existing fixed deposit account for the purpose of reinvestment in above Scheme will attract penalty at the existing rate.

 

          Abhyudaya Gold (Growth as also Investment) Deposit Scheme is being offered as a special product and it will be offered only in the deposit mobilization month to be observed from 16.07.2007 to 14.08.2007.  The Scheme targets mobilizing term deposits of Rs.150 Cr. during the month through all the branches.  Although the Scheme is proposed to be kept on tap for one month i.e. the entire period of the mobilization month, a ceiling of Rs.200 Cr. is also applicable.  It may be noted that in case deposit collection under the Scheme reaches the ceiling level of Rs.200 Cr. the bank may suspend accepting for more deposits at any time before 14.08.2007. 

 

          A study carried out before drawing up the “Abhyudaya Gold” Deposit Scheme shows that interest rates offered by us under the Scheme for deposits kept for a term of less than two years is better than the rates being offered by many other banks for comparable period.  Branches should, therefore, make all out efforts to mobilize deposits under the “Abhyudaya Gold” Deposit Scheme.  As Branches may have area specific potentials or limitations, the targets for mobilization of deposits under the Scheme are being assigned to each zone and the Zonal offices may determine sub-targets for each branch under their control in consultation with the branches.  The zone wise target in consonance with the Performance Budget 2007-08 goals of the zones work out as under: -

          It has to be noted that Scheme is being offered for a limited period from 16.07.2007 to 14.08.2007 as a special deposit mobilization drive and the target of Rs.150 Cr. (to Rs.200 Cr.) is independent of and in addition to the deposit growth targets given to each branch/zone under the Performance Budget      2007-08.  In order to ensure whole-hearted involvement of branches and their staff in making this special deposit mobilization drive successful it has also been decided to give incentive rewards to the staff of the first three branches according to the ranking earned for mobilizing the amount of deposits under the Scheme.  The details of the rewards scheme will be advised separately.

 

          Implementation of deposit mobilization programme will be monitored and reviewed by the Accounts & Inspection Dept..  Presently, branches are reporting through zonal offices, the position of aggregate deposits, advances, etc. on bi-weekly basis to the Accounts Dept..  In order to facilitate such a review by the Accounts Department, on introduction of the Scheme branches should indicate separately the number of accounts opened and amount of deposits mobilized by them under “Abhyudaya Gold” Deposit Scheme in their bi-weekly returns.

 

          To achieve desired targets a mass publicity in respect of the above scheme will be given through newspapers, banners and media (local cable T.V. operators), etc. The Marketing Dept. will make the necessary arrangements for the publicity, supplying publicity material to branches, etc. IT Dept. is also advised to create the new product “Abhyudaya Gold” Deposit Scheme in our software well in advance.

 

          Lastly, it may be understood by all concerned that the deposits mobilize under the Scheme will be very costly but they are being raised as a part of ALM exercise as a short term necessity.  There will, therefore, be no lowering of Performance Budget 2007-08 targets which are assigned to branches/zones on the basis of their perceived potentials and average growth rate in the banking industry and it has to be ensured that the Performance Budget 2007-08 targets are also fulfilled eventually.
