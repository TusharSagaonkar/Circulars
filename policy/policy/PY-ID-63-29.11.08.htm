Circular ID No. 63 dated 29th November 2008

 

Revision of Rate of Interest on Bulk Deposits

 

 

Please refer to our Circular ID No. 62 dated 20-11-2008. In order to sustain the Bank Deposits particularly from the cooperative societies/Banks, it has been decided in Board Meeting held on 28-11-2008 to enhance the interest rate on Bulk Deposit of Rs.15/- Lacs and above from 0.25% to 0.50% over and above the rate of interest being offered to General Public (including cooperative societies/Banks) and Senior Citizens. The revised rates are applicable w.e.f. 01-12-2008.

 

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of concerned staff members.

 

                                      M. C. Shetty

       Managing Director
