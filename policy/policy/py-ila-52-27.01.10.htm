Circular ILA No. 52 dated 27th January, 2010

 

Rate of Interest on SOD against Gold Ornaments.

    

 

Please refer to our Circular O.C. No. 491 dated 10.08.2009 and Circular ILA No. 51 dated 01.07.2009.

 

In terms of instructions contained in above mentioned circulars, branches are charging interest @ 10% p.a. on Gold Loans and PLR + 1% i.e. 13% p.a. (floating)  on SOD against gold ornaments. The difference of interest rates for these two facilities is considerably high although the security for both the facilities is one and the same i.e. Gold Ornaments. The prospective borrowers are reluctant to avail the facility of SOD against Gold Ornaments, as the rate quoted by us is higher as compared to the rates quoted by other Banks/ Financial Institutions.

 

In this regard, a submission was put up to the Board in its meeting held on 21.01.2010 and according to Board's decision we advise as under :-

 

The rate of interest for SOD against Gold Ornaments will be charged @ PLR – 1%  (PLR minus 1%) i.e. 11% p.a. (Floating) w.e.f. 01.02.2010 for all existing as well as new accounts of SOD against gold ornaments.

 

 

Branches are advised to note the above instructions carefully and implement the revision in interest rates with effect from 01.02.2010. Please also write to all such existing borrowal accounts as per enclosed format and retain one copy of the letter with the loan documents of the account.

 

     V. S. Morye

Managing Director
