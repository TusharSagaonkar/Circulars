Circular ILA No. 61 dated 27th September, 2011

 

Revision of lending rates w.e.f. 01.10.2011

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Please refer to our ILA NO. 59 dated 26.05.2011 whereby we have revised interest rates on Loans & Advances w.e.f. 01.06.2011 and those were linked to PLR vide Circular ILA No. 60 dated 02.08.2011.

 

In view of the recent upward changes made by Reserve Bank of India in Repo Rate, overall rates of interest on our term deposits, payment of interest on S.B. Deposits on the basis of daily products w.e.f. 01.04.2011 and coupled with 0.5% increase in rate of interest on Savings Bank Deposits w.e.f. 03.05.2011 as per RBI directives, the cost of funds has been increased substantially.

 

Considering all these aspects and in view of the recommendations made by ALCO, the Board in its meeting held on 22.09.2011 has decided to increase Bank’s PLR from 12.50% to 13.00% w.e.f.01.10.2011.  Resultantly, on most of our Credit Facilities, the rate of interest to be charged will be increased by 0.5%. We enclose the revised Chart for rates of interest on various Credit Facilities effective from 01.10.2011.

 

It will be observed from the attached Chart that there is no change in rate of interest for following types of Loans & Advance.

 

i)  a) Surety Loan

    b) Surety Loans with collateral Securities more than 50%

 

ii)  Housing Loan sanctioned upto

     a) Loans sanctioned upto and including  `.15.00 Lakh

  

     b) Loans sanctioned above `.15.00 Lakh and upto & including  `.25.00 Lakh

 

iii)  SOD IMP against immovable property(ies) to Builder(s) / Developer(s)

 

iv)  Builders & Developers and other Civil Contractors (other than Govt. Civil Contractors)

 

v)  a)        Forced loan on account of invoked Bank Guarantee

     b)   Forced Loan under L/C (Devolvement)

     (If not paid by the importer on due date)

 

vi) a)  Discounting /Purchase of  cheques

     b)  Drawal Against cheques deposited in  clearing,

 

The rates of interest on other Credit Facilities have been increased only by 0.5%.

 

Please note that as per Circular ILA No. 53 dated 17.02.2010, the rate of interest @ 12% p.a. will be continued upto 31.03.2012 on Commercial Loans and Advances sanctioned upto and including ` 100.00 Lakh at our Kankavli Branch and Branches under Udupi Region.

 

Where Bank has granted concessional/special rate for Credit Facilities to Groups for certain facilities, such Credit Facility Accounts will be charged with additional rate of 0.5% w.e.f. 01.10.2011.

Branches should note that Housing Loans under Abhyudaya Special Housing Loan Scheme from 01.07.2009 to 31.12.2010 upto Rs. 20.00 Lakh were granted with the terms of sanction that fixed rate of interest @9% will be charged upto 30.09.2011 and w.e.f. 01.10.2011 re-set clause will be made applicable and floating rate will be charged from 01.10.2011 linked with PLR.  Branches, are, therefore advised to re-set the rate of interest as mentioned in the schedule and all these housing loan borrowers should be advised accordingly by a separate letter as per     Annexure “A” enclosed herewith.

Branches, MIS Dept. and Accounts Dept. should also note to classify the housing loans sanctioned upto Rs.20.00 Lakh (Rs. 25.00 Lakh for loans sanctioned on or after 01.04.2011) under Priority Sector.

Credit Rating Interest Rates are linked with PLR. Branches should note to charge the rate whatever and wherever applicable.

Branches are advised to display prominently the changed Chart of Rates of Interest w.e.f. 01.10.2011 in Public Notice Board and also send letters to the Borrowers as per Annexures enclosed regarding upward revision in rate of interest. The office copy of this letter should be filed in respective Loan File.

Planning & Marketing Dept. shall arrange for printing of advertising material with the new rates.

I.T. Dept. shall make necessary changes on our website and in our system accordingly and also send SMS to such Borrowers where revision is made.          

Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of concerned officials/staff members at your branch and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

 

     V. S. Morye

Managing Director
