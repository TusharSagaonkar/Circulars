Circular ID No. 58 dated 6 August 2008

 

Interest Rates on Term Deposits – Premature Withdrawal

 

          Please refer to our Circular ID No. 57 dated 11.07.2008 wherein we have revised our Interest Rates on Term Deposits for various periods w.e.f. 14.07.2008.  The revised rates were applicable to only fresh deposits and on renewal of maturing deposits on their due dates.  Those who wish to renew the existing deposits with premature closure for getting higher rates, for them we have stipulated the penalty clause.

2.       We are now receiving requests from our depositors from various corners that bank should consider the renewal of the existing Fixed Deposits with premature closure but without applying the penalty clause.

3.       The matter has been reviewed at our end and it has been decided as under:

          The premature closure of existing Fixed Deposits, for the purpose of reinvestment in Fixed Deposits to avail Higher Rate should be permitted without Penalty Clause.

4.       However, while renewing the existing Fixed Deposit before maturity for the purpose of reinvestment, the following aspects should be carefully noted and implemented meticulously: -

i.        In case of premature closure of a Term Deposit for reinvestment purpose, interest will be paid/payable for the period from date of original contract upto date of premature renewal at the applicable rate without charging penalty of 1%, provided deposit after reinvestment shall remain with the bank for a period longer than remaining period of the original contract.

ii.       If such deposit is withdrawn before the expiry of the period of original contract, the penalty of 1% is to be levied from the date of original contract upto the date of the premature withdrawal after reinvestment.  If the premature withdrawal is made after due date of the original contract then the penalty is to be levied from the date of reinvestment to the date of premature withdrawal.

5.       The above position is explained in the following illustration:

A depositor had invested a sum of Rs.10,000/- @ 7% in term deposit on 01.06.2006 for a period of 35 months i.e. due on 01.05.2009.  In the wake of revision in interest rates w.e.f. 26.02.2007, the depositor applied on 01.03.2007 (i.e. on completion of a period of 9 months) for premature closure of the original term deposit for the purpose of reinvestment for a period of 29 months, @ 9% which will be due on 01.08.2009.

Case I          :        When depositor requests for premature withdrawal of deposit on 01.10.2007.

          The deposit after premature renewal on 01.03.2007 has remained with the bank only for 7 months (01.03.2007 to 30.09.2007).  As this period was less than the remaining period of original contract upto 30.04.2009, it amounts to a premature withdrawal of original term deposit and interest in such case should be paid as under:

From  01.06.2006           1% below the rate applicable for term deposit

to       28.02.2007           for 9 months as on 01.06.2006 i.e. 5.5% - 1% = 4.5%

(i.e. 9 months)

 

From  01.03.2007           1% below the rate applicable for term deposit

to       30.09.2007           of 7 months as on 01.03.2007 i.e. 6.50% - 1% = 5.5%

(i.e. 7 months)

 

Case II:       When depositor requests for premature withdrawal

of deposit on 01.07.2009.

          In this case, the deposit after premature renewal on 01.03.2007 has remained with the bank upto 01.07.2009 i.e. after expiry of the original contract upto 30.04.2009.  The penalty of 1% reduction in interest will therefore be levied from 01.03.2007, i.e. the date of reinvestment.  The interest in this case should be paid as under:

From  01.06.2006           The rate applicable for term deposit for 9

to       28.02.2007           months as on 01.06.2006 i.e. 5.5%

(i.e. 9 months)

 

From  01.03.2007           1% below the rate applicable to term deposit of 2

to       30.06.2009           years and 4 months as on 01.03.2007 i.e. 9%-1% = 8%

(i.e. 2 years & 4 months)

 

Note: In both the cases, the interest already paid to the depositor while allowing premature withdrawal on 01.03.2007 will have to be adjusted from the interest payable as worked out above.

          Branches should specifically mention the noting of "Premature renewal of FDR No………." on prematurely renewed FDRs and also in special instruction to avoid any ambiguity while making payment of interest if depositors request for premature closure of such deposits. 

 

          The above instructions will come into force with retrospective effect from 14.07.2008.
