Circular ILA No. 94 dated the 5th June 2024

 

New Housing Loans Sanctioned/To Be Sanctioned Between 01.06.2024 And 31.08.2024

To Carry Lower Interest Rates Based On Cibil Score (Credit Vision Score) And 50% Of Normal Processing Charges

 

I.     The current regime in the Bank in respect of rates of interest (ROIs) on loans and advances is in accordance with the Circular ILA No. 92 dated 15th February 2024 save and except credit facilities against pledge of gold ornaments for which ROIs were subsequently revised with effect from 1st April 2024 vide the Circular ILA No.93 dated the
30th March 2024.

 

II.  Lowering interest rate and processing charges on new housing loans over a limited period

1.   In order to boost the portfolio of housing loans over a short term, a decision has been taken by the competent authority to lower ROIs on new housing loans sanctioned and to be sanctioned during the period of three months starting from the 1st June 2024 to the 31st August 2024. Further, the ROIs on such new housing loans shall be linked to the CIBIL score (Credit Vision Score) of the borrowers.

2.   A decision has also been taken to reduce the processing charges on such new housing loans over the said limited period (from the current rates 0.25% + applicable GST for loans up to Rs.35.00 Lakh and 0.50% + applicable GST for loans above Rs.35.00 Lakh) by 50%, effectively making them 0.125%+ applicable GST for loans upto Rs.35.00 Lakh and 0.25% + applicable GST for loans above Rs.35.00 Lakh.

IV.     All the instructions in respect of ROIs contained in the Circular ILA No. 92 dated 15th February 2024 (as partially modified in respect of ROIs on credit facilities against pledge of gold ornaments with effect from 1st April 2024 vide the Circular ILA No. 93 dated the 30th March 2024) continue to remain operative.

V.   Similarly, the extant instructions on eligibility, standards, and norms for housing loans must be rigorously followed.

 

VI.   Branches are expected to make the best possible use of the three months’ window of concessional ROIs and processing charges to book substantial business in the portfolio of housing loans by galvanising the rank and file of the organisation. Cooperation and participation of staff in administrative offices and departments should also be enlisted; the heads of the departments and offices must take initiative in this behalf. Branches are advised to note and report on the lead generation by the staff members.

 

VII.   Planning and Marketing Department to canvas extensively for boosting this Housing Loan scheme which is for limited period.
