Circular ILA No. 70  Dated 21st November, 2013

Concessional Rate of Interest on Commercial Loans/Advances

at Kankavali and Udupi Region Branches

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

With a view to give impetus for business growth and to compete fairly with other Banks in Kankavali and Udupi areas, our Bank has offered concessional rate of interest @ 13% upto 31.03.2013 on all commercial Loans & Advances sanctioned upto and including Rs.100.00 Lakh at our Kankavali Branch and Branches at Udupi Region vide our Circular ILA No. 63 dated 29.03.2012.

 

Now, Board in its meeting held on 08.11.2013 has decided as under:-

 

On all Commercial Loans and Advances sanctioned upto and including Rs. 100.00 Lakh at our Kankavali Branch and Branches at Udupi Region, the rate of interest will be charged @ 13% p.a. for a period of 2 years i.e. from 1.04.2013 to 31.03.2015. This revised rate of interest would be applicable to all existing as well as for new Commercial Loans & Advances.

 

Branches at Udupi Region and Kankavali should note that the interest rates on Housing Loans, Education Loans, Rent Securitization Loans, Gold Loan, Vehicle Loan will be concessional rate  as per our Circular ILA No. 69 dated 08.05.2013 and Circular CR No. 90 dated 19.10.2013.

 

The credit rating exercise wherever applicable will be done although the rate would be @13% p.a. fixed upto 31.03.2015. However, penal interest would be charged if credit rating exercise could not be completed due to non-submission of financial statements by the borrower.  Branches are advised to inform all their existing customers as per Annexure-I  to this Circular.

 

The Incharge of Branches at Kankavali and Udupi Regions are advised to put in best efforts in mobilization of large number of Loans & Advances upto Rs. 1.00 Crore.  They should approach retailers, professionals and self-employed and other business enterprises and impress upon them the concessional rate of interest and other services offered by the Bank and persuade them to avail credit limits  from our Bank.

 

Branch In-charges of Kankavali and Udupi Region Branches will take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all concerned staff members and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

 

 

     V. S. Morye

Managing Director
