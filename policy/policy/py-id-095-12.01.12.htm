3. Further, rate of interest on our Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme (ATSS) was last revised w.e.f. 1.10.2009 vide our circular ID No. 69 dated 09.09.2009. There after although we have revised interest rates on other Term Deposits, we have not revised interest rate for deposits under ATSS. To remain competitive and mobilize the deposit for long term duration, Board in its meeting held on 11.01.2002 approved the revision in Rate of Interest for the deposits under ATSS.

4. Branches are advised to make maximum efforts also to mobilize the deposits for Short Term and Deposit under ATSS with these substantially revised rates of interest.

 

5.  Asst. Gen. Manager, Planning & Marketing Department should help branches for by providing the pamphlets, banners etc, and get the maximum benefit of revision of interest for increasing the Bank’s deposit portfolio.

 

6. Asst. Gen. Manager, I.T. Department should make necessary changes in the system and update the information on Bank’s website.
