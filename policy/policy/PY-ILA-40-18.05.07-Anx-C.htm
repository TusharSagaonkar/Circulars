Rebate of 2% on regular loans and CC accounts of erstwhile Citizens Co-op. Bank Ltd.:

 

          Erstwhile Citizens Co-op. Bank Ltd. had vide Circular No. HO/552/06-07 dated 9.3.2006 informed the Branches to allow rebate of 2% in rate of interest on all regular Loan/CC accounts w.e.f. 1.4.2005.
