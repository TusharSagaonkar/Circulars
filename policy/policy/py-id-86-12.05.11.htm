Circular ID No. 86  dated  12th May, 2011

Conversion of Term Deposit, Daily Deposit or Recurring Deposit for reinvestment in Term Deposit with out the clause of penalty @ 1%

 

         

Please refer to our Circular O.C. No. 551 dated 10th January, 2011, and subsequent Circular I.D. No. 83 dated 17th March, 2011.

 

2.       In terms of Circular O.C. No. 551 dated 10th January, 2011, we have instructed our branches as under :-

 

"On request from the depositor, branches shall allow closure of a Term Deposit, a deposit in a form of daily deposit, or recurring deposit to enable the depositor to immediately reinvest the amount lying in the aforesaid deposit with our branches for any suitable period in another Term Deposit, by paying interest in respect of such term deposit at the rate applicable for which the deposit has remained with the Bank, with charging the penalty of 1%."

 

"Branches should therefore note that penalty clause is applicable for all premature withdrawals even for the sake of reinvestment on all existing as well as future Term Deposits."

 

The above instructions came into effect from 01.02.2011.

 

3.       Considering the various representations from our depositors from nook and corner, our Board in its meeting held on 10th May, 2011 has decided to follow the same practice / policy which we were following before 1st February, 2011.

 

As such the revised instructions are as under :-

 

 

(a)  In case of request from Depositor for premature closure of Term Deposit, Daily Deposit or Recurring Deposit for reinvestment purpose, interest will be paid/payable for the period from the date of original contract up to date of premature renewal at the applicable rate without charging penalty of 1% provided deposit after reinvestment remains with the bank for a period longer than remaining period of original contract.

 

 

 

 

(b)  If such deposit is withdrawn before the expiry of the period of original contract, the penalty of 1% to be levied from the date of original contract up to date of the pre-mature withdrawal after reinvestment. If the premature withdrawal is made after due date of the original contract then the penalty of 1% is to be levied from the date of reinvestment to the date of premature withdrawal.

 

(c)  Branches are therefore advised to take a note of original contract date on renewed and reinvested Term Deposit Receipt.

 

The above instructions will come into effect from 16th May, 2011.        

 

4.       Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members at your Branch/Dept. for compliance.
