Notes on Credit Rating:

 

i) New Loans and Advances means all loans and advances eligible for Credit Rating on the date of sanction.  These will be charged interest rates as per XVIII above except takeover Priority Sector accounts with limit of Rs.25.00 Lakh and above.  The rate of interest will be charged as per changes made from time to time till one year from the date of documentation.  They will be eligible for interest rate applicable as per Credit Rating after completion of one year from the date of documentation. 

ii)  Credit Rating exercise of takeover Priority Sector accounts with limit of Rs.25.00 Lacs and above will be done in the initial stage at the time of takeover and rate of interest will be applied to them as per grading.           

iii) Builders, Developers and other Civil Contractors:  No grading is applicable. But yearly Credit Rating exercise should be done.

iv) For Mortgage Loans, Premises Loans, Rent Securitization Loan, Credit Rating is not applicable.

v)  Credit Rating is also not applicable for non-funded limits such as Bank Guarantee, L/C.  These Loans/limits should not be clubbed to existing facilities to ascertain where Credit Rating is applicable.

i)        Interest will be charged on daily reducing balance. 

 

ii)       The rebate of 1% allowed to Housing Loans is withdrawn since 01.04.2008. However, penal interest @3% will be charged for the period of default as per extant instructions.

 

iii)      Since Rebate is discontinued from 1st April 2008, the Housing Loan A/cs. eligible for rebate before 01.04.2008 and whose repayment has started after April 2007 and who have paid regular EMIs upto March 2008 but their number of EMIs are less than 12, these accounts will be entitled for rebate for the broken period upto March 2008.  Example - if the repayment has started from August 2007 and the borrower pays all EMIs regularly upto March 2008, such borrower will be entitled for Rebate for 8 months i.e. August 2007 upto March 2008. The branches are advised to credit the Rebate amount to such accounts before 15.08.2008 to avoid litigation in future as we have discontinued Rebate.

 

iv)      It may be noted that interest rate changes are not applicable to Housing Loans who have been sanctioned and disbursed Housing Loans under Fixed Rates of Interest during the period 01.07.2005 to 30.06.2006.

No prepayment charges or penal interest to Surety Loan Accounts to be levied eventhough taken over by other Bank.

3.       Credit discipline:

 

          In order to inculcate the desired credit discipline among the borrowers, branches are advised to address letters to all the borrowing customers specifically emphasizing the importance of adherence to credit discipline and that the bank will be levying penal interest for non-adherence to the discipline (Format B). It may also be pointed out to the borrowing customers that penal interest @ 3% p.a. applicable for the period of default will be levied in cases of the following defaults:

(i)       Non-repayment   of    loan   installments.

(ii)      Non-payment of interest.

(iii)     Availing excess over sanctioned limit or excess over Drawing Power.

(iv)     Non-renewal/non-review above 3 months of cash credit limit due to non-submission of documents. In case of unreviewed CC A/cs default period will end on 1st day of next month, after receipt of all documents for review.

(v)      Bills discounted not realized on due dates.

(vi)     Submission of audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account for the year ended March, after 31st December of the next immediate financial year. This is applicable for Credit Rating of borrowal accounts.

:  9  :

(vii)    Turnover in the account less than 25% of the limits.  Turnover  charges at the penal rate shall be applied for half yearly  period for borrowers enjoying  facilities for round the year business and at yearly intervals for borrowers engaged in business of seasonal nature in terms of guidelines in Circular OC No. 376 dated 14.12.2006. After identifying the borrowers eligible for levy of penal interest for turnover of less than 25%, the penalty should be levied only for the months in which monthly turnover was less than 25% of the limits.

(viii)   Non-submission of Stock and Book Debt statement at monthly intervals. Such statements showing the position of Stocks/Book Debts on the last day of the month should be received latest by 15th of the following month, Circular OC No. 311 dated 14.07.2005 should be followed for guidance in this regard.

(ix)     Auditor's certified Book debts statement: As per the decision of the Audit Committee Meeting, borrower enjoying CC & WCTL limit with limit above Rs.25.00 lacs and also with component of Drawing Power against Book Debt above Rs.25.00 lacs, are required to submit auditor's certified Book Debt statements as on 30th June and 31st December every year.  Those statements are required for Stock Audit purpose. These accounts will be charged penal interest @ 3% p.a. in case of non-submission of Auditor's certified Book Debt statements by 31st August and 28th February. However, Book Debt statements alongwith stock statements without certificate by Auditor will be accepted by 15th of the succeeding month and reckoned for Drawing Power. 

The branches are advised to send reminder letters in the month of June and December every year to all eligible borrowal accounts who are required to submit Auditor's certified Book Debts statement.

(x)      It is necessary to advise all borrowers the revised rate of interest and EMI amount.  The Draft of Notices to be sent to Loan and Cash Credit accountholders is enclosed with this Circular. The EMI for Loan Accounts should be calculated with the present amount outstanding in the Loan A/c., revised rate of interest and balance period of the loan.

            This is to inform you that due to inflationary economic conditions and wide changes in the money market, RBI have recently increased CRR from 8.25% to 8.75%.  Similarly, Repo Rate has also been increased from 8.00% to 8.50%.  With the result, Banks have to compulsorily increase the interest rates on Deposits which has forced all Banks to increase their PLR.  Though, other Banks have increased from time to time their PLR during the last one year, in order  not to pass burden of enhanced interest to our valued customers, we have not made any major changes in increasing the rates since 01.05.2007.  As we have increased interest rates on Deposits substantially and other operational costs have also increased, the PLR has been raised from 11.50% to 13.50%.  Accordingly, the revised rate of interest on your Cash Credit account is ________% w.e.f. 01.08.2008.  You are advised to please take note of the same and ensure that payment of interest in your CC A/c. is made on or before 10th of succeeding month.
