Circular ILA No. 71 dated 3rd February, 2014

Revision of Lending Rates  for Housing Loans

w.e.f. 05.02.2014

 

Considering the amount of loans and advances outstanding at present in the Real Estate Sector, Bank has to give attention to maintain the exposure of Real Estate within the limit prescribed by RBI. Accordingly, the Board in its meeting held on 21.01.2014 has decided to revise the rate of interest for housing loans w.e.f 05.02.2014 as under:  

 

Sr. No.

Types of facility

Rate of interest effective from 05.02.2014

 

Prime Lending Rate (PLR)

13.50%

 

1.

Housing Loan :

 

a)   Loans sanctioned upto and

       including  Rs.25.00 Lakh

 

b)  Loans sanctioned above                                                                  Rs.25.00 and upto Rs.70.00 Lakh  

 

 

 

PLR – 2.50

(Floating)

 i.e. 11.00%

 

 

 

PLR + 1.5

(Floating)

 

  i.e. 15.00%

 

Notes :

1. The above revision in lending rates is applicable w.e.f. 5th February, 2014.

2. The above new rates of interest would be applicable only to those loans and advances which are sanctioned on or after 5th February, 2014.

3. In case of above housing loans which are sanctioned prior to 5th February, 2014 and part disbursement therein has already taken place, then such loans will attract the old rate of interest as per Circular ILA No.69 dated 08.05.2013.

4. In case of existing Housing loans please refer to detailed guidelines in Circular ILA No.69 where –

a)   Housing loans sanctioned and disbursed during the period from 01.07.2005 to 30.06.2006 with fixed rate of interest till closure of this Housing loan accounts,  

b) Housing loans sanctioned and disbursed on or before 06.03.2012 and

c)  Housing loans sanctioned and disbursed on or after 07.03.2012 and upto 04.02.2014. 

 

Thus, the present rates of interest on existing Housing loans (fully or partly disbursed) outstanding as on and upto 04.02.2014 would remain unchanged.

 

5. a) Housing loans upto Rs. 25.00 Lakh :   

Some of the Housing Loans Applicants might have submitted their housing loan proposals at Branches and they must be under process. As Branches have assured them loan and they have entered into Sale Deed, such proposals upto Rs 25.00 Lakh should be sanctioned on or before 4th February, 2014. These loans be charged rate of interest @ 10.50% though disbursed on or after 05.02.2014.

b) Housing loan above `. 25.00 Lakh and upto `. 70.00 Lakh:

These loans sanctioned on or after 5th February, 2014 will be charged rate of interest @ 15%.  Hence their specific consent for rate of interest @ 15% p.a.       (PLR + 1.5) be obtained as per enclosed format to avoid any misunderstanding or litigation in future. 

c) Housing loan above `. 25.00 Lakh and upto `. 70.00 Lakh :

These loans sanctioned upto 3rd February, 2014 whether sanction letter is issued or not, the old rate of interest will be charged as per Circular ILA No. 69 dated 08.05.2013 

6.       As rates of interest on all existing loans remain unchanged, letters of revision in rate of interest will not be sent to them.

 

Branches are advised to take note of the above instructions carefully and ensure that the correct rates of interest are applied to existing Housing loans in terms of Circular ILA No.69 dated 08.05.2013 and new loans.  The Incharge of Branches are advised to inform the contents of this Circular to all staff members and ensure strict compliance thereof.

 

 

 

     V. S. Morye

Managing Director
