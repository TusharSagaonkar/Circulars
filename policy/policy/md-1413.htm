RULES GOVERNING STAFF BENEVOLENT FUND

 

1.       The fund is named as “Abhyudaya Co-op. Bank’s Staff Benevolent Fund”.

2.       This fund will be created out of the Net Profit with prior approval of the General Body.

3.       The amount will be contributed to this Fund by appropriation of the Net Profits, as recommended by Board of Directors and approved by the Annual General Meeting.

4.       The object of the fund is to provide financial assistance for the benefit and betterment of the employees of the Bank, as under :

(i) Medical Benefits :

Reimbursement of expenditure will be considered as per the provisions of Memorandum of Agreement entered into between the Bank and the Employees Union / Officers Association, as applicable from time to time.

(ii) Financial aid to next to kin and permanently disabled employee :

 The Financial assistance to next to kin and to permanently disable employee will be considered as per the provisions of Memorandum of Agreement entered into between the Bank and the Employees Union/Officers Association, as applicable from time to time.
