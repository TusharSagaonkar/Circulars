Circular ILA No. 40 dated 18th May 2007

 

Interest Rates on Loans & Advances

- Revision effective 1.5.2007

 

          Revision of interest rates on loans and advances made effective from 1 May 2007 has been communicated to branches by means of an IDC, vide our reference No. HO/Addl. GM/L&A/32/2007-08 dated 7 May 2007. The said IDC was comprehensive one and covered interest changes as well as other related instructions in the form of explanatory notes. With a view to putting the records straight this formal ILA Circular which incorporates the contents of the aforesaid IDC is being issued for information, use and record of all concerned.

 

 

           A reference is invited to Circular ILA No. 39 dated 12 March 2007 advising the revised rates of interest introduced with effect from 1 April 2007.  It was pointed out therein that very significant changes had taken place in the economy during the last 3/4 months and to tackle the problem of the growth in the rate of inflation, Reserve Bank of India had initiated measures like raising the Repo rates, increasing the CRR requirements, raising the provisioning requirements on bank advances under standards assets in certain categories, etc.  It was also indicated in the Circular that in order to offset the impact of these measures taken by RBI and the inflationary pressure, shrinking margins, etc. a moderate upward revision of the lending rates of our bank was approved by the Board in the meeting held on 21 February 2007 and the revised lending rates were made applicable with effect from 1st April 2007.  However, RBI has  subsequently taken measure to further tighten liquidity in the economy by (i) increasing Repo rate by 0.25% to 7.75% w.e.f. 31.3.2007 (3.4.2007) and, (ii) raising the CRR requirement  from 6.00% of DTL to 6.25% of DTL from 14.4.2007 &  further to 6.50% of DTL from 28.4.2007. The hike in CRR, and Repo rate effected by RBI has led to banks increasing their interest rates on deposits and advances.  Our present rates of interest on advances, even after the revision made effective from 1.4.2007, are lower compared to other banks.  In order to be in line with the banking industry in general and also to sustain a desired level of margin and profitability further upward revision in the rate of interest on loans and advances has been approved by the Board in the meeting held on 28.4.2007. x)           The penal interest will be 3% p.a. over the rate applicable as per the existing grading. Credit rating shall be completed within 7 days at branch and 8 days at Zonal Office. It will be the responsibility of Branch and Zonal Office to complete this exercise within 15 days of the receipt of audited balance sheet and profit and loss statements.  If the gradation is revised at any time subsequently and penal interest continued to be charged, it will be charged at 3% p.a. over the revised rate of interest for the facility. Since last date of submission of audited balance sheet and profit and loss accounts statements is 31st December, the penal interest will be charged from 1st January next year.

It may also be pointed out to the borrowing customers that penal interest @ 3% p.a. applicable for the period of default will be levied in cases of the following defaults:

(iv)    Non-renewal/non-review above 3 months of cash credit limit due to non-submission of documents. In case of unreviewed CC A/cs default period will end on 1st day of next month, after receipt of all documents for review.

After identifying the borrowers eligible for levy of penal interest for turnover of less than 25%, the penalty should be levied only for the months in which monthly turnover was less than 25% of the limits.

(viii)  Non-submission of Stock and Book Debt statement at monthly intervals. Such statements showing the position of Stocks/Book Debts on the last day of the month should be received latest by 15th of the following month, Circular OC No. 311 dated 14.7.2005 should be followed for guidance in this regard.

These accounts will be charged penal interest @ 3% p.a. in case of non-receipt of Auditor certified Book Debt statements by 31st August and 28th February. However, Book Debt statements alongwith stock statements without certificate by Auditor will be accepted by 15th of succeeding month and reckoned for Drawing Power.
