Circular ILA No. 45 dated 11 August 2008

 

Interest Rates on Export Credit

w.e.f. 01.08.2008

 

          Please refer to our Office Order No. 245 dated 19.03.2008.  All designated branches in "A", "B" and "C" Categories are advised that consequent upon the increase in our PLR, the Board of Directors in their meeting held on 05.08.2008 have reviewed the position and revised the Interest Rates on Export Credit w.e.f. 01.08.2008, which are as under.  Our rates are most competitive.  Please take suitable note of the following instructions and act accordingly.
