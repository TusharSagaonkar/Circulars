Circular ID No. 125 dated 30th May, 2020

 

Interest Rates on

Domestic/NRO/NRE Saving Deposits

________________________________________________________________________________

 

         

I.                 A review was recently taken, in the meeting of the Asset Liability Committee (ALCO) dated 15th May, 2020, with regard to cost of deposits in the current competitive environment & a view emerged therein that there was a need to revise interest rates being offered by Bank on Domestic as well as Non Resident Saving Deposits. A submission in this behalf was placed before the Board meeting dated 28th May, 2020 in which the same met with approval.

 

II.    In accordance therewith, it is hereby being advised that, w.e.f. 1st June, 2020, the revised rates of interest on Domestic as well as NRO & NRE Saving Deposits shall be 3.50 % p.a.

 

III.   On the subject of “1% Additional Interest for Staff’, there are extant instructions vide Office Order No. 503 dated 30th August, 2018. The said instructions, subject to conditions & compliances stipulated therein, continue to remain operative.

 

IV.    I.T. Dept. shall make necessary changes in the system to give effect to this revision w.e.f. 1st June, 2020. Arrangements shall also be made to ensure that interest for the period of six months ending on 31st August, 2020 (to be applied on 1st September, 2020) consists of two parts, i.e. interest for the first three months i.e. March, April and May, 2020 is calculated at 4 % p.a. & interest for the second part comprising June, July, and August, 2020 is calculated at 3.5 % p.a.

 

V. Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all the staff members at your Branch/Dept. for compliance.

 

 

                                     

P. S. Salian

       Managing Director
