Sub: Revision in interest rates in all loan accounts

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

        A reference is invited to Circular No. ILA 41 advising revision of interest rates on Loans and Advances w.e.f. 1.10.2007.

 

          In this respect, branches are advised to follow the guidelines issued as per IDC No. HO/Addl.GM/L&A/223/07-08 dated 26.9.2007 as mentioned in the Circular No. ILA – 41 on Pg. No. 3. The same is reproduced below:-

 

          To generate the process for increase in no. of installments for Housing Loans having  less than 180 installments.

i.      The execution of process is as follows.

The process programme is loaded in Accountant and Managers mol under Limit Master as Tenure Calculation.  First run this programme in Trial Mode, by entering drawing power as on date as 1-10-2007 and account opening date as 01-01-1964.  If you are satisfied with the output then run the programme once again in Apply Mode to create new limits as on 1-10-2007. 

 

   ii.    It is decided to increase the amount of EMI for Housing Loan only for account having 180 installments.  To calculate the revised EMI amount a program has  been loaded in Limit Master as EMI Calculation in Accountant and Managers mol.  This is just a help programme to derive new EMI amount.  Branches are requested to create manually new limits for those accounts with effective date as on 01-10-2007 with new EMI amount and set ideal balance as on 01-10-2007 as  disbursement amount in the second screen of Limit Master.  Retain other fields like sanction date, Sanction Limit amount, Equated y/n, Interest funded y/n, Frequency, No. of installments, Slab amount in the first screen as it is in the earlier limit.

 

          The said assignment is to be completed on  31st October 2007 positively.
