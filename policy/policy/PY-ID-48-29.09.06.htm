Circular ID No. 48  dated 29  September 2006

 

Interest Rates on Deposits - Revision

 

I.       Upward Revision of  Interest Rates on Term Deposits  of  General Public, Co-op. Societies, etc. :-

 

 

          The Abhyudaya Tax Saving Deposit Scheme (ATSS) has been introduced w.e.f. 23 September 2006, as advised in Circular OC No. 363 dated 11 September 2006. Higher interest rates of 8.5% and 9% have been offered for fixed deposits under the scheme  kept  by the General Public and Senior Citizens respectively. In the context of launching of the Scheme the Assets and Liability Management Committee (ALCO) reviewed in their meeting held on 8 September 2006, the general  rising trend in interest rates on term deposits offered by other banks. The Committee also considered  the fact that the structural liquidity of our bank  showed a mismatch in outflows and inflows over the period of  3 years and upto 5 years.   In order to keep interest rates offered by our bank  in alignment  with prevailing rising trends in the banking industry and also to facilitate correction of the  asset liability mismatch by raising more term deposits in longer maturity slots of 3 to 5 years and longer period upto 10 year, ALCO recommended  upward revision of interest rates on term deposits for 3 to 5 years and over 5 years and upto 10 years.  The ALCO recommendation was approved by the Board in their meeting held on 20 September 2006, raising the interest rates on term deposits of longer duration as under :-

          Further, it is clarified for information of all concerned that there is no change in the maturity periodwise slabs for which term deposits may be accepted and the rate of interest applicable for each period,  advised in Circular ID No. 46 dated 5 February 2005, remain unchanged.

 

II.  Interest Rates on Staff Deposits :-

          A reference is also invited to the Circular ID 10 dated 2 July 1996, advising that additional interest not exceeding 1% p.a. would be allowed on savings on term deposits account opened in the  name of  -

 

a)

a member or a retired member of the bank’s staff, either singly or jointly with any other member or members of his/her family, or

 

b)

the spouse of a deceased member or a deceased retired member of the bank staff.

 

c)

an Association or a fund, members of which are the members of the bank’s staff.

 

        However, it has been observed that some of our staff members are taking benefit of the said facility but at the same time avoid TDS by keeping the deposit with first name of spouse and spouse submitting 15-G form.  In order to remove this discrepancy, it is decided that in cases where benefit of additional interest is offered to staff members on deposits, it should be specifically ensured that the staff member must be the first holder of deposit in case of deposits kept in joint names. It is also reiterated that  declaration from the staff member/depositor stating that the  monies deposited or which may, from time to time be deposited into such account, shall be monies belonging to the  staff in case of (a) and of the depositor in case of (b) and (c) above.
