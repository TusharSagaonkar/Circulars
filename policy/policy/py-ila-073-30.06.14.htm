Circular ILA No.73 dated 30th June, 2014

 

Revised Rate of Interest on Housing Loans w.e.f. 01.07.2014

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The advances of the Bank as on 24.06.2014 were ` 5547.16 Crore against deposit of ` 9417.52 Crore.  The Credit Deposit Ratio is only 58.90%.  The Credit Deposit Ratio of the Bank as on 31.03.2013 was 63.57% which got reduced to 61.09% as on 31.03.2014 and it has been further reduced to 58.90% as on 24.06.2014.  This is going to affect the interest income on loans and advances.  Secondly, as per Performance Budget for 2014-2015, the thrust is on retail loans and advances upto ` 2.00 Crore.  Hitherto, the Bank’s housing loans to individuals have been the best portfolio with least NPAs.  It is observed that due to high rate of interest in housing loan as compared to rate of interest offered by Nationalized Banks and other large    Co-op. Banks, number of new housing loans sanctioned have reduced considerably.  A detailed note was placed before the Board Meeting held on 24.06.2014 and it was decided to reduce the rate of interest as under on new housing loans sanctioned on or after 01.07.2014 and already sanctioned housing loans of which first disbursement is made on or after 01.07.2014.

        It may please be noted that the earlier rates were 11% upto ` 25.00 lakh and 15% above ` 25.00 lakh and upto ` 70.00 lakh.  Accordingly, the amount of EMI would be reduced proportionately.  The benefit of lower amount of EMI should be made known to our loanees and sureties.  Branches are, therefore, advised to send letter to loanees and all sureties, who were sanctioned housing loan during the period 05.02.2014 to 30.06.2014; as per Annexure and retain one copy thereof in the housing loan file of each loanee. This will go a long way in image building of the Bank, which may please be noted.

 

Branches are advised to bring to the notice of new customers regarding the above reduction in rate of interest, for the purpose of marketing the housing loan product.  This would also assist staff members and officials to achieve the individual target set under Housing Loans in the Bank’s Action Plan.

Planning & Marketing Dept. shall arrange for printing of advertising material with the new rates.

I.T. Dept. shall make necessary changes in website and in the system accordingly.

Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of the officials/staff members concerned at your branch and ensure compliance.
