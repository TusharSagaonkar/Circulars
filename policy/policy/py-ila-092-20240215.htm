Circular ILA No. 92 dated 15th February 2024

 

Revision of Lending Rates w.e.f. 16th February 2024

 

I.     The Prime Lending Rate of the Bank has undergone a change from 11.50% to 12.25 % as approved by the competent authority on the 7th February 2024.

 

II.    The revised rates of interest on Loans & Advances which are to take effect from the 16th February 2024 consequent upon the change in the PLR are given in the chart enclosed herewith and marked as Annexure-I.

 

III.  Further, the maximum rate of interest which may be applied inclusive of additional incidence of interest if any, on any credit facility cannot exceed 17.25% p.a.

 

IV.  In respect of EMI-based loans, either the EMI or the amortization schedule or both will need revision consequent upon the upward revision of the ROI. The choice of revising any or both of them will be given to the borrower subject however to the examination of the monthly income of the borrower, his/her earning-life span and the number of maximum EMIs permissible under the relative scheme.

 

V. In respect of Additional Interest to be charged for “Foreclosure/takeover of Loans & Advances Accounts” and other “Guidelines on Loans & Advances Interest Rates”, branches shall refer to principles laid down in the extant Credit Policy.

 

VI.  The instructions for applying charges for breaches of credit discipline agreed to by the borrowers are being issued separately.

 

VII.   SMS, Notice on the Bank’s website and Notice at branches

1. The IT dept. shall send a general SMS to the borrowers in respect of interest rates having been changed.

2. The IT department shall also display a suitable notice to this effect on the Bank’s website.

3. A notice as per Annexure-II hereto shall be displayed on the Notice Board at each branch.

 

VIII.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all Officials/Staff members at your branch and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

 

 

     P. S. Salian

Executive Officer
