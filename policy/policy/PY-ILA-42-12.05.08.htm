Circular ILA No. 42 dated 12 May 2008

 

Interest Rates on Loans & Advances

- Revision in Housing Loan Interest Rate

effective from 01.05.2008

 

          A reference is invited to page 2 item no. 2 of our Circular ILA No. 41 dated   1st October, 2007 advising the revision of interest rate on Loans & Advances under Housing Loan Scheme effective from 1st October, 2007.  With a view to bring rationalization in the interest rates on housing loans, the Board in its meeting held on 12th April, 2008 have modified the existing instructions and have revised the rates of interest applicable to Housing Loans with effect from 1st May, 2008.  The changes approved by the Board are as under: -

 

Sr. No.

Existing Guidelines

Revised Guidelines

1.

Housing Loan: -

 

(SDL/ECS)

a) Upto Rs.20.00 lacs - 10.50% (Floating)

b) Above Rs.20.00 lacs to Rs.25.00 lacs - 12.00% (Floating with Rebate of 1%)

All other Housing Loans: -

(Non SDL/Non ECS/Businessmen, etc.)

c) Upto Rs.20.00 lacs - 11.50% (Floating with Rebate of 1%)

d) Above Rs.20.00 lacs to Rs.25.00 lacs - 12.00% (Floating with Rebate of 1%)

 

The interest rates on Housing Loans have been revised w.e.f. 1.5.2008 as under: -

All Housing Loans: -

i. Upto Rs.20.00 Lakh – 10.50% (floating)

ii. Above Rs.20.00 Lakh - 11.00% (floating)

 

The Rebate of 1% is withdrawn w.e.f. 1.4.2008.  However, penal interest @ 3% be levied for the period of default as per extant instructions. 

 

 

2.

In case of mortgaged property given on rental basis, Tripartite Agreement should be executed between Bank, borrower and Licensee.

i. To Apply commercial rate of interest to borrowers who have rented their flats. 

ii. Joint Declaration-cum-Undertaking to be obtained instead of Tripartite Agreement (format enclosed).

 

 

 

…2

- 2 -

         

          In view of the change in the Interest Rates on Housing Loans as above, branches are advised as under:

 

1.  Suitable Notice should be displayed prominently on the Branch Notice Board by the branches regarding a) change of interest rate, b) withdrawal of rebate and c) application of commercial rate of interest to the accounts of the borrowers who have rented the flats. 

 

2.  Branches should also send Notices to the Borrowers and Sureties regarding change of interest rate, withdrawal of rebate and commercial interest rate to be applied to the housing loan accounts where the borrowers have rented the flats.

 

3.  It may be noted that interest rate changes are applicable only to existing housing loans who have opted for floating rate of interest and to new housing loan accounts.

There is no change of interest rate for the Housing Loan Accounts who have opted for 'Fixed Rate' and the loans have been duly sanctioned accordingly, incorporating the clause of 'Fixed Rate' in our Sanction letter (remember – not 'In-principle' approval letter).

 

          The detailed instructions in regard to modification in Credit Policy 2008-09 have already been covered in IDC Ref. No. HO/Addl.GM/L&A/42/ 2008-09 dated 23rd April, 2008.  The concerned officers are advised to go through the revised guidelines in detail and follow the same meticulously.
