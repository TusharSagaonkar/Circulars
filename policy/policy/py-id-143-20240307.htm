Circular ID No. 143 dated 7th March 2024

I.        Launching of “ABHYUDAYA 100” and “ABHYUDAYA 451” Schemes

II.      Modification in “ABHYUDAYA 200” Scheme

III.    Discontinuation of “ABHYUDAYA 500” Scheme

IV.     Reduction in interest rate on Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme

V.       All instructions to come into force from the 11th March 2024

_______________________________________________________________________________

I.    Previous instructions

1.   Attention is invited to ID circulars numbered 139 and 140 dated the/11st December 2023 and 15th December 2023 respectively through which instructions were issued in respect of two special fixed deposit schemes namely “ABHYUDAYA 500” and “ABHYUDAYA 200”

2.   Attention is also invited to the ID circular No. 138 dated the 26th May 2023 mentioning the interest rate on deposits under the Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme (ATSS), among others.

 

II.  With the approval of the competent authority, revised instructions as under are being issued. All of them are with effect from 11th March 2024.

1.   Two new Special Fixed Deposit Schemes namely “ABHYUDAYA 100” and “ABHYUDAYA 451” of duration respectively of 100 and 451 days are introduced.

2.   The “ABHYUDAYA 500” is being discontinued from 11.03.2024.

3.   The “ABHYUDAYA 200” is modified so that the stipulation of a minimum individual deposit size of Rs. 1 Lakh stands removed.

4.  Further, the interest rate on deposits under Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme (ATSS) has been reduced to 6.25 % p.a.

5.   The relevant particulars of the three Special Fixed Deposit Schemes [i.e. updated part of the existing “ABHYUDAYA 200”,/1all the particulars of the two new, namely “ABHYUDAYA 100” and “ABHYUDAYA 451”, and common particulars across all the three schemes (regarding loans against deposits and accepting only fresh deposits/matured deposits)] are given in Para III below.

 

III. 1. Details of the “ABHYUDAYA 100” scheme

i.     Duration of the scheme: - 100 days

ii.   The code assigned to it is “AB100"

a.   Interest rates: - 5.50% p.a. for deposits up to Rs. 1.00 Crore.

b.   6.00% p.a. for bulk deposits (i.e. of Rs. 1.00 Crore and above).

c.   No additional interest for senior citizens.

iii.   Special instructions for mobilisation: -Efforts should be made to mobilise maximum deposits under the scheme by approaching housing societies in the vicinity of branches.

iv.    Zone-wise targets for deposit mobilisation up to March 2024 under the scheme are assigned as under,

i.     Navi Mumbai Zone -/1Rs. 40.00 cr.

ii.   Mumbai Zone -/1Rs. 40.00 cr.

iii. Pune zone -/1Rs. 20.00 cr.

2.  Details of the “ABHYUDAYA 451” Scheme

i.     Duration: - 451 days

ii.   The code assigned to it is “AB451”

iii.  Interest rates: - a. 7.60 % p. a. for other than senior citizens.

/1b./18.10 % p. a. for senior citizens.

 

3.  Common features of the three Special Deposit Schemes (“ABHYUDAYA 100”, “ABHYUDAYA 451” and “ABHYUDAYA 200”)

i.     Eligible depositors:- General Public, Trusts, NRO Depositors, Co-op. Societies including Housing & Credit Societies

ii.   An additional interest of 1% will be given to staff.

iii.  Deposits of any amount of size more than Rs. 1000/- can be accepted under any of these schemes.

iv. ONLY fresh deposits/ matured deposits will be accepted under these schemes.

v.   If existing deposits are prematurely closed for re-investment under these schemes, the penalty for premature closing as per rule will be applicable.

vi. Features for loans against deposits under these schemes.

a. The amount of loan will be up to 90% of the accrued value. However, a maximum of 95% of Face Value may be allowed, if applied for by the customer, by the sanctioning authority as a special case with prior approval of the Chief Executive Officer.

b. The ROI on the loan will be 1% above the FDR rate; the interest on the loan will have to be serviced regularly.

IV. The interest rates and rules for premature payment of deposits and all other terms, and the general rules for terms deposits shall continue as per the extant instructions.

V.   Please bring the contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the members of the staff and mobilise deposits to the maximum extent as per the expectations.

 

                                     

P. S. Salian

       Executive Officer
