2.     We hereby state that by an agreement of Lease/Leave and License dated ___________ entered between us, the Borrower has given the said mortgaged premise on lease/licence to Mr./Mrs./M/s.___________________________(Lessee/Licensee) for a period of _________month/ year on monthly compensation of Rs.________________ and security deposit/premium/advance of Rs._________________.

4.     We hereby agree and undertake to make payments of monthly lease rent/compensation every month to the Bank at its Branch office or Administrative Office mentioned hereinabove, on the due dates payable by Licensee to Borrower under the Leave & License /Lease Agreement and in case there is no such agreement, on or before 10th date of every month, to appropriate the said amount towards repayment of interest or further interest and principal or EMI or any other amount payable by Borrower in connection with the said loan account or other dues to the Bank.

6.     We hereby further undertake that the payment of lease rents/compensation by the Licensee/Occupier to the Bank shall continue till the dues of the Bank are fully paid or the Licensee vacates and hands over possession of the premises to the Borrower or Bank.

 

7.     We herby agree and undertake not to modify the terms and conditions of the said Lease /Leave and License Agreement without prior written permission of the Bank.

 

8.     The Borrower hereby undertakes that in case the lease rent/compensation is not paid by the Licensee, as agreed hereinabove, and even if paid and the same is not sufficient to fully liquidate the agreed installment of repayment and interest, the Borrower shall make the payment of the balance or full installments (EMI), as the case may be with interest and other costs and dues payable by him/her/it.

 

9.     We further undertake to pay all rates, taxes, cesses, fees, revenue, duties, charges, assessment with penalty, if any, as and when demanded by the concerned authority and also maintenance and other outgoings in respect of said mortgaged premises and shall observe and perform all rules and regulations pertaining to the same and will not do or omit to do or suffer to be done, anything, whereby the mortgage security as created in favour of Bank, be affected or prejudiced in any manner whatsoever.

 

 

10.   We further declare and undertake that we have not done and we will not do any act of omission or commission, which would deteriorate the value of said premise or which would amount to sale, transfer, mortgage or in any way encumbering the right, title or interest, into and in respect of mortgaged property.

 

11.   We hereby undertake to permit inspection of the said mortgaged premise whenever the Bank demands for the same and make every possible provision and facilitate and co-operate the Bank officials without raising any objection for the same.

 

12.   It is declared and undertaken by the parties herein that the mortgage of the premises shall continue till the entire dues of the Bank are paid and discharged and the mortgage is released and “No Dues” certificate is issued by the Bank in writing.

 

13.   We specifically hereby declare and undertake unconditionally, to handover vacant and peaceful possession of the said mortgaged property/premises to the Bank or its Nominee/s as desired by the Bank, in case of enforcing the mortgage on account of the default in repayment or for violation of any of the terms of loan/limit granted by the Bank to the Borrower.
