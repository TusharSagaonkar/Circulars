Circular ILA No. 85 dated 29th October, 2019

 

Downward Revision in Rates of Interest on Housing Loans Sanctioned

on or after 01.11.2019 to Women Borrowers

 

 

I.                 1) Please refer Circular ILA No. 83 dated 22nd January, 2019. Attention of branches is particularly invited to the Row No. XI. iv. (a & b) of the Table of Interest Rates annexed to the said circular. The said row indicates the interest rates which were applicable for Housing Loans sanctioned on or after 01.06.2017.

 

2) Subsequently, ROI for Housing Loans sanctioned w. e. f. 1st October, 2019 was reduced by 0.10 % and such reduced rates were communicated through the Circular ILA No. 84 dated 27th September, 2019.

 

II.  1) A view has since emerged that the 0.10% reduction in ROI on Housing Loans sanctioned w. e. f. 1st October, 2019 has not worked as a sufficient incentive to Women Applicants seeking housing loans from our Bank, and competition demands that we reduce ROI for Women Borrowers further. The matter was placed in the Board meeting dated 23rd October, 2019 and it was approved therein to reduce the ROI for Housing Loans to be sanctioned on or after 1st November, 2019, by 0.25 %, to joint borrowers such that first name among them is that of a woman.

 

2) Therefore, the ROIs for Housing Loans (which are always joint) such that the first name among them is that of a woman, to be sanctioned on or after 1st November, 2019, will be as under.

 

Housing Loans in which the first joint borrower is woman

Rate of Interest (%) p.a.

Effective Rate (%) p.a.

i)   Housing loan upto Rs. 28.00 Lakh

PLR – 4.75

8.25

ii)  Housing Loan above Rs. 28.00 Lakh and upto & including Rs. 70.00 Lakh

PLR – 4.65

8.35

 

 

 

3) In order that the benefit of such concessional interest reaches women-house-owners, it should be ensured that the name of the woman among the joint borrowers appears first, not only in the loan documents but also in the Sale Deed/Agreement for Sale in respect of the property being acquired.

 

III.   1) The structure of ROI in respect of Housing Loans sanctioned up to 30th September 2019 will remain same as that advised through the Circular ILA No. 83 dated 22nd January, 2019.

 

2) The ROI in respect of Housing Loans sanctioned on or after 1st October, 2019 and up to 31st October, 2019 will remain same as that advised through the Circular ILA No. 84 dated 27th September, 2019.

 

3) The interest rates structure in respect of all other loans and advances will continue to be the same as communicated through the Circular ILA No. 83 dated 22nd January, 2019.

 

4) IT department is hereby instructed to update the interest rate structure on the Bank’s website in respect of the specific category.

 

5) Marketing department is instructed to give publicity to the special concessionary facility to the specific category.

 

IV.   Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all officials/staff members at your branch and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

 

     P. S. Salian

Managing Director
