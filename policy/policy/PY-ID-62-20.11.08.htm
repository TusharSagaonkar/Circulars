Circular ID No. 62 dated 20th November 2008

 

Discontinuation of offering 0.5% additional interest than

General Public to Co-operative Societies

w.e.f. 01/12/2008

 

 

Please refer to our Circular I.D. 59 dt. 10th October, 2008.  Presently we are offering 0.5% additional interest to Co-operative Societies and to Senior Citizens on different time maturities from 181 days to 120 months. Offering of 0.5% additional interest on Fixed Deposits received from Senior Citizens for various time maturity is as per the directives issued by RBI. But offering of 0.5% additional interest to Co-operative Societies has been objected and disallowed by the RBI inspecting officials and commented upon in their recent Inspection Report.

 

The matter was put to Board in its meeting held on 18-11-2008, and as per the decision taken in the meeting we advise as under:-

 

Offering of 0.5% additional interest to Co-operative Societies on their Fixed Deposits will be discontinued w.e.f. 01/12/2008. The rate of interest to be offered to Co-operative Societies will be at par with the rate applicable to General Public w.e.f. 01-12-2008.

 

Branches should therefore note that on Fixed Deposits received on or after 01/12/2008 from Co-operative Societies the rate of interest will be offered at par with the rate applicable to General Public.

 

At the time of renewal of Fixed Deposit Receipt(s) of Co-operative Society(ies) maturing on or after 01/12/2008 the rate of interest will be offered which is applicable to general public.

 

The instructions in regard to acceptance of Bulk Deposits in all categories, will remain unchanged.

 

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned staff members at your branch.

                 

                                                                 M.C. Shetty

                                                             Managing Director
