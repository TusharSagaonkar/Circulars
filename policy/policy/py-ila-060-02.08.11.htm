Circular ILA No. 60 dated 2nd August, 2011

 

Lending Interest Rates to be quoted with the

parameter of PLR

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

 

We have vide ILA No. 59 dated 26.05.2011, recently revised Interest Rates on Loans & Advances w.e.f. 1.06.2011. All our Rates of Interest are floating in the nature except fixed rates of interest for Housing Loans sanctioned and disbursed during the period from 01.07.2005 to 30.06.2006 (Vide Circular ILA No. 35 dated 30.06.2005 and subsequent clarification vide IDC No. HO/ADDL.GM/L&A/115/ 2007-08 dated 21.07.2007) and Housing Loans granted under Abhyudaya Special Housing Loan Scheme from 01.07.2009 to 31.12.2010 upto Rs.20.00 Lakh and rarely in some special cases. RBI Officials during the recent inspection pointed out that floating rates should be linked to some parameters like Base Rate, PLR or one year Term Deposit Rate etc. as reference rate.

 

2.     In view of the above, Board in its meeting held on 15.07.2011 has decided to link the interest rates on Loans & Advances with our PLR. Accordingly, the Schedule of rates of interest w.e.f. 01.06.2011 is attached herewith wherein all rates are linked to PLR except where fixed rates have been offered. You will find that there is no change in effective interest rates for all types of Loans & Advances.

 

3.     Branches/Departments should note that in all the Sanction Letters, Documents, D.P. notes,  any correspondence regarding Loans & Advances, etc. while quoting the rate of interest it should be quoted with the linkage to PLR and then with effective rate.

Example :  Rate of interest for Surety Loans will be noted as under :

 

PLR + 2.5% i.e. 15 % p.a.  

 

4.     The Dy. General Manager (Central Law) shall prepare all the formats of the documents to be executed alongwith sanction letter for new loans with necessary changes and forward the same to Credit Dept. for making onward arrangements for printing and distribution of revised formats among branches. Till such time branches/department will make changes manually at all relevant papers/documents with branding rubber stamp to read as : “The rate of interest is floating and is __________ % above/less than the present PLR ________% i.e. ___________ % p.a.”

 

5.     Although the special/concessional rate of interest being applied for Udupi branches and Kankavali Branch will remain the same as per our Circular instructions. Henceforth these rates will be mentioned with PLR linkage.

 

6.     I.T. Dept. will display the revised system of mentioning rate of interest with PLR linkage on Bank’s website.

 

7      Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of concerned officials/ staff members at your branch / department and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

 

 

 

     V. S. Morye

Managing Director
