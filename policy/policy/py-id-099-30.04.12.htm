Circular ID No. 99 dated 30th April, 2012

 

Revision  in Interest on Term Deposits - for Bulk Deposits Only

 

As per Reserve Bank of India guidelines Banks are free to fix interest rates on domestic term deposits. The banks are required to offer uniform rates on deposits below `. 15.00 Lakh for the same maturity. In case of deposits of `. 15.00 Lakh and above banks may discriminate in the matter of rate of interest between one deposit and another, accepted on same date and for the same maturity, on the basis of size of deposit. However, in the Monetary Policy Statement 2012-13 dated 17.04.2012, RBI has mentioned its observation that there are wide variations in banks’ retail and bulk deposit interest rates, making it unfair to retail depositors.  With a view to take corrective steps and to reduce wide variation for some slabs in retail and bulk deposits, Board in its meeting held on 27.04.2012 streamlined the rates of interest on bulk deposits w.e.f. 02.05.2012 as under :-

Please note that henceforth there will not be any negotiation in regard to offering of interest rates for bulk deposits.

 

2. BULK DEPOSITS :-

The Bulk Deposit means deposit of ` 15.00 Lakh and above accepted in a single day in any individual branch under single deposit receipt.

 

3. Interest Rates on retail deposits i.e. deposits for the amount of less than `. 15.00 Lakh will remain the same as mentioned in our Circular ID No. 98 dated 20th March, 2012.
