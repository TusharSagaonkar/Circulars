Circular ID No. 115 dated 21st December, 2015

 

 

Revision in Interest Rates on Term Deposits w.e.f. 01.01.2016.

         

Considering the rising cost of deposits & softening interest rates scenario in Banking Industry as a whole and as per the recommendation of ALCO Meeting held on 11.12.2015 and approval of the Board in its meeting held on 16th December 2015, it has been decided to revise rates of interest on Term Deposits for certain slabs for the General Public as well as for Senior Citizens.

4.     Further it is also decided to reduce interest rates for deposits under ATSS Scheme by 75 bps w.e.f. 1.1.2016 i.e. from 9.75% to 9%

 

Other instructions:

 

     i.        The rate of interest for Staff deposit for all slabs/durations will be 1% more than the rates applicable for the General Public.

 

   ii.        The Staff/checking Officials of Term Deposit section at the branch should check the correctness of interest rates quoted on deposit receipt as per above instructions before handing over the receipt to the depositor.

 

  iii.        I.T Dept. is advised to display this information on Bank’s website and make necessary changes in the system.

 

  iv.        All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposit issued from time to time continue to remain in force.
