Sub: Revision in delegation of powers & other changes

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In the Board Meeting held on 4.5.2007, the delegated powers to the officials including Managing Director have been revised. We enclose the Annexure XI – 'A' and 'B' with revised delegated powers for the perusal of all officials at branches and zonal offices. They are advised to exercise their delegation as per the amount indicated in the enclosed Annexures with immediate effect (9.5.2007).  The existing terms and conditions for using the delegated powers remain unchanged. We would be sending Office Order suitably which may please be noted.

 

Other Changes:

(i)         Due to large amount of sanctioned but not disbursed loans and advnaces, it has been decided not to consider new loans and advances proposals above Rs. 5.00 crores. In case of existing borrowal accounts, additional request from the priority sector borrowal accounts would be considered as per the requirements and upto the exposure limits.        In case of existing non-priority sector borrowal accounts, their request for additional credit limits above Rs. 5.00 crores would be referred by branches to zonal office which in turn will refer to Head Office for prior approval.

(ii)         All loans and advances proposals above Rs. 15.00 lacs would be routed through Zonal Office. Thus  all loans and advances proposals upto Rs. 15.00 lacs would be accepted/processed and recommended for sanction/rejection by the Incharge of the branches and sent directly to Loans and Advances Dept., Head Office for placing before the                Addl. GM/M.D.  and in the Board Meeting.

(iii)        The amount of loans and advances proposals, above Rs. 1.00 crore (both fund based and non-fund based) would be accepted at Zonal Offices for In-principle scrutiny. Thus, all new loans and advances proposals upto Rs. 1.00 crore will be accepted, processed and recommended by Branches and then forwarded to zonal office for their scrutiny and recommendations and onward submission to Head Office.

 

(iv)      Loan amount Eligibility

I] Surety Loan upto Rs. 2.00 lacs:

a)  Take home salary/income  increased from Rs. 3,000/- to Rs. 3,500/-.

In case of net salary/income of the applicant is Rs. 7,000/- p.m. and take home salary/income increased from Rs. 3,500/- to Rs. 4,000/- in case of net salary/income above Rs. 7,500/-.

      b) Take home salary/income means the net salary that would be in the hands of the applicant/loanee after deducting the EMI amount of the loan being sanctioned. The formula of working out the eligible loan amount is the same as illustrated in the Credit Policy – 2006-07 with change in EMI factor.

 

II]  Housing Loan:

          a)      Loan amount upto Rs. 10.00 lacs -

Net take home salary/income Rs. 4,500/- after EMI amount of loan.

          b)      Loan upto Rs. 15.00 lacs – take home salary/income Rs. 5,000/-

c)       Loan  above  Rs. 15.00 lacs –  take  home salary/income  Rs. 7,500/-.

The earlier criteria of minimum take home salary or 35% of total Gross Salary whichever is higher is deleted.

III]   Mortgage Loan:

a)      To individuals, self-employed persons   -  Loan upto Rs. 10.00 lakh

          professionals                                        for the period of 5/7 years

 

b)      To business enterprises (firm/Co.)      -  Loan limit upto Rs. 25.00       

          for use in business                                       lacs against own flat/house

                                                                                   and 

                                                                   Loan limit  - Rs. 50.00 lacs

                                                                   against  gala/office, factory

                                                                   premises etc. subject to

                                                                   repaying capacity.

                                                                   Period – 7 years.

IV]     Housing Loan – Sureties:

          Where the Housing Loan agreement is in the joint name of husband and wife  and both are employed in reputed company/organization and having good repayment capacity, for loan upto Rs. 15.00 lacs, one surety either family member or outside with Gross Salary above Rs. 10,000/- and net salary above Rs. 7,000/- be obtained.

 

          For Loan above Rs. 15.00 lacs, the present practice of two sureties to continue.

 

V]      Car Loan: 

          One surety with Net Salary of Rs. 10,000/- or above be obtained.

 

VI]     While sanctioning loans and advances, all fund based and non-fund based limits will be taken together . In case of existing Term Loans, the present outstanding will be considered instead of limits sanctioned for determining the delegation of officials.

Example:     ABC & Co. enjoy CC limit of Rs. 10.00 lacs and Term Loan of Rs. 20.00 lacs, the outstanding in the Term Loan is Rs. 5.00 lacs, they approach for additional CC or TL limit of Rs. 10.00 lacs. Then for exercising the delegation, the total limits will be considered as Rs. 25.00 lacs and not Rs. 40.00 lacs                 (CC Rs. 10.00 lacs + TL – Rs. 5.00 lacs + new CC or TL Rs. 10.00 lacs).

 

VII]   Valuation of Properties:

 

(i) For valuation of flats/house/gala/Office, with value upto  Rs. 10.00lacs, valuation by Bank Officials will be relied upon as presently done.

(ii) In case of value of more than Rs. 10.00 lacs of flat/house/gala, office and other immovable properties including land, plot and machineries, factory building etc.,  the valuation by  professionally qualified  independent valuer will  only be relied upon.

 

VIII] It has been decided not to sanction surety loans against income proof by Affidavit and there is no established business.
