Circular ID No.89 dated 13th June, 2011

Interest Rates on Shubhamkaroti - II Scheme Revision of Rate of Interest

For Bulk Deposits

 

         

Please refer to our Circular ID No. 87 dated 12th May, 2011.

 

To retain the existing Bulk Deposit as well as to attract more and more new Bulk Deposit in the present era of tough competition from other nationalized and leading Co-operative Banks, the Board in its meeting held on 10th June, 2011 decided to revise the Interest Rates for Bulk Deposits for Shubhamkaroti-II Scheme for 500 days launched w.e.f. 16th May, 2011 :

The revised rates for Shubhamkaroti-II Scheme w.e.f. 13.06.2011 will be as under :

10.25 %

For General Public (for staff members 0.50% additional)

10.50 %

For Sr.Citizens and Co-operative Societies

10.75 %

For Bulk Deposits for all categories for Rs. 15.00 Lakh & above upto Rs. 50.00 Lakh

11.00 %

For Bulk Deposits for all categories for Rs. 50.00 Lakh & above

 

2.     In case of Bulk Deposits kept by the depositors under our Shubhamkaroti-II Scheme launched w.e.f. 16th May 2011, if these depositors request for premature withdrawals and desire to keep their deposits to take advantage of revised interest rates under Shubhamkaroti-II, such requests may be considered without charging 1% penalty.  The applicable interest for the period for which the deposit remained with the Bank will be paid without 1% penalty and deposit will be renewed for further 500 days from the date of their request.

3.     The Bulk Deposits shall be accepted in a single day under a single deposit receipt at any individual Branch.

 

4.     All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposits, issued earlier from time to time, continue to remain in force.

 

5.     We reiterate our earlier instructions that Branches will apply penalty @ 1% while allowing pre-matured withdrawals of Term Deposits. At the time of accepting Bulk Deposit, branches should inform the depositors regarding the Bank's Policy for the premature withdrawal. The premature withdrawal is allowed by charging of 1% penalty on applicable interest for the period the deposit remained with the Bank.

 

6.     Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all concerned staff members at your branch for getting maximum bulk deposits.

                                      

V. S. Morye

       Managing Director
