Circular ILA No.77 Dated 1st June, 2016

 

Revision of Lending Rates w.e.f. 01.06.2016

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

During the period April 2015 to March 2016, we have reduced rate of interest on Deposits from time to time.  During the same period, the RBI have also reduced the Repo Rate from 8.50% to 6.50%.  The PLR of the Bank as per audited Balance Sheet for the year ended 31.3.2015 was 13.50%.  During the year, the cost of Deposits has reduced from 7.87% to 7.49%.  It is further expected to reduce from 7.49% to 7.40% by 31.3.2017.  The PLR based on Balance Sheet as on 31.3.2016 works out to 13%.  The same has been approved in the Board Meeting held on 16.4.2016.  Considering the increased competition in marketing of Loans & Advances, the lower rate of interest offered by other Co-op. Banks and Nationalized Banks and shifting of our good accounts to other Banks to avail the benefit of lower rate of interest, it was felt necessary to reduce rate of interest on Loans & Advances.  Accordingly, in the ALCO Meeting held on 13.5.2016, the revision in rates of interest on loans and advances since 1.6.2016 was recommended. The same has been approved in the Board Meeting held on 25.5.2016. The revised rates of interest  on Loans  & Advances w.e.f. 1.6.2016 are as per Chart enclosed herewith.

 

2.     Procedures for modification of ROI in OMNI System:-

     i.        It has been decided to make changes in rate of interest for Housing Loans, Gold Loans, Surety Loans, NSC Loans and Education Loan through I.T. Dept. For all other Loans & Advances, the changes in rate of interest would be made manually by Staff/Officials at Branches.

   ii.        In case of CC/DBD/SBD/Channel Credit/RCL accounts, feeding of revised rates of interest should be done by the staff and officials at branches. In case of Fort and Bhiwandi branches where concessional rate of interest has been given to Paper & Stationery Traders and Textile Manufacturers and Traders, changes in rates of interest should be done by staff/officials at Branches.

  iii.        In case of CC & other working capital limits and Secured Loan accounts which are eligible for Credit Rating, revision in interest rates would be made manually as per the enclosed chart. It is therefore necessary to ensure that proper Credit Rating Grade is fed in the system.  The official who has been entrusted with the work of authorization should ensure that the entry of proper Grading based on audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss account as on 31.3.2015 is made and changes in interest rates are done as per Chart.  

Though Credit Rating  based on audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss a/c. as on 31.3.2016 is to be completed by 31st December 2016, if any account has already submitted the same audited financial statements as on 31.3.2016, the Credit Rating should be done immediately and they should be charged new ROI based on revised Credit Rating Grade.

  iv.        In case of Kankavali and Udupi Branches, special ROI is given to certain Loans & Advances accounts upto Rs. 100.00 Lakh and above Rs. 100.00 Lakh to Rs. 500.00 Lakh, they have to ensure that correct rate of interest is fed in the Limit Master.  The same would be done by staff and officials at those Branches manually. 

   v.        In order to retain some good accounts, we have offered lower rate of interest to them to be competitive with the rate of interest offered by other Banks to avoid takeover by other Banks. These borrowers have been given fixed/concessional rate of interest for 1 or 2 years. In case, revised rate of interest is lower than the concessional rate of interest then they will be offered the revised lower rate of interest applicable to all borrowal accounts.  In case the concessional rate of interest is lower than rates applicable since 1.6.2016 then concessional rate would be continued as per the terms of sanction i.e. one or two years as the case may be.

  vi.        The list of accounts wherein the changes in rates of interest have been done  by the I.T. Dept. would be sent to each branch by I.T. Dept. with the details of previous rate of interest and new applicable rate of interest. Branches have to check the details and confirm to I.T. Dept. about the correctness. In case of any discrepancy, the same should be brought to the notice of I.T. Dept. for modification. Thereafter, I. T. Dept. will generate the notices. It is the responsibility of the Incharge of branches to ensure that notices are generated by I.T. Dept., and ispatched by Development Dept.

vii.        Rates of Interest to Weaker Section Advance:- In terms of CR No.94, it was decided to grant 0.50% concession in Rate of Interest for two years to all types of loans and advances sanctioned under Weaker Section Advances. This concession was not to be extended to certain categories of loans and advances i.e. loan against gold ornaments, housing loan, education loan, vehicle loan and SOD against FDR/LIC/Govt. Securities. Wherever branches have extended these advances and have also noted in the sanction letter the concessional rate of interest, the rate will continue till the maturity of the loans and advances. In case of new loans and advances under Weaker Section, the 0.50% concession in rate of interest would also be extended till maturity of the loans and advances. 

viii.        The rates of interest on Case filed/SRO/ARB accounts, the existing practice of application of rate of interest will continue.

3.     Procedures for sending Notices by I.T. Dept., Development Dept. & Branches:-

     i.        The Notice of reduction in rate of interest to all Housing Loan accounts and Secured Loan accounts and Education Loan Accounts (Individuals) would be generated by the I.T. Dept.  Development Dept. will arrange despatch of these notices. The second copy of such Notices would be sent to branches.

   ii.        In case of CC/DBD/SBD/Channel Credit/RCL accounts, Branches should arrange to send the Notices prepared manually through Post in their area.  The Notices should be signed by the Accountant/Manager/Sr. Manager/Asst. Gen. Manager of the branch.

  iii.        In case of Educational Institutions, notices should be generated manually at Branches and handed over to the authorized person of the institute against acknowledgement of the institute.

I.T. Dept. should ensure that notices to Educational Institutions are not generated as these accounts are also categorized as ESCLN.

  iv.        In case of Weaker Section Advances, the notices with 0.50% concession would be generated manually by staff/officials at branches and also despatch  through Post in their area.

4.      Other Instructions:-

a)   In case of Surety & Gold Loans, as the numbers of loan accounts are large and there is reduction in ROI, it has been decided not to send notices to the loanees. Instead, reduction in rate of interest by 0.50% would be displayed on website of the Bank, at prominent place in the branch premises through posters and notice on the Notice Board for the customers. 

b)  There are chances of some Loan accounts approaching the branches for reducing the EMI amount.  These cases will be few.  Branches are authorized to consider their request provided there is no change in the number of balance EMIs and maturity date of the loan remains unchanged. Branches should report such cases to their ZOs for ratification.

c)   Planning & Marketing Dept. shall arrange for printing of advertising material with the new rates. They should give wide publicity for revision and reduction in rate of interest on all types of Loans & Advances.

d)  I.T. Dept. shall make necessary changes in our Bank’s website and in our system accordingly.

e)   Inspection Dept. shall ensure that change in rate of interest is done properly through Concurrent Audit/Inspection Dept. by inspection of some accounts of all types of loans.          

 

5.      It may be noted that except fixed interest rates loans, there is reduction of 0.50% in interest rates on all types of loans and advances.  It is therefore, need of the hour to increase retail loans including Housing Loans and House Repairs/Renovation loans substantially in the coming months. The Incharge of branches are advised to involve all staff members in marketing of retail loans and advances and ensure that the targets given are achieved well in advance. 

 

6.      Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all officials/staff members at your branch and ensure strict compliance.
