2.       Please note that rate for Staff Term Deposit of above 12 months and upto and inclusive of 24 months will be 0.5% more (instead of 1.00%) than the rate applicable for General Public and Trust.

 

          The rate of interest for Staff Term Deposits for all other slabs will be 1% more than the rate applicable for General Public and Trust.

 

3.       It is observed that Bulk Deposits from Co-operative Banks and Credit Societies are often withdrawn and deposited with other Banks who offer better rates.  Thus, there is no guarantee of continuation of such deposits after maturity thereof.  As against this, the small depositors are most loyal depositors who continue to deposit more and more amount with our Bank and do not divert their funds to other Banks for getting little higher rate of interest. These depositors are the backbone of the Bank for years together and hence there should be more thrust for mobilization of deposits from small depositors.  Similarly, large number of Co-operative Housing Societies have supported our Bank in a big way by keeping their deposit with us. Hence more thrust should also be put in to mobilize deposits from Co-operative Housing Societies.  Thus, it has been decided to put in best efforts in mobilization of deposits from Public,  Co-operative Housing Societies and Trusts rather than Co-operative Banks/ Co-operative Credit Societies.  In case of urgent need as also to achieve yearly deposit targets, Bank may come out with a Special Deposit Scheme at appropriate time for mobilizing Bulk Deposits from Co-operative Banks/ Co-operative Credit Societies.

 

4.       Marketing Department will do the needful for advertising and supplying the banners/marketing material to branches.

 

5.       I.T. Dept. is advised to arrange to display this information on Bank's website and make necessary changes in the system.

 

6.       All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposits, issued earlier from time to time, continue to remain in force.

       Managing Director
