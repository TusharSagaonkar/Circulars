MICR outward:

Monday to Friday - Delivery time for the bank at RBI NCC after encoding and tally is between 6.30PM to 7.00PM. However, Intercity (NCLG) returns presentation has to be done by 6.00PM and NCLG presentation by 6.30pm.

 

As it requires extra manpower to send for NCLG as well as MICR, both deliveries are sent with the same person by 6.30PM. 

 

Branches are requested to remove all pins/staples/perforation strips from cheques; else it is possible that RBI may reject/return the entire clearing lot presented by our Bank, for not doing the same.

 

Branches should send lots of 200 cheques only and no non-MICR cheques are to be included.

 

Kindly do not mix up lots as lots are tallied while encoding.

The same branch's lots are encoded by different persons and in case the cheques are mixed up then it may not be possible to tally the same in time and that specific Branch's CSC will be kept pending.

Because of this delay, NCLG presentation also may not be possible.

 

Figures mentioned on the delivery sheet and CSC printouts must be same.  Please DO NOT commit any adding mistakes.

In case where a cheque requires special attention of Clearing Dept. e.g.: where the MICR code of the drawee bank is not encoded or wrongly encoded etc., you are requested to pin a small chit to the cheque with appropriate remarks and keep the cheque on top of the lot.

 

IT refund order:

IT refund order advises are separated from the cheques. These are separately dropped in the drawee bank's boxes at RBI NCC. While the cheques are processed by RBI NCC the return advises are collected from the boxes by the Service Branches of the drawee banks. Our responsibility ends with dropping the advise at RBI.

In case a RO is returned for the reason "Advise not received" despite the same having been sent, you are requested to re-present the same along with a letter addressed to the drawee bank stating the facts and requesting the RO be passed.

As a matter of precaution, Payee may be advised to keep a copy of the RO advice, and this RO advice copy may be presented in such cases.

 

VERY IMPORTANT:

Kindly ensure that every cheque sent for clearing bears the branch's crossing stamp.  Besides the protection that the stamp provides it is also the easiest way of determining the presenting bank-branch.  In case there is no crossing stamp the drawee bank may not be able to return the cheque properly and also the clearing dept will have no way of determining the correct branch where the cheque has to be sent to.

 

We will provide all branches with a directory of service branches of all banks on a floppy if required, and the same has been also uploaded on the mail server (PUBLIC FOLDERS-CENTRAL CLEARING DEPT.-DIRECTORIES). You are requested to print and keep with you the same. You will no longer be dependent on clearing dept. for telephone nos. of other banks.

 

Similarly we will send to Information Technology Dept. the list of MICR Codes of all banks, they are supposed to ensure that the database at the branches is updated accordingly.

 

MICR inward:

It is a common reason put forward by branch managers that they are unable to send returns in time because they have received the delivery late. To rectify this situation we have already taken many measures such as increasing staff strength in the morning session, increased the number of couriers, reduced the average number of branches serviced by each courier, installation of ISDN line /INFINET connection for faster down load of data from RBI website etc. With a view to ensuring even earlier delivery we will now be sending to you your inward cheques after tallying only the no. of cheques and not the amount i.e. there will be no patti. You are requested to ensure that the cheques received at the branch are first tallied with the RBI printouts for both no of cheques as well as amount. Agreed that none of the branches have printer adding machines, however excel program can be used instead.

 

CRC data files are uploaded on the mail server (PUBLIC FOLDERS-CENTRAL CLEARING DEPT.-CRC) between 7.00AM and 7.30AM. Branches may download and use the same. These are the same files, which we send you on a floppy.

 

Clearing differences normally arise for the following three reasons.

Cheque listed not received / cheque recd not listed:   

This difference will be detected at the time of counting the cheques at clearing dept itself and will be kept with us.

 

Encoding amount differs from cheque amount:  

In such situation the Branches are to return the instrument for encoded amount only. This is the practice followed by most other banks.

 

Listing amount differs from cheque amount/encoded amount:

In such cases you are requested to inform Central Clearing Department immediately and ensure that copies of the instrument and the relevant printout reach the clearing dept by 3.30pm. This is necessary, as we are required to upload all receivable/payable data to RBI by 4.00pm.

 

TIPS for locating Clearing differences due to encoding errors:

§ While authorizing, cheques passed by STP (straight through process), actual cheque amounts to be verified with posted amounts.

§ While authorizing, cheques passed manually, encoded amounts to be verified with posted amounts.

 

Kindly note that, Branches are requested not to change the CRC total amount without concurrence of the Central Clearing Dept. Else, for settling any differences that arise, whether receivable or payable will be the responsibility of the branch.

 

Branches, which open late, may stagger the timings of their Clearing staff to enable adhering to the new clearing schedules.

 

Central Clearing Department is uploading, daily, the CSC returns report as well as the CRC data on the mail server. This data is available by 7.30 AM, hence Branches need not wait for the courier to arrive, they may download the files and use/process them.

 

CRC returns: (Inward returns i.e. returns from inward cheques)

Procedure followed at clearing dept is as follows:

§ We receive the floppy as well as cheques and printout

§ The data on floppy is checked with that on the printout.

§ If correct then the data is appended to a master file.

§ The cheques are, then sorted, bank wise.

§ This procedure is repeated for every branch.

§ After all branches are completed then the cheques are bunched bank wise and counted.

§ The collated data is queried and bank wise summary listing taken.

§ This is tallied with the actual no. of cheques.

§ Any differences are rectified.

§ The final data file is uploaded to RBI.

§ A bank wise listing is prepared and attached to the return cheques and,

§ The cheques are dropped in respective banks boxes at Amar building fort by 5.30PM

 

Very Important:

RBI has warned us that this return data file has to be uploaded by 4.30 latest, after which they will not accept the same.

The data uploaded to RBI must tally in every way with the bank wise listing and actual cheques dropped to various banks.

In order to ensure compliance with the above it is imperative that we receive all branches delivery by 3.50 pm latest.

This is what we have aimed for when we set courier timings.  Branches may note that the RBI does not give us any concession and so the Clearing Dept. cannot accommodate delays in any way. 

 

A few deficiencies noticed in the returns are listed below; your co-operation in rectifying the same will go a long way in making Clearing work easier.

 

1.Accuracy of data:

Some times, floppy with file containing inaccurate data are received.

i.e. Data does not match with printout as few cheques may have been added/removed after creation of the file.

 

2.Non-receipt of data:

Many times blank floppies and sometimes, empty floppy boxes are sent. In such cases, manual entry of every cheque needs to be done, which is time consuming. As no checking is done, the manual work is also prone to errors. Hence checking the floppy before sending is mandatory.

 

3.Cheque return Memo:

The OMNI system used by all branches has a provision for printing cheque return memo. This memo is better than the printed memo supplied by the stationery dept as it is much smaller in size and it bears on its face the presenting bank's details, and the work of sorting bank wise becomes easier. Branches are hence requested to use these memos only and also ensure that they are cut vertically to be slightly smaller in length than the cheque.

 

4.Re-presented Cheque being returned:

If the cheque to be returned is one that has been presented on more than one occasion and by different banks kindly check the RBI printout to verify the last presenting bank's details.

 

5.NCLG returns:

Kindly take printout and copy file on floppy before entering NCLG return cheques in computer. Use yellow colored return memo supplied by stationery dept.

 

6.Upload of Data file:

Ensure that your branch return data file is uploaded on the mail server immediately, as in case of late delivery Clearing Dept. may use the data to append the master file.

 

7.Removal of return cheques:  

Clearing Dept. does not have the authority to remove return cheques at the Branch's request. Any such request must be made in writing and routed through the respective Zonal Office, which in turn will issue directions to us.

However, such directions/instructions can be complied with only if received before 4.00pm. We will send the cheque/s to the Branches along with next days delivery but accounting for the same should be done as per instructions from Accounts dept. preferably the same day)

 

8.Inclusion of late return cheques:

When the branch with concurrence of the presenting bank wishes to late return a cheque received on the previous day then it will suffice, if you send the same along with the return cheques of the day with appropriate remarks in the printout. This cheque/s will be manually entered. Accounting entries for this will be decided by the Accounts Department.

Branches are sincerely requested to take stringent measures to curb high percentage of returns. RBI has already summoned and warned us on this count on many occasions, we may be imposed penalty on this count in future.

 

We have been informed by RBI that very soon, they will be revising the return timings to 3.30 pm.

ECS:

The original ECS cycle was as follows:

 

Day 1       File uploaded to RBI by sponsor bank

Day 2       File downloaded and printed by service branches of receiving banks

Day 3       Settlement day. The day of actual debit/credit to beneficiaries account

Day 4       Returns to be sent to service branch

Day 5       Returns to be uploaded to RBI and final settlement

 

The RBI has in stages reduced this cycle by removing day 2 and day 4 hence the present cycle is as follows:

 

Day 1       File upload by sponsor banks by 12.30pm, from 4.30 pm onwards file is available on RBI server to be downloaded and printouts taken by service branches of receiving banks

Day 2       Settlement date. Debit /credit to be given to accounts returns to be sent to service branches

Day 3       Final settlement by 11.30AM

 

As seen form above the returns have to be sent to Clearing Dept. on the settlement day itself. In case of exigencies, late return may be accommodated up to 9.00 am on day 3 but after that, Branches will have to take up the matter with the sponsor Banks directly, there is nothing Clearing Dept. can do.

 

ECS returns should be in the prescribed format only, a copy of which will be available on mail server shortly; it should preferably be typewritten/printed or in case it is handwritten, the writing should be legible. 

 

For returns, the following data only are required: Account no., name of account holder, ECS item sequence no (most imp) and amount.

 

ECS returns have to be sent back on settlement date, just like CRC returns, hence a covering reminder will not be sent, as was being done earlier.

 

Our Information Technology Dept. has been requested to find out ways to STP(Straight thru process) our ECS data. This is similar to the way the CRC data file is processed, and will save much time and effort and also improve accuracy.

 

Branch officials are requested to ensure at the time of signature verification on ECS mandate forms that the account holder and the beneficiary of the service for which the mandate is given is one and the same person, else we will receive instructions for debit where the Account no. & name differs and we will have to return the same, adding to the workload and risk.

HDFC/ICICI cheque issue:

HDFC/ICICI cheques are not demand drafts - payment can be stopped.

After the branch issues the cheque a daily statement is sent to Clearing Dept. which in turn collates all the data of the day and sends a printout and data file to HDFC/ICICI around 11.00 am the next morning for HDFC/ICICI to update their database. At the same time we transfer the total amount of money to the respective bank. This is just like our Pay order issue system.

 

As and when the cheque is presented at any of their clearing centers the cheque is compared with the database and passed. If the data is not available with them it will be difficult to pass the cheque. In such cases HDFC/ICICI calls up to ask for confirmation. It is difficult to figure out which branch has issued any particular cheque and take confirmation from that branch. However due to different branch timings and especially on Saturdays it may not be possible to contact the branch and thus there are chances that the cheque may be bounced. In order to avoid such unpleasant situation, the following guidelines are recommended:

 

1. Cheque issue statement has to be sent on the same day of issue.

 

2. Cheque issue statement has to be in prescribed format - will be available on the mail server soon.

 

3. Statement must be typewritten/printed. Or at least ensure that the handwriting is legible.

 

4. If any cheque has been cancelled the same should also be mentioned in the statement.

 

The procedure for cancellation of issued cheque has already been communicated, yet many Branches follow different methods, for convenience the procedure is as follows:

 

1. Obtain the cheque from the customer along with letter (for your records) stating that he/she wants the cheque cancelled, then on the same day a letter addressed to the respective bank (HDFC or ICICI) that you have changed the place the cheque is payable to "Mumbai", has to be sent.

 

2. If the cheque is payable to any particular bank then the payees name should also be changed to "Abhyudaya Co-op. Bank Ltd". Please note all alterations on the instrument, are to signed.

 

3. Present the cheque in local clearing the next day for credit to the customers account.

 

In case of ‘Stop Payment’:

In the case that a cheque is lost in transit or the account holder for any other reason wants the cheque stopped then: 

 

1. Obtain a letter from the customer to this effect - letter to kept with you.

 

2. Send a letter addressed to the bank (HDFC/ICICI) stating the same to us. We will forward this letter to the respective bank.

3. After the cheque is stopped, and when a confirmation is received, it will be forwarded to the respective issuing Branch. You may then credit the purchasers’ account.

Summary of all transactions:

The reasoning behind summary is as follows:

 

1. Branches send/receive to/from Central Clearing Department various instruments/advices etc.

 

2. Accordingly at branch level, H.O. a/c id debited or credited. The Central Clearing Department too sends to a/c department a corresponding set of figures asking to debit/credit the branches.

 

3. Often as in the example mentioned below these sets of figures might not match and this is reflected in the reconciliation and may become apparent only after a gap of 45 days, by which time it may be too late to rectify.

 

Example: Branch XYZ returns ECS debit items of Rs.10000=00 but due to oversight the ECS returns go directly into the ECS file and do not reach Clearing dept. In such case the branch will debit H.O. a/c with Rs.10000=00 but the clearing dept will not advise to accounts dept to credit the branch with Rs.10000=00. In case this happens on the first of the month, by the time branch reconciliation is prepared and sent to the respective branch it may be the 15th of next month. 

 

To avoid such unpleasant situations Mr.R Gawde of IT department has prepared a summary program, the printouts of which you have been receiving for the past months. The Program is under development, and there are some modifications yet to be made for which your suggestions are welcome.

 

§ A printout in duplicate will be sent every morning.

§ Please verify each item of debit/credit and in case of any discrepancies notify the Central Clearing Department immediately on telephone.

§ For record purpose Branches are requested to send us the acknowledgement copy in return delivery.

§ Also before calling up to report any discrepancy kindly check whether the error is at your branch itself.

 

Foreign currency denominated instruments collection:

The Central Clearing Department does only the job of collecting foreign currency denominated instruments. Those instruments equivalent to less than USD 50 are sent to Canara Bank, Parel for collection once a week. All other instruments are collected through Development Credit Bank. Kindly ensure that you send along with the instruments the declaration cum undertaking form else the cheque will be returned. Development Credit Bank takes a minimum of 21 days for collection to which 3 days may be added (1 day for forwarding the cheque to them and 2 days for realization and intimation to the branch), hence earliest the credit can be given is on the 24th working day. Kindly do not enquire before expiry of this period.

 

Important:

Other foreign exchange related work like obtaining foreign currency/drafts and inward/outward remittances etc. is handled by the Loans & Advances Department H.O. through Development Credit Bank therefore any enquires on such account must be made with the Loan Department at Head Office.

Swift no. of Development Credit Bank is DCBLINBB043

 

Cash position reporting:

All branches are supposed to report to Central Clearing Department their end of day cash positions.

In this regard we would like to state that the work is limited to taking down the main cash and ATM cash position as also requirement/remittance. This data is noted down and is faxed to cash department in the morning.
