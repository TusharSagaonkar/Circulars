Circular ILA No. 78 Dated 8th September 2016

CREDIT RATING – MODIFICATION

Revision in Rate of Interest for the Accounts eligible for

Credit Rating

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

As per Credit Policy of the Bank, Credit Rating (Grading) exercise is done in respect of all the Borrowers enjoying only cash credit limit and other working capital facilities  above Rs. 10.00 Lakh (combined) or only Term Loans above Rs. 25.00 Lakh (combined). If a customer enjoys both CC and other working capital limits (combined) & Term Loan (combined), then either of them being more than Rs. 10.00 Lakh will be subject to gradation.

 

Thus, the Borrower with cash credit limit or other working capital facilities (combined) of Rs. 5.00 Lakh and Secured Loan (combined) of Rs. 11.00 Lakh or vice -versa will be eligible for credit rating.

 

At present, we are having 5 Credit Rating Grades. The difference in marks between two grades is 10, thus making a vast gap between two grades. Difference of one mark can change the grade thus, affecting the credit rating of the borrower.

It is observed, that there is difference in the Rate of Interest (ROI) between two grades as well as in the ROI between Priority and Non-Priority Borrower. It may be noted that very good non-priority customers are paying  higher ROI, only because they are non-priority clients. It is one of the reasons that good clients are shifting to other Banks for better ROI.

 

Therefore, it was proposed that there should be 7 grades and the difference between two grades should be reduced to 5. Also new grading marks for “AAA” grade is now proposed at 85 & above instead of previously 90 marks & above. Furthermore, new ROI is suggested for Priority and Non-Priority Sector Advances and benefit of lower interest i.e. 0.50% is passed on to all grades pertaining to Non-Priority Sector Advance. We are herewith enclosing Annexure A: (Guidelines for Credit Rating of existing borrowal accounts) & Annexure B: (Guidelines for credit rating for takeover, new projects & existing business proposals from other banks).

 

It is to be noted, that we have made changes in Annexure A, whereas Annexure B remains unchanged.

 

The Credit Rating of existing Borrowal accounts should be done as per revised Annexure- ‘A’. The exercise for assigning fresh credit rating should be carried out and completed by 31st December 2016 on the basis of the audited Balance Sheet and Profit and Loss account as on 31st March 2016.

 

Borrowal Accounts, which are subject to Credit Rating, has to submit Audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss A/c statement for March every year latest by 30th November for fresh gradation. In case of the Borrowal Accounts, which are eligible for the Credit Rating, are unable to submit the Audited Balance Sheet as they are not eligible for tax audit then Credit Rating shall be done on the basis of C.A. Certified Balance Sheets. 

 

It is emphasized that there is need for all Borrowers to be aware of the reduction in the rate of interest and the benefits of credit rating available by submitting latest audited financial statements at the earliest. This will inculcate the habit of submitting financial statements in time. Branches are advised to write to all Borrowers eligible for credit rating as per Annexure – C so that they can submit audited statements well in time to avail benefit of lower rate of interest upon higher gradation.

 

In case of Takeover of Standard proposals from other Banks for above Rs.25.00 Lakh and new proposal from accountholder of other Bank with no credit facilities but having established business having Audited P&L A/c and B/S of Previous year, approaches our Bank for credit limits for expansion of the same business activity for above Rs.25.00 Lakh, its Credit Rating should be continued as per Annexure– ‘B’

 

In order to pass on the benefit of lower interest rates to the Borrowal accounts, credit rating should not be confined only at the time of renewal/review of the credit limits.

 

The Credit Rating should be done by the Branches as and when the latest Audited Financial Statements are received and the same should be forwarded to Zonal Offices. The Zonal Offices shall approve the Credit Rating of Borrowal Accounts of total exposure excluding the non-funded facilities upto Rs.100.00 Lakh and of above Rs.100.00 Lakh shall be sent to Credit Dept., Head Office for approval. A consolidated Report of Branch wise Accounts shall be sent to Credit Dept., Head Office for record in the first week of the January, next year, by the Zonal Offices. Branches shall immediately enter the respective grades in the system of respective accounts.   

 

Accounts for which grading is not applicable:

Credit Rating is not applicable for loans sanctioned to Individuals like Housing Loans, Mortgage Loans, Rent Securitization Loans, Education Loan, Gold Loan, SODGOLD, SODIMP, SOD against Immovable Properties (outside Region), SOD against Govt. Securities, Vehicle Loans and Premises Loans.

 

Accounts which have to be graded even though ROI is not linked to grading:

Credit Rating of credit facilities extended to Paper and Stationery Traders at Fort Branch, Educational Institutions, Builders & Developers, Other Civil Contractors, Secured Loan to Medical Practitioners and other Professionals, SODIMP to Builders & Developers, Rent Securitization Loan & Premises Loan to firms, SODIMP to Educational Institutions, Tourist Vehicle Operator  & Textile Manufacturing & Traders should be done, though interest rate is not linked to Credit Rating.

 

 

The MIS Dept is informed to make the necessary changes in the Grading Sheet and the Branches are advised to use the revised grading sheet. Incase grading is already done for borrowal accounts based on the financial statement as on 31.03.2016, the branches are hereby directed to redo grading/credit rating as per new Grading Sheet.

 

The IT Dept shall make necessary changes in our Bank’s website and in our system accordingly.

 

Inspection Dept shall ensure that change in rate of interest has done properly.

 

Please take a suitable note of above instructions and bring the content of this circular to the notice of all officials/staff members at your branch and ensure strict compliance.
