Circular ILA No. 57 dated 23rd December, 2010

 

Housing Loans – Closure of Abhyudaya Special Housing Loans Scheme

&

Revision in Rate of Interest for Surety Loans, Gold Loans & Housing Loans

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

 

Please refer to our Office Order No. 292 dated 14th August 2009, wherein we have advised our Branches regarding launching of 'Abhyudaya Special Housing Loan Scheme' w.e.f. 01.07.2009 with fixed Interest Rate @ 9% p.a. for all Housing Loans sanctioned upto ` 20.00 Lakh. Initially the scheme was open for 9 months and the benefit of fixed interest was extended upto 31.03.2011. Subsequently the scheme was extended in phases for sanction of Housing Loans upto 31.12.2010 i.e. all Housing Loans sanctioned from 01.07.2009 to 31.12.2010 upto ` 20.00 Lakh would be covered under the scheme and the benefit of fixed rate of interest @ 9% p.a. was extended upto 30.09.2011 for the Housing Loans sanctioned under this scheme. Now as per the Board decision in its meeting held on 21.12.2010, the Abhyudaya Special Housing Loan Scheme will close as at the close of business on 31.12.2010. From 01.01.2011 onwards branches will consider all the New Housing Loans Proposals upto `.20.00 Lakh under our normal Housing Loan Scheme.

Note :

i)             Housing Loans upto `.20.00 Lakh under Special Abhyudaya Housing Loan Scheme which are sanctioned on or before 31.12.2010, can be disbursed on or after 01.01.2011.

ii)           Branches shall not issue Sanction Letter Code 1061 and shall not use Housing Loan Agreement Code No. 1062 for the Housing Loan proposals sanctioned on or after 01.01.2011. Branch-in-charge and the concerned signing officers will be held personally responsible for any wrongful act in this regard. These stationery items should be withdrawn and destroyed at branch level itself.

Branches shall use Sanction Letter Code No. 579 (Floating rate) and Housing Loan Agreement Code No. 596 (Floating rate) for the Housing Loan proposals sanctioned on or after 01.01.2011.

iii)          The fixed rate of interest @ 9% on Housing Loan sanctioned under Special Abhyudaya Housing Loan Scheme will be continued upto 30.09.2011. Thereafter any changes in rate of interest will be made only on receiving specific instructions from Head Office.

iv)          As some of the applicants may have old pamphlets of Abhyudaya Special Housing Loan Scheme with interest @ 9% fixed upto 30.09.2011, it is possible that they may claim fixed rate of interest in case of Housing Loan proposal sanctioned on or after 01.01.2011 under our normal Housing Loan Scheme for which presently the rate of interest is @ 10% (Floating). Hence letter as per enclosed format shall invariably be obtained from all loanees and kept with documents/security file.

v)            In the Housing Loan accounts, where there is change in Rate of Interest, it should be communicated to all such account holders through our Bank's Website, by displaying the information on Branch Notice Board, by way of sending suitable SMS and also by a separate letter.

In these accounts where repayment period is less than 180 months, branches may extend the repayment period with the consent of Loanees for not exceeding 180 months keeping the EMI unchanged. If repayment period has already been scheduled for 180 months, EMI should be revised with appropriate amount and should be communicated to all such account holders.

vi)          Service Charges for Housing Loans shall be continued to remain 0.6% of the sanctioned amount.

vii)         Branches are advised to remove all posters/pamphlets other advertising material regarding 'Abhyudaya Special Housing Loan Scheme' on 31.12.2010.

viii)       Planning & Marketing Dept. shall arrange for printing of advertisement material with the new rates.

ix)          I.T. Dept. shall make necessary changes on our website and in our system accordingly.

 

Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all officials/ staff members at your branch and ensure compliance.
