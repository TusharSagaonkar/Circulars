Circular ILA No. 87 dated 4th March, 2021

 

Revision in Rates of Interest on Housing Loans and Gold Loans w.e.f. 1st March, 2021

 

         I.    Rates of interest on Housing Loans and Gold Loans were advised the last time under cover of the circular ILA No. 86 dated 30th April, 2020.

       II.    Several developments in the economy since then necessitated revision in rates of interest on Housing Loans and Gold Loans. The matter was therefore reviewed and a submission in this behalf recommending changes was placed before the Board meeting dated 26th February, 2021. The same met with approval.

 

      III.    The revised rates of interest on Housing Loans and Gold Loans, thus approved, which take effect from 1st March, 2021, are given in the Annexure hereto. It may be noted that the revision in rates of interest under these two categories is not only for new loans but also for the loans currently on our books, with the exception of Housing Loans which have been granted at a fixed rate.

 

     IV.    Instructions in respect of concessional rate of interest to women borrowers are reiterated hereunder.

1.   Housing Loans sanctioned to women on or after 1st June, 2017, singly, or jointly with others such that first name among them is that of a woman, shall carry a lower rate of interest of 7.70 % for Housing Loan upto Rs. 35 Lakh.

2.   In order that the benefit of such concessional interest reaches women-house-owners, it should be ensured that the name of the woman among the joint borrowers appears first, not only in the loan documents but also in the Sale Deed/Agreement for Sale in respect of the property being acquired.

 

       V.    In view of the changes being only downward, there is no need to exchange letters with the borrowers individually.

 

     VI.    1. IT department is hereby advised to send appropriate SMS to the borrowers in the specified categories.

2. IT department is further advised to make necessary arrangements for change in ROI on Housing Loans and Gold Loans in Omni as well as on website of bank.

 

   VII.  The interest rates structure in respect of all other loans and advances will continue to be the same as communicated through the Circular ILA No. 86 dated 30th April, 2020.

  VIII.    Please bring contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

Annexure

     P. S. Salian

Managing Director
