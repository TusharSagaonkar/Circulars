Circular ILA No. 56 dated 26th October, 2010

 

Textile Manufacturing Units –

Enhancement of Rate of Interest

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

 

Presently vide our Circular ILA NO. 51 dated 01.07.2009, we are charging concessional rate of interest to Textile Manufacturing units @ 11% w.e.f. 01.07.2009 irrespective of the grading of the accounts, as this particular sector was passing through the bad phase of recession.

 

          Considering the increase in the cost of funds, our Board in its meeting held on 19.10.2010 decided to charge @ 11.50% w.e.f. 01.11.2010 to all Textile Manufacturing Units, irrespective of the grading till further instructions as under :

 

 

Sr. No.

Types of facility

 

Rate of Interest (%)

w.e.f.

Stipulations/ Remarks

 

XVIII

Textile Manufacturing Units

11.5%

Irrespective of grading till further instructions. However, exercise of grading is compulsory.

 

Although interest @ 11.5% will be charged to all Textile Manufacturing Units irrespective of grading, the exercise of grading is compulsory for all these borrowers enjoying only Cash Credit Limit above Rs.10.00 Lakh or only Loan limit above Rs.25.00 Lakh or combination of both these facilities having limit more than Rs.10.00 Lakh under any facility. For this purpose it is mandatory for every such unit to submit audited financial statements and Balance Sheet within a prescribed time limit, otherwise penal rate of interest would be applicable to these units, which please note.

          Please also note that amount of EMI will have to be revised where Loans to Textile Manufacturing Units have been sanctioned after 01.07.2009, so as to liquidate the loan account within the prescribed time schedule.

Branches are advised to send a letter to all Textile Manufacturing Unit Account holders regarding upward revision in rate of interest. The office copy of this letter should be filed in respective loan file. The draft letter to be sent to Textile Manufacturing Units is enclosed herewith.

Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all officials/ staff members at your branch and ensure compliance.
