Circular ID No. 134 dated 1st April 2022

RELAUNCH OF “SHUBHAMKAROTI – III”

___________________________________________________________________________________________

 

         

I.   “Shubhamkaroti-III”, a deposit scheme for a period of 18 months with the rate of interest at 7 % p. a. was launched vide the Circular ID No. 128 Dated 17th October 2020. Acceptance of deposits under the scheme lasted until 31st December 2020. The deposits mobilised under the scheme have started coming up for maturity and this phenomenon will last till 30th June 2022 (with the exception of a very few cases in which the maturity will extend beyond 30th June 2022 by a couple of days due to placement of deposits against the instruments tendered, subject to clearing, on the last two days of the timeline for placing deposits).

 

II.  In terms of a submission placed before the Board meeting dated 28th March 2022, it was proposed to relaunch the “Shubhamkaroti-III” scheme along similar lines as at the time of the original launch, subject, however, to a reduced rate of interest at 6.25 % p. a. The submission met with approval. Accordingly, the scheme is being relaunched on the auspicious occasion of Varshapratipada (Gudhipadava) i.e. Chaitra Shuddha 1, Shalivahan Shaka 1944 Shubhakrita Sanvastara (2nd April 2022). The details of the scheme are given below for ready reference.

1.        This is a relaunch of the “Shubhamkaroti-III” deposit scheme.

2.        Date of launch: - Varshapratipada (Gudhipadava), 2nd April 2022.

3.        Date of closure: -Deposits under the scheme shall be accepted for limited period and  the closure date for this special scheme will be intimated in due course of time.

4.        Period for which the deposits will be accepted: - 18 months    

5.        Types of deposits permissible: - QID and QICRIP (No additional interest for QID scheme).

6.        Rate of Interest: - 6.25 % p. a., which is uniform for all categories of persons, including staff, senior citizens & entities. (Note: - Rates of interest currently applicable for deposits for all the maturities, including for the maturity of 18 months outside the “Shubhamkaroti-III” scheme, remain unchanged).

7.        Persons & entities eligible to place deposits: - Members of the General Public, Staff Members, Senior Citizens, Co-op. Societies, other bodies & entities.

8.        Non-Residents are not eligible to place deposits under this scheme.

9.        Whether bulk deposits can be accepted? - Yes.

10.     All the other instructions and terms and conditions, applicable to Domestic Term Deposits, issued earlier from time to time continue to apply to the deposits under the instant scheme.

11.     Branches will open accounts under two heads for the two types of deposits, namely “SHUB3QIC” for QICRIP and “SHUB3QID” for QID, as hitherto, in the Banks CBS (OMNI system).

 

III.   Focus during deposit mobilization

1.       While targeting potential depositors under the scheme, Branches are advised to focus more on the mobilisation of such deposits from Individuals, Senior Citizens and Cooperative Housing Societies, as such deposits are more stable in nature as compared to the bulk Deposits from Co-op. Credit Societies / Banks.

2.       There should an endeavour to get all the deposits “Shubhamkaroti-III” renewed either under the Bank’s regular deposit schemes or the relaunched “Shubhamkaroti-III”.

 

IV.   Marketing Department is advised to urgently do an advertisement of the scheme, and supply to branches banners, pamphlets and other marketing material in respect thereof.

 

V. I.T. Dept. is advised to arrange for the display of information about the scheme on the Bank's website.

 

Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members at your Branch/Dept. for compliance.

                                     

P. S. Salian

       Managing Director
