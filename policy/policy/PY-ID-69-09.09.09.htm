Circular ID No. 69  dated 9th September 2009

 

Interest Rates on Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme (ATSS)

– Revision in Rates of Interest

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Please refer to our Circular O.C. No. 363 dated 11.09.2006 and Circular ID No. 61 dated 27.10.2008.

         

2.       In view of the changed structure of interest rates on Term Deposits, Board of Directors have decided to revise the interest rates for Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme (ATSS) in their meeting held on 04.09.2009. The revised rates for Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme (ATSS) will be as under which will be made effective form 1st October, 2009.

 

          General Public      -         8.25%

          (including Staff)

 

          Senior Citizens     -         8.50%

 

 

3.       Branches are advised to take note of the above revision in interest rates of Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme (ATSS) and ensure compliance.

 

5.       All other instructions issued earlier from time to time relating to the terms and conditions applicable for term deposits under Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme (ATSS), will remain unchanged.

 

                                      

V. S. Morye

       Managing Director
