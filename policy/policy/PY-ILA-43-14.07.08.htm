Circular ILA No. 43 dated 14 July 2008

 

Individual Housing Loans

- Revision in Policy and Interest Rate effective from 01.08.2008

------------------------------------------

1.       Pursuant to the directives received from RBI vide their Circular UBD.PCB.Cir.No. 16/09.09.001/06-07 dated 17.10.2006, our Bank was granting Housing Loans upto the limit of Rs.25.00 lacs per beneficiary of a dwelling unit.  Recently RBI vide their Circular UBD.PCB.Cir.No.42/09.09.001/08-09 dated 15.05.2008 has permitted our Bank – being a Tier II Bank – to grant housing loans to individual upto a maximum limit of Rs.50.00 lacs per beneficiary of a dwelling unit.

 

2.       Accordingly, as a matter of policy our Board of Directors in its meeting held on 24.06.2008 has decided to increase the housing loan limit upto Rs.50.00 lacs per beneficiary of a dwelling unit and consequently delegated powers for sanction of Housing Loans of Managing Director have been revised from the existing Rs.25.00 lacs to Rs.50.00 lacs.

 

3.       For arriving at Eligible Amount of Loan, the minimum take home salary after EMI amount of loan sanctioned should be taken into account as under: -

Limit for Housing Loan              Amount of Minimum take home salary

                                                               (i.e. home maintenance cost)

Upto Rs.10.00 lacs                               Rs. 4,500/-

Rs.10.00 to 15.00 lacs                          Rs. 5,000/-

Rs.15.00 to 25.00 lacs                          Rs. 7,500/-

Rs.25.00 to 35.00 lacs                          Rs.10,000/-

Rs.35.00 to 50.00 lacs                          Rs.15,000/-

 

4.       The Board in its meeting held on 10.07.2008, revised the rate of interest on housing loans as under: -

                                                                        Revised w.e.f. 01.08.2008

I)        Upto Rs.20.00 lacs                                       12.00% (floating)

Above Rs.20.00 lacs to Rs.30.00 lacs             13.00% (floating)

Above Rs.30.00 lacs to Rs.50.00 lacs             13.50% (floating)

II)       In case flat/house is given on rental basis      15.00% (floating)

 

          The above rates will be applicable to all new loans as well as to existing housing loan accounts w.e.f. 01.08.2008.

 

5.       In view of the revision in the Interest Rates on Housing Loans as above, branches are advised as under:

i.       Suitable Notice should be displayed prominently on the Branch Notice Board regarding change of interest rate. 

ii.      Branches should also send Notices to all the existing loanees and sureties regarding change of interest rate. 

iii.     It may be noted that interest rate changes are not applicable to housing loans who have been sanctioned Housing Loan under Fixed Rates of Interest during the period 01.07.2005 upto 30.06.2006.  However, if the borrower gives the flat/ house on rental basis, then the borrower to whom housing loan was granted under fixed rate of interest will also be charged commercial rate @ 15.00% p.a. (floating).

iv.      Commercial rate of interest is charged to borrowal accounts when flat/house is given on rental basis.  When the borrower submits a letter stating that the licensee has vacated the flat/house and the property is in the possession of the borrower, the normal rate of interest applicable for Housing Loan under 4(I) above may be applied after verifying the fact by visit/inspection of the flat/house.       

 

6.       All other relevant instructions given in this regard, from time to time, should be followed carefully.

 

 

                                                                                             M. C. Shetty

     Managing Director
