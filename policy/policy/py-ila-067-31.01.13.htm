Circular ILA No. 67 dated 31st January, 2013

Revision of Lending Rates w.e.f. 01.02.2013

 

Please refer to our Circular ILA No. 64 dated 01.05.2012, Circular ILA No. 65 dated 22.05.2012 and Circular ILA No. 66 dated 31.08.2012.

 

Reserve Bank of India in their 3rd quarter review of Monitory Policy 2012-2013 held on 29.01.2013 have reduced the Repo rates under LAF by 25 basis point from 8% to 7.75%.  Reserve Bank of India have also reduced Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) by 25 basis point from 4.25% to 4% effective fortnight beginning 09.02.2013.  Some Banks including State Bank of India have already reduced the rate of interest on Housing loans, car loans and other loans.  In order to be competitive in rate of interest on loans and advances and to attract new Loans and advances to our fold, the Board of Directors in its Meeting held on 30.01.2013 have approved the changes in rate of interest in regard to the following types of Loans and Advances.

 

i.             Housing Loan

ii.           SOD against Immovable property

iii.         Mortgage Loan

iv.         Vehicle Loan

The new changed Rate of Interest are made effective since 01.02.2013.
