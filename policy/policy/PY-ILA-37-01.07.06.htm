Circular ILA No. 37 dated  1st July 2006

Interest Rates on Loans & Advances

-------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Interest rates on loans and advances of the bank laid down in terms of circulars ILA No. 35 & 36 dated 30.6.2005 and 2 March 2006 were reviewed by the Board in the context of the prevailing conditions of the economy, lending rates of other banks, business prospects and customer expectations etc. in their meeting held on 14 June 2006. It has been observed that our bank was already offering lending rates for various facilities that were comparable to the most competitive rates offered by the banks for similar facilities and there was little scope for change or reduction of the existing rates in majority of loan categories. Nevertheless, the Board has adopted a few modifications to add flexibility to the rates that would be offered to the customers in group borrower and large borrower categories. The lending rates applicable w.e.f. 1.7.2006 as approved by the Board and explanatory/procedural notes on various related aspects are given in the following paragraphs:

Notes on I to XV:

i)             Interest  will be charged on daily reducing balance.

 

ii)           Interest on Housing Loans

 

(a)Hitherto the interest on Housing Loans was fixed. It has  been decided to allow fixed eligible interest rates of 8% or 9% to all Housing Loan Accountholders who have been disbursed loans upto 30.6.2006. 

 

(b)The rate of interest on Housing Loans disbursed since 1.7.2006 will be floating rate of interest.   

 

(c) From 1st July 2006, fixed rate of interest on Housing Loans is discontinued.

 

(d)The rebate of 1% allowed to Housing Loans other than those under SDL/ECS will continue.

iii)          No prepayment charges will be levied. However, penal interest rate @ 2% p.a. will be levied for the period loan account was serviced i.e. from date of disbursement to closure of the account and in  case of cash credit A/c. from the date of latest documentation. This is applicable only to Loan & Cash Credit Accounts taken over by other banks.

iv)         Penal rate of 3% p.a.  will be levied for the period of default on the amount of overdue.  Penal rate of 3% p.a. will also be levied for the period of default on the amount of overdue in housing loans.

 

v)           In case of Cash Credit NPA A/c., penal interest will be 3% p.a. from the date of NPA till it is regularized.

 

vi)         The rate of interest in respect of loans/secured OD to staff & officers upto limit of Rs. 2.00 lac against NSC/KVP/RBI Bonds & LIC  will be 9% p.a. The amount of loan will be 100% of the accrued value of NSC/KVP, 100% of the face value of RBI Bonds and 100% of the surrender value of LIC Policies. The staff members and officers should own the said securities solely or jointly with his/her spouse.

 

XVI.  Case filed and SRO accounts – The rate of interest after filing of cases against the defaulters will be the rate as per Demand Promissory Note i.e. the rate of interest prevailing at the time of disbursement of loans and advances.

Notes on XVII - Interest Rates based on Credit Rating:

 

1)   The interest would be charged on daily reducing balance.

 

2)   No prepayment charges will be levied. However, penal interest rate @ 2% p.a. will be levied for the period loan was serviced i.e. from date of disbursement to closure of the account and in case of cash credit A/c. from the date of latest documentation. This is applicable to Loan and Cash Credit Accounts taken over by other bank. 

 

3)   The special rate of 10.50% p. a. to all Paper and Stationery Traders of Fort Branch will continue.  However, yearly Credit Rating should be done of accounts eligible for credit rating.

 

4)   The concessional rate of interest @ 11% p.a. to Educational Institution will continue. However, yearly Credit Rating should be done.

 

5)   The rate of interest for a new customer will be at 12%. However, the same would be reduced to 11%, 10.75% and 10.50% from second year provided the credit rating of the borrower as per existing  Annexure – II is ‘A’, ‘AA’ and ‘AAA’ respectively. 

 

6)   Rate of interest will be revised as per gradation done every year.  The rate of interest on Term Loan accounts will also be revised as per gradation of Cash Credit Account wherever borrower enjoys both limits and is eligible for credit rating.

 

7)  In order to link the interest rates to the credit rating of borrowal accounts effectively, credit rating exercise  should not be confined only to the time of  renewal/review of the credit limits.  The  grading should be done as and when the latest audited financial statements are received and  the same should be forwarded through Zonal Offices to H.O. for approval.  

 

8)      There is need to create awareness among borrowers about  the reduction in the  rate of interest and dependence of the  benefit on the credit rating acquired  by them.  This will inculcate  the   habit   of submitting financial statements in time.  Branches are advised to write to all eligible borrowers  whose facilities are subject to  Credit Rating  as above to submit audited statements well in time and avail the benefit of lower rates upon higher gradation. 

 

9)      The penal interest will be  3% p.a. over the rate applicable  as per  the existing  grading.  If the gradation is revised at any time subsequently and penal interest continued to be charged, it will be charged at 3% p.a. over the revised rate of interest for the facility.

 

10)   In case the gradation is done on any day of a month, the revised rate of interest will be applicable from the 1st day of that month.

3.      General Instructions:

i.       Implications of credit discipline:

As pointed out in the opening para our lending rates are in alignment with the market conditions and rates charged by other competing banks. Our customers are not therefore likely to switch over to other banks. With this assurance branches should strive    to   ensure   that the quality of our loan assets remains good and the borrowers adhere to the prescribed credit discipline. In order  to inculcate such discipline   among the borrowers,  branches are advised to address letters to all the borrowing customers specifically pointing out that the bank will be levying penal interest for non-adherence to the discipline. The penal interest be levied in case of the following defaults:

(i)     Non-repayment   of    loan   installments

(ii)    Non-payment of interest

(iii)    Availing    excess  over   sanctioned      limit or excess over  Drawing Power

(iv)   Non-renewal/non-review above 3 months of cash credit limit due to non-submission of documents

(v)    Non-payment  of  L/C or bill discounted   on   due   dates

(vi)   Submission of final/audited Balance  Sheet  and  Profit   &   Loss  Account   for   March after 30th November etc.

(vii)   MTO in the cash credit account is less than 25% of the limits.  In case of cash credit accounts with sub-limit against Term Deposit (FDRs), MTO should be reckoned with the balance amount of limit (i.e. total limit less  sub-limit against FDR while charging penal interest).

 

In case of unreviewed CC A/cs. default  period will  end  on 1st day of next month after  receipt of all documents for review.

 

Penal Interest will be applicable for the period of default only.  

 

ii.      Negotiated rates of interest for Surety Loans and Group Loans:

 

In order to promote marketing  of Group Loans, Managers at Branches should take the necessary initiative with the help of ZOs. The DGMs should get actively involved and organize   joint visits, etc.  to  the  companies/establishments  in  the area of the

branch   to   promote  group loans. The areas of negotiations may

include even the interest rate for group loans with freedom for recommending  rate of interest between 10.50% to 12.00% p.a. A detailed note justifying the offer of lower rate of interest should be forwarded to HO within 8 days of negotiations and before conveying   formal    decision   of the bank to the applicants.  The

proposals received from Branches &  Zonal Offices will be further scrutinized by the Loans and Advances Dept. and placed before the Managing Director through the Addl. General Manager.  The proposal will be placed before next Board Meeting for final decision in respect of rate of interest.

 

 

iii.     Negotiation of Rate of Interest  to large borrowers with limits above Rs. 1.00 crore:

       

The rate of interest since 1.7.2006 are lower and comparable with the rate of interest of other banks.  However, there may still be some borrowers, particularly the large ones and their associate group accounts, who may still be lured by other banks with offers of  charging  lower  interest  and  other  concessions.   Such   cases  

should  be  examined on a priority   basis   with   particular   emphasis   on  standing and reputation of borrowers/group and their value for bank and recommendations for any further concession may be forwarded by the branch through zonal office to the Loans and Advances dept. at Head Office which shall recommend  such cases to the Board for  special treatment, if found necessary and justifiable.

 

Others:

 

1.   The concessional rate of interest to Steel manufacturers at 10%, paper and stationery merchants of Fort Branch at 10.5% and educational institution at 11% will continue till further instructions.

 

2.   Similarly, concessional rates already given to some of our valued customers at PLR or below PLR will continue till further instructions.

 

4.     It may be noted that the review of our lending rates and their revision, wherever necessary, has been done to make our rates market comparable  and customer friendly.  All concerned should carefully study the elements of flexibility added, particularly in respect of group loans to the employees of reputed business/industrial houses as also offer of negotiated rates to large borrowers and try to woo the prospective customers in these segments and take necessary initiatives for  enlarging  the loan business of our bank. It should specifically be noted that in other categories of loans and advances, no change/reduction has been made as our lending rates for these facilities are market related and on lower side in the industry and with efficient and aggressive marketing efforts, it should be possible for branches to compete with other banks.
