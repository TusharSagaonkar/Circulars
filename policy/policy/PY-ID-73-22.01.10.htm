Circular ID No. 73 dated 22nd January, 2010

 

Interest Rates on Bulk Deposits

of Rs.50.00 Lakh and above

 

         

 

Please refer to Our Circular ID No. 72 dated 22nd December, 2009.

 

2.       With a view to avoid probability of mismatch situation in our Assets & Liabilities, the recommendations made by ALCO were put up to the Board in its meeting held on 21.01.2010 and as per Board's decision the following rates would be made applicable with immediate effect i.e. from 22.01.2010 for Bulk Deposits of Rs.50.00 Lakh and above to be deposited  in a single day in any individual Branch by all the categories of the depositors i.e. General Public, Registered Trust, Co-operative Societies including Co-operative Housing Societies & Co-operative Banks and Senior Citizens.

 

i)        Above 12 Months and upto and              7.75%

          inclusive of 18 Months

 

ii)       Above 18 Months and upto and              8.00%

          inclusive of 36 Months

 

3.       The rates for the Deposits to be accepted for other than buckets mentioned above (as i and ii ) for any amount will be applied as per our Circular ID No. 72 dated 22nd December, 2009 for different categories mentioned therein. 

 

 

4.       All other instructions relating to the conditions applicable to domestic term deposits, issued earlier from time to time, continue to remain in force.

 

 

5.       Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members at your branch for immediate compliance.

 

                                      

V. S. Morye

       Managing Director
