Circular ID No. 54 dated 3 November 2007

 

Interest Rates on Term Deposits

Additional Interest for Bulk Deposit

 

 

        A reference is invited to Circular ID No. 52 dated 28 May 2007 advising upward revision of interest rates on term deposits, effective from 1 June 2007.  A special dispensation of additional interest on bulk deposits of individuals, kept for period of over 12 months was offered in terms of para 3 of the circular.  The Co-op. Societies and Sr. Citizens who were being paid interest on their term deposits at higher rates were excluded from the eligibility for such higher interest even on their bulk deposits.  The position in regard to payment of additional interest to co-op. societies and urban bank who usually keep term deposits of large amounts and are paid additional interest by most other banks was reviewed and following the review, the Board has decided, in their meeting held on 30 October 2007, to pay additional interest on bulk deposits of co-op. societies and other urban banks, over and above the interest rate (higher) being paid to them in terms of Circular ID No. 52 dated 28 May 2007.  It is accordingly advised that on all new bulk deposits kept by co-op. societies and other urban banks for period above 12 months branches may pay additional interest as under with effect from 1 November 2007

 

Volume of Deposits      Additional Interest Payable over and     

above the extant rate

 

          Rs.50.00 lacs to Rs.1.00 crore                               0.25% p.a.

          Above Rs.1.00 crore                                              0.50% p.a.

 

          It should be noted that the above additional interest is payable prospectively on fresh deposits and matured and renewed deposits, premature withdrawal of deposits even for reinvestment will, however, be subject to penalty at the prevailing rate, as advised in IDC MD-920/446/2007-08 dated 13 July, 2007.     

 

 

                                                 M. C. Shetty

      Managing Director
