Circular ILA No. 79 Dated 15th April, 2017

 

Revision of Lending Rates w. e. f. 01.04.2017

                                           

 

I. 1. Interest rates on deposits in our Bank have been reduced by 1.50 % during the past Financial Year (April 2016 to March 2017), after several factors having been weighed carefully.

2.The PLR of the Bank was worked out to 13% on the basis of Balance Sheet for the year ended 31.03.2016. The PLR for FY 2017-18 has been worked out to 12 % based on the interim balance sheet of the Bank as on 31.12.2016. This has been possible mainly due to the downward revision in rates of interest on Term Deposits and also due to cost containment measures taken by the Bank. The revision in PLR to 12 % has been approved in the Board Meeting held on 10.03.2017.

3.In consequence of reduction in PLR & with a view to countering the increased competition in marketing of Loans & Advances (as evidenced by shifting of some of our good accounts to other Banks owing to their lower rate of interest), it has been decided to reduce rate of interest on Loans & Advances from 1st April, 2017. The revised rates of interest on Loans & Advances w. e. f. 1st April, 2017 are as per the Chart enclosed herewith.

 

II. Procedures for modification of ROI in OMNI System:-

1. It has been decided to make changes in rate of interest for Housing Loans, Gold Loans, Surety Loans, NSC Loans and Education Loans through I.T. Dept. For all other Loans & Advances, the changes in rate of interest shall be made manually by Staff/Officials at Branches.

 

2. In case of Fort and Bhiwandi branches where concessional rate of interest have been given to Paper & Stationery Traders and Textile Manufacturers & Traders, changes in rate of interest shall be done by staff/officials at the Branches.

 

3. (a) In case of Cash Credits, other working capital limits and Secured Loan accounts which are eligible for Credit Rating, revision in interest rates as per the enclosed chart shall be made manually by staff/officials at Branches. It is therefore necessary to ensure that proper Credit Rating is fed in the system. The official who has been entrusted with the work of authorization of rate of interest for such an account shall ensure that the entry of proper Credit Rating based on audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss account as on 31.03.2016 is made and change in interest rate is done as per the Chart.

 (b) Credit Rating based on audited Balance Sheet and Profit & Loss Account as on 31.03.2017 is required to be completed by 31st December 2017. However, if any accountholder submits the audited financial statements pertaining to 31.03.2017 earlier than 31st December 2017, the Credit Rating shall be done immediately and the account shall be charged new ROI based on the revised Credit Rating.

 

4. In case of Kankavali and Udupi Branches, special ROI is given to certain Loans & Advances accounts (a) up to Rs. 100.00 Lakh and (b) above Rs. 100.00 Lakh up to Rs. 500.00 Lakh. These branches will have to ensure that correct rate of interest is fed in the Limit Master. The same shall be manually done by staff and officials at those Branches.

 

5.We have offered lower rate of interest to some good borrowers in order to retain their accounts in the face of competition from other banks. These borrowers have been given fixed/concessional rate of interest for 1 or 2 years. In case revised rate of interest applicable as per the chart enclosed is lower than the concessional rate of interest extended already, the revised lower rate of interest generally applicable will be offered to them. In case the concessional rate of interest already extended is lower than the rate applicable as per the chart attached hereto, the concessional rate will be continued as per the terms of sanction i.e. for one or two years as the case may be.

 

6. The list of accounts wherein the changes in rate of interest have been done by I.T. Dept. will be sent to each branch by I.T. Dept. with the details of previous rate of interest and applicable new rate of interest. Branches shall check the details and confirm to I.T. Dept. about the correctness. In case of any discrepancy, branches will bring the same to the notice of I.T. Dept. for modification. Thereafter, I.T. Dept. will generate the notices. It is the responsibility of the Incharge of branches to ensure that notices are generated by I.T. Dept., and dispatched by Development Dept.

 

III. Defaults & Levy of Penal Interest Therefor:-

1. Non-compliance of Terms & Conditions:-

(i)    Penal interest @ 1% will be levied for non-submission of auditor-certified Stock & Book Debt statements as on 30th September by 30th November.

 

(ii)   Penal interest @ 1% will be levied in case the monthly turnover in the account is less than 25% of the sanctioned limit.

 

(iii)   In case of accounts coming under Credit Rating System, last date for submission of audited Balance Sheet and P&L account for any financial year is subsequent 30th November. Penal interest @1% will be levied from the following 1st January if Credit Rating exercise cannot be completed by 31st January in the absence of necessary audited Balance Sheet and P&L Account.

 

(iv)  In case of an Educational Institution, the last date for submission of audited Balance Sheet and P&L account is 15th March of the next year. In case of default, penal interest @1% will be levied from 1st April of the year following the Financial Year concerned if the Credit Rating exercise cannot be completed by 31st March in the absence of necessary audited Balance Sheet and P&L Account.

 

(v)  In case of Cash Credit and Term Loan accounts turned into NPAs, penal interest will be charged @1% p.a. on entire outstanding amount from the month following the date of NPA till it is upgraded to Standard Asset.

 

(vi) Penal interest @1% will be charged for (a) non-payment of EMI/interest, (b) irregularities such as excess drawals, absence of Drawing Power due to non submission of Stock & Book Debts statements & failure to maintain Drawing Power etc. and, (c) credit limits remaining un-renewed or un-reviewed in the absence of necessary financial statements.

 

(vii)            Penal interest will be levied @ 1% on outstanding amount in the relative credit facility/facilities if the charged asset/assets remain(s) un-insured, the period of application of such penal interest running from the date of expiry of insurance policy till its renewal.

Note:-

Branches should charge 1% penal interest for a default. However, maximum penal interest charged is limited to 1% for more than one default.

 

2. Penal charges for foreclosure/takeover of Loans & Advances A/cs.:-

(a)  In case of Housing Loan accounts proposed to be taken over by other banks, foreclosure charges/prepayment penalties will not be levied whether rate of interest is floating or fixed.

 

(b)  Also, in case of other loans (both secured & unsecured) in the names of individuals proposed to be taken over by other banks, foreclosure/ prepayment penalties will also not be levied.

 

(c)  In case of Loans & Advances sanctioned to non-individual Loanees/ borrowers proposed to be taken by other Banks/FIs, foreclosure/ prepayment penalties @ 2% will be levied on outstanding amount as on the date of closure.

 

(d)  In case of Loans & Advances closed before maturity by non-individual borrowers through their own sources, no foreclosure charges/penalties will be levied.

 

3. In respect of Loans/SODs against NSC/KVP/RBI Bonds/FDR (Accrued Value)/ LIPs (Surrender Value) to Officers & Staff of our Bank, rate of interest will be 1% less than what is applicable to the borrowers of the Bank. The employee should own the said securities solely or jointly with spouse (as per MOU).

 

IV. In case LATD/SOD is sanctioned against Term Deposits to a Proprietorship firm, Partnership Firm, Limited Liability Partnership Firm or Private Limited Company and, if the said Term Deposits are in personal name(s) of the Proprietor/Partners/ Directors, the Term Deposits should not be treated as belonging to Third Party and rate of interest applicable will be 1% above TD interest rate. In case the LATD/SOD is sanctioned against third party deposits, rate of interest will be 2% above the interest applicable to Term Deposits.

 

V.          Facility for Housing Loan Borrowers for switchover to current rate of interest:- Our housing loan borrowers will be permitted to switch over to the current slab for interest on Housing loan by charging switchover fee of 1% on outstanding amount of the Housing loan provided their past performance of repayment in the loan account is satisfactory and all the sanctioned terms and conditions including creation of charge with the authorities concerned and insurance coverage etc. have been complied with (Refer Circular CR No. 165 Dated 04.08.2016). The switchover fee will be attracted once more in those cases where the borrower had previously applied for switchover and paid similar fee.

 

VI. External Credit Rating - Tie up with CRISIL for availing their services: - A concession of 0.25% in rate of interest in respect of credit facilities is available to enterprises which earn any of CRISIL’s top 3 ratings and also any of our Bank’s internal top three ratings.

 

(For example, if our borrower is internally rated as any of the three i.e. AAA, AA or A, and is also simultaneously rated by CRISIL as any of their top three i.e. MSE 1/SME 1, MSE 2/SME 2 or MSE 3/SME 3, a concession of 0.25% in rate of interest will be offered in what would otherwise have been applicable according to our own internal rating).

 

VII. Procedures for sending Notices by I.T. Dept., Development Dept. & Branches:-

1.  The notice of reduction in rate of interest in respect of all Housing Loan accounts, Secured Loan accounts and Education Loan Accounts (Individuals) will be generated by the I.T. Dept. Development Dept. will arrange dispatch of these notices. The second copy of such Notices will be sent to branches.

 

2.  In case of CC/DBD/SBD/Channel Credit/RCL accounts, Branches should arrange to send manually prepared notices through Post in their area. The notices should be signed by the Accountant/Manager/Sr. Manager/ Asst. Gen. Manager of the branch.

 

3.  In case of Educational Institutions, notices should be generated manually at Branches and handed over to an authorized person of the institute against acknowledgement.

 

I.T. Dept. should ensure that notices to Educational Institutions are not generated as these accounts are also categorized as ESCLN.

 

4.  In view of accounts of Surety Loans, Gold Loans & NSC Loans being in large numbers and ROI having been reduced, it has been decided not to send notices to the loanees availing themselves of such loans. Instead, reduction in rate of interest by 1.00% would be displayed on website of the Bank, at a prominent place in the branch premises through posters and also by way of a notice on the Notice Board for the customers.

 

VIII.      Other Instructions:-

1.  There are chances of some borrowers approaching branches for reducing the EMI amount. Such cases are expected to be few. Branches are authorized to consider their request provided there is no change in the number of balance EMIs and maturity date of the loan remains unchanged. Branches should report such cases to their ZOs for ratification.

 

2. Planning & Marketing Dept. shall arrange for printing of advertising material with the new rates. They should give wide publicity to the revision and reduction in rate of interest on all types of Loans & Advances.

 

3. I.T. Dept. shall make necessary changes in our Bank’s website and in our system accordingly.

 

4. Inspection Dept. shall ensure through Concurrent Audit that change in rate of interest is done properly by inspecting randomly sampled accounts of all types of loans.

 

IX.   It may be noted that except loans extended on fixed interest rates’ basis, there is reduction in interest rates on all types of loans and advances. It is therefore, need of the hour to increase loans & advances including Housing Loans and loans for Repairs & Renovation substantially in the coming months. The Incharge of branches are advised to involve all staff members in marketing of retail loans and advances and ensure that the targets given are achieved well in advance.

 

X.  Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all officials/staff members at your branch and ensure strict compliance.
