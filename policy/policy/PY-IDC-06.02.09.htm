Sub.: Bancassurance – Tie-up with Life Insurance Corporation of India

for marketing life insurance plans .

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

You are aware that our Bank was having tie-up arrangement with ING Vysya Bank Life Insurance Company Ltd. for 3 years from 07-11-2005 to 06-11-2008. The fee based income from this business with ING Vysya Life Insurance Company Ltd. was far below of our expectations. We have therefore, discontinued the arrangement vide our IDC bearing No. HO/G.M./L&A/477/2008-09 dated 04-12-2008 As per the Board's decision giving more thrust on fee based income without which we can not progress, we have entered into a Bancassurance Agreement with Life Insurance Corporation of India for the purpose of sale of Life Insurance products through all our offices. LIC being a Nationalized corporation, their assurance is backed by Government of India and as such their products are easily acceptable by the general public without any hesitations.

For this purpose, we have applied for Corporate Agency from LIC of India and are in the final stages of procuring the same. Under the Corporate Agency Model our Bank will distribute Life Insurance Products through our network of offices, for which we will be paid First Commission and Renewal Commission. This will result in improved profitability of our Bank.

Monthly targets will be set for procuring Life Insurance Business. Every Branch In-charge will have to comply with the respective target set for it. For this purpose each Branch Manager will have to identify a capable person who will undergo requisite training and be designated as a 'Specified Person'. Such a person will be required to approach customers through a dedicated desk – Insurance Corner. The 'Specified Person' looking after this desk will have to carry out an insurance need analysis, suggest appropriate plans and close the sales. All assistance will be provided by the nearest LIC Branch.

To facilitate implementation of this arrangement LIC will be imparting quality training to all Heads of Branches/Departments and other concerned Executives/ Officers  on 11th and 12th February, 2009 at "LIC of India, Mumbai Divisional Office III, New India Assurance Building, DTC Hall, Ground Floor, Santacruz (West), Mumbai – 400 054". We are enclosing a list of Executives and Officers alongwith attending dates of training. Please note that no leave will be granted and attendance is compulsory. All should be present at the Venue at 10.00 a.m. sharp. Tea and Lunch will be provided at the Venue.

In case any official is unable to attend on a particular day he/she should inform to Mrs. Maggie Fernandez, Chief Insurance Executive on Mobile No.9819881827 and/or Mr. S. R. Choudhari, Accountant on Mobile No. 9869853506.  

We hope to achieve stupendous success in this new field of effecting Insurance Sales through our Banking infrastructure.
