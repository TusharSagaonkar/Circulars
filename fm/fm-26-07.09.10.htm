Investigation in CID CB.Cr. No. 95/08 &

          We are in receipt of Indian Banks' Association letter No. Legal/CIR/1092 dated 18th August, 2010, enclosed with a letter No. SP/CID/EOC/212/2010 dated 12th August, 2010 alongwith a Lookout Notice received by them from the office of the Superintendent of Police, CID, Economic Offences Cell, Panaji, Goa.

         

2.      We enclose a copy of the letter of the Superintendent of Police, CID, Economic Offences Cell, Panaji, Goa and Lookout Notice for your information and necessary action.

 

3.      Please verify from your branch record whether the lookout person has opened any account at your branch in different names, or be vigilant if lookout person is approaching your branch with an intention to open New Account. You are therefore advised to go through the photographs of account holders and if lookout person is identified, please inform to the Principal Officer – Asst. General Manager (Inspection Dept.) Vashi who will convey the matter to the Superintendent of Police, Economic Offences, Goa and if felt necessary the message will be conveyed by contacting them on telephone No. 0832-2428267, Ext. 377, 09850475037.

 

4.      Please take suitable precautionary note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all concerned staff members and ensure strict compliance.
