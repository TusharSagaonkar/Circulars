Circular FM No. 33 dated 31st July, 2015

 

Precautions to be taken to prevent Frauds in

Co-operative Housing Societies Accounts.

 

Our branches are maintaining Savings/Current and Fixed deposits accounts of various Housing Co-operative Societies.  Large Co.operative Housing Societies generally appoint a Manager to manage the affairs of the society. The Manager is entrusted with the work of all financial matters of the society and as such the charge of all the Society records including the cheque book and fixed deposit receipts held by the Society with the Banks has been handed over to him. There are instances where such Managers appointed by Large Housing Co-op., Societies have played mischief and defrauded the Housing Society and the Banks by withdrawing monies from the accounts maintained by the Housing Society. The modus operendi of such frauds is as under:

 

2. Initially by performing good work and with showing apparent loyalty towards the society, the Manager wins the trust of the office bearers of the society. Afterwards with treachery he skillfully obtains signature(s) of the office bearers on cheques, FDRs, letters, etc., and makes withdrawals from society’s accounts by keeping the office bearers in darkness. At times the signature(s) of the office bearers could be forged by the Manager or the Manager may intimate the Bank about change in office bearers even though there has been no actual changes in office bearers and then the Manager formulates the fraud by forging the signature(s) of so called new office bearers. The KYC of these new office bearers are submitted from the records available with the Housing Society. Further, for defalcating the monies of the Society, the Society’s Manager pre-maturely or on due date closes the fixed deposits held in the name of the Society pretending that the expenses are to be incurred for major repairs of the Building etc. After credit of the FDR proceeds to the Savings Bank Account of the Society, the amounts are systematically withdrawn from the Savings Bank Account by large cash withdrawals and also by cheques received in clearing favouring the personal name or proprietary firm in name of the Society’s Manager maintained with some other Banks.

 

3. In this connection, apart from the extant guidelines, we advise the branches to take following precautions:

 

i.           Withdrawal on due date or premature closing of FDRs of Housing Society:

Generally Housing Societies are keeping FDRs for Major Repairs, Sinking Fund and general purposes. If the deposit is kept by the Society for Sinking Fund say “XYZ Housing Society Ltd.-Sinking Fund” then such deposits are not allowed to be closed unless there is approval of the General Body.

 

Other type of FDRs can be encashed on due date or by premature closing by giving discharge of the office bearers on the Fixed deposit which is to be supported by letter from the Society alongwith Resolution of the Managing Committee for encashing the same on due date or by pre-mature closure of the specific FDR.

 

All cases of encashment on due date or pre-mature closure of FDRs of Housing Societies are to be handled by Accountant / Manager/ In-charge of Branch personally, making detailed enquiries directly with the office bearers about reasons for closure of FDRs and ensure that the request is supported by proper documents like resolution & proper discharge on FD receipt by the office bearers, etc.

 

If the Society’s Manager or any other Staff comes for closure of FDRs along with all requisite papers then it is necessary for the Branch to obtain telephonic confirmation from at least the Chairman/Secretary and Treasurer of the Society and the details of such confirmation with full details must be noted by the Bank official on separate paper and kept with the FDR. In case of FDRs of Rs. 500 lakh and above visit to a society and discussion with the Chairman/Secretary and the Treasurer is must.

 

ii.         Cash Withdrawals from SB/ CD Accounts: Normally Cash withdrawals from Society Account is to be restricted to very limited extent. For day to day petty  expenses cash is required which as per latest model Bye-laws No.144, Housing Society cannot keep in hands more than Rs.5000/-. Moreover, Bye law No.145 stipulates that all payments in excess of a particular amount shall be made by crossed Account Payee Cheque. Payment to be made to Municipality in respect of property Tax, Water Bill and also to other sevice provider companies like MSEB, BEST, Torrent, etc., should be made by Account Payee Crossed Cheque or payorder and not in cash. In case of Repairs work payment by A/c. Payee Cheque has to be done by the society. Cash should not be paid. Payment of salary of watchman etc. is also made by cheque.

 

There is, therefore no reason for big cash withdrawals from Housing Society Accounts except for the payment to sweepers to be made in cash which will not exceed Rs. 1000/- to Rs. 10000/- p.m. and hence cash payment cannot and should not be made in a routine and casual manner. It is necessary that proper due diligence is done by the Branch Incharge/Accountant before making any exceptional large Cash payment from Housing Society accounts. Branch should obtain proper telephonic confirmation from Chairman/Secretary and Treasurer of the society (who should not be the bearer of the instrument) and record the same on the reverse of the cheque. 

 

iii.          Compulsory SMS facility on mobile of office bearer: As a precautionary & preventive measure, Branches should extend the SMS facility to the existing Chairman/Secretary and Treasurer of society without insisting written request from society, and accordingly transactions in the account should be reported to    office bearers of the society on their mobiles and modification and updation of Mobile No(s) is to be done promptly whenever there is change of mobile number or office bearers.

 

iv.        Special precaution to be taken by the bank officials while noting change in office-bearers.  Whenever there is change in the office bearers of the Society, the outgoing Chairman should introduce the new office bearers of the Society by consenting/giving NOC on the Resolution of the Managing Committee conveying to the Bank the new signatories to the account. KYC of new office bearers shall be obtained and they should remain present personally with the copy of resolution and with original KYC documents for verification. In the event of any doubt the old signatories to the account should be contacted for necessary confirmation. Once the new office bearers are taken on record, the mobile numbers of the new Chairman, Secretary and Treasurer should be obtained and updated in our records so that they can be contacted for confirmation of any transaction as and when required and also for enabling  them to receive SMS alerts.

 

v.          Balance Confirmation Letter: In respect of Savings/Current Deposit Accounts of the Housing Societies, Branches should note to obtain Balance Confirmation Letter on Half yearly basis duly signed by the office bearers of the society which will help the Bank in prevention and early detection of fraud. Such Balance confirmation letters should be preserved by the Branch in a separate file.

 

4. Branches are advised to take suitable note of the above instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all Officials/Staff Members at Branches & ensure strict compliance.
