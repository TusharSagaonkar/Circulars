Circular FM No. 32 dated 14th January, 2015

Fraudulent opening of Bank accounts with fake KYC documents – Prevention of Frauds

 

Please refer to our circular FM No. 30 dated 15.09.2014 advising branches the modus – operandi used by fraudster(s) to defraud Banks and the steps to be taken by branches to prevent similar such frauds in our Bank.

 

Based on the communication received from Reserve Bank of India (RBI) vide Circular No. RBI.2014-15/11/DPSS (Che)/731/01.02.003/2014-15 dated 05.01.2015, we  advise  Modus-Operandi of the fraud as under:-

 

1.   ‘A fraudster is opening bank account by submitting forged document. Cheques are deposited for credit of his account and amount is withdrawn within short span of time. Afterwards it is found that, fraudster is not traceable’.

2.   ‘ Drawer claims he has not issued the cheque’

3.   ‘Wrong debit to account as cheque(s) presented were fraudulent and original cheques are in possession of the drawer’. 

 

In most cases it is observed that the fraudster is depositing cheques at a branch other than the home branch.

 

Branches should be careful while accepting cheques for clearing presented with adhesive tapes. Also no cheque with any alteration shall be accepted. Further, branches are advised to ensure that amount of cheques deposited for credit of his account are commensurate with the income declared in the Account Opening Form. In such cases branches should also verify once again KYC documents and if necessary visit the place of residence and confirm their genuineness. In all doubtful cases discreet enquiries should be made.     

 

The list of name of the fraudster, details of documents used by the fraudster for opening accounts in various banks as provided by RBI is enclosed herewith for information.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and go through the incidences given in the list and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at the Branch and be careful and take more than ordinary precautions to avoid such type of frauds at your branch.
