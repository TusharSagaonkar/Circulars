Circular FM No. 29 Dated 27th June, 2014

Fraud in Gold Loan Account

 

We wish to bring to your notice that a fraud has been reported in Gold Loan Account, details of which are as under:-

 

2. Cases reported from Marve Link Road branch, Malad and Dadar Branch: One  Mr. Kamlesh Mohan Jain had availed three gold loans from Marve Link Road, Malad  (2) and Dadar (1) branches. On failure to repay the loan, branch had taken efforts to recover the loan by sending notices. However, these notices were returned undelivered by the postal department with the remarks ‘addressee not found’.  Visits to residence and contact through Telephone/Mobile was also turned futile. CIBIL report indicated that he had obtained 15 gold loans from different financial institutions/Banks, including 3 gold loans from our Bank. Revaluation and inspection of the gold ornaments indicated that some part of the chain are of pure gold and remaining parts are gold plated and it was difficult to identify fakeness in gold ornaments through naked eyes and only after applying acid test, fakeness was detected. The modus operandi of the fraud is as under:-

 

The fraudster has keenly observed that while checking the genuineness/purity of the gold ornaments like chains etc., the valuers are testing only the hook of the chain and accordingly judging the purity of the gold for entire ornament and arriving at the value of the gold ornament. Mr. Jain has taken advantage of this procedure and pledged the ornaments which are having hooks and managed to prepare the ornaments with only hooks made with pure gold and other part of the ornaments gold plated.  

 

3. The matter was taken with the gold valuers of both branches by Legal & Recovery Dept. The gold valuers have admitted their fault in valuing the gold ornaments.

 

4. We have already cautioned all our Gold valuers about the above incident and have made modification in the present terms and conditions in the letter to be sent to the valuers, while appointing them as Bank’s Gold Valuers.

 

5. We request all Branch Managers and Staff concerned to be extra vigilant and exercise utmost caution during the process of gold valuation. Please ensure that the Gold Appraisers test the purity of the gold by taking into account the entire gold ornament and not part of the ornament and accordingly certify its valuation. It is therefore advised that a responsible Officer, say Asstt-Accountant/ Accountant/ Manager of the branch should remain present while the gold ornaments are evaluated by the valuers and our officer should ensure that each ornament is valued in its entirety and not in parts, to avoid such type of frauds in the Bank in future.

 

6. You are advised to bring these instructions to the notice of the Officials/ Staff concerned and ensure strict compliance.
