Circular FM No. 27  dated  3rd November, 2010

Accused Person involved in Bank Dacoities / Robberies.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          We are in receipt of Indian Banks' Association letter No. Legal/Cir/Fr/1/1648 dated 26th October, 2010, alongwith a copy of letter No. O.W.10889 /10 dated 22.10.2010, received by them from Borivali Police Station, regarding investigation of a case of a group of 7 to 8 accused persons mostly from Bihar involved in bank dacoities / robberies inter-state. Their involvement is disclosed in some of the banks whose names are mentioned in the letter.  We enclose a copy of the said letter of the Borivali Police Station addressed to IBA which is self explanatory.

 

2.       Branches are advised to scan all existing accounts to ensure that no account is held by or linked to any of the individuals mentioned in the letter. Before opening any new account; it should also be ensured that the name/s of the individual customer does not resemble with names mentioned in the enclosed letter. If you find any such person / account(s) at your branch please furnish the details of the Person / account(s) alongwith the statement of account(s) of each accused to Secretarial Dept. who will advise the matter to appropriate Police Authorities for further action.

 

 3.      Please take suitable precautionary note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all concerned staff members and ensure strict compliance.
