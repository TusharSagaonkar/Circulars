Circular FM No. 30 dated 15th September, 2014

Fraudulent opening of Bank accounts with fake KYC documents

 

The Reserve Bank of India vide Circular RBI.2014-15/4/DPSS (Che)/296/01.02.003/2014-15 dated 02.09.2014 have communicated the modus operandi of fraud committed in banks, as detailed below:-

 

“While examining Panel for Resolution of Disputes (PRD) cases, it was observed that some fraudsters had managed to open accounts with fake KYC documents at various bank branches for collection of forged/altered/replicated cheques. Generally the proceeds of these forged/altered/replicated cheques collected were withdrawn within a short span of time through ATM card/at non-home branches and thereafter, the fraudsters were not traceable. In most of the cases, the fraudulent cheques were deposited at a non-home branch by the fraudster”. Some of the names of the fraudsters, details of documents used by the fraudsters for opening accounts in various banks/branches, are furnished in the Annexure for information and they have been uploaded in AML software for name screening.

 

The RBI has advised all banks to circulate the above information amongst the staff of clearing operation in the centralized locations as well as the branches, to sensitize them and also to ensure compliance with the CTS procedural guidelines, KYC norms and maintaining of accounts in terms of RBI directives so that perpetration of such frauds in the Banking System can be prevented. Branches have been further advised to check their database of the account holders for the details of these fraudsters (who may have recently opened the account even though no fraud have been committed so far) and exercise due diligence to prevent recurrence of similar frauds.

 

Further, in terms of IBA Circular No. PS&BT/RBI-CTS/155 dated June 14, 2014, it has been advised that in case the presenting bank does MICR reject, presenting bank is required to verify the reason for MICR reject, double check on availability of all CTS 2010 features, whether the payee account is active, notify drawee bank in case of high value instruments etc. As ECPIX provides a MICR rejects/repair flag data to indicate the action to the drawee bank, the Branch/Central Processing Cell may exercise necessary due diligence as required including calling of physical instrument in terms of Section 64(2) of amended Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881. Branches are advised to exercise due diligence in respect of high value cheques deposited in a non-home branch before presenting the same in clearing.               

 

The Broad modus operandi used by fraudsters is being circulated to enable the branches to become aware about the techniques and methods used by fraudsters and thereby prevent incidence of fraud in our bank.

 

Branches are advised to cross verify the names of the fraudsters as per enclosed list and the details of documents used by the fraudsters for opening accounts and notify the Head Office in case of any similar such modus operandi attempted in our Bank.

 

Please take suitable note of these incidences and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at the Branch and be cautious and take extra ordinary care while dealing with similar cases.
