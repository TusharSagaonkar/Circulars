Circular FM No. 22 dated 1st February 2006

 

Fraudulent Cash Withdrawal against forged Banker's Cheque

miss-representing as inter bank cash drawal

-------------------------------------------------------------------------

          Appended below are the contents of the letter dated 04th January 2006, addressed by the Chief Vigilance Officer of Allahabad Bank to the Indian Banks' Association.  The modus operandi adopted by the culprits was ingenious and shrewd and they have succeeded in defrauding the reporting bank despite ordinary care exercised by them.  Branches are advised to take a note of the peculiar modus operandi and guard themselves against any such incidence.   

 

 

Manager

              Secretarial Dept.

 

ALLAHABAD BANK

Ref. No. HO/VIG/FF-1/F-Misc/80                                        04.01.2006.

 

The Chief Executive,

Indian Banks' Association

Mumbai- 400 005.

 

Dear Sir,

 

Ref : Fraud to the tune of Rs. 10,00,000/- at our Haridwar Branch, Uttaranchal.

 

An incident of fraud by way of payment to Haridwar Upper Road Branch of Bank of Baroda against forged Bankers Cheque have been surfaced, the details of which are given hereunder.

 

On 15.12.2005, at about 10.30 A.M. our branch received a telephone call from one Mr. Chauhan from Bank of Baroda, Upper Road, Haridwar Branch inquiring whether there was sufficient cash balance to which our branch replied in affirmative as they had sufficient cash balance. Later on the branch official made a telephone call to the branch of Bank of Baroda inquiring as to whether a Pay Order or T.T. would be issued to which the Bank of Baroda advised that they do not need cash.  Then the branch official also enquired from other Branch of Bank of Baroda at Narsingh Bhavan, Haridwar wherefrom it was advised that they also do not need cash.

 

However, at 10.40 A.M., a telephone call came again to the branch purportedly from Bank of Baroda, Upper Road, Haridwar Main Br. confirming that they are deputing their two employees to receive cash along with a Banker's Cheque of Rs. 10,00,000/- and request cum authority letter attesting signature of the person who will receive the cash.  At about 11.30 A.M., two persons along with a Cash Box came to the branch and handed over the Bankers Cheque and Authority Letter to the branch official, who after completing required formalities made payment to the authenticated person purported to be of Bank of Baroda.

 

On 16.12.2005, the branch sent the Bankers Cheque through clearing when the Manager of Bank of Baroda, Upper Road, Haridwar advised that the Bankers Cheque is forged/ fake and they have not received any cash against the said instrument.  Thus fraud to the tune of Rs. 10,00,000/-has been perpetrated.

 

In view of the above, you are requested to advise the member Banks to exercise caution in such a situation.
