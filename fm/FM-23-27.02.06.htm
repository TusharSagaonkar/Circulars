Circular  FM  No. 23 date 27/2/2006

 

Opening bogus Accounts with Cooperative Banks and collecting

stolen / forged pay orders, dividend warrants, etc.

 

          The Maharashtra State Cooperative Banks' Association Ltd., Mumbai, have been provided by Panvel City Police, photographs of two fraudsters who have been operating in and around Panvel /Navi Mumbai, etc.  The culprits open deposit account in the name of housing society with false and fabricated documents and such account is used for collecting pay orders, dividend warrants, etc. which are evidently stolen and / or forged. In the incidents reported they have used urban banks to perpetrate the frauds. The Association's letter furnishing specific details of the frauds committed so far and photographs of the culprits is reproduce overleaf. The branches are advised to take note of the reported activities of the fraudsters and take necessary  precausations to prevent the fraudsters from accessing our bank for carrying out their criminal activity. In this connection, attention is invited to KYC and AML Policy and procedures circulated under the Circular OC No. 336 dated 8 February 2006 and branches are advised to note that according to the Customer Acceptance norms specified therein such account is non-acceptable for the bank.  If the culprits  approach any branch for opening account, in all probability with different assured names, the case should be handled carefully. As advised in the said circular  the dealing staff should ensure that culprits, if and when, they approach the branch should not be tipped off that the bank has advance information about their criminal acts and intentions. They may be kept engaged under the pretext completing of procedural formalities and Panvel  Police should be contacted immediately on the given telephone numbers and informed of the developments.  The branch should also make immediate contact with the local Police Station as well as our Vigilance  Officer. It should be noted all this action has to  be taken  promptly and discreetly without arousing any suspicision in the minds of the fraudsters present in the branch. Even if the persons in the photographs make enquiry about account opening and without pursuing formalities walk away, the fact that they approached the branch may be intimated to Panvel Police as it may provide useful clue to them for apprehending the criminals.

 

    M.C. Shetty

Managing Director
