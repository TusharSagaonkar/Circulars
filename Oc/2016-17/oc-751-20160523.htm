Circular OC No.751 dated 23rd May, 2016

Return of CRC Cheques

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          As per the Policy, it is responsibility of staff of CPC Dept. to verify correctness of inward clearing instrument such as date, amount in word and figure, signature, genuineness of instrument etc.  Centralized Clearing Dept. returns Inward clearing (CTS) for technical reasons without referring the same to the Branches.  Only cases where funds are short or use of discretionary powers is required are referred to the Branches through their TOD module.

 

          It has been brought to our Notice that even good customers commit mistakes during the first few days of January, previous year is mentioned e.g. ‘2015’ is written instead of ‘2016’.  Other areas of concern are: The issuer of cheque is an Institution like Co-op. Housing Society, Co-op. Credit Society or cheques are issued in favour of Payees such as LIC, Telephone companies, Electricity companies, Tax Authorities etc. CPC Dept. is returning these cheques without referring to the In-charge of Branches. Dishonouring such cheques creates displeasure amongst the customers which could be avoided to a large extent, if the cases are referred to the concerned Branch In-charges.

 

          The Central Processing staff of Central Clearing Dept. returns the cheques for following technical reasons without making any reference to the Branches:-

1.      Payees’s name missing

2.      Unsigned cheques

3.      Irregularly drawn cheque – Amount in words and figures differ

4.      Post Dated Cheques

5.      Stale cheques

6.      Wrongly listed cheques – usually in amount

7.      Cheques crossed to two banks

8.      Payee’s endorsement irregular or not confirmed by Banker

9.      Already encashed cheques

10.    Altered cheques

11.    Contrary to Special Crossing/instructions – cheque is issued for Rs. 6000/-,   “not over states Rs. 5000/-”

12.    Image quality failed cheques and/or not received in P2F session

13.    Non-CTS cheques presented in CTS session and vice-versa

14.    Payment stopped cheques.

 

During the time of MICR clearing, the instruments were received by Central Clearing Dept. At that time they could get the necessary corrections done in the cheques before storing them. In CTS clearing, the instruments are retained by the presenting banks and the tenor of the cheques cannot be changed.

Thus there is a risk in passing these cheques, as only images are received and the images form a record of the settlement which are legally valid under the NI Act.  Notwithstanding  the risk, to provide better customer service, CPC Dept. and Branches are advised to adhere to the following:-

     i.        Customers other than Co-op. Housing Societies/Credit Societies:- Honouring of cheques with mistakes in Date or such other technical reasons  mentioned above should be passed only in case of cheques up to Rs.10,000/-. In case of cheques payable to MTNL/BSNL/BEST/MSEB/Tax Authorities and Govt./Semi Govt./Local Authorities etc the cheques may be honoured up to an amount of Rs.50,000/-. The decision in these cases is to be taken by the In-charge or other Manager or Accountant of the Branch.

   ii.        Whenever cheques are issued by Credit Societies in favour of individuals/ firms/companies, the In-charge of Branch/other Manager/Accountant is authorized to pass cheques received in CRC for the technical reason mentioned above.

  iii.        Whenever cheques are issued by Co-op. Housing Societies in favour of Municipal Corporation/Nagar Palika or other local bodies for payment of taxes/water bill/electricity bill etc., the same with above technical irregularities are to be passed by the In-charge/other Manager/Accountant.  The maximum limit of amount of cheque is Rs. 50,000/-.

  iv.        Such instances should not be of repetitive nature from the same accountholder.

   v.        The exercise of above powers is to be done subject to adequate and clear balance in the account.

  vi.        Branch In-charge should take full responsibility for honouring such instruments and has to obtain and keep on record, suitable request letter duly signed by the accountholder/authorized signatories if any within 7 days of honouring of the cheque.

vii.        The staff and officials of branch should contact the concerned accountholder on Telephone/Mobile to ascertain the genuineness and/or to deposit cash in the account to honour such cheques.

viii.        On a Public Holiday and CTS working day when the Branches are closed, CPC will have no option but to return these cheques.

The In-charge of branches and officials and staff in CPC Dept. are advised to implement the above instruction effective from 23.05.2016.  The Asst. Gen. Manager, Central Clearing Dept. is advised to take necessary steps in the matter.  The In-charge of branches should inform all officials and staff at branches about the above instructions and ensure that same are implemented to provide better services to the customers.

 

 

V.S. Morye

Managing Director
