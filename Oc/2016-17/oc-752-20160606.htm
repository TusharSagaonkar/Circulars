Circular OC No.752 Dated  6th  June, 2016

Monitoring of cash transactions in accounts maintained

by Credit Societies (Patpedhis) at branches

- Stoppage of cash payment to the customers/vendors of these societies

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          Please refer to Circular OC No. 558 dated 11.5.2011.  As per the guidelines of RBI, it was decided to open SB/CD a/c. of Co-op. Credit Societies and to collect A/c. Payee cheques drawn for an amount not exceeding Rs. 50,000/- in favour of customers of such Credit Societies. 

 

          Of late, it is observed that branches are allowing payment of cash even to the customers of Co-op. Credit Societies. The payment includes payment for disbursement of loans as well as payment of Term Deposits with Co-op. Credit Societies. RBI Circular as well as our Circular OC No. 558 dated 11.5.2011 do not permit the payment of cash to the customers/vendors of the Co-op. Credit Societies.  However, the payment to the society could be made if the cheques drawn are in order and payment is made to the Society through its authorized representative.

 

          The payment by the Bank of the matured Term Deposits to the customers of the Credit Societies is not allowed.  If the payment is of Rs. 20,000/- or more it would further amount to violation of provisions of section 269 (T) of I.T. Act. It will also amount to violation of RBI guidelines.  Similarly, the payment of loan disbursement to the loanees of Co-op. Credit Societies by the Bank could be treated as violation of RBI’s AML directives.    

 

          In view of the above, it has been decided that cash withdrawal in Co-op. Credit Societies account could be allowed for their own purposes only.  The cash payment for payment of loan disbursement and payment of matured FDs should not be allowed to their customers since 20.6.2016.  The cash payment to the society should be made to the authorized person of the society.

 

          As some Branches have extended the above facilities to the Co-op Credit Societies, branches are now advised to send letters as per enclosed format to all their Co-op. Credit Societies accountholders. The letters should be sent by Speed Post/Hand Delivery by 8.6.2016.  The Incharge of branches are also advised to personally visit the Co-op. Credit Societies who are satisfactorily operating Deposit accounts and maintaining FDRs and apprise them that on account of RBI and  FIU-IND treating such cash payment as violation of their guidelines, cash payment would not be allowed in future.

 

          The Incharge of branches are advised to take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all staff members/officials particularly the Head Cashier and other cashiers.

 

 

V.S. Morye

Managing Director
