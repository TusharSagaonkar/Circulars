Circular OC No. 756 dated 31st August, 2016

Facility for Exchange of Soiled/Mutilated/Imperfect Notes

__________________________________________________________

 

Please refer to Office Order No. 361 dated 21st December, 2010, Circular OC No. 630 dated 28th May, 2013 and Circular OC No. 639 dated 22nd August, 2013 wherein detailed guidelines were issued in regard to ‘Clean Note Policy’ and ‘Facility for Exchange of Soiled/Mutilated/Imperfect Note’ to be followed by branches, CCDs and at Head Office.

Reserve Bank of India vide Master Circular No. DCM(NE) No.G-1/08.07.18/2016-17 dated 18th July, 2016 has issued Latest guidelines on abovementioned subject. The said circular was placed before Board Meeting held on 29th July, 2016 and it has been decided to implement the same at all our branches, CCDs and at Head office.

          Detailed guidelines to be observed in this regard are as follows:-

 

1.   Clean Note Policy

1.1  Objective

You are aware that objective of the Reserve Bank of India Clean Note Policy is to give the customers good quality currency notes and coins while soiled notes are withdrawn out of circulation. Thus acceptance of soiled notes from our customers and to provide them clean issuable / fresh notes would go a long way in improvement of customer service and customer satisfaction.

 

1.2  Examination of Bank Notes :

It has been observed that cashiers on Cash Counters at our Branches do not sort notes into re-issuable and non-issuable i.e. Soiled notes, Pre-2005 Series Banknotes etc. It should therefore be ensured that while accepting, verifying and counting the cash at the branch counter / CCDs, it is shroffed and sorted out into 3 categories viz. (i) Issuable, (ii) Non-issuable i.e. Soiled, Pre-2005 Series of Banknotes and (iii) Counterfeit notes. The CCDs and the branches which have been equipped with Note Sorting Machine(s) should necessarily process all the notes received especially Rs.100/- and above denominations. Only issuable/fresh notes should be issued over the counters to the customers at all branches. CCDs should remit only issuable / fresh notes to branches. While loading cash in ATM(s), branches should note that only fresh/issuable notes are feeded in the ATM(s). It must be ensured that in no case, the counterfeit/soiled notes, Pre-2005 Series Banknotes are mixed up, even inadvertently, in the cash issued to the customers or feeded in ATMs and while sending the remittance by CCDs to branches. If such notes are found in the packets of any denomination delivered to the customers or found in the remittance sent by the CCD(s) to the branches, the concerned Cashier & Head Cashier will be held responsible for any penalty charged by Reserve Bank of India, and in addition to this, Bank will initiate appropriate action against these employees.

In respect of detection and reporting of Counterfeit Notes detailed guidelines are issued Vide our Office Order No. 484 dated 22nd August, 2016.

 

1.3  Customer Service :

(i)      All our branches should provide the following customer services, more actively and vigorously to the members of public including our customers without any discrimination on all working days.

(a)    Meeting the demands of our customers for good quality notes and coins of all denominations while withdrawing money from their accounts by Cheque/withdrawal slip.

(b)    Exchange of Soiled, Mutilated, Defective Notes.

(c)     Provision of good quality Notes at ATMs.

(d)    Accepting Coins and notes including soiled notes of any denomination on counters from general public including our customers for exchange or for credit of customers' accounts maintained at the branch.

(e)    None of the concerned staff member should refuse to accept small denomination notes and / or coins tendered by a member of public or customer at the counters either for exchange or for credit of customers' accounts.

(f)      The availability of the above-mentioned facilities in our branches should be given wide publicity for information of the public at large.

 

 

2.   Liberalized definition of Soiled Notes

In order to facilitate quicker exchange facilities, the definition of soiled note has been liberalized. A ‘soiled note’ means a note which has become dirty due to normal wear

and tear and also includes a two piece note pasted together wherein both the pieces presented belong to the same note and form the entire note with no essential feature missing. These notes should be accepted over bank counters for credit to accounts of the public maintained with bank.

However, in no case, these notes should be issued to the public as re-issuable notes and shall be deposited in currency chests for onward transmission to RBI offices as soiled note remittances for further processing.

 

3.   Exchange of Soiled Notes/Cut Notes:-

In order to facilitate excellent customer service, the following types of soiled and cut notes should be freely accepted from the members of public including our customers by all our branches for exchange or for credit of customers' accounts. Maximum precautions should be taken to ensure that these notes presented for exchange or for crediting the amount for customers' accounts are not counterfeit notes.

 

 3.1 (a) Single Numbered Notes – Re. 1/- Rs.2/- & Rs.5/-

 Note presented should not be in more than two pieces. No essential feature of the note should be missing. Both the pieces should be of the same note. Number of note should be intact.

 

 (b) Double numbered notes – Rs.10/-, Rs.20/-, Rs.50/-, Rs.100/-

 Rs.500/- & Rs.1000/-

  The note presented should not be in more than two pieces. No essential feature of the note should be missing. Both the pieces should be of the same note. Number on both the places should be tallied and should be intact.

 

3.2  The above types of accepted notes should be carefully pasted with brown tape and not with plastic tape. These notes will be treated as soiled notes and be kept along with other soiled notes.

 

3.3  These unfit notes shall, in no case, be issued to the public as reissuable notes but shall be remitted to Head office.

 

3.4  Writing number, name or any other sign on notes by tellers, cashiers, Head Cashiers is not allowed. It is against the Clean Note Policy of Reserve Bank of India. It disfigures the watermark impression and other features of the note and renders it difficult for easy recognition. Branch-in-charge should endeavour to educate staff, customers in this regard. Under the present system of mechanized processing of banknotes, inscription or scribbling on any part of the banknote would render it to be classified as unfit for reissue.

 

3.5  Preparation of separate packets (100 pieces) of soiled notes with specially marked Note Slip in Red colour.

(a)Branches / CCDs should prepare separate packets (100 pieces) covered with Red Coloured Note Slip for soiled notes.

 

(b)    The Stationery Dept. should make necessary arrangements to provide the Red Coloured Note Slips to all branches / CCDs to be used for preparing soiled note packets.

 

(c) Branches should remit the complete packet containing 100 pieces upto the denomination of Rs.100/- and not in loose pieces to CCD(s) except on Half yearly and yearly closing days. CCDs in turn will remit these soiled note packets to Currency Chest branch as per arrangement.

 

4.   Exchange of Mutilated Notes

A mutilated note is a note of which a portion is missing or which is composed of more than two pieces. Reserve Bank of India has permitted Bank to exchange mutilated currency notes which are genuine and where mutilations are such as not to cause suspicion or fraud. Refund value of these notes is, however, paid as per RBI (Note refund) Rules 2009. In this regard branches are advised as follows:

·        On acceptance of mutilated notes for refund, receipt is to be issued to tenderer and it should be recorded in a separate register.

·        Branches should send such mutilated notes to CCD Head Office for adjudication in accordance with Reserve Bank of India (Note Refund) Rules, 2009.

·        CCD Head Office will present such mutilated notes to currency chest and it is expected that credit of exchange value will be received within 30 days of its presentation/acceptance by currency chest.

 

Reserve Bank of India (Note Refund) Rules, 2009 are enclosed herewith for knowledge of our concerned staff / officials at Branches / Departments. (Annexure – I).

 

5. Non-acceptable Notes :

Following types of currency notes should not be accepted by Branches/ CCDs.

 

 

5.1 Extremely brittle, burnt, charred, stuck up Notes

Notes which have turned extremely brittle or badly burnt, charred or inseparably stuck up together and therefore, cannot withstand normal handling, shall not be accepted by the branches from the customers. Instead, the customers holding such notes may be advised to tender these notes to the concerned Issue Office of Reserve Bank of India.

 

5.2 Notes bearing slogans / political messages, etc.

Any note with slogans and message of a political nature written across it ceases to be a legal tender and the claim on such a note will be rejected under Rule 6(3)(iii) of Reserve Bank of India (Note Refund) Rules, 2009 and such notes shall not be accepted at branches. Similarly, notes which are disfigured may also be rejected under Rule 6(3)(iii) of Reserve Bank of India (Note Refund) Rules, 2009.

 

 5.3 Deliberately cut notes

The notes, which are found to be deliberately cut or tampered with, if presented should be rejected under Rule 6(3)(ii) of the Reserve Bank of India (Note Refund) Rules, 2009. Although it is not possible to precisely define deliberately cut, a close look at such notes will clearly reveal any deliberate fraudulent intention, as the manner in which such notes are mutilated will follow a broad uniformity in the shape / location of missing portions of the notes, especially when the notes are tendered in large numbers. The details of the case such as the name of the tenderer, number of notes tendered and their denominations should be reported thereafter to the Deputy/General Manager, Issue Department, under whose jurisdiction the branch falls. The matter should also be reported to local police in case a large number of such notes are tendered.

 

6. Uncurrent Coins

The coins of denomination of 25 paise and below, issued from time to time, ceased to be legal tender for payments with effect from June 30, 2011 in terms of Gazette Notification No.2529 dated December 20, 2010 issued by the Government of India.

 

7. Display of Notice Board

All branches are required to display at their premises, at a prominent place, a board indicating the availability of note exchange facility with the legend, "SOILED/MUTILATED NOTES ARE ACCEPTED AND EXCHANGED HERE" for information of general public. Branches should provide facility for exchange of notes and coins not only to their customers but also others. However, they should ensure that the note exchange facility is not cornered by money changers / dealers in defective notes.

In addition to above, Citizen Charter for notes and coins should be displayed on the notice Board (Annexure II).

 

8. Monitoring and Control

(a) Asst. General Manager, Vigilance Department, Nodal Officers at Zones, Regional Offices designated as per Office Order no. 484 dated 22nd August, 2016 may pay surprise visits to the branches and report the position of compliance in this regard to Head Office. Cash Department at Head Office will review such reports and take prompt remedial action, wherever necessary.

(b) Any non-compliance in this regard shall be violation of instructions issued by the Reserve Bank of India.

 

The Internal inspecting officials/concurrent auditors, during the course of inspection should verify adherence of the above instructions by the branches & note their observations in their inspection reports. In case of any violation, special report in that regard should be submitted and placed before Board.

 

9. Development department should ensure that Counterfeit/soiled note detection machines are provided to Branches, CCDs to segregate issuable and soiled notes in terms of above instructions.

 

10. Training of staff:-

Principal of staff training college is advised to arrange multiple one day Crash Programmes on the Security features of banknotes, Cash Management, facility for exchange of Soiled, Mutilated, Imperfect notes as per guidelines contained in Reserve Bank of India (Note Refund) Rules, 2009. If necessary he should take assistance of the officers from the nearest issue office of RBI.

 

Kindly bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members/officials working in Cash Dept. at branches/CCDs, for achieving the objectives of Clean Note Policy and complying with the guidelines issued by Reserve Bank of India in respect of Facility for Exchange of Soiled/Mutilated/Imperfect Notes.

 

 

 

P. R. Shetty

Managing Director
