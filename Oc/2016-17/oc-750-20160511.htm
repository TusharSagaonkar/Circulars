As a Corporate Insurance Agent our Bank is offering Life Insurance services through Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) and General Insurance Services through The New India Assurance Company Ltd. (NIAC). Bank has always aimed at providing best in class financial services products to its customers. Given the growing need of health insurance and to extend these services to our customers, under the new Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDA), (Regulations of Corporate Agents) Regulations 2015, our Bank’s Board in its meeting held on 15th February, 2016 has decided to enter into an agreement with M/s Religare Health Insurance Company Ltd. (RHICL), which is a standalone health insurance company, engaged in the distribution & servicing of health insurance products. Health Insurance is a strong need of the hour, especially given its low market penetration levels. With growing healthcare costs and rising medical inflation, the Bank should strive to extend the services of health insurance to all its customers. The Health Insurance industry has been consistently growing at 25% every year over last 10 years and gives our Bank an opportunity to take advantage of the growing market, with relevant and customer friendly products offered by Religare Health Insurance. Religare Health Insurance products will be made available to customers through our Bank branches w.e.f.16th May, 2016.

 

2. Company Profile – Religare Health Insurance

M/s. Religare Health Insurance Company Limited (RHICL) is a prominent player in the Standalone Health Insurance market and comes from a strong background of Healthcare as well as Financial Services. Promoters of Religare Health Insurance have dominant presence in healthcare services including diagnostics and wellness through below mentioned companies:

B.ASSURE – Retail Critical Illness and Personal Accident Product for Loan

 Customers

Assure is a comprehensive benefit plan that takes care of 15 Critical Illnesses along with a Personal Accident cover. It presents some unique offerings for the customers forming the asset base of Banks. This product is designed specifically keeping in mind the customer’s health risks and Bank’s financial risk in the event of any unforeseen health conditions encountered by the customer. The process of attachment of Religare Products to Bank’s Loan customers can be enabled by funding the premium basis customer request letter and thus driving higher attachment rates. This is an effective way to help the customer meet the need of cash at the time of loan disbursal.

 

Loans are one of the biggest financial responsibilities of an individual and failure to fulfill this causes tremendous emotional trauma to the individual and his family while at the same time increases the NPAs of the lender / Bank.

Given the rise in incidence rate of life threatening diseases which alter the lifestyle of the individual and at most times also diminishes his or her ability to earn, it is important for an individual to secure his loan in event of diagnosis of any of the given Critical Illnesses or Accidental Death or Permanent Total Disablement.

The Key Benefits - Coverage in Loan Attachment Plan of Assure:

§  Customers can be offered the product at the time of sanction or first disbursal of the loan for a Sum Insured equal to the loan amount where the entire Sum Insured is paid on diagnosis without any bills.

§  The cover for Critical Illness or Accidental Death and Permanent Total Disability are strongly relevant for Banks because in the current health scenario, the mortality rates are getting lower but morbidity rates are on the rise.

§  Annual Health Check-up

§  Second Opinion once in every year

§  HIV cover up to Rs. 25 Lac Sum Insured

C. SECURE – Comprehensive Personal Accident Product

A Benefit Plan suitable for all customer segments covering not only Death, but also disability, Hospitalization etc. arising due to accident. Accidents are a reality of life. An individual is exposed to risk of accidents practically everywhere. Even at home there are many potential avenues where an accident might occur.

 

Secure - Key Product Features:

To provide protection against the hardships due to any above mentioned eventuality, Religare Health Insurance’s comprehensive Personal Accident product – ‘Secure’ is an ideal solution since it goes much beyond the current realm of Personal Accident coverage and offers comprehensive benefits with each risk in mind.

§  Accidental Death

§  Permanent Total Disablement (PTD)

§  Permanent Partial Disablement

§  Reconstructive Surgery

-    In case of severe accidents, surgery may be required to rejoin a severed or a separated body part and such surgeries are highly expensive. This benefit provides for such surgeries with a much higher indemnity limit.

§  Fractures - In case of complex fractures, a lump sum benefit is provided to the customer as per benefit table

§  Burns - In case of complex severe burns, a lump sum benefit is provided to the customer as per benefit table

§  Major Diagnostic tests - Reimbursement for the expenses incurred for carrying out any major diagnostic tests like CT Scan, MRI etc. consequent to an injury and are undertaken on written advice of a medical practitioner and are conducted with in 3 months of occurrence of injury.

§  Disappearance - In the event that an accident leaves the insured missing (forced landing, stranding, sinking, wrecking, etc.) for more than 1 year and the insured's demise has been reasonably established, sum insured will be paid to the nominee

§  Domestic Road Ambulance - Ambulance can be a life saver in emergency situations. It significantly increases the chances of survival and ensures timely treatment

§  Nursing Care - If a nurse is prescribed by the doctor to recover at home, the expenses are paid under this benefit

§  Mobility Cover - In the event of need for prosthetics, the cost of the same is covered

§  Child Education Benefit - A lump sum is paid for the child’s education in the event of death or permanent total disability of the covered parent

§  Add-on Package: Accidental Hospitalization

-          Hospitalization Expenses: Provides indemnity cover for hospitalizations or day care surgeries arising out of accidents

-          Compassionate Visit: If an accidental hospitalization has occurred out of home, this benefit pays for travel of an immediate family member from home location to the location of treatment to take care of the insured

-          Daily Allowance

4. Procedural Guidelines for Procuring Health Insurance Business:

§  Field Staff Support by Religare Health Insurance Company – RHICL will deploy manpower (called Insurance Officer) to our branches who will assist the Bank staff in soliciting health insurance business. Each Insurance Officer will be mapped to 5 to 6 branches of our Bank and he / she will assist in conversion of health insurance business from these allocated branches by working on the leads provided by the branch staff. Branch officials / staff should inform the customer about our Bank being a Corporate Agent of Religare Health Insurance and pass on the lead to the concerned Insurance Officer.

Branches should maintain lead register at their end, which will be updated by the Insurance Officer for all leads received by him from a branch and talk to these customers for conversion by properly explaining the need for health insurance and providing a suitable solution from Religare Health Insurance. The status of the lead register shall be updated on weekly basis and the same shall be shared with the branches to monitor the business. The Specified Person (SP) who has passed the IRDA examination on Life Insurance services at the branches / departments is competent authority to sign on the Health Insurance proposal application form.

The Manpower allocation matrix to branches will be communicated to all branches by the Insurance department on the exchange server.

5. Detailed Claim Process

 

Cashless Treatment at Network Hospitals Process Flow:


Step 1 : Claim Intimation

·         In case of an emergency hospitalization, policy holder should call and inform RHICL at 1800-200-4488 within 24 hours of admission. However, if the hospitalization is planned, policy holder should intimate Religare Health within 48 hours prior to his / her admission by calling on the same number or writing to RHICL at customerfirst@religarehealthinsurance.com

Step 2 : Initiating the process for Pre-Authorization

·         A Pre-Authorization form will be available at the hospital's Insurance / TPA desk

·         Insured person has to fill the first section of the form by giving their personal details and hand over signed Pre-Authorization form to hospital's Insurance / TPA desk, for them to fill up the balance details.

·         Hospital will fax the completed Pre-Authorization form to Religare Health at 1800-200-6677.

Step 3 : Processing a request for Pre-Authorization

·         Religare in-house medical team will review the case and documents submitted by hospital.

·         If the request for Pre-Authorization is approved, customer and the hospital will be duly informed.

·         In case of any information deficiency or further information requirement, customer and the hospital will be regularly intimated to ensure resolution of the same at the earliest.

·         If the request for Pre-Authorization is not approved, it only indicates that RHICL is not able to process the customer’s request based on the requisite information available with them at that point of time. In such cases, the customer may claim for reimbursement of expenses after discharge from the hospital.

Reimbursement of treatment expenses 


Step 1 : Claim Intimation

In case of emergency, customer should call and inform RHICL within 24 hours of his admission. However, if the hospitalization is planned, customer should intimate RHICL, 48 hours prior to admission. The following information is to be provided during the claim intimation-

·         Policy Holder's Name.

·         Claimant's Name & Customer ID.

·         Hospital details.

·         Diagnosis and Treatment details.

·         Approximate claim amount.

·         Date of admission

RHICL will provide a reference ID for all future communication pertaining to the claim request.

 

Step 2 : Initiating Claim process (also applicable for Pre/Post Hospitalization claims)
The Claim form can be downloaded from RHICL web-site.


The completed and duly signed claim form has to be sent to RHICL along with the following documents -

·         Duly completed and signed Claim form, in original

·         Valid photo-id proof

·         Medical practitioner's referral letter advising Hospitalization

·         Medical practitioner's prescription advising drugs / diagnostic tests / consultation

·         Original bills, receipts and Discharge card from the Hospital / Medical Practitioner

·         Original bills from pharmacy / chemists

·         Original pathological/diagnostic tests reports/radiology reports and payment

 receipts

·         Indoor case papers

·         First information Report, final police report, if applicable

·         Post mortem report, if conducted

·         Any other document as required by the company to assess the claim

Step 3 : Claim Processing and Reimbursement

·         RHICL In-house medical team will review the case and documents submitted by the customer.

·         If the request for reimbursement of expenses is approved, customer will be duly intimated by RHICL.

·         In case of any information deficiency or further information requirements, customer will be communicated instantly to ensure resolution of the same at the earliest.

·         If the request for claims is declined, customer will be communicated the same along with valid reason(s) for rejection. However, if the insured/ insured's representative has further documents to enhance/substantiate his case for claim, the same can also be sent to the company; and if found rational, the case will be reopened for review of the documents and response, if any.

·         RHICL will ensure that the customers are updated at all important stages of the claim process. To help serve the customers better, please ensure the following-

The Pre-Authorization / Claim form is filled completely, sincerely and truly and all the required documents are submitted along with the form in original, wherever specified. Retain a copy of the duly filled form and documents sent to the Insurance company.

 

RHICL will provide a reference ID for all communication pertaining to claim request.

 

Customer should quote that reference number for all communication related to the claim. The claim form and the original documents can be submitted directly to RHICL local office or through Insurance Officer assigned to the branch or directly to:

6. Role of Branches :

 

i.     Branches should route the new as well as renewal business directly to RHICL through the field officer of RHICL deputed to them.

ii.   All branches, including out station branches, will be attached to nearest office of the RHICL. This list will be made available on exchange server in Insurance Folder by the Insurance department.

iii.  Insurance Department, Vashi has advised RHICL to inform their offices to extend full support to our branches for smooth functioning of Health Insurance work. Branch in-charges are advised to keep close rapport with officials of designated branches of RHICL.

iv.  Branches have to maintain record of all leads; Policy & Claim details provided to RHICL and update the same on daily basis in the pre-printed register provided by Insurance Department. Details of total business marketed under each category of Insurance Product is to be forwarded to Insurance Department for maintaining consolidated record at their end.

v.   Claims of the customers will also be forwarded directly to RHICL office through the field officer of RHICL allotted to the branch. Customer should be advised to retain Xerox copies of the documents before submission of claim form.

 

Procedural aspects :

 

a.   Commonly used stationery and proposal forms for Health insurance products will be made available by RHICL to branches. Branches are requested to maintain sufficient stock of proposal forms. Please note that photocopies of these forms are not accepted by the Offices of RHICL.

b.   Proposal Forms, to be submitted to RHICL should be legible & it should be filled preferably in CAPITAL LETTERS to enable the RHICL to feed the correct data at their end. Feeding of wrong data by RHICL, due to illegible handwriting may result in repudiation of legitimate claims, for which the concerned branch official will be held responsible.

c.    Branches should collect the pay in slip, specially designed for premium collection, for Religare Health Insurance to avoid delay. These slips will be made available to branches by the Insurance department. Branches can also collect crossed cheque / P.O / DD in favour of “Religare Health Insurance Company Limited" duly signed by the proposer.

d.   Premium shall be calculated as per templates to be provided to branches for different kinds of policies. All branches shall send the proposals directly to the designated offices of RHICL on the same day or latest on the next working day.

e.   Policy documents will be directly delivered to the customer’s address mentioned on the proposal form.

 

7. Role of Insurance Department, Vashi:

 

a.  Insurance Department will act as a coordinator between RHICL & branches for providing necessary support for procuring new as well as renewal of policies, Marketing support, early claim settlement etc.

b.  Will provide preprinted lead, issue & claim register to branches for proper maintenance of record.

c.  Guide branches / customer and help them in resolving their queries.

d.  Shall make arrangement for imparting zone-wise training to In-charge of the branches and concerned staff members handling Insurance business at branches with the help of officials from RHICL & staff training college.

e.  Will transfer the commission received from RHICL to the branches on monthly basis, along with the MIS on branch wise business performance.

f.   As and when new products are introduced or changes are made in the existing products by RHICL the same will be communicated to branches by Insurance department.

 

 8. Role of Marketing Dept

 Bank’s Marketing Department shall provide  adequate marketing material at all our branches. The sales support material  shall comprise of posters, banners, danglers, standees and leaflets which will  be made available at all branches. For additional marketing material branch  staff may contact Insurance Department.

 

9. Role of Credit Department, Head Office :

In association with the Insurance department and RHICL will design specific products for different loan types considering the need of the customers. The same will be rolled out through branches.

 

10. Role of I.T. Department :

     Will ensure that the premium calculator provided by RHICL for the health insurance products is made available through system at all our branches.
