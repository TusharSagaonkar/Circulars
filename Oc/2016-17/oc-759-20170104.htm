Circular OC No. 759 dated 4th January, 2017

 

Implementation of Value Added Services in ATMs

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

I.  National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) has introduced Value Added Services (VAS) such as (1) Card to Card Payment, (2) Cheque Book Request, (3) Statement Request, (4) Mobile Banking Registration and (5) Aadhar Number Seeding, to be made available to the customers at ATMs through National Financial Switch (NFS) network. In the Board Meeting dated 16th December, 2016, it was decided to implement these services in our Bank.

 

II.          These services are interoperable i.e. our customer having Rupay Debit Card can use our own Bank’s ATM or any other Bank’s ATM under NFS Network, for availing the value added services as above. Short description of the above services is given below:

1. Card to Card Payment (C2C): Through this service, Debit Card Holders of our Bank can transfer money to any other debit card holder at our own Bank’s ATM or other Bank’s ATM under NFS Network. Under this, our Debit Card Colder customer has to know only the debit card number of the beneficiary. Maximum limit for fund transfer under this value added service is Rs 5000/-.

2. Cheque Book Request: Through this option, our Debit Card Holder customer will be able to register a request for issuance of cheque book at our own Bank’s ATM or any other Bank’s ATM under NFS Network.

3. Statement Request: Through this option, a customer will be able to register a request for statement of account at our own Bank’s ATM or any other Bank’s ATM under NFS Network.

4. Mobile Banking Registration: Through this option, a customer will be able to register a request for Mobile Banking. At the time of registration, system will validate the mobile number with the mobile number existing in CBS. If the validation goes through, the system itself will accept the request.

5. Aadhar Number Seeding: Through this option, a customer will be able to register request for seeding of his/her Aadhar Number for Direct Benefit transfer (DBT) i.e. for crediting subsidies declared by various Central/State Government authorities under Govt. schemes . However, Bank will have to validate the Aadhar number with UIDAI before seeding the Aadhar number.

 

III. 1. We have tested all the options as above. However, initially, we are implementing only following services for the use of our Debit Card Holder customers:

                            i.   Cheque Book Request

                           ii.   Mobile Banking Registration

                         iii.   Card to Card Payment

 

2. After implementation as above, branches will have to follow the procedures as under on daily basis.

 

i.   Cheque Book Request:

·      The request received through this option will be stored separately in the system. Branches will be able to generate the list of customers who have requested for cheque book through ATMs. In this scenario, Branches are advised to note that there will not be any physical cheque-book-requisition slip. Branches have to create Cheque Book Master and keep the cheque book ready at the branches on the basis of report generated through the system alone.

·      The Branches will get the report in OMNI 3.0 in the below mentioned Menu.

Report è ATM/Rupay/POS è Cheque Book Request

·      In this case, Debit Card and PIN constitute the authentication. In fact, at the time of entering the request at ATMs by a customer, a message will be displayed to collect cheque book from the branch.

ii.  Mobile Banking Registration:

·      At present, customers have to visit branches personally for Registration of Mobile Banking Services for filling up the registration form. However, in case of request through an ATM, there will be no need for any written confirmation from the customer in this behalf. A customer will be able to make a request for Mobile Banking Services at either our own Bank’s ATM or at any other Bank’s ATM under NFS Network, only through Debit Card & PIN authentication.

·      In this scenario, all requests will be stored separately in a table and a report will be made available to branches. On the basis of this report, branches have to carry out Mobile Banking Registration. However, before carrying out Mobile Banking Registration, branches will have to check proper KYC and availability of PAN of the customers. Branches are advised to note that PAN is must for Mobile Banking Registration.

·  The report will be available in OMNI 3.0 in the below mentioned Menu.

Report è ATM/Rupay/POS è MBR Request

 

iii.  Card to Card Transactions:

·      Vouchers of Card to Card transactions will be generated in the following self authorized batch.

 

1)ATMTRF-1

2)ATMTRF-2

3)     ABBTR-P

4)ABBTR-P2

 

IV. In case of any further clarification or guidance in regard to Value Added Services (VAS,) you are advised to contact IT Dept.

 

V. Marketing Dept. is advised to take all necessary steps to popularise the Value Added Services (VAS) among our existing as well as new customers.

 

VI. Branches are advised to take suitable note of the above instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all Officials/Staff Members at Branches.

 

 

 

P. R. Shetty

Managing Director
