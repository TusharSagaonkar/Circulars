Circular OC. No. 748 dated 6th April, 2016

Resignation of Membership

Procedure to be adopted at Branches and Share Department

 

We have issued various instructions/guidelines in respect of the procedure of acceptance, verifications and placing before the Board Meeting the Resignations of Members of the Bank. The provisions laid down in the Byelaws of the Bank mentioned (in Point No. 10) for withdrawal from Membership are :

a)       A member may withdraw and resign his membership after one year and giving at least 3 months’ notice in writing and withdraw his share capital with the approval of the Board of Directors. The approval shall not be given while such a member is indebted to the Bank either as a borrower or as a surety. During any co-operative year, the aggregate withdrawals shall not exceed 10% of the total paid-up share capital as at 31st March of the preceding year.

b)      A member who withdraws and resigns his membership will not be allowed to become a member again for the period of one year from the date of his resignation.

Thus it should be ensured that :

1.       A Member may withdraw and resign his/her Membership only after one year of Membership.

2.       A Member who wants withdrawal from Membership should not be indebted to the Bank either as a Borrower or as a Surety.

3.       Withdrawal of part amount of Share Amount is not allowed as Bye-Laws do not permit the same.

4.       While accepting Share Resignation he/she should be guided that after resignation & only after receiving Share Money he/she cannot become Member again for a period of one year from the date of his/her resignation.

It is also observed that branches do not follow the uniform procedure for finalisation of acceptance of the resignation of shareholder causing delay and inconvenience to them.  In order to streamline the finalization of resignation of membership number and expeditious payment of share amount to them, branches are advised to strictly adhere to the following guidelines.

1.       Share Resignation forms should be accepted at Base Branch or at Share Department.

2.       Acceptance of Share Resignation Form, with Share Certificate and Identity Card, Verification of Signature, Scrutiny of Form, Issuance of Acknowledgment etc is to be done by the Asst. Accountant/Accountant or Branch In-charge and all queries raised by the Member/Shareholder are to be resolved by them by guiding them politely.

3.       Not to accept incomplete forms in any nature i.e. forms without duly attached Certificates, Signature not tallied as per our record, Share Membership not more than one year old, form submitted for withdrawal of partly Shares, in case of deceased claim form, Share Resignation form [Code No. 70 (a)] should be attached with supporting documents etc.

4.       On acceptance of Form, it should be acknowledged by any Officials of the above and be entered in separate register to be maintained at Branch/Share Department by mentioning Date/Time of acceptance, and Signature of Official accepting the Form. Format of Register is attached herewith.

5.       The contact details of Share Department are to be given to person resigning the Shares and inform him that in case of any further query or enquiry he/she should contact directly to Share Department to avoid miscommunication and maintain uniformity in passing on the information to Shareholders.

6.       The accepted forms [Code No. 70 & 70(a)] completed in all respect should be forwarded immediately on the same day to Share Department.

7.       On receipt of form at Share Department it should be scrutinized immediately and in case of any query or observance of incomplete form details, should be returned back to Branch and Branch Official should contact the said Member immediately to resolve the query and to avoid any complication/dispute in future.

8.       After scrutiny at Share Department it should be recorded serially at their end in Inward Register. Format of Register is attached herewith.

9.       Hard copy of Liability Report to be generated at Share Department on the day of recording Share Resignation form in their Inward Register and should be forwarded to respective Branch as well as at Recovery Department on the same day for checking Direct/Indirect Liability of Member.

10.   Respective Branch and Recovery Department should check the Direct/Indirect Liability as per their record and forward the Liability Report on the same day of its receipt to Share Department.

11.   On completion of all necessary formalities, the accepted Share Resignation forms are to be placed by Share Department before ensuing Board Meeting for approval on First In First Out basis.

12.   On approval of Share Resignation in the Board Meeting, the Share Amount is to be credited to Members Account or paid by issuance of Pay Order as per mandate given by Member at the time of submission of Share Resignation form.

13.   Branches, Share Department, Recovery Department should observe all above guidelines scrupulously to avoid inconvenience to Members/Shareholders. The In-charge of Branches and Share Department should appraise their staff members of the above procedure so that expedious service to Share Holders is extended.

 

 

 

V.S. Morye

Managing Director
