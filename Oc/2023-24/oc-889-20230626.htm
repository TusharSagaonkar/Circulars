Circular OC No. 889 dated 26th June 2023

Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016-Amendments

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.    Introduction

Reserve Bank of India vide the Master Direction-Know Your Customer (KYC), 2016 bearing ref. no. DBR.AML.BC.No.81/14.01.001/2015-16 dated the 28th April 2023 has communicated its updated version incorporating the changes/amendments therein to date for implementation by all the Reporting Entities (REs) including our Bank. The revised Master Direction also covers all the latest applicable amendments to the Prevention of Money-Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA) and rules framed thereunder for Preventing Money Laundering (ML) and Countering Financing of Terrorism (CFT).

 

II.  The measures for Customer Due Diligence (CDD) as updated through the Master Direction for some categories of non-individual customers are as under.

(Note:- As all the sub-Paras below refer to Section 16 of the Master Direction which essentially pertains to the Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Procedure in the case of Individuals, parts thereof which are applicable in our Bank are given in the Annexure to the instant circular).

 

1.     Companies

The documents that need to be obtained: -

i.     Certificate of incorporation

ii.    Memorandum and Articles of Association

iii.   Permanent Account Number of the company

iv.  A resolution from the Board of Directors and Power of Attorney granted to its managers, officers or employees to transact on its behalf

v.   Documents which otherwise apply to individuals being beneficial owners as specified in Section 16 of the Master Direction, relating to the managers, officers or employees, as the case may be, holding an attorney to transact on the company’s behalf

vi. The names of the relevant persons holding senior management positions

vii.        The registered office and the principal place of its business, if it is different

 

2.  Partnership firms

The documents that need to be obtained: -

i.     Registration certificate

ii.   Partnership deed

iii.  Permanent Account Number of the partnership firm

iv. Documents which otherwise apply to individuals being beneficial owners as specified in Section 16 of the Master Direction, relating to managers, officers or employees, as the case may be, holding an attorney to transact on its behalf

v.   The names of all the partners

vi. Address of the registered office, and the principal place of its business, if it is different.

 

 

3.  Trusts

The documents that need to be obtained: -

i.     Registration certificate

ii.   Trust deed

iii.  Permanent Account Number or Form No.60 of the trust

iv. Documents which otherwise apply to individuals being beneficial owners as specified in Section 16 of the Master Direction, relating to managers, officers or employees, as the case may be, holding an attorney to transact on its behalf

v.   The names of the beneficiaries, trustees, settlor and authors of the trust

vi. The address of the registered office of the trust

vii.        List of trustees, and documents which otherwise apply to individuals being beneficial owners as specified in Section 16 of the Master Direction, for those discharging the role as trustee and authorised to transact on behalf of the trust.

 

4.  An unincorporated association or a body of individuals

 In respect of the above, certified copies of each of the following documents or the equivalent e-documents thereof shall be obtained :-

i.     Resolution of the managing body of such association or body of individuals

ii.   Permanent Account Number or Form No.60 of the unincorporated association or a body of individuals

iii.  Power of attorney granted to transact on its behalf

iv. Documents which otherwise apply to individuals being beneficial owners as specified in Section 16 of the Master Direction, relating to managers, officers or employees, as the case may be, holding an attorney to transact on its behalf

v.   Such information as may be required by the RE to collectively establish the legal existence of such an association or body of individuals.

Explanation-A: Unregistered trusts/partnership firms shall be included under the term ‘unincorporated association’.

Explanation-B: The term ‘body of individuals’ includes societies.

 

5.  Sole Proprietary Firms -

Section 28 of the Master Direction has been amended to clarify that “Registration certificate” as a proof of business/ activity in the name of the proprietary firm includes “Udyam Registration Certificate (URC) issued by the Government”.

 

III. Updation/Periodic Updation of KYC

1.   Aadhaar OTP-based e-KYC in non-face-to-face mode has been permitted to be used for periodic updation. Declaration of current address, if the current address is different from the address in Aadhaar, shall not require positive confirmation in this case. The staff handling such updation must, however, ensure that the mobile number for Aadhaar authentication is the same as the one available with the Bank in the customer’s profile, in order to prevent any fraud.

2.   Instructions on the obligation of customers in terms of the requirements of PML Rules have been inserted which require the Bank (as also all the REs) to advise the customers that in order to comply with the PML Rules, in case of any update in the documents submitted by the customer at the time of establishment of a business relationship / account-based relationship and thereafter, as necessary, customers shall submit to the Bank the update of such documents. This shall be done within 30 days of the change in the documents for the purpose of updating the records at the Bank’s end.

 

IV. Shell Banks

 The definition of ‘Shell Bank’ has been amended to read as under –

“Shell bank means a bank that has no physical presence in the country in which it is incorporated and licensed, and which is unaffiliated with a regulated financial group that is subject to effective consolidated supervision. Physical presence means meaningful mind and management located within a country. The existence simply of a local agent or low-level staff does not constitute physical presence”.

 

 V. Correspondent Banking

The instructions regarding correspondent banking relationships have been aligned with international standards. Banks are required to ensure that prior approval of the senior management is obtained for establishing new correspondent banking relationships. Banks shall use the publicly available information which, inter alia, should include (1) information relating to the reputation of the respondent institution (2) the quality of supervision in the concerned jurisdiction and (3) whether the respondent institution has been subjected to any investigation or regulatory action relating to Money Laundering / Terrorist Financing. Further, definitions of ‘correspondent banking’ and ‘payable-through accounts’ have been added.

 

VI. Customer Acceptance Policy

1.   Where the Bank is suspicious of money laundering or terrorist financing, and it reasonably believes that performing the CDD process will tip off the customer, it shall not pursue the CDD process, and instead file an STR.

2.   Instructions have been amended to clarify that additional information, not specified in the internal KYC Policy of the Bank, must be obtained with the explicit consent of the customer.

3.   Where the GST number is available, the same shall be verified through the search/verification facility provided by the issuing authority.

 

VII. Enhanced due diligence in respect of non-face-to-face customer onboarding

1.   In respect of such cases, certain additional enhanced due diligence measures have been added in Section 40 of the Master Direction. These measures include –

i.     The Bank shall verify the current address through positive confirmation before allowing operations in the account,

ii.   PAN shall be obtained from the customer and shall be verified,

iii.  customers shall be categorized as high-risk customers, and,

iv.  accounts opened in non-face-to-face mode shall be subjected to enhanced monitoring until the identity of the customer is verified in a face-to-face manner or through V-CIP, etc.

2.   Account opening using Aadhaar OTP-based e-KYC, in non-face-to-face mode. Bank is now required to ensure in such cases as above that transaction alerts, OTP, etc., are sent only to the mobile number of the customer registered with Aadhaar.

(Note:-However, we do not onboard customers on non-face-to-face basis as of now).

 

VIII. Risk Categorisation

1.   Section 12 of the Master Direction has been amended to include the provision that “Broad principles may be laid down by the REs for risk-categorisation of customers.” The indicative list of parameters for risk categorization has been expanded to include geographical risk covering customers as well as transactions, type of products/services offered, the delivery channel used for delivery of products/services, types of transactions undertaken, etc. Bank shall treat the risk categorization and reasons for risk categorization of customers as confidential.

2.   The above is an Action Point for the KYC-AML department.

 

IX.  CDD on the basis of downloaded documents by using KYC Identifier

Section 16 of the Master Direction has now been amended to specifically provide that Banks can obtain KYC Identifier with explicit customer consent to download KYC records from CKYCR, for the purpose of CDD.

 

X.   Instructions on the CKYCR

Instructions have been amended to ensure that KYC documents downloaded from the CKYCR, whose validity has lapsed, must not be used for KYC purpose by the Bank.

 

XI.  Hiring of Employees and Training

1.Updated instructions have been given under the Master Direction on

i.     Adequate screening mechanism while recruiting personnel,

ii.   Endeavours to ensure high integrity and ethical standards of staff handling KYC/AML matters, and

iii.  Ongoing training programme in KYC/AML/CFT policy for the staff (a) placed on the frontline dealing with customers, and (b) those handling compliance.

2.    The HR department is instructed to take appropriate steps in compliance of the above.

 

XII. Please bring the instructions contained herein to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
