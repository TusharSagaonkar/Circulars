Circular OC No. 899 dated 17th October 2023

DAILY DEPOSIT SCHEME - RULES AND PROCEDURES

 

I.   The Daily Deposit Scheme (DDS) which was initially operational at a few select branches of the Bank has been extended to more and more branches in phases.

 

II. The rules governing the DDS have also been reviewed and modified, and they are as under. The revised rules will be applicable to all the accounts under DDS including those opened prior to the issue of the instant circular, and to all the DDS agents including those currently operating. The rules need to be noted by branches carefully.

Rules governing the DDS

1.       Any individual can open one or more account/s in his/her name. The daily deposit, called “subscription” may be made with a minimum of ₹50/- or in multiples thereof. The amount of the subscription will not be variable. In case of default on any day, the defaulted amount should be remitted along with the next day’s amount.

2.       An account shall run only for 12 months. The period of deposit will also not be variable after opening an account.

3.       KYC

             i.    Photocopies of the KYC documents and the account opening form shall be collected by the DDS Agents and shall be submitted to the branch (to which he/she is attached) for a preliminary examination. The Branch-In-charge or any other official designated for the KYC purpose shall, thereafter, perform KYC. It may be noted that eKYC is waived for DDS accounts. The branch shall send the customer an Address Confirmation Letter (Thanks Letter). While it will be permissible to open the account and start accepting the subscription after collection of the application form with the accompanying documents and performance of the KYC, the receipt of acknowledgement of the Address Confirmation Letter (Thanks Letter) must eventually be followed up and ensured within 15 days of opening the account.

           ii.    In case of a difference between the address as per the officially valid POA and the one mentioned by the prospective customer towards his local address (typically in the case of migrants), additional steps such as obtaining a declaration followed by a visit by a staff member at the declared place of residence and filing of a visit-report, as prescribed for other accounts, shall be taken by the Branch-In-Charge or the official designated for the KYC. Acceptance of subscription can start thereafter.

4.       The period of deposit shall be noted prominently in the passbook.

5.       The subscription shall be accepted only through the authorised DDS agent of the bank. The DDS agent shall issue a receipt to the depositor every time.

6.       i. Bank will provide hand-held machines at its cost to the DDS Agents for

(a)        recording daily collections,

(b)        issuing cash receipts to the depositor, and

(c) maintaining records of balance. The machine is required to be connected to the CBS system of the Bank (OMNI) for crediting amounts to DDS accounts on a daily basis.

ii. The DDS agents shall take a second printout of each subscription and tender it with a scroll listing out the collected subscriptions to enable branches to enter collection dates in the Omni system.

7.       The interest on the deposit will be paid at the rate applicable to the saving deposit, which is at present 2.75% per annum. Whenever the interest rate on the saving deposit changes, the new rate shall be made applicable for all accounts of DDS deposits opened. The interest however will be calculated on Daily-Products-Basis.

8.       The branch staff shall tally the collection amount through a scroll printout and the tallied amount shall be reported to the In-Charge concerned before the cash is accepted from the DDS agent.

9.       Every month, a balance confirmation letter from depositors shall be obtained in respect of at least 5% of total accounts chosen randomly. The depositor shall, in such cases, be asked to check the balance in his account as per his own calculations/record with the balance noted in the passbook before confirming the balance.

10.   Customers should be educated by the Bank’s staff for obtaining and verifying the receipts (counterfoils) issued by the DDS agent so as to confirm the tendered amount.

11.   No dispute raised by the depositor relating to the payment of the deposit amount/s will be entertained unless the complaint is supported by the cash receipts issued by the DDS Agent.

12.   Loans up to 75% of the balance will be allowed against the DDS accounts, provided there is a balance of ₹1000/- or more in the account at the time of sanctioning the advance. The rate of interest on such loans will be 4% over the rate of interest offered on the deposit. The repayment period of the loan shall be shorter than or the same as the period till the date of maturity of the deposit that is the advance shall not continue beyond the date of maturity of the deposit.

13.   No advance shall be sanctioned against the DDS amount standing in the name of a minor.

14.   If a depositor so desires, he may convert the outstanding balance in the DDS account on the date of maturity into a fixed deposit.

15.   (i) Penalty for premature payments

·        In case of premature closure of an account, interest will be calculated at one percentage point below the rate applicable to the particular DDS deposit. No interest will be paid if the account is prematurely closed before the completion of three months.

·        In case of premature closure of the Daily Deposit for the purpose of reinvestment in Fixed Deposit, interest will be paid for the period from the date of the original contract up to the date of premature payment, without charging a penalty of 1%, with rate of interest ruling on the date of renewal provided deposit after conversion in Term Deposit Account being reinvested remains with the bank for a period longer than the remaining period of the original contract.

·        If such renewed deposit is withdrawn before the expiry of the period of the original contract, a penalty of 1% will be levied from the date of the original contract up to the date of the pre-mature withdrawal after reinvestment. If the premature withdrawal is made after the due date of the original contract, then the penalty is to be levied from the date of reinvestment to the date of premature withdrawal. A suitable remark on the Term Deposit Receipt will be made while issuing the same to the depositors.

16.   Commission to be paid to DDS Agents will be at a rate of 2.5% of the amount collected except to DDS agents working for branches in Udupi Region to whom the commission payable will be 3% of the amount collected. Branches should deduct TDS from the commission payable to DDS Agents as per Section 194H of the Income Tax Act, 1961.

17.   Every DDS Agent shall furnish cash security of ₹5000/- or any such amount as may be prescribed from time to time in the form of a fixed deposit. He/She shall also furnish one personal surety-cum-indemnity duly executed by a guarantor who is an Income Tax payer and is acceptable to the Bank.

18.   i. A DDS Agent shall ordinarily maintain not less than 100 accounts and not more

than 350 accounts at any point in time. A new agent should achieve the target of enrolling 100 accounts within a year of his/her appointment to be eligible to continue his/her agency.

ii. If any DDS agent is not able to maintain a minimum of 100 accounts at the end of the first year and during any other year thereafter, he/she will be instructed through a letter as per the Annexure-I-A hereto to improve the performance so as to reach the benchmark of 100 accounts within the next year. If the DDS still does not reach the benchmark in the next year, he/she will be instructed to reach the same at least in the subsequent year through a letter as per Annexure-I-B hereto. If the DDS Agent cannot reach the benchmark despite giving him/her one more year to do so, the matter of discontinuing his services may be examined.

19.   The earlier limit of Rs.20,000/- on aggregate daily collection of the agreed subscription of all DDS accounts introduced/canvassed by an agent is now removed. As such, the DDS agents will not be eligible for the transport allowance, so it will not be paid.

20.   i. The DDS Agent must remit the collections made on a particular day immediately

on the next working day. Delay in remittance will not be condoned. No commission will be paid for the collection amount deposited after the next working day i.e. on the 3rd working day of collection and thereafter.

ii. IT department is advised to ensure that there is a menu in Omni to enter the date of collection of Subscription by the DDS agent.

iii.              Branches will invariably enter such a date in Omni.

iv.             To ensure that commission does not get paid on the amount of subscription remitted into the bank account later than the 2nd working day of its collection from the customer (i.e. on 3rd working day of collection and onwards), branches must make entry of the date of collection of the subscription by the DDS agent from the customer by relying on the entry in the scroll submitted by the DDS agent and as evidenced by the second printout of each subscription. In this connection, please refer Para 6.ii. above.

21.   Whenever a DDS Agent has to be absent from making the collection, he shall inform the Branch-In-charge in advance. If the absence is for a short period, say three or four days, he may collect the arrears of Daily Deposits from Depositors on resumption of duty.

22.   If the DDS Agent has to absent himself from work for a longer period either on account of having to go out of his place of operation or for any other reason, he shall submit the necessary leave application in advance also stating therein whether he proposes to make any alternate arrangements for collection of daily deposits on his own responsibility. The alternate arrangement shall be approved by the Branch-In-Charge locally by making and recording DUE DILIGENCE. Such an arrangement shall not last more than 15 days in any case. If the absenteeism of the DDS agent continues beyond a period of 15 days, the matter may be referred to the Zonal Office for making an arrangement on a firmer footing.

23.   If an account is prematurely closed before the completion of three months from the date of commencement of the deposit and the amount therein is also withdrawn, the Bank will recover from the agent the entire amount of commission already paid to him/her. However, if contributions are discontinued by the depositor for any reason but the amount is not withdrawn before the already determined date of repayment, then no recovery of the commission (already paid) will be made.

24.   Each DDS Agent will be required to open a Recurring Deposit account of three years with a monthly instalment of Rs.500/- p.m. for the basic slab of Rs.20,000/- of daily collection and the Bank’s lien shall be marked thereon. In case daily collection exceeds Rs.20,000/-, even if in any single instance, one additional RD account of 3 years with a monthly instalment of Rs.500/- p.m. for each additional slab of Rs.20,000/- of daily collection shall be required to be opened by the DDS agent. Bank’s lien shall also be marked on such additional RD accounts. Upon maturity, the proceeds of the Recurring Deposit will be kept as a fixed deposit to be renewed periodically. The relative FD Receipt will be sent to the HRM Dept for keeping in the file/record of the DDS Agent concerned. However, those agents working for the Bank over a long period AND who have already kept a sizable amount in the Benevolent fund will have no compulsion to open a fresh Recurring Deposit account. No loans shall be given to the DDS agents against the balances in their RD / Fixed Deposit accounts or Benevolent Fund.

25.   Interest will not be paid to a depositor if an account is closed within three months and the amount is also withdrawn from the Bank.

26.   Interest calculations shall be checked by the officer concerned before making payment at the time of closure of the account.

27.   The Branch-In-charge should review the position on a monthly basis and instances of breach of the condition of immediate remittance of daily collection should be referred to the Head office to initiate/start an appropriate course of action.

 

III.   Other points

1.       The entries of the collection made by the DDS agent shall appear in the Bank’s CBS system (OMNI) automatically after the hand-held machine is connected to it (the CBS). Therefore, it is obligatory on the part of the staff member in charge of the scheme to make sure that the cash collected through the DDS machine is deposited in the Bank on a daily basis.

2.       IT department is advised to ensure the development of the menu as indicated in Para 20 ii. Above.

3.       The record of the DDS agent is required to be duly signed by the staff member concerned (Officer, Assistant Manager or the Branch-In-charge).

4.       Branches shall do monthly reporting on the DDS scheme, agent-wise, to the respective Zonal Office as per Annexure-II hereto.

5.       Zonal offices shall monitor the DDS scheme to prevent fraud.

6.       The Marketing department will arrange to compile a progress report on the scheme by collecting information from Zonal Offices and submit it to the ALCO for review in its meetings. A copy of the progress report shall be supplied by the Marketing department to the Secretarial Dept., Head Office on monthly basis.

7.       The IT department will extend technical support to branches, the HRM department and the Marketing department for operational and reporting purposes.

8.       The Central Law department is instructed to revise the text of the Agreement of the Bank with DDS agents so that the changes in (i) the responsibilities of DDS agents, (ii) requirements which they are required to fulfil, and (iii) financials, consequent upon the changes in the scheme as per the instant circular get reflected in the revised text.

9.       A timeline of 15 days hereof is hereby prescribed for the completion of this task and the supply of the revised text to the HRM department by the Central Law department.

10.   The DDS agents who will be engaged hereafter to handle the scheme shall execute the Agreement as per the text under revision as per the sub-Para 8. Above.

11.   As regards the DDS agents who are already engaged, execution by them of the Agreement as per the text under revision shall be arranged by the HRM department within two months of finalization and supply of the text by the Central Law department as per the sub-Para 8. And 9. above.

 

IV. We look forward to the branches identified for operating the DDS by garnering deposits up to the optimum extent. Please bring the contents of the instant circular to the members of the staff concerned for its effective implementation.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
