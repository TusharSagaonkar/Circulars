Circular OC No. 909 dated 26th March 2024

·     Change in periodicity of letters to KYC - Due account holders including notice for Partial freezing of non compliant accounts

 

·     Maintaining records of letters returned undelivered

_______________________________________________________________________________________

I.    Attention is invited to the Para. No.5.9 (C) of the extant KYC, AML, CFT & CRC Policy - 2024-25. It is mentioned therein that, letters/reminders shall be sent to the KYC-Due account holders on monthly basis through Reg.AD/Speed Post requesting the account holder to submit their latest photograph and the KYC documents/Self Declaration for updation.

 

II.   Guidelines for KYC updation in accounts where it is due and imposing debit freeze in respect of non compliant accounts.

A) KYC Updation:

In response to our letters/ reminders, customers typically approach the bank for KYC updation. The instructions to be followed in such cases are reiterated below:

1.   Physical presence of Customer is not required for KYC/Profile updation.

2.   Certified copies of documents by mail or post, or through their representative in person, are also acceptable.

3.   If there is no change in KYC details, a self declaration will also suffice.

4.   In respect of individual accounts, KYC should be updated by obtaining any one of the six OVDs, duly self-attested, accompanied by the CIF-I.

5.   In respect of non-individual accounts, documentary proof duly self-attested along with CIF-II should be obtained as required under their respective constitution.

6.   Signatures of customers on self-attested documents shall be required to be verified from the OMNI before updating the KYC to avoid impersonation and fraud.

7.   Customer can submit the required KYC documents/Self Declaration at any of the branches of the bank. The receiving (non base) branch, after verifying the signature of the customer from OMNI shall forward the documents to the base branch for KYC Updation in OMNI.

 

B)  Debit Freeze in respect of re-KYC non-compliant accounts:

a)  Role of Centralised KYC/AML Department -

The Centralised KYC/AML Dept. shall follow the procedure as under for updation, or Debit Freeze in its absence, in Re-KYC Due accounts:

1.   One month prior to the re-KYC- Due date, the Centralised KYC/AML Dept. shall send “Advance KYC- Due Intimation Letter” to all the eligible customers through Speed/Regd. AD post, requesting them to submit the required KYC documents.

2.   If a customer does not submit the required KYC Documents within 30 days of our letter as above, the Centralised KYC/AML Dept. shall send a “Final Reminder Letter”, through Speed / Regd. AD post, informing the customers, the date on/from which “Partial freezing” will be imposed in their account.

3.   The Centralised KYC/AML Dept. shall also arrange to send Email/SMS (if the email and mobile number are registered with the bank) to re-KYC due customers every month.

4.   The Centralised KYC/AML Dept. shall arrange to send the list of all such non-compliant KYC-Due accounts to the Branches for imposing “Partial freeze” after the date mentioned in the reminder letter. After imposing of the freeze, all the credits shall be allowed but the debits through all the channels, including ATM/POS/Mobile Banking etc. will be disallowed. The cheques drawn by customers on their account under debit freeze shall also be returned. However, the charges for return of Cheques or any other instruction of payment from the account, electronic or otherwise, due to debit freeze only on account of non-KYC updation shall not be recovered.

 

b) Role of the Branches :-

1.   Branches shall impose debit freeze in all re-KYC non compliant accounts with reason “As per KYC Norms” (Freeze Code - 15) in the list of accounts sent by the Centralised KYC & AML Dept.

2.   As per instant instructions in the KYC, AML, CFT & CRC Policy, the branches shall not allow any debit transactions in accounts under debit freeze “As per KYC Norms” (Freeze Code - 15).

3.   Before allowing operations in such accounts, the required KYC Documents shall be obtained and kept on record.

4.   Branches shall ensure that the KYC Master is also updated before removing the Debit Freeze and allowing operations in the account.

 

C)  Record of Letters returned undelivered

The post office returns all the undelivered letters for reasons such as “Left”, “Not available”, “Not known”, “Address not found”, “Incomplete address” etc. to the respective branches. In cases of returned undelivered letters, branches should adhere following guidelines:

1.   In case of a letter returned undelivered for the reason “Incomplete address”, branches should refer to the account opening form/the KYC updation form, and update the complete address in the Omni database. Data in respect of City, Area, Postal/Zip code, State, Country has to be entered in the respective field of the Customer Master/ Customer Address Master.

2.   All undelivered letters are to be preserved and kept in a separate file for record and verification purposes. The reasons for letters returned undelivered should be recorded in the “Special instruction” in the Account master in the Omni database.

3.   Proper preservation of undelivered letters is crucial from the point of view of the cases where the account is debit frozen for the reason of “As per KYC Norms” (Freeze Code - 15). This is required to obviate the situations which may arise when cheques issued and/or debit mandate given by customers are returned unpaid for the reason of debit freeze imposed in the account and the customer challenges that he was not put on notice.

4.   Branches should pay special attention to allowing operations in debit frozen accounts. They must obtain CIF-I / CIF-II form along with the required KYC in such cases. The necessary updation shall accordingly be done in the KYC master and Main customer master.

5.   Once KYC requirement is fulfilled and the KYC Master and Main Customer Master are updated, the “Special instruction” entered in Account Master should be removed accordingly.

 

III. Please bring the instructions contained herein to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned and ensure strict compliance.
