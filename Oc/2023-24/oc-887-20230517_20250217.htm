Circular OC No. 887 dated 17th May 2023

1.   Identification of NPO account and Determination of Beneficiary Owners - Amendments to Prevention of Money-Laundering (Maintenance of Records) Rules – 2005.

2.   Instant circular in supersession of Circular OC No. 886 dated 21st April 2023

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

 

I.    Introduction

1.   Recently, the Central Government vide their notification dated 7th March 2023 made some amendments in the Prevention of Money-Laundering (Maintenance of Records) Rules – 2005. The amendments and actions required by branches to become compliant with them are given in subsequent paragraphs.

 

2.  We had issued the previous Circular OC No. 886 dated 21st April 2023 on the basis of the amendments in PML rules as then understood by us. The RBI has since issued the Master Direction-Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016 (No.RBI/DBR/201-16/ 18 master Direction DBR.AML.BC.81/14.01.001/2015/16) updated as on 28th April 2023, incorporating the amendments in the PML Rules, 2005, giving better clarity on the subject. The Master Direction indicates that the percentage determinants of beneficial ownership in respect of Partnerships, Unincorporated Associations or Bodies of Individuals remain at the previous levels. Therefore, we are issuing the instant circular in supersession of Circular OC No. 886 dated 21st April 2023.

 

II.  Definition of a Non-Profit Organisation (NPO)

1.   An NPO is defined as under.

“Non–profit organization means any entity or organization, constituted for religious or charitable purposes referred to in clause (15) of Section 2 of the Income Tax Act, 1961 (43 of 1961), i.e. registered as a trust or a society under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (21 of 1860) or any similar State Legislation or a Company registered under the Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 (18 of 2013)”.

2.   In view of the above, Branches need to review the classification of the existing NPO accounts and retain their NPO classification only if they fulfil the following criteria.

i.     The Entity/Organization should be a Charitable/Religious Institution

AND

ii.   It should have/be one of the following.

a.   Registration Certificate issued by the Charity Commissioner under the Bombay Public Trusts Act,1950 (Now Maharashtra Public Trusts Act,1950),

OR

b.   Registration Certificate issued by the Assistant Registrar of Co-operative Societies under the Societies Registration Act, 1860,

OR

c.   It should be a Pvt. Ltd. Company registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013.

3.   The same criteria as per sub-Para 2. above shall apply for any new account to qualify as an NPO. The entity seeking to open an account with NPO status will be required to additionally furnish a certificate of registration on the Darpan Portal of NITI Aayog (for which, see sub-Para 4. below).

 

4.   Once an account at your branch is identified as an NPO, its holders will be required to submit to the branch a Registration Certificate towards having registered on the “Darpan Portal” of NITI Aayog. If the entity asserting to be an NPO is not registered on the Darpan Portal, branches are advised to send a letter to such non-compliant entity in the format as per Annexure hereto. The record of registration, upon receipt, is to be preserved by the Bank for a period of five years after the business relationship with the entity ends or the account gets closed.

 

III.     Rules pertaining to Beneficial Owner (BO)

1.   Amendments in rule 9 sub-rule (3)

                      i.       In clause (a) in the Explanation, in clause (1), for the words “twenty-five”, the word “ten” shall be substituted;

                    ii.       In clause (e), for the word “fifteen”, the word “ten” shall be substituted;

 

2.   Our previous instructions on Beneficial Owner (BO)

In terms of our Circular OC No. 619 dated 15.02.2013 on the subject of “Determination of Beneficial Ownership in regard to Accounts of Legal entity”, the following instructions were issued to the branches.

 

3.   “For the determination of BOs, in case of Partnership Accounts15% of the capital or profits sharing by partner/s need be considered, for BOs in Private Limited Company Accounts 25 % of shares or capital or profits be considered for each director, in case of Trust Accounts 15% or more interest in the trust by the Trustees be considered and in case of unincorporated association or body of individuals 15% of the property or capital or profits be considered. Where no natural person is identified as detailed above, the identity of the relevant natural person who holds the position of senior managing official or person exercising ultimate effective control over the entity be considered as Beneficial Owner of the said entity.”

 

4.   Revised instructions herein on Beneficial Owner

Now, as per the Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016 (No.RBI/DBR/201-16/18 master Direction DBR.AML.BC.81/ 14.01.001/2015/ 16 ) updated as on 28th April 2023, the percentage of shares or capital or profits to determine the Beneficial Owner(s)of legal entities of all types, shall be as shown in the table below.

5.    Need to re-examine the accounts and re-identify the Beneficial Owners. In view of the above changes, branches need to re-examine the existing accounts of Private Limited Companies and Trusts, to re-identify the Beneficial Owners of each of them and update the same in the Omni database.

 

IV.  Inspection Dept. to check the compliance of the above instructions at the time of inspection of the branches.

 

V.   Please bring the instructions contained herein to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned and ensure strict compliance.
