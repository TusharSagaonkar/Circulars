Circular OC No. 904 dated 16th February 2024

Deceased Constituents Interest on Term Deposit Paid Prematurely

or after Maturity to Survivor(s), Nominee or Legal Heirs - Clarifications

_________________________________________________________________________________

I. The Inspection department has reported that deviations from the laid down instructions have been observed in a few branches in the matter of payment of interest on Term Deposits of deceased constituents to the Survivor(s), Nominee or Legal Heir(s) when payment is required to be made before or after the date of maturity as per the Bank’s record. Therefore, the instructions on the subject are clarified hereunder.

 

1.   Right of survivor(s), nominee or legal heir(s) to seek premature payment without attracting penalty

(i)    In the case of a Deceased Constituent [whether sole or joint with another/other(s)], the survivor(s), nominee or legal heir(s) as may be the case, can seek premature payment of a Term Deposit.

(ii)   If premature payment is sought by them as mentioned above, the proceeds of the Term Deposit with interest at the rate ruling on the date of placement of the deposit (or its last renewal under a live mandate) for the actual period the deposit remained with the Bank, must be paid to them.

(iii) No penalty for premature payment is to be recovered in such a case.

[Note-A: The Survivor(s), Nominee or Legal Heirs, instead of seeking premature payment may continue the deposit (whether during the original term or the last renewed term) till maturity to take benefit of the contracted rate of interest].

 

2.   If a previously placed Term Deposit has matured before the constituent is deceased, its renewal on the date of maturity is valid. The proceeds of the renewed Term Deposit need to be paid in the normal manner upon the next maturity, or without penalty before the next maturity at the applicable rate.

 

3.   In the case of a Term Deposit of a Deceased constituent lying unclaimed with the Bank after its maturity (whether original or the last one), the Survivor(s), Nominee or Legal Heirs are entitled to receive interest on the contracted maturity proceeds at the rate of interest applicable to Saving Deposit, or that applicable for a Term Deposit for the overdue period as ruling on the date of maturity, whichever is lower.

(Note-B: - As the ROI on Fixed Deposits in our Bank for all maturities is higher than that on Saving Deposit, in current practice, only Saving Deposit interest will be payable).

 

4.   Single depositor dies before the date of auto renewal

i.   If a constituent maintaining a TD account with a mandate for auto-renewal dies before the completion of the original term but the Term Deposit gets auto-renewed on the expiry of the term (for want of notice to the Bank, or unawareness of the Nominee, Legal Heirs etc., or non-disclosure by them of the information of the death), the auto-renewal happening in the CBS (Omni) is not valid as the constituent is not alive on the date of the auto-renewal.

ii. In such a case, the calculations must be reworked, and the proceeds as per the original term of the deposit with further interest thereon be paid to the Survivor(s), Nominee or Legal Heirs at the saving deposit rate.

5.   One, or less than all, of the joint depositors with survivorship mandate dies/die before the date of auto-renewal (i.e. at least one of the survivors is alive on the date of auto renewal)

i.     If one or less than all of the joint depositors with survivorship mandate dies/die before the date of auto-renewal (i.e. at least one of the survivors is alive on the date of auto-renewal), the auto-renewal which already happens in the CBS (Omni) is valid.

ii.   In such a case, if premature payment is sought by the survivor(s), or nominee or legal heirs (if all of the joint depositors subsequently die), the proceeds of the Term Deposit with interest at the rate ruling on the date of its last renewal being under a valid mandate) for the actual period the deposit remains with the Bank, must be paid to them.

6.   Any one of the joint depositors with the deposit payable jointly (i.e. without a survivorship mandate) dies before the date of auto renewal

i.     If even one of the joint depositors of the deposit payable jointly (i.e. without a survivorship mandate) dies before the date of auto renewal, the auto-renewal which already happens in the CBS (Omni) is NOT valid.

ii.   In such a case, the calculations must be reworked manually and the proceeds as per the original term of the deposit with further interest thereon be paid to the Survivor(s), Nominee or Legal Heirs at the saving deposit rate.

II.      A few key examples are given in Annexure hereto which will help clarify the rules.

 

III.    Branches are advised that the rules should be followed strictly and no deviation therefrom should take place.
