Circular OC No. 898 dated 11th October 2023

Non-Face-to-Face facility to Customers for KYC Updation

Provision of Digital Platform

 

 

I.    Background

1.  The RBI has, from time to time, taken measures to rationalise the KYC related instructions taking into account the available technological options for enhancing customers’ convenience within the framework prescribed under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 (PMLA) and rules framed there under.

 

2.   As per the present guidelines of RBI, if there is no change in KYC information, a self declaration to that effect from the individual customer is sufficient to complete the re-KYC process.

 

II.  Guidelines issued to Banks to provide facility for self-declaration through non-face-to-face Digital channels

1.   The RBI, in keeping with its above mentioned policy has, in Para 38 of its Master Direction No. RBI/DBR/2015-16/18 -DBR.AML.BC.No.81/ 14.01.001/ 2015-16 dated the 25th February 2016 (updated on 28th April 2023) has advised banks to provide facility of self-declaration to the individual customers through various non-face-to-face channels such as registered email-id, registered mobile number, ATMs, digital channels (such as online banking / internet banking, mobile application), letter, etc., without need for a visit to bank branch.

 

2.   The instructions also provide that in case of only a change in address, customers can furnish revised / updated address through any of the channels indicated above, after which the bank will be required to undertake verification of the declared address within two months.

 

III.   Action on RBI’s guidelines

1.   In the “KYC/AML/FATCA/CRS” Executive Committee Meeting held on 15th February 2023, it has been decided to act on the RBI guidelines and provide online facility for KYC updation to the customers of our Bank.

 

2.   Accordingly, our Bank has developed an Application with the help of M/s. Chic Infotech Pvt. Ltd. for periodic updation of KYC details of customers through DIGITAL channels to be hosted on our Bank’s Website https://www.abhyudayabank.co.in It can be accessed by customers through Internet (Desktop) or mobile phone. This will obviate the need on the part of customer to visit the branch for KYC updation periodically.

IV.  The standard operating procedure for online updation of KYC documents is detailed as under: -

A.  Action at Customer’s Level

i.     This Digital Platform of KYC updation will be available only to the customers having their mobile number (linked to UIDAI–Aadhaar) and registered in our Core Banking System.

ii.   On feeding of customer number/15 digit account number, a customer will be validated through SMS/OTP sent by the Bank to his/her registered mobile number.

iii.  Aadhaar will be validated through the OTP sent by UIDAI to the linked mobile number of the customer.

iv. Customer’s PAN shall be verified through PAN-NSDL.

v.   Authentication through an OTP shall confirm genuineness of the customer. The Customer will then be issued an online receipt confirming the submission of self-Declaration for Re-KYC. The receipt shall bear an Acknowledgement.

 

B.  Action at Centralised KYC & AML Department :

1. An officer in the Centralised KYC & AML Department will login to the Administrative section of Re-KYC portal. He/she will verify the records submitted by the customer online and will either Approve or Reject the same (the latter due to a mismatch between the data as per the Bank’s records vis-a-vis Data provided by the customer and that received from UIDAI and PAN-NSDL).

 

2. Whenever,

i.     the name as per the Bank’s Records matches with the name obtained from UIDAI and PAN-NSDL, and

ii.   the address as per the Bank’ Records matches with the address received from UIDAI and also that uploaded by the customer, the Centralised KYC & AML Department will approve the Re-KYC submitted by the customer for updating the same centrally in the KYC master.

 

3. The data generated from the Re-KYC performed by customer and approved at the level of Centralised KYC & AML Department will be compiled in an Excel sheet at the Centralised KYC & AML Department. An Officer working in the Centralised KYC & AML Department will generate Physical Self-declaration in respect of each customer on the basis of the data contained in the Excel sheet. Each Self-declaration will be approved by the Departmental Head for updation in the KYC Master.

 

4. A Copy of the Self Declaration duly authenticated by the Centralised KYC & AML Departmental Head will also be forwarded to the respective branch for reference and record purpose.

V.   The instant circular is issued to enable the staff and officials at branches to guide the Re-KYC due customers regarding the availability of and the procedure for online KYC Updation in our Bank.

 

VI.  The Planning and Marketing Dept. shall arrange to appropriately design and send posters to the branches for information of the customers, describing the procedure for online Re-KYC.

 

VII. Please bring the instructions contained herein to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
