Circular OC No. 919 dated 23rd December, 2024

Introduction of Additional General Insurance Services through a

Corporate Insurance Tie-Up with SBI General Insurance Company Ltd.

 

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI), (Regulations of Corporate Agents) Regulations 2015, allows Corporate Insurance Agents to provide Insurance services through nine (9) Insurance companies each under Life, General & Health services. We are pleased to announce that Abhyudaya Co-operativeBank has entered into a Corporate Insurance Agency agreement with SBI General Insurance Co. Ltd. (SBIGI) on 16th December, 2024. This collaboration enables us to offer a comprehensive range of general insurance products from SBIGI. The partnership with SBIGI will become effective from 23rd December, 2024 and their products will be available through all Abhyudaya Bank branches.

 

Overview of the Partnership:

Corporate Insurance Agency: In our continuous endeavor to provide a wide range of financial services, Abhyudaya Bank, as a Corporate Insurance Agent, has been offering Life Insurance services through Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC), HDFC Life Insurance Co. Ltd. (HDFC), SBI Life Insurance Company Ltd. (SBILI); alongside General Insurance services through The New India Assurance Company Ltd. (NIAC), Bajaj General Insurance Co. Ltd. (BAGIC). Further we offer Health Insurance services through Care Health Insurance Services Ltd. (CHICL).

Market Opportunity: The General insurance sector presents significant growth potential, especially considering its low market penetration. This partnership not only addresses the growing needs of our customers but also provides an avenue for increasing the Bank's fee-based income.General Insurance is essential for protecting customer assets and given its low market penetration levels, it offers significant opportunities for the Bank to enhance fee-based income.

 

1(a) About SBI General Insurance:

SBI General Insurance Company Limited, was established in 2009, through a joint venture between the State Bank of India (SBI) and Insurance Australia Group (IAG). Headquartered in Mumbai, SBI General Insurance provides a comprehensive range of General Insurance products.

2. Support and Training:

SBI General Insurance has agreed to assign field staff to assist our branches in canvassing their insurance policies. The list of field staff assigned to branches by SBI General Insurance will be made available on the Exchange Server in the Insurance folder. Branches are advised to cross-sell these products to all existing & prospective customers. Branches should also encourage customers with existing General Insurance policies from other insurance companies with whom Bank has not tied - up to explore switching their policies to SBI General Insurance company under our corporate agency tie-up.

Branches are advised to obtain consent from proposers by obtaining their signatures on SBI General Insurance’s proposal forms along with the premium amount. Renewals of existing policies should be facilitated by obtaining the necessary forms well in advance of their renewal dates.

3. Instructions:

Commonly used proposal forms and related instructions for SBI General Insurance policies will be made available on the Exchange Server in the Public Folder of the Insurance Department. Branches are permitted to use printouts or photocopies of these forms.

4. Procedure for Claim Lodging:

In case of a claim, customers may contact SBI General Insurance via their Toll-Free Number: 1800 102 1111. Customers should inform SBI General Insurance at the earliest or within 24 hours of the incident. Detailed instructions for claim lodging will be available in the Insurance folder on the Exchange Server. Branches must assist customers in lodging claims promptly and ensure they retain photocopies of the submitted documents for their records.

5. Role of Branches:

·         For the customers who desire to obtain general insurance policies through SBIGI, branches must route all new and renewal business directly through the designated field staff of SBI General Insurance assigned to them.

·         Maintain a record of all leads, policies, and claims related to SBI General Insurance in the designated register provided by the Insurance Department.

·         Regularly update the Insurance Department on the progress of business generated under SBI General Insurance policies.

  • Branches should reconcile the commission received from Insurance Department along with the register maintained at branch on monthly basis and ensure that there is no income leakage. In case of inquiry contact Insurance Department – Vashi.

·         Assist customers in claims processing and ensure timely submission of documents to SBI General Insurance.

 

6. Role of Insurance Department, Vashi:

  • Act as a coordinator between SBI General Insurance and branches, guide branches and providing necessary support for procuring new and renewal policies.
  • Provide marketing materials, preprinted forms, and registers to branches.
  • Ensure timely transfer of commission received from SBIGI Insurance to branches along with branch-wise business performance MIS.
  • Communicate updates on new products or changes in existing policies to branches.

7. Role of Marketing Department:

The Marketing Department will provide adequate promotional materials, including posters, banners and leaflets to all branches. For additional materials, branches may contact the Marketing Department.

8. Role of IT Department:

The IT Department, in association with the Insurance Department, will integrate systems and provide software for premium collection and IRDA-compliant MIS and data maintenance.

9. Training:

Training Managers from SBI General Insurance will conduct sessions for branch staff to ensure a thorough understanding of their products and processes. These sessions will be arranged in consultation with the branches as required.

10. Targets:

Annual target for general insurance business will be set for branches to achieve. All third-party fee-based business are closely monitored and any loss of business opportunities due to negligence will be viewed seriously.

Kindly ensure all staff members and officials at your branch/department are informed of these guidelines. The Bank aims to extend SBI General Insurance products to as many customers as possible. Each employee’s efforts are crucial for making this partnership a success and increasing the Bank’s fee-based income.
