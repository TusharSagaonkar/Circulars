Circular OC No. 918 dated 30th November 2024

Introduction of Additional Life Insurance Services through a

Corporate Insurance Tie-Up with SBI Life Insurance Company Ltd.

_____________________________________________________________________

 

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI), (Regulations of Corporate Agents) Regulations 2015, allows Corporate Insurance Agents to provide Insurance services through nine (9) Insurance companies each under Life, General & Health Insurance services.We are pleased to announce that Abhyudaya Co-operative Bank has entered into a Corporate Insurance Agency agreement with SBI Life Insurance Co. Ltd. (SBILI). This collaboration enables us to offer a comprehensive range of life insurance products from SBILI. The partnership with SBILI is effective from 28th November, 2024, and their products will be available through all Abhyudaya Bank branches.

 

Overview of the Partnership:

Corporate Insurance Agency: In our continuous endeavor to provide a wide range of financial services, Abhyudaya Bank, as a Corporate Insurance Agent, has been offering Life Insurance services through Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC), HDFC Life Insurance Co. Ltd. (HDFC) alongside General Insurance services through The New India Assurance Company Ltd. (NIAC), Bajaj General Insurance Co. Ltd. (BAGIC). Further we offer Health Insurance services through Care Health Insurance Services Ltd. (CHICL).

Market Opportunity:The life insurance sector presents significant growth potential, especially considering its low market penetration. This partnership not only addresses the growing needs of our customers but also provides an avenue for increasing the Bank's fee-based income.

 

About SBI Life Insurance Company Ltd.:

SBI Life Insurance, a joint venture between the State Bank of India (SBI) and BNP Paribas Cardif, is a leading player in the Indian life insurance sector. It wasestablished in the year 2001 and is Headquartered in Mumbai. The company offers a comprehensive range of insurance products, including protection, savings, investment, retirement plans and loan linked products catering to the diverse financial needs of individuals.

 

Diverse Distribution Channels: The company utilizes multiple distribution channels, including bancassurance, and has a strong geographical presence.

 

Key Features of SBI Life Insurance Products:

SBI Life Insurance provides a variety of products tailored to customer needs:

·         Protection Plans: Securing the financial future of families in the event of untimely demise.

·         Savings and Investment Plans: Helping customers build wealth for important life goals through disciplined wealth creation.

·         Retirement Plans: Ensuring a steady income stream and financial stability during post-retirement years.

·         Term Insurance: Offering higher insurance coverage at an affordable price.

 

Credit Life Insurance: Providing insurance to customers that protects their loan amount in the event of untimely demise.

Procedural Guidelines for Procuring Business:

·        Branch Engagement: Business Development Managers (BDM) from SBI Life will be assigned to our branches to assist in promoting and servicing SBI Life products. Branch staff should engage with customers and maintain a lead register to track potential sales.

·        Field Staff Support from SBI Life Insurance: SBI Life will assign shared BDMs to our branches to help conduct insurance business and act as a liaison between the Bank and SBI Life. Branches should actively engage with customers and inform them about the insurance products available through our partnership with SBI Life.

·        Documentation: All insurance proposals must be signed by a Specified Person (SP) who has completed the IRDAI examination.

·        Lead Management: Branches are required to maintain a lead register to track potential customers. The lead register must be updated daily and reviewed by the Branch Head on a weekly basis to monitor progress.

·        Proposal Submission: The Specified Person (SP) at the branch who has passed the IRDAI exam will be responsible for signing proposal forms and ensuring their timely submission.

·        Claim Process: Customers should be guided to register claims promptly using the claim intimation form. Claims can be submitted through various channels, including direct submission to SBI Life representatives at our branches.

·        Commission and Fee Income: Commission from insurance sales will be credited to the Bank’s head office account on a fortnightly / monthly basis. Insurance department will transfer the same to branches who must reconcile these entries on monthly basis.

Training Support:

SBI Life Insurance will conduct comprehensive training sessions for branch staff, focusing on product knowledge and customer interaction strategies. This will ensure that our staff are familiar with SBI Life products and processes. These sessions will be organized by our Bank’s Insurance Department in collaboration with SBI Life.

Training sessions will be conducted at our training center in Vashi or directly at branches, in consultation with branch heads on a need-to-know basis. The objectives of the same are as under:

  • Develop a structured training strategy for Bancassurance that includes both SBI Life and our Bank’s staff.
  • Conduct a comprehensive needs analysis of skills and knowledge requirements.
  • Implement a detailed approach with a robust process to ensure timely delivery.
  • Establish a predefined roadmap to enhance employee efficiency.

Support the development of behavioral and soft skills for staff and sales leadership.

A Few Things to Advise Customers for Smooth Claim Settlement:

  • Read the terms and conditions carefully.
  • Keep your contact details up to date.
  • Pay premiums regularly to ensure that your policy is in force & remains active.
  • Share details of your insurance policy and the claim process with your loved ones.
  • Understand the specifics of your policy to know what is covered.
  • Check the beneficiary name in the policy; the nominee can be changed at any time.
  • The nominee name should be changed if:

o   The registered nominee has passed away during the term of the policy.

o   The policy is reassigned in the name of the customer after they have repaid the Bank loan for which the policy was used as collateral.

 

For further details on insurance claims through SBI, please refer to: https://www.sbilife.co.in/customer-service/claims

Procedural Aspects:

  • Commonly used stationery and proposal forms for SBI Life insurance products will be provided to branches by SBI Life Insurance. Additionally, SBI Life Insurance utilizes a mobile application for logging proposal forms.
  • Premiums will be calculated according to templates provided to branches for different types of policies. All branches must send proposals directly to the designated offices of SBI Life Insurance on the same day or, at the latest, by the next working day through the SBI Business Development Manager (BDM) assigned to the branch.
  • Customers may pay the insurance premium through online modes. Branches may also collect crossed cheques, Pay Orders (P.O.), or Demand Drafts (DD) made out in favor of “SBI Life Insurance Company Limited,” duly signed by the proposer.
  • Please note that original policy documents will be delivered directly to the customer’s address as mentioned on the proposal form.

Role of Branches:

  • Branches should route all new and renewal business directly through the Business Development Manager (BDM) assigned by SBI Life at the branch.
  • All branches, including outstation branches, will be connected to the nearest SBI Life Insurance Company office. This list will be made available in the Insurance Folder on the Exchange Server by the Insurance Department.
  • Branches must maintain a record of all leads, policies, and claims submitted to SBI Life Insurance. This information should be updated daily in a designated register. Details of the total business marketed under each category of insurance product should be forwarded to the Insurance Department for consolidated record-keeping and reconciliation.
  • SBI Life, in consultation with the Head Office and the Insurance Department, will undertake various marketing activities to cross-sell insurance to Bank customers through branches.
  • Claims from customers must be forwarded directly to the SBI Life office or the representative assigned to the branches. Customers should be advised to retain photocopies of the documents before submitting the claim form.
  • Achieve the targets set by Head Office for SBI Life and all other insurance companies on monthly basis.

 

Role of the Insurance Department, Vashi:

a.   The Insurance Department will act as a coordinator between SBI Life Insurance and branches, providing necessary support for procuring new and renewal policies.

b.   The department will guide branches and customers, helping them resolve their queries / grievances.

c.   Arrangements will be made for zone-wise training for branch in-charges and staff members handling insurance business, in collaboration with officials from SBI Life Insurance and the staff training college.

d.   The department will transfer commissions received from SBI Life Insurance to the branches on a monthly basis, along with a MIS report on branch-wise business performance.

e.   Whenever new products are introduced or changes are made to existing products by SBI Life, the Insurance Department will communicate these updates to the branches.

f.    The department will provide marketing materials and support to branches.

 

Role of the Marketing Department:

The Bank's Marketing Department will ensure that all branches are equipped with co-branded brochures and other promotional materials to raise awareness of SBI Life products and assist in customer engagement. The sales support materials will include posters, banners, danglers, standees and leaflets. For additional marketing materials, branch staff may contact the Marketing Department at Vashi.

Role of the Credit Department, Head Office:

SBI Life Insurance, in association with the Insurance & Credit Department, will roll out Insurance products specifically for loan customers.

Role of I.T. Department:

SBI Life Insurance, in collaboration with the Bank’s IT department will explore the options to integrate the systems and rollout online process for policy solicitation &premium collection.
