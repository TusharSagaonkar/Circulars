Circular OC No. 933 dated 26th September 2025

Continuous Clearing

____________________________________________________________________________________

 

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) in its monetary policy committee meeting held between 6th August 2024 to 8th August 2024 announced a system of Continuous Clearing of cheques on August 8, 2024. The RBI proposed continuous clearing of cheques to speed up payments and enhance efficiency. Cheque Truncation System (CTS) currently processes cheques with a clearing cycle of up to two working days. To improve the efficiency of cheque clearing and reduce settlement risk for participants, and to enhance customer experience, it is proposed to transition CTS from the current approach of batch processing to continuous clearing with 'on-realization-settlement'. Cheques will be scanned, presented, and passed in a few hours and on a continuous basis during business hours. The clearing cycle will reduce from the present T+1 days to a few hours.

In continuation of the above RBI has issued a circular No.RBI/2025-26/73 CO.DPSS.RLPD.NO.S536/04-07-001/2025-2026 dated August 13, 2025. As per the said circular RBI has decided to transition of CTS to continuous clearing and settlement on realization in two phases. Phase 1 shall be implemented on October 4, 2025 and Phase 2 on January 3, 2026.

Modalities for Continuous Clearing with Settlement On-realization in CTS:

1. Single presentation session with continuous delivery:

a)   There shall be a single presentation session from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

b)  Cheques received by the branches shall be scanned and sent to the clearing house by the banks immediately and continuously during the presentation session.

c)   The clearing house will in turn release the cheque images to drawee banks on a continuous basis.

2. Continuous inward processing and confirmation by banks:

a)   The confirmation session shall start at 10:00 AM and close at 7:00 PM.

b)  For every cheque presented, the drawee bank shall generate either positive confirmation (for honored cheques) or negative confirmation (for dishonored cheques).

c)   Each cheque will contain the ‘Item Expiry Time’ which indicates the latest time by which confirmation for the presented instrument needs to be provided by the drawee bank.

 

d)  Processing by drawee banks are to be done continuously throughout the day and on a real time basis as soon as cheque images are received.

e)   Information of positive/negative confirmation shall be sent by the drawee banks to the clearing house immediately after processing.

 

3. Time available for inward processing:

a)   During phase 1 (From October 4 to January 2, 2026), drawee banks shall be required to confirm (positively / negatively) cheques presented on them latest by end of confirmation session (i.e. 7:00 PM) else those will be deemed to have been approved and included for settlement. Item expiry time for all cheques shall be set as 7:00 PM in phase 1.

b)  In Phase 2 (from January 3, 2026), the item expiry time of cheques shall be changed to T+3 clear hours. For example, the cheques received by drawee banks between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM will have to be confirmed positively or negatively by them by 2:00 PM (3 hours from 11:00 AM). 4. Settlement on realization:

a)   No accounting entries (settlement) will be posted for presentation of cheques.

b)  Starting from 11:00 AM, settlement will be arrived every hour till the end of confirmation session, based on the positive confirmations received from drawee banks and cheques considered deemed approved.

c)   No accounting entries shall be passed for cheques with negative confirmation.

 

5. Releasing payment to customers:

a)   On completion of settlement, clearing house shall release the information of positive and negative confirmations to the presenting bank.

b)  The presenting bank shall process the same and release the payment to the customers immediately, but not later than 1 hour from successful settlement, subject to usual safeguards.

 

The new system is expected to improve cheque clearing efficiency, reduce settlement risk for participants, enhance customer experience and bridge the gap between traditional cheque payments and instant electronic transfers.

 

The guidelines for introduction of Cheque Truncation System (CTS) have been issued to branches in the past vide circular O.C.No.620 dated 20th February, 2013 and subsequent circulars issued thereon. Further guidelines on Positive Payment System under CTS was issued vide circular Ref No 861 dt. 18.10.2021.

 

Outward presentation : Currently we are collecting the CTS instruments from the branches in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane District. After UV checking, we are scanning all the instruments received from the branches and processing the instruments in In-house Hosted module of M/s.V-soft. The outstation branches are scanning the CTS instruments in Webbic/Agile/mobile App provided by M/s.V-soft and sending the data to us for onward presentation.

There will be change in system of clearing, once the continuous clearing started in two phases from 04/10/2025.

The cheques deposited by the customer at respective branch should be stamped accordingly and will have to be presented immediately. Since the responsibility of the genuineness is on the presenting bank, branches have to be very vigilant and have to follow CTS 2010 guidelines and our Clearing policy. Central Clearing Department will process the cheques for onward submission to NPCI and credit will be given to the respective customer's account once confirmation from the drawee bank and settlement report thereof from NPCI is received.

Considering the change in process and timely service to customers, for smooth implementation of continuous clearing the branches have been segregated on the basis of their average daily volume as large, medium and small. Scanners have been provided to the branches having medium and large volume and for branches having small volume Wings M-Clear mobile app (RDC) has been provided by M/s. Vsoft for the scanning work to be carried out smoothly.

Inward : Currently clearing department is receiving the Inward Extract file and images from M/s.V-soft in the morning. Clearing department is uploading the same in our CBS and then the verification is being done. The instruments which are in order and in those accounts sufficient balances are available; same are debited to the respective accounts. The instruments received with technical errors are directly returned by Central Clearing Dept and the remaining instruments are referred to the Branches, for their further action. The cutoff time for returns by branches is 1.00 p.m., so as to upload the inward returned files to NPCI before their cutoff time.

After continuous clearing becomes operational the scenario will be changed. As per Phase 1 there will be a single presentation session from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. and as per the Phase 2 the NPCI session starts at 10.00 a.m to 4.00 p.m. with hourly settlement in our E-kuber account. Clearing Department will be receiving the inward instruments every 15 minutes from 10.00 a.m on every working day. Confirmation of either to pass or return the instrument within the allotted time i.e. T+3 clear hours from hourly session closure is must. The inward instruments will reflect expiry timing within which positive/negative confirmation to be given. If no such conformation is given the pending inward instruments will be treated as deemed honored. Due to a time constraint Central Clearing Dept will be unable to refer any pending instrument to all branches. Therefore branches have to inform all our valued Customers to keep sufficient balance in their account before issuance of the cheques. Further PPS request as applicable should be submitted by our customer in advance to avoid any return of the instrument by central clearing dept. After verifying with item wise report, Central Clearing Dept have to release the funds on hourly basis for the honored instruments.

Branch role and responsibilities:

1.   Cheques should be in compliance with CTS 2010 guidelines to avoid any fraud/forgery. The CTS 2010 guidelines are posted in Exchange Server - Public Folder - Central Clearing.

2.   Timely acceptance of cheque with date and time and entry in our CBS so as to generate mismatch report to avoid wrong posting if any. The cut-off time is 3.00 p.m. The cheques received after 3.00 p.m. with the stamp “Too Late for Today’s clearing” will have to be presented on the next working day for the first session at 10.00 a.m.

3.   Put legible crossing stamp of the bank/branches across the cheque.

4.   UV checking of all the cheques by the branches is a must. UV verified stamp should be affixed on the back side of cheque with staff number and signature. For checking as per CTS 2010 guidelines it should ensure mainly that:

a)   Bank Logo/Emblem- Glow under UV,

b)  CTS-2010 Watermark- "CTS-INDIA" watermark in Oval shape become clearer under UV with normal light,

c)   Verify Void Pantograph: Ensure the cheque does not display “Void” in pantograph. It may become visible when the cheque is scanned, photocopied or tampered.

5.   Branch to ensure PPS confirmation for limit of Rs 5.00 Lakh and above so as to honor the cheque

6.   No alterations on the cheque are allowed.

7.   To write the correct account number in readable form on the backside of the cheque.

8.   If customer has put rubber stamp of A/c number, it should be clear or else to be written correctly.

9.   Branches provided with scanner will have to immediately scan all the cheques after UV checking. In case of rejection if any the same has to be confirmed with central clearing dept and then rescan for further processing at Central Clearing Dept.

10.               Branches provided with RDC M-clear Mobile App will have to scan the cheques after UV checking and filled all the required details as per the training/PPT given on the same. It will be sole responsibility of the respective branch to check the correctness of data entry i.e. account number, amount, date of cheque, payee name and other details filled at the time of scanning.

11.               To verify posting with the help of pay-in-slip with CTS batch and to tally on hourly/daily basis to avoid non/wrong posting. In case of any discrepancies, the same should be informed to Central Clearing Dept immediately for necessary reconciliation/rectification.

12.               KYC of the customers is to be updated on regular basis to avoid any fraud/forgery to protect the interest of the Bank and our staff members as under CTS 2010 the major responsibilities lies on the collecting Banker/Branches.

13.                Cleared stamp should be affixed after clearance of cheques.

14.               In case of any difficulty of scanning of cheque through Mobile App/Scanner or other technical issue, it should be immediately reported to Central Clearing Dept & IT Dept to resolve it for smooth functioning & to extend better customer service in presenting the CTS instruments at the earliest. PPT for use of mobile App and scanner is available in Exchange Server - Public Folder - Central Clearing.

15.               Any lapses/irregularities/complaint from customers in handling CTS activities will be viewed seriously and concerned Staff / Officer /In charge will be held accountable.

 

Central Clearing Department role and responsibilities:

1.   Immediate Data entry of all the cheques presented by those branches where scanners are provided & uploaded in E-desk/SSO module. In case of any discrepancies or non presentation same is to be communicated immediately to respective branches for resolution/rectification.

2.   Cheques presented through RDC M-clear Mobile App will be processed by Central Clearing Dept for onward submission to NPCI.

3.   Timely presentation of cheques to NPCI ensuring and adhering continuous clearing guidelines.

4.   To ensure that the guidelines for PPS has been followed.

5.   Release of posting after each and every session after confirming and tallying with the settlement report.

6.   To monitor continuously the E-Kuber account balance with RBI for maintenance of CRR with the help of our Treasury Department.

7.   In case of inward cheques after verification, the cheques which are in order and are having sufficient balance in account will be honoured and remaining/pending cheques will be returned directly without referring to branch and the return file will be uploaded to NPCI before expiry of the respective session/timings.

8.   To provide support and guide all the branches for smooth functioning of Continuous Clearing.

9.   Daily reporting of Inward and Outward position to Treasury Dept/Higher Authorities.

10.               To download and keep record of report from DEM Server for reconciliation.

 

I.T. Department role and responsibilities:

1.   Continuous support for smooth functioning of Clearing/Nach activities to Central Clearing Dept and Branches.

2.   Uninterrupted system and network support to Central Clearing Dept and Branches.

3.   Timely renewal of all certificates/licenses required for continuous clearing/NACH e.g.HSM Card, Class III certificate for CTS, odyssey encryption & decryption etc.

4.   To provide hardware and software support to branches and Central Clearing Dept.

5.   To provide data of PPS (positive pay system) about issuance of large amount of cheques (Rs.5.00 lacs and above) by our customers.

6.   To ensure timely service and to resolve any CBS issue from vendor immediately.

7.   To make necessary SLA/AMC with the CBS/CTS vendors.

8.   To make arrangement for display of participation of our bank in continuous clearing on Website.

9.   To develop Report Manager/make available required report in CBS.

 

Role of Marketing Department :

1.   Web advertising.

2.   Display of Customer Awareness notice of continuous clearing services on the notice board and also on Led T.V unit located at the branches.

 

Since it is modified system of clearing for improvement in customer service, it is necessary that staff handling the work of clearing at the branches is vigilant while accepting and presenting the cheque.

 

Branches, Central Clearing Department and I.T. Dept should take a suitable note of the above instructions carefully and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

Chief Executive Officer
