Circular OC No. 927 dated 27th May 2025

Opening Three Bank Accounts by Real Estate Developers (Builders) in Respect of every Real Estate Project Registered with MahaRERA

I.     Background

1.    Instructions on the subject of opening additional / new current accounts by Real Estate Developers (Builders) under the Provisions of RERA in Respect of New Projects in the State of Maharashtra were issued through Circular OC No.767 dated the 21stJuly 2017 and Circular OC No.770 dated the 28thJuly 2017.

2.    As per earlier Circulars, a separate project-wise bank account was required for depositing 70% of the amounts collected from the allottees (buyers of flats, shops, tenements, or other real estate units) to be used exclusively towards land and construction cost of the respective project. The promoter could also maintain a regular Current account for other general transactions.

II.   New Guidelines issued by MahaRERA:

1.    MahaRERA, vide Notification No. MahaRERA/Secy/464-70/2024 dated 25/07/2024, has mandated the opening of three designated Bank Accounts in a Scheduled Bank for each Real Estate Project, to be opened prior to applying for project registration.

2.    The three bank accounts are to be named as under.

A.  RERA Designated Collection Bank Account of the Project

B.  RERA Designated Separate Bank Account of the Project

C.  RERA Designated Transaction Bank Account of the Project

III.  Internal instructions for Opening and Maintenance of Accounts

In view of the above, guidelines as under are being issued for opening and maintaining current accounts (under the provisions of RERA) of Builders / Promoters in respect of projects under the jurisdiction of MahaRERA. Branches shall open the accounts with the account code mentioned in the title of the respective Para below.

A.   RERA Designated Collection Bank Account of the Project:

Account code: CDRERAC

The entire amount collected from the allottees shall be deposited in the aforementioned account.

 

B.   RERA Designated Separate Bank Account of the Project:

Account code: CDRERAS

From account designated and referred to as ‘A’ i.e. RERA Designated Collection Bank Account of the Project, an auto- sweep facility shall transfer 70%of the amount deposited therein to the Account referred to as ‘B’ i.e. RERA Designated Separate Bank Account of the Project, to be used for land and construction costs.

 

C.   RERA Designated Transaction Bank Account of the Project:

Account code: CDRERAT

From account designated and referred to as ‘A’ i.e. RERA Designated Collection Bank Account of the Project, an auto-sweep facility shall transfer 30% of the amount deposited therein to the Account referred to as ‘C’ i.e. RERA Designated Transaction Bank Account of the Project, for administrative and other project related expenses.

 

IV.  Accounts of a project for the execution of multiple Builders & Promoters

1.    In the case of a real estate project with multiple Builders & Promoters, a RERA designated Master Bank Account shall be opened, from which funds will be transferred to the Account referred to as ‘A’ above, opened in the name of each of the Builders & Promoters.

2.    Separate instructions for Master Bank Accounts will be issued when such cases arise.

 

V.   Additional Internal instructions

1.   Apportionment and transfer before and after Auto-Sweep is put in place

              i.     Instructions to the IT department

To develop Auto-Sweep functionality to automate 70/30 fund transfers from the Collection Account (CDRERAC) to Separate (CDRERAS) and Transaction (CDRERAT) Accounts respectively.

             ii.     Instructions to the Branches –Manual apportion and transfer until the Auto-Sweep facility is put in place.

As the development of the Auto-Sweep facility may take some time, branches shall MANUALLY apportion and transfer the 70% and 30% of the amount of each deposit in the RERA Designated Collection Bank Account of the Project(CDRERAC) to –

(a)            70% to Designated Separate Bank Account of the Project(CDRERAS) and

(b)            30% to Designated Transaction Bank Account of the Project(CDRERAT).

(c) Branches need to ensure that at the end of the day, Balance in CDRERAC(Collection) account should not be more than minimum balance required.

 

2.   The present project accounts of the Builders / Promoters on our books

Branches shall obtain applications from the Builders / Promoters currently maintaining the project-specific accounts for their closure and open three new designated accounts under the revised guidelines.

VI.  Reiteration of earlier instructions and their extension to the new current accounts of promoters/builders under new guidelines of MahaRERA

1.    Existing Customers: If the Builder /Promoter is already an existing Customer of our Bank, there is no need to obtain additional KYC documents for opening project-wise accounts other than Account Opening Form (AOF-2).

2.    New Customers - The necessary KYC documents will be required as per the extant guidelines along with the Customer Information Form (CIF-2), Account Opening Form (AOF-2) and Customer Information Form (CIF -1 to be taken for all Authorised Signatories) for opening new Current Accounts of Legal Entities or Proprietorship firms. A new customer number will be generated.

3.    Customer Number usage :For opening project-wise accounts, whether of an existing customer or a new customer, the same customer number will be used for opening various project-wise accounts of the same Promoters / Builders. After opening the project-wise account, the Branch-In-Charge under his /her signature will write to the Chief Manager / AGM, IT Dept. for making necessary modifications of the project name in the Account Master for such specific accounts, as per the format given in Annexure I, for the accounts opened under the same customer ID.

 

In view of above, the Accounts Naming Convention would be as given below :

Main Customer Master Name A/c. Project Name

For Example - “M/s. XYZ Developers” is opening a RERA CD Account with us for his “Palav’s Phase I” project, then the Customer Master will be created as “M/s. XYZ Developers” and account master to be modified for adding the name of “Palav’s Phase I”. Finally, the nomenclature of account would be – “M/s. XYZ Developers A/c Palav’s Phase I”

 

4.    The Builders / Promoters can withdraw amount from such separate project-wise account i.e. CDRERAS after it is certified by an engineer, an architect and a chartered accountant (in practice) specifically stating that the withdrawal is in proportion to the percentage of completion of the project. However, due to the relaxation given by Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority, it has been decided to obtain a self-declaration from the promoter once in every quarter (as per Annexure II).

 

5.   It should be noted that the Cheque Book for such newly opened Current Account(CDRERAS - RERA Designated Separate Bank Account & CDRERAT - RERA Designated Transaction Bank Account) with the specific project-name should be issued only after the Builders / Promoters submits the RERA Registration Certificate to the branch. It should be ensured that the cheque book is issued only after a suitable name-change is given effect in the related Account Masters.

 

6.    For opening all the three CDRERA accounts as mentioned in paragraph II(3) above, branches are advised to obtain only one Account Opening Form (AOF2). Branches shall prepare a stamp of proper size (as given below ) and put the same on the top of Account Opening Form (AOF2) for writing account numbers.

Note : It is to be ensured that all the three accounts ( CDRERAC/CDRERAS/CDRERAT) get same account numbers for each project wise RERA Account.

 

7.    Attention of branches is also invited to the Circular CR No. 183 dated the 18thFebruary 2017 on the subject of “Current Accounts Maintained by Cash Credit Account Holders with Other Banks” in which the necessity to adhere to inter-bank discipline while opening Current accounts of borrowing parties in order not to encourage diversion of funds, and the precautions to be taken in this behalf, have been highlighted. Branches are advised to ensure that the real estate Developers (Builders/Promoters) do not resort to diversion of funds by opening additional current account with us. Therefore, it has been decided to continue obtaining an Undertaking- cum-Indemnity from the real estate developer (Builders/Promoters) party desirous of opening a current account with us before acceding to their request. The draft of the Undertaking-cum-Indemnity to be obtained in this behalf is given in Annexure III of this circular. Needless to add that all other formalities including KYC compliance should be completed before any such account is opened.

8.    The instructions issued in the current circular are applicable only to Branches located in the state of Maharashtra. The Branches in the states of Karnataka and Gujarat shall continue to follow the earlier instructions as per OC No.767 dated the 21stJuly 2017 & OC No.770 dated the 28thJuly 2017.

 

9.    Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

Chief Executive Officer
