Circular OC No. 940 dated 17th December, 2025

“Your Money, Our Priority”

One year Campaign for re-activation of Inoperative Accounts

and settlement of Unclaimed Deposits

______________________________________________________________________________

I.    Introduction

1.    Reserve Bank of India vide their letter dated 01.10.2025 has introduced a “Scheme for facilitating Accelerated Payout – Inoperative Accounts and Unclaimed Deposits” for all the banks registered under the DEA Fund scheme to encourage banks to actively pursue customers/depositors for re-activation of their inoperative accounts and return of their unclaimed amounts lying with DEA Fund to the rightful claimant in a timely and efficient manner.

2.    The Scheme aims to reduce both the stock of existing unclaimed deposits and fresh accretion of flows to the DEA Fund by encouraging return to the rightful claimants. All inoperative accounts at banks and unclaimed deposits transferred to DEA Fund which remain unsettled, are covered under the scheme. The scheme would be open for a period of 1 year i.e. from 01.10.2025 to 30.09.2026.

3.    Inoperative/Dormant account and Unclaimed Deposits are explained as under:

a)   Inoperative Account- A savings/ current account shall be treated as inoperative, if there are no ‘customer induced transactions’ in the account for a period of over two years as defined in our policy on Inoperative/Dormant Accounts and Unclaimed Deposits.

 

b)  Unclaimed Deposits- The credit balance in any deposit account maintained with the Bank, which has not been operated upon for ten years or more, or any amount remaining unclaimed for ten years or more as mentioned in RBI Letter No. RBI/2013-14/527 DBOD.No.DEAF Cell.BC.101/30.01.002/2013-14 dated 21st March, 2014 under paragraph 3(iii) of the “Depositor Education and Awareness” (DEA) Fund Scheme, 2014.

(Note: Our Bank has Policy on Inoperative/Dormant Account & Unclaimed Deposits which is to be referred from KYC & AML Public Folder)

II.   Campaign in our Bank “Your Money, Our Priority”

To promote the revival and support the task, our Bank is introducing one year campaign “Your Money, Our Priority” for re-activation of Inoperative Accounts & settlement of Unclaimed Deposits transferred to DEA Fund-RBI.

Campaign is focusing on the A) re-activation of Inoperative accounts to Operative status and B) settlement of Unclaimed Deposits.

 

III.  Our Aim

1.    Regulatory compliance & efficiency: Bank will improve their Regulatory Compliance by streamlining the KYC updation.

2.    Enhanced customer Trust & Goodwill: Proactively helping customers for recovering their forgotten money, builds trust and improves the Bank’s reputation, potentially leading to stronger long-term customer relationships & will help to create/expand business opportunities by offering additional product & services.

 

IV.  List of action proposed under this campaign are as under:

A.   Role of Branch

a)  For revival of Inoperative/Dormant Accounts :

1.    KYC/AML Dept. will provide list of Inoperative/Dormant Accounts on monthly basis to branches/B. D. & O. for tracing the customers to activate their accounts.

 

2.    Each branch shall entrust the task of tracing and contacting the depositors by all the methods as under:

i.      Customers will be contacted over the phone or through email

ii.     Visit to customers residence/ office/ business place.

iii.   Contact the customer by approaching the introducer or the neighbours and getting information on his/her present whereabouts.

 

3.    Branches will be required to address queries received from customers and extend necessary assistance for completing KYC formality as per latest KYC guidelines.

(Note: Refer Policy on KYC & AML, CFT & CRC (2025-26) from KYC & AML Public Folder) for revival of account.

 

Points to be noted:

                                i.       As the account turns Dormant due to there being no customer-induced transactions in the account for the previous more than two years, branches should invariably request the customer to do at least one transaction after activation of the account & keep the account operative.

                              ii.       KYC updation is mandatory prior to activation – A financial service/transaction cannot be activated without completion of the initial KYC process i.e. updation of KYC Master in the system.

4.    Further branches to carry out updation of required fields as per given format – Annexure A (ensure the data is entered in the format and guidelines provided in the format) immediately after completing of KYC formalities & activation of account.

5.    Branches will submit the updated data as per Annexure A to Accounts Dept. on or before 5th of the succeeding month (i.e. within 5 days) on monthly basis for confirmation and reporting to RBI.

b)  For settlement/claim of Unclaimed Deposits (Transferred to DEA Fund-RBI) :

 

1.    List of Unclaimed Deposits will be shared with branches by Accounts Department on monthly basis.

 

2.    Each branch requires to take efforts for tracing and contacting the Unclaimed depositors by all the methods mentioned in para IV.A.(a)(2).

 

3.    Branches should verify the details & conduct KYC exercise and after being satisfied may refund the deposits alongwith interest in consultation with the Accounts Dept. (The Accounts Department performs the necessary calculations to determine the final Claim amount). Refund may be claimed by branches from HO by submitting “Unclaimed Deposits: Common Claim Application Form” - Annexure B & send copy to Accounts Department for record.

 

(Note: Branches are advised to refer to instructions given in Circular OC No. 801 dated 16.08.2018, on the subject “The Depositor Education and Awareness Fund (DEAF) Scheme, 2014 – Section 26A of Banking Regulation Act, 1949 – Operational Guidelines”).

 

B.   Role of KYC & AML Department

1.    KYC/AML Dept. will provide list of Inoperative/Dormant Accounts on monthly basis to branches/B.D.&O. for tracing the customers to activate their accounts.

2.    KYC Department will guide the branches for mandatory KYC updation required for reactivation of Inoperative/Dormant accounts & ensures the branches adheres to guidelines set by RBI under the Master Direction on KYC.

C.   Role of Accounts Department

1.    Accounts Department will provide list of Unclaimed Deposits (Transfer to DEA Fund) to branches/B.D.&O.

2.    To finalize data of activated accounts as per Annexure A & Annexure B submitted by Branches on monthly basis.

3.    To prepare report in RBI prescribed format on quarterly basis & the same will be certified by Internal Auditor of the bank. After certification, Accounts Dept. will submit the report to RBI within one month from the end of the Quarter.

4.    The review of Inoperative/Dormant Accounts and Unclaimed Deposits submitted to RBI will be undertaken by Accounts Dept. on monthly basis and report of such data will be kept ready for audit purpose, if necessary.

5.    Submission of Claim/Reporting:

                        i.        Accounts Department should submit the Claim form –Settlement of Unclaimed Deposits/Reactivation of Inoperative Accounts for payout on quarterly basis in the prescribed format within one month at the end of each quarter to RBI duly signed by authorised Senior Executive of the Bank.

                      ii.        RBI will settle the claim of payout for the successful activation of Inoperative accounts & settlement of Unclaimed Deposits on the basis of Ageing category & Amount settled/reactivated within 30 days from its quarterly submission. Accounts department will pass necessary entries after receipt of claim of payout from RBI.

6.    To place Quarterly report of Claim Form submitted alongwith claim received from RBI before the Administrator’s Committee for review and noting.

D.   Role of B.D.&O.

B.D. & O. will review & monitor progress of campaign of branches on weekly basis.

E.   Role of Inspection Department

1.    The Inspection Department (Internal Auditors) will verify on monthly basis that the identification & proper classification of Inoperative/Dormant Accounts to Operative (Annexure A) are as per RBI guidelines, on the basis of list provided by Accounts Department.

 

2.    Certification for verification of data (Settlement of Unclaimed Deposits/Reactivation of Inoperative Accounts) prepared by Accounts Department as per RBI prescribed format on Quarterly basis before submission to RBI.

F.    Role of I.T. Department

To generate proper report with actual/correct date of reclassification of Inoperative Accounts to Operative Accounts & submit the same to Accounts Department in the format provided by Accounts Department on monthly basis

Branches should make all-out efforts to ensure that Inoperative Accounts /Unclaimed Deposits of customers are converted into operative accounts & create/expand business opportunities by offering additional product & services.

 

Branches/Departments are advised to ensure strict compliance with the above directives, so as to ensure that the drive is carried out successfully.

 

 

Chief Executive Officer
