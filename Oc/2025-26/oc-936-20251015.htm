Circular OC No. 936 dated 15th October 2025

Obtaining CKYC Number from New Customer before

On-boarding, (Opening of SB\CD\TD\LN Accounts)

 ____________________________________________________________________________________

 

In view of the recent regulatory changes mandating that CKYCR (Central KYC Registry) uploads be completed within T+1 day from commencement of an account-based relationship (as against the earlier timelines of 10 days for fresh on on-boarding and 7 days for re-kyc, it is now mandatory to obtain and confirm the CKYC number of any proposed customer before opening an account.

 

The requirement arises from RBI’s Master Direction - Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016 (Updated as on August 14, 2025) and CERSAI’S operational guidelines. Accordingly, branches shall strictly follow the instructions given below with immediate effect from issue of this circular.

 

1.       Collection of CKYC number Prior to On-Boarding

Before opening any account (SB/CD/TD/LN), Branch branch staff must obtain from the proposed customer their CKYC Number (Central KYC Number), if already allotted\known.

Customers can retrieve their CKYC Number through any of the following methods:

1. Give a missed call to 7799022129 from their registered mobile number.

2. Download from CERSAI portal – www.ckycindia.in

3. Fetch through Digi Locker

 

Note: Many customers, especially those in urban areas, already possess CKYC numbers due to investments in insurance, securities, or other financial products. Hence, branch staff must specifically ask all prospective customers (and/or their family heads / CA, etc) about the availability of a CKYC Number before initiating account opening, and record the same in the CIF form.

         (As per CERSAI, over 106 Crore Indians already possess CKYC number.)

 

2.       Verification and Download of CKYC Details

The CKYC Number provided by the proposed customer must be verified and details downloaded from the CERSAI portal prior to On-Boarding of customer (account creation in CBS).

For such customers, no OVD ( Officially Valid Documents ) is to be collected, if the details given on the CIF and details downloaded from CERSAI are unchanged.

The branch shall:

a.    Immediately send the CKYC Number (as provided by the customer) along with the duly filled-in and signed Consent Form ( till CIF 1 integration is enabled) to CDEC–CKYC Section through the exchange server in the format given in Annexure.

 

b.    Call the CDEC–CKYC Section immediately after sending the mail.

c.     When CDEC initiates data download from CERSAI, an OTP will be sent to the customer. The branch must collect and promptly share this OTP with CDEC–CKYC Section to enable data retrieval from CERSAI portal.

 

Note : The CDEC – CKYC Section will operate between 8 a.m. – 9 p.m. (in two shifts) to support double-shift branches. Staff details for this purpose will be shared separately.

 

3.       Exceptions – When OVD is Required

Customer shall not be required to submit the same KYC records or information or any other additional identification documents or details, unless–

              i.         there is a change in the information of the customer as existing in the records of CKYCR; or

            ii.         the KYC record or information retrieved is incomplete or is not as per the current applicable KYC norms; or

           iii.        the validity period of downloaded documents has lapsed; or

           iv.        the it is necessary in order to verify the identity or address (including current address) of the customer, or to perform enhanced due diligence or to build an appropriate risk profile of the customer.

 

4.       Where CKYC Number is Not Known or Not Available.

If the customer is unsure or does not have a CKYC Number, the branch shall:

a.   Send the duly filled Consent Form through the exchange server in the format given in Annexure to CDEC–CKYC Section.

b.   CDEC will attempt to locate the CKYC record using PAN, OVD, and Date of Birth on the CERSAI portal.

c.   If a match is found, CDEC will share the downloaded CKYC document with the branch (after OTP verification through the branch).

d.   If no match is found, CDEC will confirm the same through email reply.

 

5.       When No CKYC Record Exists

Where no details of customer are available on CERSAI portal, the Branches will follow the existing account opening procured as per standing instructions issued vide various OC/OO. Branches shall ensure timely creation of Customer Master ( data entry & authorization in respective screen/Master ( Express Customer Master, DMS Master, CKYC Master etc.) and document scanning in iSolve-IDMS has to be completed on the same day ( before EOD). The CKYC number will then be generated automatically and communicated to the customer via SMS / email within T+1 day.

 

6.       Existing Customers Requesting CKYC Number

Where our existing customer approaches us for knowing their CKYC number, then

1.   Check our CBS-DMS master for checking the availability of the CKYC Number.

2.   If no CKYC number is found in our CBS-DMS master, ask the customer to retrieve it using of the three methods enumerated in Point No. 1 above.

3.   Once CKYC Number is received, send the CKCY number to CDEC – CKYC section ( in the attached format ) along with consent form of the customer for verification and download of CKYC record.

 

7.       Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned and ensure strict adherence to this new procedure.

 

Chief Executive Officer
