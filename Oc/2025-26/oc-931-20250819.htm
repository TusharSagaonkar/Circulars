Circular OC No. 931 dated 19st August 2025

Policy on KYC, AML, CFT & CRC (2025-26)

 

I.                Our "Policy on KYC, AML, CFT & CRC” was last reviewed on 5th March 2024 with the suffix “2024-25” to include all the operative instructions on the subject issued by Reserve Bank of India until then. The same was posted in the Public Folder of the KYC & AML for guidance and follow up action by branches and the departments concerned.

 

II.    The RBI has since then, made amendments in the Master Direction on Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction-2016, on 06/11/2024. The consequent revised instructions have already been communicated to the branches through the Bank’s internal circulars.

 

III.   The Bank’s policy on the subject has now been revised and updated in light of the amendments in the Master Direction of the RBI. The same titled " Policy on KYC, AML, CFT & CRC -2025-26" is being circularised herewith after due approval by Administrator’s Committee and comes into effect immediately. The Policy is being placed in the Public Folder of the KYC & AML under titled “KYC & AML Policy”.

 

IV.  The Bank’s instant revised and updated Policy on KYC, AML, CFT & CRC will be in force until the next review. The policy will be reviewed annually or at an earlier date, as may be required by the Bank. “Post the approval of the Policy, any changes made by the RBI on the rules and regulations in respect of KYC, AML, CFT & CRC shall form an integral part of the Policy, pending their formal inclusion at the time of the next renewal of the Policy.”

 

V.    As the document contains important and confidential information in respect of our Bank's policies, all the members of the staff are advised not to part with any copy thereof whether in part or in its entirety, and particularly avoid/sharing information therein with any other Bank or a person outside the Bank.

 

VI.  Please bring the instructions contained herein to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

 

Annexure

Chief Executive Officer
