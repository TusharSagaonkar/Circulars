Circular OC No.929 dated 30th June 2025

Remittances of Funds through RTGS and NEFT Revised Limit w.e.f. 01st July 2025

 

RBI vide its circular No.CO.DOS.CSITEG.SEC No 9/31-01-015/2024-25 dated 14/08/2024 issued guidelines regarding Strengthening of SFMS related IT and Operational Controls. As per said circular all transactions should be directly remitted from CBS to Beneficiary Bank without intervention of RTGS / NEFT Cell. Also refer our Circular OC No. 917 dated 12th November 2024.

(I)     As per Circular OC No. 917 dated 12th November 2024, all RTGS/NEFT outward transactions upto Rs.10.00 lakh was routed directly from Branches to the Beneficiary Banks’ Branches without involving the RTGS/NEFT cell.

 

(II)    The required changes as mentioned in above Paragraph were placed before Administrator’s Committee & met with approval.

 

(III)  The system will push the RTGS/NEFT transactions upto Rs.10.00 Crore directly from Branch to the Beneficiary’s Bank’s Account through SFMS. These transactions will now be authenticated by a Server Level Digital Certificate at STP (Straight Through process) Level without involving the Digital Signature of RTGS/NEFT cell Staff.

 

(IV)  For management of funds, the confirmation mail of RTGS/NEFT outward transactions should be sent to Treasury Department & RTGS/NEFT cell for transactions of Rs.50.00 lakh & above before initiating the transactions.

 

(V)   As there is no check at RTGS/NEFT Cell, Bank has decided to fix threshold limit of Rs.10.00 crore for outward RTGS/NEFT transactions beyond which No amount can be remitted.

 

(VI)  If any Branches/Departments want to remit fund above Rs.10.00 crore then Branches/Departments should intimate the amount one day prior to RTGS/NEFT Cell through e-mail. Further, the mail should be sent from mail ID of Branch/Departmental Head only.

 

(VII) Branches/Departments should note that the maker and checker at the Branch/Department would be held responsible for all RTGS/NEFT transactions just like other fund transfer transactions. Hence Staff and Officials of Branches and Departments are advised to exercise utmost care and more than ordinary diligence while entering/checking details of all RTGS/NEFT outward transactions. Branches/ Departments should note that once the transaction is authorized in CBS system the same cannot be cancelled irrespective of the amount.

 

 

(VIII)  The IT Department is advised to incorporate the above revised procedure in the OMNI system.

All Branches are advised to note the above changes in the procedure and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all Officials/Staff and ensure compliance.

 

 

Chief Executive Officer
