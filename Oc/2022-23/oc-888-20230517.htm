Circular OC No. 888 dated 17th May 2023

 

 

1.     Service Charges for non-maintenance of minimum balances in on saving/current accounts.

2.     Minimum balance to be reckoned on monthly average basis and not on the basis of single-day instances

3.    Retrospective effect from 1st April 2023

         

I.   Background

Please refer to Circular OC No. 884 dated 1st April 2023 and the Service Charges Manual circularised under its cover. The reference is particularly invited to entries at Sr. No. 1 (a) and Sr. No. 2 (a) pertaining to the charges to be applied to the savings and current accounts in which balances are not maintained at the minimum levels indicated therein. The minimum balance in the accounts were implicitly reckoned on each single day instance for the application of the service charge.

 

II.      Modification with retrospective effect in method of reckoning minimum balance

1.   The matter has now been reexamined and it has been decided to reckon the minimum balances in saving and current accounts on the basis of monthly average [instead of (implicit) each single day instance].

2.  This change in the method of reckoning the monthly average balance is with retrospective effect from 1st April 2023.

 

III. All other charges and the manner of determining their incidence as given in the said Service Charges Manual remain the same.

 

IV. Instructions to IT department.

IT department is hereby being instructed to build the necessary logic in calculation on monthly average balances in respect of the saving accounts and current accounts (differently for branches in different regions or for groups of branches as indicated in the Service Charges Manual under reference). Needless to add that the charges should be applied on the accounts automatically.

 

V.  Instruction to branches to calculate and refund the service charge applied from 1st April 2023 on the basis of reckoning of single day instance

Branches are hereby being instructed to identify the services charges applied due to non-maintenance of the minimum balance in saving and current accounts reckoned on the basis of (implicit) single day instances and reverse and refund them to the customers.

 

VI.             Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
