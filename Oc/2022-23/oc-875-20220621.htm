Circular OC No. 875 dated 21st June 2022

Policy on Managing Risk in Outsourcing of Financial Services

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.   In terms of the circular bearing reference No. RBI/2021–22/64 DOR. ORG. REC.27/21.04.158/2021-22, the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has advised Banks to conduct a self-assessment of risk in outsourcing financial services, and align it with the guidelines contained therein. Accordingly, Planning & Marketing Department had placed a submission in the Board meeting held on 20th January, 2022 on Guidelines for Managing Risks in Outsourcing of Financial Services and the same met with approval.

 

II.    The policy, thus duly approved, is hereby being circularised as Annexure – I for strict adherence there to by all the offices and departments who engage vendors and entities for the purpose of conducting financial services on behalf of the Bank. We are also enclosing Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for easy reference & compliance as Annexure – II for all the offices and departments who engage vendors.

 

III.   In this connection, further specific instructions are being issued as under.

1.    The department concerned and responsible for the activity shall prepare the proposal in respect of the outsourcing arrangement in light of the policy, and also prepare a draft of the contract with the vendors incorporating therein the Service Level Agreements (SLAs) including, but not limited to, clauses pertaining to performance, penalties, reporting, and manpower deployment.

2.    The proposal for the outsourcing arrangement, together with the draft of the contract / agreement, shall be put up to the Board for in-principle approval.

3.    Upon receiving the in-principle approval, the draft of the contract/ agreement shall be sent to the Central Law Department for vetting.

4.    The duly vetted draft shall be executed by the departmental head after getting fulfilled all the prerequisites such as Earnest Money Deposit etc.

5.    The activities which are outsourced shall be reviewed periodically and, in any case, at least three months before the expiry of the contract, by the concerned department. To enable a qualitative review, a careful record of the breaches of SLAs etc. shall be maintained.

 

IV.    Role of the Planning and Marketing Department

1.    The Planning & Marketing Department is required to review the outsourcing policy in consultation with the IT Department on a periodical basis.

2.    It is also required to compile the information of all the activities outsourced by the Bank and to keep track of them.

3.    The information in respect of outsourcing by all the departments is to be submitted by the Planning & Marketing Department to the Board for review.

4.    The list of critical outsourced activities is to be supplied by the Planning & Marketing Department to the Inspection and IS Audit Department for ensuring that regular audit is carried out of such activities.

5.    In order to enable it to carry out this function, all the departments and offices are advised to send to the Planning & Marketing Department, the details of the activities currently outsourced by them.

6.    Subsequently, all the arrangements of outsourcing, whether renewal of existing ones or new altogether, shall be reported by the department concerned to the Planning & Marketing Department for compilation.

 

V.   Role of the IT Department

 

The IT department is entrusted with the task of maintaining the data of outsourced activities in a dedicated portal under Centralised Data Maintenance.

 

VI.  Departments at Head Office, Zonal Offices and Regional Offices will maintain at their end the records of services and activities outsourced by them.

 

VII. Please bring the contents of this instant circular to the notice of all the members of staff concerned for compliance.
