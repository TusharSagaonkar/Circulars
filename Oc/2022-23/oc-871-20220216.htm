Circular OC No. 871 dated 16th February 2022

Setting up Customer Service Committees

Functions and Operationalisation

I.  Background

1.    Excellent customer service in banking industry is a right of customers. Apart from this, it is a key differentiator among various players when considered against the backdrop of universalisation of banking products and technology.

2. i.    Reserve Bank of India has also issued circulars on the subject. Earlier Master

  Circular no. RBI/2005-06/129 UBD. BPD. (PCB).MC.No.10/09.39.00/2005-06 dated 18th August 2005 gave instructions, including implementation and monitoring of 15 core recommendations in this behalf, to UCBs. The latest Master Circular applicable to UCBs on the subject of Customer Service is the one bearing no. RBI/2015-16/61 DCBR.CO.BPD.(PCB).MC.No.15/ 12.05.001/ 2015-16 dated 1st July 2015, and the same amplifies and supplements the previous recommendations on the subject in light of the developments in the banking sector and technology, and further includes the recommendations of the Committee on procedure & performance audit on public services (CPPAPA) as also others.

ii.  The instructions which are already implemented centrally in our Bank are summarised in the table given in Annexure-I hereto. They are required to be monitored at Branch level every month, & at Board Level every 6 months i.e. in June & December.

iii. It is noteworthy that some of the recommendations as per the latest Master circular from RBI require time for implementation as they involve deliberation, planning and/or expenditure. The departments concerned are advised separately to initiate action in those matters.

 

II.  Approval by Board to set up Customer Service Committees in a three-tiered structure

While many aspects of customer service stand already covered through various systems and mechanisms adopted by Bank, a need was identified to set up Customer Service Committees at three levels, namely Board, Zonal Offices and Branches, through which a system of periodical review and monitoring of the level of customer service, particularly in light of the instructions from RBI, would be put in place. A submission in this behalf was placed before the Board meeting dated 27th September 2021 and the same was approved for implementation. Accordingly, instructions as under are being issued.

III.   Constitution and functioning of committees

1. The constitution of committees and periodicity of their meetings shall be as per Annexure-II (at branch level), Annexure-III (at the level of Zonal Office) and Annexure-IV (at the level of Board). It should be noted that constitution of the committees is different for different levels.

2. Agenda for the meetings of the three-tiered committees as per sub-Para 1. above, recordings of minutes [only in objective form (i.e. “Whether compliant: YES/NO”) of substantive action points] with resultant action [in the form of Action-Taken-Report (ATR), which shall be integrated with minutes], and reporting of Minutes-cum-action-points-and-ATR, are also indicated in the relative Annexures.

3. i)    The   process  of  examination  of  standards  of  customer  service  to remove

  deficiencies has been built from Branches upwards through Zones to the level of the Board. The process of monitoring, review, feedback and instructions is designed to take place from higher level downwards.

ii)  Minutes-cum-action-points-and-ATR by branches shall be transmitted by them to the Zonal Customer Service Committees by email at the end of each month.

iii) a) Each Zonal Customer Service Committee shall examine branch-wise Minute-

 cum-action-points and ATR in its meeting to be held in each month.

b) The residual points (i.e. after leaving out those resolved at the lower level, thus requiring no deliberation at the higher level) out of the Minutes-cum-action-points-and-ATR received from branches shall be listed for deliberation and action at Zonal Office. While listing, non-substantive and highly case-specific points may be edited out.

c) Zonal Customer Service Committee may take up deficiencies perceived at its own end, deliberate thereupon and prepare a consolidated Minutes-cum-action-points-and-ATR  incorporating action points for the Zone which shall also include residual points culled from counterpart reports from branches.

d) Zonal Customer Service Committee shall submit such consolidated Minutes-cum-action-points-and-ATR to the Secretarial department at Head Office at the end of each month of the meeting at Zonal Office level.

4. i.    Henceforth, the reports  (a)  on complaints,  and,  (b) in respect of “Delays in

  Settlement of Claims to Assets of Deceased Constituents” (vide IDC bearing Ref. No. MD-920/1638 /2020-21 dated 4th February 2021), shall be compiled by Zonal offices on the basis of similar reports from branches under them, and submitted along with the Minutes-cum-action-points-and-ATR to the Secretarial department. Review thereof will constitute two agenda points of the Board Level Committee on Customer Service; in this connection, please see sub-Paras d) and e) of sub-Para iv. below.

ii.  Secretarial department shall consolidate the “Complaints received at H.O. Level, complaints received from ATM Recon & I. T. Dept under the head “Report on complaints”  & also consolidate the report on “Claims to the assets of deceased constituents” for whole Bank. These reports will form part of agenda for monthly meeting of Board Level Committee on Customer Service.

iii. a. To facilitate compilation and consolidation of reports on complaints,  tables are provided in Annexure-II, Annexure-III, and Annexure-IV. Information to be filled in them should be accurate, qualitatively as also arithmetically. All the complaints should be included; the principle of unique inclusion due to commonality across several agenda-points shall not apply in the case of number of complaints [i.e. the point no. iv. b] below will not be applicable for compiling this table] as, otherwise, there will be no arithmetical accuracy. At the same time, care should be taken to avoid double counting, as several complaints are referred by senior levels to lower levels for action/ resolution/comments. As a rule, lower level in the hierarchy should include such cases in their statistics.

 

b. Monitoring “Delays in Settlement of Claims to Assets of Deceased Constituents” and compilation of the relative report

·     As there is already a system of reporting by branches of “Delays in Settlement of Claims to Assets of Deceased Constituents” (vide IDC bearing Ref. No. MD-920/1638/2020-21 dated 4th February 2021), it will be sufficient for branches to attach the said report (Annexures I and II of the said IDC) to the “Minutes-cum-action-points-and-ATR” of the meeting of Branch Level Committee on Customer Service. A mention of having attached the said report on “Delays in Settlement of Claims to Assets of Deceased Constituents” shall however be made in the Minutes-cum-action-with-ATR.

·     Each Zonal Office will check reports on “Delays in Settlement of Claims to Assets of Deceased Constituents” received from branches under its control and consolidate it into a report for the Zone which will be attached to the “Minutes-cum-action-points-and-ATR” of the meeting of Zonal Committee on Customer Service. A mention of having attached the said report for the Zone on “Delays in Settlement of Claims to Assets of Deceased Constituents” shall however be made in the relative Minutes-cum-action-with-ATR.

 

iv.  a) Secretarial department at Head Office will examine the Minutes-cum-action-points-and-ATR received from Zonal Offices, and edit and consolidate the same for Bank as a whole, for being placed before the Board Level Committee on Customer Service.

 

b) While editing, common points across branches or zones may be given representation uniquely. Non-substantive and highly case-specific points, which may have erroneously passed through screening at branch or zonal level and therefore got included, may be eliminated. However, points in respect of some cases which have wider implications for remedial action or policy formulation shall be retained.

c) Secretarial department will include the report on complaints as consolidated from those received from Zonal Offices and place it before the Board Level Committee on Customer Service.

d) Likewise, Secretarial department will include the report on status of claims made to the deposits/contents of lockers as compiled from reports received from Zonal Offices and place it before the Board Level Committee on Customer Service.

e) Secretarial department shall also include therein some action points which may come in its knowledge through circulars from RBI, IBA etc. and deliberations of the meeting of Nodal Officers convened by RBI.

f) The Whole-bank Minutes-cum-action-points-and-ATR, together with

·        proposed action if any in respect of points remaining unresolved during the internal process of upward reporting,

·        further supplemented by proposed action points identified by Secretarial department through circulars from RBI, IBA etc., and,

·        action points emerging from meetings of Nodal Officers convened by RBI, shall be placed by Secretarial department before the Board Level Committee on customer service to be convened every month.

v.  It should be clear from the above that the structure of Minutes-cum-action-points-and-ATR is different for the Customer Service Committees at three levels.

vi. a)   Board Level Committee on Customer Service may give directions for action in respect of some specific cases remaining unresolved and/or decide upon modification of an existing policy or formulation of a new policy to plug loop holes in the prevailing systems and procedures to further the cause of excellent customer service.

b)   It may invite inputs from various departments for modifying the systems and procedures for improvement of customer service. Suitable action may be decided after examination of inputs.

c)    Responsibility of communicating action decided by Board Level Committee on Customer Service to respective departments/offices/branches etc. shall be that of the Secretarial department.

 

IV. It is emphasised that the process of monitoring and review of the standards of customer service with a view to making them excellent is accorded the highest priority. Therefore, all the branches, offices and departments shall explain the importance of this circular to the members of the staff and follow the process in letter and spirit.

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
