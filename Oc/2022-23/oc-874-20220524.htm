Circular OC No. 874 dated 24th May 2022

 

1)    Change in forms for KYC Updation – to be obtained from Individual and Non-Individual entities ;

2)    Operations in KYC Due Accounts,  and

3)    New Account Opening Form of NRE/NRO/FCNR/RFC Deposit Accounts

 

I.  Change in  forms for KYC Updation to be obtained from Individual and Non-individual  entities

1.     As per extant RBI guidelines, Bank is required to carry out the KYC process afresh (updation) for all legacy customers as per their Risk Profile at following intervals:

i.      High-Risk customers               : - Every 2 years,

ii.     Medium-Risk customers         : - Every 8 years

iii.   Low-Risk customers                :-  Every 10 years

2.     The process for Risk Categorization based on various parameters is executed by our system every 6 months and its results get reflected in the Customer Master  2nd screen in the field “AML Rating” (in three categories namely 1-Low Risk, 2-Medium Risk or 3-High Risk). The said Risk Categorization process is executed for all the customers.

 

3.     All the customers in respect of whose accounts the period of 10, 8 or 2 years (as per the respective risk profile since their last KYC updation) has crossed/lapsed, are sent letters reminding them to update the profiles/KYC details once again. At the time of doing any transaction a pop-up message “KYC Details Due from the Customer. Continue ?” to alert the front office staff to procure the KYC Documents from the KYC-due customer is flashed on the screen.

 

4.     KYC updation is an ongoing process and the Bank is required to strictly adhere to the instructions in this behalf. Follow-up, appropriate to the Risk-profile of the customers, is done from time to time by our Centralised KYC/AML Dept. by sending letters (Advance / 1st letter / Reminder letter), and text messages (SMS) to all KYC Due Customers.

5. i. In most cases, a customer, either on his/her/their own or because of the public

 awareness campaign, individual letter, telephone call, text message, or visit by the branch staff, approaches the base branch to provide his/her/their latest KYC documents or Declaration towards the KYC updation.

ii. Currently, the  instructions  to  the  front  office  staff  in  cases as per sub-Para i.

above are to provide the customers with the Form no.1100 (for Individuals) and the Form no.1101 (for Legal Entity customers) for obtaining, under authentication, the information necessary for updating the basic Profile of the Customer. The said form is also known as the “KYC Profile / Updation form”.

6. i. Now it has been decided to discontinue obtaining the said two forms i.e. in

 the FATCA Executive Committee Meeting held on 23rd May, 2022.

a.  Form No. 1100 for “Individual”

b. Form No. 1101 for “Non-Individual” entities

ii. a. Henceforth, the CIF-I form, duly filled in, will be obtained from an Individual Customer for Profile updation (instead of Form No. 1100).

 b.   Similarly, the CIF-II form, duly filled in, will henceforth be obtained from a Non-Individual Customer for Profile updation (instead of Form No. 1101).

However, in both the above cases (6.ii.a. and 6.ii.b.), the CIF Form must accompany the respective OVDs. If there is no change in the constitution/address of the customer, in such case the New Declaration format for Individual/Non-Individuals should be obtained as a substitute for the CIF-I / CIF-II form. The new format of Declaration has been enclosed herewith for ready reference and the same is also placed in the Public Folder of the KYC/AML Dept.

II.   Operations in KYC Due Accounts :

Currently, the Clerical Staff at front desks such as Cash, RTGS/NEFT, Term Deposits and Internal transfers Counters allow transactions in the KYC due accounts without asking the customers to update the KYC, even though the message of  “KYC Details Due from the Customer. Continue?” is flashed/ displayed by the System.

Now, it has been decided that the Clerical Staff shall not allow Debit or Credit transactions in the KYC due accounts without obtaining the written approval of Officers/Asstt. Managers concerned, in order to ensure that KYC and Profile are updated in all KYC due accounts as part of the Statutory Compliance.

III.  New Account Opening Form - NRE / NRO / FCNR / RFC Deposits Accounts

1.   Our existing NRE / NRO / FCNR / RFC account opening form has been modified and printed anew to include the FATCA/CRS declaration and consent to be obtained from the customer for Aadhar authentication services. The newly printed account opening form for NRE/NRO account bears code no.FX(NRE)01-1030/2000/S.E/1-2021. The said form is to be used for the opening of accounts w.e.f. 1st June 2022.

 

2.   The stock of old (NRE/NRO) account opening forms bearing code no. FX(NRE)01-1030/1000/M.P.P/09-2021, lying at branches is to be destroyed at their level under intimation to Development Department.

 

3.   All the NRE/NRO customers approaching the bank for KYC updation, are necessarily required to submit a CIF-I form along with KYC documents. Mere submission of a declaration will not suffice in their case due to the reason that there is no provision for FATCA/CRS declaration in the prescribed “Self-Declaration” format.

 

4.   All NRE / NRO / FCNR / RFC Deposits Accounts should be opened ONLY in Account type “6. NRER” and “7. NROR” in OMNI System.

IV.   Inspection Dept. is instructed to check the compliance of the above instructions at the time of inspection of the branches.

 

V.    Please bring the instructions contained herein to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned and ensure strict compliance.

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
