Circular OC No. 883 Dated 24th March 2023

 

1.  Latest Change in Instructions Regarding Locker Agreements

2.  Replacement of all “OLD” and “NEW” Existing Locker Agreements with

“REVISED” Locker Agreements to be completed before 31st December, 2023

________________________________________________________________________________________

 

I. 1. The Reserve Bank of India (RBI), recently issued the communication bearing Ref. No. RBI / 2022 – 23 / 168 dated 23rd January, 2023 informing Banks that the previous model of the Locker Agreement finalized by IBA, and adopted and circulated by our Bank under cover of the Circular OC No. 872 dated 31st March, 2022 for use by branches, required revision.

2. The IBA was advised by RBI to revise the model Locker Agreement.

3. The IBA has now come out with the draft of the “REVISED” Locker Agreement. The same has been examined and adopted by our Bank for immediate implementation.

 

4.   Definitions:

i.     The form of Locker Agreement which was used in our Bank prior to 31st March, 2022 is defined as “OLD”. It has already been discontinued.

ii.  The form of the agreement supplied under cover to our Circular OC No. 872 dated 31st March, 2022 is defined as “NEW”. (The same is being discontinued with immediate effect).

iii.    The form of agreement which the IBA has now come out with and, upon examination has been adopted by our Bank, is defined as “REVISED”. (This form is now to be used without exception).

 

II.  Interim action advised through previous communication:

1.   In anticipation of the “REVISED” form, all the branches were already advised through a separate communication (IDC No: PL & MKT / 432 / 2022 – 23 dated 07th February, 2023) to stop the process of replacing the “OLD” locker agreements with the agreements in the “New” form.

2.    In respect of the customers taking lockers on hire for the first time after the aforesaid communication, the branches were advised to use the “NEW” form. While entering into a contract on the basis of the “New” form in such exceptional cases, the branches were instructed to inform the customers, through a letter as per the Annexure there to, that after the receipt of the “REVISED” form of the locker agreement, the customers would need to re-execute the locker agreement, albeit at the Bank’s cost towards stamp duty.

3.   The process of getting executed “REVISED” locker agreements from the existing customers from whom “NEW” agreements were not obtained, would start after receipt, adoption, and internal circulation of the “REVISED” FORM.

4.   In respect of the existing customers from whom the “NEW” forms were already obtained, “REVISED” forms would be necessarily obtained after they became available to branches. The cost of stamp duty incurred while replacing the “NEW” locker agreement with the “REVISED” locker agreement would be borne by the Bank.

5.   After the “REVISED” forms of locker agreements became available to branches, stamp duty there on would be borne by the customers seeking to hire a new locker as is the regular norm.

 

III.   Updated instructions for action:

1.  As mentioned in Para I.3 above, the “REVISED” form of the Locker Agreement has now been received from the IBA and is adopted by the Bank. It is attached herewith as Annexure. It is also placed in the Development Department folder on Exchange Server.

2.  In all cases of hiring out new lockers, only the “REVISED” Locker Agreement will be executed; the stamp duty for the same will be borne by the customers.

3.  If an existing customer, who has not executed the “NEW” Locker Agreement, now comes for execution of a Locker Agreement (which may be on his / her own; if not, he / she should be invited by the branch for this purpose), charges for the “Revised” Locker Agreement will be borne by the customer.

4.  The customers who have already executed the “NEW” Locker Agreement (provided under Circular OC No. 872 dated 31st March, 2022) also need to be invited to the branch for the execution of the “REVISED” Locker Agreement. The stamp duty for the same (i.e. the “REVISED” Locker Agreement) will be borne by our Bank in such cases.

 

IV. The existing stock of forms of “OLD” & ”NEW” Locker Agreements must not be used henceforth. They should be effectively destroyed.

 

V.  Goals for “REVISED” Agreements, action plan, and follow up reports as prescribed by RBI.

1.   The last date by which all the hired-out lockers must be supported by duly executed “REVISED” Locker Agreements is 31st December, 2023.

2.   In order to enable reaching the above goal, RBI has set interim goals such that at least 50% and 75% of the existing Locker customers execute the “REVISED” Locker Agreements respectively by 30th June, 2023 and 30th September, 2023.

3.   It is necessary for branches to send intimations to the customers who have not executed the “REVISED” Agreements.

4.   a. The IT department is hereby instructed to send an SMS to all the Locker-hirers

whose mobile numbers are registered with the Bank, at monthly intervals, alerting them to the need for execution by them of the Revised agreement.

b. The text of the SMS shall be supplied by the Planning and Marketing department.

5.   As sending of positive confirmation to the customer before the day-end of his / her Locker-operation through email & SMS is laid down in the Locker Policy, branches are advised to ensure that the Mobile numbers and email IDs of all Locker-holders are updated in the Omni System. This will help the IT department and branches in covering most of the Locker-hirers through SMSes and emails respectively.

6.   As the process of execution of the “REVISED” Locker Agreement is to be reported to RBI on monthly basis through the DAKSH Portal of RBI, branches providing the locker facility are instructed to furnish information as per the table below on monthly basis to the Marketing Department through the Exchange Server.(marketing2677@abhyudaya.com)
