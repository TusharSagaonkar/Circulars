Circular OC No. 881 dated 6th March 2023

CASH MANAGEMENT POLICY

 

 

I.  The Cash Management Policy was previously circularised under cover of the Circular OC No. 863 dated 1st January 2022.

 

II.         Since the date of the policy as above, RBI issued a few more general and specific instructions on the subjects of Note Refund Rules, Detection and impounding of counterfeit Notes, Facility for Exchange of Notes & Coins, maintenance of adequate cash in ATMs, and overall Cash Management. Further some additional points which are in the interest of the Bank have also been identified. The Cash Management Policy has thus been further revised, to include the aforesaid instructions and points.

 

III.       The policy was placed before the Board meeting dated 27th February 2023 and the same met with approval.

 

IV.        The revised and updated Cash Management Policy is being circularised herewith. All the staff members concerned shall hereafter refer to this policy document and not previous ones.

 

V.          We are posting the said “Cash Management Policy” on Exchange Server - Public Folder – Secretarial – Circulars – Cash Policy.

 

VI.  Staff-members and officials in Branches, and officials and executives in administrative offices and specialist departments (like KYC/AML, vigilance and inspection), should thoroughly read the policy and its annexures.

 

VII.     It is being emphasised that the policy is to be implemented meticulously and effectively so as to attain the three objectives of (1) protecting bank’s interests, (2) providing excellent service to customers as well as general public, (3) and ensuring regulatory compliance.
