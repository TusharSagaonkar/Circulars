Circular OC No. 779 dated 2nd December, 2017

Disposal of Pledged Ornaments Delivery of Which Not Taken

Disposal of Valuable Articles Found in Branch Premises

_______________________________________________________________________________________

I.                 Sometimes, customers availing themselves of Gold Loans pay up the dues but do not take delivery of the ornaments pledged in spite of notices sent to them to come to the Branch in this behalf. In such cases, Bank is “Bailee” under the Indian Contract Act and is required to follow a procedure in keeping with its duties as a bailee. A need had arisen to lay down policy for disposal of such ornaments.

 

II.  Further, there are also instances when valuable articles are inadvertently left behind by visitors to our branches. In such cases also, responsibility of Bank is same as that of a bailee (section 71 of Indian Contract Act).

 

1. Most of the times; it is easy to trace owners & return articles to them, albeit upon a suitable acknowledgement & indemnity, because:

 

                      i.   The articles bear names or markings facilitating inquiry.

 

                     ii.   CCTV captures image at the time of a person dropping, placing or leaving behind the articles, and checking of CCTV footage can help establish ownership of the articles and their eventual return.

 

                   iii.   Many times, it is possible to identify the persons who visited the branch (in case of a small branch) or a particular area within the branch (such as locker room or areas adjacent to it, area near the counter or desk around which the articles were found etc.). Discreet inquiries can be made with such shortlisted visitors so as to ascertain ownership of the articles and their return.

 

2. However, there are also instances where it is not possible to trace owner of such articles. Currently, there are no instructions to deal with such instances. A need was perceived to include them also in the policy under formulation.

 

III.   The policy to deal with situations as above was therefore formulated & put up to the Board. The same met with approval on 22nd November, 2017. We are communicating instructions as approved in the policy through the instant circular as under.

 

1. In all the cases under both the categories, the articles should always be taken over by joint custodians at the branch.

2. i. In respect of pledged articles delivery of which is not taken : A note

recording closed account no., name & address of the customer, description of ornaments & their approximate value should be prepared. The note should be signed by joint custodians

ii. In respect of valuable articles found in branch premises:

a. A note recording description of the articles and date, time, circumstances and location of the same being found should be prepared & signed by joint custodians.

b.   If it is a piece/s of jewellery or some other valuable article(s) (e. g. antique piece) valuation of which can be done through a professional valuer, the same should be got done. Otherwise, an impressionistic valuation may be made by the Officer (Joint Custodian) which should be independently determined, approved & recorded by the Branch -In-Charge.

 

3. The particulars of the article(s), the note, & valuation certificate if any, should be recorded in a separate register to be maintained for the purpose. The article(s) together with the note etc. should continue to remain in joint custody of the Assistant Manager and the officer in charge of cash and should be stored in the locker which is earmarked for Branch’s own use. The article(s) together with the note, & valuation report if any, will be subject to periodical stock taking, verification during custodians’ transfers & inspection.

 

4. Safe Custody Charges (currently at Rs.300/- per month) with incidental GST are levied for safe keeping of ornaments delivery of which is not taken by customers in spite of closure of gold loan account. Safe Custody Charges at the same rate should be calculated for eventual recovery from claimant(s) in respect of articles found in branch premises.

 

5. In respect of articles found in branch premises, Branch will continue making all out efforts for tracing & identifying the true owner(s). All the efforts made to trace the true owner(s) should be carefully documented so as to establish that due diligence has been exercised. This is necessary to fulfill the responsibility as bailee. Information should be given to the local police station if value of the article is more than Rs. 25,000/-. If the branch is successful in such tracing & identification, the article should be delivered to the claimant after recovery of accumulated safe custody charges & applicable GST as mentioned in Para 4 above, after obtaining from him a receipt for the article(s) & an indemnity letter as per the Annexure hereto.

6.  (i) In respect of articles found in branch premises,

 

a.     when such efforts for tracing & identifying the true owner fail and accumulated safe custody charges equal or exceed 2/3 of the valuation on record, or

 

b.    when the claimant in respect of articles found refuses to pay safe custody charges

 

AS ALSO

 (ii) In respect of pledged articles delivery of which is not taken and,

 

a.   when accumulated safe custody charges equal or exceed 2/3 of the valuation on record, or

 

b.   the pledgor-borrower of refuses to pay up the accumulated safe custody charges,

 

proposal to sell the article(s) through public auction (permissible under Section 169 of the Indian Contract Act) should be submitted to Regional/ Zonal Office. After permission is received from Regional Zonal Office, the article(s) should be sent to Recovery Department to be sold at their level through public auction. Claimant, if known and on record, or the pledgor-borrower, as the case may be, should be notified about the auction so that he/they may participate in the auction, if he/they so choose/s. The proceeds should be credited to Miscellaneous Income Account. Needless to add that appropriate GST will have to be paid.

 

IV.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
