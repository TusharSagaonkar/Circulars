Circular OC No. 776 dated 21st October, 2017

Launching of “Platinum Rupay Debit Card”

_________________________________________________________________________________________

I.  We had introduced Rupay Debit Card in the year 2014. Currently, we are issuing Classic Rupay Debit Cards to our customers. Recently, NPCI came out with one more variant i. e. “Platinum Debit Card” which is, as on date, having following additional features/offers to the card holders.

 

1. Personal Accident Insurance: RuPay offers Personal accident Insurance (accidental death or permanent disablement) of Rs. 2,00,000 for the Platinum debit cardholders.

 

2. Lounge Access : RuPay Platinum Domestic Airport Lounge Access Programme provides access to the participating lounges two times per calendar quarter per card in any of the 30 Plus Airports’ Lounges in 11 cities like Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad etc. The terms and conditions of Domestic Lounge Access are given in Part A of Annexure-I.

 

3. Concierge Service: Concierge service is available on 24 X 7 basis across India. A toll-free number 1800-26-78729 is given for services, list & terms and conditions of which are given in Part “B” of Annexure-I.

 

4. Cash Back Offer on Utility bills: 5% cash back on utility bill payments (capped at Rs.50 per month per card). Cash back is applicable on Utility bill, payment of Telephone, Mobile, Electricity, Gas, water and Sanitary Bills.

 

5. Merchant offers and benefits: A host of merchant offers and benefits for RuPay Platinum card holders are tied up by NCPI to aid banks in driving activation and usage. Currently, there are two offers as under:

 

i)      Croma Offer : Flat Rs. 500 Off on minimum purchase of Rs. 2000 and above to RuPay Cardholders.

 

ii)    Apollo Pharmacy Offer:

a.  Apollo FMCG (Private Label Products)-15% (300+Apollo Products).

b. Medicines - 10% off (except DPCO Drugs).

c.  FMCG Products - 5% Off.

 

iii) Further offers will be communicated from time to time as & when they are firmed up through postings on Bank’s website.

 

II.   1 Submission number IT/34/2017-18 dated 19th May 2017 for launching of Platinum RuPay Debit Card in the Bank, with recommendations for charges therefor and consequent necessary enhancement for daily PoS limit, was made in the Board Meeting dated 25th May, 2017 & the same received approval. Thus, Bank has decided to launch the Platinum RuPay Debit Card for the customers whose monthly income is Rs. 25,000/- & above.

 

2. The new “Rupay Platinum Debit Card” has been designed with the help of M/s. MCT Cards Technology Pvt. Ltd. and same has been approved by NPCI.

 

3. Related stationery items like Welcome letter, User Guide etc. have also been modified so as to provide additional information as under:

 

i)   Welcome letter :

·     Insurance of 2 Lakh for accidental death or permanent disability.

·     Access to Domestic & International Airport Lounges.

·     Cash back offers on Utility Bill Payments.

 

ii) Modification in User Guide :

·     Annual Fees, Re-Issuance of additional card/add/Lost card = Rs. 150/-

·     Charges are excluding GST.

·     To Hotlist the card-“Inform the bank immediately on customer care or give Missed call from your registered mobile number to 9223110011 to block the debit card if your card is lost/stolen.”

 

iii)                Pouch & Envelope: Text has been suitably modified.

 

4.   Development Department is hereby being advised to modify Application Form for e-Channel services. In the meanwhile, Branches are advised to amend the form suitably to take specific request for Platinum card and to get declaration of monthly income being more than Rs. 25,000/-.

 

5.   Bank has increased daily PoS limit from Rs. 50,000/- to Rs. 1,00,000/- ; and inclusive of ATM withdrawal limit of Rs. 25,000/-, the combined limit is now Rs. 1,25,000/-.

 

6.    Annual fees & fees for re-issuance of additional card/add/Lost card will be Rs. 150/-. GST will be extra (please see first & second bullet under point no. II.3.ii above).

 

III. As regards navigation in Omni software, from the stage of registration of request for Platinum RuPay card from a customer till authorising the ATM link master before handing over the RuPay Platinum Card to the customer, screen shots of various stages of data entry are provided in Annexure-II.

 

IV.With the addition of this new product to our range of e-Channel Services, we expect that it will be promoted & marketed by all the staff including staff at branches.

 

V.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff for ensuring strict compliance.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
