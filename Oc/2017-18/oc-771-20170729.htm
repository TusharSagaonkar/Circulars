Circular OC No. 771 dated 29th July, 2017

 

Prevention of Money-laundering (Maintenance of Records) Second Amendment Rules, 2017

Obtaining AADHAAR Number for Existing and New Accounts

__________________________________________________________________________________

 

I.                 Prevention of Money-Laundering rules which have been amended recently, require Banks to obtain the AADHAAR number (or proof of application of enrolment for AADHAAR) as well as the Permanent Account Number–PAN- (or Form 60 in lieu thereof) from all the customers desirous of opening bank accounts. In case a client (“customer” to a Bank), eligible to be enrolled for Aadhaar and eligible to obtain a Permanent Account Number, referred to in sub-rules (4) to (9) of rule 9 of PML Act, does not submit the Aadhaar number as well as the Permanent Account Number at the time of commencement of an account-based-relationship with a Reporting Entity ( i. e. a “Bank” in our case), it shall be obligatory on the part of the client (“customer”) to submit the same to the Reporting Entity (the “Bank”) within a period of six months from the date of commencement of the account-based-relationship.

 

II.  In view of the amendment as above to PMLA rules, it has been decided as under:

 

1.    For New / Prospective Customers desirous of opening an Account, following procedure needs to be followed:

 

                   i.   Aadhaar number or proof of application of enrolment for Aadhaar should be obtained at the time of opening the account itself from all the customers desirous of opening a Bank account.

 

                 ii.   In Case a customer desirous of opening a Bank account with us does not have an Aadhaar Number or proof of application of enrolment for Aadhaar, an undertaking as per the Annexure hereto should be obtained from him/her to submit his Aadhaar number within six months of the application for opening the account but in no case later than 31st December 2017.

 

2.    For Existing Account Holders, following procedure needs to be followed:

 It shall be mandatory for the exiting customers to submit a self-attested copy of his Aadhaar Card to the bank for the purpose of linking Aadhaar Number to his Customer Number. The customer will be required to mention his/her Account Number as well as Customer Number on the self attested copy of the Aadhaar Card.

 

3.    For all the customers approaching the bank, Aadhaar linkage should be done invariably after e-KYC authentication using biometric device.

 

4.    The details of the Aadhaar number obtained should be entered in the Omni application software as follows: Go to the menu option “Masters àCustomer Master àExpress customer master à Modify”, Enter Customer Number, move to Aadhar No and feed the 12 digit Aadhaar Number provided by the customer and “commit” the same. After this, get authorized the modification done for the said Customer.

 

5.    It should be ensured at Branches that linkage of Aadhaar number to the respective customer account is done meticulously and the Aadhaar number is linked correctly to the respective Customer Number.

 

6.    In case an existing customer provides Aadhaar Number/Aadhaar Card, it should be ensured that his existing address in our records matches with the address on the Aadhaar Card provided by him/her. In case it differs, either proof in respect of his/her latest address or a self-declaration in that behalf should be obtained as per our existing procedure.

 

III.   In terms of PMLA rules, if a prospective or existing customer fails to submit Aadhaar by 31st December 2017, his/her said account shall cease to be operational through application of debit freeze by the Bank until the time the Aadhaar number is submitted by the customer.

 

IV.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff for ensuring strict compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
