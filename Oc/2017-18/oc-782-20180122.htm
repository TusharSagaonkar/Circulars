Circular OC No. 782 dated 22nd January, 2018

 

New “Maharashtra Shops and Establishment Act, 2017”

No Registration Certificate to Entities with Less than Ten Workers

Implications for Opening Account of a Business Entity in Maharashtra

“Receipt of Intimation” of Commencement of Business to be Acceptable

__________________________________________________________________________________________

 

I.                 The new “Maharashtra Shops and Establishments (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 2017” (“The New Act”) has come into effect from December 20, 2017 in replacement of the earlier law. We provide features of the “The New Act” noteworthy for us in the Annexure-II hereto. The major change relevant to us in terms of the “The New Act” is that establishments having less than 10 workers in employment can & must do their own online registration and they would not be issued any Shop & Establishment Licence. The Facilitator (who is the renamed authority under “The New Act”) will issue a Receipt of Intimation to such Firm/ Establishment, in a prescribed form and manner the details of which shall be recorded online in a register. 

 

II.  Business entities need to open bank accounts for business purpose, be they pertaining to deposits, or loans & advances. Among deposit accounts, current account is a necessary mode for all business entities to route transactions (although, current accounts by non-business individuals are also possible & sometimes seen). As part of KYC documentation for accounts (including current accounts) of business entities, we currently obtain, among others, a valid & current Registration Certificate under the Shops & Establishment Act. With the change as above in terms of the “The New Act”, we are hereby instructing branches to obtain in respect of establishments having less than 10 workers, a printout of the “Receipt of Intimation”, downloaded from the site of Govt. of Maharashtra, after getting it signed by the authorized signatory (ies) of the business entity; and keep it in lieu of Registration Certificate under the Act. In order to establish the veracity of the “Receipt of Intimation”, Branches will log into https://aaplesarkar.maharashtra.gov.in and access the menu as per the Annexure-I hereto. After verification, branches will obtain the screenshot of the “Verify Your Authentication Certificate” & keep it attached to the “Receipt of Intimation” submitted by the business entity. Please note that the current Registration Certificates will be valid until their expiry and the establishments concerned will thereafter follow the differential procedures under the new act depending upon the number of workers in their employment. All other instructions under account opening including calling for various documents & their examination for KYC compliance etc. remain unaltered & in force.

 

III.   IT Department is herby being advised to ensure that branches get access to https://aaplesarkar.maharashtra.gov.in site.

 

IV.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
