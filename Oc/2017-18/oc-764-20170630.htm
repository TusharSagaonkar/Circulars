Circular OC No. 764 dated 30th June, 2017

Compliances to be made under GST Act Applicable w.e.f. 1st July, 2017

 

 

I.    INTRODUCTION:

 

1.  What is GST :

GST i.e. Goods & Service Tax will be implemented from 01st July, 2017 and the current Service Tax charged on various service charges collected from our customer will be subsumed in GST i.e. instead of charging Service Tax, our Bank will now charge GST.

 

2.  Rate of GST :

The rate of GST is 18% for all kinds of Banking and Financial Services. Hence, for charges levied to customers for various services rendered by our Bank, the tax rate has increased from current 15% to 18% i.e. by 3%.

 

3.  GST Registration Number :

Under current taxation regime, our Bank had single Service Tax Registration Number for all of our 111 branches. Under GST, registration will be state-wise and hence our Bank is having 3 GST Registration Numbers, one each under the states of Maharashtra, Karnataka and Gujarat. Thus, Branches within Maharashtra State will have one GST Number, Branches in Karnataka State will have another GST No. and Branches in Gujarat State will have yet another GST No.

GST numbers in the three states are as under.

State

GST Number

Maharashtra

27AAAAA0300L1ZC

Gujarat

24AAAAA0300L1ZI

Karnataka

29AAAAA0300L1Z8

 

4.  Concept of CGST, SGST and IGST :

i)   GST are of three types - CGST i.e. Central GST, SGST i.e. State GST and IGST i.e. Inter-state GST. If the supplier and receiver of goods or services are in the same state, then two GST will be levied i.e. CGST and SGST; and if supplier and receiver of goods or services are in different states, then one GST will be levied i.e. IGST.

 

ii) In case of our Bank, we will charge CGST and SGST to our customers since the branch from where the service is provided and customer to whom the service is provided are in the same state. Out of our total GST @ 18%, CGST share will be 9% and SGST share will be 9%. For all types of GST, GST registration number is same as mentioned in point no. 3 above.

 

 

5.  Concept of reverse charge :

Until now, if Bank made any purchases/or availed any service from a vendor who was unregistered, Bank was not required to pay any tax on the same. Under GST, for any transaction with an unregistered vendor (i.e. purchase of goods / services), we (Bank) are required to pay GST on the same. Hence, all Departments/Branches should avoid purchases from URD (unregistered dealer) to the maximum possible extent. Further, if purchases from URD are required to be made for some reason, Departments sanctioning the expenditure are advised to take into consideration such costs while evaluating the quotations etc.

 

II.   INSTRUCTIONS TO BRANCHES :

 

1.  Collection of GST Number of our customers :

 

i)      Like our Bank, our customer will also get registered under GST and will have a GST Registration Number. The GST number of our customer is required by us for the purpose of issuance of invoices and filing of GST returns which is explained in detail later in this circular.

ii)   GST number is issued only to a person carrying on any business/profession etc. Others will not have GST number. E.g. if a salary earner has account with us, he will not be having a GST number. If a current account holder is having an account with us, we need to collect GST number as he is engaged in business/profession etc.

iii)  It is not necessary that every person having business will have GST number since GST is not mandatory for a business, turnover of which is below Rs. 20 lakh per annum. GST is also not mandatory for an entity which is providing goods or services which are exempt from taxes.

iv)  We have made following arrangements to give intimation to our customers to submit their GST numbers to us:

a)     Display of Notice Board at Branches;

b)     Sending SMS to all customers whose Mobile Numbers are available on record.

Apart from this, all the Branches are requested to intimate customer whenever he/she visits branch.

v)    While collecting GST number from a customer, branches are requested to obtain photocopy of GST registration acknowledgement or GST registration certificate whichever is available with the customer. The GST number is a 15 digit number, in which first 2 digits are state code i.e. 27 for Maharashtra, 24 for Gujarat and 29 for Karnataka. Next 10 digits are PAN of the person. Hence, branches should check accordingly while accepting the GST number. GST number will be in the name of the business (i.e. in case of a Partnership Firm, it will be in the name of Firm and not in the name of a Partner. Similarly, for a Company, it will be in the name of the Company) or in the name of the Proprietor or Proprietorship Firm.

vi)  The GST number in OMNI is linked to Customer Master. If a proprietor is having 2 proprietorship firms, then he may have 2 GST numbers. In such a case, while updating GST number in OMNI, branch should take care and update GST number accordingly.

 

vii)Branches should note that wherever customer has provided GST number, the same should be immediately and correctly updated in OMNI. Non-feeding or wrong feeding of GST number of customer in OMNI will attract penalty under GST Act, as all the returns will be uploaded by placing reliance on GST number entered in the customer master.

 

2.  Updating of GST number in OMNI :

I.T. Dept. has provided option/menu for updating of GST no. of the customers wherein branches are required to select the customer number. Branches will select the State where customer is registered and make the entry of GST number in the field provided.

3.  Issue of Invoice :

 

i)      Under GST Act, Bank needs to issue an invoice to its customer towards any kind of charges collected from them. Features of invoice are as under:

a.  The Invoice should be named as ‘Tax Invoice’.

b. The Invoice should contain the Name, address and GST number of our Bank. (e.g. if charges are collected by any branch in Maharashtra, the GST number will be that pertaining to Maharashtra State).

c.  The Invoice will contain the Name, Address and GST number of the customer.

d. The amount of charges collected and GST charged on the same and its total.

e.  HSN code.

f.   Signature.

 

ii)    For each and every charge collected, invoice is to be issued and every invoice will have a unique serial number. HSN code is a unique code allotted for financial services under GST Act and it will remain same for all types of charges collected from customers.

 

iii)  To comply with this requirement, our I.T. Department will make necessary changes in our OMNI software so that invoices (as mentioned above) can be generated through the system.

 

iv)  Invoices will be generated for all customers whether or not they are registered under GST and whether or not they are conducting business/profession. Please note that Branches are required to print the invoice only if customer demands it and not otherwise.

 

4.  GST return :

 

i)      Data of all invoices generated is to be submitted to government on monthly basis. This activity will be centralized at Accounts Department and hence no compliance in this regard is required at Branch level. In case any issue or query is received from customer regarding reporting of the data, Branches should contact Accounts Dept. in this behalf.

 

ii)    Before contacting Accounts Department branches should:

a.  Check whether correct GST number is updated in system.

b. Check the invoice of the month concerned for which query is raised and ensure that all charges levied to that customer during the month are covered in the invoice.

 

5.  GST paid on expenses :

i)      In the same way as our Bank collects from or charges GST to our customer, our vendors charge GST to us on various goods purchased and services availed by our Bank from them. E.g. if Bank has purchased computer from M/s. ABC, then M/s. ABC will levy GST on the invoice issued by them towards purchase of computer and Bank will pay total amount including GST to M/s. ABC. Bank gets set off of GST paid to our vendor against the GST collected from customers and to get this benefit of set off, Bank needs to report the details of all purchases made from vendors and services received from the vendors to the Government. These details are to be uploaded invoice-wise on the portal provided by the Government. Most of the expenses are released/paid by Bills Dept centrally. Therefore requisite compliance for such expenditure will be done by Bills Dept. centrally and not by branches.

 

ii)    However, some expenses such as telephone bill, electricity bill, all petty cash expenses, sweeper’s salary, rent of premises etc. are incurred by branches themselves and they are not routed through Bills department. In such cases, branches are required to make the following compliances.

a.  Obtain invoice for every expense paid.

b. Ensure that GST is shown separately in the invoice if the shopkeeper/vendor is registered under GST. If GST registration number is printed on the invoice, it indicates that the shop is registered under GST. In such a case, branches should insist that GST is shown separately in the invoice. If the shopkeeper/vendor has charged GST in invoice but has not printed his GST number, then insist that the GST number be mentioned in invoice.

c. I.T. Department will provide Expenses Module in OMNI. The entry for any kind of expenses is to be made through “Expenses Module” provided in OMNI only. In case of any deviation, the staff/official concerned will be held personally responsible for any loss/penalty to the Bank.

d. While making entry, separate entry for each expense is to be made on daily basis. Branches are currently passing entry for petty cash at the end of month. Similarly, if branches incurred travelling expenses 5 times in a month, then a single entry is currently passed in concerned PL head at the end of the month. This system is not allowed from now onwards. Entry of expenses will be made on daily basis and separate entry will be made for each expense and no aggregation is to be made. If rent is paid to 4 persons who are joint owners of the premises, then 4 separate entries will be made for rent.

e.  While making entries, branches should compulsorily mention vendor name, invoice number, invoice date, GST number, CGST amount, SGST amount

and narration. In case of expenses where GST number is not available, select the option of GST Number as “No” in the requisite field. In case of small expenses where invoice number is not available, enter “INV-N.A”. If GST is not levied in the invoice, then enter “0” in CGST and SGST field.

f.   Currently, branches are originating the amount of service tax charged in various invoices e.g. telephone bill, rent etc. to H.O. Now, no such amount is to be originated to H.O. at the time of making payment. Entry will be made in Expenses Module as described above. The amount entered in CGST and SGST field will be debited to separate GL Head created for the same. GL head created for CGST amount is “CGSTREC1”, and for SGST, it is “SGSTREC1”. Branches, at the end of every month, will originate the Balance in these GL heads to H.O. In case of rent, branches are currently originating only 50% of service tax charged to H.O. This will also be discontinued and normal procedure for all expenses as mentioned above will become applicable to rent payments also.

g.  Once branches have entered the required data in fields of Expenses Module, following entry/voucher will be passed in OMNI.

PL Expenses Head ---Dr

CGSTREC1 ---Dr

SGSTREC1 ---Dr

 PCASH/POADJ etc. as the case may be. ---Cr       

h. Branches will immediately send the original copy of invoices wherein GST is charged to Accounts Department for record. Please note that any delay in receipt of invoices beyond 5 days from the date of entry will result in corresponding delay in filing GST return and consequences such as penalty will fall on Bank.

 

III. INSTRUCTIONS TO DEPARTMENTS SANCTIONING AND RELEASING EXPENSES THROUGH BILLS DEPARTMENT :

 

1.   Collection of GST number from Vendors :

For availing the benefits of GST levied in invoice by our various vendors, we need to have their GST numbers with our record. Therefore, all departments are required to collect GST numbers from their respective vendors and forward the same to Accounts Department / Bills Department in the following format.

Name of the Organization/Vendor

Registered Address under GST

State

GST Provisional ID

Email & Phone No

 

 

 

 

 

 

2.  Updating of GST number in OMNI :

Option will be provided under Vendor Master in OMNI. Bills Department will update GST number for all vendors in Vendor Master.

 

3.  Obtain Tax Invoice from Vendors :

i)   To claim input tax credit under GST, we should have a TAX INVOICE issued and signed by vendor containing the following details.

a) The word “TAX INVOICE”.

c)  Name, Complete address and GST No. of vendor.

d) The invoice should be numbered and dated.

e)  Our Bank’s name, address and GST no. as a Recipient of goods / service.

f)   Description of Goods purchased or service availed.

g)  HSN code of Goods purchased / Accounting code of services availed.

h) GST rate and GST amount (CGST/SGST/IGST should be shown separately).

i)   Signature of vendor or its authorized person.

ii) In case of purchases from unregistered dealers, a general invoice will be obtained. In case of purchases from URD, we are liable to pay GST on such expenses.

 

4.  No benefit of input tax credit if the tax is not deposited by vendor :

If the vendor who has charged and collected GST from us through invoice but has not paid the same to the Government, Bank will not be able to claim the input tax credit (set off) of the same. The same will result in increased cost for our Bank. In order to avoid such instances, it is suggested that, in case of regular parties, a clause in agreement should preferably be added that Bank will retain some amount if it observes that vendor is not depositing, or depositing late, the amount of GST collected by him to Government, & further if a vendor continues to make such default, Bank can terminate the contract.

 

5.  Advance payment of expenses :

 

i)   The input credit is available only at the time of completion of work. Hence if the advance payment is made to any vendor, Bank can claim input credit only when final invoice is raised by the vendor even though Bank has paid GST at the time of making advance payment. Therefore, if any payment is released in advance, obtain receipt voucher at the time of payment and when the work is completed, obtain tax invoice from Vendor for total amount and send the same to Bills Dept.

ii) Till the final tax invoice is not received, payment will be kept under ‘Advance for Expenses A/c’ by Bills Department.

 

6.  Refund of payments :

Obtain credit note from vendor in case of Refund of payments / deduction from invoice amount. Credit note will contain all the details of tax invoice except heading will be “credit note” and not “tax invoice” and the reference of original invoice no. will also be there.

 

7.  Lapse of input tax credit :

It may be noted that input tax credit of GST charged in invoice will lapse within a period of one year from the date of invoice or 30th September from the end of Financial Year whichever is earlier. E.g. if an invoice dated 01.08.2017 is received for payment after 31.07.2018, then Bank will not be able to claim input tax credit of GST levied in invoice.

Hence before obtaining invoice, ensure that the date of invoice is not beyond the period as above. If the same is beyond the period as above, special approval from Board will be required as GST levied will form additional cost for the Bank.

 

8.  Separate Bill for each state and separate bill for Departments :

 

i)   In case of a common vendor for all branches, obtain 3 separate invoices for branches in 3 states. The bill will be raised on separate GST numbers in each state. Single invoice will suffice for branches in single state.

ii) In case of purchases made or services availed by/for H.O. or any Department as well as by/for branches, obtain separate invoice for H.O./Departments and Branches though located in single state. E.g. in case of AMC in branches and departments in Maharashtra, obtain separate Bill for Department and for branches.

 

9.  Expenditure which are common in nature :

i)   In case of expenditure which is common in nature and cannot be allotted to a branch or apportioned among branches, it is necessary to distribute the input tax credit of GST charged on such invoice to 3 states in the ratio of turnover in each state. In such cases, invoice will be obtained in the name of Head Office only. Head Office will play the role of Input Service Distributor for the branches located in the state of Gujarat and Karnataka.

ii) Accounts Department will allocate the input tax credit amongst states in the ratio of turnover of each state.

 

IV. INSTRUCTIONS TO ALL DEPARTMENTS INCURRING PETTY CASH EXPENSES :

 

1.     Certain expenses such as telephone bill, electricity bill, petty cash expenses, sweeper’s salary etc. are incurred by departments themselves. In such cases, all the instructions relating to entry of expenses to Branches as per point no. II. 5 above will also be applicable to Departments while making petty cash expenses.

2.     At present Accounts Department is making entry for petty cash for following departments-

DGM Navi Mumbai, DGM Central Zone, DGM Western Zone Mumbai, IT Dept., CDEC Dept., RISK Management, Development Dept., Marketing Dept, Inspection, IS Audit, KYC/AML, CRS and Stationary Dept.

From now onwards, each department will make entry of their petty cash expenses themselves except for following departments.

a.   CRS Dept.

b.   Stationary Department.

(Entry for these two departments mentioned above only will be made by Accounts Department).

V.   INSTRUCTIONS TO DEPARTMENTS GENERATING INCOME :

 

1.     Certain departments are generating income such as Incentives from NPCI, NEFT Inward charges received, Commission from LIC, New India Assurance etc. The departments which are identified as those generating income are Forex-Dadar, Integrated Treasury, ATM Recon., Clearing Dept., Third Party Product, Head Office & Pune Zone. All such departments are advised to take up the matter with I.T. Department to generate invoices for such income in consultation of Accounts Department.

2.     If any of these departments are using different software (other than OMNI) for making entries, then such departments are advised to contact their software vendors to make necessary changes required under GST with help of I.T. and Accounts Department.

 

VI. INSTRUCTIONS TO REGIONAL OFFICES IN AHMEDABAD AND UDUPI :

 

1.     While sanctioning and releasing expenses, instructions pertaining to Departments sanctioning and releasing expenditure through Bills Department are to be followed.

2.     While making petty cash expenses, related instructions in point II. 5 above are to be followed.

3.     RO Ahmedabad and RO Udupi are required to use ‘Expenses Module’ for making entries of expenses and send copies of invoices wherein GST is levied to Accounts Department.

4.     R.O.s are required to identify a responsible person to deal with GST authority, co-ordinate among branches under their region & ensure compliance with instructions issued under various circulars. The In-charge / staff concerned should communicate with Accounts Department for filing all the returns & payment of GST for branches under their region.

5.   GST returns and payments pertaining to the state of Gujarat and Karnataka will be handled by Accounts Department with the support of R.O.s.

 

VII.     INSTRUCTIONS TO BILLS DEPARTMENT:

 

1.     Follow up with Regular Vendors through respective Departments to obtain GST Registration No. and update the same in OMNI.

2.     Before making payment to vendor, ensure tax invoice is submitted by Vendor containing all the details.

3.     In case of payments made in advance, ensure receipt voucher is given by vendor. Also ensure that the vendor has provided Tax Invoice after final payment is made.

4.     Follow up should be made on continuous basis if there is any mismatch in ITC (Input Tax Credit) in respect to data uploaded by the vendor in form GSTR 1.

5.     Make Invoice wise entry in Bills Module and fill all the details such as invoice number, date, description of Goods / Service along with HSN/Accounting code, GST amount for smooth functioning at the time of uploading data online and for matching of Input Tax Credit.

6.     Generate the data at the end of month for expenses on which input tax credit is claimed and expenses on which GST is to be charged on reverse basis through the reports provided in Bills Module.

7.     Segregation of input tax credit in three states i.e. Input tax credit eligible to be claimed in Maharashtra, Gujarat and Karnataka and common input tax credit for all branches.

 

VIII. INSTRUCTIONS TO ACCOUNTS DEPARTMENT :

 

1.     Calculate GST liability separately for each state at the end of every month.

2.     Generate data of invoices issued to customer through report provided in OMNI.

3.     File the return of outward supply based on the report.

4.     Distribute common input tax credit to Branches in Maharashtra, Gujarat and Karnataka.

5.     File the return of inward supply based on data prepared by bills department, branches, departments, regional offices.

6.     Work out GST liability under reverse charge.

7.     Payment of GST.

8.     Maintain records of returns filed, input tax credit claimed, working of GST and invoices copies.

9.     Ensure compliance with provisions of GST Act and issue necessary guidance / instructions to branches & departments.

10. Resolve queries of Branches and other Departments.

 

IX. INSTRUCTIONS TO I.T. DEPARTMENT :

 

I.T. Department should make following changes in OMNI through Infrasoft to enable compliance with GST Act and Rules.

1.     Introduction of GST number field in Customer Master.

2.     Generation of Tax Invoices serially numbered for every charge collected from customer whether through system or manually by branch. Separate serial number for branches in Karnataka and branches in Gujarat as there is separate GST number for them.

3.     Generation of one monthly invoice for all charges collected from an account of a customer during that month.

4.     Facility to branch to view and print invoices.

5.     Generation of report of tax invoices in a specified format for uploading the return of outward supply.

6.     Generation of credit note in case of reversal/waiver of charges along with reference to original invoice number and its report.

7.     Changes in Bills module such as introduction of additional fields like GST Number, GST amount.

8.     Generation of report of expenditure in required format.

9.     Provision of Expenses Module for branches and other departments to enter the details of petty cash and other expenditure & generation of report of the same in required format.

10. Separate GST (CGST and SGST) entry in OMNI for all charges collected either through system or manually by branch.

11. Printing of GST number of the Bank on loan and account statement given to customers.

12. Generation of tax invoices towards Income earned at various H.O. Departments like FX-Dadar, Third Party Product, Clearing, Treasury, ATM Recon., Recovery Dept. etc. and incorporation of the same in the return of outward supply. (E.g. LIC Commission, Commission from NPCI, RBI, Reliance etc.).

X.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members concerned and ensure strict compliance.
