Circular OC No. 784 dated 9th February, 2018

On-Boarding to Central KYC Registry (for Individuals)

Central KYC Registry [CKYCR] (for Individuals)

Operational Guidelines Related to CIF-I

Information Required from a Prospective Customer

Precautions to be Taken by Branch while Feeding Data in OMNI/DMS Master.

____________________________________________________________________________________

I.                 Please refer our Circular OC No. 757 dated 31st December, 2016, wherein detailed guidelines have been issued regarding the implementation of Central KYC Registry.

II.  Our IT Department has now completed the testing of upload / download of CKYC data to CERSAI on Test bed as well as live environment. The activity of updating the downloaded data (as received from CERSAI) in CBS is also complete. During the process of uploading of CKYC data to CERSAI, IT Department has come across various shortcomings due to incomplete information which gets carried over onto CERSAI platform through upload. The uploaded information is based on our Customer Information Form (CIF-I) and it helps in generation of 14 digit CKYC registry number. The 14 digit CKYC registry number, once generated, gets directly communicated to the respective customer, by the CERSAI on customer’s registered mobile number. For generation of successful 14 digit CKYC registry number, it is necessary that our CIF-I form is duly filled-up in all respects, and all mandatory information is complete. We are issuing following instructions, for ready reference of all branches, in respect of details that need to be mandatorily obtained from customers through the CIF-I form:-

Fields that are Mandatory and must have information in them (detailed below as per the existing CIF_I form)

Field No. 2: Personal Details –

(i)   Applicant’s Name: {Title (Mr./Mrs./Ms./Mas.), Surname, First Name and Middle Name},

(ii)  Name of Father or spouse: {Title (Mr. /Mrs.), Surname, First Name and Middle Name}. This field is mandatory even if Customer has PAN No. or is filling Form No.60/61.

 

a)  If applicant is a married woman and not having/providing PAN card details, then filling in full Name of her Father here is Mandatory. In such a case, Name of her Spouse should not be filled in field 2(ii).

b)  If applicant is a married woman and is having/providing PAN card, then it is mandatory to obtain either Spouse Name or Father’s Name in sub field 2(ii).

(iii) Name of Mother: {Title (Mrs./Ms.), Surname, First Name and Middle Name }

(iv)Maiden Name: If the applicant is a married woman and not having a PAN card, then her maiden name with Title (Ms.) should invariably be obtained.

 

Note :- It is possible that in extreme exceptional cases, only Mother’s name is provided by a Customer (i.e. Father’s name is not disclosed by the customer). In such cases, CERSAI may refuse to issue CKYCR no. However, branches should nevertheless open such Customers’ Accounts.

 

Field No. 3: Birth Details: Date of Birth is mandatory. Place of birth & Country of birth sub-fields are Optional and are required only if Customer is not an Indian Citizen.

 

Field No. 4: Gender: Male / Female / Transgender.

 

Field No. 5: Nationality : Indian / Others (if others, then Country Code for given Country as per ISO 3166 List (Annexure-1) is mandatory in fields 2 & 9 for sub-field “Country”).

 

Field No. 6: Aadhaar Card No.: Give here 12 digit Aadhaar Number.

 

Field No. 7: PAN No.: Give PAN Number as per documentary proof.

 

Field No. 8: Form No.60/61: Tick Yes in case of non-availability of PAN No. If PAN No. is available, Tick NO 

 

Field No. 9: Present Residential Address / Correspondence Address: Permanent Address with name of the City (Town or Village), District, State, Pin Code and Country fields are mandatory. Since AADHAAR number has been made mandatory, address as mentioned on AADHAAR card should be entered here in CIF-I form.

In case of address in India, Pin code number should match with the district mentioned.

In case of ‘out of India address’, State and PIN Code sub-fields are not mandatory but are to be marked as XX for State and 000000 for Pin Code in the CIF-I form.

For Country field, Country Code as per ISO 3166 List (provided in Annexure-1) is mandatory.

Sub-fields “Phone No. (with STD/ISD Code) in this block, Customer’s Landline number prefixed with STD/ISD Code and sub-field “Mobile No.” (a 10 Digit number) prefixed with Country Code is mandatory.  However do not feed Mobile Number with a prefix 0.

 

Field No. 10: Permanent / Native Place Address: Permanent i.e. Native Place Address with name of the City (Town or Village), District, State, PIN Code and Country fields are mandatory.

This field may contain some information, which is the same as in field no.9 above.

However, when Present address and Permanent address differ and when account is opened on the basis of self declaration for local address, (i. e. in cases where address on Aadhaar Card does not match with customer’s current address), branches should ensure that field no 9. has “Local Address” and field no.10 has address as per Aadhaar Card.

Again here, in case of address in India, PIN code number should be matching with the district mentioned and in case of out of India address, State and Pin Code sub-fields are to be marked as XX for State and 000000 for Pin Code in the CIF-I form.

For Country field, Country Code as per ISO 3166 List (provided in Annexure-1) is mandatory.

 

Field No.11: KYC Details: For sub-field “Copies of Document Enclosed”, please Tick ü in respective boxes named as A to G and Z as per OVD document type provided for KYC.

For sub-fields “Proof of Identity Details” and “Proof of Residence Details”, mention letter “A” to “G” or “Z” as per the type of document type provided. In other sub-fields named as “Document No.”, “Place of Issue”, “Issued Date” and “Expiry Date”, mention available information as per the document. Please note that in case the document type is either “Passport” or “Driving License”, the expiry date of the said documents needs to be mentioned in the form and is mandatory. Expired Passport or Driving Licence is not acceptable and should not be accepted nor should its details be fed in this field, as they are invalid.

 

Note 1: A] Acceptable Documents for Proof of Identity (PoI) – Any one of the OVDs or Identity Card issued by Central/State Government Depts., Public Financial Institutions, Public Sector Undertakings, Regulatory Authorities, Statutory Authorities, Scheduled Commercial Banks and Letter issued by the Gazette Officer with a duly attested photograph of the person. 

B] Acceptable Documents for Proof of Address (PoA) – All OVD’s except PAN Card or other

documents such as utility bill, which is not more than two months’ old issued by any service provider extending any of the services like Electricity, Telephone, Postpaid Mobile telephone, Piped Gas, Water bill etc. are acceptable. Moreover, any document, like Property Tax Bill, Municipal Tax Bill, Bank Statement/Post Office Account Statement, Pension or Family Pension Payment Orders (PPOs) having an address of the retired person is acceptable. Furthermore, Letter of Allotment of Accommodation in its Quarters or any public / private property which is given by an employer, if the employer is one amongst Central / State Government Department / Statutory or Regulatory Authorities/ Public Sector Undertakings / Scheduled Commercial Banks / Financial Institutions and Listed Companies is acceptable. Documents, issued by Government Departments of Foreign Jurisdictions and Letters issued by Foreign Embassies or Missions in India, having proper address, may be considered as PoA.

 

C] Further, it is to be noted that the name mentioned on CIF-I form by the prospective customer should exactly match with the proof of Identity (POI) as submitted by the customer without any compromise and that there should not be any spelling mistake in the mentioned and provided Names. It is acceptable if the order of Surname, First Name and Middle Name on any of the OVDs and that on form differ. Our IT Dept. has ensured that the mapping of Personal Details specifically in case of Name is as per requirement of CERSAI compatible standards.

 

Field No.12: Personal Information: Under this block in the CIF-I form, we acquire Customer Profile for monitoring purpose and it is not obligatory for a Customer to provide all the information. However for CERSAI’s CKYC Registry requirements, it is mandatory on the part of a customer to provide information for the sub-fields “Residential Status”. It is mandatory for

customer to Tick ü in one of the four option boxes; and for “Occupation” sub-field, either Tick ü S-Service with Ticking üone out of the three sub options of S-Service available therein OR Tick ü O-Others, with Ticking üone out of the five sub options of O-Others available therein. Therefore, it is mandatory for customer to Tick ü one of the option boxes for the field “Residential Status”, and also one of the option boxes provided for field “Occupation”.

 

Note 2: Furthermore, branches are advised to obtain some information for a few other fields for better monitoring of account transactions. These sub-fields are “Education”, “No. of dependents”, “Employed with”, “Employer / Business details”, “Gross Annual Income” and “Source(s) of Income”. These sub-fields have multiple options against which are boxes for appropriate selection with a Tick ü.

 

Field No.13: FATCA/CRS Compliance:

Under this block, there is “Section 1: Declaration of Tax Residency/Citizenship”.

In case the prospective customer falls under this purview and is a Non Resident Indian / Foreign National / Person of Indian Origin and where he/she is resident outside India for tax purpose, obtain information for field “Address of the jurisdiction of Tax Residence”, as it is mandatory.

Another sub-field “Tax Identification Number (TIN) /Functional Equivalent” need not be reported if the same has not been issued by the said jurisdiction. However, if the said jurisdiction has instead issued a High Integrity Number (e.g. Social Security Number, Insurance Number, Citizen / Personal Identification Number, Service Code Number, Resident Registration Number etc) which is at an equivalent level of identification, it should be obtained and noted under sub-field “Tax Identification Number (TIN) /Functional Equivalent”.

Under this block of FATCA, there is “Section 2: Details of Related Person, which although printed on CIF-I form under FATCA, has no relation with the Title. A related person is apparently a person/guardian who operates an account on behalf of a Minor customer whenever a Minor’s account is opened. CERSAI has informed us that whenever a minors’ account is opened (i.e. of an individual having age below 18 years), it is mandatory to obtain the name of Father / Mother / Natural Guardian of Minor and is to be mentioned under “Name of Related Person” sub-field. In the next sub-field, “Related Person Type”, branches should Tick ü in “Guardian of Minor” option box. In the third sub-field, “KYC Number of Related Person”, mention the CKYCR Number which has been issued by CERSAI to that related person. If KYC Number of the related person is not available, only then in the last sub-field, “Proof of Identity”, Tick mark ü should be made in respective option boxes. Moreover, branches will obtain a certified copy of OVD from the Related Person and scan the same for uploading to CERSAI portal.

 

The above instructions are apart from our earlier instructions mentioned in Circular No 510 dated 3/12/2009 for opening an Account of Minor which continue to hold good.

Note 3: Branches should invariably scan any one of the OVD’s of the natural guardian of the minor customer (i.e. of an individual having age below 18 years), as mentioned above while creating a new customer master for a Minor’s Account. The CDEC department should enter data in the related person area in DMS Master. 

IT Department has also created separate look-up codes for City and Area in the in CBS. Branches should refer the respective look-up while creating Address Master of the new customer in respect of City and Area codes. It may be noted here that City code in our Master pertains to District code and not to any city or place. District referencing City Name or Code, Area referencing Taluka Name or Code and Pin Code are mandatory as per CKYCR Registry requirements. Importantly, care should be taken that the PIN code Number should match with the District mentioned; else, the uploaded master to CKYC Registry shall be rejected.

 

III. Instructions for a New/Prospective Customer approaching our bank branch to open any deposit /loan account, with a CKYC Registry Number already obtained by him.

In case, a new/prospective customer approaches our bank’s branch to open any deposit/loan account and is holding a CKYC registry Number allocated to him by CERSAI through any other Banking associate; KYC documents need not be insisted upon to open a Deposit Account. The Branches will be provided with necessary digital signatures to verify the CKYCR number mentioned by the New/Prospective Customer approaching our bank branch to open any deposit /loan account through the CERSAI portal. If the CKYCR number provided by the Customer is matched by CERSAI portal, then our bank officials need not insist upon submission of KYC documents for opening an Account. The said form shall then be forwarded to CDEC Dept. which will download the CKYC documents of the prospective customer from CERSAI. If the uploaded CKYCR Number has a matching record like name and other KYC details; only then such an account shall be made “operative”; and relevant records of KYC documents downloaded from CERSAI shall be kept on record. In case no match is found for

 

uploaded CKYCR number, such an account holder (new customer) shall be requested to submit proper KYC documents. In case a new prospective customer has approached the bank for an advance (loan account only), all the requisite KYC documents must be obtained by the branch as a policy requirement. The instructions are being issued anticipating that such cases may emerge in future as CKYCR number shall be a new feature in banking sector and our staff needs to be ready to face such situations.

 

IV.   In view of the fact that provision of Digital Signature to branches may take up some time, branches should, in the meanwhile, refer the cases of New/Prospective customers, already holding a CKYC registry, to CDEC Dept. for validation.

 

V. Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure strict compliance.
