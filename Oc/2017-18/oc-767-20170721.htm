Circular OC No. 767 dated 21st July, 2017

 

Opening of Additional / New Current Accounts by Real Estate Developers (Builders)

under the Provisions of RERA in Respect of New Projects in the State of Maharashtra

__________________________________________________________________________________

I.                 The Real Estate (Regulation & Development) Act, 2016 (RERA) has come into effect from 1st May 2017; and consequent thereupon, the MahaRERA Authority has been set up in the State of Maharashtra. Under Section 37 of the RERA Act and Regulation 38 of MahaRERA (General) Regulations 2017, the MahaRERA Authority has been vested with powers to issue directions and orders to Promoters, Real Estate Agents and Allottees from time to time for effective implementation of the Act. One of the sections of the Real Estate (Regulation & Development) Act, 2016 (RERA) mandates real estate developers (builders/promoters) to open project-wise separate bank accounts in a scheduled bank , route funds relating to transactions covering the cost of construction and land cost specific to that specific project through such a separate account, and apply funds only for these two purposes. These specifically opened accounts shall be further monitored for compliance under RERA. The specific sections in respect of opening of project-wise accounts, the proportion of routing funds through them, stipulations for withdrawal of funds and compliance monitoring are given below in brief.

1. In terms of the provisions of the RERA, under section 4(2) (1) (D) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016, promoter is required to deposit seventy per cent of the amounts realised for the real estate project from the allottees in a separate account to be maintained in a scheduled bank to cover the cost of construction and the land cost, and the account is to be used only for that purpose.

 

2. Further, amount from the separate account shall be withdrawn by the promoter after it is certified by an engineer, an architect and a chartered accountant in practice that the withdrawal is in proportion to the percentage of completion of the project. The promoter is also required to get his separate project-wise account audited within six months after the end of every financial year by a chartered accountant in practice, and is required to produce a statement of accounts duly certified and signed by such chartered accountant, and during such audit, it will also be verified that the amounts collected for a particular project have been utilized for the project and the withdrawal has been in compliance with the proportion to the percentage of completion of the project.

 

 

3. Upon representation by banks in the above matter, the Maharashtra Government under provisions of Rule 5 of the Maharashtra Real Estate (Regulation and Development)(Registration of Real Estate Projects, Registration of Real Estate Agents, Rates of Interest and Disclosures on Website) Rules, 2017, modified the above instructions so that the promoter may not be required to submit these certificates with the said bank. The instructions allow the promoter instead to submit self-declaration to the bank once every quarter to the effect of having held such certificates on record and undertaking to submit them when required. It may be noted that this relaxation is given only by MahaRERA.

 

II.  In view of the above, we are issuing guidelines for opening and maintaining Current Accounts of Builders/Promoters in respect of project under the jurisdiction of MahaRERA under the provisions of RERA as under.

 

1. A Promoter is required to register each project separately with the Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA) or MahaRERA Authority (in Maharashtra).

 

2. The Promoter has to open project-wise separate bank account for depositing seventy (70%) per cent of the amounts for the real estate project realised from the allottees (i. e. from parties booking flats, tenements, shops or units of any other kind in the project) from time to time, to cover the cost of construction and the land cost, and has to apply such amounts only for the specific purpose. The promoter will be allowed to open a regular CD account for his regular transactions apart from such project-wise account.

 

3. As regards, KYC documents that need to be obtained from the Promoters / Builders / Developers, there is no need to obtain additional KYC documents for opening project-wise accounts other than Account Opening Form (AOF-2) if the Builder /Promoter is already an existing Customer of our Bank.

 

4. For opening any Current Accounts in the name of Builders/Promoters who are not currently customers of our bank, the necessary KYC documents will be required as per the extant guidelines along with Customer Information Form (CIF-2), Account Opening Form (AOF-2) and Customer Information Form (CIF -1 to be taken for all Authorised Signatories) for opening new Current Accounts of Legal Entities or Proprietorship firms. Obviously, a new customer number will be issued in such cases.

 

5. However, for opening project- wise accounts, whether of an existing customer or a new customer, the same customer number will be used for opening various project-wise accounts of the same Builder /Promoter. After opening the project-wise account, In-charge of the Branch under his /her signature will write to AGM, IT Dept. for making necessary modifications of the project name in the

 

 

Account Master for such specific accounts, as per the format given in Annexure II, for the additional account opened under same customer ID.

 

6. The promoter can withdraw amount from such separate project-wise account after it is certified by an engineer, an architect and a chartered accountant (in practice) specifically stating that the withdrawal is in proportion to the percentage of completion of the project. However, due to the relaxation given by Maharashtra Real Estate Regulatory Authority, it has been decided to obtain a self-declaration from the promoter once in every quarter (as per      Annexure I ).

 

7. It should be noted that the Cheque Book for such newly opened additional Current Account with the specific project-name should be issued only after the Promoter/ Builder submits the Certificate of Registration issued by the Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA) or the MahaRERA Authority (as the case may be) to the branch. It should be ensured that the cheque book is issued only after suitable Name-change has been effected in the related Account Master.

 

III.   We also invite attention of branches to Circular CR No. 183 dated 18th February, 2017 on the subject “Current Accounts Maintained by Cash Credit Account Holders with Other Banks” in which the necessity to adhere to inter-bank discipline while opening Current accounts of borrowing parties in order not to encourage diversion of funds, and the precautions to be taken in this behalf, have been highlighted. In view of the fact that we are currently not taking additional exposure to the real estate sector, it has been considered necessary to ensure that real estate developers (builders) do not resort to diversion of funds by opening additional current account with us. Therefore, it has been decided to obtain an Undertaking- cum-Indemnity from the real estate developer (builder) party desirous of opening a current account with us before acceding to its request. We attach herewith the draft of the Undertaking-cum-Indemnity (Annexure III) to be obtained in this behalf. It is needless to add that all other formalities including KYC compliance should be completed before any such account is opened.

 

IV.  We are in the process of issuing separate circulars pertaining to new current accounts to be opened in respect of projects within jurisdictions of Gujarat Real Estate Regulation & Development Authority and Karnataka Real Estate Regulation & Development Authority.

 

V.    Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
