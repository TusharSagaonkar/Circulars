Circular OC No. 778 dated 17th November, 2017

OPENING OF SAVING BANK/CURRENT ACCOUNT OF A PROPOSED CO-OPERATIVE HOUSING SOCIETY

FOR APPLYING TO AREA DEVELOPMENT BODY (E.G. CIDCO-MHADA) FOR ALLOTMENT OF PLOT

________________________________________________________________________________________

I.                 Currently, there are instructions on record, vide Office Order No. 305 dated 26th September, 2009, to open Saving and Current Account in the name of a proposed co-operative housing society for occupied buildings wherein not less than 50% flats are occupied, subject to the stipulations contained therein and upon obtaining a letter from the promoters as per Annexure-A thereto.

 

II.  We received recently a request for opening a Saving Bank Account from promoters of a proposed Co-operative Housing Society who intend to apply for allotment of plot(s) from area development authorities such as CIDCO, MHADA etc. for eventual construction of a building/buildings for the benefit of members’ residence etc. if & when such an allotment materialises. The matter was examined and considering that there was merit in the request, a note was put up in the Board Meeting dated 17.11.2017 which met with approval.

 

III.   It will therefore be in order to open a Saving or Current Account in the name of a Proposed Co-Operative Housing Society which is being set up initially for the purpose of applying to some area development authority for allotment of plot(s) to be followed by construction of building for the benefit of members if & when the proposed society receives allotment. Branches should follow the procedure as detailed in Annexure-I hereto for opening such an account.

 

IV.  A letter as per Annexure-II hereto should be obtained from promoters-cum-initial office bearers so as to protect Bank’s interests until such time the society remains unregistered. The letter-cum-undertaking essentially ensures that (1) any deletion/ substitution / updating in the list of members will be with the consent of all the existing members, (2) in case of objection by any one of the members of the proposed society, Bank will have the authority to freeze the A/c, (3) no credits into account from persons other than members of the society will be caused by the society or allowed by the Bank and (4) as soon as society is registered, promoters-cum-initial-office-bearers will inform the Bank in respect thereof and submit a copy each of Certificate of Registration and Bye-Laws approved by the Registrar of Co-op. Societies.

 

 

V.    Needless to add that branches should obtain the set of Account Opening Forms comprising of AOF-II, CIF-II & CIF-I (of all authorised signatories along with KYC documents namely PAN and Aadhaar number towards Proof of Identity and Proof of Address) duly filled up.

 

VI.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
