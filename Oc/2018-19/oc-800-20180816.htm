Circular OC No. 800 dated 16th August, 2018

Master Circular for Compliances to be made under GST Act

 

 

I.    INTRODUCTION:

 

1. What is GST:

GST i.e. Goods & Services Tax is implemented w.e.f. 01st July, 2017. With the introduction of GST, Service Tax collected from our customer is subsumed in GST i.e. instead of charging Service Tax, our Bank now charges GST.

 

2. Rate of GST:

The rate of GST is 18% for all kinds of Banking and Financial Services. Hence, for charges levied to customers for various services rendered by our Bank, the tax rate is 18%. (Tax rate is different for sale of Gold or other assets during the course of recovery and for sale of dead stock).

 

3. GST Registration Number:

Under GST, registration is state-wise and hence our Bank is having 3 GST Registration Numbers, one each under the states of Maharashtra, Karnataka and Gujarat. GST numbers in the three states are as under.

 

State

GST Number

Maharashtra

27AAAAA0300L1ZC

Gujarat

24AAAAA0300L1ZI

Karnataka

29AAAAA0300L1Z8

 

GST Reg. numbers should be prominently displayed at each Branch.

 

4. Concept of CGST, SGST and IGST:

i)   GST are of three types - CGST i.e. Central GST, SGST i.e. State GST and IGST i.e.  Integrated GST. If the supplier and receiver of goods or services are in the same state, then two GSTs will be levied i.e. CGST (9%) and SGST (9%); and if supplier and receiver of goods or services are in different states, then one GST will be levied i.e. IGST (18%).

ii) In case of our Bank, place of supply of service is the location of the Branch from where the service is provided and place of receipt of service is the address of the customer in the records of the Bank. If the customer is holding his account with  a Branch in Maharashtra and approaches a Branch in Gujrat to carry out some transaction, then in a such case,  the Branch in Gujrat is the place of supply of service and address of customer i.e. Maharashtra is the place of receipt of service. In that case,  IGST will be levied by Branch in Gujrat as it has collected charges from the customer in Maharashtra. If the customer’s address and the Branch from where service is provided is in the same state, then CGST and SGST, both, will be levied.

iii)            Out of total GST of 18%, CGST share will be 9% and SGST share will be 9%. IGST is single tax charged @ 18%.

 

5. Concept of reverse charge on purchase/service from Unregistered vendor:

Until now, if Bank made any purchases/or availed any services from a vendor who was unregistered, Bank was not required to pay any tax on the same. Under GST, for any transaction with an unregistered vendor (i.e. purchase of goods / services), we (Bank) are required to pay GST on the same. Hence, all Departments/Branches should avoid purchases from URD (unregistered dealer) as far as possible. Further,if purchases by Departments from URD are unavoidable for some reason, Departments sanctioning the expenditure are advised to take into consideration such costs while evaluating the quotations etc.

 

Please note that Exemption from payment of GST on purchases/services from unregistered vendor is availble till 30th Sep, 2018. Hence, Bank is not liable to pay GST on reverse charge till 30.09.2018.

 

II.   INSTRUCTIONS TO BRANCHES:

 

1. Collection of GST Number of our customers:

 

i)   The GST registration number of our customer is required by us for the purpose of issuance of invoices and filing of GST returns.

ii)  GST number is issued only to a person carrying on any business/profession etc. Others will not have GST number. E.g. if a salary earner has an account with us, he will not be having a GST number. If a current account holder is having an account with us, we need to collect GST number as he is engaged in business/profession etc.

iii)             It is not necessary that every person having business will have GST number, since GST is not mandatory for a business turnover of which is below Rs. 20 lakh per annum. GST is also not mandatory for an entity which is providing goods or services which are exempt from taxes.

iv)While collecting GST number from a customer, branches are requested to obtain a photocopy of the GST registration certificate of the customer. The GST number is a 15 digit number, in which first 2 digits are state code, i.e. 27 for Maharashtra, 24 for Gujarat and 29 for Karnataka. Next 10 digits are PAN of the person. Hence, branches should check accordingly while accepting the GST number. GST number will be in the name of the business (i.e. in case of a Partnership Firm, it will be in the name of the Firm and not in the name of a Partner.Similarly, for a Company, it will be in the name of the Company. Please note that in case of proprietary firm, GST No. can be in the name of the proprietor. 

 

v)  The GST number in OMNI is linked to Customer Master. If a proprietor is having 2 proprietorship firms, then he may have 2 separate GST numbers or 1 single GST number (at the option of the proprietor). In such a case, while updating GST number in OMNI, branch should take care and update GST number to proper customer ID.

vi)Branches should note that wherever customer has provided GST number, the same should be immediately and correctly updated in OMNI. Non-feeding or wrong feeding of GST number of customer in OMNI will attract penalty under GST Act, as all the returns will be uploaded by placing reliance on GST number entered in the customer master. Branches are requested to take specific care if GST number contains O (letter ‘O’) or 0 (number ‘0’), I (letter ‘I’) or 1 (number ‘1).

vii)  Before collecting GST number, Branches should ensure that, the state code in the GST number and the address of the customer is matching. E.g. customer having address in Maharashtra should have GST number starting from 27 etc.

viii)In case customer has submitted multiple GST numbers, then branches shall accept and update the GST number pertaining to the state which the customer has provided as part of the address.

 

2. Updating of GST number in OMNI:

The option to update the GST registration number is provided in OMNI 3.0 under Master>>GST Module>> Customer GST Mapping. Please note that Branches shall update only one GST number for a customer ID.

3. GST on Income :

GST is applicable to all kinds of charges collected from our customers including amount collected towards reimbursement of actual expenses incurred.

Exceptions to this are as under:

i)       Amount collected towards Share Capital

ii)     Interest Income on all kinds of loans and advances

iii)   Income from investment (Interest / Discount / Dividend etc.)

iv)    Insurance claim received (if any) for loss of dead stock

v)     Exchange gain on foreign currencies between Bank to Bank transactions

vi)    Bad Debt written off recovered

vii)  Reversal of any provisions

Please note that GST is applicable for sale of Gold or other Assets during the course of Recovery of loans, sale of Dead stock, sale of scrap, money conversion, interest on cash outlay of funds, Nominal Fees and Entrance Fees (including PNCPS fees) also.

Branches should note that any reimbursement of actual expenses incurred shall be recovered alongwith GST @ 18%. E.g. Visit charges, postage etc.

Also, any charges that are applied to any loan account will also attract GST @ 18%.

4. Income Module :

i)   Various charges collected by branches should be entered in a proper manner in OMNI to ensure generation of invoice and reporting of GST transactions in GST return.

ii) There are certain charges which are run by I.T. Dept periodically (e.g. minimum balance, SMS charges etc). Some charges are run by system automatically at the instant when the transaction takes place (e.g. charges for withdrawal from ATM). Some charges are run by branches through specific programmes (e.g. TOD charges etc.) provided in the system. Some charges are to be collected by selecting specific activity (e.g. payorder, BGISSUE etc). In all such cases, GST is automatically collected by system.

iii)            Apart from the above, all charges collected are entered manually (e.g. service charges for loans, Duplicate payorder, e-payment of taxes etc) by branches. For collecting such charges, branches should use Income Module provided in OMNI 3.0 under Master>>GST Module>>Income Module. Branches can collect charges by entering the account number of the customer from whom charges are to be collected.

iv)            Any manual charges entered in the system without the use of Income Module will not be captured for generation of invoice and for the reports generated for the purpose of GST return. Please note that staff member/officer concerned will be held responsible for any loss to the Bank due to the same, such as GST credit is not passed on to customer, any fine / penalty for non reporting etc.

v) No Module is provided till date for charges collected in cash by I.T. Dept. Hence charges collected in cash are  to be entered manually in the system. Invoice will not be generated for such transactions. As far as possible, branches should avoid collection of charges in cash.

 

5. Issue of Invoice:

 

i.   Under GST Act, Bank needs to issue an invoice to its customer towards any kind of charges collected from him. Features of invoice are as under:

a.  The Invoice should be named as ‘Tax Invoice’.

b. The Invoice should contain the Name, address and GST number of our Bank. (e.g. if charges are collected by any branch in Maharashtra, the GST number will be the one pertaining to Maharashtra State).

c.  The Invoice will contain the Name, Address and GST number of the customer.

d. The amount of charges collected and GST charged on the same and its total.

e.  SAC code.

f.   Signature.

ii.  Branches should issue invoices to customer as and when customer demands it.

 

iii.                Branches can generate invoices from following menu in OMNI 3.0.

Report>>GST Report>> Customer Invoice

iv.             Please note that Invoices will not be generated for the transactions which are entered manually in the system. Branches should take due care while making the entries of charges collected from customers.

 

6. GST return:

 

i)   Data of all invoices generated is to be submitted to government on a monthly basis. This activity is centralised at Accounts Department and hence no compliance in this regard is required at Branch level. In case any issue or query is received from a customer regarding reporting of data, the Branch concerned should contact Accounts Dept. In this regard, please note that only those invoices will be reported which are entered in the system by following proper procedure. If entries are passed manually, then the reporting will not be made in the return.

ii) Before contacting Accounts Department, branches should:

a.  Check whether correct GST number is updated in system.

b. Check the invoice of the month concerned for which query is raised and ensure that all charges levied to that customer during the month are covered in the invoice.

c.  Ensure that no charges are collected manually.

 

7. Reversal /waiver of service charges collected:

Our Bank collects various charges from customers. Sometimes, Branches reverse the charges collected due to various reasons such as waiver on customer's request, wrongly collected or excess collected etc.

It may be noted that, Branches shall reverse GST alongwith the charges if the reversal of charges is on account of waiver within same month.

If charges recovered in  a month are subsequently waived, reversal of GST thereon will not be permissible.

In cases other than waiver, such as charges wrongly collected or collected in excess etc., Branches can reverse the charges alongwith the GST amoun thereon.

Previously, we  didn’t have any module to pass entries for reversal of charges. Now, our I.T. Department has provided a module for passing reversal entries of charges collected.

 

The option is provided under the GST Module >Reversal of Income(Credit Note) as follows.

 

Now onwards branches should reverse the charges collected (either with GST or without GST) through the above module only.

 

In above module:

ü  Charges collected date: Branch should enter original date of collection of charges, which now Branch wants to reverse.

ü  Invoice no. : Enter the original invoice no. Branch can get the invoice no. from Report> GST Report > Branch wise GST Report or Report> GST Report>Cust. Invoice Report .

          After entering the above details, the fields of Customer Name, GST No., Address will    be auto populated.

ü  PL Account ID: Branch shall enter the PL code where the charges shall be debited. (the field generally auto populated)

ü  Cr Account ID: Branch shall enter Br. Code and Account No. of the customer where the charges are to be credited. (the field generally auto populated)

ü  Charges Amount: In this column, amount of charges originally collected by the Branch against the invoice no. specified above will be reflected directly after entering original Invoice number and date.

ü  Amount to be reversed: Branch shall specify the amount of charges to be reversed to the customer. e.g. if Branch has collected Rs. 1000/- towards Loan Service charges and borrower is granted 40% waiver. Here Branch will enter Rs. 400 as amount to be reversed. Please note that the amount to be reversed cannot exceed the charges originally collected.

ü  CGST/SGST Amount: The CGST and SGST or IGST amount will be auto calculated. Here, Branches shall note that GST will  be reversed in case of waiver of charges to customer if within same month, GST shall be reversed only in cases where charges were wrongly collected or excess collected. In case of reversal due to waiver, branch shall make CGST/SGST/IGST as "0" (zero) if not reversed in same month.

ü  Reason Code: Pick the correct reason carefully.

ü  Narration: Mentioned narration briefly.

 

Note: Branches should carefully check Charges amount, PL Code & Cr. Account ID from GST Report.

 

Use TAB while passing entries.

 

After creating and authorizing entries in the above module, following entry will be directly passed in OMNI.

 

PL Account - --- Dr

CGSTPAY/SGSTPAY/IGSTPAY --- Dr (if any)

 To Customers Account          ---Cr.                  

                  

All Branches and Departments are hereby advised to use the above module at the time of reversal of charges collected.

 

Please note that Branches shall not pass entry manually henceforth; else it will result in incorrect reporting of the data in GST returns.

8. GST paid on expenses:

i)   Our vendors charge GST to us on various goods purchased and services availed by our Bank from them. E.g. if Bank has purchased computer from M/s. ABC, then M/s. ABC will levy GST on the invoice issued by them towards purchase of computer and Bank will pay total amount including GST to M/s. ABC. Bank gets Input Tax Credit of GST paid to our vendor against the GST collected from customers and to get this benefit of set off, Bank needs to report the details of all purchases made from vendors and services received from the vendors to the

Government. These details are to be uploaded invoice-wise on the portal provided by the Government. For expenses which are released/paid by Bills Dept centrally, requisite compliance for such expenditure will be done by Bills Dept. centrally.

ii) For expenses such as telephone bill, electricity bill, all petty cash expenses, sweeper’s salary, rent of premises etc. incurred by branches/department themselves, branches/departments are required to make the following compliances.

a.  Obtain invoice for every expense paid.

b. Our Bank’s name and GST Number should be strictly mentioned on invoice. If our Bank’s GST No. is not stated on invoice/bill our bank will not able to claim input credit, Concern Staff/officer will be responsible for any loss of input credit to the Bank. Also ensure that GST No. of the vendor is shown separately in the invoice if the shopkeeper/vendor is registered under GST. If vendor’s GST registration number is printed on the invoice, it indicates that the shop is registered under GST. In such a case, branches should insist that GST amount is shown separately in the invoice. (For goods/services which are exempt from GST. GST will be NIL even if vendor is registered and if the vendor is registered under composition scheme, then GST will not be charged by the vendor). If the shopkeeper/vendor has charged GST in invoice but has not printed his GST number, then insist that the GST number shall be mentioned in invoice.

c.  I.T. Department has provided ‘Expenses Module’ in OMNI. The entry for any kind of expenses is to be made through “Expenses Module”only. In case of any deviation, the staff/official concerned will be held personally responsible for any loss/penalty to the Bank.

d. While making entry, separate entry for each expense is to be made on daily basis. E.g. If rent is paid to 4 persons who are joint owners of the premises, then 4 separate entries will be made for rent.

e.  While making entries, branches should ensure following.

·      Select GST Y/N properly. If the vendor has GST number, then “YES” should be selected even if vendor has not levied GST in the invoice. Branches/Departments can mention GST amount as ‘0’ in such cases.

·      Mention vendor name i.e. the name of vendor from whom goods are purchased or services are obtained. (Some Branches are mentioning the name of the staff or PL head e.g. in case of Postage, Branch should mention the name of Post office and not the name of staff who has brought the postage stamps or some Branches are mentioning vendor name as ‘Other repairs’ / ‘Other expenses’ etc which is PL description and not the vendor name).

·      Mention GST number of the vendor in the GST number field (some branches are mentioning our Bank’s GST number in this field which is incorrect)

·      Mention Invoice number and Date correctly (some branches mention entry date in the invoice date field, some branches are mentioning voucher number in the invoice number column or entering 0 or 1 etc.)

·      Mention HSN/SAC code in “HSN Code” field. (Many branches are entering either 0 or 1 in that field). This field is applicable only if GST is levied in bill.

·      Mention Invoice value, GST amount as mentioned in the invoice. Branches can adjust rounding of difference in Invoice value. GST amount should be exactly tallied with the bill/invoice. Where vendor is having GST number but has not charged GST in bill, Branches should mention GST as ‘0’.

·      If GST is levied in the invoice, then branches has to show the GST amount. We have come across certain instances where branches have not entered GST amount in “Expenses Module” even if GST is mentioned in the bill/invoice.

·      Sometimes branches/departments are booking expenses where invoice is not raised in our Bank’s name, e.g. a few branches are paying insurance premium of ‘case file accounts’ etc. wherein invoices are raised in the name of borrower etc. In such cases, branches should mention GST as ‘0’ even if the same is charged in the invoice. In such cases total amount including GST will be debited to concerned PL head.

·      If branches are paying the expense directly by debiting to customers account (e.g. stock audit bill) and not by debiting to our P&L, then branches should not use “Expenses Module”.

·      Branches have to ensure that entries for all expenses irrespective of whether GST is levied or not (except interest on deposit, reversal of service charges collected from customers, quarterly provisions) are passed through “Expenses Module” only. Repeated instructions were issued by Accounts Department from time to time. Inspite of the same, we have come across some  instances where branches had passed the entries manually.

·      The amount entered in CGST and SGST fields will be debited to separate GL Heads created for the same. GL head created for CGST amount is “CGSTREC1”, and for SGST, it is “SGSTREC1”.

·      Once branches have entered the required data in fields of Expenses Module, following entry/voucher will be passed in OMNI.

PL Expenses Head --- Dr

CGSTREC1 --- Dr

SGSTREC1 --- Dr

PCASH/POADJ etc. as the case may be.---Cr      

If GST is zero, no entries will be passed in CGSTREC1 and SGSTREC1.

f.   Branches will scan the invoices on daily basis wherein GST is charged (except for postage and Entertainment Expenses) and send the same through exchange server on next day to a separate ID created on exchange server as GSTBILLS@abhyudayabank.com immediately. Original invoices wherein GST is charged should be sent on quarterly basis within 5 days from the end of the quarter to Accounts Department, Vashi for record. E.g. invoices pertaining to the expenses entered during Jan-Mar will be sent on or before 5th April etc, Branches in Karnataka and Gujarat (including regional offices) should not send the hard copies of original invoices to Accounts Department.

 

9. Short GST collection:

The staff/officer concerned will be held responsible for non collection or short collection of GST on charges levied to customers.

 

III. INSTRUCTIONS TO DEPARTMENTS SANCTIONING AND RELEASING EXPENSES THROUGH BILLS DEPARTMENT:

 

1. Collection of GST number from Vendors:

It is the responsibility of sanctioning department to collect GST Number of new Vendors and inform the same to Bills Department in the following format for necessary updation in OMNI.

Name of the Organization/Vendor

Registered Address under GST

State

GST Provisional ID

Email &Phone No

 

 

 

 

 

 

2. Obtain Tax Invoice from Vendors:

i)   To claim input tax credit under GST, we should have a TAX INVOICE issued and signed by vendor containing the following details.

a) The word “TAX INVOICE”.

a)  Name, Complete address and GST No. of vendor.

b) The invoice should be numbered and dated.

c)  Our Bank’s name, address and GST no. as a Recipient of goods / service.

d) Description of Goods purchased or service availed.

e)  HSN code of Goods purchased / SAC of services availed.

f)   GST rate and GST amount (CGST/SGST/IGST should be shown separately).

g)  Signature of vendor or its authorized person.

ii) In case of purchases from unregistered dealers, a general invoice will be obtained. In case of purchases from URD, we are liable to pay GST on such expenses. Exemption is provided from this liability till 30th June 2018.

3. Release of Bills within stipulated time:

i)   All Departments releasing the payments of various bills through Bills Dept. are requested to ensure that bills pertaining to a month are released on or before 10th day of succeeding month. (IDC Accts/8/18-19 dated 5th Apr. 2018)

ii)  The same is required to ensure smooth filing of GST return and to take input credit of the same.

iii)             Please note that delay in release of Bills beyond the stipulated time will result in mismatch in GST returns of our Bank and vendor, which may result in disallowance of input tax credit.

 

4. No benefit of input tax credit if the tax is not deposited by vendor:

If a vendor has charged and collected GST from us but has not paid the same to the Government, Bank will not be able to claim the input tax credit (set off) of the same. The same will result in increased cost for our Bank. In order to avoid such instances, it is suggested that, in case of regular parties, a clause in agreement should preferably be added that Bank will retain some amount if it is observed that vendor is not depositing, or depositing late, the amount of GST collected by him to Government & further if a vendor continues to make such default, Bank can terminate the contract.

 

5. Advance payment of expenses:

 

i)   The input credit is available only at the time of completion of work. Hence if the advance payment is made to any vendor, Bank can claim input credit only when final invoice is raised by the vendor even though Bank has paid GST at the time of making advance payment. Therefore, if any payment is released in advance, obtain receipt voucher at the time of payment and when the work is completed, obtain tax invoice from Vendor for total amount and send the same to Bills Dept.

ii) So long as  the final tax invoice is not received, payment will be kept under ‘Advance for Expenses A/c’ by Bills Department.

 

6. Refund of payments or deductions from invoice amount:

Obtain credit note from vendor in case of Refund of payments / deduction from invoice amount. Credit note will contain all the details of tax invoice except that the heading will be “credit note” and not “tax invoice” and the reference of original invoice no. will also be there.

 

7. Lapse of input tax credit:

It may be noted that input tax credit of GST charged in invoice will lapse within a period of one year from the date of invoice or 30th September from the end of Financial Year whichever is earlier. E.g. if an invoice dated 01.08.2017 is received for payment after 31.07.2018, then Bank will not be able to claim input tax credit of GST levied in invoice.

Hence before obtaining invoice, ensure that the date of invoice is not beyond the period as above. If the same is beyond the period as above, special approval from Board will be required as GST levied will be the additional cost for the Bank.

 

8. Separate Bill for each state and separate bill for Departments:

 

i)   In case of a common vendor for all branches, obtain 3 separate invoices for branches in 3 states. The bill will be raised on separate GST numbers in each state. Single invoice will suffice for branches in single state.

ii) In case of purchases made or services availed by/for H.O. or any Department as well as by/for branches, obtain separate invoice for H.O./Departments and Branches though located in single state. E.g. in case of AMC in branches and departments in Maharashtra, obtain separate Bill for Department and for branches.

iii)            To avoid disputes with vendors at later stage, it is advised that departments should inform the vendors in advance (before issuing Purchase order) the requirement of separate tax invoices for separate states where services are provided or goods are supplied.

 

9. Expenditure which are common in nature:

i)   In case of expenditure which are common in nature and cannot be allotted to a branch or apportioned among branches, it is necessary to distribute the input tax credit of GST charged on such invoice to 3 states in the ratio of turnover in each state. In such cases, invoice will be obtained in the name of Head Office only. Head Office will show the inter-deparment/inter-branch sale to distribute the input tax credit amongst three states.

ii) Accounts Department will allocate the input tax credit amongst states in the ratio of turnover of each state.

 

IV. INSTRUCTIONS TO ALL DEPARTMENTS INCURRING PETTY CASH EXPENSES:

 

1. Certain expenses such as telephone bill, electricity bill, petty cash expenses, sweeper’s salary etc. are incurred by departments themselves. In such cases, all the instructions relating to entry of expenses to Branches as per point no. II-8 above will also be applicable to Departments while making petty cash expenses.

 

V.   INSTRUCTIONS TO REGIONAL OFFICES IN AHMEDABAD AND UDUPI:

1. While sanctioning and releasing expenses, instructions pertaining to Departments sanctioning and releasing expenditure through Bills Department are to be followed.

2. While paying petty cash expenses, related instructions in point II above are to be followed.

3. RO Ahmedabad and RO Udupi are required to use ‘Expenses Module’ for making entries of expenses.

4. GST returns and payments pertaining to the state of Gujarat and Karnataka will be handled by Accounts Department with the support of R.O.s.

 

VI. INSTRUCTIONS TO BILLS DEPARTMENT:

 

1. Before making payment to a vendor, ensure tax invoice is submitted by the Vendor containing all the details.

2. In case of payments made in advance, ensure that receipt voucher is given by the vendor. Also ensure that the vendor has provided Tax Invoice after work is completed/final payment is made.

3. Follow up should be made on continuous basis if there is any mismatch in ITC (Input Tax Credit) in respect to data uploaded by the vendor in form GSTR 1.

4. Make Invoice-wise entry in Bills Module and fill all the details such as invoice number, date, description of Goods / Service along with HSN/Accounting code, GST amount for smooth functioning at the time of uploading data online and for matching of Input Tax Credit.

5. Generate the data at the end of month for expenses on which input tax credit is claimed and expenses on which GST is to be charged on reverse basis through the reports provided in Bills Module.

6. Segregation of input tax credit in three states i.e. Input tax credit eligible to be claimed in Maharashtra, Gujarat and Karnataka and common input tax credit for all branches.

 

VII.  INSTRUCTIONS TO ACCOUNTS DEPARTMENT:

 

1.    Calculate GST liability separately for each state at the end of every month.

2.    Generate data of invoices issued to customers through report provided in OMNI.

3.    File the return of outward supply based on the report.

4.    Distribute common input tax credit to Branches in Maharashtra, Gujarat and Karnataka.

5.    File the return of inward supply based on data prepared by bills department, branches, departments, regional offices.

6.    Work out GST liability under reverse charge.

7.    Payment of GST.

8.    Maintain records of returns filed, input tax credit claimed, working of GST and invoices copies.

9.    Passing of necessary entries in OMNI and overall control on various GST related heads in GL and PL.

10. Ensure compliance with provisions of GST Act and issue necessary guidance / instructions to branches & departments.

11. Resolve queries of Branches and other Departments.

 

VIII.   INSTRUCTIONS TO I.T. DEPARTMENT:

 

1. I.T. Department should make necessary changes in OMNI through Infrasoft to enable compliance with GST Act and Rules as required.

IX.      Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members concerned and ensure strict compliance.
