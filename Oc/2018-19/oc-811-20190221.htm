Circular OC No. 811 dated 21st February, 2019

Offering Life Insurance Services through

Exide Life Insurance Company Ltd.

 

 

I.   Our Bank has been extending insurance services, as corporate agents, in respect of Life, General Risks & Health, to our customers under the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDA), (Regulations of Corporate Agents) Regulations, 2015. The three companies for which we currently work as corporate agents are Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC), The New India Assurance Company Ltd. (NIAC) and Religare Health Insurance Services Ltd. (RHIC).

 

II.  Life Insurance is need of the hour, especially given its low market penetration levels. It also gives an opportunity to increase Bank’s fee based income. The Life Insurance industry has been consistently growing over the last decade and gives our Bank an opportunity to take advantage of the growing market, with relevant and customer friendly products. Therefore, our Bank’s Board in its meeting held on 27th November, 2018 decided to enter into an agreement each with (1) Exide Life Insurance Company Ltd., & (2) Max Life Insurance Services Ltd., both of which are Life insurance companies engaged in the distribution & servicing of life insurance products. Exide Life Insurance products have been made available to customers through our Bank branches w. e. f. 7th February, 2019.

III.      1) About Exide Life Insurance

 

                      i)   Exide Life Insurance Company Limited commenced operations in 2001-02 and is head quartered in Bengaluru. The company is 100% owned by Exide Industries Limited. The company serves over 15 lakh customers and manages assets of over Rs. 12,500 Crore. During the financial year 2017-18, the company earned Total Premium Income of over Rs. 2,500 Crore.

 

                     ii)   Exide Life Insurance distributes its products through multi-channels viz. Agency, Bancassurance, Corporate Agency & Broking which are Direct Channels, and Online channels as well. The Agency channel comprises 45,000+ advisors who are attached to over 200 company offices across the country. Bancassurance Channel has the highest number of tie ups i.e. with 68 Co-operative Banks spread across the country which are having a network of over 3,500 branches. The company also offers group life insurance solutions.

 

                   iii)   The company is focused on providing long term protection and savings solution plans and has a strong traditional product portfolio with a consistent bonus track record. The company has ISO 9001:2015 quality certification for all Customer Service processes and the ISO / IEC 27001:2013 for information security management.

 

2) Life Insurance Products

                      i)   Exide Life Insurance follows a "customer centric approach" while designing its products. Product portfolio offers insurance products that cater to every financial need at all the life stages. Exide Life Insurance has a bouquet of products customized for Bank’s customers to take care of their varied needs and provide comprehensive coverage viz.:

a) Protection: Solutions help build a strong foundation for a family’s financial future.

b) Savings and Investment Solutions help make disciplined contribution towards savings and wealth creation to accomplish life goals.

c) Retirement and Pension Plans help prepare for a happy retirement which enables in continuing the standard of life lived during earning years.

 

              ii)   Details of products in each of these categories are available on https://www.exidelife.in/products/

 

             iii)   Planning Tools & Calculators

Exide provides a multitude of Planning Tools and Calculators to help in financial planning. Details on Planning Tools & Calculators from Exide are available on the following link.

https://www.exidelife.in/planning-tools-calculators

 

3) Procedural Guidelines for Procuring Business:

                      i)   Field Staff Support by Exide Life Insurance Company – Exide Life will deploy personnel called Relationship Managers (RM) to our branches. These Relationship managers report to the Territory Managers (TM) and they in turn report to the Cluster Manger (CM). Each Relationship Manager will be mapped to some branches of our Bank and he / she will assist in Life insurance business from these allocated branches. Branch officials / staff should engage with the customer and educate him/her about our Bank being a Corporate Agent of Exide Life Insurance. Each branch shall maintain a lead register at its end. The status of the lead register shall be updated daily and reviewed on weekly basis by In Charge of the branch to monitor progress of the business. A staff member at our branch/department who has passed the IRDAI examination on Life Insurance services will be ‘The Specified Person’ (SP) functioning as competent authority to sign on the proposal form.

 

                    ii)   The Manpower allocation matrix will be communicated to all branches by the Insurance department. The matrix will also be made available in the Insurance Folder on the Exchange Server.

4) Detailed Claim Process:

Procedure is designed to ensure that claims are settled with ease and minimal documentation.

                      i)   Guide the customer to register his claims as soon as the loss has occurred.

                    ii)   Use the claim intimation form (made available to branches by Exide representative). This form is also available at

a) Nearest Exide Life Insurance Branch Office list available on

https://www.exidelife.in/branch - locator

b) Online claim registration on website of Exide

https://www.exidelife.in/customer-service/ claims

c) Written request to Exide - Claims@exidelife.in

 

d) Customer should fill the form completely and attach required documents. The same can be submitted at:

·      Exide Life Insurance Branch Office

https://www.exidelife.in/branch-locator

·      Mailed to Claims Department of Exide at:
Exide Life Insurance Company Limited,
1st Floor, J P Techno Park, No. 3 / 1,
Millers Road, Bangalore - 560 001.

·      OR handed over to Exide Life Relationship Managers assigned to our branch.

 

e) On receipt of claim documents, branches may issue acknowledgement to customer on the photocopy of the same. The documents if acknowledged by the branch should be handed over to the representative of Exide at the earliest with proper acknowledgement. Policy number can be used for all queries about the claim till such time that the claim is decided.

 

f)  A Few Things To be Advised to Customers For Smooth Claim Settlement

·      Read the terms and conditions carefully.

·      Always keep contact details up to date.

·      Pay premium regularly to ensure that the policy is in force (active).

·      Share details of insurance policy & the claim process with your loved ones.

·      Always understand the details of the policy to know what is covered.

·      Check the beneficiary name in the policy. The nominee can be changed at any point of time.

·      The nominee name should be changed if:

o  The registered nominee has died during the term of the policy,

o  The policy is reassigned in the name of our customer, after he has repaid the Bank loan for which the policy was used as collateral.

 

g)          For further details on insurance claims through Exide, please refer to: https://www.exidelife.in/customer-service/claims

 

5) Role of Branches:

i)      Branches should route the new as well as renewal business directly through the Relationship Manager deputed by Exide Life at the branch.

 

ii)    All branches will be attached to the nearest office of the Exide Life Insurance Company. This list will be made available in the Insurance Folder on the Exchange Server by the Insurance department.

 

iii)  Branches have to maintain record of all the leads, Policy & Claim details provided to Exide Life Insurance and update the same on daily basis in the pre-printed register provided by Insurance Department. Details of total business marketed under each category of Insurance Product are to be forwarded to Insurance Department for maintaining consolidated record at their end.

 

iv)  Exide Life in consultation with Head Office and Insurance department will undertake various marketing activities to cross sell insurance to the Bank customers through branches.

 

v)    Claims of the customers have to be forwarded directly to Exide Life office or the representative of Exide assigned to branches. Customer should be advised to retain photocopies of the documents before submission of claim form.

 

6) Training:

Exclusive Training team for Bancassurance partner works as a catalyst in business procurement. Training Managers from Exide would work more as business enablers, as their performance is measured on branch productivity & team activation. Such training would be conducted through our training centre at Vashi or directly at branches in consultation with the In-charge of branches on need-to-know basis.

 

                i)   Objectives:

a) Structured Training Strategy for target group which includes both Exide Life and our Bank’s staff.

b) A comprehensive need analysis of all the skills and knowledge requirements.

c) The implementation of tie-up will be done with a robust process to ensure timely and quality delivery.

d) A predefined road map to enhance the efficiency of the employees.

e) In addition to this, the team will also lend their support in development of all behavioural and soft skills for staff and sales leaders.

7) Procedural Aspects:

i)      Commonly used stationery and proposal forms for Exide Life insurance products will be made available by Exide Life Insurance to branches. Exide Life Insurance also uses Mobile application to login the proposal form.

 

ii)     Branches should collect the pay in slip, specially designed for premium collection, for Exide Life Insurance, to avoid delay. These slips will be made available to branches by the Insurance department. Branches can also collect crossed Cheque duly signed by the proposer or P.O. / DD in favour of “Exide Life Insurance Company Limited".

 

iii)   Premium shall be calculated as per templates to be provided to branches for different kinds of policies. All branches shall send the proposals directly to the designated offices of Exide Life Insurance on the same day or latest on the next working day through the Exide staff assigned to the branch.

 

iv)   Please note that the original policy documents will be directly delivered at the customer’s address mentioned in the proposal form.

8) Role of Insurance Department, Vashi:

Insurance Department will

i)      Act as a coordinator between Exide Life Insurance & branches for providing necessary support for procuring new as well as renewal of policies.

 

ii)     Will provide preprinted lead, issue & claim register to branches for proper maintenance of record.

 

iii)   Guide branches / customers and help them in resolving their queries.

 

iv)   Shall make arrangement for imparting zone-wise training to In-charge of the branches and staff members concerned handling insurance business at branches with the help of officials from Exide Life Insurance & staff training college.

 

v)     Will transfer the commission received from Exide Life Insurance to branches on monthly basis, along with the MIS on branch-wise business performance.

 

vi)    As and when new products are introduced or changes are made in the existing products by Exide Life Insurance, the same will be communicated to branches by Insurance department.

 

vii)  Will provide marketing material and support to branches.

 

9) Role of Marketing Dept –

Bank’s Marketing Department shall provide adequate marketing material at all the branches. The sales support material shall comprise of posters, banners, danglers, standees and leaflets which will be made available at all the branches. For additional marketing material, branch staff may contact our Insurance Department at Vashi.

 

10)          Role of Credit Department, Head Office :

Exide Life Insurance in association with the Insurance & Credit Department will roll out Insurance products for loan customers.

 

11)          Role of I.T. Department :

Exide Life Insurance in association with the Insurance department will integrate the systems and roll out the process for premium collection.

 

IV.   Kindly take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff and officials concerned at your Branch / Department. Our Bank endeavours to extend the products & services of Exide Life Insurance to as many customers as possible. Each employee is urged to make this partnership a success by contributing to the effort and increasing the fee based income of our Bank.
