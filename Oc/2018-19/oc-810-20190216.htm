Circular OC No. 810 dated 16th February, 2019

On-boarding UPI Platform for Fund Transfers through Mobile Phones

 

I.Bank has introduced one more customer service-centric-facility, namely “Unified Payment Interface (UPI)”. UPI is an NPCI initiative. We have on-boarded the UPI platform as an Issuer.

 

II.  What is UPI: UPI or Unified Payments Interface is an immediate real-time payment system that helps in instantly transferring funds between any two bank accounts through the platform of mobile phones. UPI is a concept that allows multiple bank accounts to get into a single mobile application.

 

III.   Features of UPI

UPI app comes with many amazing benefits to help the customer to make all types of financial transactions without requiring them to share bank details. Here are some of the features of UPI:

 

1)     Bank details of the customer are required only once, i.e. while registering for UPI, after which, he/she is required to provide his/her UPI ID or Virtual Payment Address (VPA) for transferring funds. There is no need on the customer’s part to provide IFSC code, bank account number or password(s) for any transaction.

 

2)     By using a Single Mobile Application, multiple accounts or different bank accounts can be linked.

 

3)     Single Click-2 Factor Authentication :- While being aligned with the regulatory guidelines, it still provides for a very strong feature of seamless single click payment.

 

4)     A customer does not have to wait to add the payee before he/she can transfer money to the payee’s account. The funds are credited/debited immediately to the respective accounts.

 

5)     UPI works on the IMPS system and hence is available 24x7.

 

6)     Transactions can be done with anyone having a UPI ID.

 

7)     UPI app also works as a mobile wallet and hence can be used to make purchases (both offline and online), recharge mobile or DTH subscriptions and pay utility bills. This is in addition to paying or receiving money.

 

8)     The UPI app has a money collector feature which sends a notification to the customer’s debtor as a payment reminder. The moment the debtor approves the notification, the debt amount is credited to the customer’s account.

 

 

IV.   Eligibility :- As per the NPCI directive, only Debit-Card-holders are eligible for UPI. Further, the mobile number which the customer proposes to use at the time of registration is required to be linked to the customer’s account(s). Currently, we have allowed only saving, current & STCC products for receiving/paying funds on UPI platform.

 

V. Since we have on-boarded UPI as an Issuer, our customers need to download UPI application of any other bank/organization. At the time of registering for UPI, it is mandatory for the customer to possess & declare mobile number as also Debit card number. On the basis of the Debit card, the system will automatically identify the card issuing bank; and on the basis of Mobile number, the APP will fetch all the accounts linked to the said Mobile number including joint accounts, if any, provided first customer number is same across all such joint accounts.

 

VI.   1. Procedure for UPI registration:-

•    Step 1: Download and Install PhonePe app from Google Play store (PhonePe app is mentioned as an example, being one among several options available. Customers may be advised to download any app of their choice & convenience and not necessarily PhonePe).

•    Step 2: Select your preferred language.

•    Step 3: On login page, enter your mob no. and set 4 digit password.

•    Step 4: Link your bank accounts using ‘my money’ option in phonepe.

•    Step 5: In ‘my money’ option under payment methods, click on UPI bank accounts.

•    Step 6: After performing step no 5, next screen opens with bank names. Select Abhyudaya Bank from the list.

•    Step 7: Set up your UPI PIN by providing last 6 digits of debit card and expiry date of debit card.

•    Step 8: Once the steps as above are completed, VPA gets created as Mobile No (99******35)@ybl.

•    Step 9: Send, receive, collect money using virtual payment address (VPA), Account number + IFSC.

 

(Note: Functionality of the UPI App may differ from App to App)

 

2. When Single Mobile Number is Linked to More Than One Customer No. :-

If a Mobile number is linked to more than one customer number, following error will occur: - “Multiple Customer No. Link with Same Mobile No.” In such a situation, branches should counsel the customer to deregister other customer numbers (than the one desired to be retained for UPI transactions), and seek mandate from the customer for deregistration (as per Annexure-I hereto) from the customer in this behalf. The process of deregistration is explained in Annexure-II hereto. Alternatively, branch can guide the customer to use different mobile numbers for accounts with different customer numbers.

 

3. Virtual ID:-

The virtual ID is created by the APP in the format of mobile number @ bank id at the time of registration.

 

4. Limit per Transaction:-

In our bank, we have currently set a limit of Rs. 25,000/- per transaction.

 

VII. As you are aware, our customers harbour a grouse that we are not extending Internet Banking and this is held out by them as one reason for our Bank not being competitive enough in the industry. Extension of funds transfer service on UPI platform will help redress customers’ grievance in this behalf. Therefore, all branches are advised to actively promote the UPI platform among our customers and also offer it to the prospective customers as a marketing proposition.

 

VIII.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned at your Branch/Office/ Department and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
