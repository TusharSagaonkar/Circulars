Circular OC No. 792 dated 7th May, 2018

Customer Rights Policy

_____________________________________________________________________________________

I.                 Our Bank’s Customer Rights Policy prepared on the basis of RBI directives vide their letter No. CEPD.CO.PRS.No.5128/20.66.001/2014-15 dated 27.01.2015 was circularized under cover of Circular OC No. 714 dated 24th June, 2015. The same was recently reviewed & the reviewed policy was approved in the Board Meeting dated 28.03.2018. The policy document is attached herewith for the information of branches.

 

II.  It may be noted that the customer and Bank, both, have the right to be treated with curtsey in terms of the policy. While preparing the policy, Bank has made every effort to ensure that applications/documents/agreements are framed transparently and they can be easily understood by common person. Further, Bank has prepared its products taking into consideration needs of its customers from all the segments of society and from all types of income groups.

 

III.   IT Dept. is advised to put this Customers Rights Policy on Bank’s website for information of our customers and general public.

 

IV.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
