Circular OC No. 795 dated 8th June, 2018

Customer Due Diligence

KYC Documents to be Obtained while Opening New Accounts of Natural Persons [Individuals]

 

 

I.   Please refer OC Circular 673 dated 15th September, 2014. Reserve Bank of India has revised & updated, through RBI/DBR/2015-16/18 on 20th April, 2018, the Master Direction DBR. AML. BC.No.81/14.01.001/2015-16 dated February 25, 2016 on the subject of “Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016”. The changes in the Master Direction indicate increased emphasis on Aadhar, PAN and officially Valid Documents (OVDs). There is also a redefinition of OVDs. In view of this, revised instructions are issued as under due to changes necessitated in respect of accounts of Natural Persons (Individuals).

 

II. The revised Master Direction does not mention Aadhar & PAN among the Officially Valid Documents. However, they are now almost mandatory for the reasons mentioned below.

1. It is a must to obtain Aadhar from all those individuals who are eligible to get the same. This also holds true for accounts of non-individuals so that Aadhar is required to be furnished in respect of Authorised Signatories, Power of Attorney holders & Beneficial Owners of the account to be opened.

 

2. Where the person(s) desirous of opening an account is/are not eligible for Aadhar, Branch will have to obtain a declaration to this effect from the person(s) concerned. We do not anticipate such persons approaching our bank for opening accounts and therefore, this provision is somewhat theoretical for us.

 

3. Thus Aadhar & PAN, both, are must in respect of practically all the persons who are desirous of opening an account with our Bank. These are the two most important documents for starting an account based relationship with the Bank.

III.         1. When Aadhaar Number is received by branches at the time of opening an account i. e. at the time of establishing an account based relationship, branches must carry out biometric e-KYC authentication. The explicit consent of the customer for such authentication, which is required under the Master Direction, is already a part of the application for account opening.

 

2. In case of existing accounts where Yes/No authentication is carried out (through bulk verification procedure advised in terms of Circular OC No. 791 dated 19th April, 2018), branches must ensure to carry out biometric based e-KYC authentication within a period of six months of carrying out Yes/No authentication. Branches will be provided with the lists of such accounts with the dates of their YES/NO authentication.

 

i.   Yes/No authentication in respect of beneficial owners of a legal entity shall suffice in respect of existing accounts and in respect of establishing an account based relationship with a legal entity.

 

ii.  a. In   case  a  customer   eligible   to  be   enrolled   for  Aadhaar  does  not submit the Aadhaar number  at the time of commencement of an account based relationship with us, the customer must produce the proof of application for enrollment for AADHAAR (with Enrollment Identification number) which should not be more than six months old. Where the applicant producing the enrolment for Aadhaar does not produce PAN but submits Form 60 in lieu thereof, Officially Valid Documents (OVDs) for Proof of Address (POA) & Proof of Identity (POI) must be obtained together with a recent passport size photograph. In such a case, the Customer must submit the Aadhaar (and Permanent Account Number or Form 60) within a period of six months from the date of commencement of the account based relationship. In case the customer fails to submit the documents as above within the aforesaid six months’ period, debit as well as credit freeze will have to be applied on the account till the time they are submitted.

 

b. A Declaration-cum-Undertaking as per Annexure I should be obtained from the applicant in such a case.

o  The Central Government, in consultation with the R.B.I. , further amended the PMLA Rules, 2015 on 16th May 2018 and allowed an exemption to provide account based services to clients who are unable to undergo biometric authentication owing to injury, illness or infirmity on account of old age or otherwise, and such like causes.

 

o  In such special cases of account holders who cannot come in person to undergo biometric authentication for the reasons stated above, branches may accept any other alternate and viable means of identification. Such alternate and viable means of identification include any other OVDs apart from Aadhaar card. 

 

iii.   a. All the existing customers of the bank must submit the Aadhaar number    (and Permanent Account Number or Form 60 in lieu thereof if that too has not been submitted) by such date as may be notified by the Central Government.

 

                                                                         

b. In case the customer fails to submit the Aadhaar number and Permanent Account Number or Form 60 in lieu thereof by such date, the said account shall cease to be operational till the time the said document(s) is/are submitted by the customer.

 

c.  It should be noted that at least two notices shall be served upon the customer for compliance with the above before such date.

 

IV. 1. If a customer applying to open an account furnishes Aadhaar but the address of the applicant as per Aadhar is different from his current address (which should fall within the service area of the branch where the account is sought to be opened), he/she must produce one of the five OVDs mentioned in Para 2.i. below which has the current address recorded therein.

2. i. In terms of the latest Master Direction, the Officially Valid Documents are only five and they are as under:

a.  Passport

b. Driving License

c.  Voter’s Identity Card issued by the Election Commission of India

d. Job Card issued by NREGA

e.  Letter issued by the National Population Register containing name & address

ii. If name of the person as mentioned in any of the above five documents is different from the current name of the person and the difference i. e. change in name is supported either by a marriage certificate issued by the State Government or Gazette Notification, it will be deemed as an Officially Valid Document.

 

3. i. In case the OVD from amongst the five listed above OVDs furnished by the customer does not contain the current address updated therein, the following documents shall be deemed to be OVDs for the limited purpose of proof of address, and for a limited period of three months.

a. Utility bill which is not more than two months old of any service provider (electricity, telephone, post-paid mobile phone, piped gas, water bill);

 

b. Property or Municipal tax receipt;

 

c.  Pension or family pension payment orders (PPOs) issued to retired employees by Government Departments or Public Sector Undertakings, if they contain the address;

 

d. Letter of allotment of accommodation from employer issued by State Government or Central Government Departments, statutory or regulatory bodies, public sector undertakings, scheduled commercial banks, financial institutions and listed companies and leave and licence agreements with such employers  allotting official accommodation;

 

ii. The limited relaxation in respect of Proof of Address is interpreted to mean that a person, who has recently moved in the service area of the branch with which an account is sought to be opened, may submit a document from a. to d. in Sub-Para 3. i. above belonging to some other person at whose place he is staying.

 

iii.           It has been decided additionally to accept a self-certified photocopy of Valid & Registered Leave & Licence Agreement in favour of the applicant for the limited purpose of proof of address, for a limited period of three months.

 

iv.           A Declaration-cum-Undertaking as per Annexure II shall be obtained from the applicant in cases such as those mentioned in Sub-Paras 3.ii & iii. immediately above.

 

v.  Any of the documents from a. to d. in Sub-Para 3.i.  above belonging to the other person at whose place the applicant for account-opening is staying, must be accompanied by a written Declaration of Consent as per Annexure III by such other person for having allowed the applicant to reside at the declared local address, although such a letter by itself is no more recognised as an OVD for proof of address, vide Sub-Para no. 4.B.ii. below.

 

vi.    In respect of cases under Sub-Para 3.ii above, the Assistant Manager or such other person as may be designated by the Branch In-Charge in this behalf shall visit the place indicated in the application as the current address, and record his observation in the visit report which should be kept together with the set of Account Opening Forms.

 

vii.           The customer must submit Aadhaar or one of the five OVDs updated with current address within a period of three months of submitting any one of the above documents.

 

viii.           If the customer fails to comply with the condition as per Sub-Para 3.vii immediately above, debit as well as credit freeze will have to be applied on the account till the time Aadhaar or one of the five OVDs with updated current address is submitted by the customer.

 

4. It should be noted that in terms of the revised Master Direction, the following documents do not find mention as Proof of Identity (POI) and Proof of address (POA), which were then permissible under  simplified measures (for low risk customers), and therefore should not be accepted.

A.   Proof of Identity (POI):

i.       Identity card with applicant’s photograph issued by Central/ State Government Departments, Statutory/ Regulatory Authorities, Public Sector Undertakings, Scheduled Commercial Banks, and Public Financial Institutions;

ii.     Letter issued by a Gazetted officer, with a duly attested photograph of the person.

 

B.   Proof of address (POA):

i.       Bank account or Post Office savings bank account statement;

ii.  Declaration from a relative that the said person is a relative and is staying with him/her. (This will however be required in support of the documents mentioned in Para IV.3.i.above).

iii.                Document issued by Government Departments of foreign jurisdictions and letter issued by Foreign Embassy of Mission in India.

 

V.  The instructions contained in the instant circular supersede those contained in Para 2 of the Circular OC No. 790 dated 19th April, 2018 (including instructions to obtain Annexure I attached thereto in respect of prospective customer not holding Aadhaar or enrolment for the same, which is now replaced by the Annexure I attached to the instant circular).

 

VI.   Further, refer Para 3 of the Circular OC No. 790 wherein it was advised that branches should, on the CIF/AOF form, acquire the Name as spelt on Aadhaar Card and also for feeding the Names in Customer / Account Master in OMNI Database, instead of Name as spelt on Pan Card.  Many branches have since represented that there are many mistakes in Aadhaar records, such as incomplete name, mere initials for First name/Middle Name and wrong spellings as against the actual one which customer desires to have for his bank account etc.  In view of the above it has been decided as under :

 

1. Name as per Aadhaar is not same as the one Customer wants to have (ignoring minor mistakes in spellings like Sangita spelt as Sangeeta or Vithal spelt as Vitthal) : Account should be opened by ensuring that Aadhaar Number is available for the Customer and e-KYC (Biometric Authentication) is carried out successfully. In all such cases, the Name as spelt on Pan Card is to be considered as the correct one provided the same (correct) spelling is printed on Pan Card. The same Name should be fed in OMNI Master in the order of Surname, First name, Middle name as per our existing practice. 

 

2. Where Pan Card is not submitted and Form No. 60/61 is submitted in lieu of Pan Number along with the Aadhaar Number: In such cases, branches will open account strictly as per the Name as spelt in the Aadhaar Record and in the same order as per existing practice i.e. Surname, First name, Middle name. In such case, once again the e-KYC (Biometric Authentication) must have been successfully carried out for the customer.

 

3. Where both the Pan Card and Aadhaar Card Names are having spelling mistakes and customer requests to have some minor change in Name as against the one which is provided in one of these documents: Such requests shall not be acceded to. The customer will be advised to approach respective authorities to get necessary changes scripted on the said documents. In case of his insistence, we may open the account as per the same name as available in any one of these two documents.

 

VII. It has also been decided to repeal the instructions contained Para 6.iii of Circular OC No. 791 (to advise the customer to carry out change in name in Aadhaar if it differs from the one in our Account Master’  or  obtain  request  as per Annexure thereto from an existing account holder to carry out minor change in spelling of the name in Account Master to match with that in Aadhaar record);  because all the Yes/No authentications must now be followed with bio-metric authentication within a period of six months.

 

VIII.  IT Department shall introduce appropriate trigger & check in the OMNI software, in respect of accounts which are opened in the absence of AADHAAR card (but on the basis of AADHAAR enrollment), to count the date of expiry of the six months’ period since the date of account opening for applying the mandatory freeze vide Para III.2.ii.a. above. Similar trigger & checks should also be introduced to count the three months’ expiry of the limited concession in respect of current address as per the Para IV.3. & its sub-Paras above, for applying the mandatory freeze of debits and credits in respect of non-compliant accounts as per the stipulation.

 

IX.   Until such time triggers & checks as above are developed in OMNI software, Branches will diarise, for applying freeze, the dates of expiry of six months for accounts opened in the absence of AADHAAR but on the basis of enrollment therefor vide Para III.2.ii.a. above, and expiry of three months’ period of concession for updating the local address in AADHAAR or the OVDs vide Para III.3. & its sub-Paras above.

 

X.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
