Circular OC No. 809 dated 5th February, 2019

Collection of Requisition Slip before Delivering Cheque Book Applied for through Mobile Banking

 

 

I.                 When customers request, across the counter, for issuing a new cheque book, the current practice in the Bank is to collect the duly signed requisition slip (provided in the previous cheque book).

 

II.  The facility of requisitioning a cheque book through mobile banking has now been extended to customers. Customers, opting for such requisitioning, collect the cheque book from their home branch, after receiving SMS from the bank to do so. There are currently no specific instructions to collect from the customer the requisition slip which is provided in the previous cheque book & that is perhaps why branches are not collecting the same while delivering the new cheque book requisitioned through mobile banking. This exposes the bank to the risks arising from customers being in possession of more than one requisition slip.

 

III.   Therefore, it has been decided as under :

 

1) All the branches shall collect from the customer the requisition slip provided in the previous cheque book before delivering the new cheque book to him under request through mobile banking.

 

2) Arrangements are being made to modify the message currently being sent to the customers in this behalf, to add that they need to tender the requisition slip provided in the previous cheque book, at the time of collecting the new one being issued under mobile banking.

 

3) It may happen that a customer may not bring along with him the requisition slip belonging to the previous cheque book while visiting the branch to collect the new cheque book inspite of a clear SMS to him to do so. In such an eventuality, the officer/Asst. Manager concerned should tear off & destroy the requisition slip in the new cheque book to be issued in the presence of the customer.

 

IV.  Incidentally, we advise that the date of issuance of the last cheque book to the customer should be invariably checked in the OMNI system by the officer concerned at the branch, for collection of charges if any as applicable as per the current Manual on Service Charges.

 

V.    Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
