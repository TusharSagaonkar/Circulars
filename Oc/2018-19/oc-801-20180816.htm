Circular OC No. 801 dated 16th August, 2018

 

The Depositor Education and Awareness Fund (DEAF) Scheme, 2014 – Section 26A of Banking Regulation Act, 1949 – Operational Guidelines

 

 

1. In terms of the RBI Circular No. RBI/2013-14/614 DBOD. No. DEAF Cell.BC.114/ 30.01.002/2013-14 dated May 27, 2014, amounts remaining to the credit of any account in India with any bank which has not been operated upon for a period of ten years or any deposit or any amount remaining unclaimed for more than ten years is required to be transferred by Banks to RBI for crediting to DEAF Account. We had issued the Circular OC No. 667 dated 10th June, 2018 providing information about, & issuing instructions on, the subject.

 

Considering the changes over the period, the earlier circular OC No. 667 dated 10.06.2014 is revised hereunder. Henceforth, Branches shall refer the following instructions for the said purpose. 

 

2. For this purpose, the amounts to be credited to the Fund shall be the credit balance in any deposit account maintained with bank which has not been operated upon for ten years or more, or any amount remaining unclaimed for ten years or more. Such accounts include :-

 

a) Savings bank deposit accounts;

 

b) Fixed or term deposit accounts;

 

c) Cumulative/recurring deposit accounts;

 

d) Current deposit accounts;

 

e) Other deposit accounts in any form or with any name;

 

f)  Cash credit accounts having credit balances;

 

g) Loan accounts having credit balances after due appropriation by the bank;

 

h) Margin money against issue of Letter of Credit/Guarantee etc., or any security deposit;

 

i)   Outstanding demand drafts, pay orders, bankers cheques, sundry deposit accounts, vostro accounts, inter-bank clearing adjustments, unadjusted National Electronic Funds Transfer (NEFT) credit balances and other such transitory accounts, unreconciled credit balances on account of Automated Teller Machine (ATM) transactions, etc.;

 

j)   Undrawn balance amounts remaining in any prepaid card issued by banks, but not amounts outstanding against travellers cheques or other similar instruments which have no maturity period;

 

k) Rupee proceeds of foreign currency deposits held by banks after conversion of foreign currency to rupees in accordance with extant foreign exchange regulations;

 

and

 

l)   Such other amounts as may be specified by Reserve Bank from time to time.

 

3. As per RBI directives, branches shall calculate cumulative balances in all such accounts (i.e. accounts which are unclaimed or not operated for a period of ten years or more) along with interest accrued (@ 4% from the date of closure till the date of transfer of funds to RBI-DEAF) and such amounts due shall be transferred to Accounts Dept., Vashi . Accounts Dept., in turn, shall transfer the amount for credit of the Depositor Education and Awareness Fund (DEAF) Account maintained at RBI.

 

4. Crediting the Fund in Electronic form only

 

Accounts Dept. shall remit the amounts due by generating a single entry (as defined in the Scheme), in electronic form through portal facility of the E-Kuber (Core Banking Solution) of Reserve Bank of India (RBI), to a designated account created for the Scheme, viz. “DEAF Account 161001006009”. Accordingly, the amount required to be transferred to the Fund can be credited to the Depositor Education and Awareness Fund (DEAF) Account, specified above, maintained with RBI (within banking hours) on the last working day of the month. Our Bank has been allotted a unique “Bank DEAF Code” by the RBI, for operating the Fund which is 0545. While remitting amount to the DEAF Account, Accounts Dept. shall indicate our “Bank DEAF Code”.

 

5. Procedure to be followed by Accounts Dept. for crediting the Fund

 

i)   Own Account – This facility is available under the service “DEAF Service” of the E-Kuber portal. When any bank is crediting its own amount due to the Fund, it has been instructed to furnish its DEAF code in the “Bank DEAF Code” field. Detailed breakup with number of accounts and amount of the deposits such as interest bearing deposits, non-interest bearing deposits  or other credits (i.e. any amount other than deposits remaining unclaimed), is required to be furnished  in the fields provided  of the aforementioned service in the portal. Other credits remain  non-interest bearing.

 

ii) Member-banks Account – Instructions issued by RBI in this behalf do not apply to us at present.

 

6. Returns prescribed

 

Accounts Dept. shall furnish returns, duly audited, to RBI in the form and manner prescribed. In this regard, bank is advised to furnish returns duly audited as per details given below:-

 

Form I - Accounts Dept. shall submit a consolidated return on the date of transferring the amount to the Fund furnishing the total amount credited (indicating separately the amount of interest bearing deposits, non-interest bearing deposits and other credits transferred). In respect of eligible amounts/accounts transferred pertaining to each branch to the Fund, bank shall maintain complete details viz., name of customer, account number, amount including interest accrued transferred to the Fund, date of transfer to the Fund and other related documents, etc. These details/documents shall be maintained by Branches. The same shall be maintained by Accounts Department branchwise.

 

Form II - In case of demand for receiving back the deposits/credit balances etc. including interest or otherwise from a customer/ depositor whose unclaimed amount/deposit had been transferred to Fund, branch shall repay the same to customer/depositor, along with interest if applicable; and lodge a claim through Accounts Dept. for refund from the Fund for an equivalent amount paid to the customer/depositor. Even in respect of any claim for refund of part amount by the depositor whose unclaimed amount/inoperative deposit had been transferred to the Fund, the Accounts Dept. shall claim the entire amount transferred to the Fund in respect of such depositor along with interest payable, if any, from the Fund and pay it out to the depositor. The details of the refund made by the branches in each calendar month should be furnished by Accounts Dept. in Form II by 15th of the subsequent month.

 

Form III - A reconciliation certificate in Form III (Annex. 5) signed by two senior officers, other than those involved in transfer and refund claims for unclaimed deposits, as also by the concurrent auditors of the bank, certifying aggregate of the balances of the banks as shown in their general ledger with the amount reflected in the DEA Fund account of RBI at the end of June and December every year is required to be submitted to RBI. This certificate must reach RBI, in original, not later than July 31 and January 31 respectively.

 

7. Accounts Dept. shall furnish the above returns, even if ‘NIL’, to the RBI at the periodicity indicated above. The Form II duly certified by the auditors shall be forwarded in original to Chief General Manager, Reserve Bank of India, Department of Banking Operations & Development, Central Office, DEAF Cell, 12th Floor, Shahid Bhagat Singh Road, Fort, Mumbai – 400001 by Post.

 

8. While bank is not required to provide customer-wise details in case of refund claims in Form II, bank is, however, required to maintain customer-wise details of claims at their end, duly certified by their concurrent auditors, which Reserve Bank may seek at a later stage during supervisory review process.

 

9. Audit

 

On the date of transferring the amount to the Fund, the Accounts Dept. /Branches  should maintain customer-wise details verified by the concurrent auditors, including payment of up-to-date interest accrued that has been credited to the deposit account till the date of transfer to the Fund, with respect to interest bearing deposits. With respect to non-interest bearing deposits and other credits transferred to the Fund, customer-wise details, duly audited, should be maintained by Accounts Dept. /branches. The concurrent auditors should also verify and certify that, as per the banks’ books, the returns have been correctly compiled by the bank in the monthly and yearly returns submitted to RBI. The above returns shall  also be  verified  by the  statutory auditors at the time of annual audit and an

 

Annual Certificate shall be obtained from statutory auditors and forwarded to RBI, certifying that the returns have been correctly compiled by the bank.

 

The Statutory Auditors’ Annual Certificate should also be forwarded at the above address along with a scanned copy in PDF format by e-mail.

 

10. Authorized Signatories

 

The Accounts Dept., as advised, shall furnish a true copy of the Resolution of the Board of Directors authorising two officials designated as authorized signatories, who would operate the account jointly, for the claims/refund on behalf of the bank from the Fund. The specimen signatures of the authorised signatories shall be duly attested by the Chairman, Executive Director or Chief Executive Officer.

 

11. Disclosure in Notes to Accounts

 

All such unclaimed liabilities (where amount due has been transferred to DEAF) should appear as “Contingent Liability – Others, items for which the bank is contingently liable” under Schedule 12 of the annual financial statements. Bank shall disclose the amounts transferred to DEAF under the notes to accounts as per the format given below.

 

(Amounts in Rs. crore)

 

Current year

Previous year

Opening balance of amounts transferred to DEAF

 

 

Add: Amounts transferred to DEAF during the year

 

 

Less: Amounts reimbursed by DEAF towards claims

 

 

Closing balance of amounts transferred to DEAF

 

 

 

12. The following action shall be taken by Branches and HO Depts:

 

A. Instructions for Branches:

 

I.   Every month, the Branches shall, by following the procedure, transfer the Matured and unclaimed deposit amount of 10 years and above to Accounts department along with the Statement (as per Annexure - A) on or before 15th of subsequent month. For example, Matured and Unclaimed deposits for ten years and above as on 30th June 2018 shall be transferred to Accounts department on or before 15th July 2018. While transferring such accounts, branches shall calculate & account for interest at the rate applicable for Saving Deposit (currently 4%) from the date of closure of the account till the date of transfer of the account to DEAF.

 

II.  If any amount transferred to RBI is subsequently claimed by depositors, branch incharge should verify the details and conduct KYC exercise, and after being satisfied may refund the deposit along with interest @ 4% from the date of transfer of funds

 

to RBI-DEAF till 30.06.2018 and interest @ 3.5% (reduced by RBI w.e.f. 01.07.2018) from 01.07.2018 till the date of claim. Thereafter, refund may be claimed by Branches from HO by submitting form as per Annexure B. Subsequently, Accounts department shall claim the amount from RBI –DEAF account by completing due formalities.

 

Branches are required to maintain the lists as per Annexures and make them available to in-house Inspection department, concurrent auditor and statutory auditor.

 

B.Instructions for Accounts Department:

Accounts Department :

·      Shall consolidate the statements sent by Branches,

·      Obtain the required audit Certification from Concurrent Auditors and Statutory auditors,

·      Transfer the amount to RBI DEAF account as a single entry, and

·      Submit necessary Returns as per the Circular to RBI before the due dates.

 

C.Instructions for Inspection department:

Inspection department is advised to inspect the procedure being followed at Branch level as required by RBI. The scope of Concurrent auditors is being extended to inspect the procedure being followed at Branch level as per the RBI Circular and submit their certification regularly in the Concurrent audit report.

 

13. Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
