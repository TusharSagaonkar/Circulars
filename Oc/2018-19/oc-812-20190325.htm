Circular OC No. 812 dated 25th March, 2019

Procedure and guidelines for launch of Long Term (Subordinated) Deposits Series II

____________________________________________________________________

 

1.Pursuant to applications made to Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and Central Registrar of Co-operative Societies (CRCS), seeking their permission to launch Long Term (Subordinated) Deposits (LTD)-II, CRCS vide letter dated 25th March, 2019 and RBI vide letter No. DCBS.MRO.BSS-II/4325/12.07.006/ 2018-19 dated 13th March, 2019 have permitted our Bank to issue Long Term (Subordinated) Deposits (LTD) – Series II to the extent of Rs. 75 Crore (Rupees Seventy Five Crore only).

 

2. Board in its meeting held on 12.12.2018 has decided to issue Long Term (Subordinated) Deposits (LTD) Series II from 25th March, 2019 The terms and conditions of the issue are as under:

 

Particulars

Features

Permission & CRAR aspects

1.   The Bank has obtained permission of Central Registrar of Co-operative Societies (CRCS), New Delhi and Reserve Bank of India (RBI) for the said issue.

2.   Amounts raised through LTDs will be eligible to be treated as Lower Tier II Capital of the Bank and will be treated as Long Term (Subordinated) Deposits.

Issue Size

Minimum investment in LTD will be Rs.5,000/- and thereafter in multiples of Rs. 1000/- aggregating to Rs. 75 Cr. Issue & maturity amount will be at par. LTDs will be issued on “First come, first served basis”.

Maturity

LTDs will have maturity of 7 (Seven) Years and 3 (Three) months from the date of issue.

Rate of Interest

Rate of interest on LTDs will be 9% p.a. payable Quarterly on 30th June, 30th September, 31st December & 31st March of every year, till date of maturity, on the amount accepted under LTD (Series II). Interest on LTDs will not be payable after the date of maturity.

The option of ‘Cumulative’ interest on deposit is not available under LTDs.

Interest payment mode

1)    By credit to Savings / Current account in case of customer/s maintaining Account with our Bank.

2)    By payment through electronic mode in case of customer/s NOT maintaining Account with our Bank.

Interest payment date/s

In case any Interest Payment Date(s) and /or Date (s) of Redemption fall(s) on a Holiday, Sunday or any Public Holiday declared under Negotiable Instrument Act 1881, interest payment/redemption of principal will be made on the next working day (i.e. a day on which banks are open for business).

To be issued to

LTDs will be issued to individuals (excluding NRIs) (single or joint), Registered organization, Private or Public Limited Company, Partnership Firms, Registered Public Trusts, HUF, Regd. Co-op. Societies (excluding Urban Co-op. Banks). LTDs can be issued to members and non-members of the Bank, including those outside the area of operation of the Bank.



Closure date

LTD (Series II) issue shall remain open till the date of closure which will be notified by the Bank on its website www.abhyudayabank.co.in and on the notice board of the Bank’s Head Office and branch offices.

Acceptance of LTD (Series II) will be stopped by the Bank on receipt of the total amount of Rs. 75 Cr. (Rupees Seventy Five Crore only), which is the maximum amount permitted by RBI. The Bank reserves the right to accept lesser amount of LTD without assigning any reason.

Transferability

LTD receipt is not transferable.

Nomination

Nomination facility is available under the Multi State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002 as applicable for capital instruments.

Transmission

In the event of death of the sole LTD depositor or death of the last surviving LTD depositor, the LTD Certificate shall be transmitted in the normal course in the name of the nominee registered with the Bank/Legal heir/s on complying with the requisite formalities and after submission of KYC documents.

Procedure for repayment on maturity to nominee /legal heirs in the event of death.

The Long Term (Subordinated) Deposit holder/s may, by signing a Nomination Form, Nominate any person or persons to receive, on maturity, the amount of Long Term (Subordinated) Deposit from the Bank on the death of the sole Long Term (Subordinated) Deposit holder/death of the last surviving holder as per the respective provisions of Multi State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002 and the Bye laws of Abhyudaya Co-operative Bank Ltd., or if there is no person or persons so nominated, the Bank shall pay, a sum representing the value of such Long Term (Subordinated) Deposits, to any such person or persons as may appear to the Board of Directors as heir/s or legal representative/s of the deceased Long Term (Subordinated) Deposit holder/s on executing a deed of indemnity in favour of the Bank and the Bank shall thereupon be absolved from all liability in respect of such deposit.

Loan against LTDs

No advances/overdrafts shall be granted against security of LTDs. The Bank shall not recognize any lien, charge or other encumbrance on LTDs.

DICGC Cover

LTDs will not be eligible for insurance cover from Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC).

Seniority of Claims

LTDs will be subordinated to claims of depositors & other creditors, but would rank senior to claims of the shareholders, including holders of preference shares (both Tier I & Tier II). Among investors of LTDs included in lower Tier II, claims shall rank pari passu with each other.

Options for payment of principal before maturity

Bank reserves right of “Call Option” (i.e. repaying LTDs before date of maturity at option of Bank) which may be exercised after 5 years from the date of issue, with prior permission of the RBI. LTD shall not have “Put Option” or a “Step up Option”.


Redemption/ Repayment

LTD is a part of Tier II capital and is not a normal deposit. Hence, no pre-mature withdrawal/closure for LTD is available. Further,

A) Repayment of LTDs at the time of maturity shall be made with the prior approval of RBI (Department of Co-operative Bank Supervision) subject inter alia to the following conditions:

1)  Bank’s Capital to Risk Weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR) is above the minimum regulatory requirements prescribed by RBI.

2)  The impact of such repayment does not result in the Bank’s CRAR falling below or remaining below the minimum regulatory requirements prescribed by RBI.

B) Repayment of Long Term (Subordinated) Deposits will be made by credit to the Savings/Current account holders of our Bank and by Electronic mode in the case of non-account holders of our Bank, on maturity.

Form of holding

The Long Term (Subordinated) Deposits Certificate will be issued only in physical form.

Voting Rights

LTD holders will not be eligible for voting rights.

Tax deducted at source

Interest Income from LTDs is subject to Tax deduction at source (TDS) as per the extant provisions of Income Tax Act, 1961.

Jurisdiction

All disputes/transactions are subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of the City of Mumbai.

KYC

LTDs would be subject to adherence to KYC Guidelines

Rejection

The Bank reserves the right to reject incomplete application form without assigning any reasons.

 

3. In order that the issue is conducted smoothly, duties & responsibilities are assigned to different departments and branches.:

 

IT Dept.:

·               Open GL code – liabilities side (Cr) - : Long Term (Subordinated) Deposits – Series II –(LTD-II). Print definition under Borrowings.

LTD-II to have following restrictions:

·                    The term shall be 7 (Seven) Years and 3 (Three) months from the date of issue;

· No advances/overdrafts shall be granted against security of LTDs. The Bank shall not recognize any lien, charge or other encumbrance on LTDs.

·                                                             No facility of pre-mature withdrawal/closure is available for LTD.

·               To open GL code – Liabilities side (Cr) - : Interest Payable –LTD-II. Print definition under Other Liabilities

·               To open PL code – Expenditure side (Dr) - : Interest on LTD-II. Print definition under interest expenses.

·               Rate of interest payable on LTDs is 9 % p.a. payable quarterly at the end of June, September, December and March every year.

·               Printing of LTD receipts (as per present Term deposit receipt printing): Apart from all the fields as per present Term deposit receipt printing, to carry following information:

o   Period : 7 years and 3 months

o   Interest: Quarterly @ 9% p.a.

·               Run Quarterly Interest Programme

·               TDS as applicable to any other interest earning deposits product of the bank.

·               MIS reports and Interest Certificate to include LTD product at all places.

·               Display information of this issue on our Bank’s website.

 

Development dept:

Print and make available Application-forms-cum-Offer documents and LTD Certificates to all Branches.

 

Marketing dept:

Ensure that all the branches receive advertisement material in advance. Arrangements to be made for advertisements through media and display by way of hoardings/banners at prominent places etc.

 

Accounts dept:

·                    To monitor the amounts mobilized so that it does not exceed the permitted amount of Rs. 75 crore.

·                                Reporting to RBI.

 

4. Duties & responsibilities of Branches :

a)     Branches shall issue application forms specially prepared for LTD to customers and General Public. All the required details, will have to be filled in. Necessary KYC documents will be obtained while accepting the application(s).

b)     Specially designed LTD Certificates will be issued against the deposits. Printing procedure will be the same as is currently followed for Term deposit printing.

c)     Procedure for payment of interest shall be as per the present interest on QID. TDS shall be deductible as applicable.

d)     Interest on the LTDs shall be liable to tax under the Act. The tax on such interest shall be deducted by the branch in accordance with the provisions of section 194A of the Income Tax Act. However, branches can obtain 15G/15H forms as per eligibility.

e)     Branches shall ensure that all the terms & conditions of the issue as mentioned in point no. 2 are followed.

f)      Right of nominee: In the event of death of the sole holder of LTD, or death of all joint holders of LTD in respect of which a nomination is in force, the nominee is entitled to get LTD Certificate transferred in his/her name. For this purpose, the nominee shall make an application to the Branch In-charge in the prescribed form supported with death certificate(s) of sole/joint holders of LTD and his/her own officially valid KYC documents. The payment will be made only on maturity of the LTD.

g)    In case of replacement of lost or destroyed LTD receipt :

i)       If LTD receipt is lost, stolen, destroyed, mutilated or defaced, the person entitled thereto may apply for the issue of a duplicate receipt to the branch from where the receipt was issued.

ii)     Every such application shall be accompanied by stating particulars, such as number, amount and date of the receipt, and the details about such loss, theft, destruction, mutilation or defacement and proof of police complaint if made.

iii)   If the officer in-charge of the branch is satisfied that there is indeed a loss, theft, destruction, mutilation or defacement of the certificate, he shall issue a duplicate receipt on the applicant furnishing an indemnity bond with appropriate stamp duty in the prescribed form with one or more approved sureties or with a bank guarantee.

iv)   Provided further that where such application is made with respect to a receipt which is mutilated or defaced, of whatever face value, a duplicate receipt may be issued without any such indemnity bond, surety or guarantee, if the receipt mutilated or defaced is surrendered and the receipt is capable of being identified as the one originally issued.

v)     A duplicate receipt issued under sub-paragraph (iii) shall be treated as equivalent to the original receipt for all the purposes of this scheme.

 

5. Please bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of your department/branch and follow the instructions meticulously.
