‘ABHYUDAYA AROGYA SURAKSHA’

As a Corporate Insurance Agent, our Bank is offering Health Insurance services through M/s. Religare Health Insurance Company Ltd. (RHICL). Board in its meeting dated 28th August, 2018 approved the proposal of RHICL to offer Group Care plan to customers of our Bank. Group Care plan so offered to customers is named ‘Abhyudaya Aarogya Suraksha’. Our Bank would be the master policy holder and only the customers having operative account with Abhyudaya Co-op. Bank Ltd. are eligible for enrollment under this policy.

 

Illness & Medical emergencies arise unexpectedly. In times of rising medical cost & in the absence of adequate amount of health insurance cover they can cause a lot of stress. This could either compromise quality of healthcare support to the customer’s family or lead to financial distress due to high medical bills.

 

This Group Care Plan offered through RHICL is a comprehensive health insurance plan specially designed for the customers of our Bank and is not be offered to other customers. Under Abhyudaya Aarogya Suraksha (Group Care) plan, customer can port his cover to other Individual Health Insurance policies offered by Religare Health Insurance, subject to portability guidelines of such individual health policy under following conditions:

a) Master Policy holder chooses not to continue under this plan.

b) If customer chooses not to continue the enrollment under this plan, the premium payable under this plan shall be reviewed on annual basis and may be subject to revision.

 

‘Abhyudaya Aarogya Suraksha’ (Group Care) plan which is specially designed for customers of our Bank would be offered to customers from 23rd October, 2018. Details of Abhyudaya Aarogya Suraksha (Group Care) plan are as under:

 

Abhyudaya Aarogya Suraksha (Group Care) plan works on the principle of indemnity. This means that RHICL will pay customer only what he has spent for medical treatment during hospitalization.

 

Under the policy RHICL pays for – Room Charges, Nursing Expenses, Intensive Care Unit Charges, Surgeon's Fee, Doctor's Fee, Anaesthesia, Blood, Oxygen, Operation Theatre Charges, etc.; if customer is admitted to a hospital for in-patient care, for a minimum period of 24 consecutive hours.

 

RHICL also cover medical expenses if customer undergoes a day care treatment for certain listed elements under this policy which might not require customer to stay hospitalized for 24 hours or more.

Administrative or Non-Medical expenses are generally excluded in indemnity policy.

Customer can avail tax benefit on the premium paid towards his health insurance policy, as per the prevailing Income Tax laws (u/s 80D).

Proposed Issuance Process:

 

1.   Customer will submit the duly filled and signed application form to RHICL through our branch.

-      The application form has debit instruction authorizing Bank for debiting premium amount from customer’s account.

 

-      Branch staff should check & verify the account details mentioned by customer & his signature as per Bank’s record. If account details & signature are as per Bank record, then Bank official should accept application form and affix Bank stamp & signature on the same. Official should transfer the premium amount to

Religare Health Insurance current account which is held at our Vashi branch Account Number is 016021100117550.

 

Branch staff should enter “Proposal Number & Proposer Name” in narration, while transferring the premium amount to the above mentioned current account of M/s. Religare Health Insurance Company Ltd. In this plan, customer can pay his premium through fund transfer arrangement with an account held in our Bank only, & cheque / cash for premium amount will not be accepted from any customer.

 

-      Proposal Form also contains auto debit mandate for debiting the renewal premium. Customer will authorize our Bank to debit his account number which is mentioned in application form on due date (yearly basis) with the applicable renewal premium. Please note: The premium amount is subject to change due to change in GST as specified from time to time by the Government of India or due to medical inflation or change in plan / Age of member(s).

 

2.   Currently Abhyudaya Aarogya Suraksha (Group Care) scheme login process is physical. Branch staff will handover the application form to RHICL official for further processing.

 

3.   Once the reconciliation of data is done and the amount is transferred, RHICL will issue the policy and dispatch the certificate to the customer via standard post.

 

Cancellation of Policy and Endorsement Process:

Cancellation and Endorsement requests will be managed by RHICL and customer can place these request with RHICL through its customer care Number or email ID.

Cancellation - Customer may give 15 days’ notice in writing, to RHICL, for cancellation of his Policy, in such case RHICL shall from the date of receipt of notice, cancel the Policy and refund the premium for the unexpired period of this Policy at the short period scales as mentioned below, provided no Claim has been made and full premium has been paid under the Policy.

Endorsement - There are 2 types of endorsements that the customer may do viz.:

a. Premium bearing endorsement - Those endorsements having an impact on the premium amount for e.g. : Change in Date of Birth, Policy Cancellation.

b. Non-Premium bearing endorsement - Those endorsements having no impact on the premium for e.g. : Rectification in Name, Address, Mobile No.

 

Free Look Period: After purchasing the policy if the customer find it unsuitable, he can cancel and return the policy to RHICL. These policies come with a free look period of 15 days.

In case the policy is cancelled on customers request the refund amount is paid to customer through NEFT on the basis of Bank information provided by him.

During Inquiries from the customers for Endorsement, Cancellation or Claim should be guided to guide customer care number of RHICL i.e. 1800 200 4488 or the customer should be guided to write to customerfirst@religarehealthinsurance.com.

Renewal Process:

1.   At the time of renewal, RHICL will share the MIS with Insurance / IT department with all the details of customers who opted for auto-renewal (mandatory for all customers to enroll).

2.   On the basis of the above MIS, Bank will debit the customer’s account and update it with success / failure (max up to 3 attempts) with transaction details and resent the success / failure data to RHICL for reconciliation and policy renewal.

 

Note: Risk Commencement Date will be T+1; where T is Payment Receiving Date or data entry date whichever is later.
