Circular OC No. 806 dated 2nd January, 2019

Documentation of Processes to be Followed in Delivering  Products & Conducting Operations

_______________________________________________________________________________________

 

I.                 There were standard processes & practices followed by branches while delivering products and conducting operations. Over the years, divergent practices have proliferated across branches regarding the processes and practices. Therefore, it has been decided to document and make them available to branches for the sake of uniformity. Work relating to some of them has been finalised. The processes and practices in respect of which consolidation has been done (as per the index attached) are hereby being circularised.

 

II.  We are posting the compilations on Exchange Server - Public Folder – Secretarial – Circulars – Processes for Products for the facility of all the branches & departments. All the members of the staff at branches, administrative offices and the departments concerned are advised to go through their contents and adhere to the provisions therein meticulously.
