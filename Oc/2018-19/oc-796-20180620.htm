Circular OC No. 796 dated 20th June, 2018

Participation in Central Plan Scheme Monitoring System (CPSMS)

Seeding of Aadhaar Number to Any Account/Switching Over from One Bank to Another

 

 

I.   Please refer Circular O.C. No. 614 dated 30th January, 2013 & Circular OC No. 700 dated 23rd January, 2015.

 

II.  Our Bank has started participating in Aadhaar Based Payment System through NPCI since 2013. We are obtaining consent in physical form from customers and their Aadhaar number is seeded to their Bank accounts. The Account holders have to submit their 15 digit bank account number and 12 digit Aadhaar Card number to the concerned agencies such as gas agencies, Central/State Govt. departments etc. for receiving subsidies. The agencies upload the data to the site of Central & State Government projects of Central Plan Scheme Monitoring System (CPSMS). Based on the data fed at branches, a Mapper File is centrally generated daily at our Central Clearing department and is uploaded to NPCI site. The subsidy amount is directly credited to the bank accounts of the customers.

 

III.   1. NPCI had made it very clear that one of the important conditions of the

 Aadhaar seeding is to obtain explicit informed consent from the customer for receiving subsidy and other credits. Banks need to follow one of the following methods for obtaining explicit consent:

a)    Paper based consent i.e. consent in physical form

b)    Consent in electronic form

 

2. There are standard formats of the consent, paper-based as well as electronic. The paper-based format currently in vogue is finalised by IBA in 2018 & is circulated for implementation.

                                                                                   

3. NPCI decided on 26th October, 2017 that if more than one bank uploads the Aadhaar number in NPCI Mapper, the Mapper would hold only the last record as updated. Accordingly, NPCI routed the transactions to that bank which had uploaded the data latest in the NPCI Mapper. It was later revealed that a few private sector banks had seeded their customer’s account, without obtaining explicit informed consent for seeding customer’s accounts with Aadhaar number for availing credit of ABP system, resulting in large amount of subsidy getting routed to those banks. Customers had complained that they were not aware of the switch over.

 

4. Therefore, NPCI through another Circular instructed Banks that if any customer wishes to change Aadhaar seeding to another bank, he has to submit name of his current Bank.

 

5.    A List of IIN (Institute Identification number) has been supplied to all Banks and Banks have to now mention the IIN number of the current Bank for uploading the request of switch over by the customer. Additionally, the Bank which uploads the data to NPCI Mapper file has to give confirmation that it has obtained requisite consent of the Aadhaar holder for the switch-over. The Banks should not use any other means like Aadhaar mapping query, look up facility or data from any government departments to get the current Bank IIN.

 

6.   Central Clearing department has already mailed the revised format of consent to branches with necessary instructions & IT department has modified the relative programme in the OMNI software for Aadhaar seeding. Branches are feeding data in the new program. Our Bank is yet to introduce the electronic consent form.

 

IV. The instructions as above are summarised below for strict adherence by branches.

1. The customers, who have already seeded their account with Aadhaar number for availing DBT facilities with other Bank, and wish to change it now, will have to submit the name of their current Bank.

 

2. NPCI has allotted IIN Number to all Banks and branches have to feed the IIN number of the customer's current Bank in the data to be uploaded to NPCI.

 

3. The list of IIN Number is stored in the Public Folder of Clearing Department in the Exchange Server.

 

4. If a customer’s request is uploaded to NPCI without feeding IIN number of current Bank, NPCI will reject the entire file. If the Customer is submitting request to us for seeding his account for the first time, then IIN of our Bank has to be mentioned in the data.

 

5. The mechanism as above is put in place to ensure that seeding of Aadhaar number to any account / switching over to other banks, is done only with explicit informed consent of the customer; and Banks cannot unilaterally link their customer's account with Aadhaar number to get credit of Government subsidies.

 

6. Branches will need to issue acknowledgment to the customer for the Aadhaar seeding request. They will further need to maintain proper records of application forms.

 

 

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
