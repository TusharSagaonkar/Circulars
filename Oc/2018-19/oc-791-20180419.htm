Circular OC No. 791 dated 19th April, 2018

 

 

Bulk Verification of Aadhaar Numbers of Existing Customers

 

___________________________________________________________________________________

 

AADHAAR Authentication & Bulk Authentication- Information & Procedure

 

I. 1.   Aadhaar authentication means the process by which the Aadhaar number along with the demographic information or biometric information of an Aadhaar-number-holder is submitted to the UIDAI for its verification. The UIDAI (repository) verifies the correctness or otherwise, on the basis of the information available with it.

 

2.     Aadhaar Authentication provides a digital, online identification platform so that the identity of Aadhaar number holders can be validated instantly, anytime & anywhere.

 

UIDAI offers Aadhaar-based authentication as a service which can be availed by Requesting Entities (Government / Public and Private sector entities or Agencies / Banking or Non-Banking Financial Institutions). This service from UIDAI can be utilized by the Requesting Entities to authenticate identity of their Customers / Employees / other Associates (based on matching of information on their personal identity).

 

Aadhaar Authentication may be carried out through the following modes:

 

§   Demographic authentication: The Aadhaar number and demographic information of the Aadhaar number holder is matched with the information of the Aadhaar number holder available with the UIDAI.

§   One-Time PIN (OTP) based authentication: A One Time PIN (OTP), with limited time validity, is sent to the mobile number and/ or e-mail address of the Aadhaar number holder registered with the Authority (UIDAI) or generated by other appropriate means. The Aadhaar number holder shall provide this OTP along with his Aadhaar number during authentication and the same shall be matched with the OTP generated by the Authority.

§   Biometric-based authentication: The Aadhaar number and biometric information submitted by an Aadhaar number holder are matched with the information stored with the UIDAI. This may be fingerprint-based or IRIS - based authentication or other biometric modalities based on biometric information stored with the UIDAI.

§   Multi-factor authentication: A combination of two or more of the above modes may be used for authentication.

 

3.     In our Bank, it has been decided to carry out Aadhaar Authentication through the following two modes:

 

             i.    Demographic authentication: The Aadhaar number and demographic information of the Aadhaar-number-holder obtained from the Aadhaar-number-holder is matched with the demographic information of the Aadhaar-number-holder available with the UIDAI. The Demographic information of the Aadhaar number holder means his/her Name, Gender, Date of Birth and Address.

 

            ii.    Biometric-based authentication: The Aadhaar number and biometric information submitted by an Aadhaar-number-holder is matched with the biometric information of the said Aadhaar-number-holder stored with the UIDAI. This may be fingerprint-based or iris-based authentication based on biometric information stored with the UIDAI.

 

4.     Kindly note that mere updating of AADHAAR numbers does not complete the process of AADHAAR linking. It is obligatory for the Bank to authenticate the AADHAAR number obtained from its Customer, by use of any one of the authentication modes including the two mentioned above.

 

       i.      Biometric-based authentication (e-KYC) :

The e-KYC based authentication is normally used at the time of opening of new accounts and while doing KYC updation of customers.

 

     ii.    Demographic authentication:

As per OC No. 771 dated 29th July, 2017, instructions were issued for obtaining AADHAAR Number for Existing and New Accounts (as mandated under Prevention of Money-laundering-Maintenance of Records- Second Amendment Rules, 2017). Our branches are obtaining copy of AADHAAR card from existing (legacy) customers and entering the Aadhaar no. in Express Customer Master field with or without authentication. Due to the concerted efforts made by branches till date, our Bank has completed data entry in respect of around 5.66 Lakh customers. However, AADHAAR numbers collected in most of the cases are not authenticated. Authentication of Aadhaar number of an Aadhaar card holder is mandatory as per Section 8 of Chapter III on Authentication of the Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act, 2016. We need to complete it either through the available biometric or demographic information of the customer. Since the number of customers, from whom Aadhaar Number has been obtained but not authenticated is large, it has been decided to use the Bulk Demographic Authentication mode for AADHAAR verification.

 

5.     Procedure for Bulk Demographic Authentication – at KYC & AML Cell, Vashi :

Our KYC & AML Cell, Vashi will upload onto UIDAI site for verification, the data file containing the details of AADHAAR numbers submitted by our existing customers along with their names available in OMNI database. After matching names of our customers in our database with the AADHAAR database, UIDAI will return the bulk file with remarks YES or NO. YES represents all successful records for which Aadhaar number has matched and authenticated. NO represents all unsuccessful records. All successful records (with ‘Yes’ response) will be then updated to our CBS database and all unsuccessful records ( with ‘No’ response) will be sent back to the respective branches for further follow up and authentication through Biometric Based Authentication, ‘e-KYC’. Such customers, for whom the Demographic Authentication has failed, will be informed through SMS to contact their base branch for Biometric authentication (e-KYC).

 

6.       Work at branches in respect of failed records generated through the attempt made by KYC & AML Cell, Vashi with regard to bulk authentication, will start after all such unsuccessful records are sent to the respective branches for further follow up for Biometric Based Authentication, ‘e-KYC’. KYC & AML Cell, Vashi will inform the customers, for whom the demographic authentication has failed, through SMS to contact the respective base branch to get this completed.

 

Procedure at Branches for further action in respect of records where Bulk Demographic Authentication has failed.

 

i.         Upon receipt of the SMS, the customer is supposed to approach his / her base branch.

 

ii.       The base branch will advise the customer to do e-KYC and the Branches will take printout of customer’s details.

 

iii.      The Branch will ensure that the name of the customer mentioned in print out exactly matches with the name mentioned in our customer database. In case of minor discrepancies in name (spelling mistake such as ‘Dipali’ instead of ‘Deepali’, ‘Sandip’ instead of ‘Sandeep’ etc.), the branch should advise the customer to get the name corrected in AADHAAR database with UIDAI by approaching Government Authorized Agencies. Alternatively, the branch should make correction in the name in our Bank’s database by obtaining a declaration from the customer as per the format provided in Annexure I hereto. Please note that for any major change in name in OMNI database, such as name change after marriage etc., branches will need to adhere to the existing guidelines; and a copy of the Gazette notification or Marriage Certificate will be necessary for such changes.

 

iv.   Branches should note that in case of major discrepancy(ies) / difference(s) in the details of the customer as per our CBS database and those revealed in the attempt of authentication of available Aadhaar number of customers through e-KYC, the said customer must be asked to close the existing account and open a new account exactly as per the Aadhaar Name.

 

v.     A reminder will be sent by IT Department to all those customers having unsuccessful record of attempted matches, and are either not approaching branches to do e-KYC or are not co-operating. After due follow-up, a decision regarding continuance/operations in the account will be taken in the light of further instructions from Government of India or RBI.

 

Branches are advised to note that, out of various modes of Aadhaar Authentication, Biometric Based Authentication (e-KYC) is the best mode of authentication. Hence,

e-KYC must be done in respect of new Account of Customers and in respect of unsuccessful records generated during Bulk Demographic Authentication.

 

          Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff for ensuring strict compliance.
