Circular OC No. 797 dated 20th June, 2018

Launching of POS Machines under Referral Model

_____________________________________________________________________________________

 

I.As you are aware, there is increasing use of plastic money in the economy. Government is also placing more emphasis on cashless transactions. Business & merchant establishments are installing Point of Sale (POS) Machines through arrangement with banks or other agencies so that their customers’ credit/debit cards can be swiped on the machines resulting in mutual convenience. Many of our competitors have arranged to deploy POS machines at their customers’ business places. This has helped them in retaining merchant-customers and in turn contributing to CASA deposits.

 

II.  Many business & merchant establishments maintaining current or cash credit accounts with us often requested us to provide POS machines so as to attract more customers & reduce the risk of cash handling. We have now firmed up an arrangement with M/s. MSwipe Technology Pvt. Ltd. (MSwipe) so that the company provides POS devices with slip printing option to our customers under referral system. Key features of the arrangement are as under:

1.   RBL and Corporation Bank (Settlement Banks) will provide acquirer BINs of NPCI, VISA & ICA of MasterCard and conduct settlement Bank functions.

 

2.   MSwipe will deploy terminals, manage the merchant relationship and conduct field service activities & reconciliation.

 

3.   Prizm Payments will provide VAP and MIP connectivity to VISA and MasterCard respectively and conduct back-end services for Card transactions done through the POS terminals.

 

 

 

 

5.Our Bank will retain the CASA relationship of the merchant-customer.

                                                                                              

6. Back-end Requirements

There is no need of access by MSwipe to our Bank’s servers.

                                                                                                                            

 

7.  Data Security

                         i.    MSwipe transactions process is PCI certified – the highest security standard set by Visa and MasterCard.

 

                        ii.    Data travelling on public network is encrypted.

 

                      iii.    In most cases, data will travel through a secure private network.

 

8.  No Risk to our Bank

                         i.    Bank will not face investment risk because there is no capital expense or AMC.

 

                        ii.    Bank will not face any risk arising from card transactions fraud as there will be separate agreement between Merchant & MSwipe.

 

                      iii.    MSwipe will assume all charge back related risk for card transactions done on Abhyudaya Bank MSwipe provided terminals.

 

                      iv.    There are different plans available to customers. Representatives of MSwipe will explain them to the customers and sell the most appropriate plan suitable for him. Details of the same are given in the Annexure for information of members of the staff.

 

III.  1. With a view to operationalising the arrangement, a bipartite agreement between the Bank and MSwipe is being executed.

 

2.  In the meanwhile, branches should identify merchants who maintain current & cash credit accounts with us & are interested in getting POS machines installed for the facility of their customers. While we have to promote this scheme among our customers, care may be taken to identify only those customers in whose business model a POS machine may fit in. After explaining the advantages of having a POS machine & ascertaining their willingness, full details of the customer may be forwarded to the IT Department in the following excel format via Exchange Mail to Mr. Ajinkya Kadam and Mr. Dnyaneshwar Mondkar.

3.  IT Department will examine the information provided by branches as above and share the details of suitable cases with MSwipe.

 

4.  IT department will recommend names of merchants to Mswipe for installation of POS machines. MSwipe will collect KYC documents from those merchants who are willing to possess the machines, execute individual agreements with them & collect initial payment. Risks relating to secrecy & mandatory compliances with regard to KYC including Aadhar authentication will be borne by MSwipe & they will be accountable for breaches if any.

 

5.  MSwipe will deliver the POS machine and establish terminal within 10 days from the date of submission of complete & valid documents by a customer with realisation of payment from him.

 

IV. Benefits to our Bank

1. We have launched this scheme in November, 2017 under referral model & it will ensure immediate entry in the market & will help retain CASA deposits.

 

2. Fees of Rs. 500/- will accrue to us under each successful referral and income, albeit marginal, will also accrue to us for every Debit card and Credit card transaction.

 

3. There is no risk of business & credit card misuse, as the risk is owned by MSwipe.

 

4. There is no capital or operational expenditure involved.

 

5. There is no technology risk under the model.

 

6. Last, but not the least, there will be customer retention, and potential to cross-sell other products will also increase.

 

V.   Targets & monitoring their achievement

1. Branches will be given targets by Planning & Marketing Department for POS machines to be installed at their customers’ places through MSwipe.

 

2. Branches will analyse reasons for leads not getting converted into actual installation during a period of one month. Bottlenecks if any in the process of acquisition of POS by customers may be sorted out by branches through intervention to the extent possible.

 

3. Conversion of leads into actual installation should be monitored to ensure that Bank’s revenue per installation is received & there is no income leakage.

 

4. Zonal Office concerned will monitor fulfillment of targets on a monthly basis.

 

VI.   In as much as some POS machines have already been installed in anticipation of firming up of the arrangement, the scheme comes into immediate effect from the date of the circular.

 

VII.  All the branches are advised to actively market for business of POS under referral model. We shall watch with interest the initiative shown by the staff across entire hierarchy.

 

VIII.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
