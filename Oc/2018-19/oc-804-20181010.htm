1. TDS PROVISIONS:-

TDS on interest payment on deposits (Section 194A) : If interest paid / payable on all time deposits of a person with bank as a whole, including interest on Matured Term Deposits, Recurring Deposits, Daily Deposits, etc (but excluding Saving Accounts & NRE deposits) exceeds Rs.10,000/- during a Financial Year, then TDS is to be deducted @10% for PAN holders or 20% in case PAN is not submitted. However, the limit for the purpose of deducting TDS on interest on deposits for senior citizens is Rs.50,000/- during a Financial Year (vide amendment in the Finance Budget,2018).

       The above limit is not applicable for TDS on interest paid on NRO deposits. TDS will be deducted on entire amount of interest paid/credited on NRO deposits during a financial year even if it does not exceed Rs. 10,000/- or Rs.50,000/- as the case maybe.

  From F.Y. 2015-16 onwards, TDS is deducted on projection basis, i.e. if projected interest payment on deposits with all branches together during a Financial Year exceeds Rs. 10,000/- or Rs.50,000/- (as the case maybe), TDS will be deducted from 1st instance of interest credit/payment itself.

Interest on RD, DDS is being considered for deduction of TDS from 01.06.2015 (Amendment vide Finance Bill 2015).

Ø  TDS on Rent (Section 194-I) : If the rent paid (including arrears, if any) during the financial year exceeds Rs. 1.80 lakh per person, then TDS will be deducted @10% for PAN holders or 20% (in case PAN is not submitted) on rent amount excluding GST. If landlord submits exemption certificate u/s 197 of the Income Tax Act, then TDS will be deducted at the rate mentioned in the exemption certificate. If the landlord submits Form 15G/Form 15H, then TDS will not be deducted subject, however, to the limits applicable to Form 15G/Form 15H (which are mentioned in detail in point no. 3). Branches are required to send a copy of Form 15G/15H accepted from the landlord in respect of payment of Rent to Accounts Dept every year without fail.

 

i.             TDS on Commission (Section 194H): In case any commission is paid by branches to DDS agent & the amount of commission exceeds Rs. 15,000/- during the financial year, TDS will be deducted @ 5% (if PAN is submitted) or 20% (in case PAN is not submitted). If agent/broker to whom commission is being paid submits exemption certificate u/s 197 of the Income Tax Act, then TDS will be deducted at the rate mentioned in the exemption certificate.

 

ii.           TDS on Payment made to Contractors (Section 194C): In case any payment is made to contractors/sub-contractors and either a single instance of payment involves an amount exceeding Rs. 30,000/- or the aggregate of amount paid to a

single contractor during the Financial Year exceeds Rs.1,00,000/-, Tax at Source will be deducted as follows:-

 

If Payment is made to an individual/HUF

TDS @ 1% for PAN holders or 20% in case PAN is not submitted

If Payment is made to any other person (i.e. Company, Partnership Firm, Trust/AOP/BOI, etc)

TDS @ 2% for PAN holders or 20% in case PAN is not submitted

 

 

i.  TDS on Payment made to NRO (Saving Account & Term Deposits) (Section 195):

If Interest is paid to NRO other than a foreign company : TDS is applicable to NRO Savings Account, R.D. and all types of Term Deposit Account on any sum paid as interest @ 31.20% (30%+4% Health & Edu. Cess) if the interest amount is less than or equal to Rs.1 crore, and @ 35.88% (30% + surcharge 15% + 4% Health & Edu. Cess ) if the interest amount is above Rs. 1 crore.

 If NRO is a resident of a country with whom Govt. of India has entered into DTAA (Double Taxation Avoidance Agreement), then TDS rates as mentioned in DTAA will be applied. In such cases, attested copies of PAN card and Valid Visa issued by the country of residence will have to be obtained. [In this connection, refer Forex Circular No. 33 dated 14.03.2012, Forex Circular No.121 dated 30.05.2018 for finding the TDS rates & Names of countries]. Also refer Forex Circular No. 92 dated 18.05.2015 regarding obtaining Tax Residency Certificate (TRC)]. If TRC is not submitted by the deductee, then TDS is to be deducted @ 31.20 % (plus surcharge if applicable) and not as per the rate mentioned in DTAA. In this regard, extant forex circulars to be referred.

Please note that the interest limit of Rs.10,000/- or Rs. 50,000/- for non-deduction of TDS is not applicable to NRO. Further,, a customer having NRE/NRO deposits/accounts cannot keep domestic deposits. Furthermore, an NRE/NRO customer cannot submit Form 15G/Form 15H. If a customer has kept domestic deposits alongwith NRE/NRO deposits, then TDS @ 31.20% will be required to be deducted from interest on the domestic deposits also. Branch should get these domestic deposits converted to NRO/NRE deposit in consultation with FOREX Dept.

Please note that due to non-deduction or non-payment of TDS, the relative expenses (i.e. the expenses such as Interest paid to customers on time deposits/rent paid to landlord etc., on which TDS is not deducted), will be disallowed as per the provisions of the Income Tax Act,1961.

2. Tax at Source will not be deducted (TDS will not apply) on interest payment on deposits:-

ix.          if the interest is within exemption limit i.e. Rs.10,000/-, or Rs.50,000/ for senior citizens, as the case may be for the entire Financial Year,

x.           if Form No. 15G/15H is submitted by the customer (detailed procedure for acceptance of Form 15G/Form 15H is provided in point no. 3)

xv.        Interest paid on deposits from Banking company to which the Banking Regulation Act, 1949 applies, also does not attract TDS.

3. Acceptance of Form No. 15G or 15H from customers.

i.       In case a Customer is having Deposits with more than one Branch, any of the Branches can accept the form 15G/15H, update the same in OMNI and (necessarily) give acknowledgement of the same to the Customer.

ii.     Single form will be accepted for all Deposits of a Customer kept at all the Branches.

iii.    Any branch can accept Form 15G/15H.

iv.    Note that provision of accepting declaration of investments is not applicable for Form 15G.

v.     Form 15G/15H can be printed through OMNI 3.0.

vi.    Don't accept Form 15G/Form 15H if:-

a. The customer does not have PAN number (Accepting Form No.60/61 in place of PAN is not permitted in such cases).

b. The customer is minor. (The parent/guardian of the minor with whom the minor's deposits are clubbed can submit the Form 15G/Form15H. In such a case, Form 15G/Form 15H should be fed in the group ID only).

c. The customer is a Company or a Partnership Firm.

d. For deduction of TDS in respect of Professional Fees (Sec.194 J), Commission or Brokerage (Sec.194 H), Contractor (Sec.194C) & NRO/NRE i.e. NRO customers (Sec.195).

vii. As per provision of Section 277 of the Income Tax Act, 1961, penalty for false submission of Form No. 15G/15H is rigorous imprisonment for a term which shall not be less than six months and may extend upto seven years with fine.

5.5 Reversal of TDS of Customers submitting form 15G/15H after application of TDS

 

  Please note that, TDS once deducted and paid cannot be reversed. However, we may allow reversal of TDS as a special case to those Customers who submit form 15G/15H before the end of that particular month, provided the same has not been remitted to Govt. In such cases, following procedures should be followed by branches:

a.     Customer can submit form 15G or 15H till the end of the month concerned to avail benefit of reversal of TDS already deducted during the month. In such case, Branches should modify 15G/15H Master to make TDS “No” with appropriate reason code (as mentioned in point no. 5.2) immediately after submission of form 15G/15H.

b.     TDS will not be reversed for those customers who submit form 15G/15H after the end of the month concerned. Please take note that once TDS is already remitted to Government, it will not be reversed.

c.      TDS will be reversed directly through Auto reversal programme (provided by I.T. Department) centrally for all Branches by Accounts Department. Branches are not required to reverse TDS manually in such cases. The reversal will be made on the immediate next working day of the following month.

d.     Transfer of TDS reversed to Customer In case of MID/QID Deposit – TDS entries pertaining to MID/QID Deposits and reversed through auto reversal programme by Accounts Department as mentioned in point (iii) above will be transferred to INTPAY account. Branches are advised to credit the same to the Account concerned of the Customer; or issue Pay Order/DD or effect transfer through NEFT/RTGS as the case may be.

e.     Reversal of TDS in case of Closed Deposits – If the Deposit from which the TDS is deducted is Closed or Auto Renewed & the Customers submit the form 15G/15H towards the end of the month, then, in such case, TDS will be reversed by Auto Reversal Programme as stated above and the amount of TDS will be parked in separate General Ledger Head i.e. TDSREV. Branches are required to transfer such reversed TDS to the Account of the Customer, issue Pay Order or remit the TDS by NEFT as the case may be.

5.8. Payment of TDS.

 

Due Dates for deposit of TDS in Government account:

a) All sums deducted as TDS by branches shall be paid to the credit of the Government on or before 30th day of April where interest, commission, fee , rent etc. are credited to beneficiaries’ accounts or paid otherwise in the month of March: and

b) In any other case, within seven days from the end of the month in which the deduction is made, i.e. on or before 7th of next month.

 

Branches should originate the amount of TDS on Interest on Deposits, NRO Deposit, Rent, DDS Commission and Payment made, to Contractors to Accounts Department on or before 5th day of the month following concerning month. The amount originated should match with the corresponding GL balance of TDS of the respective branch. If the branch is unable to provide the TDS sheet within the prescribed date given by Accounts Dept, then branch in charge and the staff concerned will be held responsible for any interest on delayed payment of TDS for that month.

Accounts Department consolidates the amount of TDS received from Branches and makes payment of TDS deducted on Interest on Deposits (u/s 194A), TDS on NRO Deposits (u/s 195), TDS on Rent (u/s 194I), TDS on Contractors (u/s 194 C) and TDS on Commission to DDS Agent (u/s 194H). Branches are not to send/fax challan for payment of TDS of branches.

Payment of TDS will be made under single TAN for all Branches by Accounts Department.

Please note than once TDS is deducted and paid to the Government, the same will not be reversed/refunded under any circumstances.

Premature closure of deposit:- If as a result of premature closure of term deposits, revised interest projected from all branches for balance deposits plus interest already provided (from all branches) does not exceed Rs. 10,000/- (or Rs.50,000/-in case of a senior citizen), then TDS already deducted and remitted to Govt. Account will not be reversed. Branches should direct the customer to claim refund of TDS in his Income Tax Return. If TDS is deducted but not yet remitted to Govt. Account, the same can be reversed by branches.

 

            Interest levied for failure to Deduct Tax or failure to pay after deduction of Tax.

      Interest levied for failure to Deduct Tax: Interest is payable @ 1% for every month or part thereof from the date on which tax was deductible to the date of deduction of such tax. E.g. if TDS on interest on deposits for the month of December is deducted on 15th Feb (due date for which is 31st Dec), then interest will be paid for 3 months i.e. from Dec to Feb since the due date of deduction falls in the month of December and date of actual deduction falls in the month of February.

      Interest levied for failure to pay after deduction of tax: Interest is payable @ 1.5% for every month or part thereof for failure to pay or delay in the payment of the tax deducted at source. Interest is payable from the date of deduction of tax to the date of payment to the Government i.e. month in which TDS is deducted to the month in which TDS is paid.

The Staff-members /Officers concerned, who handle TDS work shall be held responsible and accountable for delay in deduction, deduction at wrong rate and or/payment of TDS liability to Government Account. (In this behalf, refer Circular O.C. No.621 dated 06.03.2013). The penalty imposed/ amount of interest levied shall be recovered from the staff/officer concerned, for delay in deduction / payment.

5.10. Checking of TDS deduction – Role of the Branches

 

          To check deduction of TDS (i.e. short/excess deduction of TDS) at their end before originating entry to Accounts Department. Any mistakes identified should be rectified immediately. The activity to be used by branches for deducting or reversing TDS is mentioned in point no. 5.6 above.

          If error identified after entry is originated, intimate Accounts Department immediately to incorporate the correct data in TDS Return. If TDS has been paid after identification of error, then in case of excess TDS deducted, it will not be refunded, and in case of short deduction of TDS, officer/Asst. Manager and staff concerned will be held responsible for late deduction of tax and interest on delayed payment of TDS.

          TDS on Provision: If Branches / Departments have made any provision towards expenses liable to TDS such as rent, commission, professional fees or any other expenses, on account of quarterly/yearly closing, Branches / Departments shall deduct TDS on the same at the time of making provision itself and include the same in the TDS sheet provided to Accounts Dept.

In case of a customer with NO PAN, TDS will be deducted @ 20% through system. If such customer immediately submits PAN after deduction of TDS but before remittance to Govt. A/c, branches can reverse half of the TDS amount (i.e. 10%) and pay the balance. But, if PAN is submitted after payment of TDS to Govt. A/c, no reversal will be made, but TDS will be deducted @10% from further interest payments.

Even though system will deduct TDS @ 20% in case of No PAN customer, branches should check deduction of TDS at their end. Also, if customer submits PAN, branches should change TDS rate to 10% in the customer master.

          Checking of TDS work: TDS workings, should be checked by a responsible officer i.e. Officer, Asst Manager or Manager to ensure the correctness of the data, and to ensure compliance with TDS provisions mentioned in this circular. The soft copy of the working of TDS should have the name and contact number of the person preparing the same, checking the same, and also that of the branch in-charge.

 

 

5.11. Mistakes due to Wrong Data Entry in OMNI

 

Deduction of TDS is based on information entered in OMNI. Following Wrong Data Entry affects deduction of TDS-

          Wrong PAN – TDS will get deducted at higher rate or TDS defaults will be generated or credit of TDS will go against a wrong PAN number.

          Wrong Date of Birth – In case of Senior Citizen, TDS will get deducted even if eligible for form 15H or interest limit of Rs.50,000/- u/s 194 A.

          Wrong Customer Type – In case of non-individual customers, when customer type is selected as “individual” instead of “others/business”, then Form 15H is generated. As already mentioned previously, Form 15H can be given only by individuals (except proprietorship firm where, the proprietor submits the Form). Therefore, entering the customer type for non-individual customers as individual results in wrong application of interest limit for the purpose of deduction of TDS in case of Form 15G/H, as Form 15H is generated instead of Form 15G.

          Wrong TDS Reason Code –

                Customer has submitted form 15G/15H but appropriate reason code has not been entered by the Branch in the System,

                Submission of form 15G/15H wrongly noted in the Customer Master,

                TDS Reason Code is “Yes” for Regd. Co-operative Society,

In above cases, TDS will be wrongly deducted. Hence, branches are advised to take precaution.

          Grouping of Customer Numbers – Sometimes there is wrong Grouping of Customers (e.g. grouping of Husband and Wife’s Customer Number) or grouping is not removed even if Minor becomes Major. Similar other cases are seen in respect of Wrongly Grouped Customers. In such cases, interest will get clubbed and TDS will get deducted under the name of Main Customer. Hence, there should be correct Grouping of Customer IDs, as also TDS Reason Code should be updated in Main Customer and not in Sub-Customer. It may be noted that if details are updated in Sub- Customer, the same will not get considered while application of TDS.

 

Example : A Customer No. 123 is grouped under Customer No. 345, then PAN and TDS Reason Code is to be updated to Customer No. 345 and not in 123.

          More than 1 Customer ID for the same customer – In such cases, there is a possibility of Short Deduction of TDS.

For any mistakes due to wrong data entry in OMNI, branches (i.e. the staff concerned and the branch-in-charge) will be held responsible and they will be liable to make good the losses if any.

 

5.12. Issue of Form 16A (TDS Certificates)

 

          Accounts Department will download form 16A on a quarterly basis.

          The due date of downloading Form 16A from TRACES is 15th of the month following the end of the month of filing of TDS return.

          The form 16A will be made available in OMNI 3 and the same shall be intimated to branches by Accounts Department.

 

5.13. Maintenance of TDS Records –

 

TDS records are to be preserved for a period of 8 Financial Years.

Branches and Account Department should maintain records as under :

          Records to be maintained at Branches

        Form 15G or 15H collected from Customers.

        List of Customers submitting form 15G/15H in Excel file.

        List of Deductees as mentioned in point 5.7.

        List of NRO Deductees as mentioned in point 5.7.

        List of Exemption Certificate as mentioned in point 5.7.

        Details of TDS deducted on Rent in format mentioned in point 5.7.

                Details of TDS deducted on Commission to DDS Agent in format mentioned in point 5.7.

                Details of TDS deducted on payments made to Contractors in format mentioned in point 5.7.

The above records are required for tax audit purpose.

          Records to be maintained at Accounts Department

                Month wise Consolidated list of

        Deductees

        NRO Deductees

        TDS on Rent

        TDS on Commission to DDS Agent

        TDS on payment made to Contractors.

        Consolidated list of Exemption Certificates

        Challan Acknowledgment

Quarterly list of form 15G/15H as generated from OMNI for TDS Return

        Copy of 26Q & 27Q (TDS Return) & TDS Return Acknowledgment

        Soft copy of Form 16A

 

5.14. Correction of default

 

In case of errors/corrections in the TDS Return filed and reported in TRACES, they shall be corrected by Accounts Department on request received from branches through e-mail via exchange server, once in a month as approved by the board vide Submission No. Accts/87/2017-18 dated 31/07/2017. Branches should provide the required data to Accounts Department immediately for filing corrected e-TDS Return on time. The time taken for processing the corrected return by TRACES is subject to site/server issues at their end.

 

Branch in-charges are advised to allot minimum one Clerk and one Assistant Accountant for TDS work. The in-charge of the Branch and staff handling TDS work will be held responsible for any errors in TDS work.

 

6. Tasks to be performed at Accounts Department

 

          To run Auto Reversal Programme on the next working day of the following month.

          To follow up with Branches to obtain the list of Deductees, NRO Deductees, TDS on Rent, TDS of DDS Agent’s Commission, TDS on payment to Contractors and list of Exemption Certificate as mentioned in point no. 5.7 on or before 5th day of the month.

          To ensure that the list forwarded by Branches is in required formats and tallied with General Ledger Balance as on 1st/2nd day of the month at Branch level (after auto reversal program is completed by Accts Dept).

          To consolidate the lists received from all the Branches and prepare single list for payment of TDS on Interest on Deposits, Rent, DDS Commission and Payment to Contractors.

          To make payment of TDS on or before the due date of payment of TDS i.e. 7th day of the Month.

          To generate the data of Form 15G/15H on Quarterly basis for the purpose of filing TDS Return.

          To file quarterly return of Form 15G and Form 15H with Income Tax Department on the basis of data received from our I.T. Dept on or before 15th of the month following the quarter.

          To prepare TDS Returns after end of each quarter and file the same on or before 31st or 30th of month following the quarter. TDS Return will contain the consolidated data of TDS on Interest on Deposits, NRO Deposits, Rent, DDS Commission, Payment to Contractors, Details of Exemption Certificates, Details of Customers submitting form 15G/15H. The data of TDS of Bills dept. will also be added to the TDS Return.

          To download form 16A on or before 15th of the month following the end of the month of filing of TDS return.

          To make available to branches Form 16 A in OMNI 3 duly signed as mentioned in point 5.12.

          To make necessary correction of default (if any) and accordingly file revised returns.

          To keep record of all necessary TDS data.

          To conduct the TDS Snap Audit.

          To arrange training to the staff who are handling the TDS work at Branches, with the help of I.T. Department and disseminate information regarding TDS procedure among all the Branches.

          To make necessary changes in existing OMNI 3 Software and provide the required fields in OMNI Software in consultation with Accounts Department and M/s. Infrasoft.
