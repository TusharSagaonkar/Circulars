Circular OC No. 817 dated 26th June, 2019

1) Opening of SB/CD A/c in the name of proposed Co-op. Hsg. Society

And

2)    Societies Formed for the Purpose of Applying to Area Development Body (e.g. Cidco-Mhada) for Allotment of Plot

 

 

I.    In supersession of the Office Order 305 dated 26th September 2009 & the Office Circular no OC 778 dated 17th November 2017, following revised guidelines are being issued for opening SB / CD accounts of a Proposed Co-op. Hsg. Society, and a Society formed for the purpose of applying to Area Development Body for Allotment of Plot :-    

 

1)  To obtain letter / Order from the Office of the Deputy Registrar of Co-op Societies giving permission for opening of Proposed Society’s accounts with reservation of Name clause in the name of the Proposed Society.

2)  To obtain Resolution for operating account by minimum 2 members out of total 3 office bearers i.e. Chairman, Secretary and Treasurer, together with their KYC Compliance. The resolution should be signed by all the members present in the meeting.

3)  To obtain a self-attested copy of the minutes of the meeting of the proposed Co-op. Hsg. society including names and signatures of members present in the General Body Meeting wherein resolution is properly recorded authorizing to open a SB/CD account with our Bank/Branch.

4)  To obtain a copy of the latest list of members with their signatures with flat Nos. for branch-record/reference.

5)  To obtain a declaration stating that as soon as the society is registered, promoters will inform the Bank and submit a copy of the Certificate of Registration as well as a copy of the bye-laws approved by the Registrar of Co-op. Societies.

 

II. Operational Instructions:

 

1)  The account of such a proposed Co-op Housing society will be operational for only two months.

2)  Only credits will be allowed in the account. No withdrawals should be permitted.

3)  If the promoters do not complete the procedure of registration within two months, permission of the Deputy Registrar of Co-op Societies will be required to withdraw the amount lying in the account, or to close the account.

 

III. Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
