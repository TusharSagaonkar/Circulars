Circular OC No. 827 dated 7th November, 2019

GUIDELINES IN RESPECT OF NEW TIE UP OF OUR BANK WITH BAJAJ ALLIANZ GENERAL INSURANCE COMPANY LTD.

FOR GENERAL INSURANCE SERVICES

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

I.    Introduction:

1)   Currently, our Bank, as Corporate Insurance Agent, is offering Life Insurance services through LIC of India (LIC) since 25/02/2009 & Exide Life Insurance Company Limited since 07/02/2019, General Insurance services through The New India Assurance Company Limited (NIAC) since 25/02/2012, and Health Insurance services through Religare Health Insurance Company Limited (RHICL) since 01/04/2016.

 

2)   As per the notification from Insurance Regulatory & Development Authority of India (IRDA) (Registration of Corporate Agents) Regulations 2015, Corporate Agents are permitted to offer Insurance services by entering into agreements with three Insurance companies in each segment, i.e. Life, General & Health insurance.

 

3)    General Insurance is need of the hour, especially given its low market penetration levels. It also gives us an opportunity to increase fee-based income while ensuring security of customers’ assets. General Insurance industry has been consistently growing over the last decade and our Bank can take advantage of the growing market through distribution of relevant and customer-friendly products. Therefore, our Bank’s Board in its meeting held on 7th August, 2019 decided to add agency of one more General Insurance Company, namely Bajaj Allianz General Insurance Company Limited (BAGIC), in addition to our existing Corporate Agency of The New India Assurance Company Limited (NIAC). Our Bank has accordingly entered into an agreement with BAGIC on 17/10/2019. General Insurance products through BAGIC will be made available to customers through our Bank branches w. e. f. 11th November, 2019.

 

II.   Bajaj General Insurance (BAGIC) & Our Contract with it

 

1)    Bajaj General Insurance Company Limited commenced operations in May, 2001 as a joint venture between the Bajaj Finserv Limited owned by the Bajaj Group of India and Allianz SE, a German Financial Company; and is headquartered in Pune. During the financial year 2018-19, the company earned Total Premium Income of over `11,059 Crore.

 

2)    BAGIC has agreed to assign field staff to assist our branches in canvassing their insurance policies. The list of field staff assigned to branches by BAGIC would be made available on the Exchange Server in Insurance folder.

 

After training in respect to BAGIC general insurance products is imparted to staff members, branches should cross-sell general insurance products of BAGIC to all SB, CD and Deposit holders. Branches should also convince borrowers / customers /account holders, who have taken insurance policies from other General Insurance companies than New India Assurance Company Ltd. under our Corporate Agency code, to take their policies from BAGIC through us. Branches are advised to obtain consent of the proposer-borrowers in this behalf by obtaining signatures on general insurance proposal forms of BAGIC. They should also collect from the borrowers the necessary premium amount. To ensure that an existing policy of our customer from other insurance company gets switched over to BAGIC, the BAGIC proposal form should be obtained well in advance of its renewal date.

 

3)  BAGIC has allotted branch site codes to all of our branches. In this behalf, kindly refer the Annexure hereto. The list also contains branch-wise mapping details with the offices of BAGIC along with the names of field Staff assigned to branches same information is also available on Exchange Server in Public Folder. Branches are advised to invariably mention in all proposal forms sent to BAGIC, our Bank’s agency code “10077147” and branch site code as given in the Annexure, so that the commission in respect of the policies canvassed / marketed through a branch is received by the Bank and can be passed on to that branch.

 

4)    Each branch is attached to the nearest office of BAGIC. Branch-In-Charges are advised to maintain close rapport with officials of the designated office of BAGIC. Insurance Department, Vashi has informed the Head Office of BAGIC to advise their offices to extend full support to our branches for smooth operation of the agency.

 

III. Instructions

1)  Proposal Forms:

Commonly used proposal forms used for insurance through BAGIC and related instructions-material are made available on Exchange Server in Public Folder of Insurance Department. Kindly note that printouts / photocopies of these proposal forms are also accepted by BAGIC.

 

2)  Procedure of Lodging Claim:

      In case of an unfortunate incident, Toll Free Number of BAGIC is 1800-209-5858 through which the customer suffering loss may lodge the claim. The insured must inform BAGIC by calling up the BAGIC office at the earliest, or within 24 hours of happening of the incidence. The contact / fax number / e-mail address of the policy-issuing branch is mentioned in the policy document. Customer can also send an e-mail to BAGIC office. The written communication should reach BAGIC office within 7 days of the incident. The affected, damaged assets / items should be kept as they are, until inspection / survey is carried out by the surveyors appointed by BAGIC.

 

Note: The above procedures & guidelines are not exhaustive but illustrative. This information is provided to branches to enable them to guide customers. Any Insurance Company, including BAGIC, may seek more information for settlement of the insurance claim, depending on the nature of claim.

 

3)  Role of Branches:

a)     Branches should route new as well as renewal business in respect to BAGIC policies directly through the field staff deputed by BAGIC at the branch.

b)    Branches will need to maintain record of all the leads, Policies & Claim- details provided to BAGIC and update the same on daily basis in the pre-printed register provided by our Insurance Department exclusively for insurance through BAGIC. Details of total business marketed under each category of Insurer are to be forwarded to the Insurance Department for maintaining consolidated record at their end on monthly basis.

c)     Bajaj Allianz General Insurance, in consultation with Head Office and Insurance department, will undertake various marketing activities to cross-sell insurance to the Bank’s customers through branches. Branches should extend full cooperation to BAGIC for achieving their sale targets.

d)    Branches shall reconcile on monthly basis the amount of commission received from Insurance Department with the entries of commission receivable in the register maintained at branch, and ensure that there is no income leakage. In case of un-reconciled entries, Insurance Department – Vashi may be contacted.

e)     Claims of customers must be forwarded directly to Bajaj Allianz General Insurance office or the representative of Bajaj Allianz General Insurance assigned to respective branches. Branches shall make entry of the customer’s claim in the claim column of the register provided by the Insurance Department.

f)      Customers should be advised to retain photocopies of the documents before submission of the claim form.

 

4)  Role of Insurance Department, Vashi:

a)    Act as coordinator between BAGIC & branches for providing necessary support for procuring new as well as renewal insurance business.

b)    Provide preprinted Lead, Issue & Claim Register to branches for proper maintenance of record.

c)    Guide branches / customers and help them in resolving their queries.

d)    Transfer commission received from Bajaj Allianz General Insurance to branches on monthly basis, along with the MIS on branch-wise business performance.

e)    Communicate to branches details of changes as and when they are made in the existing products or as & when new products are introduced by BAGIC.

f)     Provide marketing material and support to branches.

 

5)  Role of Marketing Department:

Marketing Department shall provide adequate marketing material to all the branches. The material shall comprise posters, banners, danglers, standees and leaflets. For additional marketing material, branch staff may contact our Insurance Department at Vashi.

 

 

 

6)  Role of IT Department:

IT department will integrate the systems in respect of all the insurance services wherever required, in association with the Insurance department, and roll out the process for premium collection in respect to BAGIC policies. The Department will provide software to take care of IRDA compliance requirements, generation of MIS and data maintenance.

 

7)  Training:

Training Managers from Bajaj Allianz General Insurance would work more as business enablers, as their performance is measured on branch productivity & team activation. Such training would be conducted through our training centre at Vashi or directly at branches in consultation with the In-charge of branches on need-to-know basis.

[Note: The above guidelines are not exhaustive but illustrative, and are intended to provide basic guidance to branches. Branches may seek further guidance from Insurance Department or BAGIC depending on the nature of a specific Insurance Policy and considering specific requirements of our customers.]

 

IV. Please Note that all third party fee-based business is monitored closely. If any third-party business opportunity is lost due to casual approach of the Branch / Zonal / Regional office, it will be viewed seriously.

         

V.   Kindly take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff and officials concerned at your Branch / Department. Our Bank endeavours to extend the products & services of Bajaj Allianz General Insurance Company Limited to as many customers as possible. Each employee is urged to make this partnership a success by contributing to the effort and increasing fee-based income of our Bank.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian
