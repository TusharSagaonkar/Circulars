Circular OC No. 818 dated 27th June, 2019

Charges on RTGS & NEFT Transaction

 

 

I.    In supersession of the schedule of charges for NEFT Transaction provided in Para 5 A) on Page 4 of the “Policy on Remittances of Funds through RTGS/NEFT” circularised under Circular OC No. 798 dated 10th August, 2018 and, the schedule of charges for RTGS transactions provided in Circular OC No. 815 dated 30th May, 2019, the charges on RTGS & NEFT transactions have been revised as under w.e.f. 1st July, 2019.

 

 

Slab

Applicable Charges

RTGS

From Rs. 2.00 Lakh to Rs. 5.00 Lakh

Rs. 24.00 plus GST

RTGS

Above Rs. 5.00 Lakh

Rs. 49.00 plus GST

 

NEFT

Upto Rs. 10,000/-

Rs. 2.00 plus GST

NEFT

Above Rs. 10,000/- upto Rs. 1.00 Lakh

Rs. 4.00 plus GST

NEFT

Above Rs. 1.00 Lakh upto Rs. 2.00 Lakh

Rs. 14.00 plus GST

NEFT

Above Rs. 2.00 Lakh

Rs. 24.00 plus GST

 

II.  IT department is hereby being advised to make the necessary changes in the OMNI software.

 

III.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
