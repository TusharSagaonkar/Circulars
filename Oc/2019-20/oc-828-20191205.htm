Circular OC No. 828 dated 5th December, 2019

Issuance & Encashment of Duplicate FDRs Due Diligence

_______________________________________________________________________

 

I.   Issuance & encashment of duplicate FDRs has been a fraud-prone area. The circular OC No. 783 dated 3rd February, 2018 made a mention of some lapses which make it possible for miscreants to perpetrate frauds on the bank in such transactions; and also incorporated guidelines which must be followed by operating staff at branches to prevent such frauds. Recently, one more fraud has surfaced in this area, raising serious concerns about lack of seriousness on the part of staff while dealing with such matters. We narrate below the essential aspects of the fraud, the lapses on the part of our staff which facilitated its perpetration and the learning points emerging from the episode which must be followed meticulously.

 

II.       1) There were two customers having identical names holding SB accounts with distinct identities, SB A/c nos. & customer IDs. The accounts were with two different branches.

 

2) One of these two customers placed funds in FDR with her branch. The maker while creating the FDR field in the OMNI system, made the mistake of picking up customer ID of the other customer and thus the address of other customer got printed on FDR. There was no checking by checker of input data from the application form for the FDR. The SB A/c no. of the actual depositor was entered in the FDR field and in turn on the printed FDR.

3) The other customer (who had not placed the funds for the FDR) received notices for renewal, because of her address having been captured in the FDR field of the OMNI system. The FDR got auto renewed on four occasions in the absence of any specific instructions to the contrary.

4) The other customer approached the branch in whose books the FDR was accounted for, demanding issue of a duplicate one. The branch readily agreed to issue the duplicate, on the basis of a non-standard request letter, upon an unstamped indemnity, without conducting the KYC exercise & without even verifying the signature from that on the application for the original FDR. The signature of the witness who posed as a customer of the branch was also not verified from the relevant records. The duplicate FDR was issued within two days of the request, and request for its premature payment was accepted and acted upon again on the date of issue itself without verification including that of the signature, with that of the original depositor.

5) The premature payment of the duplicate FDR was made into the (wrong) SB A/c. of the other customer, even when the FDR field in the OMNI system as also the duplicate FDR, both, bore the correct SB A/c no. of the real depositor.

 

6) The mistake came to light when the real depositor contacted the branch for payment of the FDR.

7) All efforts to recover the money mistakenly paid to the other customer have not so far been successful.

III.  The mistakes on the part of staff members handling the transactions have been listed hereunder:

1) Address of the customer was picked up from the wrong ID for posting in the FDR field. The same got printed on the FDR.

 

2) This resulted in notices of maturity etc. reaching the wrong person. The mistake was on the part of maker in choosing the wrong ID as well as checker whose duty it was to check the input data from the application form filled in by the customer. The mistake was compounded by the second signatory of the FDR by omitting to verify the particulars independently.

 

3) When the other person sought duplicate FDR, her application was not in the standard format. The indemnity was unstamped.

 

4) Signature of the applicant for duplicate FDR was not verified by the maker, checker and the second signatory, from that on the original application for FDR. Signature of the witness posing as yet another customer was also not verified from the relative customer master. The standard check-points which are printed on the standard application form for issue of a duplicate FDR were not followed.

 

5) The application for issue of the duplicate FDR should have been referred to the Branch Head. This was not done. (The duplicate FDR itself was, however, signed by the Branch Head).

 

6) The request for premature payment of the said duplicate FDR was entertained by the branch on the same day as the date of its issue. Such an attempt should, in fact, have alerted the branch staff to be more cautious & should have led to examine bona fides of the person seeking such payment. This was not done.

 

7) Signature of the person seeking to obtain premature payment on the strength of the duplicate FDR should have been verified at least at that stage with that of the original depositor on the application for the original FDR. This was not done, either by maker, or checker, or the Branch In-charge.

 

8) The account no. of the real depositor was posted in the FDR A/c opened in the OMNI System and the same was printed on the first FDR issued. Yet, the proceeds of the duplicate FDR, prematurely paid, were credited to the account of the other person even though the system showed the account number of the real depositor. The maker & checker, both, have shown utter dereliction of duty while affording the credit.

 

IV.   In light of the above, we reiterate our instructions contained in Para III of the OC No. 783 dated 3rd February, 2018 on the subject of duplicate FDRs and/or their payment, premature or otherwise, with addition of a few more,

 

1) When a customer approaches for duplicate Fixed Deposit Receipt; or presents one for payment and/or renewal, either prematurely or upon maturity, the staff concerned SHOULD NOT AUTOMATICALLY PRESUME that:

 

a.  (i) The person demanding the duplicate FDR is indeed the depositor, and/ or

(ii) There is no lien against the FDR either as primary or collateral security.

 

b.  (i) The person presenting the FDR for renewal or payment is its owner, and/or

(ii)         There is no lien against the FDR either as primary or collateral security, the same being in his possession.

 

2) a. Customer must always be identified invariably as per the Bank’s procedure. Proof of Identity & latest Proof of Address should be called for afresh and name and address should be compared with those on record as per the application obtained at the time of the original deposit. Particular care must be taken while comparing names & signatures as there is always a possibility of different persons having similar or nearly similar names.

 

b. Customer ID & the SB a/c no. to which the proceeds are mandated for credit should be doubly ensured for correctness.

c. The copying of data from one field to another should be with application of mind to guard against mistakes caused by working mechanically.

d. The checker should always verify the input data from the application forms for the relative transaction submitted by the customer.

e. Physical appearance of the customer should be discreetly (i.e. without making the person conscious so as to avoid embarrassment) compared with the photograph on Proof of Identity document.

 

f.   Inquiry should be made in the Omni system to ascertain whether there is lien marked against the FDR. The inquiry should be against the FDR no. as well as against the name of the depositor.

 

g.  When the result of inquiry regarding loan against the name of the person or TDR is positive, the matter should be investigated and the Branch In-charge should satisfy himself / herself that the attempt on the part of the customer was innocent. In case of suspicion, the matter should be reported to the Zonal Office. In such a case, the FDR should obviously be not paid out or renewed.

 

h. Sanctity of maker/checker concept should be maintained. The checker’s basic duty to go over the entire primary inputting of data to eliminate or rectify mistakes on the part of maker should be performed diligently.

 

i.   The documentation, be it application for issuance of a duplicate FDR, form of indemnity with adequate stamp duty, or any other, must be in standard form & according to laid down instructions.

 

j.   The Branch In-charge is expected to give a managerial look to unusual cases such as issuance of duplicate FDR, premature payment of a duplicate FDR in short span of its issuance etc.

V.  It is very clear that important instructions to follow the systems & procedures in the matter of issue of duplicate FDR and its encashment, whether upon or before maturity, are not being followed meticulously. The staff members in all the positions involved in the transactions must act with utmost diligence failing which strict disciplinary action will be taken.

 

VI.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at the Branch and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
