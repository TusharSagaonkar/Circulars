Circular OC No. 815 dated 30th May, 2019

RTGS System – Extension of Timings for Customer Transactions

I.                 Please refer to our Circular OC No. 798 dated 10th August, 2018 wherein instructions are given for Remittances of Funds through RTGS.

II.  In terms of RBI Circular RBI/2018-19/189 DPSS (CO) RTGS No. 2488/04.04.016/ 2018-19 dated 28th May 2019, RBI has decided to extend the timings for customer transactions in RTGS from 4:30 pm to 6:00 pm. Accordingly, the RTGS time window with effect from June 01, 2019 will be as under:

IV.  In the above circumstances, Branches are advised to immediately enter & authorize the transaction to avoid customer complaints, as the charges will be levied by RBI on the basis of settlement of transaction at their end. Since 01.04.2016 the RTGS charges including time varying tariff are collected by the SYSTEM on the basis of timings of entering the RTGS transaction in CBS (OMNI). The Inspection Department is advised to verify the timings of transactions and that the charges are collected as per above instructions.

 

V. Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at the Branch and ensure compliance.
