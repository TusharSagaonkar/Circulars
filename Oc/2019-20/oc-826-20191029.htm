Circular OC No. 826 dated 29th October, 2019

 

1) Procedure for Updation of Change of Address in Omni Database

2) Obtaining Copy of Registered Leave and License Agreement for Opening of All Types of Deposit Accounts

3) Obtaining Particulars of Landmark with Permanent/Current Address.

4) Consent of the Relative

5) Obtaining, as proof of Business, Udyog Aadhaar Registration Certificate and Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum or Receipt of Intimation of Commencement of Business under Shop & Establishment Act, 2017 or “GST Registration Certificate”

________________________________________________________________________________

 

I.    Procedure for Updation of Change of Address in Omni Database :-

1)    In the executive committee meeting of KYC & AML Officials, concern was raised that many Rupay Debit cards are being returned by postal authorities for the reasons such as ‘incomplete address’, ‘not known’, ‘addressee left’, ‘wrong Pin code’, ‘Room No. not mentioned’, ‘Building/Chawl name not mentioned’ etc. Concern was also raised over the reports that a customer, who is approached telephonically regarding returns/non-delivery, states that his/her new address with the necessary proof is submitted by him/her to the Bank, but has not been updated by the bank in its database.

2)    The KYC/AML Cell has observed that branches are not following the proper procedure for change of address as communicated by the existing customers, in the OMNI database. Secondly, complete address is also not obtained while opening new accounts, e.g. particulars such as Room No. / Chawl No., landmark (near/opposite) and PIN code are also not sought & recorded. All these factors are resulting in non-delivery of Rupay Debit Cards and Address Confirmation Letters.

3)    In order to prevent lapses as mentioned above, Branches should, henceforth, adhere to the following procedure for recording change of address as communicated by the customers, in the Omni Database :-

 

a)    Obtain Modification Form with supporting document towards proof of address duly self attested and verify it with the original.

 

b)    Go to Customer Master – Customer Address Master – Click on Create – Feed new customer address – Click on Commit.

 

c)     Go to Account Master – Account Address (New) – Click on Create – Screen will display two addresses (Old and New) – Go to Select Button - Tick on checkbox of new address – Click on Commit - After clicking on the Commit button, screen will display Linked Y (on new address).

 

4)    After following the procedure as above, new address will be linked to the Customer Master as well as the Account Master and all the future correspondence will be sent at the updated address.

 

II.   Obtaining copy of Registered Leave and License Agreement for Opening of all Types of Deposit Accounts.

 

It is observed that branches are not strictly adhering to the guidelines regarding obtaining a copy of Registered Leave and License Agreement while opening any of the various types of Deposit accounts as mentioned in Circular OC No. 816 dated 15th June 2019. It is reiterated that branches should invariably obtain a copy of the Registered Leave & License Agreement as proof of address while opening an account wherever current address is not mentioned in the Officially Valid Documents (OVDs).

It may be noted that it is mandatory to arrange a visit to the declared address before issuing Passbook / Cheque book / Rupay Debit Card wherever account is opened by obtaining ‘Self Declaration’.

 

III. Obtaining Particulars of Landmark near the Declared Address along with the Permanent / Current Address

 

Branches should invariably obtain particulars of some Landmark near the declared address such as “Near / Opposite XYZ School / College, Temple, Mall, Office, Bank” etc. The same should be entered in the OMNI database, so that it will be easy to deliver Bank’s Letters, Notice, Rupay Debit cards to the customers.

 

IV. Consent of the Relative

If the customer is staying with his / her relative and is not having deemed OVD in his / her own name, then the Consent of the Relative should be obtained. It is hereby clarified that in all such cases where a relative is giving consent for co-residence, the relative should also have Saving / Current account with our Bank.

 

V.   Obtaining Udyog Aadhaar Registration Certificate and Udyog Aadhaar Memorandum or Receipt of Intimation of Commencement of Business under Shop & Establishment Act, 2017 or “GST Registration Certificate” as proof of Business for opening Current account.

 

It is observed that while opening a current account, proprietary concerns submit ‘Udyog Aadhaar Registration Certificate and Memorandum or Receipt of Intimation of Commencement of Business of Intimation” or “GST Registration Certificate” towards business proof. In the recent past, it was observed that miscreants use this document for opening an account and cheat the Bank. The said Registration Certificate is obtained online and it can be misused easily.   

 

Therefore it has been decided to obtain a Reference letter as per the Annexure from an existing current account holder of our Bank, stating that he knows the applicant, that the applicant is engaged in business, and that he is aware of the purpose of the Reference which is for opening a current account by the applicant.

 

VI. Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at the Branch and ensure compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
