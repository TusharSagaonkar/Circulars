Circular OC No. 825 dated 18th September, 2019

Financial Inclusion – Access to Banking Services

Basic Savings Bank Account (BSB A/c)

____________________________________________________________________

 

I.Reference is invited to the Circular OC No. 608 dated 4th December, 2012 & Circular OC No. 612 dated 29th January, 2013 on the captioned subject.

 

II.  RBI has instructed banks to further expand the scope of facilities under the BSB A/cs. in terms of its circular DCBR.BPD(PCB/RCB)Cir.No.02/13.01.000/2019-20 dated 2nd August 2019. Accordingly, we advise and instruct as under:

1) With effect from 1st September, 2019, the condition of not accepting local cheques exceeding Rs.5000/- in favour of account holder for credit into the account is done away with. Now, an instrument for any amount can be accepted for credit into an BSB A/c. through clearing or otherwise.

 

2)  A ‘Basic Savings Bank Account’ (BSB a/c) should be considered a normal banking service available to the public at large. The facilities which are extended under such an account without any charge are as under:

                             i)   Such an account does not have the requirement of any minimum balance.

                           ii)   The services available in the account include deposit and withdrawal of cash at bank branch as well as ATMs, receipt/credit of money through electronic payment channels or by means of deposit/collection of cheques drawn by Central/State Government agencies and departments.

                         iii)   While there is no limit on the number and value of deposits that can be made in a month, account holders are allowed a maximum of four withdrawals in a month, including ATM withdrawals.

                          iv)   Facility of ATM card or ATM cum Debit card is now without any charges.

                           v)   No charge is to be levied for non-operation/activation of in-operative ‘Basic Savings Bank Account’ (BSB a/c).

 

3) Opening of 'Basic Savings Bank Accounts' (BSB a/cs) are subject to RBI instructions on “Know Your Customer” (KYC) / “Anti-Money Laundering” (AML) issued from time to time. Hence, branches shall ensure that a person opening an account under Basic Savings Bank a/c (BSB a/c) complies with all the requirements under KYC rules/ Anti-Money Laundering (AML) act.

 

4) Holders of 'Basic Savings Bank Account' (BSB a/c) will not be eligible for opening any other savings bank account at any branch of our bank. If a customer has any other existing savings bank account in our bank in which the name of the sole /first accountholder matches with the name in the intended BSB A/c, he / she will be required to close the same within 30 days from the date of opening the 'Basic Savings Bank Account' (BSB a/c). A person opening an account under Basic savings Bank account (BSB a/c) is required to sign an undertaking as per Annexure, declaring to the effect that he either does not have any other Saving Bank account or, if he has one, he will close the same within 30 days.

 

III.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at the Branch and ensure compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
