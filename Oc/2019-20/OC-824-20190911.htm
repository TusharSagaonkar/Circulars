Circular OC No. 824 dated 11th September, 2019

Internet Banking Facility Made Available to Companies, Trusts, Co-op. Societies,

Partnerships & Joint Personal Accounts with Joint Operations Subject to Conditions

_______________________________________________________________________

 

I.                In terms of OC Circular No. 822 dated 5th August, 2019, it was clarified that internet banking facility shall not be available to Companies & Trusts. Partnership Firms & Joint Accounts of individuals with mandate for joint operations were also precluded from availing the Internet Banking Service.

 

II.             1) Subsequent to the issue of the aforementioned circular, we received requests for extending the internet banking facility to co-operative societies. In light of the request, the matter of extending facilities to Companies, Trusts & Partnership/Individuals accounts with mandate for joint operations was revisited, while considering the same for cooperative societies, and it has now been decided to extend the facility to all the above- mentioned types of accounts.

 

2)The entities whose accounts otherwise have mandates for joint operations (either due to their constitution or choice) but need to entrust the User ID & Password, to be used for internet banking, to a single person need to declare & undertake as under to protect Bank’s interests, that

i)      they assume the risk of a single person conducting the operations through User ID & Password although the mandate for operations is joint by design,

 

ii)    the Company, Trust, Co-operative Society, all the partners of a Partnership Firm with mandate for joint operations, or individuals constituting the account holders of a personal account & giving mandate for only joint operations, as the case may be, assume the responsibility for maintenance of confidentiality of the User ID & Password, and also for transactions which may get conducted through compromise of the said User ID & Password,

 

iii)  although the financial transactions which are currently possible under Bank’s internet banking facility are to & from linked accounts of the entity, the entity agrees that this facility of doing financial transactions will not be extended to the accounts forming subject matter of the instant circular. (While we are seeking express consent from the entities concerned for not allowing financial transactions, branches will need to notify IT Department in each individual case by email to disable the facility of transfer of funds among linked accounts of the entity concerned).

 

III. Apart from the standard application / letter(s) prescribed for customers seeking internet banking, the entities will be required to act, & provide documents, as under :

 

1)Board of a company, trust or a co-operative society desirous of availing themselves of the internet facility shall hold a meeting of the Board of the company, the board of trustees, or Managing Committee of the society, as may

be the case, & record of minutes of the meeting on the lines of the applicable draft containing necessary resolution therefor from among those provided in the set of Annexures I (A,B,C) hereto.

 

2)The Board of the Company, Trust or Office-bearers of the Society will give a Declaration-Cum-Undertaking as per the applicable draft from among those provided in the set of Annexures II (A,B,C) as provided hereto.

 

3)A partnership firm giving mandate for joint operations on the account will give Declaration-Cum-Undertaking as per Annexure III. Similarly, individuals maintaining account jointly shall also give Declaration-Cum-Undertaking as per Annexure IV.

 

IV. It may be noted that obtaining Declaration-Cum-Undertaking does not absolve the functionaries at the Branch from the responsibility of exercising vigilance in respect of conduct of the account.

 

V.    Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at the Branch and ensure compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
