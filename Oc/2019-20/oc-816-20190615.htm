Circular OC No. 816 dated 15th June, 2019

KYC Documents to be Obtained while Opening

New Accounts of Natural Persons [Individuals]

 

 

I.                 Reserve Bank of India has revised & updated, through RBI/DBR/2015-16/18 on 29th May, 2019, the Master Direction DBR. AML. BC.No.81/14.01.001/2015-16 dated February 25, 2016 on the subject of “Know Your Customer (KYC) Direction, 2016”. The changes in the Master Direction are as under :-

 

1)   The revised Master Direction does not mention PAN among the Officially Valid Documents. Nevertheless, it is now mandatory to obtain PAN or Form 60 for opening a new account.

 

2)   In terms of the latest Master Direction, the Officially Valid Documents (OVD) are only six and they are as under:

a)  Passport,

b) Driving licence,

c)  Proof of possession of Aadhaar number,

d) Voter's Identity Card issued by the Election Commission of India,

e)  Job card issued by NREGA duly signed by an officer of the State Government

f)   Letter issued by the National Population Register containing details of name and address.

 

If name of a person as mentioned in any of the above six documents is different from the current name of the person, and the difference i.e. the change in name is supported either by a marriage certificate issued by the State Government or Gazette Notification, it will be deemed as an Officially Valid Document for temporary period of three months.

 

3)   Where the OVD furnished by the customer does not have updated address, the following documents shall be deemed to be OVDs for the limited purpose of proof of address:-

a)  Utility bill which is not more than two months old of any service provider (electricity, telephone, post-paid mobile phone, piped gas, water bill);

 

b) Property or Municipal tax receipt;

 

c)  Pension or family Pension Payment Orders (PPOs) issued to retired employees by Government Departments or Public Sector Undertakings, if they contain the address;

d) Letter of allotment of accommodation from employer issued by State Government or Central Government Departments, statutory or regulatory bodies, public sector undertakings, scheduled commercial banks, financial institutions and listed companies and leave and licence agreements with such employers allotting official accommodation;

 

The customer shall submit OVD with current address within a period of three months of submitting the documents specified at ‘3’ above.

 

II.  1) In view of the above, it has been decided to obtain Revised Declaration-cum-Undertaking (Annxure - I

) towards Proof of Current Address for a temporary period of 3 months. Along with this declaration-cum-undertaking, following documents should be obtained as applicable :-

 

i)      If deemed OVD is not in the name of customer, then obtain a copy of the Registered Leave & Licence Agreement,

Or

ii)    If Leave & Licence Agreement is not registered, then obtain one of the deemed OVDs in his / her name,

Or

iii)   If the customer is staying with his / her relative and not having deemed OVD in his / her name, then obtain Consent of the Relative (format enclosed) and relevant KYC documents of the relative.

 

2) To summarize, for opening all types of Individual accounts, branches shall obtain PAN / Form 60, OVDs with current address or Declaration-cum-Undertaking in the format as per Annxure - I &/or Consent of Relative if the OVD does not have updated address.

3) We are seeking guidance from RBI regarding action to be taken, if any, when a customer having opened an account on the basis of Declaration-cum-Undertaking for current address, does not submit one of the OVD, with current address incorporated therein within a period of three months from the date of account opening. Branches will be advised accordingly in due course.

 

III.   1) Customers, desirous of opening an account (but not for availing any benefit from Government under the DBT scheme), may offer Aadhaar as one of the Officially Valid Documents. RBI has issued instructions that, in such cases, first 8 digits of the Aadhaar No. should be masked so as to keep confidentiality of the Aadhaar data. Accordingly, we are issuing following instructions.

i)      Branches should enter the full Aadhaar no. in customer master.

ii)    Thereafter, the first eight digits of the Aadhaar no. in the photocopy of the Aadhaar card should be blackened as under.

(e.g. Aadhaar No. nnnn nnnn 9999)

iii)   The set of scanned account opening forms to be sent to the CDEC for further data entry, should contain the Aadhaar photocopy with such partially blackened Aadhaar no.

iv)   IT department is hereby instructed to hide/mask the first eight digits of the Aadhaar no. in the customer master.

 

2) It may be noted that Aadhaar no. should not be hidden/masked in respect of accounts opened to receive benefits under DBT.

 

IV.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
