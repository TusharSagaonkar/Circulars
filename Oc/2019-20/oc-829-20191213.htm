Circular OC No. 829 dated 13th December, 2019

Availability of NEFT System on 24x7 basis

_________________________________________________________________________

 

         I. Please refer Circular OC No. 798 dated 10th August, 2018 wherein instructions are given for Remittances of Funds through RTGS/NEFT.

 

       II. In terms of RBI circular no. RBI/2019-20/111 DPSS (CO) RPPD No.1097/04.03.01 /2019-20 dated 6th December, 2019, RBI has decided to extend availability of NEFT on 24*7 basis.

 

     III. 1) In view of the above, it has been decided that the above facility shall be made available to our customers from 16th December, 2019 on 24*7 basis. The first settlement thereunder will take place after 00:30 hours on 16th December, 2019 (i.e. during the night between 15th December, 2019 & 16th December, 2019, half an hour past midnight).

 

 2) In this regard, Branches are advised to note the following:

 

i)      There will be 48 half-hourly batches every day. The settlement of first batch will commence after 00:30 hours and the last batch will end at 00:00 hours.

 

ii)   The system will be available on all days of the year, including holidays.

 

iii)   In case the transaction gets rejected due to technical reason, the amount will be credited to sender’s account (our customer’s a/c).

 

iv)   After 19.00 hours (7.00 p.m.), the maximum NEFT outward amount is restricted up to Rs. 9,99,999/- per transaction.

 

v)    NEFT through Mobile Banking is available 24*7 on all days of the year, including holidays. Maximum NEFT outward amount under the Mobile Banking will be restricted up to Rs. 1,00,000/- per day.

 

      IV.    Branches to note that there will not be any change in existing timing of RTGS Service. The RTGS system will be available on all days of the year except Sunday, 2nd & 4th Saturday and RTGS holidays. The RTGS time window remains the same, which is as under :
