Circular OC No. 950 dated 18th July 2026

Treatment of and Interest Payment on Matured Term Deposits (MTDs)

____________________________________________________________________________________

I. Background

In terms of OC No. 660 dated 03.04.2014, the Bank revised its Term Deposit account-opening form, to include a standing instruction from the account holder authorizing the Bank to automatically renew the deposit on its due date, for a similar term at the interest rate prevailing at the time of maturity. This provision was introduced to safeguard depositor’s interest and to ensure continuity of investment.

 

Accordingly, where no instruction is received from a depositor on the maturity date, the Term Deposit is automatically renewed on the next working day for a similar term, at the interest rate prevailing on the date of renewal.

 

As a customer service measure, the Bank sends maturity intimation via SMS to all depositors 15 days in advance of the maturity date and again after renewal, enabling depositors to provide timely instructions and avoid any loss of interest.

 

Certain deposit types as mentioned below are excluded from the auto-renewal process for the reason mentioned their against:

 

•     Special Schemes: Auto-renewal process could not be generated for deposits under Special Schemes i.e. AB100, AB200, AB451, AB500, SHUB2QIC/SHUB2QID, SHUB3QIC/SHUB3QID, SHUBHAM401 as these schemes are launched for specific purpose & limited period only.

•     RD (Recurring Deposit): As per regulatory definition, type of deposits under Recurring nature could not be defined as a part of Term Deposit.

•     ATSS (Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme): Not eligible for auto-renewal

•     Due to existing technical glitches for calculating interest for MID/QID, auto renewal system does not work effectively for interest payout schemes.

 

It has been observed that, notwithstanding repeated advisories, several branches have failed to execute renewal instructions on matured deposits in a timely manner. As a result, significant balances are lying in MTD accounts and are being withdrawn by depositors upon branch visits if they observed that the present applicable rate of interest is lower than existing & amount is not renewed.

 

II. Objective

Non-renewal of matured deposits, or the mere parking of maturity proceeds under the 'TDMTD' GL head, results in interest being applied at the Savings Bank rate from the date of maturity - a rate invariably lower than the contracted Term Deposit rate. This situation leads to depositor dissatisfaction and potential complaints.

 

The purpose of this circular is to establish a uniform and transparent procedure for all branches for handling domestic and NRE/NRO Fixed (Term) Deposits that are not renewed or withdrawn upon maturity, and to ensure timely and accurate treatment of all Matured Term Deposits (MTDs).

 

III. Identification and Action Plan

1.  Review of Maturing Deposits: Branches shall generate a report of all Term Deposits maturing in the forthcoming month by the 20th of the preceding month, to enable proactive action.

Branches are advised to use the following Navigation path in OMNI for identifying maturing / matured deposits:

OMNI → Report-1 → TERMDEPOSITREPORT-II →TDMaturityListing-I

2.  Customer Outreach: Branches shall contact depositors well in advance of their maturity dates to obtain explicit instructions for proper treatment to be given to matured term deposits.

 

3.  Daily Monitoring: Branch In-charges shall monitor the relevant GL on a daily basis. All deposits classified under MTD status shall be manually renewed within 7 days of the maturity date in accordance with the provisions set out in Sections IV, V, and VI of this circular.

 

 

 

IV. Interest Payable on Matured Term Deposits (MTDs)

Where a Term Deposit matures and the proceeds remain unpaid or unclaimed with the Bank, the maturity amount shall attract interest at the rate lower of:

•     The Savings Bank rate applicable as on the date of maturity (variable Savings Bank rates applicable during specific tenure periods as revised from time to time); or

 

•     The contracted rate of interest on the matured Term Deposit.

 

 

1. Renewal of Matured Term Deposits

a)  Renewal within 7 Days of Maturity

·        Manual Renewal of Non-Auto-Renewed Deposits: Where a renewal request is received within 7 days of maturity for a deposit not under auto-renewal, the deposit shall be renewed with effect from the original maturity date for the tenure requested by the depositor. The applicable interest rate shall be the rate prevailing on the original maturity date for the requested term.

·        Modification of Auto-Renewed Deposits: Where a depositor approaches the Bank within 7 days of maturity and deposits has been auto-renewed, requests for change to a shorter tenure, a product switch, or a partial renewal of the auto-renewed deposit, the deposit shall be renewed with effect from the original maturity date as per the depositor's instructions. No penalty shall be levied in such cases.

 

b) Renewal after 7 Days of Maturity

·        Non-Auto-Renewed Deposits: Where a deposit has not been renewed under the auto-renewal process and a renewal request is received after 7 days from the maturity date, interest for the overdue period (i.e., from the date of maturity to the date of renewal/closure) shall be paid at the prevailing Savings Bank rate. The renewed deposit shall be effective from the date of the renewal requestand not from the original date of maturity.

·        Auto-Renewed Deposits: Where a depositor approaches the Bank more than 7 days after auto-renewal and seeks a change in tenure (shorter period), a product change, or partial renewal of the auto-renewed deposit, a penalty of 1% shall be levied on the applicable interest rate. The renewed deposit shall be effective from the date on which the depositor approaches the Bank.

2. Closure of Matured Term Deposits

The Bank may, at its discretion, permit closure and withdrawal of a Term Deposit before the completion of its agreed term, subject to valid discharge from all deposit holders and the following conditions:

a)  Closure within 7 Days of Maturity / Auto-Renewal: Where a depositor requests closure and withdrawal within 7 days of the maturity date or auto-renewal date, the Bank shall close the account. No interest shall be payable in such cases, as the minimum deposit period of 7 days has not been completed.

b)  Closure on or after 7 Days of Auto-Renewal: Such cases shall be treated as premature withdrawals. Interest shall be paid after levying the applicable penalty (presently 1%) on the rate applicable for the period for which the deposit actually remained with the Bank. Valid discharge from all deposit holders/signatories shall be obtained on the reverse of the Term Deposit Receipt.

c)  Re-Investment Exception: Where premature closure of an renewed deposit is sought for the purpose of re-investment of original principal amount/ maturity amount for a longer tenure, no penalty shall be levied.

3. Deposits in the Name of Senior Citizens with Survivorship Clause

The Bank offers an additional rate of interest on Term Deposits held in the name of a Senior Citizen as the first-named depositor, jointly with another person, under an Either or Survivor clause. The following conditions shall apply:

a)  Auto-Renewal: On the due date, the deposit shall be auto-renewed for a similar tenure at the interest rate applicable to Senior Citizens.

b)  Deceased Depositor - Post-Renewal: In the event of the death of the Senior Citizen after the date of auto-renewal, the additional Senior Citizen interest rate shall be applicable only up to the date of the next maturity (i.e., the maturity of the renewed deposit). From the date of maturity onwards, the general public rate shall apply.

c)  Deceased Depositor - Pre-Renewal: In the event of the death of the Senior Citizen before the date of auto-renewal (i.e., before the maturity date):

·        Interest shall be paid at the contracted rate up to the date of maturity.

·        From the maturity date to the date of actual payment, interest shall be at the general public rate. Any additional interest paid for this overdue period shall be recovered at the time of final payment.

d)  Discharge: At the time of payment, branches shall ensure that the Senior Citizen is existing and that the Term Deposit Receipt is duly discharged by the Senior Citizen. Payment shall be made only after this verification.

 

V. Product-Wise Treatment of Matured Term Deposits

To ensure operational efficiency and uniformity across branches, all Matured Term Deposits (MTDs) shall be treated in accordance with the following product-specific instructions:

Product

Treatment on Maturity

FDR / KEYDEP / MID / QID / QICRIP (including NRE/NRO Term Deposits)

Renew manually under the same scheme for a similar tenure at the interest rate prevailing on the date of maturity, subject to conditions in Section IV.

Note: Prior to renewal of any NRE Term Deposit, branches must verify the current residential/NRI status of the depositor.

Recurring Deposit (RD) / CF

Close immediately upon maturity. Credit the maturity proceeds to the depositor's Savings or Current Account as per existing standing instructions.

For RD accounts with a lien marking: renew under QICRIP for a similar tenure at the prevailing rate (subject to Section IV conditions), and re-apply the lien on the renewed QICRIP.

 

Product

Treatment on Maturity

Abhyudaya Tax Saving Scheme (ATSS)

Renew for a further tenure of 5 years under QID / MID / QICRIP at the prevailing interest rate (subject to Section IV conditions). Note:The renewed deposit will not carry any ATSS benefits (no 80C tax exemption, no mandatory lock-in).It shall be treated as a standard term deposit.

Matured Deposits without PAN

Do not renew deposits of Rs. 50,000/- and above (or where aggregate deposits exceed Rs. 5,00,000/- during the financial year) where PAN has not been furnished.

Contact the depositor to obtain PAN / Form 97(earlier Form 60). Deposits below Rs. 50,000/- may be renewed immediately subject to Section IV conditions.

Special / Discontinued Schemes (AB100, AB200QIC, AB451QIC, AB500QIC/QID, SHUB2QIC/QID, SHUB3QIC/QID,SHUBHAM401)

These schemes have been discontinued. Deposits maturing under discontinued special schemes must be manually renewed within 7 days of the maturity date under regular deposit schemes (MID / QID / QICRIP) for a similar tenure with similar interest payout, subject to Section IV conditions.

VI. Record Maintenance and Compliance

1. Lien-Marked and Earmarked MTDs

Branches must exercise utmost care to ensure that all renewed MTDs are correctly lien-marked or earmarked as applicable. The following steps shall be followed for lien-marked Fixed Deposits:

i.     Verify that the lien is current and subsisting. Note the Account Number and the instructions of the competent authority.

ii.   Temporarily remove the lien.

iii.  Calculate the Savings Bank interest applicable on the unrenewed Term Deposit from the date of maturity to the date of renewal.

iv. Compute the total renewal amount: Maturity Amount + Savings Bank Interest = Amount to be renewed as a Fresh Fixed Deposit.

v.   Re-apply the lien marking on the newly renewed Fixed Deposit.

2. Certificates and Documentation

i.     Enter renewal remarks in the Account Master under the Special Instructions field for all MTDs.

ii.   Fresh FD certificates shall NOT be printed proactively. The original FD certificate, which is with the depositor, remains valid.

iii.  A fresh (renewed) certificate may be printed only when the depositor visits the branch and produces the original FD certificate, or a renewal entry may be made on the reverse of the original certificate. In cases involving the manual renewal of special deposit schemes, a fresh Term Deposit Account opening form must be obtained from the depositor.

iv. KYC compliance of the Term Deposit account is mandatory and must be verified and confirmed on the date of renewal.

v.   Matured deposits outstanding for more than 10 years shall not be renewed, in accordance with RBI guidelines& without following due process of KYC compliance.

3. MTD Renewal Register

Branches shall maintain a dedicated MTD Renewal Register / Record for all manually renewed matured deposits. This register shall include lien-marking remarks wherever applicable. This record shall serve as the primary reference for audit compliance and the resolution of depositor disputes.

 

4.   IT Department is instructed to provide the necessary system support for the auto-renewal of Term Deposit accounts on their due dates in cases where depositors fail to produce the TDR along with instructions, as described above. IT Department is allow instructed to make necessary arrangements for sending SMS as per paragraph 3 of section I.

 

5.   Accounts Department is directed to continuously track and monitor Matured Term Deposit position. Furthermore, Department will act as the centralized support to assist branches with any customer operational or queries regarding these Matured Term Deposits.

 

6.   Inspection Department is instructed to verify that all branches are strictly complying with the guidelines outlined in this circular regarding processing/settlement, renewal, interest payment and record maintenance of Matured Term Deposits.

 

 

With the issue of these circular, the existing circulars, instructions, and guidelines relating to Matured Term Deposits stand repealed.

 

Branches and Departments are requested to take note of the above instructions and ensure that the contents of this circular are brought to the attention of all concerned staff. Strict adherence to these guidelines is required to safeguard depositor interests and uphold the Bank's service standards.

 

 

 

Chief Executive Officer
