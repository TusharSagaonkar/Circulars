Circular OC No. 951 dated 18th July 2026

Maintenance of Current Account/s with Other Bank/s and Reconciliation – Instructions to Follow

 

I. The Branches as well as the Departments are maintaining Current Account/s with other Bank/s for the following purposes:

1.   To Deposit Cash & to receive remittances through RTGS.

2.   NOSTRO A/c for Dadar, Vashi & Pimpri Branch’s customer transactions.

3.   For issuing Bank Guarantee/s to customers.

4.   for Treasury ATMRECON & Clearing Dept. operations.

5.   For e_Payment of Taxes at Account/s Dept & Pune Zone.

6.   For Franking Documents of Customers for New Panvel & Vashi Branch.

7.   Outward & Inward Remittance other than USD.

8.   For providing LC & BG facility to customers.

9.   For HDFC / ICICI DD funding / OBC by Branches, if any.

There may be some other purposes also. Since the balances kept in such Bank/s are in financial nature, it is the responsibility of the concerned Branches / Departments to operate the said Account/s judiciously and maintain the balances as per requirement.

In view of the implementation of the “One Nation One Grid” system, our Bank/s’s Pay Orders are now acceptable across India and are payable at par. Accordingly, there is no requirement to maintain the Account/s solely for the purpose mentioned at point No. 9, as the Pay Orders/Cheques issued by our Bank/s are eligible for same-day clearing across the country.

II. It is observed that, sometimes-substantial balance is kept in the current Account/s maintained with other Bank/s which results not only into financial loss to the Bank/s i.e. non earning of interest on this idle balance but also affects the investment opportunities of such funds.

It also affects the Interbank Exposures for the purpose of investment with other Bank/s, which is subject to adherence of regulatory norms.

At the time of review, it was also observed that some of the Bank/s are not accepting soiled notes and some of the Bank/s insist for a float of 3 days or more for withdrawal of the fund.

III. Accordingly, Branches/Departments are instructed to implement parameters / guidelines as given below.

a. Non acceptance of Soiled Notes:

Branches/ Departments are advised to send a suitable letter on Bank’s Letter Head to Bank/s where Current Account/s is maintained and who are not accepting soiled notes while depositing Cash (Letter Format –I attached). However Currency Chest / Branches to take care that they should not be large amount of soiled notes lying in currency. (refer Office Order CGM-204/433/2026-27 dated 07.07.2026 on subject of – Strict Compliance – handling of soiled notes)

b. Reduction in Float days :

Branches & Departments are advised to send a suitable letter on Bank’s Letter Head to Bank/s where Current Account/s is maintained with request for reduction in Float days from xxxxxxxx days to T+2 days. (Letter Format –II attached).

Also, Cost Benefit analysis should be done by related Branches & Departments where float is more than T+2 days i.e. if said fund could have been invested @ 5%(which is minimum Rate of Interest as per RBI SDF Rate) instead of keeping balance in Current Account/s with other Bank/s without interest for specific period.

c. Inoperative/ Dormant Account/s:

Wherever the Current Account/s maintained with other Bank/s are Inoperative or Dormant, such Branches & Departments are advised to review for continuation of such Account/s or else take steps to close the same by following due procedures.

d. Reconciliation Procedure:

It is of the prime importance that the exercise of reconciliation Current Account/s maintained with those Bank/s to be carried out and balance should be tallied in mirror Account/s with us on monthly basis. There should not be pending entries to be reconciled over a long period.

Further it is responsibility of concerned Branches / Departments that the unreconcilied entries should be passed immediately in our Mirror Account/s so that there is no mismatch in the balances. For said purpose it has been instructed to the Branches / Departments that they should verify the statement of Account/s maintained with the concerned Branches on weekly basis / at the time of deposit of cash in concerned Bank, so that if any entry debited to our Account/s such has Cash Processing Charges, Part Debit of the amount pertaining to soiled notes not accepted etc. can be passed in Mirror Account/s with us. This will result in avoiding the pending entry/ies to be reconciled on monthly basis. especially on Quarterly / Half Yearly / Yearly Closing (it should be confirm before prior to one day of Closing).

There should always be practise to obtain the Debit / Credit Advice if any from concerned Bank/s on the same day for which said Bank/s may be instructed suitably.

All concerned Branches and Departments are instructed to follow under mentioned procedure on monthly basis without fail. (to be submitted to accounts Dept.)

1.   Send monthly Current Account/s statement by 10th of succeeding month.

2.   Send Quarterly Balance Confirmation Certificate obtained from relevant Bank/s.

3.   Send Reconciliation Statement for all Current Account/s maintained after passing necessary reconciliation entries by the concerned Department & Branch as per availability Bank/s statement on monthly basis.

4.   Whenever possible Branches/Depts. should avail Internet Banking facility of concerned Bank for timely view of account statement & reconciliation thereof.

IV. Accounts Department: To monitor the Balances in Current Account/s of all Bank/s on regular basis i.e. at least fortnightly basis and two days prior of each Quarterly / Half Yearly / Yearly Closing. Also followup with Branches / Department if substantial Balance is observed. The following statement should be prepared for review by Higher Authorities whenever required.

V. Treasury Department: Since balances outstanding in Current Account/s is part of interbank exposure it is the responsibility of Treasury Department to provide the details of Bank/s wise balances in Current Account/s whenever there is reporting of interbank exposure in monthly submissions including the matters placed before the Investment Committee.

VI. Inspection Department: To verify that proper reconciliation procedure is followed and confirm that all the reconciliation statements / Balance Confirmation Certificate are obtained at Accounts Department/ Branches.

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members concerned and ensure strict compliance at your Branch/ Department.
