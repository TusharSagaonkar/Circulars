Circular OC No. 953 dated 29th July 2026

Offering e-Franking Services

_________________________________________________________________________________________

 

The Bank has obtained licences for installation and operation of e-Franking Machines at New Panvel Branch and Vashi Branch for providing e-Franking facilities to the General Public as well as for the Bank's own documentation requirements from the Chief Controlling Revenue Authority (CCRA), Government of Maharashtra, It should be noted that the Bank was offering (Impression base) Franking Service during the year 2009 till 31.12.2022 the date of discontinuation.

 

The objective of this facility is to facilitate payment of stamp duty through an authorised electronic mode in accordance with the provisions of the Maharashtra Stamp Act and the rules, guidelines and instructions issued by the Chief Controlling Revenue Authority (CCRA) from time to time.

 

Apart from meeting the Bank's operational requirements for loan documentation and other instruments, the facility is also expected to provide better customer service, enhance the Bank's visibility amongst the General Public and generate additional fee-based income through service charges and commission income.

 

All branches including the authorised branches to provide e-Franking services (New Panvel & Vashi) shall carefully study these instructions and ensure strict compliance. Wherever any subsequent instructions are issued by the Chief Controlling Revenue Authority (CCRA), Government of Maharashtra or any competent authority, such instructions shall prevail over the provisions of this circular.

 

The Branch Head / Manager of the authorised branches shall be personally responsible for ensuring compliance with these guidelines and maintaining proper records relating to e-Franking operations.

 

1. Procurement and Installation of e-Franking Machine

(a) Procurement

At present, e-Franking machines Model B700/V-2 on approval of the Administrative Committee have been procured by the Marketing Department from M/s. Pitney Bowes (NYSE: PBI vendor). 

 

(b) Installation

The e-Franking Machine have been installed at the location approved by the Chief Controlling Revenue Authority i.e. at our New Panvel and Vashi Branches.

 

It should be noted that the machine has to be installed at a separate counter; under the direct supervision of officials authorised by the Branch Head; away from public access; with adequate security arrangements and with proper locking facilities. The machine shall not be accessible to customers from across the counter.

 

(c) Display Boards

Marketing Department will provide each authorised branch a poster / display board informing Availability of e-Franking facility for prominently display along with:

Branch timings for e-Franking service.

List of documents not permitted for e-Franking.

Applicable Service Charges.

Contact details for customer grievances.

Display Board / Poster shall be placed at the entrance of the branch offering Franking Service and at the e-Franking counter.

 

(d) Working Hours

As prescribed by the Chief Controlling Revenue Authority, e-Franking shall be carried out only between: 10.00 a.m. and 5.00 p.m. on working days (Monday to Friday), excluding Government and Bank holidays.

Any impression made outside the prescribed hours shall be treated as invalid.

 

Branches shall notify the public of their e-Franking timings within the above permissible period. No document belonging either to the Bank or to any member of the public shall be e-Franked outside the prescribed timings.

 

2. Procedure for Loading and Reloading Funds in the e-Franking machine (Also Refer Annexure – I)

Loading and reloading of funds shall be carried out strictly in accordance with the workflow prescribed in Annexure – I. The process shall be as follows:

1. The authorised branch shall debit the designated General Ledger Account "EFRANKIN" for the amount proposed to be loaded.

2. The transaction shall be originated to the Accounts Department.

3. The Accounts Department shall make payment through the Government Receipt Accounting System (GRAS) using the NIC Portal.

4. The payment shall be made against the specific machine (meter number) allotted to the concerned branch.

5. After successful payment, the Accounts Department shall obtain the Government Receipt Number (GRN), verify the Tax ID and provide to the branch who will complete the loading process.

6. Commission permitted by the Government shall be deducted upfront while loading or reloading the machine.

7. Branches shall ensure that adequate balance is always available in the machine to meet public requirements.

 

Whenever the available balance falls below ₹1.00 lakh, the branch shall immediately initiate reloading.

 

The maximum loading limit per branch shall be as under:

Branch Maximum Balance

Vashi ₹ 6,00,000/-

New Panvel ₹ 6,00,000/-

 

No loading beyond the approved limit shall be undertaken without approval from Head Office.

 

Internal Control

To strengthen operational controls, loading and reloading shall be carried out under the Maker–Checker principle. The official initiating the transaction shall not authorise the same transaction. All loading transactions shall be verified by the Branch Manager or an authorised supervisory official. Supporting documents, GRN, system-generated acknowledgements and accounting entries shall be preserved for audit.

 

Minimum and Maximum Value for e- Franking:

The minimum and maximum amount that can be e-Franked through the machine shall be as prescribed by the Chief Controlling Revenue Authority. Presently:

 

Minimum: ₹ 100/

Maximum: ₹ 10,000/

 

Any revision made by the Government shall automatically become applicable.

 

Where stamp duty exceeds the permissible limit, customers shall be advised to obtain Electronic Secure Bank and Treasury Receipt (eSBTR) through any authorised Banks.

 

3. Procedure for e-Franking of Documents

(a) Application

Every applicant shall submit the prescribed application form as per Annexure-II duly completed and signed. The branch shall verify the completeness of the application before undertaking e-Franking.

 

(b) Mode of Payment

Stamp duty may be accepted through - Cash (subject to statutory limits); Pay Order issued by our Bank Demand Draft / Pay Order issued by another Bank.

 

Where payment is made through instruments drawn on another Bank, e-Franking shall normally be undertaken only after realisation of funds.

However, if written confirmation regarding issuance of the instrument is received from the issuing Bank and the branch is fully satisfied about its genuineness, e-Franking may be undertaken. Such confirmation shall be retained on record.

 

(c) PAN and KYC

Wherever applicable under Income-tax, Prevention of Money Laundering (PMLA) or any other statutory requirements, PAN and KYC details shall be obtained before accepting payment.

For cash receipts exceeding the prescribed statutory limit - currently ₹ 50,000/- copy of PAN shall be obtained, verified and retained for record.

 

(d) Receipt

An official receipt shall be issued immediately for every payment received towards stamp duty and service charges.

 

(e) Service Charges

The Bank shall recover service charges as approved by the competent authority.

 

Presently the charge shall be:

₹30 per document (inclusive of GST).

The accounting of service charges, GST, CGST and SGST shall be carried out as per extant accounting instructions.

 

(f) Use of e-Franking Machine

The authorised e-Franking Machine shall be used exclusively for impressing stamp duty on documents for which the Bank has been authorised by the Chief Controlling Revenue Authority (CCRA). Under no circumstances shall the machine be used for any purpose other than that permitted under the authorisation.

 

E-Franking shall be carried out only during the prescribed hours notified by the CCRA. Any impression made outside the authorised timings shall be treated as invalid and may attract regulatory action.

 

Before undertaking e-Franking, the authorised official shall verify that the application form is complete, the applicable stamp duty payment has been received and accounted for the document is eligible for e-Franking and adequate balance is available in the e-Franking machine. The authorised official shall ensure that the correct stamp duty amount is entered before printing the impression.

 

(g) Certification of e-Franking

Immediately after the e-Franking impression is made, the authorised official shall certify the impression by recording the following particulars below the impression:

 

Serial Number and date as recorded in the Sale Register.

Name and complete address of the purchaser.

Stamp duty amount (in words).

Licence/Authorisation Number.

Signature of the authorised official with name of the Bank and Branch.

 

The impression shall be affixed on the first page of the document in such a manner that a portion of the impression appears on the written matter without obscuring any important particulars such as dates, names, consideration amount or signatures.

 

4. Statutory Registers and Records

Each authorised branch shall maintain the following statutory records in the prescribed formats with stamps and Signature& stamp of Revenue Department on Register:

1. Register of Sale of Stamps (Form VF/UF-5) – Annexure III.

2. e-Franking Machine Posting Register (Form VF/UF-6) – Annexure IV.

3. e-Franking Machine Record Book (Form VF/UF-7) – Annexure V.

4. Application Forms received from customers.

5. Daily Reconciliation Register.

6. Machine Loading and Reloading Register.

7. Machine Repair and Maintenance Register.

8. Licence Renewal Register.

9. Complaint and Incident Register.

 

All registers shall be maintained neatly, updated daily and preserved for the period prescribed under the Bank's Record Retention Policy and applicable statutory requirements.

 

5. Daily Reconciliation and Internal Controls

At the close of each working day, the authorised official shall reconcile:

Balance available in the e-Franking Machine, General Ledger balance, Register of Sale of Stamps (VF/UF-5), Posting Register (VF/UF-6), Machine balance as reflected in the system.

Any discrepancy shall immediately be brought to the notice of the Branch Manager and reported to Marketing Department/Head Office without delay.

The reconciliation shall be jointly verified and signed by the Maker - Operator (Clerk) and the Checker (Officer / Assistant Manager).

The Branch Manager shall independently verify and certify the reconciliation at least once every week.

A monthly reconciliation certificate shall be forwarded to Marketing Department along with the prescribed monthly returns.

 

6. Maker–Checker Controls

The following Maker–Checker controls shall invariably be followed:

The official receiving the application and entering the transaction shall not authorise the same transaction.

Loading or reloading of the machine shall always require supervisory verification.

 

Daily reconciliation shall be independently verified.

Any correction, cancellation or adjustment shall require approval of the Branch Manager.

 

Under no circumstances shall blank impressions be taken or trial impressions be made on original documents.

 

7. Monthly Returns

Each authorised branch shall submit the prescribed monthly statements (Annexure VII and Annexure VIII) to Head Office on or before the 5th day of the succeeding month.

 

Marketing Department shall consolidate the returns and submit the same to the Office of the Superintendent of Stamps before the 10th day of every month.

 

Branches shall ensure that all returns are complete, accurate and duly certified by the Branch Manager.

 

8. Procedure for Incorrect Impressions, Losses and Damages

(a) Wrong Impression

If an incorrect amount is inadvertently impressed on a document, the following procedure shall be followed:

The wrongly franked portion shall be carefully removed.

The original impression shall be preserved.

A photocopy of the impression shall be pasted in the prescribed register.

Necessary authentication shall be made by the authorised official.

 

All corresponding entries shall be made simultaneously in the relevant statutory registers.

 

(b) Refund of Stamp Duty

Refund of stamp duty in cases other than incorrect impressions shall be governed by the provisions of the Maharashtra Stamp Act and the Rules framed there under.

 

Wherever approval of the Additional Chief Secretary (ACS), Deputy Inspector General of Registration (DIG) or any other competent authority is required, branches shall process the claim accordingly. Branches shall maintain complete records of all refund cases.

 

9. Incident Reporting

The Branch Manager shall immediately report the following incidents to Marketing Department / Head Office, the Chief Controlling Revenue Authority (CCRA) and the authorised vendor, wherever applicable:

 

Machine malfunction, Seal tampering, Attempted fraud or misuse, incorrect meter readings, Physical Damage, Theft or loss, Fire or Flood, Cyber or System Security Incident. Any other event affecting the integrity of e-Franking operations.

 

No further transactions shall be undertaken until necessary permission is received from the competent authority.

10. Renewal of Authorisation

The e-Franking Machine shall never be operated without a valid authorisation.

Branches shall apply for renewal at least one month before expiry of the licence.

Normally Licence Expiry Date is: 31stDecember, each year

Application to be submitted by each branch for renewal before: 30thNovember

The prescribed renewal fee, presently ₹1,000/-, shall be paid in accordance with the instructions issued by the CCRA.

The Branch shall maintain complete renewal records in Form VF/UF-6 and the prescribed formats issued by the Registration and Stamps Department.

Failure to obtain timely renewal may result in suspension or cancellation of the licence, for which the Branch Manager shall be held responsible. Annexure - VI

 

If the Bank decides not to renew the licence, the machine shall be surrendered to the CCRA immediately upon expiry of the authorisation. Refund of the unused balance shall be claimed strictly in accordance with the provisions of the Maharashtra Stamp Act and the applicable Government guidelines.

 

11. Repairs and Maintenance

Routine maintenance, servicing, replacement of parts and cleaning of the e-Franking Machine shall be carried out only by the authorised service provider.

 

In the event of any malfunction:

1. The Branch shall immediately discontinue use of the machine.

2. The Branch Manager shall inform:

a. M/s. Pitney Bowes India Pvt Ltd, Bhandup West, Mumbai – 400078. Kalpeshsatish.mehta@pb.com.

b. Marketing Department - Vashi and

c. Chief Controlling Revenue Authority (CCRA)

3. Meter readings and machine balance shall be recorded.

4. Necessary entries shall be made in the statutory registers.

5. The machine shall be repaired only by the authorised vendor.

 

The repaired machine should normally be returned within 15 days. Any delay beyond this period shall be investigated by the Branch Manager and reported to Marketing Department / Head Office.

 

The machine shall not be shifted from its approved location without prior approval of the CCRA.

 

Business Continuity

In the event of power failure, internet disruption, portal outage or machine breakdown, no manual or alternative franking shall be undertaken using the machine. Customers shall be informed appropriately and guided to the nearest authorised e-Franking centre.

 

Branches shall resume operations only after restoration of the system and verification of machine functionality.

12. Accounting Procedure 

The accounting entries relating to loading of funds, service charges and inter-branch transactions shall be passed strictly in accordance with the accounting procedure prescribed by the Accounts Department.

 

(a) Loading of Funds in e-Franking Machine

At the time of loading funds, the amount transferred to the Government shall be net of the commission receivable by the Bank, as permitted by the Government of Maharashtra.

 

Illustration

Amount to be loaded:

 

₹ 5, 00,000/-

 

Government Commission (0.50%):

 

₹ 2,500/-

 

Net amount transferred through GRAS:

 

₹ 4, 97,500/-

 

 

The accounting entry shall be:

 

Debit: GL – EFRANKIN A/c

₹ 5, 00,000/-

Credit: Head Office (Vashi Accounts Department)

₹ 4, 97,500/-

Credit: PL/10084 (Commission on e-Franking) A/c

₹ 2,500/-

 

The percentage of commission shall be as prescribed by the Government from time to time.

 

(b) Service Charges

The Bank shall recover service charges for e-Franking as approved by the competent authority.

Presently, the service charge is ₹ 30/- per document (inclusive of GST).

The accounting treatment shall be:

> Debit : Customer Account / Cash

₹ 30.00

> Credit : PL/10079 (Franking Charges Income)

₹ 24.60

> Credit : CGST Payable

₹ 2.70

> Credit : SGST Payable

₹ 2.70

 

The applicable GST rate shall be as notified by the Government from time to time.

 

(c) Inter-Branch e-Franking

Where documents received from other branches are e-Franked by an authorised branch, accounting entries shall be passed as prescribed by the Accounts Department.

 

Branches shall ensure that proper originating entries are passed; documentary records are preserved inter-branch entries are reconciled promptly.

 

13. Documents Not Permitted for e-Franking

The following categories of documents shall not be e-Franked unless specifically permitted by the Chief Controlling Revenue Authority:

 

Insurance documents, Share Transfer Deeds, Foreign Bills, Hundis, Broker Notes, Bills of Exchange, Bills of Lading, Debentures, Insurance Policies, Promissory Notes, Proxies, documents requiring Judicial or Adhesive Stamps. Documents which are already executed (Sign by the parties)

 

Any other document prohibited under the Maharashtra Stamp Act or CCRA guidelines.

 

Where there is any doubt regarding admissibility, the matter shall be referred to the Chief Controlling Revenue Authority through Marketing Department before undertaking e-Franking.

 

14. Safe Custody and Security of the Machine

The e-Franking Machine is a Government-authorised instrument and shall be safeguarded with utmost care.

 

Accordingly the machine shall remain under the custody of authorised officials only, during business hours of the branch it shall not be left unattended; after business hours, it shall be kept under lock inside the strong unit; all accessories, seals, cartridges and records shall also be kept securely.

 

The Branch In-charge / Manager shall always ensure proper physical security of the machine.

 

15. Password, Access Control and Cyber Security

Where operation of the e-Franking system requires login credentials or portal access, the following controls shall be observed:

User IDs shall be allotted only to authorised officials.

Passwords shall remain confidential and shall not be shared.

Passwords shall be changed periodically in accordance with the Bank's Information Security Policy.

Login credentials shall not be written on or near the machine.

 

Any suspected compromise of credentials shall immediately be reported to Marketing Department, CISO and Head Office.

Branches shall also comply with the Bank's Cyber Security and Information Security Policies.

 

16. Insurance

Insurance of the e-Franking Machine, stamp duty balance loaded therein and associated risks, shall be arranged through the Bank's Insurance Department before commencement of operations.

 

Branches shall ensure that insurance coverage remains valid throughout the period of operation and immediately report any lapse to Compliance Department at Head Office.

 

17. Audit, Inspection and Compliance

Operations relating to e-Franking shall be subject to Internal Audit; Inspection by Head Office - Compliance Review and Inspection by the Chief Controlling Revenue Authority or any Government authority. Branches shall produce all records, registers and documents whenever called upon to do so.

Any irregularity observed during audit or inspection shall be rectified immediately and compliance reported to Marketing Department within the stipulated time for compliance.

 

18. Responsibilities of the Branch Manager

The Branch Manager shall be responsible for ensuring compliance with the Maharashtra Stamp Act and CCRA guidelines; safe custody of the machine; validity of licence and timely renewal; proper maintenance of statutory registers; daily reconciliation; monthly reporting; correctness of accounting entries; compliance with Maker–Checker controls; reporting of incidents; preservation of records; training of staff operating the machine.

 

Failure to comply with these instructions may invite regulatory action against the Bank and disciplinary action against the officials concerned.

 

19. Preservation of Records

The following records shall be preserved for the period prescribed under the applicable statutory provisions and the Bank's Record Retention Policy:

 

Customer Applications, Receipts, Sale Registers, Machine Registers, Record Books, Monthly Returns, Daily Reconciliation Statements, Loading and Reloading Records, Complaint Register, Incident Reports, Repair Records, Licence Renewal Documents, Government Receipts (GRN).

Branches shall ensure that these records are readily available for inspection.

 

20. Miscellaneous Instructions

Branches shall ensure that adequate stock of ink cartridges and consumables is always available; staff operating the machine receive adequate training from the authorised vendor; no unauthorised person operates the machine; blank or trial impressions are never taken on original documents; every impression is checked immediately after printing; the machine is used only while a valid authorisation is in force; no alteration is made to the machine without prior approval of the authorised vendor and CCRA; the machine is neither sold, transferred nor disposed of without the approval of the competent authority; any change in location of the machine is immediately reported to Marketing Department / Head Office and the CCRA; bona fide members of the public are not denied e-Franking facilities without valid reasons recorded in writing.

 

21. Interpretation

In the event of any ambiguity or conflict between this circular and any instructions issued subsequently by the Chief Controlling Revenue Authority (CCRA), Government of Maharashtra or any other competent authority, the latest statutory instructions shall prevail.

 

22. Effective Date and Implementation

These guidelines shall come into force with immediate effect.

All authorised branches shall commence e-Stamping/e-Franking services immediately and submit a confirmation of operational compliance to the Marketing Department within  7 (seven) working days from the date of issuance of this circular.

All Branch Managers and Heads of Departments shall bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned staff members and ensure strict compliance with these guidelines. All eligible loan documents and other Bank documents requiring stamping shall be processed through the Bank's e-Franking service wherever applicable.

Branches shall encourage customers to avail themselves of the Bank's e-Franking facility. Where urgent stamping is required, customers may be guided to approach the nearest authorised e-Franking branch directly.

The e-Franking facility shall also be effectively utilised as a cross-selling initiative to enhance customer service and generate fee-based income for the Bank.

 

 

 

 

Chief Executive Officer
