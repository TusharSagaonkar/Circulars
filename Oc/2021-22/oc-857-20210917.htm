Circular OC No. 857 dated 17th September 2021

Board of Management Constitution, Role and Functions Process of Transmission of Proposals and Submissions

 

I.   Introduction

The matter of setting up a Board of Management (BoM) in each UCB was under contemplation of RBI for some time and draft guidelines in respect thereof were in public domain for discussion and suggestions. Upon receiving opinions and suggestions, RBI, through its circular dated RBI/2019-20/128 DoR (PCB).BPD.Cir.No.8/12.05.002/2019-20 dated 31st December 2019 titled “Constitution of Board of Management (BoM) in Primary (Urban) Co-operative Banks (UCBs)” issued instructions and guidelines in this behalf.

II.  Design and broad framework.

1.   BoM is designed to comprise of persons with special knowledge and practical experience in banking to facilitate professional management and focused attention to the banking related activities of the UCBs. Members are to be chosen based upon qualification, expertise, track record, integrity and other ‘fit and proper’ criteria. BoM is to consist of persons having special knowledge or practical experience in respect of one or more of the following matters, namely,

i.         Accountancy

ii.       Agriculture and rural economy

iii.      Banking

iv.      Co-operation

v.       Economics

vi.      Finance

vii.    Law

viii.  Small scale industry

ix.      Information Technology

x.       Any other subject, in opinion of the Reserve Bank, is useful to the UCB.

2.   BoM (excluding CEO) is to have a minimum of five members. The maximum number of members in BoM is not to exceed twelve. The CEO (MD in our Bank) is a non-voting member.

3.   Members of BoM can be drawn from members of the Board of Directors provided they meet the criteria specified. However, not more than 50 per cent of the BoM members can be from BoD. Under all circumstances, BoM is to have at least two members from outside the BoD.

4.   Chairman of the BoM is to be either elected by the members of BoM from amongst themselves or appointed by the BoD. Under no circumstances is the Chairman of the BoD to be appointed as Chairman of the BoM.

5.   Every UCB is required to maintain proper record of the minutes of the meeting and the same are to be put up to BoD.

6.   The quorum for the meeting is two-third of the total members of BOM.

7.   Members of BoM are to be paid allowance / sitting fees for their services as provided for in the bye-laws of UCB or with the approval of BoD.

8.   The tenure of BoM is co-terminus with the tenure of BoD.

 

III.  Enabling provisions in our Bye Laws

Enabling provisions in Bye-Laws of Bank for setting up of BoM were made by passing appropriate resolutions in the 57th Annual General Meeting of Bank held on 5th December, 2020. The same were duly approved by the Central Registrar of Cooperative Societies. Bank has accordingly set up BoM which has started functioning with effect from 22nd June 2021.

V.   Functions of Board of Management (BOM)

The BoM is to exercise oversight over the banking related functions of Bank. It is to assist BoD in formulation of policy and any other related matters specifically delegated to it by the BoD for proper functioning of bank. The functions of BoM include the following.

1.   Rendering expert advice on all proposals being put up to BoD or any Committee of the BoD for sanction of loans.

2.   Recommending action for recovery of NPAs, One Time Settlement or Compromise Settlement and assisting the BoD in monitoring the same.

3.   Overseeing management of funds and borrowings.

4.   Recommending proposals for investment of bank’s funds as per policy approved by BoD.

5.   Oversight on internal controls and systems and risk management.

6.   Exercising oversight on implementation of computerisation, technology adoption and other incidental issues.

7.   Overseeing internal audit and inspection functions including compliance.

8.   Oversight on complaint redressal system.

9.   Assisting BoD in formulation of policies related to banking functions, such as loan policy, investment policy, recovery policy, ALM and Risk management, and further ensuing those policies are in tune with RBI guidelines.

10.           Any other responsibility as may be delegated to it by BoD.

Note :- In the event of BoD having different opinions on the recommendations of BoM, they are to be recorded in writing, with reasons.

VI.   Functions of BoD vis-à-vis those of BoM

1.   The BoD is responsible for the general direction and control of Bank.

2.   It continues to look after all the administrative functions as spelt out in the Co-operative Societies Act applicable to our Bank.

3.   BoD continues to be the apex policy-setting body and constitutes various committees, including BoM, to assist BoD in carrying out its responsibilities. It continues to delegate powers to the various committees as considered appropriate. BoD is to ensure that there are no conflicts in powers delegated to the Committees of the BoD and BoM.

 

VII. Functions of CEO (MD in our Bank) vis-à-vis BoD and BoM

1.   The CEO is under the general superintendence, direction and control of BoD and exercises such powers and discharge such functions as may be delegated by the BoD.

2.   He/she is an ex-officio member of BoD and also that of BoM.

 

VIII.  RBI’s powers over BoM and CEO

1.   RBI has powers to remove any member of BoM and/ or the CEO if the person is found to be not meeting the criteria prescribed by RBI or acting in a manner detrimental to the interests of the bank or its depositors or both.

2.   The BoD is required to seek concurrence from RBI before removing any member of BoM / accepting the resignation tendered by any member of BoM/CEO.

3.   RBI has powers to supersede the BoM if functioning of BoM is found unsatisfactory.

4.   After removal of the member of BoM or CEO or supersession of BoM, BoD is to appoint a new member or CEO or constitute a new BoM as the case may be within a period of three months. As an interim arrangement, BoD may carry out the functions of BoM.

 

IX.  Process of routing proposals, policies, and, in general, matters which are to be submitted to and/or approved by BoD and its sub-committees

1.   It is hereby instructed that, henceforth, all the departments and offices shall submit, proposals, policies, and, in general, submissions on matters and subjects to be seen and/or approved by BoD and its Credit sub-committees, to Board of Management.

2.   Proposals or submissions to be received by BoM at least seven days in advance of meeting of the BoD/committee of the BoD.

BoM examines the proposals, policies, notes and submissions on various matters and subjects and this requires time. In view of the requirement of three days’ exclusive time for BoD or its sub-committees for scrutinising proposals, policies,

notes and submissions, it will be necessary for all the offices and departments preparing the proposals or submissions to send them to the BoM at least seven days in advance so that BoM may get three clear days to vet the proposals or submissions, give their inputs or opinions and transmit them to the BoD.

3.   Material part of the opinion, inputs or views of the BoM shall be recorded in the minute book of BoM as well as on the proposals or submissions under signature of chairman of the BoM and MD. This will be taken into consideration by BoD or its sub-committee while taking decision in the matter.

 

X.   It is the responsibility of the branches/departments concerned to ensure that the process for placing matters before BoD or its sub-committees is strictly followed.

 

XI. Efficacy and efficiency of the system of BoM hinges upon the quality of proposals or submissions made by offices or departments and their timely submission, i.e. within the timeframe stipulated above, to the BoM. Therefore, all the concerned officials are hereby instructed to be utmost meticulous and prompt in this behalf.
