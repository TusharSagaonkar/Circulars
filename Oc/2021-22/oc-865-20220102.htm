Circular OC No. 865 dated 24th January 2022

1.   Public Financial Management System (PFMS)

2.   Enabling auto-validation by Govt/Semi-Govt agencies of customers’ accounts with us for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) and other matters

3.   Onboarding by Bank

____________________________________________________________________________________

 

I.  Background

1.   PFMS

Public Financial Management System (PFMS) is a real time online integrated financial management system of Office of Controller General of Accounts, Ministry of Finance. It has established itself as a safe, secure, efficient and robust financial network of payment, receipt and accounting system of Government of India (GoI). It also provides a Management Information System for the Government of India, its Ministries/Departments and the sub-National Governments.

2.   Better control over DBT passed on by Govt. treasuries/programme Implementation agencies

PFMS has enabled successful delivery of payment services from Government Treasuries and Program Implementing Agencies (PIA) into beneficiaries’ accounts with the capability that the executive, legislature, internal and external auditors, and the citizen can access all financial transactions pertaining to government expenditure. PFMS tracks the fund disbursement from Government of India under all Central Sector and Centrally Sponsored Schemes and reports ultimate utilization under these schemes at different levels of implementation. Better control over Direct Benefits Transfer (DBT) is ensured through PFMS.

3.   Catering to several schemes of Govt.

PFMS caters to over 9048 Schemes of the Central, State & UT Governments for fund utilization and tracking as well as payments and MIS for schemes identified for DBTs. The system has expedited direct payments from paying units to citizens, government employees, contractors and other entities receiving payments, and facilitated paperless transactions in a secure environment, reducing overall cost and promoting green banking.

4.   Responsibility to report financial transactions under Foreign Contribution Regulation Act (FCRA)

As per the directive of GoI, financial transactions such as receipt or utilization of any Foreign Contribution by any person coming under FCRA are required to be reported by banks within 48 hours, whether or not such person is registered or granted prior permission under the FCRA, 2010. Failure to submit the data attracts penal action against authorities of Bank.

5.   Automatic and direct receipt of IT refund into account of Assessee

Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) has put processing of income tax refunds on digital platform. The prerequisite to this process is to get the account number, name of the account holder and PAN from banks for validation at their end before initiating electronic refunds. The process has been set up in association with NPCI.

II. Need for Bank to onboard the platform

1.    PFMS is a GoI initiative and banks were directed to join the platform by developing the necessary IT interface.

2.    A large number of banks had already joined PFMS. The banks joining PFMS have an edge over other banks in garnering those deposit accounts which currently are, or are in future likely to be, beneficiaries of DBT. The entities which are subject to monitoring of financial transactions by Govt. also prefer to maintain accounts with banks which are on the PFMS platform.

3.    Considering the factors as above, Bank has recently onboarded the PFMS platform enabled by appropriate IT developments.

III. Under PFMS, files are processed centrally in Bank and do not require intervention by branches

1.    Clearing department has been entrusted with the responsibility of processing the files received under PFMS and monitoring the MIS reports posted on the portal.

2.    There is no technical or manual intervention involved at branch level.

3.    However, branches must ensure that the data entry is done correctly to obviate the possibility of rejection of files.

IV. Need for branches to propagate the message of having onboarded PFMS

1.    As mentioned in Para II.2. above, there are clear advantages of PFMS. With our Bank having onboarded the platform, many individuals, firms and Non-profit organisations (NGOs etc.) in need of a PFMS-enabled banking service can now be persuaded to open their accounts with us.

2.    Therefore, we instruct branches not only to positively handle the inquiries regarding our joining PFMS, but also market this capability to add to our accounts and mobilise deposits.

V.    Please bring the contents of the instant circular to the notice of the staff at branch and ensure effective propagation of the information and message.
