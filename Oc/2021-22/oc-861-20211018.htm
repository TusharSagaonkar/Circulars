Circular OC No. 861 dated 18th October 2021

Positive Pay System Under CTS

 

I.  Introduction

1.   In terms of the Circular No. RBI/2020-21/41 DPSS.CO.RPPD NO. 309/ 04.07.005/2020-21 dated 25th September 2020, Positive Pay System (PPS) under Cheque Truncation System (CTS) was opened for adoption by Banks, including urban cooperative bank such as ours.

2.   PPS enables banks to submit the cheques-issuance data collected from their customers for validation during the clearing process. This prevents frauds being perpetrated through forged/fictitious cheques due to record of prior alert from customers in respect of cheques issued by them.

3.   National Payment Corporation of India (NPCI) was advised to develop the relative facility and make it available to participant banks. NPCI has since developed the same and made it available for on boarding by banks. Banks in turn are required to enable it for all the account holders issuing cheques for amounts of Rs.50,000/- and above. While availing of this facility for cheques issued with amounts ranging between Rs. 50,000/- and above up to less than Rs. 5,00,000/- is at the option and discretion of the account holder, the same has been made mandatory by Bank for cheques issued by our customers for amounts of Rs. 5,00,000/- and above, in deference to the guidance in this behalf by RBI.

4.   There is a need to create adequate awareness among customers on features of Positive Pay System through various mechanisms such as SMS alerts, display in branches, display across/through ATMs and their cubicles, post on Bank’s website, and through internet banking.

5.   Our Bank has developed utilities to match the input/output files under CTS mechanism to enable processing of inward/ outward instructions received from customers across banks under PPS.

6.   PPS is being launched in our Bank with immediate effect.

 

II.Positive Pay System (PPS)

1.  Concept

        i)    The concept of PPS involves a process of confirming in advance the key details of cheques issued. In our Bank, three channels are made available to customers for providing the confirmation, namely

a)   Letter to be submitted across the counter in Bank’s (ANY) branch,

b)  Through mobile phone using a special App developed for the purpose, and,

c)   Internet banking.

       ii)    The format of the confirmation in any of the channels contains certain minimum details of the cheque issued like date, cheque no., name of the beneficiary / payee and amount. An additional field, to be filled by the customer at his/her/their option, towards “Purpose of issue of cheque” is also provided These details are cross checked by CTS with the presented cheque. Any discrepancy is flagged by CTS through a standardized ID to the drawee bank as well as presenting bank, and both the banks are expected to take redressal measures.

     iii)    The PPS platform is made available to member banks and their customers (Corporate, Retail and Individual) for notifying or uploading the details of the cheques issued. The system is common for all the GRIDs.

     iv)    Banks are required to validate the customer before accepting the data under PPS in order to avoid data-entry errors. Only minimum data like amount and payee’s name are to be obtained from customer. Remaining data is to be auto-generated from CBS. CPPS System validates uniqueness of the uploaded record, using the combination of MICR code and cheque no., account no., SAN (Unique Short Account Number) and TRAN Code (Transaction Code). It is to be noted that a duplicate record uploaded by any bank will be rejected by PPS. Hence, sufficient control is built and exercised to ensure data sanctity and uniqueness.

       v)    Although printing of SAN on instruments in MICR band is currently not mandatory, our bank have printed SAN on our instruments. The relative field (for SAN) in the file prepared for upload to DEM (DATA EXCHANGE MODULE) will be populated with default codes “000000” or “999999” where SAN is not printed (on cheques printed by some other banks).

 

2.   Process

        i)    After cheques are issued, customers confirm the issuance to their banks with the minimum basic details.

       ii)    On collecting the details, member banks prepare and push the details in NPCI-defined format to clearing house through their DEM (DATA EXCHANGE MODULE).

     iii)    Necessary authentication and data validation process is to be followed by banks before pushing data to NPCI.

     iv)    The uploaded file is validated for syntax and it is checked whether all the mandatory fields are present. If mandatory fields are blank, the file is rejected.

       v)    After validation, the data is updated in the PPS database and response shared with bank for the uploaded file.

     vi)    Thereafter, cheques presented by banks in presentation session are validated against PPS data by NPCI.

   vii)    An identifier is provided as part of inward file (PXF file) on the matching status of data of instruments with the PPS data.

  viii)    The data of instruments which are getting validated with PPS is also provided as report for additional reconciliation or matching if any required by drawee banks.

     ix)    Information appropriately taken out from the same report is also to be shared by the presenting banks for sharing it with the customers lodging the cheques to enable them to take preventive action if any is required.

       x)    The flags which are provided as part of the inward file to drawee banks are as under.

a.   “P” – instruments validated with PPS. No mismatch in cheque number, Drawee bank MICR and Amount.

b.   “D” – Duplicate instrument already presented. NO mismatch in cheque number, Drawee bank MICR and amount.

c.    “Y” – amount mismatch. Cheque number and Drawee bank MICR have matched.

d.   “Z” – Duplicate instrument already presented. Amount mismatch. Cheque number and Drawee bank MICR have matched.

e.   “N” – Instrument not part of PPS data.

     xi)    Based on the flag, banks can validate the inward received. In case of need, branches can obtain/get confirmations from customers, on the issuance of the instruments, before processing.

 

3.  Advantages of PPS

        i)    Receiving bank has advantage of additional information enabling cross verification before paying the instruments.

       ii)    Presenting bank gets the details of the instruments it is presenting, thereby mitigating the risk of fraud before the payment is released.

 

4.  RBI to entertain disputes under Dispute Resolution Mechanism under the Payment and Settlement Systems only if PPS is in place.

Disputes in respect of cheques of amounts of Rs. 5,00,000/- and above will be entertained by RBI under the Dispute Resolution System only if the system of confirmation under Positive Pay System is implemented and adhered to.

III.  Implementation in our Bank

1.Cheques issued by our customers on accounts with us.

        i)    We have enabled notification of cheques issued by our customers under PPS through following three modes.

a)   Written confirmation submitted at any of our branches (not necessarily only as Base branch but any branch of our Bank)

b)  Separate Mobile App named “Abhyudaya Bank – Cpps” is launched. It is available on Play Store (Android)/AppStore (IOS)

c)   Through Internet Banking

       ii)    a) Mandatory information which a customer is required to provide through the

three modes mentioned above is as under.

·           Cheque no.

·           Amount

·           Name of payee

b)  In addition, information regarding “purpose” may be provided optionally by customer. Under Mobile App and Internet Banking, it may be put in Remark Column.

     iii)    Through confirmation form submitted by customer in writing at any of our branches

The confirmation form, specimen of which is furnished as Annexure-I, in respect of issuance of cheque(s) (there may be more than one cheque in any one instance) submitted at branch should be scrutinized and it should be ensured as under.

a)   The cheque form belongs to the cheque book issued to the customer, particulars of which have been registered in OMNI. (For this to happen, Branches need to ensure that “Cheque book” issue details of all the customers are updated in OMNI).

b)  Data of stale cheques (3 months older on the date of submission of application) should not be accepted.

c)   Cheque issued as on a future date (post-dated cheque) may be accepted.

d)  All the authorized signatories/signatory as per the operational instructions in the account must sign on the confirmation form. Only the original confirmation form will be entertained by branches. Scanned image, fax, photocopy, print of soft copy, email request etc. must not be accepted by branches.

e)   Each confirmation form must be acknowledged with full signature by the staff-member manning the relative desk under the Name and Staff Number stamp. Date and time of receipt must be mentioned therein. Similar signature under the rubber stamp of Name and Staff Number with time and date must be recorded on the confirmation form.

f)    The cut off time for effective data entry for any particular working day shall be 6:00 pm. All confirmation letters received on any day later than 6:00 pm shall be branded with the stamp “Received late for the day of tender. Accepted for data entry and upload on the next working day”. Acknowledgement slip should also be likewise branded in such cases and authenticated under the common signature and rubber stamp of name and Date of the staff member.

g)   All these particulars (including “Received late for the day of tender. Accepted for data entry and upload on the next working day” if that be the case) must be recorded under authentication immediately in the “Confirmation Forms received under Positive Payment System” format of which enclosed as Annexure-II.

h)  Data entry should be accurate. There is no scope for modification and deletion of an entry once it is submitted in Positive Pay Data entry portal.

i)    Menus for data entry from the confirmation/application form received from customers at branches are shown through the screen shots given in Annexure-III.

 

     iv)    Through Abhyudaya Positive Pay App named “Abhyudaya Bank – Cpps” under mobile banking

a)   The application is developed and launched, with necessary filters and validations built therein, by IT department so that customers can give confirmation of cheques issued.

b)  The cut-off time for generation of the file of confirmed cheques through the App for uploading on DEM (DATA EXCHANGE MODULE) platform is 6:00 pm. In order that branches may give guidance to customers regarding use of this App, screen shots of registration in the said App are given in Annexure-IV.

 

       v)    Through Internet Banking

a)   Relative screenshots of inputting by and responses to customers giving confirmation of cheques issued are provided in Annexure-V. This information is necessary for staff at branch-level to enable them to guide customers in case of need.

b)  IT department has provided inbuilt filters and validations in the programme.

c)   The cut-off time for generation of the file of confirmed cheques through the Internet Banking module for same-day uploading on DEM (DATA EXCHANGE MODULE) platform is 6:00 pm.

d)  It is possible under Internet Banking for customers to upload files containing confirmation in respect of multiple cheques in one single instance.

     vi)  Pay orders and drafts issued by Bank

a)   Pay orders issued by our Bank will be compiled and uploaded on PPS platform by the respective Branches. Necessary tool for uploading will be provided by our I.T. Dept.

b)  Cheques/drafts drawn on HDFC/ICICI Bank issued by our branches will be communicated to respective Banks through advice for clearance in PPS by Centralised Clearing Department.

 

2.   When a cheque presented by other banks and drawn on us with confirmation recorded, is received in clearing

        i)    When a cheque is received in clearing for payment matches with the confirmation registered with Bank, the same will get paid if otherwise in order, without any need for reference to the base branch or the drawer-customer.

       ii)    When the PPS system throws up a mismatch or anomaly as indicated in categories “D”, “Y”, “Z” and “N” in Para II.2.x (b, c, d and e), clearing department will raise a query with the branch of the drawer and branch will have to obtain confirmation from the drawer in writing. Branch of the drawer will render assistance to the clearing department in contacting the drawer-customer and obtaining justification and confirmation for payment or otherwise.

     iii)    In the absence of justification and confirmation in writing for payment from the drawer-customer, the mismatching or anomalous cheque will be returned.

3.Cheques lodged by our customers for collection/realisation drawn on other banks

        i)    We get indication through PPS whether the cheque shows mismatch and is likely to be returned without getting honored.

       ii)    We should normally alert our customer regarding likely adverse fate as above unless there are circumstances which warrant circumspection and restraint.

     iii)    If funds are lent or parted against such instruments tendered for realisation, steps should be taken to recover the amount or remove the cheque from cover for DP of an advance account.

 

IV.      Instructions for IT department

1.  A system of auto-generation of messages to drawer-customers should be put in place in respect of

        i)    Registration of their confirmations,

       ii)    Receiving in clearing all the cheques of amounts of Rs. 50,000/- and above, with or without confirmation.

     iii)    If cheques (of Rs. 50,000/- and above) are received with confirmation but are duplicate, or show mismatch with the data under confirmation, the alert message should pick up the description “duplicate” or the description of particular type of mismatch.

 

2.Pay orders and drafts issued by Bank

Necessary tool for uploading by branches the information regarding Pay orders issued by them will be provided by I.T. Department.

 

3.Notice of launch: -

    i)    Posting information regarding PPS, and its immediate launch as on today, on Bank’s website.

   ii)    Sending of messages to registered mobile numbers of customers regarding immediate launch of PPS as on today.

 

V.   Instructions for Marketing department

Marketing department will arrange for printing, display and distribution of posters/ handbills/publicity material in respect of launch of PPS and its salient features.

 

VI.  Instructions for Branches

        i)    NOTICE as per Annexure-VI shall be immediately displayed in the banking hall.

       ii)    PPS should be explained to the staff members so that customers can be guided appropriately.

     iii)    The customers who typically issue cheques of large meetings should be informed about the salient features of the PPS and requirements as under should be communicated to them.

a)   Necessity to mandatorily confirm to Bank about issuance of cheque for Rs. 5,00,000/-and above through one of the three available modes mentioned above.

b)  Advisability to give confirmation in respect of cheques issued by them for amounts of Rs. 50,000/- and above and less than Rs. 5,00,000/-.

c)   Need to furnish accurate and complete data through any of the three modes mentioned hereinabove.

d)  Need to pay prompt attention to and act upon the text messages (SMS) from Bank in respect of mismatch etc. In appropriate cases, furnishing written instructions to Bank, as to whether to make payment in spite of the mismatch or otherwise.

 

VII.     In view of the advantages of the Positive Pay System to customers as also Bank, and mandatory instructions from RBI to implement the same, Clearing department, IT department and branches are instructed to implement the same meticulously. Branches are further instructed to publicize and promote the same actively.

 

VIII.   Please bring instructions contained herein to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
