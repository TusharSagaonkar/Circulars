Circular OC No. 868 dated 25th January 2022

1.   Correction in Existing Category-wise Classification of Accounts & Customer Masters

2.   Correct Category-wise Classification of New Accounts & Creation of New Customer Masters

 

 

I.   In the recently concluded RBI inspection of our Bank [XXXVIII RBI Inspection for period from 1st April 2020 to 31st March 2021], the Inspecting officials pointed out that “Customer’s Occupation / Nature of Business” is not updated in the bank’s CBS (OMNI). A scrutiny of our existing Customer Master & Account Master was therefore made which revealed the following.

 

1.                               Scrutiny of Accounts in Individual category.

i.       During the scrutiny of Customer masters of several accounts such as those of Firms, HUFs, Trusts, Societies, Clubs, Associations, Mandal etc. were observed to have been opened in Individual Category. When Customer Masters of such entities are opened in “Individual” category, there are problems in capturing the Customer Master details such as business done or nature of activity undertaken by them.

ii.     “Sole Proprietorship Firms” are business units although they are owned by Individuals. Therefore, it is appropriate to open the Customer masters of such accounts in the “Others” category. Opening Customer Master for accounts of “Sole Proprietorship Firms” in “Individual” category also leads to non-capture of important business details in Customer Master.

 

2.                               Scrutiny of Customer Master Opened in “Others” category.

Some of the customers opened in “Others” category, who are meant to be engaged in business etc. are shown as “House wife”, “Salaried employee”, “Not categorized” etc. Even otherwise, many important fields pertaining to Customer Masters in “Others” category such as “Org. Type”, “Nature of Business / occupation / line of activity”, “Place of Establishment”, “Date of Establishment”, “Registration Number”, “Annual turnover / Income of customers” are left blank in CBS [ OMNI ] System.

 

II.  The report on the matter as above was presented before the meeting of “KYC/AML/FATCA/CRS Executives Committee” with recommendations for corrective action. Upon receiving approval therefor, it is being instructed as under.

1.     Customer Master (CIF) and Account Master (AOF) form should be duly filled in all respect and no column should be kept blank.

2.     To obtain Customer Information Form (CIF) for opening of Fixed Deposit Accounts.

3.     No Customer Master is to be created at Head Office, Share Dept. and Recovery Dept. level.

4.     Current Accounts of Sole Proprietary concerns :

i.      All Customer Masters for Current Accounts of Sole Proprietary concerns must be created/opened in ‘Customer Category & Type’ as “Others” instead of “Individual”.

ii.     Further, if proprietor of the firm also holds a Saving Account, a new Customer Master should be created while opening of new Current Account in the name of Proprietary concerns.

iii.   However, existing Customer number of such Saving account holders should be entered in the Group Customer Number of the newly created Customer Master for opening of the Current Account of the “Sole Proprietorship” concern.

iv.   At the time of opening of Current Account of “Sole Proprietary” concerns of existing Individual Customer maintaining a Saving Account, the existing Customer master should be accordingly updated with respect to the Profile/Profession/Line (Nature) of Business activity in “Business type” (in case of customer master opened as “Others”) and in “Employer Code” (in case of customer master opened as “Individuals”).

 

5.     There will be no change in Customer Masters created for opening Current Accounts specifically in Individual name. However, the nature of business has to be fed in Employer Code alongwith selecting / feeding appropriate Profession Type (such as small business, self employed, Business Women, Architect, Consultants etc) in Business Details Screen.

 

6.   Customer Master & Account of a women before and after Marriage:

If a women having her account in the Maiden name, approaches branch for change in the name in the account, there will be following two scenarios :

i.      The women may agree to have a new account. In such a case, a new account with relative new Customer and Account Master may be created.

ii.     The women may insist upon continuing with the previous account but in the post marital name. In such a case, only a new Customer Master should be created and updated in the account Master. The old Customer Master with maiden name should be made inoperative. However, the old Customer number in maiden name should be entered in Group Customer Number field in basic details screen.

 

7.     The customer master of all existing as well as new non-individual customers such as Trust, Housing Society, Association, Club, Mandal, HUF, etc. (irrespective of type of account i.e. whether Saving or Current Account) should be categorised as “Others” in “Customer Category & Type” in OMNI.

 

8.     Data Purification Report :

                i.   IT Dept. has developed a utility to extract the data of Customers and accounts, which have been opened under wrong “Customer Category & Type” i.e. as “Individual” instead of “Others” based on analysis of 4th Character of PAN and

provides output of mismatches between Customer Type and Account Type, Membership Type ( Regular / Nominal ) between Customer Master and Account Master. The report is developed for data purification of Customer Masters & Account Master opened for opening accounts of Association of Person, Body of individuals (BOI), Company, Firm / LLP (Limited Liability Partnership), Government Agency, HUF (Hindu undivided family), Artificial Juridical Person , Local Authority and Trust. Branches may access the said utility through the following path :

OMNIEnterprise Reporting Services

Home > Reports for Branch > Reports > Other Supporting Reports > Data Purification >R009011_Customer_Ind_To_Oth

 

After ascertaining the discrepant accounts and their customer masters by accessing the utility as above, branches may go through the “Instructions to be followed, while modifying Customer from Individual (Type-1) to Non-Individual (Type-2)” which are given through screen shots in the Annexure II hereto.

 

                     ii.   In view of the contents of the sub-Para 5(i) above, branches are now advised to

a.     verify all the account opening forms and KYC updation forms (in Binders or otherwise) pertaining to existing/legacy accounts, but more particularly those in respect of whom mismatches have been identified by the Utility,

b.    ensure that all the fields in the Omni system w.r.t. correct customer type and Account Type ( as per lookup available in OMNI system ) are correctly filled in [illustrative appropriate pairing of Customer Type & Account Type is given in the Annexure II hereto ], and,

c.     the data-base is updated.

 

9.     Further, branches should ensure that when customer master is opened of various accounts under one common PAN, Customer type of all accounts under common PAN should be same.

To illustrate an example – main Trust having name “ MAHATMA EDUCATION SOCIETY “, opens accounts of various Schools / Colleges, such as “Mahatma Education Society Canteen A/C” , “M E S Pillai College Of Arts Com & Sci Designs Inst”, “Mahatma Sports Club”, “Mahatma E.Soc.Pillai Ins.Of Management Studies & Research” etc., where it should have the same customer title as “TRT” and Customer Type as “13 - Non Profit Orgn.,(Trust)” and in no case, it should be other than “13 - Non Profit Orgn.,(Trust)”. 10. While opening new accounts, the Account Opening Form must be closely scrutinised to ensure that all the information including that required for classification under “Individual’ or “Others” is available. If the classification is determined as “Others”, the details of the business or economic activity must be available. All the information must be entered in the Omni system.

 

III.   CDEC Dept. to ensure that all the respective fields of Customer Information Form (CIF) are entered in CBS (OMNI) database at the time of data entry work.

 

IV.  Please bring the instructions contained herein to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned and ensure strict compliance.
