Circular OC No. 850 dated 12th April 2021

Process for Reconciliation of ATM-Failed-Transactions

Accountability & Recovery of Penalty

 

         I. As per RBI’s Circular dated 20th September, 2019, our Bank, like all the other banks, has been required to reverse ATM-failed-transactions proactively within a maximum period of T+5 calendar days, with effect from 15th October, 2019. In case of failure on the part of Bank to do so, compensation @ Rs.100/- per day beyond T+5 calendar days needs to be paid, again proactively, to the customer. NPCI is collecting such penalty from our Bank in case of ATM-failed-transactions. This matter was reviewed in the Audit Committee meetings dated 3rd October 2020 and 2nd February 2021. It has now been decided in the Board meeting dated 30thMarch, 2021 to put in place areas of responsibility and timelines for reconciliation of ATM-failed-transactions. Thereunder, accountability shall be determined for non-reconciliation and non-adjustment of difference by the timeline prescribed. The process is given hereunder and the same will have to be followed by all the concerned for reconciliation of ATM-failed-transactions with effect from 16th April 2021.

 

1) ACQUIRER TRANSACTIONS :

i)    a) The staff working in ATM Recon dept. is required to attend to duties on

holidays. The duties include reconciliation work. ATM Recon department is required to carry out Reconciliation of Acquirer ATM transactions on T+1 calendar day. Thus, if a transaction is done on 22nd April 2021, the same will be required to be reconciled on 23rd April 2021. If a transaction is done on 13th August 2021, the same will be required to be reconciled on 14th August 2021, although 14th August 2021 is a Holiday. Further, if a transaction is done on 14th August 2021, it will have to be reconciled on 15th August 2021 regardless of 15th August 2021 also being a Holiday.

b) There are several issues raised by counter party banks i.e. Issuers (whose transactions are routed through ATM switch) under Dispute Settlement Mechanism of NPCI. They may be Chargeback/Pre-arbitration/ Arbitration/ Good-Faith and Deferred Chargeback requiring Credit Adjustment/Debit Adjustment. ATM Recon dept. handles them by relying on branch-ATM tally.

c) There are also On-Us transactions which are not routed through ATM switch. They are reconciled within our Bank.

(A glossary of terms used in ATM Recon work is given in Annexure hereto).

 

ii)   As part of reconciliation process, ATM Recon dept. is required to cross-check the status of un-reconciled transactions with the soft copy of EJ. If status of any transaction as per EJ is unsuccessful and status in Acquirer file of the NPCI

is successful, then ATM Recon dept. shall send an e-Mail at ID of the Branch-In-Charge and the Custodian concerned, both, latest on the T+2 calendar day by 11.00 a.m. for remitting the amount representing the surplus (due to undispensed/ partially dispensed) cash to ATM Recon dept. on such T+2 calendar day through Transfer Entry (Originate).

 

iii) a) If branch receives the e-Mail on T+1 calendar day and it is a holiday for it, or

it receives the e-Mail on T+2 day (time for which cannot be later than 11:00 am on such day) the branch shall act on the e-Mail mentioned in sub-Para ii) above from ATM Recon department latest on T+2 calendar day, and if T+2 calendar day is also a holiday for that branch (as will it be for branches over the weekends around second and fourth Saturdays of month), it may act on T+3 calendar day. For example, if transaction takes place on 23rd April 2021 and ATM Recon department sends an e-Mail in respect thereof to the branch either on 24th April 2021 (T+1 calendar day) or latest by 11:00 am of 25th April 2021 (T+2 calendar day), T+1 and T+2 calendar days are, both, Holidays for branches. In this case, the branch may act on the e-Mail on 26th April 2021 (T+3 calendar day). However, it must be noted that when the action becomes permissible for the branch on T+3 calendar day due to two intervening holidays, the said action must be carried out by branch before 9:30 am on such T+3 calendar day.

b) If there are three consecutive holidays for a branch, the branch must, in any case, act on the e-Mail at least on the T+3 calendar day (even if a holiday) if not earlier and the timeline for action for the branch shall be 9:30 am on such T+3 calendar day. For example, if a transaction takes place on 13th August 2021, ATM Recon department will send e-Mail in respect thereof either on 14th August 2021 or latest by 11:00 am of 15th August 2021. In this case, 14th, 15th, and 16th August 2021 are holidays. Therefore, the branch must act on the e-Mail by 9:30 am on 16th August 2021, if not earlier.

 

iv) a) In case soft copy of EJ is not available at ATM Recon dept. due to technical

reasons, it shall be required to send an e-Mail at ID of the Branch-In-Charge and the Custodian concerned latest on the T+2 calendar day by 11.00 a.m. Un-reconciled status of the entries in question shall be confirmed by the branch based on the hard copy of JPs at branch level, and copy thereof shall be submitted by the Branch-In-Charge/Custodian on the same day to ATM Recon dept. In case of a branch having holiday on T+1 calendar day, or consecutive two or three holidays after the T day, the branch shall act in the same manner as indicated in sub-Para iii) above.

b) If there is surplus cash (due to non-dispensing/short dispensing) at the branch, amount thereof shall be originated to ATM Recon dept. through IBT with proper details. A separate intimation having transferred the amount shall be sent by e-Mail by the branch to ATM Recon dept.

 

v)   a) In case (as above) there is no remittance of amount/response by the branch

on T+2 calendar day or such other first calendar day worked out as timeline considering holiday(s) for the branch, ATM Recon department may originate

the debit amount of the un-reconciled transaction to the branch through IBT with details thereof on the T+3 calendar day or latest on T+4 calendar day [the latter case arising when there are two consecutive holiday(s) for the branch], and make the corresponding credit adjustment against the debit originated by NPCI through DMS portal.

b) The alternative of ATM Recon. department originating debit to the branch, in case of failure of branch to originate the amount to the department through IBT, is put on hold for the time being in anticipation that branches will fulfill their responsibility promptly without fail.

c) This matter will be reviewed based on experience of the mechanism set out above.

 

2) RECONCILIATION OF ON-US ATM TRANSACTIONS :

The responsibility of reconciliation of On-us ATM transactions i.e. where NFS switch is not involved is that of the ATM Custodian and the Branch-In-Charge concerned. Together, they are required to ensure that pro-active credit in respect of On-us ATM failed transactions is given to the customer’s account within a maximum period of T+5 calendar days; else eligible compensation is required to be paid to the customer as per the above-referred RBI circular.

 

3) OTHER PRECAUTIONS :

i)   a) If any ATM-failed-transaction is due to a technical issue like failure of link,

machine breakdown or EJ Files not getting pulled at Central Server, then ATM Recon dept. is required to inform the branch to make available to the ATM Recon dept. a soft copy of the EJ file from the ATM machine. If a soft copy of the EJ file is not made available by the branch, then the responsibility for reconciliation pertaining to the day/period shall fall on the branch itself, and NFS-related excess amount if any shall be informed by the branch to the ATM Recon dept. so that the timeline of T+5 calendar days for affording the relative credit to the customer’s account is met & penalty is saved.

b) ATM Recon department shall maintain meticulous log of all the technical issues like failure of link, machine breakdown or EJ Files not getting pulled at Central Sever so that shifting of responsibility to branch as per sub-Para a) above is justified.

ii)   a) Contacting IT department & taking Back-Up of EJ files by branch

custodian before allowing an engineer to attend to the ATM machine, and before allowing him to leave site:- When an engineer from the vendor/ maintenance company visits ATM for loading or updating software in ATM machine, access to the ATM for him shall be allowed only after contacting IT Dept. & seeking their permission for the same through e-Mail. In such a case, the custodian concerned shall be required to take backup of EJ files first, and the engineer allowed to upload/update the software only thereafter. The Custodian shall also be required to seek permission from IT Dept, through telephone followed by e-Mail confirmation, before the engineer can leave the site. If this is not done and there is a loss of EJ data, then the custodian concerned shall be held responsible for the failed transaction.

b) Procedure for registering call to vendor/maintenance company of ATM, monitoring downtime and precautions to be taken

·        Branch should not directly call engineer from the vendor/maintenance company of the ATM. He must be called by registering a call with the company through proper channel. Ticket number of the complaint should be obtained and recorded.

·        Downtime log should be carefully maintained and monitored. In any case, a complaint lodged with the vendor/maintenance company of the vendor must be attended to by the latter within four hours. Shri. Anant D. Dhanpawde (Staff no. 1717, mobile no. 9892670735) or, in his absence or non-availability, Shri. Rakesh D. Bhujbal (Staff no.1041, mobile no. 9920983920) must be kept informed about the complaint, the downtime and eventual resolution of the complaint over landline/mobile telephone as also through e-Mail.

·        Custodian should ask the engineer to take back-up in case of faulty ATM. A proper visit report should also be obtained from the engineer and kept on record.

c) Cash in ATM is to be tallied by the custodian daily. If custodian proceeds on leave, he must tally the cash before such proceeding on leave; otherwise, he shall be responsible for shortages and penalties.

 

iii) Data base of custodians of ATM and Change/transfer of custodians

a)   All branches to whom ATMs are linked shall furnish following data to ATM Recon department regarding ATMs and their custodians by 15th April 2021.

“ATM-ID, Name of custodian, Staff no., Mobile no., e-Mail address”

b)  Any subsequent change in the above data base due either to change, transfer or any other reason must be communicated by the Branch-In-Charge concerned to ATM Recon department. If there is failure to furnish such information promptly, and there is occasion inviting penalty due to ATM-failed-transactions, the same shall be recovered from the Branch-In-Charge concerned.

 

4) Date of implementation and Accountability

The procedure as above for reconciliation of ATM-failed-transactions is put in place for strict implementation w.e.f. 16th April 2021. If there is delay in reconciliation of On-Us and NFS-Acquirer ATM-failed-transactions resulting in compensation to be paid by Bank from this date of implementation onwards, amount of the same shall be recovered from the Branch-In-Charge/Custodian at the branch and/or the staff-member(s) concerned at ATM Recon dept., after establishing the facts pertaining to acts of commission or omission on their part in light of the process as above & determining accountability.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members at your Branch/Department and ensure compliance.
