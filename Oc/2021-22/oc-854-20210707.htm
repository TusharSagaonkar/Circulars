Circular OC No. 854 dated 7th July, 2021

Re-introduction of e-KYC authentication

KYC documents to be obtained while opening new accounts of natural persons [individuals]

 

 

I. 1. In terms of the Circular No. OC 826 dated 29th October, 2019, wherever current address of an applicant for opening an account is not mentioned in the Officially Valid Documents (OVDs), instructions have been given to obtain a copy of Registered Leave and License Agreement in the name of the applicant and/or consent-letter from the relative (with whom the applicant is resident), along with self-declaration towards proof of address.

 

2. Subsequently, RBI amended its Master Direction on KYC on 9th January 2020, and made it permissible for applicant to give only a self-declaration in respect of current address (which is not recorded in any of the OVDs, or is different from the address as per the identity information available in the Central Identities Data Repository ) to the Regulated Entity “RE” such as our Bank, and if it (i.e. Bank ) carries out authentication of the Customer’s Aadhaar number using e-KYC authentication facility provided by the Unique Identification Authority of India(UIDAI).

 

3. The permission by RBI as per sub-Para 2 above does away with the method of obtaining a copy of Leave and License agreement or a consent-letter from a relative with whom the applicant is resident. Thus, stage was set for introduction of e-KYC authentication. However, such reintroduction required procurement and setting up of Aadhaar Data Vault, process for which was initiated.

 

4. Now, with the process of implementation of Aadhaar Data Vault having been completed, it has been decided to re-introduce e-KYC authentication in the bank while opening accounts of new customers.

 

5.  Similarly, wherever, e-KYC authentication of the customer has to be performed (apart from at the time of account opening ), his/her consent must be obtained in preprinted Stationery form bearing Code No. 1147 and kept on the record.

 

II.  Operational instructions

1. Branches shall

a. carry out e-KYC authentication using Biometric devices as advised in Circular OC No. 737 dated 21st January 2016,

b. make use of Aadhaar based e-KYC while opening new account/s and also while updating KYC profiles,

c.  take printout of the response document (e-KYC document),

d. preserve the response document along with the relative account opening form or the relative updation documents for record purpose,

e. scan the response document (e-KYC documents) along with other KYC documents and account opening form, and forward the same electronically to CDEC at Vashi,

f.   ensure that photograph of the person and his address as per CIF/AOF form and those as per response received from UIDAI match with each other,

2. Masking of Aadhaar Number :

                          i)  Branches must mask/hide first 8 digits of Aadhaar number on response document received from UIDAI for maintaining confidentiality (e.g. Aadhaar No.   9999)

                         ii)  However, it may be noted that, in respect of accounts opened to receive benefits under DBT, Aadhaar no. should not be hidden/masked.

 

3. If e-KYC authentication is not successful for some technical reason or if an applicant/existing customer is unable to perform biometric authentication owing to injury, illness, infirmity on account of old age etc., and such like reasons, branches may accept any other alternate and valid means of identification. Such alternate and valid means of identification include any other OVDs apart from Aadhaar card. The OVD submitted other than Aadhaar Card must bear current / latest address mentioned on it.

 

4. IT department is hereby instructed to replace the 12 (twelve) digits of the Aadhaar no. with 10 Digit numerical reference number in the customer master.

 

III. Situations of matching or unmatching of current address in Account Opening Form with that shown in OVD submitted while conducting account opening exercise

1. Where proof of address mentioned in Account Opening Form matches exactly with OVD submitted and e-KYC response documents

In such a case, branch may go ahead & open the account. Branch shall send Address Confirmation Letter (Thanks Letter) to the customer. On receipt of acknowledgement, branch shall issue passbook, chequebook, and shall extend all types of e-channel services.

2.Where proof of address mentioned in Account Opening Form differs from that as per OVD submitted

In such a case, branch should carry out e-KYC authentication using Biometric devises, keep the response document on record, and obtain a declaration, as per Annexure-I regarding current residential address of the customer. After obtaining the declaration, branch shall arrange personal visit at the declared address of the applicant before issuing passbook, chequebook, and before extending all types of e-channel services. This visit report must be kept along with the account opening form.

 

Note:- Please note the intention of the instant procedure which is to ensure that a customer is not unnecessarily asked to submit additional proofs in respect of current address when proof of permanent address is already available and successful e-KYC is done and kept on record.

 

IV. Screen shots of menus of e-KYC authentication, Aadhaar Vault and sub-menus of Customer Master with explanatory notes are given in Annexure-II for clearer understanding of operating staff.

 

V. Branches and offices are advised to take suitable note of these instructions and ensure that all the staff members become fully acquainted with the important modifications advised through this circular. A special staff meeting at the branch may be held to bring these changes to the knowledge of the staff members. Branches are further advised to ensure strict compliance with instructions contained herein.
