Circular OC No. 855 dated 30th July 2021

 

Bharat Bill Payment System Providing Facility for Payment of Utility Bills at Branches as Agent of Indusind Bank Limited

 

I. Currently, we extend facility to our customers for payment of utility bills through Abhyudaya Mobile Banking Service under an arrangement entered into with Bill Desk which is an Operating Unit of Bharat Bills Payment System. Such a facility is not at present available to customers at branches across the counter.

 

II.   It is now decided to launch the facility of payment of utility bills to the customers visiting our branches under an arrangement with IndusInd Bank Limited which will be the Operating Unit of Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS). It may be noted that the current arrangement with Bill Pay will continue under Abhyudaya Mobile Banking Service and the new service is being extended to those customers who visit our branch.

 

III.  Information in brief about Bharat Bills Payment System is given in Annexure-I.

 

IV. Implementation

1.   Bharat Bill Payment System (BBPS) will be access through URL “192.168.138.3:8181/cbsbbps/login.do” (User Id and Password will be same as used to login for OMNI)

2.   While there is scope for extension of facility against tender of cash, whether to walk-in visitors or our own customers, we are, for the time being, providing the facility to customers only against cheque for debit to account, and not against cash. The facility against tender of cash may be extended to walk-in visitors or our own customers in future.

3.   There will not be any charges levied on customers making use of the facility. Bank will earn revenue through its share of the charges which Utility Service Providers shall pay to IndusInd Bank Limited.

4.   While there is provision for issue of receipt for successful payment, it should be issued only if insisted upon by the customer, because SMS is generated in respect of a successful payment.

5.   Screen shots of menus for registering transactions in Omni system, with explanatory notes, are given in Annexure-II.

6.   A pilot run of Utility Bills Payment Facility to our customers in branch set up under BBPS (through IndusInd Bank Limited as “Operating Unit”) has already been conducted successfully in five branches.

7.   It will now be launched across the entire network of branches on 5th August 2021.

V.   Operating staff is instructed to go through the Annexure-II hereto (which provides screen shots of menus with explanatory notes) and be in readiness for Bank-wide launch.

 

VI. Marketing department is hereby advised to prepare and supply the necessary publicity material to branches and also provide publicity support.

 

VII.   Day to day reconciliation of transaction under BBPS to be done by our Reconciliation Department (Vashi).

 

VIII.  Please bring contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned and ensure successful implementation and promotion of the facility.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
