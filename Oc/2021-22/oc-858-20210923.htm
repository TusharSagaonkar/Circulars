Circular OC No. 858 dated 23rd September 2021

Availability of Cash in ATMs Penalty of Rs. 10,000/- per Incident for Cash Out for more than 10 Hours

 

I.  Introduction

It has been the concern of RBI, being issuer and manager of currency, that currency should always be available to the public at large. In the current times when there is preference by public to withdraw cash from ATMs rather than doing same across bank-counters, there is added emphasis on stocking up ATMs with cash adequately.

 

II. Recently, RBI reviewed availability of cash through ATMs. Its findings and remedial action, as advised through circular no. RBI/2021-22/84 DCM (RMMT) No.S153/ 11.01.01/2021-22 dated 10thAugust 2021, are as under.

1.   ATM operations are affected by cash outs leading to non-availability of cash and cause avoidable inconvenience to the members of the public.

 

2.   Banks must strengthen their systems/ mechanisms to monitor availability of cash in ATMs and ensure timely replenishment to avoid cash outs.

 

3.   Banks are now required to submit system generated statement on downtime of ATMs due to non-replenishment of cash to the Issue Department of RBI under whose jurisdiction these ATMs are located.

 

4.   Such statements are to be submitted for every month within five days of the following month i.e., first such statement for the month of October 2021 is to be submitted on or before 5th November 2021 to the RBI’s Issue Department concerned.

 

5.   Non-compliance in this regard will be viewed seriously. Cash-penalty is also introduced. Cashout at any ATM of more than ten hours in a month will attract a flat penalty of Rs. 10,000/- per ATM.

 

6.   The system of monitoring and imposition of monetary penalty is to take effect from 1st October 2021.

 

7.   The Scheme of Penalty is to be administered by Issue Departments of Regional Offices of RBI.

 

8.   In RBI, the Competent Authority to impose penalty is the Officer-in-Charge of the Issue Department of the Regional Office under whose jurisdiction the ATM in question is located.

III.Implementation

1.   In compliance of instructions from RBI, IT department was advised to put in place a robust system for monitoring the availability of cash in ATMs. They are now ready with such a system and it will go live shortly.

2.   The programme devised by IT dept will ensure that whenever the cash limit in ATM reaches the limit of Rs.2.00 Lakh, an SMS will be sent to the Branch-In-Charge & the Asst. Manager concerned advising them to replenish cash in the ATM immediately.

3.   The responsibility to maintain cash in ATM at the stipulated level is primarily that of the joint custodians of cash at the branch to which the ATM is linked. The Branch-In-Charge, as part of overall management function, will do the monitoring at branch level.

4.   There should be frequent interaction between branch and the CCD to which it is linked, on the subject of overall adequacy of cash including that in the linked ATM.

5.   Downtime of each ATM for want of cash (“cash out” incidents) shall be monitored by the branch responsible therefore. It will ensure timely replenishment to avoid cash-outs.

6.   Cash out incidents with downtime should be immediately recorded and reported to the office of General Manager (Operations & Compliance), with a copy each to DGM of Zone and to the Official-In-Charge of the CCD concerned.

7.   In each incident of cash out, immediate steps must be taken by branch to replenish cash. If there is shortage of cash at the branch, replenishment must be sought from the CCD. If replenishment does not happen with four hours of start of downtime, branch must escalate the matter to the office of General Manager (Operations & Compliance).

8.   In all instances of cash penalty being imposed by RBI for cash out at ATMs beyond tolerance time, accountability will be determined on the basis of acts of omission on the part of the staff-members involved and the amount will be recovered from employee(s) found to be responsible.

 

IV.   Please bring contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the staff-members concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
