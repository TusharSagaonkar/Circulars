Circular OC No. 852 dated 29th April 2021

Issuance of Platinum RuPay Debit Card to SB Youth Account Holders

 

I. Guidelines were issued in respect of SB Youth Account vide Circular OC No. 510 dated 3rd December 2009. Modifications therein were communicated vide Circular OC No. 603 dated 11th October 2012. In terms of the latter circular, it was advised that branches can issue ATM cards to those customers holding SB Youth accounts who are of age 18 years and above.

 

II.   In terms of a relatively recent circular i.e. Circular OC No. 781 dated 6th January 2018, guidelines were provided on issuance of Platinum RuPay Debit cards, either at the first instance or at the time of renewal of an existing card, in respect of most of the types of accounts. However, SB Youth accounts (as also PMJDY accounts) were excluded from its applicability and Classic RuPay Debit Cards continued to be extended to such account holders.

                       

III.  NPCI vide its circular NPCI /2019-20/RuPay/003 dated May 23, 2019 has communicated about discontinuation of insurance on Classic RuPay Debit Cards, except that it will be offered on Classic RuPay Debit Cards issued (only)to PMJDY account holders.

 

IV. Decision to issue Platinum RuPay Debit Card to SB Youth Account holders

1.   In light of the above, Bank has, through a Board Meeting dated 30th March 2021, approved issuing Platinum RuPay Debit Cards to customers holding SB Youth accounts having age over 18 years.

2.   Charges for Platinum Rupay Debit Card to the SB Youth account holders have been waived.

3.   It may be noted that a Platinum RuPay Debit Card carries with it an insurance cover of Rs. 2 Lakh. This insurance cover is available with conditions that,

i.   The customer should have used his/her card for e-com or at PoS Terminal at least once in 45 days before accidental death or partial / permanent disability and,

ii. The nominee submits the claim form within 90 days from death of the customer.

4.   It may be noted that in case a customer does not wish to have a Platinum RuPay Debit Card issued to him/her, a declaration as per Annexure hereto should be obtained from him/her for towards the same.

 

V.   In view of the benefits to the target group of customers of insurance cover and concession in charges, branches should ensure replacement of Classic RuPay Debit Cards held by them with Platinum Rupay Debit Cards.

 

Please bring contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the staff members and ensure compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
