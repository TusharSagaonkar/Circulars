Circular OC No. 860 dated 18th October 2021

Modifications in Deposit Accounts and Lockers

Design of Modification Register

Method of Recording and Filing

 

 

I.     There is a system in our Bank to record changes in information (hereinafter called modifications) pertaining to customers who hold deposit accounts with us, and have taken lockers on hire. Some changes are required to be made upon requests from customers. Bank is required to make KYC updates periodically. KYC updates are also done in special situations such as change from Dormant/Inoperative status to Operative. All such modifications are required to be recorded in Modification Register to be maintained in each branch.

 

II.   While the system as mentioned in Para I above is in vogue in Bank since very early times, the design of the Modification Register, procedure for recording entries therein, and filing of papers pertaining to the modification cases, are not standardised. This has resulted in wide variation in the manner in which the register is maintained. In some branches, the register has been reduced to a Kachcha book. Utility of register is also reduced due to non-numbering or wrong-numbering of entries therein and non-tagging of the set of documents with the number of the entry. When some particular modification is disputed by a party, it becomes challenging for Bank to establish its truthfulness in the absence of meticulous recording and filing.

 

III.  1. In view of the above, a standard form of Modification Register is being prescribed as under. Branches will draw rows in accordance with it in a currently available “Many Columns Register” supplied by Stationery Department and bring it into immediate use.

 

 

 

 

Date

Modification No.

Account No.

Type of account

Name(s)

Account-holders (previous to modification)

Type of modification (to be selected and written only from the alternatives indicated in the circular)

Signature of clerk

with name and staff no. under stamp

Signature of official with name and staff no. under stamp

Signature of record keeper with name and staff number towards having taken over the relative documents bearing entry no. and having filed it

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

2. Stationery department is hereby being instructed to get printed, eventually, Modification Register as per the design above and supply one to each branch.

 

3. Branches shall however bring into use the standard pre-printed Modification Register supplied by Stationery department only after completely using up the improvised register to be introduced immediately as per sub-Para 1. Above.

IV. Modifications

1.  Their types

i.       Change in signature(s)

ii.     Change in signatory(ies)/office bearer(s)

iii.    Change in mode of operation

iv.    Change from minor to major

v.     Change from maiden name of a lady to her post-marital name

vi.    Change in address

vii.  Change in mobile no. registered for receiving SMS alerts (ATM/Debit Card transactions)

viii.Change in mobile no. registered for receiving SMS alerts for other transactions (paid service)

ix.    Change in mobile no. for Abhyudaya Mobile Banking

x.     Periodical KYC update (POI & POA) for AML purpose

xi.    Special KYC update for converting dormant/inoperative account into operative one

xii.  Profile update (Income, occupation, profession) for AML purpose

xiii.Other than listed (to be specified)

Note-A: -Particulars of nomination, whether first for an existing account or cancellation of an existing nomination or change therein, shall not be recorded in Modification Register as particulars regarding nomination are required to be entered in nomination register.

Note-B: - The system of recording changes in Modification Register shall be only in respect of deposit accounts and lockers.

2.  Text of entry

Record of modification in the register shall be brief (exactly as per the alternatives listed above) and only one against one serial number. If there are multiple modifications on a single occasion, they should be entered and numbered separately.

3.  Modification no.

i.     Each modification shall bear a running serial number for the branch. If branch has an existing series of modification numbers, the same should be continued.

ii.   Modification number must be entered in the Modification Register in the column assigned therefore.

iii.  Modification number must be entered in the “Narration Field” pertaining to the account in CBS system.

iv.   All the documents collected to carry out modification, after due verification, shall be stapled or stitched together with a label bearing modification number.

All individual papers forming the set of the modification case shall bear the common modification no. in delible pencil (i.e. which can be erased) so that a paper loosened and separated from the set can be kept back in the set.

Note for IT department-A: - Currently, a new narration in the “Narration Field” overwrites the previous one and, therefore, history of modification is not available in CBS system. IT department is advised to enable retention of previous entries in the narration filed to readily provide history of modifications when required).

Note for IT department-B: - IT department is further advised to explore possibility of creating mandatory field for data entry in respect of a modification, i.e. no modification should be possible without the relative data entry and its authorisation in CBS system.

 

4.  Filing of papers pertaining to modification cases

i.     Each set of modification case, duly stapled or stitched, and tagged with modification no., shall be handed over to the record keeper, at the end of the day.

ii.   Record keeper shall maintain all the sets of modification cases in a common file in the order of modification number.

iii.  It is reiterated that type-wise separate files of modification cases need not be maintained.

iv.  Needless to add that papers pertaining to nominations, their cancellation or modification are not to be filed in the file of modification cases. Papers pertaining to nominations which are obtained later than the time of account opening and are therefore required to be filed separate from AOFs, are to be filed in a separate file.

 

V.   Each Branch-In-Charge is advised to bring instructions as above to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned and ensure their strict compliance.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
