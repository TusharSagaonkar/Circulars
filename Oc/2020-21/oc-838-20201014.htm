Circular OC No. 838 dated 14th October, 2020

Nomination Facility on Deposit Accounts and Safe Deposit Lockers

Mandatory Guidance on Advisability of Nomination

Recital by Depositor/Locker-hirer for having received Such Guidance

Mandatory Acknowledgement of Nomination, its Cancellation or Variation

______________________________________________________________________

I. Please refer instructions on mandatory guidance regarding advisability of nomination, and recital by depositor or locker-hirer in respect of having received such guidance, vide Part-II “Guidance to the customers on advantages of Nomination facility and Survivorship mandate” of Model Operational Procedure for Settlement of Claims of Deceased Depositors of the Circular OC No. 443 dated 27th June, 2008. Please also refer instructions regarding mandatory acknowledgement to be given in respect of nomination, its cancellation, or variation thereof (for Deposit Accounts as also Safe Deposit Lockers), vide Para1 and 2 of the Circular OC No. 475 dated 26th March, 2009.

 

II. Inspection department, in its recent compliance report, has pointed out deviations in respect of instructions as above. The situation needs to be remedied forthwith.

 

III.In view of the above, formal procedures as under are being prescribed for meticulous adherence.

1.  Mandatory guidance on advisability of nomination, and recital to be recorded by Depositor/Locker-hirer for having received such guidance

i.     Position is satisfactory for deposit accounts:- The position of adherence to the instructions in this behalf is satisfactory for deposit accounts because it is facilitated by a preprinted recital in the common Account Opening Form itself for deposits by individuals [see second line under point no. 5 of the second page of the AOF-1 (Account Opening Form) for deposits for individuals; however, the staff entrusted with the account opening exercise must ensure that check mark (√) is put by the customer(s) in (only) one of the two boxes against the second clause in the said line (i.e. Nomination required Yes

 No)].

ii.   Position needs to be remedied for locker accounts: -

a.   The position needs to be remedied in respect of Safe Deposit Lockers as text of such recital is not available in a pre-printed form in any part of the documentation for allotment of a Safe Deposit Locker to a customer.

b.   In view of the position obtaining as per sub-point a. above, all the customers taking a locker on hire must be informed, at the time of execution of documents, about advantages of Nomination Facility; and nomination should be recommended to them.

c.    A recital as per the Annexure-I must invariably be obtained from all the Locker-hirers, towards having been informed and given recommendation by the Bank’s staff about the Nomination facility, and the customer having chosen to nominate, or consciously not to nominate, as the case may be, under his/her/their signature(s).

 

2.  Mandatory acknowledgement to be given of Nomination in respect of all the Deposit Accounts and Safe Deposit Lockers

i.     In respect of every customer who has tendered an Account Opening Form for a deposit account or has applied for a Safe Deposit Locker, and has chosen Nomination facility and filled in the relative statutory Nomination Form, acknowledgement of the Nomination Form furnished must be given as per the Annexure II-A hereto by quoting the type of the form, i.e. DA 1(for deposit account) or SL 1/SL 1A (for Safe Deposit Locker).

 

ii.   If a customer later cancels the nomination, acknowledgement of such cancellation must be given as per the Annexure II-B hereto quoting the type of the form which shall be DA 2 (for deposit account) or SL 2 (for Safe Deposit Locker).

 

iii.  If a customer tenders a form of variation of nomination, acknowledgement of such variation must be given as per the Annexure II-C hereto quoting the type of the form i.e. DA 3 (for deposit account) or SL 3/SL 3A (for Safe Deposit Locker).

 

 

3. Recordkeeping of recital (in respect of Safe Deposit Locker -see Para III.1.ii.c above) and office copy of acknowledgement of nomination, its cancellation or variation (for all the Deposit Accounts of individuals as well as Safe Deposit Lockers- see Para III.2. above).

                      i.       The documents mentioned in the caption immediately above are loose papers, not having been integrated, as yet, with the set of application & other papers obtained at the time of opening the relative deposit account/ allotment of locker.

 

                     ii.       Therefore, care will have to be taken at branches to file the nomination form in respect of locker and recital in respect of deposit account as well as locker, together with the relative set on the first occasion itself. It may be noted that a recital in either case shall always be on the occasion of account opening/allotment itself whereas a nomination may be either on such first occasion or later.

 

 

                   iii.       Cancellation or variation of nomination shall obviously take place later & therefore their filing with original documents will require more care.

 

IV. EVENUAL INTEGRATION OF RECITAL/ACKNOWLEDGEMENT WITH ACCOUNT OPENING FORM (FOR DEPOSITS OF INDIVIDUALS) & APPLICATION FOR SAFE DEPOSIT LOCKER

1.   The matter of integrating,

                      i.       acknowledgement (with an office copy) of having registered nomination, with the set of Account Opening Form (for deposit accounts of individuals) and Application for Safe Deposit Locker, and,

                     ii.       recital by a locker-hirer(s) towards having been informed and given recommendation by the Bank’s staff about the Nomination facility, and he/she/they having chosen to nominate, or consciously not to nominate, as the case may be, under his/her/their signature(s), is under consideration.

 

2.   Such integrated forms will be supplied to branches when the stocks of current forms get exhausted.

 

3. a. Upon the integration through pre-printing as aforesaid, the lapses in recording recitals and issuing acknowledgements of nominations are likely to reduce & need for separate filing of recitals & office copies of acknowledgements will also be obviated.

 

b. Branches will follow the process of obtaining these documents and their careful filing with the respective original sets until such time the pre-printed stationary forms are brought into use.

 

4. However, it is being reiterated that the need to carefully file the cancellations of & variations in nominations, together with the set of original documents for account-opening (for deposits) and allotment (for safe deposit lockers) will continue, as customers will obviously file them at some later stage.

 

V. Please bring contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the staff members concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

Annexures

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
