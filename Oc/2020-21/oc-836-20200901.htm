Circular OC No. 836 dated 1st September, 2020

NEWLY OPENED CURRENT ACCOUNT

1)   Mandatory Visit to The Address of The Party Before Issuing Cheque Book and Allowing Transactions

2)   Branch In-Charge to Record on Visit Report Desirability of Continuing Account and having Reviewed Documents

______________________________________________________________________

 

 

I.Attention of branches is invited to the Circular OC No. 725 dated 9th November, 2015 in terms of which branches were informed regarding a few current accounts getting fraudulently opened by unscrupulous parties by scheming to show the delivery of the mandatory “Letter of Thanks” at their declared address through complicity of postal staff or security personnel of the housing-industrial society/building complex. Branches were therefore advised to re-introduce the strict procedure of visit by a staff-member to the address declared to the Bank by any new party whose current account gets opened, and issue cheque book and allow transactions only when, during visit, address of the party is confirmed and economic range of its operations is ascertained to Bank’s satisfaction. The staff-member who visits such declared place of the party (business place, residential-cum-business place or otherwise) needs to be of the rank of officer and above and he/she should compile and submit a report in the format annexed to the said circular.

 

II.  1. We regret to observe that there are several deviations from the instructions laid down in the circular under reference. The deviations include deputing, for the visit, staff members of clerical cadre, or officers who are not well-versed with the principles of risk-management. Officer who makes such a visit does not follow all the points in the format of the Visit Report (which, in itself, serves as a check-list) and leaves many boxes therein blank. Furthermore, not every Branch In-Charge goes through the Visit Report carefully; if visit report is gone through, it is not always that he/she records his/her opinion as to the desirability of continuing the Current Account or otherwise.

 

2. In the recent past, some frauds have surfaced which were facilitated due to the deviations as above.

 

3. We are therefore reiterating in Para III hereunder extant instructions on the subject.

 

4. We also enclose the format of the Visit Report which has been slightly modified to include one additional check-point for the Branch In-Charge in respect of review of documents submitted by the party for opening the account.

 

III.   Instructions

1) After a new Current Account is opened at the branch, the mandatory “Letter of Thanks” has to be sent. In addition, a staff-member from the branch of the rank of officer or above shall visit the place of residence/business of the party at the address declared in the application form, immediately or latest on the next working day of having opened the Current Account, so as to ensure correctness of the declared address.

 

2) The Branch In-charge shall allot this duty by issuing an Office Order among officers and staff-members above the rank of officer, and ensure its strict compliance. The Branch In-Charge may also undertake such visit himself/herself without diluting its rigour in any manner. The functions of reporting, forming and recording opinion, and reviewing documents, will get combined at his/her own level in such a case.

 

3) During the visit to the business place/residence, not only is correctness of the declared address to be established; the range and scope of economic and business activity of the party is also to be ascertained and recorded.

 

4) Apart from the above, the officer should make discreet inquiries about the party in its neighbourhood as to how long are they established at the address, and also gather such other information as may have bearing on the status of the party.

 

5) The visiting officer shall submit his/her report, under signature with rubber stamp of name and staff no., to the Branch In-Charge in the format as per the Annexure hereto. The report must include adequate information so as to enable the Branch In-Charge to draw conclusion on desirability of continuing the Current Account.

 

6) Upon submission of the Visit Report, the Branch In-Charge should:

a) Take a conscious decision, based on its contents, regarding desirability of continuing the account,

b) Review the documents including KYC documents submitted by the party while opening the Current Account,

c) Ensure that the acknowledgement of the mandatory “Letter of Thanks” sent earlier at the party’s address through postal channel is received back duly acknowledged,

and,

d) Record his/her opinion regarding desirability of continuing the relationship, and also record that the documents submitted by the party at the time of opening the account have been reviewed to his/her satisfaction, under his signature and stamp (with name and staff no.) at the bottom of the Visit Report.

 

7) Cheque book should be issued to the party and operations on the account should be allowed by the branch only after the entire process as per points numbering from 1 to 5 above is satisfactorily completed.

 

IV.   Other instructions

1) The Visit Report with Opinion and Documents-Review Report of the Branch In-Charge recorded thereon shall be carefully filed with the Current Account opening form and KYC documents.

2) Instructions issued from time to time regarding obtention of all the other KYC documents remain unchanged.

3) All other instructions contained in the Circular OC No. 709 dated 21st April, 2015 in regard to visits to be made for a new Savings Bank Account remain unchanged. Branches should note to visit the declared address of a customer before issuing Saving Bank passbook/cheque book in every case of insufficient/doubtful address.

 

V. Inspection Dept. is instructed to issue necessary instructions to the Concurrent Auditors and their Officials for verification whether the procedure as above is being followed by branches.

 

VI.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff-members concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
