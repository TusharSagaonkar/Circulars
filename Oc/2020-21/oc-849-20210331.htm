Circular OC No. 849 dated 31st March, 2021

 

 

Manual on Service Charges – Effective from 1st April, 2021

         

 

         I.  Charges for various services extended by us to customers are periodically revised after weighing inputs invited as well as received from branches and Departments. In keeping with this practice, the Manual of Service Charges has been revised and Board’s approval thereto obtained in its meeting held on 30th March, 2021. It also includes charges for Foreign Exchange Transactions. The Service Charges Manual which is common for all the Branches and Departments and effective from 1st April, 2021, is attached herewith. All the officers and staff members at Branches and Departments are advised to go through the Manual on Service Charges carefully and ensure that correct and appropriate charges are levied w. e. f. 1st April, 2021.

 

       II.    The Service Charges manual is also available in Public Folder -> Development Dept.

 

      III.    Branches are advised to note that additional/ special benefits, to which certain types of Accounts are eligible, have not been specified in the New Service Charges Manual Booklet. However, Branch In charge has to allow such concessions to the special types of Account Holders e.g. Senior Citizens/ Educational Institutions / Registered Societies etc. and to Saving Deposit/ Current Deposit/ Cash Credit accounts based on the merits of the account operations.

 

     IV.    Gold Appraisal charges and incidence of Additional Interest/ absolute amounts in respect of breach of credit discipline (on CC/ FLXLN/ SOD/ LATD/ Term Loan A/cs) will not be part of the Service Charges Manual but will be advised through the circulars in CR series (on subjects pertaining to Credit Policy & Recovery) issued from time to time.

 

       V.    The collection of charges from our Staff/Officer for services such as Loan application forms, Gold appraisal charges, Locker rent/delay charges, Interest, and additional interest, CRC / ECS return charges, Actual postage & travelling cost wherever applicable, Demat Charges, Franking charges will continue though specifically not mentioned in Service Charges Manual. Charges for checking of mortagagibility for Housing Loan applied by Staff will be free for the 1st occasion, subsequently the same will be charged i.e. from 2nd occasion onwards.

 

     VI.    PL Code Sheet is also provided in the Manual for immediate reference and ensuring proper accounting of Income Head.

 

   VII.    It may be noted that all operational instructions/ circulars/IDCs issued earlier from time to time remain in force and the Service Charges Manual does not supersede operational instructions. Branches/ Departments are advised to make a reference to the Development Department for clarification, if any required.

 

  VIII.    Officials from internal Inspection Department are advised to verify and ensure that correct service charges at the revised rates as mentioned in the Manual are levied to the customers w. e. f. 1st April, 2021.

 

     IX.    Inspection Dept. should inform the concurrent auditors about the revision in service charges and the concurrent auditors should verify adherence to the charges as per the revised Service Charges Manual.

 

       X.    I.T. Dept. is advised to modify the software modules to take care of the revision in service charges effective from 01.04.2021 and make necessary changes on our Bank’s website.

 

     XI.    Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all staff members concerned and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
