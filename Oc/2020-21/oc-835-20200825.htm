Circular OC No. 835 dated 25th August, 2020

Furnishing e-Statement of Saving Account to Customers by e-Mail

Need to Obtain e-Channel Services Form and Registration of e-Mail ID

______________________________________________________________________

I.  The traditional mechanism of providing information to saving account holders in respect of transactions in their accounts is through entries in passbook which a customer has to present periodically at branches for updating. Many customers find it inconvenient to visit branches for this exercise. During the current COVID-19 pandemic, a customer’s visit to his/her branch for periodically updating passbook has remained not merely inconvenient; it has become risky for obvious reasons. The importance of the need for customers to have a record of entries made by bank into his saving account can, however, never diminish.

 

II. 1. There exists a facility for those saving accountholders who have opted for internet banking to view the statement of account through the same. However, many of our saving accountholders have not opted for internet banking and the reasons could be varied. Not all the customers have the necessary technological set up to avail themselves of this facility. Further, many of them have a discomfort or hesitation in going through the two-step authentication procedure mandatory for internet banking.

 

2. A customer can make use of Balance Enquiry/Mini Statement menu available through Abhyudaya Mobile Banking facility, smart phones having become fairly common. However, there remain two issues. The first is that Abhyudaya Mobile Banking also requires two-stage authentication with which many saving accountholders are uncomfortable. The second is that a Mini Statement cannot substitute a statement of account or passbook.

 

III.  1. The matter has been examined and, as an alternative, it has been considered advisable and feasible to push periodical statements of accounts through emails to the customers.

 

2. In the account opening form, there is already a provision for seeking by customers of e-Statement facility and, in the CIF-1 form which is a part of the set of account opening documents, there is a provision for recording email address of the customers. However, customers need to specifically file the e-Channel Services form to get the e-Statement facility and the number of such customers choosing to seek e-Statement through e-Channel services form is very small.

 

IV. 1. In view of the above, it is hereby being advised to actively promote the e-Statement facility among all the customers who have previously not opted for the same.

2.   Towards this end, our staff members during their interaction with saving accountholders should suggest to them to fill in the e-Channel Services form and specifically choose the e-Statement facility.

i)       Relative forms (e-Channel Services) should be readily available to the front-line staff so that they can be liberally distributed among visiting customers.

ii)     Customers should be made aware of the fact the form is also available for download from the bank’s website.

iii)    A customer obtaining the forms should be requested to fill it up and tender during the same visit, or tender the same to any nearby branch of our bank in case of a downloaded form or a form collected through a representative.

iv)    A duly filled in and tendered form shall be checked in the normal course for particulars as to account no. etc. and signature(s) of the accountholder(s).

v)      Relative fields in OMNI shall be promptly filled in at branches and data-entry authorised.

vi)    IT department shall push all the new registrations for e-Statement facility once in every quarter.

 

V.  1. Although it may not be necessary, it is nevertheless being brought to the attention of staff-members, for clarity, that the e-Statement facility does not involve any charge for customers. This message may also be spread among customers.

 

2. Further, a customer having once chosen the e-Statement facility is not precluded from getting his passbook updated, should he/she or his representative present the same for this purpose across the counter. Any request by a customer in this behalf must be positively responded to.

 

VI.    Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of the instant circular to the notice of all the members of the staff and ensure meticulous compliance.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
