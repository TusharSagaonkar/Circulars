Circular OC No. 848 dated 22nd March, 2021

TDS on Cash withdrawals u/s 194N of the Income Tax Act, 1961

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

 

I. Under Section 194N of the Income tax Act, 1961, introduced in terms of the Finance Bill, 2020, Tax Deduction at Source (TDS) was made applicable on cash withdrawals from 1st July, 2020. According to the provisions of this section, TDS is required to be deducted on cash withdrawals made by a customer, if such withdrawals exceed the floor prescribed in the Act.

 

II. 1. The new section contains the following provisions: -

 

Filing of Income Tax Returns

Particulars

Rate of TDS required to deducted by Banks (also by Co-op cr. Societies and Post Office)

If a customer files his Income tax returns for the previous three financial years i.e. 2016-17 (A.Y. 2017-18), 2017-18 (A.Y. 2018-19) and 2018-19 (A.Y. 2019-20)

If the aggregate amount of cash withdrawn from 01.04.2020 to 31.03.2021 is more than Rs. 1 crore

2% on the amount exceeding Rs.1 crore.

If a customer does not file his Income tax returns for all the previous three financial years i.e. 2016-17 (A.Y. 2017-18), 2017-18 (A.Y. 2018-19) and 2018-19 (A.Y. 2019-20), the time limit for which has expired under sub-section (1) of Section 139.

If the aggregate amount of cash withdrawn from 01.04.2020 to 31.03.2021 is more than Rs.20 lakh but up to Rs. 1 crore. and

If the aggregate amount of cash withdrawn from 01.04.2020 to 31.03.2021 is more than Rs. 1 crore

2% on the amount exceeding Rs. 20 lakh.

And 5% on the amount exceeding Rs.1 crore.

 

2. Further, if a customer has multiple accounts maintained with different branches of our Bank and the PAN card in respect of all these accounts is the same, then for the purpose of Section 194N, cash withdrawals from all these accounts shall be aggregated.

                                

3. In this connection, please refer IDC No. Accts/87/2020-21 in which the provisions of the Section 194N of Income Tax Act, 1961 have been explained in detail.

 

III.    Manual recovery of TDS

1.   Our OMNI system has not been modified, as yet, to comply with the provisions of Section 194N. Therefore, TDS is required to be recovered and accounted for by branches manually.

 

2.   While effecting recoveries as above, there are matters such as insufficient balance and customers’ queries, which are required to be dealt with. When the balance is insufficient, timely deduction of TDS becomes difficult resulting in late payment thereof to the authorities involving interest on late payment. In some cases, deduction of TDS is doubtful altogether due to unavailable balance.

 

3.   Responsibility of the branch staff concerned and need to inform customers

i.     Customers who withdraw cash of Rs. 20 lakhs or more in a Financial Year are likely to withdraw cash on a daily basis. The cashier and head cashier who are responsible for handling cash at branches, should remain alert to such cash withdrawals. They should bring to the notice of the Branch-In-Charge the instances of high frequency and high volume of cash withdrawals.

 

ii.   Under extant instructions, high value transactions are allowed only after they are authorised by Head Cashier/Asst. Manager/Officer-in-charge/ Manager. Therefore, instances warranting TDS under the said section are easily identifiable.

 

iii.  The said Section 194N was introduced in as early as September, 2019. Amendments in the Section were made effective from July, 2020. Therefore, each Branch-in-charge is expected to have identified customers who have withdrawn or are likely to withdraw, in the aggregate, cash of more than Rs. 20 lakh. The branch-in-charge should monitor the accounts of such customers regularly and inform them in writing (in the format as per Annexure hereto) regarding applicability of provisions of the Section 194N. The customer should be clearly informed that if the cash withdrawn during the year exceeds Rs. 20 lakh and ITR copies are not submitted, TDS shall be deducted to the fullest applicable extent, and, until such time the TDS amount does not get recovered, further withdrawals from the account will not be permitted.

 

iv.  Assistance to be provided by IT department and Accounts department for generation and furnishing of relevant information to branches

a.   IT department shall generate on every Friday data-sheets in respect of bank-wide transactions which are likely to come within the ambit of the aforesaid section 194N and make them available to Accounts department for further processing the same day.

 

b.   Accounts department shall process and screen the data by applying such filters as considered appropriate and, in maximum two working days after receipt of raw data from IT department, furnish relevant processed data to each branch.

 

c.    IT department and Accounts department shall forthwith facilitate processes as mentioned in sub-Paras a. and b. above.

 

v.   Collection and record-keeping of copies of ITR and recovery of TDS

a.   Copies of ITR for the 3 years as per the table given in Para II.1. above, filed by the customers coming under the said section, are to be collected by branches. Staff handling TDS work in respect of other transactions at the branch should be given the responsibility of following up for copies of ITR and deduction of TDS under the section under reference also.

b.   The ITR copies should be kept in a separate file and should be maintained by branches for 8 years. Soft copies of the ITRs may be maintained at the branch; a regular backup of the same should also be maintained.

c.    Branch only needs to inform Accounts Dept., through exchange server via e-mail, that it has obtained the copies of ITRs of the customer along with the necessary details for record. This should be accompanied by confirmation that the copies of ITR obtained are correct in respect of details such as PAN, the assessment years, etc.

d.   It may be noted that scanned copies of the ITRs are not to be sent to Accounts Dept.

e.   The officer to whom the said task is entrusted should take a timely review of the status of copies of ITRs remaining to be obtained on record, and follow up with customers until compliance.

f.     With sufficient awareness of the provisions of the section 194N of Income Tax Act as mentioned in sub-Para III.3.i, ii and iii. above and assistance available from IT department and Accounts department as mentioned in sub-Para III.3 iv. above, no delay at branches will be brooked in respect of exchange of letter as per the Annexure, collection of TDS as also collection of IT returns.

 

IV. Accountability :- In the event of any discrepancy or lapse, the staff-member concerned and the official-in- charge shall be accountable therefor. In case of non-recovery of TDS, the same, together with interest if any for delayed payment to the authority, shall be recovered from the staff-member/official concerned.

 

V.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all staff members at your Branch/Department and impress upon them the need to correctly and timely comply with the provisions of Section 194N. Please also ensure meticulous compliance of the instructions.

 

 

 

P. S. Salian

Managing Director
