Annexures

 

 

 

Under Circular OC No. 841 dated 24th November, 2020

Policy on High Percentage of CRC/NACH Returns

Action against – Current/Savings Bank/Cash Credit

Account Holders

Updated as on 24th November, 2020

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

1.  Introduction :

 

RBI has been emphasizing from time to time the need and urgency for monitoring the incidence of CRC/NACH Returns. The tolerance level for such returns as per RBI is currently at 4%. A caution letter is issued to the Bank as when the same is exceeded. The Bank has to furnish cogent reasons for exceeding the same and steps taken to ensure that customers issue cheques backed by sufficient balance in their accounts with the Bank. Proper monitoring and strict control at the Branch level will inculcate a sense of discipline in the customers thereby helping in reduction of the incidence of CRC/NACH Returns. This will also facilitate smooth functioning at the branches.

Frequent dishonor of cheque of even smaller amounts is a matter of concern and bank has to take appropriate actions in those accounts, where such dishonor of cheques occurs. Such recalcitrant customers should not be dealt with laxity.

2. CRC Returns follow-up Actions:

i.     In respect of Savings Bank/Current Deposit Account holders whose cheques are returned unpaid for any reason on 3 occasions in a month, branches will send notices requesting them to ensure proper operations in the account. First such notice will be sent as per Annexure ‘I’.

 

ii.   If the Savings Bank/Current Account holders are not taking any cognizance of our first notice and continue to issue cheques on their accounts, and branch has to return such cheques for any reason, a second notice as per Annexure ‘II’ will be sent to these customers requesting them to close their accounts maintained with the branch within 7 days of the receipt of the notice.

 

iii.  If the Savings Bank/Current Account holders do not visit branch for closing accounts as per our 2nd notice, branch will close the accounts on 11th working day of our 2nd notice (the said period of 11 days being inclusive of 3 transit days for delivery of the notice) and advise the customers accordingly as per Annexure ‘III’.

 

iv.  Branches should maintain proper record of CRC/NACH cheques returned (inward) of such accounts, notices, letters issued to accountholders and acknowledgements thereof before closing these accounts.

3. CRC Returns of Members of the Bank:

Branches should check and verify whether such defaulting accountholder is a member of our Bank. If such is the case, account should not be closed and he/she should be further persuaded, by visiting his/her place of residence/business if possible, to operate the account satisfactorily. If such account holder is not having any direct/indirect liability towards the Bank as borrower/surety and if he/she is willing to resign the membership of the Bank by submitting the share certificate(s) & resignation, after making the payment of his/her share money, his/her Savings Bank/Current Deposit Account may be closed.

4. High Value Returns:

It is stipulated for account holders with cheque facility that, in the event of dishonor, for want of funds in the account, of a cheque valuing rupees one crore and above, drawn on a particular account of the drawer on four occasions during any financial year, no fresh cheque book would be issued. This is apart from other action which bank will take against such delinquent account holder.

5. ECS /NACH Mandate Returns:

In case of accounts where ECS/NACH mandates are frequently dishonored, bank will take appropriate steps including issuance of notices and levying penal charges, as done in case of dishonor of cheques for want of funds. The action to be taken shall be as stated hereunder:

i.     In respect of Savings Bank/Current Deposit Account holders whose mandates are dishonored for any reason on 3 occasions in a month, branches will send notices requesting them to ensure maintenance of sufficient balance in their account. First such notice will be sent as per Annexure ‘VI’ with suitable modifications.

 

ii.  If the Savings Bank/Current Account holders are not taking any cognizance of our first notice and the incidence of dishonor of ECS/NACH Mandate continues, a second notice as per Annexure ‘VII’ will be sent to these customers requesting them to close their accounts maintained with the branch within 7 days of the receipt of the notice.

 

iii. If the Savings Bank/Current Account holders do not visit branch for closing accounts as per our 2nd notice, on 11th working day of our 2nd notice (the said period of 11 days being inclusive of 3 transit days for delivery of the notice) branch will close the accounts and advise customers accordingly as per Annexure ‘VIII’.

 

iv. Branches should maintain proper record of ECS/NACH mandates dishonored of such accounts, notices, letters issued to accountholders and acknowledgements thereof before closing these accounts.

 

6. CRC Returns – Technical Reasons:

Where cheques are dishonored for technical reasons, levy of service charges shall be as under:

a)   Cheque return charges shall be levied only in cases where the customer is at fault and is responsible for such returns. The illustrative, but not exhaustive, list of returns, where the customers are not at fault is given as an Annexure to our Policy on Collection of Cheques/Instruments.

b)  Cheques that need to be re-presented without any recourse to the payee, shall be so represented in the immediate next presentation clearing not later than 24 hours(excluding holidays) with due notification to the customers of such re-presentation through SMS alert, email etc.

 

However, the service charges for return of cheques, except for what is stated above, shall be as per the ‘Manual of Service Charges’ issued by the Bank from time to time.

7. Other Guidelines:

a.   Before issuing new cheque book to the customer, branches should verify past transactions in the account. If past record is not satisfactory, branches should not issue new cheque book to such customer. No new cheque book should be issued to the Savings & Current account holder if the number of CRC return cheques for financial reason exceeds 10 within 3 months and 3 in last 30 days from the date of receipt of duly signed cheque requisition slip.

b.   Customers should be educated to maintain sufficient balance in the account before issuing cheque(s). Notice should be displayed prominently in branch premises appealing customers “Not to issue Cheques without arranging for sufficient funds in the account”.

c.    For Cash Credit account holders whose cheques are returned unpaid for any reason on 3 occasions in a month, notices should be issued to such customers advising them to ensure proper operations in the account. First such notice should be sent as per Annexure “IV”

d.   If the Cash Credit account holders are not taking any cognizance of our notice and continue to issue cheques on their accounts and branch has to return such cheques for any reason, a second notice as per Annexure “V” should be sent to these customers requesting them to meet the Branch-In-charge within seven days of receipt of this notice.

e.   In case of Cash Credit accounts where instances of cheque-returns are significant in number, branches are advised not to issue new cheque book on such accounts in a routine manner. Branches should refer the matter to Zonal office and cheque book should be issued only after approval by Dy. Gen. Manager, Zonal office.

f.     Cash Credit accounts, on which cheques are returned unpaid on 3 occasions, should be cautioned in writing and should be invited to the Branch for discussion. Branch should submit the report of such discussion to the respective Zonal office. If instances of cheque-returns are repeated, then the customer should be called to Zonal office for discussion, and if cheque-returns recur on 3 more occasions thereafter, the customer should be called for discussion with the General Manager (Credit). In case instances recur thrice thereafter, customer should be called before Audit committee.

g.   In case of customers enjoying any type of fund-based or non-fund based credit facility, dishonor of cheques from their account for want of funds will be considered as an important negative factor while considering any of their proposals for renewal/enhancement/sanction of credit facility.

h.   Central Clearing department should monitor return-percentage on daily basis. Central Clearing Department issues letters on monthly basis to branches with individual return percentage exceeding 4. Copies of the letters are sent to the Zonal offices concerned. Dy. General Manager, Zonal Office should take review on the basis of such letters and initiate corrective action I n respect thereof in monthly meeting.

i.     Central Clearing Department should review the matter quarterly. If return percentage in respect of a branch exceeds 4 for 3 consecutive months, the branch In-charges will be counselled in monthly meeting called by the Dy. General Manager concerned. If the return-percentage for any branch continues to exceed 4 per cent even thereafter, and the laid down norms are violated continuously for 6 months, then such Branch In-charges should be asked to present themselves before Audit Committee.

j.     If High value cheques are returned frequently for want of sufficient balance in the account, then such transactions may not be bona fide transactions and they are not acceptable. Branches should study such cases in detail to ascertain the necessity of reporting them as suspicious transactions.

k.   Data in respect of dishonored cheques for amount of Rs 1.00 Lakh and above should be made part of Bank’s MIS on constituents and branches concerned should report such data to their respective controlling office.

l.     Branches should compile data in respect of cheques drawn in favor of stock exchanges and dishonored, irrespective of the value of such cheques. The relevant information will also be given by branches to their controlling offices.

m. Central Clearing Department should place before Audit Committee every quarter, consolidated data in respect of dishonored cheques of Rs 1.00 crore and above.

n.   Bank will charge progressively high charges for dishonor of cheques from the customer’s account, for want of funds, depending on the number of instances of cheque dishonor.
