Office Order No. 221  dated 7  November 2006

 

 

Delegation of powers to the Managing Director for sanctioning  composite

Car Loans and Housing Loans upto Rs. 40.00 lacs

 to the Proprietors/Partners/Directors of firms/companies/trustees.

 

 

          We have been receiving proposals for Car Loans and  Housing Loans from Proprietors/Partners/Directors of the firms/companies.  In case the total credit facilities applied/availed by the firms/companies exceed Rs. 15.00 lacs, inclusive/exclusive of amount of Car Loan or Housing Loan applied, such proposals are required to be placed  before the Loan Board  Meeting for sanction.  This is resulting in delay in sanctioning of Car Loans and Housing Loans and causing customer dissatisfaction.  In order to ensure expeditious  sanction of loans in such cases the Board in their meeting held on 23.10.2006, have approved delegation of powers to the  Managing Director to sanction composite loan applications for Car Loan and  Housing Loan facilities to such Proprietors/ Partners/Directors of the firms/companies/trustees where Car Loan and Housing Loan component of the facility does not exceed Rs. 15.00 lacs and Rs. 25.00 lacs respectively.

 

          All concerned are advised to take a note of the enhanced delegation and accordingly  forward the applications for composite loans as above to the Head  Office for Managing Director’s approval through the concerned Zonal  Office.  The delegation of powers in respect of all other matters to the Managing Director and other officials at various levels as advised in Office  Order Nos. 189 dated 30 November 2004, OO-210 dated 29 April 2006 and OO-213 dated 6 July 2006 remains unchanged.

                                                                                          R. D. Gangal

                                                                                      General  Manager
