Office Order No. 245 dated 19 March 2008

 

Interest Rates on Export Credit – Foreign Exchange Business

 

All designated branches in "A", "B" and "C" Categories are advised that the Board of Directors in their meeting held on 08.02.2008 have since reviewed the position of Interest Rates on Export Credit.  It has been decided that the Branches should implement the following instructions in this regard:

NOTES:

 

 

 

1. The interest rates advised are also applicable for Rupee advances against export bills sent on collection basis.

3. 100% Refinance is available from EXIM Bank on Post Shipment loans granted under Deferred Credit Exports.  Branches are advised to arrange for the refinance by applying to EXIM Bank who will refinance on a case-to-case basis.  However, while availing refinance, branches are to ensure that the interest rate on refinance is economical and Bank’s earns a spread of at least 2% under the transaction.
