Office Order No.  229 dated  21  June  2007

 

Misuse of Internet Connection

 

 

          While implementing the Core Banking Solution (CBS) project internet connections have been provided  as integral part of the technology package to the all departments of Head Office  in Mumbai, Vashi and Zonal Offices  for facilitating better utilization of IT facility. Bank has also provided separate  internet connection through outside agency like SIFY Broadband, MTNL TRI Band, Hathway Broadband, etc. for departmental use.  It has, however,  come to the notice of the Management  that some of the Officials and staff are using the internet  facility for their share trading activities, personal e-mails,  chatting, etc. which amounts to using the facility  for unauthorized  and unofficial purposes which is  not  permissible and  all the staff members are cautioned  against such use of the facility. 

 

          Any staff member  found to be using internet connection for non-official purpose/personal business/communication or any application that may be considered unauthorized and injudicious by the bank will be  dealt with  sternly and subjected to appropriate disciplinary action including withdrawal of his pass word and  authority to access  the network facility  which please note.

 

          The contents of this Office Order  may be brought to be notice of all the staff working in the branch/zonal office/ department  for their information and guidance.

 

         M. C. Shetty

                                                                                Managing Director
