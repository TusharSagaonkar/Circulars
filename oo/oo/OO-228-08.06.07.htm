Office Order No.  228   dated  8 June  2007

 

Measures for prevention of Terrorism

Lists of Terrorist Individuals/organizations.

 

 

          Further to the Office Order No.227 dated 21 March 2007 on the captioned subject additions/amendments to the lists since received from the Reserve Bank of India  vide their letter UBD.(PCB) CO.BPD.Cir No. 27/14.001.062/2006-07 dated 23 January 2007 indicating names of individuals and entities  as under are forwarded to all branches for information, record and   necessary action at their end.

 

i)

Change of information  in the case of two individuals to UN 1267 Committee’s List as per Annex I.

ii)

Names of entities added/amended in the 1267 Committee’s Consolidated List between April 25 and October 04, 2006  as per Annex II.

iii)

List of individuals/ entities belonging to or associated with  The Taliban and Al-Qaida Organizations updated on October 04, 2006, as per Annex III.

 

          Branches are advised to ensure  compliance of the instructions issued  by the Head Office  in the past regarding use of the lists of terrorist  individuals/ entities  provided by the Reserve  Bank from time to time. 

 

          As advised in Office Order No. 202, para 2, only one  copy each of the lists for the use  and record  of the branch is enclosed.  A copy of this Office Order, without enclosures is circulated  for information and for the sake of completing the records at Zonal Office and Departmental levels.

 

 

         

            M. C. Shetty

           Managing Director
