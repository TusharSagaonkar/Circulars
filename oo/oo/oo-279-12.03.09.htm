Office Order No. 279 dated 12th March, 2009 

 

Economy in our Expenses

 

 

          You are aware that recession all over the world is creeping for last more than a year and Indian Economy is severely affected from the month of September, 2008. Banking Industry cannot remain away from the severity of this recession. Although this year our profit position is comfortable, there is no certainty about the gravity of the recession and also no body can say how long it will remain and when the economy will be out of grip of the recession. Keeping in view all these aspects and to safeguard ourselves from likely adverse impact on our National Economy in general and on Banking Industry in particular in near future we will have to make efforts to cut down the undermentioned controllable expenses with immediate effect to maintain the profitability and to achieve the target set for profit in the years to come.

 

i)          Use of Electricity Power :-

Our branch offices and other departments should make efforts in minimizing the use of electricity power by switching off Fans, Air Conditioners, Lights, etc. whenever they are not required. During the lunch hours care should be taken to switch off the Fans, Air conditioners & some of the lights, which are not necessary. It is observed that though few tube lights are required to enable staff to take a lunch, all the lights are on resulting into excess consumption of electricity power which can be avoided. It has also been observed that whenever the sweepers come early morning to perform their duties at the branches/administrative offices they usually start all lights, fans including Air Conditioners. While sweeping the premises the dust enters in the running Air Conditioners affecting their performance and  causing more consumption of electricity.

 

Branch-in-charges, Departmental heads should ensure that sweepers are not using Air Conditioners and using minimum lights where they are working. Air Conditioners should be made on at the beginning of business hours and only during the business hours. Our branches should also use the UPS on every Saturday for atleast 3 to 4 hours under the strict supervision of Branch-in-charge. This will help us to reduce our consumption of regular power supply and checking of proper charging of batteries of UPS and confirming that UPS is in working condition.

 

ii)          Stationary :-

Every member should take maximum care to economize our stationary items. Wastage of stationary items should be totally avoided. Branches should note that indent of stationary items should be based on previous year's consumption and minimum required quantity. Stationary Dept. as well as Development Dept. should discontinue printing of some of the printed stationary items if their use is very less at branches. Such items could be put on exchange server alphabetically, which branches can use without any hardship.   

iii)         Xerox :-

Expenses on Xeroxing should be kept at minimum. If some forms are required in large quantity a suitable indent may be made to Stationary Dept. who will make necessary arrangement of its printing and supply to branches. Various required forms are available on our Exchange Server. Branches should make maximum utilization of this facility.

iv)         Telephone :-

Use of Telephone should be made wherever and whenever it is necessary. Branch-in-charge, Departmental Heads should ensure that no private calls are allowed to be made from the office phone. During working hours no employee should be allowed to use the personal mobile for his personal work which hampers customer service.

v)          Traveling Expenses :- 

Traveling expenses should be kept at a minimum level.

vi)         Remittance of Cash & Security arrangement :-

Remittance of Cash from branches to Head Office and vice-versa should be made whenever it is essential. You are aware that the idle cash is loss to the Bank. Branch-in-charges, therefore, should ensure that the minimum balance of cash is kept at the branch depending on the total volume of branch receipts and payments on an average basis.

vii)        Use of Vehicle and Fuel Expenses :-

Use of Bank's Vehicles should be made only when it is essential so that Fuel Expenses can be curtailed for a considerable amount.

2.       While making economy in our controllable Expenses every staff member should note that there will not be any compromise in providing excellent customer service. It should be ensured that customer service is not hampered at any cost. The very purpose of this communication is to curtail expenses by all means to improve profitability of the Bank which may please be noted.

 

 

V.S. Morye

Managing Director
