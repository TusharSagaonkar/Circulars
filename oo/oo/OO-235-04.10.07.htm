Office Order No. 235 dated 4 October 2007

 

Transaction Charges for RTGS and NEFT Facilities

 

        A reference is invited to para 9 of Office Order No. 230 dated 28th July, 2007 wherein transaction charges to be levied for inward/outward fund transfer under the RTGS/NEFT facilities had been laid down.  The charges as above prescribed by us have been reviewed vis-a-vis those levied by other banks for similar facilities and it has been decided to levy charges for RTGS/NEFT transactions as under: -

 

        A.  Outward: -

RTGS/NEFT: - Rs.1.50 per 1000 subject to minimum Rs.50 and maximum Rs.1500/- per transactions/remittances

 

B.  Inward: -

     Rs.25/- per transaction/remittances

 

        These revised service charges have been made operative from  28th September, 2007 in supersession of the earlier instructions in this regard.  It is however advised that in case service charge at the rates specified in the earlier instructions have been collected in respect of transactions from 28th September, 2007 till the date of receipt of this office order, such cases may not been re-opened for collection/refund of charges, as the case may be,  at the revised rate/s.

 

                                                                                                    M.C. Shetty

                                                                                      Managing Director
