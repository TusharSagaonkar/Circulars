Office Order No.204 dated 17 October 2005.

 

Customer Service – Maintenance of complaint Register

and provision of Complaint Box for customers.

 

 

            The RBI Inspectors have pointed out during the discussion with the Board at the conclusion of the inspection that customers’ grievances at branch level were on increase and appropriate procedure was required to be put in place so as to reduce the number of complaints as also for proving prompt and satisfactory redressal of the grievances.  The Branch Managers are, therefore, advised to ensure that the suggestions/ Complaint Box supplied to the branch is fixed at prominent place in the customer enclosure. If any of the branches are not having complaint box they may approach Development Dept for the necessary arrangement. A notice in bold letters reading   “Suggestions/ complaints, if any, may be put in the complaint box provided for the purpose or the customers can record their complaints/ grievances/ suggestions in the Complaint Register maintain with the branch in-charge”,  may be displayed at the prominent place in the branch.  The Manager/ Accountant should check the suggestions/ complaints dropped by the customers in the Complaint  Box  every day and scrutinize the complaints/ suggestions.  If the grievance can be solved at the branch level, he should take immediate steps to contact the customer and resolve the problem and obtain a letter from the complainant confirming a satisfactory resolution of the complaint.  The customer’s letter should be kept on record.  If the problems pertain to policy matters and can not be solved at the branch, then they may be forwarded with the branch comments to Dy. General Manager of the Zone for redressal.  Where the complaints /issues raised require Head Office approval, for the solution proposed,  the Dy. General Manager may forward such complaint to the Head Office for the decision.

 

2.         Besides, all branches and departments should maintain a Complaint Register which should be made available to the customers on demand for recording their grievances/ suggestions. Similar procedure as in case of complaint put by the customer in the Complaint Box should be followed for examining the complaints/ suggestions and the redressal of the grievances recorded in the Compliant Register.

 

3.         The Zonal DGM should verify the Complaint Register as well as the complaints received through the Complaint Box and record his comments on the quality and promptness of redressal and corrective/remedial measures taken by the branch, in the branch visit note and the deficiencies observed during the visits should be pursued by the Zonal Office for proper rectification.

 

                                                                                                            Managing Director
