Office Order No. 209   dated  27 February  2006

 

BANKING OMBUDSMAN SCHEME 2006

 

            A reference is invited to the Office Order No. 156 dated 9 October 2002 informing  branches, zonal offices, departments about the Banking Ombudsman Scheme 2002, introduced by RBI as a customer grievance redressal mechanism.  The Scheme has since been revised by Reserve Bank for enlarging the scope and coverage of the Scheme.  The new Scheme replaces the Scheme of 2002 and is  called as the Banking Ombudsman Scheme 2006.  This new Scheme has come into force w.e.f. 1 January 2006.  The enlarged scope of the new Scheme  includes customer complaints regarding certain new areas such as credit card complaints, deficiencies in the providing the promised services even by the banks' sales agents,  levying service charges without prior notice to the customers and non-adherence to the fair practices code as adopted by banks.  Besides, it provides for online  submission of complaints, changes in the appeal mechanism against the awards issued by the Banking Ombudsman  and a few other administrative changes.  Chapter IV of the Scheme that specifies the "Procedure  for Redressal of Grievance" under the  Scheme is reproduces in the Annexure I for information of all concerned.  With regard to the requirements to be complied with by the bank as specified under para 15 of Chapter IV ibid, it is  observed that there is no change in the stipulation  that banks should display prominently and in easily noticeable manner, in all office premises, a signboard indicating purpose of the Scheme and name and address of the Banking Ombudsman to whom the aggrieved parties can complain.  Further, it is required that the fact that the designated officer of the bank would make available for perusal  a copy of the Scheme, if anyone desires to do so, should also be notified prominently.  Branches, Zonal offices and Departments have already been provided with appropriate signboards to that effect by the  Development Department  which they  can continue to display as hitherto with a small change indicating that copy  of Banking Ombudsman Scheme 2006 is available for perusal instead of the year 2002 presently mentioned on the Board displayed. (this can be done by just pasting a slip reading 2006 over 2002 on the existing signboard) It is further clarified that respective Zonal  DGMs will continue to be the designated officers for branches in their respective zones and the Sr. Manager, Secretarial Department will be the designated officer for Head Office departments for the purpose of the Scheme.  A copy of the Scheme  will be provided to each of them for  lending it to  the

customers for perusal, as required under the Scheme.  It is also indicated for information of all concerned that the Manager /Sr. Manager/ AGM holding charge of the Secretarial Department at Head Office  from time to time  will work as the Nodal Officer required to be appointed by the bank in terms of para 15 (3) of Chapter IV of the Scheme.

2                                It may be pointed out that like  the earlier Scheme it has been specified in the new Scheme also that before  making a complaint to the Banking Ombudsman, the complainant should first approach  his bank for redressal of his grievance.  This requirement provides opportunity for ensuring that everything possible is done to secure customer satisfaction by resolving his complaint.  Reiterating the advice in para 2 of the Office Order No. 156 it is emphasized that  all concerned should note to take advantage of this procedure and ensure that occasions of customers filing complaints against our branches/ offices  with the Banking Ombudsman would be kept to the  minimum possible which is necessary for image building of the bank.

 

M.C.Shetty
