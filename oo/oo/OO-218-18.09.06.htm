OFFICE ORDER NO. 218 DATED 18th SEPTEMBER, 2006

 

 

Formation of a Specialized Credit Cell and Recovery Call Centre at Vashi  for Vashi, Turbhe, Banking Complex, Koparkhairane & Mobile Branches.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          It  may be recalled that  with  a view to developing credit appraisal expertise and expediting disposal of credit proposals,  a Specialized Credit Cell for attending to the credit  functions for a cluster of branches was created for Abhyudaya Nagar, Parel and Sewri branches at Abhyudaya Nagar  branch premises since 6.10.2005 vide  Office Order No. 206  dt. 10.11.2005. The arrangement has been working successfully and significant improvement  in the process of credit appraisal and promptness in  disposal of proposals  to a great extent have been achieved.  The Board, therefore, decided in their meeting  held on 29.8.2006 to  replicate the model and approved formation of a  Specialized Credit Cell at Vashi for Vashi, Turbhe, Banking Complex, Koparkhairane and Mobile branches on similar lines.  The Cell will  work under the Asst. General Manager supported by  a dedicated group of staff with organizational structure similar to that of Abhyudaya Nagar Cell.

 

           The organizational structure, functional role, operational modalities of the Specialized Credit Cell and Recovery Call  Centre (the Cell) are explained in the following paragraphs.  The details of consequent changes in the staff strength and the functional responsibilities of the five affiliated branches are also indicated therein

(i) Location :

          The Cell is located at the bank’s  premises at Vashi, on 1st floor.

 (iii) Functions :

          The Cell will attend to acceptance,  scrutiny, appraisal, recommendation and sanction of all credit proposals (irrespective of the amount) except loans against FDRs, Govt. Securities, Gold loans and Cash Credit (SOD) against FDR/NSC/KVP/RBI bonds, LIC policies, etc. Turbhe, Banking Complex, Koparkhairane and Mobile Bank, branches will continue to sanction such loans.  In case of Vashi branch, it will sanction loans only against FDR and Gold Ornaments and   all other loans, including those mentioned above will be processed and  sanctioned by the Cell. Besides, all the five branches will sanction and issue BGs against 100% margin as per delegation.  Sanction of facilities by the Cell will include new sanctions, renewals, renewals with enhancement and also review of the cash credit facilities.

 (v) Other functional aspects :

(a)      The AGM is the In-charge of the  Sp. Credit Cell at  Vashi.  He will report to the Addl. General Manager, Head Office.  A Sr. Manager is provided to Vashi branch in place of the AGM withdrawn from the Vashi branch.  The Sr. Manager along with the Manager of the branch will look after the day-to-day business of Vashi branch including entire housekeeping.

 

(b)      The officials and staff at above five branches will arrange to provide appropriate loan/CC application forms, along with list of  documents required, to  their customers.  They will interview the customers and guide them for filling the loan/CC applications forms, membership application forms, consent letters of sureties, and other documents required.  The branches should provide similar service even to those  members of public who are not their customers and guide them properly.  It should be noted that the branch staff will be liable for disciplinary action if complaints are received at the Credit Cell/ Zonal Office/HO that the customers/potential customers enquiring about or approaching for credit facilities at branches are not being guided / helped satisfactorily and are merely being advised to approach the Credit Cell at Vashi.

 

(c)      Before disbursement of the loan sanctioned by it, the Cell will ensure that the procedures like assignment, mortgage/pledge/hypothecation/creation of ROC charges  etc.  and all other formalities necessary for creation of the bank’s charge on the securities, principal and collateral, are completed. No work that is of material importance upto the stage of and related to the disbursement should be kept pending. The files will be sent to     branches after completing the documentation and other procedures. A dummy file should be kept at the Cell for further follow-up in respect of assignment of LIC Policies, etc.  wherever such follow-up is required.  Pursuing such cases will be the responsibility of the Cell. 

(d)      Action for discharge of securities such as reassignments, release of pledged/mortgaged properties, etc.  should  be attended to by the branches after closure of the relative loan accounts that have been sanctioned by the Cell.

 

(e)      The Cell will maintain separate Securities/ Documents Registers/Mortgage Register  in respect of the borrowal accounts at each branch. These registers should be up-dated, maintained properly before sending the files to branches.

 

(f)      Immediately after receipt of the file by the branch from the Cell, the branch in-charge should arrange for completing the entry of all details in the limit masters.  In respect of loans above Rs. 2.00 lacs and upto Rs. 5.00 lacs including Housing Loan, the branch in-charge should arrange post disbursement inspection within one month through the Clerks/Asst. Accountant of the branch.  The review and post-disbursement inspection of all loans and advances above Rs. 5.00 lacs should be done by the Accountant/Manager/Sr. Manager at branches. It is the ultimate responsibility of In-charge of branches to ensure post-disbursement inspection of all loans and advances.

 

(g)      After pre-sanction visit and verification carried out jointly by the branch in-charge and AGM of the Cell, the files of the customers seeking facilities of above Rs. 7.00 lacs and upto Rs. 15.00 lacs will be scrutinized by the Cell and sent to H.O. directly with recommendation of the Manager/AGM of Cell. The present guidelines of routing loans and advances proposals above Rs. 10.00 lacs through Zonal Offices are not applicable to the Cell.  Thus, all proposals above Rs. 10.00 lacs and upto Rs. 15.00 lacs will be directly sent by the Sp. Credit Cell to Head Office with their recommendation.

 

(B) Recovery Call Centre :-

          As a forerunner to total bank net working where follow-up for recovery in respect of defaulters of all branches can be pursued from a single centre, a Recovery Call Centre will also be made operational as a part of the Specialized Credit Cell covering five branches of Vashi, Turbhe, Banking Complex, Koparkhairane and Mobile Branch.  The organizational set up and functions of the Recovery Call Centre will be as under :

(i) Organizational Set up :-

          Call  Centre will be provided with following staff :

 

Cadre

No. of Staff

Asst. Accountant

1

Clerk

2

 

          The Call Centre staff will work under the direct supervision of the Manager/AGM of the Cell.

 (ii) Functions :-

(a)      At  the present level of technology application in our bank, initial follow-up upto generation and issuing of all recovery notices to the defaulters will continue to be attended by the concerned branches.

 

(b)      The  DGMs of respective zones will ensure during their visits to branches that generation of loan/cc notices, dispatch thereof and subsequent follow up is made by the branches.  However, telephonic reminders and further follow up would be made by the Call Centre at the Credit Cell when established.

 

(c)      I.T. Dept. will ensure that back-up of previous day in respect of the five branches will be loaded early next day in the computers of the Cell and follow up with the borrowers through telephonic reminder will be pursued  by the Call Centre.  Maintenance of records of the follow-up, action taken by the Recovery Call Centre will be done through the systems.

 

          The Recovery Call Centre will be set-up after connectivity of branches is ready & operational.

 

          Until such time as the Recovery Call Centre is not set up, the follow up with defaulters will continue to  be made by branches which please note.

(C) Responsibilities and Role of branches in supporting the Cell :-

(a)      It should be noted that though the credit appraisal and sanctioning in respect of all the five  branches covered under the new arrangement will be taken care of by the Cell, the Sr. Managers and the Managers of these branches will be responsible for achieving the deposits, loans, profit and NPA targets, monitoring of loans and  advances including post disbursement inspections, recovery of overdues in defaulter accounts, etc.  They will ensure regular and timely receipt of stock/book debt statements every month, obtaining adequate insurance cover for the securities charged in the bank’s favour, such as stock of goods, plant and machineries, moveable and immoveable properties, etc.  The Sr. Manager/Manager/Accountant should visit the defaulters with limits above Rs. 5.00 lacs and arrange for visit by Clerks/Asst. Accountants to all defaulters upto Rs. 5.00 lacs.

 

(b)      Sr.Manager/Manager of these five branches should visit the unrenewed / unreviewed CC accounts and ensure that renewal/review/ enhancement proposals are received at the earliest.  They should extend support and guidance that may be required by the borrowers.  The review/ renewal proposals thus procured should be forwarded to the Cell for further processing and sanction with required documents/financial statements.  In respect of review/renewal of CC Accounts, the In-charge of the branch should ensure that such proposals are received atleast 1 month before due dates along with all documents. It is the prime responsibility of branches to ensure timely renewal and review of cash credit accounts.

                                                                  

(c)      Timely receipt of financial statements like audited balance sheet, profit and loss account and other financial data from the borrowers is of crucial importance for monitoring borrowal accounts, particularly the bigger accounts as also for their reviews/renewals. Many of our borrowers default  in these matters.  Branch-in-charges who are relieved of the major work with formation of the Cell should follow-up regularly with all CC account borrowers, obtain audited financial statements regularly and complete Credit Rating exercise in respect of all eligible borrowers and keep the Cell informed.

 

(d)      The Sr. Manager/Manager of these branches should market loans and advances by daily visits to individual units, traders, institutions, corporate offices.  They should canvass group loans under ECS/SDL facility and ensure achievement of loan targets for the year.

 

(D) Conclusion :-

          The Specialized Credit Cell will start functioning with effect from 25.09.2006 at  the bank’s Vashi  premises.  The idea in forming such specialized unit is to equip the staff of our bank to meet the challenges posed by the present day competitive and hi-tech system in operation in the banking industry.  As indicated at the               beginning    of   the   circular,  the  performance  of the  Abhyudaya Nagar Sp. Credit Cell and the branches brought within its scope, customer response, and the Management’s assessment of its efficacy have encouraged forming of  more such Specialized Cells catering to varied customer requirements of different branch clusters.  The concerned branches, their staff and the staff who will be posted in the Cell have special responsibility in ensuring success  of the arrangement.  All other officers and staff of the bank are also advised to take a note of the formation of  the new Cell  for our Navi Mumbai branches as this organizational model is intended to be extended to branches in other areas shortly.
