OFFICE ORDER NO.  216    DATED   18  AUGUST, 2006

Prevention & Suppression of Terrorism

Lists of individuals and entities belonging to or associated with:

(a)      Taliban and Al-Quida organizations

(b)      Abu Sayyaf Group Foundation

(c)      South East Asia terrorist organization Jamaah Islamiya (JI)

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A reference is invited to Office Orders No. 155, 170, 171, 178 and 202 furnishing lists of various terrorist organizations and individuals identified as involved in terrorist activities. The lists have been  compiled by the UN, the US Govt. and Govt. of India from time to time.  Based on the directions issued by RBI in this context, branches have been given appropriate instructions for stopping operations, freezing the accounts, reporting the existence of such accounts, if any, etc. A new consolidated list of individuals and entities has since been notified by Govt. of India as per UN Security Council resolution. The new list contains the names of individuals and organizations belonging to  or associated with Taliban and Al-Quida and as advised by RBI it supersedes all previous versions of the lists issued by the UN Security Council. A copy of the relative the relative ‘Schedule’ containing the new revised list adopted by the UN Security Council and received with the Govt. of India Gazette Notification dated 23.6.2006 is enclosed as Annexure - I.  

 

          In addition to the above, RBI has advised that Govt. of India have decided to freeze the assets of three leaders of Abu Sayyaf Group Foundation in India viz. (i) SALI JR., Jainal Antel; (ii) SAHIRON, Radulan and (iii) HAPILUN, Isnilon.  The personal details of these three individuals are given in Annexure - II.

 

          Similar decision of freezing their assets has also been taken by Govt. of India in respect of  individuals associated with South East Asia terrorist organization Jemaah Islamiya (JI). The names and other details of these individuals are given in Annexure - III.

 

2.       Instructions have been laid down in Office Order No. 155  and modified as necessary from time to time in the subsequent Office Orders on the subject, about the actions to be taken by branches in respect of accounts of individual and entities grouped under (a) above  and listed in Annexure - I. Branches are advised to ensure compliance of the said instructions. As for action to be taken in regard to account of the organization mentioned at  (b) and (c)in the caption, it is advised that in case any account of individuals named in the  particulars given in Annexure II and Annexure III is maintained at any branch of our bank, the branch should exercise due diligence and take action to suspend operations and freeze the account with immediate effect.  Existence of any such account should be reported immediately to the Head Office, Secretarial Dept. for reporting the position to RBI and obtaining their further instructions in the matter. 

         

3.       Branches are advised to carefully go through the long list of names of individual and entities involved in terrorist activities and ensure that our bank’s services are not utilized in any manner whatsoever, by the proscribed individuals and entities for carrying on their anti-social activities and terrorist operations.

 

4.       Attention of branches is also invited to para 2 of the office order No. 202 and they are advised to maintain proper record of the lists of individuals/entities supplied to them from time to time.

 

                                                         

                                                                                   M. C. Shetty

                                                                              Managing Director

 

Encl : Annexures I, II, and III.
