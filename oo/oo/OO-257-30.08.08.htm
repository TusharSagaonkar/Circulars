Office Order No. 257 dated 30 August, 2008

 

Discontinuation of Pension Scheme (PS),

Daughter’s Marriage Scheme (DMS),

Loan Linked Deposit Scheme (LLDS),

Cash Certificate (C. Cert.)

and Lakhpati Scheme (LS)

 

        In response to the decision taken in Business Planning Committee Meeting held on 09.05.2008 to discontinue the existing two Term Deposit Schemes viz. Pension Scheme (PS) and Daughter’s Marriage Scheme (DMS) and further suggestion put in their meeting held on 29.07.2008 to discontinue another three Term Deposit Schemes viz. Loan Linked Deposit Scheme (LLDS), Cash Certificate Scheme (C. Cert. S) and Lakhpati Scheme (LS), the entire matter was put up to the Board in its meeting held on 22.08.2008 for its decision.  On the basis of decision taken by the Board we advise as under: -

 

2.     The following Schemes have been discontinued w.e.f. 01.09.2008

i.    Pension Scheme (PS)

ii.    Daughter’s Marriage Scheme (DMS)

iii.   Loan Linked Deposit Scheme (LLDS)

iv.   Cash Certificate Scheme (C. Cert. S)

v.   Lakhpati Scheme (LS)

 

3.     The reasons for discontinuance of these schemes are as under: -

i.    Poor or no response from the depositors for these Schemes.

ii.    Popularity of these schemes have been abundantly reduced amongst the customers.

iii.   As per the recent guidelines RBI has restricted to accept the deposits for more than 10 years period.

iv.   The deposits under PS and DMS Schemes which are still operative for more than 10 years are being paid interest at the rates applicable at the time of opening of the accounts which in many cases is much higher than the existing rates of interest on Term Deposit.

v.   In the scheme where due date is not certain, branches usually refuse to continue the deposit beyond 10 years as per extant RBI directives which many times invite complaints at branch level and at the level of higher authorities also.  We intend to avoid such complaints in future.

 

4.     In view of the above, we advise you as under: -

a.   Do not accept fresh deposits under above mentioned five schemes w.e.f. 01.09.2008

b.   Close the existing deposit accounts under DMS, LLDS, C. Cert. S. and Lakhpati Schemes on maturity with due intimation to Customers concerned.

c.    Contact existing Accountholders of Pension Scheme and convince them for closure of their accounts for getting better benefits of prevailing high interest rates on Term Deposits.

       

    Please follow the instructions meticulously.
