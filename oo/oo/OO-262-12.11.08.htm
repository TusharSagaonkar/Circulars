Office Order No. 262 dated 12 November 2008

 

Sharing ATMs of BANCS Network

 

 

We have pleasure in advising you that our Bank has joined the ATM Network of BANCS w.e.f. 02.10.2008.  BANCS is the only group having PSU, Private, Foreign and Co-operative Banks in its fold.  At present BANCS is having 14 member Banks, out of which 10 are Co-operative Banks.  The group is presently having about 6000 ATM outlets.

 

2.     Benefit of BANCS Network ATM:

i.    By sharing BANCS network, we can facilitate our customers for their transaction on other banks' ATMs wherever presence of our bank ATM does not exist.

ii.   No need to install new offsite ATMs wherever presence of BANCS group ATM exist.

iii.   Our outside branches which are in centralized environment and online e.g. Udupi, Ahmedabad branches can also issue ATM cards to the customers.  Those customers can transact on BANCS Network ATM.

 

3.     At present there are two types of functions available.

A)  Acquirer Scheme:  Other Banks' customers can come to our ATM for transaction.

B)  Issuer Scheme:  Our Bank's customers can go to other banks' ATMs for their transactions.  We as Abhyudaya Co-op. Bank Ltd. will act as an Issuer in the Group.  That means our ATM Cardholders can transact on the below listed Banks' ATMs.

i.    Axis Bank

ii.   IDBI Bank

iii.   Bank of India

iv.   Bank of Bahrain & Kuwait

v.   Shyamrao Vithal Co-op. Bank Ltd.

vi.   Cosmos Co-op. Bank Ltd.

vii.  Greater Bombay Co-op. Bank Ltd.

viii. Ernakulam Dist. Co-op. Bank Ltd., Cochin

 

C)   The transactions allowed in the network are:

i.    Fast Cash Withdrawal

ii.   Cash Withdrawal

iii.   Balance Enquiry

5.     ATM Cards:  At present we have two different type of card in our Bank:

i.    Track Three Cards: This type of cards issued in the beginning to our customers, when branches were in stand alone mode and in distributed environment.  This type of card will not function on BANCS network.

ii.   Track Two Card: We have issued track two cards under series 504487, which can be accessible in BANCS network.

 

        The Customers with Card ID begin with 504487 series can transact on the BANCS network ATMs.  All other cardholders who wish to have this facility will apply for the New ATM Cards by making simple application to the concerned branches.  On receiving application, branches shall hot list old cards and issue new cards.  These new ATM Cards shall be issued free of cost.
