Office Order No.  295  dated  9th  September, 2009

Straight Through Processing (STP) of RTGS/NEFT remittances

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You are aware that Bank is a direct member of Infinite System since May 2002, and we have been granted type ‘A’ membership for RTGS & NEFT.  As on date 100 Banks are members of RTGS with 57,109 RTGS enabled branches and 91 banks are members of NEFT with 56,630 NEFT enabled branches.

 

To reduce systemic risk in the Payment and Settlement system, RBI has been encouraging the use of electronic form of remittance through RTGS /NEFT.  In order to increase the usage of the system, RBI has capped the current service charges to be levied on outward remittances through RTGS & NEFT to Rs.50/- & Rs.25/- respectively resulting in steep reduction in the service charges.  Further, the inward remittances were also made free. The reduction of charges led to increase in the number of RTGS/NEFT transactions, both Inward & Outward.  

 

2.       Under the present system, all RTGS/NEFT remittances are sent from RTGS/NEFT Cell under Treasury Dept. where Participant Interface (PI) is installed.  When a customer approaches a branch for remitting money through RTGS/NEFT, request form to remit money is obtained duly filled in.  After verification of the details, the branch debits the customer’s A/c and transfers the amount to Head Office by crediting RTGS or NEFT-Outward A/c under ABB module.  Subsequently, the request form is faxed to RTGS/NEFT Cell   with signatures of two authorized officials. After getting faxed copy of the request form and getting credit from the branch for the corresponding remittance request, the RTGS/NEFT Cell enters the transaction details on the PI.  The amount is remitted after it is authorised by concerned official. This process takes 10-15 minutes time resulting into delay in remittances.  In case of Inward remittances, the RTGS/NEFT Cell credits the amount to the Customer’s Account directly under ABB module.  

As RTGS/NEFT Cell has to rely on the faxed copy of remittance request, there are chances of errors in remittances and it is also fraught with risk of fraud.  To overcome all such problems and to render better and quick customer service, we have decided to switch over to Straight Through Processing of RTGS & NEFT remittances.  The IT Dept. has prepared software in  Omni  enabling  the  branches to create   outward   remittance  messages  as   per   the   format   prescribed   under RTGS/NEFT rules and tested the same in many branches.  Having confirmed that the outward messages created by these branches have been executed successfully on the PI., IT Dept.  has informed that  Straight Through Processing (STP) can be rolled  out to all the branches.

          Safety measures / procedure that would be adopted by branches for proper functioning of Straight Through Processing of remittances would be as under:-

 

3.      Outward Remittance:

Operations at Branch Level:

i)             On receipt of request from Customer, official will verify whether the details of remittance such as name of the beneficiary, IFSC Code of the beneficiary’s bank,  beneficiary’s account No & type of account are correctly mentioned in the request form.  The officials will also verify the signature of the Applicant with specimen on record and confirm availability of funds in the account.  

ii)           Asst. Accountant or Accountant will approve requests for remittance upto Rs.2.00 Lakh.  In case the request for remittance is above Rs.2.00 Lakh, Accountant or Manager of the Branch will approve the remittance by signing the remittance request form.

iii)         The clerk to whom RTGS/NEFT work has been assigned will enter the details of the remittances in outward remittance module for RTGS/NEFT in Omni.  On completion of the remittance message the system will automatically debit the Customer’s account with the total amount of remittance including commission, credit Head Office (RTGS/NEFT –Outward Online Account) to the extent of amount to be remitted and Income account to the extent of commission received.

iv)          If a Customer approaches the branch where he is not maintaining his account and requests for RTGS/NEFT remittance, the branch which receives such request will first debit the amount including commission charges to the account of the Customer under ABB, park the amount of remittance in the Remittance Suspense A/c (REM-SUS A/c) and credit the amount of commission to Income A/c.  While generating remittance message, in the Ordering Customer's field, REM-SUS A/c to be entered instead of Customer's account number which will automatically debit the REM-SUS A/c on completion of the remittance message.  Since the account master details of the remitter will not be available automatically, Ordering Customer's details such as account number, name of the account holder, address of the account holder are to be entered separately. (Name & A/c number are to be entered in the next field after Ordering Customer field and Address to be entered in the subsequent three fields)  The branch should ensure that there is no balance in REM-SUS A/c at the end of the day. 


v)            The above said procedure is also required to be followed when more than one account are required to be debited for making one remittance.

vi)          All the columns of the Ordering Customer & Beneficiary Customer's details are mandatory and are to be filled without any special characters.

vii)        The Asst. Accountant/ Accountant at the branch will authorize remittance transactions.

viii)       Once message is created at the branch level, the system will generate Transaction Reference Number & UTR No.  The branch will maintain record of all the messages created on the basis of Transaction Reference Numbers/ UTR Numbers with the signatures of the officials for having authorized the messages.

ix)          Once the message is created and authorized the credit entry to H.O. (RTGS/NEFT Outward Online A/c) should not be cancelled without written confirmation from the RTGS/NEFT Cell.  (On the basis of message received from the branches payment message will be released on the RBI PI which cannot be cancelled/ recalled).

x)           After authorization of remittance message in Omni, the remittance message is forwarded to RTGS/NEFT Dept. for final authorization and releasing to RBI PI.

xi)          It will be the responsibility of the branch officials to verify the correctness of the details of the remittance message created in Omni.  Similarly branches will ensure that remittance message   reaches RTGS/NEFT Cell through omni before the time stipulated by RBI/ Bank from time to time.

xii)        In case RTGS/NEFT Cell is not in the position to process the remittance message sent by the branch for any reason, the entry generated will be cancelled by the branch at the written advise of RTGS/NEFT Cell.

 

4.      Operations at RTGS/NEFT Cell:

i)       When outward remittance message in Omni is received from the branch, the officials at RTGS/NEFT Cell will match each message with credit received from branches for that particular remittance. 

ii)      After ensuring that branch has credited HO (RTGS/NEFT Outward Online A/c) the message will be transferred to Participant Interface (PI).  Officials will release the message for payment after attaching their Digital signatures allotted by IDRBT-CA.

iii)     NEFT Outward messages will be released with Digital Signature of one official.  RTGS Outward remittances upto Rs.10.00 Lakh will be released on PI with Digital Signatures of two officials (Creator + 1 Authoriser) and remittances above Rs.10.00 Lakh will be released with Digital Signature of three officials (Creator + 2 Authorisers). 

 

5.      Inward Remittance:

Inward remittances received from other banks will be directly credited to respective accounts of the beneficiary by the RTGS/NEFT Cell under ABB module after authorization by 1 official as provided in Omni system.  In case of non-availability of the beneficiary’s account, the remittance will be returned back to the remitting bank within the same business day.

 

6.      General Precautions :

i)       After implementation of the STP system the existing procedure of faxing request forms will be discontinued.  As such the RTGS/NEFT Cell will not have any details of the remittance request at the time of releasing the message on Participant Interface.  Whatever message is created by the branch officials will be released on the Participant Interface on the basis of credit received from the branch.  Therefore, the entire responsibility of ensuring correctness of the message will be on the branches. RTGS/NEFT Cell will be monitoring the position of funds in the RBI account and releasing the messages on the basis of availability of funds.

ii)      RTGS/NEFT Cell will arrange to update the IFSC Nos. of participating Bank/branches in Omni.

iii)     Branches and RTGS/NEFT Cell will reconcile the entries in RTGS/NEFT Outward A/c on day to day basis.

iv)      Branches will generate various RTGS/NEFT reports and keep record of the same.    

v)       All the instructions given in Office Order No. 230 dated 28/06/2007 & Circular O.C. No. 484 dated 24.04.2009 are to be followed for RTGS/NEFT remittances.

 

7.       The Straight Through Processing of RTGS/NEFT remittances will be made effective before 31st October 2009 at all branches. By this time the I.T. Dept. alongwith Treasury Dept. will provide necessary training to at least 4 to 5 officials from each branch regarding how to create and authorize remittance messages.

         

I.T. Dept. will also arrange to install STP software module at the branches before 31.10.2009.

         

8.       Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at the Branch and ensure compliance.
