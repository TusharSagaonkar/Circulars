Officer Order No. 211 Dated 29TH April 2006

 

Customer Service

 

  Branches are aware that with a view to improving customer service and achieving maximum customer satisfaction customers’ meetings  are convened from time to time and on the basis of the feed back obtained at such meetings, measure are taken to improve  our customer services.  The suggestions made by the customer at one such recent meeting were examined and discussed at the Budget and Performance Review Meeting held on 25.3.2006 and the following instructions  are issued to branches for implementation  with immediate effect.

 

I)       Intimation of CSC return to depositors :

  In case of  CSC returns, it was decided that  the customers should be given immediate intimation of cheques returned unpaid in clearing.  The concerned customers should be contacted on phone the same day and informed about return of cheque. It is suggested that for their convenience, branches may note down the telephone numbers of the customers when they come to deposit the cheques. The names of the customers contacted should be noted down.  When the customer fails to collect  the cheque within two days, intimation should be sent to them through post on the third day and a copy of intimation be kept on record.  Development Dept is in the process of printing the format  of intimation on Inland Letter from which will save  postage cost for the bank.  In the meanwhile, you may continue sending the intimation as per  prevailing practice. You may be aware that customers can file criminal case U/s 138 for cheque bouncing within the specifies time.  Therefore, there should not be any lapse on the part of the bank in discharging the duty of  intimating CSC return in time.  The Branch Incharge should  educate the staff in this matter and see that there is no delay in sending intimation to customers.

 

II) Single Window service to a customer :

Looking into various aspects under the present scenario, it may not be possible for the bank to provide such facility to our customers.  However, it was decided that if any customer insists that single window service be provided to him, service such as issue of cheque book and cash, etc. may not be denied on the ground that such service is not provided / available in our bank from one window / counter. 

All the branch staff should be advised in their monthly meeting that if and when any  customer  insists  on  single   window   service  being  provided to  him,   the    staff

 

concerned should go out of the way and ensure that all the services are provided to such customers at one point.  The staff should adhere to customer friendly procedures and see that customers get satisfactory services from  the branch.  Refusal of banking services of  any kind that are normally provided  by the branch customers will be viewed seriously.

 

III) Service  at Cash Counter :

            Whenever there is  big rush and long queue  at the cash counters for withdrawal/ deposit  of cash, customers who come to deposit big amounts say, over Rs. 1.00 lac and more or customers intending to withdraw  similar large amounts and have to stand in the line are exposed to risk of being  noticed  and targeted   by anti-social elements in and outside the bank.  It is, therefore, decided that the Branch Manager/ Head Cashier should make appropriate arrangements for providing immediate payment/ receipt facility  to such customers, if necessary by opening a separate counter. For providing such service to the customers, as  and when necessary, the Head Cashier/ Branch Manager should keep appropriate surveillance on work of the Cash Counters and customer comfort/discomfort outside the counter.

 

  M.C. Shetty

                                                                                    Managing Director
