OFFICE ORDER NO. 225 DATED 15TH FEBRUARY, 2007

 

 

Sub : Registration under Multi-State Co-op. Societies Act –

Association of employees in the

Management Decision Making Process

 

          Consequent, upon the registration of our bank under Multi-State Co-op. Societies Act, 2002 w.e.f. 11th January 2007, section 73BB of  the Maharashtra Co-op. Societies Act no longer applies to our bank. There is no mandatory provisions similar to the one in the said section in the Multi State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002 for the nomination of staff directors.  However, section 42 of Multi State Co-operative Societies Act 2002 lays down that the multi state co-op. society shall devise such procedure, as may be specified in the Bye-laws or in the administrative instructions, for association of the representatives of employees of such Multi-State Co-operative Societies at such level or bodies as may be specified in the bye-laws or the instructions issued in this regard, in the management decision making process. In this connection, the Special General Body Meeting held on 2nd December, 2006 authorized the Board to make necessary provision for association of the employees in the management decision making process. 

         In exercise of the powers under the provisions of the Section 42 of MSCS Act and the authorization given by the Special General Body Meeting held on 02.12.2006 the Board has decided in its meeting held on 25th January, 2007 as under :

Two employee representatives, from amongst full time confirmed salaried employees of the bank, will be appointed, to attend Board Meetings and participate in the management decision making process. They will participate in the deliberations and also share responsibilities inherent in the management decisions.  They will also be entitled to draw sitting fees and other allowances, etc like other directors of the bank.  But, they will not be entitled to be elected as Chairman / Vice-Chairman or be entitled to vote in the election of the Chairman / Vice-Chairman.

         Accordingly, Mr. S.N. Shetty, Accountant and Mr. K.T. Kadam, Asst.Accountant are appointed as staff representative director with immediate effect.

         The above administrative instructions will continue to be in force till further orders.

 

          M. C. Shetty

          Managing Director
