11. INCREMENTS :

A. (i) ANNUAL INCREMENTS :

All the confirmed Executives shall be granted annual increments from the first day of the month in which he is confirmed in the bank’s service.  All the new entrants shall be fitted at the starting basic pay of the respective grade. The first annual increment shall be granted after one year’s confirmed service.

 

(ii)     STAGNATION INCREMENTS :

Those Executives who will reach the end of their respective scale after the signing of this Office Order shall be given one last drawn increment in the alternative year of stagnation, in the said pay scales. 

 

Those executives whose service is extended beyond age of 60 years, their salary will be fixed at the date of 60th year (Original date of Retirement) and the same salary and allowance will be given during period of extension.

 

B)      Increments for Additional Educational Qualifications :

d.   LL.M.                                                                        One

e.    Chartered Accountant                                              One

f.    Chartered Financial Analyst (From ICFAI)                  One

g.   Certified Treasury Manager (From ICFAI)                   One

h.   Certified Investment Manager (From ICFAI)      One

i.    DTRIM (from IIBF)/MBA / PGDBM/MMS             One each

j.    Cost & Works Accountant (ICWA)                     One

m.  Cisco Corporation Course (CCNP   )                  One

n.   Oracle Course – OCA/OCP/ OCM/ OCDBA            One Each

o.   Certified Information Security Manager(CISM)  One

p.  Certified Information System Security Professional

               (CISSP)                                                                      One

After submitting the proof for passing of the above qualifying examinations, increments shall be released to the Executive from the 1st of the next month, following the month in which the results of the exam are declared.
