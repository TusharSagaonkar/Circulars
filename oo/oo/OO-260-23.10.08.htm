Office Order No. 260 dated 23 October 2008

Transaction Charges for RTGS and NEFT Facilities

 

          Please refer to our Office Order No. 235 dated 04.10.2007 and Office Order No. 246 dated 11.04.2008 and                        Page No.9, Item No. 9A for collection of outstation cheque (IBC/OBC) informed in ‘Service Charges Manual’ w.e.f. 01.05.2008.

 

2.  Reserve Bank of India, in exercise of the powers conferred by Section 18 of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 (Act 51 of 2007), without prejudice to permissions/approvals, if any required under any other law have issued certain directives vide their Circular DPSS.CO.No. 811/03.01.03 (P)/ 2008-09 dated 08.10.2008 which have been approved by our Board in its meeting held on 22.10.2008.  Accordingly we advise charges for Electronic Products and for outstation cheque collection as under:-

 

2.1 Charges for Electronic Products:

a) Inward RTGS/NEFT/ECS transactions – free, no charges to be levied.

 

b) Outward transactions:

i)

RTGS – Rs.1 to 5 Lakh

Not exceeding Rs.25 per transaction

 

Rs.5 lakh and above

Not exceeding Rs.50 per transaction

 

ii)

NEFT – Up to Rs.1 Lakh

Not exceeding Rs.5 per transaction

 

Rs.1 lakh and above

Not exceeding Rs.25 per transaction

 

c) These charges shall be applicable for all types of transactions, including inter-bank funds transfers.

 

d) Cheque return charges laid down vide our Circular OC No. 452 dated 30.08.2008 will remain unchanged.

 

2.2 Outstation cheque collection charges:

 

a)

Up to Rs.10,000

Not exceeding Rs.50 per transaction

 

Rs.10,000 to Rs.1,00,000

Not exceeding Rs.100 per transaction

 

Rs.1,00,001 and above

Not exceeding Rs.150 per transaction

 

b) Charges for return unpaid outstation cheques:-

50% charges as mentioned under Item No.2.2(a) above + actual postages will be recovered from the customers.

c)    The charges mentioned above against item No.2.2 (a) will be all inclusive.  No additional charges such as courier charges, out of pocket expenses, etc., should be lived from the customers.

 

d)   To reduce the clearing cycle and to promote electronic modes of payment, the drawee branches should use electronic modes like RTGS/NEFT, wherever available, to remit proceeds to the collecting bank branch.

 

e)   Branches will make increased use of Speed Clearing and National Clearing facilities for providing efficient service.

 

3. The above charges are applicable only to transactions originated and payable within India.

 

4. The provisions of this circular shall not be applicable to cash handling charges levied by banks for handling large value cash transactions.

 

5. No branch should refuse to offer the products to its customers or decline to accept outstation cheques deposited by its customers for collection.

 

6.    The above instructions will come into force with immediate effect.

 

 

M. C. Shetty

Managing Director
