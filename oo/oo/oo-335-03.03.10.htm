Office Order No. 335  dated 3rd March, 2010 

Provision of Personalised Cheque Book (PCB).

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          The Board in its meeting held on 19.02.2010 has decided that Bank should issue Personalised Cheque Books to account holders.

          The issue of Cheque Books is centralized at MIS- Dept., Vashi (3rd Floor) and will be under the supervision of Manager- MIS Dept.

                     

The modalities and the procedure for issue of Personalised Cheque Books (PCBs) is enumerated as below:                                           

1)   Guidelines and procedure to be followed at Branches :

(i)           PCB will be issued to account holders as stated herein:

a)    SB account holders maintaining minimum average balance of Rs.25,000/- during the past 3 months

b)    CA holders maintaining minimum average balance of Rs.25,000/-  during the past 3 months.

       Note : To verify the average balance, branches can simply see the latest statement of accounts for 3 months and decide the eligibility. The deficiency in average balance upto 10% may be considered for issue of PCB.

c)    FLXLN/STCC : for all accounts

d)    Cash Credit /SOD with Credit limit of Rs.5 Lakh and above.

e)    NRE-SB- All account holders

f)     NRO-SB- All account holders

g)    The procedure for requisition of Cheque Book(s) though Internet will be started in due course for all Account Holders using Internet Banking Facility.

 

(ii)         PCBs will be issued only on specific request from account holder.

(iii)       Branches will accept the request from account holder on the same format prescribed for “Request letter for cheque book” for first time and subsequently on the cheque book requisition slip. Request has to be made for 30/60/90 leaves.

(iv)        PCB will be charged @ Rs.2/- per cheque leave. The amount shall be collected either in cash or to be debited to account if authorised by the account holder and credited to branch PL Account No. 10043 under the head Cheque Book Charges (additional/unused) A/c. before generating the request. IT Dept. will come out with programme for centralized collection of cheque book charges in due course.

(v)      After due scrutiny and signature verification on cheque book requisition  “form” / “slip” and after having received the charges,  branch  will feed the details of request in OMNI as directed in AN-1 attached to this circular.

(vi)    While feeding cheque book details, Branch should select/feed “Signatory Title” correctly and carefully as stated below :-

(a)     SB Account:- There is no “Signatory Title” field hence no problem.

(b)     CA Account/CC Account/FLXLN/STCC/SOD Account:       

  (I) If account is maintained by an individual, then nothing is to be selected in the field of "Signatory Titles" and this field should be left blank. This will enable program to print personal name on cheque leaves at the Right bottom. Branches are directed to take utmost care on choosing this field.

 (II)  If account is maintained by firm, trust, company etc. then select correct designation of signatory as per specimen signature for that account and enter the same designation from the look up available for “Signatory Title”. This will enable printing of signatory as per following format:  

                                                                                              

For ABC Co. Ltd.              For ABC Co. Ltd.               For ABC Co. Ltd.

                                Or                                    Or          

Managing Director                   Director                              Partner

 

A lapse by branch could misprint a cheque book entirely at branch risk.

 

(vii)  Branch will inform to customer to collect cheque book from Branch on 4th working day even though on 3rd working day branches will be receiving the printed cheque books from MIS Dept.

(viii) In emergency branch will issue non-personalised cheque book to a customer (may be of 10 leaves or so, which will be made available by Development Dept.)

 

2)  Guidelines and Instructions to MIS Dept.:-

(i)       MIS Dept. shall educate staff working on PCB generation to maximise usage of stationeries by properly loading and handling them and usage of machine by taking 3 cheque books at a time (of same quantity)

(ii)     Every day morning MIS Dept. will upload the branch sequential file to the PCB- Server maintained at MIS. This activity is to be done from a OMNI PC as directed in

ANNEXURE - 1.

(iii)   Once the data is down loaded into PCB server, MIS Dept. will generate cheque books branch wise/head wise as per the convenience/requirement.

(iv)    Cheque issues will be made from 1 to 99999 for each account holder with unique code of his own account number in MICR line so that there is no possibility of duplicate series. OMNI takes care of previous issues and allows fresh issues from that level. Similarly PCB system also maintains account wise record of cheque issues.

(v)      The cheque books have to be bound after checking them properly for each account number to avoid interchanging of cheque leave(s).

(vi)    MIS Dept. will pack the cheque books securely and deliver them to respective branches on the same day, through a special courier to Clearing Dept. from where they will be dispatched to respective  branches  on 3rd working day  morning (from the date of request).

 

MIS Dept. will print two copies of Branch-wise cheque book issue report from PCB system to be sent to Branches alongwith cheque books. The courier person will get acknowledgement on one such copy and handover to MIS Dept. who will maintain it in concerned branch file.

 

MIS Dept. will take out a consolidated cheque issue report for the day and cross check with branch wise issue and keep it in records with the signatures of issuer and in-charge of MIS Dept.

(vii)  MIS Dept. will keep track of printing machine status and inform to vendor in routine. The two printers to be used alternatively for one week each.

(viii) MIS Dept. to keep cheque stationery under lock and key with authorized person and to maintain report of issues both physical as well as of printing and to be reconciled daily so as to avoid misuse of the stationery.

(ix)    Machine will be used only by authorized employees of MIS Dept. having knowledge of system. It has to be closed securely after completion of printing work of the day.

(x)     Blank cheque leaves to be ordered when the balance of the stock level comes to 50,000 leaves. Order will be in a lot of 2,00,000 cheque leaves.

(xi)    The other instructions to be noted carefully by MIS Dept.

a)   Each machine is under AMC for 6,00,000 cheque leaves or 3 years which ever is earlier.

b)   Print cartridge can print up to 40,000 to 45,000 leaves with perfect clarity but requires monitoring after 40,000/- leaves printing. Cartridge has to be changed periodically through the vendor free of charge up to printing of 6,00,000 cheque leaves and there after @ Rs.25,000/- per cartridge unless we renew the complete contract.

c)    Each machine capacity is 6,00,000 cheque leaves printing free of any cost except stationery. After that usually some spare parts need to be changed to bring the machine to running condition like a new machine. Additional cost would be Rs.20,000/- to Rs.30,000/- per cycle of 6,00,000 leaves. Initially life could be even more.

d)   Machine gives status of usage of printer cartridge on the basis of which demand can be placed well in advance to vendor for cartridge.

e)   Machine gives report of capacity utilization of cheque leaves printing to know time of AMC renewal.

f)    System implemented for PCB is of M/s Dadajee Dakjee Infotech Pvt. Ltd. having office at 367, Sane Guruji Marg, Agripada, Mumbai Central, Mumbai - 400 008 and Phone No. 43330555. It comprises of printer comprehensive of MICR cartridge and all other parts up to warranty period and their interface software with Infrasoft. Any breakdown calls have to be elevated to them directly by MIS Dept.

Note: These cares are to be taken on daily basis through a routine procedure to be adopted by MIS In-charge.

 

3)  General:-

(i)      Initial implementation for 3 months will be looked after by I.T. Dept. Thereafter the overall monitoring of all activities under PCB will be done by Development Dept.

(ii)     On the basis of Board decision earlier, we have worked out a 15 digit Account Number which will be automatically printed on Personalised Cheque Books. First Three digits will be Branch Code, next 4 digits will be Product Code and balance 8 digit existing account number. Branch need not feed it. Automatically it will be printed on the basis of product mapping parameter set in the system. While mapping products, care has to be taken to have sub code of SB, CC type accounts as per parameter (Example:- SB, Zero Balance SB etc, FLXLN, STCC etc).

(iii)    Whenever NECS are going to come in inward clearing, they will be honoured through product mapping which is developed by  M/s. Infrasoft. Thus account holders can pass on these account numbers to service providers.

(iv)     Inspection Dept. will inspect activities under PCB periodically during their routine inspection.

             

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff at the Branch/Department and ensure compliance.

Annexure – 1

 

Instructions for Branches :-

 

When ever there is a request by a customer for Personalised Cheque Book, Branch has to

Enter the request in the following screen.

 

Masters à Personal Chq. Module à Request Master

 

Screen shot of Request Master

 

 

Important instructions :

1.)    In alpha code select  : PPP

2.)    Start Instrument No : System will automatically generate instrument no.

(Method of instrument generation, we have set an unique number in the system for each product to avoid duplication of instrument no. While entering cheque book request, first digit of instrument no. will always start with the unique number which we have set for that particular product)

3.)    At Par Y/N  : N ( No)

4.)    Joint Holder YN : in case of Joint Account set as Y if joint name to be printed on cheque book

5.)    Sr. No :  Set the Sr. No. of Joint account holder, whose name to be printed on the cheque book.

6.)    Book Size: 30 leaves for SB type products,   and 30/ 60/ 90 leaves for Current, Cash Credit type products.

7.)    Signatory Title : In case of CC and CD type account, if account belongs to a firm then select signatory title from the pick list available under look up and if account is in individual name then signatory title field should be kept blank.

 In case of SB Type accounts system does not ask for Signatory Title.

8.)    Courier on Day   :  At present keep blank.

 

 

Instruction for MIS Dept., :-

 

 

After creation of request master, on every subsequent working day, a sequential file need to be generated at Central Cheque book Printing Dept.(MIS Dept)  at Vashi.  This sequential file will contain the details of cheque book request of all branches for all products.

 

Sequential generation program:

 

Masters à Personal Chq. Module à Seq. Generation for printing

 

Screen shot of sequential generation

 

By using above sequential file Personalized cheque will be printed at Central Cheque book printing Dept., Vashi through Dadajee Dhakjee System.

                        

 

Note : 15 digits account no. will be printed on the cheque book.

(Bifurcation of 15 digits is, 3 digits for branch code, 4 digits for product code in numeric and 8 digits for account no)     Last six digits of Account no will be printed as Special Series No on MICR band.                                           

 

            

After printing of cheque book one more sequential file will be generated in Dadajee Dhakjee System. The same is to be uploaded in OMNI software through following option

 

Masters à Personal Chq. Module à Seq. upload

 

At this stage actual instrument stock register as well as issue register will be created in OMNI system.
