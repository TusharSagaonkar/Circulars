OFFICE ORDER NO. 212 DATED 20TH MAY, 2006

 

Sub : List of Duties and Responsibilities of the

       Dy.Gen.Manager-in-charge, IT Dept

 

            Consequent upon creation of the special post of Dy.Gen.Manager as In-charge of the IT Department and posting of Mr. D.G. Kurlawala as the Dy.Gen.Manager of IT Department, it has been decided to lay down the duties and responsibilities of the Dy.Gen.Manager, IT Department.  Implementation of the CBS project and anywhere banking facilities and also the implementation of various IT projects of the bank will be his responsibility as the Dy.Gen.Manager.  He will adhere scrupulously to the approvals / instructions of higher authorities and the Board of Directors while discharging his duties and implementing IT projects of the bank.  The duties and responsibilities of the Dy.Gen.Manager of IT Dept are specifically enumerated as under :

1.      Overall supervision of functioning of IT Department and implementation of various policies of the bank, RBI, Co-operative Department and other regulatory authorities including IS security policy.

2.      Maintenance of hardware, software and various licences procured by the bank for implementation of the IT projects.

3.      Establishing Annual Maintenance Contracts with various service providers in the month of March every year so as to ensure uninterrupted AMC services of hardware and software since 1st April of the next year.

4.      Taking review of services provided by the AMC vendors and ensure that services are provided strictly in terms of AMC agreements.  To make payment of AMC charges as approved by the higher authorities within his delegated power and refer those matters to higher authorities, which are within delegated power of higher authorities / Board of Directors.

5.      Preparing of empanelment of vendors every year in respect of IT related services by following due procedure including advertisement in newspaper calling for expression of interest, etc for the same.  He will call for tenders for purchase of hardware, software from the panel vendors by following due process/procedure of the bank.  He will open tenders in the presence of atleast two officers of IT Department.  Enter details of the tenders in the Register of Tenders.  After communicating with the vendors and verification of the tender prices with prevailing market rates of hardware and software, he will award contracts to the vendors as per his delegated authority.  He will record in writing in each case justification for awarding the contract to the particular party.  In case of amount higher than his delegation, prepare a note and submit it to the higher authorities with his recommendations for approval / rejection of the tender / tenders.

 

6.      After receipt of hardware and software and other equipments related to IT Department, he will arrange to verify through IT officials whether supply of hardware / software is as per the purchase order.  After proper installation of the same and verification supported by certification from IT Officer, he would make payment of the bill or recommend it to higher authorities as the case may be.

7.      He will ensure that all existing as well as newly purchased / acquired IT related equipment, hardware, etc is fully insured and is always kept so insured.

8.      He will maintain proper inventory of software licenses and the hardware installed at branches / offices / departments and bank as a whole.

9.      He will also ensure that the inventory of hardware and software of each establishment of the bank tallies with the Dead Stock Register and arrange for disposal of unused hardware on ongoing basis, preferably at half yearly intervals. He will maintain a proper record of disposal of unused hardware, refer to higher authorities for write-off if required.

10.  He will enter into various Contracts / Agreements with the vendors and for this purpose he will seek the help of Law Officer (Development Dept) and Sr.Manager (Legal & Recovery Dept) whenever required.  He will also ensure that BGs or corporate guarantees are obtained while purchasing hardware, software and other IT related services as per the requirements of higher authorities.

11.  He will implement IS security policy and monitor the security of bank’s data.  As regards the maintenance of backup data storage, he will also ensuer that data on cartridge tapes, hard disks / CDs, etc are stored properly at branches / zonal office and that back-up data of earlier years is made easily available to the higher authorities as and when required.  Similarly, back up data meeting the requirements of customers as per the old record preservation procedure should be arranged through branches / offices and maintained for the bank as a whole.

12.  He will ensure installation of Anti Virus Software at all branches / zonal offices / departments so that there is no loss to the data at any time.  He will preserve the licence in this regard.

13.  He will be responsible for maintaining of leased lines and ISDN lines and introduction of latest media of transmission of data in respect of CBS and TBA to facilitate Any Branch Banking, Anywhere Banking, Internet Banking, etc.

14.  It will be his responsibility to ensure introduction / adaptation of the latest technology in respect of hardware, software and transmission of data / voice mail.

15.  He will also be responsible for working of the Share Department maintaining records of direct and indirect liability of the members, generating various returns of RBI which is presently attended by the Share Dept.  

16.  He will be responsible for collecting information required for building up MIS database from branches / zonal offices / departments, etc and keeping such MIS data updated on ongoing basis and submitting the information to the Management from time to time according to the prescribed time schedule/s.  He will ensure that such data base is built up according to the specifications/ requirements laid down by RBI and communicated by the Head Office.

17.  It will be his responsibility to ensure that the software packages installed and in use at branches and other office establishments of the bank are appropriate and suitable for the related job requirements and they operate free from any fault and are not misused for storing and/or generating incorrect or false data / information.

18.  He will also be responsible for developing or arranging to get developed new software programmes or modify the existing programmes as and when required.

19.  He will be responsible for convening the meetings of the Computerisation Committee, preparing agenda for such meetings, recording and keeping record of their minutes and taking follow up action for implementing the decisions and submitting periodical review in respect of the Committee’s working to the Board.

20.  He will be responsible for general supervision of the work handled by IT Department staff within the Department and during their field duties.  He will exercise similar supervision over working of the staff of the AMC contractors and any other outside agency who are authorized to handle, use, maintain, repair, etc banks IT systems.  The supervision will be done by him from the angles of the economy, secrecy of the bank’s and customer’s records, vigilance and preventive vigilance.

21.  It will also be his responsibility to submit to the Board a detailed review of the working of IT Department.  The report should cover, among others, progress of the projects in hand, difficulties experienced and irregularities observed in the use and application of IT systems, evaluation of customer response and reactions on the automated services provided by the bank proposals for upgrading the services in line with the developments in knowledge and information technology and their application in the banking industry, etc.

22.  He will be responsible for preparing capital and revenue expenditure budgets of the department for every financial year and place it before the Board and get their approval in the last quarter preceding financial year.

23.  It will also be his responsibility to control expenditure of IT Department and keeping it within the budget provisions.

24.  He will ensure upgradation / updating of information put up on the bank’s website from time to time.

25.  In addition to the above, he will discharge any other duties and responsibilities that may be entrusted to him by the higher authorities from time to time.

   

                                                                                                M.C. Shetty

                                                                                          Managing Director
