Office Order No.  231 dated   28 June  2007

 

 

Leave record of the employees – Decentralisation of work

 

            The Personnel Dept., have recently undertaken critical review of the daily working of the department and existing systems and procedures followed for carrying on of their functional responsibilities.  One of the existing procedures that was reviewed related to the maintenance of the leave record of the employees working in branches / departments.  Presently, branches / departments send every month a copy of the muster roll alongwith leave record of the employees to the Personnel Dept with whom the work of keeping the leave record of the staff is centralized.  On a review of the system, as mentioned above, it has been decided to decentralize maintenance of leave record including the activity of leave posting in respect of employees posted at branches / departments.  Accordingly, branches / departments should maintain leave record of the employees attached to their office and do the posting of leaves of all kinds i.e. Casual Leave, Sick Leave and Privilege Leave immediately on sanction and maintain the record with themselves.  The copy of the muster roll which is presently sent to Personnel Dept every month may hereafter be forwarded to Personnel Dept once in three months.  However, only those branches/departments where there are any instances of employees whose leave record is bad and in whose case salary-deductions are to be effected, should fax the details of the leave record (without Muster Roll copy) in respect of the concerned employees only, every month, as at present to the Personnel Dept.

 

The new procedure will be effective from the quarter beginning 1st July, 2007.

  

                                                                                                  M.C. Shetty

                                                                                          Managing Director
