Detection and Impounding of Counterfeit Notes

Designation of district wise Nodal Bank officer Establishment of Forged Note Vigilance Cell

Please refer to Office Order No. 361 dated 21st December, 2010, Circular OC No.564 dated 12th August, 2011 and Circular OC No.722 dated 04th September, 2015 wherein detailed guidelines on ‘Detection & Reporting of Counterfeit Currency Notes’ and procedure to be followed by Cash Dept.(CCD) at Head Office and Cashier/Head Cashier at Branches, have been issued.

Reserve Bank of India vide Master Circular No. RBI/22/2016-17 with Ref.No. DCM(FNVD)G-6/16.01.05/2016-17 dated 20th July, 2016 has issued latest guidelines on ‘Detection and Impounding of Counterfeit Notes’. In Para No. 7 of above referred Master Circular RBI has advised all banks to appoint designated ‘District-wise Nodal Bank Officer’ and also advised to notify the names of Designated Nodal Officers to the concerned Regional office of Reserve Bank of India and Regional / Nodal Police Authorities. Further in Para no. 8 of above referred Master Circular the RBI has also advised all Banks to establish ‘Forged Note Vigilance Cell’ at the Head office of the bank.

Accordingly, in the Board Meeting held on 29.07.2016 it was decided to designate ‘Nodal Bank Officers’ and establish ‘Forged Notes Vigilance Cell’ in the Bank.

 Branches, CCDs, Designated Nodal Officers, Principal Officer KYC /AML Cell, Incharge of Forged Notes Vigilance Cell have to observe the following guidelines:

1   Processing of Counterfeit Note(s) detected in cash tendered by the customers at the branch.

1.1 Banknotes tendered over the counter / received directly at the branches/CCDs through bulk tenders should be examined for authenticity through machines.

 1.2 No credit to customer’s account is to be given for counterfeit notes, if any, detected in the tender received over the counter or at the branches/CCDs.

 1.3 In no case, the counterfeit notes should be returned to the tenderer or destroyed by the bank Branches/CCDs. Failure of the banks to impound counterfeit notes detected at their end will be construed as willful involvement of the bank concerned, in circulating counterfeit notes and penalty will be imposed for violation of RBI Directives and concerned Cashier/Head cashier and In-charge of the Branches/CCDs will be held responsible.

 1.4 A counterfeit note detected in the cash received from the customer across the counter shall be impounded in presence of the tenderer.

 1.5 Stamping and impounding of the counterfeit banknote

(a)      Notes determined as a counterfeit one, shall be stamped as "COUNTERFEIT BANKNOTE" & should be impounded in the prescribed format. ( Annexure I )

(b)     Each such impounded note shall be recorded in a separate register, under authentication of Head Cashier and Branch-in-charge / Manager.

1.6 Issue of Receipt to the Tenderer

a)   An acknowledgement receipt in the format as per Annexure - II should be issued to the tenderer by the Branch, after stamping the note as ‘”Counterfeit Note”. The receipt in running serial numbers, should be in duplicate and should be authenticated by the Cashier/Head Cashier as well as by the tenderer. Notice to this effect should be displayed prominently at the branches near the cash counter for information of the public as under :

'If counterfeit note(s) is/are found in the cash tendered at branch cash counter, such note(s) will be impounded and receipt there against will be issued to the tenderer. In no case counterfeit note shall be returned to the tenderer or destroyed.'

b)  Acknowledgement receipt is to be issued even in cases where the tenderer is unwilling to countersign the receipt with a suitable remark in the register.

1.7 Examination of the Banknotes before Issuing over Counters, Loading of

 ATMs and Remitting to Currency Chest.

a)   The Branches should re-align their cash management in a manner so as to ensure that cash receipts in the denominations of Rs.100 and above are not put into re-circulation without the notes being machine processed for authenticity. Branches/CCDs should note that non-compliance will be construed as violation of RBI Directive.

b)   In order to obviate complaints regarding receipt of counterfeit notes through ATMs, and to curb circulation of counterfeit notes, it is imperative to put in place adequate safeguards/checks before loading ATMs with notes. Dispensation of counterfeit notes through the ATMs would be construed as an attempt to circulate the counterfeit notes by the bank concerned.

c)    Detection of counterfeit notes in CCDs/Branches remittances is also liable to be construed as willful involvement of the CCDs/Branches concerned in circulating Counterfeit Notes and may attract special investigation by police authorities.

d)   Penalty at 100% of the notional value of counterfeit notes, in addition to the recovery of loss to the extent of the notional value of such notes, will be imposed under the following circumstances:

i)     When counterfeit notes are detected in the soiled note remittance of the bank.

ii)    If counterfeit notes are detected at Branches/CCDs during Inspection/Audit by RBI.

1.8       The definition of 'counterfeiting' in the Indian Penal Code covers currency notes issued by a foreign government authority as well. In case of suspected foreign currency note received for opinion from the police and government agencies, etc., they should be advised to forward the case to the Interpol Wing of the CBI, New Delhi after prior consultation with them.

1.9       The Government of India has framed Investigation of High Quality Counterfeit Indian Currency Offences Rules, 2013 under Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act (UAPA), 1967. The Third Schedule of the Act defines High Quality Counterfeit Indian Currency Note. Activity of production, smuggling distribution and circulation of High Quality Counterfeit Notes has been brought under the ambit of UAPA, 1967.

2 Reporting of Data to RBI, Role of branches :

a)   Data on counterfeit notes detected by all the branches/CCDs of the bank shall be reported in the prescribed format, on a monthly basis. A statement (Annex III, IV & VI) showing the details of counterfeit notes detected in the bank branches/CCDs during the month should be forwarded to the Nodal Officer i.e. Asst. General Manager of respective Zonal Office/Regional Office, who in turn will forward to the Principal Officer KYC/AML Cell, Vashi and Copy to the Asst. General Manager forged Notes Vigilance Cell, Vashi before 4th of Next Month for onward submission to concerned Issue Office of Reserve Bank, so as to reach them by 7th of the next month.

b)  A "NIL" report may be sent in case no counterfeit note has been detected during the month.

3 Designating Nodal Bank Officer:

RBI has advised all banks to designate Nodal Bank Officer, district-wise and notify the same to the concerned Regional Office of RBI and Police Authorities. All cases of reporting of counterfeit note detection should be done through the Nodal Bank Officer. The Nodal Bank Officer will also serve as the contact point for all counterfeit note detection related activities.

3.2            Roles and Responsibilities of Nodal Bank Officers :-

a)  To co-ordinate and gather information from Branches/CCDs under their control before 4th of every succeeding month.

b) For cases of detection of counterfeit notes upto 4 pieces, in a single transaction, reported by Branches/CCDs under their jurisdiction as mentioned in Para 3.1, a consolidated report in the prescribed format (Annex III), the Nodal Bank Officer is advised to send the report to the police authorities or the Nodal Police Station, along with the suspect counterfeit notes, at the end of every month.

c)  For cases of detection of counterfeit notes of 5 or more pieces, in a single transaction, the counterfeit notes reported by Branches/CCDs should be forwarded by the Nodal Bank Officer to the local police authorities or the Nodal Police Station for investigation by filing FIR in the prescribed format under their jurisdiction as mentioned in Para 3.1, as per prescribed format (Annex IV) on immediate basis. Thus, a branch upon detecting 5 or more notes in a single instance should immediately report the same to the Bank’s Nodal Officer so as to file FIR with Nodal Police Station as designated.

 

d) A copy of the consolidated monthly report and FIR’s should be sent to the Principal Officer, KYC & AML Cell, Vashi for reporting to FIU-IND and RBI with a copy marked to Forged Note Vigilance Cell (FNV Cell), Vashi for their reporting to RBI. The consolidated Zone/Region wise report of previous month should be submitted on or before 4th of succeeding month to both the above Cells.

e)  Acknowledgement of the police authorities concerned has to be obtained for note/s forwarded to them, both as consolidated monthly statement and FIRs, if any. Further, if the counterfeit notes are sent to the police (nodal police station) by insured post, an acknowledgement of receipt thereof from the police should be invariably obtained and kept on record. Proper follow-up of receipt of acknowledgement from the police authorities is necessary for future reference.

f)   In case, any difficulty is faced by the Offices / Branches due to reluctance of the police to receive monthly consolidate statement / filing FIRs, the matter may be sorted out, by the Nodal Bank Officer in consultation with the Nodal Officer of the Police Authority designated to coordinate matters relating to investigation of counterfeit banknotes cases. The list of Nodal Police Stations may be obtained from the respective Regional Office of Reserve Bank.

g) A “NIL” report should be prepared in case there are no Counterfeit Notes that have been detected by the branch during a month and the same be sent to the respective Nodal Officer, PO-KYC/AML Cell & FNV Cell.

                                                                                                      

4    Role and Responsibilities of the Principle Officer KYC/AML Cell of the Bank :

a)  It is decided to designate and entrust the responsibility to Mr. Sanjay V. Wani, Sr.Manager & Principal Officer of the Bank, for serving as the central contact point for co-ordination and compilation of data on detected Counterfeit Notes and its submission to Police Authorities done by the Nodal Bank Officers of respective Zonal and Regional Offices. In addition, the submission of Counterfeit Currency Report to Reserve Bank of India and FIU-IND on monthly basis, before the statutory reporting date is also the responsibility of the Principal Officer, His responsibilities include,

i) Dissemination of instructions issued by the Reserve Bank of India on counterfeit notes to Bank’s branches/CCDs.

ii)  To Monitor the implementation of above instructions.

iii) To compile the data of detected counterfeit notes and its submission to

 Reserve Bank and FIU-IND, New Delhi as per relevant instructions.

iv) Follow-up cases of counterfeit notes, with police authorities / designated nodal officers.

 v) To ensure physical verification of all Counterfeit Notes reported and which

 are lying at Branches/CCDs at half yearly intervals.

 

b) Under Rule 3 of Prevention of Money Laundering Rules, 2005, Principal Officers of banks are required to report information on cash transactions, where forged notes have been used as genuine notes to Director, FIU-IND, Financial Intelligence Unit- India, 6th Floor, Hotel Samrat, Chanakyapuri, New Delhi-110021, within seven working days.

 

5    Establishment of Forged Note Vigilance Cell at Head Office :

 

 Asst. Gen. Manager, IS Audit & Vigilance Dept is appointed as Incharge of Forged Note Vigilance Cell and he is advised to perform the following functions.

a)   To maintain record of reported Counterfeit Currency Notes that has been received from branches/ CCDs through Nodal Officers as per Annexure III, IV and VI.

b)  Sharing the information compiled by KYC/AML Cell with Managing Director/ Chief General Manager and report all cases of acceptance/issue of Counterfeit notes over the counters.

c)   Conducting periodic surprise checks at Branches/CCDs where shortages/ defective /counterfeit notes etc. are detected.

d)  Ensure facilitating detection of Counterfeit Notes. All branches/CCDs/back offices should be equipped with Ultra Violet Lamps/other appropriate bank note sorting detection machines. In addition, all CCDs should be equipped with verification processing and sorting machines and should be used to their optimum capacity.

e)   Forged Note Vigilance Cell shall submit status report on a quarterly basis, covering the aforesaid aspects to the Chief General Manager, Department of Currency Management, Reserve Bank of India, Central Office, Amar Building, Fourth Floor, Sir P. M. Road, Fort, Mumbai 400 001, and to the Issue office of the Regional office of Reserve Bank under whose jurisdiction the FNV Cell is functioning, within a fortnight from the conclusion of the quarter under report. The said report should be sent by mail. No hard copy need to be sent.

f)    In order to update the record of the addresses of the Forged Note Vigilance Cell, Vashi the bank shall furnish by e-mail, in the prescribed format (Annex V), the address etc. particulars to the Reserve Bank every year, as on 1st July. No hard copy need to be sent.

g)   The procedure of email requires configuration of Outlook 2007 Startup. For installation of the same the Asst. General Manager, Vigilance Dept should take assistance of the IT Department.

h)  Banks should also monitor the patterns / trends of such detection and suspicious trends / patterns should be brought to the notice of RBI /Police authorities immediately.

 

i)    The progress made by banks in detection and reporting of counterfeit notes to Police, RBI, etc. and problems thereof, should be discussed regularly by attending the meetings of various State Level Committees viz. State Level Bankers’ Committee (SLBC), Standing Committee on Currency Management (SCCM), State Level Security Committee (SLSC), etc.

j)    To ensure that guidelines issued by RBI in regard to detection and impounding of counterfeit currency notes are implemented by Branches, CCDs, Nodal Officers and Principal Officer, KYC/AML Cell effectively and ensure its total compliance.

 

6   Preservation of Counterfeit Notes Received from Police Authorities:

a)   All Counterfeit Notes received back from the police authorities/courts may be carefully preserved in the safe custody of the bank and a record thereof be maintained by the branch concerned. Principal Officer of KYC/AML Cell shall also maintain a branch-wise consolidated record of such Counterfeit Notes with a copy to Asst. General Manager, Forged Note Vigilance Cell for their record.

b)   These Counterfeit Notes at Branches/CCDs should be subjected to verification on half yearly basis (on 31st March and 30th September) by the Branch-in-Charge/Incharge of CCDs concerned. These notes should be preserved for a period of three years from the date of receipt from the Police authorities.

c)    Counterfeit Notes may thereafter be sent to the concerned Issue Office of Reserve Bank of India with full details.

d)   Counterfeit Notes, which are the subject matter of litigation in the court of law should be preserved with the Branch/CCDs concerned for three years after conclusion of the court case.

7 Detection of Counterfeit Notes - Training of Staff:

a)   It is necessary to ensure that the cash handling staff at branches/CCDs is fully conversant with the security features of a banknote and all instructions regarding Cash Management issued from time to time. Designs of bank Notes issued by Reserve Bank of India since 1967 is as per Annexure VII.

b)   Principal of Staff training College in consultation with Principal Officer KYC/ AML Cell and Asst. General Manager Vigilance Department is advised to organize one day crash programmes on the security features of banknotes and Cash Management for concerned staff members. If necessary, he may take assistance of the officers from the nearest Issue Office of RBI.

 

Please note that there is no change in existing procedure of detection and impounding of counterfeit notes. Only the process of reporting to Police Authority is to be done through the Bank’s Nodal Officer and a copy of the same be sent to KYC/AML Cell for onward reporting to RBI & FIU-IND & to FNV Cell for record purpose. The present procedure followed by the KYC /AML Cell for reporting to RBI and FIU-IND shall remain unchanged. All other instructions and guidelines contained in Office Order 361 dated 21st December, 2010, Circular OC No.564 dated 12th August, 2011 and Circular OC No.722 dated 04th September, 2015 will remain unchanged. The additional procedure of reporting through the Bank’s Nodal Officer should be strictly adhered to by all the Branches/CCDs. The instructions detailed above shall come in to force with immediate effect.

 

 In-charge of Branches/CCDs are advised to bring the contents of above Circular to the knowledge of their Staff members and ensure its strict compliance.
