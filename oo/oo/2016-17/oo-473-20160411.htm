Office Order No. 473 dated 11th April 2016

Recovery Action Plan for the year 2016-2017

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

          Please refer to Office Order No. 468 dated 19th January 2016 on Recovery Action Plan for the year 2015-16. Considering the high amount of NPAs, it has been decided to continue the Recovery Action Plan with more vigorous efforts during the current financial year. This calls for continuous follow-up with Irregular and NPA accounts with the following objectives:-

 

1.   To prevent fresh NPA accounts.

2.   To recover maximum amount in Irregular and NPA accounts.

3.   Up-gradation of NPA accounts.

 

a)      The distribution of NPA accounts for visits, follow-up, issue of Notices and visit reports should be made among all staff and officials at branches as under :-

 

(i)

Loans Upto Rs.3.00 Lakh

(Irregular/NPA/ARB/SRO)

:

All Clerks & Peon will be allotted accounts equally. Asst. Accountant/ Accountant will head the teams. They will monitor performance of each staff on day-to-day basis.

(ii)

Loans Above Rs.3.00 Lakh upto Rs.50.00 Lakh

(Irregular/NPA/ARB/SRO)

:

All accounts to be allotted equally among Asst. Accountant/ Accountant. Second Manager/ Branch Incharge will monitor these accounts.

(iii)

Loans Above Rs. 50.00 Lakh

(Irregular/NPA/ARB/SRO)

:

Second Manager/Branch Incharge will monitor these accounts.

(iv)

Cash Credit Accounts

(Irregular/NPA/Inoperative ARB/SRO)

:

Second Manager/Branch Incharge will monitor these accounts.

In case of single Manager, some of the CC accounts will be allotted to the Accountant.

         

 

Monitoring of Accounts means to visit the loanee and sureties, preparation of Reports and continuously follow-up telephonically/through written Notices with the loanee and sureties for recovery of Bank's dues.

 

          The above distribution of accounts is in the nature of guidance. The Incharge of the Branch is authorized to distribute the accounts among all staff members taking into account the work load at the Branch and the capacity of each staff member. He is authorized to make necessary changes. However, it should be ensured that all staff members are involved in the recovery work.

 

They will visit the loanee and sureties, prepare Reports and continuously follow-up telephonically/through written Notices with the loanee and sureties for recovery of Bank's dues.

 

 

b)     Procedure to be followed at the Branches:-

 

               i.      The Incharge of the Branch shall hold the Meeting of the staff members and form the Teams consisting of 4-5 Clerks and Sub-Staff at the concerned Branch. The teams will be headed by Asst. Accountant/Accountant at the concerned branch. The Asst. Accountant/Accountant shall also follow-up the accounts allotted to them.

 

              ii.      Distribute amongst all staff all Irregular/NPA/ARB/SRO Accounts including Managers/Sr. Managers/Asst. Gen. Managers.

 

            iii.      The Leader of each Team (Asst. Accountant/Accountant) shall take review of recovery work done by the staff of his Group on daily basis and shall report to the Branch Incharge on weekly basis.

 

             iv.      The Incharge of the Branch shall take review of the performance of each Group on weekly basis and shall submit report to concerned Zonal Office on fortnightly basis.

 

              v.      If any of the Official/staff to whom the recovery work is allotted is not co-operating or not completing the work allotted to him/her, the concerned Branch Incharge shall immediately report the name of such staff to Dy. General Manager/Asst. Gen. Manager of concerned Zone/Regional Office who will take necessary action and further report to HRM Dept. The copy of report to Zonal Office be sent to the Asst. Gen. Manager, Secretarial Dept., HO for the perusal of the Managing Director.

 

             vi.      The recovery efforts would involve personal visits to Loanees and Sureties, issue of Notices as noted above and preparing detailed Visit Reports as per attached format. The Report should contain the details of discussion with the Loanee and Sureties etc.

 

           vii.      Team Leader at Branch is responsible for performance of each member under him and hence it is his/her responsibility to report to higher authorities about non-performance or non-co-operation by team members.

The assignment of distribution of accounts amongst all staff members is to be completed on or before 18th April 2016 by all branches.

 

Special follow-up in Gold Loan and SOD against gold ornaments :-

Despite guidance/instructions to these loanees, the repayment is not forthcoming regularly. With the result, most of the accounts become NPAs.

 

It is therefore necessary to focus on recovery in loan and SOD against gold ornaments and to ensure that the accounts are upgraded as Standard Accounts or timely action is taken for sale of gold ornaments.

It should be an endeavour of Branch Incharge to ensure that there are very few Gold Loan NPAs at the end of every month.

 

c)     Renewal/Review of Unrenewed/Inoperative CC accounts and incidences of fraud etc.

(i)      To review the unrenewed CC accounts and initiate action for renewal of the CC account or to pursue action for recovery of outstanding balances in the account. The Branch Incharge should ensure that the borrower should submit renewal application one month before the expiry date and ensure that the renewal formalities are completed before expiry date of the relative limit.

 

(ii)    To review Inoperative CC account once in a month and contact accountholders, ascertain the reasons for non-operation of the accounts and take necessary steps for revival/regularization of account.

 

(iii)   It is only after exhausting all actions at branch level and where it is considered absolutely necessary to initiate legal action, the cases should be referred to Zonal Offices for further action.

 

(iv)   If any incidence of fraud/tampering with the security by the borrower, raids/ attachment by Govt. Depts. such as Income Tax, Excise, Sales Tax, Enforcement Dept. etc. on the borrower is detected/comes to the knowledge of the Branch Manager, the same should be immediately brought to the notice of concerned Zonal Office as well as General Manager (Credit) and General Manager (Legal & Recovery) with detailed report.

 

All unrenewed/unreviewed CC accounts should be renewed/ reviewed by 15th of respective month. Branches should ensure that all unrenewed/unreviewed CC accounts are renewed/reviewed every month on ongoing basis till 28.02.2017 so that there shall not be any unrenewed/unreviewed CC accounts due upto 31.3.2017.

 

d)     Recovery Team attached to Zonal Offices:-

The Zonal Offices have been provided with special team for recovery work. The Recovery team members attached to Zonal Offices will assist branches as per the direction of Zonal DGM. However, they will perform the duties as per the above duties entrusted to staff and officials at branches.

 

e)     Action Plan for Recovery Dept. (H.O.):-

       

i)        To take appropriate action for filing case before Arbitrator/DRT or concerned Authority/Court in respect of cases, where it has been decided to initiate legal action as per time bound programme.

 

ii)       To ensure that the cases are filed promptly and to attend to various courts and pursue the cases regularly for securing quick disposal.

 

iii)     To obtain Award and Execution Certificate from Co-op. Court, Recovery Certificate from Asst. Registrar, Co-op. Societies in respect of cases already filed under the provisions of MCS Act 1960 and obtain Award/Recovery Certificate from Arbitral Tribunal/DRT as the case may be and get executed the said Awards/Recovery Certificates through appropriate Authority.

 

iv)     To keep liaison with Special Recovery Officer and/or Recovery Officer of DRT and assist them in recovery of dues in cases referred to them.

 

v)       To initiate actions under provisions of SARFAESI Act 2002, as per manual of instructions.

 

vi)     To seize the vehicles financed by bank after receipt of report from the concerned branch and make arrangements for up keep of the vehicles/excavators etc.

 

vii)   To get the valuation done of properties/vehicles/excavators taken into possession through Govt. approved valuer on bank’s panel and get the reserve price fixed from Board on the basis of valuation report.

 

viii)  To dispose of the seized vehicles/Excavators taken into possession after fixing Reserve Price by calling Tenders after obtaining the orders of competent Authority/Board.

 

ix)     To dispose of the properties taken into possession by SRO on the execution of decree/award or under SARFAESI Act after fixing Reserve Price through Public Auction after obtaining the orders of competent Authority/Board.

 

x)       To take appropriate action for the sale of Gold ornaments periodically and regularly.

 

xi)     To take suitable action in respect of other securities such as Shares, NSC, LIC Policies, etc., which are not adjusted at branch level where decision has been taken by the Board to sell/realize the securities and recover the dues.

 

xii)   To place various matters before the Recovery Sub Committee and Recovery Board Meeting as per the present practice and instructions from HO in future.

 

f)      Review of large Suit filed/Decreed Debts (with principal outstanding above Rs.100.00 Lakh) : -

 

The Committee of Executives consisting of Managing Director, General Manager (Legal and Recovery), Dy. Gen. Manager (Central Law), All Dy. Gen. Managers of Legal and Recovery Dept. and Sr. Manager, Legal Dept. will take review of legal/recovery action initiated against the defaulted borrowers, take corrective action to remove the bottle necks, if any for speedy disposal of the matter pending before various forums and recommend to Board additional measures required to be taken for faster recovery.

 

g)     Review of Irregular Accounts:-

 

The Recovery Sub Committee of the Board will take review of all irregular accounts at branches for corrective action and/or Legal/recovery action with limits of Rs.25.00 Lakh and above. Respective DGMs of Zonal Offices will place list of such accounts before Sub-Committee for review.

 

h)     Visits by the members of the Board and Executives:-

As hitherto, the General Manager (Legal and Recovery), Managing Director and Members of the Board would continue to visit the defaulters for expeditious recovery.

 

i)      Follow-up in Prudential Write Off (PWO) Accounts :-

The Incharge of PWD Dept., Manager and other staff members will continue/pursue legal & recovery actions and also follow-up by Notices/visits to each account as done hitherto.

Considering the large number of defaulters files in PWD Dept., staff in HO, Parel and Depts. at Vashi and other HO Depts. will be entrusted the recovery work of accounts in Prudential Write Off Dept. They have to visit the loanee and sureties and make efforts for recovery of these accounts as per the above guidelines. The officials in the category of Managers to Asst. Gen. Managers would also be entrusted some branches with large NPAs for monitoring the performance of those branches and also assisting them in their recovery efforts.

 

The above work will be assigned by the General Manager (Legal & Recovery).

 

The above Action Plan for Recovery of overdues in irregular accounts and dues in NPA/SRO/ARB accounts is to be implemented at all levels. The goal of branches should be ‘Zero’ NPAs or minimum NPAs and of the Bank as minimum NPAs as on 31.03.2017. We enclose the Annexure containing the Roles of Regional/ZOs, Legal & Recovery Dept., DGMs and General Manager of Legal & Recovery Dept. and the Recovery Sub Committee.

 

The Incharge of Branches and DGMs of Zonal Offices are advised to immediately implement the above Recovery Action Plan, review the efforts at each level weekly and ensure that there is drastic reduction in NPAs of the Bank by 31.03.2017.

 

 

 

 

 

V.S. Morye

Managing Director
