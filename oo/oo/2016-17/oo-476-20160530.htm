Office Order No.  476  Dated 30th May , 2016

Delegation of Powers to Dy.Gen.Manager,  Legal & Recovery

and Dy.Gen. Manager, Central Law Dept

 

Vide Office Order No. 421 dated 30th July 2013 Delegation of Powers to Sanction Expenditure has been given to Officers/Executives. Subsequently vide Office Order No. 472 dated 28th March, 2016 enhanced delegation of powers are given to/1Mr.Puneetkumar R. Shetty, General Manager as those exercised by the Managing Director.

At Present there is no specific delegation of powers given to Dy. Gen. Manager – Recovery Department and Dy. Gen. Manager – Central Law Department

In the board meeting held on 25.05.2016, it was decided to give following delegation of powers to Dy. Gen. Manager – Legal and Recovery Department (Shri/1Pradeep V.Kamat), / (Shri Mangesh S.Rane) and Dy. Gen. Manager – Central Law Department (Shri Anthony P. Noronha) towards sanction of following expenditures.

1.   Sanction of Revenue Expenditure – (including Petty Cash) – `. 1.00 Lakh (per month)

2.   Telephone/Electricity Bills/Maintenance Charges of Premises/Water Bills – All actual Bills.

3.   Legal Expenses - At present the schedule of fees of advocates approved by the Board are sanctioned by MD/CGM/GM. Since schedule of fees for Panel Advocates has been approved by Board, Advocate Fees upto `1.00 lakh per bill be approved by Dy. Gen. Manager – Legal and Recovery Department (Shri/1Pradeep V.Kamat), / (Shri Mangesh S.Rane) and Dy. Gen. Manager – Central Law Department (Shri Anthony P Noronha) respectively. The bills exceeding amount of `1.00 lakh should be approved by Managing Director/Chief General Manager/General Manager. In case of Sr.Advocates/Solicitors, the fees will be sanctioned by the Managing Director upto `1.00 Lakh per appearance. In case of charges above `1.00 Lakh, Managing Director will sanction the expenditure with the consent of the Chairman/Vice- Chairman & obtain ratification of the Board in the next Board Meeting. Dy. Gen. Manager – Legal and Recovery Department (Shri Pradeep V.Kamat)/(Shri Mangesh S.Rane) and Dy. Gen. Manager – Central Law Department (Shri Anthony P. Noronha) has to ensure that above Powers delegated to them are exercised with due care & diligence and all the guidelines issued vide office orders from time to time

while exercising the delegated powers by officers and executives should be observed scrupulously Proper record is to be maintained for exercising the above delegated powers. The monthly statement of Delegation of Powers exercised by Dy.Gen.Managers, Recovery Department and Dy. Gen. Manager – Central Law Department. is to be placed before Board meeting of next month for review and ratification.

 

Inspection Department is advised to verify & check all the Bills approved for sanction towards payment of Advocate fees, sanction of Revenue expenditures as well as release of payment of Bills , Revenue expenditures, Petty cash expenses at Recovery Department as well as at Central Law Department, on quarterly basis by assigning the job to Concurrent auditor /through Inspection Dept.
