OFFICE ORDER NO. 487 Dated 4th October 2016

 

TOD/Overdrawal facility to our regular CC accountholders in exceptional cases

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

 

          We have been receiving representation from the branches that existing regular clients with good past record requests for TOD of small amount to honour cheques issued by them for payment of salaries, electricity bills, taxes etc.. The request for TOD is not considered by the branch as it is not under their delegation which has resulted in customer dissatisfaction. The matter was placed before the Board Meeting held on 28.9.2016 and it has been decided to delegate powers for sanction of small TOD facility as under with immediate effect:-

 

Particulars

Dy. Gen. Manager, Zonal Office

Branch In Charge

Temporary Overdraft facility

in CC a/c. upto 7 days

Rs. 5.00 Lakh or 5% of CC limit,

whichever is lower

Rs. 2.00 Lakh or 5% of CC Limit,

whichever is lower

 

          The branches & Zonal Offices are advised to follow below mentioned instructions while considering request for TOD.

(i)  TOD facility to be extended only to the regular clients of the Bank considering their credit need and exigency. This delegation is to be exercised sparingly.

(ii)This TOD shall be over and above interest TOD but excess drawals should always be within arrived drawing power. Branches should avoid overdrawls at the end of the month.

(iii)               The overdrawals or TOD allowed to be cleared within 2-3 days but in any case should not exceed 7 days.

(iv)               The request of the borrower for TOD should not be a routine. In case the borrower requests for frequent TOD on continuous basis, then the Branch Incharge/Zonal DGM shall revisit the sanctioned limit and ascertain whether the borrower is in need of additional working capital.

(v)The Cash Credit accountholder shall submit a letter (as per enclosed format) requesting for TOD/overdrawal with reason and should also maintain the time period by which the account is regularized. Such time period should not exceed 7 days. Further borrower will undertake to pay additional 1% interest rate over the applicable rate of Interest on the overdrawn amount.

(vi)               If request for TOD amount is more than Rs. 2.00 Lakh and upto Rs. 5.00 Lakh, the branches should obtain oral/written approval from Zonal DGM through Email/Fax.

(vii)               Branch Incharge shall inform Zonal Office the date on which the TOD has been cleared.

(viii)             The Branch Incharge shall keep the record of sanction of such TOD’s at Branch level and at the end of the every month will send the data to Zonal office for ratification (as per enclosed format).

(ix) The Dy. Gen. Manager of the Zone shall keep the record of sanction/ratification of such TOD’s and at the end of every month will send the data to Head Office, Credit Dept. by the 5th of succeeding month for placing it before the Board for review.

(x)  Credit Dept., Head Office shall consolidate the data received from Zonal Offices and place it before the Board for ratification.

 

 All the Executives and Officers concerned are advised to exercise the powers judiciously and in conformity with all extant instructions governing grant of Loans & Advances as also the instructions contained in the Credit Policy.
