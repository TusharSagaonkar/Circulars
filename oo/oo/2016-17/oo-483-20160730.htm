Office Order No. 483 dated 30th July, 2016

Contest for Bank’s Staff Members

 Mobile Banking Service & Rupay Debit Card.

         

          Bank has been taking various initiatives to offer technology services to our customers. Accordingly, Bank is offering Rupay Debit Card and Mobile Banking services to customers. Recently, Bank has introduced Bill Payment services through Mobile Banking and the Mobile Banking application which was available on Android playstore is also made available on Apple’s app store for iPhone users.

          Bank’s Mobile Banking app is at par with other Bank’s Mobile banking app. With the introduction of Bill Payment service, the usage of Bank’s Mobile banking service will further increase.

          In order to increase the use of the Mobile Banking service & Rupay Debit Card among the customers, it is important for our Staff members to be conversant with these services and product. Once staff members are comfortable and confident with the Mobile Banking service & usage of Rupay Debit Card, these services can be easily popularized amongst Customers. Bank had launched First Contest for bank staff members on Mobile Banking Services and informed to the staff vide Office Order No. 464 dated 7th January, 2016 and got desirable results.

          Now to get popularize above new services Board in its meeting held on 25th July, 2016 approved to launch a contest for Bank’s Staff Members to encourage use of Mobile Banking Service & Rupay Debit Card.

Detailed guidelines of the contest are as under:-

1.   The contest is for Bank’s employees’ /Officers/ Executives working at Branches, Departments of Head Office, Regional Offices and Zonal Offices.

2.  The contest period will be from 1st August, 2016 to 31st August, 2016

3.   Only primary mobile number of the staff linked to STCC account number under the Bank’s Mobile Banking service and Rupay debit card will be considered for the contest.

4.   Staff members should not misuse the facility & should avoid doing redundant / repetitive transactions.

5.   Decision about authenticity of a transaction will be at Management’s discretion.
