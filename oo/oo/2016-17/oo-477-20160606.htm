Office Order No.477 dated 6th June, 2016

Celebration of 52nd Foundation Day of our Bank  on 25th June, 2016

 

It is decided to celebrate 52nd Foundation Day Function of our Bank on 25th June 2016.  Since last 7 years we are celebrating Foundation Day of our Bank on 25th June every year. Such celebration has helped our Bank to share our achievements with our Shareholders, Depositors and High networth Customers and enhance the image of our Bank.

1. For Celebrating 52nd Foundation Day on 25th June, 2016 the Board in its meeting held on 28th April 2016, has decided as under:-

2. Foundation day will be organized and celebrated at each and every branch and also at Head office on 24th June 2016 (25th June being Bank Holiday). The premises should be kept clean and decorated on the day of celebration so that it looks pleasant and rejoicing.

i)       To arrange , a function at Abhyudaya Bank Hall, Abhyudaya Bank Bldg, Sector No. 17, Vashi, Navi Mumbai -400 705 on 25th June 2016 at 6.00 p.m.

ii)           To invite 500-600 Top Depositors, Borrowers, Panel Advocates, Contractors, Premises owner, Gold Valuers, Office bearers of Co-op. Credit Societies having deposit with us, for the function.

iii)         The function to be attended by Board of Directors, Executives and In-charge of the Branches/ Departments of Mumbai, Navi Mumbai & Thane area.

iv)         It is necessary to present corporate look of our organization to our esteemed guests. Therefore, all the Gents officials & Executives attending the function should wear Suit or Blazer & Lady Officials should be in Sarees.

v)           To launch Health Insurance Service during the function.

                   

Further, to make the function a grand success and to maintain proper protocol for the invitees, dignitaries attending the function, duties have been delegated among various departments, executives & officials as under:-

a)  Secretarial Dept.

i)            To invite Shareholders, Top Depositors, Borrowers, Delegates, Panel advocates, contractors etc. through branches & various departments.

b) Marketing Dept.

i)            To print invitation cards.

ii)           Arrange for entertainment programme

iii)         Arrange coverage of function –Video shooting & photograghs.

 

c)  Development Dept.

i)    Arrangement of Decoration, Flower Decoration etc. at Abhyudaya Bank Hall

ii)     Catering Arrangement of Dinner for appx. 500 delegates.

iii)   Obtaining necessary permission from different authorities for conducting programme.

iv)   Arrangement for Valet Parking.

d)                     Welcome at main Entrance
