Office Order No. 481 dated 4th July, 2016

Changes in Working Hours of  Branches

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Please refer to our Office Order No.454 dated 12.09.2015, and Office Order No. 457 dated 13.10.2015 whereby the changes in working hours of the branches as approved in the earlier Board Meetings have been informed to the branches.

 Now, we have received representations from some of the branches & suggestions from customers for change of working hours for rendering better customer service. Accordingly, after discussion with Employees Union regarding changes of branch timings, in the Board meeting held on 30.06.2016 it has been  decided to change the business hours and the working hours of following branches w.e.f 18.07.2016 as under :-

·        All branches are advised to take a note of the above changes. The above branches where the working hours have been changed are advised to display the Notice Board of revised working hours for the customers information on 06.07.2016.

·        Branches are advised to remove the Old Notice Board of business hours and replace New Notice Board of business hours on 18.07.2016.

·        Development Dept. is advised to make necessary changes in revised working / business hours timings and provide necessary Notice board of revised timings to the branches for display. The Development Dept is also advised to inform the changes of branch working hours to the competent authorities.

·        All instructions & changes in working hours/Business hours of other than above mentioned branches issued vide Office Order No.454 dated 12.09.2015 remain unchanged.

Branches/Depts. are advised to take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents to the notice of all the staff members at branches/departments for strict compliance.
