Office Order No. 319  dated 16th December 2009 

Housing Loan Scheme

 

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

         

         

I)       Margin on Housing Loan Scheme :

 

i)     In terms of our Circular ILA No. 51 dated 1st July 2009 we have instructed our branches to keep margin @ 25% on Agreement Value + Stamp Duty and Registration Charges while granting Loans under Housing Loan Scheme.

         

ii)    Other banks and financial institutions like HDFC are financing to the extent of 85% to 90% of the Agreement Value + Stamp duty and Registration Charges keeping margin of 15% to 10% only. The applicants who desire to obtain Housing Loan from us are being diverted to other banks and HDFC because of our higher margin which is @ 25%.

 

iii)   The matter was put up to the Board in its meeting held on 12.12.2009 and with a view to have healthy competition with other Banks/Financial Institutions  and to give boost to our housing loan scheme and also to increase loans under priority sector, it has been decided to keep margin as under :

iv)   In Kalyan, Dombivali and Badalapur areas most of the Builders are small and not of high repute, therefore Search Report and Title Clearance Report is insisted by all other Banks in the area and the cost amounting to about Rs.1,000/- to Rs.1,500/- thereof is being borne by the Bank and recovered from the Borrower being the Service Charges. The board has also decided to follow the same practice followed by other Banks at our Kalyan, Dombivali, Badalapur and Kankavali branches.

 

II) Housing Loan at Kankavli, Kudal, Malvan, Sawantwadi, Vengurla and other small towns/villages around these places:

 

We are receiving large number of queries for sanction of loans for purchase of flat / house in Kankavli, Kudal, Malvan, Sawantwadi, Vengurla and other small towns/villages around these places from the people who are permanently settled and well established in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane and Pune in their service / business and having their native place in Sindhudurg District and desire to purchase second home in Kankavli, Kudal, Malvan, Sawantwadi, Vengurla and other surrounding places, nearby to their birth place which is generally a village. These people have got good repayment capacity and the loan requirement would be around Rs.10.00 Lakh to Rs.15.00 Lakh. The matter was placed before the Board in its meeting held on 12.12.2009 and it has been decided as under:

i)             Applications of these persons desiring to purchase dwelling unit at Kankavli, Kudal, Malvan, Sawantwadi, Vengurla or at nearby surrounding places will be accepted at our Dadar Branch.

ii)           The loan proposals will be jointly processed by both the Manager, Dadar Branch and the Manager, Kankavli Branch with their mutual coordination as under :

(a)  On receiving information from The Branch Manager Dadar Branch, the Manager/ officials of Kankavli Branch will visit the said property and submit detailed Visit Report to Dadar Branch. They will meet the Builder / Owner (Seller) in person and ensure genuineness of sale of flat/house. They will also adhere to the other instructions given in our Credit Policy as regards the reputation of the Builder, his past record and whether construction is authorised one. The Mortgageability / Title Clearance thereof will be arranged by the Manager Kankavli Branch through the Panel Advocate in Kankavli.

(b)  Visit to residence and business place / service place of the applicant and surety/ies settled / residing at Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, Panvel, Bhiwandi, etc. would be made by staff of Dadar Branch. Taking into consideration the repayment capacity and the financial aspects of the applicant, worth of surety/(ies) and on the basis of Visit Report and mortgageability/ Title Clearance Report submitted by Kankavli Branch, the Manager, Dadar Branch will recommend the loan proposal for sanction to Dy. Gen. Manager (Central Zone)/ General Manager (Credit)/ Managing Director depending on the quantum of loan. The proposal will be submitted directly by the Manager Dadar Branch to the sanctioning Authority. 

                                          

iii)         The Loan will be sanctioned by Dy. Gen. Manager (Central Zone) / General Manager (Credit)/ Managing Director within their discretionary powers, as the case may be.

 

iv)          As the execution of equitable mortgage is not permitted in Kankavli being not notified town in the gazette, the execution of Equitable Mortgage and other documentation will be made by Disbursement Section, Loans & Advances Dept. at Head Office and loan amount will be disbursed from Head Office.

v)            The Loan Account will be maintained at our Kankavli Branch, as such monitoring of property and the follow up for recovery will be the responsibility of Kankavli Branch. However our Legal & Recovery Dept. and Dadar Branch will help them for recovery of loan installments on specific request from Kankavli Branch, if visits to borrowers/sureties are required to be made.

 

Above instructions will come in to force with effect from 12.12.2009.

 

We are sure that with this arrangement we would be in a position to extend housing loans to large number of Borrowers desiring to have their dwelling unit at Kankavli, Kudal, Malvan, Swantwadi, Vengurla and nearby towns and surrounding small places and converting their dreams into reality with our finance. The In-charge of all branches are advised to make note of the above instructions, discuss contents of this Office Order with all staff members and ensure that applicants are advised to contact Dadar Branch for processing of their Housing Loan Proposals.

 

The above two measures viz. reduction in the margin and arrangement of accepting and processing the Housing Loan Proposals at our Dadar Branch would definitely help to boost our Priority Sector Advances under Individual Housing Loan Scheme.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and ensure compliance.
