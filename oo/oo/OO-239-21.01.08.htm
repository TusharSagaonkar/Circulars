Office Order No. 239 dated 21 January 2008

 

Measures for prevention of Terrorism

Lists of Terrorist Individuals/organizations.

 

          A reference is invited to Office Orders No. 227 dated 21.03.2007 and Office Orders No. 228 dated 08.06.2007 and various office orders on the above subject issued earlier.  We have since received from the Reserve Bank of India a copy of an updated Consolidated List of individuals and entities belonging to or associated with the Taliban and/or the Al-Qaida organization.  Original script has been added to the entries of 141 Taliban individuals on the Lists and identifying information has also been amended in the designation field in the entries of 67 Taliban individuals from the same list with a view to clarifying that the designations of senior Taliban officials refer to position held by them during the Taliban Regime.  All amendments have been marked with the underlined and strikethrough in the entries.  The list is enclosed as Annexure I.  We have also received from RBI changes made in Consolidated List by virtue of amendments to identify particulars of 23 individuals in Al-Qaida Section of the List of individuals and entities.  The same is enclosed as Annexure II.

 

2.       Further, a copy of additions of name of 3 individuals to the Consolidated List of Al-Qaida Section received from RBI is enclosed as Annexure III.

 

3.       The Consolidated Lists of individuals/entities banned supplied earlier may be suitably updated at the Branch level.  Branches may ensure to cross check all account opening applications and transactions carried out in the accounts with the updated Consolidated List and report the position in respect of accounts having any resemblance with individuals/entities listed therein to Head Office for onward transmission to RBI. 

 

4.       As advised in Office Order No. 202, para 2, only one copy each of the lists for the use and record of the branch is enclosed.  A copy of this Office Order, without enclosures is circulated for information and for the sake of completing the records at Zonal Office and Departmental levels.

 

            M. C. Shetty

           Managing Director

Encl:  As above
