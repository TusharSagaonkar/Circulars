Office Order No. 205 dated 2nd November, 2005

 

Providing Banking facilities to Visually Impaired Persons

 

            A reference is invited to our IDC No.HO/MD/920/412/2005-06 dated 22nd July, 2005 on the above subject.  It has since been advised that the solution to the problem suggested by IBA to the Dy.Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities (as conveyed in the Annexure to the above IDC) was not found satisfactory by him and he made a reference to the Court of the Chief Commissioner for Persons with Disabilities.  The reference was considered at a hearing on the matter where the Ministry of Finance, RBI, IBA and other concerned parties were heard and the Court passed an order which the banks are required to implement.  Accordingly, RBI advised IBA to convey the Order to its member banks for their information and compliance.  The operative part of the Court Order is reproduced in the Annexure for guidance of and implementation by all branches.  It should be noted that the Court Order is mandatory and should be implemented in letter and spirit by our branches.  The advice contained in the aforesaid IDC stands superseded by the Court Order.

 

2.         As for the items (d) & (e) of para 14 of the Court Order, our IT Dept is advised separately to examine the Court directors and place their proposals / suggestions for their implementation before the Board and seek guidance / orders.

 

3.         It was advised in the last para of the IDC cited above to keep the staff informed about the policy advised therein.  In view of the change brought about by the Order and its mandatory nature, the staff may be informed about it afresh.  The need to render courteous and polite service to such customers may also be reiterated.

 

                                                                                                                 M.C. Shetty

                                                                                                Managing Director
