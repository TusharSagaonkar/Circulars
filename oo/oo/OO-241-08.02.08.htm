Office Order No. 241 dated 8 February 2008

 

Opening of Foreign Exchange Department

 

          After commencement of economic reforms process there is progressive liberalization in Foreign Exchange related transactions with a view to providing adequate Foreign Exchange facilities to common persons.  RBI has widened the scope of activities which Authorized Foreign Exchange Dealers are eligible to undertake so that large segment of the population can undertake a variety of transactions without approaching RBI.  The Board of Directors in the meeting held on 22.02.2007 had decided that our Bank should undertake Foreign Exchange Business in terms of RBI's policy of issuance of Foreign Exchange Dealership licence to Multi-State Scheduled UCBs and accordingly RBI was requested to grant the licence.  The necessary amendments to the Bye-laws of the Bank were passed in the 44th Annual General Meeting held on 29.07.2007 to enable the Bank to undertake full fledged Foreign Exchange business.  The said amendments were approved by the Central Registrar of Co-op. Societies, New Delhi.  The Bank has received the licence of the Authorized Dealer Category I dated 20.11.2007 from RBI.  Thereafter the Bank has also registered itself as a member of Foreign Exchange Dealers' Association of India (FEDAI).

 

The full fledged Foreign Exchange Department of the Bank will be inaugurated at the Bank's premises at Dadar on 18.02.2008.  The operational modalities of the foreign exchange dept. are explained in the following paragraphs:

1.      As per RBI's categorization of branches for conducting FE business, branches which are maintaining Foreign Currency accounts (called, NOSTRO accounts) with banks abroad are called "A" category branches.  Branches which have been designated for conducting FE business independently are called "B" category branches (Designated branches).  All the other branches are called "C" category branches (Non-designated branches).

2.      The Head Office Department looking after administrative supervision and control will be named as Foreign Exchange Department which will be presently located at our Dadar branch and it will be under the charge of the Asst. General Manager supported by dedicated team of staff members.  Our Bank's NOSTRO accounts will be maintained at Dadar branch.  This will be the only "A" Category branch of our Bank.  It will also act as a Designated Branch for independent handling of FE transactions.

3.      In addition to Dadar branch it has been decided to designate Vashi, Ghatkopar, Andheri and Deccan Gymkhana (Pune) branches as Designated "B" Category Branches (DBs)

4.      All the remaining branches would be in "C" category and are attached to any one of the above referred Designated Branches depending upon their geographical location and other operational conveniences for the purpose of carrying out their FE business transactions.  Accordingly, they will transact the FE business for their branch customers and forward the same to the Designated Branches as under:

i)       All Pune Zone branches will forward their FE business to Deccan Gymkhana branch.

ii)     All Navi Mumbai Zone branches will forward their FE business to Vashi branch.     

iii)    Central Zone & Western Zone branches are grouped under Designated 'B' Category branches of Dadar, Ghatkopar and Andheri as under:

6.      A book titled "FOREIGN EXCHANGE BUSINESS" covering various segments of the FE business will be sent shortly for the benefit of our branches/offices.

7.      Operational Guidelines for conducting various FE transactions taking into account the RBI guidelines from Exchange Control angle and internal guidelines from credit angle will be sent to the DBs for compliance.

8.      Delegation of power for branch officials for handling various types of FE transactions will be as per the chart enclosed as Annexure I.

9.      Instructions will be sent soon to the Non-designated branches with regard to the handling of documents including cheques, currency notes, etc. before forwarding the same to the DB concerned for further processing and accounting.

10.    In case of any queries pertaining to FE business from any existing/prospective customers, the branches should try to attend them on their own as far as possible and should they require any assistance in this regard, they may contact the Foreign Exchange Department, Dadar.
