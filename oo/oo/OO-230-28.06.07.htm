Office Order No.  230   dated   28 June  2007

 

 

Implementation of National Electronic Fund Transfer (NEFT) System

- Guidelines

----------------------------------------

 

          In order to reform the payment and settlement systems for financial  transactions and to make  our  payment and settlement systems globally  acceptable, RBI  has initiated several measures in the recent past.  Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) and National Electronic Fund Transfer (NEFT) systems are two  such IT enable  transaction  platforms  introduced by RBI in this context.  Our bank  is already  a member  of the RBI run RTGS Gateway and   inter bank  transactions, including  clearing and CCIL  settlements, are carried out  by us through RTGS.  RBI has indicated in one of their communications in  December 2006, that transactions of the value  below Rs. 1.00 lac will not be routed  through RTGS and instead banks  should use another payment  Gateway already in operation viz. NEFT through  which RBI will facilitate inter bank transaction of any amount.  Our  bank has acquired  the requisite SFMS (Structured  Financial Messaging System) software  in order to  facilitate access to the NEFT of RBI and consequent  efficient  customer service.  RBI  has given deadline  to RTGS enabled urban banks  for joining and activating   NEFT service.  It has, accordingly, been decided by the bank to activate   SFMS software  and provide our account holders fund transfer facilities under NEFT system with effect from 1 July 2007.  NEFT Cell has been created within the Treasury Dept. to handle  electronic  fund transfer work.  The Cell is already  attending to RTGS transactions, the guidelines  in respect of  the same had  been  circulated in terms of Office Order No. 214 dated 7 July 2006. The guidelines for functioning of NEFT and for the  fund transfer  through NEFT/RTGS platforms  are given below for  the guidance of and adoption by all concerned,  handling  customer transactions that fall within the ambit  of NEFT/RTGS system.

 

Initially bank has decided to do the NEFT/RTGS fund transfer transactions centrally through the NEFT Cell attached to Treasury Dept.  This dept. is equipped with three staff members for fund transfer operations as required by system.  The department is headed by Mr. M.H. Pharande, Manager – I.T. Dept., in addition to his present assignments and will remain under the control of Treasury Dept.  The transactions will be carried out by NEFT Cell, initially from the NEFT Cell (Branch Code No. 901).

NEFT Cell has separate telephone line nos. 2526 0179 and 2526 0109 and dedicated fax line 2526 0172.  For mailing of message for fund transfer through Exchange Server the E-mail ID “neftcell”  is to  be used.  This E-mail ID will be utilized exclusively for fund transfer messages from branches / departments to the NEFT Cell.  The Cell can also be contacted through telephone lines of Treasury Dept. in case of emergency only.  Contact persons in the Cell are -

The Business hours of the Cell will be 9.00 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. on weekdays and 9.00 a.m. to 12.00 noon on Saturdays.  However, branches will entertain the customer request for fund transfer on the same day only up to 3.00 p.m. on weekdays and 11.30 a.m. on Saturdays.  

 

3.  I.T. Dept. has opened separate batches for NEFT transactions:-

a.            NEFT-CR (Batch to be used by NEFT Cell for NEFT inward)

b.            RTGS-CR (Batch to be used by NEFT Cell for RTGS inward)

c.            RTGSDR-M (Batch to be used by Branch in morning session for RTGS outward).

d.            RTGSDR-E (Batch to be used by Branch in evening session for RTGS outward).

e.            NEFTDR-M (Batch to be used by Branch in morning session for NEFT outward).

f.             NEFTDR-E (Batch to be used by Branch in evening session for NEFT outward).

 

Further, I.T. Dept. has opened following  GL level accounts in NEFT Cell :

i)     RTGS–INW  ii) NEFT-INW iii) RTGS- OUT  iv) NEFT-OUT v) RTG-I–RT 

vi) NFT-I-RT vii) RTG–O–RT viii) NFT-O-RT & ix) H.O.

 

RTGS–INW/NEFT-INW account will be used by NEFT Cell for routing the inward transfers to the respective Beneficiary accounts at different branches through ABB activity. 

RTGS- OUT/NEFT-OUT account will be used by branches for crediting the outward transfer amounts under RTGS/NEFT respectively.  Branches will have no right to reverse/cancel the entry. 

RTG-I–RT/NFT-I-RT account will be used by NEFT Cell for returns of the outward remittances.

RTG–O–RT/NFT-O-RT account will be used by NEFT Cell for returns of inward remittances.

 

  1. Fund transfers of Rs.1,00,000/- and above is possible through RTGS where the transfer will take place in real time.  Fund transfer of any amount is possible through NEFT where the transfer of funds will be made in batches as described in point no.5 below.  The IFSC Codes of the branches and Treasury Dept. remain same for RTGS/NEFT transfer facilities.  IFSC (Indian Financial System Codes) of branches are the code addresses of the concerned branch and bank for the NEFT/RTGS participant member banks to remit the funds under the system.  Thus, each branch has to  remember and give its IFSC number to the account holder to enable him to get the inward remittances to his account from remitter of other banks.  List of IFSC Codes of our branches is given in ANNEXURE-I. 

 

  1. NEFT transactions will be settled by RBI in six batches at 9.30 a.m., 10.30 a.m., 12.00 noon, 1.00 p.m., 3.00 p.m., 4.00 p.m. on all weekdays except Saturdays.  On Saturdays, there will be three settlements at 9.30 a.m., 10.30 a.m. and 12.00 noon.  NEFT operations will be allowed on all RTGS working days. Any request from the customer if processed by the branch after scheduled time, will be considered for remittance by Cell on next  batch/day. 

 

At present, we have inter-bank (R42) transactions under RTGS.  This is now extended for customer transactions (R41).  RTGS operations are allowed between 9.00 a.m. to 3.30 p.m. for customer transactions and up to 5.30 p.m. for inter-bank transactions.  Therefore, customer request for fund transfer under RTGS will be allowed up to 3.00 p.m. on weekdays and upto 11.30 a.m. on Saturday at branch so as to enable the branch to send the request to the Cell who can remit the fund within the RBI stipulated time.   

 

Branches will be provided with a Fax machine as a backup measure in case of failure of scanner used in the messaging facility system.

 

  1. NEFT Operations (Branch Level)

     A.   Outward Remittance at the request of Account Holders:

i.     Branch for remitting funds through RTGS/NEFT can obtain the concerned Electronic Fund Transfer request form as per enclosed format (ANNEXURE – II) (Code No. 1011) from the account holder.  Details like (a) Beneficiary Bank Name and IFSC Code, (b) Beneficiary Account Name and Account Type and Number in Beneficiary Bank and; (c) Amount to be remitted and (d) Contact No. of our Account holder are very important and have to be clearly understood by the branch before processing the remittance request.  Normally, the account holder has to inform the mode of transfer (NEFT or RTGS) he is opting for.  In absence of the option by the account holder funds will be transferred under NEFT. 

 

ii.   Branch, on getting a request from their Account Holder, will verify the signature and contents of application.  Any material alterations require full authentication of the Account Holder.  Branch will also check if sufficient balance is available in the account  of customer  for transfer. 

 

iii.  Branch will make posting through ABB activity by debiting remitter’s (Account Holders) account in their branch and crediting the same to respective outward account (refer (iii) and (iv) of  point no.3) maintained in NEFT Cell through respective relevant batch (refer (c) to (f) of point no.3).  Single shift branches will use only morning batches (NEFT DR-M, RTGS DR-M).  Such entries are to be treated as “ABB transfer entries”.  The branch official has to authorise the same under usual procedure.  The Electronic Fund Transfer forms for fund remittance through RTGS/NEFT needs to be signed by two authorised signatories of branch along with their respective signature codes just as they sign on Pay Order before sending the same to the NEFT / RTGS Cell.  This will signify that the remittance request is from the Account Holder of branch, proper account is debited for remittance, signature of Account Holder is verified and will be treated as the request from branch to NEFT Cell to transfer funds to the Beneficiary mentioned by the remitter.  Branch has to record NEFT/RTGS transactions separately  in a register which will be supplied shortly.   

 

iv.  Branch is not authorized to reverse/cancel the entries from NEFT-OUT & RTGS-OUT a/c.

 

v.    Narration for voucher entry in the OMNI system to be given by the branches as per the following format :

To < Beneficiary Bank IFSC Code > <Beneficiary Account No.>  <Beneficiary Name> 

 

vi.  After doing the transaction as mentioned in (iii above), branch has to scan the request form and email through exchange server to “neft cell” under the email ID of one of the authorised signatories of the remitting branch who has signed the request form. 

 

vii. If a request is received after closure of business hours of RTGS/NEFT, branch has to follow the procedure as per guidelines mentioned in (iii & vi above), but actual fund will be transferred by NEFT Cell on next batch/day.  Customer has to be apprised accordingly by the branch. 

 

viii.           In case an account holder requests for an outward remittance of Rs.10,00,000/- and above, an advance notice / intimation of atleast one day has to be obtained from him.

ix.  Before handing over the branch to Data Centre, branch has to ensure that respective morning and evening batches of RTGS/NEFT are closed and sealed.

 

B.  Inward Remittance for credit to Customers Accounts:

     When there is an inward remittance from other Bank, the NEFT Cell will give the credit in OMNI System to concerned account holder of a branch.  These credits are given as soon as the credits are received in batches in case of NEFT and through RTGS from RBI.  Branch will receive the credit details for their records through Exchange Server  (from public folder “neftcell” ) for various NEFT batches after each settlement under NEFT and for the RTGS transfers.  Branch has to verify from the statement received from Cell to ensure actual credits to their accounts.  The credits received after closing of the branch business hours can be verified from ABB Offline batch (ABBTRF1).            Operational instructions for this under OMNI system, Request form scanning procedures and messaging through Exchange Server are enclosed as ANNEXURES– III - A and III - B respectively.

Branches should communicate to the customers about the receipt of inward remittance in their accounts through NEFT/RTGS system in the prescribed format ANNEXURE - IV-A (Code No. 1012) and IV-B (Code No. 1013) whichever is applicable.

 

7.  NEFT Operations (NEFT Cell)

A.   Outward Remittance:

i.   As soon as the Cell receives email message through Exchange Server, it has to verify if the funds to be remitted are credited by branch to respective outward accounts (refer (iii) and (iv) of point no.3) at NEFT Cell and that full details of fund transfer are provided by customer and verify the signatures of branch officials as per signature codes confirmed by our bank which is available at all branches/depts.  If the request is in order, the Cell will post the entry through NEFT - SFMS or RTGS by debiting the amount to respective outward accounts (refer point no.3). 

 

ii.  Two officials  in the Cell will verify and authorize the outward remittances.

 

iii. Cell will ensure that the amount is remitted as per the request of remitter (Account Holder/Branch) of funds. 

 

iv.  Remittance above Rs. 10.00 lacs  need approval of Treasury Dept.

 

v.   Batch-by-Batch Cell will verify the remittances under NEFT.

 

vi.  In the morning, Cell has to verify respective outward accounts (refer point no.3)      and if there is any fund transfer of previous day (after Cell closes) pending to be transferred, then  execute the transfer as per outward procedure.

 

vii. The outward remittances to be recorded in the NEFT / RTGS outward register. 

 

B.  Inward Remittance

      On receiving the inward remittance messages from RBI, the Cell will verify the details from OMNI System as to the correctness of IFSC, name of account, account number and branch and;

 

i.      If the details are complete in all respects, Cell will first credit the RBI-batch total amount to NEFT–INW or RTGS-INW a/c of NEFT Cell through NEFT-CR or RTGS-CR batch respectively and then post the entries branch-wise in OMNI System through ABB activity.  The entries will have to be authorized by authorized officials in Cell as a normal transfer credit.

 

ii.     On crediting the amount to the correct Beneficiary account, Cell will forward the information sheet to concerned branch through email in Exchange Server. (To public folder “neftcell”).

 

iii. The inward remittances to be recorded in the NEFT / RTGS inward      register. 

 

Return of the messages:

i.     If there is a return of outward remittance in the inward remittance, it should be treated as a normal inward remittance and credit be given to account by the Cell under the OMNI System through respective inward return account.

 

ii.   The return of inward remittances will be treated similar to the outward remittances.  Such returns from the last batch of NEFT will be credited to NFT–O–RT a/c in NEFT Cell.  Next day morning, the Cell will remit the return amount from NFT–O-RT a/c as a normal outward fund transfer in the first NEFT batch.

 

C.   Day End Activities:

i.     At the day end, Cell has to verify that all received transactions are credited to various accounts and all debits are sent out.

 

ii.   There should be daily reconciliation of NEFT inward and outward transactions with respective NEFT inward and outward messaging reports of RBI generated through SFMS/RTGS.

 

iii. Cell has  to pass the necessary accounting  entries through proper voucher with           authentication, as per Point No. 8.

 

iv. Cell has to close and seal the NEFT–CR & RTGS-CR batches before the day end.

 

v.  Generate  Trial  balance of the Cell  and send to Accounts Dept. on day         to  day      basis alongwith HO vouchers (RBI Current A/c).

 

D.  Monthly Reports of Branch-wise RTGS & NEFT transactions will have to be placed before the Board by Cell for their perusal in the Board Meeting of ensuing month.
