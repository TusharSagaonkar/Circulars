Office Order No. 327 dated 27th January, 2010 

Centralized Data Entry Cell

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

The Bank has decided to centralize the data entry work of Current and Savings accounts of all Mumbai and Navi Mumbai area branches. On trial basis 3 branches  viz. Nehru Nagar, Vashi and Hill Road Branch were taken for the job. Gradually, all other branches in the above referred areas will be taken on job starting from branches of Navi Mumbai Zone from 27.01.2010 and onwards. Shortly, MIS Department will inform migration dates of western and central zone branches. This Centralized activity will be carried out at the MIS Department situated at DR-Site on 3rd floor of our Vashi premises. For the smooth functioning of this activity we give below the procedural guidelines as well as responsibilities of Branches and MIS Department.

A. 1.  Procedural Guidelines for Branches :

1.1. At the time of opening Current/Savings Bank Accounts, branches should follow the instructions issued by the Head Office from time to time and observe Know Your Customer (KYC) norms. All the mandatory Data required to be filled-in in the OMNI system, must be obtained from the customer, and the other Data, where available, is to be obtained from the customer as per the provision of existing account opening form. After accepting the Account Opening Form the branch will feed the bare minimum details in screen provided in the menu with the name "Customer open for branch" and "Account open for branch". Once these screens are entered, the entries should be authenticated by the branch. The Customer Number and the Account Number should be noted properly on the Account Opening Form in the space provided and on all other relevant documents by using the numbering machine. All the details obtained from the customer should be clearly & neatly written on the Account Opening Form and should be readable. The photos should be pasted properly. Initial Deposit should be collected from the customer in cash and the entry shall be made in the Omni System and then Passbook shall be issued to the customer.

1.2  Branch should not use the present 'customer open' and 'Account open' screens for entering the SB/CD Accounts but will use these screens for all other types of accounts.

1.3  The Account Opening form along with relevant documents/papers will be sent to MIS Department Vashi in the preprinted envelops meant for such movement through the regular courier on the next working day.

1.4  Courier person will pick up these envelopes and make delivery to Clearing Department, who in turn will arrange for onward delivery to MIS Department in order to reach the envelopes latest by 2nd day evening at 5.00 p.m.

A.2.   Responsibilities of the Branches

2.1    Branch is responsible for obtaining all the relevant documents for opening the account and complying 'Know Your Customer' (KYC) norms.

2.2    Signature verification of the introducer.

2.3    Sending of account opening forms along with relevant documents/papers to MIS Department for completion of Data Entry.

2.4    Sending confirmation letter to introducer, who have not come personally to the branch for giving introduction.

2.5    In case any Account Opening Form alongwith the set of documents has been returned back from MIS Dept. detailing the shortfalls, Branches should rectify the shortfalls in Data, making necessary enquiries with the Customer and resubmit the documents to Centralized Data Entry Cell.

2.6    To obtain and send, any modification details as and when requested by account holder, to Centralized Data Entry Cell, after opening the account.

2.7    Normally Passbook should be issued after completion of data entry by the Centralized Data Entry Cell. However if customer insists for a passbook, it can be issued by the branch by writing address manually.

2.8    Branch can issue cheque book after verification of complete data entry done by MIS Dept., and after having satisfied with the address and receiving introducer's confirmation.

2.9    In case of wrong delivery of packet(s) carrying Account Opening Forms, the branches should send back packet(s) to MIS Department immediately.

 

B) 1)  Procedural Guidelines for MIS Departments :

 

1.1  The Centralized Data Entry Cell will be established at MIS Department  and the Cell will do the Data Entry on 2nd working day itself.

1.2  Centralized Data entry cell will send address confirmation letter to the account holders.

1.3  If there are any shortfalls in data and if Central Data Entry cell is unable to feed the complete data, the entire set of documents shall be returned to the respective branch, pointing out the shortfalls. In this case Central Data Entry cell will scan the signature of the customer only after receiving back the set of documents from the branch rectifying the shortfall(s) and completion of data entry.

1.4  After the completion of data entry all the documents shall be retained at Central Data Entry cell from a cut of date.

B. 2)  Responsibilities of the MIS Department

2.1    Complete the entire work of feeding Data entry as per the account opening form and other documents provided by the Branch.

2.2    Inform the branch regarding shortfalls in Data, if any, along with the return memo/letter.

2.3    Signature scanning, after complete data is feeded. 

2.4    Sending address verification letter to the account holder.

2.5    Maintenance and preservation of Branch-wise/Zone-wise Account Opening Forms with all other relevant documents/papers securely at Central level.

2.6    To make Data entries in regard to additions/ alterations/modifications of information received from the Branches as and when the customer requests to the Branch and Branch submits to MIS Department and keeping them securely with original Account Opening Forms.

2.7    KYC to be checked by MIS Department.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at the Branch/Department and ensure compliance.
