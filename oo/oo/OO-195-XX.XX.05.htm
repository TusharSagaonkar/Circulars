Office Order No.195 dated

 

Delegation of powers to the branch in-charges for sanctioning unsecured advances-Modification of the procedure for exercise of the powers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          A reference invited to office orders No. 195 dt. 7th June 2005 on the captioned subject it was advised that the Managers, Sr. Managers and Managers in-charge of branches were authorized to purchase cheques, allow drawals against clearing of cheques with personal security, upto Rs. 2.00 lakhs and also to allow TOD in CC/DD accounts upto 10 percent of the limit sanctioned or Rs. 2.00 lakhs, whichever is less. It was laid down in this context that such  exercise of powers by the Sr. Managers/Managers has to be done in consultation with the Dy. Gen. Manager of the Zone, subject to subsequent ratification of the action of the branch in-charge by the Dy. Gen. Manage. It was represented to the Head Office by the branch in-charges that it was difficult to implement the procedure laid down i.e. consulting the Dy. Gen Manager before exercising the delegated discretionary powers. It almost nullified the delegation of powers and resulted in customer dissatisfaction. The representation was considered by the Board and it has been decided that Sr. Managers and Managers in-charge of branches may exercise the delegated powers as above without prior consultation with the Dy. Gen. Manager provided the facilities are allowed to regular customers with good track record.  The branch in-charge should however, send details of the such accounts where the facility is allowed to the Dy. Gen. Manager immediately for ratification. It may however be noted that as advised in para 2 of the Office order NO. 195 exercise of the discretionary powers should be done judiciously and the branch in-charge would be responsible for ensuring that the facilities allowed are regularized by the customers promptly.                                                                                                 

 

                                                                                          M. C. Shetty.

                                                                                      Managing Director.
