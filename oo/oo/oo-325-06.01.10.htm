Office Order No. 325 dated 6th January, 2010 

Discrepancies :    Cheque Discounting Facility, Other Facilities and appropriation of Drawing Power

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

         

It is of grave concern to us that despite our detailed guidelines and proper instructions regarding the implementation of cheque discounting Facility and other facilities at Branches, the various discrepancies were brought to our notice. We narrate few of them as under :-

i)         Discounting of cheques issued by other than Government/Semi Government Corporations and Local Self Government Bodies have been restricted to Rs.50,000/- to CC/CD account holders. Branches have discounted the cheques for more than Rs.50,000/- issued by Private and Ltd. Companies.

ii)       Discounting of Cheques in C.C. Accounts has been permitted only when the D.P./ Limit is available in the account. Branches have discounted the cheques without ascertaining the fact whether D.P. / Limit is available in the account or not.

iii)     When discretion has been vested to purchase/discount the DDs/Pay Orders/Bankers Cheque issued only by Nationalized Banks, Private and Commercial Banks, Scheduled Co-op. Banks upto Rs.50,000/- in S.B. Accounts, other instruments have been purchased/ discounted by the branches violating the Bank's instructions and exceeded their  discretionary powers.

iv)      When required and adequate amount of C.C. limit is granted against hypothecation of stock and Book Debts, normally there should not arise any need for discounting of local cheques. Only in rare cases and under exceptional circumstances and dire need of the funds, customer's request for discounting of local cheques upto Rs.50,000/- should have been honoured. It has been shocked to know that in some of the C.C. accounts, Local cheques are being discounted on regular basis and in some cases it is found that there is no other credit in the C.C. Accounts except the credit by discounting of local cheques. This also indicates that funds are diverted for other purpose.

v)        Excess drawing  being converted into NPA :-

       Due to indiscriminate purchase/discounting of cheques without taking into account the availability of D.P./Limit, if the outstanding in the C.C. Account is more than D.P. / Limit continuously for more than 90 days it amounts to out of order status of the account, necessitating the account to be classified as NPA.

vi)                In many cases it is found that branches have purchased/ discounted Post dated cheques which is against the law and practice of Banking.

 

During the recent Reserve Bank of India inspection, the Reserve Bank of India inspecting officials have also commented adversely on the above discrepancies and viewed those as Serious Irregularities.

 

The matter was put up to the Board in its meeting held on 19.12.2009. Our Board took very serious view and expressed its concern towards such incidences at branch level. We, therefore, with the cost of repetition reiterate the important extract of the instructions issued in the past for your immediate notice and to take corrective action at your end.

 

(a)         "We have instructed the Branches to discount the cheques issued by Government / Semi Government Corporations and Local Self Governing bodies, upto the limit of Rs.5.00 Lakh to the Managers/Sr. Managers of Branches, Rs.7.50 Lakh to AGMs of the Branches and Rs.12.00 Lakh to AGMs of Udupi and Ahmedabad and full Powers to DGM and above to CC A/c holders having good track record subject to availability of D.P./Limit and for reputed Current A/c Holders upto Rs.5.00 Lakh to AGM of the Branch and Rs.2.00 Lakh to the Managers/Sr. Managers of the Branches and Rs.6.00 Lakh to AGMs Udupi and Ahmedabad and Full Powers to DGM and above. In case of Savings Bank account holders powers are restricted only upto Rs.50,000/-."

(b)         "Discounting of all other cheques (issued by the Private & Ltd. Companies) are restricted to Rs.50,000/- to all CC/CD Account holders only and not for Savings Bank Account holders. It is observed that the Branches are indiscriminately discounting the cheques although proper guidelines are issued to them. It is also observed that some of the Branches are discounting other cheques for any amount in Saving Bank Accounts."

(c)          "The sanction of limits for Cheque Discounting and Against Clearing facilities to CC accountholders is against the security of hypothecation of Stock and Book Debts, which covers the outstanding in WCTL, Cheque discounting limit, Against Clearing Limit, and Cash Credit limits. In case of sanction of SBD/DBD facilities, the discounting of bills is done out of the Book debts hypothecated to the Bank and hence these limits should also be reckoned under the overall Drawing Power limit."

(d)         Post Dated Cheques are discounted which is against the normal banking practice. In this context we would like to quote Section 6 of Negotiable Instruments Act, 1981 which says :

"A cheque is a bill of exchange drawn on a specified banker and not expressed to be payable otherwise than on demand."

As such in case of post dated cheque, it is doubtful whether the holder is getting the status of a holder in due course as holder can not  have  right to demand and recover the amount from the drawer of the cheque and holder's right is being postponed till the date mentioned on a cheque, and hence discounting of post dated cheques involve higher risk. Moreover it is deadly against the Law and Practice of Banking as such it should be totally avoided. We, therefore, advise our Branches not to discount/purchase post dated cheques under any circumstances".

 

(e)         "The cheque discounting limit is considered as daily limit irrespective of whether earlier cheque discounted is realized or not. With the result, though there are unrealized cheques which are already discounted on previous day, fresh cheques to the extent of the limits are discounted. Thus, cheque discounting limit is exceeded everyday. In case of return of the discounted cheques, the outstanding in cheque discounting limit will exceed the limit. Practices followed by branches are in contravention of the directives of RBI as also the instructions issued by the Bank from time to time."

                               

Example :

A manufacturing unit M/s. Uma Manufacturing Co. is enjoying the following limits.

 

Cheque Discounting         -        Rs.30.00 Lakh

Against Clearing              -        Rs.10.00 Lakh

 

(a) On 01.01.2010 the Branch discounted a cheque of      -  Rs.20.00 Lakh

     On 02.01.2010 the Branch discounted a cheque of      -  Rs.20.00 Lakh

     (without ascertaining the fact that cheque

    discounted on 01.01.2010 has been realized or not

    and the limit available was Rs.10.00 Lakh only).

 

(b)  On 03.01.2010 cheque discounted on 01.01.2010

      was returned unpaid.

 

On the same day Branch discounted another cheque of  - Rs.25.00 Lakh

Rs.25.00 Lakh considering that limit is Rs.30.00 Lakh,

although limit was not available which was only

Rs.10.00 Lakh.

In this example on 02.01.2010 and 03.01.2010 the

Branch has exceeded the limit sanctioned to the unit

for both days.         

(f)          "Ad-hoc facilities being granted beyond the Limits/Drawing Powers sanctioned to the units shall be treated as Clean Advance as per RBI guidelines. We, therefore, propose that these facilities will be sanctioned within the discretionary powers delegated for Clean Advances to various officials and top executives. (Discretionary Powers of the officials/executives is Rs.2.00Lakh for Clean Advance as per extant instructions)."

(g)         "Facilities to be extended against clearing, Cheques discounting etc. will be made only after verifying the genuineness of the transactions and only when Limit/Drawing Power is available. This can be verified from the Book Debt Statement submitted by the borrower and also during inspection visits by branch officials. Branches are once again warned to discontinue the wrong practices if followed and ensure strict adherence to the above instructions. In Cheque Discounting Limit, the cheques drawn by individual persons, proprietor/partnership concerns are not to be discounted unless special concession is made to some accounts by the higher authorities in writing." 

 

The In-charge of all Branches are therefore advised to strictly adhere to RBI directives and guidelines issued by our Bank from time to time. Kindly note that violation of RBI directives and our instructions will be viewed very seriously and concerned officials will be personally held responsible, as these observations are of serious nature and will be viewed very seriously by our Board as well as by RBI for which any punitive and disciplinary action will have to be faced.
