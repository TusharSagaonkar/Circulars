Office Order No. 210  dated 13th  April 2006

 

Delegation of Powers to Sanction Expenditure

 

            A reference is invited to Office Order No. 126 dated 20/8/1998.  The Annexure III of the Office Order gives details of delegation of powers to sanction revenue expenditure.  These powers were partially modified vide Office Order No. 184 dated 16/08/2004.

 

            The matter relating to difficulties experienced by the Branch Managers in sanctioning routine expenses of repairs and maintenance of equipments, furniture fixtures, premises, etc. was discussed in the meeting of Budget and Performance Review Committee as well as in the meeting of  Board of Directors.  Powers for sanctioning revenue expenses (including petty cash) upto Rs. 20,000/ per month for Asst. General Managers /Senior Managers and Rs. 15,000/- per month for the Managers have been delegated as per office Order No. 126 dated 20/08/1998.  However, powers of sanctioning expenses of repairs and maintenance are exercised by the Development Dept. Taking into consideration the recurrent nature of such expenses and urgency with which   repairs have to be undertaken it has been decided to fix a sub-limit within the powers delegated to the  branch in-charges for incurring revenue expenditure. Accordingly, the Board in their meeting held on 22/03/2006 have approved delegation of powers to the  branch in-charges for incurring such revenue expenditure on repairs and maintenance as under:-

 

Asst. Gen. Manager /

Sr. Manager

 

Manager

Repairs and Maintenance

Rs. 5000/-

Rs. 4000/-

 

            It has to be noted that the powers as above  are to be exercised within the overall limit for sanctioning of revenue expenditure (including petty cash) as stated in Office Order No. 126 dated 20/08/1998 i.e. Rs.20,000/- per month for Asst. General Manager / Senior Manager  and Rs. 15,000/- per month for the Managers which remains unchanged.  The branch in -charge exercising the powers will do so under intimation to the concerned Zonal Office.

 

                                                                                            M. C. Shetty

                                                                           Managing Director
