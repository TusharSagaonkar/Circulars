Office Order No. 589 dated 15th October 2024

Decentralization of Data Entry Work of Saving Accounts and Current Accounts across Branches (w.e.f 16th October 2024)

 

Background:

At present, account opening and data entry work pertaining to the Saving and Current Accounts is carried out partly in branches and partly in the CDEC as mentioned in the Office Order No. 327 dated the 27th January 2010. The data entry pertaining to other accounts (i.e. Term Deposit) is being done in branches themselves.

 

Now, it has been decided to decentralize the Data Entry work pertaining to Saving and Current Accounts so that it will be done in branches instead of CDEC Department. Henceforth, all the work related to opening of SB, CD and TD accounts, verification of KYC Documents (necessary prior to account opening), and the entry of data contained in AOF1/AOF2/CIF1/CIF2/TD1 into the Omni database will be done by the branches. However, the work of generation of CKYC number will continue with CDEC. The revised work flow pertaining to account opening and data entry will be as under:

 

1.  Role and Responsibilities of Branches:

i.         At the time of opening Current, Saving and Term Deposit accounts, branches shall follow the instructions issued by the Head Office from time to time and observe the “Know Your Customer” (KYC) norms.

ii.       Branches shall ensure that the details filled in by the customer in the Account Opening Form and copies of the KYC document obtained are readable.

iii.      Name screening of the prospective customers shall be done immediately on receiving the account opening form so as to prevent opening of account by anti-social elements, criminals, terrorists and banned entities.

iv.     Customer unification shall be ensured i.e. there shall be “One Customer = One Customer Number (BI-UNICUS)” in the Bank.

v.       E-KYC shall be done in respect of all the individuals, joint holders, partners, directors, trustees and authorized signatories.             

vi.     Entire data required mandatorily to be filled in the OMNI system must be obtained from the customer. Once the Account Opening Form is obtained from a customer, the branch will feed all the mandatory as well as optional fields in Omni system, based on the details mentioned by the customer in the AOF1/AOF2/CIF1/CIF2/TD1 depending upon whether the account falls in the category of Individual(s) or that of a Non-individual entity.

vii.    The photographs of Account holders/Authorized signatories should be obtained and properly pasted on the CIF1.

viii.  Initial deposit should be collected from the customer in Cash, or by way of a cheque from customer’s own account with another bank.

ix.     Addendum 2 towards FATCA/CRS declaration must be obtained from all Non-individual entities along with the Account opening form.

x.       Branches shall scan the Account Opening Forms (AOF1/AOF2/CIF1/CIF2/ TD1) along with the KYC documents within two days from the date of opening the account so that data gets forwarded to the CDEC for generation of the Central KYC number.

xi.     Branches shall continue sending address confirmation letters (Thanks Letter) to the accountholders.

xii.    In respect of current accounts, it is mandatory to confirm the address of business as well as residence by visiting them, before issuing cheque book.

xiii.  The cheque book, ATM (debit) card and passbook shall be issued to the customer only after receiving acknowledgement of the address confirmation letter from the customer or visit by branch officials.

xiv.  Each customer shall be assigned Risk according to the profile of the customer, i.e. High/Medium/Low on the day of opening of the account.

xv.    Beneficial owner shall be identified and recorded in respect of all the legal entity accounts such as Partnership, Private Ltd Companies & Trust accounts as under.

i.         Company : Individuals entitled to more than 10% of Shares/Capital/Profits.

ii.        Partnership : Individuals entitled to more than 10% of property/Capital/ Profits

iii.      Trust : Individuals entitled to 10% or more interest in the trust. In case of public trust natural person exercising ultimate effective control.

iv.       Unincorporated Association or Body of Individuals: Entitled to more than 15% of Shares / Capital/ Profits

Where no natural person is identified under Sr. No. iii. and iv. above, the identity of the relevant natural person who holds the position of senior managing official or person exercising ultimate effective control over the entity be considered as Beneficial Owner of the said entity.

xvi.  Wherever the present address mentioned in the account opening form differs with the one in the OVD submitted by the customer, the customer’s place of residence as declared in the Account Opening Form must be visited, & visit report should be kept with account opening form.

xvii.Branches must maintain and preserve the Account Opening Forms along with all the other relevant documents/papers securely at their level.

xviii.           Branches to ensure that all accounts (SB/CD/TD etc) to have valid nomination.

 

2.  Role and Responsibilities of CDEC Department :

The CDEC shall

             i.       Generate/Assign a C-KYC number to each Individual and Non-individual account opened at branches, within 5 working days from the receipt of scanned CIF-1/CIF-2 forms from the Branches.

           ii.       Check and verify the customer numbers issued by branches to ensure customer unification in the Bank.

         iii.       Process issuance of personalized cheque book fed by branches.

         iv.      Preserve and maintain (AOF1/AOF2/CIF1/CIF2/TD1) and other relevant documents received from branches in electronic form.

           v.       Ensure verification of PAN Numbers with the NSDL/ UTIITSL in respect of all customer accounts opened at branches.

 

3.  Role of Information Technology Department

The IT department shall

i.         Reconfigure the DMS OMNI module and provide therein menus for creation/ modification/updation of customer data and make them accessible to branches for seamless data entry. Access of the same should also be given to CDEC, if need arises for correction in data fetched for issuance of CKYC number.

ii.       Provide support to branches for timely scanning of documents.

iii.      Assist the CDEC for generation of the C-KYC number.

 

 

4.  Role of Audit and Inspection Department

The internal/concurrent auditors shall

             i.       Verify account opening forms and KYC documents pertaining to all the newly opened accounts at branches on a regular basis to cover 100% of the accounts opened during the audit period.

(NOTE:-Currently, the AOF carries a certificate by the CDEC towards accepting and verifying the AOF and CIF together with KYC. This responsibility will now shift to the Concurrent Auditors/staff from the Inspection department).

           ii.       Ensure timely compliance of the queries raised in the Audit and Inspection reports.

 

The contents of this instant Office Order shall be effective from 16th October 2024, and is to be brought to the notice of all the members of the staff concerned in Branches, Offices & Departments.

 

 

P.S. Salian

Chief Executive Officer
