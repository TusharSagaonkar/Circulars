Office Order No. 593 dated 7th January 2025

Using of Mobile phone by staff for Login to CBS (OMNI) through Time Based One Time Password (TOTP) & SOP thereof

__________________________________________________________________

BACKGROUND

IMPLEMENTATION OF TWO- FACTOR AUTHENTICATION: 

Reserve Bank of India has directed all banks to implement dual factor authentication to login Core Bank system. Accordingly, Bank in the year 2021 has implemented dual factor authentication for logging into CBS NETWORK through a Biometric device. All branches and departments are enabled with 2-factor authentication for logging into CBS. However, IT Dept is receiving requests from branches and departments for disabling Biometric access of the users due to various reasons such as service unavailable, device malfunctioning or the users are not able to validate their fingerprints for accessing the CBS due to skin related issues etc.  Apart from these issues, branches/departments where the earlier version of Windows operating system is still in use then in such cases the system doesn’t support installation of biometric device on such machines.

 

Due to the referred issues, the Two Factor Authentication system was not yielding the desired results and hence the regulatory requirements remained non-complied.

 

PRESENT ACTION :

Taking into consideration all the above facts and the mandatory need to ensure 2FA as per regulatory guidelines and comply with Re-BIT observations, it was discussed to find an alternate solution and after due deliberations it was proposed to introduce the 2FA using TOTP (Time Based One Time Password) system for the staff who need to access the CBS & replace existing Biometric device 2FA System. The proposal to implement the system (TOTP) was duly approved.

 

STATUS OF BIOMETRIC DEVICES POST INTRODUCTION OF TOTP.

The Branch Incharge should arrange to disengage the Biometric devices supplied to the branches and installed on the CPU’s. The disconnected Biometric devices need to be collected and packed in a box and a list of such devices to be prepared. The box containing Biometric devices should be handed over to IT Dept under due acknowledgment. Any CBS user who fails to use 2FA using TOTP due to any unavoidable reasons, the Branch/Dept must retain some Biometric devices for usage at their end and need to seek permission in writing from IT Dept officials.

 

STEPS FOR IMPLEMENTING TOTP:

Our CBS vendor M/s Infrasoft Technologies (M/s Kiya.ai) have provided an application for both Android and IOS devices which needs to be downloaded by the OMNI user on their personal mobile handsets. IT Dept have checked 2FA authentication by TOTP under UAT environment. For installing the TOTP App the OMNI users will be required to download the application which will be sent on Exchange Server to all Branch / Dept. In-charges. The Branch/Dept. In-Charges need to download the Application for Android system from exchange server on their mobile Handset and share with their Branch/Dept. staffs on their respective individual handset. For IOS users the application is available on Application store and can download the same directly. The OMNI users need to install the application on their mobile handset, which will in turn have to be mapped to Device ID generated on individual handset in CBS through the masters provided. A document for enabling TOTP authentication in system has been uploaded in the Public Folder of I.T Dept. on Exchange Server. Branches/Depts. need to follow the instructions given in document.

In order to ensure smooth implementation of the Two Factor Authentication system through TOTP, Branches can contact following IT Staff for TOTP installation and support.

FRESH GUIDELINES:

As the TOTP system will require the staff to use their Smart phones, the earlier communication referred vide Office Order No.498 dt.18/12/2017 wherein the usage of Mobile phones and the guidelines thereof were mentioned elaborately, needs to be revisited and appropriate modified instructions need to be circulated for successful implementation of the system and comply with regulatory guidelines.

 

Now, as the staff will be required to use their personal mobile handsets for generating OTP for gaining access to the CBS the earlier guidelines issued vide Office Order No.498 dt.18/12/2017 for restriction on use of mobile phones by staff for personal work – Deposits of handsets with Assistant Manager stands redundant and hence the said office order no 498 is deemed to be withdrawn & cancelled.

 

In place of the erstwhile Office order 498, we herewith issue fresh guidelines & SOP vis-à-vis the implementation of TOTP as below:

In some cases where the staff are not able to gain access for login  into OMNI CBS from their existing mobile devices which are not supporting the TOTP application, then the said users need to avail the service of Biometric device for CBS login as was implemented earlier. However, in no circumstances the user will be excused/exempted from the 2FA authentication system that please note. Any CBS user who fails to use any one method of 2FA i.e TOTP or biometric login, will not be able to access the CBS. The users who have been assigned the OMNI user code need to access the CBS system on regular basis.

 

A report of all such users who are not using the CBS system in spite of being allotted a code will be put up for review before the management for appropriate action.

 

Nevertheless, it should be noted by all the staff that the concession of using the mobile handsets during office hours is given chiefly for generating the OTP for login to the CBS & in case if the staff is found using the Mobile Handset for accessing social media/playing game/watching sports/movies etc and for any other activity that is purely unofficial will be subject to strict disciplinary action, and such Mobile Handsets may be impounded by the Branch/Department In-charge.

         

In order to ensure that the guidelines mentioned in this office order are followed in true word & spirit , surprise periodic visit should be undertaken by the officials of respective zonal/cluster offices/ regional offices & by Inspection/HRM officials and report their findings to the CGM.

 

Please bring the instructions of this office order to the notice of all members of the staff at your branch/office, and implement them without any deviation whatsoever.

 

 

P.S. Salian

Chief Executive Officer
