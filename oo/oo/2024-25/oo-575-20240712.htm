Office Order No. 575 dated 12th July 2024

1.           Designation of Shri. Anant D. Dhanpawde, Asst. General Manager as CISO (Chief Information Security Officer)

2.           Shri . Anandkumar M. Kinare, Asst. General Manager as Head of the IS Audit Dept.

 

 

I.  Please refer to Office Order No. 545 dated 9th May 2022 on the subject of Designation of Shri. K.S. Tulsulkar, Sr. Manager as Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) - Duties & Responsibilities.

 

II.  Shri. K. S. Tulsulkar as Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) has resigned from Services of the Bank w.e.f. 29/11/2022. Due to the resignation of CISO, a vacancy for the post of CISO has arisen. In order to comply with the regulatory guidelines it was decided to appoint CISO. Recently, Shri Anant D. Dhanpawde has been promoted from Chief Manager to Asst. Gen. Manager. Considering his ability to handle and experience of various fields relating to operations and security of IT department, it was decided to designate Shri Anant D. Dhanpawde as Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).

 

III.  Accordingly, Shri. Anant D. Dhanpawde has taken over as the “Chief Information Security Officer” in the Bank with effect from 15/05/2024. The duties and responsibilities of the CISO are already given in Annexure I of Office Order No. 545 dated 9th May 2022. The same are given (with a change in the authority to whom the CISO will now report) in Annexure I hereto for ready reference.

 

IV.  The duties and responsibilities of Shri. Anandkumar Kinare as Information Systems Auditor and Head of the IS Audit Department shall be as per Annexure-II hereto for ready reference. He will report to the General Manager (Accounts, Audit & Treasury Dept.)

 

V.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department.

 

 

 

Chief Executive Officer

Annexure-I

The Duties and Responsibilities of the CISO

 

I.     He will report directly to the Chief Executive Officer. However, he will have a working relationship with the DGM (IT) to keep himself informed about the IT infrastructure and operations so as to build effective security in IT across the bank, in tune with business requirements and objectives.

II.     He will coordinate the security related issues/implementation internally, and also with relevant external agencies.

III. He will be responsible for articulating and enforcing the policy of the bank to protect its information assets, which will cover:

1.  Information Security Policy and Strategy – Inputs and Enhancements.

2.  Security guidelines and measures to protect data and systems.

3.  Information Security Risk,      Threat,      Vulnerability Assessment, Review, Management, Monitoring and Reporting – on a continuous basis

4.  Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Planning – Security Inputs and Enhancements

5.  Security Operations Centre and Incident Management.

6.  Business Case for Information Security Investments and Expenditure.

IV.     He will be responsible for the under-mentioned tasks:

1.       Setting Key Goal Indicators and Key Performance Indicators of the Information Security Program.

2.       Laying down enforceable rules.

3.       Organising Information Security Awareness training.

4.       Maintaining Security Posture and Profile of the Bank at expected levels.

5.       Active collaboration and communication with business and operating units.

6.       Internal and external security intelligence.

7.       Drawing security    organization     structure  with well-designed roles and responsibilities.

8.       Compliance with regulatory requirements on Information Security.

9.       Investigations in IT frauds and mitigation measures.

10.   Appointing & guiding of team of IT Security Experts for audit assignments.

11.   Conduct Information Security and Task Force Committee Meeting quarterly and keep all Information Security related issues, alerts/ advisories/circulars and important points raised by Security Operations Center in this meeting with recommendations for necessary review and action.

 

V.   The above scope is not exhaustive but illustrative. CISO shall be required to do any other work related to above and/or other work as entrusted to him from time to time by the Management.

 

 

Annexure-II

The duties and responsibilities of the

Information Systems Auditor and Head of IS Audit Dept.

 

1.       To ensure the compliance of I.S. security policy approved by the Board,

2.       Arrange to prepare and update I.S. Audit manual and checklist as per regulatory guidelines,

3.       Arrange to draw up the programme for half yearly/yearly I.S. Audit of Branches/ Depts. and regulate the work of I.S. Audit staff, adopt appropriate systems and practices for conducting IS Audit on annual basis covering all the critically important branches/departments in terms of nature and volume of business. Such audits should be undertaken preferably prior to the statutory audit so that IS Audit Reports are available to Statutory Auditors well in time for examination and for incorporation of comments if any in the audit reports. IS Audit reports are to be placed before Audit Committee/Board and compliances are to be ensured within the time frame as outlined in audit policy.

4.    i) Arrange to conduct I.S. Audit of Branches / H.O. Depts as per policy of the Bank.

 ii) Major Audit objectives are,

a)    safeguarding IS Assets and IT Processes by finding vulnerabilities during audit,

b)    Verification of Data Integrity and Security,

c)     Evaluation of system effectiveness and efficiency,

d)    Verification of compliance of internal guidelines and procedures in addition to legal, regulatory and statutory requirements, and,

e)    Segregation of duties between Information Security Section and Technical Section.

5.       Put up notes before higher authorities / Audit Committee of the Board for changes to be made in I.S. Audit Policy as and when required,

6.       Arrange to put up major synopses / executive summaries of I.S. Audit reports before Higher Authorities / Audit Committee of the Board,

7.       Ensure timely submission of compliance of I.S. Audit reports by Branches / Depts.,

8.       Ensure that Bank’s IS Assets/ Resources and IT processes are dependable, controlled and protected from misuse at all times by finding process gaps during audit,

9.       Carry out IT systems audit at periodic intervals and submit a report on their status to Audit committee of the Board,

10.    Verify Data Integrity and Security during audits performed,

11.    Evaluate System Effectiveness and Efficiency during audits performed,

12.    Verify compliance of internal guidelines and procedures in addition to legal, regulatory and statutory requirements,

13.    During performance of Audits Take review of:

i.      General Computer Control,

ii.     Application Control,

iii.   Data integrity,

iv.   Network Controls and connectivity set up,

v.    Application Software support from IT Dept.,

vi.   Branch Hardware set up and Operating System Access controls,

vii. ATM operations,

viii.          Vendor Contracts (SLA),

ix.   Business Continuity Planning (BCP),

x.    Implementation of IS policy guidelines which are to be complied with by the branches and departments,

14.    Identify and assess potential risks to bank’s operations,

15.    Assess the means of risk mitigation and safeguarding of IT assets,

16.    Review the adequacy of controls established to ensure compliance with the policies, plans, procedures and business objectives,

17.    Assess the level of compliance of established procedures / controls,

18.    Assess the reliability and security of financial / management information and the systems and operations that provide this information.

19.    Provide role-based training on information security to the workforce. Evaluate effectiveness of training to continuously upgrade it.

20.    Keep track/follow up of implementation by IT Department of alerts and advisories issued by regulatory bodies; also share relevant information with all staff to the extent they are concerned.

21.    Conduct VAPT of critical IT Assets to maintain security posture of bank at expected level.

22.    Keep track/follow up of security incidents reported to Regulatory Bodies by taking necessary feedback from IT Dept and report it to Audit committee on annual basis. Help Vigilance Dept in investigation in IT frauds and mitigation measures.

 

The scope of the role as given above is illustrative and not exhaustive. Bank may assign such other duties and entrust such other responsibilities to the incumbent as exigencies may require from time to time.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian
