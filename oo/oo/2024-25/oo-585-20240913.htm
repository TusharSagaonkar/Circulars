Office Order No. 585 dated 13th September 2024

Allocation of Duties and Responsibilities of CGM and GMs

 

I.     Reference is invited to the Office Order No. 556 dated 22nd May 2023 on the subject of/1“Re-organization of Corporate Structure of the Bank”. The Office Order broadly covered the corporate structure in the bank reporting and control lines, among others, of the General Managers. The position of the Chief General Manager has now been filled and he will be responsible for operational issues of the Bank.

 

II.   A few changes have since been made in the corporate structure such as RBI mandated supersession of the Bank’s Board by the Administrator assisted by a committee, and a churn in the positions of members of the senior management accompanied by lateral induction of new members therein.

 

III.  There are now two verticals in the Bank namely (1) Business and (2) Operations. CEO/ Chief Business Officer will be the head of the Business vertical.

Mr. Premnath Salian is the Chief Executive Officer with focused responsibility of business growth.

1.  Role and functions of the CEO

i)     CEO will now lead the Business vertical, he has been freed from the regular day-to-day operations of the Bank. He will focus on marketing of Deposits and Advances. Following executives shall directly report to him.

a)   Mr. Tushar Salastekar, General Manager (Business Development & Operations).

b)   Mr. Virsen Gurav, Dy. General Manager, Pune Zone.

c)    Mr. Gangadhar Narkar, Dy. General Manager, Business Development.

(However, in respect of operational matters, the abovementioned functionaries shall report to the Chief General Manager).

ii)      All credit and recovery matters to be placed before the Administrator’s Committee will be routed through the Chief Executive Officer. In addition to the above, the CEO will represent the bank before all the regulatory authorities such as the RBI and the Central Registrar, Cooperative societies.

 

2.  CGM/Chief Operations Officer will be the head of the Operations vertical.

The position of the CGM has been reinstated and Shri. Barun Raj Upadhyay is placed in the said position.

Role and functions of the CGM

i)  The CGM will be entrusted with all the matters relating to compliance and internal operations.

ii) The executives who will report directly to him are as under.

a) Mr. D. Venkatesan, General Manager (Administration and IT).

b) Mr. Rajesh Paralkar, General Manager (Finance and IS Audit)

c)  Mr. Pradeep Mandke, Chief Compliance Officer.

d) Mr. Adhikrao Kumbhar, Chief Risk Officer.

e)  Mr. Anant Dhanpawade, Chief Information and Security Officer.

f)   Ms. Jagruti Arte, Head of Internal Audit.

g) Mr. Ajit Shukla, Assistant General Manager (Vigilance) and CVO

iii) All operational matters to be placed before the Administrator’s Committee will be routed through the Chief General Manager. As per regulatory requirement CCO, CRO, CISO & HIA are vest with the control of Administrator’s Committee.

 

3. The control and reporting structure in respect of the executives and the functionaries working under them is shown graphically in Annexure – I.

4. The roles, functions and responsibilities of the General Managers and the functionaries directly working under them are given in Annexure-II.

5. All the roles and functions in the instant circular and Annexure-II are suggestive and not exhaustive. A detailed office order in this behalf will be issued is due course.

 

IV. All the previous instructions regarding duties and responsibilities in the various office orders whether mentioned generally in the text of the relative office orders or specifically in their annexures, shall hold good unless they are inconsistent with the current management chart in the Annexure.

 

V.   All the members of the Senior Management, Executives, other officials and the staff members are advised to note the instructions herein carefully and follow them meticulously.

 

P.S. Salian

Chief Executive Officer
