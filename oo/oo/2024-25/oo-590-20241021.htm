Office Order No. 590 dated 21st October 2024

I.    Name and Staff No. must appear below signature in all letters, notes, reports, papers, and documents under standardized stamp

II.   Each document bearing signature of an official (including a verified copy/photocopy) must additionally indicate the branch name and the designation of the official

I.   Please refer to the Office Order No. 187 dated the 8th November 2004,  the Office Order No. 296 dated the 10th September 2009, and the Office Order No. 524 dated the 20th December, 2019, issuing instructions to the staff members regarding the manner of subscribing signatures on various letters, reports, notes, papers and documents authored, authenticated or verified by them. Instructions were also given that each staff member shall invariably indicate his/her Name and Staff Number beneath the signature subscribed by him/her on each paper and document. Further, instructions were also issued that the signature of each staff member must conform to his/her specimen signature on the record of the Bank.

 

II.  It is observed that the instructions as above are not being followed by most of the staff-members. Verification of copies/photocopies by comparing with the originals is similarly done in a very casual manner. Verified documents, letters, notes, reports, and similar other papers and documents bear only initials or shortened signatures which are not recognisable because they are not as per the specimen signatures of the staff-members concerned on the record of the Bank and are subscribed without Name, Staff number and Designation of the staff member concerned.

 

III.  Similarly, the stamp reading “Document Verified with Original” is missing on the photocopies of the OVDs etc. Many times, round stamp of the branch is affixed with initials. The texts, sizes and shapes of the rubber stamps are also often divergent. This makes it difficult for the immediate recipient, auditor, controlling authority or investigator to identify the signatory, and the branch to which he/she belongs leading to several further problems.

 

IV.  Therefore, it is being advised as under.

 The shapes, sizes, and the texts of two rubber stamps, the first reading “Document Verified with Original” and the second recording the “Staff Member’s Name, Staff Number and Designation”, have been standardised as under.

1.    Initially, 2 Nos. of “Document Verified with Original” stamp shall be supplied to the branches by Centralised KYC/AML Dept. In case of the number falling short of the requirement, Branches are advised to prepare the “Document Verified with Original” stamp at their end as per specification given above. However, the In-charge of the Branch shall ensure that the stamps used are of standard size and specification given above.

2.    All the staff-members must invariably subscribe their signatures to the letters, notes, reports, papers and documents (including credit-related documents) which they have authenticated or verified, in strict accordance with their specimen signatures on the record of the Bank. The rubber stamp depicting the Name of the Staff Member, his/her Staff Number and Designation (with the standardised shape, size, and text) must be put under the signature.

3.    In case of transfer of the staff-member, he/she shall carry the stamp specific for him/her to the branch/office of transfer.

4.    In the event of loss/misplacement of the staff-specific stamp, charges for making available a new stamp shall be recovered from the staff member concerned.

5.    In respect of each verification of a documents photocopy, the rubber stamp “Document Verified with Original” (with the standardised shape, size and text) must be put on the copy on which the signature of the staff member verifying the copy shall be subscribed, with his (staff-number and designation) rubber stamp being further put under the “Document Verified with Original” stamp.

6.    It should be noted that use of non-standardised stamps is prohibited.

V.   Branches shall submit a certificate to the KYC-AML section by 31st October of having got above mentioned stamp prepared and issued to each staff member, who is required to author, authenticate, or verify letters, reports, notes, papers and documents.

VI.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all the staff members / officials concerned at your Branch/ Department and ensure meticulous compliance.

 

 

P.S. Salian

Chief Executive Officer
