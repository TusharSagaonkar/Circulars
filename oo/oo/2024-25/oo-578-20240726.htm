Office Order No. 578 dated 26th July 2024

Discontinuation of Outsourced Security in the Bank

Deployment of subordinate staff as Sub-staff (Caretaking function)

______________________________________________________________________

I.   With the objective of effective utilisation of existing surplus staff in the subordinate cadre, our Bank initially deployed some such personnel as ATM attendants vide the Office Order No. 568 dated the 23rd November 2023. Subsequently, as an austerity measure, the bank decided to discontinue the outsourced security for our ATMs vide the Office Order No. 574 dated the 12th June 2024.

 

II.  In view of the need for continuing further austerity measures, it was decided in the meeting of the Administrator’s Committee dated the 10th June 2024 to do away with the outsourced security altogether from branches. To utilize the available staff in the subordinate cadre optimally in the emerging scenario, it has further been decided to discontinue the exclusive ATM Attendant function currently entrusted to the staff in the subordinate cadre and entrust with them the overall caretaking function of branches.

 

III.  It has also been decided to impart proper training to the Sub-staff now being entrusted with the caretaking function.

 

IV.  The Bank has security personnel recruited out of ex-servicemen who hold gun licenses and guns. They were designated as Security Guards. They will continue to be assigned duties in shifts depending upon the assessment of the security situation by the Branch-In-Charges in consultation with the Special Officer (Security and Premises).

 

V.   Any member in the subordinate cadre now entrusted with the caretaking function will normally be deployed in shifts other than those in which a Security Guard is deployed.

 

VI.  At branches, there may arise situations when Security Guard and Sub-staff (Caretaking function) may remain absent in some shift(s). The Branch-In-Charge concerned shall under such circumstances, with the involvement of staff at the branch, share common responsibility of the caretaking function at the branch.

 

VII.     Further, the branches in Karnataka and Gujrat are currently not provided with Security Guards and the Sub-staff (Caretaking function). The Branch-In-Charges for those branches shall organise the caretaking function in their branches by involving the entire staff thereat.

VIII.   The duties of a Sub-staff (Caretaking function) shall be as under:

1.        He shall report on duty 30 Minutes before the shift schedule and he will be carrying the Key of the Branch premises for opening branch in the morning and closing at the close of business / working hours of the branch, whichever is later.

2.        While on duty, he will always remain in the uniform currently prescribed for the sub-staff. The bank reserves the right to change the uniform if deemed necessary.

3.        He shall take a round in and around the branch premises prior to taking position at a designated point decided by the bank / Manager, to satisfy himself that nothing had gone wrong during the previous period when there was no Sub-staff (performing the Caretaking function) in attendance. Such situation shall typically arise at the beginning of the first shift of the branch on a working day.

4.        He will position himself at a designated point decided by the bank / Manager, and observe the movements of the persons entering and exiting the branch.

5.        He will report to the Branch-In-Charge / Branch Manager in case of any suspicious movement of a person(s) or vehicle(s), some baggage, packet etc. lying unattended, or such things noticed /found generally in and around the branch premises. He may in emergent situations, seek help from the local persons, members of the public, or from police if required for their examination/verification. However in all other situations he shall do so with the permission of his Branch-In-Charge / Branch Manager / Superiors.

6.        He should request any person using mobile phone in the branch for a time longer than usual to go outside the branch and complete the communication so as to prevent disturbance in the working of the branch.

7.        He shall see for himself the locations of all the alarm buttons in the branch and test them on a weekly basis.

8.        He shall periodically check whether the fire extinguishers in the branch are due for refilling and report to Branch Manager in case refilling is overdue for any of them and fire alarms are in working condition.

9.        He must not leave his assigned place during the duty hours without informing the Branch-In-Charge / Branch Manager / superiors.

10.     He will not allow any person with firearms to enter the premises; such a matter shall be reported by him to the Branch-In-Charge immediately.

11.     He shall check prior to leaving the premises at the end of his duty that the Strong Room / the Room with the Cash Safe/ Lockers is/are properly secured under lock and key by the official(s) concerned.

12.     He must keep himself in the know about: -

                         i.    Locations of fire extinguishers generally in the branch and especially in the ATM cabin, and their operation.

                       ii.    Locations of the main switch panel of the electric power supply.

                      iii.    Locations of emergency alarm switch, MCP (Manual Call Point for fire), and Fire Alarm Panel.

                     iv.    Important telephone numbers including those of the nearest Fire Brigade Station, nearest Police Station, the Branch-In-Charge and the Special Officer (Security & Premises), and call them in emergencies.

13. When cash is being replenished in an ATM by the joint custodians, he should be extra alert to prevent any pilferage, hold-up, or worse incidents. To ensure this, he should remain alert outside the cabin.

14. The allotment of duties across shifts in double-shift branches will be adjusted considering criticality of time of the day. Typically, a person with adequate experience in Caretaking functions shall be placed in the first shift and a sub-staff / Caretaker in the second shift.

15. During an emergency such that the Sub-Staff (Caretaking function) or the Service Provider or both does/do not come on duty, the Branch-In-Charge will take all the    staff into confidence about it so that the entire staff, aside from their regular work, will be keenly observant of the happenings in the branch. Alternatively, another     member of sub-staff at the branch may be assigned the caretaking function for the day.

IX.  Holidays

The Sub-staff (Caretaking function) will have public holidays/weekly (Sunday)/ second-fourth Saturday offs like other employees of the branch.

X.   Leave

The Sub-staff (Caretaking function) shall continue to be entitled for the leave as that to their cadre.

XI.  Role of the Branch-In-Charge

The Branch-In-Charge is expected to :-

i.         Exercise overall control over the security of the branch including the ATM through the Security Guard and Caretaking through the Sub-staff (caretaking function). (He will also exercise overall control even otherwise).

ii.       Work in close coordination with the Special Officer (Security & Premises).

iii.      Maintain a security instructions file at the branch and keep it up-to-date.

iv.     Examine the security file and take it into custody as part of the overall taking-over exercise of the branch.

v.       Not ask Security Guards and Sub-staff (Caretaking function) to perform messengerial or any other tasks while on duty.

vi.     Ensure sufficient lighting in and around the premises and the ATM lobby.

vii.    Test the emergency lighting systems to ensure that they are in good working condition.

viii.  Keep the cameras installed in ATM cabins and branch premises in good and functional condition. (If not, they should be got repaired immediately with the help of the IT Dept.) Previous night footage of CCTV of ATM Cabin should be checked by Managers and also at regular interval during day time.

ix.     Ensure that cash levels in ATMs are optimal and not excessive. Branches should check the pattern on withdrawals from ATMs on weekdays and during weekends/ holidays and dynamically decide the cash position at ATMs.

x.       Monitor that rolling shutters, collapsible grills and normal glass doors of all the entrances are invariably closed and locked after working hours.

xi.     Monitor that the doors, windows, grills, ventilators etc. are in proper working condition and securely closed at the time of closing. All electrical appliances, lights, fans are switched off before the doors are closed and locked.

xii.    Monitor that only one out of more than two gates is kept open during business hours.

xiii.  Monitor that the main gate is closed and kept locked till the cash is distributed; only thereafter should the entrance be unlocked and opened for public.

xiv.  Ensure that the important telephone nos. (viz. those of Police Station, Fire Brigade, Hospitals, Branch Incharges etc.) are displayed at a prominent place in the ATM Cabin as well as on the Branch premises.

xv.    Get removed/disposed of immediately any unused items which are considered as ‘Fire hazard’.

xvi.  Get fire extinguishers inspected to ensure that refilling is not overdue and that all of them are in serviceable condition.

xvii.Inspect the premises to look for any damage, loose or dangling wires, temporary wiring without plug/sockets, multiple connections from single point etc., and get them immediately removed, and also look for necessity of repairs.

xviii.       Inspect the strong room and safe deposit locker room to ensure that they are properly maintained as per the laid down instructions and the operations therein are performed strictly according to the laid down systems and procedures.

xix.  Liaise with different authorities’ viz. Police, Fire Brigade, etc.on a continuous basis.

xx.    Permit entry in the branch premises before and after the working hours and on Sundays and holidays only on written request justified on the basis of reasons.

xxi.  Call the Special Officer (Security & Premises) in case of any difficulty.

 

XII.   The Branch In charge, Security Guards, and the Sub-staff (Caretaking function) and also the entire staff present in the premises shoulder the responsibility for the safety of property and personnel at the branch and therefore should work whole heartedly and in unison for this purpose.

 

XIII. Kindly bring the contents of above Office Order to the knowledge of all Staff Members at Branch & ensure its strict compliance.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Chief Executive Officer
