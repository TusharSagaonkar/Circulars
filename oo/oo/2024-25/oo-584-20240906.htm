Office Order No. 584 dated 6th September 2024

Establishment of Clusters of Branches in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Nashik townships for Business Development

 

I.     The Bank is currently experiencing challenges on the fronts of low-cost deposits, retail and MSME loans, credit risk mitigation and regulation. Inherent in these challenges are those pertaining to Net Interest Margin (NIM), NPAs (present and potential) and appraisal standards. It is necessary to make all out efforts by all the offices and branches in the Bank to surmount the challenges and make the Bank win on all the fronts. However, considering the urgency to demonstrate significant results, it is necessary to make a big thrust by taking up the challenges head on in select pockets. With this end in view, it has been decided to group branches in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Nashik townships into clusters for making intensive efforts at business development, NPA management, and super-efficient customer service.

 

II.   It is expected that the establishment of clusters will result in the following.

1.   Intensive coverage of the retail market in geographies where it exists in concentration.

2.   Personalized service to our retail P segment and MSME customers.

3.   Improvement in asset quality through centralized appraisal and sanctioning (by domain experts), streamlined processes, tight Turn Around Time (TAT), and audit compliance.

4.   Freedom to branches from credit appraisals and sanctions, thus enabling them to focus on deposit mobilization, marketing, and follow up of weak accounts.

VIII. Function of clusters

The team in the cluster office will attend to the scrutiny, appraisal, and sanction of all the retail loan proposals (in accordance with the sanctioning powers delegated to different personnel at that level) except loans and overdrafts against the primary security of FDRs, Govt. Securities, Gold ornaments and NSCs/KVPs/ RBI bonds, LIC policies, etc., which will be processed and sanctioned at branches in the clusters. Documentation and disbursement of loans sanctioned at the cluster office will be done at branches where the loan accounts are to be maintained. Sanction of facilities by cluster offices will include new sanctions, reviews, renewals, enhancements and reductions in cash credit facilities.

 

IX. Shortening Turn Around Time (TAT) for loan-processing and sanctioning

The specialized teams focused on proper assessment, appraisal and processing retail loans are being put in place in the cluster design to ensure a smoother workflow and faster approvals. This in turn is expected to shorten the Turn Around Time (TAT) for loan processing and sanctioning leading to enhanced customer satisfaction. The details of product-wise TAT being prescribed are given in Annexure-I

XIV.             Role and responsibilities of branches in supporting the Clusters

1.     After sanction, the loan files will be sent from the cluster offices to branches where the accounts are or will be maintained. Entire security documentation in respect of loans sanctioned by clusters will be done at branches. The procedures like assignment, mortgage/pledge/hypothecation, etc. and all the other formalities necessary for creation of the Bank's charge on securities, whether primary or collateral, are to be completed by branches./1No work of material importance upto the stage of and related to the disbursement should be kept pending. Branches must ensure that, post sanction, the terms and conditions of sanction are invariably complied with before disbursement.

2.     Branches will address discrepancies, if any identified, and queries, if any raised, during concurrent and internal audits by placing before the auditors the necessary documentation and information.

3.     Each Branch-In-Charge will be responsible for achieving the goals of deposits, loans, profit and NPA reduction, and will be further responsible for monitoring of loans and advances, including post disbursement inspections, recoveries in overdue accounts, etc. He/she will ensure regular and timely receipt of statements stock / book debt statements, obtaining adequate insurance cover for the securities (such as stocks of goods, plant & machineries, moveable & immovable properties) charged to the Bank, etc. All accounts of loans and advances, including newly sanctioned accounts, on the books of the branch should be allotted to staff members at the branch for recovery and follow-up.

4.     Each Branch-In-Charge should ensure visit to defaulters with limits above/1Rs. 5.00 lakhs for effective recovery. The Asst. Manager(s)/Officer(s)/Clerk(s) should be assigned, through proper allocation, the job of visiting all the defaulters with limits up to Rs. 5.00 lakh.

5.     The Chief Managers/Managers of branches in the cluster should visit the borrowers with un-renewed/un-reviewed CC accounts and ensure that proposals for renewal/review/ enhancement are received in time. If required, the borrowers may be given guidance in this behalf. The review/ renewal proposals thus procured, complete with the required documents/papers/financial statements, should be promptly forwarded to the cluster officesfor further processing, scrutiny and sanction./1In respect of review/renewal of CC Accounts, the Branch-In-Charge should ensure that the proposals in this regard along with all the documents are received atleast 1 month before the respective due dates. The timeline for proposals above Rs. 50 Lakh will be two months before the due date.

6.     Timely receipt of financial statements like audited balance sheet, profit and loss account and other financial data from the borrowers is of crucial importance for monitoring borrower accounts. It is particularly so in case of high value accounts with exposure limit of Rs.1 Crore and above for their timely review/ renewal. In the absence of timely receipt of the said financial statements, Branch in-charges should follow-up regularly with all the CC account borrowers and obtain audited financial statements regularly and send the files to clusters offices to enable completion of the relative Credit Rating exercise.

7.     Each Branch-In-Charge in clusters should market the Bank’s loans and advances and deposits through regular visits to the industrial units, educational institutions, corporate offices, etc. It will be his/her duty to ensure that goals for business and profit allotted by the Head Office are achieved on monthly basis.
