Office Order No. 545 dated 9th May 2022

 

1.       Designation of Shri. K. S. Tulsulkar, Sr. Manager as CISO (Chief Information Security Officer)

2.       Shri. Kiran Rane, Asst. Gen. Manager to function as Head of the IS Audit Dept. in addition to his current function as “Head-Vigilance Dept.”

 

 

Please refer to Office Order No. 517 dated 24th June 2019 on the subject of Designation of Shri. K. M. Rane as Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) - Duties & Responsibilities. Subsequently, Shri. K.S. Tulsulkar was designated as Information Systems Auditor and Head of the IS Audit Dept., vide Office Order No. 539 dated 3rd November 2021.

 

In the 116th Audit Committee Meeting dated 21st March 2022 it was decided to shift CISO dept. to Kurla to ensure direct supervision of the Bank’s critical IT infrastructure at the Data Centre. Accordingly, a submission to designate Shri. K. S. Tulsulkar as Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) was placed before the Managing Director. The experience and qualifications of Shri. K. S. Tulsulkar were taken into consideration while making the submission. The submission received approval on 21st April 2022.

 

Accordingly, Shri. K. S. Tulsulkar has taken over as the Chief Information Security Officer” in the Bank with effect from 21.04.2022. The duties and responsibilities of the CISO are already given in Para II.2. of Office Order No. 517 dated 24th June 2019. The same are given (with a change in the authority to whom the CISO will now report) in Annexure I hereto for ready reference.

 

The duties and responsibilities of Shri. Kiran Rane (in addition to his current ones as Head of Vigilance Department) as Information Systems Auditor and Head of the IS Audit Department shall be as per the Paras II. and III. of the Order No. 539 dated 3rd November 2021 (which was issued at the time of the placement of Shri. K. S. Tulsulkar in that position). They are also given in Annexure-II hereto for ready reference. The combined designation of Shri. Kiran Rane shall henceforth be “Head - IS Audit and Vigilance Department”. In his combined role, he will report to the Dy. General Manager (Inspection, IS Audit & Vigilance Dept. )

 

Information Security Committee

1. CISO is convener of Information Security & Task Force Committee Meeting; Members of the security committee are the same as given in our Cyber Security Policy (Task Force).

2. IDRBT in its communication “IT Governance Series for the Indian Banking Sector” has recommended quarterly frequency for meetings of the Information Security Committee to regularly monitor the information security and risk management processes and corrective actions to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements. Minutes of these meetings should be placed in the immediate Board Meeting.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department.

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
