Office Order No. 554 dated 21st January 2023

Corrigendum to Office Order No. 553 dated 10th January 2023

 

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.     Attention is invited to Office Order No. 553 dated 10th January 2023 on the Subject “Risk Management – change in Periodicity of Meetings of Five Committees from Monthly to Bimonthly” wherein Periodicity of all the five committees was mentioned as Bimonthly with immediate effect.

 

II.   It may be noted that among the 5 committees mentioned in above referred Office Order, i.e. the meeting of Asset Liability Committee (ALCO) will be held at monthly interval. The periodicity of the remaining four committees i.e.

i)      Risk Management Executive Committee

ii)     Credit Risk Management Committee

iii)   Market Risk Management Committee

iv)   Operational Risk Management Committee

will continue to be held at Bi-monthly intervals.

 

III. Therefore, it is advised that Office Order No. 553 dated 10th January 2023 is hereby withdrawn and instant Office Order is being issued instead.

 

IV. Please bring the above changes to the notice of all the executives, officials and staff members concerned.

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
