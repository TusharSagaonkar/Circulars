Office Order No. 547 dated 4th July 2022

Master Directives related to Data Purification, Updation and Refinement

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Our Bank has decided to transform the current CBS with new robust and state of the art software shortly. While our IT dept. has initiated the primary work of the CBS Transformation, the project’s success entirely depends upon the co-ordination and co-operation amongst all the relevant & critical departments coupled with the wholehearted support by branch staff.

 

A major area of concern during the entire CBS transformation project is the below par standard of upkeep of crucial data in our Database. During the migration process, it will be of utmost importance that the Data that will eventually get transferred to new system needs to be pure, accurate and correct. Any sort of Data mismatch due to incorrect or wrong input of data will lead to possible inordinate delay in launching of the new CBS, thus derailing the activity and missing the envisaged deadline.

 

As the said CBS migration activity is a time bound and planned exercise, taking into consideration the regulatory compliance and better customer service, it is pertinent that the data that is being handled and fed in the system is up to the mark in order to ensure smooth migration.

 

The term "Garbage in, Garbage out" originated in the computer science and information technology fields to illustrate the fact that the quality of the output received from a computer program depends on the quality of the information that was input. The term has since expanded in meaning to other fields, finance among them.

 

In the past successive RBI inspections, the RBI inspecting officials have pointed out that many crucial customer related information is not properly updated in the CBS which resulted in wrong submission of data to the regulatory authorities. This projects a poor image of our Bank.

 

Our Internal Inspection team & the Concurrent Auditors also face a challenge during their course of Inspection as many times error prone reports are generated due to the wrong feeding of data by staff at branches /departments. Such type of wrong reporting to both internal as well as external authorities entails a considerable amount of doubts and notions about the sanctity of the data, due to certain notable deficiencies.

 

Further, incomplete and non- updated data in the OMNI system also leads to hampering of customer service. It is observed that a large number of ATM /Rupay cards are being returned by postal authorities for various reasons such as incomplete address, room no. not mentioned etc., however in most of the cases it is reported that customer has already submitted their latest KYC documents but the same have not been updated by the branch staff in the database. Specific directions have already been issued in this regard by the Head Office vide OC 826 dt. 29.10.2019.

 

It should be noted that over the period, our Bank through various communications have impressed upon the staff & Officials about the need for periodic review & up-dation of data and ensure correct feeding of data in order to avoid the wrong submission and reporting of data to various regulatory authorities. Our higher authorities have also time and again emphasized the need of accurate and correct feeding of data in the OMNI system for generation of perfect MIS reports that are crucial for monitoring. However, it is regretfully noted that issued directions are not being followed scrupulously by the branches in true word & spirit, thus leading to wrong reporting that causes major embarrassment in front of regulatory authorities.

 

A more recent communication Circular OC 868 dt. 25.01.2022 was also issued wherein the need for correction in existing category wise Classification of Accounts & customer master etc., was stressed upon. However, inspite of the specific instructions, it is still observed that most of the fields are not yet updated in the bank’s CBS (OMNI).

Now in order to streamline the entire  process of data purification before the stage of Data migration to the new CBS so that the reports that are generated through the system i.e. from Customer Master, Limit Master, Loan Basic  Detail Master, Security Details Master & other relevant details Master are captured properly from correct and accurate inputs from the staff at branch/dept. level, it is now been decided to introduce the within mentioned detailed guidelines that needs to be adopted by the branches/depts.., on an ongoing basis concept  with a strict time frame schedule.

 

Role of I.T. Department.

I.T. Dept. will keep a host of readily available reports on the Reporting Server at branch level and also periodically add other crucial reports where gaps are observed for timely compliance by the Branches.

 

Further, I.T. Dept. will arrange to provide Offline Training to one Officer of each Branch from Mumbai/Navi Mumbai/Thane regions at I.T. Dept., Kurla shortly for taking up the task of Data purification of their respective branches. The details of such Training program will be informed in due course to all the concerned entities. Simultaneously, an Online Training program will also be arranged by I.T. Dept. to Officers stationed at outstation branches for the said task. Once such training is complete, through the trained staff the Branch In-charges need to create awareness amongst the staff of their respective branches about the need of accurate Data feeding in the OMNI system. This will help in ensuring unification and purification of Data across the branches.

 

Role of Branches/Departments

The Branch In-charge should assign the task of Data purification/refinement to designated staff in their respective branches for addressing the identified gaps. The staff will access the Reports kept in the Reporting Server for timely compliance and get the same authorized by an Officer/Asst. Manager immediately and thereafter on an ongoing basis concept. A certificate in regards completion of Data purification related task to be submitted to respective Zonal Office alongwith Branch Managers certificate.

 

Role of CDEC Dept.

To ensure that all the respective fields of Customer Information Form (CIF) are entered in CBS (OMNI) database at the time of Data entry work. The In-charge of CDEC Dept. to follow-up continuously with branches and to submit a monthly report of the non- compliance / wrong feeding of Data by the branches to the respective Zonal offices and other higher authorities and further oversee the activities of the proposed separate Data Purification Cell.

 

Role of MIS /KYC/ AML / Credit dept. H.O.

To send periodic reports containing gaps / mismatch data etc. for updation of necessary fields in the OMNI system, by confirming the details in the respective loan files/Account opening forms etc. as the case maybe to the branches for timely compliance under advice to the respective Zonal Offices.

 

Role of Inspection Department

Inspection dept., will keep a continuous follow-up with the branches for the completion of the hitherto pending data refinement /purification task and thereafter on an ongoing basis. A monthly report of the pending count at branches should be compiled and submitted to the Chief Compliance Officer and higher authorities for review.

 

Role of Zonal Offices

The task of data refinement and purification on ongoing basis will be monitored by the respective Zonal offices of branches under their control. A certificate from the Branch In-charge about completion of Data purification related task must be obtained on monthly basis as a part of Branch Manager Certificate as already instructed in various earlier communications e.g. refer OC 416 dt. 29.10.2007. A list of the work completed in regards the Data refinement by branches should be presented before the Board for review on monthly basis.

 

Role of proposed DATA PURIFICATION CELL

The present count of gaps observed and identified in the Database is voluminous. There are certain areas where the said gaps need to be addressed at Branch Level mandatorily i.e. the correct data inputs need to be done in the database only after going through the documentary evidences available on record by way of account opening forms, documents available in loan/CC files, application etc., documents submitted for KYC etc., However, there are certain areas of gaps where any particular branch data can be purified and refined at a central location by accessing the reports and after identifying the gaps, compare the available data with the Masters in the Database and simultaneously rectify the said gaps through a dedicated team of staff /Officers. Due to such centralized activity a considerable volume of work can be completed at a fast pace and in time before actual Data Migration process begins from existing OMNI to new CBS in the near future.

 

The composition of the separate Data Purification Cell that will be set up shortly at our Vashi premises under the In-Charge of CDEC Dept. will comprise of Three Officers and Six Clerks. The said team will be entrusted with the Data Purification related work and the work progress of the team will be supervised by the CDEC Dept. In-Charge on continuous basis and a monthly report of the activities undertaken etc. will be submitted to the higher authorities for review. A separate Office Order for formation of Data Purification Cell and its activities will be communicated shortly.

 

Please take a careful note of the above instructions and bring the contents to the notice of all the concerned staff/officials for strict compliance. Any sort of lapse, laxity, casual and lethargic approach in the said task by the branches/departments will be viewed seriously.

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
