Office Order No. 549 dated 22nd August 2022

Enhancement in Staff Cash Credit facility made available to the Officers/Executives

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In terms of our Office Order No. 548 dated 22nd August 2022, we have enhanced Staff Cash Credit facility to our employees. Accordingly STCC facility to our Officers and Executives have been also revised and MOA with the Association has been entered and signed by both the parties. The salient features of Staff Cash Credit facility is as under with effect from 15.07.2022:

 

Personal Loans & Advances:

 

Cash Credit Facility:

Category

Enhanced amount in existing cash credit facility

(i)

DGM/ GM/ MD

Rs. 7.00 Lakh

(ii)

Asst. General Manager

Rs. 4.50 Lakh

(iii)

Sr. Manager

Rs. 3.50 Lakh

(iv)

Manager

Rs. 3.50 Lakh

(v)

Directly recruited Officers on their confirmation

Rs. 3.50 Lakh

(vi)

Directly recruited Officers on completing 2 years

Rs. 3.50 Lakh

 

a)   There is no change in the clause of purpose.

b)  All permanent Officers those who are on payroll of the bank services as on the date of signing of this settlement will be eligible for the aforesaid enhanced amount of Cash Credit Facility. 

c)   Those who join services in bank after the date of this agreement shall be entitled to the existing limits of Cash Credit Facility stipulated in the agreement dated 22.06.2021 and they will be entitled to benefits of this agreement only after they complete 4 years of confirmed service from the date of their confirmation.

d)  Those who have submitted their resignation from the services of the bank and are serving notice period or otherwise shall not be entitled to the benefits of Cash Credit facility and/ or benefits under this agreement. 

 

All Officials and Executives are advised to note changes made as above in staff cash credit facility and avail the same.

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
