Office Order No. 307 dated 1st October, 2009

Monthly visits to Branches by DGMs of Zones and AGMs of

Regional Offices

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          Our Dy. General Managers of all Zones and Asst. General Managers of Udupi and Ahmedabad Regional offices are visiting branches under their control for the purpose of business development, to meet VIP Customers, to see whether monthly targets for Deposits, Loans and Advances, LIC and Forex business, etc. have been achieved and may be arranging a meeting with entire staff of the branch to make critical evaluation on working and progress of the branch wherever necessary.

 

2.       However, in regard to these visits, no fixed time schedule and reporting system has been prescribed. We, therefore, advise that Dy. Gen. Managers of Zonal Offices and Asst. Gen. Managers of Udupi and Ahmedabad regions should make visits to branches under their control on monthly basis and convey their observations to Branches for their necessary action on various aspects as per Annexure - A on the next working day of their visits alongwith their specific guidance for business promotion and other matters.

 

3.       Corrective action taken by Branches should be conveyed to their Dy. General Managers / Asst. General Managers within 15 days of the visit. The follow up of these Visit Reports should be made scrupulously at Zonal Offices and Regional Offices.  

 

4.       Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at the Branch and ensure compliance.

 

V.S. Morye

Managing Director
