Office Order No. 304  dated  23rd September, 2009 

Auction of Gold Ornaments of Regional Office, Udupi

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

         

          You are aware that as per procedure laid down by Bank, Recovery Dept. undertakes the sale of gold ornaments through Panel Valuers when loanee defaults in repayment after maturity of the loan and the account has been classified as NPA. Branches send notices, make visits to the loanees' residences and in case of no recovery, final notice is given to repay loan and that Bank will arrange to sale the gold ornaments for recovery of entire dues. Branches send the concerned packets containing Gold Ornaments to Recovery Dept., Mumbai, for further action. The procedure being followed at our Branches in Mumbai/Navi Mumbai can not be followed in toto in Udupi. The procedure followed by erstwhile Janatha Co-op. Bank Ltd. and being followed by most of the other Banks in Udupi and Mangalore areas is to auction the gold ornaments. In order to follow the similar procedure, the matter was put up to the Board in its meeting held on 10.08.2009 and accordingly it has been decided to follow the under noted procedure for branches of Udupi Region only.

 

1)       Udupi Branches will send notice (As per Annexure – A) by Regd. A/D Post to all defaulters of Gold Loan Accounts where no repayment is forthcoming, immediately after maturity of the loan account and the account has been classified as NPA. This will be followed by visits to loanees' residence. After 15 days of first notice, Final Notice advising them that Bank will arrange for sale of gold ornaments if no repayment of entire dues are made within 15 days from the date of the notice                            (As per Annexure – B).

2)       If there is no response from the loanees, list of such defaulters will be prepared by Branches and sent to Regional Office, Udupi.

3)       Regional Office will send Intimation of Sale of Gold Ornaments by Regd. A/D Post to all these Borrowers before publication of Sale Notice. (As per Annexure – C).

4)       After 15 days of Intimation Notice, an advertisement for Auction of Gold Ornaments will be published in a local newspaper with the list of defaulters alongwith their outstanding amount. In the same advertisement, Date, Time and Venue for auction of Gold Ornaments will also be published.

5)       Regional Office will send Final Notice to all Borrowers alongwith the copy of advertisement released in Newspaper by Regd. A/D post before 21 days of date of auction. (As per Annexure-D).

6)       In the meantime, if there is no response from the Borrowers, all branches will handover the packets containing Gold Ornaments to Asst. Gen. Manager / Designated Officer at Regional Office against due acknowledgement.

7)       A.G.M. Regional Office / Designated Officer from Regional Office will take charge of packets containing the Gold Ornament(s) by opening Branch seal and will verify the ornament(s) as per the documents and will again keep in their safe custody with their own seal and will give acknowledgement to the Branch.

8)       Regional Office will send letters to Panel Gold Appraisers (Valuers) for Valuation of Gold Ornaments and will obtain quotations from minimum two Gold Valuers.   (As per annexure – E).

 

9)       Regional Office will inform the branches to recover Notice Charges, Valuation Charges, Visit Charges, Advertisement Charges and all other charges by debit to the Borrower's Accounts.

 

10)    Auction will be held at Regional Office, Udupi on the date published in the advertisement. Date, time and Venue will be strictly adhered to avoid legal complications.

 

11)    Reserve price will be fixed by Regional Office, Udupi, taking into consideration, present market rate, purity of the Gold Ornament(s) and contents of pure gold in the ornament(s) and Valuation by the Gold Appraisers (Valuers) on Panel.

 

12)    The Bidders on the spot will be allowed to inspect the Gold Ornaments and offer their bids in writing. They should be allowed to improve upon their bid amount on the original bid sheet in writing with their authentication during the bid process.

 

13)    Regional Office will accept highest bid in each loan account and settlement will be done on the spot against receipt of cash. Cash amount received will be appropriated towards gold loan account and balance amount, if any, will be credited to borrower's S.B./C.D. A/c. with due intimation by post. If there is no S.B./C.D. A/c of the borrower, Pay-order in the name of the Borrower will be issued and will be sent by Regd. A/D Post on the address recorded at the branch. (As per Annexure – F).

 

14)    After auction of the Gold Ornaments, if sale proceeds are less than the outstanding debit balance in the account, Asstt. Gen. Manager, Regional Office, Udupi will submit his recommendations to Dy. Gen. Manager (Legal & Recovery) either for write-off or for filing a suit and take necessary legal action against the Borrower depending on the debit balance amount remained in the Gold Loan Account and possibility of recovery from other assets/sources etc.

 

15)    It is the responsibility of the In-charge of the Branch and AGM, Regional Office Udupi and DGM Pune, to ensure that auction of gold ornaments is completed within 90 days of maturity of Loan Accounts.

 

Branch-in-charges of Udupi Region and officers/staff at D.G.M., Pune and Udupi Regional Office are advised to take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all the concerned staff and ensure compliance.
