Office Order No. 525 dated 17th February, 2020

Contest for Bank’s Staff Members, Branches & Departments

for Encouraging Mobile Banking Services, UPI & Rupay Debit Card

Contest-V

_______________________________________________________________________

 

I.   1) The Abhyudaya Mobile Banking service was launched in the month of July, 2015. It is available on Android enabled Smartphone and Apple enabled Iphone. Mobile Banking offers host of services such as balance enquiry, instant fund transfer, utility bill payment etc. Since the launch, the number of customers who have enrolled for mobile banking services through our Bank is on a steady increase, the current number of customers registered for such services being 2.5 Lakh.

 

2) Rupay Debit Card is another technology-enabled service which works across NFS networked ATMs, Point of Sale (POS) machines and on e-commerce websites. Instructions regarding facility of UPI for Fund Transfer were communicated to branches vide Circular OC No. 810 dated 16th February, 2019. These services offer great convenience to our customers.

 

3) There is however a need to promote the services aggressively among customers, and to present their availability as a selling point to them.

 

II.   1) In order to boost the use of the Mobile Banking Services, UPI & Rupay Debit Card among customers, it is important that our staff members themselves understand their importance, and learn & adopt them, so that they can thereafter work better as Bank’s ambassadors.

 

2) With a view to encouraging our staff to learn & adopt technology enabled services so that they can participate in promotion of these schemes among our customers, our Bank had conducted four contests for them. The contests received moderate response.

 

3) With the same object of encouraging continuous self-learning among the staff members, and promoting the usage of Mobile Banking service and Rupay Debit Card service through such staff among customers, a decision has been taken to conduct the 5th contest on Mobile Banking & Rupay Debit Card services for the staff members, branches and departments of our Bank.

 

III.  Guidelines for the contest are as under:

1)   The contest is open for Bank’s Employees /Officers/ Executives working at Branches, Departments of Head Office, Regional Offices and Zonal Offices.

2)   Contest is also for Branches and Departments. The parameter for success of branches and departments will be a combination of number of participants and individual score of participants.

3)  The period of contest will be from 18th February, 2020 to 17th March, 2020.
