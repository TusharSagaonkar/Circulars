Office Order No. 524 dated 20th December, 2019

 

Signatures of Staff Members/Managers

on Important Documents/Communications

Names to be Indicated

 

I.                 Please refer Office Order No. 187 dated 8th November, 2004 & Office Order No. 296 dated 10th September, 2009. Instructions were issued in terms thereof that wherever staff members are required to subscribe their signatures (say on TDS certificates, scrutiny notes, sanction letters, loan proposals, case file decisions, IDCs etc.), they must conform to the specimen signatures on the record of the Bank. Instructions were also given for all the staff members to invariably mention name beneath the signature subscribed on the papers & documents.

 

II. 1) It has been our observation that the instructions as above are not being followed by most of the staff-members which makes it difficult, whether to the immediate recipient, or an authority scrutinising/verifying the same later, to determine the signatory, leading to several problems. Therefore, instructions are hereby being reiterated that all the staff-members shall invariably subscribe their signatures on the papers/documents (including credit related documents) which they need to authenticate, as per the specimen signatures on the record of the Bank. They shall also invariably write their name beneath such signatures. They may brand their “Name & Staff No. ______” stamp in such cases as an alternative to writing the name.

 

2) Further, an author of any communication (such as IDC, Letter, Note etc.) to another branch/office is typically indicated by designation alone. In the absence of a signature which does not conform to the legible specimen signature on record, it becomes difficult to determine identity of the author. It is therefore being advised that author of any such communication must authenticate it in the style as under:

 _____ sd_______

 [Name of the author]

 [Designation :- ______________]

[_________________ Branch/Office]

 

III.  Any omission on the part of any staff-member :

1) To sign as per the specimen signature & write/brand his/her name below the signature on any document of the type indicated in Para II. 1) above.

 

And

 

2)    Omission by the author of any communication of the type indicated in Para II. 2) above to add his/her name to the designation (apart from the name of branch/office) at the end thereof, will be viewed with disfavour.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/Department.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
