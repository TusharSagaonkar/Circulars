Office Order No. 517 dated 24th June, 2019

 

Designation of Shri. K. M. Rane as Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

Duties & Responsibilities of the CISO

_______________________________________________________________________

 

I.  Our bank is reaching out to customers through various new channels such as Web based CBS Banking, Electronic Clearing / Fund Transfer (RTGS/NEFT, Rupay–e-Commerce) internet banking and mobile banking, Forex etc. In view of the above and having regard to risks emanating from adoption of new technology, the Bank has to implement an Internal Control framework, based on various standards, our own control-requirements and the current RBI guidelines.

 

II.   1. In order that the overall IT related risk-management is done effectively & efficiently, it was considered necessary to designate an officer at senior level as Chief Information Security Officer (in place of Shri. S. H. Pathan who has retired). The matter was placed in the Board meeting dated 24th May, 2019 and it was decided therein to designate Shri. K. M. Rane as the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).

 

2. The duties and responsibilities of the CISO shall be as under:

                i)   He will report directly to the Chief General Manager. However, he will have a working relationship with the GM (IT) to keep himself informed about the IT infrastructure and operations so as to build effective security in IT across the bank, in tune with business requirements and objectives.

 

              ii)   He will coordinate the security related issues / implementation internally, and also with relevant external agencies.

 

             iii)   He will be responsible for articulating and enforcing the policy of the bank to protect its information assets, which will cover:

a)  Information Security Policy and Strategy – Inputs and Enhancements.

b) Security guidelines and measures to protect data and systems.

c)  Information Security Risk, Threat, Vulnerability Assessment, Review, Management, Monitoring and Reporting – on a continuous basis

d) Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Planning – Security Inputs and Enhancements

e)  Security Operations Centre and Incident Management.

f)   Business Case for Information Security Investments and Expenditure.

 

             iv)   He will be responsible for the under-mentioned tasks:

a)  Setting Key Goal Indicators and Key Performance Indicators of the Information Security Program.

b)  Laying down enforceable rules.

c)   Organising Information Security Awareness training.

d)  Maintaining Security Posture and Profile of the Bank at expected levels.

e)  Active collaboration and communication with business and operating units.

f)    Internal and external security intelligence.

g)  Drawing security organisation structure with well designed roles and responsibilities.

h)  Compliance with regulatory requirements on Information Security.

i)    Investigations in IT frauds and mitigation measures.

j)    Appointing & guiding of team of IT Security Experts for audit assignments.

 

3. The above scope is not exhaustive but illustrative. CISO shall be required to do any other work related to above and/or other work as entrusted to him from time to time by the Management.

 

4. Information Security Committee

i) CISO will be part of Information Security Committee; Members of security committee are same as given in our Cyber Security Policy (Task Force).

ii) IDRBT in its communication “IT Governance Series for the Indian Banking Sector” has recommended quarterly frequency for meetings of Information Security Committee to regularly monitor the information security and risk management processes and corrective actions to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements. Minutes of these meetings should be placed in the immediate Board Meeting.

III. Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
