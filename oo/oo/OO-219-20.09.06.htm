OFFICE ORDER NO. 219   DATED  20  SEPTEMBER, 2006

 

Sanctioning of Loans against Term Deposits

Delegation of Powers to the Asstt. Accountant/Accountant

In-charge of Bhandup Ext. Counter.

 

          A reference is invited to Office Order No. 215 in terms of which the In-charge of the Extension Counter (EC) is authorized to disburse loans upto Rs. 10.00 lacs to individual borrowers who have their deposit account/s at the EC and are sanctioned such loans by the appropriate higher sanctioning authority.  According to the permissible functions of ECs as per the relative RBI Circular  dated 28 April 2006, the officials at the EC can be authorized to sanction advances against fixed deposits  of the customers.  Accordingly, in order to provide such service at our EC the Board has decided to delegate  powers to Accountant/Asstt. Accountant In-charge of Bhandup EC to sanction loans upto Rs. 3.00 lacs to individual depositors of the EC  against their own fixed deposits.  This delegation of power is made effective  immediately.

 

          Further, it has been  observed  that some of the vital issues pertaining to Bhandup Extension Counter are handled by or reported to H.O. Depts./Zonal Office directly,  without  routing such matters  through the In-charge of Bhandup  branch, which is base branch for Extension Counter.  This practice is not consistent with the devolution of authority and functional role of the base branch at Bhandup. In this context it has to be noted that the In-charge (AGM/Sr. Manager/Manager) of Bhandup branch has the overall charge of control of the EC and therefore supervision and control of the EC is his responsibility.  All the matters pertaining to the Bhandup EC should, therefore, be routed through the base branch in order to enable the branch In-charge to discharge his responsibility effectively.  All concerned at the Extension Counter /Bhandup branch /Central Zonal Office/ H.O. Departments are advised  to take a note  of the protocol and observe it hereafter, meticulously. 

 

 

                                                                                 M. C. Shetty

                                                                                                 Managing Director
