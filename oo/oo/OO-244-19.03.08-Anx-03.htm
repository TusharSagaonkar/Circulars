Annexure to

 

Office Order No. 244 dated 19 March 2008

 


Annexure III

 

 

Service Charges for Sundry Remittances

 

1.      Inward Remittances:

a.       Encashment of foreign currency notes/foreign currency traveller cheques:

i.        Issue of encashment certificates to the encasher on banks Letter Head (upto Rs.15000/-)                

Flat Charge of Rs.25/- per certificate.

ii.       Issue of encashment certificates to the encasher on Security Paper (beyond Rs.15000/-)                

Flat Charge of Rs.50/- per certificate.

 

b.      Purchase of foreign currency instruments such as DDs/cashier cheque/bankers' cheques/personal cheques/International Money Order/postal orders etc.

i.        Commission @ 0.25%

ii.       Interest charges to be separately collected for the transit period of 15 days @ 3.50% over our BPLR.

iii.      All out of pocket expenses such as postages, telegramme charges etc are to be separately recovered.

 

c.       Purchase of instruments drawn in Indian rupees and payable in India such as DDs/cashier cheque/bankers’ cheques/personal cheques/ International Money Order/postal orders etc.

i.        Commission @ 0.50% (in case instrument is drawn locally no commission is to be charged.)

ii.       Interest charges to be separately collected for the transit period of 15 days @ 3.50% over our BPLR.

iii.      All out of pocket expenses such as postages, telegramme charges etc. are to be separately recovered.

 

d.      Receipt of foreign inward remittances through SWIFT and crediting the proceed to the beneficiaries account: Flat charge of Rs.200/- per remittance to be recovered.

 

2.      Outward Remittances:

a.       Issue of foreign currency notes/foreign currency traveller cheques:

A flat charge of Rs.200/- is to be collected along with all out of pocket expenses such as postages, telegramme charges etc. are to be separately recovered.

 

b.      Issue of foreign currency DDs payable abroad:

A flat charge of Rs.200/- is to be collected. (in case of students applying for DDs for examination/enrolment/fee payments/membership etc. - 25% of the above charge may be collected).

 

c.       Issue of foreign currency money transfer by SWIFT

i.        A flat charge of Rs.200/- is to be collected along with out of pocket for SWIFT @ Rs.600/- per message or those levied by our correspondent bank whichever is higher.

ii.       Issue of money transfer in Indian rupees by SWIFT

Commission @ 0.30% minimum Rs.250/- is to be collected along with out of pocket for SWIFT @ Rs.600/- per message or those levied by our correspondent bank whichever is higher.
