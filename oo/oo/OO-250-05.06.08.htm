Office Order No. 250 dated 5 June, 2008

 

Staff Matter  

 

As per the terms of MOU dated 01.12.2007 entered by the Bank with the Co-op. Banks Employees' Union - Unit – Abhyudaya Co-op. Bank Ltd., it has been decided as under:

 

1.    Third Housing Loan:

The rate of interest for third staff housing loan shall be @ 6.5% p.a. till closure of the loan account.  All other terms and conditions as mentioned in Annexure "E" of the MOA dated 26.05.2006 in case of housing loans shall be applicable to third housing loans.

 

2.    Special Loan B:

The members of the staff who have availed both housing loans and have repaid the same and have not availed 3rd Housing Loan shall be eligible for Additional Loan upto Rs.1.00 lac (for House Repair/Furniture & Fixtures, etc.) over and above the Special loan-A and Cash Credit Facility.

The members of the staff who have not availed 2nd and/or 3rd Housing Loan shall be eligible for Additional Loan up to Rs.1.00 lac (for House Repair/Furniture & Fixtures etc.) over and above the Special loan-A and limit of Cash Credit Facility.

Maximum limit of Total Loans and Advances inclusive of Cash Credit limit shall not be more than Rs.3.25 lacs and Rs.3.75 lacs as applicable vide MOA dated 26.05.2006.  However, in such a case the concerned staff member shall permanently forfeit his/her entitlement to avail second and/or third housing loan during the span of his/her remaining service.  All other terms and conditions mentioned in MOA dated 26.05.2006 applicable to Special Loan B shall remain applicable for loan facility mentioned above without any change.

 

3.    Education Loan:   

The Bank shall sanction Education Loan to all staff members upto Rs.5,00,000/- (Rupees Five lacs only) for undergoing higher studies to their son(s) and/or daughter(s).  The loan will be considered for sanction subject to repaying capacity of the staff as mentioned in MOA date 26.05.2006 in respect of various concessional loans.  The rate of interest charged for Education Loan to staff will be @ 1.5% less than that applicable to general public for Education Loan from time to time.  Eligible courses will be higher education

 

such as Engineering, Medical, MBA, etc.  Moratorium period will be provided to this staff Education Loan as it is provided to Education Loan to general public in terms of Bank's Loan Policy, which is subject to change from time to time.  The said loan shall be repayable in maximum 60 EMIs.  

 

The overall sanction limit under any circumstances (Housing Loan + Educational Loan) shall not exceed Rs.15,00,000/- (Rupees Fifteen lacs only).

Concerned staff member shall be required to offer One Surety of permanent Officer/Employee acceptable to the Bank and creation/extension of Bank’s charge on residential flat or any other adequate security acceptable to the Bank.

 

4.    DBF Qualification:

All the benefits/weightage given to DBF course (from IIBF) such as increment, marks or eligibility for promotional exam as mentioned in the MOA dated 26.05.2006 stands cancelled retrospectively w.e.f. 01.12.2007.

 

5.    Night Shift Allowance:

The Bank shall pay total fixed allowance @ Rs.165/- per night to the concerned staff members (Clerk/Asstt. Accountant/Accountant) who are posted for night shift.  The staff posted for night duty will not be required to work for more than 14 days at a stretch in a calendar month in the night shift and not for a period exceeding 90 nights in one financial year.  No lady staff will be posted for the said night shift.

 

6.    Posting to Mumbai from Pune area Branches:

The employee(s) of the erstwhile Citizens Co-operative Bank Ltd., if posted at the branch(s) situated in Mumbai area will be paid Mumbai Posting Allowance as under in addition to entitlement of 2 days Special Leave for joining their place of duty situated in Mumbai Area:

a) Rs.3,500/- p.m. to Assistant Accountant

b) Rs.3,000/- p.m. Clerical cadre

c) Rs.2,500/- p.m. to Sub Staff

 

7.      Please bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff at your branch.

 

 

 

 

M. C. Shetty

Managing Director
