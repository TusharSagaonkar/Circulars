Office Order No. 249 dated 15 May, 2008

 

Housing Loan to staff of erstwhile Citizens Co–operative Bank Ltd.

 

It has been decided to revise/enhance the rules governing sanction of ‘Housing Loans’ to the staff of erstwhile Citizens Co–operative Bank Ltd. Extract of the aforesaid revised rules are as under:

The Board of Directors may, subject to the availability of funds, sanction individual housing loans to its permanent employees, or to the Co-op. Housing Society if formed exclusively by these employees.  Loan may also be sanctioned to a permanent employee who has been allotted a flat/house on outright purchase basis by a Government agency such as MHADA, CIDCO etc. for purchasing the Flat/House or for repayment of loan already taken therefor.  

·         Purpose:

The loan will be available to the eligible individual staff members of erstwhile Citizens Co–operative Bank Ltd. for acquiring residential accommodation on ownership basis either a Bungalow or new flat or flat on resale basis within the area or jurisdiction of the Bank provided the housing society/proposed society or the builder of the building in which the flat is being acquired by the individual employee, agrees and has no objection to execute the documents in favour of the Bank and also register the charge of the Bank by executing a mortgage deed. Individual employees shall ordinarily take a flat in a Registered Co–operative Housing Society/proposed Society, and will also be sanctioned loan for purchasing flat/house in M.H.B. colony having registered Co–operative Housing Society.

·         Area:

The flat proposed to be purchased should be under command area of branch Katraj towards Satara, Shirur towards Nagar Road, Hadapsar towards Sholapur, Khandala towards Mumbai.  In respect of flats to be purchased in Mumbai area, the proposal will be considered on merit basis by Bank’s Management 

·         Maximum Loan Limits:

Housing Loan shall be granted upto 50 times of Gross Monthly Salary of the employee or maximum limit, whichever is lower. Maximum 90% advance may be granted against purchase price keeping 10% margin to be contributed by the employee.

·         Number of times Housing Loan can be availed:

The loan will be available to the eligible individual staff member of erstwhile Citizens Co–operative Bank twice during entire service tenure.

·         Repayment:

The Repayment period will be maximum 240 months; subject to the residual length of service. The recovery of housing loan installments will be made as per equal monthly installments formula the Bank is applying for the customers of general public who avail Housing Loan from the Bank.

·         Security:

In respect of accommodation acquired on “ownership basis”, the title deed of the flat or the building should be deposited with the Bank for creating equitable mortgage as a security till final payment of loan alongwith interest. Rules framed by Bank in respect of creation of Bank’s charge on the security and completing of mortgage formalities in respect of housing loans as applicable to the general public will be applicable for Staff housing loan.

·         Surety:

2 permanent staff members for loan amount acceptable to the Bank.

·         Rate of Interest:

The rate of interest shall be 8% p.a.

·         Terms and Conditions:

1.   While granting housing loan, staff seniority, his length of service, his other liabilities, repayment capacity etc. will be taken into account.

2.   Second housing loan will be considered after the gap of 3 years from the date of sanction of 1st housing loan and on liquidation of entire outstanding of first loan.

3.   All other terms and conditions regarding Staff Housing Loan for these employees will be as applicable to existing staff of the Bank.

4.   The Management keeps the rights reserved to make any changes in the rules as and when situation warrants.
