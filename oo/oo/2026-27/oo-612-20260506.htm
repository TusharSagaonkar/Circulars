Office Order No. 612 dated 6th May 2026

Incidence of Attempted Fraud Cases for availing Loan against Fake/Fabricated LIC Policy at two of our branches Mulund (West) & Vashi have been reported.

 

 

During the last three weeks of March 2026, two incidences of attempt to defraud our Bank by availing Loan against Surrender of Fake LIC Policy have been reported.
The attempt was thwarted due to alertness of staff at the branch.

At Mulund Branch:

§  The Mulund Branch Staff accompanied with the applicant to LIC Branch at Ambernath. However, the staff was asked to wait and was only provided with an acknowledgement copy.

§  Pending receipt of assigned Policy and Surrender Value, the Branch carried out KYC verification based on ADHAR & Pan which was found to be in order.

§  Upon receipt of surrender value details via speed post, the branch developed suspicion and conducted inquiry through its LIC Agent. It was then revealed that the policy was fabricated and the original policy had already been closed long back.

§  Subsequently the Policy documents with assignment were received by branch through Speed Post.

§  Further verification with LIC Agent of the Bank confirmed that the policy was expired and had been fabricated with the intention to defraud the Bank.

Due to timely verification and due diligence by the branch, the loan was not sanctioned, thereby preventing a potential financial loss to the bank.

At Vashi Branch:

§  The applicant along with branch staff and one person known to be agent had personally been to LIC Branch 91G at Dombivli.

§  Our staff was asked to seat outside and Applicant Namole went inside the office counter and thereafter provided the acknowledgement on Bank’s letter for registering assignment of the Policy.

§  The Applicant submitted a Surrender Value document issued by 91G Branch of LIC indicating surrender value of approx. Rs.63.00 lakh. However the applicant did not provide the original document or a copy to the branch, which raised initial suspicion.

§  During discussions, the branch staff noted inconsistencies, particularly as the applicant being resident of Andheri Mumbai had approached the Vashi branch. Further clarification regarding premium installments and future installments was also not satisfactorily provided.

§  Upon verification it was observed that the sum assured under the policy was only Rs. 5.00 lakh instead of Rs.90 lakh reflected in the submitted documents. Additionally the policy was also in the name of Mrs. Radhika Vijay Bahulekar and was found to be in lapsed condition.

§  Noticing that branch was conducting discreet inquiries, the applicant withdrew the originals along with surrender value details.

§  This clearly indicated an attempt to misrepresent facts and fraudulently obtain a higher loan amount.

Due to timely verification and due diligence by the branch, the loan was not sanctioned, thereby preventing a potential financial loss to the bank.

 

Instructions to all Branches:

v Our branches are advised to take careful note of the above two attempted frauds occurred at above two branches.

v Staff should become more vigilant when an unknown person approaches the Branch for loan against LIC Policy for such huge surrender value amount.

v Our Branch staff should not handover our request letter, LIC policy to applicant for assignment of LIC policy at LIC Office. Our staff should personally visit LIC branch to complete the process.

v Any surrender value letter, assigned policy should not be accepted brought by hand by the applicant (the proposed borrower).

v Each policy’s surrender value should be reconfirmed / verified by referring it to LIC Agent of the Branch.
