ANNEXURE – I

 

GUIDELINES FOR THE OPENING AND MAINTENANCE OF THE NRE & NRO ACCOUNTS BY THE BRANCHES:

 

1)      Account Opening Form for NRE/NRO/FCNR/RFC Deposits Accounts in FX (NRE) 01 is to be obtained.

 

2)      KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC) Guidelines need to be scrupulously followed while opening the accounts. Accounts may be introduced by the Indian Embassy in the country in which the NRI is a resident. It can be introduced by any of our existing customers as per the guidelines in this regard for opening domestic SB accounts.  If the NRI is already having an SB account with our bank, no separate introduction needs to be obtained. (In such cases, the existing SB account is to be designated into an NRO account)

 

3)      Alongwith the application and photograph, photocopy of the Passport with valid visa or work permit is to be obtained

 

4)      As regards the initial deposit for opening the NRE accounts, it can be in any of the following:

i)   Foreign Inward Remittances either in Foreign Currency or in Indian Rupees received through normal banking channels.

 

ii)  Transfer of funds from other NRE accounts maintained by the same person or other NRIs with any of our other branches or with other banks. In such cases, it shall be ensured that funds are transferred from NRE accounts only by obtaining a suitable letter from the branch or bank concerned.

 

iii) From the maturity proceeds or interest payments on FCNR deposits. 

 

iv) Proceeds of encashment of Foreign Currency Travellers Cheques or Currency Notes tendered by the NRI in person when he/she is on a visit to India.

 

Under No Circumstances, Local Currency (Indian Rupees) Is To Be Accepted For Opening NRE Accounts.

 

5)      For opening NRO accounts, in addition to what is stated in item (4) above, Indian Rupees representing legitimate dues in India of the account holder can be accepted. This includes current income like rent, dividend, pension, interest etc .as also sale proceeds of assets including immovable property acquired out of rupee/foreign currency funds or by way of legacy/inheritance.

 

6)      NRO account (current/savings) can be opened by any person resident outside India, including foreign tourists on short visit to India on a tourist visa, with funds remitted from outside India through banking channel or by sale of foreign exchange brought by him to India.

 

7)      Opening of NRE Accounts in the names of individuals of Bangladesh/ Pakistan nationality requires prior approval of Reserve Bank of India

 

8)      Account should be opened by the account holder himself and not by the holder of power of attorney in India.

 

PERMITTED CREDITS FOR NRE ACCOUNTS

 

a.  Proceeds of remittances to India in any permitted currency.

b.  Proceeds of

 

i)       Personal cheques drawn by the account holder on his foreign currency account.

ii)      Traveller cheques, bank drafts payable in any permitted currency including instruments expressed in Indian rupees for which reimbursement will be received in foreign currency, deposited by the account holder in person during his temporary visit to India, provided the AD bank/bank is satisfied that the account holder is still resident outside India.

iii)     The travellers’ cheques/drafts are standing/endorsed in the name of the account holder and in the case of travellers’ cheques, they were issued outside India.

 

c.  Proceeds of foreign currency/bank notes tendered by account holder during his temporary visit to India, provided

i)       The amount was declared on a Currency Declaration Form (CDF), where applicable.  Presently where the aggregate value of the foreign exchange in the form of currency notes,  bank notes or travellers cheques brought in by such person at one time exceeds USD 10,000 – or its equivalent and / or the aggregate value of foreign currency notes brought in by such persons at any one time exceeds USD 5,000 – or its equivalent, and

ii)       the notes are tendered to the AD bank in person by the account holder himself and the AD bank is satisfied that account holder is a person resident outside India.

 

d.  Transfers from other NRE/FCNR accounts.

e.  Interest accruing on the funds held in the account.

f.   Interest on Government securities and dividend on units of mutual funds, provided the securities/units were purchased by debit to the account holder’s NRE/FCNR account or out of inward remittance through normal banking channels.

g.  Maturity proceeds of Government securities including National Plan/Savings Certificates as well as proceeds of Government securities and units of mutual funds sold on a recognised stock exchange in India and sale proceeds of units received from mutual funds, provided the securities/units were originally purchased by debit to the account holder’s NRE/FCNR account or out of remittances received from outside India in free foreign exchange.

h.  Refund of share/debenture subscription to new issue of Indian companies or portion thereof, if the amount of subscription was paid from the same account or from other NRE/FCNR account of the account holder or by remittance from outside India through normal banking channels.

i.   Refund of application/earnest money/purchase consideration made by the house building agencies/seller on account of non-allotment of flat/plot cancellation of booking/dealers for purchase of resident/commercial property, together with interest, if any (net of income tax payable thereon), provided the original payment was made out of NRE/FCNR account of the account holder remittance from outside India through normal banking channels and the AD bank is satisfied about the genuineness of the transaction.

j.   Transfer of balance from EEFC/RFC/RFC (D) Accounts consequent to change in status from resident to non-resident.

k.  Current income like rent, dividend, pension, interest etc. of NRIs, provided ADs are satisfied that the credit represents current income of non-resident account holders and income tax thereon has been deducted/paid/provided for as the case may be.

l.   Any other credit if recovered under general or special permission granted by Reserve Bank.

 

PERMITTED DEBITS FOR NRE ACCOUNTS

 

a.  Local Disbursements

b.  Remittances outside India

c.  Transfer to NRE/FCNR account of the account holder or any other person eligible to maintain such account.

d.  Investment in share/securities/commercial paper of an Indian company or for purchase of immovable property in India provided such investment/purchase is covered by the regulations made, or the general/special permission granted, by the Reserve Bank.

e.  Any other transaction if covered under general or special permission granted by the Reserve Bank.

 

PERMISSIBLE CREDITS FOR NRO ACCOUNTS

 

i)       Proceeds of remittances received in any permitted currency from outside India through normal banking channels or any permitted currency tendered by the account-holder during his temporary visit to India.  Foreign currency exceeding USD 5000/- or its equivalent in form of cash should be supported by Currency Declaration Form.  Rupee funds should be supported by Encashment Certificate, if they represent funds brought outside India.

ii)       Transfers from rupee accounts of nonresident banks.

iii)      Legitimate dues in India of the account holder.  This includes current income like rent, dividend, pension, interest, etc. as also sale proceeds of assets including immovable property acquired out of rupee/foreign currency funds or by way of legacy/inheritance.

 

PERMISSIBLE DEBITS FOR NRO ACCOUNTS

 

i)       All local payments in rupees including payments for investments subject to compliance with the relevant regulations made by the Reserve Bank.

ii)       Remittance outside India of current income in India (like rent, dividend, pension, interest of NRI/PIO) of the account holder net of applicable taxes.

iii)      Remittance up to USD One million, per financial year (April, March), for all bonafide purposes, to the satisfaction of the AD bank.

iv)      Settlement of credit card holders to the extent of the card limit for use of credit cards issued by banks in India, subject to the conditions for use of the international credit cards by residents.

Any other transaction if covered under general or special permission granted by RBI.

Note I

 

a)  When a person resident in India leaves India to Nepal and Bhutan for taking up employment or for carrying on business or vocation or for any other purpose indicating his intention to stay in Nepal and Bhutan for an uncertain period, his existing account will continue as an resident account.  Such account should not be designated as Non-resident (Ordinary) Rupee Account (NRO).

b)  ADs may open and maintain NRE/FCNR (B) Accounts of persons resident in Nepal and Bhutan who are citizens of India or of Indian origin, provided            these accounts are remitted in free foreign exchange. Interest earned on NRE/FCNR (B) accounts can be remitted only in Indian rupees.

c)  In terms of Regulations 4 (4) of the Notification No. FEMA 3/2000 RB dated May 3, 2000 ADs may open and maintain Rupees accounts for a person resident in Nepal/Bhutan.

d)  The regulations relating to the various deposit schemes available to Non-Resident Indians have been notified vide Notification No. FEMA.5 dated 3rd May 2000, as amended from time to time.  The relevant Notifications an A. P. (DIR Series) Circulars have been placed on our website forex@abhyudayabank.net and may please be referred to for full details.
