Office Order No. 499 dated 1st January, 2018

Duties & Responsibilities of Assistant Manager & Officer

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Consequent upon Memorandum of Settlement entered into by Bank with the Co-op. Bank Employees Union, erstwhile positions of Accountant & Assistant Accountant have been redesignated as Assistant Manager & Officer respectively. The redesignation has also necessitated reiteration of duties & responsibilities which were allotted in the names of corresponding previous positions. There was also a need to realign some duties to eliminate duplication. The revised list of duties & responsibilities mapped with the redesignated positions is given below.

 

I.  Duties & Responsibilities of Assistant Manager :

 

1)    Passing of CRC Cheques against clearing.

2)    Approval for big payment

a.For Savings Bank and Current account operations : Cash withdrawal upto Rs.50.000/-

b. For CC / OD account operations : Cash withdrawal upto Rs.1,00,000/-

3)    Payment to third party and payment without pass book by using withdrawal slip will be authorized without any limit.

4)    Approval for ABB transactions for cash withdrawals by third party:

a.     For SB Accounts             :        Rs. 25,000/-

b.    For Current Accounts     :        Rs. 50,000/-

5)       Allowing withdrawal in advance against clearing facility Rs.50,000/- per party per day.

 

Assistant Manager will exercise powers as delegated to erstwhile Accountants vide our Office Order No. 306 dated 30th September, 2009 and they will get the actions ratified by the Branch-in-Charge at the end of the day or on the next working day.

 

6)       Handling day-to-day housekeeping functions of the Branch.

7)       Opening of new deposit accounts with observance of KYC norms :

a.     Verification of Proof of Residential Address,.

b.    Verification of photographs.

c.     Other usual information to be sought from the customers etc.

 

8)       Ensuring, following & bringing about compliance with KYC norms for old/earlier accounts.

9)       Acceptance of nomination form for appointment of nominee as well as forms for cancellation/change of nomination and issuing acknowledgement thereagainst.

10)   Signing of Pass book.

11)   Issuing and signing POs as the joint signatory.

12)   Authorizing issue of PO to Non Account-holders.

13)   Issuance of FDR renewal notices.

14)   Approval for accepting cheque in maiden name of woman depositor for credit to the account in post-marital name, after verifying the change in marital status from available documentary proof.

15)   Allotting field work to clerical staff that are posted in the morning / evening shift.

16)   Allocation of duties to staff members on daily basis.

16A) Collecting personal cell phones from all the staff members in switch-off mode upon their reporting, holding custody of such cell phones during the day, & returning each cell phone to the respective staff member at the time when he/she leaves office.

17)   Upkeep of Branch premises

18)   Receiving/checking/recommending claims pertaining to deposits/articles of deceased constituents.

19)   Handling entire work related with Safe Deposit Lockers including, but not restricted to:

a.  Opening of New Locker accounts

b.    Daily Locker operations with signature & photograph verification.

c.  Breaking-open a locker

d. Custody of locker keys etc.

20)   Authorization in respect of Stop-Payment of cheque(s) and attending to related enquiries.

21)   Work related to clearing.

22)   Term Deposit

a.     Opening of New Term Deposit Account

b.    Renewal of Term Deposit

c.     Payment of Term Deposit on due date

d.    Approval for payment of Term Deposit before maturity

e.     Signatory for issue of TD Loans

23) a. Issue of Cheque Books to new account-holders.

 b. Issue to other regular account holders.

 c. Joint custody of Cheque Books, Pay Orders, FD Receipts and other security items.

24)   Follow-up for recovery.

25)   Attending to public enquiry in person / on telephone.

26)   Assuming charge as joint custodian of Cash at the branch including ATM Cash.

27)   Checking of cash.

28)   Checking of IBC/OBC/BD/CRC.

29)   Checking and arranging to forward MIS/DGM reports and other periodical returns.

30)   Taking every step for offering excellent customer service.

31)   Attending to customers' complaints and ensuring that best customer service is extended to all customers/shareholders.

32)   Checking and submission of all periodical returns on or before respective due dates.

33)   Sanctioning and disbursing of NSC Loans, Gold Loans and Loans against Term Deposit Receipts as per laid down procedure.

Action in respect of sanctioning and disbursing of these loans will be ratified by the Branch-in-charge at the end of the day or on the next working day.

34)   Compliance of all Inspection & Audit Reports.

35)   For payment against Salary Cheques and other un-cleared effects up to a limit of Rs.25,000/-.

36)   Returning of CRC cheques (other than Cash Credit).

37)   Supervision of ATM related work and attending to ATM related complaints during working hours.

38)   Inviting 15G/15H forms from customers in respect of exemption from TDS. Follow up with customers in respect thereof. Other work relating to 15G & 15H forms.

39)   Verification of Pan Card application and submission thereof to the Demat & Pan Card Services Cell as per laid down procedure.

40)   Verification of Demat Account Opening Application along with KYC documents and submission thereof to the Demat & Pan Card Services Cell as per the laid down procedure and performance of duties allotted as checker and other duties in this regard.

41)   Checking and follow-up action for collection of Foreign Currency Instruments.

42)   Checking and follow-up action in respect of Inward / Outward remittances of Foreign Currency and other Foreign Currency Transactions.

43)   Providing proper guidance to customers and opening of NRO / NRE Accounts and also FCNR, EEFC and RFC accounts if the Branch is designated for opening of these accounts.

44)   Completion of initial formalities for purchase and sale of Foreign Currency.

45)   Work relating to Loan Dept.

a.     Attending to loan enquiries from customers.

b.    Recovery, follow-up and visits to defaulters' office and residence.

c.     Action to be initiated for recovery of overdues.

d.    Checking and recommending proposals involving Fund-Based/Non Fund-Based Credit.

e.     Visits to units enjoying CC facility for verification of stock/ book debts as per stock statement submitted by Borrowers. Also visits for review and renewal purpose.

f.      Checking of Bank Guarantee Documents/Agreements.

g.    Checking of solvency proposals.

h.    Checking of OBC / IBC.                    

i.      Checking of LBD / DBD.

j.      Signature on Bank Pay Order/ Pay Order towards Loan Disbursement and release of sanctioned loans and advances after compliance of terms & conditions.

k.     Custodian of gold packets/other valuable articles jointly with Officer.

l.      Joint custody of Loan A/c. security files with Daftary / Record Keeper.

m.  Arranging to visit places of applicants submitting new loan proposals / small amount CC proposals/Housing Loan proposals, and preparation, appraisal & submission thereof with Visit Reports.

n.    Ensuring renewal of General Insurance Policies.

o.     Post-Disbursement follow up.

p.    Drawing Power Maintenance and follow up.

q.    Liaison with various departments such as Income Tax, Commissioner (GST), etc.

r.      Issuing instructions to Officer(s)/ Clerk(s) to prepare the documents and get them executed in respect of sanctioned Loans and Advances.

s.     Arranging delivery of original documents on completion of all the formalities as per Loan Policy Manual.

t.      Any other duties in absence of the Manager / Incharge etc.

46)  Granting single & final approval by signing the forms as listed in the Annexure hereto so that they are no more required to be referred to Manager and above.

47)   Duties which may be allotted in regard to any other new products.

48)   Any other work allotted by Manager/Sr. Manager/Asst. General Manager.

 

          The above mentioned are the duties and responsibilities of the Assistant Manager and he/she is bound to perform these duties. At branches where Loan Dept. is not separately established, the Assistant Manager posted at such branches will have to perform all the duties mentioned above. However, an Assistant Manager posted in Loan Dept. will work as In-charge of the Loan Dept., and more thrust will be given by him for work related to Loan Dept. in addition to such of the above duties as may be assigned out of those mentioned above.

         

II.   Duties & Responsibilities of Officer :-

 

1)       Authorizing following operations :

a.     Cash withdrawal without passbook.

b.    Conversion of single account into joint account.

c.     Admitting change of signature, change of address, etc.

2)       Custody and Management of Lockers.

3)       Cash management – indenting cash from Head Office and / or remitting cash to Head Office.

4)       Joint custody of cash, gold packets and other valuables.

5)       Arranging delivery of gold ornaments on closure of Gold Loan Accounts.

6)       Issue and signing Payment Orders as the joint signatory.

7)       Custody, supply and maintenance of record of stocks of security items like FD Receipt Forms, PO Forms, Cheque-Books of Correspondent Banks and all other security forms etc.

8)       Voucher authorisation.

9)       Signature verification.

10)   Reporting irregularities, if any, to the higher authorities e.g. return of cheques for want of funds.

11)   Attending to customer query, balance enquiry as well as inquiry from public in person as well as on telephone.

12)   Cheque Book authorization.

13)   Releasing clearing effects.

14)   Follow up for recovery in defaulting accounts, initiation of action for recovery in overdue accounts.

15)   Acceptance and authorization of Stop Payment request.

16)   To ensure up-to-date House Keeping.

17)   Post-disbursement follow up.

18)   Verification and authorization of scanned signatures for all accounts.

19)   To assist the Manager for housekeeping functions of the Branch.

20)   Opening of new deposit accounts with compliance of KYC norms :

a.   Verification of Residential Address Proof.

b.   Verification of photographs.

c.    Other usual information to be sought from the customers, etc.

         

21)   Acceptance of nomination form, its processing, cancellation of/variation in nomination and issuing acknowledgement thereagainst.

22)   Authorization for conversion of single account into joint account.

23)   Admitting and authorizing change of signature, change of address, etc.

24)   Closing of deposit accounts as per laid down procedure.

25)   Issuance of FDR renewal notices.

26)   Accepting cheques in maiden name of woman depositor for credit to the account in post-marital name, after verifying the fact with the available documentary evidence.

27)   Entire work relating to Safe Deposit Lockers :

a.   Opening of New Locker accounts.

b.   Daily Locker Operations with signature & photograph verification.

c.    Breaking-open of Locker.

(To be checked by Assistant Manager on day-to-day basis.

28)   Computer related modification work.

29)   Term Deposit :

a.   Opening of New Term Deposit Account.

b.   Renewal of Term Deposit.

c.    Payment of Term Deposit on due date.

30)   Verification of cheque book requisition slips.

31)   Follow-up for recovery.

32)   Checking of IBC/OBC/BD/CRC.

33)   Obtaining of Thumb Impression of Illiterate A/c. Holder and its attestation.

34)   Preparation and Checking of MIS/DGM reports and other periodical returns.

35)   Attending to customers' complaints and ensuring that excellent customer service is extended to all customers/shareholders in every possible way.

36)   Compliance of all Inspection & Audit Reports with the help of departmental head concerned.

37)   Upkeep of Branch premises.

38)   Checking/recommending of Deceased Deposit Claim(s).

39)   Checking and follow-up for collection of Foreign Currency Instruments.

40)   Checking and follow-up of Inward / Outward remittances of Foreign Currency and other Foreign Currency Transactions.

41)   Giving proper guidance to customers and opening of NRO / NRE Accounts and also FCNR, EEFC and RFC accounts if the Branch is designated Branch for opening of these accounts.

42)   Initial formalities to be completed for purchase and sale of Foreign Currency.

43)   Duties to be allotted in regard to any other new products.

44)   Duties of Accountant in his/her absence.

45)   Any other duty assigned by superior authority.

46)   Any other work allotted by Manager/Sr. Manager/Asst. General Manager/superior authority.

 

Many of the duties allotted to Assistant Manager and Officer are overlapping. The In-charge of the Branch will allocate duties to Assistant Manager and Officer depending upon the work pressure in various segments.

 

III.    Duties & Responsibilities of Officer entrusted with duties pertaining to Cash Dept. :-

 

1)       Cash management – indenting cash from Head Office and / or remitting cash to Head Office. The position should be communicated one day advance after closure of day's transactions through Exchange Server / Telephone.

2)       Authorizing issue of cheque-books to customers and custody of security forms like cheque books/pay orders/FD Receipts, etc.

3)       Attending ATM related work.

4)       CSC / CRC checking

5)       Maintenance of Petty Cash.

6)       Authorisation of Cash Receipts and Payments.

7)       IBC / OBC checking.

8)       Distribution of coins to customers/ general public and maintenance of coin distribution Register. To get it audited from the Internal Auditor.

9)       To ensure that no soiled note is mixed with issuable notes, to arrange to send such notes to Central Cash Dept., Head Office, after segregation and pasting with brown tape.

10)   To ensure that clean note policy is adopted at the branch.

11)   As and when counterfeit notes are found, he/she will intimate the same to the branch In-charge to lodge FIR with the nearest Police Station and will then prepare CCR report to be submitted to the Principal Officer through Zonal DGMs with a copy to Secretarial Dept. for submitting the relative returns to RBI.

12)   Reconciliation of ATM difference on day-to-day basis.

 

IV. Duties & Responsibilities of Asst. Manager / Officers posted at Administrative Offices in various departments at Head office/Zonal office/ Regional office :-

 

The Head of the Department / Deputy General Manager / Assistant General Manager (Region) will allot the duties to Asst. Manager & Officer as per requirement of the Departments/office, and Asst. Managers & Officers will perform the allotted duties in accordance with the internal office order to be issued by Head of the Department / Deputy General Manager / Assistant General Manger (Region).

 

V. Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at the Branch/ Department and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

 

P.R. Shetty

Managing Director
