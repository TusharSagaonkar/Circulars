Office Order No. 498 dated 18th December, 2017

1.   Restriction on Use of Mobile Phones by Staff for Personal Work

2.   Deposit of Handsets with Assistant Manager

3.   Suspension & Disciplinary Action for Breach of Instructions

 

 

I.                 Attention of all members of the staff is invited to previous instructions on the captioned subject (and on misuse of computers) i. e. Office Order No. 450 dated 28th May, 2015 which in turn refers to Circular No. HRD/2134/2014 dated 23rd October, 2013 and IDC No. HO/MD-920/409/2014-15 dated 27th June, 2014.

 

II.  We observe with acute displeasure that members of the staff are wantonly disobeying the instructions issued in terms of communications mentioned above causing serious impediment in Bank’s work, damaging standards of customer service and providing reason to our customers to raise frequent complaints. Members of the staff need to be alive to the fact that the spectacle of our staff engaging themselves in private conversation on mobile phones in the presence of customers by itself shows our organisation as unprofessional and lacking in official decorum, although it may not sometimes be directly affecting customer service.

 

III.   In order to arrest the trend as above, we are issuing instructions as under for strict compliance.

1.     i. All the members of the staff will deposit, at the time of reporting on duty,

 their personal handsets with the Assistant Manager to whom they report

 while signing the muster.

 

ii.     The handset must be in switched-off mode at the time of deposit and should be labeled or tagged with the Staff-Name & Number for identification.

 

iii.   The Assistant Manager to whom the handsets are delivered shall make arrangement to store them during the day and return to the respective member of the staff at the end of his work in branch/office.

 

2.     Members of the staff shall not carry any handset on their person during working hours except upon specific exemption as provided for hereunder.

 

3.     Members of the staff whose work involves touring, & visiting other offices and residence or business-place of customers, or running errands, will approach the Assistant Manager, seeking special exemption from the rule no. 1.i above.

 

4.     In charge of branches and officials/executives heading offices shall be allowed to carry a handset on their person for official use. No other member of the staff will normally be allowed to carry a handset on their person.

5.     If it is required to make an exemption from the rule as per point no. 3 above, the In-Charge of the branch and official/executive heading the particular office will grant a specific permission in this behalf.

 

6.     A list of members of the staff who are specifically exempted from the requirement of lodging the handsets with the Assistant Manager will be maintained in HRM department. The same will be updated from time to time.

 

7.     If any member of the staff, not being one who is specifically exempted, is found to carry on their person a mobile handset, the handset will be impounded by In Charge of the branch/ Head of the Office. It will be immaterial whether the handset was used or not.

 

8.     The person found to carry, unauthorisedly, a mobile handset on his/her person will be suspended immediately & disciplinary action will be initiated against him/her forthwith.

 

9.     Zonal Office will conduct surprise visits by their officials/executives to branches under their control to check & ensure that the instructions as above are being followed. The visits should be organised to cover all the branches under the control of Zonal Office at least once in every quarter. A monthly compliance report as per Annexure hereto, will be submitted by Zonal Office/Regional Office to HRM department with details of deviations, if any, and action taken in respect of such deviations.

 

10. Henceforth, only Branch-In-Charge and Assistant Manager will have direct landline telephones available at their desks for official use. Direct landline telephones will be available at the desks of Assistant Managers and above in administrative & other offices depending upon need.

 

11. Development department is hereby being instructed to arrange to disable the direct dialing facility from landline telephones at desks other than those as per the point no. 10 above.

 

12. The instructions as above come into immediate effect.

 

IV.  Please note that specific instructions as above in respect of the mobile handsets do not mean that prohibition on misuse of computers in office is in any way diluted. Previous instructions on the subject should be followed & implemented with equal rigour.

 

V.    Please bring the instructions of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff at your branch/office, and implement them without any deviation whatsoever.

 

 

 

P.R. Shetty

Managing Director
