Office Order No. 495 dated 30th May, 2017

DELEGATION OF POWERS TO DGMs

FOR HOUSING LOAN UPTO Rs. 70 LAKH

FOR TOD FACILITY UPTO Rs. 10 LAKH

WAIVER OF CONDITIONS/DOCUMENTS IN MORTGAGE SCRUTINY

____________________________________________________________________

 

 

I.    Background:

 

1.   In terms of Circular CR No.133 dated 31st January, 2015, Deputy General Managers have been vested with power to sanction Individual Housing Loans up to Rs. 60 Lakh whereas the ceiling applicable for individual Housing Loan is Rs. 70 Lakh. It has therefore been considered reasonable to enhance the relative power delegated to Deputy General Managers to Rs. 70 Lakh to obviate reference to superior authorities for only a marginally higher amount.

 

2.   Further, in terms of Office Order No. 487 dated 4th October 2016, Deputy General Managers have been vested with powers to grant Temporary Overdraft Facility up to Rs. 5.00 Lakh or 5% of CC limit, whichever is less, for a maximum period of 7 days in CC accounts of regular clients. It has been proposed to enhance the same up to Rs. 10 Lakh subject to all other conditions continuing to be applicable.

 

 

3.   Furthermore, Managers in the Credit Department (Legal Cell) were recently redeployed to the three Zonal Offices namely Western, Central and Navi Mumbai Zone; primarily to decentralize the work relating to mortgages (but also to take care of other legal work) vide Office Order 492 dated 7th April 2017. A Manager was already deployed in Pune Zonal Office to look after such work and is currently performing the role so assigned since long. In view of the above, it has been proposed that the matters related to “request for waiver of condition to submit certain documents in connection with mortgageability report and/or title report” shall be placed before the Deputy General Manager of respective Zonal Offices for taking suitable decision.

 

4.   The proposals numbering from 1 to 3 above received approval in the Board Meeting dated 11th May 2017.

(b) It should be noted that all the due care and precautions as mentioned in the Circular CR No.133 dated 31st January, 2015 or Office Order No. 487 dated 4th October 2016, as the case may be, shall be taken and discretion shall be judiciously exercised by the Deputy General Managers while taking decisions in accordance with the enhanced powers.

(c) It should also be noted that no further request for TOD above Rs. 10 Lakh is to be considered and forwarded to HO. The Branch Incharge and DGM of respective Zone should inform the party accordingly

2. Similarly, matters related to “request for waiver of condition to submit certain documents in connection with mortgageability report and/or title report” should hereafter be placed before the Deputy General Manager of respective Zonal Office for taking suitable decision. While considering such a request, the DGM concerned shall ensure that Bank’s security interest is fully protected. In case the DGM is not in a position to take a decision in a specific matter such as above, the proposal should be forwarded to Legal Cell (Mortgage Department) at Head Office with a justification thereof so that it can be placed before the higher authorities.

III. Please take suitable note of these instructions at appropriate places in the book on “Credit Policy – Credit Rules & Procedures w. e. f. 24.08.2016” and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department and ensure compliance.
