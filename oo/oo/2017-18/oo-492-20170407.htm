Office Order No. 492 dated 7th April, 2017

Deployment Of Managers (Legal) to

Zonal Offices of Western, Central & Navi Mumbai Zones

 

 

I.In order to decentralize the work relating to mortgages etc., a note for redeploying three Managers (Legal) currently working in credit department (legal cell) of Head Office by placing one Manager (Legal) at each of the Zonal Offices of Western Zone, Central Zone and Navi Mumbai Zone was put up to the Board & the same was approved in the Board Meeting dated 24.03.2017. Accordingly, one Manager (Legal) is being placed at each of these Zonal Offices. These Managers (Legal) have been assigned duties as under:

 

1. To scrutinize title deeds and documents furnished by applicants in respect of immovable property being offered as security for loans/advances of all types and to issue mortgageability scrutiny note (legal opinion)in each case (in respect of members of the public as well as members of the staff applying for housing/repair/renovation loans/Education Loans *).

2. Generating CERSAI search report of properties.

3. Checking of, simple mortgage deeds received from branches in respect of new mortgages & extensions, incidental papers, affidavits, indemnities, affidavits, reconveyances, other drafts etc.

4. Preparation of documents/deeds, General Power of Attorney, Indemnity Bonds, etc.

5. Preparation & scrutiny of Mortgage Deeds executed by the borrowers & sureties & similar preparation & scrutiny of incidental papers etc. for mortgages, and certification of their correctness before disbursement of loans.

6. Scrutiny of documents relating to ‘Project Approval’ applications received from branches.

7. Verification of documents executed by the borrowers for credit facilities of value Rs. 50.00 Lakh & above.

8. Examination of requests received from borrowers for waiver in respect of certain documents required for mortgage as per MS note/title report & recommending them on merits to the sanctioning authorities concerned.

9. To give legal opinion on queries raised by branches in respect of mortgages.(#)

10. Any other work as may be assigned from time to time.

 

(* In respect of staff loans, relative papers will be referred to the Zonal Office within whose jurisdiction the originating branch of the proposal falls).

 

(# In this behalf, the observation along the lines mentioned in the Office Order No. 287 dated 26th June, 2009 requires reiteration: The queries made to Legal Functionaries generally pertain to operational difficulties or with respect to issues which are already covered in the Circulars issued by Head Office. Such reference to legal functionaries should strictly be avoided by the branches. As regards operational difficulties, it is expected that the In Charge of Branches are well acquainted to handle such issues. As regards routine matters, branches should keep themselves updated with the various Circulars issued by H.O. from time to time and the same must be discussed in the Branch Monthly Meetings. By doing so, unnecessary references to various departments can be avoided and better customer service can be provided to our valued customers).

 

II.     With the redeployment of Managers (Legal) at the three Zonal Offices as mentioned above, following work will continue to be handled by Credit Department (Legal Cell) at Head Office and as may be modified as per policy from time to time.

 

1. With respect to Loans/Advances files wherein disbursements is made by Credit department, HO.

a)   Drafting of Mortgage Deed, Deed of Further Charge (Extension), Deed of Reconveyances & other related documents.

b)  Drafting of Declaration, Affidavit, I-Bond, Undertaking, Power of Attorney or such other incidental documents etc.

c)   Documents verification.

d)  Documentation regarding Consortium Finance.

e)   Recording of Charge with CERSAI, Generating CERSAI Report of Properties.

f)    To put up Submissions on various subjects, legal opinions, etc. in mortgage related matters and other incidental matters.

2. Other matters as may be referred by Higher Authority.

 

III.   With the placement of Managers (Legal) at three Zonal Offices mentioned above, matters involving mortgages (either by way of primary or collateral security) or any other work mentioned in Part I originating from branches under their control shall be sent only to the respective Zonal Offices.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department and ensure compliance.

P.R. Shetty

Managing Director
