Office Order No. 206 dated 10.11.2005

 

Formation of a Specialized Credit Cell and Recovery Call Centre of

Abhyudaya  Nagar, Parel and Sewri branches.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

            In the meeting of the Performance Budget Review Committee held on 04.07.2005 the participants pointed out that proper expertise in handling the work of  credit  appraisal  was  not adequately developed at the branch level. Consequently, branches were not in a position to decide and dispose of loan proposals expeditiously.  It was, therefore, recommended that a specialized Cell for attending to the credit function for a cluster of branches may be created.  Such Cell should work under an Asstt. General Manager supported by a dedicated group of staff.  It was indicated that such a system has worked successfully in some other urban banks.  A proposal for formation of first such Cell for Sewri, Parel and Abhyudaya Nagar branches was accordingly prepared and placed before the Board.  The Board considered and approved the proposal for implementation, in their meeting held on 26.08.2005.

 

The organizational structure, functional role, operational modalities of the Specialized Credit Cell and Recovery Call Centre (the Cell) are explained in the following paragraphs.  The details of consequent changes in the staff strength and the functional responsibilities of  the three affiliated branches are also indicated therein

(b)       The present AGM in-charge of Abhyudaya Nagar branch will be relieved of the branch responsibilities.  He will function as the in-charge of the Cell and the Sr. Manager posted at the Abhyudaya Nagar branch will be in fullfledged charge of the branch.

 

(c)        The other staff for the Cell have been drawn from the three branches concerned.

(iii) Functions:

            The Cell will attend to scrutiny, appraisal and sanction of all loan proposals (irrespective of the amount) except loans against FDRs, Govt. Securities, Gold loans and Cash Credit (SOD) against FDR/NSC/KVP/RBI bonds, LIC policies, etc. Parel and Sewri branches will continue to sanction such loans.  In case of Abhyudaya Nagar branch, it will sanction loans only against FDR and all other loans, including those mentioned above will be sanctioned by the Cell. Besides, all the three branches will sanction & issue BGs against 100% margin as per delegation. Sanction of facilities by the Cell will include new sanctions, renewals, renewals with enhancement and also review of the cash credit facilities.

(c)        appraisal and sanction process

            Although acceptance of credit proposals and their scrutiny is entrusted to the staff posted in the Cell, the branches from which the proposals will originate and with whom the borrowal accounts will be maintained after sanction of facilities will be responsible for ensuring borrower's identity including risks of perception (KYC Norms compliance) and genuineness of the details such as purpose, securities offered, feasibilities etc., furnished in the loan applications. It is, therefore, decided that branch in-charge with the staff of the Cell will be involved in presanction visits and verification.  Actual sanction of facilities will however, be done by the Manager/AGM of the Cell or appropriate higher authority.             It should be clearly understood and noted by all concerned that   the entire responsibility in respect of genuineness/ accuracy of the factual information recorded in the visit report will be that of the concerned visiting clerk/official, since the recommendation and decision regarding  sanction of the facilities will be based on the findings and observations of the visiting clerks/officials.

v) Other functional aspects :

(a)        As the present  AGM of Abhyudaya Nagar Branch is made the in-charge of the Cell, he will attend exclusively the work of the Cell.  He will report to the Addl. General Manager, Head Office. A Sr. Manager is provided to Abhyudaya Nagar branch in place of the AGM withdrawn from the branch to head the Cell.  The Sr. Manager is the In-charge of Abhyudaya Nagar branch.  He along with the Manager of the branch will look after the day-to-day business of Abhyudaya Nagar branch.

 

(b)       The officials and staff at Sewri & Parel branches will arrange to provide appropriate loan/CC application forms, alongwith list of documents required, to their customers.  They will  interview the customers and guide them for filling the loan/CC applications forms, membership application forms, consent letters of sureties, and other documents required.  The branches should provide similar service even to those members of public who are not their customers and guide them properly.  It should be noted that the branch staff will be liable for disciplinary action if complaints are received at the Credit Cell/Zonal Office/HO that the customers/ potential customers enquiring about or approaching for credit facilities at branches are not being guided / helped satisfactorily and are merely  being advised to approach the Credit Cell.

 

(c)        Before disbursement of the loan sanctioned by it,  the Cell will ensure that the procedures like assignment, mortgage/pledge/hypothecation, etc. and all other formalities necessary for creation of the bank's charge on the  securities, principal and collateral, are completed.  No work that is of material importance upto  the stage of and related to the disbursement should be kept pending. The files will be sent to branches after completing the documentation and other procedures.  A dummy file should be kept at the Cell for further follow-up in respect of assignment of LIC Policies, etc.  wherever such follow-up is required. Pursuing such cases will be the responsibility of the Cell. Action for discharge of securities such as  reassignments,  release of pledged/ mortgaged properties,  etc. should also be attended to by the Cell after closure of the relative loan accounts that have been sanctioned by the Cell.

 

(d)       The Cell will maintain separate Securities/Documents Registers in respect of the borrowal accounts at each branch.

           

(e)        Immediately after receipt of the file by the branch from the Cell, the branch in-charge should arrange for completing the entry of all details in the limit masters.  In respect of loans above Rs. 2.00 lacs and upto Rs. 5.00 lacs, the branch in-charge should arrange post disbursement inspection within one month through the Asst. Accountant/ Accountant of the branch.  The review and post-disbursement inspection of loans and advances above  Rs. 5.00 lacs should be done personally by the Sr. Manager/ Manager at branches.

 

(f)        After pre-sanction visit and verification carried out jointly by the branch      in-charge and AGM of the Cell, the files of the customers seeking facilities of above Rs. 7.00 lacs and upto Rs. 15.00 lacs will be scrutinized by the Cell and sent to H.O. by the Cell with recommendation of the Manager/AGM for being sanctioned by the Addl. GM/GM/M.D., as the case may be.  The present guidelines of routing loans and advances proposals above Rs. 10.00 lacs through Zonal Offices are not applicable to the Cell.  Thus, all proposals above Rs. 10.00 lacs and upto Rs. 15.00 lacs will be directly sent by the AGM of the Cell to Head Office with their recommendations. 

           

(B) Recovery Call Centre

            As a forerunner to total bank net working where follow-up for recovery in respect of defaulters of all branches can be pursued from a single centre, a Recovery Call Centre will be made operational as a part of the Specialized Credit Cell covering three branches of Parel, Sewri and Abhyudaya Nagar on experimental basis. The organizational set up and functions of the proposed Recovery Call Centre will be as under:

(i) Organizational Set up:-

            Call Centre will be provided with following staff:

                         Cadre                                    No. of Staff

                        Asst. Accountant                         1

                        Clerk                                            2

            The Call Centre staff will work under the direct supervision of the Manager/AGM of the Cell.

(ii) Functions:-

(a)        At the present level of technology application in our bank, initial  follow-up upto generation and issuing of all recovery notices to the defaulters will continue to be attended by the concerned branches.

 

(b)       The DGMs of respective zones will ensure during their visits to branches that generation of loan/cc notices, dispatch thereof and subsequent follow up is made by the branches.  However, telephonic reminders and further follow up would be made by the Call Centre at the Credit Cell. 

 

(c)        I.T. Dept. will ensure that back-up of previous day in respect of the three branches will be loaded early next day in the computers of the Cell and follow up with the borrowers through telephonic reminder will be pursued by the Call Centre. Maintenance of records of the follow-up, action taken by the Recovery Call Centre will be done through the systems.

 

            As the Recovery Call Centre is not yet set up, the follow up with defaulters will be made by branches which please note.

 

(C)  Responsibilities and Role of branches in supporting the Cell

 

(a)        It should be noted that though the credit appraisal and sanctioning in respect of all the three branches covered under the new arrangement will be taken care of by the Cell, the Sr. Manager and the Manager of these branches will be responsible for achieving   the   deposits, loans, profit and NPA targets, monitoring of loans and advances, including post disbursement inspections,  recovery of overdues in defaulter accounts, etc.  They will ensure regular and timely receipt of stock / book debt statements every month, obtaining adequate insurance cover for the securities charged in the bank’s favour, such as stocks of goods, plant and machineries, moveable and immovable properties, etc.  They should also visit the defaulters with limits above Rs. 5.00 lacs and arrange for visit by Clerks/Asst. Accountants/Accountants to all defaulters up to Rs. 5.00 lacs.

 

(b)       Sr. Managers/Managers of these three branches should visit the unrenewed/unreviewed CC accounts and ensure that renewal/review/ enhancement proposals are received at the earliest.  They should extend support and guidance that may be required by the borrowers.  The review/ renewal proposals thus procured should be forwarded to the Cell for further processing and sanction with required documents/financial statements.  In respect of review/renewal of CC Accounts, the Incharge of the branch should ensure that such proposals are received atleast 1 month before due dates along with all documents.  

 

(c)        Timely receipt of financial statements like audited balance sheet, profit and loss account and other financial data from the borrowers is of crucial importance for monitoring borrowal accounts, particularly the bigger accounts as also for their reviews/ renewals.  Many of our borrowers default in these matters.  Branch in-charges who are relieved of the major work with formation of the Cell should follow-up regularly with all CC account borrowers and obtain audited financial statements regularly and complete Credit Rating exercise in respect of borrower and keep the Cell informed. 

 

(d)       The Manager/In-charge of these branches should market loans and advances by daily visits to individual units, traders, institutions, corporate offices.  They should canvass group loans under ECS/SDL facility and ensure achievement of loan target for March 2006 by 28.2.2006 itself.

(D)       Conclusion:-

           

            The Specialized Credit Cell is functioning with effect from 6th October 2005 at Abhyudaya Nagar Branch premises.  It is first such specialized unit organized within the bank. The idea is to equip the staff of our bank to meet the challenge posed by the present day competitive and hi-tech system in operation in the banking industry. After assessing the performance of the Cell and the branches brought within its scope, customer response, the Management may consider forming more such Specialized Cells catering to varied customer requirements.  All officers and staff of the bank are advised to take a note of the formation of the Cell which marks a departure from the existing  organizational form of the bank.
