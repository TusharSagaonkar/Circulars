Office Order No. 306 dated 30th September, 2009 

Extension of office hours of Management Trainees, Managers, Sr. Managers, Asst. General Managers

& Dy. General Managers by two hours

i.e. from 6 ½ hours to 8 ½ hours

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          You are aware that Bank has undertaken branch expansion programme and embarked upon the vision of Rs.15,000 Crore business mix by March 2014.  The number of branches have increased from 39 to 78.  The Bank has also extended its area of operation in the States of Karnataka and Gujarat and also planned to extend its area all over India.  It is our aim to increase business as per the vision 2014, curtail NPAs by proper credit appraisal and rigorous recovery measures and increasing profitability of the Bank from year to year. To meet this challenging task, every Officer alongwith all staff members, should put-in extra efforts at their branches/departments.

 

2.      In terms of the Agreement with Union, the following additional duties and responsibilities have been assigned to the Accountant of the branch.

i)     Passing of CRC Against Clearing and OK for Big Payment, Payment to Third Party and Payment without Pass book will be made without any limit. For ABB Transaction Cash withdrawals for Saving Bank for Rs.25,000/- and Cash withdrawals for Current Account for Rs.50,000/- as per extant instructions. Further they are authorised to give withdrawal in advance against clearing facility and Cheque Discounting upto Rs.25,000/- per party per day.

 

They will get all the aforesaid powers exercised by them duly ratified by the Branch In-charge at the end of the day or on the next working day.

Accountants and Asst. Accountants are second line of our Managerial force and the responsibility to develop their abilities and capabilities lie with the officers who are monitoring their work. Ratification by the Managers/Sr. Managers/ Asst. General Managers of delegation exercised by Accountant does not entail responsibility on them but their role is to monitor the delegation exercised by the Accountant.  They should guide/educate Accountant in respect of credentials of the parties and the basis on which such delegation is exercised till the Accountant gets well experienced and fully conversant with these new responsibilities. The main purpose is to train the Accountant for safeguarding the interest of the Bank.

 

ii)    Entire housekeeping and day-to-day functioning of the branch

iii)      Compliance of all Inspection & Audit Reports.

iv)   Submission of various periodical Returns

v)    Attending to customers' complaints and ensuring that best customer service is extended to all customers / shareholders.

 

 3.     Thus, present operational responsibilities of Managers/Sr. Managers/ Asst. Gen. Managers of housekeeping and overseeing day-to-day functioning of branch have now been assigned to the Accountant and Managers/Sr. Managers/Asst. General Managers have been made free to concentrate on the following business activities in addition to being the overall and ultimate In-charge of the Branches:

 

a)        Marketing of Deposits & achieving targets on monthly basis - mobilization of CASA Deposits.

 

b)       NPA reduction and recovery/ legal actions - review of NPA follow up, filing of Cases, SRO actions, etc.

 

c)        Marketing of Loans & Advances – Visit to Industrial Estates, commercial establishment, etc.

 

d)       LIC Business – To achieve targets on monthly basis.

 

e)        Foreign Exchange business – to market new business – to achieve targets on monthly basis.

 

f)         Inspection of Big Units on Monthly Basis / Small Units on Quarterly Basis.

 

 

The Managers/Sr. Managers/Asst. General Managers have to involve all staff members and to ensure that the goals set for the branch by the Management are achieved on month to month basis and well before the year ending.  They should also make efforts to increase per employee business and per employee profit at their branches. Wherever applicable and necessary the Branch-in-charge shall adopt the practice of Multi-tasking and make maximum use of available staff strength for Marketing, Business Development, Recovery, Visit to NPA Accounts etc.

 

4.      At big branches like Vashi, Bhandup, Ghatkopar, New Panvel, Nehru Nagar, Nerul, etc. where an Asst. Gen. Manager and 2 Managers are posted, the In-charge of the Branch should allocate the work judiciously between these two Managers and ensure that one of the Managers is always deputed for marketing of Deposits, Advances and the other Manager is allotted the work of recovery in addition to their normal duties. The work of each staff and the work allotted to these 2 managers should be closely monitored by A.G.M. and he/she will also confirm that the staff members are wholeheartedly involved in Business Development and recovery work and that each staff is having adequate work load on all the days.

 

5.      It is observed that some officials are sitting late, putting excellent efforts & giving best results. As against this some officials are coming late, leaving early and the outcome of their efforts is far from satisfactory. The lethargy / indifferent attitude of some officials is resulting into non-achievement of expected business targets. It has therefore been decided that release of yearly increment to officials will be only after satisfactory performance in all the parameters prescribed for the purpose and clearance from General Manager, Accounts, Inspection & Vigilance Dept. and General Manager, HRD. I.T. & Development Dept. In case of Officers working at all Administrative Offices their yearly increment will be released only after the clearance from General Manager (Accounts & Inspection) and General Manager (HRD, I.T. & Development). 

 

6.      In terms of Agreement with Officers’ Association, it has been decided that the Officers would work with full vigor and diligence and put in not less than 2 hours of extra work every day.

 

          Accordingly officers posted at branches and at administrative offices should work for minimum two hours extra on all working days and one hour extra on Saturday. In-charge of the branch/Head of the Department will monitor the extended time schedule alongwith the out put given by each officer.

(i)      Additional Duties of Morning Manager:

Processing of Loans & Advances, visits to borrowal accounts, visits for renewal and review of Cash Credit Accounts, etc. To ensure that applications of Loans & Advances are disposed of in prescribed time limit, adherence to Action Plan of CC A/c, etc.

 

          (ii)     Additional Duties of Evening Manager:

Recovery and NPA management, visits to NPA A/cs, distribution and monitoring of NPA A/cs among staff, review of NPA follow up by staff, Notices to NPA A/cs – Filing of Cases, Recovery in SRO Accounts, etc. 

 

7.      It is the responsibility of Dy. General Manager of the respective Zones and Asst. General Manager of Regional Offices to ensure that all Managers/Sr. Managers/Asst. Gen. Managers of Branches adhere to extended time schedule. As regards the Departments headed by DGM, it is responsibility of DGM to ensure compliance of these instructions. The General Managers should ensure that all managerial staff adhere to the extended time schedule in all Departments under their control.

 

8.      All Managers/Sr. Managers /Asst. Gen. Managers/Dy. Gen. Managers will maintain the Visit Register and Diary. Details of every visit and its outcome will be noted in the Visit Register. The details of visit and outcome will be submitted on monthly basis to Branch-in-charge/respective D.G.M. wherein new business brought under various portfolios like Deposits (CASA & Other Deposits) in terms of number of  Accounts and amount, commercial and other type of loans and advances brought to branch books, Insurance and Forex Business brought etc. will be mentioned.

 

9.      All officials are aware that the work culture and atmosphere in Nationalized Bank, leave aside Private Banks, has completely changed during last 5 years.  The Officers and Managerial staff in Nationalized Banks like State Bank of India and other Nationalized Banks have set examples before their staff by voluntarily contributing daily 3 to 4 hours extra. They ensure that they do not leave office without completing day-to-day assignments and routine work. This is the need of the hour in our Bank. Let us work hard, complete day to day work daily, achieve all business targets monthly, increase deposits, loans & advances, reduce NPAs, increase income from other business and make this Bank a vibrant large Co-operative Bank and with highest profits every year.

 

10.    All Managers/Sr. Managers /Asst. Gen. Managers/Dy. Gen. Managers/General Managers shall take note of the instruction contained in this Office Order and ensure strict compliance since 01.10.2009.
