Forex Office Order No.266 dated 29th November, 2008 

 

Revision of Service Charges on various

Foreign Exchange Business/Services w.e.f. 1st December 2008.

 

          You are aware that our Bank on getting the Foreign Exchange License started Foreign Exchange Business w.e.f. 18th February 2008 and at that time vide our Office Order No. 245 dated 19-03-2008, we had stipulated different type of service charges to be recovered from the customers availing our foreign exchange services.

 

          At the time of issuing the above referred Officer Order the service charges were recommended on the basis of certain services which were offered normally in the area of foreign exchange by our peer Banks. However, on starting our forex business, we realized that certain category of services which were provided to the customers were not included and in few cases our service charges were either higher or lower compared with other banks. Also the Service Tax on these charges was received separately. Consequently, different staff members and different branches were levying different charges for the same service due to interpretation problems.

         

          To overcome all these problems, we have revised the service charges adding few more services which were not included earlier and making the service charges inclusive of service tax so that the errors in calculation will be avoided at the branches.

 

          The list of revised service charges is enclosed herewith.

 

          The revised Service Charges are applicable w.e.f. 01-12-2008.

 

 

 

M. C. Shetty

Managing Director

Exchange Rate

 

Foreign Exchange Department at Dadar Branch will get on going inter bank market spot buying rate and spot selling rate from Bank of India / Development Credit Bank / State Bank of India / Thomas Cook / International Currency Exchange / any other agency with whom agency arrangements are finalized and will term them as disposal rate and acquiring rate.  Thereafter, they will arrive at various rates by loading margins in these rates as under:

1.           TT Selling Rates: These rates will be quoted for following transactions:

a.   Clean outward remittances

b.   Cancellation of all purchase transactions.

c.   Cancellation of all forward purchase contracts.

d.  Payment of import bills directly received by the importer from the oversea supplier.

e.   Crystallization of overdue export bills.

 

Dadar Branch will load an exchange margin of up to 0.150% over the acquiring rate rounded of to nearest paise in case of USD / GBP / EURO. In case of AUD / CAD / JPY / NZD / SGD exchange margins to be loaded will be 0.25% and in all other cases it will be 0.50%.

 

2.           Bill Selling Rate: These rates will be quoted for undertaking all import transactions involving handling of documents by the bank.  In this case, Dadar branch will load an additional margin of 0.200% over the TT Selling rate rounded of to nearest paise.

 

3.           TT Buying Rates: These rates will be quoted for following type of transactions:

a.   Clean inward remittance (TT, PO, MT, and DD) for which cover has already been provided in or Nostro Account abroad.

b.  Conversion of proceeds of instruments sent on collection basis. (When proceeds are credited to Nostro Account.)

c.   Cancellation of outward TT, MT, PO, DD etc.

d.  Cancellation of forward sale contract.

e.   Undrawn portion of an Export Bill realized.

 

Dadar Branch will load an exchange margin of up to 0.150% over the disposal rate rounded of to nearest paise.

 

4.           Bill Buying Rate: These rates will be quoted for all export transactions involving purchase / negotiation / discounting of export bills. 

 

Dadar Branch will load 0.1% over disposal rate and also adjust the premium / discount for the normal transit period / usance period in the rate and then quote the rate for the transactions. 

 

5.           Clean Instruments Purchase Rate: In case of all inward transactions where amount is not received in the nostro account, this rate will be quoted.  Similarly, when an important customer for his genuine need request the bank to grant immediate credit against foreign currency instruments deposited by him in his account with us, we may purchase the foreign currency amount at this rate.

 

Dadar Branch shall load an extra margin of 0.15% over the TT Buying rate and round it to the nearest paise.  In such cases, interest shall be separately recovered for 15 days @ the bank’s highest lending rate. 

 

6.     Traveller Cheques Selling Rate: Whenever branches are required to issue traveller cheques to their customers this rate will be applied. 

 

Dadar Branch will be loading an exchange margin of 0.50% over the TT Selling rate, rounded of to the nearest five paise.

 

7.     Traveller Cheques Buying Rate:  Whenever branches are required to encash traveller cheques submitted by their clients this rate will be applied. 

 

Dadar Branch will be loading an exchange margin of 0.50% over the TT Buying rate, rounded of to the nearest five paise.

 

 

8.     Currency Note Buying Rate / Selling Rate: In case of Currency notes, it has been decided that generally the branches will not hold any stock of currency notes with them. We have a tie up with Thomas Cook (India) Ltd. / International Currency Exchange / Pheroze Framroze etc. whereby they have agreed to buy / provide currency notes to the branches.  They will provide rates to the branches over telephone as and when required.  The branches will then load an exchange margin of 0.50% in the rate rounded of the nearest five paise and quote it to the customer. 

 

9.     Forward Purchase Rates: Whenever export customers / other customers desires to hedge his exchange risk by booking a forward contract and accordingly request the branch to book a forward contract for him, the same will be booked after obtaining sanction at appropriate level. 

 

Dadar branch will first obtain the forward purchase rates from Bank of India / Development Credit Bank / State Bank of India for the appropriate period contract and thereafter load an exchange margin of 0.25% in the rate and rounding off to the nearest paise, quote the rate to the customer.  This rate if acceptable to the customer then forward contract application form will be taken from the customer and thereafter contract will be booked. 

 

A service charge at the flat rate of Rs. 1000/= per contract booking / cancellation will be levied.

 

Additionally, if cancellation results in outlay of funds then interest will be charged for outlay of funds @ 3.50% over PLR. 

 

10.    Forward Sales  Rate: Whenever an importer  customer/other customers desires to hedge his exchange risk by booking a forward contract and accordingly request the branch to book a forward contract for him, the same will be booked after obtaining sanction at appropriate level. 

 

Dadar branch will first obtain the forward sale rate from Bank of India / Development Credit Bank / State Bank of India for the appropriate period contract and thereafter load an exchange margin of 0.15% in the rate and rounding off to the nearest paise, quote the rate to the customer.  This rate if acceptable to the customer then forward contract application form will be taken from the customers and thereafter contract will be booked.

 

A service charge at the flat rate of Rs. 1000/= per contract booking / cancellation will be levied.

 

Additionally, if cancellation results in outlay of funds then interest will be charged for outlay of funds @ 3.50% over PLR. 

 

For all the above rates for different types of transactions, the above procedure will be followed by Dadar branch. However, for operational convenience, discretion is given to the Dadar branch in-charge. He may lower the exchange margins on all the rates as and when required considering the business potential, customer profile, market conditions etc.  However, he will be required to make a note as to the reasons for lowering the margins in specific cases. For valuable clients, he may lower the margin up to 50% of the normal margins, however in exceptional cases with information to the Additional General Manager, the margins may be lowered up to 75% of the normal margins.  But in no case margins can be lowered then these. This discretion should be judiciously used with the ultimate purpose of maximizing profit for the bank.

 

Reporting of transactions by the branches to Dadar branch:  As mentioned above Dadar branch will provide card rates for few prominent currencies to all branches daily in the morning.  These branches will normally undertake forex transactions at card rates only up to an amount of Rs.1,00,000/- (equivalent of USD 2000).  However, if transactions are to be undertaken for higher values above Rs.1,00,000/-  then the branch will be required to revert to Dadar branch  for confirmed rate.  After getting the confirmed rate from Dadar branch, the branch will undertake the transaction in its books and thereafter report the transaction. 

 

Dadar branch will keep track of all forex transactions undertaken by the bank as a whole and thereafter as per the position (over bought / over sold) the net amount will be sold / purchased from / to Bank of India / Development Credit Bank / State Bank of India.  Normally the purchase / sale to Bank of India / Development Credit Bank / State Bank of India shall be in round amounts of USD 50,000 and in multiples thereof. 

 

Dadar branch for handling the foreign exchange exposure shall be permitted to have following exposure limits:

1.   Overnite Limit:  Dadar branch may take an exposure of maximum USD 500,000 (USD Five Hundred Thousand) after the business hours and during holidays.  Any amount in excess of   USD 500,000 should be disposed off / acquired to / from Bank of India / Development Credit Bank / State Bank of India before the close of business hours.

2.   Intra Day Limits:  Dadar branch may take a maximum exposure of USD 1,500,000 (USD 1.5 million) during the business hours when the Inter Bank forex market in Mumbai is open and functioning.  Any amount in excess of USD 1,500,000 should be immediately disposed off / acquired to / from Bank of India / Development Credit Bank / State Bank of India before the close of business hours.

Dadar branch will submit a statement / report on a weekly basis (every Saturday) to the ALCO committee regarding its overall purchase / sale transactions daily, NOSTRO a/c balances, exposures undertaken etc.
