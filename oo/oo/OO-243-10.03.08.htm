Office Order No. 243 dated 10 March 2008

 

Measures for prevention of Terrorism

Lists of Terrorist Individuals/organizations.

 

          A reference is invited to Office Orders No. 239 dated 21.01.2008 and various Office Orders on the above subject issued earlier.  We have since received from the Reserve Bank of India a copy of an updated Consolidated List of individuals and entities as under:

 

1.      A copy of de-listing of one individual and twelve entities from its Consolidated List (Al-Qaida Section) carried out by the UN 1267 Committee to the Committee's Consolidated List, as per Annex I.

 

2.      A coy of Al-Qaida and Taliban Sanctions Committee's Consolidated List of Individuals and entities belonging to or associated with Taliban and/or the Al-Qaida organization last updated on December 12, 2006 by the UN Committee, as per Annex II. 

 

The consolidated list of individuals/entities banned as per UN 1267 Sanction Committee List may be suitably updated at branch level.  The branches may also ensure to cross check all account opening applications and transactions carried out in the accounts with the updated consolidated list and report those accounts to Head Office for onward transmission to RBI, which have any resemblance with individuals/entities listed therein. 

 

As advised in Office Order No. 239, para 4, only one copy each of the lists for the use and record of the Branch is enclosed.  A copy of this Office Order, without enclosures is circulated for information and for the sake of completing the records at Zonal Office and Departmental levels.

 

 

            M. C. Shetty

           Managing Director

Encl:  As above
