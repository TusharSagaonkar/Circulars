          A reference is invited to Circular OC NO.377 dated 26th December, 2006 and Circular OC No.418 dated 15th November 2007 for implementation of Any Branch Banking (ABB) – and its procedure for operation.  It has now been decided to offer facilities connected to ABB to our customers.  We, therefore issue the following instructions for implementation of ABB which will supersede the instructions contained in our both the circulars referred to above.  

  The Centralized Core Banking solution has been installed by the Bank at our Central Data Centre (CDC) with a view to provide Any Branch Banking (ABB) facilities to our customers. All the branches are connected to the Central Data Centre for the purpose.  It is a pre-requisite for connecting branches to the central server that OMNI system is loaded on the branch servers before connecting the branches to the Central Data Centre.  All Branches are connected to Central Data Centre including Head Office Departments, Zonal Offices and Regional Offices of Udupi and Ahmedabad. Core Banking OMNI software has already been introduced which is allowing the customers to have facilities like withdrawal of cash, transfer of funds and lodging instruments for clearing through any branch front office and do away with the compulsion of approaching the branch where the customer is maintaining the account. With Core Banking facilities placed in our bank, the customer of any of our branches can deposit or withdraw cash from his account with any other branch of our Bank and transfer funds from one branch to another. For smooth and effective implementation of ABB in Core Banking scenario, the procedure and practices as laid down hereunder will have to be followed for operation of cash, clearing and transfer transactions undertaken by the customers.  It has also been decided to allow restrictive multi branch access to DGM offices and HO Departments. 

 

Branch Categorization:

          In the Core Banking scenario, the branch from which a customer is carrying out his transaction will be known as the Host Branch and the branch where the customer is maintaining his account will be known as the Based Branch.

 

Types of Transactions:

a) All transactions will be grouped into two types, viz.

i) Online transactions and

ii) Offline transactions.

Online transactions will be those transactions which are carried out through the Host Branch during the business hours of the Based Branch.  Offline transactions will be the transactions put through by Host Branch after business hours of the Based Branch i.e. when Based Branch has closed and sealed the batches and handed over to Data Centre.  In case of offline transactions all such transactions will be accounted for on the next working day at the Based Branch.

 

b) In Core banking the central server will use ABBTRFR Batch for online transactions. Any transactions after day end of the Based Branch will be done through ABBTRFR1. All accounting entries will be made on the next working day and any ATM transactions (offline) done after business hours of the Based Branch will be routed   through  ABBTR-2.

c) All Branches are advised to update cheque book feeding, signature scanning, PAN No., special instruction(s) if any and authorize all the unauthorized accounts.

 

2. (i)  Withdrawal and deposit of cash in ABB environment:

 

a)   Withdrawal only by cheque will be allowed.  Under no circumstances ABB transaction should be allowed by withdrawal slip. 

 

b)   Cash withdrawals will be allowed only to the deposit account holders, who come personally for withdrawal of cash by way of cheque.

 

c)    The Maximum cash withdrawals permissible at the Host Branch under ABB will be Rs.25,000/- for SB Account and FLXLN Account and Rs.50,000/- for Current Accounts.  All transaction above Rs.25,000/- should be authorized by Accountant/Manager/Branch In-charge. 

 

d)   No cash withdrawals should be allowed to Cash Credit Account holders through ABB.

 

e)   While depositing cash/cheque under ABB, Base Branch code number and Based Branch name should be indicated on the deposit slip. For this purpose,  the Host Branch staff will put a rubber stamp on the customer’s pay-in slip and enter the code number and name of the Based Branch.

 

f)    The paid cheques/deposit slips, etc. should be retained at Host Branch.

 

 2. (ii) Procedure for making entry through ABB transaction at Host Branch:

 

a)   After log in, select normal CASH batch.

b)   Select the Account ID as ABB and select ABB activity.  On selection of ABB activity, system will display list of connected branches.

c)    Select Based Branch where the customer is having his/her account and who wants either to withdraw cash from his account or to deposit cash for credit of his/her account. Once the Based Branch is selected, operator will be in that branch where customer is having his/her account.

d)   Once the Based Branch is selected enter the account number of the customer and select related activity i.e. either debit or credit.

e)   Enter details like instrument type, instrument number, narration, amount, etc. User will be allowed to view signature/s, joint name/s. However user is not allowed to modify data of the Based Branch.

f)    After entering details of withdrawals or deposits, post the relevant entry to customer’s account at the Based Branch.

g)   The following vouchers will be generated at the Host Branch.

      a. ABB                            DR }  in case of withdrawal

                              Or

      b. ABB                             CR }  in case of deposit

            

h)   The following vouchers will be generated at the Based Branch where customer is having his account.

                1.  Party’s Account               DR

                                                               in case of withdrawal by cheque.

                 2. ABB                     CR         

 

Or

 

                 1.  ABB                                          DR  

                                                                        in case of deposit by cash.

                 2.  Party’s Account                      CR       

 

i)     To view the details of the vouchers :

·        At the Based Branch in voucher browse option place the cursor on ABB voucher and full details of the voucher like from where the cash was withdrawn or where the cash was deposited, etc. will be displayed.

·        At the Host Branch in voucher browse option similar details about the operated account of the Based Branch will be displayed.

 

j)    Software makes entries in ABB Account for reconciliation of inter branch transactions on the same day by debit/credit to the Host Branch or credit/debit to the Based Branch and vice versa.

 

3)   Transfer of fund:

      Procedure:

 

a)   The transfer of funds through cheques may be effected only for customers of our bank. Pay order/DDs, etc. may be issued by the Host Branch against cheques by making transfer entries. While issuing Pay Order/DD, account holder must sign requisition slip for Pay Order /DD.  Third party request for issue of Pay Order / DD in ABB should not be accepted. 

b)   The following transfer transactions are also allowed in ABB Module:

 

Case 1) Inter-Branch transaction from one branch (Based Branch) to another Branch (Host Branch), e.g. transaction between Kanjur Branch to New Panvel Branch. 

 

Case 2) Inter-Branch transaction from branch to branches, e.g. transaction between Branch (Kanjur) to Branch (Panvel) from Branch (Abhyudaya Nagar) is allowed.

 

Case 3) Transactions between two accounts of the same branch from other branch, e.g. transaction between one account of Branch (New Panvel) to another account of Branch (New Panvel) from Branch (Nehru Nagar).

 

Case 4) All Branches are allowed to update pass books of any of our branches.  However duplicate pass book or new pass book should be issued only by Based Branch.  

 

Case 5) Considering security aspect no statement of account should be issued at Host Branch. 

 

Case 6) Opening or closure of any account should be done only at Based Branch. 

 

Case 7) All branches are allowed to Debit /Credit large amount by way of transfer transactions. The relative cheques of transfer transactions above Rs.50,000/- in all deposit accounts in ABB will hereafter be required to be passed by Acctt/Manager/Branch In-charge.  No ABB transaction should be allowed in Dormant /In-operative account.  

 

Case 8) All Branches/Depts. are allowed to verify signatures of account holders for internal purpose such as Credit Application, Membership Application etc.  Signature verification for all external purposes such as Signature Verification for Government authorities, Transfer of Stock – Shares, ECS mandate, etc.   should be done by Based Branch only.

 

c)    Procedure for transfer transactions for credit or debit of accounts and maintenance of voucher at the Host Branch will be the same as the procedure is laid down in case of cash withdrawal or deposit of cash under ABB.      

d)   Only after debiting the account with the Based Branch, the Host Branch should affix the transfer stamp on the cheque being utilized for withdrawal and on the accompanying deposit slip.

e)   In case of transfer of funds it will be mandatory to debit the account first and release the corresponding credit subsequently.  All ABB entries should be authorized immediately.  Branches are facing difficulties in closing their batches and/or passing cheques where ABB vouchers are not authorized immediately by corresponding other Branches.  Priority should always be given to authorize ABB vouchers immediately on posting of ABB transactions.

 

4) I) Centralized Outward Clearing Module

      

       Procedure :

 

i)   On implementation of Centralized Outward Clearing Module, our branches are required to use only three batches for outward clearing.

CSC-I (for high value).

CSC-II (for morning shift).

CSC-III (for evening shift).

 

ii)  All Sundays should be marked as Holiday in calendar master

 

iii) Instruction for Double shift branches :

 On Every Saturday immediately after business hours, double shift branches have to change the holiday status of Sunday as working day.  After marking Sunday as working day, before closing ATMTFR1(online) batch, prepone the posting and funding dates of ATMTFR2 as Sunday’s date.

 

iv) On Sunday/Holiday of Based Branch, all Host Branches are restrained from entering outward clearing cheques of other Branches.  However, Branches can accept outward clearing cheques on Sunday and make entry on Monday/Next Working day. 

 

v) It is pre-requisite in OMNI outward clearing program to make single entry in ABB.    Therefore all ABB outward clearing cheques in ABBCLG should be entered separately.  No multiple cheques should be entered in the system.  

 

II) Procedure for accepting outward clearing cheques (CSC) for other branches:

 

1.    If any branch (Host Branch) accepts outward clearing cheque of other branch(Based Branch) in CSC-I (high value batch) corresponding entries will generate in TRCLG-I batch at other branch (Based Branch).

 

2.    Similarly cheques accepted in CSC-II and CSC-III batch will generate in TRCLG-II and TRCLG-III batch respectively. 

 

3.    Incase of return in CSC-I / CSC-II / CSC-III, entry will be generated in CRC-I of Host Branch and TR-RTN batch of the Based Branch.

 

NOTE : - Posting and Fund effect Date of TRCLG-I, TRCLG-II, TRCLG-III is similar to CSC-I, CSC-II, CSC-III respectively. In any case if Host Branch change the posting and fund effect dates of  CSC batches, system accordingly changes the posting and fund effect date of TRCLG-I, TRCLG-II and TRCLG-III.

III) In case of Cheque return  :

 

Cheque returned unpaid comes to the Host Branch only (Branch which has collected the cheque. In the present example it is Nehru Nagar Branch) , The outward return cheque process will be entered by Central  Clearing Dept., and Executed at data Centre along with other cheques as usual.

 

At Host Branch (Nehru Nagar Branch) return voucher will be generated in CRC-I batch

 

 

 

At Based Branch (Bail Bazar) voucher will be generated in TR-RTN batch. TR-RTN, batch is meant for outward cheque return voucher, cheques which were collected at other branch. (in this example we have shown as TR-INT, in live scenario it will be TR-RTN)

 

 

It is the responsibility of the Host Branch to return the clearing cheque to the customer who has deposited the clearing cheque at Host Branch although the customer maintains his/her account at Based Branch.

 

Working of ABB clearing system is on centralized environment and makes inter Branch clearing entry effect simultaneously.  Therefore individual branches should not give posting of all CSC batches at the Branch level.  Only centralized department is authorized to make posting of all CSC batches. 

 

5)  Term Deposit Interest Transfer:

 

Sometimes customers maintain term deposit accounts like MID or QID in one branch or at various branches and instruct branche(s) to transfer the periodical interest to their Saving Bank Account maintained at some other branch. For example a customer having S.B. A/c at Nehru Nagar Branch and maintaining F.D. accounts at Bhandup, Mulund, Ghatkopar and instructing these branches to credit the periodical interest to his/her S.B. A/c maintained at Nheru Nagar Branch. In such cases instead of issuing pay order, enter branch code of base branch where the saving account is maintained and the account number, in term deposit receipt masters, under transfer account ID column.  At the time of interest application, the system automatically transfers the interest amount to specified SB account with the Based Branch. 

 

6) Standing Instructions:     

 

One more feature of Core Banking is standing instructions to transfer funds.  Presently we accept standing instructions, if the customer maintains both the accounts in the same branch where debit has to be raised from one account maintained at the branch and credit has to be given to other account maintained in the same branch.  Henceforth  we can  enter the accounts of any other branch of our bank in standing instructions master.  e.g. : if a customer  is having loan account at Sewri branch and Savings Bank account at  Abhyudaya Nagar branch, he can give standing instructions to Abhyudaya Nagar Branch to debit his Abhyudaya Nagar Branch Savings Bank account  and credit his loan account at Sewri  branch.  Standing instructions forms should be accepted and fed  by the Branch where account is to be debited.

 

7) Records:

As per system at the time of day end balance of ABB and ABBCLG of all Branches transfer to ABBADJ account maintained at Head Office.  At the Day End, branches, both the Host Branches and Based Branches   are advised to generate ABB transaction statement for reconciliation purpose and send it to Accounts Dept. The ABB statement will provide full details of the transactions taking place at the Host Branch and the Based Branch with common advice number. The concerned branch should retain one copy of ABB transactions in its daily bunch of vouchers.

 

8) Other procedural matters:

a)   The Host Branch should put narration with date of transaction and also indicate the offline transactions so as to avoid legal implication since the offline data will be generated on the next working day at Based Branch (e.g. Offline Host Branch Dr/Cr transaction of 13/12/2008 will be generated on 14/12/2008 or on next working day at Host Branch.)

b)   The Host Branch should not entertain balance enquiry, without prior identification. 

c)    The secrecy should be maintained by all staff/officers pertaining to the transactions of other branches as in the case of their branch.

 

All the members of the staff are advised to carefully study the procedure explained above to enable them to derive optimum benefit from the automation for rendering sophisticated, efficient and prompt customer service through ABB and Core Banking which is envisaged in adoption of Core Banking solution.
