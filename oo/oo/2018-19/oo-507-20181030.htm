Office Order No. 507 dated 30th October, 2018

Allocation of Duties & Responsibilities of KYC/AML/CFT Compliance Officer

 

I.   In supersession to Office Order No. 443 dated 30th January 2015, revised Duties and Responsibilities of KYC/AML/CFT Compliance Officers deputed to branches for work related to KYC/AML/CFT will be as under with effect from 1st November, 2018.

 

          i.    To Assist Asst. Manager in opening all Saving & Current accounts and ensure data entry of AOF-I and AOF-II, CIF-I and CIF-II in Express Master.

 

        ii.    Before opening any deposit/borrowal account, generate and print “Name screening Certificate” for comparing prospective customer’s name against Terrorist List and all other negative lists.

 

      iii.    Ensure that no duplicate Customer number is created so as to adhere to and comply with the RBI guidelines on Customer Unification.

 

       iv.    Monitor effectively e-KYC procedure, escalate device issues with IT or the Vendors concerned.

 

        v.    Ensure Central KYC Registry (CKYCR) procedure introduced at branches for proper implementation and delivery of CKYCR.

 

       vi.    Ensure identification of beneficial ownership in the accounts of Corporates, Trusts and accounts of such other legal entities and enter the details of identified beneficial owners (BO) in Omni application software.

 

     vii.    Ensure that SB / CD account opening forms are scanned and uploaded on day-to-day basis to Centralized Data Entry Cell (CDEC).

 

 

   viii.    Ensure that debit freeze marked in newly opened accounts by Centralized Data Entry Cell due to lack of KYC compliance are reversed strictly after obtaining the required information and feeding it properly in the system.

 

      ix.    Ensure that account opening visit report is kept on record.

 

        x.    Ensure sending Thanks Letter to all customers who have opened new SB/CD Accounts, and ensure obtaining acknowledgement of the same before issuing passbook and cheque book to SB account holders, and cheque book to CD Account holders.

 

      xi.    Ensure obtaining KYC documents of NRE / NRO accounts such as Passport, Visa which should be verified at periodical intervals, and also that follow-up is made to obtain latest and updated copy of Visa & Passport.

 

 

     xii.    Ensure that KYC/Profile documents are properly filed in order of account number & made easily available for verification.

 

   xiii.    Attend to the Alerts generated by AML Application Software. Monitor this activity by running “Branch Alert Summary” report to understand the day-wise pending alert position.

 

   xiv.    Monitor all Special Designated Nationals (SDN) & Transaction-based alerts on day-to-day basis and ensure that no item is kept pending.

 

     xv.    Take printouts of daily reports on a regular basis of “Add / Reject transaction comment report”, and “Rule-wise violation report”’ on monthly basis. Preserve all these reports carefully.

 

   xvi.    Ensure Scrutiny of transactions reported in CTR/NTR/AIR returns for effective monitoring and identifying suspicious transactions.

 

  xvii.    Ensure proper detection and reporting of Fake Indian Currency Notes (FICN).

 

xviii.    Ensure compliance of observations made by team of KYC & AML Cell & Inspection Department pertaining to KYC & AML CFT items.

II. KYC/AML/CFT Compliance Officer shall act as a responsible link between Branch & KYC/AML Cell.

 

III.  The above duty-list for KYC/AML/CFT Compliance Officer is illustrative and not exhaustive. The staff posted/designated shall be required to perform any other duty(ies) as may be entrusted by the Branch In-charge from time to time.

 

IV.  The KYC/AML/CFT Compliance Officer may proceed on leave or get transferred to some other Branch/Office/Dept. Therefore, a proper register is to be maintained at the Branch for the purpose of recording details of the official who is on duty on a regular basis, and name of the official who is provided as a substitute for the KYC/AML/CFT compliance officer with the specific dates when he performs the role in the absence of the regular KYC/AML/CFT compliance officer.

 

V.  At branches where a separate Officer is not posted to perform the above duties, the Branch In-charge should entrust the work related to KYC/AML/CFT compliance to any Officer/Asst. Manager posted at the Branch. Such other Officer/Asst. Manager will be responsible for performing the above-mentioned duties in addition to the routine/regular duties allotted to him/her.

VI. Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all staff/officials concerned at your Branch/Department and ensure strict compliance.

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
