Office Order No. 505 dated 6th September, 2018

Contest for Bank’s Staff Members, Branches & Departments

 Mobile Banking Service & RuPay Debit Card

 

 

It has always been our Bank’s endeavor to offer technological driven services coupled with excellent customer service. In continuation with Bank’s endeavor, Bank has been taking various initiatives for staff members in the form of training to make them conversant with the new technological services introduced in our bank. After the introduction of Mobile Banking Service & Platinum Rupay Debit Card, there is a steady rise in the number of customers availing these services. As on 31st August, 2018 more than 1, 50,000 customers have registered for Mobile Banking service of our Bank. The volume of transactions through Mobile banking service is also increasing.

In order to boost the use of the Mobile Banking service & Rupay Debit Card among the customers, it is equally important to imbibe the same among our staff members. With this vision, Board in it’s meeting held on 28th August, 2018; has approved to launch 4th contest on Mobile Banking Service and Rupay Debit Card for Bank’s Staff Members, Branches and Departments to encourage use of Mobile Banking Service & RuPay Debit Card. The prime objective of rolling out this contest for the 4th occasion is to bring in participation from all staff members & to encourage continuous self learning which will ultimately boost the usage of mobile banking service & Rupay Debit Card service among customers.

Detailed guidelines of the contest are as under:

1.   The contest is open for Bank’s employees’ /Officers/ Executives working at Branches, Departments of Head Office, Regional Offices and Zonal Offices.

 

2.   Contest is also for recognizing branches and departments with maximum participation.

 

3.  The contest period will be from 10th September, 2018 to 9th November, 2018.

 

4.   Only primary mobile number & Rupay Debit card of the staff linked to STCC account number under the Bank’s Mobile Banking service and RuPay debit card will be considered for the contest.

 

5.   Staff members should not misuse the facility & should avoid doing redundant / repetitive transactions.

 

6.   Decision in respect to authenticity of a bonafide transaction will be at Management’s discretion.

Post completion of the contest period, Top 3 Staff members with maximum score out of 100 would be awarded 1st, 2nd, & 3rd prizes. If more than 2 staff qualify with the same marks result would be declared on lucky draw basis, Unsuccessful candidates in the lucky draw will be given participation prize of Rs. 500/-.

 

Prizes will be as follows:

Rs. 5,000/- (1st Prize)

Rs. 3,000/- (2nd Prize)

Rs. 2,000/- (3rd Prize)

 

Rs. 500/- each will be awarded to total 100 participants who have scored at least 60 marks in the contest.

 

If more than 100 staff members score 100 marks then the participation prize will be considered on lucky draw basis or on the basis of maximum marks scored.

 

Five branches and two departments with maximum participation from their staff members & also based on qualifying marks obtained by the staff would be awarded Prize & an appreciation letter signed by Managing Director.

Prizes will be as under:

2nd Prize Rs. 3,000/-                         2nd Prize Rs. 3,000/-

3rd Prize Rs. 2,000/-

4th & 5th Prize Rs. 1,000/- each

        If more than 5 branches / 2 departments qualify with the same percentage of participating staff members then the marks obtained by the staff of those branches / department would be considered for the contest results.

 

          Please bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all the staff members of your Branch / Department and ensure full and active participation. All staff / official should be advised to make special efforts during the contest period & guide the customers to popularize the use of our Bank’s RuPay Debit card & Mobile Banking Services.
