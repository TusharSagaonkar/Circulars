Office Order No. 509 dated 5th December, 2018

1)    1% Additional Interest for Staff

2)    Benefit to extend to

i)     Employees Retired upon Normal Superannuation

    and

ii)   Employees who have Resigned after putting in 20 Years Service without Blemish

_____________________________________________________________________

 

I.   Please refer Office Order No. 503 dated 30th August, 2018.

 

II.  It has been pointed out that the preamble of Para II & its sub-Para 1) of the said circular do not mention Retired Employees specifically as being eligible for additional interest at 1% whereas its sub-Para 2) iii) indicates that retired employees are eligible. In order to remove any doubt, it is being explicitly mentioned hereby that the retired employees (i.e. those retired upon normal superannuation) shall be eligible for additional interest at 1% subject, of course, to other conditions applicable for staff members as indicated in the circular.

 

III.   1. Subsequent to the circular under reference, it was considered necessary to extend the benefit of additional interest to the staff members who have resigned from Bank’s service before normal superannuation. A submission in this behalf was approved in the Board Meeting dated 22nd November, 2018. Accordingly, it is being advised hereby that staff members who have resigned from the bank after putting in 20 years of service (including the service in the banks which merged into ours) & have no blemish in their service record shall be eligible for benefit of additional interest at 1%.

 

2. Before extending the benefit of additional interest to the employees who have resigned, branches will seek clearance from the HRM Department that both the conditions i.e. completion of 20 years’ service & unblemished record are fulfilled.

 

IV.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
