Office Order No. 504 dated 30th August, 2018

Mobilisation of CASA Deposit & Customer Meet at the Time of Branch Anniversary

____________________________________________________________________

 

I.   The practice of celebrating Branch Anniversary along with the holding of Customer Meet at branches had been discontinued since 2015. It has now been decided to revive this celebration as an opportunity to hold Customer Meets by inviting the Depositors, Loanees and Shareholders residing in the vicinity of the branch. This way of celebration of Branch Anniversary will give us an opportunity to interact with our Depositors, Customers and come closer with them and help us in improving customer service. This will go a long way in building the image of the Bank resulting ultimately in the growth of the Bank.

 

II.  Mobilisation of CASA Deposits

         Our Bank has enjoyed the advantage of having substantial low cost deposits in the form of Savings Bank / Current Deposit Accounts which was the real strength of our Bank supporting our Bank's Profit and profitability. But over the period the ratio of demand deposits to time deposits has fallen down considerably and we have witnessed that our CASA Deposits as on 31.03.2018 stood @ 38.81% as against the time deposit of 61.19%. This situation is a cause of concern.

 

III.   In order to mobilize CASA Deposits it has now been decided that prior to celebration of Branch Anniversary, a Fortnight Campaign for mobilization of CASA Deposits at each Branch will be organized by the Branch In-charge and each Branch will have to achieve the targets for CASA Deposits as mentioned below. For this purpose Branch In-charge will form various teams at the Branch and every staff member will be given fixed responsibility for bringing the CASA Deposit Accounts. Every Branch should achieve the target fixed for the branch atleast one day before the date of Branch Anniversary.

IV. Customer Meet

1)    While celebrating Branch Anniversary only one official from zonal office DGM/AGM will attend the Customer Meet for interaction with customers of the branch.

2)    On the day of celebration, entry-gate of each branch should be decorated with flowers/garlands. Bank’s name board should be electrically illuminated. However, hanging of festoons or display of such other decorative material internally should be avoided.

3)    Sweets should be offered to all the customers visiting branches.

4)    Telephone all valued customers and extend them personal invitation.

5)    Inform the Zonal Office and members of the Board well in advance.

6)    Seating arrangement should be made properly.

7)    Make necessary arrangements to serve drinking water and tea & snacks.

8)    The premises should be kept neat, clean and well decorated on the day of celebration, so that it should look pleasant and rejoicing.

9)    All the members of the staff should be well dressed.

10)Entire Staff Members should work as volunteers and allotment of duties should be made in advance.

11)It should also be ensured that the expenses are kept to the minimum.

 

V. Minutes of the Customer Meet should be sent to Zonal Office and Head Office within 10 days of the Branch Anniversary.

 

VI.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department.

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
