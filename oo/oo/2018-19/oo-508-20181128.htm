Office Order No. 508 dated 28th November, 2018

Allocation of duties among the Chief General Manager and General Managers

 

 

I. This has a reference to the Office Order No. 494 dated 12th May, 2017 in respect of allocation of duties among the Chief General Manager and General Managers. The allocation of duties mentioned therein were subsequently modified partly in terms of Office Order No. 502 dated 28th August, 2018 whereby duties in respect of recoveries were taken away from one of the General Managers & entrusted to DGM (Recovery) exclusively (apart from making some rearrangement of functions relating to Legal & Recovery among DGMs).

 

II.  With the recent creation of a new post of General Manger, the duties of the GMs are being reallocated as under. While the duties of the CGM remain unaltered, the instant Office Order includes them also. Thus, the instant Office Order which will be effective from 1st December, 2018 gives updated allocation of duties among the CGM & GMs.

                                    

1. Decisions taken by the Chief General Manager and the General Managers; taken within their respective delegated powers and exercised in respect of cases, matters & issues put up by ZOs & Branches and various Departments under their control within the frame work of the criteria, rules and regulations laid down by the Bank; will be final, and therefore, there is no need to refer such matters to the Managing Director. However, if decisions involve some deviations from the laid down rules & regulations, such matters will have to be referred to the Managing Director with suitable recommendations to seek approval of the latter. The Managing Director may decide in such matters, if necessary by putting up the same in the Board meeting for seeking approval.

 

2. Allotment of Zones among the Chief General Manager & General Managers for Overall Performance and Facilitating Decisions in Some Areas:-

 

i.    Apart from performing the duties mentioned in the Annexure and taking care of supervision and monitoring of the various Departments allotted to them, the Chief General Manager & General Managers (except the GM-IT) have been allotted zones as under so as to bring about overall development of business, effect recoveries in NPAs and facilitate decision-making in indicated matters in the respective zones.

ii.     Generally, the Chief General Manager & General Managers are expected to undertake tasks as under in the respective zones.

a.  Visit every branch under their control once in three months.

b. Visit NPA Borrowal Accounts whenever and wherever it is necessary, monitor them and make recoveries.

c.  Supervise and monitor performance and achievements of each branch.

d. Monitor recovery drive in a concerted way from time to time.

e.  Ensure that all Branches achieve their monthly target of CASA & other Deposits, Advances, Foreign Exchange, LIC/General Insurance Business, Demat/PAN Card, NPA reduction etc.

 

iii.   Specifically, the Chief General Manager & General Managers are expected to focus their attention on the following in the respective zones.

a.  Retail loans

b. CASA deposit mobilization

c.  Recovery in NPA accounts

d. Use of delegation of powers.

 

iv.   The executives named above are further vested with powers as under to take decisions in matters pertaining to respective zones.

a.  Waiver of service charges etc.

b. Special requests (through the DGM/Branch Incharge concerned)

 

III.    The executives are further advised to submit a monthly review report before 20th of the succeeding month in respect of the branches allocated to them, detailing therein strategies previously planned for improving business/conducting activities, specific steps taken for their implementation, suggestions for further progress in this direction and strategies for the subsequent month.

IV.  All the staff concerned are advised to take note of the revised allocation of duties among the General Managers and act accordingly.
