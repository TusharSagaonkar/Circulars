Office Order No. 506 dated 26th October, 2018

1.  Revision in Delegation of Powers to Sanction Expenditure

2.  Revision in Financial Powers for Settlement of Balances in the Accounts of Deceased Customers

3.  Reporting of Exercise of Power by Heads of Department

 

 

I.1. The earlier Office Order No. 501 dated 16th August, 2018 on the captioned subject necessitated some changes for the following reasons :

 

i)       To include revised powers to various functionaries & executives in respect of Disposal of Balances of deceased customers in the absence of nomination/subsisting nomination of legal representation (i.e. succession certificate, Letter of Administration or Probate of a will).

ii)     To incorporate some powers for which there was approval but the same were not previously formally circularised.

iii)   To include powers delegated to AGM – IT in terms of Office Order No. 475 dated 30th May, 2016.

iv)    To vest the AGM (Accounts) with powers to pass/release payments without limit.

v)     To provide for powers in respect of the newly created position of DGM – IT.

vi)    To clarify that the existing structures continue for releasing payment by a junior authority in respect of expenditures approved under the delegated powers by a senior authority.

          2. The instant circular with two revised Annexures is issued in supersession of the above-mentioned earlier Office Order No. 501 dated 16th August, 2018. All the branches and the staff concerned will henceforth refer to the instant circular and not to any of the earlier circulars.

 

II.  It was considered necessary to revise & consolidate the non-credit & non-recovery related financial powers vested in & exercised by various functionaries in the bank across the hierarchy. In this behalf, particulars of submissions and sources of authority are given hereunder.

 

1. The submission in respect of powers regarding disposal of balances of deceased customers in the absence of nomination/subsisting nomination/legal representation (i.e. Succession Certificate, Letter of Administration or Probate of a Will) was approved in the Board meeting dated 18th September, 2017. The powers mentioned therein were further revised in terms of the submission dated 10th October, 2018 approved in the Board Meeting dated 12th October, 2018.

 

2. The submission in respect of enhancement of designation-wise powers exercised by all the executive officers, other than Dy. Gen. Manager (Legal & Recovery) & Dy. Gen. Manager (Central Law Dept.), regarding revenue expenditure was approved in the Board meeting dated 26th June, 2018.

 

3. The submission in respect of rationalisation of powers of General Managers for expenditure on advertisements, & bringing parity between powers for revenue expenditure of Dy. Gen. Managers (Central, Western and Navi Mumbai Zones) with those of Dy. Gen. Manager, Pune Zone, was approved in the Board Meeting dated 14th August, 2018.

 

4. The powers currently exercised by Dy. Gen. Manager (Central Law Dept.), which are as per the Office Order No. 476 dated 30th May, 2016 are to remain unchanged. However, the current practice of Dy. Gen. Manager (Legal & Recovery) exercising powers of Dy. Gen. Manager (Central Law Dept.) to settle claims in respect of deceased customers, in the absence of the former, was formally ratified in the Board meeting dated 14th August, 2018.

 

5. In terms of submission dated 20th July, 2016 by AGM - Legal & Recovery Department, some more powers were proposed in respect of expenditure required to be incurred during recovery process (namely Security Agencies’ Bills, Bills for various notices etc. placed as advertisements in newspapers, vehicle repair expenses and charges paid to various professionals).The same was approved in the Board meeting dated 29th July, 2016 and powers thereunder are currently being exercised. However, information regarding the same was not disseminated through an Office Order. The same is covered herein.

 

6. In terms of the Office Order No. 475 dated 30th May, 2016, AGM-IT was vested with powers to sanction capital as well as revenue expenditure. They are incorporated herein.

 

7. The position of DGM (IT) has recently been created. Considering the importance of procurement of computers, machinery, furniture etc. for building & maintaining IT infrastructure, adequate powers of capital & revenue expenditure have been vested in DGM-IT in terms of submission approved in the Board Meeting dated 12th October, 2018.

 

8. There is a separate Legal & Recovery Department attached to the Pune Zonal Office. It was considered necessary to vest the DGM, Pune Zone with powers to sanction legal expenses where schedule of fees for Panel Advocates is approved by the Board. A submission in this behalf was approved in the Board Meeting dated 12th October, 2018.

 

9. In order to provide for smooth release of payments/passing of bills in respect of which delegated powers to incur expenditure are already exercised. AGM (accounts) has been vested with powers to release payments without any limit in terms of submission dated 10th October, 2018 approved in the Board Meeting dated 12th October, 2018.

 

 

III.   1. Further, there is currently a practice of reporting to the Board, for noting, exercise of powers by executives in respect of Sanctioning & Releasing Expenditure. However, there is no practice of reporting Approval of Deceased Claims in the absence of nomination/subsisting nomination/legal representation (which means Succession Certificate, Letter of Administration/Probate of a Will). It was considered necessary to bring exercise of delegation in respect of Deceased Claims under reporting.

 

2. There being no uniformity in the system of whatever reporting is currently done, it was also considered necessary to standardise reporting of exercise of delegation of/ sanctioning of all non-credit & non-recovery related financial powers to the Board for noting.

 

3. Once a non-credit-non-recovery financial decision is taken, release of payment against such a decision is made typically at a junior level. Instructions in respect of release of payments sanctioned by various superior authorities are in place and respective junior officers and executives are aware of them through various circulars, office orders and approved notes. The said instructions continue to remain in place. Thus, the payments under valid financial decisions will continue to be made by respective junior authorities which have been performing the function hitherto. In as much as such acts of release of payments do not involve exercise of discretion, they are not subject to reporting, and therefore should not be included in Annexure II which is an officer-wise/executive-wise Statement of Delegation of Powers Exercised.

 

IV. 1. It has therefore been decided, as mentioned at the outset, to consolidate all the extant instructions on the subject of sanctioning non-credit & non-recovery expenditures & outgo, in supersession of

                         i)    Office Order No. 421 dated 30th July, 2013,

                       ii)    Office Order No. 475 dated 30th May, 2016,

                     iii)    Office Order No. 476 dated 30th May, 2016 &

                      iv)    Office Order No. 501 dated 16th August, 2018.

 

2. The detailed chart incorporating the powers, revised as well as retained as also newly provided for, and applicable as on the date of the instant Office Order are given in the Annexure I hereto. Henceforth, reference in respect of all the powers for sanctioning non-credit & non-recovery expenditures will be made to this Annexure I & no other source.

 

3. There were instructions on record in respect of some functionaries for limits on party-related & expense-head related specific powers. It is made abundantly clear that all such party-related & expense-head related specific powers, except for expenditures incurred by DGM (Legal & Recovery) necessary during recovery process, have been removed and the functionaries will exercise the powers upto the ceiling mentioned under the column & row applicable of the chart in Annexure I. Needless to add that expenditure on advertisements will be as indicated separately. Further, it may be noted that powers in respect of capital expenditure are indicated separately.

 

V. Proper record is to be maintained by all the executives & officers for exercising the delegated powers. The powers being exercised at the branch level are reported by them to their respective Assistant General Manager/Deputy General Manager & this system will continue as hitherto. The executives exercising their respective powers at Regional Offices/Zonal Offices as also executives at HO level shall submit the monthly report of revenue expenditure & non-credit & non-recovery financial powers in the format provided in Annexure II attached herewith, including therein a one line entry each pertaining to (1) the no. of items & the aggregate expenditure sanctioned, and (2) no. of deceased claims in the absence of nomination/subsisting nomination/legal representation and amount involved therein settled, by branches under their control during the same month at the bottom of their respective report to be placed for noting in the Board Meeting of the next month. All the monthly reports in Annexure II will be sent to the Secretarial Department for consolidation & presenting in the Board meeting.

 

VI.   It is being reiterated that only instances of exercise of delegated powers/ sanctioning powers need to be reported. Mere releasing payment of bills, delegation in respect of which is already exercised by a superior authority, should not be included therein; as such instances, rightfully, should & must belong to the report of such respective superior authority which exercised the powers to approve/sanction the financial outgo.

 

VII. Executives and Officers are advised to exercise the delegated powers judiciously, scrupulously and in conformity with the instructions & guidelines issued vide office orders, as also otherwise, from time to time. With a view to monitoring controllable expenditure, the utility bills which are found to be abnormally high than the normal trend shall be reported to Development dept. for clearance. Similarly, bills regarding Govt. dues including property taxes which are abnormally higher than the previous trend should also be referred to development dept. for clearance. Needless to add that such payments against utility bills & Govt. taxes/property taxes etc., if & when made, should be reported under Annexure - II by branch in-charges/departmental officers under whose powers they rightfully fall.

 

VIII.  Inspection Department is advised to verify & check all the bills approved/ sanctioned &/or delegation used for payment of revenue expenditure including petty cash, advocates’/counsels’/solicitors’ fees, settlement of claims to balances in the accounts of deceased customers in the absence of nomination/subsisting nomination/legal representation etc. Release of payment of approved/sanctioned bills involving revenue expenditure & petty cash expenses should also be subjected to audit/inspection on quarterly basis by either assigning the job to concurrent auditor or through Inspection Dept.

 

IX.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
