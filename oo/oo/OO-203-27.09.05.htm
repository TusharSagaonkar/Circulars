Office Order No. 203 dated 27 September 2005.

 

Exercise of the delegated powers by the Executives & Officers

Nearing retirement- Precaution to be observed.

 

 

            One of the recommendations made by Reserve Bank’s High Level Committee on frauds and malpractices in bank was that “the sanctioning powers, six months prior to the retirement of any official should be exercised by him jointly with the next higher authority”.  Reserve Bank of India have accepted this recommendation but taking into consideration the various factors and practical difficulties involved in implementing the Committee’s recommendations have advised certain procedure to be observed by the urban co-operative banks that have delegated loan sanctioning powers to their officers.  Since, the executives and officers of our bank are delegated such powers, it has been decided that the under noted procedure, on the lines suggested by RBI, should be followed for monitoring the exercise of loan sanctioning powers by the officers of the bank at various levels from the Branch Manager to the Managing Director:

 

i)          The Personnel Dept. should furnish atleast seven calendar          months in advance of the date of actual retirement from the bank’s service, the names of the Branch Managers who are due          for retirement at the end of the seventh month, on attainment of superannuation age to the concerned Zonal Office as also to the Head Office viz.  Loans & Advances Dept., Inspection Dept. and the Vigilance Officer.  Such intimation of the names should be given in  the quarter preceding the six months at the end of which the officer would retire from the bank’s service.  The intimation should contain specific request to keep a special watch on the exercise of loan sanctioning powers by the concerned Branch Manager/s.

 

            ii)         In the three months immediately preceding the date of their retirement, the concerned Branch Managers would be advised by the Personnel Dept. that the exercise of his/her delegated loan sanctioning powers would be subject to the restriction that sanction advice should not be issued and disbursement should not be made without obtaining ratification of the sanction from             the DGM of the Zone.  A copy of the communication sent to the branch in charge should also be endorsed to the concerned Zonal Office and the Inspection Dept. for necessary action at their ends.

 

            iii)        Internal inspectors should carry out scrutiny of all such loans (100%) sanctioned by the branch in-charge during the last three months of his service and report their findings of adverse features, if any, to the Vigilance Officer and Personnel Dept. or they should furnish specific “No adverse features observed in exercise of delegated loan sanctioning powers” report to the Personnel Dept/Vigilance Officer. The Personnel Dept. should process payment of superannuation dues of the Officer after       taking into account the findings and observations of the inspection/vigilance report.

 

            iv)        The DGMs/Addl. GM/GM and Managing Director whoever is due for retirement should not exercise his loan sanctioning power             singly during the period of three months immediately preceding the date of his retirement.  The DGMs should exercise the powers jointly with Addl. GM/GM and the Addl. GM/GM should exercise

it jointly with the Managing Director.  The exercise of his powers          by the Managing Director, during such period should be placed before the Board Meeting immediately following the date (or time) of sanction for ratification.  The Managing Director may cause an audit, through a suitable officer,  Concurrent Auditor, of the sanctions given by Dy. Gen. Managers/ Addl. Gen. Manager/ General Manager during the last quarter of their service before their superannuation benefits are passed/ paid to such retiring officer.  As for the sanctions by the Managing Director in the last quarter of his service the Board may cause such scrutiny of the sanctions through the Concurrent Auditors.

 

2.         The above procedure becomes operative with immediate effect.

 

 

                                                                                                M. C. Shetty.

                                                                                         Managing Director.
