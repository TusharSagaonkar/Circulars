Office Order No. 297  dated 10th September, 2009

Loans for purchase of new Taxies

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          You may be aware that as a measure of pollution control, Government of Maharashtra has banned plying of old vehicles which are completing 25 years from the date of their registration. Resultantly, the taxi owners/permit holders would approach various Banks for loans for purchase of new taxis. Most of these persons whose taxies are at present out of road or shortly will go out of road and also other new self employed persons / permit holders would be approaching our Bank for getting finance for purchase of New Taxies. The advances to transport operators fall under priority sector advances, as such Bank should grab this opportunity and make finance for purchase of new taxies.

 

          The procedure to be followed for sanction and disbursement of loan is as   under :-

1.       At the time of acceptance of the Application for Taxi Loan, the Branch will obtain Saving Bank A/c Opening Form and Membership Form. The Branch will also obtain documents for compliance of KYC Norms like copies of Proof of Residence, PAN Card, Driving Licence, Letter from the Taxi Union, 2 Photographs, etc. After verifying the documents with originals Saving Bank Account will be opened at the Branch.

2.       Taxi Loan Application will be processed in accordance with normal procedure with obtaining CIBIL Report & visit to the residence of the Applicant.

3.       Loan can be sanctioned in the group of 2 taxi owners i.e. with cross sureties.

4.       It should be ensured that the Applicant should have residential accommodation, in his own name or in the name of the family members, may be it is on rental/pagadi basis in chawls, or on ownership basis. In case the residence is on the collector's land, photo pass in their names is required to be obtained.

5.       Permit should be in the name of the Applicant. In case of widow licence, the person who drives the Taxi shall be made Co-Borrower.

6.       Declaration of income may be obtained on a plain paper as per Annexure-A if loanees do not have latest Income Tax Returns. Declaration of Income is taken from the loanee to complete one of the formalities. The visit by the Bank Official to the residence of the Applicant is a must. During the visit to the residence, the visiting official has to make discreet enquiries about the number of dependants in the family, earning of the Applicant and his family member(s), etc.

7.       Majority of the Taxi Owners / Permit Holders belong to Lower Middle Class / Poor Class People of the Society and it is hardly possible for them to make arrangement of 20% margin money. We have, therefore, as a very special case, reduced margin amount from 20% to 10%. The loan amount will be 90% of the On Road Price quoted by the Company/Dealer. The price includes Show Room Price, Painting, CNG Kit, CNG Cylinder, Carrier, Front Guard, Meter, one time tax and Registration Charges included in the Company’s/Dealer's Invoice.

8.       Branch will inform the Applicant to deposit the margin money with the Bank, which will be credited to applicant's Saving Bank Account.

9.       Branch will sanction amount of loan to various models as per their cost/price.

10.    After sanctioning of the loan, the disbursement will be made to the dealer in full by debit to Loan Account and Saving Bank Account of the applicant for margin money and the receipt thereof will be recorded alongwith the other documents at the Branch in the respective Loan File/Security File.

11.    Sanctioning Branch shall issue Pay Order in favour of Authorised Dealer and should be delivered by our staff to the Dealer advising them that hypothecation charge should be registered with RTO Authorities where Bank and Branch Name should be mentioned.  At the same time Branch should ensure that the documents in regard to Insurance of the Vehicle and Tax Paid Receipt, etc. are duly received and kept in Loan File / Security File.  

12.    Branch shall ensure that the Hypothecation Charge is recorded in the R.C. Book (Smart Card) and a copy of the same shall be obtained and kept in Security File.

13.    Vehicle shall be inspected by the Branch In-charge within one month from the date of delivery of the vehicle or issuing date of Pay Order whichever is earlier. A copy of the vehicle Inspection Report shall be kept in Loan File/Security File.

14.    The Branches shall monitor these loan accounts regularly and ensure that there will not be any overdue in the accounts.  

 

15.    All other instructions in vogue issued from time to time will remain unchanged.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at the Branch and ensure strict compliance.
