Office Order No. 534 dated 2nd August 2021

Designation of Shri. Sunil Sadvilkar (Staff No. 1141), Manager as

Compliance Officer for Foreign Exchange Business

Role, Duties and Responsibilities

__________________________________________________________________________________________

 

I.  Please refer Office Order No. 518 dated 26th June 2019 on the subject of position of Compliance Officer for Foreign Exchange Business and designation of its first incumbent.

 

II.   Consequent upon change of assignment of the incumbent, it was decided in the Board meeting dated 31st May 2021 to designate Shri. Sunil Sadvilkar (Staff No. 1141), Manager, as Compliance Officer for Foreign Exchange Business. Shri. Sadvilkar has assumed the role as Compliance Officer for Foreign Exchange Business with effect from 1st June 2021.

 

III. Role of the Compliance Officer for Foreign Exchange Business requires that official occupying the position is not involved or to be involved directly in FOREX operations.

 

IV. Role of the Compliance Officer for Foreign Exchange Business includes, but is not limited to, the following.

1.   Keeping abreast of

i.     all the information & literature on Forex compliances issued/supplied by Govt. (FEMA), RBI, FEDAI, USA Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act (FATCA), OECD [(Common Reporting Standard (CRS)], Habib American Bank (New York) and other overseas correspondent banks,

ii.   provisions regarding Prevention of Money Laundering (PML) and Know Your Customer (KYC), and

iii.  standards of customer service prescribed by RBI and commitments under Bank’s own Citizen Charter.

2.   Identifying and mitigating Trade Based Money Laundering Risk.

3.   Highlighting

i.     Politically Exposed Persons (PEP) associated with related parties, and

ii.   negative news regarding parties such as exporters, shipping companies etc.

 

V.  Duties and responsibilities of the Compliance Officer for Foreign Exchange Business include, but are not limited to, the following.

i.     Periodical uploading and updating, with the help of KYC/AML dept., of Regulatory Negative Lists such as UN list, OFAC list, SDN list and Internal list. (These shall be uploaded manually until such time automatic uploads are facilitated through a software under procurement. Automatic uploads shall be monitored by compliance officer after the same are put in place).

ii.   Introduction of controls to mitigate manual entry errors.

iii.  Correct and timely reporting as per regulatory requirements.

iv.  Recommending snap audit of Forex/MTSS transactions and NRE/NRO accounts of ‘C’ category branches.

v.   co-ordination with KYC/AML dept on Forex compliance and follow up action for resolution of related issues observed during compliance monitoring.

vi.  Ensuring compliance of

a.   All the guidelines/directions related to foreign exchange transactions issued by Reserve Bank of India and FEDAI under FEMA, A.D. circulars etc.

b.   Various provisions of Forex Trade Policy pertaining to Export & Import transactions.

c.    Instructions received from Habib American Bank (New York) or any other overseas correspondent bank with regard to not handling any transactions/ not having any dealings with certain black listed banks informed by them from time to time.

 

VI. The Compliance Officer for Foreign Exchange Business may be assigned any additional work as may be decided by Managing Director from time to time.

 

VII.  Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
