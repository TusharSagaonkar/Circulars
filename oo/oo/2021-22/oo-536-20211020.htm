Office Order No. 536 dated 20th October 2021

House Keeping

1)   Irregularities Observed During Inspections, Audits and Vigilance Action

2)   Resurfacing again after compliance

3)   Warning of Strict Administrative Action

4)   List of Common Irregularities

 

_____________________________________________________________________________________

 

I.  Background

1.   Internal departments of Bank handling inspection, audit and vigilance functions have been reporting over some time that operating staff in branches do not follow the systems and procedures laid down by Bank although exhaustive and elaborate instructions regarding them have been issued through various circulars, policy documents and IDCs.

2.  Visiting inspectors and auditors, whether internal or external, typically point out to the operating staff the irregularities which they observe during inspections and audits. The irregularities so pointed out are rectified by branches and compliance is given in respect thereof. However, during subsequent audits and inspections, the same irregularities resurface. This proves that adherence to the systems and procedures is limited only for the purpose of immediate and temporary compliance. Systems and procedures do not become integral to the work-culture of branches.

3.   Relapse of branch-functioning, after a few days of compliance, into widespread breach of systems and procedures is more prominent when the clerk and/or official handling the subject work is changed. This strengthens the view that systems and procedures are not internalised in the work culture of branches.

4.  Reserve Bank of India has noticed the frequent relapse of Bank’s systems and procedures into dysfunctionality and viewed it with disfavour. It makes the matter more serious for Bank.

5.  i. The list of most common irregularities in the area of Housekeeping which are frequently observed by inspection and audit teams across branches are listed below.

ii. Continuing adherence thereto must be ensured at all the times without exception. It is reiterated that mere one-time compliance will not be tolerated.

iii. A separate but similar Office Order No. 537 dated 20th October 2021 on the subject of Irregularities Observed During Inspections, Audits and Vigilance Action in the area of Loans and Advances is being issued simultaneously.

6.   Every Branch-In-Charge is instructed to hold a staff meeting and organise a loud reading of each of the items in the list. Implications of non-observance shall be discussed threadbare and shall be brought home to all the staff-members concerned.

7.  Bank has decided that, henceforth, breach of any system or procedure shall be sternly dealt with. It must be noted that not only will the staff-member concerned be held accountable and put under the procedure for breach of discipline; Branch-In-Charge shall also be held equally accountable for not implementing the systems and procedures on a sustained and lasting basis and shall be put under the process for breach of discipline.

II.List of common irregularities in the area of housekeeping observed during Inspections, Audits and Vigilance Action

1.     Registers such as Modification register, Duplicate passbook register, TDCLG/ LNCLG register, Cheque book issue register

i.     They are not properly maintained.

ii.   If maintained, some essential details are not recorded. Columns are left blank.

iii.  Customers signatures are not obtained.

iv.  Signatures of dealing clerks and branch officials are not recorded under Name & Staff No. stamp with date.

2.       Mandatory documents such as those for KYC, wherever required, are not obtained from customers.

3.       Postage register

i.     Postage is not tallied on monthly basis.

ii.   It is not maintained in a proper format i. e. there is incomplete information.

iii.  In some branches, sub-staff is handling the postage register.

4.       Gold packets

i.     In some branches, sub-staff is independently sealing gold packets.

ii.   Gold packets/ articles are kept in the custody of clerk for a long time before sealing the same.

iii.  Officer is not signing on the Yellow Tag / White Tag of the Gold Packet.

iv.  In Gold packets released register, column of closure date, date of release & signature of officer/ Joint custodian are kept blank. Signatures of officials/joint custodians should invariably be under the rubber stamp of staff name and number.

5.     Conversion of Minor’s account to that of a Major

If the minor had no PAN Card while opening account, details of Form 60 are not sent to Accounts Dept.

6.       Issue of cheque book to a new customer

i.         Cheque book is issued before receiving address confirmation letter.

ii.       When the place of residence is on rental basis, the place is to be visited and got confirmed before issue of cheque book. This is not done.

7.       Converting dormant account into operative

i.     Photocopies of KYC documents & signature of customers are not verified.

ii.   Asst. Manager is not recording remarks under signature and stamp (of Name and Staff No.) in KYC application form.

iii.  Entry of KYC updation is not made in Modification Register.

iv.  Filing system is not proper. Whenever the modification application along with KYC is asked by the auditors for verification, the same is not produced by the branch, due to improper filing.

v.   Branches are not monitoring transactions in those accounts status of which is converted from Dormant to Normal. Branches should monitor transactions in such accounts for at least the subsequent six months.

8.       Transfer entries are made without obtaining proper mandate / Instrument from the customer. It should be noted that this is permissible only when right of set off is to be exercised.

9.       It is also noticed that many branches are doing the transfer entry in TR-INT instead of TR batch.

10.   ATM cards are issued to joint holders where mode of operation is ‘jointly’ or ‘any two’ or ‘first & second compulsory’.

11.   Charges as per latest Service Charges Manual are not collected at the time of providing services such as those indicated in bracket hereinafter (cheque book issue charges, mortgageability charges, TOD charges, duplicate passbook charges, LIC assignment/reassignment charges, cash deposit charges, preliminary sanction letter for housing loan, revalidation charges, notice charges, loan application charges etc.)

12.   Guidelines as per Circular OC No. 838 dated 14th October 2020 are not adhered regarding Nomination facility on Deposit Accounts and Safe Deposit Lockers.

13.   Guidelines as per Circular OC No. 834 dated 21st May 2020 regarding letters to Un-operative Locker Holders for KYC updation are not adhered to.

14.   In case of HUF accounts, new HUF declaration is not obtained where minor co-parceners become major.

15.    1% additional interest rate is given on Fixed Deposit of relative of staff even when the account is single or, in case of account jointly with staff-member, the name of the relative is first.

16.   In case of a newly opened current account, the place of business/residence of account holder is not visited as required as per OC No. 836 dated 1st September 2020.

17.   Cheque Books are issued by sub staff. Stock of cheque books is in the custody of sub staff.

18.   Reconciliation of failed ATM transactions is not done in time.

19.   Many branches are not following the guidelines as per Circular OC No. 841 dated 24th November 2020 regarding action to be taken in respect of high percentage of CRC/NACH returns.

20.   Many times, several boxes/rows of visit report in respect of a new CD account holder (as per the new format given in Circular OC No. 836 dated 1st September 2020) are kept blank.

21.   In some branches, accounts of “Proposed Housing Societies” are still continuing with same status over two to three years. Follow up in respect of the same is not done.

22.   In case of a customer applying for cheque book through mobile banking, requisition slip is not collected at the time of handing over the cheque book.

23.   In most of the branches, whenever a customer approaches for premature closure of FDR, approval of Branch-In-Charge is not obtained.

24.   Many columns of Form 15G & 15H are kept blank; signature of Branch-In-charge is not taken.

25.   Locker Operations

i.     On many occasions, signature of Asstt. Manager is not found in the Locker Attendance Visit Register.

ii.   Most of the Locker Key Deposit Receipts are not discharged by the account holder.

iii.  Further, branches are frequently found to be not making entry in OMNI at the time of locker operation.

26.   Some branches are not following the procedure advised through the Circular OC No. 749 dated 27th April 2016 regarding allowances paid to staff in excess of expenses incurred, which are taxable under Income Tax Act and are therefore to be considered under salary for the purpose of deduction of tax.

27.   Branches are not collecting Balance confirmation letter from the Housing societies as per FM/33 dated 31st July 2015.

28.   Branches need to generate and take printout of, for monitoring, daily report of transactions exceeding Rs. 10.00 lac as per Circular OC No. 731 dated 10th December 2015 along with GL & PL daily print out. This is not being done.

29.   When the KYC/AML compliance Officer proceeds on leave or is transferred to another branch, recording particulars of leave or transfer of the official who was on duty and the name of official who has been provided either as a temporary substitute for the regular staff or in permanent replacement of the previous official, in a proper register, is not done as required under OO No. 443 dated 30th January 2015.

30.   Blank security forms such as cheque books and FDRs are not kept under lock and key.

31.   Payment from Petty Cash is being made without any approval of In-charge on Petty cash vouchers.

32.   Operations in staff accounts are not being monitored.

33.   There are many cases of Form 60 & 61 being obtained, but not filled up completely.

III.1. Under sub-Para 7 of Para I above, it has been stated that Branch-In-Charge shall also be held equally accountable for not implementing the systems and procedures on a sustained and lasting basis and shall be put under the process for breach of discipline.

2. In this context, each Deputy General Manager during his/her visit to any branch will call for compliance reports of the last Inspection/Audit/Vigilance Report and check whether compliances were done as a matter of permanent course-correction or merely as a one-time exercise soon forgotten thereafter. Instructions are being issued to call up explanation in the latter case immediately upon its coming to notice, followed by required administrative action.

IV.      Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department and ensure strict implementation.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
