Office Order No. 542 dated 25th January 2022

Setup of Centralized KYC & AML Dept. for Transaction Monitoring

through AML Software & Role of Branches

_________________________________________________________________________________

  I.   Background

1. i.  Our  Bank  has  decentralized  setup  for  Transaction  Monitoring  through  AML

software since September 2010 under which there are 4 levels for monitoring   Transactions Alerts (two at Branch Level and two at Head Office Level), including their closure or escalation. The two Levels at Branch are as under.

a.  Branch Money Laundering Reporting Officer (BR –MLRO), and,

b. Branch Head / Designated Manager at branch level.

ii. Under the present system, the functionaries at each level including the two at branch level are empowered to close an alert, if they are satisfied about genuineness of the transaction.

 

2.   Inspecting officials from RBI during their Inspection of our Bank for the FY 2019-20 considered the above position as inappropriate and opined that the activity of analysis, documentation and management of alerts could be delegated to functionaries in the AML Compliance Team operating centrally, with decision as to whether an alert is reportable or not being left to the office of the Principal Officer (PO).

 

3.   In compliance of the RBI Inspection report, it was decided in the Board Meeting held on 27th September 2021 to create a Centralised setup for Transaction Monitoring through AML software.

 

 II.   Implementation

In implementation of the above decision, it has been decided as under:

1.   A Centralised setup for transaction monitoring, called Centralised KYC/AML Dept., is being set up at Head Office (at Nerul) and Current KYC/AML Dept. will be merged into it.

2.   It will function with the aid of AML software. 

3.   It will be housed in Bank’s Nerul premises.

4.   Setup will be launch after the relative software goes live.

5.   The contact numbers of the new Centralised KYC/AML Dept. will be 08169922124 and 08169921271

6.   After the launch of the setup, the responsibility of closing alerts will shift from Branch to the Centralised KYC/AML Dept. The date of such shift will be advised shortly.

 

III.   Staffing and Organization of Centralised KYC/AML Dept.

The Centralised KYC/AML Dept will be headed by the Assistant General Manager who  is  also  designated  as  the  Principal  Officer  (PO)  in  Bank  under PMLA, 2002.

The composition of personnel in the Dept. will be as under.

1. Principal Officer (PO) - (AGM as mentioned above)

2. One Manager (KYC/AML)

3. Principal Officer (PO) and Manager (KYC/AML) will be assisted by:

A.  Core Team consisting of 1 Asst. Manager, 7 Officers and 1Clerk

B.  Transactions Monitoring Team consisting of 4 Asstt. Managers as “Reviewers” (1 Reviewer for each Zone) and 12 Officers as “Analysts” (3 Analysts to assist each “Reviewer”)

 

IV.   Parts of Bank’s KYC/AML/CFT Policy are being amended, and responsibilities and procedures are being redefined as under to fulfil the objective of setting up the ‘Centralised setup for Transaction Monitoring’.

 

 V.   Process and Flow of Transaction(s)(Alerts) :

1.  All alerts generated by AML software ( for the whole Bank ) after the day-end activity will get reflected, for the purpose of monitoring, in Centralised KYC/AML Dept.(Code– 039) instead of respective branches. All the alerts shall be attended to & closed by Centralised KYC/AML Dept., Nerul.

2.  The process of monitoring/escalation of transactions at Centralised KYC& AML Dept. shall be as under: -

i.     Officers (Analysts) assisting the Asstt. Manager (Reviewer) for a particular Zone shall scrutinize the transactions pertaining to branches in that Zone keeping in mind the perspective of profile of the customer, past trend of transactions, Line/Nature of activity / business etc. Depending upon his/her judgment, he/she will either close the alerts or escalate them to the Asst. Manager (Reviewer) concerned.

ii.   The Asst. Manager (Reviewer) shall scrutinize each transaction escalated to him keeping in mind the perspective of profile of the customer, past trend of transactions, Line/Nature of activity / business etc., and if necessary, shall refer the same to the branch concerned for Enhanced Due Diligence. After this exercise, if the transaction is found to be unsatisfactory, he/she will escalate the transaction to Manager, KYC/AML. He/she will be authorized to close the other transactions at his/her level.

iii.  The core team assisting the Manager, KYC/AML as well as Principal Officer (PO) will prepare the Ground of Suspicion (GOS) notes in respect of the alerts escalated to Manager, KYC/AML for filling STRs.

The core team will perform following duties: -

a.  Assist the Manager (Centralised KYC/AML Dept.) and the Principal Officer (PO) in filing all the Statutory Reports such as CTR, NTR, CBWTR, CCR to various regulators,

b. Provide support to branches for name screening, KYC related activities and,

c.  Carry out other miscellaneous day-to-day work such as Account Information to RBI, FIUIND, ED, Income Tax Authorities and various Law Enforcement Agencies.

 

iv.  a. Manager (KYC/AML), after a detailed scrutiny, shall decide in respect of each

alert referred to him, whether to close or escalate the same to PO with proper GOS for onward reporting to FIU-IND, New Delhi.

b.  He / She  will  escalate  such  alerts  to  Principal  Officer (PO)  as, in his/her

    opinion, require escalation. He will be authorised to close the other transactions at his level.

v.   Principal Officer (PO) shall take the final decision in respect alerts escalated to him/her by Manager (KYC/AML) to file the STRs or otherwise and, in cases considered reportable by him/her, shall file STRs within 7 days of Final Decision to FIU-IND, New Delhi.

vi.  On sample basis, there will be re-examination by Reviewer, Manager, Centralised KYC/AML Dept. and Principal Officer (PO), of alerts closed at levels respectively below them.

 

VI.   Roles & Responsibility of Staff / Officers posted at Centralised KYC/AML Dept. shall be as under:

1. Analyst (Officer) –

i.     He / she will scrutinize the alerts appearing on dash board for the purpose of monitoring transactions. He/she will analyze the transactions (Alerts) on following Parameters : -

a. Profile: – Profession / Occupation / Income / turnover etc. and Line/Nature of business activity of the customer.

b. Product:- Abhyudaya Small Savings Bank Account, Salaried Account, Student Account, Minor’s Account, Senior Citizen’s Accounts, Employee’s Zero Balance Account, PMJDY-SSB & PMJDY-BSB etc.

c. Account-vintage: - How old is the customer’s relationship with Bank.

ii.   After scrutiny, if the Analyst is satisfied about the genuineness of the transaction and does not consider it reportable, he/she will close the alert at his/her level with proper comments / remarks.

iii.  If an Analyst is not satisfied about the genuineness of the transaction, he/she will escalate the transaction(s) (Alerts) to the Reviewer (Asst. Manager) for further review.

iv.  He/she will ensure profile updation of customers by contacting respective branches.

v.   He/she will take print out of “Report of Add-Comment-Rejected” on daily basis for the Alerts commented by him.

 

2. Role of Reviewer (Asst. Manager)

a)   He/she will guide the Officers in the team for proper monitoring of transaction(s) (Alerts).

b)   If a transaction referred to him/her matches with the profile of the customer and the Reviewer is satisfied about the genuineness of the transaction and further does not consider it reportable, he/she will close the alert at his/her level.

c)   If a transaction does not match with profile of the customer but the customer is maintaining account with regular operations, Asst. Manager will raise queries through written communication to the branch concerned for confirmation of the transaction on points such as source of funds, utilization of funds etc. Upon receiving response, if the Reviewer is satisfied that the transaction is not reportable, he/she may close the alert at his/her end.

d)   If the branch concerned is not able to identify the source and/or utilization of funds on the written communication, such transaction(s) shall be escalated by Reviewer to the Manager, KYC/AML Team for further scrutiny and action.

e)   Reviewer will verify & check the alerts closed by Analyst on random basis under his/her charge and will endorse his/her confirmation on “Report of Add-Comment-Rejected” which will be generated on daily basis.

 

3. Role of Manager, Centralised KYC/AML Dept.(working with assistance from Core Team)-

a)   On receiving reference of any suspicious transaction(s)(Alert)from a Reviewer (Asst. Manager), the member of the Core Team shall re-verify the transaction(s) once again keeping in view the declared profile and line of activity of the customer. If the transaction(s) of the customer is/are found unsatisfactory, the team shall call for the Account Opening Form & latest KYC Documents from the Branch concerned and place all the details before Manager, Centralised KYC/AML Dept.

b)   Manager, Centralised KYC/AML Dept. will form an opinion to file an STR or otherwise in respect of each alert referred to him/her. He/she will close those alerts in respect of which he/she has formed an opinion not to file an STR.

c)   Manager, Centralised KYC/AML Dept. will choose alerts closed by each Reviewer on random basis and scrutinise them with the assistance of the core team. He will examine if the standards of scrutiny are correctly applied while deciding the closure and/or if there is anything unusual not justifying closure. Where there is a deviation from standards or some unusual matter not justifying closure, he will subject the matter to his/her independent examination. Such cases will be decided by him either for final closure or for   escalation to Principal Officer (PO).

d)   Manager, Centralised KYC/AML Dept. will place before Principal Officer (PO) the Transaction(s) Alert(s) identified by him/her as suspicious with recommendations for filing an STR. Towards this, he will select Ground of Suspicion (GOS) from among the following: -

·          Proceeds of crime

·          Complex Transaction

·          No economic rationale

·          Financing of Terrorism

 

4. Principal Officer (PO) - 

i.   Principal Officer (PO ) shall take final decision whether to file an STR or close the alert in respect of transactions placed before him by Manager, Centralised KYC/AML Dept. with recommendations to do so. If decision to file the same is taken, he/she will upload the STR to FIU-IND, New Delhi within 7 days from the date of conclusion and in case of rejection/closure of Alerts, he/she shall record reason for the same. 

ii.  Principal Officer (PO) will choose alerts closed by Manager (KYC/AML)  on random basis and scrutinise them with the assistance of the core team. He will examine if the standards of scrutiny are correctly applied while deciding the closure and/or if there is anything unusual not justifying closure. Where there is a deviation from standards or some unusual matter not justifying closure, he will subject the matter to his/her independent examination. Such cases will be decided by him either for final closure or for filing STR.

 

5. Role of Branches - Branches will have the responsibility to,

i.     continue the existing Name-screening procedures and ensure customer unification (BI-UNICUS), while on boarding new customers,

ii.    undertake periodical updation of all KYC due accounts,

iii.  update Customer Profile in the OMNI software in terms of details such as Profession/Organization type/Business type/Income/Annual Turnover and ensure that none of these fields remains blank in any case (as these fields constitute key elements and important parameters for attending to alerts centrally),

iv.  reply to the queries raised by KYC & AML Dept within 2 working days from the receipt of communication. The reply to the communication has to be sent through email on Exchange Server [amlalertsmonitoring] by Asst. Managers / Incharges of the Branch only , and,

v.    continue to monitor & forward the STRs in following offline Scenarios:

a. Matters of Law Enforcement Queries

b. Media Reports

c. Public Complaints

d. Complaints/problems by/of Business Associates

e. Employee Initiated

f.  Offline Red Flag Indicators ( As per Annexure I hereto)

VIII. 1. The personnel manning the three levels of Analyst, Reviewer, and Manager(KYC/AML) shall be responsible for, apart from transaction monitoring, their actions and decisions in respect of scrutiny, and closure or escalation.

 2. Apart from overall management control of the department, KYC and AML  compliance and  transaction monitoring, PO will be responsible for his/her actions and decisions in respect of closure of alerts  or filing of STRs which are not considered fit for closure.

 3. Members of the Core team will be responsible for, apart from transaction monitoring and general assistance to Manager (KYC/AML) and PO, scrutiny and recommendations made by them in respect of closures or STRs.

 4. Branches will be responsible for transaction monitoring at their level and for proactively providing inputs whenever occasion warrants as also by way of feedback when called for.

 

IX.   It is to be ensured that there is no tipping off to the customer at any level or time while doing Enhanced Due Diligence for confirmation of transactions.

 

 X.   Internal Auditors/Concurrent Auditors will carry out an independent evaluation of the controls, for identifying high value transactions. Concurrent/internal auditors will verify the compliance with KYC/AML policies and procedures. They will specifically scrutinize and comment on the observance of KYC norms and the steps taken towards prevention of Money Laundering by Branches.

 

More specifically, Inspection Dept. shall ensure verification of AML Alert(s) of transaction(s) of Rs. 5.00 Lakh and above, closed by the Centralised KYC/AML Dept., to certify that the decision of closure was correct.

 

XI.   All the branch In-charges are instructed to bring the contents of the instant Office Order to the notice of all the members of the Staff/Officials for strict compliance.
