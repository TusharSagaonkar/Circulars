Office Order No. 532 dated 23rd April 2021

Alternate Days Working at Branches/Departments/Offices

 

I. As you are aware the country is facing second wave of COVID-19 pandemic. It has caused a huge economic disruption and also led to a loss of many human lives. Governments of states including those in which our network of branches operates have been taking several measures for more than one year to bring the pandemic under control. Now with the second wave rearing its head with more virulence and force, Government of Maharashtra has come out with a set of updated directives dated 21st April 2021 titled “Break the chain”. The directives have been necessitated by an emergency to contain the pandemic.

 

II.   Consequence upon and in implementation of the said directives, following measures are being implemented in the Bank for the state of Maharashtra with immediate effect.

1)      All the double shift branches shall now work in single shift. Working by the Staff should be reorganised in the same manner as was advised in our earlier      Office Order No.526 dated 20th March 2020. Business hours of (Double Shift / Single Shift) branches shall be uniformly from 10:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.

2)      Administrative Offices and Branches will function at 50% staff strength. Thus, there will be two teams, each comprising half the regular strength, and these two teams shall work on alternate days. Working hours for the staff shall be from 10:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.

3)      ATMs will remain operative on 24X7X365 basis. Back-office services viz. Cash Loading in ATMs, Core Banking Solution functionality and its maintenance, functioning of Data Centre, Data/Disaster Recovery Centre, ATM Recon and all back office work pertaining to ATMs, Security Operation including Cyber Security, RTGS/NEFT Cell, Clearing department, Bank’s Treasury, Forex department and Swift Centre, and such other offices as are performing specialist functions shall continue to operate in the normal and regular manner.

4)      Staff strength on any working day should be kept at the bare minimum. The Branch In-charge should, at his discretion, assign duties to staff members on alternate days depending upon the strength at the branch while ensuring conduct of business.

5)      Alternate-day working model may not be resorted to if absenteeism is high and it becomes difficult to run the branch with half of the regular strength. In that event, staff members will have to work on all the working days.

6)      Work which can be done from home (such as recovery, follow up by contacting defaulters on phone, loan scrutiny, etc.) should be allotted to the staff members who stay at home on alternate working days. The Branch In-charge should review and monitor the work done by staff-members from home.

7)      Stationery Dept will work on alternate days. It must be ensured that stationery is supplied to branches and offices on regular basis and no branch is left unattended which may otherwise disrupt work.

8)      CCD Dept. – Staff members at CCD Dept may be divided into two groups except the staff members holding the cash keys. The In-charge of CCD Dept may allocate the work among staff members as per his discretion in view of exigency of work at the department.

9)      Security Guards will work on all the days. They will be paid allowance at Rs.4000/- per month.

10)   Discontinuation of 360 days’ banking facilities: The current 360 days’ banking facility at 9 select branches is being discontinued till further instructions.

11)   Keeping banking services closed on all Sundays: The system of some of our branches offering banking facility on Sundays is being discontinued. Resultantly, all of our branches shall remain closed on all Sundays.

 

III.  It is reiterated that the instructions as above come into force with immediate effect. They shall remain in force till further orders.

 

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
