Office Order No. 537 dated 20th October 2021

Loans and Advances

1)   Irregularities Observed During Inspections, Audits and Vigilance Action

2)   Resurfacing again after compliance

3)   Warning of Strict Administrative Action

4)   List of Common Irregularities

_____________________________________________________________________________________

 

I.  Background

1.       Internal departments of Bank handling inspection, audit and vigilance functions have been reporting over some time that operating staff in branches do not follow the rules, guidelines and procedures, in all areas including loans and advances, laid down by Bank, although exhaustive and elaborate instructions regarding them have been issued through various circulars, policy documents and IDCs.

2.       Visiting inspectors and auditors, whether internal or external, typically point out to the operating staff the irregularities which they observe during inspections and audits. The irregularities so pointed out are rectified by branches and compliance is given in respect thereof. However, during subsequent audits and inspections, the same irregularities resurface. This proves that adherence to the systems and procedures is limited only for the purpose of immediate and temporary compliance. Adherence to rules, guidelines and procedures has not become integral to the work-culture of branches.

3.       Relapse of branch-functioning, after a few days of compliance, into widespread breach of rules, guidelines and procedures is more prominent when the clerk and/or official handling the subject work is changed. This strengthens the view that they are not internalised in the work culture of branches.

4.       Reserve Bank of India has noticed the frequent relapse of Bank’s rules, guidelines and procedures into dysfunctionality and viewed it with disfavour. It makes the matter more serious for Bank.

5.       i. The list of most common irregularities in the area of Loans and

 Advances which are frequently observed by inspection and audit teams across branches are listed below so that they are removed once and for all, and do not recur thereafter.

ii. Continuing strict adherence to rules, guidelines and procedures must be ensured at all the times without exception. It is reiterated that mere one-time compliance thereof will not be tolerated.

iii. Similar but separate Office Order No. 536 dated 20th October 2021 on the subject of Irregularities Observed during Inspections, Audits and Vigilance Action in the area of Housekeeping is being issued simultaneously.

6.       Every Branch-In-Charge is instructed to hold a staff meeting and organise a loud reading of each of the items in the list. Implications of non-observance shall be discussed threadbare and shall be brought home to all the staff-members concerned.

7.       Bank has decided that, henceforth, breach of any rule, guideline or procedure shall be sternly dealt with. It must be noted that not only will the staff-member concerned be held accountable and put under the procedure for breach of discipline; Branch-In-Charge shall also be held equally accountable for not implementing the rules, guidelines and procedures on a sustained and lasting basis and shall be put under the process for breach of discipline.

 

II.  List of common irregularities in the area of Loans and Advances observed during Inspections, Audits and Vigilance Action

1.       Statements of accounts which borrowers are maintaining with other banks are not being obtained.

2.       “No statutory dues pending” certificate from CA is not obtained.

3.       Undertaking from borrowers “Not to pay off unsecured loans without Bank’s permission” is not obtained.

4.       Borrower does not close CD account in another Bank after availing CC facility in our Bank. Follow up for closure of the same not found in many cases.

5.       Statement(s) of existing loan account(s) with another/other bank(s) is/are not obtained.

6.       Sureties are made Nominal Members after sanction of facilityi.e.at the time of disbursement of the loan amount.

7.       Report of visit to residence and work place of borrower is either not on record, or when the same is on record, many rows /boxes in it are kept blank.

8.       Share linkage is not done as per Credit Policy.

9.       15/23 days’ norms for sanction of loan are not observed.

10.   Stamp duty is not properly paid as per the applicable Stamp Act.

11.   Declaration-cum-indemnity by mortgagor (stationery item no. 598) not obtained.

12.   Guidelines on M.S. note are not adhered to in case of property mortgaged to Bank.

13.   Evidence of follow up for obtaining RC book with Bank’s hypothecation clause not found.

14.   CERSAI registration number is not noted in OMNI system.

15.   CERSAI satisfaction is not done after closure of the ESCLN/ HSGLN accounts.

16.   Insurance policy of stock / property hypothecated/ mortgaged to Bank is not obtained.

17.   Address mentioned in insurance policy differs from that mentioned on Stock statement.

18.   In some cases, insurance is not obtained for the maximum value of stock held by the borrower.

19.   Follow up for transfer of Share certificate in name of borrower (in respect of residential unit in a cooperative housing society or gala of MSME unit in cooperative industrial estate/society) not found in many cases.

20.   End use of credit facility is not monitored and ensured.

21.   Files are not maintained as per Office Order No. 446 dated 13th March 2015.

22.   List of Sundry Creditors is not entered in OMNI system as per Circular CR No. 148 dated 16th June 2015.

23.   Outstanding balance of WCTL is not reckoned for calculation of monthly drawing power.

24.   Post disbursement visit is not done within stipulated time.

25.   Follow up of receipt of LIC policy from LIC Office, sent for assignment, not in evidence.

26.   SOD against Third party LIC policy is sanctioned; this is not permissible under Credit Policy.

27.   Red copy of gold loan application not handed over to the customers.

28.   In respect of Term Loans with sanctioned amount above Rs. 25 Lakh for purchase of machinery, photograph(s) of machine(s) is/are not kept in file.

29.   In case of housing loan accounts where agreement value is more than Rs. 50 Lakh, only FORM 26QB is obtained. Copy of Form 16 B is not obtained in most of the accounts.

30.   In case of change of surety, Branch Officials are not visiting the residence and place of business of the proposed new sureties to satisfy themselves about the genuineness of their business and financial position.

31.   More than one SODGLD cases are sanctioned to a single borrower.

32.   In gold loans sanctioned for amounts above Rs. 1 Lakh, regular member not entered in Loan other detail master.

33.   All the gold packets need to be counted/verified once in a month. This is not being done.

34.   Outward and Inward RTGS/NEFT transactions in CC accounts are not monitored as per Circular CR No. 148 dated 16th June 2015.

35.   No separate record is maintained in respect of satisfaction of charge, resulting in non-availability of data to determine whether satisfaction is done or not.

36.   Register of CC account holders maintaining accounts with another bank is not maintained. Confidential Report as per IDC No. HO/MD-920/1165 dated 8th December 2015 is not obtained.

37.   Undertaking for payment of correct stamp duty as per Circular CR No. 57 dated 27th December 2011 is not obtained.

38.   Branches are generating the CIBIL report without entering the PAN number of applicants. Attempt to generate CIBIL report by entering mere mobile number throws up a NIL report which is meaningless.

39.   XPERIAN report is not generated in the name of Proprietorship firm at the time of sanction of new facility or renewal.

40.   In spite of power at branch level for valuation of property upto Rs. 1.50 Crore, branches are obtaining valuation in such cases from valuer.

41.   In post-disbursement Machinery-visit report, branches are not showing the details of old machines as well as newly purchased machines.

42.   In case of House construction loans, Visit Report prior to each disbursement, recording examination, and certificate of completion of work is not found in many cases.

43.   Details of margin money paid in House construction loan not found in many Housing-loan cases.

44.   End-use in House Construction loans is not being monitored.

45.   Follow up for noting of charge on 7/12 extracts with authorities concerned not observed in many cases. If done, copy of 7/12 extract with Bank’s charge noted therein is not obtained or kept on record.

46.   In many loan accounts, branches obtain a surety who already stands guarantor to more than two loan accounts, which is against the loan policy.

47.   It is noticed that at the time of renewal of SOD facility, branches are not obtaining the latest surrender value of LIC policy.

48.   At the time of review of CC facility, a copy of latest business proof is not obtained.

49.   A copy of business proof in SOD accounts standing in the name of Prop. / Partnership firm name is not obtained.

50.   While sanctioning Housing Loans in Grampanchayat Area, branch officials are not visiting office of the Grampanchayat concerned for verifying and ensuring genuineness of the documents such as construction permission / plan etc. submitted by the applicant.

51.   At the time of review of CC facility, branches are not collecting the LAD.

52.   In many cases of gold loans, it is observed that branches are not collecting the NOC for pledge of Mangalsutra.

53.   Separate file is not maintained for each SODGLD case; instead, papers pertaining to all cases are kept in a common box file.

54.   SODIMP facilities are not being reviewed as required under Circular CR No. 5 dated 17th February 2010.

55.   SOD limit is released before assignment of LIC policy/NSC.

56.   Interest on FDR interest is credited to SB account instead of FLXLN account.

57.   FLXLN facility is renewed without executing fresh documents.

58.   Whenever a single surety is obtained, interview of surety is also to be taken as per Circular CR No. 143 dated 2nd May 2015. This has not been happening in respect of almost all such accounts.

59.   Net worth details of Partners/Directors/Trustees are not obtained in addition to the networth of Partnership firm / Co./Trust.

60.   Whenever borrower and surety are executing documents with left hand, a note to that effect should be made on a separate paper under signature and name of Bank official. This is not being done.

61.   Newly sanctioned loan-cases of amounts of Rs. 50.00 lakh and above are required to be sent for verification of documents to the Legal Officer of respective Zonal Office immediately before release of facility. This is not being done.

62.   A salaried applicant obtaining loan should be permanently employed in a reputed Ltd. or Pvt. Ltd. Company / Government or semi-Government organization. This is not being followed.

63.   Loan applicants produce IT papers pertaining to 2/3 years filed on the same day. This clearly indicates that they do so merely to comply with Bank’s procedures / formalities. This is particularly noticed on the part of housewives showing ostensible income from tuitions, door-to-door sale of readymade garments / sarees etc. This needs to be subjected to strict probe for genuineness. This is not being done.

64.   Operations are allowed in Un-renewed / Un- reviewed CC/SODIMP accounts beyond the grace period of one month even when applications for renewal are not received, and beyond the permissible grace period of one & half months in case applications for renewal are received.

65.   At the time of revalidation of limit, latest B/S, P/L or other income-statements are not collected. Also, the place of residence / business is not visited.

66.   Instructions in respect of acceptability of a surety in surety loans are not followed. As per policy, whenever a single surety is obtained, he or she should be from a reputed Company / Organization or Govt Employee. Further, loanee and sureties should not be employed in the same institution or organization except in case of a Group Loan and reputed Ltd Company or Government employee. This is not ensured.

67.   Scrutiny Note is incomplete. There are mistakes in Scrutiny Note. Margin Percentage is mentioned wrongly. Comments on deficit amount not made. Deduction of Housing Loan is apparent in salary slip; however, same is not commented upon

68.   .Adverse CIBIL report is ignored while sanctioning loan, thereby violating guidelines issued by Head Office.

69.   Written off / settled loan accounts reflecting in CIBIL report are not probed and no satisfactory explanation is obtained while sanctioning loan in such cases.

70.   ECS/NACH mandate and PDCs are not obtained, when salary is credited into accounts with other banks.

71.   Loans outside jurisdiction (whether considered on the basis of residence or place of business of borrower)of branch are casually sanctioned,

72.   Multiple surety / unsecured loans individually of amounts up to Rs. 5.00 lakh are sanctioned to family members residing/living together.

73.   No steps are taken to obtain cover for death under PMJJBY and PMSBY or under Life Insurance to the extent of liability of the borrower.

74.   Additional shares and service charges are collected from the loan disbursement amount and are not recovered before disbursement.

III.  1. Under sub-Para 7 of Para I above, it has been stated that Branch-In-Charge shall also be held equally accountable for not implementing the rules, guidelines or procedures on a sustained and lasting basis and shall be put under the process for breach of discipline.

2. In this context, each Deputy General Manager during his/her visit to any branch, will call for compliance reports of the last Inspection/Audit/Vigilance Report and check whether compliances were done as a matter of permanent course-correction or merely as a one-time exercise soon forgotten thereafter. Instructions are hereby being issued to call up explanation in the latter case immediately upon its coming to notice, followed by required administrative action.

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department and ensure strict implementation.

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
