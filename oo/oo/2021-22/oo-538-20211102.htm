Office Order No.538 dated 2nd November 2021

Conversion of Double Shift Branches into Single Shift Ones

Changes in Working/Business Hours of Some Branches

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

      I.   With a view to reducing overheads and rendering better customer service, suggestions were received for conversion of Double-Shift Branches into Single-Shift ones, as well as for change in office and business hours of a few branches.The matter was examined in light of inputs from the respective Zonal offices and also in the Staff Sub Committee Meeting held on 12th October 2021. The matter was finally placed before the Board meeting dated 28th October 2021wherein it was decided, w.e.f. 15th November 2021, to

 

1.  Convert the 15 branches as per the “Table A” below from Double-Shift to Single-Shift with the revised timings for working and business work indicated therein, and,

2.  Change the working and business hours of 8 branches as per the “Table B” below.

     II.   Other instructions

1.   i. Branches converted from Double-Shift to Single-Shift as per table ‘A’ above

will be working on all Sundays.

The manpower set up for working on Sundays, shall be as under:

1 Asst Manager

1 Peon

2 Officers

1 Security Guard

4 Clerks

 

 

ii. In-charges are advised to organize work among the staff members accordingly. Staff members working on Saturday will get weekly-off on Sunday and those working on Sunday will get weekly-off on Saturday. Further, staff members attending to their duties on 2nd and 4th Sundays will get compensatory off in the succeeding week.

2.   All the branches are advised to take note of the above changes. Branches whose working hours have been changed are advised to display the revised working hours for the information of customers through a proper Notice Board.

3.   Branches are advised to remove the Old Notice Board of business hours and display the New Notice Board of business hours on 15th November 2021.

4.   Development Dept. is advised to prepare, and supply to branches for display, the Notice Boards with revised working / business hours timings. Development Dept is also advised to inform the changes in working hours of branches to the authorities concerned/regulators.

 

      III.    Branches/Depts. are advised to take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents to the notice of all the staff members at branches/departments for strict compliance.
