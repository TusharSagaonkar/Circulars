Office Order No. 540 dated 10th December 2021

Allocation of duties

among

General Managers and Deputy General Managers

 

 

I.  This has reference to the various Office Orders resting with Office Order No. 508 dated 28th November 2018 in respect of allocation of duties among the Executives.

 

II.   With the recent creation of new positions of Deputy General Managers, some placements have been made and duties and responsibilities have been reallocated among General Managers and Deputy General Managers.

 

III. The instant Office Order and its Annexure which will be effective from 13th December 2021 defines the updated allocation of duties among General Managers & Deputy General Managers. Needless to add that it is in supersession of the earlier ones.

 

IV.    Decisions taken by General Managers, within their respective delegated powers and exercised in respect of cases, matters & issues put up by ZOs & Branches and various Departments under their control within the frame work of the criteria, rules and regulations laid down by the Bank, will be final; therefore, there is no need to refer such matters to the Managing Director. However, if decisions involve some deviations from the laid down rules & regulations, such matters will have to be referred to the Managing Director with suitable recommendations to seek approval of the latter. The Managing Director may decide on such matters, if necessary, by placing the same before Board meeting for approval.

 

V.   Allotment of Zones among the General Managers for Overall Performance and Facilitating Decisions in Some Areas: -

1.   Apart from performing the duties mentioned in the Annexure and taking care of supervision and monitoring of the various Departments allotted to them, General Managers have been allotted zones as under so as to bring about overall development of business, effect recoveries in NPAs and facilitate decision-making in indicated matters in the respective zones.

All the staff concerned are advised to take note of the revised allocation of duties among General Managers and Deputy General Managers and act accordingly.
