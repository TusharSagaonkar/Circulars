Office Order No. 541 dated 18th December 2021

1) Notifications of Govt. of Maharashtra to Prevent Spread of Covid-19 Pandemic

2) Mandatory Adherence to Covid Appropriate Behaviour (CAB) In Offices/ Establishments

3) Penalty for Breach of CAB by Staff and Visitors

4) Empowerment of One Official per each Establishment to Impose Fine on Behalf of Government

5) Complete Vaccination of Staff and Other Instructions

__________________________________________________________________________________________

 

 

I.                 In order to promote containment of the COVID-19 pandemic and to prevent its spread yet again, Govt. of India and Government of Maharashtra have been issuing several advisories to be followed by Govt. and semi-Govt. offices and departments, public and private commercial and non-commercial establishments, and members of the general public. Visits by people to offices, departments, establishments and malls, and gatherings in open spaces including parks, or closed spaces such as congregation halls, auditoriums etc. have been brought under strict regulations within the framework of the Epidemics Act, 1987 and Disaster Management Act, 2005.

II.  The competent authority under Government of Maharashtra under the aforesaid two enactments has given mandatory directions with regard to the management of COVID-19 pandemic through the following notification and order.

1. Notification bearing no. KORONA-2021-PRA.KRA./366/AA-5 dated 26th October 2021, and the

2. Order bearing No: DMU/2020/CR. 92/DisM-1 dated 27th November 2021,

III. The instructions contained in the notification and order are qualitatively different. This is explained below.

1.   Head of each establishment in the jurisdiction of the State of Maharashtra of any organisation is required to designate an official within the establishment with punitive powers such that responsibility is cast on him/her to ensure adoption of and adherence to the Covid Appropriate Behaviour (CAB) by,

i.       all of their employees,

ii.     visitors including customers, and,

iii.   anyone engaged in any activity of the organisations/establishments directly or indirectly, while on their premises.

 

2.   Each organisation is also responsible for availability of hand sanitisers, soap-water. thermal scanners etc. required for

i.     Enabling adoption of CAB by all the personnel, customers and visitors at the locations which are under their control or where its business is transacted or other activities are conducted, and,

ii.   Monitoring thereof by the establishment.

 

IV. The instructions in the said two notifications applicable to our Bank have been consolidated and presented below.

1.   Covid-Appropriate-Behavior (CAB)

i.   Definition: - CAB can be defined as the everyday common behaviour needed to be followed by individuals and organisations to curb the spread of the COVID 19 virus and thereby break the chain of transmission of the same. Aspects of behaviour characterised as CAB include those mentioned below and also all such rational aspects that may hinder spread of Covid 19 virus given its methodology of spread.

 

ii.  Some aspects of CAB: -

a.  Wear a mask at all times in the right way. Mask should cover nose and mouth at all times. (Handkerchief will not be considered as mask & person using would handkerchief instead of mask will be liable for fine.)

b. Maintain social distancing (6 feet distance) at all times wherever possible.

c.  Wash hands frequently and thoroughly by soap or sanitiser.

d. Avoid touching nose and mouth without washing hands with soap or without use of sanitiser.

e.  Maintain proper respiratory hygiene.

f.   Regularly clean and disinfect frequently used/touched surfaces.

g. While coughing or sneezing, cover the mouth and nose using tissue and throw used tissues in the trash; if one doesn't have tissue, one should cough and sneeze into bent elbow and not one's hand.

h. Do not spit in public places.

i.   Avoid crowds and maintain safe distance (6 feet distance) in public places.

j.   Greet anyone without physical contact.

k.  Any other rational behaviour required for avoiding spread of Covid 19 virus.

 

2.   Penalties for not adopting/enforcing CAB

i.     Penalty for not adopting CAB to be imposed by the designated officer of the establishment

Any individual not adopting CAB shall be fined Rs. 500/- for each instance of default.

ii.   Penalty for the establishment/organisation when penalty is not imposed by the designated officer of the establishment for some person’s non-adoption of CAB.

If the non-adoption of CAB by an individual (staff-member, visitor, customer etc.) is seen in any premises of organization/establishment which attracts penalty, but the penalty is not imposed (by the designated official), then, in addition to imposing fine on the individual, such organization/establishment shall also be fined Rs. 10,000/-.

iii.  Penalty for the establishment/organisation found to be regular defaulter in enforcing adoption of CAB.

If any organisation/establishment is seen to be a regular defaulter in enforcing discipline in respect of adoption of CAB on the part of staff, customers or visitors, such organization/establishment shall be closed till the notification of Covid-19 as a disaster remains in force.

 

iv.  Penalty for organisation as a whole

If an organization/establishment fails to follow CAB or Standard Operating Procedure itself, it will be liable to be imposed with a fine of Rs.50000/- for each instance. Frequent defaults shall lead to closure of the organization/ establishment till the notification of Covid 19 as a disaster remains in force.

3. Additional/overriding powers of State Government

The above-mentioned rules regarding CAB are to be followed mandatorily; violation of the same will result in fines and penalties as stated above as well as any other fine or penalty which may be levied on the violators by any disaster management authority in accordance with the Disaster Management Act, 2005. The rules/ polices for CAB shall be in accordance with the above. Any other issue regarding CAB not specifically mentioned herein shall be in accordance with the current rules/orders of the State Government that are in force.

V.Internal instructions for Bank for giving effect to the statutory instructions as above.

1. CAB

i.    Individual responsibility

Each executive, official and employee working in any of offices and branches of our Bank must adopt CAB in all of its aspects as detailed in Para IV.1.ii. above.

ii.   Designation of one official in each establishment, to enforce adoption of CAB on the part of staff as well as customers and visitors, and, authorised to impose penalty as per Para IV.2.i. above.

Head of each branch or establishment of Bank shall designate one official who will enforce adoption of CAB on the part of staff as well as customers and visitors, and will hold powers to impose penalty on anybody breaching any of the aspects of CAB.

 

iii. Head of establishment to superintend over the designated official for exercise of powers vested in the latter.

In view of stiff penalties prescribed for non-enforcement of adoption of CAB, head of each establishment will superintend over the designated official so that the latter exercises his powers.

 

2. Accounting of penalty amount (prescribed by Maharashtra State Government) recovered

i.      The amount of penalty imposed by the designated official of each establishment shall be credited to the PENMASK accounting head created in CBS.

ii.     Total credits of any previous day shall be remitted by the Accounts department the next day into in State Government Account under the following head-

Revenue Receipt

(C) Other non-Tax Revenue

(1) General Services

0070 Other Administrative Services

800 Other Receipts

 

3.  Head of office/establishment to ensure vaccination of all functionaries/ employees: - Head of each office/establishment shall arrange to ensure that the functionaries and employees on the establishment are fully vaccinated. Certificate of complete vaccination (such as two doses under the protocol for two vaccinations namely COVISHILD and COVAXIN available currently in India) shall be obtained from each vaccinated employee and held on record. In respect of those employees who are not fully vaccinated, each Head of Establishment shall arrange to ascertain the reasons of non-vaccination and, if necessary, take steps to make special arrangements with local vaccination centre/office of the Health Department for organisation of special vaccination camps so that all the functionaries and employees are fully vaccinated.

4.   Display of notice in Branch/Department/Office: - A notice as per the Annexure hereto shall be displayed at the Branch/Department/Office.

VI.  The instructions as above are as per the provisions of law. Therefore, please bring the contents of the instant Office Order to the notice of all the members of the staff and ensure strict compliance.
