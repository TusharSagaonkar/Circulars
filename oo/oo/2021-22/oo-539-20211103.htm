Office Order No. 539 dated 3rd November 2021

Creation of Information Systems (IS) Audit Department

Designation of Shri. K. S. Tulsulkar, Sr. Manager

as Information Systems Auditor and Head of IS Audit Dept.

 

 

I.     In light of the guidance received from RBI, a submission was placed before the Board meeting dated 27th September 2021 to create a separate “Information Systems Audit Department” under the direction, superintendence and control of an Information Systems Auditor who would act as its head. Such a department has accordingly been set up on 27st September 2021, by carving it out of the erstwhile “IS Audit and Vigilance Department”. Shri. K.S. Tulsulkar Sr. Manager (Staff No.1234) has been designated as Head of the “Information Systems Audit Department” and he has assumed this office and role with effect from 27th September 2021.

 

II.   Mr. K.S. Tulsulkar, as Information Systems Auditor and Head of the IS Audit Dept., shall be responsible for performing duties/responsibilities and carrying out functions which were earlier handled by Mr. Kiran M Rane, AGM, details of which are as under.

1.       To ensure the compliance of I.S. security policy approved by the Board,

2.       Arrange to prepare and update I.S. Audit manual and checklist as per regulatory guidelines,

3.       Arrange to draw up the programme for half yearly/yearly I.S. Audit of Branches /Depts. and regulate the work of I.S. Audit staff, adopt appropriate systems and practices for conducting IS Audit on annual basis covering all the critically important branches/departments in terms of nature and volume of business. Such audits should be undertaken preferably prior to the statutory audit so that IS Audit Reports are available to Statutory Auditors well in time for examination and for incorporation of comments if any in the audit reports. IS Audit reports is to be placed before board and compliance is to be ensured within the time frame as outlined in audit policy.

4.       i) Arrange to conduct I.S. Audit of Branches / H.O. Depts as per policy of the

 Bank.

ii) Major Audit objectives are,

a)   safeguarding IS Assets and IT Processes,

b)  Verification of Data Integrity and Security,

c)   Evaluation of system effectiveness and efficiency,

d)  Verification of compliance of internal guidelines and procedures in addition to legal, regulatory and statutory requirements, and,

e)   Segregation of duties between Information Security Section and Technical Section.

5.       Put up notes before higher authorities for changes to be made in I.S. Audit Policy as and when required,

6.       Arrange to put up major synopses / executive summaries of I.S. Audit reports before Higher Authorities / Audit Committee of the Board,

7.       Ensure timely submission of compliance of I.S. Audit reports by Branches / Depts.,

8.       Ensure that Bank’s IS Assets/ Resources and IT processes are dependable, controlled and protected from misuse at all times,

9.       Carry out IT systems audit at periodic intervals and submit a report on their status to Audit committee of the Board,

10.   Verify Data Integrity and Security,

11.   Evaluate System Effectiveness and Efficiency,

12.   Verify of compliance of internal guidelines and procedures in addition to legal, regulatory and statutory requirements,

13.   Take review of:

i.         General Computer Control,

ii.       Application Control,

iii.      Data integrity,

iv.      Network Controls and connectivity set up,

v.       Application Software support from IT Dept.,

vi.      Branch Hardware set up and Operating System Access controls,

vii.    ATM operations,

viii.  Vendor Contracts (SLA),

ix.      Business Continuity Planning (BCP),

x.       Implementation of IS policy guidelines which are to be complied with by the branches and departments,

14.   Identify and assess potential risks to bank’s operations,

15.   Assess the means of risk mitigation and safeguarding of IT assets,

16.   Review the adequacy of controls established to ensure compliance with the policies, plans, procedures and business objectives,

17.   Assess the level of compliance of established procedures / controls,

18.   Assess the reliability and security of financial / management information and the systems and operations that provides this information.

19.   Provide role-based training on information security to the workforce. Evaluate effectiveness of training to continuously upgrade it.

20.   Keep track of implementation by IT Department of alerts and advisories issued by regulatory bodies also share relevant information with all staff to the extent they are concerned.

21.   Conduct VAPT of critical IT Assets to maintain security posture of bank at expected level.

22.   Keep track of security incidents by taking necessary feedback from IT Dept and report it to Audit committee on annual basis. Help Vigilance Dept in investigation in IT frauds and mitigation measures.

23.   Conduct Information Security and Task Force Committee Meeting quarterly and keep all Information Security related issues, alerts/advisories/circulars and important points raised by Security Operations Center in this meeting with recommendations for necessary review and action.

 

III.   The scope of the role as given above is illustrative and not exhaustive. Bank may assign such other duties and entrust such other responsibilities to Shri. K. S. Tulsulkar as exigencies may require from time to time.

 

IV. Please bring the contents of the instant Office Order to the notice of all the members of the staff.

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
