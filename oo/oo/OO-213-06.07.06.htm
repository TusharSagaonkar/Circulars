OFFICE ORDER NO. 213 DATED 6TH JULY, 2006 

 

Formation of Zonal Office at Pune,

posting of Dy.Gen.Manager-in- charge & delegation of powers to the Dy.Gen.Manager, Pune

 

            The Registrar of Co-operative Societies, Pune has by his Order dated 30th May, 2006 read with Order dated 15th June, 2006 declared a Scheme of Amalgamation of the Citizens Co-op. Bank Ltd., Pune and our bank.  The merger has become effective from 3rd July, 2006 and the Assets & Liabilities, business and establishment of Citizens Co-op. Bank Ltd have been taken over by our bank from that date.  The branches of Citizens Co-op. Bank Ltd will thereafter function as branches of Abhyudaya Co-op. Bank Ltd., Mumbai.   

            2. Consequent upon merger of the erstwhile Citizens Co-op. Bank Ltd., Pune with its 13 branches in our bank, it has been decided to create a Zonal Office of the bank at Pune to supervise, guide and control the working of 13 branches of the erstwhile Citizens Co-op. Bank and our existing Deccan Gymkhana Branch.  The Pune Zonal Office will be located at 598, Dhanwant Plaza, Budhwar Peth, Pune - 411 002 and it will be under the charge of the Deputy General Manager.

            3. In view of the impending merger, the Board by resolutions passed in their meetings held on 9th May, 2006 and 14th June, 2006 authorised the Dy.Gen.Manager, Pune to exercise the powers of sanctioning secured loans for all purposes upto Rs.15.00 lakhs. Further, he has also been authorized to exercise sanctioning powers in respect of all other types of credit facilities as delegated to the General Manager / Addl.Gen.Manager as specified in Appendix A and B to the said Office Order No.189. He has also been delegated the same powers for incurring revenue expenditure as given to the General Manager / Addl.Gen.Manager in terms of the Office Order No.184 dated 16th August, 2004 and to incur capital expenditure upto Rs.1.00 Lakh per single transaction and take recovery action as per powers delegated to the Dy.Gen.Manager-in-charge of the Recovery Dept in terms of Office Order No.127 dt. 18th December, 1998. The Dy.Gen.Manager, Pune will exercise all these powers only in respect of branches in Pune under the control of Pune Zonal Office. The exercise of powers by the Dy.Gen.Manager will be governed by the normal banking practices and procedures and instructions issued by the bank currently in force and as may be revised from time to time in future.                                     

            For the facility of an easy reference, the powers delegated to the Dy.Gen.Manager, Pune Zonal Office as above are summarized in the Annexures I & II to this Office Order.

            4. It may be recalled that in terms of Office Order No.151 dated 22nd April 2002, our Deccan Gymkhana Branch Manager was delegated certain higher loan sanctioning powers in order to overcome difficulties arising in sending loan files to the Head Office. The higher powers delegated to the Deccan Gymkhana Branch Manager, will stand withdrawn with effect from 15th July, 2006 consequent on opening of the Pune Zonal Office.

 

 

                                                                                                  M.C. Shetty

                                                                                          Managing Director
