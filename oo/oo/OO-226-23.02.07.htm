Office Order No.   226   dated  23   February  2007

 

Appraisal  of proposals for Loans and Advances and

recommendations  of the Branch  In-charge and Zonal DGMs.

------------------------------------------------------

 

 

          Guidelines for appraisal of credit proposals and their  recommendations are issued by the Head Office from time to time and  elaborate  instructions in this regard are also laid down  in the Credit Policy of the bank.  It is, however, observed that in several cases the  recommendations made by the  branch  in-charges and the DGMs  vary substantially  although both these officials are following the same credit appraisal  norms  as set out in the aforesaid guidelines and instructions.  If  and when the  DGM’s  estimate  of PBF  and amount  recommended by him for sanction differ  from that of the branch in-charge, the  prevailing practice is  that the Zonal Office merely recommends a different amount for sanction  as estimated by the DGM or the Zonal Office requires the Branch in-charge to change the amount recommended.  Since despite following the same guidelines for appraisal  the branch in-charge and the DGM arrive at different  credit eligibility,  it is implicit  that in the view of the Zonal Office the branch in question would have made mistakes   in  credit appraisal,  analysis  of  Balance Sheet, interpretation  of various ratios preparation of the scrutiny note, etc.  It is, therefore, advised that hereafter the Zonal Offices should point out these mistakes in writing and advise the concerned branch in-charges suitably for the first instance.  In case of repetition of mistakes in determining PBF  and in preparation of the scrutiny notes, the branch in-charge should be issued a warning  Memo.  Similarly, Personnel Dept. should also be advised to initiate action against the concerned branch in-charge for concealment, wrong reporting of information, etc. as may be observed by the Zonal Office/Head Office.

 

          It may be noted that the Board who have recently reviewed  one such case have taken serious view of the matter and the above instructions are issued at the instance of the Board and should be effectively  implemented by all officials of the bank while processing credit proposals for sanction of facilities.

         

          It has also been decided that Loans and Advances Dept. at  Head Office should obtain  copy of the branch scrutiny note in respect of all credit proposals  for amounts/facilities beyond  the power delegated to the Managing Director.  Accordingly,  it is advised that branches should hereafter furnish to Head Office copies of their scrutiny notes in such cases  immediately  after the sending  the relative files to the Zonal Office.

 

          M. C. Shetty

          Managing Director
