Office Order No. 300 dated 14th September, 2009

Staff Suggestion Scheme

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

The strength of our institution truly lies in the valuable, well learned, talented and enriched human resource. Our Bank, therefore, believes that our staff on the job knows the best and can contribute significantly towards its growth and prosperity by enthusiastically participating in management of the affairs of the Bank. We therefore have pleasure to reintroduce the Staff Suggestion Scheme for our staff to stimulate creativity, encourage employees participation and recognize constructive ideas by personal rewards for their valuable suggestions.

 

We invite from each one of officers/staff of Abhyudaya family to participate in large numbers which will help to improve our quality of Customer Service, productivity, efficiency, profitability and image building of our Bank.

 

2.       The salient features of Staff Suggestion Scheme are as under :-

I.     Objectives

i)     Provide a two-way channel of communication between the Management and the employees and create a forum for participative management.

ii)    Improve the quality of Customer Service.

iii)   Improve business potential and office efficiency and productivity.

iv)   Recognize and reward the creativity and problem-solving ability of the employees.

II.    Eligibility

(a)   All members of staff and officers are eligible for awards / prizes under the scheme.

(b)   Suggestions, which are already under consideration of the Bank will, ordinarily, not be treated as eligible.

III.   Nature and other conditions for suggestions

a)   The suggestion/idea should be implementable / practicable.

b)   The suggestion/idea should directly generate additional revenue/ savings in terms cost or should simplify the existing procedure and generate saving in terms of time, or should enhance customer satisfaction index resulting to attract more customers.

Some of the areas are mentioned below :-

(i)    Reduction in house-keeping and administrative costs, e.g.

§  Electricity, water, fuel charges

§  Stationery, forms and registers, printing & publication costs, dead-stock, etc.

§  Repairs and maintenance costs.

§  Expenditure on conveyance and petrol

§  Better record management.

 

(ii)   Elimination of wasteful and unproductive practices

§  Reduction of paper work by simplifying forms and reports

§  Streamlining systems and procedures and exploring areas for greater delegation to branches / departments.

§  Reduction in delays in operations

§  Avoidance of duplication in work.

 

(iii)  Improvement in lay-out and work areas

(iv)    Improvement in Customer Service – Reduction in processing of applications for various purposes as also Counter arrangements, increase in public place and increase in Branch timings for public dealing.

(v)      Scope for review and improvement in Bank's policies, different products and business potential, reorganization of Branches / Departments, etc.

The above is illustrative list and not exhaustive. Participants may cover additional areas as they may think fit.

 

c)    The participating employee should, in his/her suggestion, provide all possible details so that it can be reviewed thoroughly from the point of implementation. The participating employee should provide details in terms of revenue generation, cost savings, time savings or how it will enhance customer satisfaction index or simplify the procedure. Wherever necessary statistical data should be provided.

 

IV.   How to make the suggestions

The suggestion should be in three parts :

(a)   Issue / Problem and its analysis, duly supported by facts and statistical data wherever relevant

(b)   Possible solution.

(c)   i)  Cost to be incurred for implementing the solution.

      (Give details of fixed as well as recurring costs.)

       ii)  Financial or operational benefits that are envisaged for the Bank.

V.    Submission of Suggestions

A suggestion on one side of page should be typewritten and shall be sent to Personnel Department in a closed cover superscribed as 'Staff Suggestion Scheme'. The name of the participant, employee number, name of the Branch / Department should be given at the beginning and participant should sign at the end.


VI.   Suggestion Scheme Evaluating Committee

The Composition of the Suggestion Scheme Evaluating Committee is as follows :

(i)       The Managing Director

(ii)     The General Manager (Accounts & Inspection)

(iii)   The General Manager (Credit, Forex, Legal & Recovery)

(iv)    The General Manager (HRD, Development & IT)

(v)      The Sr. Manager (Personnel Dept.)

(vi)    The Sr. Manager (Planning & Marketing Dept.)

The Committee could co-opt one or two members should the need arise.

VII.  Procedure for scrutiny of suggestions

The suggestion received, will be placed before the 'Suggestion Scheme Evaluating Committee', which will take a final decision regarding the acceptance and the award to the suggester. The Committee's decision will be final and no correspondence will be entertained in this regard.

VIII. Prizes

The Suggestion Scheme Evaluation Committee will determine the amount of prizes for the suggestions accepted. While determining the prizes the committee would weigh the nature and analysis of the issue/ problem, the value of the suggestion(s) and the likely benefits to the Bank in terms of saving in the cost, operations and time; increase in revenue, business promotion/profitability and greater customer satisfaction, etc. Depending upon the magnitude of the benefits to the Bank, monetary or non-monetary, by implementing the suggestion, cash prizes may range from Rs.200/- to Rs.5,000/-. In addition to cash prize the 'Suggestion Scheme Evaluation Committee' will recommend issue of letters of appreciation to all prize winners. Price distribution and handing over of appreciation letters will be at the Board Meeting on Monthly basis.

During branch/department meetings, you shall impress upon the staff to participate in the Scheme in our common endeavour to improve the Bank's productivity and secure maximum benefits to our stake-holders and win the prizes.
