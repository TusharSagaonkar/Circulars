Office Order No. 208 dated  November, 2005

 

Issue of ATM Cards to depositors opening new deposit accounts

 

 

As a customer friendly gesture, we are issuing ATM cards to the existing depositors free of charge whenever ATM is installed at a particular branch.  We have also been issuing ATM cards to all new depositors opening account with the branch once the ATM was installed.  It has been observed for the past some time that some fraudsters have succeeded in opening deposit accounts with our branches which were utilized for depositing stolen cheques/drafts/dividend warrants, etc. and  withdrawing proceeds thereof, soon after opening the account.  RBI has been emphasizing the need for close monitoring of the newly opened accounts and the procedure of opening the accounts itself has been subjected to strict compliance of KYC norms.  Instructions have been issued to branches from time to time both for ensuring compliance of KYC norms and monitoring of operations in the newly opened accounts.  Despite repeated emphasis on these safeguards, lapses have occurred and in  many cases of fraudulent accounts have  been opened and utilized to encash stolen instruments as already mentioned above.  In such cases, the ATM cards issued immediately after opening of the  concerned accounts were also misused by the culprits.  The Budget and Performance Review Committee discussed the problem at their meeting held on 23.9.2005 and on its recommendation, it has been decided that ATM card may be given immediately subject to KYC adherence and Branch In-charge is sure about genuineness of the accountholder.  If the customer is not provided ATM card facility immediately on opening the account, the decision can be reviewed by the branch in-charge after lapse of reasonable time and ATM card can be issued to the accountholder if operation of the account in the intervening period is found to be satisfactory and the depositors bonafides  conform to the KYC norms.

 

 

     M. C. Shetty

Managing Director
