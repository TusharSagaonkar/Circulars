OFFICE ORDER NO. 214 DATED 7th JULY, 2006

 

Exchange Server - Systems, Procedures and Recommendations

for the use of the Server

 

          Some of the measures taken by the bank in the direction of establishing branch (and department) connectivity is installation of Exchange Server at the Treasury Dept which is initially, proposed to be used for providing connectivity for certain operations / exchange of data/ information amongst the branches / departments. The bank has also joined the RTGS system for settlement of payments introduced by RBI.  RTGS system which was being used by our bank for inter bank settlements is proposed to be extended to the customers’ transactions so as to extend facility of prompt settlement to our customers on par with other major banks. A working group of Officers was constituted for defining the relative systems and procedures and making recommendations for application and use the Exchange Server and RTGS system.  The recommendations of the working group were examined by the IT and Treasury Departments and were placed before the Board at their meeting held on 27th May, 2006.  The Board has approved the systems and procedures suggested and recommendations made by the working group for implementation.

          A copy of each of the note on “The Systems/Procedures/ Recommendations Exchange Server” and “RTGS Systems and Procedures” for use by branches/ departments submitted by the working group is enclosed as Appendix I & II. All concerned are advised to carefully study the contents of both the notes and take immediate steps to implement the recommendations of the working group which would facilitate faster communication and significantly improve customer service and customer satisfaction. Efficient and effective use of these technologies in customer service area will enable our bank to tackle to a significant extent the problems posed by competition from new Private Sector Banks.

          Branches / Departments may approach and consult Treasury / IT Dept for clarification/guidance, if any, required on technical / operational aspects and / or difficulties experienced in implementation of the facilities.

 

 

  

M.C. Shetty

Managing Director

Encl : Annexures I & II
