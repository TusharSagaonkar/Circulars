Office Order No. 224  dated   6  December  2006

 

 

Follow-up Action for Recovery

of

Matured and/or Overdue Loan Accounts.

 

        According to an exercise carried out by our Inspection Dept. it has been observed that large number of branches were lax in monitoring and following up recovery action in respect of overdue loan accounts including matured loan accounts and allowed the overdues  in such  accounts to mount for want of action  for recovery. As on 30 September 2006, there were asmany as 511 loan accounts  having  overdues of 24 months and above and  wherein there was no repayment  since April 2006,  but no cases were filed till 31 October 2006. The aggregate amount outstanding in these accounts across 33 identified  branches  stood at  Rs.213.18 lacs. It appeared  that some of these branches were adopting   a very casual approach to this  crucial function.  It was  noted from the fact that out of 511 overdue accounts mentioned above, 73 accounts  had  outstanding balance of less than Rs. 10,000/- each. Recovery of overdues and closure of such accounts which would not have  posed  much difficulty had  remained outstanding evidently  because of the negligence  and laxity  in follow-up by the concerned  branches.  The  Board of Directors who have taken a review  of the above position in the meeting  held on 24 November 2006, were unhappy about the situation and wanted the branches  to effectively  follow-up overdue loan accounts on regular basis.  While the officers at the branch level  who have been identified as responsible  for the lapses in the recovery action have been cautioned individually,  all  the Branch Managers (including  Branch In-charges)  in general are advised to avoid  continuance/ emergence of  such  a situation  at their branches by effective and regular follow-up for recovery of overdue loan accounts as per Circular OC No. 86 dated 26 June 1998, regarding time bound programme for recovery of overdues.  It is advised in this context that branch in-charges should ensure that no account that is overdue for 24 months or more is kept pending recovery action at the branch level as on 31 March 2007. A serious view will be taken if any laxity in the matter on part of branch officers is observed in future.

 

          M. C. Shetty

          Managing Director
