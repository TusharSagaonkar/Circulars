Annexure to

 

Office Order No. 244 dated 19 March 2008

 


Annexure I

 

SCHEDULE OF CHARGES - EXPORT TRANSACTIONS

 

GENERAL:

 

1. The charges mentioned shall be recovered in accordance with the prescribed scales laid down. No deviation is permissible unless specific permission has been obtained from the appropriate authority.

 

2. The charges shall be recovered upfront unless there is a specific permission from the appropriate authority for recovering of commission back ended or in installments.

 

3. No refund of commission collected on LCs/guarantees and/or any other forex transactions can be made unless otherwise provided for herein.

 

4. All out of pocket expenses, such as correspondent bank charges, postages, courier charges, telecommunication and telegram charges including expenses incurred to secure the bank's and customer's interest shall be recovered from the customer (including employees/retired employees) in respect of all foreign exchange transactions.

 

5. Stamp duty under the Indian stamp act or the stamp act of various states on forex transactions, wherever applicable, shall be borne by the concerned customer.

 

6. Besides the scales of charges prescribed, interest on outlay of funds, shall be recovered from the customers on all foreign exchange transactions, wherever provided.

 

7. The directives, issued by the Reserve Bank of India, in respect of interest rates on export and import finance shall be strictly adhered to.

 

8. Where bank charges are to be recovered from the overseas buyers/sellers, but are refused by them, such charges shall be recovered from the customers.

 

9. These charges are exclusive of service tax which should be recovered separately.

 

Export Bills Purchased/Discounted/Negotiated

 

Type of Transaction/Charges to be levied:

 

a. For each foreign currency EXPORT bill purchased/discounted/ negotiated Rs.500/- per bill

 

b. For each Rupee bill purchased/discounted/negotiated 0.125% of the bill amount with a minimum of Rs.500/- per bill.

 

In case a purchased/discounted/negotiated bill is later converted into a collection item, the commission as applicable to collection bills shall not be charged.

In case an export bill for collection is subsequently purchased/discounted, the bank will recover the charges as applicable to export bills purchased/discounted and shall not levy the commission as applicable to collection bills.

c. For each export bill sent to another bank in India for negotiation (For reasons such as LC being restricted to the latter, the LC has been confirmed by the latter etc.) Rs.500/- per bill.

 

d.  In case of c above if pre-shipment advance has been granted against the letter of credit/export order, 0.125% of the bill amount shall be collected additionally as commission in lieu of exchange.

 

 

e. For joining in customers’ guarantees indemnities and for giving Guarantees/indemnities (if permitted by appropriate sanctioning authority) on behalf of customers to other banks in India in respect of discrepancies in documents negotiated under letters of credit.      0.25% with   a minimum of Rs.500/-per bill

 

f. For overdue export bills where the proceeds are not received in Nostro account within 6 months from the date of shipment to be referred to as GR follow-up charges Rs.250/- per quarter or part thereof per bill.

 

In case of export bills drawn on countries with externalization problems but paid in local currency and the exporter has obtained necessary approval from the Authorized Dealer/Reserve Bank of India for extension, this charge will not be levied for such extension.

 

 

EXPORT BILLS FOR COLLECTION

 

Type of Transaction/Charges to be levied:

 

a. For each foreign currency/Rupee bill sent for collection and/or under consignment arrangements: Rs.500/- per bill

However, if the bill is returned unpaid/dishonoured, the charges shall be 0.125% of the bill amount with a minimum of Rs.500 per bill.

 

These charges are also recoverable from the exporters where advance payment towards exports is received.

 

b. For each set of export documents sent by the exporter directly to the overseas buyer with the approval of the RBI while disposing off the relative GR forms.

 

i. Where advance remittance has been received by the exporter through some other Bank.  Rs.250/- per GR/PP form

ii. Where advance remittance has been received by the exporter through our Bank. Rs.100/- per GR/PP form

 

c. For overdue export bill where the proceeds are not received in India within 6 months from the date of shipment to be referred to as GR follow- up charges 250/- per  quarter or part thereof per bill

 

In case of export bills drawn on countries with externalization problems but paid in local currency and the exporter has obtained necessary approval from the RBI for extension, this charge will not be levied for such extension.

 

COMMISSION IN LIEU OF EXCHANGE is to be recovered for each transaction where exchange does not accrue to the bank @ 0.125% of the bill amount

 

i.     Where pre-shipment advance has been granted against a L/C / export order but the documents under the relative letter of credit/export order are negotiated through another bank.

 

ii.     Where the proceeds of an export bill negotiated are paid in foreign currency to another bank. (For the purpose of levying this charge, the rate of exchange shall be that which is applied to the portion converted or our bill-buying rate for the day if the entire negotiation proceeds are transferred).

 

iii. Where an export bill is sent for collection abroad but the payment is received in rupees through another bank in India.

 

EXPORT LETTER OF CREDIT

 

 

Letters of Credit include letters of credit, letter of authority, orders to negotiate, orders for payment, and all types of documents of similar nature.

 

Type of Transaction/Charges to be levied:

 

a. For each export letters of credit advised  Rs.500 per letter of credit

 

b.  For each amendment of an export letters of credit advised     Rs.250 per amendment

 

However, where an export letter of credit has been advised by another bank and has been received by us for passing on the credit to the beneficiary (for the reason that the letter of credit so stipulated or for the reason the beneficiary is our customer), we should not levy our above-mentioned advising commission. Actual out-of-pocket expenses incurred shall only be charged.

 

If the beneficiary is a non-customer of our bank, the Export LC should be forwarded by V. P. Post. These charges are to be collected by branches and credited to their commission account.

 

 c. For confirmation added to each export letter of credit:

 

(i) Commitment charge

@ 0.15% for every quarter or part thereof for the full

     validity of L/c;

 

(ii) Usance charge:

@  (a) 0.15% for bills upto 10 days sight; or

(b) 0.30% for bills over 10 days sight & upto 3 months sight or

(c) 0.30% for the first 3 months plus 0.075 % per month in excess of 3 months for bill over 3 months sight; -and-

(iii) Confirmation charges - for each credit based on risk perception on letters of credit  opening bank and the country in which such bank is situated

 

     

 

 These charges are in addition to those charges prescribed under a. & b. above.

 

NOTE:

 

• In case of extension of a letter of credit to which confirmation has been added and if such extension exceeds three months (one quarter) from the date of the letter of credit, additional commitment charge shall be recovered as above.

• In case of enhancement of the amount of a letter of credit to which confirmation has been added, additional usance charge and commitment charge shall be recovered as above on the amount so increased.

• The service charges are to be collected by the branch, which maintains the account of the exporter and credited to its commission account. Where there is a request to add confirmation by the opening Bank at its cost, charges will be collected by Foreign Exchange Department and credited to their commission account.

 

d. For each advice of transfer made under a transferable export letter of credit (whether in full or in part, and whether endorsed on the credit itself or not) Rs.250 per advice of transfer in addition to charges prescribed at a. and b. above.

 

 

 

EXPORT CERTIFICATES

 

a. For issue of certificates/attestations in respect of export transactions  Rs.50 per Bill/invoice

 

 NOTE: Exporter may include more than one invoice in a bill as per his convenience/ requirement. Charges shall be levied per bill/invoice and NOT per certificate.

 

OTHER EXPORT RELATED TRANSACTIONS

 

a. For transfer of proceeds of negotiation of documents under a restricted letter of credit received from another bank outside the centre of negotiation Commission as applicable to inland remittances.

 

b. For each reimbursement claimed under a letter of credit by another bank in India on the Vostro accounts maintained with our bank Rs.500 per reimbursement claim.

 

Generally, all of these charges will be collected by the branch, which maintains the account of the exporter and credited to its commission account.
