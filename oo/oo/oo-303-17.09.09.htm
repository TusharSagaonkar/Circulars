Office Order No. 303  dated 17th September, 2009

Special Loans Scheme for Central Railway and BEST Employees

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          On introduction of Sixth Pay Commission, the salary of railway employees has been considerably enhanced. BEST employees are also well paid. Some of these employees are eligible for surety loan more than Rs.2.00 Lakh. They have been granted loans upto Rs.3.00 Lakh repayable in 84 months by some Co-operative Banks and Nationalized Banks. It has, therefore, been decided in the Board Meeting held on 20.08.2009 to grant loans upto Rs.3.00 Lakh to these employees.

 

2.       Loans upto Rs.2.00 Lakh will be sanctioned as Surety Loans with Cross Guarantee/ Surety of colleague employees or one or two outside sureties. These loans will be governed by the rules of Surety Loan Scheme as incorporated in the Credit Policy 2009-2010. The Loanee has to pay 1.2% Service Charge and has to take shares @ 5% of loan amount above Rs.1.00 Lakh. Nominal Membership upto Rs.1.00 Lakh is to be obtained. The rate of interest will be as per ILA.

 

3.       For Loans above Rs.2.00 Lakh, Agreement for Hypothecation of furniture & fixtures is required to be obtained to make the loan as Secured Loan. The salient features of this Special Loan Scheme are as under :-

               i)      The scheme is applicable to all BEST employees and employees of Central Railway Matunga Workshop only.

 

              ii)      Employees should be regular in attendance at their work places.

 

             iii)     They should submit details of hypothecation of furniture & fixtures as per enclosed format (Annexure-A), however, bills/receipts etc. need not be obtained.

 

             iv)     The rate of interest will be PLR + 2% i.e. 14% at present and is subject to change from time to time.

 

              v)      There should be confirmed ECS or SDL from Central Railway/BEST Authorities.

 

At Central Railway Matunga there is fixed procedure for confirmation of ECS. Hence these employees should be advised to produce confirmation letter from their Authorities. In case, any employee has availed loan from other Bank after getting ECS favouring that Bank by Central Railway Authorities, unless that Bank gives NOC to change the ECS, Central Railway Matunga will not allow ECS to our Bank. Hence, only when ECS Confirmation Letter is received from the Authorities of Central Railway Matunga, loan amount will be disbursed thereafter. In the meantime, Branches can complete the formalities and sanction loan & await for ECS Confirmation.

In case of BEST Employees, a facility for locking/non-shifting of ECS of Salary Account from one Bank to another Bank is available from the BEST Authorities. For this purpose a format prescribed by the BEST Authorities will be obtained in triplicate duly signed by the applicant and will be submitted to BEST Authorities under cover of a branch letter for their necessary action. The format prescribed by BEST is enclosed as  Annexure – B and a format of Branch Covering Letter is enclosed as Annexure – C. Only on receipt of the confirmation from BEST authorities loan amount will be disbursed to BEST Employees. In the meantime, Branches can complete the formalities and sanction loan & await for ECS Locking/Non-shifting Confirmation.

 

             vi)     Branch Official will visit the office of Loanees & Sureties and confirm their ECS and their attendance. There is no need to visit their residence. However, care should be exercised to ensure that residence of loanees & sureties are of a permanent nature, may be on pagadi or on rental basis and it should also be confirmed from them that they will be staying in their residence for next 7 years unless they shift to ownership flat/house in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, Virar, Dombivli, etc. i.e. in the area of Mumbai and Mumbai Agglomeration.  

 

            vii)    Repayment of loan will be in 84 EMIs.

 

            viii)   The share money will be 2.5% on the loan amount. The surety will be nominal member.

 

             ix)     Service charge will be 1.2% on the loan amount.

 

              x)      The following documents will be obtained for loans granted above  Rs.2.00 Lakh:-

a) D.P. Note

b) Agreement for Hypothecation Plant / Machinery & Goods (with proper stamp duty) Code No. 401.

c) Undertaking – Code No. 79.

d)  Letter of guarantee – Code No. 395.

e) Code No. 335 & 336 from Employees Deduction from Salaries.

 

4.       At the cost of repetition we once again reiterate that the above guidelines mentioned in Para 3 are applicable to loans above Rs.2.00 Lakh and upto Rs.3.00 Lakh to be granted to BEST Employees and employees from Central Railway Matunga Workshop. The present rules and procedure including the rate of interest will be made applicable without any change to BEST Employees & Central Railway Employees for loans upto Rs.2.00 Lakh.

 

5.       Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at the Branch and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

V.S. Morye

Managing Director
