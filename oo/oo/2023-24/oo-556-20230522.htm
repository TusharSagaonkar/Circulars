Re-organization of Corporate Structure

Annexure - VIII to IX

 

 

I.   The Board of Directors in it's meeting held on 30th January 2023 has approved the revised corporate structure of our Bank on a FUNCTIONAL BASIS. The Corporate Structure is attached herewith as Annexure – I. In pursuance to the same, following instructions are issued for implementation.

1)  CHAIRMAN

The Chairman of the Bank shall have overall supervision of the Bank as prescribed by RBI Directives, Bye-laws of the Bank, provisions in the Multi-State Co-op Societies Act and Rules, and further directions of any other competent authorities in this behalf from time to time.

 

2)  MANAGING DIRECTOR

Managing Director shall be the Chief Executive Officer of the Bank and shall have overall functional and operational control of the Bank. He will carry out all operational and functional activities with the help of all the Departmental Heads under him and will ensure smooth functioning of the Bank as per the prescribed systems and procedures, Board Directives, RBI Directives and Guide lines, Bye-laws of the Bank, Multi State Co-operative Societies Act and Rules applicable to the Bank. He will be accountable to the Chairman and the Board of Directors for the powers exercised and functions undertaken by him.

 

3)   The functions at the Head Office level are divided among 4 main departments, and 4 sections, as under.

i)     Administration - Headed by General Manager-Administration & IT.

ii)    Credit – Headed by General Manager-Credit & Forex.

iii)  Accounts, Audit/Inspection & Treasury – Headed by General Manager - Accounts, Inspection, I. S. Audit and Vigilance, and Treasury.

iv)  Legal and Recovery – Headed by General Manager - Legal and Recovery.

The General Managers shall be responsible for all the functions and activities of their respective Departments. Further, they will be accountable to the Managing Director of the Bank. The Structures of the departments mentioned above are given in ‘Annexure – II to V’

 

SECTIONS

There shall be four sections headed by functionaries as under. They shall work under, & shall report to, the Managing Director directly.

i)       Chief Risk Officer

ii)     Chief Vigilance Officer

iii)    Chief Information Security Officer

iv)    Chief Compliance Officer                                                                    

 

The four sectional Heads as above shall be responsible for the functions and activities as laid down, or as may be laid down in future from time to time, by the Board and Reserve Bank of India. They will be accountable to the Managing Director of the Bank for their functions and activities.

 

4)   The indicative (and not exhaustive) functions of the sections mentioned above are given in Annexure - VI to this circular. The Managing Director or the Board may broaden them for smooth functioning of the respective departments.

 

5)   All the General Managers and Sectional Heads will properly divide the functions and activities among the officials allotted to each department and section. As regards the requirement of staff below the rank of AGM/Sr. Manager/Manager, they may approach the General Manager-Administration/Managing Director to get their requirements fulfilled. The General Manager – Administration and IT will assess the proper and exact requirements of staff of each Department/Section or formations thereunder and will decide or revise the requirements as and when necessary, on work-load basis.

 

6)  Responsibilities of General Managers –

i)         All the General Managers shall give a list of their items to be taken on Board / BOM, Board or Sub-Committee Agendas along with notes on each subject, well in advance.

ii)       All the proposals to be submitted to the BOARD/BOM or Sub-committees shall be prepared section-wise and, within each section, subject-wise, and shall be sent to the Secretarial Section under the Administration Department for further action. Such proposals should be sent to the Secretarial section, well in advance for further circulation to the members, to avoid delays. The proposals of each Department shall be signed by the sectional heads and the General Manager of each Department, before they are sent to the Secretarial section.

iii)      The General Manager-Administration will ensure timely submission of Agenda Papers to all the Board members, BOM members and Sub- committee members as per the dates fixed for such meetings.

iv)      Each General Manager will attend the Board or Sub-committee meeting pertaining only to his subjects so as to avoid wastage of time.

v)       General Manager-Administration along with the head of the Secretarial Section will attend the full Board Meeting to record the discussions and decisions of the Board so as to enable proper minutising of the same.

vi)      The draft minutes of the Board Meetings shall be prepared by the head of the Secretarial Section and shall thereafter be submitted to the Managing Director and Chairman through the General Manager-Administration for their approval. As already decided by the Board, the Draft minutes shall be on white paper. After confirmation in the next Board Meeting, the minutes shall be printed on green ledger paper. The final minutes shall be duly signed with seal on each page and the last page by the Managing Director and Chairman. The final minutes shall be kept in joint custody of the Managing Director and Chairman in month-wise or quarter-wise binders.

vii)    As regards the BOM and Sub-committee meetings, the head of the secretarial section or the Departmental head of the Department/ section concerned will act as Secretary of the committee and will prepare the draft minutes of the BOM or Sub-committee meetings, and will submit the same to the Chairman of the respective Sub-committee for the approval of the latter. Such minutes shall be confirmed in the next meeting of the BOM or Sub-committee. After confirmation, a copy on the ledger paper of the minutes duly signed by the Secretary and Chairman, shall be sent to the secretarial section for record and custody.

viii)   The General Manager - Administration will ensure that all the periodical reviews of functioning of the Bank as prescribed by RBI are submitted to the Board or Committees from time to time in order to ensure compliance.

ix)      All four Sectional heads in the H.O. who operate directly under the Managing Director will submit their submissions, observations and proposed actions to the Managing Director. The Managing Director in turn will forward the submissions and observations to the respective General Managers with his instructions/ remarks for recommending further action at appropriate level of the Board/Sub-committee/BOM. Each such sectional head will attend the Board/BOM/Sub-committee meetings to explain the subject matter falling under his/her section and will record the decisions given for follow up action.

x)       All the General Managers, Sectional Heads and Secretaries of Sub-committees and BOM, shall prepare the Action-Taken-Reports (ATRs) relating to the decisions pertaining to their subjects for review in the next Board Meeting or Sub-committee meeting. Their ATRs shall be submitted to the Secretarial section well in advance so as to enable the latter to prepare the consolidated ATR covering all the subjects deliberated upon in the last Board or Sub-committee meeting.

xi)      The General Managers will ensure that all the statutory, RBI & Board Directives and related stipulations are given priority in the functioning of their respective Departments.

 

7)   ZONAL SET UP

Instead of existing 4 zones, there shall be 3 zones of all the branches of the Bank. They shall be as under.

i)     Mumbai Zone – Some Branches from Current Central and Western Zones plus branches from Gujarat State.

ii)   Navi Mumbai Zone – Some Branches from Navi Mumbai Zone & some Branches from the current Central Zone.

iii)  Pune Zone – With present setup plus branches from Karnataka State and some branches from Navi Mumbai Zone.

Zonewise Branch List is enclosed as Annexure - VII.

 

8) (A) Each zone will be headed by a Dy. General Manager.

(B) The Dy. General Manager shall be assisted by three AGMs for three different areas of activity under the respective zone, one for Credit, one for Recovery and legal, and yet another for Audit and Inspection of branches. The zonal structure is given in Annexure - VIII.

9) BRANCH SET UP

(A)   The Branch Set up is given in Annexure - IX The branches having Business-mix (Deposit and Advances) of over Rs. 400 Crs. shall be under an AGM. He/she will be assisted by one Manager.

(B)  Branches having total business-mix (Deposit + Advances) of more than Rs. 200 crs. but upto Rs. 400 Crs. will be headed by a Sr. Manager.

(C)  Branches having total business-mix upto Rs. 200 Crs. (Deposit + Advances) shall be headed by a Manager.

(D)  All the Branch Heads shall be in overall control of the operational and functional activities of their Branches. They will be responsible for the overall working of the branches and shall be accountable to the respective Zonal Managers.

 

II.    All the Departmental Heads are requested to acknowledge the receipt of this circular and start immediate implementation of the same. They may divide the functions among the Dy. General Managers/Officials as per instructions contained in this circular, and may further divide them among other staff members as may be necessary in their opinion & judgement.

 

III.  All Departmental, Sectional and Zonal Heads are hereby directed to follow the instructions given in this circular for their proper implementation at various levels under acknowledgement to the Administration Department.

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
