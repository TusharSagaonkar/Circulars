Office Order No. 562 dated 1st September 2023

 Designation of Shri. Shitalchandra Khot as

 Deputy General Manager (IT-Software and CIO)

____________________________________________________________________

 

I.     Consequent upon the superannuation on the 31st July 2023 of the previous Deputy General Manager (who held the office of IT & Chief Information Officer vide Office Order No. 531 dated the 12th March 2021), Shri. Shitalchandra Khot has been designated in his place with the approval of the Board. Shri. Shitalchandra Khot took over the office’s charge on the 1st August 2023 with a small modification in his designation now to the effect “Deputy General Manager (IT-Software and Chief Information Officer)”.  He has started functioning since then in the said position. (Chief Information Officer may be indicated by ‘CIO’ in short).

 

II.   1. Since the date of the said Office Order No.556 dated 22nd May 2023 on reorganisation, an additional position of Deputy General Manager (IT) has been created through the elevation of the then incumbent Assistant General Manager (IT) who was also designated as CTO. The new position of the DGM has subsumed the previous position of the AGM. It is currently occupied by Shri. Dnyaneshwar Mondkar.  In conformity with the design of reorganisation, Shri. Shitalchandra Khot shall look essentially after the software part of the IT management function whereas Shri. Dnyaneshwar Mondkar shall look essentially after the hardware part of the IT management function. Therefore, the designation of Shri. Dnyaneshwar Mondkar will now formally be DGM (IT-Hardware and CTO).

2. It may be noted that the two functions namely (i) the Software Part of the IT Management Function and (ii) the Hardware Part of the IT Management Function cannot be delineated sharply due to many pieces of hardware coming with embedded as well as bundled software. Further, some kinds of software may require dedicated pieces of hardware or those with unique specifications. Therefore, there will be an inevitable overlap of functions of the two DGMs which must be managed in close coordination and with complementarity. However, it is being made clear at the cost of reiteration that   Shri. Shitalchandra Khot shall be the Chief Information Officer.

III. Shri. Shitalchandra Khot (as also Shri. Dnyaneshwar Mondkar), in keeping with the current re-organised corporate structure circularised through Office Office Order No.556 dated 22nd May 2023 shall seek advice and guidance from, and report to, the General Manager (Administration and IT).

 

IV. 1. On this subject, it is necessary to reiterate that the CIO is a key business player in the Bank. He is the owner of IT functions, enabling business and software alignment. Being aware that information and software are two pillars on which the bank’s corporate performance rests, he leverages information as the most powerful tool for achieving the bank’s objectives. He develops approaches and action plans accordingly.

2. Constituents of the role of Chief Information Officer (CIO) are as under:

i.       To provide leadership to the entire information technology process in the Bank.

ii.     To ensure that the organisational structure pertaining to IT function subserves the interests of the Bank.

iii.    To examine and revise, wherever necessary on an ongoing basis, IT processes so that they sustain and complement business strategies and objectives.

 

3. The CIO is responsible for the following:

i.         Information Governance.

ii.       IT Policy.

iii.      Technology Assessment and Acquisition.

iv.     Enterprise Architecture.

v.       Technology Life Cycle Management.

vi.     IT Performance Management.

vii.    IT Security

viii.  Process Improvement.

ix.     IT Investment Management.

x.       IT Leadership Management.

xi.     Defining, Implementing and Updating IT Strategy.

xii.    Aligning IT Objectives and Programmes with Enterprise Objectives and Strategies.

 

4. The DGM (IT-Software and CIO), will take the initiative in constituting the following two committees, of which he himself will be the convener.

i.   IT Strategy Committee: - This will assist the Board in choosing the right IT Strategy, and monitoring its implementation to achieve the desired results. Its meetings shall be held at quarterly intervals. Minutes of these meetings shall be placed in the Board Meeting.

 

ii. IT Steering Committee: - This will assist the Senior Management in implementing the IT strategy approved by the Board. It will include prioritisation of IT-enabled investment, reviewing the status of projects (including resolving resource conflict), and monitoring service levels and service improvements. Its meetings will be held at monthly intervals. Minutes of these meetings shall be placed in the Board Meeting.

 

5. Shri. Shitalchandra Khot, by virtue of occupying the office of the DGM (IT-Software and CIO), has become a member of the Information Security & Task Force Committee. He will closely work with the DGM (IT-Hardware and CTO), AND also with CISO to ensure that the IT security function which is fundamental in the Bank, is conducted in accordance with the expectations of the regulators and the Board.

 

V.   In addition to the role as the DGM (IT-Software & CIO), Shri. Shitalchandra Khot may be assigned such other tasks and asked to perform such other functions as the Bank may consider appropriate from time to time.

 

VI. Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch, Office or Department.

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
