Office Order No. 557 dated 15th June 2023

1.   Merger of Central and Western Zones - Combined New Zone named as Mumbai Zone

2.   Closure of Regional Offices at Ahmedabad and Udupi

3.   Branches under R. O. Ahmedabad to come directly under Mumbai Zone

4.   Branches under R. O. Udupi to come directly under Pune Zone

5.   Realignment of Processes of Operations and Business of Branches under R. O. Ahmedabad and Udupi

___________________________________________________________________________________________

I.    Background

1.   Our Bank currently operates under a three-tiered structure in the State of Maharashtra with four zones operating under the Head Office. All the branches of the Bank except those in Gujarat and Karnataka operate directly under one Zonal Office or another. However, the operational structure of the Bank in the States of Gujarat and Karnataka is four-tiered in as much as there are Regional Offices in Ahmedabad (Gujarat) and Udupi (Karnataka) which form an intermediary tier between branches in respective states and the Zonal Offices designated for the branches in these two states. The reasons for the four-tiered structure in the two states are partly historical and partly administrative. Owing to a change in the administrative perspective and also for a regulatory reason, the closure of the two regional offices was under contemplation for some time.

2.   The matter of consolidating our network of Zonal Offices has also been under consideration for some time.

 

II.   Closure of Regional Offices

1.   It has been decided in the Board meeting dated the 17th April 2023 to merge the Bank's Central and Western Zones into a single Zone named as Mumbai Zone. However, the officials will continue to work from their existing premises as hitherto.

2.   It has also been decided in the aforesaid Board meeting dated the 17th April 2023 to close the Ahmedabad and Udupi Regional Offices and bring them under the direct control of Mumbai and Pune Zones respectively.

3.   The instant Office Order, coming into immediate effect, in execution of the said decisions of the Board also lays down, in the succeeding paragraphs, the realignment of processes pertaining to the operations and business of, and control and reporting of personnel in, branches in the states of Gujarat and Karnataka.

 

III.   Control of branches

1.   The 6 branches in the state of Gujarat will operate under the administrative control of the Mumbai Zone. The complete list of branches under Mumbai Zone is given in Annexure-I.

2.   The 5 branches in the state of Karnataka will operate under the administrative control of the Pune Zone. The complete list of branches under Pune Zone is given in Annexure-II.

 

IV. In view of the need to expeditiously handle credit decisions, settlement of deceased claims, matters of expenditure, legal work, recovery and liasioning with State Govt., local bodies, etc. which may require special knowledge of states’ official languages, laws and banking practices, or performance of special tasks such as clearing in Southern Grid, it has been decided to place one Field Functionary (supported by a small team) each in the two states.

 

V.  Field Functionaries-Details

1.   For branches in Gujarat State under Mumbai Zone

i.     The Field Functionary of the Mumbai Zone, in the cadre of AGM / Sr. Manager, shall operate from the following address.

Mithakali Branch :

Jaymangal House, Block ‘B’,

Near Gandhigram Railway Station,

Mithakali, Ellisbridge, Ahmedabad – 380 009.

ii.    The In-charges of these branches will report to the designated Field Functionary.

iii.  The Field Functionary will exercise financial and administrative powers same as those given to an AGM /Sr. Manager in Zonal Office under DGM.

 

2.   For branches in Karnataka State under Pune Zone

i.     The Field Functionary of the Pune Zone, in the cadre of AGM / Sr. Manager, shall operate from the following address.

City Branch, Udupi :

“Shambhavi Fortune”,

Behind K.S.R.T.C Bus Stand,

Udupi – 576 101.

ii.    The In-charges of these branches will report to the designated Field Functionary.

iii.  The Field Functionary will exercise financial and administrative powers same as those given to an AGM / Sr. Manager in Zonal Office under DGM.

 

VI. The Field Functionaries will, apart from exercising financial and administrative powers, will look after following affairs of branches mentioned in the state of Gujarat & Karnataka (as applicable).

1.       Coordinating checking of legal/property documents through empanelled Advocates.

2.       Recovery and arbitration (filing of cases, execution of awards).

3.       Follow up of old legal cases in Civil Courts/ High Court.

4.       SARFAESI action (issuing notices, taking symbolic & physical possession)

5.       Liaising with Govt. Authorities, Registrar, Police and Revenue authorities.

6.       Attending arbitration cases and attending Courts/DC/Consumer courts in respect of cases filed by the Bank and cases filed against the Bank.

7.       Credit Monitoring including visits to borrowers, the review of the credit facilities, and other purposes.

8.       To conduct regular follow-ups of SMA-2 accounts and NPA accounts, and make joint Recovery visits along with branch staff.

9.       Six-monthly change of keys to safes, lockers etc. in respect of the branches in the states of Gujarat & Karnataka as may be applicable.

10.    Creation of CERSAI Charges.

11.    Auction of mortgaged properties and pledged gold ornaments.

12.    Annual Maintenance Contracts of Vendors.

13.    The Field Functionary placed in Udupi shall handle the clearing work for branches in Karnataka State as the work falls under the Southern Clearing Grid.

14.    Maintaining the record of the share capital of erstwhile Janatha co-op Bank and sanctioning of refund of share amount (applicable to branches as per Karnataka State).

15.    Submissions, whether in respect of the business, administration or policy,

 

VII.  The Field Functionaries and the members of their teams will be on the establishment of the respective Zonal Offices.

 

VIII.  Other matters pertaining to the Field Functionaries and their teams

1.     The leave programme of the Field Functionaries will be approved by the DGMs of respective Zones. The Field Functionaries shall sanction or decline, and generally manage, leave of absence of members of their teams.

2.     Performance appraisal of the Field Functionary will be made by the DGM of the respective Zone.

3.     Performance appraisal of members of their teams shall be done by the Field Functionaries.

 

IX.   The Title Deeds and other papers pertaining to premises of branches and offices under the ROs being closed shall be transferred to and held by the Development department. The Development department shall initiate action in this behalf.

 

X.     The IT department shall make the necessary and appropriate changes in posts and displays on the Bank’s website and displays on the Bank’s intranet (Exchange Server) by replacing references to “Central Zone” and “Western Zone”, both, with “Mumbai Zone”.

 

Please bring the contents of the instant Office Order to the notice of all the staff members concerned and ensure strict implementation of the instructions herein.

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
