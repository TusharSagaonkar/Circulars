Office Order No. 568 dated 23rd November 2023

ATM Attendants

1.   Functions, Duties and Responsibilities, Rules

2.   Supervision, Monitoring and Control of the work of ATM Attendants

___________________________________________________________________________________________

I.   As part of an administrative restructuring, a number of peons in the Bank belonging to the subordinate cadre have been designated as ATM attendants within the same cadre. The mail sent by Special Security Officer through Exchange Server on 30.05.2023 on the subject lays down their duties and responsibilities. It has however been considered necessary to prescribe their broad functions and also elaborate on the duties and responsibilities listed in the said email. With this end in view, the i

II. nstant Office Order is being issued.

 

III. Currently, all the ATMs of the Bank are onsite except three namely (1) Abhyudaya Nagar, (2) Ghatkopar, and (3) Sewree. The ATM Attendants at onsite ATMs will be on the muster of such branches and they shall report to and be primarily answerable to the Branch-In-Charge thereat. In respect of ATM attendants at offsite ATMs, they will report to and be answerable to the respective Branch-In-Charges of link branches.

 

IV.  Functions of an ATM Attendant

1.   An ATM is a machine storing cash and facilitating automatic cash transactions (in addition to a few more services). The public at large has open access to any ATM and, more often than not, such access is from outside of the branch premises. Therefore, persons with dishonest intentions can also visit ATMs and perpetrate fraud or dupe other unsuspecting customers by pretending to offer help. The major function of an ATM attendant is to guard against the happenings such as these.

2.   Apart from the ATM cabin and its immediate surroundings, there is a need to guard the area near and around the branch against suspicious characters and their movement. The ATM attendant is expected to be watchful to prevent the loitering of suspicious characters around the branch and take such steps as to protect the Bank’s interests. He is also expected to be alert to accidents and untoward happenings.

 

V.  Duties and responsibilities of an ATM Attendant

A.   An ATM attendant should

1.   take rounds in and around the ATM cabin before taking a position at the main entry-cum-exit point to ensure that nothing wrong has gone off before his taking charge,

2.   position himself outside the main entry-cum-exit point of the ATM cabin and the branch to supervise the goings on around,

3.   ensure that only one person is present in the ATM cabin at a time. However, a family member or a relative may be allowed to accompany him/her if so requested by the customer himself/herself. (Any action of the ATM Attendant to enforce discipline should be through polite persuasion).

4.   ensure that the customers entering the ATM cabin do not use goggles and helmets and do not wear fully-covered face masks, burkhas etc.,

5.   ensure that the customers use mobile phones only to a limited extent for purposes such as reading OTP for transactions (and if some customers insist on using mobile phones while inside the ATM cabin for a longer time, persuade them to come outside the ATM cabin to do so),

6.   dissuade any customer from attempting a transaction at the ATM by taking help from a stranger (instead of which he may help such customers himself if the customer seeks help),

7.   report to the Branch-In-Charge upon noticing any suspicious person or vehicle, and any unattended baggage or packet noticed /found in and around ATM cabin and branch premises for eventual verification by making inquiries with those present around, seeking help from knowledgeable persons, or police, if need be,

8.   keep a watch on the persons visiting branch premises and report to the Branch-In-Charge immediately upon anybody arousing suspicion,

9.   keep himself in the know about

i.   locations of fire extinguishers generally in the branch and especially in the ATM cabin, and their operation,

ii. locations of the main switch panel of the electric power supply,

iii.   locations of emergency alarm switch, MCP (Manual Call Point for fire), and Fire Alarm Panel,

iv.    important telephone numbers including those of the nearest Fire Brigade Station, nearest Police Station, the Branch-In-Charge and the Special Officer (Security & Premises), and call them in emergencies.

 

B.              When cash is being replenished in the ATM by the joint custodians, the ATM Attendant should be extra alert to prevent any pilferage, hold-up, or worse incidents. To ensure this, he should remain alert outside the cabin.

Other expectations

i.           Ensure the safety of property and people.

ii.         Guard against damage and attacks.

iii.       Not remain a mere static guard but also do patrolling depending upon less or more footfall in the ATM.

iv.        Keep a constant watch.

v.         Make sure everything is under control.

vi.        Work with Police when required.

vii.      Know what to do in emergencies.

viii.    Have a working knowledge of First Aid.

ix.        Know how to operate lights in the branch/ATM area.

x.         Always be on the alert for acts of violence.

xi.        Stop the entry of unauthorised people.

xii.      Be courteous to all people with whom interaction is made and treat them politely with the spirit of cooperation.

xiii.    Do not be late for assignments.

xiv.     Know how to contact your supervisor.

xv.      Be in good physical shape.

xvi.     Be honest and have dedication to the job.

xvii.   Safeguard against theft and violence.

xviii. Protect against physical and financial loss.

xix.    Direct vehicles and make sure they are safe.

xx.      Know where emergency alarms are located.

xxi.    Make detailed notes of anything unusual.

xxii.   Observe every person within your guard area.

xxiii. Always act in the best interests of the Bank.

xxiv. Answer general questions and give directions to the customers.

xxv.   Always present oneself professionally.

xxvi. Acquire basic technical knowledge of ATM operations and security equipment.

xxvii.                Know whom to call in an emergency.

xxviii.              Must have good communication skills.

xxix. Handle and resolve conflicts.

xxx.   Check blind spots and report the same.

 

VI. Duty hours, special rules of discipline (in addition to those applicable to the subordinate cadre) and specific instructions

1.   The hours of daily work of ATM Attendant (from the Bank’s permanent subordinate cadre) shall be 7 hours.

2.   The ATM attendant shall

i.       report to the place of work 15 Minutes before the schedule of the shift,

ii.     not report late or leave early (late attendance and early leaving shall be marked as absence),

iii.   always be in a proper uniform (the special cap bearing the Abhyudaya Bank logo and whistle with a lanyard being essential parts of the uniform, absence will be marked If he turns up on duty without any of its parts),

iv.    not remain in the ATM cabin for more time than necessary (as he is expected to remain outside the cabin),

v.     not leave the place for lunch or dinner,

vi.    not leave his place during duty hours without informing the Branch-In-Charge,

vii.  remain vigilant throughout the duty hours,

viii.      know by heart all the telephone numbers useful in emergencies (ensuring in addition to this that the list of such telephone numbers is displayed in the ATM cabin),

ix.    not use any separate chair or table for the sake of his duty, since he has to patrol the area,

x.     ensure that no one is allowed on the premises with a firearm and, in case of breach, immediately report the matter to the Branch-In-Charge,

xi.    go into the ATM cabin at on hourly basis as already instructed and sign the ATM attendance sheet to let the video of his so doing get captured in the CCTV camera,

xii.  make use of his mobile phone only in emergencies,

xiii.      not leave the place even after putting in 7 hours of work until the reliever reports on duty (which also applies to cases when the reliever is a security guard, whether the bank’s own or from an outside agency).

xiv. always keep the ATM cabin clean.

3.   There shall be no smoking or spitting in or around the branch premises and the ATM cabin whether on duty or otherwise.

4.  Deployment

i.       The ATM Attendants from the Bank’s permanent subordinate cadre shall not be deployed during nighttime.

ii.     In the shift where a gunman is on duty, the ATM Attendant present at that time may be given general duty.

iii.   The duty of ATM attendants will be in one of the two shifts, the first from 08:00 a.m. to 03:00 p.m. and the second from 03:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m.

iv.    At branches where only one ATM attendant is posted, his duty-timing will be from 3:00 p.m. to 10:00 p.m. When and where guards of the outsourced agency can be deployed for duty on holidays, ATM attendants will be given leave/holiday.

v.     In an emergency when neither a gunman nor an ATM Attendant is present on a day, any peon at the branch may be given the duty of an ATM Attendant. However, the information of such deployment shall be immediately given to the HRM department and the Special Officer (Security and Premises).

5.  Leave and its planning

Availment of entitled leave shall be subject to the following.

i.       The leave to which the ATM Attendant is entitled within a month must be taken in the same month.

ii.     Where there is a single ATM Attendant deployed, he will avail the public holiday/the weekly (Sunday)/second-fourth Saturday off on the same day.

iii.   Where there are two ATM Attendants deployed, none of them will avail the public holiday/weekly (Sunday)/second-fourth Saturday off on the same day. He will avail the same on any other working day of the branch (albeit in the same month). However, if any unforeseen absenteeism occurs requiring emergent duty towards the end of any month, the holiday in lieu thereof may be availed in the next month.

iv.    Where there are three ATM Attendants deployed, one out of them will avail the public holiday/ weekly (Sunday)/second-fourth Saturday off on the same day. The other two ATM Attendants will not avail the public holiday/weekly (Sunday)/second-fourth Saturday off on the same day. They will avail the same on any other working day of the branch (albeit in the same month). However, if any unforeseen absenteeism occurs requiring emergent duty towards the end of any month, the holiday in lieu thereof may be availed in the next month.

v.     The Branch-In-Charge shall plan the leave of ATM Attendants which shall include the alternate OFFs if any for weekly holidays and public holidays for any month in advance in the preceding month. The plan should ideally be prepared on the 25th (or the previous working day if the 25th happens to be a non-working day) of the preceding month.

vi.    The alternate OFFs for weekly holidays and public holidays must be planned and availed of in the succeeding week itself. In no case shall they be postponed (except in emergencies as explained above) nor shall they be combined with CL/PL.

vii.  If leave for more than three days is intended, the application therefore should be submitted to the Branch-In-Charge at least 15 days in advance.

viii.      Any “Off’ whether in lieu of weekly/public holiday must be marked off in the muster on the day of availing the “Off” itself.

ix.    The schedule of duty shifts shall be changed every month.

 

VII.           Role of the Branch-In-Charge

The Branch-In-Charge is expected to

1.   exercise overall control over the security of the branch including the ATM through the ATM Attendants and also otherwise,

2.   plan monthly deployment, leave and compensatory “offs” in advance carefully and implement it strictly,

3.   work in close coordination with the Special Officer (Security & Premises),

4.   maintain a security instructions file at the branch and keep it up-to-date,

5.   examine the security file and take it into custody as part of the overall taking-over exercise of the branch,

6.   ensure and monitor the signing by the ATM Attendant of the attendance sheet kept in the ATM cabin at hourly intervals in such a way that the video of the ATM Attendant is captured in the CCTV camera installed in the cabin,

7.   ensure that the ATM Attendant does not sleep, remain absent, or leave work during duty hours, and if he is found to indulge in any such act of indiscipline, admonish him apart from marking his absence for the day apart from reporting the act of indiscipline immediately to the Special Officer (Security & Premises)and the HRM department,

8.   enforce strict discipline and ensure adherence to the rules laid down for the ATM Attendants,

and

9.   to call the Special Officer (Security & Premises) in case of any difficulty.

 

VIII.             All the ATM attendants, the Branch-In-Charges, and other officials responsible for security of branches and ATMs thereat in the absence of Branch-In-Charges are instructed to take note of the instant Office Order and follow it strictly.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Chief Executive Officer
