Annexure II

 

SCHEDULE OF CHARGES - IMPORT

 

GENERAL:

 

1.     The charges mentioned shall be recovered in accordance with the prescribed scales laid down. No deviation is permissible unless specific permission has been obtained from the appropriate authority.

2.     The charges shall be recovered upfront unless there is a specific permission from the appropriate authority for recovery of commission back-ended or in installments.

3.     No refund of commission collected on letters of credit, guarantees and other forex transactions can be made unless otherwise provided for herein.

4.     All out-of-pocket expenses, such as correspondent bank charges, postages, courier charges, telecommunication, telegram, and telex charges, including expenses incurred to secure the bank's and customer's interest shall be recovered from the customer (including employees/retired employees) in respect of all foreign exchange transactions.

5.     Stamp duty under the Indian Stamp Act or the Stamp Act of the various States on forex transactions, wherever applicable, shall be borne by the concerned customer.

6.    Besides the scales of charges prescribed, interest on outlay of funds shall be recovered from the customers on all foreign exchange transactions, wherever provided.

7.     The directives issued by the Reserve Bank of India in respect of interest rates on export and import finance shall be adhered to.

8.     Where bank charges are to be recovered from the overseas buyers/sellers but are refused by them, such charges shall be recovered from the customers.

9.     For the purpose of levying commitment and usance charges the total amount committed inclusive of interest amount and tolerance level shall be reckoned.

 

10.    Generally these charges are to be collected by the branch which maintains the account of the importer and credited to its commission account.

 

11.    These charges are exclusive of service tax which should be recovered separately.

 

TYPE OF TRANSACTIONS/CHARGES

 

Opening of IMPORT LETTERS OF CREDIT

 

1.               For establishing an import letter of credit where 100% cash deposit as security is not taken:

       

(a)   Upto Rs.4 Crores or equivalent of the credit:

       

        Charges are:

(1)  Commitment charge - 0.15% for every quarter or part thereof for the full validity

(2)  Usance charge -    (i)  0.15% for bills upto 10 days sight; or

(ii) 0.30% for bills over 10 days sight & upto 3 month sight; or

(iii) 0.30% for the first three months plus 0.075% per  month in excess of three months for bills   over three months' sight, as the case may be.

(3) Note: Min. charges to be taken are Rs.250 per letter of credit.

 

b)             Over Rs.4 Crores or equivalent and upto Rs. 8 crores:

 

                        (i)     On the first Rs.4 Crores:   Charges as per (1) and (2) above.

                        (ii)   On the next Rs.4 Crores:

                        Charges shall be:

(1)            @ one-fourth of the rates stipulated at (1) and (2) above if the opener is a PSU, Blue-Chip Company or PLR party

(2)            @ one-half of the rates stipulated at (1) and (2) above for all others

 

                (c)   On the balance amount in excess of Rs.8 Crores:

 

Charges shall be:

(1)            @ one-Eight of the rates stipulated at (1) and (2) above if the opener is a PSU, Blue-Chip Company or PLR party

(2)            @ one-Sixth of the rates stipulated at (1) and (2) above for all others

 

Commitment charges are to be levied from the date of opening of letters of credit to the last date of its validity.

 

2.      For establishing an import letter of credit where 100% cash deposit as collateral security is taken

 

(a)   Upto Rs.4 Crores or equivalent of the credit:

       

        Charges are:

        (1)  Commitment charge   -   0.0375% for every quarter or part thereof for

                                           the full validity

(2)  Usance charge -    (i)   0.0375% for bills upto 10 days sight; or

(ii)  0.075% for bills over 10 days sight & upto 3 month  sight; or

(iii) 0.075% for the first three months plus 0.01875% per month in excess of three months for bills over three  months' sight, as the   case may be.

 

(3) Note: Min. charges to be taken are Rs.250 per letter of credit.

 

b)             Over Rs.4 Crores or equivalent

 

(i)     On the first Rs.4 Crores: Charges are as per 1 & 2 above.

 

                (ii)   On the balance in excess of Rs.4 Crores:

                        Charges shall be:

                        @ One-half of the rates stipulated at (1) and (2) above.

 

3.     For amending Import letters of credit including a revolving letter of credit:  Rs.250 per amendment

 

(a)    In the case of an amendment extending the validity period of the letter of credit beyond a three month period (one quarter) from the date of the credit, additional commitment charge shall be recovered as per scale prescribed at 1 and 2 above.

 

(b)    In the case of an amendment enhancing the amount of a letter of credit, additional usance charge and commitment charge shall be recovered as per scale prescribed at 1 and 2 above on the amount so increased. For the purposes of levying charges under this Rule, the value of the enhancement will ordinarily be considered separately without adding it to the original value of the letter of credit for arriving at the scale applicable for the purpose of levying charges.

 

(c)     In the case of an amendment altering the tenor of the bill of exchange called for under a letter of credit, additional usance charge shall be  recovered as per scale prescribed at 1 and 2 above.

 

4       For reviving or reinstating an expired import letter of credit/revolving letter of credit

(i) Option shall be with the bank but within three months from the date of expiry; and

(ii) Shall be subject to recovery of usance charge and commitment charge from the date of expiry upto the validity period of the revived letter of credit

 

Remarks:

This is a clarificatory clause included to ensure clarity of the charges to be levied.

 

The charges shall be collected right from the date of expiry of the letter of credit provided the item continues to be importable from such expiry date upto the date of revival/reinstatement as per the EXIM Policy.

 

5       For each revolving letter of credit established (As per provisions of Trade & Exchange Control Regulations and subject to specific permis­sion from sanctioning authorities).

 

(a)            Upto Rs.4 Crores or equivalent

(1)   Commitment charge - 0.15% for every quarter or part thereof on the maximum amount of drawings permitted at any one time from the date of establishment of the letter of credit to the last date of its validity; -and-

(2)            Usance charge (on the maximum amount of drawing permitted at one time) –

        (i)    0.15%   for bills   upto 10 days sight; or

        (ii)   0.30%   for bills   over 10 days sight and upto three months' sight; or

        (iii)    0.30% for the first three months plus 0.0750% for each month in excess of three   months'    for    bills over    three months' sight, as the case may be -or-

(3)   Rs.250 per revolving letter of credit - whichever is more -

 

(b)            Over Rs.4 Crores or equivalent

                        (i)     On the first Rs. 4 Crores charges as at (1) and (2) above

(ii)   On the next Rs.4 Crores:-

(a)   At   one-fourth   of   the   rates stipu­lated at (1) and (2) above if   the opener is a PSU, Blue-Chip Company, or PLR party

(b)    At one-half of the rates stipulated at (1) and (2) above for all others.

 

(c)   On the   balance   amount in excess of Rs.8 Crores:

(i)     At one-eighth of the rates stipu­lated at (1) and (2) above if the opener is a PSU, Blue-Chip Company, or PLR party

(ii)    At one-fourth of the rates stipu­lated at (1) and (2) above for all others.

 

6.      For each reinstatement under a revolving letter of credit

 

(a)    Upto Rs.4 Crores or equivalent

(1)    Commitment charge - 0.15% for every quarter or part thereof on each amount reinstated from the date of reinstatement to the date of subsequent reinstatement or the last date of the validity, as the case may be; -and-

 

(2)    Usance    charge    (for each drawing)

(i)    0.15% for bills upto l0 days sight; or

(ii)   0.30% for bills over 10 days' sight and upto three months' sight; or

(iii)  0.30%   for the first  three months plus 0.0750% for each month in excess of three months' for bills  over three months sight, as the case may be -or-

(3)    Rs.250 per reinstatement - whichever is more

 

                (b)    Over Rs.4 Crores or equivalent  

                (i)     On the first Rs. 4 Crores charges as at (1) and (2) above

 

(ii)    On the next Rs.4 Crores:

(1)            At one-fourth of the rates stipulated at (1) and (2) above if the opener is a PSU, Blue-Chip Company, or PLR party;

(2)    At one-half of the rates stipulated at (1) and (2) above for all others

(iii)    On the balance amount in excess of Rs.8 Crores:

(1)            At one-eighth of the rates stipulated at (1) and (2) above If the opener is a PSU, Blue- Chip Company, or PLR party

(2)   At one-fourth of the rates stipulated at (1) and (2) above for all others.

 

To the extent of drawings honoured (whether the letter of credit specifies the maximum amount of total drawings or the maximum amount of drawing permitted at any one time) and the charges shall be recoverable at the time of establishment.

 

7       For establishing import letters of credit on deferred payment terms:

        (a)            Upto Rs.4 Crores or equivalent -    0.50% per quarter or part thereof on

                the liability outstanding at the beginning of every quarter

        (b)   Over Rs.4 Crores or equivalent -    0.50%    per quarter or   part thereof on the liability outstanding at the beginning of every quarter

 

(1)            The commission for the entire period of the import letter of credit on deferred payment terms should be collected upfront. Prior permission of the appropriate sanctioning authority is required to collect commission in installments.

 

        (2)    The recovery of commission, either upfront or in installments (where permitted), is subject to the following:

 

        i.      Where the entire L/C commission is collected upfront the rate of commission and exchange rate (TT selling) prevailing as on the date of issuance of the letters of credit shall be applied. No further adjustment arising out of future revision, if any, in the L/C commission shall be made.

       

        ii.      Where the L/C commission is recovered on installments basis the rate of commission and exchange rate (TT selling) prevailing as on the date of recovery of each installments shall be applied.

 

        iii.     In the event of default in payment of installments interest shall be recovered at domestic commercial rate from the date of default to the date of actual payment.

 

i.e., letters of credit covering import of goods on deferred payment terms involving payments beyond a period of six months from the date of shipment over a period of time as defined in Exchange Control.

 

8.     For amending an import letter of credit on deferred payment terms: Rs.500 per amendment

 

In the case of an amendment extending the validity period of the letter of credit beyond a three month period (one quarter) for which the commission has already been collected, additional commission shall be collected at 0.50% per quarter or part thereof on the liability outstanding.

 

In the case of an amendment enhancing the amount of the letter of credit, additional commis­sion shall be collected at 0.50% on the amount so enhanced.

 

For the purposes of levying this charge, wherever the letter of credit value is enhanced, value of each enhancement will ordinarily be considered separately without adding it to the original value of the letter of credit.

(a)            Upto Rs.4 Crores or equivalent - 0.15% of the bill amount shall be recovered at the time of retirement or crystallization whichever is earlier.

(b)            Over Rs.4 Crores or equivalent    

(i) On the first Rs.4 Crores - 0.15% of the bill amount

(ii) On the next Rs.4 Crores: -       (i) @ 0.0375% if the opener is a PSU, Blue-Chip Company or PLR party

                                                        (ii) @ 0.075% for all others

(c)    On the balance amount in excess of Rs.8 Crores:

                (i)  @ 0.01875%  if the opener is a PSU, Blue-Chip company, or PLR party

(ii)  @ 0.0375%  for all others

       

10.    For each Rupee Bill received under a letter of credit:

(a)            Upto Rs.4 Crores or equivalent    0.25% of the bill amount shall be recovered at the time of retirement.

(b)            Over Rs.4 Crores or equivalent    

(i) On the first Rs.4 Crores - 0.25% of the bill amount

(ii) On the next Rs.4 Crores: -       (i) @ 0.0625% if the opener is a PSU, Blue-Chip Company or PLR party

                                                        (ii) @ 0.125% for all others

(c)    On the balance amount in excess of Rs.8 Crores:

                (i)  @ 0.01875%  if the opener is a PSU, Blue-Chip company, or PLR party.

(ii)  @ 0.0375%   for all others.

 

11     For each foreign currency bill received under a letter of credit on which no exchange benefit accrues  0.125% on the bill amount
