Office Order No.194 dated 24 May 2005                         

 

Revenue Stamps on Receipts

 

Reserve Bank of India have brought it to the notice of the Indian Banks’ Association that in terms of the amendment to Article 53, Schedule I of the Indian Stamp Act, 1899, RECEIPT as defined by Section 2 (23) of the Act, ibid, for any money or other property, the amount or value of which exceeds Rs.5000/- (Rupees Five Thousand) would attract stamp duty of rupee one.  It has been pointed out by the RBI in this regard that some banks are still insisting on payment of Stamp Duty where the amount in the receipt exceeds Rs.500/- (Rupees Five Hundred) and have requested the IBA to advise member banks to modify their procedure/ practice in accordance with the aforesaid amendment. 

 

            All concerned are advised to take a note of the change advised by the RBI and take action in future in accordance with the amendment to the Indian Stamp Act, 1899.                    

 V. S. Morye

Addl. Gen. Manager
