Office Order No. 299  dated 11th September, 2009

Payment of additional interest to staff/retired staff/staff

on contractual basis (Special Officers etc.)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

          In terms of the provisions and RBI guidelines, our Bank has decided to pay additional interest of 1% to the following categories on their Savings Bank and Term Deposits.

 

1.   Staff/Retired staff either singly or jointly with any other member or members of his/her family;

 

2.   The spouse of a deceased staff or the spouse of a deceased retired staff;

 

3.   An association or a fund, members of which are staff of the Bank with certain conditions;

 

4.   Employees of our Bank sent on deputation to another bank;

 

5.   Persons taken on deputation for a fixed duration, or on a Contract-Basis for a fixed duration i.e. special officers, etc. The benefit shall cease to accrue on the expiry of the term of deputation or contract, as the case may be;

 

With the following conditions that

a)   The additional 1% interest shall be payable only so long as the person continues to be on deputation or upto the Contracted Period. But in case of Term Deposit, the benefit of 1% additional interest shall be continued till the maturity of the deposit.

 

We once again reiterate that Bank has decided to pay additional interest of 1% to persons on contractual basis i.e. Special Officers, etc. upto the duration of contract only and not thereafter. However, in case of the term deposit(s) issued during the contracted period with the additional interest of 1%, the benefit will be extended upto the maturity date of deposits, although he/she may not be the contracted Special Officer at the time of maturity of the Term Deposit(s).

and

 

b)   In the case of employees of merged banks, the additional interest shall be allowed only if the interest of contractual rate together with the additional interest does not exceed the rate which could have been allowed if such employee were on finally employed by the Bank.

 

Bank will also pay any additional interest if allowed as per Bank's Policy for Bulk Deposits accepted from the above categories.

 

A Senior Citizen (who has completed 60 years of age) belonging to any of the above categories will get benefit of additional interest of 1% only as mentioned above and will not get any other extra benefit for he/she being a Senior Citizen, which please note.

 

Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at the Branch and ensure compliance.
