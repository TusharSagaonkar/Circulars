Office Order No. 207 Dated 16 November 2005

Customer Service – Senior Citizens & Other vulnerable categories.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

            Various measures for improving customer service are being taken by the bank from time to time.  In this context, it has been observed in light of the suggestions made by the customers at the recently held customers’ meet at Vashi and Abhyudaya Nagar that the arrangements made for providing service to Senior Citizens and some other vulnerable sections of the customers, like illiterate persons, were deficient which were resulting in customer dissatisfaction.  The position in this regard was discussed by the Board and the Top Management and the following measures are advised to be taken at branches:

I) Customer service to Senior Citizens:

As it may not be possible to provide a separate counter exclusively for the Senior Citizens because of the staff/space constraints, it has been decided at the cash counter where customers queue up for depositing or withdrawing cash, a board should be displayed that there will be separate queues for the Senior Citizen customers and other customers.  The persons in Senior Citizen queue should be given preference over those in the other queue.  This arrangement will be like the one at the local railway ticket counters in Mumbai where Ist class and IInd class passengers get preference over persons lining up for IInd class tickets.  The preference for Senior Citizens will be given irrespective of the time of their joining the queue.  Till the customers get familiar with the practice the security guard on duty at the customer enclosure, the cashier and other counter staff should counsel the customers so as to avoid creation of any ill feeling among customers, causing arguments and/or disputes.  The branch-in-charge are advised to devote special attention to these aspects and ensure smooth and successful implementation of the proposed service arrangements.

II) Service to other Vulnerable Customers:

Traditionally our bank has been known for helping the weaker and vulnerable sections of the Society and even till this day, our branches have large number of illiterate or poorly educated customers who require special attention and assistance for carrying on their banking transactions.  One of the recommendations of Reserve Bank’s Committee on Customer Service (Goipuria Committee) was that bank branches should provide “May I Help You”? counter and provide service to customers in need of assistance.  It has since been decided that our branches should display at the cash counter and near the desk provided to customers for writing cheque/deposits slips, forms etc., large size computer print outs reading “Customers in need of help/guidance may approach the Accountant” (to be displayed in English & Marathi).  The Accountant at the branch should personally guide and help the needs customers who approach for help as notified.  The help to be given will include explaining the procedures, systems, etc. and even helping in filling up the forms, etc. except withdrawal slips/cheques involving withdrawal of cash.

 

 

                                                                                                                        M.C.Shetty

                                                                                                            Managing Director
