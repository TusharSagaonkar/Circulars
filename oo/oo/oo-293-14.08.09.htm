Office Order No. 293 dated 14th August, 2009 

Customer Service -

Establishment of Customer Care Cell at Head Office

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

         

You are aware that customer is sovereign and our motto is to give service to the extent of fullest satisfaction of the customers within the laid down norms, rules and regulations.

 

          We should always be prompt, polite and proactive with customers and should help them to achieve their goals. We should go beyond the call of duty to make them feel valued. We should also offer excellence in services and every act of us should display that we are taking maximum care of our customers.

 

          We must learn to honor the customer who makes complaint(s), as the customer who makes complaint(s) wants to remain with the Bank as such he/she is more loyal to Bank rather than a customer who do not make any complaint(s) but make a flight silently by simply closing his/her account.

 

          With the above background, it is painful to note that many times the complaints received from customers at branches remain either unattended or if attended those are not dealt with properly to the satisfaction of the customers and Head Office is unaware of the unattended/partly attended complaints. We must always remember that dissatisfied customer is like an epidemic and may tarnish the image of our Bank.

 

          With a view to offer excellent customer service, retain existing customers, we advise the following measures :-

i)             Every official/staff should be aware of the 'Grievance Redressal Mechanism' which we have displayed on our Bank's website for the knowledge of customers.

ii)           At Head Office we have established Customer Care Cell under the supervision of the Sr. Manager, Secretarial Dept.. Shri. R. D. Vajpayee (Accountant) will be the In-charge of Customer Care Cell He will be provided with additional phone lines, numbers of which will be communicated shortly.

iii)         The In-charge of the Customer Care Cell will maintain proper records of all the complaints received from customers directly or through branches or e-mail and other records including the record received from branches in regard to the complaints.

iv)          At every branch, nearer to the Complaint cum Suggestion Box a board will be displayed giving suitable information to customers regarding Customer Care Cell with its phone numbers / E-mail address. (The Boards will be supplied to Branches shortly). The customer may contact on telephones directly to this Cell regarding his unsolved complaint(s) already submitted to Branch or send his grievance/complaint by E-mail to this Cell.

v)            At branch level it is advised that every complaint from the customer will be accepted in writing. Every complaint received by the branch will be registered in Complaint Register maintained at the branch. On receipt, the acknowledgement should be issued to the complainant without fail. Complaint received should be finally and satisfactorily resolved within a period of one week.

vi)          Monthly statement detailing the complaints received with dates, nature of complaints, complaints resolved with dates and complaints pending with branch with reasons therefor should be submitted by every branch to Customer Care Cell before 5th of the succeeding month through their respective DGMs / AGMs. The In-charge of Customer Care Cell  will consolidate the data received from the branches and will submit the information through Sr. Manager to the Managing Director on or before 15th of the succeeding month, for placing before the Board Meeting.

vii)        With a view to provide necessary help to Branches/ Departments, the Customer Care Cell will also analyze the reasons and areas of complaints, to take necessary steps to avoid recurrence of such complaints. If it is observed that any particular staff or official is often named in customers' complaints regarding rude and in-disciplined behavior / non-politeness of the staff / official, disciplinary action will be taken against such staff / official in the interest of the Bank.  

 

 Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff at the Branch and ensure strict compliance.

 

V.S. Morye

Managing Director
