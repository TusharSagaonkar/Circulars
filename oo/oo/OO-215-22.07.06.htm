OFFICE ORDER NO. 215  DATED 22  JULY, 2006

 

 

Business Permitted  to be undertaken at Extension Counters

-      liberalization  made by RBI

 

 

          According to the conditions prescribed by Reserve Bank of India while granting permission to urban co-op. banks for opening Extension Counters (EC) only acceptance of deposits and carrying on transactions relating to such deposits were allowed to be undertaken at the ECs.  The position in this regard has since been reviewed by Reserve Bank of India and it has been advised in terms of their Circular UBD.LS (PCB). No. 49/07.01.000/2005-06 dated April 28, 2006 that  ECs can undertake, besides the deposit related services, disbursement of loans upto Rs. 10.00 lacs, only of individual borrowers (other than loans against fixed deposits with the EC), sanctioned by the Head Office or the  base branch. The Board has taken a note of the liberalization of permissible functions of ECs made by RBI and it has been decided to provide loan disbursement facility, within the specified limit, at our Bhandup Extension Counter to individual borrowers who are sanctioned loans either by the Head Office or Bhandup branch.

 

          The permissible functions of ECs as enumerated in the RBI circular under reference are recapped for information of all concerned.

(i)

Deposit/withdrawal transactions

(ii)

Issue and encashment of drafts and mail transfers

(iii)

Issue and encashment of travellers’ cheques

(iv)

Collection of bills

(v)

Advances against fixed deposits of their customers (within the sanctioning  power of the concerned officials at the Extension Counter)

 

(vi)

Disbursement of other loans (only for individuals) sanctioned by the Head Office/base branch up to the limit of Rs. 10.00 lacs only.

 

          Bhandup branch and Bhandup Extension Counter are hereby  advised to provide disbursement facility to the customers eligible  for it and desirous  of availing disbursement of their sanctioned loan upto the prescribed limit of Rs. 10.00 lacs at the EC. A suitable computer printed notice in bold letters may be displayed at Bhandup branch and the EC for information of the customers.
