Office Order No. 528 dated 22nd January, 2021

Placement of Additional Deputy General Manager at Recovery Dept.

 

I.   1. NPAs in our Bank, which were already causing anxiety over the last few years, are on the increase due to the impact of COVID-19 and lockdowns imposed to contain its spread. NPAs as they are currently accounted for in our books do not necessarily reflect the true position, and may in fact be worse if worked out on the basis of first principles; the deviation being a result of bar imposed by a judicial pronouncement. Thus, the distinction between NPAs as they are shown in our books, and several Standard Assets which are in fact NPAs if measured on the basis of first principles of ageing of overdues or out of order status (in combination or independent of some other factors such as sudden deterioration), is only technical. There is a distinct possibility that a significant deterioration in asset quality will emerge after the judicial bar is lifted.

 

2. Further, between Term Loans and Working Capital Advances, it has been perceived that Term Loans have shown a deteriorating trend in quantitative terms whereas Working Capital Advances, by their nature, demonstrate a mixture of quantitative as well as qualitative deterioration. These points have been engaging our attention for some time.

II.  Placement of Additional Deputy General Manager for Monitoring & Recovery of Advances

1.    In light of the general worrisome scenario described in Para I.1. and the finer point of treating actual or potential deterioration of advances as a category apart as indicated in Para I.2. above, it has been decided to place an additional Deputy General Manager who will be responsible for monitoring and recovery of advances, and also for rendering assistance to the Deputy General Manager (Legal and Recovery) in overall recovery function.

 

2. i. In the current fluid situation, it is very difficult to clearly demarcate areas of responsibility between executives and the departments which they head, because of blurring of quality of loans and advances at the seams. Therefore, exercise of duties and functions by the Additional Deputy General Manager should not only be in coordination with the Deputy General Manager (Legal and Recovery) but in superordination, i.e. much more than what one would otherwise do within strict confines of a straitjacketed role. Thus, the incumbents in these two positions are expected to play complementary roles.

 

ii. While the two Deputy General Managers should closely work together in the spirit indicated above, they will formally report directly to the Managing Director.

III.   Areas of primary engagement of the Additional Deputy General Manager

1.    The Additional Deputy General Manager will monitor all the standard advances regardless of the amount, and ensure that the EMIs / overdues prescribed for each of them are recovered promptly.

2.    Qualitative deterioration of advances, apart from mere overdues, shall also attract his immediate attention. Slippage into SMA-0 category (which normally happens in case of working capital advances, but is also possible in respect of advances, either in conjunction with the working capital facility or independent of it) must be attended to with promptitude for remedial action.

3.    He will frequently visit branches, ROs and ZOs to monitor how the functionaries perform the credit monitoring function.

4.    Constitution of recovery teams at branches, work allocated to them and their results, shall be closely monitored by him. Sluggishness in their functioning must be corrected and lapses if any shall be brought on record for further action, including administrative action.

5.    Needless to add that monitoring of functioning of the recovery teams at branches will not be confined only to advances but to all the loans and advances, including NPAs.

6.    Duties & functions of the Additional Deputy General Manager are indicative and not exhaustive. Thus, he may be assigned such other work & duties as may be required considering needs and exigencies of business.

 

IV.   1. Reporting on NPAs and preparing Defaulters’ List for Bank as a whole shall be the duty of the Legal and Recovery Department.

 

2. Further, both the Deputy General Managers will be jointly responsible for the functioning of the Recovery Department.

V.  While the need for all the concerned to work synergistically and in unison has been repeatedly emphasised, Management is constrained to observe that complacency has set in among the functionaries from branches upwards right up to those holding senior positions, with the result that important functions are kept unattended in expectation that others will perform them. All the functionaries are therefore advised to shake off the complacency, examine each loan and advance by applying yardstick of age of overdues/out of order status and other deterioration, independent of the current judicial bar. Rigorous remedial measures should thereafter follow.

 

VI.   Instant Office Order comes into effect immediately.

 

VII. Please bring contents of the instant Office Order to the notice of all the staff members concerned.

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
