Office Order No. 531 dated 12th March, 2021

Extension of Designation of Shri. Sunil Shetty, Deputy General Manager, IT Dept. as

Deputy General Manager (IT & Chief Information Officer)

 

I.   1) In view of rapid innovations in banking & technology, a working group constituted by RBI in 2011 suggested a few measures, one among which was regarding “Information Technology Governance”. Creation of some positions to achieve the key objectives thereof has been a part of it. We have been giving effect to the recommendations of the working group in phases.

 

2) In our Bank, the position of Chief Technology Officer (CTO) in the area of Information Technology was created vide Office Order No. 521 dated 4th October, 2019. The Office Order No. 529 issued today itself (i.e. on 12th March, 2021) gives information about its new incumbent who has taken over from Shri. Sunil Shetty, the latter having held that position until recently. The position of Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) is already in existence since the F.Y. 2010-11 & information regarding its current incumbent has been circularised vide Office Order No. 517 dated 24th June, 2019. However, there existed a need for an authority which would provide leadership to, & shall be responsible for, entire Information Technology Function in the Bank [although in terms of design, the CISO shall not report to the CIO as mandated by the RBI working group, but directly to the compliance officer in the Bank who is General Manager (Operations and compliance)].

 

3) In view of the above, the then General Manager, IT Department was designated as CIO in terms of the submission approved in the Board Meeting dated 25th November, 2019. Upon his retirement, and leadership of IT function in Bank having been taken over by Shri. Sunil Shetty, DGM, Board has granted its approval in the meeting dated 24th February, 2021 to designate Shri. Sunil Shetty as CIO in extension of his current designation as Deputy General Manager (IT). It can be seen that the extension of designation essentially acknowledges & records conceptual expansion of his current role as Head of IT department.

 

4) Shri. Sunil Shetty will continue to seek advices and guidance from, and report to, the General Manager (Operations and Compliance).

 

II.  1) The CIO is a key business player in the Bank. He is the owner of IT functions, enabling business & technology alignment. Being aware that information & technology are two pillars on which bank’s corporate performance rests, he leverages information as the most powerful tool for achieving bank’s objectives. He develops approaches & action plans accordingly.

 

2) Constituents of the role of Chief Information Officer (CIO) is as under:

i)     To provide leadership to entire information technology process in the Bank.

ii)   To ensure that the organisational structure pertaining to IT function subserves interests of the Bank.

iii)  To examine & revise, wherever necessary on an ongoing basis, IT processes so that they sustain & complement business strategies & objectives.

 

III.   1) The CIO is responsible for the following :

·     Information Governance.

·     IT Policy.

·     Technology Assessment & Acquisition.

·     Enterprise Architecture.

·     Technology Life Cycle Management.

·     IT Performance Management.

·     IT Security

·     Process Improvement.

·     IT Investment Management.

·     IT Leadership Management.

·     Defining, Implementing & Updating IT Strategy.

·     Aligning IT Objectives & Programmes with Enterprise Objectives and Strategies.

 

IV.  Shri. Sunil Shetty, DGM (IT & CIO), will take initiative in constituting the following two committees, of which he himself will be the convener.

1)  IT Strategy Committee: - It will assist the Board in Choosing the right IT Strategy, & monitoring its implementation to achieve the desired results. Its meetings shall be held at quarterly intervals. Minutes of these meetings shall be placed in Board Meeting.

 

2)  IT Steering Committee: - It will assist the executive management in implementation of IT strategy approved by the Board. It will include prioritisation of IT enabled investment, reviewing status of projects (including resolving resource conflict), monitoring service levels & service improvements. Its meetings will be held at monthly intervals. Minutes of these meetings shall be placed in Board Meeting.

 

V. Shri. Sunil Shetty, being DGM-IT is already a member of the Information Security & Task Force Committee. With his extended designation as CIO, he will continue to be a member thereof. He will closely work with the CISO to ensure that the IT security function which is fundamental in the Bank, is conducted in accordance with the expectations of the regulators & the Board.

 

VI.  In addition to the role as the DGM-IT & CIO, Shri. Sunil Shetty may be assigned such other tasks & asked to perform such other functions as the Bank may consider appropriate from time to time.

 

VII. Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Office Order to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at your Branch/ Department.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
