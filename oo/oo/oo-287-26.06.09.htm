Office Order No. 287 dated 26th June, 2009 

Establishment of Central Law Department at H.O.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

In the general functioning of the Bank, the areas where legal assistance is normally required can be covered under the following four broad categories:

1.   Checking the Mortgageability of the property.

2.   Filing of recovery cases and execution thereof.

3.   Bank premises matters.   

4.   Misc. legal matters, such as fraud matters, legal opinions etc. 

 

The  above work will be handled by the following departments:

 

Manager (Legal), Development Dept., Vashi:   

[Henceforth the Law Officer at Development Dept. Vashi will be designated as Manager (Legal) ].

·        To examine the Title Deeds of properties proposed to be purchased by the Bank or to be taken on lease and to give opinion regarding the title of the owner.

·        To approve various agreements including Agreement for purchase of premises, Agreement for Lease, renewal of lease of Bank’s premises, agreement with various Vendors, Service Contract agreements etc.

·        To deal with legal issues in matters with respect to premises, Service contract and with various Vendors.

·        To deal with the Court cases pertaining to premises matters, service contracts & Vendors.

·        Any other work as assigned from time to time.

 

AGM/Sr. Manager, Legal & Recovery Dept. Parel

·        Initiating of action under SARFAESI Act for enforcement of security(ies) in NPA accounts and work related thereto.

·        All work related to execution of Award till closure of the account.

·        Action for recovery of dues in NPA accounts.

·        Monitoring and guidance to branches for recovery of dues.

·        Any other work as assigned from time to time.

 

AGM/Sr. Manager, Legal Section (Recovery Dept.) H.O.

·        Filing of recovery cases and further follow up till obtaining Award.

·        Attending to matters before various Courts/Authorities/Forums in respect of loan related matters. 

·        To defend the matters filed against the Bank/Recovery Officials before various Courts/Authorities concerning loan matters/execution of Awards, etc.

·        To deal with fraud matters in loan related accounts. 

·        Any other work as assigned from time to time.

 

Manager, Mortgage Dept., H.O.

·        To scrutinize the title deeds and documents furnished by the applicants for loans, particularly offering immovable property as securities and to offer legal opinion in each case. 

·        Preparation, scrutiny and registration of Mortgage/Equitable Mortgage executed by the borrowers/Sureties of the Bank and certify their correctness before disbursement of loans. 

·        Preparation of documents/deeds, General Power of Attorney, Indemnity Bonds, etc.

·        Verification of documents executed by the borrowers.

·        Any other work as assigned from time to time.

 

In so far as branches coming under the Regional Office Ahmedabad & Udupi, Pune Zonal Office, Aurangabad & Nagpur are concerned the work relating to above four categories are to be handled by the concerned offices/branches itself and wherever required, assistance of panel Advocate is to be obtained. 

 

Central Law Department :-

          At times it has been observed that these departments are faced with peculiar matters involving complicated legal issues which are required to be properly and promptly dealt with. However, it is not always possible for these departments to effectively attend to the same.  So also these departments at times require guidance on certain legal issues.  The same was discussed at the Business Planning Committee Meeting and it was felt necessary to establish a Central Law Department, which would be a one place reference for such complicated matters, which are of a non-routine nature.  The Board has also given its approval for establishment of the Central Law Department. 

          Pursuant to the above, the Central Law Department is established at Head Office under the charge of the Asstt. Gen. Manager.  The Central Law Department will deal in non routine matters involving legal issues of complicated nature. The duties of the Asstt.Gen.Manager, Central Law Dept., would include the following:

§  To guide, advise and assist the departments, Regional Offices, Zonal Offices in matters involving complicated legal issues. 

§  To impart training in matters covering legal aspects to the staff and especially to the Branch Managers, as per programme prepared by the Staff Training College.

§  To approve changes in documentation as may be required.

§  Attending to Court cases involving complicated legal issues. 

§  To recommend empanelment or dropping any advocate from the panel of Advocates, appointment of Counsel in any matter. 

§  To prepare guidelines covering legal aspects for use of Branches.

§  To approve settlement of deceased deposit claims beyond the delegation of Branches and Zonal Offices and involving complicated matters.

§  The above four legal departments shall refer the matters under their purview to Central Law Department for guidance, only in case certain policy decisions are required, complicated legal issues are involved or matter needs to be referred to High Court or Supreme Court or Central Registrar.

§  To give legal opinions in respect of complicated matters.  

§  To deal with all fraud matters (other than in borrowal accounts) by drafting suitable replies etc., and in the event any case/complaint is filed against the Bank concerning the same before any Court/Authority, then to suitably defend the case in consultation with concerned Branch/Department, if considered necessary.

§  To deal with cases of consumer forums as well as critical cases of Banking Ombudsman. 

§  Any other item of work that may be assigned from time to time.

    

          The Asstt. Gen. Manager, Central Law Dept., will report to the General Manager (Credit, Forex, Legal & Recovery) H.O.

 

It has further been observed from the queries received for legal opinion that the queries generally pertain to operational difficulties or with respect to issues which are already covered in the Circulars issued by Head Office. Such reference to Central Law Department or to Legal Department(s) should strictly be avoided by the branches. As regards operational difficulties it is expected that the Branch Managers are well acquainted to handle such issues and if any difficulty still arises then they should contact Zonal DGM. As regards routine matters branches should keep themselves updated with the various Circulars issued by H.O. from time to time and the same must be discussed in the Branch Monthly Meetings.  By doing so, unnecessary reference to various departments can be avoided and better customer service can be provided to our valued customers.
