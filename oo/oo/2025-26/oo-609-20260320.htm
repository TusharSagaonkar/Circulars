Office Order No. 609 dated  20th March 2026

 

Customer Awareness regarding Cyber Crime Helpline Number-1930

(Cyber Jagrukta Abhiyaan)

 

Introduction :

As you are aware, with the rapid increase in the use of digital banking services such as UPI, Internet Banking, Mobile Banking, ATM transactions, etc., incidents of cyber fraud are also increasing. In order to protect customers from such financial cyber crimes, the Government of India and concerned authorities have introduced the National Cyber Crime Helpline Number – 1930, which is dedicated for reporting financial cyber frauds immediately. In connection to the same, Bank has formed Enterprise Fraud Risk Management (EFRM) Department at Vashi for handling cyber fraud cases.

 

Further, Cyber Jaagrookta (Awareness) Diwas is observed on the first Wednesday of every month in India with the objective of spreading awareness among citizens regarding cyber security and prevention of online financial frauds. In this regard, all branches are requested to actively participate in this RBI awareness initiative and ensure that maximum customers are informed about the 1930 helpline and cyber fraud prevention measures.

 

Role of Branches:

All branches are therefore instructed to communicate with more and more customers and spread awareness regarding the 1930 Cyber Helpline Number and safe digital banking practices.

 

The following important points should be communicated to customers:

1. Immediate Reporting of Cyber Fraud:

Customers should be informed that if they become victims of any financial cyber fraud such as UPI fraud, phishing, OTP fraud, fake calls, or unauthorized online transactions, they must immediately call 1930 – National Cyber Crime Helpline and report the incident without delay.

 

2. Importance of Immediate Action:

Customers must be made aware that early reporting through 1930 significantly increases the chances of blocking the fraudulent transaction and recovering the defrauded amount before it is transferred or withdrawn by fraudsters.

3. Do Not Share Confidential Information:

Customers should be strictly advised never to share OTP, ATM PIN, CVV, UPI PIN, Internet Banking credentials, or personal banking details with anyone over phone calls, SMS, email, or social media.

 

4. Be Careful of Fraudulent Calls and Messages:

Customers should remain alert about fraudsters posing as bank officials, RBI officers, police authorities, or customer care representatives asking for sensitive banking details.

5. Avoid Suspicious Links:

Customers should be advised not to click on unknown or suspicious links received through SMS, WhatsApp, email, or social media, which may lead to phishing or fraudulent transactions.

6. Use Official Banking Channels Only:

Encourage customers to use only official bank mobile applications and secure websites for carrying out digital transactions.

7. Display Awareness at Branch Level:

Branches should display cyber awareness posters in Branches as well as in ATM cabin which should be visible to customers, inform customers at counters and circulate messages in local customer groups so that the information about 1930 helpline reaches the maximum number of people. We have also displayed Cyber Jagrukta Awareness posters on the Bank’s official website as well as on the login page of our Mobile Banking Application to ensure wider dissemination of awareness amongst customers.

8. Bank’s Helpline Numbers:

9653660266        : EFRM Department (Vashi)

1800223131        : Tollfree Number (Kurla)

022-68778971    :  Call Center (Kurla)

 

Role of Branch Clusters for Follow-up and RBI Compliance:

The Branch Cluster Team shall be responsible for ensuring effective implementation and monitoring of cyber fraud awareness initiatives across all branches under their jurisdiction. They shall conduct regular follow-ups with branches to ensure compliance with guidelines issued by the Reserve Bank of India and other regulatory authorities.

The Cluster Team shall also:

•       Review and verify that awareness measures (posters, customer communication, helpline display, etc.) are properly implemented.

•       Ensure timely reporting of cyber fraud incidents and adherence to prescribed procedures.

•       Conduct periodic audits and submit compliance reports to the Head Office.

•       Provide guidance and support to branches for addressing gaps and improving adherence to regulatory requirements.

All branch staffs are requested to actively participate in Cyber Jaagrookta initiatives, especially on the first Wednesday of every month, and ensure that maximum customers are contacted and made aware about the National Cyber Crime Helpline – 1930 and safe digital banking practices. Further, we request branches to keep informed to respective Clusters, Vigilance Dept & EFRM Dept, if any fraudulent cases are received at branch end. 

 

Your co-operation in spreading this awareness will help protect customers from cyber frauds and strengthen trust in our banking system.

Let us work together to create a safe and secure digital banking environment for our customers.

 

 

Annexure

 

 

Chief Executive Officer
