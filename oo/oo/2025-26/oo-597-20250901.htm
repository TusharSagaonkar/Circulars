Office Order No. 597 dated 1st September 2025

Duties and responsibilities of General Manager-

(Legal and Recovery Department)

The Bank has appointed Shri. Satish N. Talreja and he has been designated as General Manager –Legal and Recovery Department.

 

He will be the In-charge of Legal and Recovery Dept. to have overall control and is responsible for

Recovery Department-

1.   Recovery of NPA accounts and overall reduction in position of Gross NPA.

2.   Recovery in write off cases.

3.   Recovery under ARB cases.

4.   To study and put up proposals before Administrators Committee received under OTS/CSS for consideration

5.   To take actions under SARFASI Act, Cases to be filed under DRT, filling of Civil/ Criminal/ Arbitration Cases etc.

6.   To complete the process for auction of Mortgage/Hypothecated Assets taken in the possession of the Bank.

7.    Actions for classification of Willful defaulters.

8.   Submission of any data/ details of information called by Regulatory and statuary Bodies.

9.    To take all actions as per the Recovery Policy approved by the Bank and observance of guidelines / instructions issued by Reserve Bank of India/ Statutory Bodies/ Government Bodies from time to time.

10.               To update and review recovery Policy of the bank at Yearly interval as per latest guidelines issued by our Bank, RBI & other regulatory authorities.

11.               Supervision of Day to Day working of the Recovery Dept.

 

ARC Related work

1.   Follow up with ARCs to whom the accounts have been sold for early redemption of SR

2.   Visits to ARCs and provide necessary support / documents required by them

3.   Arranging meeting with officials of ARCs

4.   Monitoring of Recovery made by ARCs and appropriation of the same.

5.   Identification of NPA accounts for sale as per policy.

6.   Arranging meeting of ARC Committee and confirming of NPA accounts for sale. Inviting EOI from ARCs,

7.   To monitor all process till sale is completed and Assignment Agreement is executed.

 

 Legal and Central Law Departments-

1.    Action for all Legal matters of the Bank including giving legal opinions.

2.   Follow up and actions for pending Court cases, cases pending with NCLT etc.

3.   Handling of matters at EOW,ED etc.

4.   Preparation of guidelines for training on legal matters.

5.   Attending to all legal matters such as those pertaining to Secretarial Dept. etc., other than those pertaining to Loan Recovery.

 

Branches/Departments are, therefore, advised to ensure that any data called for by General Manager- Legal and Recovery Department and any information called for by him documents required by him for verification / examination, during his visits to the Branches / Departments, or otherwise requisitioned by him, should be made available to him promptly within the time indicated by him. Any laxity or non-cooperation in the matter on the part of the officers at Branches / Departments, if reported by the General Manager will be viewed seriously by the Management.

 

General Manager-Legal and Recovery Department will be directly reporting to Chief Executive Officer.

 

The above list is not exhaustive but illustrative. He is also required to do any other work related to above and/or other work as entrusted to him from time to time by the CEO

 

In-charge of Branches/Depts. are advised to bring the contents of above Office Order to the knowledge of their Staff members.

 

 

 

 

Chief Executive Officer
