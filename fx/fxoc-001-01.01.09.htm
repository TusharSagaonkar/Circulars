Forex Circular OC No. 1 dated 1st January, 2009

Revision in Interest Rates on Export Credit.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

        Reserve Bank of India vide their Circular Nos. DBOD.Dir.(Exp).BC.No. 88/04.02.01/2008-09 dated 28th November, 2008 and DBOD.Dir.(Exp).BC.No. 95/04.02.01/2008-09 dated 8th December, 2008 has revised the Interest Rates on Export Post-shipment credit and on Sight/Demand/Usance Bills respectively. The matter was put up to the Board in its meeting held on 22nd December, 2008. The revised rates given in Annexure 'A' are applicable w.e.f. 28th November 2008.

 

2.     Vide RBI Circular dated 08/12/2008 referred to above the following interest rates would be applicable w.e.f. 08/12/2008.

 

i)       Interest Rates on Sight / Demand / Usance Bills up to 180 days (including overdue period) shall be 3.5% below PLR minimum 10% p.a.

ii)    Interest Rates on above bills which remain outstanding beyond 180 days shall be 2.5% above PLR minimum 16% p.a.
