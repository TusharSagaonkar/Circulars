Forex Circular No. 108 dated 5th April, 2016

Remittances to Non-Residents- Changes in

Guidelines related to Form 15CA & 15CB

 

Vide our Forex Circular No. 81 dated 10.04.2014, we had advised the Branches the revised procedure for furnishing information in Form 15CA & 15CB while making payments to Non-Residents.

Section 195 of the Income-Tax Act empowers the Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT) to capture information in respect of payments made to Non-Residents, whether chargeable to tax or not. Rule No. 37BB of Income-Tax Rules has been amended vide Notification No. 93/2015 dated 16.12.2015 with a view to reduce the burden of compliance in collecting information under section 195 of Income-Tax Act. The Amended Rules will be applicable from 01.04.2016.

The significant changes in the Amended Rule are :

i.         Form 15CA and 15CB are NOT required to be furnished by a Resident Individual for remittances up to the amount permitted by Reserve Bank of India under its Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS).

(Presently, under LRS, Resident Individuals are permitted by RBI to remit up to USD 2,50,000 or its equivalent per Financial Year (April-March) for any permitted Current or Capital Account Transactions or a combination of both. Please refer Forex Circular No. 69 dated 29.08.2013 for the definitions of Current and Capital Account Transactions)

ii.       The Specified List of 28 items where submission of Form 15CA and 15CB is exempted as of now is modified and the List now contains 33 items including Payments/Advance Payment against imports.

The revised guidelines with regard to submission of Form 15CA (ANNEXURE - I), which now contains 4 Parts, are furnished below :

i)        Part A : To be submitted if the remittance is chargeable to tax and the remittance or the aggregate of such remittances during the Financial Year does not exceed ` 5.00 Lakh;

ii)       Part B : To be submitted if the remittance is chargeable to tax and the remittance or the aggregate of such remittances during the Financial Year exceeds ` 5.00 Lakh and an order from Assessing Officer has been obtained;

iii)     Part C : To be submitted if the remittance is chargeable to tax and the remittance or the aggregate of such remittances during the Financial Year exceeds ` 5.00 Lakh and a certificate in Form No. 15CB (ANNEXURE-II) from a Chartered Accountant has been obtained;

iv)     Part D : To be submitted if the remittance is not chargeable to tax except for all the remittances appearing in the SPECIFIED LIST (ANNEXURE-III).

As per amended Rules, our bank will have to submit electronically a quarterly statement for each quarter of the Financial Year in Form 15CC to the Income-Tax Authorities under Digital Signature within 15 days from the end of the quarter furnishing details all the remittances made during the quarter.

Instructions to Branches :

i)        Wherever submission of Form 15CA and 15CB is applicable, “B” category Branches Authorised to make the remittances are advised to keep one copy of Form 15CA and/or 15CB, duly filled in and signed, along with the other documents pertaining to the remittance transaction, as the same needs to be produced to the Income-Tax Authorities on demand.

ii)       “C” category Branches i.e. Branches other than the above, are required to obtain the Form 15CA and/or 15CB, if applicable, in duplicate and forward both the copies to the Designated Branches concerned.

iii)     “B” category Branches should ensure that Form 15CA submitted by the Remitter Customer bears System Generated Acknowledgement Number, before effecting the remittance.

Instructions to Foreign Exchange Department, Dadar :

Foreign Exchange Department, Dadar shall file on quarterly basis Form 15CC furnishing details of all remittances made during the quarter such as Name of the Remitter, PAN of the Remitter, Name of the Remittee, PAN of the Remittee, if any, Date of Remittance, Country to which remittance is made and purpose code as per RBI Purpose List. The Form 15CC shall be electronically uploaded under Digital Signature within fifteen days from the end of the quarter of the Financial Year to the Principal Director General of Income-Tax (Systems) or the person authorised by him.

In-charge of all the Branches are advised to note the above and bring the contents of this Circular to the Customers who are effecting Foreign Remittances and to all the Members of the Staff for strict compliance.
