Forex Circular No.114 dated 22nd September, 2016

 

Non-Resident (Ordinary) Rupee (NRO) Account –

Joint Holding with Residents

 

 

        Please refer to Forex Circular No. 56 dated 19.11.2012 on Non-resident Deposits. In terms of this Circular, Non-Resident Ordinary Rupee Account Scheme - NRO accounts can be opened jointly with a resident in India. In the absence of any specific guidelines from Reserve Bank of India right from the beginning in regard to the mode of operation in such joint NRO accounts, by default they are being operated as “either or survivor”.

 

Vide Forex Circular No 111 dated 02.07.2016 on Non-Resident Deposit Accounts, we advised all branches certain important changes in the operation of Non Resident accounts based on the revised guidelines under Foreign Exchange Management ( Deposit) Regulations , 2016.

 

One of the important changes with regard to NRO accounts is that henceforth NRO accounts can be opened on “former or survivor” basis only.

 

Accordingly,Foreign Exchange Dealers Association of India (FEDAI) vide their Circular No. SPL-10/2-16 dated 02.09.2016 has communicated to us the instructions received from Reserve Bank of India which is as under:

 

“Authorised Dealer banks may provide their NRI customers one month of notice time for changing the status of their NRO accounts from ‘either or survivor’ to ‘former or survivor’, after which the account shall automatically be converted to single person’s account till the customer approaches its AD bank for changing the status to ‘former or survivor’ ”

 

In this regard Branches are advised that, wherever NRO accounts are maintained jointly and being operated as “either or survivor”, Such NRIs concerned should be contacted immediately and bring to their knowledge about the above revised guidelines of RBI in regard to operations in their NRO Joint accounts. They should be requested to change the mode of operation in their accounts as ‘former or survivor’ on urgent basis, in any case not later than a month from the date of receipt of communication from the bank in this regard. The draft of a letter to be sent by the branch concerned to the NRIs to their overseas address as per Bank record is as per ANNEXURE. If the branch is having the mail ID of the NRI customer in their record, the letter to him/her may be mailed to expedite the matter.

 

Those NRIs who do not comply with the above guidelines within the stipulated period of one month of communication from the Bank, the account shall automatically be converted to single person’s account till the customer approaches the bank for changing the status to ‘former or survivor’.

 

If any branch is having a keenly operated NRO SB account wherein the resident joint holder is operating the account on ‘either or survivor’ basis, such resident joint account holders may be guided to avail of the facility of Letter of Authority so they can continue to operate the account subject to the restrictions on operations by the Letter of Authority (LA) holder (Please refer Forex Circular No 111 dated 02.07.2016)

 

          The in-charge of all the branches are advised to note the above and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members.

 

 

P. R. Shetty

Managing Director
