Forex Circular OC No. 3 dated  18th June, 2009

 

Collection Policy for US$ denominated instrument drawn

and payable at USA

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

        Lot of our customers are submitting US $ denominated instruments to our branches for collection for receiving the proceeds for credit of their account. It is observed that different branches are following different procedure which some times resulting in delay in collection of foreign instruments.  To overcome the situation and to have a proper system and procedure for such collections, Reserve Bank of India vide their Circular letter No. RBI/2008-09/350 UBD (PCB), Cir No. 33/16.26.00/2008-09 dated 14th January, 2009, have advised to prepare the policy for collection of US$ denominated instruments. We have, therefore, prepared our Bank's Policy for collection of US$ denominated instrument which has been approved by our Board in its meeting held on 20.02.2009.

 

        The policy guidelines and the procedure for collection of US$ denominated instruments is as under:-

      1.     The US$ Instruments from the customer having properly conducted account and well known to the Bank should only be accepted for collection/purchase/discount and not from a walk-in-customer.

      2.     The instrument drawn should be in favour of the customer. Branches should avoid accepting third party/endorsed instrument for collection, purchase or discounting.

      3.     If the value of the instrument exceeds US$ 10000/- then branches should also obtain a purpose statement (FC(REM)5) inter-alia containing following information.

a)  Name and Address of the Remitter / Issuer of the instrument.

b)  Name and Address of the Beneficiary in whose favour the instrument has been issued.

c)   The country of remittance.

d)  The purpose of remittance. (Purpose should be invariably either Family Maintenance / Savings / Education Expenses / Medical Expenses / Investments / Others – specify).

      4.     Branches should enter the instrument in a collection register (maintained manually) and the running number should be put on the instrument with a round stamp of the branch on the left hand corner of

the instrument and crossing stamp in the centre of the instrument. On the reverse of the instrument, the branches will put a rubber stamp with the signature - "payees account will be credited on realization".

      5.     Branches should thereafter forward these instruments with purpose statement (if any) to Foreign Exchange Dept. Dadar  by Internal Delivery System so that the instruments are delivered to Foreign Exchange Dept., Dadar on the very next day. Foreign Exchange Dept. will be the pooling centre for the purpose of collection. Foreign Exchange Dept. will pass liability vouchers @ Rs.40/- per US$ (Notional Rate for US$).

      6.     On receipt of the instrument through internal delivery System at Foreign Exchange Dept. Dadar the same will be entered in the system and a system generated number will be put on the reverse of the instrument with round stamp.

      7.     In case, the customer desires to avail immediate credit against the instrument presented then branches should obtain a request letter from the customer, format of which is enclosed for ready reference        (Annexure-A).  Such request should be entertained only from the customers having undoubted integrity with good tract record and well known to the bank.

a)  When the amount of the instrument is upto USD 500:            On the request of the customer, branches may recommend Foreign Exchange Dept. for immediate credit to customer's account. Foreign Exchange Dept. may accede to such request from the branches. In such cases, Foreign Exchange Dept. will debit suspense account – Foreign Currency Instrument purchased and covert the foreign currency amount @ clean instruments purchase rate. From the proceeds, they will deduct commission amount @ 0.30% (min. Rs.56/-) and postages Rs.50/-.

b)  When the amount of the instrument is more than USD 500: After sending the instrument for collection, if the customer requests for instant credit and amount is not received in our Nostro account then the branch maintaining the customer's account on receiving such request from the customer for immediate credit may request foreign Exchange Dept. to discount the instrument. In such cases, the branch should get approval from the appropriate authority as per our bank's delegation of powers. While forwarding the request to Foreign Exchange Dept. branch should mention the sanctioning authority and date of sanction in the covering IDC. In such instances, Foreign Exchange Dept. will debit suspense account – Foreign Currency Instrument purchased and covert the Foreign Currency amount @ clean instruments purchase rate. From the proceeds, they will deduct commission amount @ 0.30% (min. Rs.56/-), Interest for 15 days @ 3.5% over PLR and postages Rs.50/-.

c)   When the request is received from the customer, after the credit is received in the Nostro account and during the cooling period then the amount of Foreign Currency will be converted @ TT buying Rate. From the proceeds, Foreign Exchange Dept. will deduct commission amount @ 0.30% (min. Rs.56/-) and postages Rs.50/-

d)  In all the above cases, Foreign Exchange Dept. will reverse the liability vouchers passed earlier and in cases of a) & b) above follow up with the foreign correspondent for receiving the credit proceeds at the earliest.

e)  In case, the instrument is returned unpaid for whatever reasons, Foreign Exchange Dept. will recover the amount @ TT Selling Rate ruling on that day alongwith foreign correspondent charges if any and actual postages incurred.

f)    In case, the instrument is lost in transit after dispatch, Bank will inform the customer and provide him all factual position. In case he insists for any certificate to obtain duplicate instrument Bank will extend all assistance to him.

g)  In case, the instrument is misplaced at our end and there is a lapse on the part of our officials then Bank will refund all charges debited to customers account towards collection and also assist him in getting duplicate instrument

      8.     All the instruments received for collection will be sent to our foreign correspondent where our US$ Nostro Account is maintained. Presently, we are maintaining our US$ account with Bank of India, New York. Hence these instruments will be sent to them. Normally, these instruments will be sent by Registered AD unless the customer specifically requests for sending the same by Courier Service at the cost of customer.

      9.     Normally, our Foreign correspondent in US credits our account with the proceeds within a week's time. However, in USA, there is a concept of Cooling Period (waiting period) which is as under:-

a. US$ instrument payable in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut

SEVEN U.S. working days from value date

b. US$ instruments payable other than (a) above.

ELEVEN U.S. working days from value date.

 

    10.    In addition to this, in U.S. there is a separate law in respect of fraudulent encashment where the limitation of time for claim can be extended upto 36 month. i.e. if the instrument collected and credited to the Nostro Account is found to be fraudulent then the U.S. correspondent can debit our Nostro account upto 36 months from the date of credit to our Nostro account.

 

   11.   Branches should note both the aspects and advise the customer regarding the cooling period concept and fraudulent cheque act.

    12.    Foreign Exchange Dept., Dadar after receiving the credit in the Nostro account and completion of the cooling period, will convert the foreign currency amount in Indian rupees at the ruling TT buying rate of that day. They will deduct following charges:

i.         Collection Charges - @ 0.30% (minimum Rs.56/-) per instrument + out of pocket expenses – postage as per cost incurred, presently Rs.50/- per instrument. In case, the instrument is sent by courier service at the request of the customer then actual courier charges will be minimum Rs.500/-.

ii.       Thus, minimum charges for collection of cheque shall be Rs.106/- per instrument and maximum amount shall depend upon the value of the instrument collected.

iii.     Bank will issue FIRC (Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate) for which a separate charge of Rs.60/- per instrument will be levied.

 

    13.    Normally, the collection period shall be upto one month. However, incase for whatsoever the reasons, the instrument is not collected within a month, Foreign Exchange Dept. will follow the matter to know the fate at no extra cost till the receipt of proceeds or return of the instrument.

    14.    If the instrument is not collected for any fault or lapse on the part of the bank then bank will pay interest to the customer @ LIBOR for the delayed period beyond 40 days till date of credit to the customer's account. If the period for collection exceeds two months then the Bank will pay interest @ Banks Fixed Deposit Rate for the applicable period.

   15.   If the customer wishes to place the proceeds of the instrument in any foreign currency account such as FCNR/RFC/EEFC Account then the customer should inform the bank about his/her intentions at the time of depositing the instrument or before the instrument proceeds are converted into Indian rupees by application of TT Buying rate. In such cases, branch will issue receipt to the customer from the date of credit to the Nostro account.

 

        Please take a suitable note of these policy guidelines and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all the concerned staff at your branch and ensure compliance.
