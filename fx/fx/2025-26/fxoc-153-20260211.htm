Forex Circular No. 153 dated 11th February 2026

Export and Import of Indian Currency to or from Nepal & Bhutan

 

 

Reserve Bank of India vide A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No 18 dated 08.12.2025, has advised the following in connection with Export and Import of Indian currency to and from Nepal and Bhutan in supersession of their earlier instructions:

Banks may allow a person not being a citizen of Pakistan or Bangladesh, to-

(i)          take or send out of India to Nepal or Bhutan, and bring into India from Nepal or Bhutan, currency notes of Government of India and Reserve Bank of India Notes for any amount in denominations up to Rs 100/-;

(ii)         take out of India to Nepal or Bhutan, notes of denominations of above Rs 100/- up to a total limit of 25,000/-

(iii)       bring into India from Nepal or Bhutan, notes of denominations of above Rs 100/- up to a total of limit of Rs 25,000/-

 

All the branches are advised to note the above and bring the contents of this circular to the attention of the members of staff and customers who travel to Nepal and Bhutan.

Clarifications, if any, may be obtained from Foreign Exchange Department, Dadar.

 

 

 

 

Chief Executive Officer
