Forex Circular No. 20 dated 29th April, 2011

Revision in Service Tax on Purchase and Sale of Foreign Currency

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

         Government of India, vide their Notification No. 26.2011 dated 31.03.2011, has prescribed the following Service Tax rates, for collection of Service Tax on the Foreign Currency Transactions including money changing with effect from 01.04.2011.

 

Amount of Foreign Currency Exchanged in Rupee Value

Amount of Service Tax to be recovered for each purchase or sale transaction

Upto 1,00,000

0.1% of the amount, subject to a minimum of Rs.25

1,00,001 to 10,00,000

Rs.100 + 0.05% of the amount exceeding Rs.1,00,000/-

Above 10,00,000

Rs.550 + 0.01% of the amount exceeding 10,00,000/- Subject to a maximum of Rs.5,000

 

1)   Along with the Service Tax amount to be recovered as per the above Table, applicable Education Cess (2% of the Service Tax Amount) and Secondary and Higher Education Cess (1% of the Service Tax amount) should also be collected.

2)   The above rates are Service Tax amount to be recovered from the customer for each purchase or sale transaction (including cross currency transactions of the customers).

3)   Branches should collect service tax with applicable cess for forex transaction, which involve only ready reporting of purchase or sale.

For example : Transactions involving outward remittances out of EEFC balances or crediting to EEFC account does not involve reporting of purchase or sale and hence, no Services Tax need be collected.

4)   With regard to cross currency transactions, branch shall calculate the Indian Rupee equivalent of the respective currencies at the inter bank rate and the higher rupee value of the two currencies to be considered for arriving at the Service Tax.

5)   All Interbank Foreign Exchange transactions with any bank including a bank located outside India or money changer, are exempt from the levy of Service Tax.

6)   The Service Tax with applicable cess should be collected when the transaction is put through by the customer or when the actual delivery is taken under forward contract. In other words, Service tax should not be collected at the time of booking of forward contract. This guideline is applicable to Cross Currency forward contracts also.

7)   The amount of Service Tax with cess shall be netted off before giving final credit to the customer's account in case of purchase transactions or debited to customer's account alongwith the transaction amount in the case of sale transactions. In other words, please ensure collection of Service Tax with applicable cess while handling the transaction itself.

8)   As regards accounting of the Service Tax collected from the customers, branches shall use the following GL Account Heads :

Service Tax Payable A/c…………………..(STPAY)

Education Cess Payable A/c …………….(ECPAY)

Higher Education Cess payable A/c……(HECPAY)

 

         Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all concerned members of the staff at your Branch/ Department and ensure compliance.
