Forex Circular No.145 dated 5th March 2024

Fair Lending Practice-Penal Charges on Loan/1accounts – Pre & Post Shipment export finance

 

Our Bank is extending Pre & Post Shipment Export finance to our exporter customers, both in Rupee and in Foreign Currency. The Rate of Interest (ROI) is fixed as per the guidelines of Reserve Bank of India which is linked to our Prime Lending Rate (PLR).

 

As per our Interest rate directives on Export Credit, in the case of Pre-shipment credit, if the shipment does not take place on or before the due date of the PC even after extension of time for the shipment, we recover our finance by charging higher rate of interest for the entire period of advance. This ROI is inclusive of Penal Interest rate/1of 2%.

 

Similarly, in the case of Post-shipment export finance, if the related export bill is remains unpaid or returned unpaid and advance is recovered from the exporter, we collect interest by charging higher rate which is inclusive of Penal Interest rate of 2%.

 

Reserve Bank of India, vide their communication RBI/2023-23/53-DoR.MCS.REC.28 / 01.01.001/2023-24dated 18.08.2023, has observed that penalty, if charged, for non-compliance of material terms and conditions of loan contract by the borrower (exporter, in our case) shall be treated as ‘penal charges’ and shall not be levied in the form of ‘penal interest’ that is to added to the rate of interest charged on the advances. RBI further states that there shall be no capitalization of penal charges i.e., no further interest computed on such charges.

 

RBI has advised that banks shall not introduce any additional component to the rate of interest and the quantum of penal charges shall be reasonable and commensurate with the non-compliance of material terms and conditions without being discriminatory within a particular loan/product category.

 

RBI has advised that banks shall formulate Board approved policy on penal charges or similar charges on loans, by whatever name called.

 

RBI has advised that the quantum of such a charge and the reason for levying the same shall be clearly disclosed to the customer in the loan agreements and in the Sanction Terms & Conditions. It should be also displayed in the Bank’s website under Interest rates and Service Charges.

 

RBI has stipulated that the revised instructions on penal charges shall into effect from 01.01.2024, however, as per their notification Ref:RBI/2023-24/102 DoRMCS.REC.61/ 01/ 01.01.001/2023-24 Dt: 29Tth December, 2023, considering the certain clarification & additional time sought/1by the Regulated Entities (RE) to configure their internal/1system and operationalization the changes, they have extended the timeline for the implementation of the instruction by 3 months, and will now be applicable from 01st April, 2024 in respect of all the fresh loans availed from/101st April, 2024/1and in case of existing loans the switchover to the new penal charges regime shall be ensured from next review/renewal date falling on or after 1st April, 2024, but not later than 30th June, 2024.

 

Keeping the above in view, our Chief Executive Officer has on 28th December 2023 permitted that in regard to Export finance, both in INR and in FC, a fixed penal charge as detailed below may be collected from the exporters instead of levying a penal interest of 2% p.a. at the time of recovery of our advance:

All the branches are advised to note the above and bring the contents of this circular to our exporter customers and all the members of staff.

Designated Vashi and Pimpri branches are advised to note the above for strict compliance.

Role of the following Departments for incorporating this changes:

1.   Accounts Dept.:

Accounts Department to inform the IT department to create a new P/L head “Penal charges on Export Finance” in the General Ledger

2.   I.T. Department:

The Vendor shall be informed to remove “Penal Interest” in the parameters and add “Penal Charges” at the rates as stipulated above in the Laser Soft/Prophesy and to ensure that the same is posted to our CBS system without manual intervention.

3.   Legal Department:

To suitably modify AGREEMENT FOR FBP/FBD/FBN FACILITY (Agreement for Foreign Bill Purchase/ Discount/ Negotiation) Code no.1048 and AGREEMENT FOR HYPOTHECATION OF STOCK UNDER PACKING CREDIT FACILITY, Code no.1049 to include the details of the penal charges.

4.   Development Dept.:

To print both the modified documents, viz, AGREEMENT FOR FBP/FBD/FBN FACILITY (Agreement for Foreign Bill Purchase/ Discount/ Negotiation) Code no.1048 and AGREEMENT FOR HYPOTHECATION OF STOCK UNDER PACKING CREDIT FACILITY, Code no.1049

5.   Forex Department:

1. To issue communication to the existing exporters.

2. The charges to be included in Service Charges manual of the Forex dept., subject to review every year.

 

Forex Department shall co-ordinate with all concerned departments to ensure that the above changes have been duty carried out latest on or before 15th March, 2024.
