Forex Circular No. 135 dated 10th August, 2021

 

Revision in Interest Rates on Export Credit w.e.f. 01.08.2021

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

I.                Please refer Forex Circular No.126 dated 30th April, 2020 relating to interest rates on Export Credit effective 1st May, 2020.

 

II. Consequent upon reduction in the Bank’s Prime Lending Rate (BPLR) by 100 basis points i.e. from 12.00 % to 11.00 % and revision in Rate of Interest on Loans & Advances w.e.f. 1st August, 2021 it has been decided to revise interest rates on Export Credit w.e.f. 1st August, 2021. The revised interest rates are enclosed herewith.

 

III.   As we are also extending Export Credit in Foreign Currency to our exporters at interest rates linked to LIBOR, we have furnished the interest rates applicable for such credit along with the interest rates for Rupee Export Credit.

 

IV.   All the in-charge of the branches are advised to note the above and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members and exporter customers.

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
