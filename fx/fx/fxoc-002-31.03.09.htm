Forex Circular OC No. 2 dated 31st March, 2009

 

Tax Deduction at Source (TDS)

for NRE/NRO Deposit Accounts

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

        Tax on Non Residents is deductible u/s 195 of the Income Tax 1961. The position is as follows:

 

Nature of A/c.

 

TDS Rate for

Interest less than 10 Lakh

TDS Rate for

Interest 10 Lakh & above

1.    NRO

  i) Interest on Term          Deposit

and/or

  ii) Interest on Saving Bank

30.90%

 

 

 

 

33.99%

 

2.    NRE

Exempt u/s 10(4). Hence not liable to TDS.

 

 

 

The rate of 30.90% is arrived as follows:

30% + 2% Education Cess + 1% Secondary & Higher Education Cess

 

The rate of 30.99% is arrived as follows:

30% + 10% Surcharge + 2% Education Cess + 1% Secondary & Higher Education Cess.

 

Non-Resident cannot file declaration in Form No. 15G/15H for non-deduction of tax at source.

 

Non-Residents can however apply for a lower deduction of tax certificate u/s 197 from the Income Tax Officer and file the same with the person deducting tax. This Certificate will specify the rate at which tax has to be deducted.

 

Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all the concerned staff at your branch and ensure compliance.
