Forex Circular No. 150 dated 28th October, 2024

Interest Equalization Scheme on Pre and Post Shipment Rupee Export Credit – Extension

 

 

We invite your kind attention to Forex Circular No. 146 dated 19th March 2024 relating to Interest Equalization on Pre-Shipment and Post Shipment Rupee Export Credit to eligible exporters.

At the instance of the Government of India, Reserve Bank of India had advised the banks to extend interest equalization of 3% for MSME manufacturer exporters exporting under any HS (Harmonized System) line and 2% for Manufacturers and Merchant Exporters exporting under 410 HS Line for the period 01st April, 2024 to 30th June, 2024.

Reserve Bank of India vide their Notification No. RBI/2024-25/71 DOR.STR. REC.41/04.02.001/2024-25 dated 29th August, 2024 have further extended the Interest Equalization scheme for Pre and Post-Shipment Rupee Export Credit effective from 01st July, 2024 to 31st August, 2024 with the total of the scheme capped at Rs.750 Crore and Rs.1.66 Crore per Importer-Exporter code (IEC) for the said extended period of the scheme. Since the circular was received very late i.e. just 2 days before the end of the extension of the scheme, Forex Department had sought the approval from the Administrator’s committee on 03rd September, 2024 and implemented the scheme.

Again, in a fresh notification from the Reserve Bank of India under reference RBI/2024-25/76/DOR.STR.REC.41/04.02.001/2024-25 dated 20th September, 2024, further extending the scheme from 01st September, 2024 to 30th September, 2024, only for MSME manufacturer exporters with a cap of Rs.5.00 Crore per IEC for the extended period. As for manufacturer exporters and Merchant Exporters under non-MSME category, the scheme has been closed on 30th June, 2024. Apart from these modification, other terms and conditions remains the same. The same was approved by the Chief Executive Officer on 30.09.2024

However, in all 2 above extensions, no Forex Circulars were issued due to late receipt of the notification.

Now we are in receipt of a fresh RBI notification Ref:No.RBI/2024-25/80/ DOR.STR.REC.45/04.02.001/2024-25 Dt:09.10.2024, extending the IES from 01.10.2024 to 31.12.2024 for Pre & Post Shipment Rupee Export Credit only for MSME manufacturer exporters, subject to the following conditions:

a)   Fiscal benefits of each MSME, on aggregate, will be restricted to Rs. 50 lakhs for the Financial Year 2024-25 till December 31, 2024.

 

b)   Accordingly, MSME manufacturer exporters who have already availed equalization benefits of Rs. 50 lakhs or more in the Financial Year 2024-25 till September 30, 2024, will not be eligible for any further benefit in the extended period.

In-charge of all the branches are advised to note the above and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members and exporter customers and ensure compliance.

 

 

 

P.S. Salian

Chief Executive Officer
