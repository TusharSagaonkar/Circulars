Forex Circular No 125 dated 22nd January, 2020

 

Special Non-Resident Rupee Account ( SNRR account)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

I. Attention of branches is invited to our Forex Circular No. 115 dated 18th October 2016 furnishing the salient features and guidelines for opening and maintenance of following Non-Resident Deposit accounts:

1)   Non-Resident (External) Rupee Account Scheme -NRE

2)   Non-Resident Ordinary Rupee Account Scheme -NRO

3)   Foreign Currency (Non-Residnet) Account ( Banks) Scheme-FCNR(B)

 

II. 1. In terms of paragraph 7 of part II of Master Direction No.14 dated January 01,2016 on “Deposits and Accounts”, as amended from time to time, any person resident outside India, having a business interest in India, may open a Special Non-Resident Rupee account (SNRR account) with an authorized dealer for the purpose of putting through bona fide transactions in rupees.

2. i. Vide A.P. (DIR Series) Circular No. 09 dated 22..11.2019, Reserve Bank of India has now permitted opening of SNRR accounts by persons resident outside India for the following purposes also:

a.   External Commercial Borrowings in Indian Rupee (INR);

b.   Trade Credits ( Buyer's Credit/Supplier's Credit) in INR;

c.    Trade ( Export/Import) invoicing in INR ; and

d.   Business related transactions outside International Financial Services Centre (IFSC) by IFSC units at GIFT City (Gujarat International Finance Tec-City), such as administrative expenses in INR outside IFSC, INR amount from sale of scrap, Government incentives in INR etc. The account will be maintained by bank in India ( outside IFSC).

(IFSC: It is a jurisdiction that provides financial services to resident and non-resident Indians in foreign currencies. GIFT IFSC is a deemed foreign territory dealing in foreign currency. The units in IFSC are recognised as non-resident entities under the FEMA regulations by Reserve Bank of India).

ii. The salient features of SNRR account are as under:

a) The SNRR account will carry the nomenclature of the specific business for which it is opened and not earn any interest.

b) The debits/credits and the balances in the account should be incidental and commensurate with the business operations of the account holder.         

c)  The operations in the SNRR account should not result in the account holder making available foreign exchange to any person resident in India against reimbursement in rupees or in any other manner.

d)  The balances in the SNRR account can be repatriated outside India.

e) The tenure of the SNRR account should be concurrent to the tenure of the contract/period of operation/the business of the account holder and in no case should exceed seven years. Approval of Reserve Bank shall be obtained in cases requiring renewal. However, this restriction of seven years shall not be applicable if the acccount is opened for the purposes mentioned in Para II(2)(i) above from a to d. Further, this restriction shall not be applicable to SNRR accounts opened by a person resident outside India for the purpose of making investments in India in accordance with Foreign Exchange Management (Transfer of security by a person resident outside India) Regulations, 2017, as amended from time to time.

f) Transfers from any NRO account to the SNRR account are not permitted.

g) All transactions in the SNRR account will be subject to payment of applicable taxes in India.                                                                                  

h) SNRR account may be designated as resident rupee account on the account holder becoming a resident.

i) The amount due/payable to non-resident nominee from account of a deceased SNRR account holder may be credited to the NRE/ NRO account or direct remittance outside India through normal banking channels.

j) Opening of SNRR accounts by Pakistan and Bangladesh nationals and entities incorporated in Pakistan and Bangladesh requires prior appproval of Reserve Bank.

 

III. As certain additional precautions need to be taken for opening and maintaining the SNRR accounts, it has been decided to open the SNRR accounts at our Dadar branch only. Whenever our branches receive any enquiry on opening of SNRR account, the same may be redirected to our Foreign Exchange Departent, Dadar for their handling the matter.

 

IV. All the Branch Incharges are advised to note the above and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members.
