Forex Circular No. 128 dated 14th October, 2020

Tax Collection at Source on Remittances under Liberalized Remittance Scheme

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

         I. Attention of branches is invited to the Forex Circular No. 107 dated 01.04.2016 and Forex Circular No. 94 dated 10.06.2015 on Reserve Bank of India’s Liberalized remittance Scheme (LRS). Under the scheme, all resident individuals including minors are permitted to effect outward remittances upto USD 250000, in any financial year (April to March) for any current account or capital account transactions or a combination of both, subject to certain conditions. It is mandatory for the resident individual to provide his/ her Permanent Account Number (PAN) to make remittances under the LRS scheme.

 

        II.   Introduction of Tax collected at Source (TCS) of 5% on foreign remittances out of India was announced in the Union Budget 2020. Accordingly, a new section 206C(1G) to levy TCS on LRS remittances effective 01st October, 2020 was inserted in the Income Tax Act, 1961 as detailed below.

 

“(IG) Every person: -

 

a)   Being an authorised dealer, who receives an amount, for remittance out of India from a buyer, being a person remitting such amount out of India under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme of the Reserve Bank of India:

 

b)   Being a seller of an overseas tour program package, who receives any amount from a buyer, being the person who purchases a package.

 

Shall at the time of debiting the amount payable by the buyer or at the time of receipt of such amount from the said buyer, by any mode, whichever is earlier, collect from the buyer, a sum equal to five per cent of such amount as income-tax:

 

Provided that the authorised dealer shall not collect the sum, if the amount or aggregate of the amounts being remitted by a buyer is less than seven lakh rupees in a financial year and is for a purpose other than purchase of overseas tour program package:

 

Provided further that the sum to be collected by an authorised dealer from the buyer shall be equal to five per cent. of the amount or aggregate of the amounts in excess of seven lakh rupees remitted by the buyer in a financial year, where the amount being remitted is for a purpose other than purchase of overseas tour program package:

 

Provided also that the authorized dealer shall collect a sum equal to one half per cent of the amount or aggregate of the amounts in excess of seven lakh rupees remitted by the buyer in a financial year, if the amount being remitted out is a loan obtained from any financial institution as defined in section 80E, for the purpose of pursuing any education

 

Provided also that the authorized dealer shall not collect the sum on an amount in respect of which the sum has been collected by the seller:

 

The above TCS provision shall not apply if the buyer is-

 

 (a)   liable to deduct tax at source under any other provision of the Act and has deducted such amount.

 

 (b)  the Central Government, a State Government, an embassy, a High Commission, legation, commission, consulate, the trade representation of a foreign State, a local authority as defined in Explanation to clause (20) of section 10 or any other person notified by the Central Government in the Official Gazette for this purpose subject to such conditions as specified in that notification.”

 

      III.    The changes are summarized hereunder :

1. Tax collected at Source (TCS) at 5% shall be on aggregate forex transactions under LRS exceeding INR 7.00 Lakh in a financial year.

2. For remittances towards pursuing overseas education, TCS at 0.5% shall be applicable, if the amount remitted is obtained through a loan from financial institution as defined under Section 80 E of the I T Act.

3. For remittances to Foreign Tour Operators through the Bank, 5% TCS of the total amount remitted shall be applicable and the amount remitted will not be subsumed under the threshold limit of INR 7 Lakh mentioned above.

4. The above provision is effective from October 1, 2020.

5. For calculating the threshold limit of Rs. 7.00 lakh, remittances made from 01st April,2020 should be considered. For example, if we have made total remittances of Rs.7,50,000/- from 1st April,2020, then the same is subject to TCS on all the remittances done on or after 1st October,2020. The TCS rate shall be determined depending on the purpose of the remittance.

6. It may be noted that for the purpose of calculating the threshold limit of Rs.7.00 lakh, remittances made for the purpose of foreign tour package shall not be considered, as such TCS shall be deducted @ 5% on the entire amount remitted. The same can be summarized as follows: -

      IV.    Role of branches :

1.   Whenever any resident individual (KYC compliant account holder) approaches for LRS remittances, branch officials should apprise him/her about the TCS on LRS.

2.   FORM A2 (LRS) submitted by the remitter should be complete in all respects. Branch officials should ensure that the purpose of transaction is mentioned correctly (i. e. whether remittances to Tour operators/ for pursuing education under finance from Financial Institution defined under Income Tax Act/ other LRS).

3.   Branch officials should ensure that sufficient balance is maintained in the account for total remittance amount + applicable TCS (5% OR 0.5%) + applicable charges (viz commission, SWIFT charges, foreign bank charges and GST).

4.   Branch official should certify the KYC compliance and due diligence for source of funds before forwarding the FORM A2 and other related documents (scanned copies through exchange server or Rediff mail) to the designated branch.

5.   Branches under Western Zone and Central Zone will forward the FORMs to Dadar (Forex), Branches under Navi Mumbai Zone will forward the FORMs to Vashi (Forex) and Branches under Pune Zone will forward the FORMs to Pimpri (Forex).

6.   TCS certificates generated by Accounts Dept. will be mailed to designated branches and the designated branches will forward the same to the respective branchesfor printing and issuing to their customers.

7.   In case of remittances for pursuing overseas education, branches should specify whether the proceeds for it is done out of education loan (from financial institutions defined under the Income tax act) or from own contribution for deducting correct TCS.

       V.   Role of Designated branches (Dadar, Vashi & Pimpri) :

1.   All designated branches will maintain records of all LRS remittances (including transactions routed through YES Bank/ Federal Bank) during the current FY 2020-21 undertaken for their own branches, and for branches in their respective zones.

2.   Designated branches (Vashi and Pimpri) will also deduct TCS on remittances under LRS routed through YES Bank Ltd/ Federal Bank, before forwarding them to Dadar Forex.

3.   All designated branches will originate the TCS amount collected during the month to Accounts Dept on the 1st working day of the succeeding month.

 

      VI.   Role of Accounts Dept:

1.   Tax originated by designated branches shall be deposited in Government account within one-week under Centralized TAN: MUMA01872D.

2.   Quarterly TCS returns in form 27EQ shall be filed before the due date under our centralized TAN MUMA01872D.

Returns due date: Q1 - 15th July

                             Q2 - 15th Oct.

                             Q3 - 15th Jan.

                             Q4 - 15th May.

3.   TCS certificates shall be issued in form No.27D within 15 days of filing of returns, by downloading from TRACES website; the same will be forwarded to respective designated branches.

4.   Will help designated branches to resolve any issue of TCS default that may arise after the processing of the TCS Return.

 

    VII.    Branches are advised to note carefully the amended provisions of CBDT as above and bring the contents of this communication to the notice of customers and the staff members concerned at the Branch.
