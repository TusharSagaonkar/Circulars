Forex Circular OC No. 4 dated 1st July, 2009

 

Revision in Interest Rates on Export Credit w.e.f. 01.07.2009

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

        You may be aware that up till now our Bank was permitted to determine our own lending rates taking into account our cost of funds, transaction costs etc., with the approval of our Board.

        At the instance of Government of India, now, Reserve Bank of India vide their Circular No. UBD.PCB.Cir. No. 3/13.05.000/2008-09 dated 18.06.2009 have informed us to extend Interest Subvention of 2 percentage points w.e.f.                   1st June, 2009 on pre and post shipment rupee export credit extended for certain employment oriented export sectors as under:

i.             Textiles (including Handloom)

ii.           Handicrafts

iii.         Carpets

iv.         Leather

v.           Gems and Jewellery

vi.         Marine Products, and

vii.        Small & Medium Enterprises (as defined below)

 

Enterprises engaged in the manufacture or production, processing or preservation of goods as specified below:

i)            A micro enterprise is an enterprise where investment in plant and machinery (original cost excluding land and building and the items specified by the Ministry of Small Industries vide its notification No. S.O. 1722(E) dated 5th October, 2006) does not exceed Rs.25 Lakh.

ii)          A small enterprise is an enterprise where the investment in plant and machinery (original cost excluding land and building and the items specified by the Ministry of Small Industries vide its notification No. S.O.1722(E) dated 5th October, 2006) is more than Rs.25 Lakh but does not exceed Rs.5 Crore; and

iii)         A medium enterprises is an enterprise where the investment in plant and machinery (original cost excluding land and building and the items specified by the Ministry of Small Industries vide its notification No. S.O.1722(E) dated 5th October, 2006) is more than Rs.5 Crore; but not exceed Rs.10 Crore.

 

Reserve Bank of India have further stipulated that the Interest Rate after subvention should not fall below 7 percent.

The matter was put up to the Board in its meeting held on 30.06.2009 and as approved by the board we give below the revised Interest Rates on Export Credit, with effect from 1st July, 2009.
