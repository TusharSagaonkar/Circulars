Forex Circular No. 23 dated 2nd September, 2011

 

Money Changing Business for Non-Customers

 

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Reserve Bank of India  has sent a communication to all the banks ( A D Category banks) bringing to their notice that they are not extending money changing activities to the non-customers  which is causing  hardship to the  public.

 

Money changing activities involve Encashment (purchase) of Foreign Currency Travelers Cheques /Currency Notes (FC TC/CN) and Release (sale) of foreign exchange in the form of FC TC/CN.

 

To ensure that the banks are following their guidelines, RBI officials are conducting surprise checks at the bank branches posing as non-customers.

 

As per our existing guidelines, all our branches are authroised to encash FC TC/CN upto USD 250 or its equivalent in other currencies to non-customers such as foreign tourists or NRIs on a visit to India  who are not having account relationship with us.

 

As regards Sale of FC TC/CN to non-customers, currently no guidelines have been issued to our branches.

 

As per the general provisions of the Reserve Bank of India under Forex Facilities for Resident Indians, we can sell foreign exchange upto the prescribed limits for various purposes by way of TCs / CNs to non-customers.

 

Selling of foreign exchange to non-customers means selling to resident Indians (excluding foreign nationals and NRIs ) who are not having any account relationship with our bank.

 

As regards the payment to be collected from them, the following are the guidelines of RBI:

 

Payment in Rupees against sale of foreign exchange:

 

A) Cash UPTO Rupee equivalent of Rs50,000/-

                                                                        

When Rupee equivalent exceeds Rs 50,000/-

 

i)            By a crossed cheque  drawn on the applicant’s bank account   OR

ii)          Crossed cheque on the bank account of the firm/company sponsoring the visit of the applicant, or Banker’s Cheque/Pay Order/Demand Draft.

                                                                                               

 

B) However, irrespective of the rupee equivalent, whenever foreign exchange is released for the purposes of Education Abroad, Medical Treatment, Employment and Emigration, payment for such sale is to be received only by means of a cheque or demand draft.

 

While releasing foreign exchange in the form of Foreign Currency Notes, branches are advised to be guided by the contents of our Forex Circular No.10 dated 12th May 2010.

 

RBI has asked all the banks to ensure that permissible forex related services including money changing facilities are being provided at our branches even to the non-customers at competitive rates and after ensuring adherence to the extant KYC/AML/CFT guidelines.

 

All our branches are advised to note the above carefully  for the sale of foreign exchange to non-customers ( excluding foreign nationals and  NRIs) after strictly adhering to the KYC/AML/CFT guidelines and also after the clearance of the instrument tendered by such non-customers  which has been presented in clearing where the  payment  exceeds Rs 50,000/-.

 

Please bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the staff members without fail. Whenever any non-customers approach our branches, they should not be turned away for the reason that they are not having accounts with us.

 

You may contact the Foreign Exchange Department, Dadar, Mumbai for any clarification in the matter.
