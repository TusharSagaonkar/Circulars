Forex Circular No 118 dated 4th July, 2017

 

Goods and Services Tax (GST) on Purchase or Sale of Foreign Currency including Money Changing w.e.f. 01.07.2017

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Goods and Services Tax (GST) has been implemented by the Government with effect from 01.07.2017. A detailed Circular OC No.764 dated 30.06.2017 has been issued on various aspects of implementation of the GST including compliance with the related statutory requirements.

 

GST are of three types- CGST i.e Central CST, SGST i. e State GST and IGST i. e. Integrated CST. If supplier and receiver of goods and services are in the same state, then two GST will be levied i. e. CGST and SGST; and if supplier and receiver of goods or services are in different states, then one GST will be levied i.e. IGST.

 

GST should be collected on purchase or sale of foreign currency including money changing activities. It may be noted that for collecting GST on forex transactions, deemed value of the said transaction should be first ascertained for the purpose of levying the GST.

 

Govt. of India vide their Draft GST Valuation Rules, has prescribed the following GST rates.

 

Amount of purchase or sale of foreign currency (In Rupees value)

Value deemed for purchase or sale transaction

 GST to be recovered at the rate of 18%

Upto Rs. 1,00,000/-

One per cent of the gross amount of purchase or sale of foreign currency, subject to a minimum amount of two hundred and fifty rupees

18% of one per cent of the gross amount of purchase or sale of foreign currency, subject to a minimum of Rs. 45/-

 

For example , if value of gross amount of currency exchanged is Rs. 50,000/- , then the deemed value is Rs. 500/-and GST to be recovered at GST 18% is Rs. 90

 

It should be noted that minimum GST recoverable is Rs. 45 (i.e. GST to be recovered at 18% on minimum deemed value of Rs. 250/)

 

 

Rs. 1,00,001/- to Rs. 10,00,000

One thousand rupees and half a per cent (0.50%) of the gross amount of currency exchanged for an amount exceeding one lakh rupees and up to ten lakh rupees.

Example:

If value of currency exchanged is Rs. 5,00,000/-, GST is to be computed as under:

For first one lakh, deemed value is Rs. 1000/-

 

For remaining amount of Rs 4,00,000/- , deemed value is Rs. 2000/- ( i.e. 0.5% of Rs. 4,00,000/-).

 

So total deemed value is Rs. 3000/-. GST to be recovered is Rs. 540 (Rs. 3000 multiplied by GST rate of 18%

Above Rs. 10,00,000/-

Five thousand and five hundred rupees and one tenth of a per cent (0.1%) of the gross amount of currency exchanged for an amount exceeding ten lakh rupees, subject to maximum amount of sixty thousand rupees

Example:

If value of currency exchanged is Rs. 20,00,000/- , GST is to be computed as under:

For first ten lakh, deemed value is Rs. 5500/- For remaining amount of Rs. 10,00,000/-, deemed value is Rs. 1000/- ( i.e. 0.1 % of Rs. 10,00,000/-)

 

So total deemed value is Rs. 6500. GST at 18% is Rs. 1170/-

 

However, if value of currency exchanged is Rs. 5,55,00,000/-and above, deemed value is Rs. 60,000/- and GST to be recovered is Rs. 10,800/-( i.e. Rs. 60,000 multiplied by GST rate of 18%).

 

So maximum GST recoverable at 18% is

Rs. 10,800/-

 

Please note that recoverable GST computed as above will change, if proposed rate of GST at 18% is revised.

 

NOTE:

 

1)   The above rates are GST to be recovered from the customer for each purchase or sale transaction including cross currency transactions of customers w.e.f. 01.07.2017. GST should be collected only for those purchase or sale transactions, which involve ready or forward reporting of purchase or sale.

 

2)   Transactions involving outward remittances from out of balances held in EEFC accounts or crediting to EEFC accounts do not involve forex reporting and hence, GST need NOT be collected.

 

3)   With regard to cross currency transactions, branch to calculate the Indian Rupee equivalent of the respective currencies at the interbank rate and the higher rupee value of the two currencies to be considered for arriving at the deemed value and GST thereon.

 

4)   GST should not be collected at the time of booking of forward contract. It should be collected only on the delivery date when the currencies are exchanged.

 

5)   GST on purchase or sale of foreign currency should be collected by the branch which has originated the transaction.

 

 GST on Deemed value of the forex transaction is to be paid as under:

 

ñ For Services provided by a Branch within the State (Intra-state):

 

 SGST @9% to be paid to local State Government and

 CGST@ 9% would be paid to Central Government

 

ñ For Services provided by a Branch outside the State (Interstate)

 

 IGST @ 18% to be given to Central Government

 

GST collected from the customers should be credited in the General Ledger as follows:

 

 CGSTFXPY- CGST Payable A/c (Forex)

 SGSTFXPY- SGST Payable A/c (Forex)

 IGSTFXPY – IGST Payable A/c (Forex)

 

Please contact Foreign Exchange Department, Dadar for clarification, if any.

 

In-charge of the branches are advised to note the above and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all members of staff.
