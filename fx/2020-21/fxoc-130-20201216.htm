Forex Circular No. 130 dated 16th December, 2020

FX- Retail Trading Platform

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------         

I.   Clearing Corporation of India Ltd (CCIL) under the direction of RBI, had provided access to FX-clear retail trading platform to retail customers through an Internet Based application which would provide more transparency and better pricing to customers. This application helps customers access the market through their respective bankers (Relationship Banks). The Internet Based application is an electronic platform where the orders placed by retail customers are matched anonymously and automatically. The entities involved are Retail customers, Fx-clear members and their branches, & CCIL.

 

II. CCIL made this application “go live” on 5th August, 2019. Our Bank has, since beginning, been approved for implementation of the subject platform. Internal clearance for the bank joining the platform was duly given in the Board Meeting held on 24th July, 2019. However, just as the process prescribed by CCIL was to be taken up for implementation, the COVID-19 pandemic necessitated postponement of non-critical operations. Therefore, operationalizing the scheme through Bank and onboarding our customers was required to be kept in abeyance. Relative instructions on the subject (in respect of operationalization of the scheme and onboarding our customers) are now being issued in terms of the instant circular.

 

III.   1) CCIL will provide the URL for Bank/Branch/Customer so that a customer is enabled to use the Fx-Clear retail trading platform after his/her/their registration process is completed. The main (H.O.) forex trading branch on the platform for our Bank is Treasury Dept at Kurla (E). Two trading branches have been permitted to work on the platform. They are Foreign Exchange Dept, Dadar, & Vashi.

 

2) In order that work is conducted according to the Maker/Checker concept,

i)      Three admin users are to be assigned at Treasury Dept viz. AGM/Manager/Asst Manager etc.

ii)     Two users in the minimum are to be assigned at Trading branch level (Branch-In-Charge/Manager/Asst. Manager etc.)

 

IV.   Roles of Treasury Department, Kurla (E) & Trading Branches

1)  Role of Treasury Dept, Kurla (E)

i)     Addition of Trading Branches as per necessity & potential.

ii)     Setting up of Markup (Margin) & Assigning of Swap Quotes including forward points.

iii)   Maintenance of Currency Position.

iv)   Liaising with CCIL & Branches.

 

2)  Role of Trading Branches (Forex Dept., Dadar/Vashi)

i)          Customer Registration & limit setting in consultation with Treasury Dept, Kurla (E).

ii)     KYC Compliance for customers to be carried out & kept updated at all the times.

 

V.  A customer desirous of joining the platform needs to proceed as under:

i)          An online agreement shall be required to be executed between customer and CCIL

ii)    Additionally, one undertaking by the customer shall be got executed in favour of the Bank before the customer is registered at trading branch level. It will attract non-judicial stamp duty of Rs. 600/-. In case of a corporate, the undertaking should be additionally supported by a copy of the Board resolution, adopted in this behalf by their Board in its meeting, duly signed by Chairman of the Board/Company.

 

VI.   Charges / Timings etc.

i)         One-time registration charge of Rs.300/- for Individual & Rs.1000/- for Non-Individual customers (plus GST as applicable) to be collected by the branch from each customer.

ii)     Transaction amount of up to USD 50000 per day shall be exempted from any charges. However, charges for transactions beyond USD 50000 per day (cumulative for all Relationship Banks taken together if multiple Relationship Banks exist for a customer) shall be collected @0.0004% of INR value of the trade plus GST as applicable.

iii)   Charges as per Point VI (i) & (ii) above shall be collected by Trading Branches from their customers and remitted to Treasury Dept.

iv)   Only spot and forward transactions would be made available to customers as of now.

v)     The session timings for customers would be restricted from 9.00 am to 4.30 pm on working days subject to changes if any by RBI/CCIL/Relationship Bank. The platform will not be available on Saturdays, Sundays & Bank Holidays.

vi)   A customer shall not undertake speculative transactions in currencies. There must be an underlying obligation or commitment for the transactions conducted on the platform.

 

VII. The specimen of the undertaking which will be required to be obtained by the Trading Branch from the customer, is given in the Annexure hereto.

 

VIII.   Branches are advised to market availability of the platform among all the potential customers so that the customers & Bank are mutually benefitted.

 

IX.   Please take suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all members of the staff concerned at the Branch and ensure compliance.

 

 

P.S. Salian

Managing Director
