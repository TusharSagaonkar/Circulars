Forex Circular No.30 dated 20th October, 2011

Western Union Money Transfer

          1. Introduction    

In order to boost our fee based income, widen our customer network in India and abroad, Bank has entered into an arrangement with M/s UAE Exchange and Financial Services Ltd. as a sub-agent for their Money Transfer Products, ‘Xpress Money and ‘MoneyGram’.

The Reserve Bank of India has recently directed the Overseas Entities/Indian Agents authorised to operate in-bound cross border Money Transfer Service Scheme (MTSS) not to enter into any agreement with any entity regulated by Reserve Bank of India to function as Agent/ Sub-agent incorporating any kind of exclusivity clause in the contracts.  This has enabled us to offer multiple money transfer services through our branch network.

In terms of Board approval in its Meeting held on 19.10.2010 and consequent RBI approval, Bank has tied up  with M/s Weizmann Forex Ltd.(WFL), a representative of Western Union Financial Services Inc. USA for extending money Transfer Services of ‘Western Union’ through our branches.

2. The salient features of the product of Western Union Money Transfer (WUMT) are as under :

                         i)    Remitters (mainly NRIs) in foreign countries desirous of sending money instantly to their near and dear ones in India can use services of M/s Western Union Money Transfer who are one of the prominent Money transfer operators.

                       ii)    Western Union Money Transfer is a person to person Inward remittance product wherein M/s Weizmann Forex Ltd., is authorized under the Western Union Representation Agreement and also by the Reserve Bank of India under the India Money Transfer Service Scheme to provide money transfer service in India on behalf of Western Union.

                     iii)    Maximum amount of payout in India is restricted to US $ 2500 per transaction.

                      iv)    Payment to beneficiary in cash can be paid upto `.50,000/-(Rupees fifty  thousand only) per transaction.  Any amount above this limit will be paid by way of Bankers cheque or Demand draft or credit to beneficiarie’s account.  For foreign nationals who produce their  PassPort  with Valid Visa,  full amount over INR 50,000 can be paid in cash.  In case only refugee card ID is produced, amounts over INR 50,000 need to be paid through account payee Bankers cheque, DD, direct account transfers.   For foreign nationals also the maximum payout cannot exceed equivalent of US $ 2500.

                          v)  A restriction of a maximum of 12 transactions to be received in a calendar year by a single beneficiary.

                        vi)  Money received under this product cannot be credited to NRE, NRO account of the remitter.

                      vii)  All remittances should be only for personal use of beneficiaries and their family members and for foreign tourists visiting India.

                     viii)  Minor cannot be the beneficiary for the product of Western Union transactions as documents signed by minor do not have any validity in the court of Law.

                        ix)  Each Individual can be paid only once per day.  Exceptions (like Medical) should be taken up with the concerned officials of Weizmann Forex Ltd., / Western Union.

                       x)    Only Individual to Individual remittance is allowed.

                        xi)  No remittance under commercial transactions shall be allowed in India under this  product. 

                      xii)  Transactions are to be in the name of Individuals. Care should be taken to ensure that money is neither sent from nor paid to a firm, company or an Institution (charitable/otherwise)etc.

                    xiii)  Only International inbound money transfer is allowed. Money cannot be sent from India to other countries.

                     xiv)  Money transfer is not allowed within India.

                       xv)  Payments to be made in Indian Rupees only.         

                     xvi)  Commission payable to the bank will be remitted separately by M/s Weizmann Forex Ltd.  The commission on the transactions under no circumstances be debited to the account of M/s Weizmann Forex Ltd.

3.     User Identification At Branches

     All our branches will be allotted specific Terminal –ID by Western Union along with ‘On the terminal training’ by representatives from Weizmann Forex Ltd.(WFL).  Branches have to identify one officer and one clerical staff to handle the transactions.  The details of one officer and one clerical staff be provided to WFL for authorization and allotment of User-ID.  Each branch will be provided with the following security IDs by WFL.

  One terminal ID (Alphanumeric – 4 characters)

  One operator ID (Numeric – 3 characters)              

  One password    (Alphanumeric – 3 to 8 characters)

4.        Western Union Money Transfer Product will work as under:

A)   Process for SENDING the money by the Remitter

                             i)    Remitter approaches one of the outlets/agents of M/s Western Union Money Transfer in other countries.  He /She fills in prescribed form “To send Money Form” and pays the amount to be remitted in foreign currency along with the commission/charges .  

                           ii)    Remitter gets a Receipt and Money Transfer Control Number (MTCN)

                         iii)    MTCN along with the amount remitted is informed to the beneficiary by the remitter.

B) Process for RECEIVING the money in India.

                           i)     Receiver on receipt of the information of remittance along with the MTCN personally    approaches any authorised location of Western Union Money Transfer, fills up “To receive Money-(TRM) Form”. Produces valid ID Proof. Transaction is verified.  Receiver gets money and receipt. 

5.     Stepwise Procedure  to be followed at branch

The Beneficiary of the remittance after being informed by the sender of the MTCN would approach any of the Western Union agents.  Suppose a beneficiary approaches one of our branches, the concerned officer at the branch shall follow the following steps:

Step I

Deliver beneficiary a “To Receive Money Form” (TRM) (Yellow colour Top Border).  It is a replica of the TSM(To send money form), which is filled by the sender at the remitting country. TRM forms will be supplied to the branches in sufficient number by WFL.:4:

Step II

Request the beneficiary to fill the following details in the TRM.  The following details are mandatory which the beneficiary must provide.

i.   MTCN (CODE in 10 digits)

ii.  Name of the person who has sent the money.

iii. Place from where the money was sent.

iv. Receiver’s name and address with ID Proof.

v.  Amount expected.

The branch will not process a request if the customer does not give all the above mentioned details.

After receiving the duly filled in TRM form, the branch dealing official(s) must insist for valid identification as per step III and check its expiry, if any, before processing and making the payment.

Please note that if any of the above 5 details are found missing on the form or are found to be incorrect, the paying agent would be held liable in case payment made, has resulted into error.

Step III 

To avail protection for making payment in due course under Sec 10 of N.I. Act the KYC guidelines must be adhered to and therefore ID proof must be properly scrutinized along with the MTCN.  The IDs acceptable for the transaction are:

1.   Valid Passport.

2. Valid Driving license (Learners License is not accepted).

3. Ration Card with photograph.

4. Voter identity card

5. PAN Card

     6. Bank passbook with photograph (applicable only if customer has a Pass Book of our bank, if the Customer has any other Bank Pass Book the same is not valid)

     7. State Government and Central Government Employee Photo ID.  

However foreign nationals in India on a tour can also receive money from abroad and the identification document acceptable is a passport of any nation other than India with a Valid Visa.

ADDRESS PROOF ACCEPTABLE FOR TRANSACTIONS

(Where ID Card does not have the address of the beneficiary)

1. Valid Passport

2. Telephone Bill

3. Ration Card

4. Voter Identity Card

5. Driving License

6. Letter from employer (i.e. Government Dept., Public Sector Corporations, Bank and well known Companies)

7. ID card issued by Government to its employees

Step IV

The amount received should not be more than US$ 2500 or equivalent per transaction and the maximum number of remittances received by a single beneficiary should not be more than 12 in a year.   However this will be taken care of by Western Union.

Step V

The branch should log in to the Western Union site through the software provided by the WFL and check details of the remittances disclosed by the beneficiary on TRM.  The MTCN on the TRM furnished by the beneficiary must tally with the one displayed on the screen.

Step VI

If all the details in TRM tally with the details as displayed on the screen and the identity of the beneficiary has been ascertained as per step III and the concerned officer is satisfied about the genuineness of the beneficiary, branch shall block the payment on the system after filling all relevant details of the beneficiary.

Step VII

Take the screen print of the transaction and get the same authenticated by the beneficiary/ receiver.

Step VIII

Ensure that the following documents have been procured from the beneficiary.

1.   TRM form duly filled and signed by the beneficiary customer in triplicate.  The customer copy should be delivered to the beneficiary.  The other two copies should be retained.

2.   Copy of Photo ID and Address proof duly signed by the beneficiary. The copies should be authenticated by the dealing official at the branch after verifying the same with the originals.

3.   Two copies of the Screen-print Transaction will be generated through the system.  The customers copy will be handed over to the customer. The acknowledgement of the customer will be taken on the Banker’s copy.

Step IX

One copy of TRM (Sub –Agent copy) is attached with the original debit voucher as a permanent record for any future reference.

The second copy of TRM (original copy), copies of ID and address proof and Screen print transaction slip should be preserved in a separate file for facilitating replies to queries, dispute settlement and related correspondence.   This will be under the control of the dealing officer.

Important Points to be noted by Branches

                             i)  In no case a payment exceeding `.50,000/- is made in cash to any Indian citizen.  However amount above `.50,000/- can be made to a foreign national.

                           ii)  In no case, a payment is made if details of the sender or the details of the receiver are not available.

                         iii)  If the details on the TRM tendered by the beneficiary are not matching with the details appearing on the WUMT Screen, the payment shall not be made.

                          iv)  In no case payment is made to any other person other than the beneficiary himself.  No authority letter/ power of attorney or any other means is considered for payment to be made to third party.  The beneficiary must come personally.

                            v)  In no case a payment is made if the beneficiary fails to provide the proof of his identity.

8. De-Blocking of Payment

In case the branch has wrongly blocked a remittance or the identity of the beneficiary is not found satisfactory at the time of execution of payment, the transaction needs to be De-Blocked immediately.  For this purpose, the branch would be required to contact their Relationship Manager, WFL – 1800-22-5656 Helpline for necessary assistance or any of their queries on business.

9. Other Operational Instructions and MIS

                     i)        Western Union Money Transfer will be operational in all the branches excluding Mobile branch, Vatva and Udyavara.  Arrangements are being made to display signage boards in this regard in all our branches.

                   ii)        Branch Incharges are advised to bring this new MTSS product to the notice of all staff members so that as and when a receiver (beneficiary) approaches to any of the staff member at a branch, he/she(staff) is aware of the product and should also provide proper guidance and assistance as well in enabling the receiver to obtain payment without any hardship.

                   iii)      Branch Incharge will assign the duties related with Western Union Money Transfer payment to an Accountant/Asst.Accountant and staff at branch.  The said Accountant/Asst. Accountant and staff concerned shall update themselves of the various procedural aspects of Western Union Money Transfer. Guidelines provided in this Circular should be grasped carefully and a copy of this circular should be retained with the Branch Dealing Officer / staff and should be handed over to new incumbent(s).

                    iv)      Branch Incharge must ensure that proper stop gap arrangement are in place at the branch to extend uninterrupted services in the absence of regular officer –in-charge/staff due to leave/deputation on training etc.

                      v)      In order to raise standards of our customer service further high, Branch staff should ensure to make payment to receiver (beneficiary) with NO HASSLES and within the shortest possible time.

                    vi)      Paying branches shall not impose any fees or charges upon any Recipient and shall not split, share or discount charges, compensation or other benefits received for the Western Union Money Transfer service with or to any other customer.

                  vii)      Branches should not process any transaction for which they have information or doubt about it being a commercial transaction.  Call Centre of Western Union should be informed immediately about such transaction on Toll free number 1800 22 9222, 1800 22 5656(Weizmann Forex Ltd.), 1800 425 1851 (Western Union)

                   viii)    If any branch has already any information or receives any information regarding breach of any of the above guidelines, it is required that the same be immediately brought to the notice of Weizmann Forex Ltd., / Western Union Money Transfer.

                    ix)      To prevent frauds, there is an imminent need on the part of Branch Manager/ Accountant and all other staff members to remain vigilant.  They must not be simply guided by the appearances and looks of the receiver and should ascertain the identity of the receiver by all means to their satisfaction.

                     x)      Though suitable arrangements have been made to impart training to our staff members connected with the Western Union Money Transfer, to get further assistance in case of problems associated with Western Union Money Transfer, branches should contact Foreign Exchange Department (Dadar Branch) on Telephone Nos. 24384640/44/47.

                    xi)      Please note that Western Union Money Transfer is in addition to our existing two other money transfer arrangements viz. ‘Xpress Money’ and ‘MoneyGram’.

       10. Compliance

All the branches shall maintain records of Money Transfer in accordance with the Service Requirements.  Such records are the property of Western Union and are subject to audit and review by the network Agent, RBI and Western Union representative at any time.

These records are to be maintained for at least 10 years as per the Prevention of Money Laundering Act 2002, and any amendments thereto.

Branches cannot under any circumstances alter the amount paid to the receiver.  They cannot pay excess or short money to the receiver or indulge in

any promotional activity, which may lead to unfair advantage to the receiver.  Security of consumer information has to be ensured at all times.

All branches are committed to ensure compliance to Western Union Anti-Money Laundering Policy, which is as follows:

·                    To assist anyone through this service to launder money from any criminal source and to conceal the true ownership of the proceeds would be a crime.  Branches are committed to ensure strict monitoring of the service to prevent such misuse of the service.

·                    Branches are required to exercise Know Your Customer (KYC) and exercise the normal due diligence on all customers in accordance with RBI regulations.  Any violation of Exchange Control Regulation should not be resorted to.

·                    Situations that suggest Money Laundering like receipt of very frequent transfers to the same receiver over time (e.g. more than once a day) or the same individual using different names receives money regularly, should be monitored on a daily basis.

11.Reconcilation of Account

The product calls for highest degree of Reconciliation between payment branch and Nodal branch (Dadar, Mumbai). It is therefore advised that:   

a)       Each branch making payment under Western Union Money Transfer shall accord top priority to reconciliation of entries.

 

b) Nodal branch will cross verify the monthly statement of account received   fromWeizmann Forex Ltd with the entries in their Current account and reconcile the outstanding entries, if any, by prompt follow up.

12.Commission on Western Union Money Transfer

The applicable commission on Western Union Money Transfer will be originated at regular intervals by Nodal Branch to the respective branches.

 The branches should respond and credit the amount to the P& L - Commission on Western Union Money Transfer A/c.
