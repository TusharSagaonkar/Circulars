Forex Circular O.C. No. 5 dated 24th July, 2009

 

Money Transfer Service Scheme (MTSS)

Express Money of UAE Exchange and Financial Services Ltd.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

You are aware that our Bank has started Foreign Exchange Business since 18.02.2008. To develop and increase the Foreign Exchange Business, mobilization of Non-Resident Deposits is one of our thrust areas. To assist our branches, we were persistently endeavoring to develop tie-ups and correspondent relationship with other trustworthy financial agencies. Accordingly, the proposal for tie-up with UAE Exchange and Financial Services Ltd. has been approved by our Board in its meeting held on 27.08.2008 and also the arrangement has been approved by Reserve Bank of India vide their letter No. UBD/MRO/BSS/10105/12.07.006/2008-09 dated 30.06.2009, subject to observation of following guidelines :

 

2.       Guidelines issued by Reserve Bank of India for working of arrangement : 

                   i)      Only person to person remittances are permissible under these arrangements.  No company can avail facilities for remittance under this arrangement.

                  ii)      A single remittance should not exceed US$ 2500 or its equivalent in other currencies. 

                 iii)     Maximum 12 transactions are permitted for a single recipient/beneficiary during the calendar year.

                 iv)     Remittances up to Rs.50,000/- can be paid in cash. Remittances exceeding Rs.50,000/- should be paid by credit to S.B./C.D. A/c of the recipient/beneficiary or only by Cheque / Demand Draft / Pay Order without any charge.

 

UAE Exchange as well as our Bank will be responsible for observing KYC / AML norms and RBI guidelines. Our Dadar Branch will become the nodal branch for receiving all Inward Remittances from UAE Exchange under the strict supervision of our Foreign Exchange Dept. On receipt of remittance, Foreign Exchange Dept. will inform the concerned branch where the remittance will be effected. In case, the recipient / beneficiary approaches the paying branch after receiving information from the applicant directly then paying branch will seek instruction from Our Foreign Exchange Department who in turn will guide and monitor the branch regarding the transactions to be undertaken. 

UAE Exchange has opened a current account with our Dadar Branch in which sufficient funding will be done by them so that their payment instructions are undertaken at all of our branches.  The arrangement will work at all the branches of our Bank. 

3.      Arrangement will work as under :

               i)      A person in a Foreign Country approaches UAE Exchange or its correspondent Banks and applies for remittance of funds and makes payment along with Service Charges in foreign currency. UAE Exchange or its correspondence will then convert the amount in Indian Rupees at their prevailing exchange rates.

              ii)      Applicant will give name of the recipient/beneficiary and will also mention name of the paying Bank and its branch wherefrom the recipient/beneficiary will receive the funds.

             iii)     On receipt of funds the Receiving Bank/Remitting Bank will provide a Xpin Number to the applicant.

             iv)     The applicant will inform this Xpin number to the person whose name he/she has given in the application as recipient/beneficiary.

              v)      If applicant has given our Bank's branch name in the application, the Recipient/Beneficiary will approach our concerned branch.

             vi)     The Paying Branch will then provide to the recipient / beneficiary "Xpress Money Service Receive –Now" Form for filling Xpin number and all other details of remittance.

             vii)    The Recipient/Beneficiary will be asked to provide photo ID proof for verification. As an extra care, branch may also take residence proof, however residence proof is not mandatory. If the recipient/beneficiary is our customer and the branch has already complied with the KYC norms as per our Bank’s policy, the amount of remittance will be credited to his/her account without insisting upon ID Proof/Residence proof.

            viii)    The Paying Branch will then forward to Dadar Branch either through Exchange Server or by fax a copy of "Xpress Money Service-Receive –Now" Form duly certifying that photo ID, residence proof, etc. of the recipient / beneficiary has been verified for authorization and clearance of payment. However, it should be noted that the copies of original documents obtained from the recipient / beneficiary should be retained by the Paying Branch.

             ix)     Dadar Branch will then log on UAE Exchange website using Internet Explorer and confirm the transaction. Once, confirmation is received for this transaction from UAE Exchange, Dadar Branch will debit the amount to UAE Exchange account and credit the amount to Foreign Exchange Collection Account (FECA) and will then advise to Paying Branch to effect the payment by debiting FECA.

              x)      In case of non-customer, remittance only upto Rs.50,000/- will be made in cash to the recipient/ beneficiary. It should be noted that remittance in excess of Rs.50,000/- should not be paid in cash. It should be paid by our Pay Order/Demand Draft without any charge. Request for part payment in cash and part payment by DD/PO should not be entertained. If recipient/beneficiary is our customer, the amount of remittance will necessarily be credited to his/her account.

4.       Our Foreign Exchange Department at Dadar will coordinate with UAE Exchange and Financial Services Ltd. and arrange for circulation of all necessary forms and make arrangement for providing stationery required for undertaking MTSS transactions. They will also arrange for a training programme for at least 2 staff members from each branch, one each from the category of clerk and Accountant/Manager before launching this product. Money Transfer Service Scheme (MTSS) will start functioning w.e.f. 3rd August, 2009, which please note.

          Branches are advised to go through the above instructions and be in preparedness for launching the product w.e.f. 3rd August, 2009.

          Please take a suitable note of these instructions and bring the contents of this Circular to the notice of all the concerned staff at your branch.
