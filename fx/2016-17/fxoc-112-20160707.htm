FOREX CIRCULAR No. 112 Dated 07th July, 2016

Issuance of Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Please refer Forex Circular No.15 dated 28.12.2010 on the issuance of Foreign Inward Remittance Certificate. It deals with the purposes for which FIRCs may be issued and also a clarification with regard to issuing of FIRCs for inward remittances relating to the credit to NRE accounts.

 

Reserve Bank of India vide its AP (DIR Series) Circular No, 74 dated 26.05.2016 expanded the scope of Export Data Processing & Monitoring System (EDPMS). Foreign Exchange Dealers’ Association of India (FEDAI) vide their Circular SPL-09/2016 dated 08.06.2016 has informed that the additional modules of the EDPMS would capture details of all Inward Remittances including advance against exports. EDPMS would also handle issuance of e-FIRC wherever necessary.

 

FEDAI has, therefore, advised all the member banks to discontinue with immediate effect issuance of FIRC for any export related payment. FEDAI has also stated that FIRC may be issued ONLY for inward remittance covering FDI/FII (Foreign Direct Investment/Foreign Institutional Investment). Such FIRCs should clearly show the validity period of maximum one year. In all other cases of inward remittances, there is no provision for issuing FIRCs.

 

In our Bank, all the designated branches for direct handling of forex transactions Dadar, Vashi, Fort, Pimpri & Mithakhali are authorised to issue FIRCs at the request of the beneficiaries for inward remittances.  All these designated branches are advised to note the above and discontinue issuing FIRCs with immediate effect for any purpose except for the purpose of FDI/FII.

 

The in-charge of the branches are advised to note the above important change with regard to the issuance of FIRCs and bring the contents of this circular to the notice of all the members of the staff and customers concerned.
