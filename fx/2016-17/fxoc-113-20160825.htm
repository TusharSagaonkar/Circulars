Forex Circular No. 113 dated 25th August, 2016

Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010 – Receipt of foreign contribution by Individuals/NGOs/ Organizations from certain donors

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Under Section 46 of the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010 Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), Government of India forwards to Reserve Bank of India a list of donor agencies which are placed under “prior permission category” and any funds flow from these donor agencies to any individual/NGOs/Association has to be effected only after clearance from the Foreigners’ Division, FCRA Wing, Ministry of Home Affairs, Govt of India, New Delhi.

 

Vide Forex Circular No. 89 dated 18.02.2015 we have provided the following list of Donor Agencies placed under “ prior permission category” stating that remittances from them should be credited to the accounts of the beneficiaries only after clearance from the MHA as mentioned above:

 

1) Danish International Development Agency (DANIDA)

2) Danish Institute of Human Rights (DIHR)

3) Catholic Organization for Relied and Development Aid (CORDAID)

4) Dan Church Aid ( DCA)

5) Mercy Corps,USA

6) Inter Chyurch Peace Council_ Pax Christi ( IKV-PC),Netherlands

7) HIVOS, Netherlands

8) ICCO Stretegische Samenwerking (ICCO), Netherlands

9) Green Peace International

10) Climate Work Foundation (CWF),US

 

Now, Reserve Bank of India vide their communication No. DCBR. BPD AML.42/ 12.05.001/ 2016-17 dated 01.07.2016 have advised the following additional donor agencies instructing that any fund flow from them to any person/NGO/organisation should be brought to the notice of MHA so that the funds are allowed to be credited to the accounts of the recipient ONLY after clearance from MHA, FCRA Wing.

The names of the additional donor agencies:

11) Open Society Foundation (OSF);

12) World Movement for Democracy(WMD);

13) National Endowment for Democracy (NED), United States of America.

 

The Designated branches which are handling forex business are hereby advised to note the above and remittances from any of the above mentioned donor agencies are handled only after compliance with the MHA guidelines in this regard.

Our RTGS/NEFT Department is hereby advised to make a note of the above 13 donor agencies which have been furnished above and bring to the notice of Forex Department, Dadar details of any remittances received from these donor agencies for their clearance before passing the credits to the accounts of the beneficiaries.

All the branches and concerned departments are advised to bring contents of this Circular to the notice of the staff members and customers.

 

 

P. R. Shetty

Managing Director
