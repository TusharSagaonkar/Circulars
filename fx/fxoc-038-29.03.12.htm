Forex Circular No. 38 dated 29th March, 2012

 

Anti-Money Laundering (AML)/Combating the Financing

of Terrorism (CFT) Standards – Money Changing Activities

and Cross Border Inward Remittance under Money Transfer

Service Scheme (MTSS)

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 

Financial Action Task Force (FATF), Paris is a global body setting standards for anti-money laundering and combating the financing of terrorism (AML/ CFT). It is ensuring that all the countries follow the AML/CFT standards in order to protect the international financial system from Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing (ML/TF) risks.

 

In its Public Statement dated 28 October 2011, FATF has identified jurisdictions that have strategic deficiencies in AML/CFT regime. Reserve Bank of India vide their A.P.( DIR Series) Circular Nos. 77 & 78 both dated February 15, 2012 and UBD.CO.BPD (AD) Cir N0.5/14.01.062/2011-12 dated 23.02.2012 has advised us to consider the information contained in the said  Public Statement.

 

According to their Statement, FATF has identified the following countries that have strategic deficiencies that pose a risk to the international financial system:

 

Iran, Democratic People’s Republic of Korea ( North Korea), Cuba, Bolivia, Ethiopia, Kenya, Myanmar, Nigeria, Sao Tome and Principe, Sri Lanka, Syria, Turkey

 

Also, the FATF is working with the following countries in addressing the identified deficiencies:

 

Algeria, Angola, Antigua and Barbuda, Argentina, Bangladesh, Brunei Darussalam, Cambodia, Ecuador, Honduras, Kyrgyzstan, Mongolia, Morocco, Namibia , Nepal, Nicaragua, Paraguay, Philippines, Sudan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Trinidad and Tobago, Ukraine, Venezuela, Vietnam, Yemen, Zimbabwe.

 

All the branches are advised to note the above and refer to the Foreign Exchange Department, Dadar, Mumbai before undertaking any Money Changing activities or MTSS transactions originating from the above mentioned countries or any other forex transaction with any entities in the above mentioned countries.
