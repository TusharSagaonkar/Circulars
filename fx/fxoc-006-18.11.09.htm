Forex Circular O.C. No. 6 dated 18th November, 2009

 

Money Transfer Service Scheme (MTSS)

Extending the MoneyGram Product in the branches

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A reference is invited to Forex Circular OC No. 5 dated 24.07.2009 on Money Transfer Service Scheme.

 

In terms of the Board's approval in its Meeting held on 27.08.2008 our Bank has entered into an Agreement with the UAE Exchange and Financial Services Ltd. (UAEEFSL) as Service Provider (Sub-Agent) for giving service in its Products of Money Transfer Services viz. ‘Xpress Money’ and ‘MoneyGram’ through our branches. The arrangement was approved by Reserve Bank of India vide their letter No. UBD/MRO/BSS I/10105/12.07.006/2008-09 dated 30.06.2009.

 

UAEEFSL is a Public Limited Company incorporated under the Companies Act 1956 having its registered office at Bengaluru and its Head Office at Kochi, Kerala.  The Company is providing Foreign Inward Money Transfer Services through its network of branches and its service providers (Sub Agents) in India under the product name ‘MoneyGram’, as an agent of M/s MoneyGram Payment Systems, Inc., USA which is a leading global company providing payment services.

 

Out of their two products as mentioned above, 'Xpress Money' product has already been launched successfully at all our branches w.e.f. 03.08.2009 vide our Forex Circular O.C. No. 5 dated 24.07.2009 referred to above. Now we have decided to launch their another product - 'MoneyGram' the features and guidelines of which are as under :-

 

            Salient Features of the Product – MoneyGram

1.   MoneyGram is a foreign Inward remittance facility in which money transfer is effected within few minutes using the web / internet based technology.

2.   Only personal remittances such as remittances sent by NRIs staying abroad to meet maintenance expenses of their family members staying in India and remittances favouring foreign tourists visiting India are permissible under the arrangement.

3.   Trade related remittances, remittances towards purchase of immovable properties, Investments or credit to NRE / FCNR accounts etc.  or donations / contributions to charitable organizations are NOT PERMITTED to be routed under the scheme.

4.   Payments   to the recipient / beneficiary shall be made in Indian Rupees.

5.   Daily transaction limit for an individual is USD 2500.  A single remittance shall not exceed USD 2500 or its equivalent in other currencies.

6.   Remittances received upto Rs.50,000/- can be paid in cash.  Remittances exceeding Rs.50,000/- should be paid by credit  to personal SB / CD account of the recipient / beneficiary or by Cheque / Demand Draft / Pay Order without any commission / charges.  We cannot make the payment in part i.e. some portion in cash and remaining in cheque.

7.   Maximum 12 transactions are permitted in favour of a single recipient / beneficiary during the calendar year (January to December).

 

Arrangement will work as under:

 

         I.    The MoneyGram payment locations all over the world sending the remittances are called   ‘SEND AGENTS’ and the branches of our Bank making payments are called ‘RECEIVE  AGENTS’.

 

       II.    The Remitter in the Foreign Country visits any ‘Send Agent’ and remits money in foreign currency alongwith service charges.  He/She also gives the name of the recipient / beneficiary.  This amount is converted into Indian Rupees at their prevailing exchange rate and details of the remitter are entered on the MoneyGram website.

 

     III.    On receipt of funds the ‘Send Agent’ will provide a 8 digit Reference Number to the remitter which is generated by the MoneyGram system.

 

     IV.    The remitter will inform the beneficiary about the remittance and also pass on the 8 digit reference number provided by Send Agent.

 

       V.    The recipient/beneficiary approaches the ‘Receive Agent’ (i.e. our paying branch) and  furnishes  them the  8 digit Reference number.

 

     VI.    The branch should request the beneficiary to submit Photo ID proof for verification. As an extra care branch may also take residence proof, however residence proof is not mandatory.  This photo identity proof is to be obtained even though the customer maintains regular account with the Branch.

 

    VII.    After establishing beneficiary’s identity as per KYC norms, branch should handover the MoneyGram Receive Form (which is in triplicate) to the beneficiary for filling in the particulars and also request to attach the copies of the identity proof.  The Recipient/beneficiary has to record the FULL ADDRESS of   the sender/receiver   in the ‘Receive Form”. It may be noted that MoneyGram doesn’t accept address like “CHS” ‘Rd’ etc. This is not a complete and full address of the customer.  The branch should also affix their branch name or round stamp in the column ‘Office Name/Stamp”. This is required for branch identification.

     

  VIII.    The branch official who verifies and satisfies with the Photo ID Proof submitted by the beneficiary as per the KYC norms should note on the Photostat copy “verified with the original” and should append his /her full signature.      

 

 

     IX.    The paying branch will then forward to Foreign Exchange Dept., Dadar by fax  (022 24384650) a copy of “MoneyGram Receive Form” duly signed by the Accountant / Manager/Sr. Manager/Asst. General Manager at the place available on right hand bottom of the form alongwith photo ID proof of the recipient / beneficiary for authorization  and  clearance of payment.  However it should be noted that the copies of original documents obtained from the recipient / beneficiary should be retained by the paying branch. It may be noted that all transaction records including the completed Send/Receive forms, Photo ID, etc. needs to be maintained by our Bank atleast for 5 years as Bank's Record.

 

       X.    Foreign Exchange Department, Dadar will then log on MoneyGram website using  Internet Explorer and check  Sender  and Receiver information in MoneyGram  System and input the receive data on form.  Once the transaction is confirmed the system will generate a 5 digit Authorization Code which is to be copied on the 'MoneyGram Receive Form' below the column Agent's use only. Then Foreign Exchange Department, will originate the credit entry through Dadar Branch in favour of 'Receive Agent' Branch (i.e. Paying Branch) by debiting the amount to current account No. CD 300 of UAEEFSL maintained at Dadar branch to effect payment to the beneficiary. The Foreign Exchange Department, Dadar will  also inform the Authorisation code to the paying branch for noting the same on the  ‘MoneyGram Receive Form’

 

     XI.    The ‘MoneyGram Receive Form’ contains 3 copies.  The top copy is to be sent to Foreign Exchange Department, Dadar, with signature as mentioned above, the Middle copy shall be retained at the branch with details of payment made to the beneficiary under authentication of Branch Officials. The third copy shall be handed over to the beneficiary after effecting the payment. In case, cash payment (below Rs.50,000/-) is made to beneficiary, discharge on Debit Voucher should be obtained on revenue stamp as per existing rules of Stamp Act.

           

Compliance of KYC/AML Norms and Nodal Branch.

 

          Both UAEEFSL and our Bank will be responsible for observing KYC/AML norms and follow all the existing guidelines as well as guidelines to be issued from time to time by RBI.  The Nodal Branch for this arrangement is Dadar Branch. The Foreign Exchange Department attached with Dadar Branch will be responsible for generation of required reports from MoneyGram website. 

      

Prefunding and Settlement

           The Dadar Branch shall maintain the current account in the name of ‘UAE Exchange and Financial Services Ltd’.  The current account shall be pre-funded by UAEEFSL. The Foreign Exchange Department attached with Dadar Branch shall ensure that no payment is effected on behalf of UAE Exchange without holding sufficient balance in their current account maintained with the said branch.

 

Commission

       The Bank will be entitled to 70% of the commission and exchange Income received by the UAEEFSL from ‘MoneyGram’ for every MoneyGram remittance.  This commission shall be settled by UAEEFSL on a monthly basis after deducting applicable taxes as per laws.

 

         Our Foreign Exchange Department at Dadar will coordinate with UAEEFSL and arrange for training programme for atleast one staff member from each branch. The stationery including ‘MoneyGram Receive Forms’ and stickers to be displayed have been supplied to the branches alongwith the ‘start-up kit’ of ‘Xpress Money’ product. UAEEFSL will be shortly supplying sign boards to the branches. The Branch In-charges are requested to co-operate with UAE Officials for displaying of the sign boards at a prominent place, possibly at the entrance of the branches. UAEEFSL will also install at our Foreign Exchange Department, Dadar, a client software programme – viz DeltaWorks which is a Windows based programme for undertaking MoneyGram transactions.

 

UAEEFSL will also arrange for Agent ID and PIN to the Authorised Officials at the Foreign Exchange Department. This Agent ID and PIN is required to process the MoneyGram transactions.
